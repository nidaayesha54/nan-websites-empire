❓ Frequently asked questions

What is an AI voice generator?

An AI voice generator converts written text into spoken audio using neural text-to-speech models. Modern tools like ElevenLabs, Murf, and Play.ht produce voices nearly indistinguishable from humans.

Which AI voice generator is best in 2026?

ElevenLabs leads on realism and voice cloning, Murf is easiest for video voiceovers, Play.ht is cheapest at scale, LOVO suits ad-style marketing voices, and Speechify is best for read-aloud listening. Take our quiz to find your fit.

How much does AI voice generation cost?

Free tiers cover 5–15 minutes monthly. Paid plans start around $5–$25/month. Use our cost calculator for exact numbers at your volume.

Can I use AI voices for YouTube monetization?

Yes, but YouTube requires disclosure of AI-generated content and rejects low-effort, repetitive mass-produced videos. Add original commentary, editing, and value to stay monetized.

GTA 6 Lucia Mission Stuck? The Save Fix That Works

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

There is a bug in Grand Theft Auto 6 that can stop your story cold: an early Lucia mission, right after leaving a bank, can softlock — the mission simply will not let you progress. No button works, no marker appears, and the game just sits there. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and this softlock is one of the first genuine bugs players have found. There is no official fix from Rockstar yet. But there is a verified workaround, and it is the same habit that protects you from almost every other launch problem in this game: your manual save. This guide explains the workaround step by step, and how to tell a real softlock from a mission that is simply waiting for you to earn more money.

First, the most important distinction. Not every "stuck" story in GTA 6 is a bug. Some missions are money-gated: the next mission only opens once Jason and Lucia have earned enough money, a detail confirmed by Rockstar North's Rob Nelson speaking via Famitsu. If no mission marker appears and your cash is thin, the game is not broken — it is waiting for you to earn. Go rob a jewelry store, sell a car, or dive for treasure, and the marker will appear. A softlock is different: you are inside a mission, usually right after leaving a bank as Lucia, and the game will not advance no matter what you do. That is the problem this guide fixes.

The Verified Workaround: Reload and Restart

The workaround for the Lucia softlock is straightforward, and it is verified by launch reporting. Reload your last manual save from before the mission started, then restart the console fully — a real power cycle, not rest mode. On PS5, that means closing the game completely, powering the console down, and turning it back on. On Xbox, fully quit the game before relaunching. Then play the mission again from your save. Players who have done this report the mission progressing normally on the retry. There is no official fix yet, so this is the best available recovery — and it works precisely because the manual save is the one thing the game's unreliable autosave cannot give you.

That is why the save you reload matters so much. If your only save is an autosave from inside the broken mission, reloading it puts you back in the same stuck spot. The habit that saves you is keeping a separate manual save slot from before any tricky mission — one slot you never overwrite until the mission is safely done. GTA 6 gives you fifteen manual save slots, and the rule is simple: Pause, go to Game, then Save Game, before every mission that looks risky, and again after every mission that goes well. Rotate two or three slots so a bad save never strands you.

Why the Autosave Cannot Save You Here

The reason this workaround depends on manual saves is that GTA 6's autosave cannot be trusted. Autosave only fires after a mission ends — the prologue and mission checkpoints do not trigger it — and autosaves only appear in the Load Game menu, never in the Save Game menu. Crashes have already cost players hours of progress, including streamer JasonTheWeen, who lost around ten hours. If the softlock hits you and your last save is an autosave from after the mission started, you have no clean point to return to. A manual save from before the mission is the only way back.

Money-Gated Missions: When the Game Is Waiting, Not Broken

Before you declare a softlock, check your money. Some missions in GTA 6 are gated behind cash: the next mission marker only appears once Jason and Lucia have earned enough. If you are standing in Vice City with no marker and a thin wallet, the game is not stuck — it is waiting. The verified ways to earn are the same ones that fund everything else in the game: jewelry store robberies pay around $20,000 each — Victoria Lee Jeweler in Capri, and Blanc and Giroux in the Decor District, with a snap gun from Chapter 3 breaking in silently. Stealing cars and selling them right next to a Pay 'N' Spray is the low-effort staple. Kenton's treasure dives pay around $1,400 per wreck with almost no risk, once they unlock after the mission "Island Dreams".

The big bank vaults pay up to $205,000, but only at the end of Chapter 4 — they are the endgame, not the early fix. For a money gate, the mid-tier methods are the answer. Earn, watch for the marker, and only if the marker still does not appear after earning should you suspect a bug. And protect the money you earn: pocket cash is lost when you die or get arrested, and it does not carry into Chapter 2. Stash spare money under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom, and open the BuckMe bank account once the mission "Buck Me" is available, then deposit your cash at ATMs. Banked money is safe.

What to Do If the Workaround Fails

If reloading a pre-mission manual save and restarting the console does not fix the softlock, try an older save — a slot from further back, before you entered the mission area at all. The bug appears tied to the state of the mission when it started, so a save from before that state is the cleanest reset. If you have no manual save at all, your options are limited: try a full console restart and replay from the nearest autosave in the Load Game menu, and be prepared for the possibility that you will need to replay a stretch of the story. That is the honest truth — and it is exactly why the manual-save habit matters so much in a game where missions cannot be replayed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Lucia softlock fixed by Rockstar yet?

No — there is no official fix yet. The verified workaround is to reload your last manual save from before the mission and restart the console fully. Keep a manual save slot from before tricky missions so you always have a clean point to return to.

How do I know if my mission is softlocked or money-gated?

A softlock happens inside a mission — usually right after leaving a bank as Lucia — and the mission will not advance. A money gate happens between missions: no marker appears because Jason and Lucia have not earned enough. If you are between missions with no marker, check your cash and go earn before assuming a bug.

The Bottom Line

The Lucia softlock is real, and there is no official fix yet — but the workaround works: reload a manual save from before the mission, restart the console fully, and play it again. The deeper lesson is the same one GTA 6 keeps teaching on launch week: save manually after every mission, keep a slot in reserve before the risky ones, and never trust the autosave. Five seconds of saving is the cheapest insurance in Vice City.

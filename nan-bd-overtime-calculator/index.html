Overtime Rules in Bangladesh

The standard workweek in Bangladesh is 48 hours — 8 hours a day over 6 days. Any work beyond that is overtime, payable at twice the ordinary rate of wages under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006. The accepted conversion is monthly basic salary ÷ 208 (208 = 26 working days × 8 hours) to find the hourly wage.

Overtime Formula

Overtime Pay = (Monthly Basic Salary ÷ 208) × 2 × Overtime Hours
The hourly wage is your basic salary divided by 208; the overtime rate is double that, multiplied by every OT hour worked.

Example

Basic salary BDT 20,000, 20 overtime hours: (20,000 ÷ 208) = BDT 96.15/hr; overtime rate = BDT 192.31/hr; total OT pay = 192.31 × 20 = BDT 3,846.

Legal Limits

  • No worker may work more than 10 hours in a day including overtime, or 60 hours in a week including overtime (Section 102).
  • Average weekly hours including overtime must not exceed 56 hours over the year.
  • Overtime is calculated on basic salary — not gross salary with allowances.
  • Employers must keep a record of all overtime hours worked.
  • Work beyond 8 hours/day or 48 hours/week automatically counts as overtime.

FAQs

What is the overtime rate in Bangladesh?

Double (2×) the ordinary rate of wages under the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 — the same for workers in factories, shops and most establishments.

Why divide by 208?

208 = 26 working days × 8 hours per month. It is the standard convention to convert a monthly basic salary into an hourly wage for OT computation.

Is overtime paid on gross salary or basic?

On basic salary — house rent, conveyance, medical and other allowances are not part of the hourly wage base.

How much overtime can I work in a week?

A maximum of 60 hours per week including overtime (so up to 12 OT hours beyond the standard 48), and no more than 10 hours in a single day.

Do managers get overtime?

Overtime provisions cover workers under the Labour Act; employees in higher managerial or decision-making positions are generally exempt.

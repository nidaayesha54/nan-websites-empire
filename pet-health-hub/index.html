Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about pet wellness tracking and this site.

Is PetHealthHub a substitute for my veterinarian?

No. PetHealthHub provides wellness information and tracking tools only. It is not a substitute for professional veterinary advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Always see your vet for medical concerns.

Is the vaccination tracker free to use?

Yes. The vaccination tracker, weight tracker, and medication reminder tools are completely free and run in your browser. Your pet's data is saved privately on your own device.

What vaccinations does my puppy need in the USA?

Core puppy vaccines in the US include DHPP (distemper, adenovirus, parainfluenza, parvovirus) and rabies, typically given in a series from 6 to 16 weeks of age. See our vaccination tracker and 2026 puppy schedule guide for details, and confirm with your vet.

How often should I weigh my pet?

Most vets recommend weighing adult dogs and cats monthly, and puppies and kittens every 1–2 weeks while growing. Use our free weight tracker to log weights and spot trends early.

What should be in a pet emergency kit?

A pet emergency kit should include 3+ days of food and water, medications, a leash and carrier, vaccination records, a first-aid kit, and your vet's contact information. Read our Pet Emergency Preparedness Guide for the full checklist.

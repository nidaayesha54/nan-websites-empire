Frequently Asked Questions

When is a dog considered senior?

Small breeds are usually considered senior around age 10–12, medium breeds at 8–10, large breeds at 7–8, and giant breeds as early as 5–6. Use our senior age calculator for a precise life stage.

How often should a senior dog see the vet?

Most senior dogs benefit from checkups every 6 months instead of once a year, since age-related changes can progress quickly. Build a wellness checklist for a personalized schedule.

How much does a senior dog cost per year?

Senior dogs typically cost $1,000–$2,500 per year in the USA, including vet exams, bloodwork, food, and supplements. Our cost estimator breaks it down by size.

What should I feed my senior dog?

Senior dogs usually do best on lower-calorie, highly digestible food with joint-supporting nutrients like glucosamine and omega-3s. Read our senior food guide for details.

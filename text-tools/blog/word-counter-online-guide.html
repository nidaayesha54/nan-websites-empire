Why Word Count Matters

Essays & assignments: Teachers set word limits to test conciseness. Going 10% over or under can cost marks — always check before submitting.

SEO: Longer, complete content tends to rank better for competitive keywords. But padding hurts — every word should earn its place.

Social media: Every platform has hard limits. A post cut off mid-sentence looks unprofessional.

Reading time: Readers decide in seconds whether to commit. "5 min read" converts better than a wall of text with no estimate.

Character & Word Limits Cheat Sheet (2026)

  • X/Twitter post: 280 characters
  • Instagram caption: 2,200 characters (125 visible before "more")
  • LinkedIn post: 3,000 characters
  • Facebook post: 63,206 characters (but 400 is the engagement sweet spot)
  • TikTok caption: 4,000 characters
  • YouTube description: 5,000 characters (first 150 show in search)
  • Meta description (SEO): ~155 characters
  • SEO title tag: ~60 characters
  • College essay: 250–650 words (Common App: 650 max)
  • Blog post (SEO): 1,200–2,000 words

Frequently Asked Questions

How many words is a typical blog post?
Most SEO-focused blog posts run 1,200–2,000 words. News articles are 300–800 words. In-depth guides often exceed 2,500 words.
What is the ideal word count for SEO?
There is no magic number, but pages ranking on page one of Google average around 1,400–1,900 words for competitive topics. Quality and completeness matter more than raw count.

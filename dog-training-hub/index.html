❓ Dog Training FAQs

Quick answers to the questions every dog owner asks.

When should I start training my puppy?

Start the day your puppy comes home, usually around 8 weeks old. Keep sessions to 3–5 minutes, several times a day, using treats and praise. Early training shapes habits before bad ones form.

How long should dog training sessions be?

5–10 minutes for adult dogs, 3–5 minutes for puppies. Short, fun, frequent sessions beat one long boring session every time. Always end on a win.

Is crate training cruel?

No — when introduced properly, dogs see the crate as a safe den. Never use it as punishment, keep sessions positive and gradual, and follow our crate training guide for the exact steps.

How do I stop my dog from barking so much?

First find the trigger: boredom, alarm, fear or attention-seeking. Then train a quiet cue, reward silence, and remove or desensitize the trigger. Our stop-barking guide walks you through it.

Do I need treats to train my dog?

Treats speed learning enormously, especially for puppies. Use small, high-value treats at first, then gradually replace them with praise, play and real-life rewards like going outside.

Can an old dog learn new tricks?

Absolutely. Dogs of any age learn with positive reinforcement. Senior dogs may learn a bit slower, but training also keeps their minds sharp — try our command tracker to log their progress.

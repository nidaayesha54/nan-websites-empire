GTA 6 Stats: Strength, Cardio and Focus Explained

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Jason and Lucia run on three stats, and the game never quite explains them in one place. Strength is your maximum health. Cardio is your stamina. Focus is the slow-motion time that lets you line up a shot in a gunfight. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and this guide explains all three: what each one does, how to raise it, the rule that makes two of them fade if you stop training, and the five-dollar trick that farms the third.

It matters because the stats are not flavour — they are the difference between surviving a shootout and not. A character with full Strength takes more damage before going down. A character with full Cardio sprints further and recovers faster. And a character with full Focus gets more slow-motion seconds in every fight. The players who train early will feel the difference in every mission.

Three Stats, Level 4 Maximum

All three stats max out at level 4. Strength, shown as a pink heart, is your maximum health — more Strength means you can take more damage before you go down, and it comes from weight training and pull-ups. Cardio, shown as a green lightning bolt, is your stamina for sprinting and other sustained activity, and it comes from running, cycling, swimming, kayaking and gym cardio. Focus, shown as a purple eye, gives you more time in slow motion during combat, and it comes from shooting-range challenges, fishing, sports and meditation.

The three stats are tracked separately for Jason and Lucia, and each character trains on their own — though if both of them go to the gym together, they both train, no matter which one you are playing. Your character's appearance changes with the training too: regular workouts build muscle, and neglecting workouts while over-eating puts body weight on. The Fitness app on your character's phone shows your stats, your muscle and body fat, and when your next workout window opens.

The Fade Rule: Strength and Cardio Slip Away

Here is the rule that catches everyone out: Strength and Cardio fade if you stop training. Each stat has a workout window — roughly once a day, shown on the Fitness app — and if you miss the window, the level slips. It takes about three or four full workouts to level a stat up by one, and the max level is 4, but missing windows costs you the gains. The practical rhythm is to train at least every two in-game days while you are building up, and every three days once you are at the max — daily if you are still climbing.

Two trophies are built around this rule. Strength Regime requires Jason and Lucia to hold level 4 Strength together for five consecutive in-game days. Cardio Regime uses the same five-day rule for Cardio. That means the endgame is not just reaching level 4 — it is holding it, both characters, for five days straight, without missing a window. Plan for it: the gym is a habit, not a one-time visit.

Focus: The Stat That Never Fades

Focus is different. Every Focus level you earn is yours to keep — it never fades, never slips, and never needs maintenance. That makes it the best stat to farm early, and there is a cheap way to do it. Go to Rodgers Ranch and Shooting Range on the north-eastern edge of Port Gellhorn in Kelly County, pay the clerk $5 for a waiver pass, and choose the Market Range Beginner challenge. The challenge takes about five seconds, and it awards Focus every time you complete it. Repeat it, and the Focus bar grows.

Fishing, golf, pool, basketball and meditation also raise Focus — the calm activities are the point, and the game is explicit about it: the mindful stuff builds Focus. Petting dogs and cats raises Focus too, while littering costs it — the game tracks good behaviour and bad, and the small choices add up. Eating, sleeping and showering, by contrast, only give buffs for the current session. Focus Mode itself — click both sticks — slows time in combat and highlights enemies in red, interactable objects in yellow and cash in green, and a bigger Focus bar means more slow-motion seconds in every fight.

Where to Train, and What It Costs

The cheapest gym in the game is at home. In Chapter 1, Jason's house in the Keys has weights on the terrace — a bench press, a deadlift bar and a set of kettlebells — and that is where you build Strength for free before the story moves on. Cardio is free everywhere: leave the car parked for a short trip and run or cycle there instead, and the stamina builds itself. Gyms later in the game reportedly require memberships, and the gym minigame is all triggers: squeeze both fast, let go slowly, finish the set before stamina runs out, then eat or drink something with protein to bank the gains. Always finish a workout, or the gains are lost.

Food and drink tie into the same system. Eating and drinking before heading out buffs health, stamina and Focus, and you can eat or drink while driving, shooting or running — so the morning routine is the cheapest stat menu in the game: a coffee for a stamina buff, a protein shake for health, then train. A protein shake taken before training grants a temporary stamina and strength boost worth roughly two extra reps per set, according to launch-week reporting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the highest level for stats in GTA 6?

Level 4, for all three stats. Strength and Cardio can slip if you miss workout windows, so they need maintenance. Focus levels are permanent — once earned, they stay.

How do I raise Focus fast in GTA 6?

Pay $5 at Rodgers Ranch and Shooting Range in Kelly County and repeat the Market Range Beginner challenge — it takes about five seconds and awards Focus each time. Fishing, golf, pool, basketball, meditation and petting dogs and cats also raise it.

Do stats fade in GTA 6?

Strength and Cardio do — miss a workout window and the level slips, reducing your maximum health and stamina. Focus never fades. The Fitness app on your phone shows when each workout window opens.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 stats are three bars with two rules: Strength and Cardio max at level 4 and fade if you stop training, Focus maxes at level 4 and stays forever. Train at Jason's house for free in Chapter 1, farm Focus for $5 at the shooting range, and check the Fitness app every day. A Lucia with a full Focus bar is a frightening person — and a Jason with full Strength is a hard one to kill.

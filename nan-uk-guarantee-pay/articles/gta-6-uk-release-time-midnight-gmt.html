GTA 6 UK Release Time: When It Unlocked in Britain

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 unlocked in the United Kingdom at 12:00 AM GMT on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. For players in London, Edinburgh, Cardiff and Belfast, that meant the game became playable the moment Thursday began — not at a single worldwide hour, but at midnight in Britain's own time zone. This guide explains the UK unlock moment, the rolling launch that put New Zealand first, the pre-load window that opened nine days early, and the pre-order bonus deadline that was still running on launch day.

The launch was not one moment for the whole world, and understanding that is the key to every other question about it: why New Zealand played first, why the pre-load dates differed between consoles, and why your account's store region — not your city — decided when the game unlocked for you.

The UK unlock moment

The game unlocked at midnight GMT in the United Kingdom, because the UK is on Greenwich Mean Time in November. London, Edinburgh and Cardiff all hit midnight at the same moment, so the whole country started playing together. The release schedule followed the regional PlayStation Store listings, which each carry the timestamp matching local midnight in their own region: 00:00 UTC for the UK store, 05:00 UTC for the US store, which is midnight Eastern, and 23:00 UTC for the German store, which is midnight Central European Time.

That is why no single worldwide hour can be given for this game. The launch was a rolling midnight by country, beginning at 12:00 AM NZDT in New Zealand and running across the globe as each country reached midnight on November 19 — nineteen hours from the first unlock to the last. A player in New Zealand started about eighteen hours before the US store opened.

A rolling midnight, not one global hour

Your PlayStation or Xbox store region decides the hour, not your city. The US store opens at midnight Eastern for the whole country, so a player in California unlocks at the same moment as a player in New York, in Eastern terms. The same rule applies everywhere: one unlock moment per store region, each at midnight in that region's main time zone.

There was one Xbox exception worth knowing. Microsoft's announcement said the game was playable on day one through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox One and Xbox Series X|S, starting at 1 a.m. Eastern Time on November 19 — which is 06:00 UTC, or 6 a.m. in the UK. Cloud players in Britain therefore started later than players with the game installed on a console, even though both were playing the same launch.

Pre-load: nine days early

Digital pre-loading opened on November 10, nine days before launch, so players who had pre-ordered could download the game in advance and be ready at midnight. On PlayStation 5, pre-loading started at local midnight; on Xbox Series X|S, it began globally at 00:00 GMT. The game's own support information recommends having at least 212GB of free storage space, and the download itself is about 209.8GB on PlayStation 5 and 225.3GB on Xbox.

Boxed copies reached shops on November 12, but they hold a download code instead of a disc, so buying the box did not save the download — the code had to be redeemed first, and only then did the pre-load begin. Pre-loading did not give early access in any case: the game stayed locked until midnight on November 19, whatever time the download finished.

The bonus deadline

One clock was still running on launch day: the pre-order bonus. All pre-orders and purchases made before November 20, 2026, include the Vintage Vice City Pack, plus a free month of GTA+ for digital pre-orders. Microsoft's version of the offer was stricter for the Ultimate Edition: buy it before November 19 at 23:59:59 on the Microsoft Store, and the pack and the free month of GTA+ come with it.

In the UK, the game costs £69.99 for the Standard Edition and £89.99 for the Ultimate Edition on the PlayStation Store, with the same prices on Xbox. The Ultimate Edition is digital only, and its extra content — vehicles, weapons, outfits and shops — unlocks chapter by chapter through the campaign rather than all at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

What time did GTA 6 unlock in the UK?

At 12:00 AM GMT on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Could UK players play early using the New Zealand trick?

Playing early through a New Zealand store needs a separate purchase on a New Zealand-region account, because the unlock follows your account's store region, not where you are sitting. It is a separate buy, not a setting you switch on your UK account.

Is there a PC version?

No PC version has been announced. The game launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only.

The bottom line: in the UK, GTA 6 unlocked at midnight GMT on November 19, after a nine-day pre-load window and a rolling midnight launch that started in New Zealand. If you bought before November 20, the Vintage Vice City Pack and a free month of GTA+ came with it — and the only thing that decided your hour was your store region.

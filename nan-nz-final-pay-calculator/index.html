Leaving a job in New Zealand? Your final pay must include any wages owed, unused annual holidays (paid at the greater of your ordinary weekly pay and average weekly earnings), and an 8% holiday pay component on gross earnings since your last leave anniversary — under the Holidays Act 2003. How final pay works in New Zealand (2026)

When your employment ends — resignation, redundancy, dismissal, fixed-term expiry, or retirement — your employer must include these in your final pay:

1. Unused entitled annual holidays

After 12 months of continuous employment you're entitled to 4 weeks of annual holidays a year. Any you haven't used must be paid as if you took them at the end of your employment, at the greater of your ordinary weekly pay and your average weekly earnings (gross earnings over the last 12 months ÷ 52).

2. The 8% component

On top of that you get 8% of your gross earnings since your last leave anniversary, less any amount already paid for holidays taken in advance or on a pay-as-you-go basis. If you leave before 12 months you have no week-based entitlement at all — your holiday payment is simply 8% of gross earnings since you started.

3. Everything else owed

Wages for hours worked since your last pay, notice pay (if you were required to work it and didn't, or vice versa), owed public holidays and alternative holidays, and any lump sums or redundancy compensation in your employment agreement.

Unused sick leave, bereavement leave and family violence leave are not paid out. There is no statutory redundancy payment in New Zealand — any redundancy compensation comes from your employment agreement.

Worked example

An employee with 2 years' service, ordinary weekly pay $1,400, gross earnings in the last 12 months $76,700 (average weekly earnings $1,475), 2 unused weeks of leave, and $24,000 gross earnings since their last anniversary:

Unused holidays: 2 weeks × $1,475$2,950
8% component: 8% × $24,000$1,920
Holiday portion of final pay (gross)$4,870

Frequently asked questions

What is the 8% everyone talks about?

It's the annual holiday payment for the part of your current leave year you worked but haven't yet banked as an entitlement: 8% of gross earnings since your last leave anniversary (or since you started, if under 12 months). It's the component most people forget to check.

Is my final pay taxed?

All figures here are gross. Your employer deducts PAYE, ACC, KiwiSaver and student loan as normal. Lump-sum payments like holiday payouts can be taxed at a special lump-sum rate depending on your tax code.

Do I get notice pay in my final pay?

If your agreement requires notice and you were paid in lieu of working it (or your employer required you to work it), the amount lands in your final pay. Reasonable notice must be given even if your agreement is silent on length.

Is unused sick leave paid out?

No. Unused sick leave, bereavement leave and family violence leave are not paid out when employment ends — only annual holidays (and alternative holidays / public holidays owed).

When must final pay be paid?

You and your employer can agree a date (often your last day), but at the latest it must arrive by the next pay day after your employment ends.

Am I owed redundancy pay too?

Only if your employment agreement provides it. New Zealand law has no minimum statutory redundancy payment — check your agreement and any negotiated terms.

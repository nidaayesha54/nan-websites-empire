Educational estimates, not a professional measurement

NAN Paint Calculator is an educational budgeting tool. Its results are arithmetic estimates based on standard formulas (net paintable area × coats ÷ coverage per litre, plus a 10% wastage allowance). They are not a professional quantity survey and not a painter's quotation.

Why estimates vary

Actual paint needed varies with surface porosity, texture, product, application method, temperature and humidity. Coverage figures printed on paint cans are measured under laboratory conditions; rough plaster, unsealed surfaces and dark-to-light colour changes can all increase consumption. Always check the coverage on your own paint can and confirm quantities with your painter before buying.

Prices change

Cost estimates use the per-litre prices you enter. Market prices for paint, primer and labour change over time and vary by city and brand — the calculator's cost output is only as current as the prices you type in.

Premium FAQ

Why is landlord insurance more expensive than homeowners?
Insurers price in higher risk: tenants file claims more often than owner-occupants, turnover increases damage risk, and loss-of-rent coverage adds a benefit homeowners policies don't include. Typically 15–25% more. Details in our 2026 cost guide.
What's the biggest lever to lower my premium?
Raising the deductible from $1,000 to $2,500 typically cuts 15–20% off the premium. Bundling with your auto insurer adds another ~10%. Security systems and claim-free years help too.
Does the state really matter that much?
Yes — weather and litigation risk drive it. A frame duplex in Florida can cost double the same building in Ohio. High-risk states face more carrier restrictions and higher base rates.

Coverage FAQ

Should I insure for market value or rebuild cost?
Rebuild cost — what it costs to rebuild the same house at today's construction prices. Market value includes land, which can't burn down. Insuring for market value often means overpaying premiums.
How much loss-of-rent coverage do I need?
A year of rental income is the common benchmark — repairs after a serious fire often take 6–12 months. Multiply your monthly rent by 12, or use the cost estimator to see what the extra coverage adds.
Is $300K liability enough for a landlord?
It meets most lender requirements, but slip-and-fall lawsuits can exceed $300K. Many landlords carry $500K or add a personal umbrella policy for $1M+ protection. See what landlord insurance covers.

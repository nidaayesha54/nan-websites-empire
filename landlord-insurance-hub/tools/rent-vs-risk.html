Worth-It FAQ

Can landlords self-insure instead?
Some experienced landlords with many units self-insure minor damage, but even they carry liability coverage — one tenant injury lawsuit can exceed $100K. Self-insuring the building works only if you can absorb a total loss without going bankrupt.
What if my property is paid off with no mortgage?
No lender means no requirement — but the risk is the same. A paid-off property is a larger asset at risk. Most paid-off landlords keep at least a high-deductible policy.
Does landlord insurance cover my tenant's belongings?
No — it covers your property (building, appliances you own). Tenants need their own renters insurance, which many landlords now require in the lease.

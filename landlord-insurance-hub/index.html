Frequently Asked Questions

How much does landlord insurance cost in 2026?
The average US landlord insurance policy costs roughly $1,200–$1,800/year — about 15–25% more than a comparable homeowners policy. Property type, location, age and coverage limits move the number up or down. Try our free quote estimator.
What does landlord insurance cover?
Typically: the dwelling structure, other structures, landlord-owned personal property (appliances), loss of rental income after a covered loss, and liability for tenant/visitor injuries. Read the full coverage breakdown.
Is landlord insurance required?
No state mandates it, but mortgage lenders almost always require it on rental properties. A standard homeowners policy excludes tenant-occupied homes.

Canada Government Jobs 2026

The Canada Revenue Agency is recruiting SP-04 officers for processing and compliance roles at the Jonquière Tax Centre in Quebec — five job profiles, salaries of $65,389 to $73,595, and a closing date of 30 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » Canada Government Jobs » Articles

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is recruiting SP-04 level officers for processing and compliance roles at the Jonquière Tax Centre in Quebec. The posting carries a salary range of $65,389 to $73,595 per year and closes on 30 October 2026 — one of the last major CRA application windows of the year.

Five specific job profiles are available under this pool: EFILE Officer, Compliance Services Officer, Assessment Processing Officer, Assessment Accounts and Benefits Processing Officer, and Processing Officer for Accounts, Benefits and Rebates. Core responsibilities include analyzing and processing tax files, resolving non-routine enquiries from taxpayers and benefit recipients, preparing audit and examination plans, conducting legislative research, and maintaining quality statistics on completed work.

The Five Job Profiles

  • EFILE Officer — electronic filing operations and taxpayer support;
  • Compliance Services Officer — compliance files; requires introductory accounting credentials from a recognized post-secondary institution or professional accounting association;
  • Assessment Processing Officer — assessment of tax returns and files;
  • Assessment Accounts and Benefits Processing Officer — accounts, benefits and rebate processing;
  • Processing Officer for Accounts, Benefits and Rebates — high-volume processing with quality targets.

Eligibility & Requirements

  • Education: minimum secondary school diploma — though the Compliance Services Officer profile requires additional introductory accounting credentials;
  • Language: bilingual imperative BBB/BBB — intermediate proficiency in your second official language, assessed before appointment;
  • Work arrangement: employees at the Jonquière Tax Centre work on-site a minimum of four days per week, with some positions requiring five-day attendance.

How to Apply

1
Open the CRA careers page (or the Government of Canada jobs portal) and find the SP-04 Jonquière staffing pool.
2
Confirm you meet the BBB/BBB bilingual requirement for the profile you want — language testing happens before appointment.
3
Prepare a resume emphasizing processing, compliance, audit or accounting experience with concrete examples.
4
Complete the screening questionnaire carefully — back every claimed competency with verifiable proof.
5
Submit before 30 October 2026 and watch your inbox for written-exam invitations.

Temporary — But Worth It

Temporary federal appointments are often the first rung into the CRA: internal candidates get priority for subsequent permanent competitions, and tax-centre experience transfers directly across CRA processing streams. If you hold BBB French/English and want into the federal public service before year-end, this pool is the most direct route currently open.

CSIS IT Analyst Jobs 2026 — Apply by Nov 30

Canada's Security Intelligence Service is hiring permanent IT Analysts in Ottawa, Gatineau and Montreal at $87,459 to $106,386 across 8 technology specialisations — recent graduates welcome, closing November 30, 2026.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » Canada Government Jobs » Articles

The Canadian Security Intelligence Service (CSIS) is hiring IT Analysts on a permanent basis in Ottawa (Ontario), Gatineau and Montreal (Québec), advertising salaries of $87,459 to $106,386 per year. The posting — reference 26-968-07-142, last updated on the official Government of Canada site on 22 October 2026 — carries a closing date of 30 November 2026.

CSIS frames technology as a central pillar of its operations: the roles are pitched as a direct contribution to national security, both within Canada and abroad. Eight broad specialisations are on offer, spanning cyber security, networks, cloud, business intelligence and end-user systems — so the competition covers the full IT stack rather than a single niche.

Notably, the posting welcomes recent and upcoming graduates, with a two-year post-secondary programme as one of three accepted education pathways — though a citizenship condition and enhanced security screening still apply.

The 8 Specialisation Streams

  • IT and cyber security — protecting CSIS systems and investigating digital threats;
  • Network technology and security — designing and securing classified networks;
  • Server and cloud infrastructure — running the compute backbone behind intelligence work;
  • Business intelligence — turning data into decision-ready intelligence products;
  • IT service management — keeping mission-critical services running;
  • End-user systems — supporting the devices and tools officers rely on daily;
  • Application support — maintaining bespoke intelligence applications;
  • Communication technology — secure voice, video and messaging systems.

Who Can Apply — Education and Citizenship

  • Education: three alternative thresholds — a two-year post-secondary programme in a relevant field; an undergraduate degree plus one year of experience; or a technologist diploma plus two years of experience. Higher degrees may count as experience;
  • Foreign credentials: accepted with a recognised Canadian equivalency assessment;
  • Graduates: recent and upcoming graduates are explicitly welcome;
  • Citizenship: a citizenship condition applies — confirm the exact requirement on the official poster;
  • Clearance: expect enhanced screening, which can add weeks or months — apply early.

Salary and Why These Roles Stand Out

The $87,459–$106,386 band sits above comparable federal IT ranges, and it is a permanent appointment — not a term or casual contract. CSIS roles include the standard federal package: defined-benefit pension, health and dental coverage, and bilingualism premiums where applicable. The trade-off is the clearance process and intelligence-work constraints — no remote work from abroad and strict information-handling rules.

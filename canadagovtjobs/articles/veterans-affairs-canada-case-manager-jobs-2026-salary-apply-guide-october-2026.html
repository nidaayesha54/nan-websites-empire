What a VAC Case Manager Does

Case managers carry a caseload of veteran clients and coordinate the services each client needs. Typical duties include:

  • Assessing client needs — health, rehabilitation, financial and family circumstances;
  • Developing case plans with measurable goals, agreed with the veteran and their family;
  • Coordinating services — connecting clients with VAC programmes, community health providers and partner organisations;
  • Monitoring progress through regular follow-up and adjusting plans as circumstances change;
  • Documenting casework to professional standards, including notes, reports and referrals.

The role demands judgment, empathy and organisation in equal measure — you are managing real lives, real crises and real bureaucracy at the same time.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Education or experience in social work, nursing, occupational therapy, psychology, community services or a related field — federal postings typically accept an equivalent combination of education and experience;
  • Canadian citizenship or permanent residence, as with most federal competitions;
  • Eligibility for Reliability Status security clearance;
  • Willingness to work in North Bay, Ontario, with the client-facing and field-travel demands the role involves.

Because this is a professional (not student) competition, there is no single degree that unlocks it: candidates from nursing, social work, veterans' services, community health and adjacent fields are routinely considered where their experience maps to the statement of merit criteria.

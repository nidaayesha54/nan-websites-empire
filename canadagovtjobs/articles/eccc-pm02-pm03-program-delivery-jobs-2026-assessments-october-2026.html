Canada Government Jobs 2026

Environment and Climate Change Canada is hiring PM-02 Program Delivery Assistants and PM-03 Program Delivery Officers for a federal energy-retrofit initiative — assessments run October 21 to November 2, 2026, with a tentative start in December 2026.

✅ Updated October 21, 2026
Home » Canada Government Jobs » Articles

Environment and Climate Change Canada (ECCC) is hiring across two program-delivery streams — Program Delivery Assistant (PM-02) and Program Delivery Officer (PM-03) — to deliver a landmark federal energy-retrofit investment: modernizing one million homes, lowering household energy bills and cutting emissions. Exams and interviews are scheduled for 21 October to 2 November 2026, with a tentative start in December 2026.

These are delivery roles on a national initiative with a visible, measurable target — moving retrofit investments toward the one-million-homes goal. For candidates who want federal work with a tangible environmental payoff, the mission is the draw.

The timeline is explicit — and tight. The posting warns that candidates unavailable during the 21 October to 2 November assessment window may not be able to continue, so this competition rewards readiness.

The Two Streams Explained

  • Stream 1 — Program Delivery Assistant (PM-02): supports delivery of the energy-retrofit investment — the administrative and processing backbone of the initiative;
  • Stream 2 — Program Delivery Officer (PM-03): the officer-level role, with greater responsibility for program delivery and stakeholder coordination;
  • Shared mission: modernizing one million homes, lowering household energy bills and cutting emissions;
  • Term appointment: specified-period positions tied to initiative funding, with the possibility of extension;
  • National pool: the pool may staff similar PM-02 and PM-03 positions across Canada under various tenures.

Key Dates and Conditions

  • Assessments: exams and interviews run from 21 October to 2 November 2026;
  • Availability rule: candidates who cannot attend during that window may not be able to continue in the process;
  • Tentative start: December 2026;
  • Official languages: candidates may complete the process in the official language of their choice — English or French;
  • Accommodation: assessment accommodation is available; request it through the process described on the official posting.

Why This Competition Matters

Federal competitions with this level of scheduling detail — an assessment window, a start month and an explicit availability warning — tend to move fast, because the program they serve has its own funding clock. The one-million-homes target gives these roles something many term positions lack: a visible finish line. Just remember what term (specified-period) means — the position is tied to initiative funding, with extension possible but not guaranteed. If you can commit to the assessment window and a December start, this is a high-impact entry point into federal program delivery.

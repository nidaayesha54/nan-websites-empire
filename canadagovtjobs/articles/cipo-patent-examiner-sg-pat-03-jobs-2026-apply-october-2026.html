Canada Government Jobs 2026

The Canadian Intellectual Property Office is hiring 6 Patent Examiners (SG-PAT-03) in Gatineau — starting at $87,347 to $107,392 with a full-time telework profile and applications closing October 26, 2026.

✅ Updated October 22, 2026

The Canadian Intellectual Property Office (CIPO), part of Innovation, Science and Economic Development Canada (ISED), is hiring six Patent Examiners at the SG-PAT-03 training level in Gatineau, Quebec — advertised at $87,347 to $107,392, closing 26 October 2026.

Patent examiners are the technical gatekeepers of Canada's innovation system. They read patent applications, search the world's published prior art, and decide whether an invention clears the legal tests of novelty, inventiveness and utility — then write clear, defensible office actions explaining that decision to applicants.

The standout detail is the flexibility: a telework profile allows full-time remote work from anywhere in Canada, and first-time public servants can receive up to $5,000 in relocation assistance.

What a Patent Examiner at CIPO Does

  • Review patent applications — reading detailed technical disclosures across mechanical and adjacent engineering fields;
  • Search prior art — combing patent databases and technical literature to assess novelty;
  • Apply patent law — testing each application against the requirements of novelty, inventiveness and utility;
  • Write office actions — drafting clear, legally sound written decisions communicated to applicants and agents.

Education and Experience Requirements

CIPO sets a hard technical bar for this competition — the degree requirement is non-negotiable:

  • Education: a bachelor's degree in mechanical engineering, or in civil, aerospace, biomedical, mechatronics or mining engineering;
  • Language: English essential — bilingual positions are designated, but this competition's linguistic profile is English;
  • Assessment: candidates face a written examination plus interviews, testing both technical reading ability and clear written communication;
  • Citizenship: as with most federal appointments, priority goes to Canadian citizens and permanent residents — check the poster for the exact wording;
  • Foreign credentials: degrees earned outside Canada need a recognized Canadian equivalency before appointment.

Salary and Career Progression

New hires enter at SG-PAT-03, advertised at $87,347 to $107,392, and advance to SG-PAT-04 (official range $101,393 to $124,188) after roughly two years of supervised practice. Combined with the telework profile, federal benefits and up to $5,000 in relocation assistance, the total offer competes with private-sector engineering salaries.

CRA AU-01 Auditor Jobs 2026 – Apply by Nov 5

The Canada Revenue Agency is staffing AU-01 auditor positions across Ontario — $73,100 to $95,710 per year for accountants, CPA candidates and tax professionals — closing November 5, 2026.

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) is hiring for AU-01 auditor positions across Ontario — income tax auditor and excise tax auditor roles — advertised at $73,100 to $95,710 per year, closing November 5, 2026.

The AU-01 level is the entry point of CRA's professional audit stream — the people who check that corporations, partnerships and trusts pay the tax they owe. It is one of the most direct routes into a federal career for accounting graduates and CPA students.

What CRA AU-01 Auditors Do

  • Auditing returns, books and records — examining accounting records and supporting documentation to confirm compliance with CRA-administered laws;
  • Planning audits — deciding how each audit will be conducted, scoping risk areas and setting the review approach;
  • Researching legislation — reviewing statutes, case law, CRA publications and policies to support findings;
  • Documenting work — preparing working papers, letters and reports that justify assessments;

Education and Eligibility Requirements

You must meet the CRA's minimum education standard for the AU–Auditing group by the closing date:

  • CPA eligibility: eligibility for a designation from a recognized professional accounting association; or
  • Accounting degree: a degree from a recognized postsecondary institution with an acceptable specialization in accounting; or
  • Law degree: an LL.B., J.D., B.C.L. or LL.L. that meets the educational requirements for admission to a Canadian law society or the Quebec notaries order;
  • Document deadline: education credentials, including transcripts showing course codes, names and grades, must be uploaded to your candidate profile by the closing date — applications without them may be rejected;
  • Who can apply: persons living in Canada, plus Canadian citizens and permanent residents abroad — preference to veterans, then citizens and permanent residents;
  • Language: various requirements by pool (English essential and bilingual imperative profiles both appear in CRA audit postings).

Salary, Benefits and CPA Training

The Ontario AU-01 competition is advertised at $73,100 to $95,710 per year. Beyond base pay, CRA positions commonly include:

  • CPA pre-approved training — CRA is a recognized training office, so audit roles can count toward CPA practical experience requirements;
  • Career progression — the AU stream leads upward to senior auditor, rulings and technical advisory roles (AU-03 and above), with substantially higher pay bands.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the CRA AU-01 auditor salary in Ontario?

The Ontario AU-01 posting is advertised at $73,100 to $95,710 per year. CRA pay scales are reviewed periodically, so confirm the live rate on the official poster.

What qualifications do I need for a CRA AU-01 auditor job?

The CRA's minimum education standard for the AU–Auditing group: CPA designation eligibility, a degree with an acceptable accounting specialization, or a qualifying law degree.

Who can apply for CRA auditor positions in Ontario?

Persons living in Canada, plus Canadian citizens and permanent residents living abroad. Preference goes to veterans first, then citizens and permanent residents.

When does the CRA AU-01 Ontario competition close?

Reported as November 5, 2026 — confirm the live closing date on the official CRA careers posting before applying.

What do CRA auditors do day to day?

They audit taxpayers' returns, books and records, plan audits, research legislation and case law, and prepare working papers, letters and reports.

Is the CPA designation required for AU-01?

Not strictly — a qualifying accounting-specialization degree or law degree also meets the standard. But CPA is a strong asset, and CRA roles can be part of CPA pre-approved training.

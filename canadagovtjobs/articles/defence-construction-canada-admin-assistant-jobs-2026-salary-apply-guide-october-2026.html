Canada Government Jobs 2026

Defence Construction Canada is hiring Administrative Assistants in Halifax, Nova Scotia and Winnipeg, Manitoba — paying $51,566 to $67,035, with applications closing October 21, 2026.

✅ Updated October 20, 2026
Home » Canada Government Jobs » Articles

Defence Construction Canada (DCC) is hiring Administrative Assistants in Halifax, Nova Scotia and Winnipeg, Manitoba, with an advertised salary of $51,566 to $67,035 and a closing date of 21 October 2026. The postings are among the most accessible federal-adjacent opportunities closing this week: no degree is required, and admin experience translates directly into a strong application.

DCC is a federal Crown corporation that supports the Department of National Defence by delivering infrastructure and environmental services at military bases and establishments across Canada. Its administrative staff keep regional site offices running — managing documents, coordinating schedules, supporting project teams and acting as the first point of contact for contractors and clients.

The two postings (Halifax and Winnipeg) carry identical salary ranges and closing dates, which usually signals parallel competitions for similar roles in different regions. Apply to the location you are willing to work in — DCC does not automatically consider you for both.

What an Administrative Assistant at DCC Does

  • Document and records management — preparing, formatting and filing correspondence, reports and project documentation;
  • Scheduling and coordination — managing calendars, booking meetings and arranging travel for site-office staff;
  • Client and contractor liaison — answering enquiries, directing calls and visitors, and maintaining professional front-desk operations;
  • Financial support tasks — processing invoices, tracking office budgets and reconciling petty cash or expense claims;
  • Meeting support — preparing agendas, taking minutes and following up on action items.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Education: typically a secondary school diploma with administrative experience, or a post-secondary diploma in office administration/business;
  • Experience providing administrative, clerical or front-office support in a professional environment;
  • Computer proficiency — Microsoft Word, Excel, Outlook and general office systems;
  • Eligibility to work in Canada and to obtain Reliability Status security screening;
  • Strong organisation, communication and time-management skills; bilingual English/French is an asset.

How to Apply

4

5

Why DCC Is Worth a Look

Defence Construction Canada combines the stability of the federal ecosystem with a mission-driven construction portfolio: its admin staff work alongside engineers and project managers delivering real infrastructure at defence establishments. The $51,566–$67,035 range is competitive for administrative roles outside Ottawa, and Crown corporation employees typically access the federal pension and benefits regime. With both postings closing 21 October 2026, this is a short window — prepare your examples now and submit early.

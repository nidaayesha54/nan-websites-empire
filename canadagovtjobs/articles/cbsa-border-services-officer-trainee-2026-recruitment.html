CBSA Border Services Officer Trainee 2026 (FB-02): Recruitment, Training & Salary Guide

CBSA is recruiting Border Services Officer Trainees (FB-02) for 2026. Here is every stage of selection, what the Officer Induction Training at Rigaud College involves, and how to apply.

CBSA Border Officer 2026 Recruitment

What a CBSA Border Services Officer Does

The Canada Border Services Agency (CBSA) keeps Canada’s borders open to legitimate trade and travel while blocking threats — from contraband and immigration violations to inadmissible goods. Border Services Officers work at land crossings, airports, marine ports and inland enforcement offices, and the 2026 developmental trainee intake (FB-02) is the main way in.

The current recruitment (reference BSF26J-019776-000031) is hiring trainees at various locations across Canada — with one important condition: it is a condition of employment to accept an assigned posting anywhere in Canada.

Every Stage of the 2026 Selection Process

CBSA recruitment is famously thorough. All candidates must complete each of these steps:

  1. Information session — a mandatory session introducing the role and the selection process.
  2. Entrance exam — testing reasoning and judgment relevant to border work.
  3. Interview — behavioural questions about integrity, decision-making and dealing with the public.
  4. Document validation — proof of identity, education and eligibility.
  5. Psychological assessment — suitability for law-enforcement duties.
  6. Physical Abilities Test (PAT) information session and assessment — the job is physically demanding.
  7. Medical assessment — fitness for duty.
  8. Canadian firearms courses — officers carry firearms, so safe handling is mandatory.
  9. Second-language evaluation — for bilingual positions only.
  10. Security screening — the enhanced clearance required for border enforcement.

Officer Induction Training at Rigaud College

Candidates who clear selection are invited to the Officer Induction Training Program (OITP), run from the agency’s primary training centre — the Canada Border Services College in Rigaud, Quebec (some training may take place at other CBSA locations).

The program is approximately three weeks of facilitated online distance learning, followed by fifteen weeks of in-residence training. While participating, recruits receive a tax-free allowance of $525 per week to cover basic expenses, plus lodging (room with individual bathroom) and meals while at the college.

How to Apply

  1. Find the live posting. Search the GC Jobs portal for “Border Services Officer Trainee” and confirm the intake is open — CBSA runs intakes on a schedule, not continuously.
  2. Attend the information session. This is a required first step — missing it ends your application.
  3. Prepare for the entrance exam and interview. Focus on situational judgment, integrity scenarios and clear communication.
  4. Stay physically ready. The Physical Abilities Test is a real hurdle — train for it alongside the application.
  5. Be flexible on location. You will be asked about preferred work locations, but the condition of employment is acceptance of a posting anywhere in Canada.
Selection from application to offer typically takes many months — treat it as a long process, keep your documents current, and do not make irreversible relocation plans until you have a firm posting letter.

FAQs

What is the CBSA Officer Induction Training Program?

The OITP is about three weeks of online distance learning followed by fifteen weeks of in-residence training at the CBSA College in Rigaud, Quebec.

How much is the CBSA trainee allowance?

Recruits receive a tax-free allowance of $525 per week during the OITP, plus lodging (room with individual bathroom) and meals during in-residence training.

Do CBSA recruits have to relocate?

Yes — it is a condition of employment to accept an assigned posting anywhere in Canada. You will state preferred locations during selection.

What are the CBSA selection stages?

An information session, entrance exam, interview, document validation, psychological assessment, physical abilities test, medical assessment, firearms courses, second-language evaluation for bilingual posts, and security screening.

Is a university degree required for CBSA trainee roles?

The trainee posting emphasises meeting all essential qualifications listed on the ad — check the specific process requirements on the official posting before applying.

RCMP Recruitment 2026: Join as Officer — Hiring Drive, Training & Salary Guide

The RCMP needs 3,413 officers this fiscal year against a hiring target of 1,195. Here is the eligibility, every selection stage, the 26-week Depot training and how to apply.

The Royal Canadian Mounted Police is hiring right now — and the scale is historic. Per the 2026 Auditor General report, the RCMP needed 3,413 frontline officers in fiscal year 2025–26 against an official hiring target of only 1,195: actual demand is nearly three times the plan, with vacancy rates above 7% in 9 of 11 provinces and territories policed by the force. For policing aspirants, this is the strongest hiring window in decades.

Where the 3,413 Officers Are Going

The force needs 2,213 Regular Members to stabilize operations plus 1,000 personnel for a federal policing expansion covering cybercrime, financial crime, transnational organized crime and border integrity. The 2026–27 Departmental Plan commits to hiring 1,000 personnel, supporting FIFA World Cup 2026 security, increasing the cadet recruitment allowance, and streamlining recruitment and training to get members operational faster.

Eligibility: Who Can Join

You must be a Canadian citizen or hold permanent resident status for at least three years, be 18 or older, be proficient in English or French, hold a valid, unrestricted Canadian driver’s licence, and have a Canadian high school diploma or equivalent. The force also recruits Civilian Members and Public Service employees in support and technical roles.

How to Apply: 6 Steps

  1. Confirm every qualification — citizenship/PR, age, language, licence, education.
  2. Apply online via the RCMP’s official recruitment channels at rcmp.ca and joinrcmp.ca.
  3. Attend a recruiting session such as Project Red Serge to meet recruiters and learn the process.
  4. Pass the written exam and interview assessing judgment, integrity and communication.
  5. Clear medical, physical and psychological screening — fitness standards apply.
  6. Pass field investigation and security clearance, then join a 26-week troop at Depot.
Northern and rural divisions have the highest vacancies — some, like BC RCMP’s Northern Recruiting Initiative, guarantee regional placement for applicants who commit to policing there. Flexibility on location improves your odds.

FAQs

Is the RCMP hiring right now in 2026?

Yes — Regular Members, Civilian Members and Public Service employees. The 2026 Auditor General report put need at 3,413 frontline officers for FY2025-26 against a target of 1,195.

What are the minimum qualifications to join?

Canadian citizenship or three years of permanent residence, 18+, English or French proficiency, a valid unrestricted Canadian driver's licence, and a high school diploma or equivalent.

How long is RCMP training?

26 weeks at the National Training Facility (Depot) in Regina, Saskatchewan, with food and lodging paid.

Where will I be posted?

Based on operational need; northern and rural divisions need members most, and some guarantee regional placement.

What do RCMP support roles pay?

Advertised support inventories pay about $51,642-$79,511 per year. Verify current figures on rcmp.ca.

The Canadian Security Intelligence Service is hiring a Facility Officer in Montreal, Toronto, Windsor, Hamilton, Fredericton and Halifax — paying $87,459 to $106,386, with applications closing October 31, 2026.

✅ Updated October 21, 2026

The Canadian Security Intelligence Service (CSIS) is hiring a Facility Officer across six Canadian cities — Montreal, Toronto, Windsor, Hamilton, Fredericton and Halifax — with an advertised salary of $87,459 to $106,386 per year and a closing date of 31 October 2026. It is a rare chance to apply facility-management expertise inside one of the country's most distinctive federal workplaces.

Facility officers keep the organization's physical footprint running safely and efficiently — coordinating contractors, writing project specifications, estimating costs and making sure repairs and upgrades meet strict workplace health and safety requirements.

The multi-city footprint is the detail that matters most. Rather than a single-office competition, the posting spans Quebec, Ontario, New Brunswick and Nova Scotia — widening the eligible pool considerably. The firm gatekeeping rule: Canadian citizenship is required, and foreign credentials need recognized Canadian equivalency.

What a Facility Officer at CSIS Does

  • Contractor coordination — directing and monitoring external contractors on CSIS premises;
  • Project specifications and estimates — writing technical specs and cost estimates for facility projects;
  • Maintenance contract management — administering service contracts so buildings stay compliant and operational;
  • Repairs and upgrades — coordinating corrective and planned upgrades to building systems;
  • Workplace health and safety — ensuring facilities meet health-and-safety obligations for occupants;
  • Office relocation and workplace planning — supporting moves and planning office space as needs change.

Education and Experience Requirements

The posting offers three alternative paths, so candidates from degree, diploma and trades-technician backgrounds can all qualify — provided the experience bar is met:

  • Path 1: an undergraduate degree in Mechanical, Electrical, Architecture or a related field, plus 2 years of relevant experience;
  • Path 2: a college diploma or certification in mechanical or electrical engineering, plus 4 years of relevant experience;
  • Path 3: a post-secondary certificate or diploma in a related field, plus 6 years of relevant experience;
  • Foreign credentials: education completed outside Canada must be supported by a recognized Canadian equivalency;
  • Citizenship: candidates must be Canadian citizens to be considered for this CSIS role.

Why This CSIS Opening Stands Out

Federal facility-management roles at this salary band — up to $106,386 — are uncommon outside the National Capital Region, and six-city coverage makes this competition unusually accessible across central and eastern Canada. The three education paths keep the door open for candidates who built careers through college diplomas and hands-on experience rather than a university degree. The trade-off is the process: CSIS competitions involve rigorous screening, so treat 31 October 2026 as a hard deadline and apply with complete documentation.

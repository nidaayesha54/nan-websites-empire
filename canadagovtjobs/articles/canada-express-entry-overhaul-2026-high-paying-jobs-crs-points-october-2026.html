Canada Government Jobs 2026

Canada has overhauled Express Entry selection: from November 3, 2026, qualifying high-wage job offers earn up to 150 extra CRS points — and the eligible occupation list is packed with senior government and public-policy roles.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » Canada Government Jobs » Articles

Canada has announced the biggest overhaul of Express Entry selection in years. Under the October 2026 announcement from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC), candidates holding qualifying high-wage job offers will receive up to 150 additional Comprehensive Ranking System (CRS) points — and the old Labour Market Impact Assessment (LMIA) rules are being removed for qualifying offers with effect from 3 November 2026.

For skilled workers with their sights on Canadian public-sector careers, the timing could hardly be better. The government's published eligible-occupation list for the new points includes senior government managers and officials, government managers in health, social, economic and education policy development, commissioned police officers, fire chiefs and judges — roles that sit squarely in the government-jobs world this portal covers.

The most recent draw underlines how competitive the system remains: on 15 October 2026, IRCC issued 2,500 invitations to apply for permanent residence through the Canadian Experience Class, with a minimum CRS score of 519.

Note: The 150 bonus points apply only to qualifying high-wage job offers in eligible occupations — not to every job offer. Candidates must still meet the eligibility requirements of one of the three federal programmes managed through Express Entry, and invitations remain subject to ranking and tie-breaking rules.

Government Occupations on the Eligible List

The published eligible-occupation list reads like a directory of senior public-service careers. Confirmed government-related occupations include:

  • Senior government managers and officials;
  • Government managers in health and social policy development and programme administration;
  • Government managers in economic analysis, policy development and programme administration;
  • Government managers in education policy development and programme administration;
  • Other managers in public administration;
  • Commissioned police officers and related occupations in public protection services;
  • Fire chiefs and senior firefighting officers;
  • Judges, lawyers and Quebec notaries.

Eligibility Requirements

  • You must qualify for at least one Express Entry programme: Canadian Experience Class (skilled Canadian work experience), Federal Skilled Worker Programme (work experience, education, language) or Federal Skilled Trades Programme;
  • You need a qualifying high-wage job offer in an eligible occupation — meeting the CRS score alone is not enough;
  • Language tests and educational credential assessments must be valid when you receive an invitation.

How to Position Yourself for the New Points

1
Create or refresh your Express Entry profile on the IRCC website, making sure education, language results and work history are current.
2
Target high-wage offers in eligible occupations — senior government, policy, health, engineering and IT management roles carry the new bonus.
3
Understand the 3 November 2026 change: for qualifying offers, the old LMIA-based rules no longer apply — confirm the offer meets the new criteria.
4
Keep language tests and credential assessments valid; an expired IELTS or ECA can cost you an invitation even with bonus points.

Frequently Asked Questions

What changed in Canada's Express Entry in October 2026?

IRCC announced that qualifying high-wage job offers will earn up to 150 extra CRS points, and the old LMIA rules are removed for qualifying offers from 3 November 2026.

Which government jobs are on the eligible-occupation list?

Senior government managers and officials, government managers in health, social, economic and education policy development, other public-administration managers, commissioned police officers, fire chiefs and judges.

What was the latest Express Entry draw?

On 15 October 2026 IRCC issued 2,500 invitations under the Canadian Experience Class, with a minimum CRS score of 519.

Do I still need an LMIA for a job offer?

For qualifying job offers under the new rules, the old LMIA requirements no longer apply from 3 November 2026 — but the offer must meet the new qualifying criteria.

Can foreign workers apply directly for Canadian federal government jobs?

Most federal positions require Canadian citizenship or permanent residence. The Express Entry overhaul helps skilled workers secure PR faster, which then opens the door to federal competitions.

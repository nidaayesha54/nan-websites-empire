Canada Government Jobs 2026

Justice Canada is looking to hire 12 experienced counsel for some of the government's most challenging and high-profile priorities — in corporate and commercial law, real estate law and aboriginal law.

✅ Updated October 20, 2026
Home » Canada Government Jobs » Articles

Justice Canada is hiring 12 experienced lawyers under its #CanadasLegalTeam campaign, advertised on 13 October 2026. The department is seeking counsel for corporate and commercial law, real estate law and aboriginal law portfolios, described as "some of the Government's most challenging and high-profile priorities".

The Department of Justice Canada is the federal government's legal service provider: its lawyers draft legislation, represent the Crown in litigation, and advise every federal department and agency. A counsel position with Justice Canada means working on files that shape national policy — from major commercial transactions and federal property portfolios to aboriginal rights and treaty litigation that is actively redefining Crown-Indigenous relations.

Justice Canada is also explicit about workforce diversity: the campaign notes it is "building a skilled and diverse workforce that reflects the population it serves" and encourages candidates in employment equity or equity-deserving groups to self-declare in their application. For experienced practitioners in private firms, in-house departments or provincial legal services who want to move into public service, this is a direct-entry door.

What Each Practice Area Involves

  • Corporate and commercial law: advising on government procurement, public-private partnerships, Crown corporation governance, and commercial litigation on behalf of federal institutions;
  • Real estate law: managing the federal property portfolio — acquisitions, disposals, leases, expropriations and real property transactions across the country;
  • Aboriginal law: litigation and advisory work on aboriginal and treaty rights, consultation duties, land claims and the evolving legal framework of Crown-Indigenous relations;

Eligibility Requirements

  • Membership in good standing with the bar of a Canadian province or territory (or eligibility for call);
  • Significant recent experience in the relevant practice area — this campaign targets experienced counsel, not new calls;
  • Canadian citizenship or permanent residence, as with most federal competitions;
  • Eligibility for federal security screening (Reliability Status or Enhanced Reliability, depending on the role);
  • Strong legal research, drafting and advocacy skills; bilingual ability is an asset for many Justice positions.

Salary: How Federal Lawyer Pay Works

Justice Canada lawyers are classified under the federal Law Practitioner (LP) group. Senior counsel postings (LP-03/LP-04) advertise salaries well into the six-figure range; the exact band is always stated in the notice. Federal LP roles also carry the public-service pension, generous leave and professional development funding.

Canada Federal Student Jobs 2026: FSWEP Eligibility, Pay & How to Apply

FSWEP is the federal government's main student hiring program — thousands hired yearly across 300+ cities and towns. Here is who qualifies, where students work, typical pay and how to apply.

As the country’s largest employer, the Government of Canada hires thousands of students every year through the Federal Student Work Experience Program (FSWEP) — in more than 300 cities and towns, from offices and laboratories to ships, farms, historical sites and national parks. If you are a student seeking paid, career-building work in 2026, FSWEP is the program to know.

Who Can Apply: FSWEP Eligibility

You qualify if you are a full-time student in a recognized secondary or post-secondary program, are returning to full-time studies next term, meet the legal minimum working age in the work province or territory, and are legally allowed to work in Canada. Final-year students not returning can work part-time until graduation. Students with disabilities holding full-time status, and accredited adult retraining participants, are also covered.

Pay & the Path to Full-Time Work

Pay varies by education level. Recently advertised federal student postings ranged from about $18.84 to $38.38 per hour (e.g., Agriculture and Agri-Food Canada research affiliates), while Global Affairs Canada’s Cadieux-Léger Fellowship carries a bursary of up to $54,000 for up to 12 months. FSWEP also bridges into permanent work: recent graduate postings have required graduation within two years plus at least one FSWEP or co-op work term — with no other experience required.

How to Apply: 5 Steps

  1. Create a GC Jobs account at jobs.gc.ca with your contact details.
  2. Open the Ongoing Student Recruitment Inventory on the Public Service Commission’s student page.
  3. Complete Education and interests — program, level, graduation date, fields of interest.
  4. Set availability and locations — summer, fall or winter; full-time or part-time.
  5. Upload a tailored résumé (Excel, writing, analysis, tech tools, research, customer service) and keep it updated.
High volumes mean some competitions use random selection among qualified applicants — not everyone is contacted. Apply early each cycle and keep your profile current.

FAQs

Who is eligible for FSWEP?

Full-time students in recognized programs who are returning to full-time studies, meet the legal minimum working age, and are legally allowed to work in Canada.

Where do FSWEP students work?

300+ cities and towns — offices, labs, ships, farms, historical sites and national parks, across IT, science, communications, enforcement and more.

How much do federal student jobs pay?

Varies by education level; recent postings ranged from about $18.84 to $38.38 per hour. Check each poster for the exact rate.

How do I apply?

Create a GC Jobs account at jobs.gc.ca, open the Ongoing Student Recruitment Inventory, complete your profile, set availability and upload your resume.

Can FSWEP lead to a permanent job?

Yes — some recent-graduate competitions require graduation within two years plus one FSWEP or co-op term, with no other experience needed.

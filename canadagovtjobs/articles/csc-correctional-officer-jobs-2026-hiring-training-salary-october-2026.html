Canada Government Jobs 2026

The Correctional Service of Canada is recruiting Correctional Officer I candidates for federal institutions across the country — a structured three-stage training program, strong federal benefits, and a current recruitment window closing 31 December 2026.

The Correctional Service of Canada (CSC) is one of the largest federal employers in the public safety sector, and it is running an active recruitment campaign for Correctional Officer I (CX-01) positions at federal correctional institutions across the country. Current listings advertise postings at federal correctional facilities across eight provinces, with applications closing 31 December 2026. For jobseekers who want a stable, pensionable federal career with a clear training pathway, this is one of the most accessible uniformed roles in the Government of Canada.

Unlike many federal jobs that demand a university degree, the Correctional Officer stream is built around a structured three-stage training program that prepares recruits for the realities of institutional life — security procedures, dynamic security, conflict resolution and inmate casework. Candidates who complete training join a career ladder with seniority-based progression and a full federal benefits package.

What Correctional Officers Do

Correctional officers are the front line of Canada's federal corrections system. Day-to-day duties typically include:

  • Supervising inmate movement and maintaining security within the institution;
  • Conducting patrols, counts, searches and security checks;
  • Responding to incidents and emergencies according to established procedures;
  • Writing reports and documenting inmate behaviour;
  • Supporting rehabilitation by modelling respectful, consistent behaviour and participating in case-management activities.

Eligibility & Requirements

CSC screening typically includes:

  • Online application through the Government of Canada jobs portal (emploisfp-psjobs);
  • Written assessments covering judgement and reasoning;
  • Medical examination and psychological assessment — both mandatory for the CX group;
  • Enhanced reliability status security clearance;
  • Physical ability testing aligned to the demands of institutional work.

Candidates must be prepared for the full demands of the job from day one — the posting language stresses readiness to handle the operational realities of a correctional environment.

The Three-Stage Training Program

Every successful candidate must complete CSC's three-stage correctional officer training program before taking up the role independently:

1
Stage 1 — Orientation: introduction to CSC's mission, legislation, policies and the correctional officer's role within the criminal justice system.
2
Stage 2 — Correctional Training Program (in-residence): intensive skills training covering security procedures, use of equipment, dynamic security, communication and conflict management, and emergency response.
3
Stage 3 — On-the-job coaching: supervised practice at an institution under experienced officers, with formal assessment before confirmation in the role.

Why This Recruitment Matters

Federal correctional recruitment drives of this scale signal ongoing staffing needs across CSC institutions — which is good news for applicants, because it means multiple intakes and more places to compete for. For anyone seeking a federal career with a structured paid training program and public-service benefits, the Correctional Officer stream is one of the strongest bets in the 2026 federal hiring landscape.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the deadline for CSC Correctional Officer jobs 2026?

Current listings advertise applications closing 31 December 2026, but postings can close early if enough qualified candidates are found — apply as early as possible.

What salary do Correctional Officer I candidates earn?

Listings report approximately C$77,510 to C$97,266 per year, with shift premiums and overtime pay adding to base salary in many cases.

Do I need a university degree to become a correctional officer?

No university degree is required. CSC selects through written testing, medical and psychological assessments, security clearance and a three-stage training program.

Where are the CSC Correctional Officer jobs located?

Current recruitment covers federal correctional facilities across eight provinces of Canada.

What does the three-stage training program involve?

Orientation, an in-residence Correctional Training Program covering security procedures and conflict management, and supervised on-the-job coaching at an institution.

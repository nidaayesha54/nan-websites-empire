CRA Jobs 2026: Income Tax Auditor & SP-04 Officer Hiring — $99,864–$122,817 Salaries

The Canada Revenue Agency is running multiple hiring rounds in late 2026 — from $99,864+ tax auditor roles to accessible SP-04 officer posts. Here are the openings, salaries and deadlines.

CRA Jobs 2026 Hiring Tax Auditor

The CRA Is One of Canada’s Biggest Federal Hirers

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) administers federal tax laws, benefits and credits across the country, and it hires at scale every year. Late 2026 is no exception: the agency has advertised high-paying professional tax roles, mid-level officer positions and genuinely accessible entry-level posts. Whether you are an accountant or someone with a secondary-school diploma, there is a CRA pathway worth knowing about.

This roundup covers the key 2026 hiring rounds — salaries, eligibility, locations and the deadlines that matter most.

High-Paying Tax Auditor Roles ($99,864–$122,817)

The top-paying CRA vacancies of the season were income tax auditor, international tax auditor, business valuator and tax avoidance auditor roles based in Atlantic Canada. These positions offered between $99,864 and $122,817 per year and were aimed at experienced professionals: applicants needed accounting qualifications alongside several years of Canadian tax compliance experience.

That particular round closed on 13 October 2026 — but CRA re-posts auditor pools regularly, so set a GC Jobs alert for “income tax auditor” if this is your field.

SP-04 Officers in Jonquière — Closing 30 October 2026

Still open: the CRA is hiring SP-04 officers at the Jonquière Tax Centre in Quebec, with a competitive package of $65,389 to $73,595 per year. This is a temporary opportunity with the potential of full-time permanent employment.

What you’ll need

  • Education: a Secondary School Diploma (some positions prefer introductory accounting or financial accounting)
  • Languages: proficiency in English and French
  • Security: eligibility for reliability-status security clearance

The work covers analysing and processing taxpayer files and benefit applications, verifying assessments and amendments, investigating enquiries, and communicating with taxpayers by phone and in writing. Applications close 30 October 2026.

Entry-Level CRA Posts in Sudbury — No Experience Needed

For newcomers to the federal public service, the most accessible CRA round advertised SP-01 to SP-04 positions in Sudbury, Ontario paying $46,904 to $73,595 annually. These roles required little or no prior work experience, making them among the most accessible federal government jobs available this year. The closing date was 27 November 2026.

How to Apply for CRA Jobs in 2026

  1. Create a GC Jobs account. Almost all CRA vacancies are posted on the official federal portal — jobs.gc.ca — or the CRA careers page.
  2. Save targeted searches. Use keywords like “SP-04”, “income tax auditor” and your preferred location, and turn on email alerts — CRA rounds can be short.
  3. Check the linguistic profile. Many CRA posts (especially in Quebec) require English + French. Only apply where you meet the stated profile.
  4. Tailor the application to the qualifications. For auditor roles, foreground your accounting credentials and Canadian tax experience; for officer roles, emphasise file processing, accuracy and client communication.
  5. Apply before the deadline. Federal portals close at the exact posted time — late applications are not considered.
Bilingual (English/French) candidates have a major advantage at CRA — a large share of tax-centre roles carry an imperative bilingual profile. Check each posting’s language requirements before applying.

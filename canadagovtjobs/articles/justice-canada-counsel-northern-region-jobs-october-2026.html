Justice Canada Counsel Jobs 2026 – Apply by Dec 31

Justice Canada is hiring Counsel (LP-02) for the Northern Region — Yellowknife, Iqaluit and Whitehorse — at $130,382 to $206,386 plus isolated-post allowance, closing December 31, 2026.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » Canada Government Jobs » Articles

The Department of Justice Canada is staffing Counsel (LP-02) positions in its Northern Region — with work locations in Yellowknife (Northwest Territories), Iqaluit (Nunavut) and Whitehorse (Yukon) — advertised at $130,382 to $206,386 per year, plus an Isolated Post Allowance of roughly $7,936 to $41,365 per year, with applications closing December 31, 2026.

The Northern Region's legal team — about 15 lawyers plus support staff — provides advisory and litigation services to federal departments operating north of 60. The competition has an immediate need for one litigation position and two advisory positions, with a pool for similar counsel roles.

Northern government lawyering is a distinct practice: counsel represent the Attorney General of Canada in the Federal Court and territorial courts, with a broad advisory practice spanning Aboriginal, environmental, tort, contract and administrative law.

What Northern Region Counsel Do

  • Litigation counsel — represent the Attorney General of Canada in actions and judicial reviews in the Federal Court and territorial courts, including defending actions involving Crown Indigenous Relations and the RCMP;
  • Advisory counsel — legal advice on Aboriginal law, environmental law, torts, contracts, real property, statutory interpretation and administrative law, including some tribunal proceedings;
  • Operational demands — extended hours including weekends, travel as required, and Health Canada medical clearance for incumbents and dependents under the Isolated Posts directive.

Essential Qualifications

  • Occupational certification: eligibility for membership in the Bar of a Canadian province or territory, or the Chambre des notaires du Québec — plus continued membership in good standing of the territorial law society;
  • Experience: significant experience providing legal services as counsel — roughly four years of counsel-level practice;
  • Language: English essential; security: Reliability, Secret or Top Secret clearance;
  • Assets: litigation or advisory experience in Aboriginal, constitutional, administrative, civil, Crown, environmental or police law — plus experience on Territorial North legal issues.

Salary and Northern Benefits

The LP-02 band of $130,382 to $206,386 already exceeds most southern legal salaries at comparable experience — and the northern package adds:

  • Isolated Post Allowance — approximately $7,936 to $41,365 per year on top of base salary, varying by location and circumstances;
  • Vacation Travel Assistance — once or twice per year for each eligible household member;
  • Relocation assistance and government housing (available in Iqaluit, Nunavut);
  • Pension and benefits — federal pension, group insurance, plus career development plans with education assistance funding.

Who Can Apply — Eligibility and Preference

  • Open to: residents of Canada, plus citizens and permanent residents abroad;
  • Preference order: veterans, then citizens and permanent residents — except Nunavut (enrolled Inuit first) and Yukon (Yukon First Nations first);
  • Relocation: appointees must relocate to Whitehorse, Yellowknife or Iqaluit;
  • Deadline mechanics: the poster closes December 31, 2026, but applications are pulled in batches — applying early can bring earlier consideration.

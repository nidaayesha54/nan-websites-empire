How to Apply

  1. Create a jobs.gc.ca account — the official Government of Canada jobs portal.
  2. Search by classification (AS, EC, PM, CR) and set up email alerts for new postings.
  3. Prepare your résumé using the Government of Canada résumé builder; screen-in criteria are scored strictly.
  4. Submit before the posted closing date — federal competitions close at 23:59 Pacific Time.
  5. Complete any online assessments; most federal processes take 8–12 weeks from application to offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for Government of Canada jobs?

All federal hiring runs through jobs.gc.ca. Create an account, build your résumé in the portal's builder, and apply to each competition before its closing date and time.

What are the EC, AS, PM and CS codes?

They are job classification groups used across the federal public service: AS (Administrative Services), EC (Economics and Social Science Services), PM (Programme Administration), CS (Computer Systems Administration). Pay scales are published per group and level.

Can non-Canadians apply for federal government jobs?

Yes — jobs.gc.ca postings are open to the public, but Canadian citizens and permanent residents get preference. Most roles also require eligibility for Reliability Status security clearance.

What salary can I expect in a federal government job?

Entry-to-mid roles typically pay $60,000–$95,000 CAD with a defined-benefit pension and strong benefits; senior specialists exceed $100,000 CAD.

How long does the federal hiring process take?

Most competitions take 8–12 weeks from the closing date to an offer, though some pools take several months. Create pools even if you're not selected — many departments hire from them.

Is French required for federal jobs?

Many positions are designated bilingual, but most non-supervisory roles outside the National Capital Region are English-essential. Language requirements are stated on every poster.

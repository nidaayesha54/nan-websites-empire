How Much Is GTA 6 in the UK? Prices and Best Deals

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and in the United Kingdom the headline prices are simple: 69.99 pounds for the Standard Edition and 89.99 pounds for the Ultimate Edition. But the headline price is not the price most people actually paid. Boxed copies — which contain a download code, not a disc — were widely available for 64.99 pounds, and a few routes brought the price down further still. This guide lists every UK price, the best deals, and whether it is worth waiting for a sale.

The first thing to know is that there is no disc. Every boxed copy of GTA 6 is a download code in a box, on both PlayStation and Xbox, so "boxed" and "digital" are the same game — the only difference is the price and where you buy it.

The Headline Prices: 69.99 and 89.99 Pounds

The Standard Edition costs 69.99 pounds on the PlayStation Store and the Xbox Store, and the Ultimate Edition costs 89.99 pounds. The 20-pound difference buys 16 story extras: the 1995 Grotti Cheetah, the Shitzu Squalo speedboat, the 1967 Vapid Dominator Buggy, the Dinka Enduro motorcycle, the Crest Kayak, a mod kit for Jason's Vapid Ganado pickup, outfits, tattoos, and access to five Ultimate-only shops. There is no early access, no extra missions and no different story in the Ultimate Edition — the extras are items and cosmetics that unlock across the chapters.

Whether the Ultimate Edition is worth it depends on how much you care about the extras. The Squalo alone is a real boat with a real use — fishing in Gambit Bay, with an explosives crate on board — and the five Ultimate-only shops are genuinely useful. But nothing in the Ultimate Edition changes the story, and every item in it is a bonus, not a necessity. If 20 pounds matters to you, the Standard Edition is the whole game.

Boxed Copies: 64.99 Pounds Almost Everywhere

The best price most people could get without any tricks was 64.99 pounds for a boxed copy — five pounds under the digital price — and it was available at almost every major retailer. Smyths, Argos, Very, John Lewis, Currys and Sports Direct all listed the boxed Standard Edition at 64.99 pounds, checked on November 19. That is the price to beat: if you are paying 69.99, you are paying too much, because the same game in a box is five pounds cheaper at half a dozen shops.

The reason is simple. Retailers compete on boxed games and they do not compete on digital ones — the PlayStation Store and Xbox Store set their own prices, and they rarely discount a game in its launch week. A boxed copy is the same download code, in a box, for less money. The only cost is the trip to the shop or the delivery wait.

The Cheapest Routes: Asda and Cashback

A few routes went lower than 64.99. Asda listed the game at 64.97 pounds, and with a 5-pound Rewards voucher the price dropped to 59.97 pounds — though that voucher offer was only valid until November 18, the day before launch, so it is gone now unless Asda brings it back. The other route was cashback: a new TopCashback member buying from Argos could get the effective price down to about 48.37 pounds, once the cashback was counted. That is the cheapest verified price in the UK, but it depends on being a new cashback member and on the cashback actually paying out, which takes weeks.

Gift-card discounts are the other quiet way to save. Buying PlayStation Store or Xbox credit at a discount — through a supermarket offer, a rewards scheme or a gift-card reseller — and then buying the game with that credit is a real saving, and it works on the digital edition too. It is not as clean as a straight price cut, but for a 70-pound game, even a 10 percent gift-card discount is seven pounds back.

Should You Wait for a Sale?

Radio Times, looking at the launch-week picture, reckoned that Black Friday discounts on GTA 6 would be at most around 10 percent — and that is probably right. A game this big, selling this fast, does not need to discount, and Rockstar has never been quick to cut its prices. GTA 5 took years to fall meaningfully in price, and GTA 6 is likely to follow the same path. A 10 percent Black Friday cut would bring the Standard Edition to about 63 pounds — barely cheaper than the 64.99 boxed price available on launch day.

The honest advice is this: if you want to play now, buy the boxed copy at 64.99 pounds. Waiting for a sale saves you a few pounds at most, and costs you weeks of playing the biggest game of the year while everyone else is talking about it. If you are happy to wait months, the price will come down eventually — but not quickly, and not by much.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much is GTA 6 in the UK?

The Standard Edition is 69.99 pounds digital and 64.99 pounds boxed at Smyths, Argos, Very, John Lewis, Currys and Sports Direct. The Ultimate Edition is 89.99 pounds. The cheapest verified route was about 48.37 pounds through a new-member TopCashback deal at Argos.

Is there a disc version of GTA 6?

No. Every boxed copy contains a download code, not a disc, on both PlayStation and Xbox. Boxed and digital are the same game — the box is just cheaper.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 costs 69.99 pounds Standard and 89.99 pounds Ultimate in the UK, but the price most people should pay is 64.99 pounds for a boxed copy at Smyths, Argos, Very, John Lewis, Currys or Sports Direct. Asda and cashback routes went lower, and Black Friday is unlikely to beat 10 percent. Buy the box, save five pounds, and play now.

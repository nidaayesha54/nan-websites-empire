GTA 6 Basketball: Can You Actually Play It?

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and one of the most talked-about screenshots in the game shows Jason Duval playing basketball. There is a hoop outside his home. Another screenshot appears to show Jason and Cal Hampton playing together. For a game set in a city with its own professional basketball team, the Vice City Narcos, the screenshots look like a promise — but Rockstar has not confirmed that basketball is actually playable, and the difference between a screenshot and a feature is the whole story here.

That matters because basketball has been playable in GTA before, and players remember it. The hope is real, the evidence is real, and the confirmation is missing. This guide separates what the images prove from what they do not, so you know exactly what to expect when you walk up to a hoop in Leonida.

What the Screenshots Actually Show

Two images carry the whole basketball story. The first shows Jason Duval with a basketball hoop outside his home — a hoop, a ball, and a character who lives with both. The second appears to show Jason and Cal Hampton playing basketball together, in a scene that looks like a casual game between friends rather than a formal match. Both images are official Rockstar material, and both are real.

What they show is basketball existing in the world. Vice City has courts, it has hoops, and it has characters who play. The Narcos, the city's professional team, play at the Sahara Arena, and their jerseys appear on characters throughout the game. Sports culture is woven through Leonida in a way it never was in Los Santos or Liberty City. But a hoop in a screenshot is not a minigame, and a casual game between two characters is not a playable activity — it is a scene, and scenes in Rockstar's marketing are not always things you can do yourself.

The San Andreas Precedent

Basketball has been playable in GTA exactly once. In San Andreas, a basketball minigame unlocked after the "Tagging up Turf" mission, and it challenged you to score as many baskets as possible in a set time. It had tricks, and it had dunks, and the dunks changed depending on your character's weight — a heavy CJ dunked differently from a thin one. It was a small activity, but it was real, and players who remember it have been asking for its return for twenty years.

Every GTA since has offered sports as side activities without ever bringing basketball back. GTA 5 gave players golf, tennis, darts, pool and motor racing. The pattern is that Rockstar picks a handful of sports per game and builds them properly, rather than offering all of them badly. GTA 6's full list of playable side activities remains unannounced, which means basketball could be on it — or the hoop could be decoration, and the sport could be represented only by the Narcos, their arena and their jerseys.

What Rockstar Has and Has Not Said

The careful version of the story is this: official GTA 6 images contain several references to fictional basketball and football teams, branded jerseys, a major Vice City arena and scenes of Jason playing basketball, although playable sports have not been confirmed. That is the state of the evidence, and it has not changed since launch. Rockstar has not confirmed whether players will be able to participate in complete basketball games, join teams or attend professional sporting events.

That silence is not a no. Rockstar routinely leaves side activities unannounced until players discover them, and the basketball screenshots are more suggestive than a jersey on a billboard would be. But silence is also not a yes, and the history of GTA marketing is full of scenes that turned out to be cutscenes rather than gameplay. The honest position is to expect the hoop to be there, to hope the game is there, and to wait for someone to walk up to that hoop in the actual game and find out.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is basketball playable in GTA 6?

Not confirmed. Screenshots show Jason playing basketball, but Rockstar has not said whether basketball is a playable activity, and the game's full side-activity list is still unannounced.

Was basketball playable in older GTA games?

Yes — only in San Andreas, where a minigame unlocked after "Tagging up Turf" and challenged you to score as many baskets as possible in a set time, with dunks that changed with your character's weight.

Can you attend a Narcos game at the Sahara Arena?

Rockstar has not confirmed whether players can attend professional sporting events. The arena is a confirmed location; what you can do inside it has not been announced.

The Bottom Line

The hoop is real, the screenshots are real, and the hope is reasonable — but playable basketball in GTA 6 is unconfirmed. Walk up to that hoop when you get the game, try it, and you will know before any guide does. Until then, treat basketball as the most promising maybe in Leonida.

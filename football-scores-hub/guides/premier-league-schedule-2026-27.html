Key dates of the 2026-27 season

The Premier League 2026-27 season follows the familiar rhythm: 380 matches across 38 gameweeks, typically kicking off in mid-August 2026 and wrapping up in mid-May 2027. The winter schedule includes the traditional festive fixtures around Boxing Day, and the final day sees all ten matches kick off simultaneously.

How the fixture list is built

The schedule is generated by computer with a long list of constraints: police requests (rival fans can't be home on the same day), TV broadcast slots, European competition dates, and travel fairness. Clubs receive the full list in June, but kickoff times shift through the season as broadcasters pick games — which is why we always advise confirming times before matchday. Derbies and dates to circle

Every season has its tent-pole fixtures: the North London derby (Arsenal vs Tottenham), the Manchester derby (City vs United), the Merseyside derby (Liverpool vs Everton), and the North West derby (Liverpool vs Manchester United). These usually land on weekend TV slots and sell out fast — mark them early.

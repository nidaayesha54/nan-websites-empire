Champions League Format Explained

Last reviewed: October 2026 · Reading time: 5 min

The 36-team league phase

Since 2024-25, the Champions League scrapped the old 32-team group stage. Now 36 clubs form a single league table, and each team plays 8 matches against 8 different opponents (4 home, 4 away). Points work exactly like a domestic league: 3 for a win, 1 for a draw. If you can read a league table (see our guide), you can read the UCL table.

Knockout playoffs

After the league phase, the top 8 teams go straight to the round of 16. Teams finishing 9th to 24th enter a two-legged knockout playoff; the 8 winners join the top 8. Teams finishing 25th or lower are eliminated from Europe entirely — there is no dropping into the Europa League anymore.

Round of 16 to the final

From the round of 16 onward it is classic knockout football: two legs (home and away) through the quarter-finals and semi-finals, decided on aggregate score, with extra time and penalties if level. The final is a single match at a neutral venue.

How teams qualify

Most clubs qualify through their domestic league position — typically the top 4 in England, Spain, Germany and Italy.

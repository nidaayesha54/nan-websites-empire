What each column means

ColumnMeaning
PPlayed — matches completed
WWon — worth 3 points each
DDrawn — worth 1 point each
LLost — worth 0 points
GDGoal difference — goals scored minus goals conceded
PtsPoints — (W × 3) + D

Tiebreakers: what decides level teams

When teams finish level on points, the order is decided by tiebreakers. In the Premier League the order is: goal difference, then goals scored, then head-to-head results. La Liga uses head-to-head first. Our interactive league table sorts by points then goal difference by default — click any header to re-sort.

Reading the zones: title, Europe, relegation

The table tells three stories at once. The top fights for the title and Champions League places (usually the top 4–5 in big leagues). The middle is the race for Europa League spots. The bottom three (in a 20-team league) are relegated. Try entering results in our points simulator to see how one weekend can flip all three stories.

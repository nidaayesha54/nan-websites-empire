Frequently Asked Questions

Are the live scores on FootballScoresHub really live?

Sections labeled Live (API) attempt to pull fresh data from free public APIs. Everything labeled Updated daily is our editorial snapshot refreshed once a day — it is not a live feed, and we never present it as one.

Which leagues does FootballScoresHub cover?

Our tools cover the Premier League, La Liga, Bundesliga, Serie A and Ligue 1, plus Champions League explainers.

How does the points simulator work?

Pick a league, enter hypothetical results, and the league table tool recalculates positions instantly so you can see how results would change the standings.

Can I add fixtures to my calendar?

Yes — the match schedule planner exports any league's weekly fixture template as a .ics file for Google Calendar, Apple Calendar or Outlook.

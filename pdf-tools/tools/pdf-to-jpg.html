Common questions

What quality are the images?

Pages are rendered at 2× zoom by default (roughly 144 DPI), which is sharp for screens and documents. Choose Ultra for print-level detail.

Can I convert password-protected PDFs?

Only if you know the password — this tool does not remove protection. Use our protect/unlock guidance first if it's your own file.

Is there a page limit?

No hard limit, but very long PDFs (hundreds of pages) render faster on a desktop browser.

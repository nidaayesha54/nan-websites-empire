    Common questions

    Will my images lose quality?

    No. Images are embedded at their original resolution and only scaled to fit the page — no recompression is applied.

    Can I mix JPG and PNG?

    Yes. Any mix of JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, and WebP works. Everything is normalized internally before building the PDF.

    Is there a page limit?

    No hard limit, but very large batches (hundreds of high-resolution photos) are easier on a desktop browser.

Common questions

What kind of encryption is used?

Real AES-256 encryption — the same standard banks and governments use — applied by the qpdf engine running entirely in your browser. It stops anyone without the password from opening the file.

Can the password be recovered if I forget it?

No. Encryption happens on your device and nobody else ever sees your password or file, so there is no "forgot password" option. Keep a copy of the password.

Will it work in Adobe Reader and other apps?

Yes. The protected PDF uses the standard PDF password format, so any PDF reader will ask for the password on open.

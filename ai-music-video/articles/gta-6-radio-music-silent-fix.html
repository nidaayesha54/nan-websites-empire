GTA 6 Radio Music Silent? Here's How to Fix It

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

You are driving through Vice City, the dialogue is clear, the engine sounds perfect, the city hums — but the radio is dead silent. No music from any station, no matter what you select. This is one of the stranger GTA 6 issues, because everything else works: it is only the music that has gone. The good news is that it is almost always a settings problem, not a broken game, and the fix is usually quick.

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The radio is a huge part of the game's atmosphere — the stations, the licensed music, the talk radio — so a silent radio stands out immediately. This guide works through the causes in order, from the most common to the least.

First Checks: Volume and Station

Start in the game's own audio menu. Open settings, go to audio, and look at the music and radio volume sliders separately from the master volume and the effects and dialogue sliders. It is entirely possible for the music slider to be at zero while everything else is fine — and easy to do by accident when adjusting volumes in a hurry.

Then check that a station is actually selected. The radio can be switched off entirely, and if it is off, no station will play no matter what you do. Select a station — any station — and give it a few seconds. If music starts, the radio was simply off. It sounds too simple to be the answer, but it is the answer more often than players expect.

Match Your Speaker Mode

If the volume and station are fine, check the console's speaker mode against your actual setup. The console's audio output settings let you choose between stereo, surround, and other modes — and if the mode does not match the speakers or headset you are actually using, some audio channels can drop out. Music is often the first thing to go when the channel mapping is wrong.

On PS5, go to Settings, Sound, Audio Output, and confirm the output device and format match your setup. If you are using TV speakers, make sure the console is not set to a surround mode your TV cannot reproduce. If you switched from a headset to speakers — or the other way around — check the settings again: the console does not always follow the switch on its own.

Xbox: Party Chat May Be Muting the Music

On Xbox, there is a specific culprit worth checking: Party Chat. In GTA V, being in a Party Chat could suppress licensed music — the game ducks the radio when voice chat is active — and players report the same behaviour in GTA 6. If you are in a party, leave Party Chat, then relaunch the game and test the radio again.

One honest caveat: the Party Chat effect is documented behaviour from GTA V, and it is not yet confirmed as a GTA 6 issue. But it costs nothing to test — leave the party, relaunch, and listen. If the music returns, you have your answer. If it does not, you have ruled out the most Xbox-specific cause and can move on to the general fixes.

If the Music Is Gone Everywhere

If no station plays on any speaker or headset, restart the game fully. Highlight GTA 6, close it completely, and launch it fresh — do not resume from rest mode or quick resume, which can hold onto the broken audio state. After relaunching, check the radio again before changing anything else.

Also check for a game update. Launch-week patches have addressed audio issues, and an update may already contain the fix. And if you use a headset, test with the TV speakers instead: if the radio plays on the speakers but not the headset, the problem is the headset's own settings or its spatial-audio mode, not the game.

Radio Silent vs No Sound at All

It helps to be precise about which problem you have. If dialogue, effects, and music are all gone, that is a different issue: on PS5, check the DualSense mute button first — holding it mutes all sound output, and the blinking orange light next to it gives it away. But if dialogue and effects play normally and only the music is missing, the problem is in the radio path: volume, station, speaker mode, or Party Chat. Fix the right problem and you will fix it faster.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the GTA 6 radio broken for everyone?

No. A silent radio is usually a settings issue — music volume, no station selected, a mismatched speaker mode, or Party Chat on Xbox. The stations themselves work; something in the chain is muting them.

Can I get the music back without restarting the game?

Sometimes. Try the volume and station checks and, on Xbox, leaving Party Chat first. If those do not work, a full relaunch is the next step — it clears the audio state that a quick resume can preserve.

The Bottom Line

A silent radio in GTA 6 is almost always a settings problem: the music volume, the station selection, a speaker mode that does not match your setup, or Party Chat on Xbox. Check those in order, relaunch the game cleanly if needed, and the music comes back. And if every sound is gone, not just the music, check the controller mute button — that is a different fix entirely.

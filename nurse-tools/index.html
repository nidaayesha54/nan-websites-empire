Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a nurse make in 2026?
The average RN earns about $101,000/year nationally, ranging from ~$71,000 (South Dakota) to ~$146,550 (California). Specialty nurses and advanced practice roles earn significantly more.
What is the highest paying nursing job?
CRNAs (Certified Registered Nurse Anesthetists) earn around $248,000/year on average, followed by Nurse Practitioners (~$132,000) and Nurse Midwives.
How much do travel nurses make?
Typical travel packages total $95,000–$150,000+/year including tax-free stipends — an effective $55–$75/hour. Use our travel nurse pay analyzer to compare offers.
Is nursing school worth it financially?
Yes — our career path planner shows a BSN-RN nets hundreds of thousands more than a CNA over 10 years, and tuition typically pays for itself within the first working year.
Where does this salary data come from?
State wage figures are estimates based on the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics (BLS) Occupational Employment & Wage Statistics, May 2024, adjusted to 2026 levels. Specialty differentials are modeled from BLS and published salary surveys.

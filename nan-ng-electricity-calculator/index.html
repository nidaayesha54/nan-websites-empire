How Nigeria's Electricity Tariff Bands Work

The Nigerian Electricity Regulatory Commission (NERC) prices electricity by service-based bands, set at the feeder level. Your address determines your band, not how much you use:

Band A — 20 to 24 hours of supply daily≈ ₦209.50/kWh
Band B — 16 to 19 hours of supply daily≈ ₦62.50/kWh
Band C — 12 to 15 hours of supply daily≈ ₦53.00/kWh
Band D — 8 to 11 hours of supply daily≈ ₦45.00/kWh
Band E — 4 to 7 hours of supply daily≈ ₦35.00/kWh

Band A customers pay the highest rate (their subsidy was largely removed in April 2024, when the rate jumped from ₦66 to about ₦209.50/kWh). Bands B–E remain partly subsidised. Figures differ slightly between distribution companies — Ikeja Electric, Eko Electric, Abuja Electric, Port Harcourt Electric, Enugu, Ibadan, Kano, Kaduna, Jos, Benin and Yola each publish their own approved schedules.

How to Find Your Band

Check the DisCo app or website for your area (for example Ikeja Electric's "customer feeder verification" lets you enter your account or meter number), ask on your last token receipt, or simply compare your last recharge against this calculator — the rate that makes the numbers match is your band.

Worked Example

Band B, rate ₦62.50/kWh, VAT added: a ₦5,000 recharge buys 5,000 ÷ (62.50 × 1.075) ≈ 74.4 units. At 7.5 kWh/day that covers roughly 10 days of household use.

Tips to Stretch Your Units

Switch heavy appliances (iron, water heater, washing machine) off when idle; run them in one window rather than all day. LED bulbs cut lighting load by ~80%. A Band A feeder gives the most hours but at 3–4× the cost per unit of Band B — track whether your actual supply matches your band and log a complaint with your DisCo (and NERC) if it doesn't.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many units will ₦1,000 buy in Nigeria?

It depends on your band. On Band B (~₦62.50/kWh with VAT), ₦1,000 buys roughly 15 units. On Band A (~₦209.50/kWh with VAT), it buys roughly 4.4 units. Use the calculator above with your band's exact rate.

Is VAT included in the per-kWh rate?

It varies. Most DisCos quote the NERC-approved band rate and add 7.5% VAT on top when issuing the token. The calculator lets you choose either treatment.

Why does the same recharge buy different units each month?

NERC applies a lifeline/consumer-protection framework based on total monthly consumption, so the effective rate can shift as you cross usage thresholds in a month. Fixed service charges, arrears and debt deductions also reduce units from a given payment.

Does my DisCo matter?

Slightly. The bands are national, but each DisCo's NERC-approved schedule can differ by a few naira per kWh — for example Band B ranges roughly ₦56 to ₦63.50 across DisCos. Match the rate on your token for the most accurate estimate.

How do I know which band I'm on?

Check your DisCo's feeder-verification page with your meter/account number, or divide your last recharge by the units received — compare the result against the band table above.

# StockWatch — Inventory Alert Manager

**Problem it solves:** Shop owners discover shortages only when a customer complains — meanwhile cash sits locked in overstocked dead items. Both cost real money every week.

**What the demo shows:** "Al-Noor Mart" grocery shop — 12 products with live stock levels, automatic **LOW STOCK** (amber) and **OUT OF STOCK** (red) warnings, reorder quantity suggestions with estimated order value, +/− stock adjust buttons, a supplier-ready reorder list (copy to clipboard), and a full stock-in/out activity log.

**Scenario:** Pain/solution contrast — 9 customers turned away for cooking oil while Rs 18,000 sits in dead biscuit stock vs alerts that fire before the shelf is empty.

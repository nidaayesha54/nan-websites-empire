# Bookly — Appointment Scheduler for Salons & Clinics

**Problem it solves:** Salons miss calls while staff are mid-appointment — clients give up and walk to the competitor, and paper diaries cause double-bookings.

**What the demo shows:** "Glow Salon" — a live weekly calendar where clicking any free slot opens a booking modal (client, phone, service with duration/price); long services auto-block multiple slots; occupied slots **refuse** double-bookings; today's schedule panel with one-tap cancel; services list with durations and prices.

**Scenario:** Pain/solution contrast — 11 missed calls during facials vs clients booking themselves at 1:47 AM while the salon sleeps.

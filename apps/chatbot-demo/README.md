# Business Chatbot Demo — "Beanie" for Bloom & Bean Café

A WhatsApp-style chat demo showing exactly what an AI chatbot feels like on a business website or WhatsApp Business account.

## Business problem it solves
Businesses lose customers every night: questions about hours, menu, bookings, and delivery go unanswered until morning — by which time the customer has gone to a competitor. Staff also waste hours answering the same 10 questions daily.

A chatbot answers instantly, 24/7: hours, menu/prices, table bookings, delivery, location, offers, dietary options — and hands off to a human only when truly needed.

## What's inside
- `index.html` — complete single-file app (no build step, no backend)
- WhatsApp-style chat UI: bubbles, typing indicator, quick-reply chips
- Smart keyword engine (JS): 12 intent groups — hours, menu, booking, delivery, location, WiFi, dietary, offers, jobs, greetings, thanks, goodbye
- Graceful fallback to a human agent for unknown questions
- Fictional business "Bloom & Bean Café" — swap in any business in minutes

## Try it
Open `index.html` and ask: "What are your hours?", "Show me the menu", "Book a table", "Do you deliver?"

## Production version
The live version connects to the WhatsApp Business API, takes real bookings, sends order confirmations, and integrates with POS/CRM systems.

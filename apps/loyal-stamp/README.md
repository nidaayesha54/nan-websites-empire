# LoyalStamp — Digital Loyalty Cards (Demo)

**Problem it solves:** Paper punch cards get lost, left at home, or punched wrong — and the business never learns who its real regulars are. Repeat customers drift away with zero warning.

**What the demo shows:** The counter view for "Chai Adda" café (Johar Town, Lahore) — member lookup by phone number (the number IS the card), a tap-to-stamp card visual with animation (buy 9, get the 10th free), automatic reward trigger with SMS code, plus the owner dashboard: total members, stamps today, rewards redeemed, return rate, live activity feed.

**Try it:** look up 0300 1234567 (or any number to register), tap empty stamps, complete a card to trigger the free-chai reward.

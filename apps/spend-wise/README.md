# SpendWise — Expense Tracker for Solopreneurs (Demo)

**Problem it solves:** Receipts live in gloveboxes and pockets. At tax time there's a shoebox of faded paper — and tens of thousands in legitimate deductions that can't be proven, so they're never claimed.

**What the demo shows:** "Bilal Raza," a ride-hailing driver in Lahore — 10-second expense entry, monthly spending by category as a Chart.js doughnut, per-category budget alerts that fire before overspending, and a tax-time summary ("You spent Rs X on fuel — deductible!") with one-tap export for an accountant.

**Try it:** add an expense and watch the chart, budgets and tax summary update live; push Fuel over budget to trigger an alert.

**Live demo:** https://nan-spend-wise.surge.sh

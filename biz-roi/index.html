Frequently Asked Questions

What is a good ROI for a small business? +
A good ROI for a small business is typically 100% or more over the investment horizon — meaning you double your money. Payback within 12 months is considered healthy. See our industry benchmarks guide for details.
How do I calculate business ROI? +
ROI = (Total Profit - Investment Cost) / Investment Cost × 100. Enter your startup cost, monthly revenue and expenses in our free calculator to get your ROI, payback period and profit projection automatically.
What is the difference between ROI and break-even? +
Break-even tells you when revenue covers costs (zero profit). ROI tells you how much profit you earn relative to what you invested. Use our break-even calculator for the first question and the ROI calculator for the second.

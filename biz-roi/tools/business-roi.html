How this calculator works

Monthly profit = monthly revenue − monthly expenses. Total profit = monthly profit × months in your analysis period. ROI = (total profit − startup cost) ÷ startup cost × 100. Payback period = startup cost ÷ monthly profit. Money multiple = total profit ÷ startup cost.

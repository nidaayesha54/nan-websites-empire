7 AI Video Prompts That Actually Work (With Recipes)

October 12, 2026 · 8 min read · By AIVideoMakerHub

The difference between amateur and professional AI video is rarely the tool — it's the prompt. After testing hundreds of generations across Runway, Kling, Luma, Sora, Pika and Hailuo, these seven formulas consistently deliver.

The universal formula

[Subject] + [action] + [camera movement] + [lighting] + [mood] + [style]. Always name the camera move — tools default to static or drifting shots without one.

1. Product hero shot

"Matte black wireless headphones rotating slowly on a reflective dark surface, macro dolly-in, dramatic rim lighting, premium tech commercial, photorealistic."

2. Talking presenter

"Friendly woman in her 30s speaking directly to camera in a modern office, subtle head movement and natural blinking, shallow depth of field, soft daylight, photorealistic." — Works best on Kling for natural motion.

3. Cinematic b-roll

"Aerial drone shot gliding over misty pine forest at dawn, slow forward push, golden hour light, cinematic, 35mm film look." — Luma excels here.

4. Food close-up

"Steam rising from a bowl of ramen, chopsticks lifting noodles, extreme close-up, slow motion feel, warm restaurant lighting, appetizing, photorealistic."

5. Lifestyle / fashion

"Model walking toward camera on a sunlit city street, coat flowing in breeze, tracking shot at waist height, golden hour, fashion film, photorealistic."

6. Abstract background loop

"Flowing liquid gradient in deep blue and teal, seamless loop motion, soft glow, minimal, elegant background animation."

7. Creative effect shot

"Vintage photograph of a street scene transforming into a living color video, magical morph effect, nostalgic to vibrant." — Made for Pika's Pikaffects.

Pro tips: keep clips 5–10 seconds; generate 2–3 takes per scene; avoid complex hand interactions (still the #1 artifact); add negative prompts like "blurry, distorted faces, extra fingers" where supported.

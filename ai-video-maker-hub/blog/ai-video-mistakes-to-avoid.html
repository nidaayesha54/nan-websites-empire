9 AI Video Mistakes That Make Your Content Look Cheap

October 12, 2026 · 7 min read · By AIVideoMakerHub

AI video tools are powerful, but sloppy technique screams "AI-generated" in the worst way. Here are the nine mistakes we see most often — and the fix for each.

1. Overcomplicated prompts

Cramming five actions into one prompt guarantees mangled output. Fix: one action per clip, 5–10 seconds each.

2. Ignoring the camera

No camera direction = flat, drifting shots. Fix: always specify the move — dolly-in, tracking shot, aerial push, slow pan.

3. Hands doing complex things

Hands remain AI video's weakest point. Fix: avoid typing, counting fingers, or intricate gestures; use negative prompts like "distorted hands, extra fingers".

4. Generating long clips in one go

Quality degrades fast past 10–15 seconds on most tools. Fix: generate short scenes and edit them together (Kling supports longer clips if you need them).

5. Skipping the script

Generating visuals before writing the script produces pretty nonsense. Fix: script first, then prompt scene by scene — see our business workflow.

6. No captions

80%+ of social video is watched muted. Fix: burn in captions on every business video.

7. Mixing art styles across scenes

Photorealistic scene 1 + anime scene 2 = incoherent video. Fix: lock one style keyword ("photorealistic") in every prompt.

8. Publishing free-tier watermarks

Nothing says amateur like a watermark. Fix: any commercial output needs a paid plan — even $8/mo removes it. Read our free vs paid guide.

9. No AI disclosure where required

Platforms increasingly require AI-content labels. Fix: check each platform's disclosure rules before publishing. Curious how detectable your footage is? Run it through the free AI Video Detector to see what detection tools pick up.

The golden rule: AI video rewards iteration. The pros regenerate, re-prompt and re-edit — the "one perfect prompt" is a myth. Budget 30% regeneration into every project.

How to Make AI Videos for Business — Step-by-Step Workflow

Step 1 — Define the goal and write the script

Every business video needs one job: sell, explain, or train. Write a 60–150 word script first (about 1 minute of spoken video per 130 words). Structure it as hook → problem → solution → call to action. AI tools can't fix a weak script — this is where the quality comes from.

Step 2 — Pick the right AI video maker

Match the tool to the job: Runway for polished brand ads and editing, Kling for realistic human presenters, Luma for fast b-roll, Sora for cinematic realism. Not sure? Our 60-second quiz recommends one based on your use case, or compare all six side by side. Confirm your plan includes commercial use rights.

Step 3 — Generate scenes (prompting that works)

Break your script into 5–10 second scenes and prompt each separately — short clips are far more reliable than long ones. A strong prompt formula: [subject] + [action] + [camera move] + [lighting/mood] + [style]. Example: "Smiling barista handing a coffee across a sunlit café counter, slow dolly-in, warm morning light, photorealistic."

Generate 2–3 variations per scene and keep the best. Expect to regenerate about 30% of clips — that's normal, and still nearly free compared to reshoots.

Step 4 — Add voiceover and music

Record or synthesize the voiceover from your script (AI voice tools pair well here), then lay royalty-free music underneath at −20dB. Captions are non-negotiable for business video — 80%+ of social viewing happens muted.

Step 5 — Edit, brand and publish

Assemble clips in any editor (CapCut, Premiere, or Runway's own suite), add your logo, brand colors and end-card CTA. Before publishing an ad, disclose AI generation where the platform requires it. If you handle UGC or influencer content, it's worth running final cuts through the free AI Video Detector to understand how AI-generated footage reads to detection tools — useful context for disclosure decisions.

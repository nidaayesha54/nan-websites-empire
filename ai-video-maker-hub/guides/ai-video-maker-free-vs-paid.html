The 4 hidden limits of free plans

1. Watermarks

Free outputs are almost always watermarked. Fine for drafts and personal tests — unusable for client work or ads.

2. Resolution caps

Free tiers typically top out at 720p. Paid plans unlock 1080p and 4K, which matters for YouTube and any fullscreen placement.

3. No commercial rights

This is the big one: most free tiers explicitly prohibit commercial use. Monetized YouTube videos, ads and client deliverables require a paid plan.

4. Generation queues

Free users get lowest priority. During peak hours a 10-second clip can take 30+ minutes — paid users skip the line.

When is paid worth it?

  • You publish regularly — even 4–8 videos a month justifies $8–$12/mo when one traditional video costs $500+.
  • You need commercial rights — required for ads, clients and monetized content.
  • You need 1080p+/no watermark — non-negotiable for professional output.
  • You iterate fast — paid priority queues save hours weekly.

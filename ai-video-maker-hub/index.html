Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best AI video maker in 2026?

It depends on your use case: Runway Gen-4 leads for professional editing, Sora 2 for realism, Kling for lifelike motion, Luma for speed, Pika for creative effects, and Hailuo for the best free tier. Our comparator ranks them by your filters.

Is there a truly free AI video maker?

Yes — Hailuo (MiniMax) and Kling both offer usable free tiers with daily credits. Free plans typically watermark output and cap resolution at 720p.

How much does an AI video cost vs traditional production?

A 60-second marketing video traditionally costs $500–$5,000+. With AI tools, the same video typically costs $2–$20 in generation credits. Use our cost calculator for a personalized estimate.

Can I use AI video makers for commercial projects?

Yes — paid plans of all six tools we track allow commercial use. Free tiers usually don't. Always check the current terms of service.

How can I tell if a video was made by AI?

Look for artifacts like odd hands, flickering textures and unnatural motion physics. For a reliable check, run it through the free AI Video Detector, which analyzes common AI-generation signals.

GTA 6 Cheat Codes: Why Yours Don’t Work Yet

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

You typed in a GTA 6 cheat code and nothing happened — twice, with different codes. Before you decide the code list is fake, here is the truth: the codes are real, but none of them work until you finish the game. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar has changed how cheats work in a way that breaks every habit players learned from GTA 5. In GTA 5 you could enter a code from the start. In GTA 6, cheats are a reward for finishing the story — and each one has to be earned separately. This guide explains the new system, the codes that are confirmed, and the catch that comes with using them.

The Short Answer: Cheats Are Locked Behind the Story

GTA 6 has 61 cheat codes, one of the largest sets in the series — but the game only hands them out after the story. The Cheats section does not exist until the final mission is done. It lives under Progress Menu, then Cheats, and it stays hidden for the entire campaign. Eurogamer tested saves from different points in the story and confirmed the tab only appears once the main story is finished, and GamesRadar+ and GameSpot report the same gate. The story is 91 main missions long, according to reporting from PC Gamer and Dexerto — so the lock is not a small one. It is the whole game.

That is why every code you enter now does nothing: the game is not rejecting your input because you typed it wrong, it is ignoring it because the cheat does not exist yet in your save. No code — not even the money cheat — can skip the story, and there is no way to open the Cheats tab early.

How the New System Works

Once the story is finished, the system opens in two steps. First, the Cheats tab appears in the Progress Menu, listing all 61 cheats across five categories: Player, Combat, Vehicle Spawn, Environment and Screen Filters. Second, each cheat has to be unlocked with its own challenge before its code will do anything. Finishing the story unlocks four cheats immediately: the $250,000 money cheat, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force and Cycle Weather. The rest need tasks — completing 100 workouts for Body Armor, earning 25 gold medals at shooting ranges for No Reload, escaping the police 25 times, catching 100 fish, and more. Most of those requirements overlap with the 100% completion checklist, so they tend to unlock on their own while you clean up activities after the ending.

Entering a code is different too. Open the phone with Up on the D-pad, hold L1 and press R1 on PS5 — LB and RB on Xbox — then enter the D-pad sequence. Every code uses only the D-pad, so the inputs are identical on both consoles, and there are no phone-number cheats this time. Entering the same code again turns that cheat off, and a correct input does nothing until that specific cheat is unlocked.

The $250,000 Code — and the Catch

The code everyone wants is the money cheat, and it is the most widely confirmed code in the game: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right, reported identically by Screen Rant, Beebom, GameRiv, GamesRadar+ and GameSpot. It adds $250,000 to your cash the moment you enter it — after the story, once it is unlocked. It is the only money cheat in the game, and it is generous: a quarter of a million dollars for one D-pad sequence.

But the catch is real. The moment you activate a cheat, the game disables trophies on PS5 and achievements on Xbox — and it blocks saving for the rest of that session. That means the $250,000 never carries over to a normal save file: you cannot save with a cheat active, so the money exists only until you quit. Cheats are a playground, not a shortcut to a richer save — create a manual save first, enjoy the chaos, and accept that the session does not count.

What to Do Instead During the Story

Since no cheat works during the story, the money has to be earned the normal way — and the game is built around that. The story itself gates progress behind money: there are points where you need to make money to advance the mission flow, and the missions, heists and side jobs are how you get it. Bank your cash as you earn it, because the wanted system and the world both punish carrying too much around, and spend on what the next mission needs rather than on the first shiny car you see.

Spotting the Fake Code Lists

Launch day did not kill the fake cheat lists — it changed them. The red flags are now easy to spot. Any code that claims to work from the first mission is fake: the Cheats tab does not exist until the story ends, so no code works mid-story. Any GTA 5 phone number — the 1-999 codes — does not work in GTA 6 at all. Any list that asks you to download an app, complete a survey, or pay for a code is a scam, not a cheat list. And any video promising a code that skips the story is lying: the story gate is confirmed by every major guide.

Stick to the lists from the outlets that tested the shipped game — GamesRadar+, GameSpot, Eurogamer, Rock Paper Shotgun and PowerPyx — and treat any code not reported by at least two of them as unconfirmed. As of launch day, 17 to 19 of the 61 codes have been published with matching inputs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I use cheat codes from the start of GTA 6?

No. The Cheats tab does not appear until you finish the main story — all 91 missions — and each cheat then needs its own unlock challenge. Entering a code earlier does nothing.

Why did the $250,000 code not work for me?

Either you have not finished the story yet, or the cheat is not unlocked in your save. The code is Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right — but it only works after the credits, entered on the phone with L1 + R1 held. And remember: you cannot save with a cheat active, so the money does not carry over.

Do GTA 5 cheat codes work in GTA 6?

No. GTA 5's phone-number codes and face-button combinations do not work in GTA 6. The new codes are D-pad sequences only, entered through the phone, and only after the story is finished.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6's cheat codes are real — 61 of them — but they are the reward, not the shortcut. Finish the 91-mission story, open the Cheats tab, earn each code with its challenge, and use them knowing the price: no trophies, no saving, no carry-over. The $250,000 is waiting at the end. Play the game to get there.

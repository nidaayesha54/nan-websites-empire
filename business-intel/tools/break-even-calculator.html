How break-even works

Break-even units = Fixed Costs ÷ (Price − Variable Cost). The (Price − Variable Cost) part is your contribution margin — what each sale contributes toward covering fixed costs. Break-even revenue = Break-even units × Price.

Levers: raise price, cut variable costs, or reduce fixed costs — each lowers your break-even point. If your realistic sales are far below break-even, the business model needs rework before you launch.

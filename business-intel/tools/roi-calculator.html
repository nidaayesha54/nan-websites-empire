How ROI is calculated

ROI % = (Total Return − Investment) ÷ Investment × 100. Annualized ROI adjusts for time: (1 + ROI)^(12/months) − 1. Payback period assumes returns accrue evenly: Investment ÷ (Net Profit ÷ months).

Benchmarks: stock market ~10%/yr · good marketing campaign 300–500% ROI · strong small business investment 50–200%/yr.

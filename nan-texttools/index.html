FAQs

Is the word counter free?

Yes — 100% free, unlimited use, no signup. Everything runs in your browser.

Does my text leave my browser?

No. All counting and conversion happens locally on your device. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

How are words counted?

Text is split on whitespace. "Don't" and "well-known" each count as one word. Standalone punctuation is ignored.

How is reading time calculated?

Words ÷ 200 per minute — the average adult silent reading speed.

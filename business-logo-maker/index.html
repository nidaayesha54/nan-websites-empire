Frequently Asked Questions

Is this business logo maker really free?

Yes. All tools — industry logo templates, the logo quality checklist and the tagline generator — are 100% free with no signup, and SVG downloads carry no watermark.

Can I use the downloaded logo for my business?

Yes. Logos you customize and download are yours to use on your website, business cards, signage and social profiles.

What file format do the logos download in?

SVG (Scalable Vector Graphics) — crisp at any size, from favicon to billboard. Convert to PNG in any free editor if a platform requires it.

Which industries have logo templates?

Technology, law firms, restaurants & food, fitness & gyms, real estate, healthcare, finance, beauty & salons, construction and education.

How is a logo different from brand identity?

A logo is one mark; brand identity is the full system — colors, typography, voice and guidelines. See our Logo vs Brand Identity guide.

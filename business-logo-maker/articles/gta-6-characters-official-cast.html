GTA 6 Characters: The 8 People Rockstar Named

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Every GTA 6 guide on the internet has a characters list — and most of them are padded with rumours, leaked names and fan theories dressed up as facts. This one is different: it sticks to what Rockstar has actually published. Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar's official GTA VI site profiles exactly eight people: the two playable leads, Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, plus six more around them — Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, the rap duo Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. Those eight profiles, published with Trailer 2 on May 6, 2025, are the official cast. Everything else is either a mention, a rumour, or a guess. Here is who the eight are, what they do, and who plays them.

The Two Leads: Jason and Lucia

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are the two playable protagonists, and the first dual-lead story in the series. Rockstar's tagline for Jason is that he "wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder." He is an Army veteran working for drug runners in the Leonida Keys, trying to stay out of trouble and failing. Lucia's tagline is that her "father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk." She is just out of Leonida Penitentiary, with a plan and a temper, and the story opens with the two of them pulled into a criminal conspiracy stretching across the state of Leonida after an easy score goes wrong.

The story is about escape, not empire: two people trying to get out, and everything standing in their way. That changes the tone of the whole game — the missions, the choices and the risks all serve the question of how they get free, not how much they can build. You play both of them, and the game moves between them as the story demands. Free switching unlocks only after the "Breakfast Run" mission in Chapter 2; before that, the story chooses the character for you.

The Supporting Six

Cal Hampton is Jason's friend and a fellow associate of Brian — a paranoid stay-at-home conspiracy theorist who likes snooping on Coast Guard communications with a few beers on hand. He is the comic relief and the eyes on the water, and his tagline asks: "What if everything on the internet was true?" Boobie Ike is a Vice City street legend turned legitimate businessman, with an empire spanning real estate, a strip club and a recording studio — his tagline is "It's all about heart, the Jack of Hearts." He is the most invested in the Only Raw Records partnership, and the money behind it.

Dre'Quan Priest is the other half of that partnership: a hustler turned aspiring music mogul, more hustler than gangster, who dealt on the street only as a means to an end — music was always the real goal. He signed Real Dimez to Only Raw Records. Real Dimez are the rap duo Bae-Luxe and Roxy, one profile covering two people, and their tagline is simply "Only Raw… Records." Raul Bautista is the veteran bank robber of the group — confident, charming and calculating, always scouting for talent willing to take on the riskiest, highest-reward jobs. And Brian Heder is the shared contact who ties Jason and Cal together, the man whose work Jason is doing in the Keys when the story begins.

Who Plays Them

For a year and a half, Rockstar announced no voice cast at all — and every name you saw attached to a character before launch came from fan research, not from Rockstar. That changed on launch day. Rockstar's credits for the game, posted on its GTA VI site on November 19, name the two leads: Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval and Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos. Those are the only two performances the credits name in the launch-day reporting, and the rest of the cast remains uncredited in the sources checked so far.

That matters because the internet spent months confidently naming voice actors for these characters, and every one of those claims was a guess. If Rockstar has not published it, it is not confirmed: the eight profiles are confirmed, the two lead actors are confirmed, and everything else is a rumour until Rockstar says otherwise.

Who Is NOT Confirmed

The rumour mill has produced a long list of names — Billy, Kai, Chester, Dale, Danny, Donnie, Iris, Sam, Shanese, Tit, Vicky, YJ, Zach and more — from reported leaks, fan databases and trailer analysis. Rockstar has not confirmed any of those as final story characters. Some of them may appear in the game; some may be background names, cut content, or pure invention. Until the credits or Rockstar say so, they do not belong in a characters guide, and any guide that lists them as fact is guessing.

The same caution applies to smaller names Rockstar has mentioned without profiling — Lori Heder, Stefanie, Mendez and Wyman. They exist in the world, but they are not part of the confirmed cast list, and their roles are not spelled out. The full cast is much bigger than eight people; the confirmed cast is exactly eight profiles.

How the Cast Connects

The eight form a web, not a list. Jason connects to Lucia, to Cal, and to Brian — his work, his friendship, and his partner. Cal connects to Jason and Brian. Boobie connects to Dre'Quan through Only Raw Records, and Dre'Quan connects to Real Dimez through the same label. Raul connects to the heist network, recruiting crews for the scores that drive the story. Brian connects to Jason and Cal as the man at the centre of the Keys operation. And Lucia connects to Jason — the relationship the whole story turns on. IGN's review calls it the first GTA story built around a romantic relationship, and the choices inside that relationship can steer the story toward different endings.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many characters has Rockstar confirmed for GTA 6?

Eight official profiles on its site: Jason Duval, Lucia Caminos, Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy), Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. Real Dimez is one profile covering two people, so the eight profiles represent nine named people.

Who voices Jason and Lucia?

According to Rockstar's launch-day credits, Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval and Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos. No other cast members were named in the credits checked so far, and pre-launch voice-actor claims were all fan research, not announcements.

Can you play as anyone besides Jason and Lucia?

No — Jason and Lucia are the two playable protagonists. The other six are supporting characters you meet, work with and play alongside in the story, but you do not control them.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 has eight officially profiled characters and two named lead actors — and everything beyond that is a rumour until Rockstar confirms it. Jason and Lucia, Cal, Boobie, Dre'Quan, Real Dimez, Raul and Brian: that is the cast. Learn their names, learn their connections, and the story of Leonida will make sense from the first score.

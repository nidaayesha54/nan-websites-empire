Common Questions

Am I overpaying my wedding photographer?

The typical USA wedding photographer costs about $3,000 in 2026, with a fair range of $1,500–$6,000. Quotes above $4,500 should include full-day coverage, a second shooter, and an album or premium deliverables.

How much should a wedding DJ cost?

Around $1,800 is typical, with a fair range of $800–$3,000. Below $800, verify equipment quality and backup plans; above $3,000, expect premium production or a well-known MC.

What is the biggest hidden wedding cost?

Catering quotes that exclude tax, service charges (often 20–25%), and gratuity. Always ask for the loaded per-person total before comparing caterers.

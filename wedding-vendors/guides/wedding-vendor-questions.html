20 Questions to Ask Wedding Vendors Before You Book

Updated October 2026 · 6 min read

The difference between a great vendor and a wedding-day disaster usually comes down to questions couples didn't ask. These 20 questions — organized by category — reveal hidden fees, weak contracts, and vendors to avoid before you pay a deposit.

💰 Pricing & Hidden Fees (ask every vendor)

  1. What is the total loaded cost? Ask for everything: tax, service charges, gratuity, delivery, setup, overtime. A $5,000 quote with 25% in add-ons is a $6,250 quote.
  2. What exactly triggers extra charges? Overtime rates, additional hours, extra guests, design changes — get every trigger and rate in writing.
  3. How much is the deposit, and is it refundable? Standard is 25–50%. Know what happens if you cancel at 6 months, 3 months, 1 month.
  4. Do you charge for a tasting / trial / consultation? Many apply it toward the booking; some don't. Ask upfront.

📝 Contract & Reliability

  1. Can I see the full contract before I decide? Any vendor who won't share a contract pre-booking is a hard no.
  2. What happens if you're sick or have an emergency? You want a named backup plan, not "we'll figure it out."
  3. Do you carry liability insurance? Many venues require it. Professionals have it; hobbyists don't.
  4. How many weddings do you do per year — and per weekend? Someone doing 3 weddings in one weekend is stretched thin.

📸 Quality & Fit

  1. Can I see 2–3 complete, recent galleries — not highlights? Consistency across a full event matters more than 50 perfect shots.
  2. Have you worked at my venue before? Venue familiarity prevents day-of surprises with lighting, load-in, and rules.
  3. Who exactly will be there on my wedding day? Studios sometimes send associates. Know who you're actually getting.
  4. What's your backup equipment / contingency plan? Cameras fail, DJs' laptops crash, flowers wilt. Pros have plans.

⏰ Logistics & Timeline

  1. What time do you arrive, and when do you leave? "Full day" means different things to different vendors. Get hours in the contract.
  2. Do you handle setup and teardown? Florists, décor, and rental vendors vary wildly here — and teardown fees surprise couples constantly.
  3. What do you need from me and by when? Shot lists, song lists, seating charts, final counts — know every deadline.
  4. How do you coordinate with my other vendors? The best vendors proactively sync with your planner, venue, and DJ.

🎯 The Closer Questions

  1. What's your overtime rate? Weddings run late. A $200/hr overtime clause beats a $500/hr surprise.
  2. Can you provide 2–3 recent references? Reviews help, but a direct conversation with a recent couple is gold.
  3. What would you do differently if this were your wedding? Reveals honesty and expertise — great vendors love this question.
  4. Is there anything in your contract couples usually misunderstand? The vendor's answer tells you where past disputes happened.

🚩 Walk away if…

  • They won't put it in writing.
  • They demand 100% upfront.
  • They can't show recent full examples of their work.
  • They get defensive about basic questions.
  • The price is dramatically below market with no clear explanation.

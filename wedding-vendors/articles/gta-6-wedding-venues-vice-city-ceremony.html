GTA 6 Wedding Venues: 7 Spots for a Vice City Ceremony

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and one question has followed Jason and Lucia everywhere since: can they get married? Rockstar will not say. Rob Nelson, co-studio head at Rockstar North, was asked directly during Edge magazine's October 29, 2026 cover story and refused to confirm or deny it. That is not a yes and it is not a no — it is a door left open. This guide walks through the seven spots that make the most sense, what each one would cost in the game's own prices, and which vendors — real ones in the game's world — would make it happen.

Start with what is confirmed, because the wedding conversation is full of guesses dressed as facts. GTA 6 tracks how close Jason and Lucia are on a four-point scale, and the top level is called Hot Together. Closeness rises when you spend time together: walks on the beach, cycling, working out at the gym once a membership is bought, driving around with one at the wheel and the other scrolling on a phone. Nelson's own list, given to IGN, adds pool, mini golf, kayaking, and robbing stores and banks together. Neglect has a cost too — if the relationship does not deepen, gestures like holding hands and kissing are lost, though effort can win them back. None of that confirms a wedding. It confirms a relationship the player shapes, and a game that notices how the two of them treat each other.

1. Ocean Beach boardwalk — the sunset ceremony

The most obvious venue is also the most photogenic. Ocean Beach is where Vice City shows off: the boardwalk, the sand, the neon spilling out of the hotels as the sun goes down. A ceremony here needs almost nothing bought — the setting does the work. In-game prices help the maths: a haircut at Pearl House costs $150, drinks run $5 to $9, and a bar tip is $10. A couple arriving in a clean car, dressed up, with the ocean behind them, is the cheapest grand wedding Leonida can offer, and the one most players will picture first.

2. The Leonida Keys stilt house — the private one

Jason starts the story in the Keys, living rent-free at one of Brian Heder's properties, and the stilt houses out on the water are the quietest corner of the whole map. A wedding here is the opposite of the boardwalk: no crowd, no noise, just the two of them and the water. The Keys are also where the story's tone is softest — fishing boats, deck chairs, a life that is not flashy but easy. For a couple whose whole story is about trying to start over, the Keys are the honest choice.

3. A Vice City nightclub — the loud one

Nightclubs are one of the confirmed activities in GTA 6, and a wedding after dark in a club is the most Vice City idea on this list. The costs stack up fast here: a private dance is $300, tips are $50, and drinks add up across a whole guest list. But nobody books a nightclub wedding to save money. They book it because the photos look like the city itself.

4. Mount Kalaga National Park — the wild one

Mount Kalaga is the green heart of Leonida — hunting, fishing and off-road trails, deer at the forest edge, and the kind of open sky the city never gets. A ceremony on a ridge at golden hour, with the whole state spread out below, is the venue for players who spent their first week in the game exploring instead of robbing. It costs nothing but the drive there, and the drive is half the point.

5. Port Gellhorn — the faded-glamour one

Port Gellhorn is the town time forgot: faded signs, empty motels, a main street that has seen better decades. It is also one of the most atmospheric places in the game, and a wedding there has a story built in — two people starting over in a place that is doing the same.

6. A mansion on Starfish Island — the rich one

Vice City's richest neighbourhood has always been Starfish Island, and a mansion wedding is the fantasy version of the whole idea: white suits, a garden full of guests, a car worth more than most houses parked out front. The game will not hand you a mansion — safehouses are story unlocks and none of them are buyable — but the island is still there to drive through, and the dream is free.

7. A small chapel — the simple one

Leonida has churches, and a small chapel ceremony is the one venue that needs no money, no guest list and no spectacle. Two people, a witness, and a vow. Given that Nelson told IGN there is no gameplay benefit to the romance at all — "not a particular gameplay benefit that I can think of" — the simple version may be the truest one. The relationship in GTA 6 is about what you want for Jason and Lucia, not about what it gives you. A chapel wedding fits that exactly.

What a Vice City wedding would actually cost

Put the game's own prices together and a modest ceremony is surprisingly cheap: $150 for the haircut, $5 to $9 a drink, $10 bar tips, and a car you already own. The real cost is not money at all — it is the time spent building the relationship to Hot Together in the first place, through beach walks, gym sessions, kayaking trips and jobs done side by side. That is the part no vendor can sell you.

Frequently asked questions

Can Jason and Lucia get married in GTA 6? Rockstar has not confirmed it. Rob Nelson refused to confirm or deny it when Edge asked in October 2026, and nothing since has changed that answer.

Does the relationship give you any gameplay benefit? No. Nelson told IGN there is no particular gameplay benefit to being a couple — the romance is about what you want for them.

What raises the relationship level? Time together: greetings, holding hands, activities like pool, mini golf, kayaking and gym sessions, answering each other's texts, and doing jobs as a pair.

Can the relationship get worse? Yes. Neglect it and gestures like holding hands and kissing are lost — though effort can win the relationship back.

What is the cheapest wedding venue in the game? A beach or a chapel costs nothing but the trip there. The boardwalk at Ocean Beach needs only a $150 haircut and a clean car to look like a wedding.

Bottom line: nobody knows if a GTA 6 wedding is possible, and that is exactly why the venues matter — Leonida is full of places where it could happen, and the best of them cost less than a night out. Pick the beach if you want the photos, the Keys if you want the quiet, and the chapel if you want the truth of it.

Average Wedding Vendor Costs in 2026: The Full Breakdown

Updated October 2026 · 8 min read · Estimates based on The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study

The average USA wedding in 2026 costs about $34,200 — but that number hides enormous variation. A 60-guest brunch wedding and a 200-guest Saturday black-tie affair share almost nothing except the word "wedding." What matters is what each vendor should cost, so you can spot an unfair quote the moment you see one.

Below: the average cost of every major wedding vendor category, the fair range around it, and where couples most often overpay.

Where Couples Overpay Most

1. Catering "plus plus"

The $80/person average excludes tax, service charges (often 20–25%), and gratuity. A $95/person quote can become $125/person loaded. Always compare loaded totals — this single step saves couples thousands.

2. Photography upgrades

Second shooters, albums, and extra hours are high-margin add-ons. They're often worth it — but price them separately and negotiate; studios routinely discount packages 10–15% when asked.

3. Floral design vs. flowers

You're paying mostly for labor and design, not blooms. Elaborate installations (arches, hanging pieces) cost 3–5x what the flowers themselves cost. Repurpose ceremony florals at the reception to cut the bill nearly in half.

4. The "wedding markup"

The same DJ, cake, or transport service often costs 20–40% more when the word "wedding" is attached. Get quotes both ways where possible — or at least know the non-wedding baseline.

Regional Differences Are Huge

The same photographer charging $3,000 in Atlanta might charge $4,200 in New York and $2,600 in Nashville. Northeast and West Coast metros run 20–25% above national averages; the South and Midwest run 5–10% below. Never judge a quote against a national number alone — always adjust for your market.

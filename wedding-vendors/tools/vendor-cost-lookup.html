    How do I know if a wedding vendor quote is fair?

    Compare it to the typical range for your region. Within about 30% of typical is usually fair. Far above? Ask what extras justify it. Far below? Verify experience, insurance, and contract terms — the cheapest quote is where most horror stories start.

    Why do prices vary so much by region?

    Vendor cost of living, demand density, and local competition. Northeast and West Coast metro areas run 20–25% above the national typical; the South and Midwest often run 5–10% below.

How to Make a 30-Second AI Video With Only Free Tools

One idea → six 5-second clips → a finished video. Total cost: $0.

AD-SLOT

Tutorial · October 2026 · 7 min read

Free text-to-video tools cap clips at 5–10 seconds — so nobody hands you a 30-second video. But six short clips, stitched well, are a 30-second video. Here's the exact workflow.

Step 1: Write a 6-beat script (10 min)

Break your idea into six 5-second beats. Example — "a robot's first sunrise":

  1. Robot powers on in a dark workshop (close-up, eyes lighting up)
  2. Workshop door slides open, blinding light
  3. Robot steps outside, seeing the horizon
  4. Sun cresting over mountains, robot silhouetted
  5. Birds scattering across the sky
  6. Robot reaching toward the sun, hopeful

Step 2: Generate one prompt per beat

Use the prompt builder to keep style consistent — same style, same lighting across all six prompts. Consistency is what separates amateur from pro. Or adapt beats from the prompt library.

Step 3: Generate across free tiers

Spread generations across tools' daily credits (Kling, Hailuo, Luma, PixVerse — see the comparison table). Generate each beat twice and keep the best take.

Step 4: Stitch in CapCut (free)

  • Import clips in order, trim to exactly 5s each
  • Add 6-frame cross-dissolves between beats
  • Color-match with one LUT/filter across all clips
  • Add trending audio + auto-captions

Step 5: Publish smart

Export 1080×1920 for Shorts/Reels/TikTok. Add AI-content disclosure where the platform requires it — and sanity-check your final with an AI video detector so there are no surprises.

Total: ~1 hour, $0, one finished 30-second video. Repeat daily and you have a channel.

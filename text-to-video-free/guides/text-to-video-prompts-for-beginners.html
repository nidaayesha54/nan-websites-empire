Text to Video Prompts for Beginners

The 5-part formula behind every great AI video prompt — plus 10 starter prompts and the mistakes that burn free credits.

AD-SLOT

Beginner guide · 6 min read

Most bad AI videos aren't the tool's fault — they're the prompt's. A video model needs the same thing a film director needs: a clear shot list. Here's the formula.

The 5-part prompt formula

  1. Subject + action — who/what, doing ONE thing. ("A tiny dragon baking cookies")
  2. Camera — how the camera moves. ("slow push-in")
  3. Style — the visual language. ("cinematic photorealism, 35mm film look")
  4. Lighting & mood — ("warm golden-hour light, joyful")
  5. Quality tags — ("ultra-detailed, smooth motion")

Assembled: "A tiny dragon baking cookies in a cozy kitchen, slow push-in, cinematic photorealism, warm golden-hour light, joyful, ultra-detailed, smooth motion." Our prompt builder assembles this for you automatically.

5 beginner mistakes

  • Too many actions. "A dog runs, then jumps, then the camera flies up" — pick ONE motion per 5-second clip.
  • No camera direction. Without it, the model guesses — usually badly.
  • Contradictory styles. "Photorealistic anime watercolor" confuses the model. One style.
  • Forgetting the negative prompt. Add: blurry, distorted faces, extra limbs, watermark, text overlay, low quality.
  • Re-rolling instead of editing. Change one element at a time; don't burn credits on random retries.

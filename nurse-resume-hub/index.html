❓ Frequently asked questions

Is the nursing resume builder really free?

Yes. Every tool on NurseResumeHub — the resume builder, cover letter generator, and skills checker — is 100% free with no signup, no account, and no watermark on your downloads.

Will my resume pass applicant tracking systems (ATS)?

Yes. Our builder produces a single-column, plain-text-friendly resume with standard section headings (Summary, Experience, Education, Certifications, Skills) that ATS software parses reliably. Avoid graphics, tables, and columns, which ATS often misreads.

How long should a nursing resume be?

One page for new grads and nurses with under 10 years of experience; two pages maximum for experienced nurses. Recruiters spend under 10 seconds on a first scan, so lead with licenses, certifications, and quantified clinical achievements.

What certifications should I list on a nursing resume?

Always list your state RN/LPN license and BLS/ACLS if current. Add specialty credentials like CCRN, CEN, PCCN, OCN, RNC-OB, or CNOR, plus PALS, NRP, or TNCC relevant to the role. Read our 2026 resume examples guide for formatting details.

Do new grad nurses need a cover letter?

Yes — especially for competitive new-grad residency programs. A tailored cover letter lets you highlight clinical rotations, preceptor feedback, and patient outcomes when your work history is short. Use our free generator to build one in minutes.

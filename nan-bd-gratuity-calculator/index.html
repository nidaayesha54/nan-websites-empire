What Is Gratuity in Bangladesh?

Gratuity is a lump-sum benefit payable to a worker on termination of employment — including retirement, resignation, discharge, retrenchment or death. It is defined in Section 2(10) of the Bangladesh Labour Act, 2006 (as substituted by the 2013 amendment): the wages of at least 30 days, at the rate of the wages the worker last received, for every completed year of service (or any period exceeding 6 months); and where service exceeds 10 years, 45 days' wages for each year. It is payable in addition to any termination compensation, notice pay or allowances under the Act.

Gratuity Formula

Gratuity = (Last Basic Monthly Salary ÷ 30) × 30 × Years of Service
If service is more than 10 years, the multiplier rises to 45: (Last Basic Salary ÷ 30) × 45 × Years. Any leftover period exceeding 6 months counts as one full year.

Examples

Basic salary BDT 25,000 × 10 years: (25,000 ÷ 30) × 30 × 10 = BDT 250,000. Basic salary BDT 40,000 × 12 years: (40,000 ÷ 30) × 45 × 12 = BDT 720,000 (45-day rate applies beyond 10 years).

Key Rules

  • Based on the last drawn basic wage — house rent, conveyance, medical and overtime allowances and bonuses are excluded.
  • More than 6 months after a completed year counts as a full year.
  • Payable on retirement, resignation, retrenchment, discharge and in case of death to the worker's nominee.
  • Under Sections 20, 22 and 26, a worker terminated by the employer gets compensation of 30 days' wages per completed year or gratuity — whichever is higher.
  • Employers can pre-fund this through an NBR-approved Gratuity Fund, which is tax-advantaged for both sides.

FAQs

Is gratuity mandatory in Bangladesh?

The Labour Act defines gratuity and requires it on termination of employment; employers must pay at least the statutory rate, whether or not they maintain a gratuity fund.

Does gratuity include house rent and allowances?

No. The calculation uses the last basic wage; house rent, conveyance, medical and overtime allowances, and bonuses are excluded.

What if I worked 7 years and 8 months?

The extra 8 months exceeds 6 months, so it counts as a full year — you get 8 years' gratuity at the 30-day rate.

What changes after 10 years of service?

The rate rises from 30 days' wages per year to 45 days' wages per year of service.

Who gets gratuity if the worker dies?

On death in service, gratuity is payable to the worker's nominee or legal heirs.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my monthly loan payment?

Use the amortization formula: principal × monthly rate × (1+rate)^months ÷ ((1+rate)^months − 1). Our personal loan calculator does it instantly.

Is a personal loan cheaper than a credit card?

Usually yes — personal loans average 10–15% APR vs 20–25% on cards, and have a fixed end date. See our full comparison.

Should I pay extra on student loans?

If your rate is above ~6%, extra payments save a lot of interest. Run both scenarios in our payoff planner.

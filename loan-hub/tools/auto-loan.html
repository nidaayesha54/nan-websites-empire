How to Avoid Overpaying on a Car Loan

  • Get pre-approved at a bank or credit union before visiting the dealer — it beats dealer financing most of the time.
  • Shorter terms save interest: a 48-month loan vs 72-month can save thousands.
  • Trade-in leverage: get an online quote (Carvana, CarMax) and make the dealer match it.
  • Watch add-ons: extended warranties and paint protection are high-margin dealer profit.
  • Compare with a personal loan if you're buying from a private seller.

Standard vs Income-Driven: Which Wins?

Standard plan: fixed payments, lowest total cost, paid off fastest. Best when you can afford the payment.

Income-driven: payments based on ~10% of discretionary income, recertified yearly. Lower payments now, but interest accrues longer — and any forgiven balance after 20–25 years may be taxable.

Strategies That Actually Work

  • Pay extra toward the highest-rate loan first (avalanche method).
  • Refinancing private loans can cut rates — but never refinance federal loans into private ones (you lose protections).
  • Check all federal repayment options before choosing.

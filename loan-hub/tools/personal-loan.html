How Personal Loan Payments Are Calculated

Lenders use the amortization formula. Each month you pay a fixed amount; early payments are mostly interest, later payments mostly principal. A lower APR or shorter term cuts total interest dramatically.

Tips to Lower Your Cost

  • Compare at least 3 lenders — rates vary widely.
  • A 3-year term instead of 5 can save thousands in interest.
  • Watch for origination fees (often 1–8% of the loan).
  • Check your options with bad credit before accepting a high rate.

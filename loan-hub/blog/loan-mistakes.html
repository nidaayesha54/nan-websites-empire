5 Loan Mistakes That Cost Borrowers Thousands

1. Looking Only at the Monthly Payment

A $400/month payment sounds fine — until you realize it's a 7-year term costing $8,000 in interest. Always compare total cost, not just the monthly number. Our calculator shows both.

2. Accepting the First Offer

Rates for the same borrower can differ by 5+ percentage points between lenders. Three quotes take an hour and routinely save $1,000+.

3. Ignoring Origination Fees

A "9% APR" loan with a 6% origination fee is really much more expensive. Compare APR (which includes fees), not the advertised rate.

4. Borrowing More Than You Need

Lenders love approving you for more — every extra dollar accrues interest. Borrow the minimum that solves your problem.

5. Skipping Pre-Approval on Auto Loans

Walking into a dealership without financing is like negotiating blindfolded. Get a bank quote first with our auto loan calculator.

How the EOSB Formula Works

Article 84 of Saudi Labor Law says that when the employment relationship ends, the employer must pay an end-of-service gratuity calculated from the last wage received:

First 5 years: half a month's wage per year (0.5 × monthly wage × years up to 5).
After 5 years: one full month's wage per year (1.0 × monthly wage × years beyond 5).
Partial years are paid proportionally.

Worked Example

Last wage SAR 10,000, 8 years of service: first 5 years → 5 × 0.5 × 10,000 = SAR 25,000; next 3 years → 3 × 10,000 = SAR 30,000; total = SAR 55,000.

Resignation Rules (Article 85)

If the worker resigns voluntarily, the award is reduced by service length:

Less than 2 yearsNo gratuity
2 to under 5 yearsOne-third of the award
5 to under 10 yearsTwo-thirds of the award
10 years or moreFull award

Employer termination (other than Article 80) and natural contract expiry pay the full award regardless of tenure. A dismissal under Article 80 for serious misconduct forfeits the award entirely.

What Counts as Wage?

The calculation uses the last wage received — the basic salary plus fixed, regularly paid allowances such as housing and transport. Variable commissions, performance bonuses and one-off payments are generally excluded. Probation periods count toward service; training or apprenticeship periods do not.

FAQs

Do expats get end-of-service benefits in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. The award applies to all private-sector workers under Saudi Labor Law, Saudi nationals and expatriates alike, on the same formula.

Is the gratuity capped?

Unlike the UAE, Saudi Labor Law sets no maximum ceiling — the half-month/full-month formula keeps accruing for as long as you remain in service.

I resigned after 3 years with a SAR 12,000 salary — what do I get?

Full award = 3 × 0.5 × 12,000 = SAR 18,000. Resignation in the 2–5 year bracket pays one-third: SAR 6,000.

Is the gratuity taxed?

Saudi Arabia imposes no personal income tax, so the gratuity is not taxed locally. If you move to a country that taxes worldwide income, it may be taxable there.

When must the employer pay it?

Your final settlement — gratuity, unpaid wages and unused leave — should be paid promptly after your last working day. If payment is significantly delayed, escalate to HR, then to the Ministry of Human Resources (MHRSD).

Can my employer deduct from the gratuity?

Only for specific documented reasons such as outstanding salary advances, employer loans, or proven damage to company property. Disputed deductions can be raised with the MHRSD Labor Court.

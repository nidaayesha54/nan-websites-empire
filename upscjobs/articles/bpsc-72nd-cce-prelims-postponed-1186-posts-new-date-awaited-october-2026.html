The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has postponed the 72nd Combined Competitive Examination (CCE) preliminary test, which was scheduled for 25 October 2026. In a notice issued on 12 October, the Commission cited unavoidable reasons for the postponement without a detailed explanation, and said the revised examination date will be announced later. Candidates have been advised to watch the Commission's official website, bpsc.bihar.gov.in, for the fresh schedule.

The 72nd CCE is Bihar's flagship state-services recruitment, filling 1,186 posts across administrative, police and departmental services. A postponement this close to the exam date is disruptive — but it also hands serious aspirants extra revision time. Here is what the notice says, the full vacancy picture, and how to use the delay.

The recruitment notification initially covered 1,230 posts; 44 Sugarcane Officer posts were subsequently removed from the combined examination, and revisions were made to vacancies for Deputy Superintendent of Police and other positions — leaving the current count at 1,186.

What Aspirants Should Do Now

Do not pause preparation

A postponed prelims is almost always rescheduled within weeks, not months. Treat the extra time as a bonus revision cycle: re-run your weakest subjects, attempt full-length mocks under timed conditions, and revise Bihar-specific GK — state schemes, budget facts and current affairs of the last 12 months.

Watch only the official website

Revised dates, admit-card links and any corrigenda will appear on bpsc.bihar.gov.in first. Coaching-centre "expected date" posts are speculation until the Commission publishes a notice. Check the BPSC notice board every two to three days.

Keep documents ready

Use the delay to organise your paperwork: graduation certificates, caste/EWS certificates in the Bihar format, domicile proof and a valid photo ID. Document mismatches at the mains application stage end more candidatures than tough papers do.

BPSC 72nd CCE Prelims — Exam Pattern Refresher

The BPSC CCE prelims is a single objective paper of General Studies — the qualifying gateway to the mains. There is no optional subject at the prelims stage, and the paper tests general studies with a strong Bihar component. Because the Commission revises the scheme through notices, confirm the exact marks, duration and negative-marking rule for the 72nd CCE in the official advertisement rather than relying on older cycles.

Note: the postponement notice gave no reason beyond "unavoidable reasons" and no new date. Do not trust forwarded messages claiming a rescheduled date — only a BPSC notice counts.

Frequently Asked Questions

Has the BPSC 72nd CCE prelims been postponed?
Yes. The prelims scheduled for 25 October 2026 was postponed via a BPSC notice dated 12 October 2026. The revised date will be announced later.
How many posts are in BPSC 72nd CCE?
1,186 posts. The notification initially covered 1,230 posts; 44 Sugarcane Officer posts were later removed.
When will the new BPSC 72nd prelims date be announced?
BPSC has not given a date yet. Monitor bpsc.bihar.gov.in for the official revised schedule.
Which posts are included in BPSC 72nd CCE?
SDM (100), DSP (20), Assistant Commissioner State Tax (76), Rural Development Officer (65), Block Panchayat Raj Officer (70), Revenue Officer (365) and others.
Where can I check the official postponement notice?
On the Bihar Public Service Commission website, bpsc.bihar.gov.in, under the notices section.
Should I stop preparing because of the postponement?
No — rescheduled prelims usually follow within weeks. Use the extra time for revision and mock tests.

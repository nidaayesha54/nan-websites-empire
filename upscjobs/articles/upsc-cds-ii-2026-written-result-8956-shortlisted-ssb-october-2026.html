The Union Public Service Commission (UPSC) has declared the written result of the Combined Defence Services Examination (II), 2026 on 19 October 2026. The result PDF — dated “New Delhi, the 19th October 2026” — lists 8,956 candidates (roll numbers) who have qualified for the SSB interview stage.

The CDS II 2026 written examination was held on 13 September 2026 for entry into the Indian Military Academy (IMA), Indian Naval Academy (INA), Air Force Academy (AFA) and Officers Training Academy (OTA). If your roll number is on the list, the next five days of testing — and a thick folder of documents — are what stand between you and a course.

Course-wise Entry and What the List Means

The 8,956 qualifiers are split across the four courses course-wise in the result PDF — check the list under your course (IMA/INA/AFA/OTA), not just the consolidated roll-number search. Qualifying the written stage does not guarantee final selection; the SSB interview, medical examination and the course-wise merit list all follow.

What to Do Immediately

1
Download the result PDF from upsc.gov.in (“What’s New → Written Results”) and search your roll number — not your registration ID.
2
Register for SSB — candidates for the Indian Army must register on joinindianarmy.nic.in within two weeks of the result to receive their SSB call-up.
3
Collect your documents — original degree/provisional certificates and mark sheets; deadlines vary by course: IMA/INA originals by 1 July 2027, AFA by 13 May 2027, OTA by 1 October 2027 (per CareerIndia's reading of the notice).
4
Prepare for SSB — screening (OIR + PPDT), psychology tests, GTO tasks and the personal interview, followed by medicals.

CDS vs NDA — Don't Mix Them Up

This is the CDS (II) 2026 result — a graduate-entry exam for IMA/INA/AFA/OTA — not the NDA II 2026 written result, which was covered separately. Both UPSC cycles run in parallel in October, and mixing up the two PDFs is a common (and costly) mistake when searching roll numbers.

Honesty flag: one Jagran Josh summary line dates the result to 4 October 2026 — but the article body and three independent sources (Business Today, Times Now, CareerIndia) all say 19 October, and the official PDF is dated 19 October; we treat the 4 October line as a typo. The 451-vacancy figure appears only in Jagran Josh and is flagged as single-sourced — confirm it against the UPSC notice. Document deadlines above are per CareerIndia's reading; recheck the official PDF.

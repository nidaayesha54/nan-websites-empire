APPSC Group 1 Notification 2026 Out: 166 Posts — Apply by 27 October, Eligibility & Exam Pattern

The Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) has released the Group 1 Services Notification 2026, opening applications for 166 gazetted posts — the state's premier civil services examination. The online application window runs from 6 October to 27 October 2026. Group 1 is Andhra Pradesh's equivalent of the state civil services top tier: Deputy Collectors, Deputy Superintendents of Police, District Registrars and other Class-I officers are recruited through this single examination, making it the most sought-after state PSC notification of the year in Andhra Pradesh.

Group 1 services carry the highest prestige in the Andhra Pradesh state services — officers recruited through this examination head subdivisions, districts' key departments and police units within a few years of service. Competition is correspondingly intense: with 166 posts, the screening test alone typically draws several lakh applicants.

Services Covered Under Group 1

The 166 vacancies span the top executive services of the state government, including:

  • Deputy Collector (Revenue Department) — the flagship Group 1 post
  • Deputy Superintendent of Police (Police Department)
  • District Registrar (Registration Department)
  • Assistant Commissioner and equivalent posts in commercial taxes, labour, and other departments

The post-wise and category-wise breakup is given in the official notification's vacancy table. Your service preference order, filled during the application, matters at the final allocation stage — rank it thoughtfully, since allotment follows merit-cum-preference.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

A bachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognized university is the standard eligibility for Group 1 services, with specific physical and educational requirements for uniformed posts like Deputy Superintendent of Police. Verify the post-wise eligibility table in the official notification — particularly the physical standards for police posts — before choosing your preferences.

Age Limit

Age limits for Group 1 are notified post-wise in the advertisement as per Andhra Pradesh government rules, with the standard relaxations for SC, ST, BC, EWS and other categories. Check the cut-off date for age reckoning in the notification and ensure your category certificate is valid and current.

Exam Pattern — Three Stages

The APPSC Group 1 examination follows the classic civil-services structure:

1
Screening Test (Prelims) — the objective-type qualifying test that shortlists candidates for the mains. Only the notified ratio of candidates (relative to vacancies) moves forward.
2
Mains Examination — the descriptive written examination across compulsory and optional papers; this is the scored stage that decides the merit list along with the interview.
3
Interview / Personality Test — the final stage for mains-qualified candidates, after which the commission publishes the final selection list.

Preparation strategy: the screening test rewards breadth (current affairs, Andhra Pradesh-specific GK, polity, economy, science), while the mains rewards depth and answer-writing practice. Start mains-style writing practice even before the screening test — toppers consistently credit early answer writing.

How to Apply Online

1
Visit appsc.gov.in — open the official APPSC website and find the Group 1 notification 2026 link.
2
Read the notification fully — vacancy table, post-wise eligibility, age limits, reservation roster and the exam scheme.
3
One-time registration and application — fill personal, educational and preference details; select your examination centre carefully.
4
Pay the fee and submit by 27 October 2026 — save the submitted application PDF and registration ID for the hall-ticket stage.
Tip: APPSC notifications sometimes carry post-specific conditions (physical standards for DSP, for example) that disqualify candidates at the medical stage even after clearing the written examination. If you are opting for uniformed services, measure yourself against the notified standards now, not after the result.

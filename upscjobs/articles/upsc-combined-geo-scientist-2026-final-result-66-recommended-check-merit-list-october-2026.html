UPSC Combined Geo-Scientist 2026 Final Result Declared: 66 Recommended — Check Merit List & Next Steps

The Union Public Service Commission has declared the final result of the Combined Geo-Scientist Examination 2026 (Advertisement No. 01/2026-GEOL) on 16 October 2026, recommending 66 candidates against 69 vacancies across the Geologist, Hydrogeologist, Geophysicist and Chemist streams. The result is a roll-number merit list PDF on upsc.gov.in — public, no login needed. Four candidates are on the reserve list and six are provisional pending document verification. Below: the post-wise breakup, how to check your roll number, and next steps.

Eligibility Criteria

The examination was conducted under the eligibility conditions of Advertisement No. 01/2026-GEOL dated 3 September 2025. Post-wise educational qualifications, age limits and other conditions were notified there — refer to the official notification PDF on upsc.gov.in for the exact details.

Age Limit

Age limits and category-wise relaxations were as notified in the advertisement, with the cut-off date for age reckoning given in the notification itself. Verify these details from the official PDF.

Selection Process — How the Final Merit Was Built

The 66 recommended candidates cleared every stage of the examination cycle:

1
Preliminary Examination — held on 8 February 2026 — the objective screening stage that shortlisted candidates for the mains.
2
Main Examination — held on 20–21 June 2026 — the descriptive written stage carrying the main merit weightage.
3
Personality Test — held in September 2026 — the interview stage for mains-qualified candidates.
4
Final Merit & Recommendation — the Commission consolidated written and interview marks to publish the final merit list of 66 on 16 October 2026.

How to Check the Result

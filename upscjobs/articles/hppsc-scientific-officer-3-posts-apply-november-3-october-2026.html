HPPSC Scientific Officer 3 Posts — Apply by 3 Nov 2026

Source / Ad DateAdvertisement No. 65/10-2026, dated 7 October 2026

The Himachal Pradesh Public Service Commission (HPPSC) has invited online applications for 3 posts of Scientific Officer, Group-B (Job Trainee) in the Directorate of Forensic Services, Department of Home, Himachal Pradesh. The engagement is under the state's job-trainee scheme with fixed monthly emoluments of ₹28,000.

Applications must be submitted through the HPPSC Online Recruitment Application (ORA) portal at hppsc.hp.gov.in on or before 3 November 2026, 11:59 PM — the same date is the last date for fee payment.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Essential: at least 2nd-class M.Sc. in Forensic Science (with specialization/dissertation/course/project work in the Chemical Sciences) OR at least 2nd-class M.Sc. in Chemistry / Bio-Chemistry / Pharmacy / M.S. (Pharm.) / M.Pharm. from a recognized university
  • Experience: minimum 3 years post-qualification experience in research, training and analytical work in the above subjects (Ph.D. research work counts toward the 3 years)
  • Desirable: Ph.D. in one of the relevant subjects; knowledge of the customs, manners and dialects of Himachal Pradesh

Age Limit

18 to 45 years as on 1 January 2026. Relaxation of 5 years for SC/ST/OBC/WFF/PwD of Himachal Pradesh; HP government employees and Ex-Servicemen as per government instructions. Reservation benefits apply only to bonafide residents of Himachal Pradesh.

Application Fee

₹600 payable online by 3 November 2026, 11:59 PM. Candidates must also provide an undertaking confirming the readability of uploaded documents (matriculation certificate for age proof, degree certificates with all years' marksheets, experience certificate, valid category certificate and Bonafide Himachali Certificate).

Selection Process

1
Objective-type screening test and/or descriptive Subject Aptitude Test — minimum 45% marks for General category, 40% for reserved categories.
2
Personality test — the notification allows direct personality-test consideration when the eligible-candidate-to-vacancy ratio is six or below.

How to Apply Online

1
Visit hppsc.hp.gov.in and open “Apply Online”, then One Time Registration (OTR) for examinations.
2
Register or log in, select the Scientific Officer (Advt. 65/10-2026) post and upload all required documents.
3
Pay the ₹600 fee online and submit — well before 3 November 2026, 11:59 PM.
4
Save a copy of the submitted application for the screening and personality-test stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for HPPSC Scientific Officer 2026?
3 November 2026, 11:59 PM — both the application and the ₹600 examination fee must be completed by then on hppsc.hp.gov.in.
How many posts are in HPPSC Scientific Officer Recruitment 2026?
3 posts of Scientific Officer, Group-B (Job Trainee) in the Directorate of Forensic Services, all in the unreserved category.
What is the qualification for HPPSC Scientific Officer?
At least 2nd-class M.Sc. in Forensic Science (Chemical Sciences component) or in Chemistry/Bio-Chemistry/Pharmacy/M.S.(Pharm.)/M.Pharm., plus 3 years of post-qualification experience in research, training and analytical work.

An objective screening test and/or descriptive Subject Aptitude Test (45% qualifying for General, 40% for reserved), followed by a personality test.
What is the age limit for HPPSC Scientific Officer 2026?
18 to 45 years as on 1 January 2026, with 5-year relaxation for SC/ST/OBC/WFF/PwD of Himachal Pradesh.

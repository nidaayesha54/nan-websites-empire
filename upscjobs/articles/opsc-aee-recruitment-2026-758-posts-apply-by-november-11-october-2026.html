The Odisha Public Service Commission (OPSC) has opened the application window today, 22 October 2026, for 758 Assistant Executive Engineer (AEE) posts — Group A, Odisha Engineering Service — under Advt. No. 13 of 2026-27 (Notice No. 5762/PSC, dated 9 October 2026). Apply by 11 November 2026, 5:00 PM on the OPSC e-Chayan portal.

There is no written exam and no application fee: OPSC shortlists on a valid GATE score (90%) plus an interview (10%), so a strong GATE scorecard is the decisive asset.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational qualification: a Bachelor's Degree in Civil or Mechanical Engineering from a Government-recognised university/institution, or Associate Membership of the Institution of Engineers (India) — AMIE — in the relevant discipline.

GATE requirement: only a valid QR-coded GATE scorecard in the respective discipline is accepted (current reporting covers GATE 2024, 2025 and 2026) — mark sheets alone are not accepted, and the GATE marks must be at or above the category qualifying mark shown on the scorecard. Without a valid scorecard you cannot be shortlisted.

Age limit: 21 to 42 years as on 1 January 2026 (born between 2 January 1984 and 1 January 2005, both inclusive), with relaxation for reserved categories, women, ex-servicemen and PwBD per Odisha Government rules. The notification also carries an Odia language requirement — check the official advertisement for the exact standard.

Selection Process: GATE Score + Interview

1
Shortlisting on GATE score — roughly double the candidates per category are shortlisted on the valid GATE score (90% weightage).
2
Interview — 10% weightage; schedule notified on the OPSC website after shortlisting.
3
Document verification — tentatively from the first week of December 2026.

There is no application fee. Keep the QR-coded GATE scorecard, category certificates and degree documents ready.

How to Apply (Window Open 22 October – 11 November)

1
Open the e-Chayan portal at opscechayan.in (reach it via Advt. 13/2026-27 on opsc.gov.in) and register.
2
Enter your GATE details — a valid QR-coded scorecard in Civil or Mechanical is required for the chosen post.
3
Fill personal, educational and department-preference details and upload the required documents.
4
Submit by 11 November 2026, 5:00 PM and save the confirmation for document verification.

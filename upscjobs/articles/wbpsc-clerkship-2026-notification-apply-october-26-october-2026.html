WBPSC Clerkship 2026 Notification Out: Apply by 26 October — Eligibility, Exam Pattern & Salary

The West Bengal Public Service Commission (WBPSC) has released the Clerkship Examination 2026 notification, inviting online applications for clerk posts in West Bengal government offices. The application window runs from 6 October to 26 October 2026. The clerkship examination is one of West Bengal's most popular state-government recruitments: it offers a stable Group-C clerical career with regular promotions, and the eligibility bar — typically a Madhyamik (10th) pass with basic computer knowledge — makes it accessible to an enormous pool of aspirants across the state.

Clerks recruited through this examination are posted in the secretariat, directorates and district offices of the West Bengal government. The career progression is well-defined — clerks rise through senior clerk and head clerk grades to supervisory positions — which is why the examination draws heavy competition every cycle.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

The standard eligibility for the WBPSC clerkship is a pass in the Madhyamik (10th standard) examination of the West Bengal Board of Secondary Education or its equivalent, along with the prescribed computer knowledge / computer operation ability as notified. The official advertisement's eligibility section is the final authority — confirm the exact qualification and any typing or computer-certificate requirements there before applying.

Age Limit

Age limits are notified in the advertisement as per West Bengal government rules, with relaxations for SC, ST, OBC and other reserved categories of the state. Verify the age reckoning date in the notification and keep your birth certificate or Madhyamik admit card handy as age proof.

Exam Pattern

The WBPSC clerkship examination traditionally runs in two written parts:

1
Part I — Objective Test — the screening stage with multiple-choice questions on general studies, arithmetic and reasoning at the Madhyamik level.
2
Part II — Conventional Written Test — the descriptive stage (English and Bengali/Nepali paper as notified) for candidates shortlisted from Part I.
3
Computer / Typing Test — the qualifying skill assessment for final shortlisted candidates, as prescribed in the notification.

The final merit is prepared on the written examination marks as per the notified scheme, with the skill test qualifying in nature. Because Part I is the gateway, most serious aspirants focus first on speed and accuracy in the objective paper — arithmetic and reasoning are the scoring sections.

Salary and Career Growth

WBPSC clerks are appointed in the state government's pay matrix for Group-C clerical posts, with dearness allowance, house rent allowance and medical benefits as admissible to West Bengal government employees. Promotions follow the established channel — clerk to senior clerk to head clerk and beyond — with departmental examinations opening faster tracks for ambitious candidates.

How to Apply Online

1
Visit wbpsc.gov.in — open the official WBPSC website and locate the Clerkship 2026 advertisement.
2
Read the notification carefully — eligibility, age limits, reservation rules, exam scheme and the list of required documents.
3
Fill the online application — personal details, Madhyamik details, category and computer-knowledge declarations exactly as per certificates.
4
Upload, pay and submit by 26 October 2026 — photograph, signature and documents in the prescribed formats; save the final submitted application.
Note: The clerkship application asks for computer-knowledge details that are verified at a later stage. Do not claim a computer certificate you do not hold — document verification for WBPSC examinations is strict, and false claims lead to cancellation even after selection.

UPSC ESE 2027: 480 Engineering Posts — Prelims 31 Jan 2027

Engineering Services Examination 2027 notification recap, exam pattern, and a focused preparation plan now that the application window has closed.

UPSC ESE 2027 Notification at a Glance

The Union Public Service Commission's Engineering Services Examination (ESE) 2027 notification announced roughly 480 posts for recruitment to Group A and Group B engineering services across four disciplines: Civil Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Electrical Engineering, and Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering. The online application window closed on 6 October 2026, so no new applications can be submitted now. If you applied before the deadline, the clock is firmly on preparation — the Prelims is scheduled for 31 January 2027, followed by the Mains on 18 June 2027.

ESE 2027 Exam Pattern & Scheme

The Engineering Services Examination follows a standard three-stage selection process. Knowing the structure is the first step to building a study plan:

Stage 1 — Preliminary Examination (objective)

  • Paper-I: General Studies and Engineering Aptitude — common to all four streams, covering current affairs, general science, mathematics, engineering aptitude and related areas.
  • Paper-II: Stream-specific paper covering the core syllabus of Civil, Mechanical, Electrical or Electronics & Telecommunication Engineering.
  • Only candidates who clear the Prelims cut-off qualify for the Mains. For exact marks and weightage, always check the official notification on upsc.gov.in.

Stage 2 — Mains Examination (conventional / descriptive)

  • Paper-I and Paper-II: Both are conventional descriptive papers based entirely on the candidate's engineering stream syllabus.
  • The Mains is scheduled for 18 June 2027 — Prelims qualifiers get roughly four and a half months to shift from objective to descriptive preparation.

Stage 3 — Personality Test (Interview)

  • Candidates who qualify in the Mains are called for a Personality Test assessing suitability, communication, and engineering awareness.
  • The final merit list is prepared from Mains + Personality Test performance. The Prelims is qualifying for the next stage.

Eligibility Notes (as per official notification)

Eligibility conditions vary by stream, so the official notification is the only authoritative source. In general, applicants need an engineering degree (or equivalent qualification) in the relevant discipline from a recognized university, must meet the prescribed age limits with relaxation for reserved categories, and must be Indian citizens or eligible categories as defined by UPSC. Since the application window is closed, confirm that the eligibility details in your submitted application match the notification to avoid surprises at the Detailed Application Form (DAF) stage before the Mains.

Preparation Strategy: Now That Applications Are Closed

With the deadline behind you, every day from now until 31 January 2027 counts. You have roughly fifteen weeks from mid-October. The winning strategy is simple: finish the core syllabus early, then spend the remaining time on revision and mock tests.

1. Prioritize the stream-specific paper

Paper-II of the Prelims (and later the Mains conventional papers) are drawn entirely from your engineering stream — this is where toppers separate themselves. Allocate the bulk of daily study time to core subjects: structures and hydraulics for Civil, thermodynamics and machine design for Mechanical, power systems and machines for Electrical, and communications and digital electronics for E&T.

2. Don't neglect General Studies & Engineering Aptitude

Paper-I is common and often under-prepared. It can decide whether you clear the Prelims cut-off, so cover it consistently — a little every day beats a last-month sprint.

3. Practice with previous-year papers

Solving at least 5–7 years of ESE Prelims papers in timed conditions teaches you the question pattern, difficulty level, and time management far better than theory revision alone.

4. Plan the Prelims-to-Mains bridge

Because the Mains is descriptive, start writing practice answers alongside your objective preparation. Candidates who begin answer-writing early find the four-and-a-half-month Prelims-to-Mains gap much more manageable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many posts are there in UPSC ESE 2027?
The UPSC Engineering Services Examination 2027 notification announced approximately 480 posts across Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, and Electronics & Telecommunication streams.
When is the UPSC ESE 2027 Prelims?
The ESE 2027 Prelims is scheduled for 31 January 2027. The Mains follows on 18 June 2027 for candidates who qualify the Prelims.
Can I still apply for UPSC ESE 2027?
No — the application window closed on 6 October 2026. If you applied in time, focus on preparation; otherwise, prepare for the next cycle and watch for the fresh notification.
What is the exam pattern of UPSC ESE?
ESE has three stages: Prelims (objective Paper-I on General Studies & Engineering Aptitude plus stream-specific Paper-II), Mains (two conventional stream-specific papers), and a Personality Test. The Prelims is qualifying for the Mains.
Where do I download the ESE 2027 admit card?
The e-admit card is released on the official UPSC portals upsconline.gov.in and upsc.gov.in, roughly 2–3 weeks before the exam. No physical hall ticket is sent.

The Union Public Service Commission held the Civil Services (Main) Examination 2026 from 21 to 30 August 2026. The written result has not been declared yet, and UPSC has not announced an exact date. Based on the previous five years' timelines — the Mains result is generally declared around 60 to 75 days after the final paper — the CSE Mains 2026 result is expected in early to mid-November 2026. In the 2025 cycle, the last Mains paper was on 31 August and the written result notice was dated 11 November, a gap of about 72 days; applying the same gap to the 30 August 2026 finish lands in the first half of November.

How to Check the UPSC Mains Result 2026

The result is published as a PDF — no login, password or application number is needed to view it.

1
Visit the official website — open upsc.gov.in and ignore forwarded "confirmed date" messages on social channels; check only the Commission's site.
2
Go to "What's New" on the home page, or open Examination → Written Results.
3
Click the link titled "Written Result: Civil Services (Main) Examination, 2026" once it is activated.
4
Open the PDF and search — press Ctrl+F (or "Find" on mobile) and search for your roll number.
5
Download and save the PDF — and read the accompanying notice on DAF-II and the interview in full, not just the roll-number list.

How Many Candidates Qualify for the Interview?

For CSE 2026, 13,343 candidates qualified Prelims and sat the Mains, against 933 vacancies. UPSC usually calls about two to two-and-a-half times the number of vacancies for the Personality Test. That means roughly 1,900 to 2,300 candidates can expect an interview call this year. The Personality Test is expected to begin in early 2027, with the final result likely in spring 2027.

What to Do While Waiting: DAF-II and Interview Prep

The weeks between the Mains and the written result are best spent on DAF-II preparation rather than refreshing result pages. Candidates who qualify for the Personality Test will be required to fill the Detailed Application Form-II, which records service and cadre preferences — and the interview board uses it as the starting point of the discussion. Review every entry you plan to make: hobbies, achievements, academic background, home state and current affairs connected to them.

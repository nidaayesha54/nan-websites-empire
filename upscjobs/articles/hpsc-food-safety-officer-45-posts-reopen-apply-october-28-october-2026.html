HPSC FSO 45 Posts — Re-open 22–28 Oct, Apply Now

The Haryana Public Service Commission (HPSC) has re-opened the online application process for 45 Group-B Food Safety Officer (FSO) posts in the state's Food and Drugs Administration Department. The re-open announcement was issued on 21 October 2026, and the fresh application window runs from 22 October to 28 October 2026 (5:00 PM).

Crucially, HPSC has revised the cut-off date for determining candidates' age, educational qualifications and other eligibility conditions to 28 October 2026 — so candidates who became eligible after the original window can now apply. Applications are online only at hpsc.gov.in.

Vacancy Breakup (45 Posts)

General / Unreserved (UR)22
Economically Weaker Section (EWS)5
Backward Classes-A (BC-A)5
Backward Classes-B (BC-B)3
Deprived Scheduled Castes (DSC)5
Other Scheduled Castes (OSC)5
Total45

Horizontal reservation applies for ESM (General) – 2 posts and PwD – 2 posts (1 Low Vision, 1 Deaf/Hard of Hearing).

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

A Bachelor's, Master's or Doctorate degree in any of: Food Technology, Food Engineering, Food Processing, Dairy Technology, Biotechnology, Oil Technology, disciplines covered under “Agricultural Science”, Veterinary Science, Bio-Chemistry, Microbiology, Chemistry or Medicine (MBBS / BDS / BHMS / BUMS / BSMS / BAMS / Sowa Rigpa).

Age Limit

18 to 42 years as on the revised cut-off date of 28 October 2026 (per the 21 October re-open announcement; some earlier notices referenced 19 October 2026 — the revised date in the re-open notice governs). Age relaxation applies for SC/BC and other reserved categories as per Haryana government rules.

Application Fee

General / EWS / Other-state candidates₹1,000
SC / BC / ESM / EWS₹250
Female (Haryana) candidates₹250
PwBD (40%+ disability)Nil

Fee is payable online (debit/credit card, net banking, UPI) by 28 October 2026. After payment, candidates must take a printout of the filled form, sign it and upload the scanned signed copy — applications without the signed copy are treated as incomplete.

Selection Process

1
Screening test — held if applications are large; qualifying in nature.
2
Subject Knowledge Test (SKT) — the core merit stage on food-safety subjects.
3
Interview / viva-voce — followed by document verification and medical examination.

How to Apply Online

1
Open hpsc.gov.in and click “Apply Online”, then “Apply Online Application Forms”.
2
Select Advt. No. 25/2026 — Food Safety Officer and register with a valid email and mobile number.
3
Fill the form carefully with personal, educational and category details exactly as on your certificates.
4
Upload documents — photograph, signature and certificates in the prescribed format.
5
Pay the fee online, print the form, sign it, upload the scanned signed copy and submit — well before 28 October 2026, 5:00 PM.

Frequently Asked Questions

28 October 2026, 5:00 PM — the re-opened window runs 22–28 October 2026 at hpsc.gov.in under Advt. No. 25/2026.
How many posts are in HPSC FSO Recruitment 2026?
45 Group-B Food Safety Officer posts — UR 22, EWS 5, BCA 5, BCB 3, DSC 5, OSC 5, with horizontal reservation for ESM (2) and PwD (2).
What is the eligibility for HPSC Food Safety Officer?

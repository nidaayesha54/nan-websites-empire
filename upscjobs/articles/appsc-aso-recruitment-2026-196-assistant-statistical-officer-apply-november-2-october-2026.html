APPSC ASO Recruitment 2026: 196 Assistant Statistical Officer Posts — Apply by 2 November

The Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) has invited online applications for 196 Assistant Statistical Officer (ASO) posts in the A.P. Economics and Statistical Subordinate Service through Notification No. 20/2026 — a direct, zonal-cadre recruitment with 95% of zonal posts reserved for local candidates. The pay scale is Rs 37,640 – Rs 1,15,500. Apply 13 October – 2 November 2026 (11:59 PM) on psc.ap.gov.in after APPSC One Time Profile Registration (OTPR).

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

A bachelor's degree in Statistics from a recognized university, OR a degree in Mathematics, Economics, Commerce or Computer Science with Statistics as a paper. possessed as on 13 October 2026. Statistics must appear as a paper in your degree records — verified at certificate verification.

Age Limit

Candidates must be 18–44 years as on 1 July 2026. Relaxations: 5 years for SC, ST, BC and EWS candidates; 10 years for PwBD candidates. Confirm the category table in the official advertisement.

Selection Process

Selection is through a written examination, followed by a qualifying computer test and document verification:

1
Written Examination (CBRT) — Paper-I: General Studies and Mental Ability; Paper-II: subject paper. Each paper has 300 questions for 300 marks in 150 minutes, with 1/3 negative marking for every wrong answer.
2
Computer Proficiency Test — a qualifying test of 100 marks in 60 minutes — candidates must qualify this stage to proceed.
3
Certificate Verification — shortlisted candidates present original documents (degree, age proof, community and local-candidate certificates) before the final selection list.
Note: With 1/3 negative marking, blind guessing is costly — practise 300-question mocks within 150 minutes and keep accuracy high.

How to Apply Online

1
Complete OTPR — register on psc.ap.gov.in via One Time Profile Registration if you have not already.
2
Open Notification 20/2026 — read the vacancy table, eligibility and scheme of examination.
3
Fill the application — enter details exactly as on your certificates.
4
Pay the fee — Rs 330 total (Rs 250 for exam-fee-exempt candidates), paid online.
5
Submit before 2 November 2026, 11:59 PM — save the submitted application PDF and registration ID for the hall-ticket stage.
Tip: Do not wait for the last day — the APPSC portal typically slows down in the final hours. Submit at least 2–3 days before 2 November.

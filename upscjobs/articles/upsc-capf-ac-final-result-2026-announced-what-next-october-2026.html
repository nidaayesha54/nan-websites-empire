UPSC CAPF Assistant Commandant Final Result 2026 Out: 349 Posts — Merit List & Medical Next

The Union Public Service Commission has announced the final result of the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2026. The result covers 349 Assistant Commandant posts across India's paramilitary forces and follows the written examination, the physical and medical screening stages, and the interview/personality test. Candidates who appeared in the final stage can now check the merit list on upsc.gov.in — and those who made it must prepare for the medical examination and service allocation that follow.

CAPF AC 2026 — Key Facts

ExaminationCentral Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2026
Conducting AuthorityUnion Public Service Commission (UPSC)
Total Posts349 Assistant Commandant
Result StatusFinal result announced — October 2026
Stages CompletedWritten exam → PST/PET → Interview/Personality Test
Official Websiteupsc.gov.in

The Assistant Commandant is a Group A gazetted officer post — the direct-entry officer rank in the CAPFs. The final merit is prepared on the combined marks of the written examination and the interview, and the published list carries the roll numbers of recommended candidates in order of merit.

Forces Covered — 349 Posts

The 349 vacancies are distributed across the five Central Armed Police Forces as per the official notification:

  • Border Security Force (BSF)
  • Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF)
  • Central Industrial Security Force (CISF)
  • Indo-Tibetan Border Police (ITBP)
  • Sashastra Seema Bal (SSB)

Service allocation follows the candidate's order of preference and rank in the final merit, subject to medical fitness and the vacancy position in each force. Check the official result notice for the force-wise breakup as published by UPSC.

How to Check the CAPF AC Final Result 2026

1
Visit upsc.gov.in — open the What's New or Final Results section.
2
Find the result link — click "Final Result: Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination, 2026".
3
Download the merit PDF — the list is published in order of merit with roll numbers (and names in the consolidated list).
4
Search your roll number — use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac).
5
Note your rank — your merit position decides your service allocation; save and print the PDF.

What Happens After the Final Result

Recommendation by UPSC is not the appointment itself. The remaining stages are administrative and medical:

1
Detailed Medical Examination (DME) — recommended candidates undergo the prescribed CAPF medical standards (vision, hearing, physical fitness parameters). The schedule is communicated by the respective force or the nodal authority.
2
Review Medical Examination (RME) — candidates declared unfit in the DME may appeal for a review medical within the stipulated window.
3
Service allocation — BSF, CRPF, CISF, ITBP or SSB, based on merit-cum-preference and available vacancies.
4
Training — at the respective force academy before the first posting.

Get a private health check done now if you have any doubt about the medical standards — vision correction, weight parameters and old injuries are the commonest causes of delay at the DME stage. Keep your original certificates, photo ID and the result printout ready for every call-up.

Note: The DME schedule and joining instructions are issued after the final result — they do not appear on the UPSC site alone. Track the official websites of the allotted force as well as upsc.gov.in.

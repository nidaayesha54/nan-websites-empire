GTA 6 Price in India: What Indian Players Pay

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched in India at midnight Indian Standard Time on Thursday, November 19, 2026, ending more than thirteen years of waiting since GTA V arrived in September 2013. The price is Rs 5,999 for the Standard Edition and Rs 7,499 for the Ultimate Edition, the same on the PlayStation Store and the Xbox Store, on both PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. This guide covers the editions, the pre-order bonus, the collectible box that appeared on Blinkit, and the code region rule that catches many Indian buyers out.

India is one of the markets where the price story is straightforward — the same number on both stores, tax already included — and the complications are elsewhere: in the boxed copy, in the regional code rules, and in the fact that there is still no PC version for a country that first met the series on computers.

The prices

On the Indian PlayStation Store, the Standard Edition costs Rs 5,999 and the Ultimate Edition costs Rs 7,499. The Xbox Store lists the same two prices, so there is no platform advantage on price: whichever console you own, you pay the same. The Rs 1,500 difference buys the Ultimate Edition Upgrade — extra vehicles, weapons, outfits and shops that unlock chapter by chapter through the campaign — and the upgrade is also sold separately for Rs 1,500, so you can start with Standard and upgrade later for the same money.

Unlike Brazil or the United States, where tax is added at checkout, the listed Indian price already includes tax, according to the stores' terms. The number on the page is the number you pay. For comparison, the game costs $79.99 in the United States and £69.99 in the UK, and at Rs 5,999 it is almost exactly double what GTA V cost in India at its launch — a jump, but a smaller one than many players feared before the price was announced.

What each edition includes

The Standard Edition includes the full game: the single-player campaign and complete access to the open world of Leonida and Vice City. Pre-orders and purchases made before November 20 also include the Vintage Vice City Pack plus a free month of GTA+ for digital pre-orders, so buying at launch came with that bonus attached.

The Ultimate Edition includes everything in the Standard Edition, plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade: classic vehicles, unique vehicle customisation shops, exclusive weapons, character outfits, custom hairstyles, extra side activities and additional storyline missions connected directly to Jason and Lucia. The extras unlock chapter by chapter rather than all at once, so the Ultimate Edition is a slow drip of content across the campaign, not a single unlock on day one.

The Blinkit box

One of the most talked-about Indian launch details had nothing to do with the stores. The game landed on Blinkit, the quick-commerce app, in a collectible box available in select areas across eleven cities — and there is no disc inside, only a download code, because the game is sold purely as a download even in stores. The box is a keepsake; the game itself is a code.

That is true of every boxed copy, not just Blinkit's. Retail boxes for GTA 6 hold a download code instead of a disc, so buying the box does not save you the download: you still need to download the full game, up to 212GB, and have the storage free before you can play. Pre-loading opened on November 10 for digital pre-orders, so players who bought early could download in advance and start at midnight.

Codes and regions

The region rule is the detail most Indian buyers miss. PlayStation codes only work on accounts registered in the same regional group where they were sold, and India sits in the Rest of EMEA and Asia group — so a PlayStation code bought on holiday abroad may not work on an Indian account. Xbox codes carry no regional lock, so a copy bought anywhere can be redeemed on an Indian Xbox account.

The practical advice is simple: buy PlayStation codes from the Indian store or an Indian retailer, and do not assume a cheaper code from another region will redeem. For Xbox, the region does not matter, which makes the Xbox version the safer buy if you are shopping across borders.

Consoles and storage

The game runs on four consoles at launch: PlayStation 5, PlayStation 5 Pro, Xbox Series X and Xbox Series S. There is no PC version announced, which is a shift for a series many Indians first met on a computer — though GTA V took the same route and reached PC in 2015, two years after its console launch. The game runs at 30 frames per second on consoles during development, according to the studio's head of development, and no faster mode has been announced for launch.

Indian console prices for context: the PlayStation 5 Digital Edition costs Rs 44,990, the Disc Edition Rs 54,990, the Xbox Series S between Rs 39,990 and Rs 44,990, and the Xbox Series X between Rs 55,990 and Rs 74,999. Whichever you own, clear at least 212GB of storage before launch day, or the download will not fit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GTA 6 on PC in India?

No. No PC version has been announced, and the game launched on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only.

What time did GTA 6 launch in India?

At 12 a.m. IST on Thursday, November 19, 2026, following the rolling midnight launch in every region.

Is the Ultimate Edition worth Rs 1,500 more?

It depends on the extras. The Rs 1,500 buys the Ultimate Edition Upgrade — vehicles, weapons, outfits and shops that unlock across the campaign. If you want those, it is the same price as upgrading later; if you do not, Standard is the complete game.

The bottom line: in India, GTA 6 costs Rs 5,999 for Standard and Rs 7,499 for Ultimate, the same on both consoles, with tax included and a midnight launch. Buy PlayStation codes in India, remember the box holds a code and not a disc, and clear 212GB before you download.

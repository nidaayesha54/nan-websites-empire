The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has released the final answer key for the Assistant Prosecution Officer (Preliminary) Competitive Examination 2026 (Advt 13/2026), covering 300 vacancies. The final keys for both the General Studies and Law papers are now available as PDFs on bpsc.bihar.gov.in.

The prelims exam was held on 15 July 2026 and the provisional answer key was published on 12 August 2026, after which candidates were invited to file objections. The Commission has now reviewed those objections and published the final, binding key — this is the version the prelims result will be computed against.

What the Final Key Changes

  • Final key date: released 12 October 2026 per most outlets (a few report 13 October — see honesty flag below).
  • Papers covered: General Studies and Law — separate PDFs.
  • Objections: representations filed against the provisional key (published 12 August) have been reviewed; the final key is not open to further challenge.
  • Next stage: the prelims result is still awaited; qualifiers move to the APO Mains written examination, then an interview.

How to Download the BPSC APO Final Answer Key

1
Visit bpsc.bihar.gov.in and open the notice/advertisement section on the homepage.
2
Find the APO final answer key link under Advt 13/2026 (Assistant Prosecution Officer Prelims) — GS and Law are separate PDFs.
3
Download both PDFs and match them against your question booklet set — check your set code before comparing answers.
4
Estimate your score using the prelims marking scheme; remember that only the final key counts — provisional-key comparisons are now obsolete.

What Comes After the Final Key

With the final key out, the prelims result is the next milestone — the Commission typically publishes a roll-number list of mains-eligible candidates within weeks of the final key. Start preparing for the Mains written examination now: APO Mains is a conventional/descriptive-law paper set, very different from the objective prelims, and the gap between the prelims result and the Mains is usually short.

Also keep your law degree certificate, caste/EWS certificates and domicile documents ready — document verification follows the Mains for shortlisted candidates.

Honesty flag: the release date is reported as 12 October by the Times of India, Telegraph India and FreeJobAlert, but as 13 October by Jagran Josh, SarkariExam and SarkariJobers — we report it as 12–13 October 2026. One Hindi blog claimed specific numbers of questions were dropped from the Law and GS papers — that claim is unverified and deliberately omitted here. Download only from bpsc.bihar.gov.in; do not trust key PDFs forwarded on social media.

The Madhya Pradesh Public Service Commission (MPPSC) has notified Advt. No. 13/2026 for Law Officer posts in the Jail Department. Only 4 vacancies are on offer (2 UR, 1 SC, 1 OBC), but at Pay Level-12 (₹56,100–1,77,500) this is a rare senior-grade law opening for advocates.

The application window opened on 15 October 2026 and the normal-fee deadline is 5 November 2026 on mppsc.mp.gov.in. MPPSC has also published late-fee windows running to 18 November 2026. Eligibility, fee structure and the reported exam date are below.

The post carries Pay Level-12 (₹56,100–1,77,500) — a Group-A equivalent scale in the Jail Department.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

A Bachelor’s degree in Law (LLB) with a minimum of 2 years practice experience as an advocate.

Age Limit

21 to 40 years as on 01.01.2027. Five years’ relaxation applies for MP-domicile SC/ST/OBC (non-creamy layer), EWS, PwBD, government employees and women, with an absolute maximum of 45 years after relaxations.

Application Fee and Late-Fee Windows

MP-domicile SC/ST/OBC (NCL)/EWS/PwBD₹250
Other categories + non-MP domicile₹500
Correction fee₹50

Late-fee windows (per portal reporting): ₹3,000 for applications during 6–12 November 2026 and ₹25,000 during 13–18 November 2026. The correction window is reported open from 20 October to 7 November 2026.

Selection and Reported Exam Date

The selection follows the standard MPPSC pattern — written examination, followed by interview and document verification (confirm the exact stages in the official PDF). Secondary reporting (24x7jobalerts) places the written exam on 13 December 2026, with admit cards from 3 December 2026 — treat both dates as reported, not official, and verify on mppsc.mp.gov.in.

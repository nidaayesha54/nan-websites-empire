Whether you made the final list or not, the marks data is gold: it reveals exactly where the written and final cutoffs landed, which tells every future aspirant how many marks the written stage really demands. This guide explains how to download your marksheet and how to read the numbers.

How to Check Your UPSC CAPF AC 2024 Marks

1
Visit upsc.gov.in — open the UPSC homepage and look for the 'Marks of Recommended / Non-recommended Candidates' link for CAPF (AC) 2024.
2
Enter your roll number — use the roll number from your CAPF AC 2024 admit card, along with your date of birth where asked.
3
Download the marksheet PDF — the PDF shows your Paper 1 and Paper 2 written scores and, for interviewed candidates, the interview marks and final total.
4
Compare with the official cutoffs — UPSC publishes category-wise written and final cutoffs alongside the marks — line your scores up against them.
5
Save a copy — marks links stay live for a limited window (typically around 30 days); download and archive your PDF.

Reading the CAPF AC Cutoffs — What the Numbers Tell You

Written cutoff vs final cutoff

UPSC declares two cutoffs: the written cutoff (Paper 1 + Paper 2) that decides who reaches the PET/PST and interview, and the final cutoff (written + interview) that decides recommendation. The gap between them shows how much the 150-mark interview can move a borderline candidate — in tight years, interview performance decides dozens of ranks.

Paper 1 is the real filter

Paper 1 (General Ability and Intelligence, 250 marks, objective) is the screening paper — only candidates clearing its minimum go on to have Paper 2 (General Studies, Essay and Comprehension, 200 marks, descriptive) evaluated. Year after year, the data shows the same lesson: aspirants who treat Paper 1 as "just qualifying" and underprepare it are the ones who miss the written cutoff.

CAPF AC Selection Stages — A Quick Recap

The CAPF Assistant Commandant selection runs through a written examination (Paper 1 objective + Paper 2 descriptive), Physical Standards and Physical Efficiency Tests, and finally the interview/personality test. Medical fitness standards for the armed police forces apply throughout. The 2024 cycle's published marks now let aspirants benchmark each stage precisely.

For non-recommended candidates: UPSC also publishes the marks of non-qualified candidates, so even if you did not make the final list you can see exactly where you fell short — the most honest feedback any exam gives.

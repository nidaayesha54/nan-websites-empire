UPSC NDA II 2026 SSB Interview: Register by 23 Oct — 5-Day SSB Process Explained

The Union Public Service Commission declared the NDA & NA Examination (II) 2026 written result on 9 October 2026, shortlisting candidates for the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview — and time is now running short. Qualified candidates must register on the Indian Army recruitment portal within two weeks of the result, which makes 23 October 2026 the registration deadline. Missing this window means losing your SSB call-up even with a written-qualified roll number, so every shortlisted candidate should complete the formalities on joinindianarmy.nic.in immediately.

The NDA II 2026 cycle offers 394 vacancies across the Army, Navy and Air Force wings of the National Defence Academy (158th Course) and the 10+2 Cadet Entry at the Indian Naval Academy (120th INAC) — all courses commencing 1 July 2027. This guide covers the registration steps, documents to carry, and the five-day SSB interview structure.

How to Register for the NDA SSB Interview (Before 23 Oct)

1
Open joinindianarmy.nic.in — the Indian Army recruitment portal, not the UPSC website.
2
Log in or create an account — if you registered earlier, log in and verify your details; new users must create a candidate profile.
3
Complete the SSB registration formalities — confirm your contact details, preferences and written-exam particulars as instructed in the result notice.
4
Save the acknowledgement receipt — keep it safe; it is your proof of registration.
5
Watch your email and spam folder — your SSB selection centre and interview dates will be communicated to your registered email ID.

For portal issues, use the helpdesk email in the result notice with a description and screenshot of the error.

Documents to Carry to the SSB Centre

  • Class 10 certificate showing your date of birth.
  • Class 12 pass certificate or provisional certificate with mark sheets.
  • Valid government photo ID — Aadhaar, PAN, passport or driving licence.
  • Self-attested copies of every document, plus the originals.
  • The SSB call-up letter and registration acknowledgement.

Do not send any original certificate to UPSC — originals are produced before the SSB on the first day of the interview.

The 5-Day SSB Interview: What to Expect

The SSB is a five-day personality and aptitude assessment, and understanding its structure removes most of the fear:

  • Day 1 – Screening: OIR tests plus the Picture Perception and Description Test (PPDT), ending with group discussion.
  • Day 2 – Psychology: TAT, WAT, SRT and self-description — all timed, all handwritten.
  • Days 3–4 – Group Testing: Group discussion, planning exercise, group tasks, individual obstacles, command task and lecturette.
  • Day 5 – Personal Interview & Conference: The one-on-one interview with the Interviewing Officer, followed by the board conference where assessors finalize recommendations.

The SSB tests officer-like qualities — leadership, teamwork, initiative and composure. Be genuine: assessors spot rehearsed answers.

Tip: Fitness matters from Day 1. The individual obstacles and group tasks demand stamina — keep a daily running and basic strength routine going until your SSB date.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date for NDA II 2026 SSB registration?
Qualified candidates must register on joinindianarmy.nic.in within two weeks of the 9 October 2026 result — the deadline is 23 October 2026.
Where do I register for the NDA SSB interview?
On the Indian Army recruitment portal at joinindianarmy.nic.in. Candidates already registered there should verify their details instead of re-registering.
How many vacancies are in NDA II 2026?
394 vacancies: Army 208, Navy 42, Air Force 120 and 24 under the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme at the Indian Naval Academy.
When do the NDA 158th Course and 120th INAC commence?
Both courses commence on 1 July 2027, subject to final merit, medical fitness and eligibility.
Should I send my original certificates to UPSC?
No. Original certificates of age and educational qualification must be submitted to the Services Selection Board on the first day of the SSB interview, not to UPSC.

Written qualification is only the first gate — the SSB interview decides who actually joins the academies in July 2027. Register on joinindianarmy.nic.in before 23 October 2026, keep your documents ready, and prepare for all five days of assessment with a calm, consistent routine.

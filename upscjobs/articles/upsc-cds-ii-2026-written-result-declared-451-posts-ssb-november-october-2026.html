UPSC CDS II 2026 Written Result Declared: 451 Vacancies — SSB Interviews from November

The Union Public Service Commission has declared the Combined Defence Services (CDS) Examination (II) 2026 written result, shortlisting candidates for the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview. The result was published on 9 October 2026 for the written examination held on 13 September 2026, and the merit list PDF with qualified roll numbers is available on the official UPSC website at upsc.gov.in. This cycle fills 451 vacancies across the Indian Military Academy, Indian Naval Academy, Air Force Academy and Officers' Training Academy — and qualified candidates should now shift their entire focus to SSB preparation.

The CDS II 2026 provisional answer key was released on 16 September 2026 with objections accepted until 20 September, and the Commission has now finalized the written result after examining those objections. Interviews at the Officers' Training Academy, Chennai, are expected to begin from November 2026, with SSB boards for the other academies following in the subsequent months. If your roll number is in the PDF, the clock has started — here is what the result means and what comes next.

How to Check the CDS II 2026 Result PDF

1
Visit upsc.gov.in — the official UPSC website.
2
Open the What's New / Examinations section — look for the CDS (II) 2026 result link.
3
Download the result PDF — it contains the roll numbers and names of written-qualified candidates.
4
Search your roll number — use Ctrl+F and confirm you are checking the correct examination and year.
5
Save the PDF — keep a copy for the SSB stage and document verification.

The result PDF does not carry individual marks. UPSC will upload the marks and the written-examination cutoff only after the final result — the marks sheet stays available for 30 days from upload, so download yours in that window.

What Happens After Qualifying: SSB, Medical, Merit

  • SSB Interview: The five-day assessment — screening, psychology tests, group testing tasks and personal interview — followed by the board conference. SSB call-up details are communicated to qualified candidates through the official channels.
  • Document verification: Shortlisted candidates must submit copies of essential documents to the Commission as instructed in the result notice.
  • Medical examination: Candidates recommended by the SSB undergo the services medicals; final selection depends on merit, medical fitness and vacancy availability.
  • Final merit list: Prepared on combined written + SSB marks for the 451 academy vacancies.

Unlike NDA, the CDS entry is for graduates — IMA and OTA take any-stream graduates, while the Naval Academy and Air Force Academy require Physics and Mathematics backgrounds. The OTA (SSC) entries are particularly popular because they offer a direct short-service commission route with no academy-specific written paper beyond the standard CDS pattern.

Tip: Trust only upsc.gov.in for CDS result updates. UPSC has previously warned candidates about fake result notices circulating on messaging apps — never act on a forwarded PDF link without verifying it on the official site.

UPSC NDA II 2026 Written Result Declared: 394 Posts — Check Roll No. List & SSB Next Steps

The Union Public Service Commission has declared the written result of the National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (II), 2026 on 9 October 2026. Candidates who appeared for the examination held on 13 September 2026 can now check whether their roll number features in the qualified list. This cycle covers 394 vacancies across the Army, Navy and Air Force wings of the NDA and the 10+2 Cadet Entry Scheme of the Naval Academy — and every qualified candidate must now register for the Services Selection Board (SSB) interview without delay.

The written examination carried 900 marks in total — Mathematics (300 marks) and the General Ability Test (600 marks), with one-third negative marking for wrong answers. The result PDF lists only the roll numbers of candidates who cleared the written stage; marks are not disclosed at this point.

How to Check the NDA II Written Result 2026

1
Visit upsc.gov.in — open the What's New or Examinations section on the homepage.
2
Find the result link — click "Written Result: National Defence Academy and Naval Academy Examination (II), 2026".
3
Open the PDF — the published list contains the roll numbers of qualified candidates.
4
Search your roll number — press Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac) and type your roll number.
5
Save the PDF — download and print it; you will need it through the SSB stage.

What Next — The SSB Interview

Qualifying the written examination is only the first filter. Every qualified candidate must now appear before a Services Selection Board. The official press note accompanying the result carries the exact instructions — read it first, then act:

1
Register for the SSB interview on the Indian Army recruitment website (joinindianarmy.nic.in). Result portals report the registration deadline as roughly two weeks from the result — confirm the exact date in the official press note and do not miss it.
2
Wait for your SSB centre and date — these are allotted after registration and communicated to you.
3
Prepare your original documents — 10th and 12th certificates and marksheets, photo ID, the result PDF printout and category certificates if applicable.

The SSB is a multi-day assessment of personality, leadership potential and aptitude — screening tests, psychological tests, group tasks and a personal interview, followed by a medical examination for recommended candidates. Start physical preparation alongside: running, basic fitness and current-affairs awareness all matter at this stage.

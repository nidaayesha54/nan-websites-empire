UPSC EPFO APFC Recruitment 2026

80 Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner posts under Special Advertisement 52/2026 — applications closed 14 September; Recruitment Test on 20 December 2026.

UPSC EPFO APFC 2026: Key Facts

PostAssistant Provident Fund Commissioner (APFC), Employees' Provident Fund Organisation
Total Vacancies80 (UR 29 · EWS 8 · OBC 18 · SC 15 · ST 10)
AdvertisementSpecial Advertisement No. 52/2026
EligibilityBachelor's degree in any discipline from a recognised university
Age LimitMax 35 years (UR/EWS), 38 (OBC), 40 (SC/ST) as on the closing date
Application Window22 August – 14 September 2026 (extended from 11 September)
Recruitment Test20 December 2026 (offline, OMR-based)
Official Websiteupsc.gov.in

The Union Public Service Commission notified 80 Assistant Provident Fund Commissioner vacancies in the Employees' Provident Fund Organisation under Special Advertisement No. 52/2026. The short notice appeared on 19 August 2026, the detailed notification on 21 August, and online applications ran from 22 August to 14 September 2026 (extended from the original 11 September deadline). This year's cycle covers only the APFC post — EPFO withdrew its earlier requisition for Enforcement Officer/Accounts Officer vacancies. The Recruitment Test is scheduled for 20 December 2026.

Pay Scale and Post Profile

APFC is a Group A gazetted post at Pay Level-10 of the 7th Central Pay Commission, with a pay scale of ₹56,100 – ₹1,77,500 plus DA, HRA and other allowances, and a one-year probation period. Three vacancies are reserved for PwBD candidates as per applicable conditions.

Exam Pattern 2026

StageDetails
Recruitment Test120 objective questions · 300 marks · 2 hours · offline OMR mode
Negative MarkingOne-third of the marks assigned to a question deducted for each wrong answer
Interview100 marks — subject knowledge, current affairs, academic/professional background
Final MeritRecruitment Test and Interview weighted 75:25

Syllabus Overview

  • General English
  • Indian culture, heritage and freedom movements
  • Developmental issues and present trends in the Indian economy
  • Governance and the Constitution of India
  • General science and basic computer applications
  • Elementary mathematics, statistics and general mental ability
  • Industrial relations, labour codes and social security in India
  • Principles of accountancy, auditing and insurance
  • Current events of national and international importance

The labour-codes and social-security segment is the highest-weightage differentiator for this exam — candidates with a commerce or law background should anchor their preparation there, while others should build it from standard references.

Application Fee and Next Steps

The application fee was ₹25 for General/OBC/EWS male candidates; SC/ST/PwBD candidates and all female candidates paid nothing. With applications closed, registered candidates should now:

1
Download the notification PDF from upsc.gov.in and map the syllabus topic-by-topic against the 120-question pattern.
2
Practise OMR-format mocks — the test is offline with one-third negative marking, so accuracy discipline matters more than attempts.
3
Watch for the admit card — UPSC releases e-admit cards a few weeks before the 20 December test; download it from the UPSC website only.
4
Prepare interview material early — the 100-mark interview carries 25% weightage, so build subject notes alongside the written preparation.

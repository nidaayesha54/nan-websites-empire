The Andhra Pradesh Public Service Commission (APPSC) has issued Brief Notification No. 37/2026 (dated 13 October 2026) for 686 provisional Group-II Services vacancies — one of the largest current PSC openings in the country. Online applications will run from 10 November 2026 to 30 November 2026 at psc.ap.gov.in.

This is the pre-window sweet spot for aspirants: the brief notification confirms the posts and schedule, while the detailed notification — with eligibility, application fee, category-wise vacancies and exam dates — is due on or before 10 November 2026. Post-wise breakup and the selection structure are below; treat fee and eligibility details as pending until that detailed notice lands.

Salary bands vary by post — from ₹25,220–80,910 (Junior Accountant) up to ₹45,830–1,30,580 (Municipal Commissioner Grade-III / GST Officer).

What Is Confirmed vs What Is Pending

Confirmed in the brief notification: the 12 post categories, the provisional 686 total, the application window (10–30 November 2026) and the selection stages.

Pending the detailed notification (due on/before 10 Nov 2026): educational eligibility per post, application fee, category-wise vacancy split, exact age limits and relaxations, and exam dates. Do not treat the 18–42 age band as final — it comes from current reporting, not the official brief.

Selection Process

1
Screening Test — offline, OMR-based objective test (reported as 150 marks).
2
Main Examination — for screening-qualified candidates.
3
Computer Proficiency Test (CPT) — final stage.

Both the Screening and Main exams carry 1/3rd negative marking per wrong answer (per current reporting).

How to Apply (Window Opens 10 November)

1
Wait for the detailed notification (on or before 10 Nov 2026) on psc.ap.gov.in — it carries eligibility and fee.
2
Register on the APPSC portal once the Group-II application link goes live on 10 November.
3
Choose your post preferences carefully — Deputy Tahsildar and Deputy MPDO account for over half the vacancies.
4
Submit by 30 November 2026, 11:59 PM and keep the confirmation for the admit-card stage.

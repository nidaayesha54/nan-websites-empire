UPSC CAPF AC Physical Admit Card 2026

PST/PET/DME call letters out for 349 Assistant Commandant posts — physical tests run 13–16 October 2026. Download steps, physical standards and exam-day rules.

The Union Public Service Commission released the Physical Standard Test (PST), Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Detailed Medical Examination (DME) call letters for candidates who qualified the written examination of the Central Armed Police Forces (Assistant Commandants) Examination 2026. Only roll numbers shortlisted after the 19 July written exam can access the call letter, and the physical tests are being conducted from 13 to 16 October 2026 under a nodal authority appointed by the Ministry of Home Affairs.

How to Download the CAPF AC Physical Admit Card

1
Visit the SSB recruitment portal — open recruitment.ssb.gov.in, the official admit-card portal for this stage.
2
Open the Admit Card section — on the homepage, click the admit card tab.
3
Find CAPFs (AC) Exam-2026 — look for the link under Advertisement No. 08/2026-CAPF (PST/PET stage).
4
Enter your details — roll number, date of birth in DD-MM-YYYY format, first two letters of your name and first two letters of your father's name.
5
Download and print — take a clear A4 printout; carry it with a photo ID and your Detailed Application Form printout to the venue.

PST: Physical Standards

Measurements are strict and taken before the PET events:

ParameterMenWomen
Height (minimum)165 cm157 cm
Chest81 cm unexpanded, min. 5 cm expansionNot applicable

Weight is assessed against age-height proportion charts. Reach the venue 60–90 minutes early — late arrival after gate closure usually means refusal, and centre changes are not allowed.

PET: Qualifying Events

The PET is qualifying — candidates who fail any event do not proceed to the interview:

100-metre race16 seconds18 seconds

Shot put 7.26 kg (3 chances)4.5 metresNot applicable

What to Carry and What Happens Next

  • Printed e-admit card — without the printout, entry can be denied.
  • Original photo ID — preferably the same ID used during application.
  • Detailed Application Form printout — UPSC asked qualified candidates to complete the post-result update window and keep the submitted form ready.
  • Category/EWS/NCC certificates — if claimed in the application.
  • Sportswear and running shoes — avoid jewellery and electronic devices.

Candidates who clear PST/PET are called for the Interview/Personality Test carrying 150 marks, followed by medical standards tests. The final merit list is prepared on written-exam plus interview marks, subject to medical fitness.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are the UPSC CAPF AC PST/PET 2026 tests held?
The Physical Standard Test, Physical Efficiency Test and Detailed Medical Examination for UPSC CAPF (ACs) 2026 are held from 13 to 16 October 2026. Download the admit card from recruitment.ssb.gov.in.
Where can I download the UPSC CAPF AC physical admit card 2026?
From the official Sashastra Seema Bal recruitment portal at recruitment.ssb.gov.in, under the admit card section for CAPFs (AC) Exam-2026, Advertisement No. 08/2026-CAPF.
What are the physical standards for UPSC CAPF AC 2026?
Men: minimum 165 cm height, 81–86 cm chest, 50 kg baseline weight. Women: minimum 157 cm height, 46 kg baseline weight, subject to age-height proportion charts.
What PET events must CAPF AC candidates clear?
Men: 100 m in 16 seconds, 800 m in 3 min 45 sec, 3.5 m long jump and 4.5 m shot put. Women: 100 m in 18 seconds, 800 m in 4 minutes and 3.0 m long jump. PET is qualifying.
What comes after CAPF AC PST/PET?
Qualifiers face the Interview/Personality Test carrying 150 marks, then medical standards tests. The final merit is based on written exam plus interview marks.

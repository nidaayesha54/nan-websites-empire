How to Apply

1
Read the official notification — check eligibility, age limit, fees and vacancy details. All applications are submitted only on the official UPSC website upsc.gov.in. The Commission also issues the e-admit card there.
2
Register on the official portal — upsc.gov.in — create your account with a valid email and mobile number for the online application.
3
Fill & submit the form — upload photo, signature and documents as specified; pay the fee online and save the confirmation page.
4
Download your admit card — released 2–3 weeks before the exam on the same official portal; check your exam city and shift.
5
Understand the exam pattern — review Prelims/Mains syllabus, marking scheme and previous-year cutoffs, then track results on the official site.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the UPSC Civil Services Exam?
The UPSC Civil Services Examination selects officers for the IAS, IPS, IFS and other Group A/B central services through Prelims, Mains and Interview.
What is the eligibility for UPSC CSE 2027?
A bachelor's degree from a recognized university; general-category age limit 21–32 years with relaxation for reserved categories.
How do I apply for UPSC exams?
Apply online only at upsc.gov.in during the notification window — read the official notification PDF first.
What is the exam pattern?
Prelims (objective, qualifying) → Mains (9 papers, merit) → Personality Test/Interview (275 marks).
When will the UPSC CSE 2027 Prelims be held?
As per the exam calendar pattern, Prelims are expected in May 2027. Confirm exact dates on upsc.gov.in.
Where do I download the UPSC admit card?
The e-admit card is released 3 weeks before the exam on upsc.gov.in — no physical hall ticket is sent.

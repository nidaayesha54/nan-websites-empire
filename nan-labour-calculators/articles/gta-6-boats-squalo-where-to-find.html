GTA 6 Boats: Where to Find the Squalo and More

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and its map is built around water: Vice City on the coast, the Leonida Keys strung out to the south, the Grassrivers swamps inland, and Gambit Bay open to the sea. Boats are not a side feature in this game — they are how you reach some of its best places, how you fish, and how you get away. This guide explains every boat and watercraft in GTA 6, where to find them, and the one rule that catches every player out: you cannot keep a boat the way you keep a car.

That rule is the most important fact in this guide, so it goes first. No boat in GTA 6 can ever be registered as a personal vehicle. Cars, motorcycles and dirt bikes can be yours; boats cannot. A boat you steal, find or are given is a boat you are borrowing, not a boat you own — and the game will remind you of that the moment you expect it to be waiting where you left it.

Your First Boat: Free in Key Lento

Your first boat is free, and it is waiting behind Jason's house in Key Lento. GameSpot reported on November 19, 2026 that the fishing boat is tied up at the dock behind the house, available in every edition of the game, with no purchase and no unlock. It is yours to use for as long as Jason lives there — which is the catch. When the story leaves Key Lento, you lose the boat. It does not follow you to Vice City, and there is no way to get it back.

Use it while you have it. The Keys are the game's quietest, most beautiful water, and the fishing boat is the way to see them properly: the canals, the open water, the run out to Gambit Bay. It is also the practical way to reach the best fishing spots, including the ones Cal's side quest will send you to. The boat is free, the water is free, and the time you spend on it is some of the calmest in the game.

The Shitzu Squalo: Ultimate Edition, Chapter 4

The most famous boat in GTA 6 is the Shitzu Squalo — a pink and blue speedboat, docked at Washington Beach, included in the Ultimate Edition. Rockstar's own description of it is worth quoting: it is "made open-ocean-ready with an explosives-laden weapons crate", and it is "perfect for casting in Gambit Bay and reeling in catches of all sorts". That is a boat with two jobs: fishing, and fighting.

You cannot get it at the start. The Squalo unlocks in Chapter 4, at a dock on the Washington Beach canal — just left of the Y Hotel, according to post-launch guides — and you will find it listed in the Personal section of the map index. It is the first boat you can own in the game, in the sense that the Ultimate Edition gives it to you, but the personal-vehicle rule still applies: even the Squalo is never registered as your vehicle the way a car is. Treat it as a gift you can keep coming back to, not as property in a garage.

Kayak, Jet Ski and Airboat

The Squalo is not the only watercraft. The Crest Kayak arrives with the Ultimate Edition in Chapter 1, at Jason's safehouse — a quiet, human-powered way to explore the water, and the slowest boat in the game by a long way. The Speedophile Seashark jet ski is the opposite: fast, agile, good at jumping waves, found beached along Ocean Beach and tied to floating marina docks. And in the Grassrivers swamps, an airboat sits at a dock badged "Airgator Airboat 09", ready for the shallow water where nothing with a propeller can go.

Those three cover the whole range of GTA 6's water: the kayak for slow exploration, the jet ski for speed, the airboat for the swamps. None of them can be registered as a personal vehicle either. Find one, use it, and do not expect it to be there tomorrow.

Boats You Can See But Not Drive

The trailers show motorboats, yachts and cruise ships — big, expensive vessels that make Vice City's water look like a real place. It is not yet clear which of them you can drive. The same goes for the Squalo's appearances in the Leonida Keys in Rockstar's screenshots, and the two Squalos on trailers outside Brian's Boat Works and Marina. Brian Heder, whose bio says he runs a boat yard in the Keys, is part of the story — but a boat yard in the story is not a boat shop in the game, and no purchase price for any boat has been published.

The honest position is that the drivable list is short and the visible list is long. What is confirmed is the Key Lento fishing boat, the Squalo, the Crest Kayak, the Seashark jet ski and the Grassrivers airboat. Everything else — the yachts, the cruise ships, the motorboats in the trailers — is scenery until players prove otherwise.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the Squalo in GTA 6?

The Shitzu Squalo is an Ultimate Edition bonus that unlocks in Chapter 4, docked at a canal dock on Washington Beach, just left of the Y Hotel. Look for it in the Personal section of the map index. It is pink and blue, carries an explosives crate, and is made for fishing in Gambit Bay.

Can you keep a boat in GTA 6?

No — not in the way you keep a car. No boat can ever be registered as a personal vehicle, even the Ultimate Edition's Squalo. You can use any boat you find, but it will not be saved as yours. Jason's free Key Lento fishing boat is also lost once the story leaves Key Lento.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6's boats are free, fun and temporary. Start with the fishing boat behind Jason's Key Lento house, grab the Crest Kayak in Chapter 1, and pick up the pink Shitzu Squalo at the Washington Beach canal dock in Chapter 4. Use the jet ski for speed and the airboat for the swamps — and remember the rule: no boat in GTA 6 can ever be registered as yours, so enjoy it while you have it.

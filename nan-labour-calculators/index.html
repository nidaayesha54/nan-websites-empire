Your Rights Under Pakistani Labour Law

Overtime

  • Normal limits: 9 hours/day, 48 hours/week for adult workers.
  • Overtime is paid at double (200%) the ordinary rate of pay.
  • The ordinary hourly rate = (monthly salary × 12) ÷ (52 × normal weekly hours).
  • Many employers underpay by using basic salary only — the law counts gross salary.

Gratuity

  • Applies to commercial establishments with 20+ workmen and industrial ones with 50+.
  • Payable after more than 1 year of service (any period over 6 months counts as a year).
  • Rate: 30 days' wages per completed year; daily wage = monthly wage ÷ 26.
  • Payable on resignation, termination (except proven misconduct), retirement, or death.
  • No gratuity is due where the employer already contributes to an approved pension/provident fund instead.

FAQs

Who counts as a "workman"?

Anyone doing skilled or unskilled, manual or clerical work — courts look at the nature of duties, not the job title.

Can my employer refuse overtime pay?

No — overtime beyond 9h/day or 48h/week must be compensated at double rate. Contract workers are generally exempt.

Is gratuity paid if I resign?

Yes — gratuity is payable on resignation after 1+ year of service, except in cases of proven misconduct.

What if my employer has a provident fund?

If the employer contributes to an approved fund/pension scheme instead, statutory gratuity may not apply for that period.

AI Product Video Scripts That Sell: 5 Frameworks That Work in 2026

6 min read · October 2026

The AI footage is the easy part. What separates AI product video scripts that sell from the ones that flop is structure — the psychology underneath the words. Here are the five frameworks behind the highest-converting product videos we've analyzed, with a quick example of each.

AD-SLOT · in-article (responsive)

1. PAS — Problem, Agitate, Solution

The workhorse of direct response. Name the pain, twist the knife (show the cost of inaction), then reveal the product as the relief. Best for: problem-aware audiences, paid social.

Example: "Still losing your keys every morning? (problem) That's 10 minutes a day — 60 hours a year — gone. (agitate) KeyTag Pro ends it today. (solution)"

2. AIDA — Attention, Interest, Desire, Action

The classic. Grab attention with a bold claim, build interest with a demo, stoke desire with benefits and proof, then ask for the sale. Best for: landing-page demos and YouTube pre-rolls.

3. The UGC Story

"I was skeptical, but…" — a creator narrates their personal journey from doubt to delight. Feels like content, converts like an ad. Best for: TikTok, Reels, Spark Ads. Our template library has three UGC variants ready to copy.

4. The Demo Walkthrough

No story, just show: open on the product, walk through 3 features in 60 seconds, end with price + CTA. Best for: SaaS, gadgets, and audiences who already know the category. Pair it with our shot list generator to plan every demo beat.

AD-SLOT · in-article (responsive)

5. The Proof Stack

Lead with evidence: ratings, review quotes, before/afters, and a guarantee — stacked back to back. Best for: retargeting warm audiences who need a final push.

9 Product Demo Video Mistakes That Kill Sales

7 min read · October 2026

Most product demo videos fail for fixable reasons — not because the product is bad, but because the video breaks basic conversion rules. Here are the nine mistakes we see most, and the exact fix for each.

AD-SLOT · in-article (responsive)

1. The logo intro

Five seconds of animated logo while viewers decide to leave. Fix: start mid-action or mid-sentence. Your logo goes in the last frame, not the first.

2. Feature dumping

Listing 12 features in 60 seconds means viewers remember zero. Fix: one video = one message. Demo 2–3 features max, and tie each to a benefit.

3. No hook in the first 3 seconds

"Hi guys, welcome back to my channel" is a scroll-past sentence. Fix: open with the problem, the result, or a bold claim. See our 7 hook formulas.

4. Talking about the product instead of the customer

"We built…" "Our technology…" — nobody cares. Fix: rewrite every line from the viewer's perspective: "you get…", "you'll never…".

5. No captions

The majority of social video is watched muted. No captions = invisible ad. Fix: burn in big, readable captions on every video. Auto-generate, then correct manually.

AD-SLOT · in-article (responsive)

6. The uncanny avatar

An AI presenter that looks "almost human" triggers distrust instead of trust. Fix: if the avatar isn't flawless, switch that video to UGC-style or product-only shots. Never ship creepy.

7. Two CTAs (or none)

"Follow us, visit our site, and check the link" = the viewer does nothing. Fix: one specific CTA, shown on screen for the final 5 seconds.

8. Wrong aspect ratio

A 16:9 video posted to TikTok looks tiny and amateur. Fix: edit and export native per platform — 9:16 for TikTok/Reels/Shorts, 16:9 for YouTube, 1:1 for feeds.

9. Publishing once and moving on

One video is a lottery ticket. Fix: make 3–5 hook variants of every video and test them. Hooks are 80% of ad performance — iterate there first.

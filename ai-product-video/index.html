Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about AI product videos, scripts, and tools.

What is an AI product video?

An AI product video is a product demo or advertisement created with the help of AI tools — AI avatars, AI voiceovers, script generation, and AI editing — instead of (or alongside) a traditional film crew. They cost a fraction of studio shoots and can be produced in hours.

How do I write a product demo video script?

Follow the PAS structure: name the customer's Problem in the first 5 seconds, Agitate it (show the cost of not fixing it), then present your product as the Solution and end with one clear CTA. Our free demo script builder turns your product details into this structure automatically.

Which AI tools make the best product demo videos?

HeyGen and Synthesia lead for AI-avatar product explainers, Runway and Kling for cinematic product b-roll, and CapCut Commerce Pro for auto-generated ad creatives. See our guide to the best AI tools for product demos for a full comparison.

Do AI video ads actually convert?

Yes — when they follow proven ad structures (UGC-style hooks, problem/solution framing, social proof, and a single CTA). Our ad script templates library has 15+ formats with copy-paste scripts built on high-converting ad frameworks.

How long should an AI product demo video be?

30–90 seconds for paid social ads, 2–3 minutes for landing-page demos, and under 60 seconds for TikTok/Instagram Reels. Keep one core message per video and put the hook in the first 3 seconds.

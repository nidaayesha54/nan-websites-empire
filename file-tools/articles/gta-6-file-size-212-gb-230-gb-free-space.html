GTA 6 File Size: 212 GB, and Why You Need 230 GB Free

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it is one of the biggest games ever made — in every sense. Rockstar's own guidance says the game needs up to 212 GB of free space, depending on your device, and that single number has caused more confusion than almost anything else about the launch. Pre-load screens, store listings and the printed box all show different figures, and players have spent launch week asking the same question: how big is GTA 6, really?

The short answer is about 210 GB to download and about 212 GB installed. The long answer is that the different numbers are all the same file counted in different ways — and if you only free up exactly 212 GB, you may still be short. This guide explains every figure, where it comes from, and how much space you should actually clear.

The Official Number: Up to 212 GB

Rockstar's Pre-Load FAQ, last updated on November 3, 2026, says GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB of free space, depending on your device. That figure comes from Rockstar's November 3 Newswire post announcing that pre-loading would begin, and it is still the only size Rockstar states for the game. There is no separate PS5 figure and no separate Xbox figure in Rockstar's own material — one number, for both consoles, with the words "up to" doing a lot of work. Pre-loading opened on November 10, at midnight local time on PS5 and at 12 a.m. GMT on Xbox Series X|S, and the game is now out.

That "up to" matters. It means 212 GB is a ceiling, not a promise that every install will be exactly that size. Different consoles, different regions and different editions can all land slightly differently, which is why the numbers players actually saw on their screens did not match the official figure exactly.

The Real Numbers: PS5, Xbox and the Box

Once pre-loading opened, the real figures started coming in. On PS5, early pre-load screens reported about 209.75 GB to download and about 212 GB once installed. Those figures were reported from community captures of the first pre-load screens, not published by Sony or Rockstar — but they are close to Rockstar's ceiling, and they are the best PS5 numbers available. On Xbox, the Microsoft Store catalogue lists the package at 225.34 GB, and the console's storage screen shows about 209.86 GB for the same file.

That gap is not a different download. The Microsoft Store counts a gigabyte as one billion bytes, while the console counts in binary units of 1,073,741,824 bytes. Divide the store's 225,336,918,016 bytes by the decimal figure and you get 225.3 GB; divide it by the binary figure and you get about 209.9 GB. The store and the dashboard are describing the same package in different units. And the printed box is more cautious still: a sealed PS5 copy photographed in Germany lists a 230 GB minimum free space requirement, and Rockstar has not commented on the gap. Boxes are printed weeks ahead and often leave room for patches, so treat 230 GB as the cautious figure.

What Fits, What Does Not, and the Day-One Patch

Consoles reserve part of their drive for the system, so the number on the box is not what you get. An original PS5 has an 825 GB drive with about 667 GB usable, which means GTA 6 takes about 32 percent of it. An Xbox Series X has a 1 TB drive with about 802 GB usable, about 26 percent. The tight one is the Xbox Series S: a 512 GB drive with about 364 GB usable, which means GTA 6 alone takes well over half the drive, and you will probably have room for little else installed alongside it.

Then there is the patch. Rockstar has not announced the size of a launch-day patch, and small updates have already gone out in the days before release. One outlet reports update 01.002.001 at about 602.5 MB on PS5 and 1.6 GB on Xbox Series X|S, but Rockstar has not published patch notes, so treat those as reported figures rather than official ones. The practical advice is simple: aim for around 230 GB free rather than exactly 212 GB, and around 250 GB if you can manage it, because patches only ever make the game bigger.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many GB is GTA 6 on PS5?

Rockstar says up to 212 GB. Early pre-load screens reported about 209.75 GB to download and about 212 GB installed, but neither Sony nor Rockstar has published a PS5-specific figure.

Why does the Xbox store say 225 GB?

It is the same file counted in decimal gigabytes, where 1 GB is one billion bytes. The console counts in binary gigabytes, and the same package shows as about 209.9 GB there. The download is identical.

Is there a disc version of GTA 6?

No. The boxed copy holds a download code, not a disc, so you download the full game either way. On Xbox, cloud gaming streams the game with no install at all.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 is roughly a 210 GB download that wants about 212 GB installed, and the 225 GB figure is the same file in different units. Free up 230 GB, or 250 GB if you can, and you will have room for the game and the patches that follow it — and on a Series S, expect GTA 6 to be most of what your drive holds.

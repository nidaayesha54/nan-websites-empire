PDF to Text Online Free: 5 Methods Compared (2026)

Need the text out of a PDF? Here are the five fastest free methods, ranked by speed and convenience.

1. Browser-based PDF to text tool (fastest)

Drop the PDF into a free online extractor like our PDF to Text tool. The text appears in seconds, ready to copy or download as .txt. Nothing is uploaded — it runs in your browser. Best for: quick one-off extractions.

2. Copy-paste from a PDF reader

Open the PDF in your browser or Adobe Reader, select all (Ctrl+A), copy and paste into a document. Works fine for short documents but formatting often breaks on long ones.

3. Google Docs upload

Upload the PDF to Google Drive, open with Google Docs — it converts the PDF to editable text automatically. Free with a Google account. Best for: editing the extracted text afterward.

4. Desktop PDF software

Free tools like PDF-XChange or LibreOffice Draw can export PDF text. Best for: batch processing many files offline.

5. OCR for scanned PDFs

If the PDF is scanned images of pages, none of the above will work — you need OCR. Free options: Google Drive's built-in OCR, Microsoft OneNote's "Copy Text from Picture", or Tesseract (open source).

Quick comparison

  • Speed: browser tool wins — no install, no upload wait.
  • Privacy: browser tools that process locally (like ours) never see your file.
  • Scanned PDFs: only OCR works — try Google Drive first.
  • Formatting: expect plain text; tables and columns may need cleanup.

The bottom line

For digital PDFs, a browser-based extractor is the fastest free option. Try it now — no signup needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I extract text from a PDF for free?
The fastest free way is a browser-based PDF to text tool — drop the PDF and copy the text. It works on any digital PDF with selectable text.
Why can't I extract text from some PDFs?
Scanned PDFs are images, not real text. You need OCR (optical character recognition) software for those.

Divorce costs in Australia: the fixed fees (from 1 July 2026)

Court fees are set by the Federal Circuit and Family Court of Australia (FCFCOA):

  • Divorce application: $1,170 standard, or $390 reduced for concession-card holders or financial hardship (on a joint application, both must qualify).
  • Consent orders: $215 — how agreed property or parenting settlements become legally binding.
  • Initiating application (parenting or financial, final orders only): $455; final + interim: $610; parenting + financial: $740–$895.
  • Conciliation conference: $510; setting down for hearing: $820–$1,115; daily hearing fee: $820–$1,115.
Key fact: the divorce order only ends the marriage — it does not divide property, split superannuation or sort parenting. Those are separate processes with their own costs, and you can deal with them without ever filing for divorce. You have 12 months from the date the divorce becomes final to apply for property settlement or spousal maintenance (Family Law Act 1975, s 44(3)).

Before you apply

  • You must be separated for 12 months before you can apply for divorce.
  • A joint application is the cheapest route: one shared filing fee, no formal service, and usually no court attendance.
  • A sole application requires serving the papers — at least 28 days before the hearing in Australia, 42 days if overseas.
  • The order becomes final one month and one day after the hearing; only then are you legally divorced and free to remarry.

FAQs

How much is the divorce filing fee in Australia in 2026?

$1,170 standard, or $390 if you qualify for the reduced fee (concession card or financial hardship). On a joint application both parties must qualify.

Does divorce automatically divide our property?

No. The divorce order only ends the marriage. Property, superannuation and spousal maintenance are separate processes — agreed matters are finalised through consent orders ($215 filing fee).

How long do I have to sort out property after divorce?

12 months from the date the divorce becomes final to apply for property settlement or spousal maintenance. After that you need the court's permission, which isn't guaranteed.

Is a joint or sole application cheaper?

Joint. You share one filing fee, nobody has to be formally served, and neither of you usually needs to attend the hearing.

Can I do my own divorce without a lawyer?

Yes — you file online through the Commonwealth Courts Portal. A straightforward divorce is one of the cheapest family-law matters. Lawyers are most valuable when property or parenting is disputed.

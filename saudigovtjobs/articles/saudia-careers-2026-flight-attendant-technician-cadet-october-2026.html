Saudi Government Jobs 2026

Saudia — the Kingdom's national flag carrier — is listing October 2026 openings across cabin crew, aircraft maintenance and its Cadet Program. Check each role's eligibility before applying.

✅ Updated October 22, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

Saudia — the national flag carrier and a state-owned enterprise — is listing six openings in October 2026: Flight Attendant (posted 11 October), Aircraft Maintenance Technician, Aircraft Maintenance Support Technician, Senior Plasma Technician, Section Manager – Test Cell, and the Saudia Cadet Program (posted 17 October).

Saudia flies Boeing 777-300s, 787 Dreamliners and Airbus models from Jeddah, Riyadh and Dammam to 95+ destinations across four continents — with structured training and, for technical staff, licensed-aircraft experience that travels globally.

Honesty first: Saudi government-sector hiring is normally nationals-only, and the Cadet Program is conventionally Saudi-nationals-only. For the other roles, eligibility varies per advert — this article cannot tell you which is which. The six listings come from news roundups (October 19 and 21, 2026) by the same publisher, with no published closing dates, salaries or per-role eligibility. Verify every role on careers.saudia.com before applying.

Eligibility — the Honest Breakdown

Before you apply:

  • Saudia Cadet Program: cadet programs at Gulf flag carriers are conventionally Saudi-nationals-only — non-Saudis should not expect to qualify;
  • Flight Attendant and technical roles: eligibility varies by advert — some Saudia postings admit non-Saudis, others do not. The nationality condition is stated only on the official advert;
  • Government-sector rule: the default assumption is nationals-only unless the advert says otherwise;
  • Never pay to apply: Saudia recruits through its official portal only — walk away from any agent demanding fees.

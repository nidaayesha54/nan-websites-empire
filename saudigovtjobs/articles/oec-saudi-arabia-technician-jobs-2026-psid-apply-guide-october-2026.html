How the OEC Application Process Works

The same process applies to every OEC demand. Follow the steps in order — payment comes before submission, not after.

1
Register as a job seeker on jobs.oec.gov.pk using your CNIC and a mobile number that can receive SMS.
2
Complete your profile: upload your diploma or vocational certificate, experience letters, passport details and a photo — before any demand opens.
3
Open the demand and read it fully. Match the trade, age limit and experience requirement against your documents.
4
Generate your PSID and pay the fee. The portal issues a Payment Slip ID; pay it through the designated channel and keep the receipt number.
5
Submit your application — only after the fee is paid. Unpaid applications are not processed.
6
Save your confirmation and application number for follow-ups with the OEC helpdesk.

The PSID Step — Why the Order Matters

The PSID (Payment Slip ID) is where most first-time applicants go wrong. The portal generates a unique slip for your application; you pay the prescribed official fee against it, and only then does the portal accept your submission. Pay first, then submit — reversing the order leaves the application unprocessed. Generate the PSID inside the OEC portal, never from a link sent on WhatsApp, and keep the receipt number until submission is confirmed. For slip issues, contact the helpdesk — UAN 0311-0011-632 or helpdesk@oec.gov.pk — with your reference number.

Prepare Now for the Next Saudi Demand

New demands close fast — Ad 210's window was only 12 days. Get these ready now:

  • valid passport and attested diploma, plus experience letters from previous employers;
  • CNIC and registered mobile number kept current on jobs.oec.gov.pk;
  • a habit of checking the official OEC website and the job-seeker portal regularly.
Scam warning: Never pay an agent or intermediary for an OEC demand. If anyone asks for money outside the official PSID fee, walk away — and verify every demand on oec.gov.pk first.

Is OEC Advertisement No. 210 still open?

What is a PSID?

A Payment Slip ID generated on the OEC portal. Pay the prescribed fee through it before submitting — pay first, then submit.

Who could apply for Ad 210?

Men aged 22–40 with a relevant diploma or vocational certificate and at least 3 years of experience.

Does OEC charge anything beyond the PSID fee?

No. The official process uses only the prescribed PSID fee — never pay agents or intermediaries.

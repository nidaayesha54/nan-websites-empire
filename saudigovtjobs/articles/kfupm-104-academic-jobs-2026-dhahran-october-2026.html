Saudi Government Jobs 2026

King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM) in Dhahran is carrying 100+ academic vacancies for 2026. Some posts are Saudi-only; many are open to international applicants.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

King Fahd University of Petroleum & Minerals (KFUPM) in Dhahran, Eastern Province, is carrying 100+ academic vacancies for 2026: 104 postings were verified live on the university's Applicant Tracking System on 1 October 2026, spanning Assistant, Associate and Full Professor ranks plus lecturers, senior lecturers, instructors, graduate assistants and research scientists.

KFUPM is the Kingdom's flagship research university, ranked 63rd globally in the QS World University Rankings 2026 (per its own faculty page), with industrial partnerships including Saudi Aramco, SABIC and Halliburton. English is the university's medium of instruction.

Honesty first: the posting count is a snapshot from 1 October 2026 — recruitment is rolling and the live count changes. Some posts, such as lecturer and graduate-assistant codes in Industrial & Systems Engineering, are explicitly Saudi-nationals-only, while many faculty posts are open to international applicants (the individual advert is the controlling source). No per-post salaries or closing dates are published — verify each vacancy's live status, nationality condition and terms on the official KFUPM faculty portal before applying.

Disciplines With Openings

Verified October 2026 listings span:

  • Engineering: Electrical, Mechanical, Chemical, Civil & Environmental, Computer, Aerospace, and Industrial & Systems Engineering;
  • Sciences & Math: Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics and Geosciences;
  • Business & Humanities: Business Administration, Accounting & Finance, Management & Marketing, and Global Studies;

Subject areas span control engineering, AI, robotics and reservoir engineering — the discipline list above is illustrative, not exhaustive.

Eligibility — the Honest Breakdown

Before you apply:

  • Nationality: many faculty posts are open to international applicants, but some listings are explicitly Saudi-nationals-only — the individual advert is the controlling source, never assume;
  • Qualification: a Master's or PhD in a relevant field from a reputable institution; professorial ranks expect a strong research and teaching record appropriate to the rank;
  • English: a strong command of English is mandatory — English is KFUPM's medium of instruction, so you must be comfortable teaching, presenting and interviewing in English;
  • Experience: check each advert for rank-specific expectations — early-career candidates fit assistant-professor and lecturer posts; full-professor posts expect established records;
  • Never pay to apply: KFUPM recruits through its official portals only — walk away from any agent demanding fees.

How to Apply

1
Open KFUPM's official faculty recruitment page and the university's Applicant Tracking System — confirm the vacancy you want is still live.

4

5
Apply only through the official KFUPM portal — no intermediaries, no fees, no exceptions.

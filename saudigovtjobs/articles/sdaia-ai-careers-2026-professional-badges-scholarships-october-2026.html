Saudi Government Jobs 2026

The Saudi Data and AI Authority (SDAIA) is building the Kingdom's AI workforce in 2026: its AI Professional Badges programme has tested 150+ candidates and certified 52 specialists, while 4th-cohort AI scholarships to top-50 world universities are now open.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

The Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority (SDAIA) is moving fast in 2026 — officially the Kingdom's Year of Artificial Intelligence. Its AI Professional Badges programme, a national accreditation framework for AI and data specialists, has ramped up testing: more than 150 candidates from government and private entities, universities, research centres and technology companies sat exams in the latest round, on top of 52 specialists already awarded badges at the UNESCO Global Forum on the Ethics of AI in Riyadh.

In parallel, SDAIA has opened applications for the fourth cohort of its AI scholarship programme — announced via the Saudi Press Agency in early October 2026. Run with the Ministry of Education and the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Scholarship Program, it sends Saudi male and female students to universities ranked among the world's top 50 for bachelor's, master's and doctoral study in AI, data science, robotics, machine learning, deep learning, computer vision, NLP, big data, smart cities, generative AI and AI information security.

Expatriate AI professionals should target employer-specific postings (PIF companies, NEOM, Elm, STC) rather than these national programmes. See our expat-eligibility guide for the full picture.

Why the Badges Matter for Careers

  • Verified skill signal — employers get a single government-backed benchmark instead of guessing what "AI Engineer" means on a CV;
  • Cross-sector recognition — candidates are drawn from government entities, universities, research centres and tech companies, so the credential travels;
  • Aligned with hiring — the framework was built with the Digital Sector Skills Council and the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development, tying it directly to labour-market needs;

Eligibility Requirements

  • Saudi nationality — both the badges and the scholarships target Saudi male and female nationals;
  • For badges: working professionals or researchers in AI, data science or adjacent technical fields from government, private sector, universities or research centres;
  • For scholarships: Saudi students meeting the academic requirements of the Custodian of the Two Holy Mosques Scholarship Program for top-50 university admission;
  • Willingness to complete SDAIA's standardised assessments (badges) or university admissions processes (scholarships).

How to Apply

1
Visit SDAIA's official channels for the AI Professional Badges registration page and confirm the current testing round.
2
Choose your track — AI Engineer (model and systems development) or Data Scientist (algorithms and analytics) — and the tier you are targeting.
3
Register and complete the standardised assessment; badges are awarded on demonstrated competency, not on CV claims.
4
For scholarships: check the fourth-cohort announcement (via SPA / SDAIA) for application windows, eligible universities and required documents.
5
Prepare academic records and identification early — top-50 university admissions and scholarship processing both take months.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are SDAIA's AI Professional Badges?

A national accreditation framework certifying AI Engineer and Data Scientist specialisations across three tiers (Assistant, Associate, Expert) via standardised assessment.

How many people have been certified so far?

52 specialists were awarded badges at the UNESCO Global Forum on the Ethics of AI in Riyadh, with 150+ additional candidates tested in the latest round.

Who can apply for the badges and scholarships?

Saudi nationals — the programmes target Saudi male and female professionals (badges) and students (scholarships).

What does the 4th-cohort AI scholarship cover?

Bachelor's, master's and doctoral study at top-50 world universities in AI, data science, robotics, ML, NLP, big data, smart cities and related fields. Applications opened in early October 2026.

Can expatriates apply for SDAIA programmes?

No — these national programmes are for Saudi citizens. Expat AI professionals should apply to employer-specific roles at PIF companies, NEOM, Elm and similar organisations.

Qiddiya & Red Sea Global Jobs 2026: Careers, Salaries & How to Apply

Qiddiya — the PIF-owned capital of entertainment, sports and culture — and Red Sea Global are hiring across construction, hospitality, finance and marketing. Live roles, salaries, steps.

Qiddiya Red Sea Global Giga Projects

Home › Articles › Qiddiya & Red Sea Global Jobs 2026: Careers, Salaries & How to Apply

Two flagship giga-projects are hiring at pace in 2026. Qiddiya Investment Company (QIC) — the PIF-owned destination for entertainment, sports and culture — calls its people “Playmakers”: a diverse, international team building the Kingdom’s most anticipated destination. Red Sea Global (RSG) is staffing hospitality, asset management and development roles for regenerative tourism on the Red Sea coast. Together they offer the Kingdom’s most varied expat hiring — construction trades to luxury hospitality to corporate finance.

Qiddiya: Corporate Roles & Urgent Site Hiring

Corporate postings set a clear bar: a Manager, Projects Talent Acquisition role asks for a business/project-management degree, PMP/PRINCE2/Agile as desirable, and 6–8 years of experience. Previously advertised senior roles include a Director of Master Plan Marketing. On the ground, contractors are recruiting urgently — a September 2026 Riyadh Qiddiya posting listed Civil Engineer (3), Civil QC (3), Surveyor (4), Safety Officer (10), Store Keeper (5), Time Keeper (4) and Mechanical QC (3). Apply at qiddiya.talentera.com. Rule one, always: never pay any fee when applying.

Red Sea Global: Hospitality & Finance Careers

RSG hires across hospitality, development, finance and sustainability. Its Senior Manager – Asset Management posting (any nationality) covers hospitality and real-estate portfolio performance: cash-flow modelling, ROI analysis, board reporting and contract negotiations. Luxury hospitality roles include the AMAALA Yacht Club event manager in Tabuk (regattas, VIP galas), needing hospitality/marketing credentials and high-end activation experience. Apply at redseaglobal.com/en/careers.

How to Apply: 5 Steps

  1. Choose your track — construction/trades via contractor postings; corporate, hospitality and finance via official portals.
  2. Apply on the official portal — qiddiya.talentera.com; redseaglobal.com/en/careers — with a complete profile.
  3. Match the keywords — Primavera P6, Power BI, PMP/PRINCE2 for delivery; luxury-hospitality experience for AMAALA-tier posts.
  4. Show relocation readiness — many roles are site-based (Riyadh, Tabuk, Red Sea coast).
  5. Verify before signing — confirm offers on the company’s official domain; never pay fees.
Giga-project hiring moves in waves tied to construction milestones — register on the portals and set job alerts so the next Qiddiya or Red Sea wave reaches you first.

FAQs

What is Qiddiya hiring for in 2026?

Corporate roles (project management, talent acquisition, marketing) plus urgent construction hiring — civil engineers, QC inspectors, surveyors, safety officers, store and time keepers.

What does Red Sea Global hire for?

Hospitality, development, finance and sustainability — including asset management and luxury hospitality (AMAALA). Apply at redseaglobal.com/en/careers.

What salaries do these jobs pay?

Per Cooper Fitch 2026: Senior PMs SAR 35,000-55,000/month, Senior Civil Engineers SAR 20,000-35,000, with premiums over market rates.

Are Qiddiya jobs open to expats?

Yes — Qiddiya's team is described as diverse and international, and contractors recruit across nationalities with sponsored visas.

Should I pay a fee to apply?

Never. Legitimate PIF companies and contractors never charge application fees — use only official portals.

Saudi Government Jobs 2026

Jadarat is Saudi Arabia's unified national employment platform — open to Saudi nationals only. Here is the honest 2026 registration guide: eligibility, step-by-step signup, features, and the labour-market numbers behind it.

✅ Updated October 21, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

If you are not a Saudi national, read this first: Jadarat is for Saudi nationals only. The platform is not available to foreign nationals, and no registration path exists for expats. If an agent promises you a Jadarat login for a fee, it is a scam — walk away.

For Saudi citizens, Jadarat is the single front door to public- and private-sector jobs. Launched by HRSD Minister Ahmed Al-Rajhi in line with Vision 2030, it replaces the old fragmented portals with one unified platform — and helps employers meet Saudization targets, which is why access is restricted to nationals. Below: who can register, the signup steps, the features, and the 2026 market numbers.

Eligibility: Who Can Register

Only Saudi nationals can register on Jadarat. Foreign nationals — including expats already working in the Kingdom — cannot create accounts. This is by design: the platform channels Saudi talent into jobs and helps employers hit Saudization quotas. Any agent or post claiming otherwise is mistaken or dishonest.

Honest framing: being excluded from Jadarat does not mean being excluded from the Saudi job market. Expats should use employer career pages, licensed recruitment agencies, and government-to-government channels in their home countries.

What Jadarat Offers

  • Unified profile: one profile for both public- and private-sector applications;
  • Customised search: filters for region, career path and skill set;
  • Online interviews: employers can interview candidates through the platform;
  • Employer reviews: read reviews of hiring organisations before applying;
  • Application tracking: follow your applications through the hiring pipeline.

Registration — Step by Step

1
Go through the official HRSD channel. Start at hrsd.gov.sa and follow the Jadarat link there — never through an agent, a paid service, or a forwarded link.
2

3
Upload your CV and qualifications. Complete profiles get matched to more relevant roles.
4

5
Browse and apply directly through the platform.
6
Track and prepare: monitor application statuses and get ready for online interviews.

What Expats Should Use Instead

If Jadarat is closed to you, the honest alternatives are: employer careers pages, licensed recruitment agencies, and official government-to-government channels such as Pakistan's OEC. What does not work: paying anyone for Jadarat access, or trusting social-media lists of "Jadarat jobs for foreigners."

Frequently Asked Questions

Can expats register on Jadarat?

No. Jadarat is for Saudi nationals only; the platform is not available to foreign nationals. Any agent offering a Jadarat login for a fee is running a scam.

Who launched Jadarat and why?

It was launched by the Minister of Human Resources and Social Development, Ahmed Al-Rajhi, as a unified national employment platform in line with Vision 2030.

What can Saudi nationals do on Jadarat?

Create a profile, search public- and private-sector jobs with filters for region, career path and skill set, attend online interviews, and read employer reviews.

How do employers use Jadarat?

Employers post vacancies and recruit Saudi talent through the platform, which also supports national Saudization targets.

Where do I register for Jadarat?

Registration runs through the Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development's official channels at hrsd.gov.sa — never through agents or paid intermediaries.

What should I prepare before registering?

Have your national ID details, an up-to-date CV, qualification certificates, and a clear idea of your preferred region, career path and skill set ready.

Saudi Government Jobs 2026

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Health is running an open Physicians & Nursing recruitment for 2026 — vacancies across all regions of the Kingdom, applied for through the MOH portal and Jadarat.

✅ Updated October 17, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

Healthcare remains one of the steadiest government employers in Saudi Arabia, and the Ministry of Health (MOH) is actively recruiting. According to the ministry's official recruitment plan for 1447H, the Physicians & Nursing announcement — open since early this year — is still running through the end of the year, with vacancies covering all regions of the Kingdom. For doctors, nurses and allied health professionals, this is a live, official hiring channel — not a rumour.

MOH recruitment runs through two official doors: the MOH Recruitment Portal (via the ministry's e-services) and the unified national employment platform, Jadarat, which matches job seekers with public and private opportunities. The ministry's stated aim is building competitive advantage through the quality and efficiency of its human resources — which in practice means structured, periodic announcements across medical, technical and administrative categories throughout the year.

What the 1447H Recruitment Plan Covers

The ministry publishes a scheduled recruitment plan with periodic and permanent announcements. The current plan's headline categories include:

  • Physicians & Nursing — the major open announcement, running through year-end across all regions;
  • Allied health tracks — previous cycles have covered dental assistants, health security, respiratory therapy and cardiac perfusion technicians, showing the breadth of clinical hiring;
  • Administrative roles — documents, archives and support functions appear in the periodic schedule;
  • Specialist campaigns — cyber security and other technical roles run as separate announcements.

Because announcements are periodic, checking the MOH portal regularly — not once — is the strategy.

Eligibility & How Candidates Are Scored

MOH announcements use three weighted eligibility criteria totalling 100% (published in Arabic on each announcement). Across MOH hiring, candidates should expect:

  • Professional qualification — recognized medical/nursing credentials; Saudi Commission for Health Specialties classification where applicable;
  • Experience — weighted in the scoring criteria;
  • Single application rule — apply for only one vacancy per announcement; duplicates are excluded;
  • Complete documentation — requests missing required documents are excluded without review.

How to Apply for MOH Jobs 2026

1
Go to the MOH portal (moh.gov.sa) — E-Services section — and select Recruitment Services to register. (You cannot already be an MOH employee.)
2
Alternatively, register on Jadarat, the unified national employment platform, which also carries MOH opportunities.
3
Choose one vacancy matching your qualification — duplicated applications are excluded.
4
Upload complete, valid documents: qualifications, professional classification, experience letters and ID.
5
Track your application through the portal. For technical issues, MOH lists support at 920009080 during official working hours.

Why MOH Recruitment Is Worth Your Attention

Ministry of Health employment means government terms — structured pay scales, job security and Kingdom-wide mobility — in a health system still expanding under Vision 2030. The Physicians & Nursing announcement's year-long run is unusual: most Gulf health hiring comes in short bursts, but this window stays open, giving qualified clinicians time to prepare a strong, complete application. Register on the MOH portal and Jadarat now, apply for the single best-fit vacancy, and make sure every document is in order — in MOH scoring, completeness is the first filter.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Saudi Ministry of Health hiring in 2026?

Yes. Per the MOH recruitment plan for 1447H, the Physicians & Nursing announcement has been open since early this year and is still running through the end of the year, covering all regions of the Kingdom.

How do I apply for Saudi MOH jobs?

Register on the MOH Recruitment Portal via the ministry's E-Services (Recruitment Services), or apply through Jadarat, the unified national employment platform. Applications are free.

Can I apply for more than one MOH vacancy?

No. MOH rules state applicants must apply for only one job per announcement — duplicated applications are excluded.

How are MOH applicants scored?

Each announcement uses three weighted eligibility criteria totalling 100%, published in Arabic on the announcement page. Requests that don't meet conditions or lack documents are excluded.

Does the MOH charge application fees?

No. MOH employment services are free. Beware of third-party agents charging fees — apply only through the official MOH portal or Jadarat.

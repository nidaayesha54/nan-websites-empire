Saudi Government Jobs 2026

Saudi Arabia's Health Holding Company announced 4,291 new healthcare vacancies across 20 health clusters on October 19, 2026 — with a 15-day application window. Here is the complete guide: who can apply, which specialties, and how.

✅ Updated October 20, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

Saudi Arabia has announced 4,291 new job opportunities for healthcare professionals across the kingdom, bringing the total number of recruitment appointments, employment-linked training contracts and newly advertised vacancies in 2026 to 19,610. The announcement was made by the Health Holding Company on Monday, 19 October 2026, and the vacancies will be distributed among its 20 health clusters covering several medical and healthcare specialties.

The scale of this year's hiring is striking: the company says 12,914 Saudi nationals have already been recruited in 2026, while a further 2,405 employment-linked training contracts have been approved. The new 4,291 vacancies add to that running total. (Note the 19,610 figure combines completed recruitment, approved training contracts and jobs still open for application — it is not 19,610 currently open posts.)

The recruitment drive is explicitly intended to attract qualified Saudi professionals, address staffing needs and strengthen healthcare facilities' ability to provide high-quality services — part of the company's push to expand employment and professional development pathways for Saudi healthcare workers.

Honest note: This drive is aimed at Saudi nationals — the announcement frames it as expanding pathways for Saudi healthcare workers. Expat clinicians should look instead at private hospital groups and specialised centres, which continue to recruit internationally. Do not assume these cluster vacancies are open to non-Saudis.

How the 20-Cluster System Works

  • The Health Holding Company operates 20 health clusters serving different regions of Saudi Arabia;
  • Each cluster publishes its own vacancy list with specialties, eligibility requirements and regional postings;
  • Candidates can explore opportunities in their own region — the cluster model is designed around regional staffing;
  • Specialties span doctors, nurses, technicians, pharmacists and allied health professionals — check your cluster's portal for the exact list.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Saudi nationality for the advertised cluster vacancies (the stated target of the drive);
  • Relevant medical or healthcare qualifications recognised in the Kingdom;
  • Saudi Commission for Health Specialties (SCFHS) classification/registration where applicable;
  • Specialty-specific experience requirements as stated in each cluster's listing;
  • Ability to complete the online application within the 15-day window after your cluster announces its vacancies.

How to Apply

1
Identify your regional health cluster — the Health Holding Company operates 20 clusters across the kingdom.
2
Open that cluster's online recruitment portal and review the published vacancies, specialties and eligibility requirements.
3
Prepare your qualifications, SCFHS classification, experience certificates and national ID.
4
Submit your application online within 15 days of the cluster's announcement — late applications are not accepted.
5
Track your application on the cluster portal and respond promptly to any document requests or interview invitations.

Why This Hiring Wave Matters

The 4,291 vacancies are part of the Kingdom's structural transformation of healthcare delivery — the cluster model centralises staffing under the Health Holding Company while pushing recruitment to the regions. With 12,914 Saudis already hired this year, the programme is the single largest public healthcare recruitment effort of 2026. For Saudi clinicians, nurses and allied-health professionals, the 15-day application windows mean the decisive factor is speed: find your cluster, prepare documents now, and apply the day vacancies are published.

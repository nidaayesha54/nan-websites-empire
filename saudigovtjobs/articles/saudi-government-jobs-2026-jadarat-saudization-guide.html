The Saudi Hiring Landscape in 2026

Saudi Arabia's public sector remains one of the region's most attractive employers in 2026, advertising through one unified channel: the Jadarat platform at jadarat.sa. Two forces shape Saudi government jobs this year. The first is Saudization — the national policy, measured through the Nitaqat system, prioritizing the hiring of Saudi nationals. The second is Vision 2030, whose giga-projects generate sustained demand for engineers, planners, healthcare workers, educators, and digital specialists. This guide covers Jadarat, explains Saudization, and maps where Vision 2030 is creating real openings.

Jadarat Platform Walkthrough: How to Apply on jadarat.sa

Jadarat is the official Saudi government jobs platform — every ministry advertisement is published there, and applications submitted anywhere else are not processed. The platform is free for jobseekers. Here is how to use it.

  1. Register at jadarat.sa. Create a profile with a valid national ID or iqama number, a mobile number, and an email address.
  2. Complete your profile and upload attested documents. Add education, qualifications, experience, and certificates — degree attestation is required and can take weeks, so start early.
  3. Search open advertisements. Filter by ministry, region, category, and closing date, and read each advertisement fully: it states eligibility, required qualifications, and whether the role is open to Saudi nationals only or also to expatriates.
  4. Submit before the deadline. Late submissions are not accepted — confirm your profile matches the requirements first.
  5. Track results and complete formalities. Shortlisting and decisions are published on Jadarat — usually within three to eight weeks of closing — followed by medical and background checks, with iqama sponsorship for expatriate hires.

Saudization & Nitaqat Explained

Saudization is Saudi Arabia's national employment policy, and Nitaqat is the points-based system measuring employer compliance: employers — including ministries — are banded by the share of Saudi nationals in their workforce. The effect: most ministry roles prioritize, and often reserve, posts for Saudi citizens.

  • For Saudi nationals: Saudization works in your favor — most administrative, healthcare, education, customs, and municipal roles on Jadarat are reserved for citizens.
  • For expatriates: direct ministry posts are limited, but giga-projects, contractors, and specialized technical posts still recruit foreign talent.
  • Always check the advertisement: every Jadarat listing states its nationality eligibility — never assume without reading it.

Vision 2030 Giga-Projects: Where the Demand Is

Vision 2030 is the biggest driver of new hiring in the Kingdom. The giga-projects create demand across construction trades, urban planning, tourism and hospitality, healthcare, digital infrastructure, and entertainment — with roles at state-owned developers, their contractors, and the ministries overseeing each project. Candidates in project management, engineering, health and safety, hospitality operations, and digital services see the most openings, and the long project timelines offer a stable outlook for expatriate specialists when roles are advertised as open to non-Saudis.

Eligibility Notes

  • Saudi nationals need a valid national ID, an Absher-verified account, and attested qualifications.
  • Expatriates need a valid iqama or eligibility for new sponsorship, plus attested degrees and experience certificates.
  • Begin degree attestation early — it is mandatory for ministry roles.
  • "Saudi nationals only" roles are closed to expatriates; target project-based or specialized advertisements instead.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can expatriates apply for Saudi government jobs?

Some specialized and project-based roles are open to expatriates, but most ministry posts are reserved for Saudi nationals under Saudization.

What is Saudization (Nitaqat)?

Saudization is the national policy requiring employers — including government bodies — to employ a set share of Saudi nationals, measured through the Nitaqat banding system.

How long does the Jadarat hiring process take?

Shortlisting typically takes three to eight weeks after an advertisement closes, followed by medical examinations and background checks before a final offer.

Do I need attested degrees for ministry jobs?

Yes — attested degree and professional certificates are standard and must be uploaded to your Jadarat profile before applications are considered.

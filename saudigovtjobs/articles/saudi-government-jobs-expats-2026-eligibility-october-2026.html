Saudi Government Jobs 2026

Most Saudi government posts go to citizens — but real, legal routes exist for expatriates. Here is exactly what is open to foreigners in 2026, how the visa system works, and how to avoid the scams.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

“Can a foreigner get a government job in Saudi Arabia?” is one of the most-searched questions in Gulf employment — and the honest answer is nuanced. The Kingdom's Saudization policy reserves most direct civil-service posts for Saudi citizens, and the national recruitment platform Jadarat is built around citizen applicants. But that is not the whole story: thousands of expatriates work in Saudi Arabia's public-sector orbit every year through legitimate, well-documented channels.

This guide maps the real routes — government-to-government recruitment, giga-project contractors, utilities and specialist hiring — plus the eligibility rules and the scams to avoid.

What Expats Can and Cannot Access

  • Jadarat: the unified national employment platform is designed for Saudi citizens — foreign applicants should not expect to compete there;
  • Direct ministry posts: overwhelmingly reserved for nationals under Saudization;
  • OEC / government-brokered ads: open to the advertised nationalities (e.g., Pakistani workers in Ad 210);
  • Contractor and project roles: the largest expat channel — employers sponsor the work visa (Iqama);
  • PIF companies and giga-projects: hire internationally through their own company portals and recruiters.

The Visa System

Expat employment runs on employer sponsorship: the hiring company secures your Saudi work visa, and on arrival it is converted into an Iqama (residence permit). Standard inclusions are free or subsidized accommodation, transport and medical insurance, plus paid annual leave and end-of-service gratuity under Saudi labour law. There is no income tax on salaries.

Documents You'll Need

  • Valid passport (18+ months of validity recommended);
  • Educational and trade certificates — attested where required;
  • Police clearance and medical fitness certificate;
  • Experience letters matching the advertised requirements.

How to Apply — Safely

1
Use official channels: OEC (oec.gov.pk) for Pakistan-brokered ads, company career pages for giga-projects, licensed agencies for trades.
2
Match the advertisement exactly — age, qualification and experience cut-offs are enforced.
3
Complete payment steps through official slips and portals only (e.g., OEC's PSID) — never cash to an individual.
4
Confirm salary, contract length and benefits in the written offer before signing anything.
5
Never pay large upfront fees before a confirmed job offer — the classic Gulf-job scam.
Important: Verify every employer and agency independently. A legitimate offer names the employer, the contract terms and the visa route — anything vague, rushed or fee-first is a red flag.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can foreigners apply for Saudi government jobs on Jadarat?

Jadarat is designed for Saudi citizens — foreign applicants should use the expat routes instead: OEC government-brokered ads, giga-project contractors, utilities and specialist hiring.

What is the most reliable legal route for expats?

Government-to-government recruitment (like Pakistan's OEC advertisements) and licensed agencies with verified Saudi contracts — both leave a paper trail you can verify.

What is the deadline for OEC Ad No. 210?

20 October 2026 — HVAC and mechanical maintenance technician roles at 1,800 SAR/month, applied for through oec.gov.pk.

Do I need Arabic to work in Saudi Arabia?

Not for most technical and project roles — English is widely used on giga-projects and in utilities; basic Arabic helps day-to-day.

How do I avoid Gulf job scams?

Use official channels only, never pay large upfront fees before a confirmed offer, confirm all terms in the written contract, and verify the employer and agency independently.

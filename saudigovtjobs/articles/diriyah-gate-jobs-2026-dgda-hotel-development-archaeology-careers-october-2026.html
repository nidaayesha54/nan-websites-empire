Saudi Government Jobs 2026

The Diriyah Gate Development Authority is recruiting across hotel development, project controls, archaeology and more — one of the Kingdom's biggest heritage-sector hiring drives, run through Jadarat.

✅ Updated October 17, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

Saudi Arabia's giga-project hiring wave has a heritage flagship: the Diriyah Gate Development Authority (DGDA) is running a major 2026 recruitment drive across management, technical, engineering and administrative categories. Advertised roles include Director of Hotel Development, Project Controls Director and Archaeologist posts, alongside project management, contract management, urban planning and business consulting positions in Riyadh.

DGDA is the government entity transforming Diriyah — home to the UNESCO World Heritage Site of At-Turaif — into a global cultural and tourism destination, with world-class hospitality, retail and residential assets rising around the historic Najdi capital. Applications run through the unified national employment platform, Jadarat. For professionals in heritage, hospitality development, engineering and archaeology, this is the most significant cultural-sector hiring initiative in the Kingdom this year.

Why DGDA Is Hiring at Scale

Diriyah is one of the anchor projects of Vision 2030's tourism ambitions: a $63-billion-plus program to turn the birthplace of the Saudi state into a cultural capital with luxury hospitality, museums, retail and residential districts — all built around a protected UNESCO site. That combination creates unusual hiring breadth:

  • Hotel development leadership — directors to deliver hospitality assets to international standards;
  • Project controls — cost, schedule and risk management across a multi-year mega-program;
  • Archaeology and heritage — specialists to protect and interpret At-Turaif and surrounding sites;
  • Urban planning and contracts — the administrative backbone of a city-scale build.

Who DGDA Is Looking For

  • Senior leaders: directors in hotel development and project controls with mega-project or luxury-hospitality track records;
  • Heritage specialists: archaeologists and conservation professionals — rare, high-demand profiles in the Gulf;
  • Engineers and planners: civil, architectural and urban-planning professionals for the build-out;
  • Graduates and administrators: entry routes exist across corporate functions — DGDA explicitly targets qualified national talent alongside experienced hires.

Saudization applies across the program, with priority for Saudi nationals in line with national employment policy.

Why Diriyah Stands Out Among Giga-Projects

NEOM builds the future; Diriyah builds on 300 years of history. That makes its hiring mix unique in the Kingdom — it is the only giga-project where archaeologists and hotel directors are recruited in the same wave. For heritage professionals, there is simply no comparable employer in the region right now. For hospitality and project-controls leaders, Diriyah offers the prestige of a UNESCO-anchored development with the scale of a national mega-program. Check Jadarat for the current role list: heritage-sector windows like this do not stay open indefinitely.

What jobs is DGDA hiring for in 2026?

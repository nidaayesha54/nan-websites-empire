Saudi Aramco Expatriate Schools are hiring Elementary and Intermediate Teachers through international recruitment — expats can apply, with a reported closing date of December 31, 2026.

✅ Updated October 22, 2026

Saudi Aramco Expatriate Schools are hiring Elementary and Intermediate Teachers through Aramco's international recruitment channel — posted 15 October 2026 with a reported closing date of 31 December 2026. This is not a Jadarat posting and not nationals-only: foreign applicants can genuinely compete.

Saudi government jobs are normally nationals-only via the Jadarat platform — we state that openly. But Aramco runs its own portals with separate Saudi and international tracks, and these teaching roles sit on the international track — which is why expats can genuinely compete.

Honesty first: the details below come from two news roundups (October 19 and 21, 2026) by the same publisher — a single-source chain, with vacancy count and salary not published. Re-verify everything on Aramco's official careers portal before applying.

Why This Posting Is Unusual

  • Expat-eligible: advertised through Aramco's international recruitment track — foreign teachers can apply;
  • Not Jadarat: Aramco hires through its own portals, not the Saudi government's nationals-only Jadarat platform;
  • School setting: Aramco's expatriate schools serve the company's international workforce communities;
  • Professional bar: a recognized teaching qualification plus 3 years' experience — this is not an entry-level route;
  • English-first: strong English communication is an explicit requirement.

Qualifications in Detail

The reported requirements are specific — measure yourself against all of them:

  • Education: a bachelor's or master's degree in education;
  • Certification: a valid professional teaching qualification (PGCE, B.Ed with license, or equivalent);
  • Experience: a minimum of 3 years of classroom teaching;
  • Language: strong English communication skills — the working language of the schools;
  • Nationality and residency: the international track admits foreign applicants, but confirm the per-ad conditions — visa, licensing and residency rules apply.

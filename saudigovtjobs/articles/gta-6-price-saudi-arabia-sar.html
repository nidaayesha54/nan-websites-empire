GTA 6 Price in Saudi Arabia: What Players Pay

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and in Saudi Arabia it unlocked at midnight local time — 12:00 AM Arabian Standard Time on Thursday, November 19. If you are buying it from a Saudi account, the price you see is not the American $79.99, and it is not the same on both consoles either. This guide lists every price on the Saudi storefronts, explains why PlayStation and Xbox disagree, and shows how to pay less than the sticker price.

The figures below come from the PlayStation Store and Microsoft Store listings in Saudi Arabia, checked by price trackers in late October and on launch day. Prices can change, and the store listing on your own account is always the final word — but these are the numbers every tracker agreed on.

The prices: PS5 vs Xbox

On the Saudi PlayStation Store, the Standard Edition costs SAR 301.56 and the Ultimate Edition costs SAR 376.96. On the Saudi Xbox Store, the same two editions cost SAR 309 and SAR 389. Saudi Arabia is one of only three countries where the two consoles disagree — the others are Mexico and the UAE — and in every other country the price matches on both platforms.

In American terms, the PS5 Standard price works out to about $80.42, and the Xbox Standard to about $82.40, so the gap is small but real: about seven and a half riyals on the Standard Edition, and about twelve riyals on the Ultimate. If you own both consoles, the PlayStation version is the cheaper buy. If you own one, the difference is not worth switching platforms for — but it is worth knowing before you check out.

What each edition includes

The Standard Edition is the base game plus the Vintage Vice City Pack and, on PS5 pre-orders, one month of GTA+ membership. The Ultimate Edition adds the Ultimate Edition Upgrade: premium vehicles including the Grotti Cheetah and the Squalo speedboat, exclusive shops, extra personalised weapon and vehicle skins, and extra apparel for both protagonists. The Ultimate costs about SAR 75 more than Standard on PS5, and SAR 80 more on Xbox.

Whether the upgrade is worth it depends on how you play. The vehicles and shops are the real value — a head start on transport and a place to spend money early. The skins and apparel are cosmetic. If you plan to play the story first and explore later, Standard is enough. If you want the fastest start and the extra vehicles from day one, Ultimate is the buy.

The unlock time and the pre-load

The game unlocked at local midnight in Saudi Arabia, so players in Riyadh were playing while it was still the afternoon of November 18 in New York — the global rollout moves west from New Zealand, and Saudi Arabia sits near the front of the queue. The digital pre-load opened on Tuesday, November 10, and the download is up to 212 GB, so players who pre-loaded were ready at midnight while everyone else waited on a download.

One detail to check before you buy: it is not confirmed whether VAT is included in the Saudi store prices. The PlayStation Store switched to local currency in Saudi Arabia in early October 2026, and trackers note that the tax display can vary. Check your own account's store before you buy, and look at the final checkout total rather than the shelf price.

The one-leave-day trick

Saudi players worked out a neat trick for launch week: the game unlocked at midnight on Thursday, November 19, and the Saudi weekend runs Friday to Saturday. Take Thursday off and you get three days of GTA 6 — Thursday, Friday and Saturday — for a single day of leave. Add Sunday, November 22, and two leave days buy four consecutive days of play. It is the kind of planning that fits a launch this big: book the day early, before everyone else asks for it, and never fake a sick day — only a real medical certificate counts, and a fake one can bring serious penalties.

How to pay less

First, buy the PS5 version if you have the choice — it is cheaper on both editions. Second, watch for store sales: the Standard Edition is the one that discounts first in most regions, and a sale on the base game beats a sale on the upgrade. Third, never buy from a reseller promising a cheaper key or an early copy. The scam warnings around this launch are real — fake downloads have hidden malware, and a cheap key from an unknown seller is how accounts get stolen. The store price is the safe price.

FAQ

How much is GTA 6 in Saudi Arabia? SAR 301.56 on PS5 and SAR 309 on Xbox for the Standard Edition. The Ultimate Edition costs SAR 376.96 on PS5 and SAR 389 on Xbox.

When did GTA 6 unlock in Saudi Arabia? Thursday, November 19, 2026 at 12:00 AM Arabian Standard Time, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S at the same time.

The bottom line: in Saudi Arabia, GTA 6 costs SAR 301.56 on PS5 and SAR 309 on Xbox for Standard, and SAR 376.96 and SAR 389 for Ultimate. Buy on PlayStation if you can, pre-load before midnight, and buy from the store — never from a reseller.

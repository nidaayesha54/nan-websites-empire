Saudi Government Jobs 2026

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Education will promote more than 41,000 employees in 2026 — 28,704 teaching staff and 12,473 administrative employees — following promotion procedures completed in September.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

Saudi Arabia's Ministry of Education has announced plans to promote more than 41,000 of its employees in 2026, following the completion of promotion procedures in September. The promotions cover 28,704 teaching staff and 12,473 administrative employees across the ministry, a ministry spokesperson confirmed in late September 2026.

To be clear about what this is — and is not: these are internal promotions of existing ministry employees, not 41,000 new vacancies. But for job seekers, the announcement still matters. A promotion wave of this scale signals workforce development and upward mobility inside the Kingdom's largest government employer — and it typically moves together with recruitment activity as promoted staff vacate lower grades and the ministry refreshes its ranks.

Honest guidance for expatriates: Saudi government teaching and administrative posts — including Ministry of Education roles — prioritise Saudi citizens, and the Jadarat platform is citizens-only. Expatriate teachers should focus on private and international schools (which MoE regulates and licenses), where schools recruit internationally for English, science, maths and early-years roles.

What the Promotion Wave Means for Job Seekers

  • Career progression is real: 41,000 promotions in a single year shows the ministry actively advances staff under approved procedures and regulations — government teaching in Saudi Arabia is a career ladder, not a dead end;
  • Lower grades open up: large promotion waves historically precede fresh recruitment at entry grades to backfill positions — watch Jadarat for new MoE postings;
  • Administrative hiring too: with 12,473 admin staff promoted, support-service, HR, finance and operations roles inside the ministry are also moving;
  • Signals stability: the ministry publicly committing to workforce development is a strong indicator of continued education-sector investment under Vision 2030.

How Ministry of Education Hiring Works

New government education jobs for Saudi citizens are advertised through Jadarat, the national employment platform. Typical process:

  • Eligibility: Saudi nationality; relevant degree (education degrees for teaching posts, specialist degrees for admin roles);
  • Professional licensing: teaching posts require the appropriate professional classification/licence level;
  • Application: via the Jadarat platform when MoE postings go live — monitor it regularly, as government postings carry fixed deadlines;
  • Selection: merit-based screening against published criteria, followed by interviews and appointment procedures.

5

The Bottom Line

The Ministry of Education's 41,000 promotions will not hand you a job directly — but they confirm the Kingdom's biggest government employer is investing in its people at scale. For Saudi nationals, that means keeping Jadarat profiles ready for the recruitment that follows workforce movement. For expatriates, it is a reminder to aim at the right door: the ministry's posts are for citizens, while the booming private-school sector is where international teachers are genuinely hired.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the 41,000 MoE promotions new jobs I can apply for?

No — they are internal promotions of existing Ministry of Education employees (28,704 teaching, 12,473 admin). But large promotion waves often precede fresh entry-grade recruitment, so monitor Jadarat.

How do Saudi citizens apply for Ministry of Education jobs?

Through the Jadarat national employment platform, with the relevant degree and professional teaching licence for the advertised grade.

Can expatriates work for the Saudi Ministry of Education?

Ministry posts prioritise Saudi citizens and Jadarat is citizens-only. Expatriate teachers should apply to private and international schools, which the ministry regulates.

When were the 2026 promotion procedures completed?

In September 2026, according to the ministry's announcement in late September.

What qualifications do MoE teaching posts require?

A relevant degree plus the appropriate professional licence/classification level for the advertised grade.

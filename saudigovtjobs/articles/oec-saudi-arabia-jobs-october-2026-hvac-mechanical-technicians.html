How to Apply via oec.gov.pk with PSID Payment

All applications for OEC Advertisement No. 210 must be submitted online through the official OEC website, oec.gov.pk. The application fee is paid through a PSID (Payment Slip ID) generated during the application process. Follow these steps:

  1. Visit oec.gov.pk and open the jobs section to find Advertisement No. 210 (dated 8 Oct 2026) for Saudi Arabia.
  2. Register or log in to the OEC applicant account with your CNIC, mobile number, and email address.
  3. Select your post — HVAC Technician or Mechanical Maintenance Technician — and confirm you meet the criteria (male, 22–40, relevant diploma, 3 years' experience).
  4. Fill in the application form with personal details, education, and work experience exactly as shown on your documents.
  5. Generate the PSID and pay the application fee through the designated bank or payment channel before the deadline.
  6. Upload required documents — CNIC, diploma certificates, experience letters, and a recent photograph — in the specified formats.
  7. Submit before 20 October 2026 and save your application number for tracking.

What Is OEC?

The Overseas Employment Corporation (OEC) works under the Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis and Human Resource Development. It is the official channel through which foreign employers recruit Pakistani workers — handling demand verification, advertisement, selection, and pre-departure procedures. Because OEC is a government body, its jobs carry verified employer demand and a structured process, unlike informal recruitment through unverified agents.

PIF Companies Hiring 2026: NEOM, Qiddiya, Red Sea Global — Portals & Salaries

PIF Giga Projects Vision 2030

Home › Articles › PIF Companies Hiring 2026: NEOM, Qiddiya, Red Sea Global — Portals & Salaries

Saudi Arabia’s Public Investment Fund (PIF) companies are the engine of Vision 2030 — and the largest source of high-paying expat jobs in the Kingdom. NEOM, Qiddiya, Red Sea Global, Roshn, Diriyah, AlUla and more PIF-backed developers hire across engineering, construction, hospitality, finance, technology and marketing, with giga-project salary premiums over market rates compensating for scale, remote locations and aggressive timelines.

The PIF Career Portals: Where to Apply

Every major PIF company runs its own portal — apply directly, never through intermediaries asking for fees:

  • NEOM — careers.neom.com (careers@neom.com)
  • Qiddiya — qiddiya.talentera.com
  • Red Sea Global — redseaglobal.com/en/careers
  • Roshn — roshn.sa/careers
  • Diriyah Company / DGDA — diriyahcompany.sa/en/careers, dgda.gov.sa/en/careers
  • AlUla — careerssf.rcu.gov.sa; KAFD — careers.kafd.sa
  • Riyadh Airports — raccareers.sa; SPARK — spark.sa/page/Careers
  • RCRC — rcrcdemo.elevatus.io/jobs; Sports Boulevard — sportsboulevard.sa/en/careers
  • Aseer / Soudah — asda.gov.sa/en/link-jobs, soudah.sa/en/work-with-us

Inside Jasara PMC: A PIF Joint Venture

Jasara Program Management Company — a joint venture of the PIF, Jacobs and Saudi Aramco — delivers program management for giga-projects. Its Senior Planning Manager posting (Qiddiya unit) shows the hiring bar: Primavera P6 and Power BI proficiency, PMP or PRINCE2 certification, and experience aligning schedules with Qiddiya’s Integrated Master Schedule. Similar delivery, planning and commercial roles recur across every giga-project.

How to Apply: 6 Steps

  1. Shortlist 3–5 PIF companies matching your trade.
  2. Create a full profile on each portal — incomplete profiles are filtered out.
  3. Mirror the posting’s keywords (Primavera P6, PMP, Power BI) in your CV.
  4. Follow the companies on LinkedIn for openings and hiring updates.
  5. Prepare for multi-stage assessment — technical interviews and background verification.
  6. Let the employer sponsor your iqama — never pay any fee to apply.
Saudization reserves some roles for nationals, but giga-projects depend heavily on international expertise. Target the portals above directly and verify every opening on the official careers page.

FAQs

Which PIF companies are hiring in 2026?

NEOM, Qiddiya, Red Sea Global, Roshn, Diriyah, AlUla, KAFD, SPARK, Sports Boulevard, Riyadh Airports, Aseer and Soudah — each with its own careers portal.

What do PIF giga-project jobs pay?

Per Cooper Fitch 2026: Senior PMs SAR 35,000-55,000/month, Program Directors SAR 45,000-80,000, Senior Civil Engineers SAR 20,000-35,000, with giga-project premiums.

What is Jasara PMC?

A PIF-Jacobs-Saudi Aramco joint venture delivering program management for giga-projects — typically requiring Primavera P6, Power BI and PMP/PRINCE2.

Can expats apply?

Yes — giga-projects rely heavily on international expertise. Employers sponsor work visas and iqama.

Where do I apply for NEOM jobs?

Directly at careers.neom.com (or careers@neom.com) — never through intermediaries asking for fees.

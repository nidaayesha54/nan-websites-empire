Saudi Government Jobs 2026

Vision 2030's giga-projects are hiring welders at scale in 2026 — with employer-sponsored visas, free accommodation and reported monthly pay of SAR 1,000–3,500 depending on skill and certification. Here is the full hiring guide.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » Saudi Government Jobs » Articles

Saudi Arabia's Vision 2030 giga-projects — NEOM, Qiddiya, Diriyah Gate and Red Sea Global — represent over $1 trillion in construction investment, and they need welders at scale. Recruitment-agency postings filed in 2026 show welder and welding-helper roles open across Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and the NEOM project zone, with employer-sponsored work visas, free accommodation and contracts that are typically two years, renewable.

For skilled tradespeople from South and Southeast Asia, the Philippines and beyond, this is one of the most structured overseas-work routes available in 2026 — but it only works safely through licensed channels.

Certification is the single biggest pay lever: certified welders earn roughly double what helpers earn, and many employers let you upgrade to the certified pay band after on-site testing.

What the Job Involves

  • Performing arc, MIG or TIG welding on structural steel, pipelines or fabrication components;
  • Reading welding symbols and basic blueprints under supervision;
  • Preparing and cleaning metal surfaces before welding;
  • Carrying out routine quality checks and minor repairs on welded joints;
  • Following strict site safety protocols, including fire safety and protective gear.

Who Can Apply — Eligibility

  • Age 20–45, physically fit for outdoor industrial site conditions;
  • ITI welding certificate or prior hands-on experience (helpers need neither, but certification unlocks higher pay);
  • Gulf welding experience gets priority in shortlisting;
  • Basic understanding of safety signage and simple English instructions helps.

Documents Required

  • Valid passport (minimum 18 months validity);
  • ITI or trade certificate, if available;
  • Police clearance certificate and medical fitness certificate;
  • Passport-size photographs and any welding test/trade test certificates.

Benefits Typically Included

  • Flight ticket provided by the employer;
  • Free accommodation in labour camps or company housing;
  • Health insurance as required under Saudi labour law;
  • Overtime pay for extra shifts as per Saudi labour law;
  • Food allowance or subsidized meals in many camps.

How to Apply Safely

1
Apply only through licensed recruitment agencies registered with your home country's emigration authority (e.g., check the agency's valid RA number).
2
Carry your welding trade certificate and experience letters — be ready for a practical welding test during hiring.
3
Confirm exact salary and overtime terms in the offer letter before signing anything.
4
Never pay large upfront fees to any agent before a confirmed job offer — this is the classic overseas-job scam.
5
Verify the agency and the employer independently; legitimate recruiters welcome verification.
Important: Overseas-job scams are common in high-demand trades. A legitimate offer comes with a written contract, a named employer and a visa processed through official channels — anything less is a red flag.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the salary for welder jobs in Saudi Arabia in 2026?

Reported contract salaries range from SAR 1,000–1,400 for helpers, SAR 1,400–2,200 for experienced welders, and SAR 2,200–3,500+ for certified welders — confirm your figure in the offer letter.

Do Saudi employers sponsor visas for welders?

Yes — hiring employers sponsor the Saudi work visa (Iqama), and most provide free accommodation, health insurance and flight tickets.

How long are welder contracts in Saudi Arabia?

Typically 2 years, renewable.

How do I apply safely for Saudi welder jobs?

Apply only through licensed recruitment agencies registered with your home country's emigration authority, verify the agency's registration number, and never pay large upfront fees before a confirmed offer.

Where are the welder jobs located?

Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam and the NEOM project zone, on Vision 2030 giga-projects and in oil & gas and steel fabrication.

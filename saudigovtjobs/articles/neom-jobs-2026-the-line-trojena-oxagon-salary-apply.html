NEOM Jobs 2026: The Line, Trojena & Oxagon — Salaries, Eligibility & How to Apply

NEOM — The Line, Trojena and Oxagon — is hiring thousands in 2026. Salaries up to Rs 1,00,000 a month with free visa, housing and food. Here is who qualifies and how to apply safely.

NEOM Vision 2030 Gulf Jobs

NEOM: Saudi Arabia’s Flagship Hiring Engine

NEOM is Saudi Arabia’s flagship development under Vision 2030 — a $500-billion-class giga-project in the northwest of the Kingdom. Its headline components: The Line (a planned 170-km linear desert city with a mirror-covered facade), Trojena (a mountain ski resort) and Oxagon (a floating industrial complex). Construction is in full swing in 2026, and hiring has expanded across construction, hospitality, engineering and facility management.

For South Asian workers — especially from India, including Tamil Nadu and Kerala — NEOM companies are actively recruiting skilled and unskilled workers with unusually strong packages.

Eligibility for Indian Workers

  • Age: 21 to 45 years
  • Experience: minimum 2 years in the same field
  • Passport: at least 6 months validity
  • Medical: fitness certificate required

How to Apply — The Safe Route

  1. Use the eMigrate portal. Apply only through government-approved recruitment agencies listed on India’s eMigrate portal.
  2. Verify the RA number. Every authorised agency holds a valid RA (Recruiting Agent) number — check it on the portal before sharing documents.
  3. Never pay upfront fees. Avoid any agent asking for advance payments — this is the classic Gulf-jobs scam pattern.
  4. Prepare documents. Passport, experience certificates, photographs and a police clearance certificate are standard requirements.
  5. Confirm the employer. Ask for the hiring company’s name and the NEOM project site — legitimate recruiters provide both.
NEOM demand is strongest in construction, engineering, hospitality and facility management. Skilled trades (welding, electrical, HVAC) consistently attract the best packages — uncertified “agent promises” of office jobs on site are a red flag.

Beyond The Line: Other Giga-Project Opportunities

NEOM is the flagship, but it is not the only Vision 2030 project hiring at volume. Diriyah (the historic Najdi capital redevelopment), Qiddiya (entertainment and sports city), the Red Sea tourism developments and New Murabba in Riyadh are all generating demand for the same skill sets: construction trades, engineering, hospitality, facility management, logistics and security.

The application playbook is identical: use government-approved agencies on the eMigrate portal, verify RA numbers, and never pay upfront fees. Workers who build a track record on one giga-project — strong references, completed contracts — find it far easier to move between projects as phases complete, since the same contractors rotate across sites.

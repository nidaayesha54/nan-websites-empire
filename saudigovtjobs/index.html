How to Apply

  1. Apply through Jadarat — the official Saudi government jobs platform for ministry hiring.
  2. Complete your Jadarat profile with degree attestation (required for all ministry roles).
  3. Many ministry posts are reserved for Saudi nationals under Saudization (Nitaqat) — check eligibility on each ad.
  4. Expat applicants: confirm the role is open to non-Saudis and verify visa sponsorship terms.
  5. Shortlisting takes 3–8 weeks; offers include iqama sponsorship for expat hires.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Jadarat?

Jadarat is Saudi Arabia's official government jobs platform where ministries advertise openings and candidates submit applications.

Can expats apply for Saudi government jobs?

Some ministry roles hire expats, but many are reserved for Saudi nationals under Saudization (Nitaqat). Each listing states whether non-Saudis may apply.

What is Saudization (Nitaqat)?

Saudi Arabia's policy requiring employers — including government — to meet quotas of Saudi national employees. It means priority hiring for citizens in most ministry roles.

What salaries do Saudi ministry jobs pay?

Mid-level ministry roles typically pay SAR 9,000–19,000 per month, often with housing and transport allowances and end-of-service benefits.

Do I need degree attestation?

Yes — Saudi ministries require attested degree certificates as part of the Jadarat profile before applications are considered.

How long does hiring take?

Shortlisting usually takes 3–8 weeks after closing, with medical and background checks before the offer. Expat hires receive iqama sponsorship.

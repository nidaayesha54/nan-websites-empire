How Much to Feed My Dog: Chart & Formula 🍖

Over 50% of US dogs are overweight — and portion size is the #1 cause. Here's the chart vets use, plus the exact formula.

The Vet Formula (RER)

Veterinarians calculate resting energy requirement as:

RER = 70 × (weight in kg)^0.75

Then multiply by a lifestyle factor: 1.6 for neutered adults, 1.8 for intact, 1.2–1.4 for weight loss, 2–3 for active/working dogs, 2–3 for puppies.

Feeding Tips

  • Measure, don't eyeball: use a real measuring cup.
  • Two meals beat one: split the daily amount morning and evening.
  • Adjust monthly: feel the ribs — you should feel them easily with a thin fat cover.
  • Treats count: treats should be under 10% of daily calories.
  • Check the bag: kcal/cup varies hugely (250–500+) between brands.
General guidance only — puppies, seniors, pregnant dogs, and dogs with health conditions need vet-tailored plans. Always consult your veterinarian.

GTA 6 Chop: Is Franklin's Dog Back in Vice City?

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Ask a GTA fan to name the best character in GTA 5 and a surprising number will not say Michael, Franklin or Trevor. They will say Chop. Franklin Clinton's Rottweiler is one of the most loved characters in the whole series — a dog with a personality, a routine and a fan base of his own. And now that GTA 6 has arrived in Leonida, the question follows him: is Chop back? This guide tells you what Chop did in GTA 5, what GTA 6 has actually shown about dogs, and why a comeback is unlikely — even though the hope is completely understandable.

The short answer is that there is no confirmation Chop appears in GTA 6. The longer answer explains why that is, and what would have to be true for it to change.

Who Chop is

Chop is Franklin's dog in GTA 5, and he is not a prop. He is a Rottweiler with a daily life: players can take him for walks, play fetch with him, teach him tricks, and take him along on missions, where his scent-tracking can find hidden items and people. He has his own needs — he gets happy, he gets bored, he needs attention — and the game rewards the player for looking after him. He even had a life outside the game: the iFruit app let players look after Chop away from the console, feeding him and keeping him happy between sessions.

That is why Chop matters. Most games treat animals as decoration. GTA 5 treated Chop as a character, and players responded to that. A decade later, he is still the reference point every GTA fan uses when they talk about dogs in games — and the first name they ask about when a new GTA arrives.

What GTA 6 has shown about dogs

GTA 6 has shown plenty of dogs. Several breeds have already been spotted in the trailers and released footage, including Chihuahuas, Dobermans, German Shepherds and Rottweilers. Dogs appear in yards, on streets and on porches around Vice City and Leonida. In the Extended Look, Jason directly interacts with a dog — and Rob Nelson, who co-runs Rockstar North, told one outlet that players can study, scold or pet a dog, and that the game has even accounted for what happens after a dog does its business. Domesticated cats have been seen in the footage too.

But none of that is Chop. A Rottweiler on a street in Vice City is a breed, not a character — and no trailer shows a dog with Chop's name, Chop's owner or Chop's history. No announcement, interview or Rockstar statement has mentioned Chop in connection with GTA 6 at all.

Why a comeback is unlikely

The reason is geography and story. Chop belongs to Franklin Clinton, and Franklin lives in Los Santos, on the other side of the country from Leonida. GTA 6 follows Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos in Vice City and the Leonida Keys — a different city, a different story, and no announced link to Franklin at all. For Chop to appear, Rockstar would need a story reason to bring a Los Santos dog to a Florida-inspired state, and it would need to explain why Franklin's dog is there without Franklin.

That is not impossible — Rockstar loves a cameo, and the series has a long history of characters crossing over. But a cameo is a brief appearance, not a companion system, and there is a difference between Chop walking past in a cutscene and Chop living with Jason and Lucia. The first would be a treat for longtime fans. The second would need a story the game has not told.

What GTA 6 does with pets instead

What GTA 6 has done is build a world full of animals and a deep interaction system around them. Rockstar has said Leonida holds more than 170 animal species, and the trailers confirm dogs, alligators, flamingos, deer, pelicans, herons, gulls, wading birds and dolphins. The interaction system lets you pet a stranger's dog, and the game tracks how pets and their owners react to you. GTA Online added more on the pet side too: in the A Safehouse on the Hills update, players can name their pets and pet them — a feature fans hope becomes the minimum for the next game.

And the history is encouraging even without Chop. Rockstar has built dog companions before — Chop in GTA 5, and the camp dogs in Red Dead Redemption 2, which players could pat and which would follow them around. If GTA 6 ever gets a companion dog of its own, it will most likely follow that model: a dog that lives with Jason and Lucia, follows them, and becomes part of the story. But until Rockstar says so, that dog does not exist — and it is not Chop.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Chop in GTA 6?

There is no confirmation that Chop appears in GTA 6. Chop is Franklin's dog in GTA 5, and GTA 6 follows Jason and Lucia in Leonida — a different city and story, with no announced link to Franklin.

What dog breeds are in GTA 6?

Chihuahuas, Dobermans, German Shepherds and Rottweilers have all been spotted in the trailers and released footage. Dogs appear in yards, streets and porches around Vice City and Leonida.

Can you own a dog in GTA 6?

That is unconfirmed. No pet adoption, ownership, training or care system has been announced. Jason can pet a dog in the Extended Look, but no trailer shows a dog being adopted or kept.

The bottom line: Chop is the most loved dog in GTA history, and the hope that he returns is the sincerest compliment a fan can pay a game dog. But GTA 6 is Jason and Lucia's story in a different state, and until Rockstar says otherwise, Chop stays in Los Santos — where Franklin, and a decade of memories, keep him.

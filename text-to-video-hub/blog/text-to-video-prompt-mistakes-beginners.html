5 Text-to-Video Prompt Mistakes Beginners Make (and One-Line Fixes)

October 2026 · 5 min read

Most "bad AI video" is bad prompting. Here are the five mistakes I see in almost every beginner's prompt — and the one-line fix for each.

1. The vague subject

Mistake: "a nice dog playing"
Fix: name exactly what the camera sees — "a muddy golden retriever puppy shaking water off its fur." Concrete nouns beat adjectives every time.

2. No motion verb

Mistake: "a beautiful beach at sunset" — the model renders a postcard, not a video.
Fix: add one action: "...waves rolling onto the shore, palm fronds swaying."

3. No camera direction

Mistake: letting the model pick the shot — results feel random.
Fix: open with a camera move: "Slow push-in on...", "Aerial drone shot of..."

4. Style soup

Mistake: "cinematic photorealistic anime 8k hyperdetailed" — conflicting styles cancel out.
Fix: pick ONE style anchor per prompt.

5. Skipping the negative prompt

Mistake: accepting extra fingers and flickering backgrounds as "just how AI is."
Fix: append "blurry, extra limbs, flickering, watermark, distorted faces" — or generate a full one with our negative prompt builder.

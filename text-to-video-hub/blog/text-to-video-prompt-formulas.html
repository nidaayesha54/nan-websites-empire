7 Text-to-Video Prompt Formulas to Steal

October 2026 · 6 min read

Don't start from a blank box. These seven formulas cover the most common video types — swap in your subject and you're generating in seconds. Every formula ends with a negative prompt; build a custom one with our negative prompt builder.

1. The Product Hero

"Slow rotating shot of [PRODUCT] on [SURFACE], [LIGHTING] with soft reflections, luxurious mood, photorealistic product video."
Example: "...matte-black perfume bottle on a marble pedestal, studio lighting..."

2. The Cute Animal

"Close-up of [ANIMAL] [ONE ACTION], [slow-motion / real-time], warm golden-hour light, joyful mood, ultra photorealistic."

3. The Travel Postcard

"Aerial drone shot sweeping over [LOCATION] at [TIME OF DAY], [WEATHER DETAIL], epic mood, cinematic film still."

4. The Mini Story

"[SHOT TYPE] of [CHARACTER] [ACTION WITH OBJECT], [REACTION/RESULT], [LIGHTING], whimsical mood, [STYLE]."

5. The Ad Hook (first 3 seconds)

"Fast zoom-in on [PRODUCT/BENEFIT IN ACTION], bold [LIGHTING], high-energy mood, commercial style, crisp motion."

6. The Aesthetic Loop

"Seamless loop of [CALM ACTION], soft [LIGHTING], peaceful mood, [STYLE], smooth continuous motion."

7. The Cinematic Establishing Shot

"Wide shot of [EPIC LOCATION], [ATMOSPHERE DETAIL], dramatic [LIGHTING], epic mood, cinematic, 8k."

Make them yours

Formulas are starting points — the magic is in specific nouns. "A dog" becomes "a muddy golden retriever puppy shaking off water." For the full method, read How to Write AI Video Prompts, or let our prompt generator assemble one for you.

How to use this generator

Good text-to-video prompts have five parts: subject (who/what), action (what happens), style (the look), camera (how it's shot), and mood + lighting (the feeling). This tool assembles all five into one prompt. For a deeper breakdown, read our guide How to Write AI Video Prompts.

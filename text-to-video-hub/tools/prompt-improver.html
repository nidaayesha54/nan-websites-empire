Why prompts need improving

Video models guess everything you don't specify. A 3-word prompt like "a dog running" leaves the camera, lighting, style, and motion up to chance — which is why results feel random. The improver restructures your idea into the five-part formula that works in 2026: subject + action + camera + lighting/mood + style.

GTA 6 Trailers: All 4 Videos and What Each Showed

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the road to launch was paved with four videos. Not forty, not a drip-feed of teasers — four, spread across almost three years, each one answering a question the last one left open. This guide lists them all in order: the dates, the lengths, the songs, what each one showed, and the records they broke — plus the one video that is not a trailer at all, and the nickname that confuses everyone.

The trailers matter because they are the whole marketing campaign. Rockstar said almost nothing about GTA 6 outside these videos — no interviews for years, no gameplay demos, no steady stream of screenshots. Everything players knew before launch came from four uploads on Rockstar's own channel, and everything in this guide comes from those uploads and the reporting around them.

Trailer 1: December 4, 2023 — the reveal

Trailer 1 runs 1 minute 31 seconds, and it was supposed to arrive on Tuesday, December 5, 2023 at 9 a.m. Eastern. It actually went up on the evening of December 4, hours early, after an early copy appeared online — Rockstar posted the real thing rather than let the copy speak for it. The trailer confirmed the game's name and the return to Vice City, gave a 2025 release year on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S with no PC version, and opened on Lucia, who appears to be in prison.

What it showed was a mood piece: beaches, swamps inspired by the Everglades, neon-lit highways, clubs, alligators, mudding, car shows, and clips styled like social media posts — people filming themselves doing reckless things, a crocodile walking into a shop, a street race being organised, someone riding a hovercraft through a swamp. A second protagonist appeared unnamed beside Lucia; Trailer 2 would later name him Jason Duval. Lucia's line — "The only way we're going to get through this is by sticking together, being a team. Trust?" — leads into the pair robbing a store. The music was Tom Petty's "Love Is A Long Road", credited in the video description. And the numbers were historic: more than 93 million YouTube views in its first 24 hours, a YouTube record for a non-music video debut and a Guinness World Record for a game trailer.

Trailer 2: May 6, 2025 — the story

Trailer 2 runs 2 minutes 47 seconds, and it came out days after Rockstar delayed the game into 2026. It is longer and more focused on the people: it spends its time on Jason and Lucia's relationship and shows far more of Leonida, with Rockstar saying all of its footage was captured on PlayStation 5. The description frames the story as two people for whom things never seem to go their way, until an easy job goes wrong and they end up caught in a criminal conspiracy across the state. The end card gave a date of May 26, 2026, which Rockstar later moved.

The details fans remember are small ones: Lucia fighting a bodybuilder on the wing of a flying airplane, a snake terrarium in what the Extended Look later shows to be Jason's starting safehouse, Jason and Lucia in formal clothing that returns in a later pool-dive scene, and a screenshot released alongside the trailer showing The Rusty Anchor establishment. The music was The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together". And the numbers were bigger than Trailer 1's: Rockstar told The Hollywood Reporter that Trailer 2 drew 475 million views across all platforms in its first 24 hours, calling it the biggest video launch of all time.

An Extended Look: August 27, 2026 — the big one

The third video is not numbered, and that is where the confusion starts. Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look runs 26 minutes 48 seconds, and it debuted on Netflix on August 27, 2026 at 3 p.m. Eastern, then went free on Rockstar's YouTube channel and the GTA VI site at 9 p.m. Eastern the same evening. At almost 27 minutes it is more of a short film than a trailer, and Rockstar says it was captured entirely from in-game footage on PlayStation 5.

What it shows is the game itself: it follows Jason and Lucia through a run of jobs, mixing cutscenes, gameplay and montage — a job that turns into a shootout, a meeting at a rooftop bar, a getaway along a highway, a stretch of side activities, and a party that goes wrong, before it closes on the release date. Along the way you see character switching between the two leads, a six-star Wanted level, and the side activities that fill Leonida. The opening has Lucia released from a prison facility, with Jason arriving to pick her up, and the video confirms the full names of both protagonists: Lucia Caminos and Jason Duval, both playable. Fans call it "Trailer 3", but that is a nickname — Rockstar never numbered it, and there are only two numbered trailers.

Launch Trailer: October 15, 2026 — the final push

The fourth video is short and punchy again. The Launch Trailer arrived on October 15, 2026 at 1 p.m. UTC, runs 1 minute 29 seconds, and is a montage of new scenes built around Jason and Lucia, with Jason talking about leaving the army on bad terms and the pair in armed set pieces around Vice City. It does not set out to show new mechanics — it sets out to set up the story one last time, and it closes on the November 19 release date. The music is Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane".

That is the whole set: two numbered trailers, one long look, one launch trailer, and a launch date at the end of the last one. All four are on Rockstar's own channel, and the GTA VI site lists all four videos — only the first two carry a number. If a video you find is not one of these four, it is not one of Rockstar's trailers, however many views it has.

FAQ

How many GTA 6 trailers are there? Four videos from Rockstar: Trailer 1 (December 4, 2023), Trailer 2 (May 6, 2025), An Extended Look (August 27, 2026) and the Launch Trailer (October 15, 2026). Only the first two are numbered.

Is there a GTA 6 Trailer 3? No — "Trailer 3" is a fan nickname for An Extended Look, the 27-minute video from August 27, 2026. Rockstar never released a video called Trailer 3.

Which GTA 6 trailer should I watch first? In order: Trailer 1 for the reveal, Trailer 2 for the story, An Extended Look for the game itself, and the Launch Trailer for the final setup before the November 19 launch.

The bottom line: four videos, three years, and a launch. Trailer 1 broke the record, Trailer 2 broke it again, the Extended Look showed the game, and the Launch Trailer closed the campaign. Watch them in order, on Rockstar's own channel, and skip anything that is not one of the four.

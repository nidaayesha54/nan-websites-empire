The litter box rule every cat owner should know

One box per cat, plus one extra. So one cat gets two boxes, two cats get three, and so on. Under-boxed homes are the #1 cause of litter-box avoidance — most "cat peed outside the box" problems are really "not enough clean boxes" problems.

  • Scoop daily. Cats are fastidious; a dirty box is a rejected box.
  • Full change weekly for clay litter; crystals and pellets last longer between full dumps.
  • 3–4 inches deep is the sweet spot for clumping clay.

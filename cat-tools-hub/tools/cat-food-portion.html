The RER formula, explained

Vets calculate resting energy requirement as RER = 70 × (weight in kg)⁰·⁷⁵, then multiply by a factor for life stage and activity. A neutered 10-lb indoor adult needs roughly 220–250 kcal/day — far less than most owners guess, which is why free-feeding so often leads to overweight cats.

⚠️ Keep it safe: This tool gives estimates for healthy cats. Adjust food gradually if your cat gains or loses weight, and ask your vet before any diet plan for an overweight or sick cat.

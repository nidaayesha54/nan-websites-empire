How the cat-to-human-years formula works

Veterinarians commonly use this conversion: the first year of a cat's life equals 15 human years, the second year adds 9 more (24 total), and every year after adds 4 human years. This reflects the fact that cats mature rapidly as kittens and then age steadily.

Indoor cats typically live 13–17 years on average versus 2–5 years for outdoor cats, which is why this calculator includes the indoor/outdoor context. For the full comparison, see our guide Indoor vs Outdoor Cats: Pros and Cons.

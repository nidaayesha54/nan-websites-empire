❓ Frequently asked questions

How old is my cat in human years?

The standard formula: the first year equals 15 human years, the second year adds 9 (24 total), and each year after adds 4 human years. Use our free cat age calculator for an exact number by month.

How much does a cat cost per year in the USA?

In 2026, most US cat owners spend $700–$1,200 per year on food, litter, vet care, and supplies — before insurance. First-year costs are higher ($1,200–$2,000) due to adoption fees, spay/neuter, and startup supplies. Run your own numbers.

How much should I feed my cat per day?

An average 10-lb adult indoor cat needs roughly 200–250 calories per day, split into 2 meals. The exact amount depends on weight, age, and activity — try our food portion calculator, which uses the vet-standard RER formula.

How many litter boxes do I need?

The rule of thumb is one box per cat, plus one extra. Two cats = three boxes. Scoop daily and do a full change weekly. Estimate your litter costs here.

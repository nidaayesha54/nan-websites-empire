Frequently Asked Questions

How much food should I feed my dog per day?

A healthy adult dog typically needs 2–3% of its body weight in food per day, but the exact amount depends on weight, age, activity level, and food calories. Use our daily portion calculator for a tailored estimate.

Are treats bad for dogs?

Treats are fine as long as they make up no more than 10% of your dog's daily calories. Use our treat calculator to find your dog's safe treat budget.

How much does it cost to feed a dog per month?

Feeding a medium dog costs roughly $30–$90 per month depending on brand tier and dog size. Our food cost calculator breaks it down for your dog.

What foods are toxic to dogs?

Common toxic foods include chocolate, grapes, raisins, onions, garlic, xylitol, alcohol, macadamia nuts, and cooked bones. See our complete list of foods dogs cannot eat.

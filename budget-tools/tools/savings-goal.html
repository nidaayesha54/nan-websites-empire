How to Reach Any Savings Goal Faster

  • Automate the transfer — move money to savings on payday before you can spend it.
  • Earn real interest — a high-yield savings account (often 4%+ APY) adds hundreds over a year versus a 0.01% checking account.
  • Raise contributions with raises — save half of every raise and you'll barely feel it.
  • Break big goals into milestones — $10,000 feels far; the first $2,500 feels winnable.

Your 50/30/20 Split

How the 50/30/20 Rule Works

Take your after-tax income and allocate: 50% to needs (housing, food, transport, minimum debt payments), 30% to wants (dining out, hobbies, subscriptions), and 20% to savings (emergency fund, retirement, debt payoff beyond minimums).

It's a starting point, not a law. In high-cost cities, needs might take 60% — the point is to give every dollar a job and notice when "wants" quietly eat your savings. Read our full guide: The 50/30/20 Budget Rule Explained.

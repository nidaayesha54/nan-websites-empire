How Big Should Your Emergency Fund Be?

The classic advice is 3–6 months of essential expenses — not your full income, just what you must spend: housing, food, utilities, transport, insurance, and minimum debt payments.

  • Start with $1,000 — a mini-fund that stops small surprises from becoming credit-card debt.
  • Build to 3 months — enough for most job searches and major repairs.
  • Aim for 6 months — the full recommendation, especially for freelancers and single-income households.

Keep it in a separate high-yield savings account — accessible, but not mixed with spending money. Full breakdown: How Big Should My Emergency Fund Be in 2026?

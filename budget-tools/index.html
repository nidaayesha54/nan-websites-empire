Frequently Asked Questions

What is the 50/30/20 budget rule?

The 50/30/20 rule divides after-tax income into 50% needs, 30% wants, and 20% savings. It's a simple starting framework — adjust the percentages to fit your situation. Try it in our budget planner.

How big should my emergency fund be?

Most experts recommend 3 to 6 months of essential expenses. Start with a $1,000 starter fund, then build to 3 months, and aim for 6 months if your income is irregular. Our emergency fund calculator does the math.

How much should I save each month?

A common target is 20% of take-home pay, but any consistent amount builds the habit. Start with what you can and increase it over time. Track it with our savings goal tracker.

Are these budget tools free?

Yes. Every calculator on BudgetTools is free with no sign-up required. Your numbers stay in your browser and are never sent to us.

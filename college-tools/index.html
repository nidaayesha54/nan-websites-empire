Common questions

How much should I save for college? +
A common rule: cover one-third of projected costs with savings, one-third with income while enrolled, and one-third with aid or loans. For a child born today, four years at a public university could cost $150,000–$200,000 by enrollment time.
Is a 529 plan better than a regular savings account? +
For education savings, yes. 529 investments grow tax-free and qualified withdrawals are federal income-tax-free, while a taxable account's gains get taxed. Many states add a deduction or credit too.
What is the average student loan payment? +
Roughly $300–$400 per month on the standard 10-year plan, varying widely with balance and rate. Run your numbers in the payoff calculator to see your options.
Should I pay extra on student loans or invest? +
Compare rates: extra payments on loans above 6–7% usually beat investing; below 5%, investing often wins mathematically. Also keep an emergency fund before accelerating either.

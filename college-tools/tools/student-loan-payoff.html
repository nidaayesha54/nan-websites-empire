Frequently asked questions

How is the standard student loan payment calculated? +
Federal loans default to the Standard Repayment Plan: fixed amortized payments over 10 years, computed from your balance and interest rate with the standard loan formula.
Do extra student loan payments save money? +
Yes — extra payments go straight to principal, shrinking the balance that interest accrues on. That shortens the loan and cuts total interest, often by thousands of dollars.
What is income-driven repayment? +
Income-driven plans cap payments at a share of discretionary income (often 10%) and forgive the remainder after 20–25 years. Lower payments now usually mean more interest overall — and forgiven debt may be taxable.

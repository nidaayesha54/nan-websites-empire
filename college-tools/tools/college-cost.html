Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between sticker price and net price? +
Sticker price is the published cost before aid. Net price is what you actually pay after grants and scholarships — often tens of thousands less, especially at private colleges with generous aid.
How much does a public university cost per year? +
For 2025–26, average total cost of attendance is about $25,700/year in-state at a public four-year university, about $46,000 out-of-state, and about $64,500 at a private nonprofit — before aid.
Do college costs rise every year? +
Historically about 3% per year. This calculator applies 3% annual inflation across the years you estimate, so a 4-year total reflects rising costs, not today's price times four.

Frequently asked questions

How does a 529 plan grow? +
Your contributions are invested in portfolios you choose. Growth is tax-deferred, and withdrawals for qualified education expenses — tuition, room, board, books — are federal income-tax-free.
What happens to unused 529 money? +
You can change the beneficiary to another family member, hold it for graduate school, or roll up to lifetime limits into a Roth IRA for the beneficiary. Non-qualified withdrawals face income tax plus a 10% penalty on earnings.
How much does tax drag cost in a taxable account? +
Taxes on dividends and realized gains typically shave about 1% per year off returns, plus capital gains tax at withdrawal. Over 10–18 years that compounds into tens of thousands — the 529's core advantage.

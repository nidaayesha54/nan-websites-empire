GTA 6 Platinum: 55 Trophies, 200 Hours, 1 Missable

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

The Platinum trophy in GTA 6 is called "Vice Got a Price!" — and the price is about 200 hours. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the trophy list is now public: 55 trophies on PS5, the same 55 as achievements on Xbox, worth 1,000 Gamerscore, all of them offline and single-player. This guide is the full roadmap — the breakdown, the hardest trophy, the one trophy that may be missable, and the 100% completion checklist that sits at the centre of it all. The numbers come from the launch-era guides that worked from the game itself — PowerPyx, GTABase, Insider Gaming — because Rockstar has not published an official list, and the honest guide says where its numbers come from.

It is worth saying up front: the Platinum is rated 4 out of 10 in difficulty by PowerPyx, which makes it one of the easier Platinums in a big open-world game. The challenge is not skill — it is time. Two hundred hours, one playthrough, and a checklist that only appears after the credits. The difficulty is showing up, not being brilliant.

The List: 55 Trophies, One Platinum

Here is the breakdown. On PS5 there are 55 trophies: 1 Platinum, 2 Gold, 6 Silver and 46 Bronze. On Xbox Series X|S there are the same 55 as achievements, worth 1,000 Gamerscore in total — the Platinum becomes a Mythic achievement worth 0G, because Xbox has no Platinum. Twenty-seven of the 55 are visible from the start; 28 are hidden, and the hidden ones reveal mission names and chapter structure, so a full list is a spoiler list. All of them are offline and single-player — there are no online trophies, because there is no online mode at launch.

The two Gold trophies are the spine of the whole set. "You Gotta Know When to Get Off" is for finishing the final story mission. "A Long Road" is for reaching 100% completion, and it is the hardest trophy in the game by every guide that has rated it — not because any single task is hard, but because the checklist behind it is enormous. The Platinum, "Vice Got a Price!", unlocks when you have earned every other trophy, with the description "Congratulations! You're a Leonida Legend."

The One Missable Trophy

There is exactly one trophy that may be missable, and it is the one to know about before you start playing: "Positive Mental Attitude", a Bronze for meditating at every spot across Leonida — 18 spots in 10 locations, with Jason and Lucia. GTABase and PowerPyx both flag it as the only trophy that may be missable, and GTABase says it may need Lucia's Chapter 1 balcony meditation the morning after "Down to Earth" — a moment early in the story that, if skipped, may not come back.

The practical advice is simple: meditate whenever the game offers it, from the very first chapter, and do not skip a meditation spot because you are in a hurry. The good news is that the missable trophy cannot cost you the 100% completion checklist — "A Long Road" only asks for 15 of the 18 spots, so the checklist is safe even if the trophy is not. And if you do miss it, the only fix is a new playthrough, which is why the warning belongs at the start of this guide rather than the end.

The 100% Checklist: What "A Long Road" Actually Asks

The 100% completion checklist appears after the story, under Progress, then Stats, then 100% Completion — but progress made during the story still counts, so you are building toward it from the first mission. Insider Gaming counts 35 tasks, and GTABase groups them as 45% story and tasks, 45% exploration and activities, and 10% discovery. PowerPyx says the thresholds are lenient — roughly 60% of true completion — and nothing on the checklist is permanently missable, because every requirement can be finished in free roam after the credits.

Here are the headline numbers, from the launch-era guides. Finish the main story and 100 optional tasks. Complete all 39 gang operations, 22 bank jobs, 7 payday loan jobs and all 8 bank vaults. Fence $100,000 in narcotics, $100,000 in jewelry, $250,000 in marked bills and $200,000 in vehicles. Complete 15 of the 18 collectible card sets, from 480 cards in all. Visit 100 WAiNK locations, make 33 underwater discoveries and find 100 Cocaine Flamingos. Earn all 42 shooting range gold medals — 18 at Griffin, 24 at Rodgers Ranch. Complete 300 of the 397 stunts, and scan 200 of the 277 base-model vehicles. Finish 70 of the 80 challenges, and complete 8 Strangers & Freaks mission strands. Those numbers are guide data, reported by GTABase and PowerPyx from the game — and they are the targets to plan around.

The Roadmap: Three Steps, 200 Hours

PowerPyx lays the Platinum out in three steps. Step one is the story: play through all 91 missions, enjoy it, and meditate at every spot you find — that is the missable trophy protected. Step two is the 100% checklist: after the credits, work through the list above, including 70 of the 80 challenges — about 100 hours of clean-up on its own. Step three is the remaining trophies: the shooting range golds, the stunt counts, the races, the collectibles, and the trophies that come naturally while doing the checklist. Most of the checklist overlaps with the trophies, so the work counts twice.

Two warnings belong in any roadmap. First, cheats disable trophies for the session — the moment you activate a cheat, trophies stop unlocking and saving is blocked, so the 61 cheat codes are a playground, not a shortcut to the Platinum. Second, the "Irredeemable Criminal Profile" is permanent, according to GTABase, so read the Criminal Profile guide before pushing toward it. Neither warning is complicated; both are easy to miss, and both cost hours if you learn them late.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does the GTA 6 Platinum take?

About 200 hours, according to PowerPyx, at a difficulty of 4 out of 10, in a single playthrough. The story is 50 to 60 hours; the rest is the 100% checklist and the activity trophies around it.

Is any GTA 6 trophy missable?

One, potentially: "Positive Mental Attitude", for meditating at all 18 spots. GTABase says it may need Lucia's Chapter 1 balcony meditation. Meditate at every spot from the start, and the checklist itself is safe — it only needs 15 of 18.

Do I need to play online for any trophy?

No. All 55 trophies are offline and single-player. There is no online mode at launch, and no trophy requires one.

The Bottom Line

Fifty-five trophies, one Platinum, about 200 hours, and one trophy to watch from the first chapter. Finish the story, meditate everywhere, work the checklist after the credits, and leave the cheats alone until the Platinum pops. "Vice Got a Price!" is a long road — but it is a road, not a wall, and anyone who finishes the story can walk it.

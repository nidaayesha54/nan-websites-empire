Frequently Asked Questions

How much does travel insurance cost in 2026?
Most US travelers pay 4–10% of their total trip cost for a comprehensive plan. A $5,000 trip typically costs $200–$500. Use our free quote estimator for a personal range.
Do I really need travel insurance for a domestic trip?
It depends. If your trip is fully refundable and your health insurance covers you wherever you go, you may not need it. Take our free 60-second quiz to find out.
What does travel insurance actually cover?
Core coverages are trip cancellation/interruption, emergency medical, medical evacuation, baggage loss/delay, and travel delays. CFAR (cancel for any reason) is an optional upgrade. Read our full coverage guide.
Are these estimates financial advice?
No. All results are estimates only — not financial, legal, or insurance advice. Talk to a licensed agent before buying.

Do I Need Travel Insurance? (60-Second Quiz)

Answer all 7 questions honestly. Each answer earns risk points — your total tells you how strongly travel insurance is recommended for this specific trip.

1. Is your trip international?

2. How much of your trip cost is non-refundable?

3. Does your health insurance cover you at your destination?

4. Do you have a pre-existing health condition or take regular medication?

5. Will you do adventure activities (skiing, scuba, hiking at altitude, ziplining)?

6. Are you traveling during hurricane/storm season, or to a remote area far from hospitals?

7. Are you 65 or older, or traveling with kids/older relatives?

GTA 6 Leonida Keys: A Travel Guide to the Islands

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the game starts at the far end of the map — not in Vice City, but in the Leonida Keys, the island chain south of the city where Jason Duval lives. The Keys are where the story begins, where the first safehouse sits, and where some of the game's best money is buried under the water.

Every player starts here, and the islands teach the game gently — the first jobs, the first boat, the first plane and the first treasure are all within a few miles of each other.

Getting there: Route 1A and the bridges

The Leonida Keys are the southern island chain, strung along Route 1A from Key Lento down to Mile Zero Air Station — the game's version of the Florida Keys and the Overseas Highway, the road that runs the length of the real island chain on bridges over open water. The islands each have their own names on the launch-day map, and the chain is one of the six regions Rockstar named before launch: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. The Keys are the southernmost of them, and the road is the only way in by car.

Driving the chain is the slow way and the best way. The bridges are long and the water is on both sides. By boat is faster if you know the water, and by air is fastest of all: the seaplane in the Chapter 1 mission Clear Skies takes off from the water near the Mile Zero Air Station, and the helicopter reported on a private helipad east of Hamlet, near Key Lento, can be flown off without a wanted level. The chain has only one road, so plan the route before you leave.

Key Lento: where the game starts

Key Lento is the long key, and it is where Jason lives. His safehouse is Brian Heder's green wooden house on the shore, a weathered place on stilts by the water, and Brian's Boat Works and Marina is the boatyard next door — the old smuggler's business, and the place the game's first boat jobs come from. Lucia moves into the house when she gets out of prison, and the two of them live there until they leave town in the mission Home Sweet Home. The house is free, the rent is paid in dirty work, and the porch looks out on the water the whole game is named after.

The Island Dreams Boat Charters shop, the yellow building where the diving boat waits at the dock, is here. The first treasure dives are offshore, in the search circles the map adds after Island Dreams. The bars are loaded, the dress code is casual, and the waters next to the chain are described by Rockstar as dangerous — which in the Keys means sharks as well as currents, and the diving guide warns about both.

Mile Zero Air Station and the far end

At the far end of the chain sits Mile Zero Air Station, the game's version of the real Mile Marker 0 in Key West — the point where the road ends and the map stops. Donnie works here, tinkering with a plane by the water, and the Clear Skies mission starts here: talk to Donnie, talk to Brian at the bar up the street, then take Donnie's seaplane off the water and fly it to the intercept point in the ocean to the southeast. The air station is the Keys' airport, such as it is — a strip of tarmac at the end of the world, with a bar, a boat and a biker gang's airstrip a short flight away.

Southwind, near the station, is where Donnie's deal is set up.

Diving, fishing and the water

The water is the Keys' real attraction, and its real money. After Island Dreams in Chapter 1, Kenton Rees gets back in touch and the map adds six offshore search areas around Key Lento: the wrecks of the Nuestra Senora de la Noche with 7 items, the Nuestra Senora de la Etica Laxa with 5, the Urca de Cinco Torres with 6, the Nuestra Senora Lujuriosa with 6, the Urca de Santa Sierra with 6, and the Nuestra Senora del Ocio with 10 gold doubloons. The boat from Island Dreams waits back at the dock, with a scuba set on it — put the gear on before diving, and the metal detector and the Arras Bullhead speargun come with it. Each dive site's map card tells you how many treasures you have found, and you sell what you find to Kenton.

Fishing and kayaking live in the same water. The rules are simple: watch the oxygen meter, hold R1 on PlayStation or RB on Xbox to dive, and remember that the water is dangerous — sharks patrol the diving areas, and the game's own diving guide lists them alongside the treasure.

What to pack for the trip

First, money in the bank, not in your pocket: the pocket cap is $20,000, and cash on your person is lost when you die or get busted, so deposit at an ATM — BuckMe, the banking app, has an ATM finder — before you go diving. Second, a boat: the Island Dreams boat is at the dock after the mission, and it is the only way to reach the wrecks. Third, the scuba gear, which is on the boat and on the dock.

And pack a change of clothes: buying new ones removes the heat after a job gone wrong, and a fresh outfit is the fastest way to disappear on an island with one road.

FAQ

How do you get to the Leonida Keys in GTA 6? Drive Route 1A over the bridges from Vice City, take a boat, or fly — the seaplane in Clear Skies takes off from the water near Mile Zero Air Station, and the RydeMe app books a ride to any waypoint.

Where does Jason live in GTA 6? In Brian Heder's house in Key Lento, in the Leonida Keys — his safehouse from the start of the game until he and Lucia leave town in Home Sweet Home.

What is Mile Zero Air Station? The air station at the far end of the Keys island chain, where Donnie works and the Clear Skies mission starts — the game's version of Mile Marker 0 in Key West.

The bottom line: the Leonida Keys are the game's front door — one road, a chain of islands, a house on the shore, a boat at the dock and a fortune under the water. Drive Route 1A, bank your cash before you dive, and let the Keys teach you the game before Vice City takes over.

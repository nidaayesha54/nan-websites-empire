How to Apply

1
Read the official advertisement carefully and check eligibility criteria for the post.
2
Apply through the official portal (FPSC, PPSC, SPSC or the department's website) before the last date.
3
Download the application fee challan and deposit the fee at the designated bank branch.
4
Appear for the written test / screening on the announced date with your original documents.
5
Shortlisted candidates attend the interview, then the final merit list is published.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for FPSC jobs?

Create an account on the FPSC website, fill the online application form, deposit the test fee challan, and submit before the closing date.

What documents are required?

Typically CNIC, domicile, educational certificates, recent photographs, and experience letters where required.

Is there an age limit for government jobs?

Most posts require candidates between 18 and 28 years, with general age relaxation of 5 years as per government rules.

How can I check my application status?

Log in to the portal where you applied (FPSC/PPSC) to track admission certificates, test dates and results.

Are these listings official advertisements?

Listings here are illustrative samples. Always verify on the official government portal before applying.

Do I need to pay to apply?

A small test fee via bank challan is usually required for commission jobs; direct departmental posts are often free.

PAEC Public Notice S-15/2026: Scientific Assistant, Technician & Junior Assistant Jobs — Last Date 15 Nov 2026

The Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission (PAEC) has announced recruitment under Public Notice S-15/2026 for three posts at its establishment in Tandojam, Sindh: Scientific Assistant-II (SPS-3), Technician-II (Medical) (SPS-3) and Junior Assistant-II (Admin) (SPS-3). Applications are accepted online only through the portal hrd1902.com.pk, and the last date to apply is 15 November 2026. The initial place of posting is Tandojam, though selected candidates can be posted anywhere in Pakistan.

PAEC advertisements under the SPS (Special Pay Scale) system are among the most competitive federal recruitments for F.Sc and Matric-level candidates: they combine government pay scales with the commission's research and development establishments across the country. This notice covers the medical/paramedical and administrative streams rather than the engineering trades.

PAEC Public Notice S-15/2026 — Posts & Eligibility

PostPay ScaleEligibility Criteria
Scientific Assistant-IISPS-3F.Sc (Pre-Medical) in 1st Division
Technician-II (Medical)SPS-3Option a: Matric with Science (1st Division) + 1-year course in the relevant field from a Government-recognized institute + 3 years relevant experience
Option b: Matric with Science (1st Division) + 2-year course in the relevant field + 2 years relevant experience
Option c: F.Sc (Pre-Medical), preferably 1st Division, + 1-year diploma in the relevant field from a Government-recognized institute
Junior Assistant-II (Admin)SPS-3F.A or F.Sc (1st Division) with a 6-month Office Automation course, or an equivalent admin-related qualification
Also note: the companion Public Notice S-14/2026 advertises the same three posts for the Peshawar establishment with a last date of 1 November 2026 — Peshawar-based candidates should apply under that notice instead.

Key Conditions

  • Posting: initial place of posting is Tandojam, Sindh — but the organization reserves the right to post selected candidates anywhere in Pakistan.
  • Cut-off rule: all qualifications and experience must be complete before the closing date — results awaited after 15 November 2026 do not count.
  • How to apply: online only at hrd1902.com.pk — select the notice that matches your post and location (S-15/2026 for Tandojam). Applications by any other method are rejected.
  • Merit-based: selection follows PAEC's standard merit process; only shortlisted candidates are contacted.

How to Apply Online

1
Visit hrd1902.com.pk and open the Public Notice S-15/2026 (Tandojam) section.
2
Select your post — Scientific Assistant-II, Technician-II (Medical) or Junior Assistant-II (Admin) — and read the detailed eligibility for it.
3
Fill the online form with your educational and experience details exactly as they appear on your certificates.
4
Submit before 15 November 2026. Keep a printout or screenshot of your submitted application.
Warning: apply only through the official portal named in the advertisement. PAEC never asks for money through agents — any demand for payment is a scam.

Why PAEC SPS Posts Are Sought After

PAEC operates one of the most respected scientific and technical establishments in Pakistan — from the nuclear power plants at Karachi and Chashma to research centres across the country. The SPS pay scale system (as against the ordinary BPS system) is one of the reasons these advertisements draw heavy applications: competitive pay, structured career paths, and the professional environment of a research organization. For F.Sc (Pre-Medical) candidates, the Scientific Assistant-II route is a direct entry into laboratory and scientific support work; for paramedical diploma holders, Technician-II (Medical) offers three different eligibility routes to accommodate different training paths; and Junior Assistant-II (Admin) remains the classic administrative entry for FA/FSc candidates with computer skills.

Note that PAEC's establishment at Tandojam is in Sindh, but eligibility is not restricted to Sindh domicile — the posting is at Tandojam, and candidates from across Pakistan can apply, subject to the conditions in the notice.

NCCIA Roll Number Slip 2026: Download Physical & Written Test Slips at npftas.pk

The NCCIA application window is now closed — here is how to download your NCCIA roll number slip 2026 for the physical and written tests from npftas.pk, with the expected test schedule and physical standards.

✅ Updated October 20, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

The National Cyber Crime Investigation Agency (NCCIA), under the Ministry of Interior & Narcotics Control, advertised a major 2026 recruitment round for posts from BS-05 to BS-14. Applications went through the NPF-TAS portal (npftas.pk), and the window is now closed. If you applied in time, this guide covers your NCCIA roll number slip 2026: the expected physical and written test schedule, the physical standards, and how to download the slip.

How to Download the NCCIA Roll Number Slip 2026

Follow these steps once the slip is uploaded on the NPF-TAS portal:

1
Visit npftas.pk and open the NCCIA Jobs 2026 section.
2
Log in to your NPF-TAS account using your CNIC and login credentials.
3
Open the Roll No Slip / Test Slip option and select the NCCIA post you applied for.
4
Download your NCCIA roll number slip (PDF) and take a printout.
5
Check the test date, reporting time, test centre and instructions printed on the slip — and bring the printed slip with you on test day.
6
After the physical stage, keep checking your NPF-TAS account — a separate roll number slip is issued for the written test.

Application Fee — For Reference Only

The processing fee was Rs 2,000 per post (non-refundable), paid via 1Link-1Bill (banks, ATMs, Easypaisa, JazzCash, U Paisa) using the 22-digit PSID on the fee challan. The payment window is closed.

Eligibility Recap and General Conditions

  • Age limit: 18–25 years, with relaxation as per government rules. No relaxation is allowed in the lower age limit, physical standards or endurance test.
  • Sub Inspector / ASI: second class or Grade “C” bachelor’s/associate degree or 4-year BS in Computer Science, IT, Cyber Security, Software Engineering, Digital Forensics, Telecom/Electrical Engineering, Data Science or equivalent from an HEC-recognized university.
  • Technical Assistant: relevant degree as above plus 3 years post-qualification experience.
  • Stenotypist: Intermediate with shorthand 80 w.p.m. and typing 40 w.p.m.; must be computer literate.
  • UDC: Intermediate; selected candidates must complete a 3-week NITB IT basic training course within one year.
  • Constable: Matriculation (2nd class) from a recognized board.
  • Constable Driver: Matriculation plus a valid motorcycle and LTV licence, with driving and vehicle-maintenance knowledge.
  • A separate roll number slip is issued for each stage; original CNIC/passport and the slip are required at every stage.
  • Selected candidates can be posted anywhere in Pakistan, must pass a medical by an authorized federal civil surgeon, and get no TA/DA for test or interview.
  • Fake documents or false information lead to cancellation and legal action — zero-tolerance policy.

What to Take to the Test Centre

Bring your original CNIC, the printed slip, sports shoes and any documents mentioned on the slip — and follow the slip’s instructions if they differ from general guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the NCCIA physical test 2026 be held?

The physical/endurance test is expected during the last week of September 2026. Candidates should confirm their exact date, reporting time and venue through the official roll number slip uploaded on npftas.pk.

When will the NCCIA written test 2026 be held?

The written test (expected to be computer-based) is expected in October 2026, after the physical stage. A separate roll number slip will be issued for the written test on the NPF-TAS portal.

Where can I download the NCCIA roll number slip 2026?

Slips are downloaded from the NPF-TAS Job Portal at npftas.pk: log in with your CNIC, open the Roll No Slip / Test Slip option, select the NCCIA post and download the PDF.

What if my NCCIA roll number slip is not available yet?

Keep checking your NPF-TAS account, SMS and email. Slips are uploaded before each stage, so check again close to the expected physical and written test windows.

Is the NCCIA application window still open?

No. The application window closed in September 2026. This article is for candidates who already applied and are waiting for their test slips.

What was the NCCIA application fee?

The application processing fee was Rs 2,000 per post (non-refundable), paid through the 1Link-1Bill service using the 22-digit PSID on the fee challan. The payment window is closed.

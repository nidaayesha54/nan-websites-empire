NADRA Technologies (NTL) Jobs 2026: Flutter Developer &amp; Office Attendant — Apply by 21 Oct 2026

NADRA Technologies Limited (NTL) has announced new openings in October 2026 for two very different talent pools: software developers and support staff. The company is hiring a DAD Software Developer (Flutter) to build mobile applications and an Office Attendant for general office duties. Both posts are offered on a 3-year extendable contract with a 6-month probation, and applications are accepted online only at careers.nadra.gov.pk until 21 October 2026.

The Flutter developer role is the headline opportunity: it is open even to fresh graduates with about a year of experience, with an age limit of 30 years. The developer will build Android and iOS applications from a single Flutter/Dart codebase — exactly the cross-platform skill employers across Pakistan are paying for right now. The Office Attendant post, meanwhile, requires Middle or Matric with preferably one year of relevant experience and an age limit of 40 years, making it accessible to support-staff candidates.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nationality: Pakistani citizens only.
  • Flutter Developer: Fresh graduates or candidates with about 1 year of experience; maximum age 30 years.
  • Office Attendant: Middle or Matric with preferably 1 year of relevant experience; maximum age 40 years.
  • Contract terms: 3-year extendable contract with a 6-month probation period.

For the developer post, demonstrable Flutter/Dart ability matters most — a small app portfolio can outweigh the experience letter. For the Office Attendant post, the educational bar is modest, so Middle/Matric candidates can apply confidently.

How to Apply Online at careers.nadra.gov.pk

Applications are accepted online only. Follow these steps:

1
Open careers.nadra.gov.pk and create an account or log in.
2
Select the NTL October 2026 advertisement and choose the post you qualify for.
3
Fill in the online form and upload your documents, including your CV.
4
Review your details and submit before 21 October 2026; keep the confirmation.
5
Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted; serving government employees must produce an NOC at the interview.
Important: The deadline is 21 October 2026, applications are online only, and mobile phones and smartwatches are prohibited at test/interview venues. Verify the notice at careers.nadra.gov.pk.

NTL shortlists on merit and contacts only the shortlisted candidates for tests and interviews. No TA/DA is admissible, government employees appear with an NOC, and mobile phones or smartwatches are not allowed at the venues — leave them outside or risk disqualification.

Apply early rather than on the last day: portal congestion on 21 October could cost you the submission. And as always, apply only through the official careers portal — never pay anyone for a job offer.

OEC Korea EPS Jobs 2026: Skill Test 19–21 Oct, Final Result 30 Oct 2026 — Complete Schedule

EPS-TOPIK qualifiers move to the skill-test round 19–21 October — final result from HRD Korea on 30 October 2026. Full schedule, eligibility and salary details.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

Pakistan's Overseas Employment Corporation (OEC) 2026 recruitment cycle for South Korea jobs under the Employment Permit System (EPS) has entered its decisive final phase. After the Korean language test (EPS-TOPIK) results were announced on 2 October 2026, the OEC released the skill test schedule on 12 October 2026. Practical skill tests will be held from 19 to 21 October 2026, and the final result from HRD Korea will be announced on 30 October 2026.

The program, run in cooperation with the Human Resources Development Service of Korea, places Pakistani workers in manufacturing, agriculture, construction, shipbuilding, fisheries and services. Here is the complete timeline, who can proceed to the next stage, and what candidates should do now.

Who Proceeds to the Skill Test

Only candidates who qualified the EPS-TOPIK language test and submitted their skills documents on 5 and 6 October 2026 are eligible for the practical skill-test round. Candidates must appear at the examination venue before the time announced on their schedule — late arrivals are not entertained.

Warning: If any candidate is found to have given a false statement in their documents, they are removed from the selection procedure and remain ineligible for 3 years.

Eligibility Criteria for Korea EPS Jobs

  • Age: 18 to 39 years.
  • Education: Matric or equivalent.
  • Record: No criminal record, and no previous illegal stay in Korea — candidates who overstayed in Korea are ineligible under EPS rules.
  • Health: Physically fit for industrial work; candidates should not be colour-blind, as many factory jobs require reading machine indicators and safety signals.
  • Nationality: Male Pakistani nationals with a valid NIC and computerised passport.

Salary and Benefits

Workers placed through the EPS program earn an average salary of around 2,000 to 2,500 USD per month, with legal work-visa sponsorship, medical insurance coverage and accommodation support at many factories. Compared with many Gulf positions, Korean factory jobs generally pay higher wages, and workers can send regular remittances home. The final selection and Korea departure schedule depend on HRD Korea and the employer's own selection process.

What to Do Now

1
Check whether your name is in the skill-test schedule announced on 12 October 2026 on www.oec.gov.pk.
2
Note your exact test date, time and venue — practical skill tests run 19 to 21 October 2026.
3
Carry your original CNIC, passport and all submitted documents to the test venue.
4
Reach the examination room before the announced reporting time.
5
After the skill tests, wait for the final result from HRD Korea on 30 October 2026.
Important: Qualifying the Korean language test is not a guarantee of a job. Final placement depends on the skill test, medical fitness and selection by Korean employers through HRD Korea.

Other OEC Overseas Jobs This Month

Alongside the Korea program, the OEC regularly advertises jobs for Saudi Arabia, the UAE, Qatar, Oman, Bahrain, Greece, Romania and Japan, ranging from technicians and drivers to engineers, nurses and doctors. Applications for all OEC jobs go through the official OEC job portal only, with a modest per-job application fee paid through PSID. Always verify the advertisement on www.oec.gov.pk before paying anyone.

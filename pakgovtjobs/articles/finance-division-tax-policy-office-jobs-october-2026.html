Finance Division Tax Policy Jobs 2026 — 4 Posts (Apply 15 Nov)

The Finance Division, Government of Pakistan, is hiring four specialists for its Tax Policy Office in Islamabad — one Tax Lawyer and three Analysts (Personal Income Tax) on the SPPS-III scale. Apply online through the National Jobs Portal by 15 November 2026.

The Finance Division, Government of Pakistan, has announced four vacancies in its Tax Policy Office: one Tax Lawyer and three Analyst — Personal Income Tax positions, all on the SPPS-III scale. The three Analyst — Personal Income Tax posts are analytical roles supporting the same mission — assisting tax policy development and preparing analytical material on personal income tax (also 10 years' experience; backgrounds in taxation, accounting and financial analysis).

Terms and Conditions

The appointments are contractual under the Special Professional Pay Scales (SPPS) framework. Pay and other terms follow Establishment Division O.M. No. 1/11/2018-E.6 dated 09.04.2024, as amended; the O.M. is on the Finance Division website. Incomplete applications, non-relevant qualifications and late applications will not be entertained. Shortlisted candidates are called for interview — no TA/DA is admissible — and the Finance Division may cancel the process at any time without assigning a reason.

What the Tax Policy Office Does

The Tax Policy Office is the Finance Division's engine for tax reform: developing tax policy, reviewing proposed tax-law changes, and preparing the legal and analytical material behind government tax decisions. These roles sit at the centre of federal fiscal policy — shaping the thinking behind personal income tax changes affecting millions of filers, and a rare route from private advisory work into federal policy-making.

About the Finance Division

The Finance Division is the federal ministry for financial management, economic policy and the national budget. It oversees fiscal policy and revenue matters, and its offices — including the Tax Policy Office — produce the frameworks that guide the Federal Board of Revenue and provincial authorities. SPPS appointments bring market-competitive professional talent into federal government service on contract.

National Job Portal 2026 — How to Apply Online (NJP Guide)

The National Job Portal (njp.gov.pk) is the federal government's official online recruitment system — one profile, hundreds of ministry and department vacancies. This guide walks you through registration, profile building and submitting a correct application.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

Almost every federal government vacancy in Pakistan today — from the Finance Division and the Ministry of Railways to the Planning Ministry and regulatory bodies like SECP — is advertised with the same instruction: apply online through the National Job Portal. The portal, at njp.gov.pk, is the federal government's official online recruitment system. Instead of sending paper applications to each ministry, you create one profile and use it to apply for any advertised post you qualify for. This guide explains the full process, the documents you need, and the mistakes that get applications rejected.

Honesty flag: this is a guide to using the portal, not a job advertisement — it has no closing date of its own. Each vacancy on NJP carries its own deadline, and some posts advertised through FPSC or provincial commissions follow different systems (and sometimes a fee). Always read the individual advertisement before applying.

What the National Job Portal Is — and Is Not

NJP is the federal recruitment portal. It covers ministries and divisions of the Government of Pakistan, not provincial jobs — Punjab posts go through the Punjab Job Portal and PPSC, Sindh posts through the Sindh Job Portal and SPSC, and gazetted posts through FPSC. If an advertisement says "apply online through the National Job Portal", it means njp.gov.pk — not a Facebook page, not a WhatsApp group, and not any website that charges a fee. Government recruitment never charges money for application forms, roll number slips or test venues.

Step-by-Step: Register and Apply

1
Open njp.gov.pk (linked above) and create your account with your CNIC number, a valid email address and an active mobile number.
2
Build your profile completely: personal details, education, experience, domicile and skills — exactly as they appear on your certificates.
3
Browse the advertised posts and open the one you qualify for. Read the eligibility, experience and age conditions in full before applying.
4
Click apply for that post, answer any post-specific questions, and upload scanned copies of the required documents.
5
Review every field carefully — CNIC number, date of birth, education and experience — then submit before the post's closing date.
6
Save or print the application confirmation. Shortlisted candidates are contacted by the hiring department for tests or interviews.

Documents to Keep Ready

Before you start, keep scanned copies of your CNIC, educational degrees and transcripts, experience certificates, domicile certificate and a recent passport-size photograph. Government employees must also arrange a No Objection Certificate (NOC) from their parent department. Incomplete applications, applications with wrong CNIC numbers or dates of birth, and applications received after the closing date are not entertained — these are the most common reasons for rejection, and they are entirely avoidable.

Common Mistakes That Cost Candidates the Job

The biggest mistake is waiting until the last day — portal traffic on closing day regularly causes failed submissions, and late applications are rejected outright. The second is applying for posts you do not qualify for: irrelevant applications are screened out and waste your time. The third is trusting agents who charge for "guaranteed" selection or roll number slips — no one outside the hiring department can influence selection, and paying them only loses your money. Finally, never share CNIC copies over WhatsApp with strangers claiming to be recruiters.

After You Apply: What Happens Next

After submission, the hiring department screens applications against the advertised criteria. Shortlisted candidates are called for a test, interview, or both, and no TA/DA is admissible for appearing. If you are not shortlisted, you will usually hear nothing — that is normal, and it does not mean your profile is broken. Keep your profile updated: new qualifications and experience should be added as you gain them, because the same profile serves every future application.

Reminder: NJP is for federal posts. Provincial commission jobs (PPSC, SPSC, KPPSC, BPSC) and FPSC gazetted posts run on their own portals with their own rules — check the advertisement to see which system your post uses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the National Job Portal (NJP)?

The National Job Portal at njp.gov.pk is the federal government's official online recruitment portal. Federal ministries, divisions and departments advertise their vacancies there, and candidates apply online through a single profile.

Is there any fee to apply on the National Job Portal?

Applying through NJP itself is free. Never pay anyone who claims to arrange a government job or a roll number slip through the portal — that is a scam. Some posts advertised separately through FPSC carry their own fee, which is a different process.

What documents do I need for an NJP application?

Your CNIC, a valid email address and mobile number, educational certificates and transcripts, experience certificates, domicile certificate and a recent passport-size photograph. Keep scanned copies ready before you start the form.

Can I apply for more than one job on NJP?

Yes. One NJP profile lets you apply for multiple advertised posts, as long as you meet each post's eligibility criteria and submit a separate application for each post before its closing date.

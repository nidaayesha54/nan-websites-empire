Daanish Schools Authority Jobs October 2026: PIU & Secretariat Posts, PPS Scales — Eligibility & Apply

The Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training has opened contract appointments for the Daanish Schools Authority's Project Implementation Unit and Secretariat in Islamabad — senior project management posts on PPS-10 and PPS-11 scales.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

The Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training, Government of Pakistan, has announced a vacancy announcement for the Daanish Schools Authority, inviting applications from eligible Pakistani nationals for positions in the Project Implementation Unit (PIU) and the Daanish Schools Authority Secretariat, Islamabad. All appointments against the advertised PIU and DSA Secretariat positions will be made on a contract basis, subject to the terms and conditions approved by the competent authority.

The Daanish Schools Authority runs the network of Daanish Schools for underprivileged students, and its Project Implementation Unit executes the Authority's development projects — which is why the senior posts here carry heavy project-management requirements and PPS pay scales.

Eligibility Criteria

National Project Director (PPS-11)

  • Qualification: Minimum 16 years of education — a Master's degree in Civil Engineering, Project Management, Management Sciences, Public Administration, Business Administration, Development Studies or an equivalent discipline from an HEC-recognised university.
  • Experience: Minimum twenty (20) years of relevant post-qualification experience, including at least five (05) years of proven experience at senior management level (PPS-10 / BPS-20 or above) in managing large public-sector or development programmes or mega projects.
  • Project leadership record: Must have served as Project Director (PD) or Project Manager (PM) for at least two (02) projects, each costing Rs. 2.00 billion or above — candidates must clearly mention the name, cost, duration and nature of such projects in their application.
  • Age: Minimum 35 years.

Project Director — Gilgit-Baltistan (PPS-10)

  • Qualification: The prescribed qualification for the post as stated in the official vacancy announcement — candidates should hold the relevant advanced degree in engineering or management from an HEC-recognised university.
  • Age: Minimum 35 years.
  • Posting: One post for the Gilgit-Baltistan region, working under the PIU.

How to Apply for Daanish Schools Authority Jobs 2026

The Ministry has issued a formal vacancy announcement with the complete terms. Follow these steps:

1
Read the full vacancy announcement issued by the Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training for the PIU and DSA Secretariat positions.
2
Check the eligibility criteria — qualification, experience, minimum age and regional allocation — against your credentials for the post you want.
3
Prepare a detailed CV documenting your project leadership record, including the name, cost, duration and nature of every major project you have led.
4
Attach attested copies of degrees, experience certificates and CNIC as required by the announcement.
5
Submit your application through the channel and before the closing date stated in the official vacancy announcement.
Tip: For the National Project Director post, the application lives or dies on the documented project record — explicitly list the two (or more) Rs. 2.00 billion-plus projects you served as PD/PM on, with cost, duration and your exact role.

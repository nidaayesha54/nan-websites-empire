299 posts from BS-14 to BS-18 across 7 Punjab departments — key posts, eligibility, online application steps and the 26 October 2026 closing date.

Applications are accepted online only through the official PPSC portal, and the last date to apply is 26 October 2026.

Eligibility Criteria for PPSC Ad 11/2026

Each post in the advertisement carries its own qualification, experience and age requirements, which are spelled out in the official advertisement. Before applying, candidates should check the following carefully for their chosen post:

  • Qualification: The required degree or diploma varies by post — medical posts require the relevant medical qualifications, engineering posts require an engineering degree, accounts posts require commerce qualifications, and so on. The advertisement lists the exact requirement for every post.
  • Experience: Senior posts (BS-17/BS-18) generally require a specified number of years of relevant post-qualification experience. Read the advertisement for the exact figure.
  • Age limit: The age bracket is fixed per post in the advertisement, with the usual general age relaxation applicable as per government rules.
  • Domicile: Punjab domicile is required for these posts, and the quota distribution (open merit, women, minority and other quotas) follows the rules stated in the advertisement.
  • Registration: For medical and professional posts, valid registration with the relevant council or body may be required.

If you do not meet even one eligibility condition, your application can be rejected at the scrutiny stage, so verify every line of the advertisement against your credentials.

How to Apply Online for PPSC Advertisement 11/2026

PPSC accepts applications through its online portal only. Follow these steps:

1
Visit www.ppsc.gop.pk and open the "Advertisements" or "Apply Online" section.
2
Select Advertisement No. 11/2026 and choose the post you are eligible for.
3
Fill the online application form with your personal, educational and experience details exactly as they appear on your documents.
4
Upload a recent photograph and the required documents in the prescribed format and size.
5
Deposit the prescribed application fee through the designated bank channels mentioned in the advertisement.
6
Submit the form before 26 October 2026 and save/print the submitted application for your record.
Important: Applications received after the closing date, or submitted through any mode other than the online portal, are not entertained. For queries, PPSC can be reached on UAN 042-111-988-722.

FPSC Female Lecturer Jobs 2026: BPS-17 Posts in Federal Department of Education — Apply by 17 Oct 2026

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) has announced vacancies for Female Lecturer (BPS-17) posts in the Federal Department of Education (FED). These positions are earmarked for Islamabad Model Colleges for Girls in the federal capital, and they are open to qualified women candidates from across Pakistan. Applications are accepted online through the FPSC portal, and the last date to apply is 17 October 2026.

Teaching posts in the federal education setup are among the most sought-after government jobs for women — they offer a professional teaching environment, federal pay scales, and postings in Islamabad. Because the deadline falls in mid-October, eligible candidates should prepare their documents and submit the online application well before the closing date.

FPSC Female Lecturer Posts — Subject-Wise Breakdown

The vacancies cover the following subjects taught at the college level:

Subject GroupSubjects
Natural SciencesBiology, Chemistry, Physics, Computer Science
MathematicsMathematics
Languages & Social SciencesEnglish, Geography, Pakistan Studies, Psychology, Sociology
Note: The recruitment notice describes multiple vacancies across these subjects rather than a fixed per-subject count. Candidates must hold a Master's degree or Bachelor's degree in the relevant subject they wish to teach. Always confirm the final vacancy position in the official FPSC advertisement before applying.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualification: Master's degree or Bachelor's degree in the relevant subject from an HEC-recognised university.
  • Age limit: 22 to 33 years, including the applicable age relaxation.
  • Gender: These posts are advertised specifically for female candidates.
  • Job responsibilities: Conducting classes in the senior sections of Islamabad Model Colleges for Girls, plus discipline and examination duties.

Candidates must fulfil the domicile, qualification, age and other conditions exactly as stated in the official FPSC advertisement. Applicants who do not meet the requirements — or who submit test or fake applications — may face serious consequences, so apply only if you genuinely qualify.

How to Apply Online at fpsc.gov.pk

FPSC accepts online applications only. Follow these steps:

1
Open the official FPSC website at www.fpsc.gov.pk and go to the online application section.
2
Find the advertisement for Female Lecturer (BPS-17) in the Federal Department of Education and read the full notice carefully.
3
Register or log in, fill in your personal, educational and contact details, and select the subject matching your degree.
4
Upload the required documents in the prescribed format and check the FPSC fee instructions for the current application fee.
5
Review the complete application and submit before 17 October 2026. Save or print the application confirmation for your record.
Important: Successful applicants may edit their online application only once before the closing date to correct an error or omission — so review every entry before final submission. The deadline is 17 October 2026.

Selection Process — What to Expect

After the closing date, FPSC will announce the screening-test schedule for these posts. Candidates who qualify the written test will be shortlisted for the descriptive examination and/or interview, followed by document verification and the final recommendation. Because the competition for federal teaching posts is strong, begin revising your subject knowledge now — the screening test typically covers the subject itself plus general ability. Keep checking the FPSC website for the test schedule and roll number slips after you apply.

Women candidates from every province should note that these are fresh federal vacancies rather than a recycled notice — the subject list, the 17 October deadline and the Islamabad posting all point to a live recruitment round. Prepare your documents now: degree, transcripts, CNIC, domicile and a recent photograph, and double-check that your degree subject exactly matches the subject you select in the online form.

Rescue 1122 KPK 800 Jobs 2026: Applications Closed — ETEA Test & Physical Next

Rescue 1122 KPK's 800 regular posts closed for applications on 15 October 2026 — here is what comes next: the ETEA screening test, physical standards, selection weightage and the interview stage.

✅ Updated October 20, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Emergency Rescue Service (Rescue 1122) announced 800 regular vacancies across three categories — Emergency Medical Technician, Fire Rescuer and Driver — in September 2026. Applications were submitted online through the Educational Testing and Evaluation Agency (ETEA), and the application window closed on 15 October 2026. The window is fully closed: no new applications can be accepted now. If you applied before the deadline, the recruitment now moves to its next stages — the ETEA screening test, the physical test and the interview. This guide explains what comes next, the selection weightage, eligibility criteria and physical standards.

All 800 posts are regular (not contract) positions, divided as follows:

Selection Process and Weightage

The recruitment uses a marks-based selection process. According to the reported breakdown, selection carries 70 marks for educational qualifications, 20 marks for the ETEA test and 10 marks for the interview — a total of 100 marks. Candidates must secure at least 40 percent in the relevant screening/written test and interview stages. Candidates are initially allowed to participate in the physical and screening tests on a provisional basis; the appointing authority verifies eligibility at later stages and may disqualify anyone who fails to meet the prescribed requirements. ETEA also conducts skill-based assessments where needed, including driving tests for driver applicants. Only shortlisted candidates are called for the interview, and no TA/DA is admissible for the physical test, written examination or interview.

Eligibility Criteria for Each Post

  • Emergency Medical Technician (BPS-12): relevant paramedical diploma, nursing qualification or LHV qualification. Candidates with Health Technology or Surgical Technology diplomas are given preference. Age 22–35 years.
  • Fire Rescuer (BPS-11): F.Sc Pre-Engineering or a Diploma of Associate Engineering (DAE). Age 22–35 years.
  • Driver (BPS-06): at least middle pass education, meeting the required physical standards, and holding a valid HTV or LTV driving licence. Fresh driving licence application tokens are not accepted. Age 22–35 years.
  • General conditions: applicants must be Pakistani citizens holding a valid Khyber Pakhtunkhwa domicile, and must be willing to serve in any district of the province. Government and semi-government employees had to apply through the proper channel with an NOC. Age relaxation applies as per government rules. Women and candidates from minority communities are encouraged to apply.

Zone-Wise and Quota Allocation

The 800 posts are distributed among merit, six recruitment zones and reserved quotas for women and minorities, per the official advertisement. Because allocation is zone-based, your domicile district determines which zone’s quota you compete under — check the advertisement’s allocation table for the exact zone-wise breakup for your post.

What Applicants Should Do Now

With applications closed, preparation for the test stages begins. Follow these steps:

1
Watch the ETEA portal for your roll number slip, test schedule and venue. Do not rely on forwarded messages — the official slip is the only confirmed source.
2
Prepare for the ETEA screening test: revise your education-based syllabus (paramedical, engineering or general subjects for your post) plus general knowledge and aptitude sections ETEA typically includes.
3
Start physical training now — practice the 1-mile run against the time standard for your gender so the test day is not a surprise.
4
Keep your documents ready: original CNIC, KP domicile, diplomas and licences (drivers: valid HTV/LTV licence — tokens are not accepted), and an NOC if you are a government employee.
5
Note that participation is provisional until your eligibility is verified — make sure every condition on the advertisement holds true for you before the interview stage.

Rescue 1122 is one of KP’s most competitive emergency services, and with 800 regular posts this is a significant recruitment. Candidates who combine solid written-test preparation with genuine physical readiness will be in the strongest position when the ETEA schedule is announced.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Rescue 1122 KPK applications still open?

No. Online applications through ETEA closed on 15 October 2026. Only candidates who applied before the deadline will proceed to the ETEA screening test, physical test and interview.

When will the Rescue 1122 ETEA test be held?

The ETEA screening test schedule will be announced on the ETEA portal. Candidates should watch the portal for their roll number slip, which carries the confirmed date, time and venue.

What is the selection weightage for Rescue 1122 KPK jobs?

The reported weightage is 70 marks for educational qualifications, 20 marks for the ETEA test and 10 marks for the interview. Candidates must secure at least 40 percent in the screening/written test and interview.

What driving licence is required for Rescue 1122 Driver posts?

Drivers need at least a middle pass education and a valid HTV or LTV driving licence. Fresh driving licence application tokens are not accepted.

Can women apply for Rescue 1122 KPK jobs?

Yes. Women and candidates from minority communities are explicitly encouraged to apply. The reported physical standards for women are a 1-mile run in 9 minutes and a minimum height of 5 ft 2 in — confirm against the official advertisement.

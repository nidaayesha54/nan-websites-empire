The Two Positions on Offer

Data Protection Officer

The Data Protection Officer is a compliance-and-governance role: making sure PSW's systems handle personal and commercial data lawfully and securely. In a single-window environment — where brokers, banks and regulators all submit trade data through one gateway — this officer is the organisation's conscience on data privacy. Typical candidates bring a background in information security, data privacy law or compliance, with privacy or audit certifications a plus.

Manager SQA-I (Software Quality Assurance)

The Manager SQA-I leads software quality assurance: test planning, test-case design, execution and release sign-off for the PSW platform. For a national trade portal — where errors can hold up consignments at ports — the SQA function is mission-critical. Candidates typically hold a computer science or software engineering degree with testing experience, ideally including test automation and QA-team leadership.

About Pakistan Single Window

PSW is a public-sector company built around trade facilitation: instead of submitting paperwork to dozens of agencies, importers and exporters lodge standardised information through a single electronic gateway. It employs domain specialists (customs processes, logistics, trade policy) and technology specialists (software engineering, data governance) — the two advertised posts belong to the second group. Public-sector tech roles like these typically offer competitive compensation, a formal contract, and work on systems used at national scale.

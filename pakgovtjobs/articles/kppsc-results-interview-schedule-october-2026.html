KPPSC Results & Interview Schedule October 2026: Civil Judges DMCs, October–November Interview Programmes

The Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Public Service Commission (KPPSC) has issued a busy set of October 2026 updates: the written examination result for Civil Judges-cum-Judicial Magistrates / Alaqa Qazi (Advertisement No. 03/2026), the DMCs for that examination, the interview programme for October 2026 (with an amendment), the psychological assessment schedule for the Civil Judges posts, the interview programme for November 2026, and DMCs and results for other examinations including PMS Officer and AETO (Adv 02/2025) and Member of Service (Advertisement 07/2024). The commission's website was last updated on 16 October 2026.

For candidates, this cluster of notices means the KPPSC recruitment pipeline is moving fast right now: written results are being converted into interviews within weeks. If your result is on the list, the interview stage — and for judicial posts the psychological assessment — is already approaching.

How to Check Your Result and DMC

Follow these steps on the official website:

1
Open www.kppsc.gov.pk and look for the latest notifications / results section.
2
Download the written examination result for your post (Civil Judges-cum-Judicial Magistrates, Advertisement 03/2026) and find your roll number in the qualifier list.
3
Download your Detailed Marks Certificate (DMC) — even candidates who did not qualify should check it to see where they lost marks.
4
Qualifiers: check the psychological assessment schedule and the interview programme for your date, reporting time and venue.
5
For any correction or query, contact KPPSC at 2-Fort Road, Peshawar Cantt (091-9214131, 9213563, 9213750, 9212897).
Important: Keep both the result notification and your DMC saved and printed. You will need them at the interview stage along with your original documents.

What Happens After Qualifying — Interview and Assessment

For the Civil Judges posts, qualifiers now face the psychological assessment followed by the interview as per the published programmes. The commission has issued the October 2026 interview programme with an amendment and the November 2026 programme, which means the interview window is actively running. Candidates should prepare the standard judicial-service material — current affairs, constitutional basics, the legal framework of the judiciary, and their own optional-paper strengths — plus the practical ability to discuss legal reasoning calmly and precisely.

For other posts in the interview programmes, the drill is the same: verify your date in the official schedule, assemble your original documents (degrees, transcripts, domicile, CNIC, experience certificates where applicable), and arrive well before the reporting time. Commissions run interviews on tight timetables; latecomers are not entertained.

Learning From the DMCs

The publication of DMCs for both qualifiers and failed candidates is one of the most useful things a commission does — it turns a rejection into a study plan. Candidates who did not qualify should examine exactly which papers dragged them down: was it the essay, the precis, or a weak optional? The DMC data for the Civil Judges examination, and for the PMS Officer and AETO examinations under Advertisement 02/2025, gives aspirants concrete numbers to plan next year's preparation around. Candidates who treat the DMC as feedback rather than a verdict come back measurably stronger.

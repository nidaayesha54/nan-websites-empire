NBP Digital Banking Officer Jobs 2026 — Apply by 30 Oct 2026

The National Bank of Pakistan is hiring two Digital Banking Officers (OG-II/OG-I) — BI Development and Digital Strategy — in Karachi, Lahore and other major cities. Recruitment is through Sidat Hyder on 3-year renewable contracts.

✅ Updated October 21, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

The National Bank of Pakistan (NBP), the country's premier government-owned commercial bank, is hiring Digital Banking Officers for two specialist roles — Digital Banking Officer – BI Development and Digital Banking Officer – Digital Strategy — in the OG-II/OG-I officer grades. Both posts are part of NBP's current 10-position recruitment batch, with placements in Karachi, Lahore and other major cities. The recruitment is being managed through Sidat Hyder, and applications must be submitted by 30 October 2026.

The Two Posts at a Glance

Digital Banking Officer – BI Development is the data role: business intelligence development, analytics dashboards and data pipelines. Digital Banking Officer – Digital Strategy is the product side: shaping digital products and steering the bank's digital transformation. Both are offered in the OG-II/OG-I officer grades — entry-to-mid management level.

The key structural detail is the contract: these are 3-year renewable contract positions, not permanent NBP appointments. The two roles are part of a wider 10-position hiring batch, indicating NBP is building out its digital team this cycle.

Eligibility Criteria

Digital Banking Officer – BI Development

  • Education: Bachelor's in Computer Science, IT or Data Science from an HEC-recognized university. Master's or professional certifications preferred.
  • Experience: 2–3 years in Business Intelligence and Data Analytics — specifically Power BI, Tableau and SQL.
  • Grade: OG-II/OG-I.

Digital Banking Officer – Digital Strategy

  • Education: Bachelor's in Business Administration, Finance or Marketing (HEC-recognized). MBA preferred.
  • Experience: 2–3 years in digital strategy, product management or digital transformation — with real shipped digital products or change initiatives behind you.
  • Grade: OG-II/OG-I.

Placements are in Karachi, Lahore and other major cities — be ready to state a location preference during the process.

Salary and Contract Terms

The advertisement describes pay as market-competitive without publishing a figure — exact numbers are presumably shared with shortlisted candidates. These are 3-year renewable contracts; confirm the renewal terms in writing at the offer stage.

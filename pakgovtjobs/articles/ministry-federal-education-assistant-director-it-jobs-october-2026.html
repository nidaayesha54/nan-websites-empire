Federal Education Ministry AD (IT) Jobs 2026 — Apply by 23 Oct

The Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training is hiring an Assistant Director (IT) through the ETC/HEC portal — 16-year CS/IT degree and 5 years of experience required, test fee Rs. 1,200, deadline 23 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 21, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

The Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training is recruiting an Assistant Director (IT) through the ETC/HEC portal (etc.hec.gov.pk). The post requires a minimum of 16 years of education in Computer Science, IT or Software Engineering and 5 years of proven relevant experience across IT systems, networks, information security, databases, MIS/ERP and technical support. Applications close at 4:00 PM on 23 October 2026; the test fee is Rs. 1,200.

What the Post Requires

The Assistant Director (IT) is a senior technical role at ministry level. Applicants need a minimum of 16 years of education — a Master's or BS (Hons) equivalent — in Computer Science, IT, Software Engineering or an equivalent discipline from an HEC-recognized university. Foreign-degree holders should secure HEC equivalence early.

Five Years of Proven Experience

The experience bar is the hardest part of this post: a minimum of 5 years of relevant, proven experience across several of these domains:

  • IT systems — administration of enterprise IT environments.
  • Networks — design, deployment and troubleshooting.
  • Information security — policies, controls and incident handling.
  • Databases — administration and data integrity.
  • MIS/ERP systems — implementations and support.
  • Technical support — structured support at scale.

Only post-qualification relevant experience counts — years worked before completing the degree do not qualify. Age, qualification and experience are judged on the closing date, 23 October 2026.

The Age Limit Needs a Second Look

The source reports an age limit of 25 years — for a post requiring a 16-year degree plus 5 years of proven experience. That combination is contradictory, which suggests a reporting error (for instance, a minimum age misreported as the maximum). This is exactly why the honesty flag sits at the top: do not self-disqualify — or apply on a false assumption — without checking the official advertisement on the ETC/HEC portal.

How to Apply on the ETC/HEC Portal

Applications are accepted online only at etc.hec.gov.pk:

1
Go to etc.hec.gov.pk and register for a candidate account, or log in.
2
Fill the online application form for the Assistant Director (IT) post.
3
Upload: degrees, experience certificates, professional certifications, CNIC, a recent photograph and an NOC (government employees).
4
Pay the Rs. 1,200 test fee via 1Link/1Bill using the consumer number generated on the portal.
5
Click "Verify Online Payment", then press SUBMIT — SAVE-mode or incomplete applications are rejected outright.
6
Finish before 4:00 PM on 23 October 2026 — the portal stops accepting applications after the closing time.

Rules That Get Applications Rejected

  • Incomplete applications are rejected.
  • SAVE mode is not submission — a saved-but-unsubmitted form counts as no application.
  • Post-qualification experience only — pre-degree experience does not count toward the 5 years.
  • Closing-date judgment — age, qualification and experience are measured as of 23 October 2026.
  • Shortlisting only — only shortlisted candidates are called; no TA/DA is admissible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply?

Through the ETC/HEC portal before 4:00 PM on 23 October 2026. Age, qualification and experience are judged on this closing date.

What is the ETC test fee for the Assistant Director (IT) post?

Rs. 1,200 via 1Link/1Bill using the consumer number generated on the portal. Click "Verify Online Payment" after paying, then press SUBMIT.

Is the 25-year age limit for the AD (IT) post correct?

That is what the source reports, but it is unusually low for a 5-year-experience post. Verify the age limit on the ETC/HEC portal advertisement before applying.

Does all my work experience count for the AD (IT) post?

No — only relevant experience gained after the prescribed qualification counts, judged on the closing date.

Will every applicant be called for the test?

No — only shortlisted candidates are called; no TA/DA is admissible. Incomplete or SAVE-mode applications are rejected.

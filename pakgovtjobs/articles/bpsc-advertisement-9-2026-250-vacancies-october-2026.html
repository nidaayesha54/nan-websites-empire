BPSC Advertisement 9/2026: 250+ Balochistan Government Jobs — Apply Online by 11 November 2026

The Balochistan Public Service Commission (BPSC) has announced Consolidated Advertisement No. 9/2026, published on 9 October 2026, inviting applications for more than 250 vacancies in various departments of the Government of Balochistan. This is one of the largest Balochistan commission recruitments of the year, and it is open only to candidates holding a local domicile of Balochistan. Applications are accepted online through the BPSC portal at bpsc.gob.pk, and the last date to apply is 11 November 2026.

For Balochistan-domicile candidates — whether you are a fresh graduate, a medical professional, an engineer or an experienced civil servant — an advertisement of this size is the main gateway into the provincial government. Because more than 250 seats are spread across departments, the competition is real but the opportunity is wider than any single-department posting.

Who Can Apply

The single most important condition is domicile: you must hold a local/domicile certificate of Balochistan to be eligible. Beyond that, each post carries its own qualification and age conditions, which BPSC prints in the detailed advertisement. Recent BPSC consolidated advertisements have covered a broad mix of professions — lecturers and professors, medical officers and specialists, engineers, law officers, agricultural officers and administrative staff — so candidates from many educational backgrounds should check whether a post matches their degree.

Age is counted on the closing date of the advertisement (11 November 2026), and the Government of Balochistan grants its notified age relaxation to eligible categories. Government servants with regular service should check the relaxation rules for in-service candidates in the official notice.

How the Balochistan Zonal Quota Works

BPSC recruitment runs on a zonal quota system under the Balochistan Recruitment Policy. Recent advertisements have divided seats across zones such as Merit (All Balochistan), Kalat, Zhob, Nasirabad, Mekran, Quetta, Sibi and Quetta City, with reserved shares for women, minorities and persons with disabilities on many posts. Some posts are open merit, others are zoned — and the gender line matters too: some posts are for men only, some for women only, and most are open to both.

How to Apply Online

Follow these steps on the BPSC portal:

1
Open the BPSC website at www.bpsc.gob.pk and create or log in to your candidate profile.
2
Find Advertisement No. 9/2026 in the advertisements section and select the post that matches your qualification, domicile zone and quota.
3
Fill the online form with your personal details, educational record, experience and domicile exactly as they appear on your documents.
4
Submit before 11 November 2026. Do not wait for the last week — the portal slows down under load near every major closing date.
5
Save your application. Keep a screenshot or printout of the submitted form and any transaction receipt for your record.
Do not pay anyone. BPSC recruitment is merit-based. Nobody can sell you a seat, and anyone offering one for money is running a scam.

What Happens After You Apply

After the closing date, BPSC screens the applications and announces written test dates and syllabi on its website. Candidates who clear the screening/written stage are called for interviews, and the final merit is drawn from the combined result. Keep your CNIC, domicile, educational certificates and experience letters ready — BPSC verifies originals at the interview stage, and any mismatch with the online form can disqualify a candidate.

Because this advertisement closes on 11 November 2026, expect test schedules to appear on the BPSC website in the weeks that follow. Check the commission's site regularly through November and December rather than relying on social media dates.

Why This Advertisement Matters

Balochistan's commission advertisements arrive less frequently and in smaller numbers than Punjab's, which makes a 250+ vacancy advertisement a genuine event for the province's job seekers. Whether you are in Quetta, Turbat, Khuzdar, Hub, Loralai or Sibi, this is the same level playing field: one portal, one closing date, merit-based selection. Apply early, read the fine print, and prepare for the test from day one — the candidates who start preparing while the advertisement is still open are the ones who clear the written stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is BPSC Advertisement 9/2026?

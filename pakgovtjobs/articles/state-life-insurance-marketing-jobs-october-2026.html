State Life Insurance Marketing Jobs 2026 — Apply by 2 Nov

State Life Insurance Corporation of Pakistan is hiring on contract for its Marketing Division at Karachi — Manager, Assistant Manager and Shariah Advisor posts for graduates and master's holders aged 18–40. Apply via the official careers page by 2 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 22, 2026

The State Life Insurance Corporation of Pakistan — the country's public-sector life insurer — has announced contract-based positions in its Marketing Division at Karachi: Manager, Manager Audit & Legal Executive, Assistant Manager Marketing Strategist, Assistant Manager Data Analyst, Assistant Manager Operations & Compliance Executive and Shariah Advisor. Eligibility is reported as Graduation/Master's with 1–5 years of experience, age 18–40, and salary PKR 50,000–150,000. Apply with the prescribed application form on the official State Life careers page by 2 November 2026.

The Advertised Positions

All posts sit in the Marketing Division at Karachi and are reported as contract-based:

Manager

The senior slot — a Manager owning campaigns, distribution targets and team performance for the division's product lines.

Manager Audit & Legal Executive

A dual-hatted role combining audit (reviewing marketing processes and spend for compliance) and legal executive work. Suits candidates with a law or finance/audit background who understand marketing operations.

Assistant Manager Marketing Strategist

The strategy seat — planning product positioning and campaign calendars. Marketing or business graduates with brand/campaign experience are the natural fit.

Assistant Manager Data Analyst

The data seat — analysing sales funnels and campaign performance. Expect a statistics, analytics or computer science background with hands-on BI or dashboard work.

Assistant Manager Operations & Compliance Executive

The operations-and-compliance seat — keeping marketing workflows running inside regulatory boundaries. Candidates with operations or compliance experience should highlight it.

Shariah Advisor

The Shariah advisory seat — reviewing products and marketing material for Shariah compliance. This calls for recognised Islamic finance / Shariah qualifications.

Eligibility Snapshot

  • Education: Graduation or Master's degree (as reported — the official ad will specify per-post requirements).
  • Experience: 1–5 years of relevant experience, depending on the post.
  • Age: 18–40 years (as reported).
  • Salary: PKR 50,000–150,000 reported — varying by post and seniority.
  • Contract nature: the reported positions are contract-based, not permanent government posts — confirm contract length and renewal terms on the official ad.

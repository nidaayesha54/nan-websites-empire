PPSC Veterinary Officer & Senior Veterinary Officer Jobs 2026: 101 Posts, Cases, Last Date 26 Oct 2026

101 veterinary posts (cases 5K2026 & 4K2026) in the Livestock and Dairy Development Department — eligibility, quotas, fee and the 26 October 2026 closing date.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

The Punjab Public Service Commission has opened one of the largest veterinary recruitment drives of the year under Advertisement No. 11/2026: 101 posts in the Livestock and Dairy Development Department, Punjab. The drive is split into two separate cases — Case No. 5K2026 for Veterinary Officer (BS-17) with 40 posts, and Case No. 4K2026 for Senior Veterinary Officer (BS-18) with 61 posts. Both cases were advertised on 9 October 2026 and both close on 26 October 2026.

The two cases have different eligibility rules, quotas and fees — so read the right case number before applying. Both appointments are on a five-year contract, and applications are online only via the PPSC portal.

Posts and Titles Covered

Case 5K2026 covers Veterinary Officer, Semen Distribution Officer and Instructor, while case 4K2026 covers senior titles such as Assistant Director (HQR), Deputy Director Livestock and Senior Veterinary Officer (Animal Health). Check your own title on the PPSC case sheet, as experience requirements differ between titles.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Veterinary Officer (BS-17): DVM from a recognized institution with valid Pakistan Veterinary Medical Council (PVMC) registration.
  • Senior Veterinary Officer (BS-18): DVM plus higher qualification (M.Phil / M.Sc / Ph.D) and prescribed experience, with valid PVMC registration.
  • Domicile: Punjab domicile is required for these posts, with the advertised quotas for women, minorities and special persons applied as per the case sheets.
  • Age limit: The age bracket is fixed per case in the advertisement, with the usual general age relaxation applicable as per government rules. Check your case sheet for the exact range.
  • Registration: Valid PVMC registration is mandatory — applications without it can be rejected at the scrutiny stage.

How to Apply Online for PPSC Veterinary Posts

PPSC accepts applications through its online portal only. The two cases have separate forms and fees:

1
Visit www.ppsc.gop.pk and open the Apply Online section.
2
Select the correct case — 5K2026 for Veterinary Officer or 4K2026 for Senior Veterinary Officer.
3
Fill the online form with personal, educational and experience details exactly as they appear on your documents.
4
Upload a recent photograph and the required documents in the prescribed format and size.
5
Deposit the Rs 600 application fee for each case you apply for, through the designated bank channels.
6
Submit the form before 26 October 2026 and save a print of the submitted application.
Important: The cases are independent — eligible for both means a separate application and separate Rs 600 fee for each. Late or non-online applications are not entertained.

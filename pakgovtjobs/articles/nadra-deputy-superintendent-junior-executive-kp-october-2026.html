NADRA Jobs 2026: Deputy Superintendent & Junior Executive (Data Entry Operator) — KP Districts, Last Date 18 Oct 2026

Contract-based NADRA recruitment for Deputy Superintendent and Junior Executive (Data Entry Operator) posts in North Waziristan, South Waziristan, Tank, Lakki Marwat, Bannu and Dera Ismail Khan — apply online before 18 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

The National Database & Registration Authority (NADRA), working under the Ministry of Interior & Narcotics Control, Government of Pakistan, has announced contract-based recruitment for the posts of Deputy Superintendent and Junior Executive (Data Entry Operator — Male/Female). This hiring round is specifically for notified districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and every vacancy is open only to citizens of the district it is notified for. Each Junior Executive post is advertised separately for male and female candidates, so both men and women of the eligible districts have a clear route to apply.

All applications must be submitted online through the NADRA EPR system, and the deadline for applications is 18 October 2026.

NADRA Jobs 2026 — Post Details

PostQualificationMaximum Age
Deputy SuperintendentGraduation (14 years of education)40 years
Junior Executive (Data Entry Operator — Male)Intermediate or equivalent25 years
Junior Executive (Data Entry Operator — Female)Intermediate or equivalent25 years
Note: Recruitment is on a contract basis and is district-specific. A separate vacancy exists for male and female Junior Executives. Only citizens of the notified districts are eligible for the post advertised in their district.

Eligible Districts for These NADRA Posts

Vacancies have been notified for the following districts of Khyber Pakhtunkhwa:

  • North Waziristan
  • South Waziristan (Lower)
  • South Waziristan (Upper)
  • Tank
  • Lakki Marwat
  • Bannu
  • Dera Ismail Khan

Each post is tied to one district — confirm you hold that district's domicile before applying.

Eligibility Criteria

The headline criteria for each post are the qualification and the maximum age given above. Beyond that, NADRA has set the following general conditions for the recruitment process:

  • Only candidates meeting the prescribed qualification and age limit for the post will be shortlisted.
  • Selected candidates must provide medical fitness and character certificates at the time of joining.
  • Attested educational documents from the Higher Education Commission (HEC) or the respective regulatory body must be provided at the time of joining.
  • If a selected candidate declines the offer, it may be extended to a reserve candidate.
  • No TA/DA is admissible for the test or interview.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones and smart watches are not allowed during the test/interview.
  • Females, minorities, transgender persons and differently-abled candidates are encouraged to apply.

Deputy Superintendent is the senior post in this announcement, while the Junior Executive (Data Entry Operator) posts are open to Intermediate pass candidates.

How to Apply Online for NADRA Jobs 2026

Applications are accepted through the NADRA EPR online system only. Follow these steps:

1
Open the NADRA online recruitment portal and select the EPR application option for the advertised posts.
2
Choose the post you are eligible for — Deputy Superintendent or Junior Executive (Data Entry Operator) — and the notified district you belong to.
3
Fill in your personal, educational and contact details carefully, making sure the district and post match your eligibility.
4
Upload your photograph and required documents in the prescribed format.
5
Review the complete application and submit before 18 October 2026. Save the application confirmation for your record.
Important: The deadline is 18 October 2026. Incomplete or late applications will not be entertained. Check the official NADRA careers portal for the latest instructions before applying.

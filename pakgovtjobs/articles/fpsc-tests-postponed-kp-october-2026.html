FPSC Tests Postponed in KP October 2026 — Revised Schedule & What Candidates Must Do

Public notice dated 11 Oct 2026: Phase-III/2026 tests in Peshawar & Dera Ismail Khan postponed — affected centres, unaffected schedule and candidate action plan.

✅ Updated October 13, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

The Federal Public Service Commission has issued a public notice dated 11 October 2026 postponing the General Recruitment Tests Phase-III/2026 in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The tests were scheduled to be held at centres in Peshawar and Dera Ismail Khan from 12 to 19 October 2026. According to the notice, the postponement was made in view of the prevailing situation and on the request of candidates and invigilating staff.

What the FPSC Public Notice Says

The Commission's notice of 11 October 2026 states that the General Recruitment screening and written tests of Phase-III/2026, planned for the KP centres of Peshawar and Dera Ismail Khan between 12 and 19 October 2026, stand postponed until further orders. The decision responds to the prevailing situation in the province and to requests received from candidates and the staff deputed for invigilation duties.

Candidates appearing at centres outside Khyber Pakhtunkhwa should continue their preparation and appear on their announced dates with their admission certificates.

What KP Candidates Must Do Now

1
Do not travel to the Peshawar or Dera Ismail Khan centres on the postponed dates. The tests will not be conducted there during 12–19 October 2026.
2
Keep preparing. A postponement is not a cancellation — the syllabus and pattern remain the same, and extra days are an advantage for revision.
3
Monitor fpsc.gov.pk daily. The Commission will issue the revised schedule on its official website. Do not rely on social media rumours.
4
Keep your admission documents safe. Your existing admission certificate and CNIC will be needed when the revised schedule is announced; fresh instructions will come from FPSC if re-issuance is required.
5
Verify through official channels only. Call the FPSC helpline or check the website if any message about new dates reaches you through unofficial sources.

How FPSC Announces the Revised Schedule

Historically, FPSC publishes revised test schedules as a public notice on fpsc.gov.pk and updates the candidate dashboard where admission certificates are downloaded. The notice of 11 October 2026 confirms that a revised schedule will be issued, but no new dates have been fixed yet. Candidates should check the "News / Press Releases" section of the FPSC website regularly rather than waiting for SMS or email alerts, which can be delayed.

Bottom line: If your test centre is Peshawar or Dera Ismail Khan, your 12–19 October 2026 test is postponed. If your centre is anywhere else, your test goes ahead as planned. In both cases, fpsc.gov.pk is the only authoritative source for updates.

Understanding FPSC Test Phases

After the closing date of each consolidated advertisement, FPSC announces its test programme in phases. A phase notice lists which posts' tests will be held on which dates and at which centres across Pakistan. Phase-III/2026 is simply the third such testing phase of the year. Each phase notice is accompanied by admission certificates uploaded to candidates' dashboards on fpsc.gov.pk, which serve as the roll number slip and must be presented at the centre along with the original CNIC.

Postponements of individual centres, while uncommon, are handled the same way each time: the affected tests are rescheduled through a fresh public notice, and candidates are informed through the official website. No candidate loses their candidature because of a postponement — the application remains valid for the revised date.

Use the Extra Days Wisely: A Revision Plan

Candidates in Peshawar and Dera Ismail Khan now have unexpected extra preparation time. Here is how to use it well:

  • Revisit weak areas: Identify the 2–3 topics where you lose the most marks in practice tests and drill them daily.
  • Practice full-length papers: Attempt at least one timed 100-question mock every two days to build speed and accuracy.
  • Revise current affairs: Update your notes with the latest national developments, since the general knowledge portion rewards freshness.
  • Keep documents ready: Organise your CNIC, admission certificate, photographs and fee receipt in one folder so the revised date never catches you unprepared.
  • Stay calm and consistent: A steady daily routine beats last-minute cramming; protect your sleep in the final week before the new date.

SECP Legal Jobs 2026 — 7 Prosecution & Litigation Posts (Apply 6 Nov)

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) is hiring seven senior legal officers for its Prosecution & Civil Litigation Department — Director to Deputy Director — at Karachi, Islamabad and Lahore. Apply online at secp.gov.pk within 15 days of the 22 October 2026 ad.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » Pakistan Government Jobs » Articles

The Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) — the country's apex regulator for corporate entities and capital markets — has advertised seven senior legal positions in its Prosecution & Civil Litigation Department: Director, Additional Director, Joint Director and Deputy Director, with duty stations at Karachi, Islamabad and Lahore. Published on 22 October 2026, the ad invites applications within 15 days — a calculated closing date of 6 November 2026 (verify on the SECP careers portal). Appointments are contractual on open merit with an initial three-year term.

The Four Designations on Offer

Director (Karachi) — 1 Post

The Director leads the department's prosecution and civil litigation work. Requires an LLB or LLM, 15 years of post-qualification experience and 13 years of active High Court advocacy (max age 50). Preference for Bar-at-Law/LLM holders with a record of independently handling corporate prosecution and white-collar crime cases.

Additional Director (Islamabad) — 1 Post

Requires an LLB or LLM, 10 years of High Court advocacy and 12 years of post-qualification experience (maximum age 45) — leading prosecution matters, white-collar criminal prosecution, and regulatory legal advisory.

Joint Director (Islamabad) — 2 Posts

Requires an LLB or LLM, 8 years of High Court advocacy and 10 years of post-qualification experience (maximum age 45) across corporate and civil litigation, criminal prosecution and regulatory advisory.

Deputy Director (Islamabad & Lahore) — 3 Posts

Two posts at Islamabad, one at Lahore. Requires an LLB or LLM, 4 years of High Court advocacy and 6 years of post-qualification experience (maximum age 40), with a special relaxation: 3+ years with a regulator earns five years of age relaxation.

Employment Terms and Contract Duration

SECP will appoint on a contractual basis with an initial three-year term, extendable on need and performance. The ad states the Commission may consider regularization after three years per its criteria — not automatic. Selection is on open merit through a multi-stage process, and SECP may cancel the recruitment at any stage. Applications are encouraged from female candidates, minorities, persons with special abilities and candidates from Balochistan, former FATA, GB and AJK. No fee is mentioned; use only the official portal.

NADRA Jobs 2026: Senior Leadership Posts — Head of Internal Audit &amp; Deputy Director Governance, Risk &amp; Compliance, Last Date 25 Oct 2026

The National Database and Registration Authority (NADRA), working under the Ministry of Interior, has announced senior-level openings in mid-October 2026: Head of Internal Audit and Deputy Director Governance, Risk & Compliance (Lahore). Applications are online only at careers.nadra.gov.pk and the last date is 25 October 2026.

The Head of Internal Audit will lead NADRA’s full audit function — financial, operational, IT, forensic and compliance audits — while the Deputy Director (GRC) strengthens governance and risk frameworks at Lahore. Seats are few (2–3 senior posts), so competition will be sharp.

NADRA Senior Leadership Jobs 2026 — Post-Wise Details

PostStationRequirements & Scope
Head of Internal AuditNADRA HeadquartersLeads financial, operational, IT, forensic and compliance audits; prepares the risk-based annual audit plan; reports to the Audit Committee; 6-month extendable probation
Deputy Director Governance, Risk & ComplianceLahore4-year Bachelor’s (CS/IT/Cyber Security/Information Security/Data Science/AI) from an HEC-recognized university; 6–10 years post-graduation experience, including 3+ years in governance, risk and compliance
Note: These are a small number of senior posts at leadership level. The detailed terms, responsibilities and any quota allocations are set out in the official NADRA advertisement — verify them at careers.nadra.gov.pk before applying.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nationality: Pakistani citizens only.
  • Education (GRC): 4-year Bachelor’s (CS/IT/Cyber Security/Info Sec/Data Science/AI) from an HEC-recognized university.
  • Experience: 6–10 years post-graduation, including at least 3 years in governance, risk and compliance roles.
  • Preferred certifications: ISO-27001, CISM, CISA, CISSP; working knowledge of ISO 27001/27701/22301, NIST, CIS Controls, SOAR and EPP/EDR.
  • Age limit: Maximum 37 years on the closing date, inclusive of the 5-year general relaxation.
  • Terms: 6-month probation, extendable by the management.

For the audit head role, leadership of a full audit function — including IT and forensic audit — is decisive. For the GRC role, CISA, CISM, CISSP or ISO-27001 certification strengthens the application.

Selection Process & Important Instructions

Only candidates meeting the education, experience and age conditions are contacted. Serving government employees must produce an NOC at the interview; no TA/DA is admissible, and late applications are not entertained.

NADRA encourages applications from females, minorities, transgender persons and differently-abled candidates. Apply only through the official portal — never pay anyone claiming to secure a seat.

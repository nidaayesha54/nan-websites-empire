FBR Jobs 2026: Inland Revenue Field Formations — 57 Posts via National Job Portal, Last Date 3 Nov 2026

The Federal Board of Revenue (FBR), Government of Pakistan, has announced job openings for staff in its Inland Revenue field formations. The advertisement — published in the national press in mid-October 2026 — invites applications for a range of posts, from Assistant, Stenotypist and Data Entry Operator down to UDC, LDC, Driver, Naib Qasid and Chowkidar. Interested candidates can apply online at the National Job Portal (njp.gov.pk) before the closing date of 3 November 2026.

FBR field-formation jobs are a dependable route into the federal government for candidates with qualifications from primary to graduation — clerical and support posts in particular attract large numbers of applicants, so preparing a complete application early is essential.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nationality: Candidates must be citizens of Pakistan.
  • Qualification: As per the post table above — primary pass for support staff, Matric for LDC, Intermediate for UDC/Stenotypist, and graduation or Bachelor's for Assistant and Data Entry Operator.
  • Age limit: Per the federal government's recruitment rules as stated in the official advertisement — check the notice for the exact age bracket and applicable relaxation.
  • Employment type: The advertisement indicates the posts may be offered on a contract basis.

Candidates applying for skill-based posts should make sure they genuinely meet the typing or shorthand standard, because the skill test is qualifying — failing it ends the application even with a strong written score.

How to Apply Online via the National Job Portal

Applications are submitted online through the National Job Portal. Follow these steps:

1
Open the National Job Portal at njp.gov.pk and create an account or log in to your existing profile.
2
Search for the FBR Inland Revenue field formation advertisement and open the post matching your qualification.
3
Complete the online application form with your personal, educational and experience details.
4
Upload your photograph and documents in the prescribed format, and note the advertisement's instructions on delivering any required hard copy to FBR House, Constitution Avenue, Islamabad if asked.
5
Submit the application before 3 November 2026 and keep a copy of the confirmation.
Important: The closing date is 3 November 2026. The full vacancy notice is also available on the FBR website at www.fbr.gov.pk — always verify the advertisement there before applying or paying any fee.

Selection Process

FBR recruitment through the National Job Portal typically follows a screening test, a skill test for clerical posts (typing/shorthand), document verification and a final interview. Roll number slips and test schedules are issued online, so check the portal regularly after applying — and beware of fake recruiters asking for money: FBR never charges candidates outside the official process.

Because the clerical and support posts attract large applicant pools, the screening test usually decides the merit. Revise basic English, arithmetic, general knowledge and everyday science from Matric-level material, and — if you are applying for LDC or Stenotypist — practise your typing and shorthand daily so the skill test does not become the stage that ends your run.

ZTBL Jobs 2026 — Risk & Branch Manager Posts (Apply 25 Oct)

Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited (ZTBL) is hiring risk management specialists and a Branch Manager at Islamabad — three risk posts plus one branch leadership post, all closing 25 October 2026. Apply online only at ztbl.com.pk/jobs.

Zarai Taraqiati Bank Limited (ZTBL) — Pakistan's premier agricultural development bank, owned by the Government of Pakistan — has advertised four specialist posts in October 2026: three risk management positions and one Branch Manager post. The risk management round opened on 11 October 2026, and both listings report the same closing date of 25 October 2026 — which is tomorrow, so eligible bankers should apply today rather than waiting for the last day. Applications are accepted online only through the ZTBL careers page.

The Three Risk Management Posts

Manager IFRS-9 & Risk Analytics (AVP-I/AVP) — 1 Post

The senior post in this round. It needs a bachelor's degree from an HEC-recognized institution, with CA or ICMA (partly qualified) candidates also eligible, plus eight years of experience in a bank or financial institution. The role covers IFRS-9 expected-credit-loss modelling and risk analytics for the bank's agricultural lending portfolio. Maximum age is 45 years on the closing date.

Environment Risk Officer (OG-II) — 1 Post

This post handles environmental and climate risk in ZTBL's lending book — an increasingly important area for agricultural finance, where floods, drought and changing weather patterns directly affect borrowers. It requires a bachelor's degree and relevant banking or risk experience. Maximum age is 35 years on the closing date.

Market & Liquidity Risk Analyst (OG-II) — 1 Post

The analyst post requires a bachelor's degree and three years of relevant experience in a bank or financial institution, including at least one year in market risk and liquidity risk management. The role monitors the bank's market exposures and liquidity position. Maximum age is 35 years on the closing date.

Branch Manager (OG-I/AVP-I/AVP) — 1 Post

ZTBL is also hiring a Branch Manager in a separate listing with the same 25 October closing date. Candidates need a minimum bachelor degree from an HEC-recognized institute or university and at least 5 years of banking experience, including at least 1 year as a Branch Manager. Each candidate must name three cities or towns for placement, one of which must be a place where they were posted in a previous assignment. The bank expects deposit mobilization, agricultural credit expansion, recovery of regular and non-performing loans, and supervision of branch services and operations, along with EKYC and regulatory compliance. Maximum age is 45 years on the closing date. Female candidates and persons with disabilities are encouraged to apply.

Who Cannot Apply

The Branch Manager listing states that dual nationality holders, applicants convicted by any court of law, and applicants dismissed, removed or terminated by a previous employer on disciplinary grounds are not eligible. Only shortlisted candidates will be called for interview, and no TA/DA is admissible. The bank reserves the right to accept or reject any application without assigning a reason.

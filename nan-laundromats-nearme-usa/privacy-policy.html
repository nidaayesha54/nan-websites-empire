Privacy Policy

Laundromats Near Me USA is operated by NAN Marketing Agency (NAN Studio). This policy explains what information is (and is not) collected when you use this site.

Information we collect

The map on this site runs in your browser using open map tiles and data from OpenStreetMap. If you tap "Use my location", your browser's geolocation is used only on your device to sort listings by distance — your coordinates are never sent to us, stored by us, or shared with anyone. City searches are looked up through the public Nominatim/OpenStreetMap geocoding service, which receives only the text you type.

We do not require accounts and do not collect names, email addresses, or payment details unless you choose to contact us (see below).

Cookies and advertising

This site displays ads served by Adsterra and its partners. These third parties may set cookies or use similar technologies to measure ad performance and show relevant advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings; blocking cookies may affect some ad features but will not prevent you from using this site's tools.

Third-party services

  • Advertising: Adsterra (ad delivery and measurement).
  • Libraries/content networks: map tiles from OpenStreetMap and JavaScript libraries from public CDNs, which may log standard technical data (such as IP addresses) per their own policies.

Contact data

If you email us, we receive your message and email address and use them only to respond. We do not sell or share contact data with third parties.

Children

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

We may update this policy; the latest version is always posted on this page.

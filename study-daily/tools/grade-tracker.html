How grade projection works

Your current grade is the weighted average of everything graded so far: each score counts in proportion to its weight. The "what you need" section solves backwards from your target — if you want a 90% and 30% of the grade is still ungraded, it tells you exactly what average you need on that remaining work.

Tips

  • Weights should add up to 100% — check your syllabus.
  • Dropped lowest scores? Just leave that assignment out of the table.
  • Use the projection to decide where to spend your study hours — a heavy final deserves the most prep. See our exam prep guide.

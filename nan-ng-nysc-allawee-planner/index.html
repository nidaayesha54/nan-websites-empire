What Corps Members Earn in 2026

The Federal Government pays every corps member ₦77,000 per month, typically between the 25th and 30th, with the first payment arriving at the end of orientation camp. Some states add their own allowance — Lagos (₦15,000–₦25,000 depending on your PPA), Rivers (₦15,000), Akwa Ibom (₦10,000), Delta (₦10,000) and Enugu (₦3,800) are the best-known payers — and many places of primary assignment (PPAs) add meal or transport money.

Worked Example

Federal ₦77,000 + Lagos top-up ₦20,000 = ₦97,000/month. With food ₦27,500, transport ₦10,000, data ₦5,000, rent ₦12,500 and misc ₦6,500, spending totals ₦61,500 — leaving ₦35,500 to save, or ₦426,000 across the service year.

A cheaper deployment state can beat a high top-up: a corper in a northern state on ₦77,000 alone can save more than one in Lagos on ₦97,000, because rent and food cost far less. Compare cost of living, not just allawee.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the ₦77,000 NYSC allowance paid every month on time?

Generally yes, between the 25th and 30th. The first payment usually arrives at the end of orientation camp. Delays of 1–2 weeks happen occasionally — do not budget assuming exact dates.

Do corps members pay income tax on the allawee?

Effectively no. ₦77,000 a month is ₦924,000 a year; after pension and NHF deductions, chargeable income falls below the ₦800,000 tax-free threshold under the Nigeria Tax Act 2025. NYSC stipends are effectively tax-free.

Do state allowances actually get paid?

Mixed. Lagos, Rivers, Oyo and Enugu pay reliably; some states announce increases that never materialise. Always treat state allowance as a bonus, not your foundation.

How much can I realistically save during NYSC?

Corps members who track spending report saving an extra ₦8,000–₦15,000 a month. A realistic 12-month goal is ₦100,000–₦150,000, locked away in a separate savings account the day the allawee arrives.

Can I work a side job during NYSC?

Officially no, but in practice many corps members do remote freelance work, run small businesses, or tutor — as long as it does not clash with PPA attendance and CDS. Enforcement is minimal.

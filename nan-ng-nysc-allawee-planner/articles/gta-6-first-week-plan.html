GTA 6 First Week: What to Do in Your First 7 Days

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Your first week in GTA 6 matters more than your first hour. The opening hours teach you the controls, but the first seven days are when you build the habits that decide whether the rest of the game feels comfortable or constantly broke, tired and chased. This guide is a day-by-day plan built only on verified mechanics — no rumours, no guesses, just the systems Rockstar has shown and the ones launch reporting has confirmed.

The plan has one theme: set up your money, your rest and your kit before you chase the big scores. The players who do that in week one will spend week two enjoying the game. The players who skip it will spend week two recovering from it.

Days 1–2: learn the money rule first

Money is the first system to learn, because it is the only one that can punish you from the first mission. Cash you carry is lost if you die or get arrested, and some story missions will not open until you have earned enough. So the first habit is the deposit habit: after every job, walk to an ATM and bank what you earned. Before the story mission Buck Me opens your BuckMe account, hide spare cash under the loose floorboards in Jason's bedroom instead.

While you are learning, keep only walking-around cash in your pockets. The BuckMe app is a joint account between Jason and Lucia, so money either of them banks is money both can spend. Get into the rhythm early — job, ATM, then play — and you will never feel the sting of losing a whole job's pay to one bad moment.

Days 3–4: sleep, eat and train

By day three, start treating rest as a mechanic, not a chore. The game's health app tracks whether Jason and Lucia are well rested, and going without sleep slows the regeneration of some attributes. Sleep in your safehouse bed — Jason's stilt house in Key Lento has the first one — and choose your wake-up time. A full night restores health and stamina and buffs focus, which makes it the cheapest reset in the game.

Training belongs in the same routine. A gym membership plus three exercises a day raises health, cardio raises stamina, and focus gains are permanent while health and stamina gains fade if you stop training. Eat and sleep before you train, since the characters get more out of a session when they are rested and fed. Three short sessions a day, every day, will leave you noticeably tougher by the end of the week.

Days 5–6: buy tools before cosmetics

Once your money habit and your rest habit are set, spend your first real savings on tools, not looks. The early game rewards practical purchases: a better weapon you own, a vehicle you have saved, the kit that makes jobs easier. Cosmetics can wait — the car you drive and the gun you carry decide whether jobs succeed, and a paint job decides nothing.

This is also the time to learn the map's shape. Vice City is the centre, but Leonida is much bigger: the Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, the Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga. Drive the roads, learn where the ATMs are, and note which areas are crowded and which are empty. The players who know the map will always have an escape route; the players who do not will always be improvising one.

Day 7: review, bank and plan the second week

On day seven, stop and take stock. Check your BuckMe balance and move spare cash into Family Savings, where it unlocks price reductions and raises your checking limit. Check the health app to see whether your characters are well rested. Look at what you own — the vehicles you have saved, the weapons you have bought — and what you still need.

Then set one goal for week two. It might be a garage, a specific vehicle, or the cash target for the next money-gated mission. A goal turns random play into progress, and progress is what the game's economy rewards. The first week was about building habits. The second week is about using them.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first in GTA 6?

Learn the money rule: bank your cash at an ATM after every job, and hide it under the floorboards before the Buck Me mission opens your account. Everything else is easier once your money is safe.

How much should I sleep in the first week?

Sleep every in-game night, or whenever the health app shows your characters are not well rested. A full night restores health and stamina and buffs focus, and skipping sleep slows attribute regeneration.

Should I save money or spend it early?

Spend it on tools first — weapons you own and vehicles you save — and bank the rest. Cosmetics can wait. Some story missions need set amounts of cash, so keeping a growing bank balance matters more than looking good.

The bottom line: week one is for habits, not heroics. Bank your cash, sleep every night, train a little every day, and buy tools before cosmetics. Do that for seven days and the rest of Leonida opens up on your terms.

FBI Expo Oct 29 & DC Law Enforcement Career Fair Oct 30: 100+ Agencies Hiring at the DC Armory

The DC Armory becomes a two-day law enforcement recruiting hub at the end of October: the FBI Washington Field Office holds its first-ever FBI Expo on October 29, and the DC Army National Guard follows on October 30 with a fall Law Enforcement Career Fair featuring more than 100 federal, state, and local agencies.

The DC Armory at 2001 East Capitol Street SE, Washington, DC will host two major law enforcement recruiting events back to back. On Thursday, October 29, 2026, the FBI Washington Field Office holds its first-ever FBI Expo — an interactive recruiting event with special agents, professional staff, and specialized teams. On Friday, October 30, the DC Army National Guard hosts its 2026 Fall Law Enforcement Career Fair, with more than 100 federal, state, and local agencies expected to attend.

Military members and their families get early access one hour before the public on both days — a meaningful advantage at events where recruiters' time is the scarcest resource. Both events are in person, and registration is handled online in advance.

Thursday, October 29: The FBI Expo

The FBI describes the Expo as an interactive recruiting event, not a booth-and-brochure fair. Expect hands-on displays, demonstrations, and face time with special agents, professional staff, and specialized teams — designed to show how different FBI teams work together on real cases. Importantly, much of what the Bureau hires for has nothing to do with carrying a gun: STEM, business operations, intelligence analysis, and security roles all recruit at events like this.

When you register on the FBI's online form, you will be asked which path interests you: Special Agent, Intelligence, Business Operations, STEM, or Security and Investigations — there is even a "just curious" option. Pick the honest answer; recruiters use it to route you to the right people. The Expo runs 10:00–11:00 AM for military and family early access, then 11:00 AM–2:00 PM for the general public.

Friday, October 30: DC Law Enforcement Career Fair

The DC Army National Guard's fall fair promises more than 100 federal, state, and local agencies that are actively hiring. For anyone pursuing a law enforcement career in the National Capital Region, this is the densest concentration of recruiters you will find in a single day — federal agencies, metropolitan police departments, sheriff's offices, and specialized units side by side.

The schedule mirrors Thursday: 9:00–10:00 AM military and family early access, then 10:00 AM–2:00 PM general public. Attendee registration is handled through Eventbrite, covering both days. If you can only attend one day, pick the day that matches your target: FBI-specific careers on Thursday, breadth of law enforcement employers on Friday. Better still, attend both.

What to Bring and How to Prepare

Bring multiple printed copies of your resume — federal resumes for federal recruiters, concise one- to two-page versions for state and local agencies. Dress professionally; first impressions with recruiters genuinely matter. Prepare a 30-second introduction: who you are, your background, and the type of role you are pursuing.

Come with specific questions: current vacancies, qualification requirements, application procedures, hiring timelines, veteran hiring pathways, and background-investigation expectations. Recruiters remember candidates who ask informed questions far longer than those who just collect brochures.

Veterans: federal law enforcement agencies actively recruit veterans, and veterans' preference plus authorities like Veterans Recruitment Appointment (VRA) can significantly improve your odds. Ask each federal recruiter which veteran hiring authorities their agency uses.

After the Fair: Turning Contacts into Applications

The fair is the starting line, not the finish. Within 48 hours, follow up with every recruiter you spoke with — a short email referencing your conversation keeps you memorable. Then apply on USAJOBS.gov for the specific announcements they mentioned; in-person interest does not substitute for a submitted application.

Track each agency's timeline. Federal law enforcement hiring — especially for Special Agent and 1811 criminal investigator roles — involves written assessments, fitness tests, polygraphs, and background investigations that can stretch for many months. Starting the process now means you are positioned for 2027 hiring classes.

How to Attend the FBI Expo and DC Career Fair

  1. Register for the FBI Expo (Oct 29). Complete the FBI's online registration form and select the career path that interests you — Special Agent, Intelligence, Business Operations, STEM, Security and Investigations, or "just curious."
  2. Register for the Law Enforcement Career Fair (Oct 30). Sign up through the Eventbrite attendee registration, which covers both days of the Armory events.
  3. Arrive during early access if eligible. Military members and families get in an hour early both days — 10:00 AM Thursday, 9:00 AM Friday.
  4. Bring resumes and dress professionally. Carry multiple copies of your resume and a short introduction explaining your background and target roles.
  5. Follow up within 48 hours. Email the recruiters you met, then submit formal applications on USAJOBS.gov for the announcements they recommended.

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where is the FBI Expo?

The FBI Washington Field Office's first FBI Expo is Thursday, October 29, 2026, at the DC Armory, 2001 East Capitol Street SE, Washington, DC. Military and family early access is 10:00–11:00 AM; the general public is admitted 11:00 AM–2:00 PM.

What is the DC Law Enforcement Career Fair on October 30?

Hosted by the DC Army National Guard at the DC Armory, the fall Law Enforcement Career Fair brings together more than 100 federal, state, and local agencies that are actively hiring. Military early access is 9:00–10:00 AM; public hours are 10:00 AM–2:00 PM.

Do I need to register in advance?

Yes. The FBI Expo uses the FBI's online registration form, and the career fair uses Eventbrite attendee registration covering both days.

Is the FBI Expo only for aspiring Special Agents?

No. Registration paths include Intelligence, Business Operations, STEM, and Security and Investigations — plus a 'just curious' option. Many FBI roles are professional staff positions.

What should I bring to the career fair?

Multiple printed resume copies, professional attire, and a prepared 30-second introduction. Veterans should also be ready to discuss their preference eligibility and discharge documentation.

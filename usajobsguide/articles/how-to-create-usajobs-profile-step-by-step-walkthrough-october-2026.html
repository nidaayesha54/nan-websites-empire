How to Create a USAJOBS Profile: 2026 Walkthrough

No USAJOBS profile means no federal application. This step-by-step walkthrough covers the login.gov sign-in, every profile section (Contact, Eligibility, Demographics, Preferences), your Documents, and the settings to finish before you apply.

Every federal application starts in the same place: a USAJOBS profile. You cannot apply for any federal job without one, and your profile does far more than store your name — it carries your eligibility, your documents, your saved searches, and the information submitted with every application. Setting it up takes about 15 minutes if you do it in the right order. Here is the full walkthrough for 2026.

Step 1: Create a login.gov Account

USAJOBS now uses login.gov for its sign-in process. Login.gov is the federal government's secure sign-in service, and it is the only way to log in to USAJOBS — there is no separate USAJOBS username and password anymore. Create your login.gov account first, then continue to USAJOBS. Every future sign-in requires your email address, your password, and a one-time security code sent by text or phone call. If you already use login.gov for other government services, you can sign in with that same account.

Step 2: Complete Your Profile Sections

Once signed in, open your Profile and work through the sections. Each one matters:

  • Contact. Your name, address, phone number, email, and citizenship. Keep this accurate — agencies use it to reach you.
  • Eligibility. This is where you record your hiring-path eligibility: veteran, current or former federal employee, military spouse, and other special authorities. Answer honestly and completely — this section determines which announcements you can apply to and which documents you will need.
  • Demographics. Optional background information used for statistics only; it is not used in hiring decisions.
  • Preferences. Your desired job types, work schedules, locations, and pay grades. USAJOBS uses this information to improve your job search results, so fill it in — a complete profile gives you better matches.

Your profile information is submitted with each job application and helps prefill parts of the application, which is why keeping it current is worth the effort.

Step 3: Add Resumes and Documents

Open the Documents section next. You can store up to five resumes in your profile — build them with the Resume Builder or upload your own (PDF recommended, since it preserves formatting and page count). The Documents section also holds your supporting documents: transcripts, DD-214, SF-50, SF-15, Schedule A letter, and other proof of eligibility. Upload everything before you need it, because new uploads take hours to clear the virus scan and incomplete documents are the top preventable cause of ineligible ratings.

Step 4: Set Up Searches, Saved Jobs, and Tracking

With the profile complete, turn on the tools that make your job hunt run itself. Create saved searches (job search agents) that email you when new announcements match your criteria. Use saved jobs to bookmark announcements you want to review later. Your Home page shows the status of every submitted application package — Received, Referred, Selected — so you always know where you stand. The USAJOBS Help Center's how-to guides walk through each of these features with step-by-step instructions if you get stuck.

USAJOBS Profile Setup Checklist

  1. Create your login.gov account and set up the one-time-code authentication.
  2. Complete the Contact section: name, address, phone, email, citizenship.
  3. Complete the Eligibility section: veteran status, federal employment, military spouse, other hiring paths.
  4. Fill in Preferences: job types, schedules, locations, pay grades for better search results.
  5. Add documents: up to five resumes plus transcripts, DD-214/SF-50/SF-15, and eligibility proofs — upload early to clear the virus scan.
  6. Create saved searches with email alerts and bookmark target announcements under saved jobs.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is a USAJOBS profile required to apply for federal jobs?

Yes. You must create a USAJOBS profile to apply for any federal job. A profile also lets you save jobs, automate job searches, store resumes and documents, and track application statuses.

Why does USAJOBS use login.gov to sign in?

Login.gov is the federal government's secure sign-in service. You sign in with your email, password, and a one-time security code sent by text or phone call, protecting your account and application data.

What are the sections of a USAJOBS profile?

The main sections are Contact (name, address, citizenship), Eligibility (hiring paths like veteran or current federal employee), Demographics (optional), and Preferences (job types and locations).

How many resumes and documents can I store in USAJOBS?

You can keep up to five resumes in your profile — built with the Resume Builder or uploaded. The Documents section also holds supporting documents such as transcripts, DD-214s, SF-50s, and Schedule A letters.

How often should I update my USAJOBS profile?

Review it before every application. Your profile information is submitted with each job application and improves your search results, so keep contact details, eligibility, and documents current.

Direct Hire Authority: How Federal Fast-Track Hiring Works

Direct Hire Authority is the fastest legal route into federal service — no rating, no ranking, no waiting for a certificate. Here is how it works and how to spot DHA jobs.

Direct Hire Authority (DHA) is the federal government's fast lane. When the Office of Personnel Management (OPM) determines there is a severe shortage of candidates or a critical hiring need for an occupation, it can authorize agencies to appoint any qualified candidate — without the standard rating, ranking, and veterans preference procedures that slow competitive hiring. DHA does not lower standards: you must still meet every qualification, suitability, and background requirement. What it removes is the queue.

What Direct Hire Authority Actually Does

In normal competitive hiring, applicants are rated, ranked, and referred on a certificate, with veterans preference applied at each step. Under DHA, that machinery is set aside: agencies must still publicly announce the vacancy and use valid assessments, but once you are found qualified, you can be selected immediately. Qualified veterans are selected as they are found, like any qualified candidate.

DHA appointments go into the competitive service and generally carry a one-year probationary period. The appointment is documented on the SF-50 with code AYM plus the code for the specific DHA used.

Which Jobs Carry Governmentwide DHA

OPM maintains governmentwide DHAs that any agency can use for covered occupations. The major ones include:

  • Medical occupations (GW001) — all grades and locations for Radiologic Technologist (GS-0647), Medical Officer (GS-0602), Nurse (GS-0610, GS-0620), and Pharmacist (GS-0660).
  • IT Management, Information Security (GW002) — GS-2210 at GS-9 and above, all locations.
  • STEM positions (GW-007) — GS-11 through GS-15 nationwide, authorized through December 31, 2028, for scientific, technical, engineering, and mathematics roles where a severe shortage or critical need exists.
  • AI-related roles — DHA for IT specialists, computer scientists, engineers, and analysts at GS-9 through GS-13 working on AI, through 2028, plus Schedule A temporary appointments for AI support roles.
  • Acquisition and cybersecurity series — DHA extended through 2028 for nearly two dozen STEM, acquisition, and cybersecurity series, plus GS-12–15 criminal investigator roles involving cybersecurity.

Agencies can also hold agency-specific DHAs. The announcement itself is authoritative: it will state that the position is being filled using Direct Hire Authority.

How to Find and Win DHA Jobs

DHA announcements look ordinary, but the wording gives them away: the "Clarification from the agency" section will say the position is being filled using Direct Hire Authority. Because selection is fast, DHA announcements sometimes carry short windows or rolling cut-offs — the VA Police Officer posting, for example, runs as an open continuous DHA announcement with two-week referral cut-offs.

Your strategy for DHA jobs differs: there is no rating to game — the question is simply whether you are qualified. Make your resume prove it point by point, with month and year dates, hours per week, and every required credential. Apply early, because DHA vacancies can close once enough qualified candidates are found.

Watch out: DHA cannot be used for Schedule Policy/Career excepted-service positions — DHA granted for competitive-service roles does not transfer. If an announcement does not mention DHA, standard competitive procedures apply.

How to Apply for Direct Hire Jobs

  1. Spot the DHA wording. Look for "Direct Hire Authority" in the USAJOBS announcement text.
  2. Match the qualifications exactly — DHA selection turns on qualification, not ranking.
  3. Apply early. DHA announcements can close once qualified candidates are found.
  4. Include all credentials. Transcripts, licenses, and certifications must accompany the application where required.
  5. Expect a faster timeline. Without rating and ranking, selection moves quickly — stay reachable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Direct Hire Authority?

An OPM authority letting agencies appoint qualified candidates for shortage jobs without rating, ranking, and preference procedures.

Does Direct Hire Authority lower qualification standards?

No. You must still meet all qualification, suitability, and background requirements — DHA removes the queue, not the standards.

Which occupations have governmentwide Direct Hire Authority?

Medical occupations, IT security (GS-2210), STEM at GS-11–15 through December 31, 2028, AI roles through 2028, and extended acquisition and cybersecurity series.

How do I know if a job uses Direct Hire Authority?

The USAJOBS announcement will state the position is being filled using Direct Hire Authority, usually in the clarification or eligibility section.

Do veterans get preference in Direct Hire hiring?

Standard preference procedures do not apply under DHA, but qualified veterans are selected as they are found, like any qualified candidate.

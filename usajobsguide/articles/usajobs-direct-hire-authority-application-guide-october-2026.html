USAJOBS Direct Hire Authority Guide 2026: How to Find and Apply for DHA Jobs

Direct Hire Authority (DHA) is the federal government's fast lane for shortage occupations — no competitive rating, no ranking. Here is how DHA postings work on USAJOBS and exactly how to apply for them in 2026.

Direct Hire Authority (DHA) is one of the most applicant-friendly hiring powers in the federal government. Granted by the Office of Personnel Management for shortage occupations and critical hiring needs, DHA lets agencies appoint anyone who meets the minimum qualifications — skipping competitive rating and ranking entirely.

For job seekers, DHA announcements on USAJOBS can move much faster than standard competitive postings — but DHA has its own rules.

This guide explains how Direct Hire Authority works under current federal policy, how to recognize DHA postings, and the steps agencies recommend for applying.

How Direct Hire Authority Works

Under DHA, agencies may appoint individuals to career, career-conditional, term, or temporary positions in the competitive service. The core trade: public notice is still required — every DHA opening must be posted on USAJOBS as an announcement open to the public — but there is no rating, no ranking, and no “rule of three”.

All applicants who meet the minimum qualifications are referred to the selecting official, after HR verifies your documents. And an important caveat: traditional veterans' preference does not apply under DHA, though qualified veterans are given full consideration.

The 5-Business-Day Rule and Application Limits

Current GSA policy (HRM 9332.1D) requires DHA announcements to stay open for a minimum of 5 business days so the public gets adequate notice. Shorter open periods must be formally justified in the case file.

Agencies may also cap the number of applications they accept — the recommended floor is 150 applications — which is why popular DHA postings can close early once the limit is hit. The practical lesson: apply on day one, not day five. Many DHA postings also use standing registers with cut-off dates, where HR reviews applications in batches — missing a cut-off just rolls you to the next one.

How to Spot a DHA Posting on USAJOBS

Look for these markers in the announcement: the words “Direct Hire Authority” in the evaluation or additional-details section; language stating that a rating will not be used and that all minimally qualified applicants will be referred; and a note that veterans' preference does not apply. The CDC's own DHA application guide advises applicants to select a Direct Hiring public notice, read every section in full, then click Apply — the application itself runs through the standard USAJOBS system.

How to Apply for a Direct Hire Authority Posting

  1. Find DHA public notices on USAJOBS. Search your target occupation and scan announcements for the words “Direct Hire Authority” — or filter for shortage occupations like IT, healthcare, and STEM.
  2. Read the entire announcement before clicking Apply. Pay attention to the duties, requirements, evaluation method, and required-documents sections.
  3. Verify you meet every minimum qualification. HR reviews documents before referral; inflated claims can render you ineligible.
  4. Apply through USAJOBS immediately. With 5-day minimum windows and possible application caps, early submission protects you from early closures.
  5. Upload supporting documents up front. Transcripts for positive-education-requirement series, SF-50s, and licenses — DHA moves fast, and HR will not chase you for paperwork.
  6. Track cut-off dates on standing registers. If the announcement uses rolling cut-offs, note each date and confirm your application is complete before the next one.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Direct Hire Authority on USAJOBS?

Direct Hire Authority (DHA) is an OPM-granted hiring power that lets agencies appoint qualified candidates without competitive rating and ranking.

Does veterans' preference apply to Direct Hire positions?

No — traditional veterans' preference rules do not apply under DHA. However, qualified veterans receive full consideration.

How long do Direct Hire announcements stay open?

DHA announcements should be open for a minimum of 5 business days. Many shortage-occupation postings use standing registers with rolling cut-off dates instead.

How can I tell if a USAJOBS posting uses Direct Hire Authority?

The announcement will state that a Direct Hire Authority is being used — often in the evaluation section — and will note that no rating will be used.

Is a Direct Hire job easier to get?

DHA removes competitive ranking, but you must still meet every minimum qualification exactly as written. Inflated claims can disqualify you.

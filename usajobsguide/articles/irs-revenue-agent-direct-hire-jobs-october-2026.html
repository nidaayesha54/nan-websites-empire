The Internal Revenue Service is hiring Internal Revenue Agents (Examiners) through a Direct Hire 12-month register — one of the fastest federal hiring authorities available. Positions are being filled using Direct Hire Authority (DHA), and a recruitment incentive is authorized for eligible, highly qualified applicants. If you have an accounting background and want a stable federal career with strong pay, this register is worth your attention right now.

What Is a Direct Hire 12-Month Register?

A 12-month register is a standing pool of applicants that stays open for a full year. Instead of applying to a single vacancy with a short window, you apply once to the register, and IRS hiring managers pull qualified candidates from it as positions open across the country. It is one of the most efficient ways to get into federal service.

Direct Hire Authority lets the IRS hire without going through the traditional competitive rating and ranking process for shortage occupations. That means faster timelines from application to offer — a major advantage over standard federal hiring, which often takes 60 to 120 days.

What IRS Revenue Agents Actually Do

Internal Revenue Agents — also called examiners — conduct tax examinations of individuals, small businesses, and large corporations. Working primarily in the Small Business / Self-Employed (SB/SE) division's Field Exam function, they review financial records, interview taxpayers and representatives, determine correct tax liability, and document findings that can involve millions of dollars.

Qualifications: The Accounting Requirement

Internal Revenue Agent positions follow the OPM 0512 qualification standard, which centers on accounting education. Candidates typically need at least 24 semester hours in accounting coursework — or a combination of education and specialized experience that the announcement defines precisely. Always confirm the exact requirement in the announcement you apply under, since standards can vary by grade level.

Beyond education, competitive applicants show experience with financial analysis, auditing, tax preparation, or regulatory compliance. CPA licensure is not required for entry but strengthens your application considerably and accelerates your career progression once hired.

How to Apply to the IRS Direct Hire Register

  1. Find the announcement on USAJOBS.gov. Search for "Internal Revenue Agent (Examiner) - Direct Hire" and select the 12-month register announcement.
  2. Confirm you meet the accounting coursework standard. Gather transcripts showing your accounting semester hours before you start.
  3. Build your federal resume. Emphasize auditing, tax, and financial analysis experience with quantified accomplishments.
  4. Review the recruitment incentive terms. The Additional Information section explains eligibility for the incentive for highly qualified applicants.
  5. Submit and stay in the register. Keep your USAJOBS profile current for the full 12 months so hiring managers can reach you as vacancies arise.
Recruitment incentive details matter: Incentives for highly qualified applicants can be significant but come with service agreements. Read the announcement's Additional Information section carefully and ask the hiring contact about terms before accepting an offer.

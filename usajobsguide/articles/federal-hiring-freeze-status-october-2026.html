Federal Hiring Freeze 2026: What's Frozen, What's Exempt — and How to Find Open Jobs

The governmentwide federal hiring freeze is still on in October 2026 — but the Army just resumed hiring and thousands of exempt postings are live on USAJOBS. Here is what is frozen, what is exempt, and how to find the open jobs.

If you're watching federal careers in October 2026, the headline is confusing: a governmentwide hiring freeze remains in effect — yet USAJOBS carries thousands of open announcements, the Army just resumed hiring, and agencies are running direct hire events with five-figure sign-on bonuses. Both things are true at once.

The freeze halts most competitive hiring but leaves exceptions, Direct Hire Authority postings, excepted-service hiring, and public-safety roles open. Knowing which door your target job uses is the difference between waiting and getting hired.

This guide maps the freeze's current status — including the Army's September 25 restart — and shows exactly how to find the jobs that are genuinely open right now.

Where the Freeze Stands in October 2026

The governmentwide hiring freeze remains in effect, and across the Department of Defense, strategic hiring committees must approve civilian hiring actions. But the ice is cracking: in a September 25, 2026 memo, Army Undersecretary Michael Obadal authorized hiring up to 96% of the Army's direct-funded full-time equivalent positions, ending a freeze that lasted nearly 20 months.

The scale of the Army's loss explains the urgency: about 43,000 civilians left the Army since January 20, 2025 against only ~10,000 hires. The Army Corps of Engineers lost more than 6,000 people; Installation Management Command lost about 4,300. The Air Force and Space Force are also planning to add civilian positions.

The Exceptions That Keep Hiring Alive

The freeze was never a total stop. Direct Hire Authority announcements — like DOE's current contract-specialist postings and the Bureau of Prisons' October direct hire events — continue because they target shortage occupations. Excepted-service hiring (FEMA CORE, Title 21 scientific roles) and public-safety and mission-critical positions also proceed.

Practical rule: an announcement live on USAJOBS with a future closing date is a real opening — the freeze does not cancel postings already in motion or exempt authorities.

How to Find Jobs That Are Actually Open

First, search USAJOBS normally — then filter for Direct Hire Authority and excepted-service announcements, which move fastest. Read the “clarification from the agency” box: it names the authority, and DHA postings state no rating is used.

Second, watch the USAJOBS events page — direct hire events signal real, funded openings. Third, set saved-search email alerts for your series and grade.

How to Job-Hunt During the Federal Hiring Freeze

  1. Search USAJOBS as usual. Live announcements with future closing dates are real openings — the freeze doesn't cancel them.
  2. Target Direct Hire Authority postings. DHA jobs skip competitive rating and move fastest during the freeze.
  3. Read the agency clarification box. It names the hiring authority — excepted-service and DHA postings are your green lights.
  4. Attend hiring events. Direct hire events signal funded openings; some start the process on the spot.
  5. Set saved-search alerts. Exempt postings can appear and close fast — email alerts keep you first in line.
  6. Keep your profile ready. A complete USAJOBS profile and polished resume mean you can strike the moment the right posting drops.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a federal hiring freeze in October 2026?

Yes — a governmentwide hiring freeze remains in effect. But it has exceptions, and some agencies (like the Army) have been authorized to resume hiring.

Did the Army lift its hiring freeze?

Yes. In a September 25, 2026 memo, Army Undersecretary Michael Obadal authorized hiring up to 96% of direct-funded full-time equivalent positions, ending a freeze of nearly 20 months.

Does the freeze stop all federal hiring?

No. Direct Hire Authority postings, excepted-service hiring, public-safety roles, and authorized exceptions continue — USAJOBS still carries thousands of open announcements.

Which agencies are hiring despite the freeze?

Recent examples include the Bureau of Prisons (direct hire events), the Department of Energy (direct-hire contract specialists), the EPA (veteran hiring event), and the Army (freeze lifted).

Should I still apply for federal jobs during the freeze?

Absolutely. Apply to every announcement you qualify for — exceptions and direct-hire postings are real openings, and building your USAJOBS profile now positions you for the thaw.

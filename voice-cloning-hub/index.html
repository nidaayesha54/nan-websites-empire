❓ Frequently asked questions

What is AI voice cloning?

AI voice cloning uses machine learning to create a digital replica of a person's voice from audio samples. The cloned voice can then speak any text you provide. Quality tiers range from instant clones (1–5 minutes of audio) to broadcast-grade clones (30+ minutes).

Is voice cloning legal?

Cloning your own voice, or a voice you have explicit written permission to use, is generally fine in the US. Cloning someone else's voice without consent — especially commercially or for scams — can violate right-of-publicity, fraud, and impersonation laws, which vary by state. See our legal overview (general information, not legal advice).

How can I tell if a voice is AI-cloned?

Listen for red flags: unnaturally perfect delivery with no filler words, missing or odd breathing, flat emotion, robotic pacing, audio artifacts like warbling or metallic sibilants, and suspicious context like urgent money requests. Our detection guide scores what you hear and walks you through verification steps.

Do I need consent to clone a voice?

Yes. Ethical voice cloning requires explicit, informed consent from the voice owner, and reputable platforms verify identity before training. Generate your requirements with our consent checklist.

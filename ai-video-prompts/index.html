Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about AI video prompts.
What are AI video prompts?

AI video prompts are text descriptions you feed into AI video generators like Runway, Pika, Luma or Sora. A good prompt describes the subject, action, camera movement, lighting, style and mood so the AI renders exactly the scene you imagine.

Are these AI video prompts free to use?

Yes — all 120+ prompts are free to copy and use in any AI video tool, including for commercial projects.

Which AI video tools do these prompts work with?

They're written in plain descriptive language that works with Runway Gen-3, Pika, Luma Dream Machine, Sora, Kling, Hailuo and most other text-to-video models.

How do I write a better AI video prompt?

Include six elements: subject, action, camera move, lighting, style and mood. Our free prompt checklist scores your prompt on each element, and the prompt combiner builds one for you automatically.

How can I check if a video was really AI-generated?

Use a free AI video detector to analyze a clip for AI-generation fingerprints — handy for verifying content authenticity.

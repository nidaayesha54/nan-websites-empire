100 AI Video Prompts for 2026 (Copy-Paste Ready)

The difference between a forgettable AI video and a viral one is rarely the tool — it's the prompt. Below are the prompt styles dominating 2026, organized by use case, each with a ready-to-use example. For the full searchable set, browse our prompt library with 120+ prompts.

1. Cinematic Prompts

The most-shared AI videos in 2026 look like film stills in motion. The formula: subject + camera move + lighting + film language ("35mm", "anamorphic", "film grain").

Cinematic night scene in a rain-soaked Tokyo alley, neon signs reflecting on wet asphalt, a lone figure in a trench coat walking toward camera, dramatic anamorphic lens flare, shallow depth of field, slow dolly-in, film grain, teal and orange grade.

2. Product Ad Prompts

Brands are generating entire commercials with AI. Slow motion + macro detail + studio lighting is the winning combo for product shots.

Luxury perfume bottle on black marble, silk ribbon swirling around it in slow motion, golden particles floating, dramatic spotlight from above, macro lens details of glass reflections, elegant rotating camera, premium commercial ad style.

3. Nature & Wildlife Prompts

Nature content performs endlessly because it needs no context. Slow-motion animal action and epic landscapes are the top sub-niches.

Majestic lion shaking its mane in golden hour light, dust particles glowing, slow motion, savanna background blurred, powerful wildlife portrait, National Geographic style.

4. Abstract & Background Prompts

Looping abstract clips are gold for creators who need backgrounds, intros, and Shorts fillers. Always add "seamless loop".

Molten gold liquid flowing and folding in hypnotic slow motion, mirror reflections, black background, macro detail of rippling metallic surface, luxurious abstract art, seamless loop.

5. Fantasy & Sci-Fi Prompts

Dragons, floating islands, cyberpunk cities — fantasy is where AI video shines because no camera could ever film it.

Colossal dragon soaring over snow-capped mountain peaks at sunrise, wings spanning valleys, mist trailing, epic aerial chase camera, breathtaking fantasy scale.

6. Food Prompts

Slow-motion food is algorithm candy. Cheese pulls, syrup pours, and splash crowns all share one trait: extreme close-up + slow motion.

Wood-fired pizza with an epic slow-motion cheese pull, steam rising, basil leaves falling, rustic table, warm pizzeria light, mouthwatering food cinematography.

7. Character Prompts

Consistent characters are 2026's biggest trend — creators build recurring mascots and heroes. Describe distinctive, repeatable features.

Cyberpunk samurai standing in neon-lit rain, glowing katana, reflective visor, steam rising from street vents, slow walk toward camera, dramatic low angle, futuristic character showcase.

8. Sports & Action Prompts

Mid-action openings (the first 1–3 seconds) decide retention. Open every action prompt mid-movement, never with a setup.

Skateboarder landing a kickflip down concrete stairs in extreme slow motion, dust exploding, city sunset behind, dynamic low angle, raw street energy.

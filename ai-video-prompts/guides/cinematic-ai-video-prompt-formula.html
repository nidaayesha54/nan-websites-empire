The Cinematic AI Video Prompt Formula

Cinematic AI videos don't happen by accident. They come from prompts written like shot lists — the same language a director of photography uses on set. Here is the exact formula, in order.

The Formula

SHOT TYPE + LENS + SUBJECT + ACTION + CAMERA MOVE + LIGHTING + COLOR GRADE + FILM STOCK

Each Piece, Explained

1. Shot type

Wide establishing shot, over-the-shoulder, extreme close-up, low angle, aerial. This sets the grammar of the frame.

2. Lens

35mm, 85mm portrait, anamorphic, macro. Lens words are the strongest single style anchor in most models.

3. Subject + action

What and what's moving — the core of the scene. Keep it to one clear action.

4. Camera move

Slow dolly-in, orbiting crane, handheld follow, static tripod. Movement separates "cinematic" from "slideshow".

5. Lighting

Golden hour, blue hour, volumetric rays, neon practicals, moonlight. Light is emotion.

6. Color grade

Teal and orange, desaturated, warm vintage, high contrast. Grade words unify the palette.

7. Film stock / texture

35mm film grain, 70mm IMAX look, subtle grain. Texture sells realism.

Formula in Action

Over-the-shoulder shot, 35mm anamorphic lens: a character standing on a skyscraper rooftop at blue hour, city lights glittering below, hair and coat moving in wind, slow orbiting camera, dramatic rim lighting, teal and orange grade, subtle 35mm film grain, cinematic drama, emotional intensity.

5 Cinematic Prompt Templates

  1. The epic reveal: "Epic wide establishing shot, [subject] at [location] during golden hour, slow aerial crane move from high to low, dramatic clouds, cinematic color grade, epic scale."
  2. The intimate moment: "Intimate close-up, 85mm lens: [character] [action], warm window light on face, slow push-in, shallow depth of field, nostalgic film grain."
  3. The noir scene: "Low-angle [shot] in rain-soaked [location] at night, neon reflections, [character] walking toward camera, anamorphic lens flare, teal and orange grade, moody."
  4. The nature epic: "Slow drone descent through [landscape], mist and sunbeams, [wildlife] moving through frame, vibrant natural color, documentary realism."
  5. The tension build: "Handheld-style [shot] of [subject], [action], dramatic side lighting, subtle shake, high contrast, building tension."

Common Cinematic Mistakes

  • Too many subjects. One hero per shot — crowds dilute the model's attention.
  • Conflicting styles. "Anime photorealistic vintage" cancels itself out. Pick one.
  • No camera move. The #1 reason cinematic prompts render flat.
  • Forgetting the grade. Two extra words ("teal and orange") transform the look.

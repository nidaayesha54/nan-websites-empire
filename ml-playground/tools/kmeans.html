What's happening here? (Lloyd's algorithm)

  • Step 1 — Assign: every point joins the nearest centroid's cluster (that's why colors change).
  • Step 2 — Update: each centroid moves to the average position of its cluster.
  • Repeat until no point switches clusters — that's convergence.

Try this: set k=2 on the 3-blob sample data and watch the algorithm forced to merge two natural groups — a great intuition for why choosing k is the hardest part of k-means. Inertia (sum of squared distances to centroids) always drops — the algorithm never gets worse, but it can get stuck in a merely-good grouping. That's why pros run it several times.

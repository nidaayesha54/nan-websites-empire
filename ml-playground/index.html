❓ FAQ

Is MLPlayground really free?

Yes. Every interactive demo runs 100% in your browser with vanilla JavaScript. No signup, no server, no cost.

Do I need math skills to use the visualizers?

No. Each tool is visual and interactive — drag points, click, and watch the algorithm work. Plain-English explanations sit right under every demo.

What machine learning algorithms are covered?

Linear regression (least squares), k-means clustering, decision trees, and train/test splits with overfitting — the core building blocks of ML.

Is my data uploaded anywhere?

No. All computation happens locally in your browser. Nothing you draw or click ever leaves your device.

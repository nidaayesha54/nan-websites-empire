The Staff Selection Commission has declared the final result of the Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, 2025 on 7 October 2026. Against 3,522 vacancies for Lower Division Clerk (LDC) / Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Data Entry Operator (DEO) and DEO Grade ‘A’, 3,515 candidates stand provisionally recommended — 7 seats went unfilled, and there is no waiting list.

The merit lists and category/post-wise cutoffs are live on ssc.gov.in (Results section). The journey ran from Tier-I (November 2025) through Tier-II, tentative allocation and sliding/identity verification — the full timeline, reported cutoffs and what selected candidates must do next are below.

  • LDC/JSA UR range: 293–306 marks
Honesty note: the 3,522 vacancy figure is consistent across six independent outlets. The post-wise/UR cutoff specifics above are single-source — treat them as indicative until you open the official cutoff PDF.

What Selected Candidates Do Next

1
Download your merit-list PDF from ssc.gov.in and confirm your name, roll number, post and department allocation.
2
Wait for the allotted department’s communication — document verification and appointment formalities are handled by the department, not SSC.
3
Keep originals ready — 12th certificate, category certificate, ID proof and the SSC application printout.

If your name is missing, remember: with no reserve list, the 7 unfilled seats lapse — there is no second merit list for this cycle.

SSC GD Constable 2027 — Notification Expected Oct 2026

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) is expected to release the Constable (GD) in Central Armed Police Forces (CAPFs), SSF and Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles Examination, 2027 notification in October 2026, per the Commission's annual examination calendar for 2026-27. This is one of the biggest 10th-pass government recruitments of the year — the 2026 cycle filled 25,487 posts across BSF, CISF, CRPF, ITBP, SSB, SSF and Assam Rifles.

Reports project more than 25,000 vacancies for the 2027 cycle, with the computer-based test expected in January–March 2027. The application form will open alongside the notification on ssc.gov.in, with an application window of roughly one month.

Expected Vacancies and Forces

Forces covered: BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles (AR), SSF — and possibly NCB, as in the 2026 cycle. For reference, the 2026 force-wise distribution was: CISF 14,595; CRPF 5,490; SSB 1,764; ITBP 1,712; Assam Rifles 1,706; BSF 616; SSF 23 — a useful guide to the likely shape of the 2027 distribution, not a promise.

Expected Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Matriculation / Class 10 pass from a recognized board or university — the minimum bar for all GD Constable posts.

Age Limit

18 to 23 years expected, as on 1 January 2027, with the usual relaxations for SC/ST/OBC, Ex-Servicemen and other categories. The exact reference date and relaxation table will come only in the official notice.

Selection Process

1
Computer-Based Test (CBT) — General Intelligence and Reasoning, General Knowledge, Mathematics and English/Hindi.
2
Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) — race, height, chest and other physical standards per the notification (for the 2026 cycle: 5 km in 24 minutes for men, 1.6 km in 8.5 minutes for women).
3
Medical examination and document verification — followed by the final merit list on performance and vacancy availability.

Expected Schedule

How to Be Ready

  • Complete SSC One-Time Registration (OTR) on ssc.gov.in now — it is required before the form opens
  • Keep your Class 10 certificate, photo, signature and category documents scanned in the prescribed format
  • Watch the Notice Board section of ssc.gov.in — the notification PDF appears there first
  • Apply within the first week of the window to avoid last-day server congestion

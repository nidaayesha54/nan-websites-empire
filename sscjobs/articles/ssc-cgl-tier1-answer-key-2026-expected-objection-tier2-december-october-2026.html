SSC CGL Tier-1 Answer Key 2026 Expected Soon: Objection Process — Tier-2 in December

With the SSC CGL 2026 Tier-1 examination running from 30 September to 30 October 2026, candidates are now asking the obvious question: when will the answer key be released? The Staff Selection Commission follows a well-established rhythm — it publishes the provisional answer key with candidate response sheets after the Tier-1 window closes, opens a short objection window, and then finalizes the key before declaring the Tier-1 result. For the 10,731 advertised posts, every mark matters, so understanding the answer-key process now puts you ahead of the curve.

Tier-1 is only the screening stage: the real selection weight lies in Tier-2, scheduled for December 2026. That means the weeks between the answer key and the Tier-1 result are your most valuable preparation window — not a waiting room. Here is how the answer key cycle works, how to challenge wrong answers, and how to use the gap productively for Tier-2.

How the SSC Answer Key Process Works

1
Provisional key released — SSC uploads the tentative answer key along with each candidate's response sheet on ssc.gov.in.
2
Download your response sheet — log in with your registration number and password to compare your answers against the key.
3
Estimate your score — apply the marking scheme and negative marking to get your probable raw score.
4
Raise objections — if a key answer looks wrong, submit a challenge with supporting references within the objection window, paying the prescribed fee per question.
5
Final key & result — SSC examines objections, publishes the final answer key, and declares the Tier-1 result with the cutoff.

Objections without a valid reference are routinely rejected, so challenge only questions where you can cite a standard source — textbook, government data or an authoritative publication. The fee is refunded only if your challenge is upheld, per the SSC notice.

From Tier-1 to Tier-2: Your December Game Plan

Tier-1 marks only decide who writes Tier-2; Tier-2 marks decide who gets the post. The December Tier-2 paper covers quantitative abilities, English, general awareness and computer knowledge at a significantly higher difficulty than Tier-1. Candidates who start Tier-2 preparation only after the Tier-1 result lose the most valuable weeks of the cycle.

  • Quant: Move from Tier-1 speed drills to Tier-2 depth — advanced arithmetic, algebra, geometry and data interpretation.
  • English: Shift to comprehension-heavy practice and error-detection at Tier-2 level.
  • Mocks: Take full-length Tier-2 mocks weekly; analyze every error by topic, not just by marks.
  • Cutoff tracking: Use your answer-key-estimated Tier-1 score against previous cutoffs to set a realistic Tier-2 target.
Tip: Save your response sheet PDF the day it is released — SSC removes the download link after the objection window closes, and you will need it to verify the final key.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the SSC CGL Tier-1 answer key 2026 be released?
SSC releases the provisional answer key with response sheets after the Tier-1 examination window (30 September – 30 October 2026) closes. Check ssc.gov.in for the official notice.
How many vacancies are in SSC CGL 2026?
10,731 revised vacancies across Group B and Group C posts in central government ministries and departments.
How do I raise an objection against the SSC answer key?
Log in on ssc.gov.in during the objection window, select the disputed question, attach a supporting reference and pay the prescribed per-question fee.
When is the SSC CGL Tier-2 exam 2026?
Tier-2 is scheduled for December 2026 for candidates who qualify Tier-1.
Do Tier-1 marks count in the final SSC CGL merit?
Tier-1 is qualifying for Tier-2 eligibility; the final merit is driven by Tier-2 performance, followed by document verification.

The answer key is your first honest look at where you stand — download the response sheet, estimate your score, challenge genuine errors, and then pivot immediately to Tier-2 preparation. December will arrive faster than you think.

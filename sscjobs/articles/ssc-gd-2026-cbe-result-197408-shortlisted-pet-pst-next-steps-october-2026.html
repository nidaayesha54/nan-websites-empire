The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has declared the Constable (GD) 2026 Computer Based Examination result on 12 October 2026 — and 1,97,408 candidates stand provisionally shortlisted for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST). The CBE was held from 27 April to 30 May 2026 across four exam weeks, for 25,487 vacancies in the BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles and Secretariat Security Force.

SSC published the result in two lists (List-I and List-II) with an 18-page result write-up on ssc.gov.in. If your roll number is in the list, the written battle is won — the physical battle starts now. Here is how to check, when PET/PST happens, and what the physical standards demand.

Qualifying marks in the CBE were 30% for UR, 25% for OBC/EWS and 20% for other categories — but the actual PET/PST shortlisting cutoffs, published force-wise and category-wise in the write-up, ran far higher.

How to Check the SSC GD 2026 Shortlist

1
Visit ssc.gov.in — open the SSC website and go to the Results section for the Constable (GD) 2026 examination.
2
Download List-I and List-II — List-I covers candidates shortlisted for PET/PST against all-India vacancies; List-II covers the NIA/SSF-linked shortlist — check both.
3
Search your roll number — use Ctrl+F in the PDF; the lists are roll-number-wise, so have your admit-card number handy.
4
Read the 18-page write-up — it carries the category-wise and force-wise cutoffs — compare your normalised score to understand your margin.
5
Watch rect.crpf.gov.in — CRPF is the nodal agency for the PET/PST stage — call letters and the PET/PST schedule will be published on the CRPF recruitment portal.

PET / PST — What the Physical Stage Demands

Physical Efficiency Test (race)

The PET is a timed race: 5 km in 24 minutes for male candidates and 1.6 km in 8½ minutes for female candidates. This is the elimination round — start structured running practice today.

Physical Standard Test (measurements)

PST measures height, chest (male) and weight against the force-wise standards in the notification, with the prescribed relaxations for hill-area, tribal and other eligible categories. Candidates who fail the measurements do not run the PET.

After PET/PST: qualifiers face the Detailed Medical Examination (DME) and then Document Verification. The final merit is drawn from CBE marks of medically-fit, document-verified candidates.

What To Do Right Now If You Are Shortlisted

1
Download and save the result PDFs — keep List-I/List-II and the write-up — you will need your shortlist status at every later stage.
2
Start race training immediately — build to the 5 km / 1.6 km standard progressively; get running shoes and a measurable track or app.
3
Organise original documents — 10th certificate, caste certificate, domicile and photo ID — keep originals ready for DME and DV.
4
Track the CRPF portal weekly — PET/PST call letters, centre allocation and reporting dates appear on rect.crpf.gov.in — do not wait for SMS alerts alone.

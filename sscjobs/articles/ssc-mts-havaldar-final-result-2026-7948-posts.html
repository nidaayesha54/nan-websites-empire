SSC MTS Havaldar Final Result 2026

Final result declared 7 October 2026 for 7,948 MTS and Havaldar posts — download the merit list PDF, check DV documents and joining steps.

SSC MTS/Havaldar 2026: Key Facts

Official Websitessc.gov.in

The Staff Selection Commission declared the SSC MTS/Havaldar Final Result 2026 on 7 October 2026, completing the recruitment for 7,948 posts — 6,810 Multi Tasking Staff and 1,138 Havaldar (CBIC and CBN). Applications were accepted from 26 June to 24 July 2025, the computer-based exam began on 4 February 2026, the answer key was released on 3 March 2026, the written result came on 3 August 2026, and the Havaldar PET/PST ran from 24 to 31 August 2026. No login is needed to check the final result — it is published as a roll-number merit list PDF.

How to Download the Final Result PDF

1
Visit ssc.gov.in — the official SSC website.
2
Open the Result section on the homepage and select the "MTS" tab.
3
Click "MTS / Havaldar Final Result 2026" — the merit list PDF opens in a new window.
4
Search your roll number — use Ctrl+F to check your selection status and allotted post.
5
Take a printout — keep it for the document verification and joining formalities.

What the Two Posts Involve

PostPostsRole
Multi Tasking Staff6,810Administrative and support functions across central government offices
Havaldar (CBIC & CBN)1,138Field enforcement duties under the Central Board of Indirect Taxes & Customs and the Central Bureau of Narcotics

Havaldar candidates additionally cleared physical standards: for men, 157.5 cm height with 81 cm chest and a 1,600-metre walk in 15 minutes; for women, 152 cm height and a 1 km walk in 20 minutes.

After the Final Result: DV and Joining

  • Document verification: selected candidates report with originals — Class 10 certificate and marksheet, date-of-birth proof, category certificates where applicable, photo ID and passport photographs.
  • Medical examination: fitness check as per the post's medical standards.
  • Allotment and joining: departments issue appointment letters and joining instructions after verification — watch ssc.gov.in and the allotted department's notices for the schedule.

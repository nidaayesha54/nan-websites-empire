SSC CHSL Tier-1 2026: Exam 30 Nov–31 Dec, Dates Out

Key Details

Vacancies2,536 (LDC / JSA, DEO, DEO Grade-A, Postal/Sorting Assistant)
OrganizationStaff Selection Commission (SSC)
LocationAll India — central government ministries and departments
QualificationClass 12 (10+2) pass
Age Limit18–27 years (as per the 7 September 2026 notice)
SalaryGroup C posts — Pay Level 2 to Level 5
Last DateApplications closed 7 Oct 2026 — Tier-1 (CBE) from 30 November to 31 December 2026
Apply Linkssc.gov.in
Source / Ad DateSSC Important Notice dated 1 October 2026 (proposed schedule); notice of examination 7 September 2026

The Staff Selection Commission (SSC) has announced the examination schedule for the Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, 2026: the Tier-1 computer-based test will be held from 30 November to 31 December 2026, per the Commission's Important Notice dated 1 October 2026. The 2,536 Group C posts — Lower Divisional Clerk (LDC)/Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA), Data Entry Operator (DEO) and Postal/Sorting Assistant — will be filled through this drive.

The application window (7 September to 7 October 2026, with correction 14–16 October) is closed. SSC has not yet published the CHSL 2026 exam city details or admit cards; going by the CGL 2026 pattern, expect exam-city intimation by login and admit cards roughly 2–3 days before each candidate's exam date.

What the Tier-1 Exam Covers

  • Mode: computer-based examination in multiple shifts across the month-long window
  • Sections: General Intelligence, General Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude and English Language
  • Posts at stake: 2,536 tentative vacancies for LDC/JSA, DEO, DEO Grade-A and Postal Assistant/Sorting Assistant

The schedule is the Commission's “proposed” timetable — the notice itself says dates are tentative and changes will appear only on the Commission's website.

How to Check Your Exam City and Admit Card

1
Watch ssc.gov.in and your registered email/phone — SSC announces city intimation and admit cards only on the official portal.
2
Log in with your registration number and password; look for “Combined Higher Secondary (10+2) Level Examination, 2026 – Tier 1”.
3
Download the city slip first, then the admit card ~2–3 days before your slot; verify name, centre, shift and reporting time immediately.
4
Report any discrepancy to the SSC helpline or regional office well before your exam date.

Preparation Priorities for the Final Month

  • Lock the 15-minute sectional timing discipline SSC introduced this cycle — practice each section under a timer
  • Revise error-pattern lists in English (spotting errors, idioms) and fast arithmetic (percentages, averages, number systems)
  • Run 2–3 full mocks per week in the last month; analyze, do not just score
  • Keep photo ID + admit card + black pen ready; rough sheets and pens are provided at SSC centres

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the SSC CHSL 2026 Tier-1 exam?
From 30 November to 31 December 2026, as per SSC's Important Notice dated 1 October 2026 — the Commission calls it a proposed schedule.

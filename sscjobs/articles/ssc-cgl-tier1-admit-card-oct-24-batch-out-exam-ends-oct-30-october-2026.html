The Staff Selection Commission has released the next phase of the SSC CGL 2026 Tier-1 admit card on 21 October 2026 for candidates whose examination falls on 24 October 2026. SSC issues the admission certificate in staggered batches — roughly two to three days before each candidate's own exam date — so a "not available yet" message simply means your date is further out.

The Tier-1 window closes on 30 October 2026. Below: the released dates so far, download steps on ssc.gov.in, exam-day documents, and what follows Tier-1.

What Changed on 21 October

  • New batch live: on 21 October 2026, admission certificates were activated for candidates with the 24 October 2026 exam date.
  • Already live: phases for exam dates from 30 September through 23 October (including 1, 5–10, 12–16 and 22 October).
  • Still to come: the remaining dates up to 30 October 2026 — each batch goes live 2–3 days before its exam date.

How to Download the Tier-1 Admit Card

1
Open ssc.gov.in and log in with your registration number and password.
2
Open the Tier-1 admission certificate link for the Combined Graduate Level Examination, 2026.
3
Verify every detail — name, roll number, exam date, shift, reporting time and centre address.
4
Download and print the admit card. Keep a spare printout and a digital copy.
5
If a detail is wrong, contact your SSC regional office immediately — do not wait for exam day.

Exam-Day Documents and Rules

  • Printed admit card — entry is denied without it.
  • Original valid photo ID showing your date of birth as printed on the admit card — Aadhaar (or E-Aadhaar printout), Voter ID, Driving Licence, PAN card or Passport.
  • Two recent passport-size colour photographs, as instructed on the admit card.
  • Reach the centre before the reporting time — late entry is not permitted, and electronic devices are banned in the hall.

What Happens After Tier-1

Once the window closes on 30 October, the cycle moves ahead:

  • Provisional answer key with response sheets and an objection window.
  • Result and cutoffs — then the shortlist for Tier-2, scheduled for December 2026.
Deadline reminder: with the window closing on 30 October 2026, download your admit card the moment it goes live and verify your shift and centre the same day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the SSC CGL Tier-1 admit card 2026 out for all exam dates?
No — SSC releases it in phases. The 21 October batch covers exams on 24 October; the remaining dates go live about 2–3 days before each exam.
How do I download the SSC CGL Tier-1 admit card 2026?
Log in at ssc.gov.in with your registration number and password, open the CGL Tier-1 admission certificate link, verify the details, and print it.
When does the SSC CGL 2026 Tier-1 exam end?
The Tier-1 computer-based window runs from 30 September to 30 October 2026, with three shifts each day.
What documents must I carry on the CGL Tier-1 exam day?
A printed copy of the admit card, an original valid photo ID bearing your date of birth, and two recent passport-size colour photographs.
What comes after SSC CGL Tier-1 2026?
SSC releases the provisional answer key with an objection window, declares the result with cutoffs, and shortlists candidates for Tier-2 in December 2026.

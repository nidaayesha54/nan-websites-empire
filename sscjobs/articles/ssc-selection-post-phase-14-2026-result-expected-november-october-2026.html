The Staff Selection Commission released the tentative answer key and candidate response sheet for the Selection Post Examination, Phase-XIV/2026 on 9 October 2026 on ssc.gov.in. The objection window (₹50 per question) ran from 9 October (4 PM) to 12 October 2026 (4 PM) — and is now closed.

So this article is not about raising objections — it is about what happens next: estimating your score, the result window (broadly expected November 2026, not officially dated), and the skill-test and document-scrutiny stages that follow. One flag up front: portals report 3,003 vs 2,919 vacancies — read the honesty note before trusting either number.

Vacancy-Count Honesty Note

Conflicting reports: sarkarijobers.com reports 3,003 vacancies, while testbook.com and agrijob.in report 2,919 — flagged as “revised from 3,003”. This article reports both and does not settle on one; check the official notice for the figure against your post code.

What Happens After Objections

1
Final answer key — SSC reviews valid challenges and publishes the final key.
2
Result declaration — expected in November 2026 (expectation from reporting; not an SSC-announced date).
3
Skill tests — typing / data entry / computer proficiency, where the post requires it.
4
Document scrutiny — upload of self-attested documents on ssc.gov.in, then final merit.

Qualifying Marks and Selection

Reported qualifying marks: 30% for UR, 25% for OBC & EWS, and 20% for other categories. Selection flows from the CBT through skill tests (where applicable) to document scrutiny and final merit. Posts span LDC, DEO, Field Attendant, UDC, Lab Assistant and others at Matriculation, Higher Secondary and Graduation levels.

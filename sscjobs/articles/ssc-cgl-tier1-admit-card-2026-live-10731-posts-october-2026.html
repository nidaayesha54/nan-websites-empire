The Staff Selection Commission is releasing the SSC CGL Tier-1 Admit Card 2026 in phases instead of a single common release. The admission certificate becomes available in each candidate's login about 2–3 days before their allotted exam date. Since the Tier-1 CBT runs from 30 September to 30 October 2026, hall tickets are activating on a rolling basis — as of mid-October, admit cards are available for examinations scheduled through mid-October, with the remaining batches unlocking as their dates approach.

How to Download the SSC CGL Admit Card

1
Visit ssc.gov.in — open the official SSC website and click "Login or Register" at the top right.
2
Enter your credentials — use your registration number as the user name and your SSC registration password, plus the captcha.
3
Select the admit card link — choose "Combined Graduate Level Examination, 2026 – Tier 1 Admit Card/Hall Ticket" from your dashboard.
4
Check every detail — verify your name, photograph, signature, exam date, shift, reporting time and exam centre address; report discrepancies to the Commission immediately.
5
Print a clean copy — carry the printed admission certificate plus an original photo ID to the centre; a blurred printout can cause entry issues.

City Intimation Slip vs Admit Card: Don't Confuse Them

One distinction candidates must keep in mind is between the city intimation slip and the Admission Certificate. The city slip (released 21 September 2026) tells you the allotted city, exam date and shift for travel planning — it cannot be used as the admit card at the examination centre. The Admission Certificate, available 2–3 days before your exam, carries the detailed examination-centre address and reporting instructions and is the document that gets you inside.

If your admit card is not yet visible in your login, do not panic — this is expected under the phased release. It appears roughly two to three days before your individual exam date, not for the whole cycle at once.

Exam-Day Rules and Shift Timings

The Tier-1 CBT is being conducted across 141 exam centres in India. Key rules to follow:

  • Carry the printed admission certificate and an original photo ID — entry is not permitted without them.
  • Report at the reporting time printed on your hall ticket; late entry is not allowed.
  • The morning shift runs 10:00 AM to 11:00 AM (60 minutes); check your shift's gate-closing and reporting instructions carefully.
  • Candidates who could not complete Aadhaar-based authentication may face additional document and reporting requirements — check your hall ticket instructions.
  • PwD candidates using a scribe must download both the admit card and the scribe's entry pass; both are required on exam day.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the SSC CGL Tier-1 admit card 2026 released?
Yes. SSC is releasing the Tier-1 admit card in phases from 27 September 2026 onwards, linked to each candidate's allotted exam date. Admission certificates appear in your login about 2-3 days before your exam.
What are the SSC CGL 2026 Tier-1 exam dates?
The Tier-1 computer-based test is scheduled from 30 September to 30 October 2026 across multiple shifts and 141 exam centres in India.
How many vacancies are in SSC CGL 2026?
SSC CGL 2026 fills 10,731 Group B and Group C posts in central government departments, in pay levels 4 to 8 under the 7th CPC.
How do I download the SSC CGL admit card 2026?
Log in at ssc.gov.in with your registration number and password, select 'Combined Graduate Level Examination, 2026 - Tier 1 Admit Card', and download the hall ticket. The city intimation slip is not the admit card.
When is SSC CGL Tier-2 2026?
SSC CGL Tier-2 is scheduled for December 2026. Only candidates who clear Tier-1 (and the applicable cutoff) will be shortlisted for Tier-2.

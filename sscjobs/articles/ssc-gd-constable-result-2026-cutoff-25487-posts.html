SSC GD Constable Result 2026

Result declared for 25,487 CAPF posts — 1,97,408 candidates qualify for PET/PST. Category-wise cutoff marks, merit list download steps and what comes next.

SSC GD Constable 2026: Key Facts

PostConstable (GD) in CAPFs/SSF · Rifleman (GD) in Assam Rifles
Total Vacancies25,487 (BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP, Assam Rifles, SSF)
EligibilityMatriculation (Class 10) pass from a recognised board
Age Limit18–23 years as on 1 January 2026

Official Websitessc.gov.in

The Staff Selection Commission has declared the SSC GD Constable 2026 result along with the category-wise cutoff marks. The computer-based examination was held between 27 April and 30 May 2026 to fill 25,487 vacancies for Constable (GD) posts in the Central Armed Police Forces (BSF, CISF, CRPF, SSB, ITBP), the Secretariat Security Force and Rifleman (GD) posts in Assam Rifles. A total of 1,97,408 candidates have qualified for the next stage — the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST).

Category-Wise Cutoff Marks

The Commission released cutoffs with the result notice. Cutoffs vary by category, gender, state/UT, area and force — the snapshot below is for male candidates shortlisted against All-India vacancies of the SSF:

The minimum qualifying marks (before NCC bonus) were 30% for UR, 25% for OBC/EWS and 20% for SC/ST/ESM — but meeting the minimum does not guarantee shortlisting, which depends on the cutoff. Check the official result notice on ssc.gov.in for the complete state-wise and force-wise cutoff tables.

How to Download the SSC GD Merit List

1
Visit ssc.gov.in — the official Staff Selection Commission website.
2
Open the Result section on the homepage.
3
Find the SSC GD Constable Examination 2026 link and open the relevant result PDF.
4
Search your roll number — use Ctrl+F in the PDF to locate your number or name.
5
Download and save the PDF for PET/PST call-letter and document verification stages.

What Happens After the Result

  • PET/PST: shortlisted candidates appear for the Physical Efficiency Test and Physical Standard Test — qualifying in nature, but mandatory.
  • Medical examination: Detailed/Review Medical Examination for PET/PST qualifiers.
  • Document verification: matriculation certificate, category certificates, domicile and ID proofs in original.
  • Final merit: based on CBT marks, subject to clearing PET/PST, medical and verification.

Candidates should regularly check ssc.gov.in for the PET/PST schedule, call letters and further instructions — the Commission publishes every next-stage detail through official notifications only.

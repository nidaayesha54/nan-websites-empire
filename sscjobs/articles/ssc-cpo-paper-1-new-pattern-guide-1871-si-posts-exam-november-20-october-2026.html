The SSC CPO Paper-1 2026 is four weeks away: the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and CAPFs computer-based test runs from 20 to 28 November 2026 for 1,871 tentative posts. This cycle carries a brand-new exam pattern — and if you prepare from the old pattern, you will waste the last four weeks.

The headline changes: Paper-1 is now 100 questions in 60 minutes with 15-minute sectional timers, Paper-2 is restructured to 160 questions / 320 marks, and final selection is now based on Paper-2 marks (plus NCC bonus and post preference). There is also a new post: SI (Fire) in CISF, with 234 posts.

The New Paper-1 Pattern (20–28 Nov 2026)

Total100 questions / 100 marks / 60 minutes
SectionsGeneral Intelligence & Reasoning, General Knowledge & Awareness, Quantitative Aptitude, English Comprehension
Sectional timers15 minutes per section — you cannot bank time across sections
Negative marking−0.25 per wrong answer

The Restructured Paper-2 (Final-Selection Basis)

Total160 questions / 320 marks / 2 hours
BreakupReasoning 20×2, GA 20×2, Quant 20×2, English 100×2
Negative marking−0.50 per wrong answer
RoleFinal selection is based on Paper-2 marks (+ NCC bonus + post preference)

Paper-1 is effectively a screening gate now — the merit is decided in Paper-2. That flips the preparation logic: clear Paper-1 safely, but build your edge in Paper-2's English-heavy structure (100 of the 160 questions).

The New SI (Fire) Post (234)

  • Post: Sub-Inspector (Fire) in CISF — new for 2026, 234 vacancies.
  • Qualification: B.Sc with Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics, or a 3-year engineering diploma.
  • Age: 18–30 years (wider than the 20–25 for other SI posts).
  • Pay: Pay Level-6 — ₹35,400–₹1,12,400.

The 4-Week Strategy

1
Practise with sectional timers, not total time. Set a 15-minute timer per section in every mock — the timer is the new enemy, not the syllabus.
2
Front-load English. Paper-2 is 100/160 English questions — reading comprehension and error-spotting now decide the merit.
3
Lock the physical standards. Delhi Police male SI candidates need a valid LMV driving licence at the physical-test stage — no licence, no entry.
4
Keep Paper-2 in parallel. Don't wait for Paper-1 results to start Paper-2 prep — the gap is short and the merit is decided there.

Applications are closed (30 September) — this is a preparation guide, not a call to apply.

SSC CHSL 2026 Notification: 2,536 Posts — What's Next After Applications Close

The SSC CHSL 2026 application window closed on 7 October 2026. Here is the notification recap, the posts on offer, and your preparation checklist for the stages ahead.

By NAN Jobs Portal  |  Published: 13 October 2026  |  SSC Jobs 2026 home

The Staff Selection Commission's Combined Higher Secondary Level (CHSL) examination is one of India's biggest 12th-pass recruitments. The SSC CHSL 2026 notification invited applications for 2,536 posts, and the application window closed on 7 October 2026.

If you applied in time, the next phase begins now: status checks, admit-card release, the Tier-I computer-based test, and — if you clear it — Tier-II and document verification. This guide recaps the verified notification facts and gives you a practical checklist for the selection stages ahead.

The Posts: LDC / JSA and DEO

CHSL recruits for two broad post families in central government offices:

  • Lower Division Clerk (LDC) / Junior Secretariat Assistant (JSA) — clerical posts handling file work, noting, drafting, data entry and general office support in ministries, departments and attached offices.
  • Data Entry Operator (DEO) — posts centred on data entry and computer-based office work, with some DEO posts carrying a speed requirement in the skill test.

Both are Group C posts offering stable central-government careers with promotion channels and all-India postings.

Eligibility: Who Could Apply for SSC CHSL 2026

The CHSL notification's eligibility rules were straightforward — if you applied, confirm you satisfied all of these on the notification's cut-off date:

  • Educational qualification: 12th pass (10+2) or equivalent from a recognised board. DEO posts in certain offices may require specific subjects — the notification governs.
  • Age limit: as specified in the notification (commonly around 18–27 years) with relaxations for reserved categories exactly as published.
  • Application status: fee paid and final confirmation page saved; corrections were only possible during the correction window.

Exam Pattern: Tier-I, Tier-II and Document Verification

The SSC CHSL 2026 selection follows the Commission's standard three-stage structure:

  • Tier-I (CBT): an objective computer-based screening test with sections on general intelligence, general awareness, quantitative aptitude and English. Performance here decides who moves forward.
  • Tier-II: the mains stage, including a further computer-based component and a skill test (such as a typing / data-entry test) for applicable posts like LDC/JSA and DEO.
  • Document Verification: candidates shortlisted on merit verify certificates, caste/category documents and identity proof before final appointment.

Exact marks, timings and qualifying benchmarks are published in the official notification — work only from that document and notices on ssc.gov.in.

What Happens Next: Your Preparation Checklist

1
Check your application status and admit card — exam-city details and hall tickets appear on SSC regional websites (linked from ssc.gov.in) before the Tier-I exam. Print your admit card early and note your city and shift.
2
Lock your Tier-I preparation — revise all four sections daily, take timed mock tests on the CBT pattern, and fix speed gaps in quantitative aptitude and English.
3
Practise the skill test alongside — for LDC/JSA and DEO posts, daily typing / data-entry practice matters as much as Tier-I marks. Start now to avoid a last-minute scramble.
4
Track notices on ssc.gov.in — exam dates, answer keys, results and document-verification calls all come through official SSC notices. Ignore unofficial date claims.

SSC Stenographer Final Result 2025 Out: 1,590 Posts — Check Cut Off & DV Next Steps

The Staff Selection Commission has declared the final result of the Stenographer Grade C and Grade D Examination 2025. Along with the result, SSC published the cut off notice and the final marks of qualified and non-qualified candidates. The examination fills 1,590 posts of Stenographer in various ministries, departments and offices of the Government of India. Candidates who appeared in the skill test stage can now check the merit list on ssc.gov.in — and those who made it should start preparing for document verification.

Official Websitessc.gov.in

The stenographer selection runs in two written stages — the computer-based examination and the shorthand skill test — followed by document verification. The final result is prepared on the combined performance in the CBT and the skill test, and the published list carries the roll numbers of candidates recommended for appointment, along with their allocated post and department preferences.

How to Check the Stenographer Final Result 2026

1
Visit ssc.gov.in — open the Results section on the new SSC website.
2
Select the Stenographer tab — find the "Stenographer Grade C & D Examination 2025 – Final Result" link.
3
Download the result PDFs — SSC publishes separate lists (List-I, List-II and so on) for different grades and categories.
4
Search your roll number — use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac) and confirm your name and rank.
5
Check your marks — log in to the candidate dashboard to view your final marks and compare them with the official cut off.

Cut Off Marks — How to Read Them

SSC publishes the cut off category-wise and post-wise (Grade C and Grade D separately, and within each, by UR, OBC, SC, ST and EWS). Because Grade C (the higher grade, posted in CSSS and equivalent cadres) and Grade D have very different applicant pools, their cut offs differ substantially — compare your marks only against the cut off for your own grade and category. The cut off notice also shows how many candidates qualified at each stage, which is useful context if you are preparing for the next stenographer cycle: it tells you the real competition level, not the rumoured one.

Note: Final marks of all candidates — qualified or not — are available on the SSC candidate login for a limited window. Download your scorecard now even if you did not qualify; it is the most honest feedback on your preparation.

What Happens After the Final Result

Recommended candidates move to the last administrative stages:

1
Document verification (DV) — at the designated centre or the allocated department, with original certificates.
2
Final allocation — post and department are allotted on merit-cum-preference from the options you filled.
3
Appointment and joining — the allocated ministry or department issues the offer of appointment.

Documents to Keep Ready for DV

  • Matriculation certificate (for date of birth proof) and 12th/Graduation certificates and marksheets.
  • Caste, EWS or PwBD certificate in the prescribed format, if you claimed a reserved category.
  • Photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, passport or driving licence) and recent passport photographs.
  • Admit cards of the CBT and skill test, and the final result printout.
  • Discharge certificate for ex-servicemen, where applicable.

Get name, date-of-birth and category spellings consistent across every document now — mismatches are the commonest cause of DV delays.

SSC JE Paper-I 2026 (2–6 November): Admit Card Expected Late October — Download Steps & Exam Pattern

The Staff Selection Commission's Junior Engineer Examination 2026 enters its decisive phase: Paper-I (computer-based) is scheduled from 2 to 6 November 2026 per SSC's schedule notice dated 1 October 2026. The next milestones are the exam city intimation slip (expected 7–10 days before the exam) and the admit card (expected 3–4 days before). This guide covers the hall-ticket timeline, the Paper-I pattern, and a focused plan for the remaining days.

Admit cards are released batch-wise by exam date — candidates with 2 November slots will see their hall tickets first. Your exact date and shift are printed on your own admit card.

City Intimation vs Admit Card — Know the Difference

SSC issues two separate documents, and aspirants frequently confuse them:

  • Exam city intimation slip — shows only your allotted exam city (7–10 days before); it is not the hall ticket and cannot be used for entry.
  • Admit card — carries your date, shift, reporting time and exact centre address (3–4 days before); only this document, in print with photo ID, gets you into the hall.

How to Download the SSC JE Admit Card 2026

1
Visit ssc.gov.in — open the official SSC website and go to the Admit Card section on the homepage.
2
Select your region — admit cards are hosted on the regional SSC websites; choose the region you applied through.
3
Log in — with your registration ID or roll number and date of birth.
4
Download and print — take a clear printout; a printed hall ticket plus an original photo ID is mandatory at the gate.
5
Verify every detail — name, photograph, exam date, shift, reporting time and centre address. Report discrepancies to your regional SSC office immediately.

Duration is 2 hours, with 0.25 negative marking per wrong answer. The engineering discipline section carries half the paper — it decides the merit. Paper-I is followed by Paper-II for shortlisted candidates, then document verification.

Last-Days Plan

Engineering Discipline (100 marks)

Stop learning new topics now. Revise formula sheets and standard values, and solve the last five years' JE papers under timed conditions — SSC repeats concepts.

Reasoning and General Awareness (50 + 50 marks)

Reasoning gives the fastest returns now: series, coding-decoding and non-verbal questions. For GA, revise the last 6–8 months of current affairs plus static anchors — do not chase obscure facts.

Mock Discipline

Take one full mock every two days and analyse errors the same evening. With 0.25 negative marking, attempt what you know and skip what you don't — blind guessing sinks scores.

Important: Any "admit card out" forward before the official release is fake. Track only ssc.gov.in and your regional SSC website — the city slip comes first, the hall ticket follows.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the SSC JE 2026 admit card be released?
The admit card is expected 3–4 days before the exam. With Paper-I running 2–6 November 2026, early-slot candidates should see their hall tickets in the last days of October 2026.
What is the SSC JE Paper-I 2026 exam date?
2 to 6 November 2026, as per SSC's schedule notice dated 1 October 2026. The exam is computer-based across multiple shifts.
What is the SSC JE Paper-I exam pattern?
200 questions for 200 marks in 2 hours: General Intelligence & Reasoning (50), General Awareness (50) and the engineering discipline paper (100), with 0.25 negative marking per wrong answer.
How can I download the SSC JE admit card?
On ssc.gov.in, open the Admit Card section, select your regional SSC website, log in with your registration ID/roll number and date of birth, then download and print the hall ticket.
What is the difference between the city intimation slip and the admit card?
The city slip (7–10 days before) shows only your allotted exam city; the admit card (3–4 days before) carries your date, shift, reporting time and exact centre address and is the document required for entry.

This year's drive covers about 1,748 tentative vacancies — with first-time JE (Telecom) and Scientific Assistant (IMD) posts, and a qualifying Paper-1 whose marks will not count in the final merit. The timeline, download steps and every 2026 change are below.

City Slip and Admit Card: Expected Dates

Exam city intimation slipAround 23 October 2026 (about 7–10 days before the exam)
Paper-1 admit cardFrom 30 October 2026 (about 3–4 days before the exam)
Paper-1 exam / Paper-22 – 6 November 2026 / December 2026 (tentative)

The city intimation slip only tells you your allotted exam city, date and shift in advance so you can plan travel — it cannot be used for entry. The admission certificate (admit card) carries your roll number, reporting time and exact centre address, and is mandatory on exam day. Both come through the same ssc.gov.in candidate login.

What's New in SSC JE 2026

  • New posts: Junior Engineer (Telecom, Department of Telecommunications) and Scientific Assistant (IMD) join the drive, with a separate pattern notified for them.
  • Paper-1 is qualifying: its marks will not count in the final merit this cycle — Paper-2 decides it.
  • Four new organisations are recruiting through this year's examination; 16 exam centres have been removed.
  • About 1,748 tentative vacancies across Civil, Electrical, Mechanical and the new posts.

How to Download the City Slip and Admit Card

1
Open ssc.gov.in and log in with your registration ID (or roll number) and password.
2
Open the admit card section and find the Junior Engineer Examination 2026 link.
3
Check the city intimation slip first (expected around 23 October) — note your city, date and shift.
4
Download the admission certificate once it activates (expected from 30 October) — verify roll number, shift, reporting time and centre address.
5
Print it and keep a spare copy. Carry it with an original photo ID — Aadhaar, Voter ID, PAN, Passport or Driving Licence.

SSC does not send the admit card by post. If your exam details are still missing about a week before the exam, contact your SSC regional office with proof that you applied.

Paper-1 and Beyond: How Selection Works

  • Paper-1 (2–6 November 2026): computer-based, objective-type — qualifying only this cycle.
  • Paper-2 (December 2026, tentative): the decisive stage — its marks feed the final merit, followed by document verification and medical examination.
Deadline reminder: the city slip is expected around 23 October — check it the day it goes live so you can book travel. The admit card follows from 30 October; do not leave the download to the last evening.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the SSC JE 2026 Paper-1 exam?
Paper-1 (computer-based test) is scheduled from 2 to 6 November 2026, as per SSC's schedule notice dated 1 October 2026.
When will the SSC JE Paper-1 admit card 2026 be released?
Expected from 30 October 2026 — about 3–4 days before the exam — through the candidate login on ssc.gov.in.
When will the SSC JE exam city intimation slip be out?
Around 23 October 2026 — roughly 7–10 days before the exam. The city slip shows your exam city and date only; it is not a hall ticket.
How many vacancies are there in SSC JE 2026?
About 1,748 tentative vacancies for JE (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical, Telecom) and Scientific Assistant (IMD) posts. Some portals quote 1,699 — check the official notice.
Does SSC JE Paper-1 2026 count towards the final merit?
No — this cycle Paper-1 is qualifying in nature, and its marks will not be counted in the final merit list.

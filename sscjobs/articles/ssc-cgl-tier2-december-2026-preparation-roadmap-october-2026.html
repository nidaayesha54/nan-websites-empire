With the SSC CGL 2026 Tier-1 window closing on 30 October 2026, the focus of every serious aspirant now shifts to Tier-2 in December 2026. Tier-1 is qualifying in nature for most posts — it screens candidates for Tier-2 — while Tier-2 performance decides the final merit for the 10,731 Group B and Group C posts. That makes the six weeks between the Tier-1 end and Tier-2 the highest-leverage preparation period of the entire cycle.

SSC CGL Tier-2: Paper Structure

As per the official SSC CGL scheme, Tier-2 consists of Paper-I (mandatory for all posts) and Paper-II for candidates who opted for the Junior Statistical Officer or Assistant Audit Officer/Assistant Accounts Officer posts:

PaperForSections (as per official scheme)
Paper-IAll postsMathematical Abilities, Reasoning & General Intelligence, English Language & Comprehension, General Awareness, Computer Knowledge Module
Paper-IIJSO / AAO (opted candidates)Statistics (specialised)
DESTSpecified postsData Entry Speed Test, as applicable

Negative marking applies in Tier-2, so your attempt strategy must balance accuracy against the number of attempts. Read the official exam notice for the exact marking scheme and qualifying marks per section before you finalise your approach.

Your 6-Week Tier-2 Preparation Roadmap

1
Week 1: Audit and reset — take a full-length Tier-2 mock on day one; identify your three weakest sections and rebuild fundamentals in mathematical abilities and English comprehension first.
2
Weeks 2–3: Section depth — work through reasoning patterns, general-awareness revision and the computer knowledge module; maintain a daily formula and vocabulary log.
3
Weeks 4–5: Mock discipline — two full mocks per week under timed conditions; analyse every error, and tune your attempt order and negative-marking strategy.
4
Week 6: Consolidation — revise error logs, take lighter mocks, and shift focus to sleep and exam-day rhythm; avoid learning new topics in the final days.
5
Documents in parallel — gather degree certificates, category certificates in the prescribed format, photo ID and photographs now, not after the result.

Posts Worth Targeting in 2026

The 10,731 vacancies cover some of the most sought-after central government posts: Assistant Section Officer (ASO), Inspector (Income Tax/Central Excise), Assistant Audit Officer (AAO), Tax Assistant, Upper Division Clerk (UDC) and Auditor, among others. Group B posts carry Level 6–8 pay and faster promotion avenues; Group C posts (Level 4–5) offer strong job security for fresh graduates. Your post preference order, filled during the application or DAF-equivalent stage as per the official process, directly shapes your final allocation — research it before Tier-2, not after.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is SSC CGL Tier-2 2026?
SSC CGL Tier-2 2026 is scheduled for December 2026, after the Tier-1 examination window (30 September to 30 October 2026) ends. Only Tier-1 qualifiers are shortlisted for Tier-2.
What papers are in SSC CGL Tier-2?
Tier-2 consists of Paper-I (mandatory for all posts, covering mathematics, reasoning, English, general awareness and computer knowledge) and Paper-II for candidates who opted for the Junior Statistical Officer or Assistant Audit/Accounts Officer posts, as per the official notification.
Is there negative marking in SSC CGL Tier-2?
Yes. As per the official SSC CGL scheme, there is negative marking in Tier-2 papers. Attempt strategy should balance accuracy with attempted questions; read the exam notice for the exact marking scheme.
What documents are needed for SSC CGL document verification?
Degree certificates and marksheets, date-of-birth proof, category certificates (SC/ST/OBC/EWS) in the prescribed format, PwBD certificates if applicable, photo ID and passport photographs — as specified in the official notification.
How many vacancies are in SSC CGL 2026?
SSC CGL 2026 fills 10,731 Group B and Group C posts across central government departments in pay levels 4 to 8 under the 7th CPC.

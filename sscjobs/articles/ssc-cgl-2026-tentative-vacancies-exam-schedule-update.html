SSC CGL 2026: Tentative Vacancies & Exam Schedule — Latest Update

Everything aspirants need to know about the latest ssc.gov.in updates on the Combined Graduate Level examination — vacancy position, schedule notices, eligibility and exam pattern.

By NAN Jobs Portal  |  Published: 13 October 2026  |  SSC Jobs 2026 home

The Staff Selection Commission conducts the Combined Graduate Level (CGL) examination for Group B and Group C posts in central ministries and departments. For SSC CGL 2026, the two updates aspirants watch most are the tentative vacancy position and the exam schedule on ssc.gov.in.

This article covers what those updates mean, the post categories, eligibility, the exam structure, and how to track the schedule and vacancy PDF yourself. Treat official SSC notices as the final word.

SSC CGL Post Categories at a Glance

CGL fills Group B and Group C posts across ministries. The broad post families typically covered include:

  • Assistant Section Officer (ASO) — administrative and secretarial work in central ministries.
  • Inspector posts — field and desk roles such as CBIC/customs inspectors and preventive officers.
  • Accountant / Auditor / Junior Accountant — finance roles in offices under CAG, CGDA and similar departments.
  • UDC, Junior Statistical Officer and other cadres — clerical and statistics posts, allocated by merit-cum-preference and reservation rules.

Eligibility Notes for SSC CGL 2026

The core criteria:

  • Educational qualification: a bachelor's degree from a recognised university or equivalent for most posts; some posts (e.g. statistical posts) require specific subjects — check the notification.
  • Age limit: varies by post (typically around 18–32 years), with relaxations for reserved categories exactly as published.
  • Nationality: as specified in the notification.

Cut-off dates for qualification and age are stated in the notification — confirm you satisfy them as on the date SSC specifies.

SSC CGL 2026 Exam Structure: Tier-I, Tier-II, Document Verification

The selection process for SSC CGL 2026 follows the Commission's standard pattern:

  • Tier-I (CBT): a computer-based screening test covering general intelligence and reasoning, general awareness, quantitative aptitude and English comprehension. Only qualifiers move to Tier-II.
  • Tier-II (CBT): a detailed stage with a common paper for all posts plus additional papers for specific categories (such as statistics posts), along with a computer-knowledge component.
  • Document Verification (DV): shortlisted candidates verify original documents before final allocation.

Paper-wise marks, question counts and timings are published in the official notification — work only from that document.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does "tentative vacancy position" mean for SSC CGL 2026?
The figures are provisional — departments can revise their indents, so the final count may differ. Keep checking ssc.gov.in for revised vacancy circulars.
What is the eligibility for SSC CGL 2026?
Broadly, a bachelor's degree from a recognised university, with age limits varying by post (generally 18–32 with category relaxations). Specific posts may need particular subjects in the degree — always check the official notification.
Is Tier-II compulsory for SSC CGL 2026?
Yes. Candidates who qualify Tier-I must appear for Tier-II, which includes the common paper plus additional papers for specific post categories. This is followed by document verification.
Where do I download the SSC CGL 2026 vacancy PDF?
From the "Notices" section of ssc.gov.in. Look for the CGL 2026 tentative vacancy position notice — it is released as a downloadable PDF.

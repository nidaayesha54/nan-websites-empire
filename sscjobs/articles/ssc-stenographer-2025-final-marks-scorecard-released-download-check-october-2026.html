SSC Stenographer 2025 Final Marks Released: Download Scorecard by 6 November — Steps & DV Guide

The Staff Selection Commission has released the final marks and scorecards for the Stenographer Grade C & D Examination 2025 on 7 October 2026 at ssc.gov.in. Log in with your Registered ID and Password to download your individual marks. The final result (25 August 2026; revised FRTA on 31 July 2026) listed the qualifiers — the scorecard now shows your actual marks. The link closes on 6 November 2026: save or print your scorecard before it is withdrawn. Next: document verification by the allotted ministries and departments, then appointment letters.

Who Can Download / What You Need

SSC has uploaded the final marks for Stenographer 2025 candidates. Keep these ready:

  • Your Registered ID
  • Your SSC portal Password
  • A printer or PDF saver — the link expires on 6 November 2026, with no re-open window

Forgot your credentials? Use the “Forgot Registration ID / Password” option on ssc.gov.in well before the deadline — not on the last day.

How to Download the SSC Stenographer Final Marks 2026

1
Visit ssc.gov.in — open the official SSC portal and go to the candidate login section.
2
Log in — enter your Registered ID and Password.
3
Open the marks link — find the final marks/scorecard link for the Stenographer Grade C & D Examination 2025.
4
Download and print — save the scorecard as a PDF and take a clear printout for your records.
5
Verify every detail — name, roll number, registration ID and marks; report any discrepancy to SSC promptly.

What Happens After the Marks — Document Verification

Qualified candidates now move to document verification (DV), conducted by the allotted ministries and departments. Watch SSC notices for your DV schedule and keep these ready in original and photocopy:

  • Admit cards and scorecards of the examination
  • Original photo ID (Aadhaar / PAN / passport)
  • Educational certificates and marksheets
  • Category / disability certificates, if applicable
  • Recent passport-size photographs
Important: Do not wait for the DV call letter to download your marks — the scorecard link closes on 6 November 2026 regardless. Download first.

SSC CPO 2026 Paper-I (20–28 November): 1,871 SI Posts — Exam Pattern, PET Standards & Prep Plan

The Staff Selection Commission has scheduled the Paper-I (computer-based examination) of the Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police and Central Armed Police Forces Examination 2026 — commonly called SSC CPO — from 20 to 28 November 2026, as per SSC's important notice dated 1 October 2026. The 2026 cycle offers 1,871 Sub-Inspector posts across Delhi Police, the CAPFs and CISF (Fire). Applications closed on 30 September and the correction window (8–10 October) has ended — so the focus now shifts entirely to preparation and the admit-card timeline. Here is everything a CPO aspirant needs for the next five weeks.

Delhi Police SI is the most coveted post in this examination — a direct Group-B officer entry into the capital's police. Note the special conditions: male candidates for Delhi Police SI must hold a valid driving licence for LMV (motorcycle and car) on the date fixed for the physical tests, and CISF Fire SI has a different educational requirement (science with PCM or a 3-year engineering diploma).

Duration is 2 hours, with 0.25 negative marking per wrong answer. Paper-I is followed by the PET/PST and medical tests, and then Paper-II (English language and comprehension) for candidates who clear the physical stage. The physical standards are demanding — start running now if you have not already, because PET failures eliminate more candidates than the written paper does.

Five-Week Preparation Plan

Quantitative Aptitude and Reasoning (100 marks combined)

These two sections decide the merit for most aspirants. Drill arithmetic (percentages, profit-loss, time-work, mensuration) and reasoning staples (series, coding-decoding, syllogisms) daily with a timer — speed with accuracy is the entire game in a 200-question paper.

General Awareness and English (100 marks combined)

Cover the last 6–8 months of current affairs with a defence and policing skew, plus static GK (polity, geography, awards). For English, error detection, idioms and comprehension passages give the fastest returns at this stage.

Physical Preparation — Start Today

The PET/PST follows Paper-I, and the gap between the written result and the physical tests is short. Build a daily running habit now (the race is the elimination event), and check your height, weight and chest measurements against the notified standards early — physical disqualifications cannot be fixed in the final week.

Note: As of mid-October 2026, SSC has not released the city intimation or admit card for CPO Paper-I — both will follow the standard pattern (city ~7–10 days before, admit card ~3–4 days before your exam date) on ssc.gov.in and the regional SSC websites.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the SSC CPO 2026 Paper-I exam?
20 to 28 November 2026, as per SSC's important notice dated 1 October 2026. The exam is computer-based across multiple shifts.
How many posts are in SSC CPO 2026?
1,871 Sub-Inspector posts: 317 in Delhi Police (Executive), 1,320 in the CAPFs (GD) and 234 Sub-Inspector (Fire) in CISF.
What is the SSC CPO Paper-I exam pattern?
200 questions for 200 marks in 2 hours: General Intelligence & Reasoning (50), GK & General Awareness (50), Quantitative Aptitude (50) and English Comprehension (50), with 0.25 negative marking.
When will the SSC CPO 2026 admit card be released?
The admit card is expected 3–4 days before the exam date, with the city intimation slip about 7–10 days before. Download both from ssc.gov.in and your regional SSC website.
What is the age limit for SSC CPO 2026?
20 to 25 years as on 1 August 2026 (18 to 30 years for Sub-Inspector (Fire) in CISF), with age relaxation for reserved categories as per SSC rules.

SSC CPO 2026 Paper-I Admit Card: 1,871 SI Posts — Expected Early November, Download Steps

The Staff Selection Commission is expected to release the SSC CPO 2026 Paper-I admit card in the first week of November 2026 — roughly 7–10 days before the exam. The Sub-Inspector in Delhi Police & Central Armed Police Forces (CPO) Examination 2026 fills 1,871 Sub-Inspector posts, with Paper-I (CBT) scheduled 20–28 November 2026. Expected Hall-Ticket Timeline

SSC issues two separate documents before every exam — do not confuse them:

  • Exam city intimation slip — expected a few days before the admit card; shows only your allotted city. It is not the hall ticket.
  • Admit card — expected in the first week of November 2026; carries your date, shift, reporting time and centre address. Only this printout, with photo ID, admits you to the hall.

Hall tickets are released batch-wise by exam date — 20 November candidates will see theirs first.

Who Can Download / What You Need

Registered candidates can download the hall ticket once it goes live. Keep these ready:

  • Your registration ID or roll number
  • Your date of birth as entered in the application
  • A printer — the admit card must be carried in print; phone copies are not accepted at the gate

How to Download the SSC CPO Admit Card 2026

1
Visit ssc.gov.in — open the Admit Card section on the homepage.
2
Select your region — choose your regional SSC website.
3
Log in — with your registration ID/roll number and date of birth.
4
Download and print — take a clear printout on plain paper; keep two copies.
5
Verify every detail — name, photo, signature, date, shift and centre address; report errors to your regional SSC office.

Exam-Day Documents Checklist

  • Printed admit card — mandatory for entry
  • Original photo ID — Aadhaar, PAN or passport
  • Reach by the reporting time on the hall ticket — late entry is not allowed
  • Check your photo and signature on the admit card are correct and clearly printed
Important: Ignore “admit card out” forwards on social media — the only genuine release is on ssc.gov.in and the regional SSC websites.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the SSC CPO 2026 admit card be released?
Not officially announced yet. Expected in the first week of November 2026 — roughly 7–10 days before Paper-I (20–28 November 2026).
What is the Paper-I exam date?
20 to 28 November 2026 — the computer-based Paper-I for 1,871 Sub-Inspector posts in Delhi Police and the CAPFs.
How can I download the SSC CPO admit card?
On ssc.gov.in, open Admit Card, select your regional SSC website, log in with your registration ID/roll number and date of birth, then download and print it.
What documents must I carry to the SSC CPO exam centre?
A printed admit card and an original photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN or passport). Reach by the reporting time, and check that your photo and signature are correct on the admit card.
What comes after SSC CPO Paper-I 2026?
Paper-I qualifiers appear for PET/PST, followed by Paper-II and finally the detailed medical examination (DME) before final selection.

How to Apply

1
Read the official notification — check eligibility, age limit, fees and vacancy details. All SSC applications go through the official portal ssc.gov.in — the One-Time Registration (OTR) system is mandatory.
2
Register on the official portal — ssc.gov.in — create your account with a valid email and mobile number for the online application.
3
Fill & submit the form — upload photo, signature and documents as specified; pay the fee online and save the confirmation page.
4
Download your admit card — released 2–3 weeks before the exam on the same official portal; check your exam city and shift.
5
Understand the exam pattern — review Prelims/Mains syllabus, marking scheme and previous-year cutoffs, then track results on the official site.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is SSC CGL?
SSC CGL (Combined Graduate Level) recruits for Group B and C posts — ASO, auditors, accountants, inspectors — in central ministries.
What is the eligibility for SSC CHSL?
12th pass from a recognized board; age 18–27 years with relaxation for reserved categories.
How do I apply on ssc.gov.in?
Complete One-Time Registration (OTR) on ssc.gov.in, then fill the live application for the exam during its window.
What is the SSC GD Constable selection process?
CBT → PET/PST (physical) → detailed medical examination → final merit by marks.
Is SSC CGL Tier-II compulsory?
Yes — candidates qualifying Tier-I appear for Tier-II, which includes Paper-I (all posts) and Paper-II (JSO/statistics).
Where do I get the SSC admit card?
Admit cards are published on the SSC regional website (ssc.gov.in regional links) before each exam.

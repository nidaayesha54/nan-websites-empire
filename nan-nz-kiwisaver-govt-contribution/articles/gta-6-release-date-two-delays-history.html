GTA 6 Release Date: The Full Story of Two Delays

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launches on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. That date is the end of a very long road: the game was first shown in December 2023 with a 2025 window, and it took two public delays, three different dates and almost three years to reach the one that finally stuck. If you have followed the wait, you know the frustration. If you are new to it, the story of the delays explains a lot about how Rockstar works — and why nobody in the industry bets against its dates any more.

The short version is simple. The date changed twice, and both times Rockstar announced the change itself, on its own channels, with the same reason: more time to finish the game to the standard players expect. There was no third delay, no silent slip and no cancellation. Every date below comes from the public record, and the current one is the date the game actually launched on.

December 2023: the 2025 window

The wait started with Trailer 1 in December 2023. It ended with a simple line: coming 2025. No month, no day, no season — just a year. That vagueness was deliberate. Rockstar had been working on the game since around 2015, and the trailer was a promise, not a schedule. Take-Two, the publisher, later narrowed the window to Fall 2025 in its investor communication, which gave the wait a shape for the first time. Players spent 2024 counting down to a season that had no date attached to it.

Behind the scenes, the 2025 target was already under strain. Bloomberg's reporting from October 2026 describes a team that knew the date was tight, with multiple systems and production tasks taking longer than expected. Nothing dramatic went wrong — no single feature broke the schedule. The game was simply bigger than the calendar allowed, and Rockstar's leadership had to choose between shipping on time and shipping finished.

May 2025: the first delay

On May 2, 2025, Rockstar announced the first delay: the game would move from 2025 to May 26, 2026. The statement said the extra time was needed to deliver the quality players expect, and it apologised for the added wait. Four days later, on May 6, 2025, Rockstar released Trailer 2 — a long, detailed look at Jason and Lucia, Vice City and Leonida that softened the blow considerably. The trailer proved the game was real and close, even if the date had moved.

May 26, 2026 felt solid at the time. It was a specific day, not a season, and the game had a trailer to match. But within months, reports began to surface that the new date was also at risk. Bloomberg later described how Rockstar North's head of development, Rob Nelson, decided the May 2026 date was not enough, and how Jennifer Kolbe, Rockstar's head of publishing, asked Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick for each extension. The decision was not one person's — it was the studio asking its parent company for room, twice.

November 2025: the second delay

On November 6, 2025, Rockstar announced the second delay: May 26, 2026 would move to Thursday, November 19, 2026. Again the reason was the same — extra months to finish the game with the level of polish players expect — and again Rockstar apologised. Take-Two published its quarterly results the same day, and Zelnick told Variety that a limited amount of additional time was required for polish. The delay came a week after Rockstar dismissed some 30 to 40 staff in the UK, a detail that fed a week of speculation the studio never addressed.

That was the last delay. In May 2026, Take-Two confirmed the date had not changed and Rockstar did not plan another one, even as rumour posts kept claiming a third slip every few days. The rumours never had a source — they were recycled headlines, and the date never moved again.

The road from the final date to launch

Once November 19, 2026 was set, the rest of the calendar fell into place. Cover art arrived on June 18, 2026, and pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, with the Standard Edition at $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition at $99.99. An Extended Look presentation followed on August 27, 2026, and the launch trailer arrived in October 2026, repeating the date. Pre-loading for digital orders began on November 10, 2026 — moved up from the November 12 date announced in June — and the boxed version, which contains a download code, went on sale on November 12, 2026.

The launch itself was the biggest in the series' history: thirteen years after GTA 5 arrived in September 2013, the next chapter finally reached players. Reports in November said pre-orders were far ahead of Take-Two's expectations, and the midnight launch in New Zealand — eighteen hours before the United States — turned the date into a rolling global event.

What about PC?

The one date that never appeared is the PC version. GTA 6 launched on consoles only, and Rockstar has announced no PC date. GamesBeat reported in October 2026, citing four unnamed sources, that a PC release is planned for the first quarter of 2027. That is a report, not an announcement — and until Rockstar says it, the PC date is a rumour with a good source, not a fact.

That distinction matters, because the delay story is exactly where rumours do the most damage. Every few days for two years, a post claimed the game had slipped again, and every few days it was wrong. The real record is short: two delays, both announced by Rockstar, both for polish, and a launch date that held for a full year before the game arrived.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many times was GTA 6 delayed?

Twice. The date moved from a 2025 window to May 26, 2026 (announced May 2, 2025), and then to November 19, 2026 (announced November 6, 2025). The change from 2025 to Fall 2025 was a narrowing of the window, not a delay.

Why was GTA 6 delayed?

Rockstar said both times that it needed more time to finish the game to the standard players expect. Looking back in October 2026, its leaders said there was no single dramatic cause — parts of the game were not coming together in time.

Is GTA 6 delayed to 2027?

No. The last delay was announced on November 6, 2025, and the game launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. Only the PC version is reported for 2027, and that report is unconfirmed.

The bottom line: the GTA 6 release date moved twice, and both moves came from Rockstar with the same reason. The wait was long — nearly three years from reveal to launch — but the date that finally arrived was the one the studio set a full year in advance, and it held.

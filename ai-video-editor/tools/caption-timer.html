Caption Timing Calculator

Paste your script below. The calculator splits it into readable caption segments, times each one to your speaking pace, and exports a ready-to-use SRT file.

Tip: average English speaking pace is about 2.5 words per second. Shorts narrations often run faster (~3 wps).

5–7 words keeps captions readable on phones.

How it works

The calculator divides your script into caption segments, assigns each a duration from your words-per-second pace (with a 0.8s minimum so fast lines stay readable), and breaks segments at sentence endings when possible. The SRT export works in Premiere, CapCut, DaVinci Resolve, YouTube, and every major editor. For the full captioning workflow — transcribe, review, style, burn in — read How to Auto-Caption Videos.

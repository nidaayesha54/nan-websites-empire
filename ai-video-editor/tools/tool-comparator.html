🏆 Best overall: Descript

Editing video as text is the biggest workflow upgrade of the decade. If you make talking-head YouTube or podcasts, start here.

💸 Best free: CapCut

Auto captions, auto reframe, and beat-sync with no paywall. The default choice for short-form creators on zero budget.

Generative extend, text-based editing and object removal inside the industry-standard timeline.

Upload a long video, get ranked short candidates with captions and virality scores. Built for repurposing at scale.

How to make a chart

  1. Type or paste data lines as Label, Value — or upload a CSV.
  2. Choose bar, line or pie.
  3. Click Draw Chart, then Download PNG to save the image.

Tip: for line charts, keep labels in order (e.g. months). For pie charts, use 8 or fewer slices for readability.

Voice Script Timing Cheat Sheet

At the standard 150 words per minute pace used in US voice-over work:

30-second ad

≈ 75 words. One hook, one benefit, one CTA. Every word must earn its place.

60-second explainer

≈ 150 words. Problem → solution → proof → CTA.

IVR greeting

≈ 20–40 words. Short, calm, and always offer a human option.

Announcement

≈ 50–100 words. Lead with the news, then the details.

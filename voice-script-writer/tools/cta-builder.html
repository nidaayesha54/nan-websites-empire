What Makes a CTA Strong?

🎯 One clear verb

"Tap", "call", "download" — never "check out our stuff sometime". The listener should know the exact next step.

⏰ A reason to act now

Deadlines, limited spots, instant rewards. Without urgency, action slides to "later" — which means never.

🛡️ Zero risk

"Free", "no credit card", "easy returns". Every CTA fights an objection — name the objection and kill it.

👤 Make it personal

"For you", "your free quote". CTAs with "you" consistently outperform generic ones.

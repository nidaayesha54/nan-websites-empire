The 5 Hook Styles, Explained

❓ Question

Opens a curiosity loop the listener must close by keep listening. Best for: ads, social video.

📈 Statistic

A surprising number earns instant authority. Best for: explainers, B2B, finance. Always use a real, verified stat.

📖 Story

"Last month, someone tried…" — narrative hooks the brain like nothing else. Best for: brand stories, testimonials.

💥 Bold Claim

Challenges what the listener believes. Best for: challenger brands, product launches. Only use claims you can back up.

🕵️ Curiosity Gap

Promises hidden knowledge. Best for: announcements, teasers. Deliver on the promise or lose trust.

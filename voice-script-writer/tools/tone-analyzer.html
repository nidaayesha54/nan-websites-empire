What the Tone Analyzer Measures

📏 Formality (0–100)

High scores mean stiff, corporate language ("utilize", "furthermore"). Voice scripts usually perform best between 20–50.

💛 Warmth (0–100)

Tracks human, welcoming language ("welcome", "thanks", "together"). Warmth builds trust in the first seconds.

⚡ Energy (0–100)

Measures punch and urgency ("now", "amazing", "limited"). Ads want 50+; IVR and announcements want calm.

It also flags filler words, weakeners ("very", "really"), over-long sentences and missing calls to action. For the full writing method, see How to Write Voice Scripts in 2026.

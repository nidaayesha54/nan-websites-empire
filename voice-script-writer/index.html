Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about VoiceScriptWriter.

Is VoiceScriptWriter free to use?

Yes. All four tools — the script generator, tone analyzer, hook writer and CTA builder — are completely free with no sign-up required.

How long should a voice-over script be?

At a natural pace of 150 words per minute, a 30-second ad needs about 75 words, a 60-second explainer about 150 words, and a 2-minute narration about 300 words. Our script generator calculates word count and duration automatically.

What does the tone analyzer check?

It scores your script on formality, warmth and energy using word-list heuristics, then gives concrete suggestions — like cutting filler words, shortening long sentences and adding a call to action.

Can I use these tools for commercial voice-over work?

Yes. Every script, hook and CTA you generate is yours to use in client projects, ads, IVR systems and announcements, royalty-free.

Do I need to create an account?

No. Everything runs in your browser — your scripts never leave your device and no account is needed.

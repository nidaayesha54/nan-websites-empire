The California Department of Human Resources (CalHR) and individual departments are posting new openings weekly on CalCareers, the state's official job portal, spanning customer service, IT, conservation, administration, and skilled trades.

California hiring works differently from most states: for most classifications you must first take and pass a state exam to get on an eligibility list before you can be hired. Understanding that system is the difference between browsing postings and actually getting hired.

How California State Hiring Works: The Exam System

Nearly every California civil-service classification requires an examination — often an online self-assessment, training-and-experience evaluation, or written test. Passing puts you on an eligibility list ranked by score; departments hire from the top ranks. You can take exams for any classification you qualify for, and most exams are open continuously online through CalCareers.

The practical order of operations: 1) find a classification that fits you, 2) take its exam on CalCareers, 3) once you are on the eligibility list, apply to specific job postings with final filing dates.

Salary rule to know: California's salary rules state that candidates new to state service are generally hired at the minimum of the pay range (or the alternate range minimum). The published ranges show where you can progress, not where you start.

Benefits of California State Employment

State jobs come with a strong total-compensation package: health, dental, and vision coverage; CalPERS pension retirement benefits; generous paid leave and holidays; and programs like Savings Plus (deferred compensation).

How to Apply for California State Jobs

  1. Create a CalCareer account on the state's official jobs portal and complete your profile.
  2. Find your classification using the portal's search — read the class specifications to confirm you meet the minimum qualifications.
  3. Take the exam. Most exams are available online; your score determines your rank on the eligibility list.
  4. Apply before the final filing date. Submit electronically through your CalCareer account (mail and drop-off are also listed on some postings).
  5. Set up job alerts. Save searches for your classifications so new postings reach you before filing dates pass.
Verify before you apply: final filing dates are strict — late applications are not accepted. Always confirm the exam requirement, filing date, and duty location on the official CalCareers posting.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to take an exam to get a California state job?

For most civil-service classifications, yes. You must pass the classification exam to get on the eligibility list before departments can hire you. Most exams are available online through CalCareers.

What is a final filing date on a California job posting?

The last day applications are accepted — for example, several current DMV and Conservation Corps postings list October 22, 2026.

What salary do new California state employees start at?

Under CalHR salary rules, candidates new to state service are generally hired at the minimum of the classification's pay range.

Where do I apply for California state jobs?

On CalCareers, the state's official jobs portal. You can apply electronically through your CalCareer account, and some postings also allow mail or drop-off.

What benefits do California state employees get?

Health, dental, and vision coverage; CalPERS pension retirement; paid leave and holidays; and the Savings Plus deferred-compensation program.

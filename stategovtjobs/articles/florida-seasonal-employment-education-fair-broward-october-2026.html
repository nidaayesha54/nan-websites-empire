Florida Seasonal Government Hiring 2026: Broward County Employment & Education Fair on Oct 27

The Urban League of Broward County hosts a free Seasonal Employment & Education Fair on October 27, 2026, in Fort Lauderdale — with free haircuts, resume printing, professional headshots, and on-site hiring from 10 AM to 1 PM. Here is how seasonal public employment works in Florida and how to turn a seasonal role into a permanent one.

The Urban League of Broward County is hosting a Seasonal Employment & Education Fair on Tuesday, October 27, 2026, from 10:00 AM to 1:00 PM at 560 Northwest 27th Avenue, Fort Lauderdale, FL 33311. Entry is free — and the event bundles job-search services that usually cost money: free haircuts (9:00–10:30 AM), resume printing, a career closet, and professional headshots, followed by the hiring fair itself.

Seasonal hiring peaks every fall as public agencies, parks systems, and service employers staff up for the holiday season and winter tourism surge. In Florida, seasonal public roles — parks and recreation aides, facility staff, administrative support, and program assistants — are a proven entry point into permanent government employment. Many career public employees started as seasonal or temporary hires.

What to Expect on October 27

Arrive early: the free services run 9:00–10:30 AM, before the fair floor opens at 10:00. Get the haircut and headshot first, print fresh resumes on site, then walk into employer conversations looking and feeling prepared. The event is organized by the Urban League of Broward County Economic Services team, which also provides ongoing workforce support beyond the single day.

Bring a base resume on a USB drive or your phone so the on-site printing produces an updated copy, and bring a list of the types of roles you want — seasonal employers hire fast and often make decisions the same week.

How Seasonal Public Employment Works in Florida

Florida state and local agencies hire seasonal and temporary workers through streamlined processes that move much faster than permanent competitive hiring. Florida's People First portal lists state openings, while counties and cities post on their own career sites. Seasonal roles typically run three to six months, often with the possibility of extension.

The strategic value of a seasonal role is access: you learn the agency's systems, meet the hiring managers, and build a track record. When permanent positions open, internal and experienced candidates consistently outcompete unknown external applicants. Treat every seasonal assignment as a months-long job interview.

From Seasonal to Permanent: The Conversion Playbook

Perform like a permanent employee from day one

Show up early, volunteer for difficult assignments, and learn the agency's procedures faster than expected. Supervisors remember seasonal workers who made their lives easier — and supervisors write the recommendations that matter when permanent requisitions open.

Learn the hiring system while you are inside

Ask colleagues how permanent hiring works at that specific agency: which portal, which examinations or assessments, and how internal candidates apply. Many agencies give current employees early notice of openings before they post publicly.

Build relationships with decision-makers

Introduce yourself to managers beyond your direct supervisor. In public-sector hiring, name recognition plus a strong work record is the closest thing to a guarantee — panels and hiring managers favor candidates they have seen perform.

Education and Training Resources at the Fair

As an Employment & Education Fair, the event also connects attendees with training programs. Short-term certifications — commercial driving, healthcare support, IT fundamentals, skilled trades — can lift you from seasonal aide roles into higher-paying permanent classifications. Ask the Urban League staff which programs have current funding and employer partnerships.

How to Make the Most of the Seasonal Job Fair

  1. Arrive by 9:00 AM. Use the free haircut, resume printing, career closet, and headshot services before the hiring floor opens at 10:00.
  2. Bring a digital copy of your resume. A USB drive or phone copy lets the on-site printing produce fresh, updated resumes.
  3. Target seasonal-to-permanent employers. Ask each employer directly: "Do seasonal hires get considered for permanent openings?"
  4. Ask about training programs. Find out which short-term certifications the Urban League currently funds — they are your ladder to higher pay.
  5. Follow up the same week. Seasonal hiring moves fast; email every employer contact within 48 hours and complete any applications immediately.

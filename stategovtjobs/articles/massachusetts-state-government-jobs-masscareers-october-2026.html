How MassCareers Works

MassCareers is the Commonwealth's centralized hiring system. You create a profile once, then search and apply across every secretariat and agency from a single account. Postings show the official title, organization, location, posting date, and whether hybrid work is possible. The state's Executive Office of Energy and Environmental Affairs is also recruiting with a window running October 14 through November 14, 2026 — a useful example of the month-long windows many Massachusetts postings carry.

Massachusetts hiring note: many Commonwealth postings list "posting date" rather than a hard closing date and close when the hiring manager has enough qualified applicants. Treat every interesting posting as time-sensitive.

How to Apply for Massachusetts State Jobs

  1. Search MassCareers. Use the official job-search link in the Key Details table and filter by organization, location, and job family.
  2. Check hybrid eligibility. Several October postings note potential hybrid schedules — confirm the expectation in the full listing.
  3. Build your profile. Complete the MassCareers profile with employment history and documents so applications take minutes.
  4. Tailor each application. Mirror the posting's language for duties and qualifications; Massachusetts agencies screen closely on minimum entrance requirements.
  5. Apply promptly. With soft closing practices, early applications get the first look.

What the Civilian Hiring Unit Does

The Division Personnel Office's Civilian Hiring Unit handles the full lifecycle of civilian employment: posting positions, collecting and reviewing resumes, determining minimum qualifications for vacancies, initiating background checks, onboarding new hires, and preparing employment correspondence. It also oversees the State Police internship program — a useful entry point for students. In other words, these are real, structured HR processes, not informal hiring.

Why Civilian Law-Enforcement Careers Make Sense

  • Specialized work, public mission: Forensic scientists analyze DNA evidence that solves real cases; attorneys handle records and legal matters for a statewide agency.
  • State benefits: New York State employment typically brings pension participation, health insurance, paid leave, and job stability that private-sector equivalents rarely match.
  • Multiple entry points: Openings range from entry-level support roles to senior professional grades like SG-25, so both new graduates and experienced professionals can find a fit.
  • Geographic reach: With positions from Albany to Ray Brook to various stations statewide, you can often find openings near where you live.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the civilian employment page: Go to the New York State Police website and open the Civilian Employment section, which lists every currently available position with its deadline.
  2. Match the minimum qualifications: Each posting lists required education and experience. The Hiring Unit evaluates qualifications against the vacancy — tailor your resume to the specific posting.
  3. Submit before the deadline: With deadlines of October 17, 23, and 26 for the current openings, there is no time to waste. Submit early in case of technical issues.
  4. Prepare for a background check: Law-enforcement agencies run background checks on civilian hires too. Be ready with references and employment history.
  5. Watch for new postings: Positions close and new ones appear constantly. If none of the current openings fit, check back weekly.

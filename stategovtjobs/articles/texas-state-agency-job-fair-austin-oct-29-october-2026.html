Texas State Agency Job Fair Oct 29: 17 Agencies in Austin

Seventeen Texas state agencies will recruit in one room in North Austin on October 29. Here is the venue, the agency list, and how to prepare for a morning that covers regulators, field agencies, and public safety.

Seventeen Texas state agencies will gather for a State Agency Job Fair in Austin on Thursday, October 29, 2026, from 9:00 AM to 12:00 PM Central. The event takes place at the Workforce Solutions Capital Area North Career Center, 9001 N IH 35, Austin, TX 78753, and it is open to all job seekers. Registration is requested through Workforce Solutions' online form, and the fair runs only until noon — arrive early.

Which Agencies Are Recruiting

According to the Workforce Solutions Capital Area event page, these agencies are scheduled to attend:

  • Office of the Governor
  • Railroad Commission of Texas
  • Texas Alcoholic Beverage Commission
  • Texas Commission on Environmental Quality (TCEQ)
  • Texas Department of Agriculture
  • Texas Department of Criminal Justice, Gregory S. Coleman Unit
  • Texas Department of Family and Protective Services
  • Texas Department of Insurance
  • Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation
  • Texas Juvenile Justice Department
  • Texas Medical Board
  • Texas Military Department
  • Texas Parks and Wildlife Department
  • Texas Veteran Leadership Program
  • Texas Veterans Commission (HR)
  • Texas Water Development Board
  • Texas Department of Transportation (TxDOT)

That spread is the point. A single morning covers regulators, field agencies, public safety, social services, and the governor's own office. The event page does not list specific openings by agency, so treat the fair as a chance to meet the people who do the hiring, learn which postings are live, and find out how each agency's application process works.

What State Work Looks Like in Austin

State jobs are not only desk jobs near the Capitol. The agencies on this list hire engineers and inspectors, caseworkers and investigators, environmental scientists and accountants, IT staff, administrative assistants, park staff, and correctional officers. Some roles are based in Austin; others are posted around the state, so ask each recruiter where the job actually reports. Recruiters can also explain how the state's application system and salary schedules work, which helps if you have only ever applied to private employers.

Veterans, mark November 5 too: the Hiring Red, White & You! veterans job fair takes place Thursday, November 5, at the Millennium Youth Entertainment Complex in East Austin. It is a different event with a different venue and focus.

How to Show Up Ready

Workforce Solutions asks attendees to sign up through its online form before the event. Bring several printed copies of your resume — seventeen agencies means a lot of tables. State applications reward detail, so spell out your experience with dates, duties, and any licenses or certifications. Veterans should bring DD-214 information and ask each agency about veteran hiring preference. Ask about benefits, retirement, and training, and get a straight answer on how long each hiring process usually takes.

Your Texas Job Fair Checklist

  1. Register first. Sign up through the Workforce Solutions Capital Area online form linked in the Key Details table.
  2. Print resumes. Bring several copies — one per agency you plan to visit, plus spares.
  3. Detail your experience. State applications reward dates, duties, and licenses — prepare a detailed version of your resume.
  4. Ask about process. Each agency hires differently; ask recruiters how their application and timeline work.
  5. Follow up the same week. Apply to the live postings recruiters mention while the conversations are fresh.

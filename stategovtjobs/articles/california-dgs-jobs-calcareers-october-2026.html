The California Department of General Services (DGS) is hiring for two positions in West Sacramento through the state's CalCareers portal, both with a final filing date of October 27, 2026. The Two Openings

Printing Plant Superintendent (JC 535324) pays $7,530–$9,355 per month. The role leads print and production operations at the Office of State Publishing, overseeing the plant that produces the state's printed materials. Expect the posting to require production management experience and familiarity with commercial printing operations — read the full job bulletin on CalCareers for the exact minimum qualifications and any required examination.

Contracts Manager (Analyst II) (JC 535311) pays $6,031–$7,547 per month. The role handles contract analysis and administration supporting DGS operations — a fit for candidates with procurement, contract management, or analytical experience in a public-sector or large-organization setting. As with all California classifications, the posting will state the minimum qualifications and whether an examination or assessment is required before appointment.

Why DGS Appeals to State Job Seekers

DGS positions offer the stability and benefits package that draw people to California state service: civil service protections, a defined retirement system, and structured promotion paths. The Office of State Publishing adds a distinctive angle — it is a working production operation, not a policy office, so both roles are hands-on and operational. For candidates already in printing, production, or procurement, these postings are a direct route into state service without a career change.

How California State Hiring Works

California hiring runs through CalCareers in three steps. First, create an account and complete the Examination/Employment Application (STD 678), describing your duties in detail. Second, take the examination or assessment for each classification you are interested in — many postings require an exam before you can be appointed, and some exams carry study guides. Third, apply for the vacancy itself, tailoring your application to the posting and attaching any requested documents such as a statement of qualifications, cover letter, or resume. Applications must be submitted electronically through your CalCareers account, or by mail or drop-off with the full application package.

Final filing means final: California's final filing date is a hard deadline. Applications submitted after October 27, 2026 will not be considered for these two postings, so complete the STD 678 and any required assessment well before the date.

How to Apply for California DGS Jobs

  1. Create a CalCareers account. Use the portal link in the Key Details table and complete your application template (STD 678).
  2. Find the job control numbers. Search JC 535324 for the Printing Plant Superintendent and JC 535311 for the Contracts Manager.
  3. Complete any required exam. Check whether the classification requires an assessment before appointment.
  4. Submit before October 27, 2026. Attach all required documents, including any statement of qualifications.
  5. Track your application. CalCareers shows your application status; watch for interview or eligibility notices.

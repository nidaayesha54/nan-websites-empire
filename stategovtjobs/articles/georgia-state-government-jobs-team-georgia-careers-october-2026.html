Featured Opening: GBI Customer Service Representative 1

The Georgia Bureau of Investigation (GBI) is hiring a Customer Service Representative 1 for Open Records at $40,000 annually. This is a sensitive government position: selection requires a full GBI background investigation covering criminal, credit, and driver history, plus a polygraph examination, fingerprinting, and drug screening. The posting explicitly notes the GBI follows federal drug statutes — applicants currently using illegal drugs or controlled substances will be disqualified. If you want a foot in the door of Georgia law enforcement support work, this is a direct route.

Featured Opening: Registrar Office Assistant

Georgia Piedmont Technical College seeks a full-time Registrar Office Assistant at its DeKalb campus, paying $35,000–$37,000 commensurate with education and experience. Duties include collecting grades, maintaining student records, preparing diploma and cap-and-gown orders, and supporting the college's student information system. It is an accessible entry point into Georgia's Technical College System with a standard day shift.

Why Georgia State Jobs Appeal Right Now

Team Georgia positions offer the classic state-government package: stable employment, a defined-benefit pension through the Employees' Retirement System of Georgia, health coverage, and paid leave. The portal also flags "Hot" and "New" jobs, making it easy to spot fresh postings across agencies from Corrections and Transportation to Behavioral Health and Revenue. For job seekers in the Atlanta metro and beyond, the sheer variety — IT, finance, facilities, social services, law enforcement — means most skill sets have a matching opening.

Apply early: Georgia agencies can close postings once they identify a qualified applicant pool — the GBI states this explicitly. Do not wait for a posted deadline that may never come.

How to Apply on Team Georgia Careers

  1. Search the portal. Visit careers.georgia.gov and filter by agency, job family, location, and schedule.
  2. Read the full posting. Check the job requisition details, compensation, shift, and any disqualifiers (especially for law-enforcement-adjacent roles).
  3. Create your profile. Register on the portal to save searches and track submissions.
  4. Submit tailored materials. Match your resume to the listed duties; GBI roles may require additional background paperwork.
  5. Set up job alerts. New Georgia postings appear daily — alerts keep you ahead of early-closing announcements.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I apply for Georgia state government jobs?

Through the official Team Georgia Careers portal at careers.georgia.gov, which lists openings across all state agencies.

What is the salary for the GBI Customer Service Representative job?

The posting lists $40,000 annually for the Customer Service Representative 1 (Open Records) position.

What does the GBI background check involve?

A GBI background investigation including criminal, credit, and driver's history checks, plus a polygraph examination, fingerprinting, and drug screening.

Do Georgia state jobs offer benefits?

Yes. Team Georgia positions typically include health coverage, retirement through the Employees' Retirement System of Georgia, and paid leave — check each posting for details.

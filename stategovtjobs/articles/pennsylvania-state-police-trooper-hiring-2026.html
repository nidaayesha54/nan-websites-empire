Pennsylvania State Police Trooper Hiring 2026: Apply by Nov 27, Earn About $103,319

The Pennsylvania State Police has opened a new trooper hiring cycle, with applications due November 27, 2026. No college credits required — just a high school diploma, a valid license, and the drive to serve. After five years of service, troopers earn approximately $103,319.

The Pennsylvania State Police (PSP) has opened a new selection cycle for state trooper cadets, and the timing could not be better for applicants. Applications for the current cadet hiring cycle must be submitted through the Commonwealth's employment website by November 27, 2026. The biggest news: the college credit requirement is gone — applicants need only a high school diploma or GED. And the payoff is substantial: after five years of service, troopers can earn approximately $103,319.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Education: High school diploma or GED certificate. The previous college credit requirement has been removed — and the effect was dramatic: PSP saw a 69% increase in applicants in 2025 compared to 2022, when the college credit requirement was still in place.
  • License: A valid driver's license from any state.
  • Age: At least 20 years old at the time of application; at least 21 and not yet 40 upon entry into the PSP Academy.
  • Fitness and background: Expect physical fitness testing, a thorough background investigation, and medical and psychological screening.

What Cadet Training Involves

Cadet training lasts approximately 28 weeks and is a full-immersion experience: formal coursework in Pennsylvania's crimes and vehicle codes, physical fitness training, and hands-on instruction in police vehicles, firearms, and specialized equipment. Upon completing the academy, cadets are promoted to the rank of Trooper and begin their careers serving communities across the Commonwealth.

Why State Trooper Careers Are Surging

Across the country, state police agencies are competing hard for recruits — and loosening old barriers to do it. Pennsylvania's removal of the college credit requirement opened the door to thousands of capable candidates who were previously screened out on paper, and the 69% applicant surge proves the demand was there. For career changers, military veterans transitioning to civilian life, and young adults seeking purpose-driven work with strong pay and benefits, the trooper path offers something few jobs can: a clear mission, structured advancement, and a pension-backed career.

How to Apply

  1. Visit PATrooper.com: Review the full application requirements, benefits, and selection process details.
  2. Submit via the Commonwealth employment website: Applications for the current cycle are due November 27, 2026 — do not wait until the final week.
  3. Start fitness training now: The academy is physically demanding. Begin a running and strength program immediately.
  4. Prepare your background: Gather employment history, references, and driving records. Background investigators will verify everything.
  5. Consider related openings: The Delaware State Police is also running its 107th recruit class drive for Fall 2026 — neighboring-state applicants may qualify for multiple agencies.
Military veterans take note: PSP explicitly welcomes applicants transitioning from the military. Your service record, discipline, and leadership experience are exactly what trooper selection panels value — and veterans' preference may apply in the hiring process.

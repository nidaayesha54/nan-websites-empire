GTA 6 Counties: The 5 Counties of Leonida Explained

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and on launch day Rockstar posted a printable map of the whole state on its downloads page — a print-ready image, 5,775 by 7,900 pixels, that settles a question players had argued about for three years: how is Leonida divided? The answer is five counties, and Vice City is only one part of one of them. This guide explains the five counties, where each one sits, and how the six regions Rockstar named before launch fit inside them.

The split matters because Leonida is not one city with countryside attached — it is a whole state, and the counties are how the game organises it. The police, the sheriffs, the radio and the road signs all follow county lines, and knowing which county you are in tells you what kind of place you are driving through before you even see it.

The five counties at a glance

The launch-day map splits Leonida into five counties. Lummox County is the north, the mountainous end of the state. Kelly County is the west, along the Gulf coast. Leonard County is the center-east, between the lake and the Atlantic beaches. Vice-Dale County is the southeast, and it holds the city itself. Mariana County is the south, covering the wetlands and the island chain. Vice City sits in the southeast, inside Vice-Dale County, and it is the county seat — the biggest city in the state, and the one everything else is measured against.

That is the whole division, and it is the first map Rockstar has published for the game. Before launch, the geography came from six named regions and a handful of screenshots; the counties were pieced together from road signs, police liveries and sheriff's office branding, and different fan maps disagreed about the borders. The launch-day map replaces all of that with one picture, and the counties on it are the ones this guide uses.

Vice-Dale County: the city and the beach

Vice-Dale County is the southeast corner, and it is where most players will spend most of their time. It holds Vice City itself — the metropolis, the glass-tower downtown, the art-deco beachfront of Ocean Beach — plus Vice Beach on the barrier island to the east, the VC Port, and Vice City International Airport, printed in full on the map, southwest of the city. The county is Rockstar's version of Miami-Dade County, and it shows: the city, the beach, the port and the airport are all within a short drive of each other, and the county's roads are the busiest in the state.

Vice City is one of the six regions Rockstar named before launch, and it is the only one that is a city. The other five are places — wetlands, islands, a forgotten coast, a factory town and a national park — and they all sit outside Vice-Dale. If you never leave this county, you will see the game's biggest moments, but you will miss most of its map.

Mariana County: wetlands and the Keys

Mariana County is the south, and it is the wildest part of Leonida. It holds the Grassrivers wetlands — the mangroves, the flooded plains, the alligators — plus the Gulf towns of Watson Bay and Capri, and the Leonida Keys, the island chain strung along Route 1A from Key Lento down to Mile Zero Air Station. The Keys are one of the six named regions, and Grassrivers is another, so this one county holds two of the game's most distinct places: a swamp where the roads end, and a chain of islands where the road is a bridge.

The Grassrivers sit just north of Mariana County's edge in some descriptions and inside it in others — the launch-day map puts the wetlands in the south, southwest of the airport, and the county line follows the water. What matters for players is the feel: Mariana is where the game gets quiet, wet and dangerous, and where the airboats, the fishing and the diving all live.

Kelly County: the forgotten coast and the factory town

Kelly County is the west, along the Gulf coast, and it holds two of the six named regions: Port Gellhorn and Ambrosia. Port Gellhorn is the forgotten coast — cheap motels, shut-down attractions, empty strip malls, a dirt bike, and a raceway — the place Florida's smaller tourist spots go when the tourists stop coming. Ambrosia is the factory town at the heart of the state, home to the Allied Crystal sugar refinery and a local biker gang, with freight infrastructure and industry instead of beaches.

The two places could not be more different, and that is the point of Kelly County: it is the working side of Leonida, the coast that did not get the neon and the town that did not get the tourists. The Starlet Motel, where Jason and Lucia hide out in Chapter 2, sits in Port Gellhorn, and the county's roads are the ones you will drive when the story takes you west.

Leonard and Lummox: the east and the north

Leonard County is the center-east, running from Lake Leonida in the middle of the state to the Atlantic beaches of Waning Sands on the east coast. It is the county you cross when you drive from the city toward the north, and its lake is one of the biggest landmarks on the map — a wide stretch of water with towns on its shore and roads running around it. Leonard is the quiet middle of Leonida, and it is easy to drive through without noticing it, which is exactly why it rewards a slower look.

Lummox County is the north, the mountainous end of the state, and it holds Mount Kalaga National Park — the sixth named region, up against the state's northern border, with hunting, fishing, off-road trails and paranoid radicals in the backwoods. The park is the game's version of north Florida and Providence Canyon in Georgia: forests, canyons and rivers, a long way from the neon of Vice City, and the place where the map stops being a city and starts being a wilderness.

FAQ

How many counties are in GTA 6? Five: Lummox in the north, Kelly in the west, Leonard in the center-east, Vice-Dale in the southeast and Mariana in the south. Vice City sits in Vice-Dale County.

Is Vice City the whole GTA 6 map? No — Vice City is one of six regions Rockstar named. The others are the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park, and they sit across the five counties.

Where can I see the map? Rockstar posted a printable map of Leonida on launch day, November 19, 2026, as a free download on its downloads page, in regular and print-ready versions.

The bottom line: Leonida is five counties, not one city. Vice-Dale holds the neon, Mariana holds the swamp and the islands, Kelly holds the forgotten coast and the factory town, Leonard holds the lake and the beaches, and Lummox holds the mountain park. Learn the counties and the whole state starts to make sense.

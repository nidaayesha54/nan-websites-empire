The State of Illinois hires through a single official portal — illinois.jobs2web.com — and three postings on it have deadlines landing in the next ten days. The most urgent is the Program Integrity Auditor II role at the Illinois Department of Transportation: four vacancies in Schaumburg paying $6,460 to $9,337 per month, and it closes October 23, 2026. If you are targeting Illinois state work, that application needs to go in now.

The role sits in the DOT's Bureau of Investigations & Compliance, and the state notes it is a union position under the RC062 bargaining unit. The second DOT opening is a Prequalification Engineer (Civil Engineer VI) in Springfield, paying $7,517 to $11,692 per month and closing October 27, 2026. The third posting is a Learning and Outreach Coordinator at the Executive Ethics Commission in Springfield, paying $72,000 to $82,000 per year and closing November 1, 2026 — a non-code position that requires a resume.

Apply immediately for the Auditor II posting. Four DOT auditor vacancies closing October 23 means the application window is down to hours. Illinois accepts applications only through its electronic portal — email and paper applications are not considered, so do not waste time on unofficial routes.

What the Current Illinois Postings Offer

Illinois state postings frame the pitch as a package: flexible schedules, benefits, and retirement plans, plus structured career advancement. The current openings back that up with transparent pay ranges published on every announcement — a genuine advantage over postings that hide salary until the interview. Hybrid schedules appear across these postings, ranging from one to five days per week in the office depending on operational needs, so check each announcement for its specific arrangement.

The Department of Innovation & Technology (DoIT) is also running a Careers Webinar: Overview of IT Opportunities and the State Hiring Process on Thursday, October 22, 2026, from 2:00 to 3:00 PM — today. Illinois Application Rules Worth Knowing

Illinois postings carry a few rules that trip up first-time applicants:

  • Electronic applications only. Every posting states that applicants must apply through illinois.jobs2web.com, and that applications submitted by email or in paper form (mail, fax, hand delivery) will not be considered. State employees use the internal SuccessFactors career portal instead.
  • Leave salary history off your resume. Under the Illinois Equal Pay Act, the State does not seek, request, or require an applicant's wage or salary history — and postings explicitly tell candidates not to include it in resumes or profile materials.
  • Work authorization is required at application. To be considered for permanent employment, applicants must be authorized to work in the United States on a full-time basis. DoIT postings add that the agency does not sponsor employment visas and only accepts W-2 candidates, with no third-party submissions.
  • Veterans' preference applies. Illinois and its CMS recognize veterans' preference for positions under the Personnel Code, and there is a formal preference application for eligible candidates.

How to Apply for Illinois State Jobs

  1. Go to illinois.jobs2web.com. Search by job requisition ID (59367, 59377, 59411) to land directly on these postings.
  2. Create your profile. Build a complete profile well before the deadline.
  3. Attach a resume with no salary history. The Auditor II posting's salary-history rule applies statewide; leave compensation details out entirely.
  4. Answer every supplemental question. Incomplete applications are screened out before a human sees them.
  5. Submit before the closing date. Postings close on the listed date; the Auditor II posting closes October 23, so submit early in the day at the latest.

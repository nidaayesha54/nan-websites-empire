Tennessee State Government Jobs 2026: 262 Openings, Pay Bands & How to Apply

The State of Tennessee is hiring across agencies this October, with job boards listing 262 state government openings statewide — from Nashville headquarters roles to field positions in Ripley, Pikeville, Murfreesboro, and Cookeville.

The range shows the breadth: an HR Generalist 2 in Nashville at $4,608–$5,751/month, an Electronic Security Systems Specialist 2 at $5,151–$6,437/month, a TDOC Instructor in Pikeville at $4,627–$8,231/month, a Nutritionist 3 in Cookeville at $4,098–$5,129/month, and an Inmate Jobs Coordinator in Ripley at $3,631–$5,434/month.

Tennessee quotes pay monthly — multiply by 12 for the annual figure — and pairs it with the classic state benefits package. Here is how the openings, pay bands, and application process work.

What's Open This Month

Corrections dominates the listings. The Tennessee Department of Correction (TDOC) is hiring Case Managers, Instructors, and an Inmate Jobs Coordinator — roles supporting rehabilitation, education, and facility operations across the state. Requirements run from five years of relevant professional experience to specialized instructional credentials.

Beyond corrections: HR Generalist 2 roles advising managers on hiring, Electronic Security Systems Specialist 2 positions for candidates with a bachelor's plus four years of experience, and Nutritionist 3 openings in public-health settings. Every posting carries a job number (e.g., 10062026-81452) — quote it when you apply or inquire.

Pay Bands and the Monthly Quirk

Tennessee posts monthly salary ranges, which can look modest until you annualize them: $5,151/month is roughly $61,800/year, and the TDOC Instructor band tops out near $98,800/year. Ranges reflect the state's pay plan — your offer lands where your experience meets the band.

Add the benefits: state health coverage, retirement, and paid leave. For roles like nutritionist and instructor, the mission appeal — public health, second chances through education — is part of the compensation story too.

How Tennessee State Hiring Works

Applications run through Tennessee's official state careers portal. Search by keyword or job number, read the education and experience block closely — Tennessee screens on stated minimums — and note any necessary special qualifications, like a valid vehicle operator's license for some positions.

Build one profile and reuse it across postings, but tailor each application to the posting's language. State HR compares your experience against the announcement nearly line by line — mirror its terminology, list month-and-year dates, and submit before the closing date.

How to Apply for Tennessee State Government Jobs

  1. Search the official state careers portal. Use keywords or exact job numbers (like 10062026-81452) to pull up each posting.
  2. Read the education and experience block. Tennessee screens on stated minimums — confirm you meet them before applying.
  3. Note special qualifications. Some roles require a valid vehicle operator's license or a clean recent driving record.
  4. Annualize the monthly pay. Multiply the posted range by 12 so you compare offers accurately.
  5. Tailor each application. Mirror the posting's terminology and list month-and-year employment dates.
  6. Submit before the closing date. Incomplete applications are screened out — double-check every required field.

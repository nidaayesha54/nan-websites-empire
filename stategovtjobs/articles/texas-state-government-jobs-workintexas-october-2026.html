Texas State Government Jobs October 2026: Streamlined WorkInTexas Application, DPS & Open Roles

Texas — which calls itself the number-one state for new jobs — overhauled its state hiring process this year. In March 2026, Governor Greg Abbott and the Texas Workforce Commission (TWC) launched a streamlined State of Texas Application on WorkInTexas.com, replacing the old paperwork-heavy process with a modern two-step application: upload your resume and complete a brief questionnaire.

The change aligns state hiring with private-sector practices and opens the door to a wider pool of applicants. Agencies from Public Safety to the Governor's office have active openings.

The Streamlined Application: What Changed

Under the modernized system, job seekers submit applications for state jobs after simply uploading a resume and completing a short questionnaire on WorkInTexas.com. The update saves applicants time, creates a smoother user experience, and — in TWC's words — gives Texans "a direct link to rewarding careers" in state government. It is part of a broader state effort to cut red tape in government hiring.

Practical takeaway: the faster process means postings can move quickly from application to interview.

Featured Openings: October 2026

Texas Department of Public Safety (DPS)

Texas DPS — the agency behind driver licensing, highway patrol, and criminal records — is recruiting professionals in Austin and Weslaco across compliance, IT, and legal functions. Recent openings include a Manager for ADA & WC Compliance in Austin, a Network Support Specialist III (In-Car) in Weslaco, and a Crime Records Attorney in Austin.

Office of the Governor

The Governor's office lists current openings with published salary ranges, including Scheduler ($4,583–$5,000/month), Travel Accountant ($4,000–$4,550/month), Grant Manager ($5,000–$6,000/month), Budget and Policy Advisor ($9,166–$11,667/month), and Assistant General Counsel roles up to $14,167/month. Some postings sit on "Veteran Hold" or "On Hold" status — check the current status on the official employment page before applying.

Texas Council for Developmental Disabilities (TCDD)

TCDD is hiring a Public Policy Analyst in Austin (hybrid schedule, up to 10% travel) to monitor disability policy and support legislative advocacy — monthly salary $4,263–$5,521, part of the total compensation package for Texas state employees.

Application detail: some Texas agencies still require the standard State of Texas application form plus supplements (transcripts, writing samples) rather than a resume alone — the State Securities Board, for example, asks for a completed state application, a supplement, transcripts, a writing sample, and a resume. Read each posting's "How to Apply" section carefully.

How to Apply for Texas State Jobs

  1. Create a WorkInTexas.com account and upload a current, plain-format resume.
  2. Search by agency or keyword — filter for Texas DPS, the Governor's office, or your target agency.
  3. Read "How to Apply" on each posting. Most use the streamlined two-step process; some agencies still require the standard state application plus supplements.
  4. Complete the questionnaire and submit. Answer the brief questionnaire honestly and completely.
  5. Set job alerts so new postings in your field reach you immediately.
Verify before you apply: posting statuses (Open, On Hold, Veteran Hold) change frequently. Always confirm the current status, salary range, and application requirements on the official agency page or WorkInTexas.com listing.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the streamlined State of Texas Application?

Launched in March 2026 by the Governor and the Texas Workforce Commission, it lets job seekers apply for state jobs on WorkInTexas.com by uploading a resume and completing a brief questionnaire — replacing the older, heavier process.

Where do I apply for Texas state government jobs?

On WorkInTexas.com, the state's official job bank. Some agencies also list openings on their own employment pages, such as the Office of the Governor.

What jobs is Texas DPS hiring for in 2026?

Recent Texas DPS openings include compliance management, IT network support, and legal roles (such as a Crime Records Attorney) in Austin and Weslaco. Check the current DPS postings for live openings and deadlines.

What does 'Veteran Hold' mean on a Texas job posting?

It indicates the posting's status regarding veterans' preference processing. Check the official employment page for the current status before applying.

What benefits do Texas state employees receive?

A competitive package through the Employees Retirement System of Texas: health coverage, retirement, and paid leave.

What is NAN Zakat Calculator?

NAN Zakat Calculator is a free online tool that helps Muslims work out their annual zakat — 2.5% of net zakatable wealth above the nisab threshold. Enter your cash, gold, silver, investments and business assets, subtract your debts, and the calculator checks the nisab and computes your zakat instantly.

Private by design

Every calculation runs locally in your browser with JavaScript. We have no accounts, no databases and no analytics that see your numbers — what you type never leaves your device.

Our content promise

Our zakat guidance follows widely-accepted scholarly standards (2.5% rate, 87.48 g gold / 612.36 g silver nisab, one lunar-year hawl). Where scholars differ — for example on jewellery or pensions — we say so openly and advise consulting a qualified scholar. This site is an educational tool, not a religious ruling; see our Disclaimer.

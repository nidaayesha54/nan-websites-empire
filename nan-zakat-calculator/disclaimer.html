Educational tool, not a fatwa

NAN Zakat Calculator is an educational tool. Its results are arithmetic estimates based on widely-accepted standards (2.5% of net zakatable wealth above a nisab of 87.48 g gold or 612.36 g silver, held for one lunar year). They are not a religious ruling (fatwa) and not professional financial advice.

Scholarly differences

Scholars legitimately differ on several points this tool simplifies: whether worn gold jewellery is zakatable, how to treat pensions and retirement accounts, valuation of business partnerships, long-term debts, and which nisab (gold or silver) to apply. Where the calculator must pick one approach, it uses the most common position and says so. For complex or high-value situations, consult a qualified scholar you trust before paying.

Prices change

Nisab values depend on live gold and silver prices. The default prices in the calculator are 2026 estimates — always update them to your local market price on your zakat date.

How Baby Support Works in Singapore

The current Baby Bonus Scheme (children born before 1 April 2027)

The Baby Bonus cash gift is paid into your Child Savings Account in tranches: for a 1st or 2nd child, S$3,000 at birth, S$1,500 at 6 and 12 months, S$1,000 at 18 months, then S$400 every 6 months from age 2 to 6.5 (total S$11,000). For a 3rd and subsequent child the gift totals S$13,000.

The Child Development Account (CDA) is a co-savings account: the government gives a First Step Grant (S$5,000 for 1st/2nd children; S$10,000 for 3rd+ children born from 18 February 2025 under the Large Families Scheme) and then matches your own savings dollar-for-dollar up to the cap (S$4,000 / S$7,000 / S$9,000 / S$15,000 by birth order). CDA funds can be used at approved childcare, kindergartens and healthcare providers.

The new SG Child Support Package (children born from 1 April 2027)

Announced in 2026, the new package raises direct support to around S$70,000 per child:

Also coming: from the same announcement, parents of Singapore-citizen children aged 12 and below get 8, 10 or 12 days of childcare leave a year depending on whether they have one, two or three such children — up from 6 days (or 2 days for ages 7–12) today.
Worked example: A first child born in November 2026 falls under the current scheme: S$11,000 cash gift + S$5,000 CDA First Step + up to S$4,000 co-matching + S$5,000 MediSave grant = S$25,000 in support (plus a S$5,000 tax rebate). The same child born in May 2027 gets the new package worth about S$69,500.

FAQs

When does the new SG Child Support Package start?

For children born or adopted on or after 1 April 2027. Children born before that date stay on the current Baby Bonus Scheme — transition rules apply to births near the cutover, so confirm your eligibility on LifeSG.

Who qualifies for Baby Bonus / the Child Support Package?

The child must be a Singapore citizen. For the new package, the child must be born on or after 1 April 2027 and be a Singapore citizen.

How does the CDA co-matching work?

You save money into your child's Child Development Account, and the government matches it dollar-for-dollar up to the cap for your child's birth order. If you don't save, you don't get the match — it is "use it or lose it" while the child is young.

What are Child Credits?

Under the new package, the child receives S$2,000 a year in cash from age 1 to 16 — S$32,000 in total — providing steady support through the school years instead of concentrating everything at birth.

What is the Large Families Scheme?

For 3rd and subsequent Singapore-citizen children born from 18 February 2025, the CDA First Step Grant doubles to S$10,000 (from S$5,000), and further support for large families is being developed.

Is this calculator official?

No — it is an independent, indicative estimate based on government announcements. Your exact payout schedule is available at go.gov.sg/bbfamilyview and in the LifeSG app after birth registration.

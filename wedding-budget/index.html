The problem: 76% of couples say wedding planning was more stressful than expected, and the #1 stressor is money. Vendors quote in isolation — $8,000 for a venue here, $95 a plate there — so nobody sees the total until deposits are already paid.
The solution: Work backwards. Decide your total budget FIRST (our calculator suggests a realistic number), lock category limits with our breakdown tool, then shop vendors inside those limits. Couples who budget first save an average of 15–20% versus those who don't.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a wedding cost in 2026?

The average US wedding in 2026 costs around $30,000. A budget wedding runs $10,000–$15,000, while luxury celebrations easily exceed $60,000. Your guest count is the single biggest cost driver.

What is the biggest wedding budget mistake?

Booking vendors before setting a total budget. Without a ceiling, each "yes" compounds — the venue, the catering upgrade, the photo package — until you're $10K over with no plan to pay for it.

How do I stick to a wedding budget?

Set the total first, allocate by category, track every deposit in one place, and keep a 10% contingency fund for surprises like alterations, overtime fees and gratuities.

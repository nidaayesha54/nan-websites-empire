How much does a wedding cost?

The short answer: about $30,000 on average. The useful answer: anywhere from $5,000 to $100,000+ — and this article shows you exactly what moves the number.

What drives the cost most?

  1. Guest count (biggest factor). Each guest costs roughly $100–$180 all-in. The difference between 80 and 150 guests is about $10,000.
  2. Venue + catering (50–55% of budget). These two categories dwarf everything else combined.
  3. Geography. New York City weddings average nearly double those in the Midwest.
  4. Day of week & season. Saturday in June is peak pricing; Friday in January can save 25%+.

The costs couples forget

Beyond the big categories: alterations ($300–$800), gratuities (15–20% on catering), vendor meals, marriage license, postage, overtime fees, and a day-of coordinator. Budget a 10% contingency — you'll use it.

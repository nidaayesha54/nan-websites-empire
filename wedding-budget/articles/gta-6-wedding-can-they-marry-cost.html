GTA 6 Wedding: What It Would Take (and Cost)

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Every love story ends with a wedding, and GTA 6 is a love story — so the question was inevitable: can Jason and Lucia get married? The honest answer is that nobody outside Rockstar knows. Rob Nelson, Rockstar North's co-studio head, was asked directly in an interview with Edge magazine, published October 29, 2026, and he would neither confirm nor deny whether the couple can marry. That is not a yes, and it is not a no. Rockstar simply has not said — and this guide explains what is known, what the relationship system actually does, and what a Leonida wedding would take and cost if it ever happens.

The question matters because the wedding is the one thing the game never promises. Everything else about the relationship is confirmed and documented. The marriage is the blank space, and the blank space is where the budget planning starts.

What Rockstar has said: nothing, on purpose

The only time marriage has come up on the record is that Edge interview, published October 29, 2026, by a writer who watched Nelson play the game for 90 minutes at Rockstar North. Nelson refused to confirm or deny whether the couple can marry. Nothing else Rockstar has released mentions a wedding. In interviews with IGN and with the Japanese magazine Famitsu, Nelson goes deep on the relationship without the word marriage coming up, and Rockstar's own character pages never describe Jason and Lucia as married.

Edge did report one suggestive line: you may define the relationship's closeness and intimacy, "perhaps even its legal status". That "perhaps" is doing a lot of work, and Nelson's refusal to confirm or deny sits right next to it. Treat every "GTA 6 lets you marry" claim you see as unconfirmed until the game itself answers it — and do not plan a wedding around a rumour.

The relationship system: four levels, and the real budget

What is confirmed is a relationship you build up or let slide, with four levels and Hot Together at the top. Hold Right on the D-pad to open the partner menu and see where the couple stands, and every gesture your partner appreciates puts a heart on screen. What pushes it up: greeting your partner when you meet up, holding hands as you walk, doing activities together — from sports to robberies — and answering your partner's texts. Nelson's own list, as he told IGN, adds pool, mini golf, kayaking, and "robbing stores together, robbing banks together."

That is the real wedding budget in GTA 6: not money, but time. The relationship is yours to keep or yours to lose, in Nelson's words — "We start them wanting to be together, and wanting to give this relationship of theirs another shot. But, as the game progresses, it's yours to keep or yours to lose." The investment is hours together, not dollars, and the payoff is a story that changes with how well you look after each other.

The limits: what a wedding cannot buy

There are firm limits on the relationship, and they come straight from Rockstar. They stay partners in crime: Nelson told IGN they "will remain partners in crime" whether they are romantically close or not. There is no gameplay bonus — asked whether being a couple gives a benefit, Nelson told IGN there is "not a particular gameplay benefit that I can think of." The romance is about what you want for them. It is only Jason and Lucia: nothing Rockstar has published mentions romancing anyone else in Leonida. And crime stays separate: the relationship is its own system, apart from the Criminal Profile that rates what kind of criminals Jason and Lucia are.

The romance can also fade. Rockstar says the relationship is yours to nurture or yours to lose, and the couple can break up as a romantic pair — though the working partnership is not lost, and the circumstances of the story require them to stay together for most of it. A wedding, if it exists, would have to survive all of that: a couple that can break up, a story that needs them together, and a studio that will not say the word.

If they did marry: the Leonida price list

Suppose the answer turns out to be yes. What would a Vice City wedding cost, in prices the game actually shows? Start with the look: a haircut at Pearl House costs $150, and the city's 70 clothing stores carry the outfits. The celebration: a private dance at one of Leonida's three strip clubs — Delights Cabaret in Port Gellhorn, Nine1Nine and Jack of Hearts in Vice City — costs $300, with $50 tips during the dance. Drinks run $5 to $9 at the bar, with a $10 tip option. The getaway car: steal one and sell it at a Pay 'n' Spray, or keep the Vapid Ganado parked outside the Keys house.

None of those prices is a wedding price — the game has no wedding vendor, no ring shop, no venue booking. But they are the closest thing Leonida has to a celebration budget, and they add up to a useful truth: a Vice City wedding would be cheap by real-world standards and expensive by Leonida standards, where a $300 night out is real money. Bank your cash first. Pocket cash is lost when you die or get arrested, and a wedding fund in your pocket is a donation to the next mugger.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can Jason and Lucia get married in GTA 6?

Unconfirmed. Rob Nelson refused to confirm or deny it when Edge asked in October 2026, and nothing Rockstar has published since mentions a wedding.

How does the relationship system work?

Four levels, Hot Together at the top. Greet your partner, hold hands, do activities together, answer texts — each appreciated gesture puts a heart on screen. It can fade if you neglect it.

Does being a couple give a gameplay bonus?

No. Nelson told IGN there is no particular gameplay benefit. The romance is about what you want for the characters, and they stay partners in crime either way.

The bottom line: Rockstar will not say whether Jason and Lucia can marry, and no wedding exists until the game says so. What exists is a four-level relationship that rewards time together — and if the big day ever comes, the celebration prices of Leonida are already on the menu.

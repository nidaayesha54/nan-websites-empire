FAQs

Is this really free? Any limits?

Yes — 100% free, unlimited QR codes, no signup and no watermarks. Everything runs in your browser, so there is nothing to pay for.

Do my QR codes expire or get tracked?

No. The codes are static — your data is encoded directly into the QR pattern itself. There is no tracking redirect and no server involved, so they work forever and nobody can count your scans.

Is my WiFi password uploaded anywhere?

No. The WiFi QR code is generated locally in your browser with JavaScript. Your network name and password never leave your phone or computer.

Why won't my camera start?

Camera scanning needs two things: an HTTPS page (this site is secure) and your permission. If you denied access, allow the camera in your browser's site settings and try again — or simply upload a QR image instead.

Can I scan a QR code from a screenshot?

Yes. Use the image upload above with any PNG, JPG or WebP screenshot or photo. Make sure the full QR code, including its quiet white border, is visible and sharp.

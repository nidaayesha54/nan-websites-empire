GTA 6 Beginner Tips: Your First Day in Vice City

A few lessons are worth learning before you learn them the hard way: players have already lost hours of progress and full pockets of cash. This guide covers the seven things to do first.

1. Save After Every Mission — By Hand

The single most important habit in GTA 6 is manual saving. The autosave is unreliable: it only fires after a mission ends, the Prologue never autosaves at all, and autosaves only appear in the Load Game menu — never in the Save Game menu. Crashes have already cost players hours; one streamer lost around ten hours of progress to a crash. After every mission, open Pause, then Game, then Save Game, and save by hand. Rotate two or three of your fifteen manual slots so you always have a recent fallback, and save again before any mission that looks tricky.

2. Bank Your Cash Before You Lose It

You can only carry $20,000 in your pocket at a time, and pocket cash is lost when you die or get arrested — and it does not carry into Chapter 2. That makes the bank the most important habit after saving. Stash spare money under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom, and open the BuckMe bank account once the mission "Buck Me" is available, then deposit your cash at ATMs. There are 454 ATMs across Leonida, so there is always one nearby. Banked money is safe money. Deposit after every job, not at the end of the day.

3. Earn First — Missions Are Money-Gated

If no mission marker appears, you probably have not done anything wrong: some missions only open once Jason and Lucia have earned enough money. That is how the game is built — the next mission waits until your cash reaches the right level. So when the story seems to stall, go earn. Side jobs pay: towing, deliveries, and Pilot Pete pays around $20,000. Kenton's treasure dives pay around $1,400 per wreck with almost no risk, once they unlock after the mission "Island Dreams". Jewelry store robberies pay around $20,000 each. Earn steadily, bank everything, and the next mission will open.

4. Learn the New Wanted Rules

GTA 6 rebuilt the wanted system, and the old habits will get you caught. The police act only on what they actually witness — there is no magic GPS tracking you. If no cop and no witness sees what you did, the law does not magically find you. That changes everything about how you play: a quiet street at night is a different place from a crowded boulevard at noon, and witnesses matter as much as patrol cars — a bystander who sees a crime can report it, and that report brings the police to where you were. When the police are after you, the escape is breaking line of sight: duck down a side street, change vehicles, put distance between you and the scene. The police search where they saw you, not where you are. Stop being seen, and you stop being chased.

5. Switch Characters the Right Way

Free switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks only after the "Breakfast Run" mission in Chapter 2. Before that, the story picks the character, and some missions lock one character — that is normal, not a bug. Once it unlocks, hold Right on the D-pad to open the Partner Menu, then press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox. If the partner's portrait is greyed out, they are busy — for example, in a RydeMe — so wait and retry. Learn the switch early — the game expects you to use both leads.

6. Fix the Frame Rate Feel

GTA 6 runs at 30 frames per second on every console, and there is no 60 fps mode — that is the same on PS5, PS5 Pro, Xbox Series X, and Series S. You cannot raise the frame rate, but you can make it feel better. On PS5, clear the cache and turn off HDCP in the system settings. On Xbox, enable VRR and set the display to 120Hz, which makes the drops less noticeable. In the game, try the Motion Blur Strength slider low and then high, pull the camera back to the far view, and play in shorter sessions with a light on if the motion bothers you. Set these before your first big mission, not after it.

7. Set Up Before You Play

Before your first session, do the boring setup — it saves a ruined evening. Make sure you have 212 GB free, because that is the size of the install, and install the PS5 system software update 26.07-14.23.00 if you have not already. If the game looks locked, do not delete the 212 GB install: GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time in your store region, and after the unlock time, sign in to the account that bought it and use Restore License. Remember: there is no PC version at launch, and no online mode at launch — GTA 6 is single-player, so connection errors are your console or network. Set the game up right, save by hand, bank your cash, and Vice City is yours.

AI Video Banane Wale 10 Best Apps (2026) — Free और Paid

AI video tools ne video banana itna aasaan kar diya hai ki ab camera, actors aur editing skills ke bina bhi professional videos ban sakte hain. Lekin sawal hai — kaun sa tool best hai? Is guide mein hum 2026 ke top 10 AI video apps ki ranking de rahe hain, unki कीमत, free plan aur best-use ke saath।

1. Runway — Professional Quality Ka King

Runway ka Gen-3 model abhi industry standard hai। Text ya image se realistic video banta hai, aur built-in editing suite mein color grading, inpainting sab milta hai। कीमत: lagbhag $12/month se। Free: limited credits। Best for: marketing videos, short films, YouTube content।

2. Kling AI — Free Mein Best Quality

Chinese company ka Kling AI quality mein Runway ko takkar deta hai — aur daily free credits deta hai! Longer clips (10 second tak) aur smooth character motion iski strength hai। Best for: beginners jo free mein start karna chahte hain।

3. Luma Dream Machine — Cinematic Shots

Agar aapko cinematic, dreamy shots chahiye to Luma best hai। Iska free trial generous hai। Best for: creative shorts, Reels, artistic content।

4. Pika — Fun Effects Aur Stylized Video

Pika effects aur transformations mein strong hai — "melt", "explode", "squish" jaise viral effects। कीमत: ~$8/month se। Best for: TikTok/Reels viral content।

5. HeyGen — AI Avatar Spokesperson

Apne script ko 40+ languages (Hindi समेत) mein bolne wala realistic avatar बनाएं। Business explainers ke liye perfect। कीमत: ~$24/month se, 1 free video/month। Best for: course creators, businesses, ads।

6. Synthesia — Corporate Training Videos

140+ languages, professional templates — corporate training aur presentations ke liye #1। कीमत: ~$22/month se। Best for: companies, educators।

7. CapCut — Free All-Rounder (Beginners Ke Liye Best)

CapCut completely free hai aur AI features se bhara hai — auto-captions, Hindi text-to-speech, templates, effects। Phone par hi professional editing। Best for: EVERY beginner — pehle yahi try karein!

8. InVideo — Marketing Templates Ka Khazana

5000+ templates, stock footage library — text likho, video ready। कीमत: ~$28/month se (free watermark ke saath)। Best for: social media ads, promos।

9. Pictory — Script Se Auto Video

Blog post ya script paste karo — Pictory auto visuals, voiceover aur captions laga deta hai। Faceless YouTube channels ke liye bahut popular। कीमत: ~$19/month se।

10. ElevenLabs — Best AI Voice (Hindi समेत)

Video achha hai lekin voice robotic lage to sab bekaar। ElevenLabs 29 languages mein human-like voice deta hai — Hindi voice quality excellent hai। Free: 10,000 characters/month। Best for: voiceovers, dubbing।

विज्ञापन (Ad Slot — In-Content)

Beginners Ke Liye Kaun Sa Combo Best Hai?

Agar aap bilkul naye hain to ye free combo try karein:

  • Video editing: CapCut (free)
  • AI video generation: Kling AI (daily free credits)
  • Hindi voiceover: ElevenLabs free tier ya CapCut TTS
  • Script: ChatGPT / Claude (free)

Is combo se aap zero rupees mein professional videos bana sakte hain!

Pro Level Ke Liye

Jab earning start ho jaye to upgrade karein: Runway ($12/mo) + ElevenLabs ($5/mo) + HeyGen (avatar videos ke liye)। Total ~$40/month mein full professional setup।

FAQ

Kya main phone se AI video bana sakta hun?

Haan! CapCut, Pika aur Kling ke mobile apps hain। Phone se hi shoot, edit, AI generate — sab possible hai।

AI video se YouTube monetization milega?

Haan, lekin YouTube ki AI disclosure policy follow karni hogi — upload ke time "altered content" declare karein। Original value add karna zaroori hai।

Hindi voiceover ke liye best free option?

CapCut ka built-in Hindi TTS free aur kaafi achha hai। Better quality ke liye ElevenLabs ka free tier (10k chars/month)।

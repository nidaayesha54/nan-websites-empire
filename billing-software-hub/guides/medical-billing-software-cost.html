Medical Billing Software Cost: What Practices Pay in 2026

Updated for 2026 · 6 min read

Billing software pricing is deliberately confusing. Three vendors can quote you three totally different numbers for the same practice. Here's the 2026 pricing landscape in plain English.

The three pricing models

1. Flat monthly per provider: $200–$700

The most common model. You pay per provider per month; claims are included or capped high. Best for: practices with steady, predictable volume. Watch for: "per provider" definitions (do mid-levels count?), annual price escalators, and add-on modules for reporting or patient payments.

2. Per-claim: $199 base + $0.25–$1.50/claim

A lower base fee plus a charge per submitted claim. Best for: practices with variable volume or seasonal swings. Watch for: whether resubmissions and corrected claims count as new claims (they often do), and ERA/EFT fees.

3. Percentage of collections: 4–8%

Usually bundled with full-service billing (software + humans). Best for: practices that want zero billing staff. Watch for: the definition of "collections" (gross vs net), minimum monthly fees, and what's excluded (credentialing often costs extra).

Hidden fees checklist

  • Implementation/onboarding: $0–$5,000+ (negotiate this down for cloud platforms)
  • Clearinghouse fees: sometimes separate, $50–$200/month
  • ERA/EFT enrollment: $25–$75 per payer at some vendors
  • Patient statement mailing: $0.75–$1.50 per statement
  • Data migration: $1,000–$5,000 if switching from another system
  • Early termination: can be 50% of remaining contract — read before signing

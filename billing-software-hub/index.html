FAQ

How much does medical billing software cost in 2026?
Most practices pay $200–$700 per provider per month for cloud billing software, plus per-claim fees ($0.25–$1.50) or a percentage of collections (4–8%) for full-service billing. Use our cost comparator to model your exact scenario.
Should I do billing in-house or outsource it?
Practices with under 3 providers often save money outsourcing (4–8% of collections). Larger practices with steady volume usually pay less per claim with in-house staff plus software. Our ROI calculator does the math for you.
What is the best medical billing software for a small practice?
Small practices usually do best with affordable cloud platforms that bundle EHR + billing, charge per provider per month, and include claim scrubbing. Take our free quiz for a matched recommendation.

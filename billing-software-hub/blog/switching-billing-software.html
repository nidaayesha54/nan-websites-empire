How to Switch Billing Software Without Losing Claims

5 min read

Switching billing platforms is where claims go to die — unless you run a disciplined migration. Here's the checklist that keeps your revenue intact.

Phase 1: Before you sign (2–4 weeks out)

  • Export everything from the old system: claim history, patient demographics, payer contracts, AR aging reports.
  • Document your current days in AR and denial rate — this is your baseline to beat.
  • Confirm the new vendor's data import format and who's responsible for mapping.

Phase 2: Parallel run (2–4 weeks)

  • Run old and new systems side by side. Submit a small batch of test claims through the new clearinghouse first.
  • Verify ERA posting matches between systems before cutting over fully.
  • Keep the old system read-only (not cancelled) for at least 90 days.

Phase 3: Cutover

  • Pick your slowest billing week for go-live.
  • Assign one staffer as the migration owner — every question goes to them.
  • Watch the first two ERA batches like a hawk; most mapping errors show up immediately.

The 90-day safety rule

Never cancel the old system until 90 days of clean claims have posted in the new one. The overlap cost is trivial compared to one lost month of AR.

7 Billing Software Mistakes That Cost Practices Thousands

6 min read

  1. Buying on the demo, not the contract. Demos show the happy path. The contract shows the real fees, termination terms, and SLAs. Read it before you sign anything.
  2. Ignoring the clean claim rate. A platform with a 92% clean claim rate vs 98% means thousands of extra staff hours per year. Ask for the number in writing.
  3. Forgetting per-claim math. "$199/month" sounds cheap until you add $0.75 × 5,000 claims. Always model total cost with our cost comparator.
  4. Skipping the reference calls. Three calls to practices in your specialty reveal more than ten sales demos.
  5. No data-exit plan. If you can't export your full claim history in a standard format, you're renting a trap, not buying software.
  6. Underestimating training time. Budget 2–4 weeks of reduced productivity during any switch. Plan the go-live for your slowest month.
  7. Choosing software before deciding in-house vs outsourced. The staffing decision changes everything. Run the ROI math first.

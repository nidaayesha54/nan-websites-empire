How to Calculate ROI in 4 Simple Steps

Updated October 2026 · 5 min read

Calculating ROI takes less than a minute once you know the steps. Here's the exact process — plus the mistakes that make most people's ROI wrong.

Step 1: Add Up Your Total Cost

Write down everything you spent — not just the headline price:

  • Purchase/investment amount
  • Fees, commissions and closing costs
  • Taxes paid
  • Maintenance, repairs or improvements
  • Interest, if you borrowed to invest

Example: You buy a rental property for $200,000, pay $8,000 in closing costs, and spend $5,000 on repairs. Total cost = $213,000.

Step 2: Determine the Final Value

What is the investment worth now (or what did you sell it for), after subtracting selling costs? For ongoing investments like a business, use total profit received to date plus current value.

Example: After 3 years you sell the property for $250,000 and pay $12,000 in selling costs. Final value = $238,000.

Step 3: Apply the Formula

ROI = (Final Value − Total Cost) ÷ Total Cost × 100

Example: ($238,000 − $213,000) ÷ $213,000 × 100 = 11.74%

Step 4: Annualize It (Don't Skip This)

11.74% over 3 years is very different from 11.74% over 3 months. Convert to a yearly rate with our CAGR calculator:

Example: ($238,000 ÷ $213,000)1/3 − 1 = 3.77%/year — a much more sober picture.

3 Mistakes That Ruin ROI Calculations

  1. Forgetting costs. The #1 error. Every fee and tax counts — that's why our calculator has a separate additional-costs field.
  2. Using revenue instead of profit. Revenue is vanity; ROI runs on profit. (Marketers: see ROI vs ROAS.)
  3. Ignoring time. Always annualize before comparing two investments.

FAQ

What is the easiest way to calculate ROI?

Use (Final Value − Total Cost) ÷ Total Cost × 100 — or use ROIHub's free calculator, which does it instantly along with annualized return and profit.

Do I include my time in ROI?

For a true economic picture, yes — value your hours at a reasonable rate and add them to costs. Most people skip this, which flatters side-hustle ROI.

Why do activation functions matter?

Without a non-linear activation, a 100-layer network would collapse into a single linear transformation — no smarter than plain regression. The activation is what lets networks bend, fold, and carve up data into the wild decision boundaries you saw in the playground. The derivative (pink dashed line) is what training actually follows: where it's flat, learning stalls — the famous vanishing gradient problem that killed sigmoid in hidden layers.

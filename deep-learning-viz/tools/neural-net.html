What am I looking at?

Each circle is a neuron. Data enters on the left (your two input features, x and y), gets multiplied by weights along the edges, squashed by an activation function (tanh in hidden layers, sigmoid at the output), and out pops a prediction on the right. Training = nudging every weight a tiny bit, thousands of times, so wrong predictions hurt less. That's backpropagation — and it's running for real, right here, in plain JavaScript.

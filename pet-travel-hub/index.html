❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions pet parents ask us most.

Can I fly with my dog in the cabin in 2026?

Yes. Most major US airlines allow small dogs and cats in the cabin in an airline-approved carrier that fits under the seat, usually for a fee between $95 and $150 each way. Use our airline pet policy finder to compare exact fees and carrier size limits.

What should I pack when traveling with a pet?

Essentials include a carrier or crate, leash and harness, food and water plus extra, collapsible bowls, waste bags, medications, vaccination records, a comfort item, and a pet first-aid kit. Try our travel checklist generator for an itemized list by trip type.

How often should I stop on a road trip with a dog?

Plan a break every 2 to 3 hours of driving — water, a short walk, and a bathroom break. Our road-trip planner builds a custom break schedule based on your distance.

Do pets need a health certificate to fly?

Most US airlines require a health certificate issued by a licensed vet within 10 days of travel for pets flying in cargo, and some require one for in-cabin pets too. Read our complete flying guide for the paperwork timeline.

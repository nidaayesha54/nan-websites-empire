ROI Basics

New to return on investment? Start here.

ROI % = (Gain − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100

Example: You invest $1,000 and get back $1,300. Profit = $300. ROI = 300 ÷ 1,000 × 100 = 30%.

Different investments need different math — a stock's ROI includes dividends and fees, a house flip includes renovation and selling costs, and ads use ROAS. That's why we built 8 specialized calculators. Read our full guide: What Is ROI and How to Calculate It.

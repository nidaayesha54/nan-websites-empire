How the General ROI Formula Works

ROI % = (Gain − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100

If you invested $10,000 and received $13,500 back, your profit is $3,500 and your ROI is 35%. The annualized ROI adjusts for time: a 35% return over 2 years equals about 16.2% per year — useful when comparing a 6-month trade against a 5-year investment.

FAQ

ROI = (Gain − Cost) ÷ Cost × 100. Subtract the cost from the gain, divide by the cost, and multiply by 100.
Annualized ROI converts a multi-year return into a per-year rate: (1 + ROI)^(1/years) − 1. It lets you fairly compare investments held for different lengths of time.

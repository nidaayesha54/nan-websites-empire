Cap Rate vs Cash-on-Cash Return

Cap Rate = NOI ÷ Price × 100  ·  Cash-on-Cash = NOI ÷ Cash Invested × 100

Cap rate ignores financing — it tells you the property's raw yield. Cash-on-cash includes your down payment, so it shows what your actual cash earns (leverage boosts it). Healthy residential cap rates run 6–10%. Also see our House Flip ROI Calculator.

FAQ

Cap rates of 6–10% are generally considered healthy for residential rentals. Below 5% usually means the property is expensive relative to its income.
Cash-on-cash return = Annual net operating income ÷ Cash invested × 100. It measures the return on the actual cash you put in (down payment + closing costs), so it reflects the effect of leverage.

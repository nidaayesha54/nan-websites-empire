Don't Forget the Fees

Profit = (Sell × Amount − Sell Fee) − (Buy × Amount + Buy Fee)

Exchanges charge on both sides of every trade. A 1% fee each way on a trade that gained 5% eats 40% of your profit. This calculator subtracts both fees so you see what actually lands in your wallet. Also try our Stock ROI Calculator.

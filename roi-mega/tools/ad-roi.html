ROAS vs ROI: What Advertisers Need to Know

ROAS = Revenue ÷ Ad Spend  ·  ROI % = (Revenue − Spend) ÷ Spend × 100

A 4x ROAS means $4 of revenue per $1 of ads — which equals a 300% ROI. Break-even is 1x, but after product costs and margins most businesses need 2–3x to be truly profitable. Also see our Marketing Campaign ROI Calculator for lead-to-customer math.

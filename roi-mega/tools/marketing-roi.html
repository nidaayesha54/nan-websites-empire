Follow the Whole Funnel

Revenue = Leads × Conversion Rate × Deal Size  ·  ROI = (Revenue − Spend) ÷ Spend × 100

Vanity metrics (clicks, impressions) don't pay bills. This calculator follows the money all the way from spend → leads → customers → revenue, and shows you CPL and CPA so you know exactly what each stage costs. Also see our Ad Spend ROAS Calculator for pure ad math.

FAQ

Campaign ROI = (Revenue − Spend) ÷ Spend × 100, where Revenue = Leads × Conversion rate × Average deal size.
CPL (cost per lead) = Spend ÷ Leads. CPA (cost per acquisition/customer) = Spend ÷ Customers. CPA is always higher than CPL because not every lead becomes a customer.

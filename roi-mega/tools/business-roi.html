Payback Period: The Number That Matters Most

Payback (months) = Startup Investment ÷ Monthly Profit  ·  Annual ROI = Annual Profit ÷ Startup × 100

ROI tells you how profitable the business is; the payback period tells you how long until you get your money back. Most small businesses target 1–3 years. If the payback is "never" (monthly loss), the idea needs lower costs or higher prices before you invest. Also try our Marketing Campaign ROI Calculator to plan customer acquisition.

FAQ

Business ROI = Annual profit ÷ Startup investment × 100. Annual profit = (Monthly revenue − Monthly costs) × 12.
Most small businesses aim to recover their startup investment within 1–3 years. A payback beyond 5 years usually means the idea needs rethinking or lower startup costs.

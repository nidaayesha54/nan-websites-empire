7 ROI Mistakes That Make Investments Look Better Than They Are

Your ROI is only as honest as your math. Avoid these traps.

1. Forgetting fees and commissions

Brokerage fees, exchange fees, closing costs — they come off both sides. A stock trade that "gained 8%" after $60 in round-trip fees on a $1,000 position actually gained 2%.

2. Ignoring the time horizon

30% over 3 years is ~9.1% annualized. Compare annualized returns, not raw percentages. Use our General ROI Calculator for the annualized figure.

3. Confusing ROAS with profit

A 4x ROAS means $4 revenue per $1 of ads — but if your product margin is 25%, you broke even. Always subtract product costs.

4. Skipping holding costs on flips

Every extra month holding a flip costs loan interest, taxes, insurance and utilities. Include them in the flip calculator.

5. Not counting your own time

A flip that "made" $20,000 across 400 hours of your labor paid $50/hour — fine, but very different from passive income.

6. Using gross rent instead of NOI for rentals

Taxes, insurance, maintenance and vacancies come out first. Cap rate uses net operating income, not gross rent. See our Rental ROI Calculator.

7. Cherry-picking the winning period

"My crypto is up 200%!" — since last month's dip, while still down 40% overall. Measure from your actual buy price to today.

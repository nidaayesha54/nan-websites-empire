Frequently Asked Questions

How much is my personal injury claim worth?

Claim value depends on medical bills, lost wages, injury severity, shared fault, and state law. Our free settlement estimator gives a rough informational range using the widely used multiplier method.

How long do I have to file a personal injury lawsuit?

Each state sets its own statute of limitations — commonly 1 to 6 years. Missing the deadline usually bars the claim permanently. Use our statute checker to look up your state.

Should I accept the insurance company's first offer?

First offers are often low. Many claimants consult a licensed attorney before accepting, since signing a release usually ends your right to seek more money later. This is general information, not advice about your case.

Is this website legal advice?

No. InjuryLawHub provides general educational information only and does not create an attorney-client relationship. For advice about your situation, consult a licensed attorney in your state.

About Statutes of Limitations

What happens if I miss the deadline?

In almost all cases, the court will dismiss your lawsuit and you lose the right to recover compensation — no matter how strong your claim was.

When does the clock start?

Usually on the date of injury. But the "discovery rule" can delay the start until you knew or should have known about the injury, especially in medical malpractice and toxic exposure cases.

Are there exceptions?

Yes — deadlines may be paused ("tolled") for minors or incapacitated persons, and claims against government entities often have much shorter notice requirements (sometimes 90–180 days). This tool shows only general base periods.

Is this legal advice?

No. This is a general research reference. Statutes change and exceptions abound — verify your deadline with a licensed attorney in your state as early as possible.

Do I Have a Personal Injury Case?

Answer these 6 questions honestly. You'll get a score out of 12 and tailored next steps — a self-check, not legal advice. Only a licensed attorney who reviews your facts can truly evaluate your case.

1. How severe are your injuries?
2. How clear is the other party's fault?
3. How strong is your evidence?
4. Is there insurance coverage to collect from?
5. How long ago did the injury happen?
6. How well are your damages documented?

About This Quiz

How is the score calculated?

Each answer is worth 0–2 points. 9–12 = strong case indicators, 5–8 = moderate, 0–4 = weak. It weighs the same factors attorneys look at first: severity, fault, evidence, coverage, timing, and damages.

Can I still have a case with a low score?

Possibly — the quiz can't capture exceptions (for example, tolling rules that extend deadlines for minors) or nuances a lawyer would spot. A low score means "harder to pursue," not "definitely no case."

About This Estimator

What is the multiplier method?

Insurers and attorneys often estimate pain-and-suffering damages by multiplying documented economic losses (medical bills + lost wages) by a factor — typically 1.5× to 5× — reflecting injury severity. It is a negotiation starting point, not a rule of law.

What affects a real settlement?

Insurance policy limits, strength of evidence, your state's fault rules, the venue, and the skill of negotiation all matter enormously. Two similar injuries can settle for very different amounts.

Is this estimate legal advice?

No. This is a general educational estimate. Only a licensed attorney reviewing your specific facts can evaluate a real claim.

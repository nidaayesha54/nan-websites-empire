Personal Injury Claim Timeline Planner

Most claims follow similar stages. Check off each step as you complete it — your progress is saved in your browser. Durations are typical ranges, not guarantees.

0 of 8 steps completed (0%)

1. Get medical care immediately Day 1

Your health comes first. Medical records also become the foundation of any claim — gaps in treatment can hurt it.

2. Document everything Days 1–14

Photos of injuries and the scene, police/accident reports, witness names and numbers, and all receipts and bills.

3. Consult a licensed attorney Weeks 1–4

Most personal injury attorneys offer free consultations and work on contingency. Early advice protects your rights.

4. Investigation & evidence gathering 1–3 months

Medical records, expert opinions, accident reconstruction, and insurance coverage analysis are assembled.

5. Reach maximum medical improvement 3–12 months

Claims are usually valued after treatment stabilizes, so the full extent of injuries and costs is known.

6. Demand letter & negotiation 2–6 months

A formal demand goes to the insurer, followed by back-and-forth negotiation. Most claims settle at this stage.

7. File a lawsuit (if needed) Before deadline

If negotiations stall, a lawsuit must be filed before your state's statute of limitations expires.

8. Discovery, mediation, or trial 1–3 years

Most filed cases still settle during discovery or mediation. Only a small fraction reach trial.

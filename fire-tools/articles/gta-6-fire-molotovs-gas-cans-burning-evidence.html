GTA 6 Fire: Molotovs, Gas Cans and Burning Evidence

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and fire in Leonida is not a background effect — it is a tool. In Rockstar's 26-minute Extended Look video, released on August 27, 2026, Lucia picks a Molotov cocktail from the weapon wheel, lights it and throws it at the pair's own car, setting it on fire to destroy the evidence inside. It is one of the most memorable moments in all of Rockstar's footage, and it tells you everything about how fire works in this game: it is deliberate, it is visual, and it has consequences.

That matters because fire in GTA 6 is a system, not a single weapon. Molotovs, gas cans, explosives and even the cheat codes all treat fire differently, and knowing which one to use — and where to get it — is the difference between a clean getaway and a burning wreck with your name on it.

The Molotov: Lucia's Evidence Burner

The Molotov is the fire weapon Rockstar has shown most clearly. In the Extended Look, at around the 15:59 mark, Lucia carries a bottle with a cloth wick, lights it and throws it, and the game's weapon display shows a bottle icon with one left. Game Informer's write-up of the video says she sets fire to the pair's original car with the Molotov to destroy any evidence — the car is a liability, and fire is the solution. It is a story moment, but it is also a mechanics demonstration: fire spreads, it consumes, and it removes things from the world.

One thing to be careful about: Rockstar has not named the Molotov in its own text, and has not said how you get one. The weapons Rockstar has named in its own text are the Girardi ES9, the Klose K17 and the Hawk and Little Morgan Revolvers — just three. The Molotov is visible in Rockstar's own video, which is solid evidence, but the details of where it comes from in the game are still unconfirmed. Comparison videos of the same Molotov scene across GTA 4, GTA 5 and GTA 6 show how far the fire has come: in GTA 6, the flames spread across the ground and the vehicle with detailed smoke and lighting, where the older games showed a simpler fireball.

Gas Cans, Grenades and What the Shops Sell

The jerry can is the other fire tool, and it is confirmed in an official screenshot: Lucia carries a red gas can past a burning car. A gas can is the GTA staple for starting fires, and in a game where vehicles use fuel and there are 57 petrol stations across the map, fuel is never far away. The can is for starting fires on your own terms — pour it, light it, walk away.

What the shops sell is a different story. On launch day, the Big Shots weapons store sold two explosives — the frag grenade and the sticky bomb — and no Molotovs. That means the Molotov is not a simple shop purchase, at least at launch, and you will need to find it in the world or earn it another way. The thrown-weapons list in the game includes the Molotov, the jerry can, the frag grenade and the sticky bomb, among others, and each one has its own job: the Molotov spreads fire over an area, the grenade is instant damage, and the sticky bomb is for things you want to blow up on a timer.

Fire and the Cheat Codes

Fire also has its own corner of the cheat system, and the cheats reveal how the game thinks about burning things. GTA 6 has 61 cheat codes, and several of them are fire-related: Incendiary Ammo, unlocked by burning 100 enemies with fire damage; Exploding Bottles, unlocked by throwing 100 Molotovs; and a heavy weapon unlocked by blowing up 100 vehicles. These are not buttons you press at the start — they are rewards for doing the thing, over and over, until the game hands you the tool.

The rules matter more than the list. Cheats can only be used after beating the main story, and they are found in the Cheats List in the Progress menu. Cheats disable trophies and achievements, and you cannot save the game while cheats are active. So the fire cheats are endgame toys, not launch-week shortcuts — and the 100-enemy and 100-Molotov requirements mean you will have done a lot of burning before the game rewards you for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you get a Molotov in GTA 6?

Rockstar has not said how you get one. It appears in the Extended Look video, where Lucia picks it from the weapon wheel, but it was not sold at the Big Shots store on launch day. Expect to find it in the world rather than buy it.

Can you burn evidence in GTA 6?

Yes — the Extended Look shows Lucia torching the pair's car with a Molotov to destroy evidence, according to Game Informer's write-up. Fire consumes vehicles and what is inside them.

What are the fire cheats in GTA 6?

Incendiary Ammo (burn 100 enemies with fire damage) and Exploding Bottles (throw 100 Molotovs), among 61 total cheats. Cheats only work after the main story, disable trophies, and block saving while active.

The Bottom Line

Fire in GTA 6 is a tool with a job: Molotovs spread flames and destroy evidence, gas cans start fires where you want them, and the cheat system rewards players who burn a lot. Find your Molotovs in the world, use fire when you need something gone for good, and remember that the most realistic flames in the series also come with the most realistic consequences.

What Is the FIRE Movement? (2026 Guide)

By FIRETools · October 2026 · 8 min read

FIRE stands for Financial Independence, Retire Early — a movement of people saving aggressively, investing simply, and buying back decades of their lives. Here's the whole playbook in one page.

The core idea in one paragraph

Spend far less than you earn, invest the difference in low-cost index funds, and stop working when your portfolio can fund your lifestyle forever — traditionally 25× your annual expenses (the 4% rule). Calculate yours with our FIRE number calculator.

The math that makes it possible

FIRE runs on one variable above all: your savings rate. Save 10% and you're working ~51 years; save 50% and it's ~17; save 70% and it's under 9. Income helps, market returns help, but the savings rate is the engine — see the savings-rate calculator.

The flavors of FIRE

Coast FIRE deserves special attention: once your stash will grow to full FI on its own, retirement saving becomes optional. Check if you're there with the Coast FIRE calculator.

The honest criticisms

  • The 4% rule was built on U.S. data and 30-year retirements — longer retirements may need lower withdrawals.
  • High savings rates are far easier on high incomes; FIRE math is honest about that.
  • Healthcare before Medicare age is expensive in the U.S. and often under-budgeted.
  • Extreme frugality can cost more in life satisfaction than it buys in years.

How to start this month

  1. Track every dollar for 30 days — you can't optimize what you don't measure.
  2. Automate savings on payday; aim to raise your rate 1% per quarter.
  3. Invest in broad index funds in tax-advantaged accounts first.
  4. Re-run your FIRE number yearly and watch the years-to-FI shrink.

Barista FIRE: The Middle Path to Freedom

By FIRETools · October 2026 · 5 min read

Can't stomach full frugality, can't wait for Fat FIRE? Barista FIRE splits the difference: save enough that part-time enjoyable work covers the rest — forever.

How it works

Instead of 25× expenses, you might target 15–20× and let enjoyable part-time work cover the gap. The classic origin story: quit the corporate job, work part-time at a coffee shop (often for the health insurance), and let the portfolio handle the rest.

The math

Spend $60k/year with a $1M portfolio (4% = $40k)? You need just $20k/year of fun work to close the gap — roughly 15–20 hours a week at modest wages. The portfolio keeps compounding underneath you. Model the portfolio side with our FIRE number calculator.

Who it suits

  • People who like working but hate their job.
  • Parents who want school-hours work without career damage.
  • Anyone whose FI date is 5+ years out and wants relief now.

The risks to price in

  • Part-time work isn't always available in downturns — keep a bigger cash buffer.
  • Health insurance is the #1 budget item to nail down pre-Medicare.
  • Don't count on loving the barista job in year 12; keep skills warm.

Barista FIRE isn't quitting — it's renegotiating. For many, that's the whole point of the movement: read what FIRE really means.

Lean FIRE vs Fat FIRE: Which Path Fits You?

By FIRETools · October 2026 · 6 min read

Same movement, opposite lifestyles. Lean FIRE retires on $40k a year; Fat FIRE wouldn't get out of bed for less than $120k. Both are valid — but they demand completely different plans.

The numbers

Lean FIREClassic FIREFat FIRE
Target~20× expenses~25× expenses~33× expenses
Implied withdrawal~5%4%~3%
On $60k spending$1.2M$1.5M$2.0M

Compute all three for your spending in the FIRE number calculator.

The case for Lean

  • Fastest path out — sometimes a decade sooner.
  • Forces intentionality; many Lean folks report higher life satisfaction.
  • Works beautifully in low-cost areas or with geo-arbitrage.

The case for Fat

  • ~3% withdrawals survived nearly every historical scenario tested.
  • Room for travel, dining, generosity — no spreadsheet anxiety.
  • Buffer against healthcare shocks and long retirements.

The honest middle

Most people land between: save like Lean, spend like classic, keep Fat as the stretch goal. And remember Coast FIRE — you can lock in retirement security early, then decide how fat to go at leisure.

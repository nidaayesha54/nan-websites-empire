Why savings rate dominates

A 10% saver needs ~51 years to reach FI; a 50% saver needs ~17. The math is brutal and beautiful: every extra point of savings rate cuts both your FI number (lower spending) and the time to reach it. The table above uses the classic formula from the Mr. Money Mustache 'shockingly simple math' post.

The two levers

You can only move two levers: save more or spend less — they're the same lever, really. A raise you don't spend is pure FI acceleration. Already have a head start? The Coast FIRE calculator shows what your current stash becomes on its own.

Assumptions to sanity-check

The simulation uses constant real returns and ignores taxes, fees, and lifestyle inflation. Treat the year count as a compass, not a contract — then read how to calculate your FIRE number.

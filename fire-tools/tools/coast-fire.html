Coast FIRE Calculator

Coast FIRE = your current investments grow to full retirement funding with zero new contributions. Have you coasted already?

⚠️ Educational purposes only — not financial advice.

What Coast FIRE really means

Once your invested savings will compound to 25× expenses by retirement age, you never need to save for retirement again. Work becomes optional: cover today's bills and let time do the heavy lifting. Full explainer: Coast FIRE explained.

If you haven't coasted yet

The gap number above is your target: every dollar you invest now is worth many at retirement age. Check how fast aggressive saving closes it with the savings-rate calculator.

The catch

Coast FIRE assumes you can cover all living costs from work until retirement — it doesn't fund early retirement spending. Pair it with a realistic budget, not wishful thinking.

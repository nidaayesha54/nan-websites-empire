Frequently Asked Questions

Your FIRE number is 25 times your annual expenses — the portfolio size that supports you indefinitely at a 4% withdrawal rate. Spend $60,000/year and your number is $1.5 million.
The 4% rule comes from the Trinity study: withdrawing ~4% of your portfolio in year one (then inflation-adjusted) historically lasted 30-year retirements. It's a guideline, not a guarantee.
Enormously: a 10% savings rate means ~51 years to FI, 50% means ~17 years, and 70% means under 9 years (at 5% real returns from zero). Small increases cut years dramatically.
Coast FIRE is when your current investments will compound to your full FI number by retirement age with no further contributions — you only need to cover today's bills from work.
Lean FIRE targets ~20x expenses (minimalist, ~5% withdrawals); Fat FIRE targets ~33x (comfortable, ~3% withdrawals). Classic FIRE sits at 25x and 4%.
It's harder but the mechanics don't change: raise your savings rate by cutting big costs (housing, cars) and the timeline shrinks fast. Coast and Barista FIRE make partial progress meaningful.

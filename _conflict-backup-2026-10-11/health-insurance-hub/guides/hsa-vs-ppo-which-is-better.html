HSA vs PPO: Which Is Better in 2026?

An HDHP with an HSA and a traditional PPO are the two most common employer-plan choices. Here's the honest comparison.

What Each One Is

  • HDHP + HSA: a high-deductible health plan paired with a Health Savings Account you own. Lower premiums, bigger deductible — but triple tax advantages.
  • PPO: a Preferred Provider Organization plan. Higher premiums, lower deductible, and you can see out-of-network doctors (at higher cost) without referrals.

The Triple Tax Advantage

The HSA is the only account with all three: tax-deductible contributions, tax-free growth, tax-free withdrawals for medical expenses. At a 22% marginal rate, a $4,300 contribution saves ~$946 in taxes — real money that offsets the higher deductible.

2026 HSA contribution limits: $4,400 individual / $8,750 family, plus $1,000 catch-up at 55+.

Who Should Pick Which?

Pick the HDHP + HSA if: you're generally healthy, can afford the deductible from savings, want to max the HSA as a stealth retirement account, and your employer seeds the HSA.

Pick the PPO if: you expect major medical expenses (surgery, pregnancy, chronic care), need out-of-network specialists, or can't comfortably cover a high deductible.

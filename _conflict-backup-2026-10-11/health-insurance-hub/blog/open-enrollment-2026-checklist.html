Open Enrollment 2026 Checklist: 9 Things to Do

Don't auto-renew. Plans, premiums, networks, and your health all change yearly. Work this checklist before the deadline.

  1. List last year's medical spending. Total bills, prescriptions, ER/urgent care visits. This is your baseline.
  2. Note next year's known needs. Planned surgery? Pregnancy? New prescriptions? These flip which plan wins.
  3. Compute true yearly cost per plan. Premium × 12 + expected out-of-pocket — not premium alone. Use our comparator.
  4. Check subsidies. Marketplace buyers: run our subsidy estimator — credits can change the math completely.
  5. Verify your doctors are in-network. Search each plan's directory. Out-of-network care often doesn't count toward your OOP max.
  6. Check the drug formulary. Your prescriptions' tiers determine real costs. A cheap plan with your drug on tier 4 is not cheap.
  7. Compare HSA vs PPO. Healthy with savings? The HSA's triple tax advantage may win. Run the break-even analyzer.
  8. Read the fine print on referrals and prior auth. HMO/EPO plans may require PCP referrals; know before you need care.
  9. Set a calendar reminder for next year. The #1 money mistake is never re-shopping. Future you says thanks.

How Much Is Car Insurance Per Month in 2026?

The short answer: the average US driver pays about $170–$200/month for full coverage and roughly $60–$90/month for liability-only. The honest answer: your price could be $90 or $350 depending on six factors below.

The 6 factors that move your price most

  1. State & ZIP code — the single biggest factor. Urban ZIPs cost far more than rural ones.
  2. Age — drivers under 25 pay 60–90% more; rates drop sharply after 25.
  3. Driving record — a clean record is the cheapest discount of all. One accident: +35%. DUI: +90%.
  4. Vehicle — sports cars and EVs cost more to insure; boring sedans are cheapest.
  5. Coverage level — liability-only runs about 55% of full-coverage cost.
  6. Credit tier — in most states, better credit means lower premiums.

Buying Life Insurance in Your 30s: The Smart Playbook

October 2026 · 5 min read

Your 30s are the sweet spot: you're young enough for cheap rates and old enough to actually need coverage (mortgage, kids, growing income). Here's the playbook.

Step 1: Get your number

Run the DIME calculator. Most 30-somethings with a mortgage and kids land between $750K and $2M.

Step 2: Buy term, not whole

A 20- or 30-year term policy covers your working years at a fraction of whole life's cost. A healthy 32-year-old pays roughly $25–40/month for $1M of 20-year term. Compare for yourself in our term vs whole comparator.

Step 3: Lock the rate now

Term premiums are fixed for the whole term. Buying at 32 instead of 38 can save you 30–40% — permanently.

Step 4: Don't skip the exam (usually)

No-exam policies are convenient but cost 20–40% more. If you're healthy, the 30-minute paramedical exam pays for itself many times over.

Step 5: Layer if needed

Need $1.5M but only for 10 more years of mortgage plus longer income protection? "Ladder" two policies — e.g., $1M 20-year + $500K 30-year — instead of one giant 30-year policy. Often cheaper.

The bottom line

In your 30s: buy term, buy enough (DIME it), buy now. Revisit in your 40s as life changes.

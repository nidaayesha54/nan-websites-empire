Estimate Your Life Insurance Premium

Five factors drive your price: age, health, tobacco, term length, and coverage amount. Adjust them below to see how each one moves your monthly cost.

Insurers use actuarial life-expectancy tables; women typically pay slightly less.

What moves the price most?

Age is the biggest lever — every year you wait, premiums rise. Tobacco roughly doubles or triples your rate. Health class (blood pressure, cholesterol, BMI, family history) can swing your premium 60% or more between the best and average classes.

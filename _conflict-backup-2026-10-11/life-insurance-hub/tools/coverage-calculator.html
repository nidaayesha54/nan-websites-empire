How Much Life Insurance Do You Need?

The DIME method is what financial planners actually use: Debts + Income replacement + Mortgage + Education, minus your assets. Fill in your numbers below.

Why the DIME method?

Simple "10× income" rules ignore your mortgage, your debts, and your kids' education — the three things that actually determine what your family needs. DIME builds the number from your real obligations, so you neither overpay for coverage you don't need nor leave your family short.

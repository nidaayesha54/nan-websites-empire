How to Apply

1
Read the official notification — check eligibility, age limit, fees and vacancy details. Visit the official portal (upsc.gov.in, ssc.gov.in, ibps.in, indianrailways.gov.in) for the department you are applying to.
2
Register on the official portal — upsc.gov.in, ssc.gov.in, ibps.in, indianrailways.gov.in, indiapost.gov.in — create your account with a valid email and mobile number for the online application.
3
Fill & submit the form — upload photo, signature and documents as specified; pay the fee online and save the confirmation page.
4
Download your admit card — released 2–3 weeks before the exam on the same official portal; check your exam city and shift.
5
Understand the exam pattern — review Prelims/Mains syllabus, marking scheme and previous-year cutoffs, then track results on the official site.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Sarkari Naukri 2026?
Sarkari Naukri 2026 refers to the latest government job notifications released by central and state departments of India in 2026, including UPSC, SSC, Railway, Bank and Police recruitments.
How can I apply for a government job in India?
Check the eligibility in the official notification, register on the department's official portal, fill the online application, pay the fee if applicable, and download the confirmation page.
What is the age limit for government jobs?
The general age limit is 18–32 years with relaxations for SC/ST (5 years), OBC (3 years) and PwBD candidates as per government rules.
Are there any government jobs without an exam?
Yes. India Post GDS and several merit-based recruitments select candidates on 10th/12th marks without a written exam.
Where can I check the admit card?
Admit cards are published on the official portal of the recruiting body — upsc.gov.in, ssc.gov.in, ibps.in or the respective RRB regional website.
How do I get job alerts for new Sarkari Naukri?
Bookmark this page — we refresh listings weekly. Always verify dates and eligibility on the official government portal before applying.

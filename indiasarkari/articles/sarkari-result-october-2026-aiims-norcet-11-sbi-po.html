October 2026 is a busy month for results. Below is everything you need to know about the two biggest announcements so far — the AIIMS NORCET 11 result and the SBI PO prelims marks — plus what to do after you check your score.

AIIMS NORCET 11 Stage 2 Result 2026

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences has declared the NORCET 11 Stage 2 Result 2026. A total of 10,613 candidates have qualified in the Stage 2 examination, conducted for the recruitment of Nursing Officer posts at AIIMS and participating institutes across the country.

How to Check the NORCET 11 Result

1
Visit the official AIIMS examination portal — results are published only on the official AIIMS website.
2
Open the NORCET section — look for the "NORCET 11 Stage 2 Result 2026" notification or merit list link.
3
Download the result PDF — search the merit list with your roll number.
4
Save a copy — download and print the PDF for document verification and counselling stages.

Qualified candidates should now keep their certificates ready and watch the official portal for the counselling and seat-allotment schedule.

SBI PO Prelims Marks 2026

The SBI PO Prelims Marks 2026 scorecard link is now active. Candidates who appeared for the Probationary Officer preliminary examination can check their section-wise and overall marks, which will help them assess their chances of qualifying for the mains stage.

How to Check Your SBI PO Prelims Scorecard

1
Visit the official SBI careers portal — scorecards are available only on the bank's official website.
2
Open the "Scores of Online Preliminary Exam" link under the SBI PO 2026 recruitment section.
3
Log in with your registration number or roll number and date of birth/password.
4
Download your scorecard — check section-wise marks, the overall score and qualifying status.

What to Do After the Result

  • Check the cutoff: compare your score against the official category-wise cutoff, which is published along with or shortly after the result. Do not rely on unverified cutoff figures circulating on social media.
  • Prepare for the next stage: qualified candidates move to mains, skill tests, interviews or document verification depending on the recruitment — start preparation immediately instead of waiting for the next notification.
  • Keep documents ready: admit card, photo ID, category certificate, and educational certificates are typically required at verification.
  • Watch the official portal: schedules for the next stage appear on the same official website — bookmark it and check regularly.

How to Check Merit Lists on Official Portals

Every recruiting body publishes results only on its own official portal — ssc.gov.in, ibps.in, upsc.gov.in, the AIIMS exam portal, and the regional RRB websites (rrbcdg.gov.in). Avoid third-party result sites that ask for personal details or payments. If a result link is not loading, it may be due to heavy traffic right after declaration — wait and retry on the official portal.

How can I check my SBI PO Prelims marks 2026?
The scorecard link is active on the official SBI careers portal. Log in with your registration number or roll number and date of birth/password to download your section-wise and overall marks.

Only on the official portal of the recruiting body — ssc.gov.in, ibps.in, upsc.gov.in, the AIIMS exam portal, or rrbcdg.gov.in and regional RRB sites. Never pay or share personal details on third-party result websites.
What should I do after qualifying in a result?

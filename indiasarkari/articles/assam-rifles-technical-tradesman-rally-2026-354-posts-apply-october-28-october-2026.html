The Directorate General Assam Rifles (DGAR), Shillong has opened online applications for the Technical and Tradesman Recruitment Rally 2026 — 354 Group-C vacancies of Rifleman, Havildar and Warrant Officer across technical and tradesman trades. Assam Rifles is India's oldest paramilitary force, under the Ministry of Home Affairs.

The last date to apply online is 28 October 2026, 11:59 PM. The recruitment rally — starting with RFID-based physical tests — is tentatively scheduled from 23 November 2026 at Jorhat and Diphu in Assam. Note: every trade in this rally is open to male candidates only, and there is no state-wise quota — the 354 posts are on an all-India basis.

Major Trades on Offer

Trades in this rally include Cipher (Warrant Officer), Electrical Fitter Signal, Lineman Field, Armourer, Engineer Equipment Mechanic, Recovery Vehicle Mechanic, Electrician Motor Vehicle, Instrument Mechanic (Havildar), Welder, Upholster, Store Keeper Technical, Vehicle Mechanic, Draughtsman (Warrant Officer), Operation Theatre Technician (Warrant Officer), Physiotherapist (Warrant Officer) and Mason.

Eligibility: Who Can Apply

  • Gender: male Indian citizens only — all advertised trades
  • Age: 18–23 (Rifleman, Havildar); 18–25 for four WO trades (Cipher, Draughtsman, OT Technician, Physiotherapist), reckoned on 1 July 2026
  • Qualification: Class 10 plus ITI / diploma / degree according to the trade; Class 12 with science for the Cipher post
  • Ex-Servicemen: 10% quota in most trades; unfilled ESM posts go to non-ESM candidates of the same category
  • Fee: Rs. 100 for UR/OBC/EWS; nil for SC, ST and ex-servicemen candidates (non-refundable)

Selection Process

Selection runs through five stages:

  1. RFID-based PST/PET — Physical Standard Test and Physical Efficiency Test at the rally centres
  2. Documentation verification — carry originals plus the printed online application form and downloaded notification
  3. Computer Based Test (CBT) — 100-mark written examination
  4. Trade Skill Test — qualifying, with candidates called in a 1:4 ratio
  5. Medical examination — Detailed Medical Examination and Review Medical Examination, then a trade- and category-wise merit list

How to Apply Online

1
Go to assamrifles.gov.in and open the Technical and Tradesman Recruitment Rally 2026 application link.
2
Register with a working email and mobile number — your PST/PET reporting date comes by email and SMS.
3
Fill the form and upload documents — photograph, signature, certificates and trade proof as specified for your trade.
4
Pay the fee — Rs. 100 for UR/OBC/EWS; no fee for SC/ST/ex-servicemen.
5
Submit before 28 October 2026, 11:59 PM — only one application per candidate — and print the form for PST/PET reporting.

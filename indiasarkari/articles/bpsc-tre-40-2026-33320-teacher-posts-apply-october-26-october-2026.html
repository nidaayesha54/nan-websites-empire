BPSC TRE 4.0 2026: 33,320 Teacher Posts — Apply by 26 October, Eligibility & Exam Details

The Bihar Public Service Commission (BPSC) has opened online applications for the Teacher Recruitment Examination (TRE) 4.0 — 2026, the largest teacher recruitment Bihar has ever conducted. The vacancy count, initially announced at 32,388, has now been revised upward to 33,320 posts across primary, middle, secondary and higher secondary schools. Applications are being accepted from 25 September to 26 October 2026 on the official portal bpsc.bihar.gov.in, with the fee payable by 25 October.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Eligibility follows Bihar government norms for each school level: a graduation or postgraduation degree in the relevant subject with professional teacher training (B.Ed. or D.El.Ed. as applicable), plus the prescribed teacher eligibility qualification. Match your degrees and certificates against the official advertisement's eligibility table before applying.

Age Limit

The age limits and category-wise relaxations are notified in the official advertisement as per Bihar government rules. Verify the cut-off date for age calculation in the advertisement — it is usually the first day of the application year — and claim relaxation only for the category you genuinely belong to, with a valid certificate.

Selection Process

BPSC teacher recruitments follow a transparent, merit-based process:

1
Written Examination (TRE) — the objective-type written test is the core of the selection; the final merit list is prepared on its marks.
2
Document Verification — shortlisted candidates produce original degrees, training certificates, eligibility-test certificates, caste and domicile documents.
3
Medical Examination — as per the prescribed standards for government teachers.
4
Counselling and Posting — school allotment follows the merit-cum-preference procedure notified by the commission.

How to Apply Online for BPSC TRE 4.0

1
Visit bpsc.bihar.gov.in — open the official BPSC website and find the TRE 4.0 advertisement link.
2
Read the detailed advertisement — check the post-wise eligibility, age limits, reservation roster and exam syllabus before registering.
3
Register and fill the form — enter personal, educational and training details exactly as they appear on your certificates.
4
Upload documents and pay the fee — photograph, signature and certificates in the prescribed format; complete the fee payment by 25 October 2026.
5
Submit before 26 October 2026 — download the confirmation page and save your registration number for the admit card stage.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for BPSC TRE 4.0 2026?
26 October 2026. The online application window runs from 25 September 2026, and the application fee must be paid by 25 October 2026 on bpsc.bihar.gov.in.
How many posts are in BPSC TRE 4.0 2026?
33,320 teacher posts: 3,847 primary, 8,563 middle school, 3,877 secondary and 16,101 higher secondary. The count was revised upward from the initially announced 32,388.
What is the qualification for BPSC TRE 4.0?
A graduation or postgraduation degree in the relevant subject with professional teacher training (B.Ed./D.El.Ed. as applicable) plus the prescribed teacher eligibility qualification for the school level. Check the official advertisement's eligibility table for your level.
How will BPSC TRE 4.0 selection be done?
Through a written examination (TRE), followed by document verification and a medical examination. The final merit list is prepared on the written examination marks.
Where can I apply for BPSC TRE 4.0 2026?
Only on the official Bihar Public Service Commission website, bpsc.bihar.gov.in. Do not trust application links shared on unofficial websites or messaging apps.

Cabinet Secretariat DFO Recruitment 2026: 250 Deputy Field Officer (Technical) Posts — Apply by 15 Nov

The Government of India Cabinet Secretariat has opened online applications for the Deputy Field Officer (Technical) Recruitment 2026 (Advt. No. 02/2026), and this is one of the most attractive central government openings of the season. With 250 posts, a zero application fee, and shortlisting done entirely on the basis of GATE scores, eligible engineers and postgraduates can apply from 17 October 2026 to 15 November 2026 (5:00 PM) without writing any additional written examination. The post carries Level-7 pay in the pay matrix — roughly Rs. 1,00,000 per month in initial emoluments.

Shortlisted candidates move directly to a personal interview, followed by medical examination and document verification — a rare no-exam central government opportunity for GATE-qualified engineers and postgraduates.

Eligibility Criteria for DFO (Technical) 2026

  • Educational qualification: B.Tech or M.Sc. in the stream corresponding to your subject, plus a valid score in GATE-2024, GATE-2025 or GATE-2026 in the matching paper.
  • Age limit: Maximum 30 years as on 15 November 2026. Age relaxation for reserved categories applies as per Government of India rules.
  • Application fee: There is no fee for any category — the entire application is free.
  • Nationality: Indian citizens as per the standard central government eligibility conditions.

How to Apply for Cabinet Secretariat DFO Recruitment 2026

1
Open the official notification — go to the Cabinet Secretariat recruitment page and download Advt. No. 02/2026 to confirm your subject eligibility.
2
Keep your GATE details ready — your GATE registration number, year (2024/2025/2026) and score card.
3
Fill the online application form — enter personal details, educational qualifications and GATE score exactly as in the certificates.
4
Upload documents — photograph, signature and supporting certificates in the prescribed format.
5
Submit before the deadline — the portal closes at 5:00 PM on 15 November 2026. Submit early; do not wait for the last day.
6
Save the confirmation — download and print the submitted application for the interview stage.

Selection Process: GATE Score to Interview

There is no written examination. The Cabinet Secretariat will shortlist candidates on the basis of their GATE scores, then conduct a personal interview, followed by a medical examination and document verification. This makes your GATE percentile the single decisive factor — higher scores directly translate into a better chance of an interview call. Because the interview is the only interactive stage, prepare thoroughly on your subject fundamentals, current developments in your stream, and awareness of the role of technical officers in the government.

Tip: Since the fee is zero and shortlisting is GATE-based, apply even if your GATE score is moderate — cutoffs vary by subject, and smaller subjects like Statistics and Mathematics may see lower cutoffs.

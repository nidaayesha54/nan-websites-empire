MP Police Constable 7,500 Posts — Exam from 19 Nov 2026

Key Details

Vacancies7,500 (Special Armed Forces 700 | Other cadres 6,800)
OrganizationMadhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB)
LocationMadhya Pradesh
QualificationClass 10 pass (UR/SC/OBC); Class 8 pass for ST candidates
Age Limit18–33 years as on 6 October 2026 (relaxations for reserved categories)
Salary₹19,500–₹62,000 pay band (stipend 70/80/90% during probation)
Last DateApplication window closed 6 October 2026 — written exam from 19 November 2026
Apply Linkesb.mp.gov.in
Source / Ad DateMPESB rulebook 9 September 2026; exam schedule notices cross-checked across sources

The Madhya Pradesh Employees Selection Board (MPESB) will begin the written examination for the MP Police Constable (GD) Recruitment 2026 on 19 November 2026. This cycle fills 7,500 Constable (GD) posts — 700 in the Special Armed Forces and 6,800 across other cadres. Online applications ran from 22 September to 6 October 2026 (correction window till 11 October) and are now closed.

Admit cards for the written test are expected in November 2026, shortly before the exam starts, and will be released on esb.mp.gov.in. The exam is held in two shifts; after the written test come the physical standard test (PST), physical efficiency test (PET), medical examination and document verification.

Vacancy Breakup (7,500 Posts)

Constable (GD) — Special Armed Forces (male only)700
Constable (GD) — Other cadres6,800
Total7,500

Women candidates are eligible for the 6,800 non-SAF posts, where 35% horizontal reservation applies. The 700 SAF posts are for male candidates only.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • UR / SC / OBC: passed Class 10 (High School) under the 10+2 system or equivalent from a recognized board
  • ST: passed Class 8 or equivalent

Age Limit (as on 6 October 2026)

  • Minimum: 18 years; Maximum: 33 years for UR (MP domicile) males
  • Relaxations: SC/ST/OBC (MP) up to 38 years, women up to 38 years, Ex-Servicemen and others as per MP government rules. Other-state candidates are treated as UR.

Application Fee

UR / other-state candidates: ₹500; SC/ST/OBC/EWS (MP residents): ₹250 — payable online via card, net banking or MP Online kiosk. One source lists additional departmental and portal charges bringing the effective total to ₹700/₹350; the notification fee table is ₹500/₹250 — candidates should confirm the exact payable amount on esb.mp.gov.in. The fee is non-refundable.

Selection Process

1
Written test — computer-based, objective, starts 19 November 2026 in two shifts; qualifying in nature for the physical stage.
2
PST/PET — physical measurement (height, chest) and running/endurance tests per cadre norms.
3
Medical examination and document verification — provisional eligibility is verified by the Police Department after the physical stage.

How to Download the Admit Card

1
Visit esb.mp.gov.in — the admit-card link will appear under the MP Police Constable 2026 section once released (expected mid-November 2026).
2
Log in with your application number and date of birth.
3
Download and print the card; carry it with an original photo ID (Aadhaar must be unlocked — biometric verification is done at entry).
4
Reach the centre early — entry closes at the reporting time; phones, calculators, digital watches and sunglasses are banned.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the MP Police Constable 2026 written exam?
The written test starts on 19 November 2026 in two shifts, conducted by MPESB. Admit cards are expected in November 2026 on esb.mp.gov.in.
How many vacancies are there in MP Police Constable Recruitment 2026?
7,500 Constable (GD) posts — 700 in the Special Armed Forces (male only) and 6,800 in other cadres, where 35% of posts are reserved horizontally for women.
What is the qualification for MP Police Constable 2026?
Class 10 pass for UR/SC/OBC candidates and Class 8 pass for ST candidates, from a recognized board.
What is the salary of an MP Police Constable?
The pay band is ₹19,500–₹62,000; during probation a stipend of 70%, 80% and 90% of the minimum basic is paid in the 1st, 2nd and 3rd years respectively.
Can I still apply for MP Police Constable 2026?
No. Applications closed on 6 October 2026 and the correction window closed on 11 October 2026. Only the exam stage remains.
What is the age limit for MP Police Constable 2026?
18 to 33 years as on 6 October 2026 for UR males (MP domicile), with relaxations up to 38 years for women and SC/ST/OBC (MP) candidates.

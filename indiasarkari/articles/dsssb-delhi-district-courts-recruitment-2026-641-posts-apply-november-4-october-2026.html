DSSSB Delhi District Courts Recruitment 2026: 641 Posts (PA, JJA, Process Server, Peon) — Apply by 4 November

The Delhi Subordinate Services Selection Board (DSSSB) has notified Advertisement No. 04/2026 (30 September 2026) — the Combined Examination 2026 for Group B and Group C posts in the Delhi District & Sessions Courts and Family Courts: 641 tentative vacancies across five posts, from Senior Personal Assistant (Pay Level 8) to Peon (Pay Level 3). Applications run 6 October 2026 (12:00 noon) to 4 November 2026 (11:00 PM) on dsssbonline.nic.in; the fee is payable through SBI e-pay.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Senior Personal Assistant: graduation with shorthand 110 wpm and typing 40 wpm. Personal Assistant: graduation with shorthand 100 wpm, typing 40 wpm and computer knowledge. Junior Judicial Assistant: graduation with typing 40 wpm. Process Server: Matriculation (or 12th with LMV licence and 2 years clean driving). Peon/Orderly/Dak Peon: Matriculation. Speeds are tested in the skill test.

Age Limit

Candidates must be 18 to 27 years as on 1 June 2026. Relaxation: SC/ST +5 years, OBC (Delhi) +3 years, PwBD +10 to 15 years; ex-servicemen: military service +3 years.

Selection Process

Selection is a multi-stage process:

1
Tier-I Written Examination — the screening stage for all posts.
2
Tier-II Written Examination — the main stage, with post-wise scheme and syllabus.
3
Skill Test — shorthand/typing tests for the PA, Senior PA and JJA posts, as applicable.
4
Interview — post-wise, wherever prescribed.
5
Document Verification — original certificates and category/age proof.

How to Apply Online for DSSSB Delhi District Courts Recruitment 2026

1

2

4

5
Submit by 4 November 2026 (11:00 PM) — download the confirmation page and keep your application number safe.

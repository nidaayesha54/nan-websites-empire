The Punjab Subordinate Services Selection Board (PSSSB) has released the Group D admit card 2026 for 2,098 vacancies. Registered candidates can download their hall tickets from the official website sssb.punjab.gov.in using their registration number and date of birth. The admit card is the single mandatory entry document — candidates without a printed copy will not be allowed inside the examination hall.

The written examination is scheduled for 25 October 2026. Candidates must carry a printed copy of the admit card to the examination centre and check every detail on it — name, roll number, exam date, centre address and reporting time — immediately after downloading.

How to Download the PSSSB Group D Admit Card 2026

1
Visit sssb.punjab.gov.in — the official PSSSB website.
2
Open the Group D admit card 2026 link on the homepage or in the recruitment section.
3
Enter your login details — registration number and date of birth.
4
Submit and download — save the hall ticket and take a clear printout for exam day.

Details to Check on Your Hall Ticket

  • Candidate name, roll number and photograph
  • Examination date (25 October 2026) and centre address
  • Reporting time — reach well before it; late entry may be refused
  • Identity documents, prohibited items and other exam-day instructions

Any discrepancy — wrong name, photo or centre details — should be reported to the recruitment board before the examination, as advised in the admit-card notice. Screenshot your downloaded hall ticket as a backup, but remember that only a printed copy is accepted at the venue.

Exam-Day Instructions

  • Carry a printed admit card plus the identity documents listed on the hall ticket
  • Follow the instructions on the hall ticket regarding reporting time and prohibited items (mobile phones and electronics are normally banned)
  • Keep checking sssb.punjab.gov.in regularly for any updates on the recruitment process

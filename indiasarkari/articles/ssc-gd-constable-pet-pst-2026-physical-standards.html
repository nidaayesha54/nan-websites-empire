PST: Physical Standards

The Physical Standard Test measures height, chest and weight against the prescribed standards. These are the general standards from the official notification:

WeightProportionate to height and age as per medical standards

Relaxed standards apply for candidates from certain reserved categories and specified regions — check the official SSC GD 2026 notification for the exact relaxations applicable to you.

PET: Physical Efficiency Test

The PET is qualifying in nature — it tests running and endurance events prescribed in the official notification. PET/PST marks are not added to the final merit, but failing the PET ends the candidature. Candidates should:

  • Train for distance running now — the PET is the most common elimination stage; daily timed practice is the best preparation.
  • Check your call letter — the exact venue, reporting time and event list for your batch are printed on the PET/PST admit card issued after the result.
  • Carry the right documents — PET/PST admit card printout, original photo ID and the documents listed below.

Documents for Verification and Medical

  • Matriculation (Class 10) certificate for date of birth and qualification
  • Caste/community certificate (SC/ST/OBC) and EWS certificate where claimed
  • Domicile certificate for state-wise vacancy claims
  • NCC certificate if bonus marks were claimed
  • Original photo ID and recent passport-size photographs

Candidates who clear PET/PST proceed to the Detailed Medical Examination and document verification, after which the final merit is prepared from CBT marks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many candidates qualified for SSC GD PET/PST 2026?
1,97,408 candidates qualified the SSC GD Constable 2026 computer-based exam and are shortlisted for the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) and Physical Standard Test (PST) for 25,487 posts.
What is the height requirement for SSC GD Constable 2026?
Male candidates must be at least 170 cm tall with a chest of 80 cm (unexpanded) expanding to 85 cm. Female candidates must be at least 157 cm tall. Relaxations apply for reserved categories and certain regions as per the official notification.
Is the SSC GD PET/PST qualifying in nature?
Yes. PET and PST are qualifying — candidates must clear them to reach the medical examination and document verification, but PET/PST marks are not added to the final merit.
What documents are needed for SSC GD document verification?
Matriculation certificate, photo ID, category or caste certificate, domicile certificate, NCC certificate if claimed, and recent passport photographs — all in original with photocopies.
When will the SSC GD PET/PST 2026 be held?
The PET/PST schedule is announced by the Staff Selection Commission after the result. Shortlisted candidates should watch ssc.gov.in for the physical test call letters and venue details.

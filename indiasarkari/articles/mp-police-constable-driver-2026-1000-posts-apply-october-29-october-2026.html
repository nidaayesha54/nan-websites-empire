MP Police Constable Driver Recruitment 2026: 1,000 Posts — Apply 15 to 29 October

Madhya Pradesh has opened applications for the Police Constable (Driver) Recruitment 2026, with 1,000 constable driver posts on offer. The online application window runs from 15 October to 29 October 2026 — a short, two-week window, so eligible candidates should not wait for the last days. This is a dedicated driver-category recruitment in the state police, and the key differentiator from a general constable recruitment is the driving licence requirement: you must hold a valid licence to drive the class of vehicle the post demands, in addition to meeting the standard physical and educational criteria.

Driver-category constable posts exist because police departments run large vehicle fleets — patrol vehicles, escort vehicles, prisoner vans and emergency response units all need trained police drivers. The recruitment therefore tests driving ability alongside the usual police selection stages, and the licence is verified as strictly as any educational certificate.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Candidates are generally required to have passed the 10th standard (matriculation) from a recognized board. The decisive additional condition is a valid driving licence for light and/or heavy motor vehicles as specified in the official advertisement — the licence must be valid on the date prescribed in the notification, and learner's licences are not accepted.

Age Limit and Physical Standards

Age limits follow Madhya Pradesh government norms for police constable recruitment, with the usual relaxations for SC, ST, OBC and other reserved categories of the state. Physical standards (height, chest) and the physical efficiency test follow the prescribed police norms — check the advertisement for the exact measurements and the race timings, since driver posts sometimes carry post-specific physical requirements.

Selection Process

MP Police constable recruitments run through a multi-stage process, and the driver category adds a driving assessment to the standard stages:

1
Written Examination — the objective-type test covering general knowledge, reasoning, mathematics and science topics at the 10th standard level.
2
Physical Efficiency and Measurement Tests (PET/PMT) — race, long jump and other events plus height and chest measurement as per norms.
3
Driving Test — the practical driving assessment specific to this recruitment; your licence class must match the vehicles tested.
4
Document Verification and Medical — originals of educational certificates, licence, caste and domicile documents, followed by the medical examination.

The final merit is prepared stage-wise as notified — the written examination is typically the scored stage, while the physical and driving tests are qualifying in nature. Read the advertisement's merit rules carefully rather than assuming last cycle's pattern.

How to Apply Online

1
Open the official advertisement — read it fully on the official MP recruitment portal before starting the form.
2
Check your licence first — confirm that your driving licence is valid, of the correct class, and was issued before the cut-off date in the notification.
3
Register and fill the application — personal details, 10th certificate details, licence number and category reservation exactly as per documents.
4
Upload and pay — photograph, signature, 10th marksheet and licence copy in the prescribed formats; pay the category-wise fee.
5
Submit by 29 October 2026 — save the confirmation page and application number for the admit card stage.
Warning: The two-week window (15–29 October) is unusually short for a 1,000-post police recruitment. Portal congestion in the final 48 hours is almost certain — complete your application in the first week and do not leave document scanning for the last day.

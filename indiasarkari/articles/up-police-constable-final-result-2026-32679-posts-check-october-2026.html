UP Police Constable Final Result 2026 Out: 32,679 Posts — Check Merit List & Next Steps

The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) has declared the final result of the UP Police Constable recruitment 2026 — one of the country's largest police recruitments, covering 32,679 constable posts. Candidates who cleared every stage, from the written examination to the physical tests, document verification and medical examination, can now check the final merit list. This guide explains how to check the result, what the list contains, and the steps that follow for selected candidates.

The final result is the last selection list of this cycle. Unlike the written-exam result, which only shortlisted candidates for the physical stages, it reflects combined performance across all stages with document and medical clearance. Only candidates in this list move to appointment and training.

How to Check the UP Police Final Result 2026

The merit list is published as a PDF on the official UPPRPB portal. Follow these steps:

1
Visit the official UPPRPB website — use only uppbpb.gov.in. Result PDFs are not issued through third-party websites.
2
Open the Results section — find the link for the UP Police Constable Final Result 2026 among the latest notices.
3
Download the merit list PDF — it may be split by category or district.
4
Search your roll number — use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac) in the PDF and confirm your name, category and district.
5
Save and print the PDF — keep a printout for the joining formalities.

Cut Off and Merit List — What to Know

The board publishes the category-wise cut off with or shortly after the final result. Cut offs differ by category (General, OBC, SC, ST, EWS), gender and sometimes district, since the final merit combines the written examination and physical stages with medical and document clearance. Ignore cut off figures forwarded on social media — compare your score only against the official notice on uppbpb.gov.in.

What Happens After the Final Result

Selection does not end at the merit list. The board and the district police units complete the joining formalities in a fixed order:

1
Document verification — originals are re-checked at the time of joining, even if you cleared DV earlier.
2
Medical examination — as per the prescribed police medical standards.
3
Character and antecedent verification — conducted through the district police.
4
Appointment letter — issued with reporting instructions for your assigned district or unit.
5
Basic training — at the designated police training centre before field posting.

Keep your originals ready now: 10th and 12th marksheets, caste or EWS certificate, domicile, photo ID, NCC or sports certificates if claimed, and recent photographs. Any mismatch with your application can hold up joining — get discrepancies corrected immediately.

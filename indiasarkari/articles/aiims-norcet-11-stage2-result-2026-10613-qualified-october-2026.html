AIIMS NORCET-11 2026: Key Facts

Conducting BodyAll India Institute of Medical Sciences (AIIMS), New Delhi
ExamNursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test (NORCET)-11
PostNursing Officer, Group-B — 2,216 vacancies (Level 7 pay)
Advertisement NoticeNotice No. 103/2026 dated 24.07.2026
Stage-II Exam Date30 September 2026 (Stage-I held 12 September 2026)
Result Declared9–10 October 2026 (Result Notice No. 287/2026)
Qualified Candidates10,613 (roll-number-wise PDF)
Official Portalaiimsexams.ac.in

The All India Institute of Medical Sciences, New Delhi, has released the AIIMS NORCET-11 Stage-2 (mains) result 2026 for the Nursing Officer Recruitment Common Eligibility Test held on 30 September 2026. A total of 10,613 nursing professionals have provisionally qualified and now proceed to online seat allocation for 2,216 Group-B Nursing Officer positions at AIIMS institutes and participating centres. The result has been published in PDF format as a roll-number-wise list of provisionally qualified candidates under result notice 287/2026, available on aiimsexams.ac.in.

How to Check the NORCET-11 Stage-2 Result

1
Visit the official AIIMS exam portal — open aiimsexams.ac.in, the only official source for NORCET results.
2
Open the Results/Announcements section — look for the "NORCET-11 Stage-II Result 2026" link (Notice No. 287/2026).
3
Download the result PDF — it contains the roll-number-wise list of all provisionally qualified candidates.
4
Search your roll number — press Ctrl+F in the PDF and enter your roll number to check your qualifying status.
5
Save the PDF — keep a downloaded copy, and read the result notice fully for the next-stage instructions.

NORCET-11 2026: Qualifying Cutoffs and Statistics

Candidates had to secure the minimum qualifying benchmark prescribed in the scheme of examination. The category-wise minimum qualifying cutoffs for Stage-2 are:

CategoryMinimum Qualifying Cutoff
UR / EWS50.000%
OBC45.000%
SC / ST40.000%
PwBD (UR/OBC/SC)45.417% / 41.042% / 37.708% respectively

The qualified pool of 10,613 includes 3,152 male and 7,461 female candidates. Category-wise, the largest shares are OBC (4,676), SC (2,073), UR (1,842), EWS (1,210) and ST (812). The list also carries separate entries for PwBD candidates. AIIMS has clarified that the candidature of all shortlisted candidates is provisional and subject to verification of eligibility conditions.

What Happens Next: Online Seat Allocation

Qualified candidates now enter the online seat allocation process for the 2,216 Nursing Officer seats. As per the official schedule, the final seat position and detailed procedure will be uploaded on 30 October 2026, and online choice filling runs from 30 October to 6 November 2026 (up to 5:00 PM). Candidates will submit their preferences for AIIMS institutes and other participating institutions through the designated portal.

After the choice-filling window closes, AIIMS will carry out provisional seat allotment, followed by document verification of eligibility credentials and original certificates at the allotted institute, and then joining formalities. Keep your nursing registration, qualification certificates, category certificates (if applicable) and photo ID ready, because verification deadlines move fast once allotment is announced.

Pay, Eligibility and Post Details

The NORCET-11 recruitment is for Nursing Officer (Group-B) posts in Level 07 of the pay matrix. As per the recruitment notice, candidates need the prescribed nursing qualifications registered with the relevant council, as notified for the 2026 cycle.

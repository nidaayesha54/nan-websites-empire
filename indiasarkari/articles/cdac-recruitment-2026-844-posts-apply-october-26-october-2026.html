The Centre for Development of Advanced Computing (C-DAC), a scientific society of the Ministry of Electronics and Information Technology, is running its Just-In-Time (JIT) October 2026 recruitment cycle with around 844 project posts across 11–12 centres. The application deadline is 26 October 2026 — just days away.

Posts include Project Engineer, Senior Project Engineer / Module Lead, Project Associate, Project Manager, Tech Lead, Project Officer and Project Technician, plus HR, Admin, Finance and Purchase support roles. Read this carefully before applying: these are purely contractual posts (up to 3 years or project completion), and you must apply separately for each post on each centre’s listing at careers.cdac.in.

Who Can Apply

Qualification is post-wise: B.E./B.Tech, M.E./M.Tech, M.Sc. and other degrees. Freshers can apply for Project Associate and Project Engineer posts; senior roles (Module Lead, Project Manager, Tech Lead) need relevant experience. Age limits vary by post and centre — read the specific centre’s notification. There is no application fee across all centres.

Contract Terms (Read Before Applying)

These are purely contractual engagements — up to 3 years or project completion, whichever is earlier — with no claim to regular C-DAC appointment. Consolidated emoluments range from ₹3.6 LPA to ₹22.9 LPA grade-wise. Treat it as a high-quality project role, not a permanent government job.

Selection Process

Application screening, followed by a written / skill test where applicable, and/or an interview. Shortlisted candidates are informed only by email — watch your inbox (and spam folder) after applying.

How to Apply (One Application Per Post)

1
Go to careers.cdac.in and open the listing of the centre and post you want.
2
Check the centre’s eligibility, age limit and emoluments — they differ centre to centre.
3
Apply separately for every post — one application covers only that post; repeat for each centre/post.
4
Submit before the centre’s closing time on 26 October 2026 (generally 18:00–23:59) — no fee is payable.
5
Watch your email for screening/interview communication from C-DAC.

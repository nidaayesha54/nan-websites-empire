Overview of RRB NTPC Graduate Recruitment 2026 (CEN 06/2026)

The Railway Recruitment Board has released the indicative notice for the RRB NTPC Graduate Recruitment 2026 under Centralised Employment Notice CEN 06/2026. A total of 3,477 Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate) posts are on offer across the zones. If you are a graduate looking for a railway job in 2026, this is one of the biggest openings of the year, so apply before the window closes on 6 November 2026 at 11:59 pm.

Eligibility Criteria

The recruitment is open to eligible graduates. Candidates must hold a university degree to be considered for the NTPC (Graduate) posts. Exact post-wise educational qualifications, age limits and reservation/relaxation rules are given in the detailed notification on the official RRB portals — check the detailed notification carefully before applying, because eligibility conditions can vary from post to post and any mismatch can lead to rejection at the document verification stage.

Selection Process

Candidates under CEN 06/2026 will go through the following stages:

  • CBT Stage 1 — first computer-based test, qualifying in nature for shortlisting.
  • CBT Stage 2 — second computer-based test for shortlisted candidates.
  • Skill Test — CBAT or typing test for the posts where applicable.
  • Document Verification (DV) — verification of certificates and eligibility.
  • Medical Examination — fitness standards as prescribed for the post.

Final merit is prepared post-wise, and candidates must clear every stage in sequence to make it to the final panel.

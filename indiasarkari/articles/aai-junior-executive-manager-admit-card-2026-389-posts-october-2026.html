AAI Junior Executive & Manager Admit Card 2026 Out: 389 Posts, Exam 21 & 26 Oct — Download Steps

The Airports Authority of India (AAI) has released the admit card for its Junior Executive and Manager Recruitment 2026. Candidates who applied for the 389 advertised posts across engineering, finance, operations, law, IT and other professional streams can now download their hall ticket from the official AAI website. The online examination is scheduled for 21 and 26 October 2026 — confirm your individual exam date, shift, reporting time and examination centre from your own admit card before travelling.

OrganizationAirports Authority of India (AAI)
RecruitmentJunior Executive and Manager Recruitment 2026
Total Posts389

Exam Dates21 and 26 October 2026
Official Websiteaai.aero

The 389 vacancies span Manager and Junior Executive grades in multiple disciplines, including engineering, finance, operations, law and IT. Because the examination is spread over two dates, your admit card is the only reliable source of your personal exam date and shift — do not assume it from someone else's hall ticket.

How to Download the AAI Admit Card 2026

1
Visit the official AAI website — open aai.aero and go to the Careers section.
2
Find the admit card link — look for the Junior Executive & Manager Admit Card 2026 notification.
3
Log in — enter your registration number or roll number along with your date of birth or password.
4
Download and print — take a clear printout of the hall ticket; a printed copy is mandatory at the gate.
5
Verify every detail — check your name, photograph, exam date, shift, reporting time, gate-closing time and centre address. Report any error to AAI immediately.

If you applied for more than one post, check whether separate admit cards have been issued — download each one and note the different exam dates and centres if they apply.

Exam-Day Instructions

  • Carry the printed admit card plus an original photo ID — Aadhaar, PAN card, passport, voter ID or driving licence.
  • Reach the centre well before the reporting time. Entry closes at the gate-closing time printed on your hall ticket, and late candidates are not admitted.
  • Electronic devices are not allowed inside the hall — no mobile phones, smart watches or calculators.
  • Follow the photograph and signature instructions on the admit card, and keep a couple of recent passport photographs handy.

Read every instruction printed on your own hall ticket — venue-specific rules on the admit card override any general guidance.

What Comes After the Exam

After the online examination, AAI will move to the next steps of the cycle: the result with the list of candidates shortlisted for the following stage, and then document verification (plus any further assessment prescribed for the stream in the official notification). Keep your application printout, admit card and certificates ready, and track the AAI careers page for the result schedule instead of relying on forwarded messages.

Tip: Save the AAI careers page and check it directly for the answer key and result announcements. Unofficial "result out" forwards frequently turn out to be fake or premature.

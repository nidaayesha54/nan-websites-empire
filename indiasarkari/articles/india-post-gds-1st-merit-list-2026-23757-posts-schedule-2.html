India Post GDS Merit List 2026

The 1st merit list for 23,757 Gramin Dak Sevak posts (Schedule-II, July 2026) released 12 October 2026 — check your selection status, circle-wise lists and what happens next.

The Department of Posts issued the India Post GDS Recruitment 2026 notification on 27 August 2026 under the GDS Online Engagement Schedule-II, July 2026. One-Time Registration (OTR) ran from 31 August to 19 September 2026, followed by the online application and fee window from 2 to 21 September 2026, with an application correction window on 23–24 September 2026. The 1st merit list was released on 12 October 2026, and selected candidates now move to document verification.

How to Check the GDS 1st Merit List

1
Visit the official GDS portal — open indiapostgdsonline.gov.in, the only official source for GDS merit lists.
2
Open the candidate/results section — look for the "Schedule-II (July 2026) — 1st Merit List" link for your postal circle.
3
Select your circle and division — merit lists are published circle-wise; download the PDF for your division.
4
Search your registration number — use Ctrl+F in the PDF to find your number and check your shortlisted post.
5
Save the PDF — keep a printout for the document verification round.

Selection Process and Pay (TRCA)

There is no written exam and no interview for GDS. Selection is purely on the merit of Class 10 marks, followed by document verification and a medical examination. The application fee was ₹100 for General/OBC/EWS male candidates; SC/ST/PwBD candidates and all female candidates paid nothing.

  • Branch Postmaster (BPM): TRCA slab ₹12,000 – ₹29,380 per month
  • ABPM / Dak Sevak: TRCA slab ₹10,000 – ₹24,470 per month

GDS posts are not regular central government posts, but they offer a stable rural-postal income with periodic TRCA revisions and a path to departmental advancement.

What Happens After the 1st Merit List

  • Document verification: shortlisted candidates report to the designated divisional office with originals — Class 10 marksheet and certificate, date-of-birth proof, caste/community certificate if applicable, local-language proof, photo ID, address proof and passport photographs.
  • Medical examination: a basic fitness check as prescribed by the Department of Posts.
  • Subsequent merit lists: seats left vacant after verification are filled through 2nd and later merit lists, so candidates close to the cutoff should keep watching the portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the India Post GDS 1st merit list 2026 released?
The 1st merit list for GDS Schedule-II (July 2026) was released on 12 October 2026 for 23,757 BPM, ABPM and Dak Sevak posts. Check the circle-wise merit list PDF on indiapostgdsonline.gov.in.
Is there a written exam for India Post GDS 2026?
No. Selection is purely merit-based on Class 10 marks — there is no written exam and no interview. Shortlisted candidates go through document verification and a medical examination.
What is the age limit for India Post GDS 2026?
Candidates must be 18 to 40 years old as on 21 September 2026. Age relaxation applies for SC/ST (5 years), OBC (3 years) and PwBD candidates as per government rules.
What documents are needed for GDS document verification?
Class 10 marksheet and certificate, proof of date of birth, caste or community certificate if applicable, local-language proof, photo ID, address proof and passport photographs.
When will the India Post GDS 2nd merit list 2026 be released?
India Post releases successive merit lists to fill seats left vacant after document verification. Watch indiapostgdsonline.gov.in for the 2nd merit list schedule after the 1st list verification round.

CRPF Sports Quota Recruitment 2026: 521 Head Constable & Constable GD Posts — Apply by 11 November

The Central Reserve Police Force (CRPF) has invited online applications from meritorious sportspersons for 521 posts of Head Constable (General Duty) and Constable (General Duty) under the Sports Quota — Advertisement No. R.II-21/2026-Sports, covering 22 recognised sports disciplines. Applications run from 12 October to 11 November 2026 (23:59) on rect.crpf.gov.in, with a correction window from 12 to 14 November 2026. There is no written examination — selection is through document verification, physical standards, a sports trial and a medical examination.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

For Head Constable (GD): 10+2 from a recognised board. For Constable (GD): Matriculation from a recognised board. Every candidate must also hold the prescribed sports qualification: broadly, representation of India in recognised international competitions, or of a State/UT in the National Games, or inter-university, inter-school or Khelo India level participation in the last two years. Check the official notification for the exact disciplines and certificates.

Age Limit

Candidates must be 18 to 23 years as on 1 August 2026. Upper-age relaxation of 5 to 13 years applies for reserved categories and Ex-Servicemen as per government rules.

Selection Process

There is no written test — selection is fitness- and skill-based:

1
Document Verification — educational, sports, caste/category and domicile certificates are verified first.
2
Physical Standard Test — height, chest (male) and weight measured as per CRPF standards.
3
Sports Trial — the deciding stage, tested in your claimed discipline.
4
Medical Examination — detailed medical (DME/RME), with a review medical on appeal.
Note: Selected candidates must sign a service bond of Rs 1,00,000 for 3 years.

How to Apply Online for CRPF Sports Quota Recruitment 2026

1
Visit rect.crpf.gov.in — open the CRPF recruitment portal and find the Sports Quota 2026 advertisement link.
2
Read the advertisement — confirm your sport is among the 22 recognised disciplines and note the certificates required.
3
Register and fill the form — enter details exactly as they appear on your certificates.
4
Upload documents and pay the fee — Rs 100 for male UR/OBC/EWS candidates; no fee for female, SC/ST and Ex-Servicemen candidates.
5
Submit by 11 November 2026 (23:59) — save your application number.

The Border Security Force (BSF) has released the e-admit cards for the Head Constable (Radio Operator) and Head Constable (Radio Mechanic) Computer Based Test (CBT). The exam for these 1,121 Communication Set-Up posts will be held on Monday, 2 November 2026 — Radio Operator candidates in the morning shift and Radio Mechanic candidates in the afternoon shift.

Download your hall ticket today from rectt.bsf.gov.in — the card will not be sent by post. BSF has informed candidates about roll numbers and the admit-card download through their registered mobile number and email ID. Print at least two clear A4 copies and verify your name, post, date, shift, centre, photograph, signature and category before exam day.

Honesty note: most news portals reported the admit cards released on 21 October 2026, while the BSF notice described the cards as going live at 09:00 hrs on 22 October 2026. If your card link is not active yet, retry on rectt.bsf.gov.in during the official window.

How to Download the E-Admit Card

1
Visit rectt.bsf.gov.in — the official BSF recruitment portal.
2
Click on Candidate Login (top right of the homepage) or find the link for the HC (RO/RM) CBT E-Admit Card 2026.
3
Enter your login details — registered email ID or BSF roll number, password (or date of birth), and the security captcha.
4
Download both cards if you applied for both posts — RO and RM admit cards are issued separately, for separate shifts.
5
Verify every detail — name, CBT roll number, BSF roll number, exam centre address, shift, photograph, signature, category and PwBD status — then take at least two printouts.

What to Carry on Exam Day

  • Printed e-admit card (two clear A4 copies) — entry without it is not allowed
  • One original, unexpired photo ID — Aadhaar, voter ID, PAN, passport or driving licence — with the same name as your application
  • Two recent photographs matching the one on your application
  • PwBD candidates: disability certificate and scribe declaration, if applicable
  • If your photograph or signature on the card looks mismatched, reach the helpdesk printed on the admit card first and carry a secondary ID

BSF has also activated the exam city intimation slip alongside the admit card — check your city and centre assignment the same day you download, so there are no travel surprises.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the BSF Head Constable RO/RM admit card 2026 released?
The e-admit cards went live on 21 October 2026, with BSF's notice stating 09:00 hrs on 22 October 2026 as the official download window. If your link is not active yet, retry on the portal.
What is the BSF HC (RO/RM) exam date 2026?
The Computer Based Test (CBT) is on Monday, 2 November 2026 — RO in the morning shift (09:00–11:00 AM) and RM in the afternoon shift (01:00–03:00 PM).
What login details are needed to download the BSF e-admit card?
Your registered email ID or BSF roll number with password (or date of birth), plus the on-screen captcha, at rectt.bsf.gov.in.
What ID proof must I carry on BSF exam day?
One original, unexpired photo ID — Aadhaar, voter ID, PAN, passport or driving licence — matching your application, plus two recent photographs.
I applied for both RO and RM posts. Do I need two admit cards?
Yes — BSF issues separate e-admit cards for HC (RO) and HC (RM). Download both; you will appear in both shifts on 2 November 2026.

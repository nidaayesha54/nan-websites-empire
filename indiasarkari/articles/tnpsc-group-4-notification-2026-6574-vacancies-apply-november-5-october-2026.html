TNPSC Group 4 Notification 2026 Out: 6,574 Vacancies — Apply by 5 November

The Tamil Nadu Public Service Commission has released the TNPSC Group 4 Notification 2026, announcing 6,574 vacancies across a range of Group IV services in the state. Online applications opened on 6 October 2026 and the last date to apply is 5 November 2026. Group 4 remains the most popular Tamil Nadu state government examination because it is open to candidates with basic qualifications — a 10th/SSLC pass is enough for several of the advertised posts — and it leads to secure, pensionable state government careers.

The advertised posts typically include Junior Assistant, Bill Collector, Typist, Steno-Typist and similar ministerial cadres in various Tamil Nadu government departments. Because the exam is a single-stage written test followed by document verification, preparation is straightforward and entirely syllabus-driven. The 2026 cycle is one of the largest Group 4 drives in recent years — do not let the 5 November deadline slip.

Eligibility Criteria for TNPSC Group 4 2026

  • Educational qualification: A pass in SSLC / 10th standard is the minimum for the core Group 4 posts. Posts like Steno-Typist and Typist additionally require the prescribed shorthand/typing qualifications — check the notification for post-wise requirements.
  • Age limit: The standard Group 4 age band is 18 to 32 years for general candidates, with relaxations for BC, MBC, SC/ST and other reserved categories as per Tamil Nadu government rules. Confirm the exact limits in the official notification.
  • Language: Adequate knowledge of Tamil is required for most posts — the notification specifies the exact requirement per cadre.
  • Domicile: Candidates claiming Tamil Nadu reservation benefits must meet the state's nativity conditions.

How to Apply for TNPSC Group 4 2026

1
Register on the TNPSC portal — open tnpsc.gov.in and complete the one-time registration if you do not already have one.
2
Open the Group 4 notification — read the full advertisement and identify the posts you are eligible for.
3
Fill the online application — enter personal, educational and category details exactly as in your certificates.
4
Pay the examination fee — pay online within the application window; keep the receipt.
5
Submit before 5 November 2026 — the portal will not accept applications after the closing date.
6
Download the confirmation — save the submitted application PDF for the admit card stage.

Exam Pattern & Preparation Strategy

The Group 4 written examination is an objective-type paper covering General Studies, Aptitude & Mental Ability, and General Tamil/General English. The paper is qualifying-plus-merit in nature — your marks decide your rank and therefore your post allocation. With 6,574 vacancies, the effective cutoff is likely to be friendlier than in leaner years, but competition for the top-preference posts (Junior Assistant in the Secretariat, for instance) will still be intense.

Finish the state-board-based General Studies syllabus first, drill aptitude daily, and solve at least five years of previous Group 4 papers — the best predictor of question style.

Tip: Fill post preferences carefully in the application — once submitted, preference order decides which post you are allotted at your rank. Research department-wise postings before choosing.

Documents Required for GDS Document Verification

Verification is physical — you must appear in person with original documents plus self-attested photocopies. Keep a checklist ready before visiting the divisional office:

  • Class 10 marksheet and pass certificate — the basis of your selection; marks must match what you entered in the application.
  • Date-of-birth proof — Class 10 certificate or other valid birth proof.
  • Caste or community certificate — applicable only if you claimed SC, ST or OBC status; it must be in the prescribed central government format and issued by a competent authority.
  • Local-language study proof — evidence that you studied the official local language of the postal circle up to Class 10 (or the prescribed level), as required by the notification.
  • Photo ID and address proof — Aadhaar card, voter ID or other government-issued ID.
  • Recent passport-size photographs — as per the application specifications.

Any mismatch between the details in your application form (name spelling, marks, category) and the original documents can delay or derail verification, so cross-check everything now.

GDS Document Verification Steps

1
Download your circle's merit list PDF — open indiapostgdsonline.gov.in and download the Schedule-II 1st merit list for your postal circle; search your registration number with Ctrl+F.
2
Note your division and office — the merit list entry shows the division and office allotted to you for verification.
3
Assemble all documents — gather originals and prepare a set of self-attested photocopies of each document on the checklist above.
4
Report to the divisional office — appear in person on or before 27 October 2026; the verifying officer will check each original against your application details.
5
Complete the medical examination — after DV, you will undergo the basic medical fitness check prescribed by the Department of Posts.

India Post GDS 2nd Merit List 2026: What to Expect

Every GDS cycle generates multiple merit lists because a share of 1st-list candidates either do not report for verification or fail document scrutiny. The 2nd merit list is prepared from the remaining applications in each circle, division and community category, in the same Class 10 marks order.

Candidates who missed the 1st-list cutoff by a narrow margin should keep their registration number handy and re-check the portal regularly; cutoff marks in smaller divisions can drop noticeably in the 2nd and 3rd lists.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the India Post GDS document verification deadline for Schedule-II 2026?
Shortlisted candidates in the India Post GDS 1st merit list (Schedule-II, July 2026) must complete document verification at their allotted divisional office on or before 27 October 2026. Missing the deadline can lead to cancellation of candidature.
Which documents are required for India Post GDS document verification?
Class 10 marksheet and certificate, date-of-birth proof, caste or community certificate if applicable, local-language study proof, photo ID, address proof and recent passport photographs. Carry originals plus self-attested photocopies.
Where is the GDS document verification conducted?
Verification is conducted at the divisional office allotted to you, as mentioned in the merit list PDF for your circle. Report to that office with all original documents before the deadline.
When will the India Post GDS 2nd merit list 2026 be released?
India Post releases successive merit lists to fill seats left vacant after document verification. The 2nd merit list schedule is expected after the 27 October 2026 verification deadline; watch indiapostgdsonline.gov.in.
Is there a written exam or interview for India Post GDS 2026?
No. Selection is purely merit-based on Class 10 marks. There is no written examination and no interview; the final stages are document verification and a medical examination.

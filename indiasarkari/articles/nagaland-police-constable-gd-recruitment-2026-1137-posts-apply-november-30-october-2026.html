The Nagaland Police has released the Constable (GD) Recruitment 2026 notification — 1,137 posts (1,053 under Gauhati High Court orders + 84 backlog). Online applications open on 31 October 2026 and close on 30 November 2026 at nagalandpolicerecruitment.in.

Only Indigenous inhabitants of recognised Naga tribes are eligible — Class 6 pass (Backward Tribes) / Class 8 pass (other Naga tribes), age 18–37, Pay Level 3 salary. Eligibility, physical standards, selection and how to apply below.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Backward Tribe candidates need a Class 6 pass; candidates of other recognised Naga tribes need a Class 8 pass. Only Indigenous inhabitants belonging to recognised Naga tribes of Nagaland may apply — non-indigenous applicants are not eligible.

Age Limit

18 to 37 years as on 14 October 2026 — a notably wide band that keeps this recruitment open to a large pool of candidates.

Domicile and District Rule

Apply only for the one district for which you are eligible based on native residence and tribe — selecting the wrong district can lead to rejection. Your Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate and Tribe Certificate must be issued by the competent authority.

Physical Standards and PET

Selection runs through a Physical / Medical Standards Test first — reported standards: 5'3" height (male), 5' (female); chest 31"/33" (male), 29"/31" (female) — confirm in the advertisement. The PET includes a running test. Failing any component, or a positive opioid/alcohol test, disqualifies.

Selection Process

1
Physical / Medical Standards Test — height, chest, weight and medical fitness measured first — failures do not proceed.
2
Physical Efficiency Test — running and field events as per the notification's standards.
3
Written Test — 80 questions for 40 marks in 2 hours, per the notification summary.
4
Interview — personality assessment for written-test qualifiers; check the notification for any NCC/sports weightage.
5
Document Verification & Final Merit — education, Indigenous Inhabitant, tribe and DOB certificates verified before the merit list.

Salary and Posting

Pay Level 3 (PB Rs 5,200–20,200 + GP Rs 1,800), 2-year probation, initial posting to India Reserve (IR) Battalions.

How to Apply Online (from 31 October)

1
Wait for the portal to open — registration begins 31 October 2026 on nagalandpolicerecruitment.in — offline forms are not accepted.
2
Read the advertisement — check district-wise and tribe-wise conditions; you may apply for only one eligible district.
3
Register and fill the form — enter personal, educational and tribe details exactly as on your certificates.
4
Upload documents — education certificate, Indigenous Inhabitant Certificate, tribe certificate, DOB proof and photo ID.
5
Pay Rs 300 and submit by 30 November — fee is non-refundable; save the confirmation for the admit-card stage.

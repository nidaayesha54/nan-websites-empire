ONGC CA/CMA Industrial Trainee 2026: 50 Seats, Apply by 15 Nov

Oil and Natural Gas Corporation Limited (ONGC) has issued Advt No. ONGC/Industrial Trainee/1/2026, dated 23 October 2026, inviting applications from CA and CMA Intermediate candidates for 50 industrial-training slots across five work centres. Applications close on 15 November 2026.

The split is 25 CA + 25 CMA trainees, distributed across Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Vadodara and Kolkata. The application route is an online form linked from the ONGC notice on ongcindia.com — no written test; selection is by eligibility screening and interview.

Kolkata2 CA + 3 CMA = 5
Total25 CA + 25 CMA = 50

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualification: CA Intermediate or CMA Intermediate passed, with a minimum of 60% overall in Class 12.
  • Age: born on or after 1 January 2000.
  • Fee: not mentioned in the notification — we do not quote a fee figure.

Stipend and Duration

Stipend₹22,000–₹27,000 per month — varies by city category (X/Y) and training year
Training durationCA: 9–12 months | CMA: 15–18 months

Selection Process

1
Eligibility screening — applications are screened against the Intermediate + Class 12 + age criteria.
2
Interview — online, offline or telephonic, at the candidate's own expense.
3
Engagement — as an industrial trainee for the fixed training period.

Read this carefully: this is industrial training — a fixed-term, stipend-based engagement under ICAI/ICMAI training norms. It is not permanent ONGC employment and carries no promise of absorption. It is, however, a strong line on a CA/CMA resume — Maharatna PSU exposure counts.

How to Apply

1
Open ongcindia.com and find Advt No. ONGC/Industrial Trainee/1/2026 (dated 23 October 2026).
2
Read the notice PDF end to end — confirm your city preference and the exact document list.
3
Fill the linked online application form (hosted as a Google Form via the notice) with Intermediate pass details, Class 12 marks and city preference.
4
Submit by 15 November 2026 and keep the confirmation; watch your email for interview scheduling.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many industrial trainee slots is ONGC offering?
50 — 25 for CA Intermediate and 25 for CMA Intermediate candidates, spread across Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Vadodara and Kolkata.
What is the eligibility for ONGC Industrial Trainee 2026?
CA/CMA Intermediate passed, with at least 60% overall in Class 12, and born on or after 1 January 2000.
What is the last date to apply?
15 November 2026. Applications go through the online form linked in Advt No. ONGC/Industrial Trainee/1/2026 on ongcindia.com.
What stipend do ONGC industrial trainees get?
Rs. 22,000–Rs. 27,000 per month depending on the city category and training year.

Eligibility screening followed by an interview (online, offline or telephonic) at the candidate's own expense. There is no written test.

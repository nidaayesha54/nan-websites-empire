The Uttar Pradesh Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB) has declared the UP Police Constable Final Result 2026 on 14 October 2026 for the direct recruitment of 32,679 Constable (Civil Police) and equivalent posts. The final selection was drawn from 28.86 lakh applicants — one of the largest single recruitment exercises in the country this year.

The result is published as name-and-roll-number-wise merit lists, released separately by force and gender: Civil Police, PAC/Armed Police, Special Security Force, Women's Battalion, Mounted Police, and male and female Jail Warder cadres. Category-wise cut-off marks on normalised scores have been published alongside.

How to Check the UP Police Final Result

1
Visit uppbpb.gov.in — the official UPPRPB website.
2
Open the notice dated 14 October 2026 for the final selection result (Constable Civil Police and equivalent posts).
3
Pick your post's PDF — List 1–6 covers Civil Police, PAC, SSF, Women Battalion, Mounted Police and Jail Warder.
4
Search with Ctrl+F — the lists carry registration numbers and names; save a print-ready copy for medical and verification days.

What Selected Candidates Must Do Next

  • Medical examination and character verification come next — district-level checks are already being scheduled.
  • Keep ready: admit card, ID proofs, reservation/domicile certificates, educational documents and the printout of your selection status.
  • Scorecards (with normalised marks) are downloadable with Registration Number + Date of Birth on the result portal.
  • Non-selected candidates: the board has indicated further phased police vacancies for FY 2026–27 — watch for the next UPPRPB advertisement.
Honesty flag: all dates and figures above (result 14 Oct 2026, 32,679 posts, cut-offs, timeline) are consistently reported by Times Now, CareerIndia, Daily Jagran and CareerPower — but the authoritative record is the UPPRPB notice dated 14 October 2026 on uppbpb.gov.in. Never trust merit-list PDFs forwarded on WhatsApp/Telegram; download only from the official portal.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the UP Police Constable Final Result 2026 declared?
On 14 October 2026 by the UP Police Recruitment and Promotion Board (UPPRPB), for 32,679 Constable and Jail Warder posts.
How many candidates were selected?
32,679 — including 10,469 Civil Police, 15,131 PAC/Armed Police, 1,341 SSF, 2,282 Women Battalion, 71 Mounted Police and 3,385 Jail Warders.
Where can I check the merit list?
On uppbpb.gov.in — open the notice dated 14 October 2026 and download your post's PDF (Lists 1–6 cover the six cadres).
What was the cut-off for Civil Police?
On normalised scores: UR 266.57849, EWS 261.59726, OBC 263.70370, SC 252.81045 and ST 238.28209. Other cadres have separate cut-offs.
How was the final merit decided?
On total normalised written-examination scores, merit ranking and the preferences submitted in the application, following the state's reservation policy.
What happens after the final result?
Selected candidates go for medical examination and character verification at the district level. Keep your admit card, ID and reservation certificates ready.

ISRO SDSC SHAR Jobs 2026 – 97 Posts, Apply by 9 Nov

The Satish Dhawan Space Centre SHAR (SDSC SHAR), Sriharikota — ISRO’s primary spaceport — has released Advertisement No. SDSC SHAR/RMT/03/2026 inviting online applications for 97 posts across technical, scientific, support and driving categories. The application window opened on 19 October 2026 (10:00 AM) and closes on 9 November 2026 (11:59 PM) at apps.shar.gov.in/Recruitment/.

Technician ‘B’ posts (43) lead the count, followed by Multi-Tasking Staff (16) and Technical Assistant (14). Vacancy breakup, eligibility, the refundable-fee policy and how to apply are below.

Most posts are at SDSC SHAR, Sriharikota; some Technician posts are at Balasore, Rasayani and ADRIN Secunderabad — check the post-code table in the advertisement.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification (post-wise)

  • Technical Assistant: first-class 3-year Diploma in the relevant discipline
  • Technician ‘B’: NCVT trade certificate (ITI / NTC / NAC) — Fitter, Electronic Mechanic and other trades
  • Scientific Assistant: B.Sc. in the relevant subject
  • Library Assistant ‘A’: Master’s degree in Library Science
  • Nurse ‘B’: Nursing qualification as specified in the notification
  • Drivers: Class 10 + valid driving licence + required experience (check post-wise conditions)
  • MTS: Class 10 pass

Age Limit

18 to 35 years for most posts; 18 to 25 years for Fireman ‘A’ and MTS, as on 9 November 2026. Age relaxation applies for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD as per government rules.

Application Fee (Refundable Only on Appearing)

A uniform processing fee payable online: ₹750 (post codes 07–15) and ₹500 (post codes 16–30). It is refunded only if you appear in the written test — Women/SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen get the full fee back; others get ₹500/₹400 back (₹250/₹100 retained). Skipping the exam loses the fee. (One portal flagged a words-vs-figures quirk in the fee clause; the figures are taken as authoritative — confirm in the official PDF.)

Selection Process

1
Written test — computer-based, held across 18 cities including Ahmedabad, Mumbai, Bhopal, Hyderabad, Chennai, New Delhi and Kolkata.
2
Skill / trade / physical test — skill test, physical efficiency test or trade test depending on the post code.
3
Final merit and posting — the written-test date will be announced on shar.gov.in; only candidates who appear qualify for the fee refund.

How to Apply Online

1
Open the recruitment portal at apps.shar.gov.in/Recruitment/ — applications are online only.
2
Download the detailed advertisement (Advt. SDSC SHAR/RMT/03/2026) and confirm your post code, qualification and posting location.
3
Register and fill the form — personal, educational and category details exactly as on your certificates.
4
Pay the processing fee online (₹750 / ₹500 by post code) — remember it is refunded only if you appear in the written test.
5
Submit by 9 November 2026, 11:59 PM and save the confirmation page for the admit-card stage.

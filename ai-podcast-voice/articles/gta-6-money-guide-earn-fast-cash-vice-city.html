Learn the money rules first

Before you earn a dollar, learn the three rules that govern cash in GTA 6, because they change every decision you make. First, you can only carry $20,000 at a time. Second, pocket cash is lost when you die or get arrested, and it does not carry into Chapter 2, Third, some missions only open once Jason and Lucia have earned enough money, a detail that comes from Rockstar North's Rob Nelson speaking via Famitsu — so a thin wallet can stall the story itself. Earn steadily, bank constantly, and never carry more than you can afford to lose.

Bank your cash at every chance

Banking is the most important money habit in GTA 6. Stash spare money under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom, and open the BuckMe bank account once the mission "Buck Me" is available, then deposit your cash at any of the 454 ATMs across Leonida. Banked money survives death, arrest, and the jump into Chapter 2; pocket cash does not. Bank after every big payday and check your wallet before every risky job.

Jewelry store robberies: around $20,000 each

The best mid-game money method is also the most direct: jewelry store robberies pay around $20,000 each. The named targets are Victoria Lee Jeweler in Capri, and Blanc and Giroux in the Decor District. Plan your exit before you walk in and keep the approach quiet — the police respond to what they witness. From Chapter 3, a snap gun breaks in silently. Rob, escape, bank, repeat.

Sell cars at Pay 'N' Spray

The lowest-effort money in the game is sitting on the street. Steal a car, drive it to a Pay 'N' Spray, and sell it — the trick is to grab the car right next to the shop, so the drive is short and the risk is small. It pays less than a robbery but needs no planning and no crew — one car, one sale, one deposit at the nearest ATM. Over a whole playthrough, those small sales add up.

Treasure dives: $1,400 per wreck, almost no risk

If robberies feel too hot and car sales feel too small, treasure dives are the calm middle ground. Kenton's treasure dives pay about $1,400 per wreck, with almost no risk, and they unlock after the mission "Island Dreams". The work is simple: dive the wreck, collect the treasure, surface, and bank it. There is no police response, no getaway, and no crew to split with. The payout is modest, but the dives are repeatable and safe — the best habit for steady money without steady heat.

Bank vaults: up to $205,000 — but only at the end

The biggest payday in GTA 6 is also the furthest away: bank vaults pay up to $205,000, but only at the end of Chapter 4. That timing matters, because it means the vaults are not an early-game plan — they are the reward for playing the whole story through. Build your money with the methods above and let the vaults come when the game is ready. One vault can fund the cars, upgrades and gear for the rest of the game. Until then, treat the jewelry stores, the car sales, and the dives as your income, and the vaults as the bonus at the end of the road.

Side jobs pay from the start

Do not ignore the honest work. Fishing, pool, towing, and deliveries are all paying jobs, and they pay from the very start of the game — before the jewelry stores open up, before the dives unlock, and long before the vaults. Towing and delivery jobs pay steadily with no heat, and teach you the map — the countryside, the Grassrivers, the Keys, and the back roads of Vice City — before the story needs them. Side jobs will never make you rich in an afternoon, but they are the income that keeps you going while the bigger methods unlock.

What not to do

Three warnings, and all three will save you money. First, never pay for a money generator, a cash glitch service, or a "free GTA 6 money" link — there is no legitimate free money, and those links are scams that steal accounts. Second, never trust a "GTA 6 PC download" link: there is no PC version at launch, so any PC download is a scam too. Third, do not hoard cash in your pocket while you wait for the perfect purchase — a death or an arrest wipes it out, and it does not carry into Chapter 2. Earn it, bank it, then spend it: that order is the whole money game in one line.

Frequently asked questions

Where do I bank my cash? Open the BuckMe bank account in the mission "Buck Me", then deposit at any ATM — there are 454 across Leonida. You can also stash spare money under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom.

Do I lose money when I die? Yes — pocket cash is lost on death or arrest, and it does not carry into Chapter 2. Banked money is safe, which is why banking after every big payday matters most.

What is the biggest single payout in GTA 6? Bank vaults pay up to $205,000, but only at the end of Chapter 4. Before that, jewelry robberies at around $20,000 each are the best repeatable payday.

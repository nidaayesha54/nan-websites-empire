How to Start an AI Podcast in 2026: The Complete Step-by-Step Guide

Updated 2026-10-12 · AIPodcastVoice

How to start an AI podcast in 2026: pick a niche, plan episodes, write scripts, choose an AI voice, edit, publish on Spotify, and grow — step by step.

Step 1: Pick a niche you can sustain

The #1 killer of new podcasts isn't bad audio — it's running out of things to say by episode 8. Pick a niche where you have genuine curiosity and at least 50 episode ideas. Good AI-podcast niches in 2026: daily news briefings, niche history stories, finance explainers, true crime retellings (public-domain cases), language learning, and bedtime stories. Validate demand by searching Spotify and Apple Podcasts — if the top shows have thousands of ratings, the audience exists.

Step 2: Plan your episode format

Decide three things before you record anything: length (15, 30, or 60 minutes — pick one and stay consistent), format (solo, interview, or narrative), and cadence (weekly beats daily for beginners; consistency beats frequency). Then use our episode planner to turn each topic into a timed outline with intro, segments, and outro — plus countdown timers so you rehearse against the clock.

Step 3: Write the script

AI-voiced podcasts live or die by the script, because there's no charismatic host to rescue weak writing. Aim for ~150 spoken words per minute (a 30-minute episode ≈ 4,500 words). Write conversationally — contractions, short sentences, rhetorical questions. Our script writer generates a structured, timestamped script for interview, solo, or narrative formats; you fill in the substance. Read every script aloud once before generating audio — your ears catch what your eyes miss.

Step 4: Choose your AI voice

Match the voice to your genre: warm and conversational for lifestyle, authoritative for business, dramatic for true crime. Take the voice picker quiz for a recommendation with exact settings, then compare finalists in our TTS demo using your real intro script. For production audio, use a dedicated service like ElevenLabs, PlayHT, or Murf — see our ranked comparison of the best AI voices for podcasts in 2026. Disclose AI narration in your show notes.

Step 5: Generate and edit the audio

Paste your script into your chosen voice service section by section (easier to fix mistakes), then assemble in a free editor like Audacity or Descript. Add royalty-free intro/outro music (YouTube Audio Library, Pixabay Music, or Uppbeat), normalize loudness to podcast standards (-16 LUFS stereo), and export as MP3 at 128 kbps. Listen to the full episode on phone speakers — that's how most of your audience will hear it.

Step 6: Publish on Spotify and Apple Podcasts

You need a podcast host to generate your RSS feed — Spotify for Creators (free), Buzzsprout, Transistor, or Podbean. Upload your cover art (3000×3000 px), write a keyword-rich show description, publish your first 3 episodes at once (gives new listeners something to binge), then submit the RSS feed to Spotify, Apple Podcasts, YouTube, and Amazon Music. Approval typically takes 1–5 days.

Step 7: Grow and monetize

Growth levers that work in 2026: audiogram clips on TikTok/Reels/Shorts, guest swaps with similar-size shows, SEO-optimized episode titles, and a simple newsletter. Monetization usually starts with affiliate links in show notes, then sponsorships around 1,000+ downloads per episode, plus premium feeds via Apple Podcasts Subscriptions or Patreon. Most shows take 6–12 months of consistent weekly episodes to find real traction — plan for the long game.

Reality check: AI voices remove the recording bottleneck, but not the content bottleneck. Shows that publish consistently for a year win; shows that chase perfect audio for three months and quit don't.

Frequently asked questions

What is an AI podcast voice?

An AI podcast voice is a synthetic narration voice created with text-to-speech technology. You type or paste your script, pick a voice, and the AI reads it aloud — many 2026 voices are nearly indistinguishable from human narration for short-form content.

Are AI-voiced podcasts allowed on Spotify and Apple Podcasts?

Yes. Both platforms allow AI-generated audio as long as it is not spam, does not impersonate real people without consent, and follows each platform's content policies. Disclosing AI use in your show notes is strongly recommended for listener trust.

Do I need to disclose that my podcast uses an AI voice?

There is no single global law yet, but transparency builds trust: most successful AI podcasters note it in the show description or episode notes. The FTC expects endorsements and claims to be truthful regardless of who — or what — reads them.

How long should a podcast episode be?

It depends on your audience: 20–30 minutes suits commute listening, 45–60 minutes suits deep-dive and interview shows, and under 15 minutes works for daily news or tips formats. Consistency matters more than length — our episode planner helps you hit your target runtime.

Can I use the Web Speech API demo for my final audio?

The built-in demo is for previewing how your script sounds with different rates, pitches, and voices. For publish-quality audio, use a dedicated AI voice service like ElevenLabs, PlayHT, or Murf, which offer higher-fidelity models, voice cloning, and commercial licenses.

How do I pick the right AI voice for my show?

Match the voice to your tone, audience, and genre: warm and conversational for lifestyle shows, authoritative for business and news, energetic for comedy and sports. Take our free AI voice picker quiz — it recommends a style and the exact settings to use.

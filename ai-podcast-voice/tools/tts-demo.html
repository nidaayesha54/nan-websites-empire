How the demo works

This demo uses the Web Speech API built into your browser — no audio ever leaves your device. Pick any installed voice, tune the rate and pitch, and press play to hear your script read aloud. It's perfect for checking pacing, awkward phrasing, and whether your hook lands.

Preview vs. production

  • Preview (this tool): free, instant, private — great for rehearsal and script QA.
  • Production: dedicated AI voice services (ElevenLabs, PlayHT, Murf) offer studio-quality models, voice cloning, and commercial licenses for published episodes.

Tips

  • Read your intro at 1.0× first — if it drags there, it will drag at any speed.
  • Test the same script with 2–3 voices; the "best" voice is the one you stop noticing.
  • Mark breath points in your script with line breaks — most TTS engines pause slightly at paragraphs.

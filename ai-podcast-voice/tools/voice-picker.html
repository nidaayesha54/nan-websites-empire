How the quiz works

Each answer scores points toward five voice archetypes — Warm Conversationalist, Trusted Authority, High-Energy Entertainer, Cinematic Storyteller, and Calm Guide. Your top match comes with exact rate, pitch, and pause settings, plus a runner-up worth A/B testing.

Why voice fit matters

  • First 30 seconds: listeners decide whether to stay before your content even starts — voice is the packaging.
  • Genre expectations: a true-crime show in a bubbly voice feels wrong; match the mood.
  • Consistency: one voice style across episodes builds a recognizable brand.

Tips

  • Test your top two picks on 5 real listeners before committing — ask which they'd subscribe to.
  • Keep the same voice for at least 10 episodes; switching early confuses returning listeners.
  • Disclose AI voice use in your show notes — transparency builds trust and it's increasingly expected.

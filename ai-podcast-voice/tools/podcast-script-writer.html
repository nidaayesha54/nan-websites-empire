How the script builder works

Pick a format and the builder lays out the proven structure for it — interview (cold open, guest intro, 4 questions, lightning round, wrap), solo (hook, 3 big ideas, recap), or narrative (cold open, three acts, resolution). Every beat gets a timestamp scaled to your target runtime, a speaker label, and production cues like music and SFX.

Why scripted beats win

  • Tighter episodes: scripted shows routinely cut editing time in half.
  • Better retention: hooks and act breaks are placed where drop-off spikes.
  • AI-voice ready: the output pastes straight into ElevenLabs, PlayHT, or Murf.

Tips

  • Write the cold open last — lift the most surprising 20 seconds from your recording.
  • For interviews, send questions to guests 48 hours ahead; spontaneity improves when guests feel safe.
  • Read narration scripts aloud once and mark where you naturally breathe — those are your edit points.

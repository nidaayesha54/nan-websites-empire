How the planner works

Your target runtime is split into blocks: roughly 8% intro, 7% outro, and the rest divided evenly across your segments (leftover minutes go to the earliest segments). Each block gets a built-in countdown timer, so you can rehearse against the clock and learn your real pacing before you record.

Why timed outlines work

  • No rambling: a timer per segment forces one idea per block.
  • Consistent length: listeners subscribe to shows that respect their commute.
  • Easier editing: timed blocks map cleanly to chapters on Spotify and Apple Podcasts.

Tips

  • Write your hook word-for-word; improvise the rest from the talking points.
  • Run the timers while rehearsing once — most hosts discover they talk 20% faster on mic.
  • End every segment with a one-line bridge into the next to keep listeners from dropping off.

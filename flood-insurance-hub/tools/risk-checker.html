Is My Area at Risk of Flooding?

Answer 6 quick questions for an honest read on your flood exposure — then learn what flood zones really mean.

Flood Risk Quiz

1. How close is your home to water?

2. Has your neighborhood ever flooded?

3. What does your FEMA flood map say?

4. Does your home have a basement or sit low?

5. Does your area get hurricanes, tropical storms, or heavy seasonal rain?

6. Is the area heavily paved / developed around you?

Flood Zones, Explained Simply

🌊

Zone V / VE

Highest risk. Coastal areas with wave action. 1%+ annual flood chance plus storm-wave hazard. Flood insurance is almost always required with a mortgage.

🏞️

Zone A / AE

High risk. 1% annual flood chance ("100-year floodplain"). Mandatory insurance with federally backed mortgages. This is the classic "flood zone."

🌧️

Zone B / X (shaded)

Moderate risk. 0.2% annual chance ("500-year floodplain") or shallow flooding areas. Insurance optional — but claims here are common.

☀️

Zone X (unshaded) / C

Low risk. Outside the 500-year floodplain. Cheapest insurance — yet over 25% of NFIP claims come from these areas.

❔

Zone D

Unknown risk. No flood-hazard analysis done. Don't mistake "undetermined" for "safe" — treat it as moderate risk.

Look up your official zone: Enter your address at FEMA's Map Service Center (msc.fema.gov) or FloodSmart.gov to see your property's designated flood zone.
⚠️ Estimates only, not financial advice. This quiz is an educational self-assessment, not a professional flood study. Confirm your zone with FEMA maps and a licensed agent.

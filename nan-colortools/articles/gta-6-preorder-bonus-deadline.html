GTA 6 Pre-Order Bonus: The Deadline Is After Launch

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Here is the fact most GTA 6 pre-order guides get wrong: you do not need to pre-order to get the pre-order bonus. The Vintage Vice City Pack — the free bonus Rockstar announced with pre-orders — comes with every purchase made before November 20, 2026, one day after the game launched. Buy it on launch day, November 19, and you still get everything. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and this guide explains what the bonus actually is, the deadline that matters, the GTA+ catch that comes with digital copies, and why rushing to pre-order months early was never necessary.

It matters because the pressure to pre-order is real, and it is mostly manufactured. Digital copies cannot sell out. There is no early access at any price. The Standard Edition is $79.99 and the Ultimate Edition is $99.99 whether you pre-order or buy at launch. The only thing a pre-order buys you is the preload — and the bonus does not need it.

What Is in the Vintage Vice City Pack

The Vintage Vice City Pack is a set of in-game items that call back to Rockstar's 2002 game Grand Theft Auto: Vice City, and it is the same bonus for everyone. Every pre-order and every purchase made before November 20, 2026 gets it — Standard or Ultimate Edition, digital or boxed, PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series X|S, from any retailer. There is no store-exclusive version and no better version for paying more.

The pack is cosmetic and vehicle content only: a '55 Vapid Stanier, a two-tone classic sedan with its own garage — the Shore Court Garage in Ocean Beach — plus retro outfits and hairstyles for Jason and Lucia, and a Tommy Vercetti-inspired weapon pattern for most guns. Rockstar's support page lists three items in the pack, and the guides that break it down agree on the contents. It touches no missions, no areas and no progression. Skipping it costs you a car, a garage, two sets of clothes and a weapon finish — nothing that changes how the game plays. But it is free, it has real charm, and Rockstar has not said whether it will ever be sold as paid content after the window closes. Miss November 20, and it is gone, at least for now.

The Deadline: November 20, Not November 19

The deadline is the day after release, not the day before it — and that one-day difference is the whole point of this guide. Rockstar's stated date is November 20, 2026: buy or pre-order before then and the pack is yours, and that includes November 19 itself. One outlet says the 19th, but Rockstar's date is the one to trust. The bonus rewards buying before November 20, not buying months early. If you were waiting for reviews before committing, waiting cost you nothing on digital: the pack applies to digital purchases made before November 20 anyway.

Physical copies work slightly differently. The pack is included with pre-orders of the physical version while supplies last — and the box holds a download code, not a disc, so the "physical" copy is a code in a box. Digital buyers face no such limit: digital copies cannot sell out, and the bonus applies to any digital purchase before the deadline.

The GTA+ Catch: Free for a Month, Then It Renews

Digital pre-orders and purchases come with one more thing: a free month of GTA+, Rockstar's subscription service. Boxed copies do not include it. The free month is for GTA Online — the online mode of GTA 5, which keeps running alongside GTA 6 — not for GTA 6 itself. It gets you a recurring GTA$500,000 deposit into your GTA Online account each month, Shark Cards with a 15% bonus, free and discounted GTA Online vehicles, and the GTA+ Games Library. None of that is a GTA 6 feature, and the Xbox Store listing says plainly that Grand Theft Auto VI is a single-player experience.

The catch is the renewal: the free month auto-renews after it ends, so cancel it if you do not want to pay. That is the one real trap in the whole bonus picture — not the pack, which is free and permanent, but the subscription that quietly starts charging. If you buy digitally, set a reminder to cancel GTA+ before the month ends, unless you actually play GTA Online and want to keep it.

Is It Worth Pre-Ordering For?

Honestly, no — not for the bonus on its own. The pack is free, the deadline runs a day past launch, and there is no reason to pre-order early just for it. What pre-ordering does buy you is the preload: 212 GB downloaded before launch night, so you play the moment the game unlocks instead of waiting hours for the download. Pre-loading opened on November 10 — midnight local time on PS5, 00:00 GMT on Xbox — and for most people, that is the only real reason to commit early.

Two more things to check before you buy. First, do not mistake the $399.99 "Vice City Collection" for a deluxe edition — it is merchandise with no copy of GTA 6 included. Second, know the cancellation rules: if you paid more than 14 days before release, you can cancel any time up to the release date on both PS5 and Xbox. If you are certain you will play on day one and want the download done, pre-order. If you are on the fence, buy on launch day — the bonus will still be there.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to pre-order to get the Vintage Vice City Pack?

No. Every purchase made before November 20, 2026 gets it — Standard or Ultimate, digital or boxed, any retailer. Buying on launch day, November 19, still qualifies.

What does the free month of GTA+ give me in GTA 6?

Nothing. GTA+ is Rockstar's membership for GTA Online on GTA 5, not a GTA 6 feature. The free month comes with digital purchases only, and it auto-renews — cancel it if you do not want to pay.

Is there a physical disc version of GTA 6?

No. The boxed copy contains a download code, not a disc, and there is no boxed Ultimate Edition. The game is 212 GB, and it downloads either way.

The Bottom Line

The GTA 6 pre-order bonus is free with any purchase before November 20 — a classic car, an Ocean Beach garage, retro outfits and a weapon pattern, plus a GTA+ month on digital copies that auto-renews if you forget it. Do not rush to pre-order for the bonus: buy when you are ready, before the deadline, and spend the waiting time making room for 212 GB instead.

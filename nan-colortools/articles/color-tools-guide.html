2. HEX to RGB Converter: Understanding the Math

Converting hex to rgb is pure positional arithmetic — no approximation anywhere. A six-digit hex code like #14B8A6 is three two-digit values in base 16 (red, green, blue in that order), using digits 0–9 then a–f for ten through fifteen. Two hex digits cover 0–255, exactly the range of an 8-bit RGB channel, which is why the notation exists: it is the shortest exact spelling of a 24-bit color.

To convert one channel pair to decimal: multiply the first digit by 16, then add the second. Worked example with #14B8A6:

ChannelPairMathDecimal
Red141 × 16 + 420
GreenB811 × 16 + 8184
BlueA610 × 16 + 6166

Result: #14B8A6 = rgb(20, 184, 166). Our HEX / RGB / HSL converter does this instantly in any direction: paste #14B8A6, rgb(20, 184, 166) or hsl(173, 80%, 40%) and get all three formats back, with a live color preview and one-click copy. It accepts 3-digit shorthand (#fa3 expands to #FFAA33), with or without the leading #, and shows a clear error message on invalid input instead of silently guessing.

When to use which format: use HEX when writing CSS by hand (compact, universal); RGB when manipulating channels in JavaScript, canvas code, or APIs that want integers; HSL when you need to derive shades and hover states — to make a button 10% darker, keep the same H and S and drop L by 10%, and the hue stays perfect. Doing that in HEX or RGB requires juggling all three channels and usually shifts the hue by accident.

3. Color Palette Generator: Harmonious Colors From One Base

A color palette generator takes one base color and produces a set of colors that look good together, using classic color-harmony rules. Ours generates 5-color palettes in four modes:

  • Complementary — the base color plus its opposite on the color wheel (180° hue shift), with lighter and darker variants. Maximum contrast and energy; the classic choice for buttons and calls-to-action.
  • Analogous — colors ±15° and ±30° from the base hue. Calm, cohesive, and easy on the eyes — ideal for backgrounds, gradients and branding where everything should feel like family.
  • Triadic — three colors evenly spaced 120° apart, plus a tint and shade of the base. Bold and vibrant; great when you need three distinct accent colors that still balance.
  • Random — five fresh colors for brainstorming and mood-board sessions.

Click any palette color to copy its HEX code, or use Copy all to grab the whole palette in one go. 4. Contrast Checker: Pass WCAG Accessibility

A contrast checker measures the WCAG 2.1 contrast ratio between text (foreground) and its background. The formula compares relative luminance: ratio = (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), where L is the weighted luminance of each color. It ranges from 1:1 (same color — invisible) to 21:1 (black on white — maximum).

The WCAG thresholds you must hit:

Text typeAA (minimum)AAA (enhanced)
Normal text (below 18pt)4.5 : 17 : 1
Large text (18pt+, or 14pt+ bold)3 : 14.5 : 1

Our checker shows the exact ratio, live AA / AAA pass-fail badges for both normal and large text, and a live preview of your actual sample text on the chosen background. Enter colors via the visual pickers or paste hex codes directly. Common failures to avoid: light gray text on white, brand-color buttons with white text (many brand teals and greens fail at 4.5:1 — test before you ship), and colored text on busy image backgrounds.

Why this matters: contrast isn't just an accessibility nicety — in many regions it's a legal requirement for public and commercial sites, and readable text converts better. One 30-second check saves redesigns later.

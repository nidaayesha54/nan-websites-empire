FAQs

What is the difference between HEX, RGB and HSL?

HEX is the compact #RRGGBB form of RGB (base-16 vs 0–255 channels) — the standard in CSS. RGB expresses red, green, blue as decimals 0–255, useful for code. HSL describes hue (0–360°), saturation and lightness — the most designer-friendly way to derive shades and palettes.

What is a good contrast ratio for text?

WCAG 2.1 requires at least 4.5:1 for normal text (AA) and 7:1 for AAA. Large text (18pt+ / 14pt+ bold) needs 3:1 (AA) or 4.5:1 (AAA). Use the Contrast Checker above to test any pair.

Are my colors uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs 100% in your browser — no accounts, no servers, no tracking of your color choices.

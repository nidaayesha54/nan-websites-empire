The Three Rules of GTA 6 Money

Before the methods, the rules — because earning money in GTA 6 is only half the job. First, you can only carry $20,000 in your pocket at a time. Second, pocket cash is lost when you die or get arrested, and it does not carry into Chapter 2. Third, the fix is simple: stash spare money under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom, and open the BuckMe bank account once the mission "Buck Me" is available, then deposit your cash at ATMs. There are 454 ATMs across Leonida. Banked money is safe; pocket money is a donation to the next mugger, cop, or bad decision.

Every method below ends the same way: bank the money before you lose it. The players who get rich are not the ones who earn the most — they are the ones who keep what they earn.

Jewelry Stores: About $20,000 a Job

The best early-game money method is also the most straightforward: rob a jewelry store. Each job pays around $20,000, and the named targets are Victoria Lee Jeweler in Capri, and Blanc and Giroux in the Decor District. A snap gun from Chapter 3 breaks in silently, which matters more than it sounds — GTA 6 rebuilt the wanted system so the police act only on what they actually witness, with no magic GPS. A quiet entry means fewer witnesses, and fewer witnesses means a smaller response. Smash-and-grab in front of a crowd is the same payout with a much bigger chase attached.

Bank the $20,000 as soon as the job is done. One jewelry job fills your pockets completely — a full pocket is a pocket waiting to be emptied by a death or an arrest. Hit the store, leave quietly, find an ATM, deposit it, then plan the next one.

Steal Cars, Sell Them by the Pay 'N' Spray

The low-effort staple of GTA 6 money is the car flip: steal a car right next to a Pay 'N' Spray and sell it. It is the method for the gaps between missions — no setup, no planning, just a car, a short drive, and cash in hand. It will not make you rich in one trip, but it is the steadiest income in the game, and it is always available: Vice City is full of cars, and the Pay 'N' Spray is never far away.

The cars worth selling are the ones in good condition, and the time to sell is right after you take one — not after a long drive through a police chase. Keep it simple, keep it quick, and bank the money with the rest.

Treasure Dives: $1,400 a Wreck, Almost No Risk

For the calmest money in the game, go diving. Kenton's treasure dives pay around $1,400 per wreck, with almost no risk, once they unlock after the mission "Island Dreams". There is no chase, no wanted level, no witness problem — just open water, a wreck, and a payout. It is not the biggest money in the game, but it is the most relaxing, and it is the method to use when you want to earn without drawing any attention at all.

A few dives between missions adds up to real money, and every dollar of it goes straight to the bank, the same as any other method.

The Big Vaults: Up to $205,000 at the End of Chapter 4

The biggest paydays in GTA 6 come from the bank vaults, paying up to $205,000 — but only at the end of Chapter 4. Getting there takes the whole game, and that is the point: the vaults are the reward for the journey, not a shortcut around it. The smaller jobs along the way — the jewelry stores, the car flips, the treasure dives — are how you build the money and the skills for the big ones.

Do not rush toward the vaults. The money gates along the way — missions that only open once you have earned enough — are there to make sure you arrive prepared. Earn steadily, bank everything, and the vaults will be there when you get to them.

What Not to Do

Do not pay anyone for a "money method". The verified methods in this guide are all in the game, free, and available to every player. Do not trust a "money glitch" video, either: missions cannot be replayed, so a glitched payout cannot be repeated. And do not carry your fortune in your pocket — the $20,000 cap, the cash lost on death or arrest, and the Chapter 2 reset all punish players who hoard cash. The bank is the whole game: earn it, bank it, and it is yours forever.

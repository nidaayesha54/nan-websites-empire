What Is Statutory Bonus?

Statutory bonus is a compulsory annual payment every covered employer in India must pay to eligible employees. It was governed by the Payment of Bonus Act, 1965; from 21 November 2025 the Act stands replaced by the Code on Wages, 2019, which keeps the same core rules: the Rs 21,000 eligibility ceiling, the 8.33%–20% bonus range and the Rs 7,000/minimum-wage calculation base. Establishments with 20 or more employees must comply, and bonus must be paid within 8 months of the close of the accounting year.

Bonus Formula (2026)

Statutory Bonus = Bonus Wage × 12 × Bonus Rate%
Where Bonus Wage = your monthly wages (Basic + DA), capped at Rs 7,000 or your applicable state minimum wage, whichever is higher. The bonus rate is a minimum of 8.33% (or Rs 100, whichever is higher) and a maximum of 20%, linked to the employer's allocable surplus. Worked fewer months? The bonus is reduced proportionately.

Example

Monthly wages Rs 15,000, minimum wage Rs 9,000 → bonus base = Rs 9,000. Annual base = Rs 1,08,000. At the 8.33% minimum: Rs 8,996/year; at the 20% maximum: Rs 21,600/year.

Eligibility Checklist

  • Monthly wages (Basic + DA) of Rs 21,000 or less.
  • Worked at least 30 working days in the accounting year.
  • Employer has 20+ employees (establishment coverage).
  • Not disqualified for fraud, riotous behaviour, theft or sabotage.
  • New establishments get a short exemption window (generally up to 5–6 years from commencement, depending on circumstances).

Bonus Is Taxable

Statutory bonus is treated as salary income and taxed at your applicable income-tax slab rate. It must be included in your salary when filing your ITR.

FAQs

What counts as "wages" for bonus calculation?

Only Basic salary + Dearness Allowance (DA). HRA, conveyance, overtime, bonuses and commissions are excluded.

What if my salary is between Rs 7,000 and Rs 21,000?

You are eligible, but the bonus is calculated on Rs 7,000 or the applicable minimum wage (whichever is higher) — not on your full salary.

What is the minimum bonus I must receive?

8.33% of your annual bonus wages or Rs 100, whichever is higher (Rs 60 for employees below 15 years of age), even if the employer made a loss.

When should bonus be paid?

Within 8 months of the end of the accounting (financial) year — typically before Diwali in October/November.

Is there a bonus for contract / fixed-term workers?

Fixed-term employees covered by the establishment rules are eligible on the same 30-working-day service test; bonus is computed pro-rata on their bonus wages.

How UGC pricing works in 2026

UGC rates depend on four things: video length, your experience, usage rights, and niche. The biggest lever is usage — a video running as a paid ad is worth 2–3x more than an organic post, because it directly drives revenue.

2026 market benchmarks

  • Beginner: $150–$250 per 15–30s video
  • Intermediate: $250–$400 per video
  • Pro: $400–$750+ per video
  • Monthly retainers: $1,500–$3,000 for daily/every-other-day content

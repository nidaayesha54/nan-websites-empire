📩 Brand Outreach Templates

5 copy-paste DM and email templates that actually get replies. Personalize the bracketed parts, hit send, land deals.

📌 Golden rule: Always personalize ONE line about the brand (a product you tried, a recent ad you saw). Mass-blasted generic pitches get ignored. These templates do the heavy lifting — your personal touch closes the deal.

1. Cold DM — Instagram / TikTok

Hi [Brand Name] team! 👋 I'm [Your Name], a UGC creator in the [niche] space. I just tried your [specific product] and honestly loved [one genuine detail]. I create scroll-stopping UGC videos for brands — 15-30 sec, filmed + edited, ready to run as ads. Here's my portfolio: [portfolio link] Would you be open to a gifted collab or paid video? I can send over 2-3 creative concepts this week. Let me know! 🎬

2. Cold Email — Professional Pitch

Subject: UGC video idea for [Brand Name] 🎬 Hi [Name], I'm [Your Name], a UGC creator specializing in [niche] content. I came across [Brand Name] through [where — be specific] and had an idea I couldn't shake: [1-sentence video concept, e.g. "A '3 mistakes you're making with serums' hook featuring your Vitamin C serum as the fix."] I produce authentic, ad-ready videos (15-60 sec) that brands run on TikTok, Reels, and Meta ads. Portfolio here: [portfolio link] My rate for a single video with 3-month paid usage is $[your rate]. Happy to start with one video so you can test performance risk-free. Open to a quick chat this week? Best, [Your Name] [Phone] · [Portfolio link]

3. Follow-Up (3-5 days later)

Hi [Name]! Just floating this back up ⬆️ I actually went ahead and filmed a quick 15-sec sample concept for [Brand Name] — no strings attached: [sample link or "happy to send it over"] If you like the style, I'd love to create a full video for your next campaign. My current turnaround is [X days]. Worth a look? 😊 [Your Name]

4. Rates Pitch — When They Ask "What Do You Charge?"

Great question! Here's my current rate card: 🎬 Single UGC video (15-30s): $[rate] 📦 Bundle of 4 videos: $[rate x 3.2, rounded] 🔁 Monthly retainer (8 videos/mo): $[rate x 8 x 0.8, rounded] All packages include: ✅ Filming + editing + captions ✅ 1 round of revisions ✅ 3-month paid usage rights ✅ Raw footage available (+$50) Usage extensions and whitelisting are quoted separately. I can also tailor a package to your campaign — what did you have in mind? [Your Name]

5. Long-Term Partnership Ask

Hi [Name]! 👋 Loved working on [previous video/campaign] — [specific result if you have one, e.g. "the hook variation outperformed the brand's average CTR"]. I had an idea: what if we did a monthly content retainer? I'd deliver [X] fresh UGC videos per month, so you always have new creative to test — most brands see ad fatigue drop significantly with fresh creative every 2 weeks. My retainer rate is $[monthly rate]/month for [X] videos, which saves you [X]% vs. per-video pricing. Interested in a 3-month trial? Happy to jump on a 15-min call to map it out. Cheers, [Your Name]

🖼️ Build a UGC Portfolio That Gets Brand Deals

Zero followers? Zero clients? No problem. Here's the exact portfolio system working creators use to land paid deals.

The #1 truth: Brands don't hire your follower count — they hire your content. A portfolio of 5–10 strong spec videos beats 100K followers with weak content, every time.

Step 1: Pick 2–3 niches (not 10)

Brands want specialists. Pick niches you can film authentically at home:

  • Beauty/skincare — highest demand, endless products
  • Food & beverage — easy to film, viral potential
  • Fitness/wellness — strong retainer market
  • Tech/apps — higher rates, screen-record friendly
  • Home/lifestyle — relatable, broad brand pool

Step 2: Film 5–10 "spec" videos

Spec videos = sample ads you make for real products you already own, with no brand paying you. This is how every successful creator starts.

  1. Grab 2–3 products from your bathroom/kitchen per niche
  2. Use our script generator to write each script (hook → problem → solution → CTA)
  3. Film in natural window light, phone on a stack of books (tripod optional)
  4. Edit in CapCut (free): captions on, jump cuts every 2–3 seconds, trending audio low in the mix
  5. Export 1080×1920 vertical, under 60 seconds each

Step 3: Host it where brands actually look

  • Best: a simple portfolio page (Canva website, Notion, or Google Drive folder with thumbnails)
  • Good: a dedicated Instagram/TikTok account with your spec videos pinned
  • Avoid: sending raw Google Drive links with 40 unnamed files — curate!

Label every video clearly: "Spec ad — Vitamin C Serum (Skincare, 30s)". Make it effortless for a busy brand manager to see your range in 60 seconds.

Step 4: The 5 videos every portfolio needs

  1. Problem/solution ad — the classic converter
  2. Unboxing + first impression — shows authentic energy
  3. Testimonial style — talking head, trust-building
  4. Aesthetic/product showcase — proves you can do pretty
  5. Trend-style video — proves you understand the platform

Step 5: Pitch with the portfolio (don't wait)

Don't wait until it's "perfect" — start pitching at 5 videos. Use our outreach templates and mention one specific thing you like about each brand. Send 10 pitches a day; expect a 10–20% reply rate.

💡 Real talk: Most creators get their first paid deal within 2–4 weeks of consistent pitching with a 5-video portfolio. The portfolio gets you in the door; the pitch closes it.

Step 6: Price it right from day one

Don't race to the bottom. Start at $150/video minimum — run your numbers through our UGC rate calculator and quote with confidence. Cheap rates attract nightmare clients; fair rates attract serious brands.

🎯 Your 7-day action plan:
Day 1–2: Pick niches, write 5 scripts
Day 3–4: Film all 5 videos
Day 5: Edit + upload to portfolio
Day 6–7: Send your first 20 pitches

Frequently Asked Questions ❓

What is UGC and how do creators make money from it? +
UGC (user-generated content) is authentic-style photo and video content brands pay creators to produce for ads and social media. Creators earn $150–$500+ per video, and unlike influencers, you don't need a big following — brands pay for the content itself.
How much should I charge for UGC as a beginner? +
Beginner UGC creators typically charge $150–$250 for a single 15–30 second video. As your portfolio grows, rates rise to $300–$500+ per video, with monthly retainers of $1,500–$3,000. Use our free UGC rate calculator to get a personalized range.
Do I need followers to become a UGC creator? +
No. UGC is about the content you create, not your audience size. Brands license your videos to run as ads on their own accounts. A strong portfolio of 5–10 sample videos matters far more than follower count.
Are these UGC tools really free? +
Yes — every tool on this site is 100% free with no signup required. The rate calculator, script generator, outreach templates, and guides are all available instantly.

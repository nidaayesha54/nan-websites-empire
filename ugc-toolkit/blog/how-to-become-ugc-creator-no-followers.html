How to Become a UGC Creator With No Followers

Beginner guide · 10 min read · Updated Oct 2026

Here's the secret the influencer world doesn't want you to know: you don't need followers to get paid for content. UGC (user-generated content) creators earn $150–$500+ per video with zero audience — because brands buy the video, not your reach. This is the exact roadmap from zero to your first paid brand deal.

What UGC actually is (and isn't)

UGC is authentic-style photo and video content that brands pay creators to produce — then run as ads on the brand's TikTok, Instagram, and Meta accounts. It's not influencer marketing (no posting to your audience required) and it's not acting (relatability beats polish). If you can film a convincing product video on your phone, you can do UGC.

Step 1: Learn what good UGC looks like (Days 1–2)

Before filming anything, study the format:

  • Open TikTok → search "TikTok made me buy it" → save 20 videos you couldn't scroll past
  • Note the pattern: hook in 3 seconds, problem, demo, proof, CTA — every time
  • Watch with sound off: if the captions alone sell it, that's the standard

Step 2: Pick your 2–3 niches (Day 3)

Brands hire specialists. Choose niches you can film authentically with products you already own:

  • Beauty/skincare — biggest demand, endless products
  • Food & beverage — easy to film, viral-friendly
  • Fitness/wellness — strong retainer market
  • Tech/apps — higher rates, screen-record friendly

Step 3: Film 5 spec videos (Days 4–7)

Spec videos are sample ads you make for products you own — no client needed. Use our script generator for each script, then film:

  1. A problem/solution ad (the classic converter)
  2. An unboxing + first impression
  3. A testimonial-style talking head
  4. An aesthetic product showcase
  5. A trend-format video (POV, "things in my X that just make sense")

Gear: your phone, window light, free CapCut app. That's it. Film vertical (1080×1920), captions always on, jump cuts every 2–3 seconds. Read the full breakdown in our portfolio guide.

Step 4: Build a simple portfolio (Day 8)

You don't need a fancy website on day one. A clean Notion page, Canva site, or even a dedicated Instagram account with your 5 videos pinned works. Label each video: "Spec ad — [Product] ([Niche], 30s)". A brand manager should grasp your range in 60 seconds.

Step 5: Pitch 10 brands a day (Days 9+)

This is where deals come from. The math: 10 pitches/day × 10–20% reply rate = 1–2 conversations daily. Within 2–4 weeks, most consistent creators land their first paid deal.

Where to find brands: TikTok Creative Center (brands running UGC-style ads), Instagram hashtags like #ugccreatorneeded, and direct DMs to DTC brands in your niche. Use our copy-paste outreach templates — always personalize one line per brand.

💡 The follow-up secret: 70% of replies come after the second message. Most creators never follow up — that's your edge. Our templates include a proven follow-up that offers a free 15-second sample concept.

Step 6: Price your first deals (and raise fast)

Start at $150/video minimum — never below $100. After 5 paid videos, move to $200; after 10, $250+. Run every quote through our UGC rate calculator so usage rights and add-ons are priced in from day one.

Mistakes that kill beginners

  • Waiting until the portfolio is "perfect." Start pitching at 5 videos. Done beats perfect.
  • Generic mass pitches. One personalized line per brand 10x's your reply rate.
  • No follow-up. One polite follow-up 3–5 days later is professional, not pushy.
  • Working for "exposure." Build your portfolio with spec work; charge real clients real money.
  • Ignoring usage rights. Define paid vs. organic usage and duration in writing, every time.

FAQ

Can I be a UGC creator with no followers?

Absolutely. UGC brands pay for content, not audiences. Your videos run as ads on the brand's own accounts, so follower count is irrelevant. What matters is a portfolio of 5–10 strong sample videos.

How long does it take to get your first UGC brand deal?

Most creators land their first paid deal within 2–6 weeks of consistent effort: 1 week building a 5-video portfolio, then 2–5 weeks of daily pitching (10+ pitches/day) at a 10–20% reply rate.

What equipment do I need to start UGC?

Just a smartphone made in the last 4 years, natural window light, and the free CapCut app for editing. Total startup cost: $0. A $15 phone tripod is the only worthwhile early purchase.

How to Play GTA 6 Early: The New Zealand Trick

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S — and for players counting down the hours, the wait was the hardest part. Around 230,000 people a month search "how to play GTA 6 early on PS5," and most are looking for the same thing: the New Zealand trick. It is a real, well-known method that lets you play hours before your own region unlocks. It also carries genuine risks to your store account and payments, and a locked game that looks broken usually is not. This guide explains how the trick works, what can go wrong, and how to tell "not unlocked yet" from a real problem.

How the New Zealand Trick Works

The idea is simple, and it comes down to time zones. GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time in each store region, and New Zealand is one of the first places on Earth to reach midnight — so a console set to the New Zealand region unlocks the game at NZ midnight, hours before your own region. You are not cracking or modifying the game; you are simply telling your console you are in the region where midnight has already arrived.

Here is how players do it:

  1. Preload the game first. Download and install GTA 6 before you touch anything — the full install is 212 GB. On PS5, make sure your system software is updated to 26.07-14.23.00.
  2. Switch your console region to New Zealand. Change the region setting on your console or account to New Zealand.
  3. Wait for NZ midnight. When midnight arrives in New Zealand, the game unlocks for that region — and your console, now set to it, lets you play.
  4. Play. You are in Vice City with Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos while players in your home region are still watching the countdown.

How much earlier you get in depends on where you live — for players in the Americas and Europe, it is hours earlier; closer to New Zealand, the gain is smaller. Either way, the mechanic is the same: midnight comes to New Zealand first, and the game unlocks when it does.

The Risks: Region Switching Can Affect Your Store and Payments

This is the part most "play early" guides skip, and it matters most. Switching your console region is not a free action — it can affect your store and your payments. Pricing, payment methods, and your wallet balance are all tied to your region, so a switch can leave you with payment methods that no longer work or store credit that does not carry over.

The practical advice is straightforward:

  • Use a secondary account if you try it. Do the region switch on a spare account rather than your main one, so any store or payment disruption hits the account you can afford to disrupt — not the one holding your library, subscriptions, and payment details.
  • Do not switch regions casually or repeatedly. Treat it as a one-off decision for launch, not a habit — every switch is another chance for a store or payment setting to go wrong.
  • Check your payment method before you switch. If you have store credit or a subscription renewing soon, check what happens to it in the new region first.
  • Remember the game is single-player at launch. There are no multiplayer servers yet, so the trick buys you a head start on the story — not access to anything other players cannot get later.

For many players, the honest answer is that the trick is not worth the hassle: a few hours of early play in exchange for store and payment complications on your main account. If you do try it, the secondary account is what makes the risk acceptable.

Before You Try It: Get the Download Done First

The trick only works if the game is already installed when NZ midnight arrives — a 212 GB download does not finish in minutes. Preload early, confirm the install is complete, and make sure your console software is current (PS5: 26.07-14.23.00). Check the countdown timer, too, so you know how many hours the trick buys you.

If the Game Shows a Padlock, It Is Probably Not Broken

The most common panic on launch night is the padlock icon on the game tile — and it is usually not a bug. A locked game normally means it has not unlocked yet in your store region: GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time there, so if midnight has not arrived where your account is set, the padlock is correct behavior.

Before you do anything drastic, work through this checklist:

  1. Check the time. Has midnight arrived in your store region? If not, the game is simply locked until then.
  2. Do not delete the install. Deleting 212 GB to "fix" a padlock means re-downloading 212 GB — the padlock is almost never a corrupt install.
  3. Sign in to the account that bought the game. A game bought on one account can show as locked on another — make sure you are signed in to the buying account.
  4. Restore the license (PS5). Highlight the game, press Options, and choose Restore License. This re-syncs your purchase and clears the most common false lock.
  5. If you switched regions for the NZ trick, check which region you are in now. A game unlocked for New Zealand can lock again if you switch back before your own region's midnight — keep the region consistent until you are playing.

Only after those steps — and after midnight in your region has passed — should you suspect a real problem. In almost every launch-night case, the padlock is the clock, not the console.

Is the New Zealand Trick Worth It?

It depends on what you value. With a secondary account and the game preloaded, the trick works. If you would have to switch your main account's region and risk a store or payment headache for a few hours, the trade is poor — the game will still be there at your own midnight, padlock-free, with your account untouched.

The New Zealand trick is real: switch your console region to New Zealand and GTA 6 unlocks at NZ midnight, hours before your region. Preload the full 212 GB first, and if the game shows a padlock, remember it is usually just the clock: it unlocks at midnight in your store region, so do not delete the install, sign in to the buying account, and restore the license on PS5.

Mascot naming tips

  • Keep it to 1–2 syllables — Zippy beats Bartholomew every time.
  • Match the personality — a fierce gaming mascot named "Cuddles" confuses people.
  • Test it out loud — if a kid can't say it, simplify it.
  • Check availability — search the name + your niche before committing.

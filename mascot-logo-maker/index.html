Frequently Asked Questions

What is a mascot logo?

A mascot logo is a brand logo built around an illustrated character — an animal, robot, human or monster — that represents the company's personality, like the Michelin Man or Duolingo's owl.

Is MascotLogoMaker free to use?

Yes. The mascot style picker, quiz and name generator are 100% free, and you can download your SVG mascot logo with no signup.

What file do I get when I download my mascot logo?

You can download your mascot logo as a scalable SVG vector file or as a PNG image, ready for your website, social profiles or packaging.

Does every brand need a mascot?

No. Mascots shine for playful, friendly and youth-oriented brands. Take our free 2-minute quiz to see whether a mascot logo fits your brand.

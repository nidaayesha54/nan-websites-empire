GTA 6 Artwork: Every Image Rockstar Released

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — and long before launch day, Rockstar had already published a full gallery of artwork for the game. The studio's GTA 6 site carries 27 wallpapers, free to download, covering the two heroes, the supporting cast, the cover art and all six regions of Leonida. If you want the real images — the ones Rockstar actually made — this guide lists every piece, explains what each one shows, and tells you where to get them without paying a cent or trusting a random re-upload.

The headline number is 27. That is how many wallpapers sit in the Artwork and Wallpapers section of Rockstar's GTA 6 site, in sizes up to 5120 by 2160 for phones, PCs and tablets. The set breaks down into seven pieces of key art starring Jason and Lucia, the cover art, the cover of the game's soundtrack album, portraits of six supporting characters, and postcards of Leonida's six regions. Twenty of the 27 come in all six sizes, so the same image works as a phone lock screen or an ultrawide desktop background.

The seven pieces of Jason and Lucia key art

The seven duo pieces are Lock and Load, Robbery, Beach, 01, 02, Motel and Rooftop. Six of them also come in versions with the game's logo. Rooftop is the newest: Rockstar added it on November 16, 2026, just three days before launch. In it, Jason — wearing a U.S. Army T-shirt that nods to his time in the service — readies a pistol beside Lucia, who holds a pump-action shotgun, on a glass-railed rooftop over the water at dusk, with a Ferris wheel and a helicopter searchlight behind them. Lock and Load is the piece painted as a hand-painted mural in Brooklyn, the one that was tagged with graffiti during launch month. Each piece is a single frozen scene rather than a collage, and each one tells you something about the pair: the robbery, the beach, the motel room, the rooftop — the whole game is a crime story told in snapshots.

The cover art and the album cover

The cover art is the multi-panel comic-book collage Rockstar revealed on June 18, 2026, the same day it announced that pre-orders would open on June 25. It is the art on the game's box and on store listings, and it follows the visual language Rockstar has used for mainline GTA covers for more than twenty years: a grid of panels, each holding a character, a vehicle or a moment, arranged around the game's logo. Panels show a seaplane over the skyline, a motorcyclist doing a wheelie away from police, a woman on a beach, a bearded man with gold jewelry, a yellow sports car with scissor doors, an alligator emerging from water, an armed man in front of a bank, and a speedboat with passengers near a flying flamingo. Beside it sits the cover of Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album, the soundtrack's own key illustration. Rockstar also refreshed its website with a wide promotional image of the Vice City skyline at golden hour — container ships, lit skyscrapers and a waterfront Ferris wheel — which is website art, not box art, and works as a banner because it is wide and atmospheric rather than crowded with panels.

Six characters, six postcards

The character portraits cover Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder — six each in the download set. Jason and Lucia do not get portraits of their own in this set; they appear together in all seven pieces of key art instead. The six region postcards give each part of Leonida a vintage-postcard treatment: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, the Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga National Park. These are the images to use if you want a sense of place rather than a sense of character — a swamp, a forgotten coast, a sugar town, a mountain park, the Keys and the city itself, each in the same retro style.

Where to download them — and what to avoid

The only source Rockstar endorses is the Artwork and Wallpapers section of its own GTA 6 site, rockstargames.com/VI. Everything there is free, in six sizes, and the media page also holds 99 screenshots and nine video clips if you want more than still images. On PS5, you can also pick a built-in GTA 6 background in the Welcome hub under Change Background, then Games. What you should not do is pay anyone for these images or download them from sites that wrap them in installers: the same free files are the ones those sites are selling you, and fan-made covers dressed up as box art are fan-made no matter how polished they look. The real cover is the one Rockstar revealed in June 2026 — one design, and everything else that looks like a cover is a fan poster.

Using the art: wallpapers, screenshots and clips

The 27 wallpapers are only part of what Rockstar published. The same media page holds 99 screenshots and nine video clips, including the launch trailer, the Extended Look and the pre-order film — and the Ultimate Edition adds a digital bonus art book and a soundtrack preview of its own. If you want the images on your own devices, the sizes cover everything: twenty of the 27 come in all six sizes, from phone portraits up to 5120 by 2160 ultrawide desktops. On PS5, choose a built-in GTA 6 background in the Welcome hub under Change Background, then Games. On Xbox, the cover art works as a Home screen background. On a phone or a computer, download the size that matches your screen and you get the genuine file, not a recompressed copy from a re-upload site.

One last detail for collectors: the count has grown over time. Rockstar added the Rooftop pieces in mid-November, taking the total from 25 to 27, and the studio has a habit of refreshing the page around big moments. If the number climbs again after a trailer or an update, the new pieces will appear in the same place. Bookmark the media page, not a re-upload, and you will always have the genuine set.

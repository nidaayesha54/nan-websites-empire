27 Simple Logo Ideas for Minimalist Brands

Stuck staring at a blank canvas? These 27 minimalist logo concepts span nine industries — and every single one can be built from 2–3 basic shapes in our free shape combiner.

Tech & SaaS

  1. The orbit node: one circle with a smaller offset circle overlapping it — connection, networks, community.
  2. The upward cut: a square with its top-right corner sliced by a triangle — growth, disruption.
  3. The signal bar: three horizontal lines of increasing length — progress, analytics, momentum.

Food & Beverage

  1. The single leaf: two overlapping circles forming a leaf silhouette — fresh, organic, natural.
  2. The steam mark: two curved lines rising from a horizontal bar — coffee, tea, warmth.
  3. The half sun: a semicircle over a line — bakeries, breakfast brands, morning energy.

Fitness & Wellness

  1. The pulse line: one horizontal line with a single triangular spike — vitality, heartbeat, training.
  2. The balanced stones: two stacked circles, small over large — yoga, mindfulness, balance.
  3. The arrow up: a bold triangle pointing up inside a circle — goals, achievement, coaching.

Pets & Animals

  1. The ear duo: two triangles side by side — instantly reads as cat or dog ears.
  2. The paw dot: one large circle with three small circles above it — a minimal paw print.
  3. The tail curl: a single circle with a curved line sweeping off it — playful, friendly movement.

Finance & Consulting

  1. The stable square: a square rotated 45° (a diamond) with a horizontal line through it — trust, precision.
  2. The growth wedge: a triangle rising from a flat baseline — investing, upward trajectory.
  3. The pillar: two vertical lines of different heights — strength, partnership, support.

Creative & Agencies

  1. The blank canvas: an empty square outline with one filled corner — potential, the work ahead.
  2. The spark: a four-point star made of two overlapping lines — ideas, creativity, "aha".
  3. The frame shift: two offset squares overlapping — perspective, design thinking, layers.

Fashion & Beauty

  1. The monogram circle: your initial's first letter implied by negative space inside a circle — timeless luxury.
  2. The horizon line: a thin line through a circle's lower third — calm, editorial, premium.
  3. The drop: a single teardrop (circle + triangle merged) — skincare, purity, essentials.

Home & Real Estate

  1. The roofline: a triangle (roof) over a square (house) sharing one line — shelter, property.
  2. The keyhole: a circle stacked on a triangle — access, security, "home".
  3. The foundation: three stacked horizontal lines, longest at bottom — stability, building.

Education & Kids

  1. The open book: two triangles mirrored to suggest pages — learning, schools, courses.
  2. The rising dot: three dots ascending diagonally — progress, steps, achievement.
  3. The smile arc: a semicircle (smile) under two dot eyes — friendly, approachable, fun.

How to pick the right idea

  • Match the feeling, not the literal object. A fintech doesn't need a coin; "trust" can be a square, "growth" a triangle.
  • Steal the structure, not the logo. "Two overlapping circles" is a technique; copying a competitor's exact mark is a lawsuit. Make the proportions, colors and arrangement yours.
  • Say it in five words. If you can't describe the concept briefly ("two triangles forming a roof"), it's not simple enough yet.
  • Test before you commit. Not sure minimalism fits your brand at all? Take the 7-question quiz first.

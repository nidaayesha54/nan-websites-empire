Minimalist logo FAQs

What is a minimalist logo?

A minimalist logo uses the fewest possible elements — typically 1–3 simple shapes, 1–2 flat colors and generous whitespace — to create a mark recognizable at any size, from favicon to billboard.

Can I make a minimalist logo for free?

Yes. Our shape combiner lets you combine circles, triangles, squares and lines into a minimalist SVG logo and download the vector file with no sign-up.

How many colors should a minimalist logo have?

One or two. The classic formula is black on white (or the reverse) plus a single accent color. Read our minimalist color guide for the full breakdown.

Are minimalist logos still trendy in 2026?

Yes — flat, geometric, negative-space marks dominate because they scale everywhere, reproduce in one color, and age far better than detailed illustrations. See 2026 trends.

How do I know if my logo is simple enough?

Run it through our whitespace checker: it scores simplicity 0–100 across colors, elements, gradients, detail and scalability, with exact tips to simplify.

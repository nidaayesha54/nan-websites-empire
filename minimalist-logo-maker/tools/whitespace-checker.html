What the score measures

The checker weighs the five things that separate minimal logos from cluttered ones: color count (1–2 is ideal), element count (one idea beats three), flatness (no gradients, shadows or fine detail), scalability (readable at favicon size, works in one color), and a bonus for negative space — the hallmark of clever minimal design.

How to improve a low score

  • Cut colors first — it's the fastest win. See the minimalist color guide.
  • Merge elements: two overlapping shapes often say more than four separate ones. Try the shape combiner.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best free AI logo generator in 2026?

Ideogram is the best truly free option with no watermark and the best text rendering. Canva's logo maker gives free SVG downloads, and Looka/LogoAI offer the best paid one-time downloads.

Can I use an AI-generated logo for my business?

Yes for most generators, but check each tool's license. Free tiers may restrict commercial use or add watermarks — Ideogram and Canva allow commercial use on free plans.

Do AI logo generators create vector files?

Logo-focused tools like Looka, LogoAI, Brandmark and Canva export vector SVG/EPS files. General image generators like Ideogram and Leonardo produce raster images you must vectorize.

How do I write a good AI logo prompt?

Specify industry, style (minimalist, mascot, retro), exact color palette, key elements, mood, and what to avoid. Use our free prompt builder to assemble a complete prompt in seconds.

GTA 6 Beginner Tips: 7 Things to Do First

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — thirteen years after GTA 5 — and "GTA 6" is now drawing around 53.8 million searches a month. Searches for "gta 6 beginner tips" alone run to about 9,100 a month, and for good reason: this is a game that punishes the mistakes new players make in their first hours, and several of them cannot be undone. Missions cannot be replayed. Pocket cash vanishes when you die. And the next mission may not open until you have earned enough.

This guide gives you the seven things to do first — all verified from launch reporting — in the order that matters. Do them in your first session and you will start Vice City with your money safe and your progress protected.

1. Save Manually After Every Mission

Your first habit should be saving — manually, every time. GTA 6 gives you 15 manual save slots, and the routine is simple: open Pause, go to Game, then Save Game. Rotate across two or three slots so you always have a recent fallback.

The reason is that autosave will not protect you. Autosave only fires after a mission ends, and the Prologue never autosaves at all. Autosaves appear only in the Load Game menu, so there is no mid-mission safety net. And since missions cannot be replayed, a manual save before a mission you care about is the only way back if it goes wrong.

2. Protect Your Cash From the Start

Cash in your pocket is not safe in GTA 6. It is lost when you die or get arrested — and it does not carry into Chapter 2 at all. Whatever you are holding when the chapter turns over is gone.

The game gives you two ways to protect it. First, stash cash under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom — a hiding spot provided for exactly this reason. Second, use the BuckMe bank account, which opens up through the mission "Buck Me". Banked money survives death, arrest, and the chapter transition. The rule to live by: bank what you earn, keep only what you need for the next job in your pocket, and never walk into a risky mission carrying your life savings.

3. Earn Money Early — Missions Are Money-Gated

Some missions in GTA 6 only open once Jason and Lucia have earned enough money. Rockstar North's Rob Nelson confirmed it via Famitsu: progression is tied to your earnings, not just to the missions you have finished. Spend everything early and you can hit a wall where the next mission does not unlock until you earn your way back up.

So start earning immediately, and keep a reserve. Jewelry store robberies — including the Victoria Lee Jeweler in Capri and Blanc and Giroux in the Decor District — pay around $20,000 each. Stealing cars parked next to a Pay 'N' Spray and selling them is a steady early earner. Kenton's treasure dives pay around $1,400 per wreck once they unlock after the mission "Island Dreams". The big bank vaults, worth up to $205,000, only come at the end of Chapter 4. Treat your cash as a key to the story, not just a score.

4. Ignore Every "PC Download" Link

There is no PC version of GTA 6 at launch, and Rockstar has not announced a PC date. The game launched on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and it is single-player at launch, with no multiplayer servers yet. That means every "PC download" link, installer, or launcher you see is a scam — usually malware or a phishing page dressed up as the game.

Do not click them, do not download them, and do not enter any account details into them. If you want GTA 6 on PC, the only honest answer is to wait for Rockstar to announce it.

5. Clear 212 GB and Update Your PS5 First

Before your first session, do the boring preparation. GTA 6 needs 212 GB of free space, so clear out old games and captures before you install. On PS5, the game requires system software 26.07-14.23.00, so check for a system update and install the latest game update too.

While you are at it, check your connection. The game is single-player at launch, but sign-in and licence checks still need a stable link to PSN or Xbox Live. A wired LAN cable beats Wi-Fi for stability, and if you see a "can't connect" error, remember there are no multiplayer servers at fault — the problem is your network or your NAT type, and a full game restart is always the first fix to try.

6. Expect 30 FPS — There Is No 60 FPS Mode

Set your expectations before you play, and the game will feel much better. Rockstar confirmed to Kotaku on November 18, 2026, that GTA 6 runs at 30 fps on every console, with no 60 fps performance mode. The base PS5 dips below 30 fps in heavy combat and explosions, and the Xbox Series S is roughest — mid-20s in busy scenes, down to around 18–20 fps in shootouts.

Knowing that in advance changes how you experience it. The dips in the loudest moments are the game as it shipped, not your console failing. If 30 fps makes you feel queasy, turn the camera slower, try the Motion Blur Strength slider low and then high, use the far camera view, sit further from the TV, keep a light on, and play in shorter sessions.

7. Don't Look for the Character Switch Yet

GTA 6 has two protagonists — Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos — but you cannot switch between them from the start. Character switching unlocks only after the "Breakfast Run" mission in Chapter 2. Many new players spend their first hours hunting through menus for a switch option that does not exist yet. Once it unlocks, the switch is simple: hold Right on the D-pad to open the Partner Menu, then press R1 on PlayStation or RB on Xbox to swap between Jason and Lucia.

Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do first in GTA 6? Save manually after your first mission, bank your cash in your BuckMe account, and start earning — some missions only unlock once you have earned enough money.

Can I play GTA 6 on PC? No — there is no PC version at launch and Rockstar has not announced a PC date. "PC download" links are scams.

The Bottom Line

Your first hours in GTA 6 set the tone for the whole game. Save manually after every mission. Bank your cash and stash the rest under Jason's floorboards. Earn early, because the next mission may be waiting on your bank balance. Ignore every PC download link. Clear 212 GB, update your PS5, expect 30 fps, and do not go looking for the character switch before "Breakfast Run" in Chapter 2.

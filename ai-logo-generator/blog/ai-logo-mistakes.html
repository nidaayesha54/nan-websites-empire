7 AI Logo Mistakes Beginners Always Make

AI logo generators are powerful — but beginners keep falling into the same traps. Here are the seven we see most, and exactly how to fix each one.

1. Garbled or misspelled text

The #1 AI logo failure: your brand name rendered as gibberish. Fix: use Ideogram (best free text rendering), add "perfectly spelled text" to your prompt, and always zoom in to check every letter before downloading.

2. Falling for the watermark trap

Many "free" generators (Looka, LogoAI, Wix) let you design for free but watermark the download until you pay. Fix: check our comparison table — filter "Truly free (no watermark)" to see Ideogram, Canva and Hatchful.

3. Raster-only files

A PNG logo looks fine on Instagram but turns to mush on a sign or t-shirt. Fix: vectorize every AI-generated logo (Vectorizer.ai, Inkscape trace) or start in a tool with SVG export like Canva. Full workflow in our creation guide.

4. Vague prompts, vague logos

"Make me a cool logo" gives you clip-art soup. Fix: specify industry, exact colors, style and elements. Our prompt builder assembles a complete prompt in seconds — or copy from 50 proven examples.

5. Settling for the first result

AI's first draft is rarely its best. Fix: generate 10–20 variations per concept and change one variable at a time when refining.

6. Ignoring trademark risk

AI models remix their training data — your "original" logo can echo an existing brand. Fix: reverse-image-search your final mark and check trademark databases before investing in signage or packaging. Read AI logo vs designer for when professional clearance matters.

7. No favicon / social versions

A wide horizontal logo becomes unreadable as a profile picture. Fix: export a square icon version, a monochrome version, and a favicon from day one — most generators' brand kits (Looka, Brandmark) do this automatically.

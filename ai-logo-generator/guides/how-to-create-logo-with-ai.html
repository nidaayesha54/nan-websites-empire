How to Create a Logo with AI — Step by Step

You don't need design skills or expensive software to get a professional logo in 2026. Follow this five-step workflow using free AI tools — from blank page to print-ready vector file.

Step 1: Define your brand style (5 minutes)

Before touching any generator, decide three things: your industry, the vibe (bold? elegant? playful?), and a color direction. Unsure? Our AI logo style quiz answers this for you in 60 seconds and recommends a generator to match.

Step 2: Choose the right generator

Not all AI logo generators are equal. If your logo includes text, use Ideogram (best free text rendering). If you need vector files for print, use Canva's free SVG export or a paid tool like Looka. Compare all options in our comparison table or read the full 2026 ranking.

Step 3: Write a detailed prompt

The prompt is 80% of the result. A good AI logo prompt includes:

  • Industry + brand name — "logo for 'Brew & Bean', a coffee shop"
  • Style — minimalist, mascot, retro badge, luxury monogram…
  • Exact colors — "espresso brown and cream", not just "brown"
  • Key elements — the symbol or object to feature
  • What to avoid — "no neon, no photorealism"

Use our free logo prompt builder to assemble all of this into one copy-paste prompt, or browse 50 proven prompt examples.

Step 4: Generate and iterate

Generate 10–20 variations — never settle for the first result. Then refine: keep what works, change one variable at a time (colors, then style, then elements). Check text spelling carefully; regenerate any version with garbled letters.

Step 5: Vectorize and export

Raster PNGs from image generators won't scale for signage or print. Convert your winner to vector:

  1. Upscale the image 2–4× for clean edges
  2. Trace it with a free vectorizer (Vectorizer.ai, Inkscape's trace, or Illustrator's Image Trace)
  3. Clean up the paths and export SVG, EPS, PNG (transparent) and a favicon version

Logo-focused tools like Canva, Looka and Brandmark skip this step — they export vectors directly.

Common mistakes to avoid

Beginners consistently hit the same traps: garbled text, watermarked "free" downloads, and raster-only files that can't be printed. We documented all of them — with fixes — in 7 AI logo mistakes beginners always make.

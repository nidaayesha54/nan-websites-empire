Best AI Logo Generators in 2026 — Ranked & Tested

We tested the free tiers of the most popular AI logo generators so you don't have to. Below: the top 10 ranked by output quality, real free downloads, vector exports and text rendering — the three things that actually matter for a usable logo.

Quick ranking

  1. Ideogram — best truly free option, no watermark, best text rendering
  2. Looka — best paid all-rounder with full brand kit
  3. Canva Logo Maker — best free vector (SVG) downloads
  4. LogoAI — best style-learning engine
  5. Brandmark — best budget one-time purchase
  6. Leonardo AI — best free daily credits for concepting
  7. Hatchful by Shopify — simplest 100% free wizard
  8. Wix Logo Maker — best guided questionnaire
  9. Designs.ai — best full creative suite
  10. Fiverr Logo Maker — best AI + human hybrid

1. Ideogram — best free AI logo generator

Ideogram's free tier generates unlimited-ish logos with no watermark and — crucially — the most accurate text rendering of any free model. If your logo includes your brand name, start here. The catch: outputs are raster (PNG/JPG), so you'll need to vectorize for print. See our logo mistakes guide for the vectorizing workflow.

2. Looka — best paid all-rounder

Looka generates 300+ concepts from a short questionnaire and bundles the winner into a full brand kit (colors, fonts, social templates). Free to design; high-res files start at $20 one-time. Best if you want a professional result without a designer. Read AI logo vs designer before deciding.

3. Canva Logo Maker — best free vector downloads

Canva lets you download true SVG vector files on the free plan — rare among free tools. Templates can feel generic, but with custom fonts and colors you can get a clean, original mark. Great for startups that need print-ready files today.

4. LogoAI — best style-learning engine

LogoAI studies the styles you like and iterates, with excellent monogram and lettermark options. Free to preview; downloads from $29. Strong typography control.

5. Brandmark — best budget one-time purchase

From $25 one-time, Brandmark's color-and-font pairing engine punches above its price. Fewer customization controls than Looka, but unbeatable value.

6. Leonardo AI — best for free concepting

Daily free credits and huge style variety make Leonardo ideal for exploring 20+ logo concepts fast. It's a general image tool, not logo-specific — no brand kit, no vector export.

7–10. The rest

Hatchful is the simplest truly-free wizard (basic but watermark-free). Wix Logo Maker has the deepest personalization questionnaire but paywalls downloads. Designs.ai bundles video and mockup tools if you need a whole creative suite. Fiverr Logo Maker blends AI speed with human-made assets from $30.

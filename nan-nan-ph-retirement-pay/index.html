The RA 7641 Retirement Formula

Retirement pay = Daily Rate × 22.5 days × Years of Service
Republic Act 7641 (1992) guarantees at least one-half (1/2) month salary for every year of service. The law defines "1/2 month salary" as: 15 days of salary + 1/12 of your 13th month pay + the cash equivalent of 5 days of service incentive leave — which practitioners express as 22.5 days of daily pay per year of service.

Worked example

Monthly basic salary ₱30,000, 10 years of service: daily rate = 30,000 ÷ 26 = ₱1,153.85. Retirement pay = 1,153.85 × 22.5 × 10 = ₱259,616 (minimum). With 12 years and 7 months, the 6-month rule gives 13 creditable years → ₱1,153.85 × 22.5 × 13 = ₱337,500.

Who Qualifies

  • Age: at least 60 years old (optional retirement) up to 65 (compulsory retirement).
  • Service: at least 5 years with the establishment, including probationary employment.
  • Rounding rule: a fraction of at least 6 months counts as one whole year.
  • If a company retirement plan or CBA grants more than the RA 7641 minimum, the better benefit must be paid.
  • Retirement pay is separate from (in addition to) pro-rated 13th month pay and unused leave conversion in your final pay.

Retirement Pay vs Separation Pay

Retirement pay is a reward for long service when you retire at 60+ (RA 7641). Separation pay is paid when employment ends for authorized causes such as redundancy, retrenchment, or closure — it is not paid for resignation or just-cause dismissal unless company policy says otherwise.

FAQs

Does the 22.5-day multiplier include my allowances?

No. The statutory minimum is based on basic salary: 15 days + 1/12 of 13th month + up to 5 days of service incentive leave. Cost-of-living allowances and non-integrated benefits are generally excluded unless your company treats them as part of salary.

What divisor should I use for my daily rate?

For monthly-paid employees the common convention is salary ÷ 26 (six-day workweek basis) for this computation. Your company or CBA may use its own divisor — this calculator uses 26 as the standard.

I worked 10 years and 5 months. How many years count?

Only 10 — the fraction must reach 6 months to round up to a whole year. At 10 years and 6 months, you get credit for 11 years.

Is retirement pay taxable?

Retirement pay received under RA 7641 or an employer plan approved by the BIR is generally exempt from income tax, subject to BIR conditions. Confirm with HR or your accountant.

What if I retired before 2026 — does this still apply?

RA 7641 has been in force since 1993, so the same 22.5-day minimum applies. Only current figures like your latest salary change over time.

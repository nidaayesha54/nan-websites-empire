Frequently Asked Questions

What is AI voice acting?

AI voice acting uses text-to-speech and voice-cloning technology to generate character dialogue for games, animation, and video — replacing or supplementing human voice actors during prototyping and even final production.

Can I use AI voices in my indie game?

Yes. Many indie developers use AI voices for NPCs, narration, and placeholder dialogue. Use licensed or generated voices and disclose AI usage where required.

Which tool should I use to pick a voice for my character?

Use our free Character Voice Picker quiz — it matches traits like hero/villain/comic, age, and energy to a voice archetype with sample lines.

Are AI voices good enough for final game audio?

Quality in 2026 is high enough for many use cases. For lead characters, some studios still combine AI drafts with human-directed sessions; for NPCs and background chatter, AI voices are standard.

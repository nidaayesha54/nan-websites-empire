Indonesia THR Calculator 2026 — Tunjangan Hari Raya Holiday Allowance

Estimate your THR (Tunjangan Hari Raya) — the mandatory Indonesian religious holiday allowance. Employees with 12+ months of service get one month's wage; shorter service is paid proportionally.

Calculate Your THR

"One month's wage" = basic salary plus fixed allowances (e.g. child, housing, regional allowances). Variable bonuses are excluded.
At least 1 month of continuous work is required to qualify. 12 months or more = full THR.
For casual daily workers, the "one month's wage" is the average received over the last 12 months (or the actual shorter working period).

How the THR Formula Works

The rules sit in the Minister of Manpower Regulation No. 6 of 2016 on Religious Holiday Allowances (THR), enforced under Government Regulation No. 36 of 2021:

12 months or more of continuous service: THR = 1 month's wage.
1 to under 12 months: THR = (months of service ÷ 12) × 1 month's wage.
Less than 1 month: no entitlement.

Worked Example

Monthly wage IDR 7,000,000 with 9 months of service: (9 ÷ 12) × 7,000,000 = IDR 5,250,000. The same worker with 12+ months receives the full IDR 7,000,000.

What Counts as "One Month's Wage"?

It is the basic salary plus fixed allowances — payments the company makes regularly with a fixed nominal amount (child, housing, regional allowances). Non-fixed or irregular payments (transport based on attendance, performance bonuses, commissions) are generally excluded. If your contract uses an all-in (clean) wage with no separate allowances, that full figure is the basis.

Casual Daily Workers

For workers on daily agreements, the one month's wage is calculated as the average monthly earnings over the last 12 months — or the monthly average over the period actually worked if shorter. The same 12-month threshold applies.

When Must THR Be Paid?

THR must be paid no later than 7 days before the employee's religious holiday — most commonly Eid al-Fitr (Lebaran), but the rule also covers Christmas, Nyepi, Vesak and other recognized holidays. Payment must be in full (no installments) and in Indonesian Rupiah.

Penalties for Not Paying

Employers who fail to pay or delay THR face a 5% fine on the unpaid amount, plus administrative sanctions up to and including written warnings and temporary or full business suspension. Workers can report non-payment to the local Manpower office (Disnaker).

FAQs

Does THR count if my company pays a higher bonus?

If your employment agreement, company regulation or collective labor agreement sets a higher holiday payment, the company must pay the higher amount — the regulation is a floor, not a ceiling.

I started 3 weeks before Eid. Do I get anything?

No. The minimum qualifying period is 1 month of continuous service before the holiday.

Is THR subject to income tax?

Yes. THR is taxable income in Indonesia and is included in the PPh 21 withholding calculation — it is taxed as irregular income alongside your salary.

Do contract (PKWT) and part-time workers get THR?

Contract workers who meet the 1-month minimum get THR under the same formula. Part-time employees are entitled to THR calculated proportionally to their working arrangement.

Can THR be paid in goods or parcels?

No. The regulations require THR to be paid in money (Indonesian Rupiah). Parcels or goods do not count as THR payment.

Does THR affect my severance calculation?

THR is a separate entitlement paid before religious holidays. However, the same "one month's wage" concept (basic + fixed allowances) is used in both THR and severance (UP/UPMK) calculations.

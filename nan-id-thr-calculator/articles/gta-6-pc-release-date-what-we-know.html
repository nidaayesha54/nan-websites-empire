GTA 6 on PC: No Date Yet — Here's What We Actually Know

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — and if you are reading this on a PC, you are still waiting. There is no PC version of GTA 6, no announced PC release date, no system requirements, and no Steam or Epic store page from Rockstar. About 791,000 people a month search for GTA 6 on PC, and the search results are full of dates that do not exist. This guide separates what is confirmed from what is reported and what is simply invented, so you know exactly where the PC version stands — and what to watch for next.

That matters because the PC question is where the scams live. Any site offering a "GTA 6 PC download", an APK, a beta, or an early-access key is lying: there is no PC build to download, and fake downloads have already been traced to password-stealing malware. The only safe way to wait for the PC version is to know what has actually been said — and by whom.

What Rockstar and Take-Two Have Actually Said

Nothing — and that is the whole confirmed record. Rockstar's game page, Take-Two's November 9, 2026 earnings release, and the PlayStation and Xbox store listings all name PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S only. None of them lists a PC platform, a PC date, or PC system requirements. On Take-Two's earnings call in May 2026, CEO Strauss Zelnick repeated the company's long-standing line that its games tend to come to every platform over time — but he did not give a date or confirm a GTA 6 PC version. In an interview published in May 2026, Zelnick said GTA 6 would not launch on PC on day one. That is everything the companies have said on the record. There is no PC announcement to quote, because there has not been one.

The silence is deliberate and it is Rockstar's pattern. The studio announces a game for consoles first, sells it there, and talks about PC later — sometimes much later. The absence of a PC date is not a denial. It is Rockstar doing what Rockstar always does: saying nothing until it is ready to say something.

The Q1 2027 Report — and the February Date That Isn't Real

There is one report worth taking seriously, and one date worth ignoring. On October 13, 2026, GamesBeat reported, citing four sources familiar with Rockstar's plans, that the studio is targeting a PC release in the first quarter of 2027. Rockstar declined to comment, and neither the November 9 earnings release nor the November 19 launch announcement mentions PC — so as of launch day, the Q1 2027 window is still unconfirmed. It is a report from a named outlet with multiple sources, which makes it a different kind of claim from a rumour, but it is still not an announcement.

The February 2027 date is different: it came from an unverified report by DetectiveSeeds in spring 2026, citing unnamed developer contacts, and nobody has corroborated it since. Treat it as unconfirmed. The honest summary is short: no PC date exists, one credible report points at early 2027, and everything else is guessing.

How Long Rockstar Usually Takes

Rockstar's history is the best guide to what happens next, and it cuts both ways. GTA 5 reached PC about 19 months after its first console release in September 2013. Red Dead Redemption 2 took just over a year, from October 2018 to November 2019. If GTA 6 followed the same pattern, PC players would wait well into 2027 or 2028. A first-quarter 2027 PC launch, if the GamesBeat report is right, would be a much shorter wait than usual — a few months, not a year and a half. That is the one reason to be optimistic: Rockstar has never moved this fast before, and the report says it is planning to.

Until then, the practical advice is simple. Do not buy a "GTA 6 PC key" — there is no such product. Do not download anything claiming to be the PC version. Watch Rockstar's Newswire and Take-Two's investor updates: those are the only two places a real PC announcement will appear first. And note that Xbox Cloud Gaming streams GTA 6 to Xbox consoles only, not to PC — there is no cloud shortcut to playing it on a computer either.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is GTA 6 on PC?

No. As of November 19, 2026, Rockstar has announced GTA 6 only for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. There is no PC version, no PC date, and no PC store page.

When is the GTA 6 PC release date?

There is no announced date. GamesBeat reported in October 2026, citing four sources, that Rockstar is targeting the first quarter of 2027. Rockstar declined to comment and has not confirmed it.

Will my PC run GTA 6?

Nobody can say yet, because Rockstar has published no PC system requirements. Any "minimum specs" list you see today is invented — the requirements will only exist once a PC version is announced.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 on PC is a waiting game with no date: no version announced, one credible report pointing at the first quarter of 2027, and a history that says Rockstar usually takes a year or more. Do not pay for a PC key that does not exist, do not download anything claiming to be the game, and watch Rockstar's Newswire — that is where the real date will appear first, whenever it comes.

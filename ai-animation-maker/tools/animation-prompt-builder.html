💡 Prompting tips that actually work

  • One action per prompt. "The dragon takes off" beats "the dragon wakes up, stretches, and flies to the mountains".
  • Name the style explicitly. Generators default to generic 3D — say "anime" or "watercolor storybook" if that's what you want.
  • Lock the camera. A static or slow camera move keeps characters consistent across scenes.
  • Repeat character details in every scene's prompt so your hero looks the same in clip 1 and clip 20.

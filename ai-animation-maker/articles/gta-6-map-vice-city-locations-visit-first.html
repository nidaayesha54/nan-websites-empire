GTA 6 Map: Vice City Locations to Visit First

November 21, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, thirteen years after GTA 5 — and the first question most players ask once they are in is about the map. Searches for "GTA 6 map" run to about 879,000 a month: Leonida is enormous, and the game does not hand you a tour. Vice City is the heart of it, but the state around it is six different worlds stitched together, and the best of the game hides in the places you would never visit if you only followed the story markers.

Crossing Leonida takes around 11 minutes by car — and about 3 hours and 21 minutes on foot. Plan around it, with fast travel and routes, and a sense of which places are worth the trip.

Vice City: start at the beach

Start in Vice City itself — at the beach. Ocean Beach is the long strip of sand and neon where the city meets the sea — palm trees, art-deco hotels, beachfront boulevards in pink and teal neon. The beach is also practical: the Seashark jet ski is usually found at Ocean Beach and the marina docks, so the water is not scenery, it is transport.

From the beach, walk inland. Downtown is the dense core — traffic, roadworks, and the deep car culture the game is built around, with custom neons and engine swaps. The Decor District and Capri are where the money is: jewelry stores, formal wear, and the Victoria Lee Jeweler and Blanc and Giroux stores that pay around $20,000 a robbery. Vice City rewards walking: the night lighting is best seen on foot, at night, with nowhere to be.

The Leonida Keys: islands, wrecks and treasure

South of the city, the Leonida Keys are the island chain sitting on the doorstep of some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in America. Start from the marinas on Key Lento and work south. This is where the treasure diving lives: Kenton, the dive-charter captain, runs wreck dives that pay around $1,400 each, and there are 33 underwater discoveries in total, tracked under Progress and Collections. The dives unlock after the mission "Island Dreams", and they are almost no risk. Bring the Arras Bullhead if you plan to dive deep.

Grassrivers: the swamp

West of the city, the Grassrivers are the swamp — the most different place in the game. Airboats are the way to move here, sliding across water too shallow for a boat and too deep for a car, and the swamp hides alligators, flamingos in the lagoons, and deadlier predators than the sea. Hunting lives here too, alongside Mount Kalaga: 170 animals by fan counts, and a paying job if you know what you are doing. It can be pouring in the swamp while Vice City stays dry. Come here when you want the game to feel wild instead of urban.

Port Gellhorn: the faded resort town

Port Gellhorn is the faded resort town on the coast — what a resort looks like after the money leaves. Bargain World, the thrift store here, is where the cheapest outfit in the game lives: a full outfit from the bargain rack costs as little as $9, and the mission "How Do I Look?" in Chapter 2 sends Lucia here to buy a top and pants or shorts. Moses, based here, pays several thousand dollars per car delivered undamaged.

Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga: the inland state

Inland, Ambrosia is sugar country — flat, green, and quiet after the noise of the city. It is the part of the map most players skip, and that is exactly why it is worth a visit: the countryside, the small towns, and the feeling that Leonida is a state, not a backdrop. Mount Kalaga National Park is the other inland stop, and it is the hunting ground: the park and the Grassrivers are where the 170 animals live. The park is also where the weather changes most — mountain weather is not city weather.

Getting around: fast travel that actually works

With a map this size, driving everywhere is a choice, not a requirement. The RydeMe app is the fastest way across the state: call a ride, and the car comes to you. Paradise Taxis work the same way on the street, and buses and trains cover the fixed routes between the city and the outlying towns. One warning: if your partner is busy in a RydeMe ride, their portrait is greyed out and you cannot switch characters.

The map also hides 300 stunts — jumps, landings and speed runs — plus fishing, pool, radio stations, and the in-game phone and social media layer.

Frequently asked questions

How big is the GTA 6 map? Six regions. About 11 minutes to cross by car, about 3 hours 21 minutes on foot.

Where should I go first? Ocean Beach and Vice Beach, then the Leonida Keys for treasure diving, then Port Gellhorn for the cheapest outfit in the game.

How do I travel fast? The RydeMe app, Paradise Taxis, buses and trains. Driving is a choice, not a requirement.

What is in the water? Treasure wrecks, 33 underwater discoveries, sharks — and the Seashark jet ski at Ocean Beach and the marina docks.

Bottom line: start at the beach, dive the Keys, ride an airboat through the Grassrivers — and keep the free GTA 6 Guide app in your pocket.

Our picks by animation style

🧸 3D cartoon (kids' channels, family series)

Kling 2.x is the value king — long coherent clips with daily free credits. Runway Gen-4 wins if character consistency across a 20-episode series matters to you, and it usually does. Build your prompts with our AI animation prompt builder using the Pixar-style preset.

⚔️ Anime

Pika and Kling both handle dynamic anime action well. Pro move: generate a key frame image first, then animate it with image-to-video — your characters stay on-model instead of morphing every clip.

🖌️ 2D cartoon

Pure AI 2D is still the weakest category. Kaiber stylizes existing footage beautifully, but for original shorts the winning combo is traditional tools (Toon Boom, Adobe Animate) with AI doing inbetweening. Plan every gag first in our storyboard planner — 2D comedy is all timing.

📐 Motion graphics (explainers, ads, courses)

InVideo AI turns a script into a finished motion-graphics video fastest. CapCut (free) handles short-form motion graphics brilliantly. For custom animated elements, use Runway.

Free vs paid: what do you actually get?

Free tiers in 2026 are genuinely usable: Kling's daily credits cover roughly a minute of short-form content per day, and Hailuo's free tier is generous. The catch is always the same — watermarks, resolution caps, and queue times. If you're posting daily, a $10–$30/month plan (prices vary, check current pricing) pays for itself in saved time.

Tips for a useful preview

  • Listen for tongue-twisters — if the robot stumbles, a human will too. Rewrite the line.
  • Test at 1× first, then at your target pace. A script that works at 150 WPM may feel rushed at 170.
  • Browser voices are a draft tool, not a final product. For publish-ready AI voices, see our best AI voiceover tools guide.
  • Voices vary by device — Chrome on desktop usually offers the most English options.

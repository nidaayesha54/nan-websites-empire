Directing yourself? Read this first

  • Record direction cues in brackets right in your script — [pause], [smile], [lean in] — so performance choices survive to recording day.
  • Do a "table read" of the whole script once before recording. You'll catch tongue-twisters while it's still cheap to fix them.
  • Record 3 versions of your opening line. The first 10 seconds decide retention; give yourself options.

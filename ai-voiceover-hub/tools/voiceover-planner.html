The math behind the plan

  • Word target = minutes × words-per-minute at your chosen pace.
  • Recording time adds your pause allowance, then multiplies by a retake factor (extra takes are partial passes, not full re-reads).
  • Editing runs ~6× the video length — cutting, de-breathing, and pacing take longer than the read itself.

How it works

  • Spoken duration = words ÷ your pace. Pro creators write to the clock, not the page.
  • Filler words ("um", "like", "basically", "in order to"…) are flagged with counts so you can cut them before recording.
  • Sentences over 25 words get flagged — they're where voice talent run out of breath.
  • Reading ease tells you whether the script sounds natural aloud or reads like a textbook.

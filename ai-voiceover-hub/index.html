❓ Frequently asked questions

How many words is a 1-minute voiceover?

About 140–160 words at a conversational pace (150 words per minute). Slow narration runs ~130 words, energetic ad reads up to 170. Use our Script Optimizer to check your exact duration.

What is the best free AI voiceover tool?

For most creators, ElevenLabs' free tier gives the most natural AI voices, while your browser's built-in Web Speech API (try our free TTS preview) costs nothing for quick tests. Compare options in our 2026 guide.

How do I write a voiceover script that sounds natural?

Write like you talk: 12–18 word sentences, contractions, one idea per sentence, and read it aloud while editing. Our script optimizer checks filler words, pacing, and spoken duration.

Should I disclose AI-generated voiceover?

Yes. YouTube, TikTok, and Meta all require labeling AI-generated audio, and undisclosed synthetic voices can trigger demonetization or removal. See our blog for platform-by-platform rules.

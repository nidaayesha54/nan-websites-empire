GTA 6 Restaurants: Where to Eat in Vice City

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Vice City has always run on fast food, and Grand Theft Auto 6 is no exception. Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell are back, food is not just decoration this time — it heals you — and Miami itself has turned into a GTA 6 restaurant, with real burger joints rebranding as the game's chains for launch. This guide covers where to eat inside Leonida, what food actually does for Jason and Lucia, and where to eat like a Vice City local in the real world.

One warning up front: some of the restaurant names floating around online come from a fan-made list, not from the game. This guide marks what is confirmed and what is fan content, so you do not plan a dinner around a place that does not exist.

The chains that are really in the game

Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell are the two chains you can count on. Both are long-running GTA brands — Burger Shot has been the series' burger joint since GTA 3, and Cluckin' Bell its fried-chicken rival — and both are woven into the launch in the real world too. At the Kaseya Center in Miami, home of the Heat, Burger Shot stands serve at sections 105, 120, 308 and 324, with Cluckin' Bell holding down section 325 with chicken sandwiches, tenders and waffle fries. In Florida, the smash-burger chain Skinny Louie has been running as Burger Shot — signage, uniforms and branded packaging included — from early November through December 31, under a partnership with Rockstar.

The menus are real, too. Skinny Louie's Burger Shot menu lists a Bleeder Triple for $15.49, a Burger Shot Single for $8.99, a Double for $11.99 and a caramel milkshake for $6.99. That is the closest thing to eating inside the game without a console: the same names, the same branding, and a milkshake that costs almost as much as the burger.

What food does in GTA 6

Food is a mechanic now, not a prop. Preview coverage describes a fridge at the safehouse that does something rather than opening for show: a beer restores focus and an apple restores health. The wider healing system works the same way — pink-heart food restores health to the normal maximum, orange-heart food temporarily boosts it above the maximum, and natural regeneration is slow and stops at a white line on the health bar. Eating is how you top up between fights, and the cheapest heal in the game is a snack from a shop, not a hospital visit.

That makes restaurants practical, not just atmospheric. A meal before a robbery is preparation, the same as body armour or a silenced pistol. Stock the fridge, carry snacks, and eat before the hard part — the game rewards players who treat food as gear.

The fan list — and what it is

You may have seen a list of six GTA 6 restaurants: Hank's Waffles for post-mischief breakfast, Mason's Shrimp Shack in Port Gellhorn, Palometa Seafood Restaurant, Lucky Plucker fried chicken, The Amalfi for "legitimate" businessmen, and Crust Fund late-night pizza. It is a fun list, and the names sound right — but it comes from a fan-made social post, not from Rockstar or from the finished game. Treat it as fan content: a wishlist of places that fit Vice City, not a confirmed directory. If any of them turn up in the game, they will be worth a visit; until then, they are names on a poster.

The same caution applies to the wider food scene. Plenty of guides list in-game restaurants with confidence and no source. The confirmed names are the chains — Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell — and the rest is still being mapped by players in the first weeks.

Eating like a local in the real Miami

Miami spent the launch season turning itself into Vice City, and the food is the best part. Michael's Genuine in the Design District ran a collaboration menu through December 1, built around a Pink Flamingo cocktail for $23 served in a commemorative glass, Key West pink shrimp empanadas for $12, a mojo short rib for $52 and key lime ice cream for $11. Panther Coffee went full Vice City takeover across its Wynwood, Little Haiti, Coconut Grove and MiMo cafes, with themed decor, drinks, collectible coffee bags and exclusive merch.

The chains joined in too. Popeyes unveiled a "Spice City Sandwich" — two chicken breast fillets on a brioche bun with pickles — available November 19 to 22 from $6. Einstein Bros offered a $5 All-Nighter Egg Sandwich to rewards members on November 19 for players staying up from midnight to sunrise. MOOYAH ran a 6-STAR Combo, named after the game's six-star wanted meter, from November 19 to 29. Subway turned the Cuban sandwich into a Cubano Pulled Pork Melt. None of it is in the game — all of it is Miami eating the game, which is the same thing in a city that has always been Vice City with the serial numbers filed off.

FAQ

What restaurants are in GTA 6? Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell are the confirmed chains. Other names circulating online, like Hank's Waffles and Mason's Shrimp Shack, come from a fan-made list and are not confirmed in-game.

Does food heal you in GTA 6? Yes. A beer restores focus and an apple restores health, pink-heart food restores health to the maximum, and orange-heart food temporarily boosts it above the maximum.

Can I eat GTA 6 food in real life? In Miami, yes — Skinny Louie ran as Burger Shot through December 31, the Kaseya Center served Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell, and several chains ran Vice City menus for launch week.

The bottom line: in GTA 6, eat at Burger Shot and Cluckin' Bell, treat food as healing gear, and take the fan restaurant lists with a pinch of salt — then go to Miami, where the game is on the menu for real.

🎨 Cuisine Color Guide

Colors are the fastest way to tell guests what kind of food you serve — before they read a single word. Click a cuisine to see its full palette, then click any color to copy its hex code.

How to use these palettes

🖌️ Logo

Use the darkest color for your icon and text, the lightest for the badge background, and one saturated color as the accent ring. Try it in the logo maker.

📋 Menu

Headings in the dark color, body text in near-black on the light color, and boxes/highlights in the accent. Full method in the menu design guide.

📸 Social media

Use the same 2–3 colors in every post template. Recognition compounds: guests should spot your brand before reading the caption.

Tips for a great restaurant logo

🎯 Match your cuisine

Warm reds say Italian, black + red says sushi, browns say cozy café. See the cuisine color guide for tested palettes.

🔤 Keep the name short

One or two words read best on signage and delivery apps. Long names get shrunk until nobody can read them.

📐 Think small first

Your logo must survive as a tiny app icon and a favicon. Bold shapes and one icon beat fine detail every time.

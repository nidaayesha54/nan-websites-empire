GTA 6 Clips: Record and Share Your Best Moments

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the best moments in Leonida last about three seconds. A perfect getaway, a stunt that should not have worked, a stranger doing something absurd on Ocean Drive — all of it is gone unless you saved it. The good news is that both consoles record in the background all the time, so the clip already exists. You just have to know which button grabs it, and how to get it onto your phone before it is deleted.

This guide covers both consoles from the same starting point: the moment has already happened, or the session is about to start. It lists the exact buttons, the time limits on each console, the settings to change before launch night, and the fastest way to move a clip from the console to your phone so you can post it the same day.

PS5: the Create button does everything

On PlayStation 5, the Create button on the DualSense is the whole system. Press it after something happens and choose Save Recent Gameplay, and the console keeps anything from the last 15 seconds up to the last hour, depending on the length you set. Press it before a mission or a long free-roam run and choose Start New Recording, and it records forward for up to an hour. A double press of Create saves the last 30 seconds by default, which is the fastest option in the middle of a chase, and that length can be changed in the settings to 15 seconds, 3, 5, 15 or 30 minutes, or 1 hour.

Two settings matter before you play. First, the clip format: the PS5 ships set to WebM, which takes less space, while MP4 is the format every editor and phone app opens without trouble. A clip stays in whatever format it was saved in, so switch to MP4 before the session, not after. Second, turn on Auto-Upload under Settings, Captures and Broadcasts, Captures. With it on, clips under 3 minutes that are not 4K arrive in the PlayStation App on your phone and stay there for 14 days. Anything longer or sharper still needs a USB drive, formatted as exFAT, because the older FAT32 format cannot store a file larger than 4 GB and an hour of footage easily passes that.

Xbox: shorter memory, same idea

On Xbox Series X|S the rolling memory is much shorter, so the habit is different. Press the Xbox button and then X to save the last 30 seconds, or press View to choose a longer slice, reaching back up to 2 minutes. On the newer controller, press and hold the Share button to record a clip. If you want a long session saved, do not rely on the backward-looking options at all: open the guide, go to Capture and share, and choose Start recording before the moment begins, then stop it when you are done. That runs up to 10 minutes on the console's own storage, or up to 1 hour on an external drive.

Before you play, open Profile and system, Settings, Preferences, Capture and share, and pick the clip resolution and where captures are saved. Recording at 4K needs your TV output set to 4K, and higher resolutions mean shorter clips. Captures upload to the Xbox network without being asked, so they turn up in the Xbox app on your phone or a Windows PC, and in Recent captures in the guide on the console itself. One deadline to know: captures are deleted from the Xbox network after 90 days, so back the ones you care about up to OneDrive or an external drive.

Getting the clip onto your phone

On PS5, the PlayStation App is the fast route. With Auto-Upload on and the console waiting in rest mode with its internet connection kept alive, new clips under 3 minutes and not in 4K appear in the app, where Download saves one to your phone. Record in 1080p if the clip is headed to your phone, because the app only receives clips that are not 4K and run up to 3 minutes. For anything bigger, plug in a USB drive and copy the file directly — it is the dependable way to a PC, and from there to any editor.

On Xbox, the Xbox app is the route. Captures show up there on your phone, where you can download or share them, or you can copy them to external storage from the console. Either way, test the route before launch night: record a 30-second clip, check that it appears in the app, and confirm the quality looks right. The worst time to discover a broken transfer is after the best moment of your first session.

Long sessions and streams

Neither console records more than an hour in one continuous clip, so a whole evening needs a different plan. On PS5, start a new recording at the start of each hour. On Xbox, point captures at an external drive and start recording at the start of the session. If you stream instead, clip from the video on demand afterwards — but move fast, because Twitch keeps past broadcasts for 7 days for most streamers, 14 days for Affiliates, and 60 days for Partners and Turbo subscribers.

One more warning, from PlayStation's own help pages: some content cannot be captured, and some scenes are blocked and will not be included in your video clips. Each game can block scenes, and neither Rockstar, PlayStation nor Xbox had said which GTA 6 scenes are blocked as of early November 2026. If a cutscene comes out black in your recording, that is the game protecting the scene, not a broken setting.

FAQ

How do I save a GTA 6 clip on PS5? Press Create, then Save Recent Gameplay for anything from the last 15 seconds to the last hour, or Start New Recording for up to an hour going forward. A double press of Create saves the last 30 seconds by default.

How do I save a GTA 6 clip on Xbox? Press the Xbox button and then X for the last 30 seconds, or View for up to the last 2 minutes. For longer, use Start recording in Capture and share: 10 minutes on internal storage, 1 hour on an external drive.

How long do clips stay in the phone apps? PlayStation App clips stay 14 days; Xbox network captures are deleted after 90 days. Download the ones you want to keep.

The bottom line: on PS5 the Create button and a 1080p MP4 setting cover almost everything, on Xbox start recording before the session because the backward window is only 30 seconds, and on both consoles test the phone transfer once before you need it for real.

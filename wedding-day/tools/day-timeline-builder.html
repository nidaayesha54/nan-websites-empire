    How this timeline works

    The builder works backwards and forwards from your ceremony time. Morning prep is scheduled so hair and makeup finish with a 30-minute buffer before you need to leave. Portraits are placed in the golden window around sunset when light is most flattering. Speeches are timed between dinner courses so guests stay engaged. Every event includes a small buffer — because weddings always run a few minutes behind, and that's okay when the plan expects it.

    Pro tip: print this timeline and give a copy to your photographer, coordinator, DJ, and maid of honor. When everyone works from the same schedule, you stop being the person everyone asks questions.

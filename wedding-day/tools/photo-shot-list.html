Why a shot list matters

Professional photographers are artists, not mind-readers. A shot list doesn't micromanage them — it tells them which people and moments matter most to you. The #1 regret couples report after weddings? "We never got a photo with [grandmother / college friends / late father's brother]." A 10-minute checklist prevents a lifetime of regret.

Pair this with your day-of timeline so family portraits happen in one efficient 20-minute block instead of eating your entire cocktail hour.

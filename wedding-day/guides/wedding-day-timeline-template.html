By WeddingDayPlan · Updated October 2026 · 8 min read

The biggest wedding-day problem isn't the flowers or the food — it's not knowing what happens when. A minute-by-minute timeline turns chaos into calm. Below are three proven templates for the most common wedding styles. Copy the one closest to your day, adjust the times, and share it with every vendor.

5 Timeline Rules That Prevent Disasters

  1. Add 15-minute buffers everywhere. Hair runs late. Uncle Bob gets lost. Buffers absorb chaos.
  2. Feed your wedding party. Schedule a real meal (not just snacks) 3+ hours before the ceremony. Hungry = cranky.
  3. Do family portraits in ONE 20-minute block. Give the photographer a list of groupings in advance. Every extra grouping costs 3 minutes.
  4. Time speeches between courses. Guests listen better with food coming. Never stack all speeches at once.
  5. Share the timeline with everyone. Photographer, DJ, coordinator, maid of honor, best man. When everyone has the plan, nobody asks YOU questions on your wedding day.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a typical wedding day timeline look like?

Morning hair/makeup 3–4 hours before the ceremony, first look and portraits, a 30-minute ceremony, cocktail hour, then a 4-hour reception with dinner, speeches, cake cutting, and dancing, ending with a send-off.

How far apart should ceremony and reception be?

60–90 minutes (the cocktail hour fills the gap). If venues are separate, add travel time plus a 30-minute buffer — guests hate waiting with nothing to do.

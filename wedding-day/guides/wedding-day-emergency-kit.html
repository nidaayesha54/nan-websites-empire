Wedding Day Emergency Kit: The 40+ Item Checklist That Saves Weddings

By WeddingDayPlan · Updated October 2026 · 7 min read

Here's the problem nobody talks about: something WILL go wrong on your wedding day. A hem tears. A headache strikes. A boutonnière wilts. The couples who stay calm aren't luckier — they're just prepared. This is the exact emergency kit professional coordinators pack, plus the day-of tips that turn disasters into funny stories.

🎒 Assign a keeper: Give this kit to your maid of honor or coordinator — NOT the bride. The bride should never have to dig through a bag on her wedding day.

🧵 Fashion Fixes

  • Mini sewing kit (needle, thread in dress/suit colors, buttons)
  • Fashion tape (the #1 most-used item — fixes necklines, straps, hems)
  • Safety pins — assorted sizes
  • Hem tape / iron-on hem strips
  • White chalk (covers scuffs on shoes and dress hems)
  • Stain remover pen
  • Lint roller
  • Static guard spray
  • Extra earring backs
  • Clear nail polish (stops stocking runs)

💄 Beauty & Comfort

  • Blotting papers (better than powder for photos)
  • Lipstick / lip gloss for touch-ups
  • Travel deodorant
  • Bobby pins + hair ties (more than you think you need)
  • Mini hairspray
  • Tissues (happy tears are still tears)
  • Band-aids + blister pads (new shoes are the enemy)
  • Pain reliever (ibuprofen + acetaminophen)
  • Antacid tablets
  • Allergy medicine
  • Eye drops (for photos — red eyes ruin portraits)
  • Mints / breath spray (not gum — no chewing in photos)
  • Hand lotion (dry hands show in ring photos)

🔧 Practical Lifesavers

  • Phone chargers + portable battery pack
  • Printed timeline (2 copies) — see our timeline builder
  • Printed vendor contact list with phone numbers
  • Cash in small bills ($100–200 for tips and emergencies)
  • Snacks + water bottles (wedding party MUST eat)
  • Straws (drink without ruining lipstick)
  • Small scissors
  • Super glue (broken heel? broken nail? fixed.)
  • Umbrella or parasol (sun AND rain)
  • Bug spray (outdoor weddings)
  • Sunscreen
  • Flip-flops (for dancing — save the feet)
  • Chalk for dance floor (outdoor — marks the first-dance spot)

🎯 Day-Of Coordinator Tips (From the Pros)

1. The 30-minute rule

If anything runs late, steal time from the least visible moments — shorten cocktail hour mingling, not portraits. Guests never notice a 45-minute cocktail hour instead of 60. They WILL notice missing golden-hour photos.

2. Feed everyone on schedule

Vendors who eat on time work better. Confirm the caterer serves vendors during guest dinner — put it in the contract. Hungry photographers miss moments.

3. The "point person" system

Assign ONE non-bride person per category: one for vendors, one for family drama, one for the wedding party. All questions go to them. The couple's only job is getting married and having fun.

4. Build a weather plan B by 10 AM

For outdoor weddings, make the tent/chairs call by mid-morning — not at 3 PM when it's already raining. Decide the trigger (e.g., "60% chance of rain = plan B") weeks in advance.

5. The sunset alarm

Set a phone alarm for 30 minutes before sunset labeled "GOLDEN HOUR 📸". When it rings, the coordinator pulls the couple out for 15 minutes of the best photos of the day. This single habit produces the photos couples frame.

6. End-of-night checklist

Before the send-off, the coordinator grabs: gifts/cards box, leftover cake top tier, personal items from bridal suite, marriage license (signed!). Every year, couples lose one of these.

Pack It This Week

Don't wait until the night before. Buy the items this week, pack the bag, and hand it to your designated keeper. One less thing to think about — and the peace of mind is worth more than everything in the bag.

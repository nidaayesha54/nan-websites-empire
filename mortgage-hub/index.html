Frequently Asked Questions

How is a mortgage payment calculated?

Monthly principal & interest = P × r(1+r)ⁿ / ((1+r)ⁿ − 1). Property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI, and HOA dues are added on top of that.

How much house can I afford on my salary?

Lenders use the 28/36 rule: housing costs ≤ 28% of gross monthly income, and all debts ≤ 36%. Try our affordability calculator.

When does refinancing make sense?

When your monthly savings recover closing costs in roughly 24 months or less. Use the break-even calculator to check.

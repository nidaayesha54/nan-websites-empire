GTA 6 Garages: All 15 Locations and Prices

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the first question every new player asks about property has a short answer: you cannot buy a house. The property GTA 6 sells is garages. Fifteen of them exist across Leonida, eight can be bought with in-game money, four unlock through the story, and three arrive with your edition or a subscription. This guide is the complete catalogue — every garage, what it costs, how to buy it, and the two that come free with how you bought the game.

Start with the rule that shapes everything. Your home comes from the story, not from a property listing. Jason and Lucia live where the story puts them, and they move to new safehouses as it goes on: four in total, the Leonida Keys house, the Starlet Motel, Bayside and Ocean Beach, all unlocking through the story and none of them for sale. Rockstar's own character page says it plainly — Brian Heder, a drug runner from the Keys, is letting Jason live at one of his properties without paying rent, so long as he helps with local shakedowns. That single sentence is the only thing Rockstar has published about how Jason got a roof. Garages are the exception, and they exist for a practical reason: a stolen car you pay to register becomes yours, and it needs somewhere to live.

The eight garages you can buy

The eight purchasable garages cost $160,000 combined, and you buy them through the Vice City Real Estate app, which becomes available after the Chapter 2 mission Daily Driver. Each garage adds room for more registered cars on top of the three vehicle slots you start with, and owning all eight unlocks the Little Boxes reward. Every garage has a weapon locker and a secure place to deposit stolen goods to be fenced — that is the same function the two edition garages carry, and it is the reason garages matter beyond parking. The prices are reported from launch-era guides, not from a Rockstar price list, so treat individual figures as the community's best reading while the total — $160,000 for all eight — is the number every guide agrees on. Safehouse parking does not add registered garage spaces, so the garages are the only way to grow your collection.

The two garages that come with your edition

Two garages arrive with what you buy rather than with in-game money. The Shore Court personal garage sits a stone's throw from Ocean Beach and comes with the Vintage Vice City Pack, for any edition bought before November 20, 2026, paired with a '55 Vapid Stanier. The Paradise Garage in Watson Bay comes with the Ultimate Edition, paired with a '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy. Both carry the same description Rockstar gives them: a weapon locker plus a secure place to deposit stolen goods to be fenced. That leaves four garages that unlock through the story and one more edition or subscription bonus on top of the two named above — eight buyable, four story, three edition or subscription, fifteen in total. What nobody outside Rockstar knows yet is the exact price of each individual garage, the full list of locations, and the capacity of each one. Those details are still being mapped by players.

The four safehouses, and what they do

The four safehouses are worth knowing by name, because you will live in all of them. The Leonida Keys house is the first — Brian Heder's property, rent-free while Jason helps with shakedowns. Then come the Starlet Motel, Bayside and Ocean Beach, each handed over as the story moves. Safehouses are used to save, change clothes and rest, and their outside parking does not add registered garage spaces — that is the garages' job. They are also far more interactive than the apartments of GTA V: you can raid the fridge, change your hair, spend time with your partner, watch television and play the in-game console. The house is not yours to buy, but it is yours to live in, and the story moves you through all four before the end.

Garages only make sense alongside the rest of the car system. You steal a car, scan it with the WAiNK app, read the Cost to Register line, pay the fee, and the car becomes yours — but it needs a garage to live in, and you start with only three vehicle slots. That is why the eight purchasable garages matter: each one is more room for more registered cars, and the Scooter Bros app then delivers any registered car to you anywhere. Without the garages, the registration fees are wasted; without the registration, the garages are empty. Buy the garage first, then the car to fill it.

What you cannot buy — and what to do instead

Businesses are the other thing players ask about, and the answer is the same as houses: not shown. No preview, trailer or Rockstar page shows a business you can buy, run or earn from. GTA V paid you weekly from the properties you owned; GTA 6 has not shown anything like it, and the money comes from what you steal and sell instead. So with no house to buy, your money goes on things you will use: garages and their car spaces first, then gear, gym memberships, car upgrades and the premium Scooter Bros service. Buy the garages early — every registered car needs a home, and the fence pays around $21,000 for a good one, with damage cutting the payout. A garage is not a trophy. It is the one property in Leonida that pays for itself.

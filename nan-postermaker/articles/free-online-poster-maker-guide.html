How to Make a Poster Online (Step by Step)

Here is the exact workflow in NAN Poster Maker — it takes under two minutes:

  1. Choose a template. Three one-click layouts set everything for you: Sale (big discount-style headline on a bold gradient), Event (headline plus date/venue lines on a deep navy background), and Quote (centered, elegant, minimal). Pick the one closest to your need.
  2. Type your headline. Keep it short — 2 to 5 words. "MEGA SALE", "Grand Opening", "Eid Mubarak", "دکان کی زبردست سیل". The headline is the biggest text on the poster; it should be readable from a phone screen at a glance.
  3. Add sub text and a bottom line. The sub text carries the offer or message ("Up to 50% OFF — this weekend only"). The bottom line is for practical details: date, time, venue, phone number, or a call to action ("Shop now", "رابطہ کریں").
  4. Pick a background. Choose from solid colors and gradient presets, or use the color picker for your brand color. Bold gradients work best for sales; dark backgrounds suit elegant quotes and formal events.
  5. Fine-tune. Drag the headline-size slider until the text fills the canvas nicely, pick a text color with strong contrast, and toggle the text shadow for extra pop on busy backgrounds.
  6. Download the PNG. One tap downloads a crisp 1080×1080 image — the ideal square size for Instagram posts, WhatsApp status, Facebook, and printing small flyers.

Poster Kaise Banaye — اردو میں پوسٹر بنانے کا طریقہ

Urdu posters need special care because most design tools were built for Latin text. Here's how to do it right with a proper Urdu-capable flyer maker:

  1. Type Urdu directly. Switch your phone keyboard to Urdu and type into the headline field — for example "شاندار سیل" or "عید مبارک". Don't paste Urdu into tools that don't support it; letters can appear disconnected or reversed.
  2. Check the rendering. In NAN Poster Maker the canvas draws your Urdu lines exactly as you typed them, using the Noto Nastaliq Urdu font, centered and right-to-left. Nastaliq is the traditional, beautiful Urdu calligraphy style — far better looking than plain Naskh for posters.
  3. Keep Urdu headlines short. Nastaliq is tall and flowing; 2–4 words look best. Long sentences in Nastaliq can collide on a square canvas — move details (date, address) to the bottom line.
  4. Mixed Urdu + English works. A headline like "MEGA SALE" with a bottom line "ہفتہ 25 اکتوبر · شام 5 بجے" is completely fine — the canvas falls back to the right font per character automatically.
  5. Contrast matters more in Urdu. Nastaliq's thin strokes can vanish on busy backgrounds. Use white or yellow text on dark gradients, and keep the text shadow ON for Urdu posters.
Tip: For shop banners and WhatsApp broadcast posters, Urdu headlines get far more attention in Pakistan than English ones. A "زبردست سیل" poster will outperform "MEGA SALE" with a local audience — always design for who will see it.

7 Design Tips That Make Free Posters Look Professional

You don't need to be a designer — just follow these rules and your flyers will look like a pro made them:

1. One message per poster

A poster is not a brochure. If someone can't understand it in 3 seconds, it's too crowded. Headline = the offer, sub text = one supporting detail, bottom line = how/when/where. That's it.

2. Big headline, small details

The classic mistake is making everything the same size. Your headline should be 2–3× bigger than the supporting text. The size slider exists for exactly this — push the headline until it dominates the canvas.

3. High contrast, always

White text on dark backgrounds. Dark text on light backgrounds. If you pick a bright gradient, add the text shadow — it draws a soft dark outline that keeps letters readable.

4. Match the background to the mood

Warm gradients (red/orange/pink) shout sale, urgent, exciting. Deep navy and dark gradients say elegant, formal, premium. Teal and green feel fresh, natural, trustworthy. The Sale template's sunset gradient and the Event template's navy exist for this reason.

5. Leave breathing room

Don't stretch text edge-to-edge. The tool keeps margins automatically (text wraps within the canvas with padding) — resist the urge to max out the size slider if words start touching the edges.

6. Use the bottom line for action

Every poster needs a next step: a date, a phone number, "Shop now", "DM to order", "رابطہ کریں". Posters without a call to action get likes; posters with one get customers.

7. Stay consistent for your brand

If you post weekly offers, reuse the same background and text color each time. Followers start recognizing your posters instantly — that's free branding, and a flyer maker with saved templates makes it effortless.

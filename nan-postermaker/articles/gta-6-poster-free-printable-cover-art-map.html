GTA 6 Poster: Free Printable Cover Art and Leonida Map

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, and Rockstar marked the day with a gift that costs nothing: a printable cover-art poster and a map of Leonida, added to the game's site as a free digital download. A physical version follows shortly after launch from the Rockstar Store — double-sided, for $0.99 on its own, or free with any store purchase. For a game that ships without a disc, the poster is the thing you can actually hold in your hands.

This guide explains how to get the free download, how the physical poster works, what is still unknown about it, and the collector's box that hides a much bigger version of the same map.

The free download: what Rockstar promised

The poster was announced in Rockstar's November 3 pre-load post, the same announcement that moved digital pre-loading to November 10 and put the first size figure on the record — up to 212 GB. Tucked at the bottom of that post was the promise, in Rockstar's own words: on November 19 the site would add a printable Grand Theft Auto VI cover art poster and map of Leonida as a free digital download, and a double-sided physical version would follow shortly after launch from the Rockstar Store.

The art the poster is built on is Rockstar's multi-panel key art — Jason and Lucia at the centre of a collage of the game's world, the same cover art that has fronted the game since the release date announcement. The map is the first map artifact Rockstar has announced for players in this campaign. Everything labelled a map before now was a fan reconstruction, and the honest ones said so.

How to get the free poster

Go to the GTA 6 site on Rockstar's own website and look for the printable cover art poster and map of Leonida. Download the files, then print them at home or through a print service. That is the whole process — three steps, no account, no purchase, no code.

Two things are still unknown, and worth knowing before you print. As of early November, Rockstar had not published the format, the resolution or the paper size of the download — whether it is a PDF or an image, and whether it is sized for A4, letter or poster-scale walls. It had also not published a content list: Rockstar has named six destinations — Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park — but whether the printable labels them, shows county lines or is an art piece with the geography hinted at is not stated. Check the file before you pay for a large print.

The $0.99 physical poster

The physical version is double-sided: one side shows the full map of Leonida, the other shows the GTA 6 cover art with Jason and Lucia. It costs $0.99 on its own, or it is free as a gift with any purchase from the Rockstar Store. The free gift only applies to Rockstar Store orders — a copy of the game bought from another retailer does not qualify.

Timing is the softest part of the promise: "shortly after launch." No store listing, SKU or shipping detail was published with the announcement, so treat any site selling the poster today as a counterfeit or a placeholder. Until Rockstar posts the details, the Rockstar Store is the only place to check — and $0.99 is, by a distance, the cheapest piece of GTA 6 merch Rockstar has ever sold. For comparison, the GTA 6 collection that opened on the Rockstar Store on October 8, 2026 runs from a $4.99 sticker sheet to a $399.99 collector's box.

The big one: the $399.99 collector's box

The same poster exists in a much larger form. The Goodtime State — Vice City Collection box, at $399.99, includes a 36 by 26 inch double-sided poster, with artwork from the in-game Macca the Gator TV show on the front and a map of Leonida on the back. The box around it holds a 6-inch Macca the Gator figure with a twist-open stash base, matte black Oakley Frogskins with Prizm Sapphire lenses, a New Era 9FORTY A-Frame snapback, a pink Macca magnetic mirror, a Vice City swizzle spoon, a razor-blade keychain, a Leonida Keys crossbody bag, a Chunkee the Manatee shot glass, 9 enamel pins in a tin and 9 vinyl stickers in a stash bag.

That is a collector's purchase, not a poster purchase — but if you want the map on your wall at a size you can read from across the room, it is the only version that big. For everyone else, the free download and the $0.99 print are the same art, at the right price.

Print tips

If you print at home, use the heaviest paper your printer takes and print the map at the largest size the file allows — a small print turns the map's labels into blur. If you use a print service, ask for a matte finish: the cover art is dark and glossy paper will glare. For the map, print one copy at A4 first to check the labels are legible, then order the big one. And keep the digital files — if Rockstar updates the download after launch, the early version becomes a collector's item in its own right.

FAQ

Is the GTA 6 poster really free? Yes — the printable cover-art poster and Leonida map are a free digital download on Rockstar's GTA 6 site from November 19, 2026. The physical double-sided version is $0.99, or free with any Rockstar Store purchase.

What size is the free poster? Rockstar has not published the format, resolution or paper size. The physical version is double-sided, map on one side and cover art on the other; the collector's box version is 36 by 26 inches.

Where do I get the physical poster? From the Rockstar Store, shortly after launch. Any other site selling it now is a counterfeit or a placeholder.

The bottom line: the best GTA 6 merch costs nothing. Download the free poster and map, print them well, and if you want the big one on your wall, the $0.99 physical version is the cheapest deal Rockstar has ever offered.

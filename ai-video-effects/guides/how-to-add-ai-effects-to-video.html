The 3 ways to add AI effects to video

1. In an AI video generator (Runway, Luma, Pika): describe the effect in your prompt — the AI renders it into the clip. Fastest for generative effects like morphs and relighting.
2. In a mobile/desktop editor (CapCut, Premiere, DaVinci Resolve): apply AI-powered effects from the effects panel — slow-mo interpolation, auto reframe, background removal.
3. Hybrid: generate the hero effect with AI, then cut, caption and grade in an editor. This is the professional workflow and what this guide teaches.

Method A: CapCut (free, 10 minutes)

  1. Import your clip → tap Effects → Video effects.
  2. For slow-mo: select clip → Speed → Curve → enable AI frame interpolation (sometimes labeled "Smooth slow-mo").
  3. For zoom punch: Effects → Party → Camera In, or keyframe Scale from 100→115 over 4 frames.
  4. For glitch: Effects → Distort → Glitch; trim to 0.3s and place on cuts.
  5. Text → Auto captions, style them bold, then export at 1080×1920.

Method B: Runway (generative effects)

  1. Open Runway → Generative Video → upload your clip or start from text/image.
  2. Type the effect into the prompt, e.g.: "slow-motion close-up of a dog shaking off water, droplets frozen mid-air, cinematic lighting" — or generate one with our prompt tool.
  3. Generate, pick the best take, download and finish the edit in CapCut.

Method C: DaVinci Resolve (free, pro quality)

  1. Slow-mo: right-click clip → Retime controls → set speed → change Retime process to Optical Flow.
  2. Zoom punch: keyframe Zoom in the Inspector over 4–5 frames.
  3. Glitch: Fusion page → add TV Damage or RGB-split via Channel Booleans.
  4. Deliver page → H.264, 1080×1920 for short-form.

Mistakes that ruin AI effects

  • Stacking 5 effects on one clip — pick one hero effect.
  • Wrong intensity — a subtle aesthetic vlog doesn't want an extreme glitch. Match intensity to mood (our generator has a 1–10 slider for this).
  • Forgetting the AI label — platforms can suppress unlabeled AI content. Label it; it doesn't hurt reach when the content is good.
  • Effects with no story beat — every effect should land on a moment: a reveal, a punchline, a transition.

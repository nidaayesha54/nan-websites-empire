1. Using five effects when one would do

Beginners treat effects like seasoning — more must be better. It's not. Pick the single effect that lands on your video's key beat. Everything else is noise.

2. Wrong intensity for the mood

An extreme glitch on a soft aesthetic vlog feels broken, not bold. Match intensity to mood: our generator's 1–10 intensity slider exists for exactly this.

3. Effects with no story beat

A zoom punch on a random frame is just camera movement. The same zoom on a reveal, a punchline or a beat drop is storytelling. Every effect needs a moment.

4. Skipping the AI label

TikTok, Reels and YouTube all require AI-content disclosure. Unlabeled AI videos can be suppressed or removed — the label costs you nothing when the content is good.

5. Copying the trend instead of adapting it

Shot-for-shot copies get buried by the algorithm. Take the effect mechanic (the morph, the ramp) and apply it to your own niche — that's what the Effect Finder quiz helps you do.

Rising: invisible AI

The biggest shift of 2026: the best AI effects are the ones viewers don't notice — perfect slow-mo interpolation, seamless background cleanup, natural relighting. "Look what AI can do" edits are fading; "wait, that was AI?" edits are winning.

Fading: effect spam

Videos stacking glitch + zoom + shake + flash on every cut are getting swiped past. Audience taste matured — one confident effect beats five nervous ones.

What's next

  • Real-time effects: live AI effects during recording, not just in post.
  • Personalized effects: effects that adapt to your face, voice or brand colors automatically.
  • Longer AI clips: as generators support 30s+ clips, effects will move from "moments" to full scenes.

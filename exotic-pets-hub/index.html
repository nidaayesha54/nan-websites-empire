Exotic Pet FAQs

Quick answers to the questions new owners ask most.

What is the easiest exotic pet to take care of?
The leopard gecko. It needs a simple heated terrarium, eats insects a few times a week, and is docile and hardy. Betta fish and budgies are also excellent low-maintenance starters. See the full beginner ranking →
How much does an exotic pet cost per year?
From about $150/year for a betta fish to $1,500+ for a sugar glider or large parrot. Most beginner reptiles and small birds land between $300 and $800 per year. Try the cost calculator →
Are exotic pets legal in the United States?
It depends on your state, county, and city. Nevada is very permissive; California bans hedgehogs, ferrets, and many other species. Check your state here →
Do exotic pets need special vet care?
Yes — you need an exotic-animal veterinarian, not a standard cat-and-dog vet. Locate one before you buy, since emergency exotic care is expensive. Read common mistakes to avoid →
Which exotic pet is best for an apartment?
Quiet, small-footprint pets: leopard geckos, ball pythons, betta fish, and hedgehogs. Loud parrots like conures are usually a poor apartment fit. Take the quiz to find your match →

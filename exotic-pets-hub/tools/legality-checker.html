Important disclaimer

This tool is an educational overview, not legal advice. Laws change — sometimes every legislative session — and your city or county may be stricter than your state. Before buying any exotic animal:

  1. Confirm with your state's fish & wildlife / DNR agency.
  2. Check your city and county ordinances (some ban species the state allows).
  3. If you rent, check your lease and HOA rules too.

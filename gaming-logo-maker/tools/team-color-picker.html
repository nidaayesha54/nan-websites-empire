Color psychology for gaming teams

  • Cyan + purple — the classic esports look: high-tech, energetic, trusted.
  • Red + black — aggression and dominance; the most feared combo in competitive play.
  • Green + dark — toxic, edgy, streamer-favorite energy.
  • Gold + navy — premium, championship vibes for established orgs.

Rule of thumb: one bright primary, one supporting accent, one dark base, one light for text. Keep it consistent across your logo, overlays and jerseys — consistency is what makes a brand memorable.

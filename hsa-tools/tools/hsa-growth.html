How the triple tax advantage works

An HSA is the only account in the U.S. tax code with three tax breaks at once: 1) contributions are tax-deductible, 2) growth is tax-free, and 3) withdrawals for qualified medical expenses are tax-free. Our 2026 limit optimizer shows exactly how much you can put in.

HSA vs taxable account

In a taxable brokerage account you invest after-tax dollars and pay capital-gains tax on growth. In an HSA, every dollar skips income tax on the way in and skips tax again on qualified withdrawals — that's the gap this calculator shows. Compare account types with our HSA vs FSA comparator.

The 'stealth IRA' strategy

Many HSA owners pay medical bills out of pocket, save the receipts, and let the HSA compound for decades — then reimburse themselves tax-free in retirement. There's no deadline to claim a reimbursement.

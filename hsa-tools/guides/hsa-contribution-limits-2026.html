HSA Contribution Limits 2026

By HSATools · October 2026 · 5 min read

Go one dollar over the IRS limit and you'll owe a 6% excise tax every year until you fix it. Here's the 2026 picture — plus the traps that catch even careful savers.

The 2026 numbers (estimates)

Coverage2026 limit (est.)With 55+ catch-up

Family$8,750$9,750

The IRS finalizes limits each fall; these are the announced 2026 estimates. Find your personal max in seconds with the contribution optimizer.

Employer contributions count

The limit is a combined cap: your contributions plus your employer's. A $1,000 employer seed means $1,000 less room for you. This is the #1 cause of accidental over-contribution.

The 55+ catch-up rule

At 55 you can add $1,000 extra per year. Married couples: each spouse 55+ gets their own $1,000 catch-up, but it must go into separate HSA accounts in each spouse's name.

The last-month rule (and its trap)

Enrolled in an HDHP on December 1? The last-month rule lets you contribute the full annual amount even if you were only eligible part of the year — but you must stay eligible through the following December 31 (the 'testing period') or face taxes and penalties on the extra.

What happens if you over-contribute

  • Withdraw the excess (plus earnings) before the tax filing deadline to avoid the 6% excise tax.
  • Miss the deadline and the 6% tax applies every year the excess stays in.
  • Fix it early: most HSA providers have an 'excess contribution removal' form.

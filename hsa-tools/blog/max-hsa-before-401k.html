Should You Max Out Your HSA Before Your 401(k)?

By HSATools · October 2026 · 5 min read

It's one of the most common questions in personal finance — and the answer surprises most people: after capturing your 401(k) employer match, the HSA usually beats maxing the 401(k).

The priority order that works for most people

  1. 401(k) up to the employer match — free money beats everything.
  2. HSA to the max — triple tax advantage, no other account has it.
  3. 401(k)/IRA to the max — then fill traditional retirement accounts.
  4. Taxable brokerage — for whatever's left.

Why the HSA jumps the line

A 401(k) gives you a deduction now but taxes withdrawals later. A Roth gives tax-free withdrawals but no deduction now. The HSA gives you both — deductible in, tax-free out for medical costs. Run the lifetime difference in our HSA growth projector.

The exceptions

  • Terrible HSA investment options or high fees can erase the edge — you can roll HSA funds to a better provider.
  • If you're in the 10–12% bracket, the deduction is worth less; Roth 401(k) contributions may compete.
  • If you can't cash-flow medical bills, you'll spend the HSA instead of investing it — still useful, just less magical.

How to Hook Viewers in 3 Seconds

On TikTok, Reels, and Shorts, the average viewer decides whether to keep watching in under 3 seconds. Your opening isn't an introduction — it's a retention contract: promise a payoff worth staying for, then deliver it.

7 proven hook formulas

  1. The payoff promise: "This $3 trick fixed my sleep in 2 nights." State the result first.
  2. Open mid-action: Start at the most visual moment, then rewind. Motion in frame one stops thumbs.
  3. The contrarian take: "Everything you know about X is wrong." Disagreement is attention.
  4. The specific number: "3 mistakes killing your X." Specificity beats vagueness every time.
  5. The direct callout: "If your X isn't working, you're doing step 2 wrong." Speak to one viewer.
  6. The curiosity gap: "Wait for the third one." Tease without clickbait — you must deliver.
  7. The transformation tease: "Watch this change right in front of you." Before/afters are scroll-proof.

Sound-off design

Most short-form sessions start muted. Your hook must work as bold on-screen text in the first 2 seconds, paired with motion. Say it and show it — the combination doubles comprehension.

What kills hooks

  • Introductions: "Hey guys, welcome back to my channel" (instant swipe)
  • Logos and animated intros (burns your 3 seconds on branding)
  • Slow build-ups: "So today I wanted to talk about…" (no payoff promised)
  • Clickbait the video doesn't deliver (kills trust and future reach)

The re-hook rule

One hook isn't enough for videos over 30 seconds. Re-hook at the 25%, 50%, and 75% marks — "but the last one changes everything" — to reset attention exactly where analytics show viewers drift.

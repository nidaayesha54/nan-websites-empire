Frequently asked questions

What is the best video length for TikTok in 2026?

21–34 seconds. It's long enough to deliver real value and short enough to earn full watch-throughs and replays — the two signals TikTok's algorithm weights most.

What is the best length for YouTube Shorts?

25–35 seconds. Shorts under 40 seconds get the strongest feed velocity while keeping average view duration high.

How long should Instagram Reels be?

18–30 seconds, with a visual hook in the first 2 seconds. Loopable endings that replay cleanly get a big boost.

How do I hook viewers in the first 3 seconds?

State the payoff or open mid-action — verbally and as on-screen text, since most viewers start with the sound off. Try our hook generator for 10 tailored lines.

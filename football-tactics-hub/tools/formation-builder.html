Football Formation Builder

Drag players anywhere on the pitch. Start from a preset, rename your players, then export your lineup as an image or text.

👆 Drag a player token to move it. Click a token to select it, then rename it in the "Selected player name" box. Works on touch screens too.

How to use the formation builder

  1. Pick a preset formation or drag players freely to design your own.
  2. Give your team a name and rename players (e.g. "Rashford", "Bellingham").
  3. Export PNG to save a tactics-board image, or Copy as Text for WhatsApp / group chats.

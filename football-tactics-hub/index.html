Tactics FAQ

What is the best football formation?

There is no single best formation — the 4-3-3 suits possession teams, the 4-4-2 suits balanced, direct sides, and the 3-5-2 suits teams with strong wing-backs. Use our formation quiz to find the one that fits your squad.

What is gegenpressing in football?

Gegenpressing (counter-pressing) means pressing immediately after losing the ball to win it back within seconds, ideally high up the pitch. It was popularised by Jürgen Klopp's Borussia Dortmund and Liverpool. Read our full gegenpressing guide.

What is the difference between 4-3-3 and 4-4-2?

A 4-3-3 uses three central midfielders for control and possession; a 4-4-2 uses two banks of four, which is more compact and direct. See our full 4-3-3 vs 4-4-2 tactics guide.

Can I create my own formation with this site?

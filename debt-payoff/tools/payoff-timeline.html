Why Minimum Payments Are a Trap

On a $8,000 balance at 22% APR, a $200 minimum-ish payment means roughly $147 of it is interest in month one — only $53 touches the principal. That's why balances barely move. Every extra dollar goes 100% to principal, which is why the slider above changes the timeline so dramatically.

Avalanche vs Snowball — Quick Refresher

Avalanche: pay minimums everywhere, attack the highest APR first. Mathematically cheapest — every extra dollar kills the most expensive interest.

Snowball: pay minimums everywhere, attack the smallest balance first. You get a paid-off account fastest, and that win keeps you going.

The #1 Consolidation Mistake

People compare monthly payments and stop there. A lower payment stretched over more years often costs more total — that's exactly what the table above exposes. Consolidation wins when the rate is meaningfully lower and the term isn't stretched to the moon.

Also critical: consolidation only works if you don't run the old cards back up. Close or freeze paid-off cards (keep the oldest open for credit history). Full breakdown: Debt Consolidation: Pros and Cons.

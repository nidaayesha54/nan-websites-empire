Video Prompt Engineering FAQ

What is video prompt engineering?

Video prompt engineering is the skill of writing precise text instructions for AI video generators like Sora, Runway, Luma, Pika and Kling. A well-engineered prompt controls the subject, action, camera movement, lighting, style and mood so the AI renders the scene you actually imagined.

How do I write a good AI video prompt in 2026?

Use six building blocks: subject, action, camera language, lighting, style and mood — plus a negative prompt to block common artifacts. Our free prompt builder assembles these into a pro-level prompt automatically.

What are negative prompts in AI video?

Negative prompts tell the AI what to avoid: extra limbs, morphing faces, watermarks, blurry motion, distorted hands. Read our guide on negative prompts that fix bad outputs for a copy-paste list that works across tools.

Do these techniques work with Sora and Runway?

Yes. Everything here uses plain descriptive filmmaking language that works with Sora, Runway Gen-4, Luma Dream Machine, Pika, Kling, Hailuo and other 2026 text-to-video models.

How can I tell if a video was made by AI?

Run the clip through a free AI video detector — it analyzes motion patterns and generation fingerprints to estimate whether a video is AI-generated. Essential literacy for any prompt engineer in 2026.

Negative Prompts That Fix Bad Outputs

📚 Lesson 3 of 3🔴 Pro⏱ 15 min

Extra fingers. Faces that melt mid-sentence. A watermark from a stock site burned into your "original" clip. These artifacts are the uncanny valley of AI video — and the fix isn't a better model, it's a negative prompt: an explicit list of what the AI must not generate.

Lesson objectives

  • Understand what negative prompts do (and their limits)
  • Get copy-paste negative prompts for the 6 most common artifacts
  • Learn where to place them per tool (Sora, Runway, Luma, Pika)

What a negative prompt actually does

Diffusion and transformer video models sample from learned patterns. A negative prompt steers sampling away from unwanted patterns — it's a guardrail, not a guarantee. It works best on anatomy and quality artifacts (limbs, faces, blur) and worst on conceptual errors (wrong object, wrong physics) — those need a better main prompt.

The universal negative prompt (copy-paste)

extra limbs, extra fingers, deformed hands, morphing face, flickering, blurry motion, distorted anatomy, watermark, text overlay, logo, low quality, grainy, oversaturated, unnatural movement
💡 Pro tip: our Prompt Builder has a dedicated negative-prompt field that appends it correctly to every prompt you generate.

Artifact-by-artifact fixes

ArtifactAdd to negative prompt
Extra / melting limbsextra limbs, deformed hands, extra fingers, fused fingers
Face morphing mid-clipmorphing face, shifting facial features, inconsistent face
Flicker / pulsingflicker, strobing, pulsing light, frame jitter
Watermarks & textwatermark, text overlay, logo, subtitle, username
Soupy motion blurblurry motion, smeared movement, ghosting
Plastic / oversmooth skinplastic skin, airbrushed, waxy, oversaturated

Where negative prompts go (per tool, 2026)

  • Runway / Luma / Pika / Kling: dedicated "negative prompt" field — use it, don't cram negatives into the main prompt.
  • Sora: no separate field — append at the end: "Avoid: extra limbs, watermark, flicker." Keep it short; Sora weighs the main prompt heavily.
  • APIs (general): pass as the negative_prompt parameter alongside your prompt.

What negative prompts CAN'T fix

  1. Bad composition — rewrite the main prompt with camera language.
  2. Wrong subject — the model misunderstood; simplify and specify (see Lesson 1).
  3. Physics weirdness — partially fixable with "natural movement, realistic physics" in the positive prompt.
✏️ Exercise: Generate any human close-up with no negative prompt, then regenerate with the universal negative prompt above. Compare hands and teeth frame-by-frame — that's the lesson.

Key takeaways

  • Negative prompts are guardrails against anatomy and quality artifacts.
  • Keep one universal list; add artifact-specific terms per project.
  • Use the tool's dedicated field where it exists; append briefly for Sora.
  • Conceptual errors need a better main prompt, not more negatives.

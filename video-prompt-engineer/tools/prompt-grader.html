Improvement tips

    What the grader checks

    • Specificity (25) — word count plus concrete detail words (colors, materials, textures). Vague prompts score low.
    • Camera language (25) — shot and movement vocabulary: dolly, orbit, aerial, macro, tracking…
    • Style (20) — a named visual style so the model doesn't guess the look.
    • Motion (15) — at least one real motion verb. Stills-with-words don't move.
    • Lighting (15) — light direction, time of day, glow and shadow language.

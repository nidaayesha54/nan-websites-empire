Why mix styles?

Single-style prompts produce generic output — the model has rendered "cinematic photorealistic" a million times. Style hybrids force the model into territory with fewer reference frames, which reads as originality: "film noir × claymation" gives you something no one else is posting.

Rules of thumb:

  • 2 styles = coherent and controllable. 3 styles = experimental, expect surprises.
  • Mix one lighting-driven style (noir, cyberpunk) with one texture-driven style (claymation, oil painting) for the best balance.
  • Keep the subject simple — complex subjects + 3 styles = visual mush.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 70% rule in house flipping? +
Pay no more than 70% of the after-repair value minus repair costs. The 30% margin covers your profit, holding costs, selling costs, and the surprises every rehab hides.
What selling costs should flippers budget? +
Typically 6–10% of the sale price: agent commissions (5–6%), title and closing fees, and sometimes buyer concessions. This calculator defaults to 6% — adjust it to match your market.
What ROI should a house flip target? +
Most experienced flippers want at least 10–20% ROI or a $25,000–$30,000 minimum profit per deal. Thin margins are dangerous because one foundation surprise or permit delay can erase them.

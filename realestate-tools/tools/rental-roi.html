Frequently asked questions

How do you calculate rental property ROI? +
Divide annual net operating income by the purchase price for the cap rate, and annual cash flow (after the mortgage) by your cash invested for cash-on-cash return. This calculator does both automatically.
What expenses should I include for a rental? +
Property tax, insurance, maintenance (often budgeted at 1% of the property value per year), vacancy (5–8% of rent), and property management (8–10% of rent). New investors most often underestimate maintenance and vacancy.
What is a good cash-on-cash return? +
Many investors target 8–12% cash-on-cash. It measures what your actual invested dollars earn each year after the mortgage payment — unlike cap rate, it reflects your financing.

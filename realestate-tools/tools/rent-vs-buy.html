Rent vs Buy Calculator

Buying isn't just the mortgage — it's closing costs, selling costs, and the opportunity cost of your down payment. This calculator compares the true net cost of both paths and finds your break-even year.

The short answer Renting is usually cheaper if you'll move within ~5 years. Buying usually wins if you stay 7–10+ years. Your break-even year above is the number that matters — read how the comparison works to understand the assumptions behind it.

Frequently asked questions

How long until buying beats renting? +
Typically 5–10 years. Closing costs (~2%) and selling costs (~6%) are large one-time expenses renters avoid, so buying needs time for appreciation and principal paydown to catch up.
What is the opportunity cost of a down payment? +
Money locked in a down payment could have been invested elsewhere. This calculator assumes a 6% annual return on that money and counts the foregone growth as part of buying's true cost.
Does this calculator include home appreciation? +
Yes — 3% annual home appreciation and 3% annual rent growth. Appreciation reduces buying's net cost, while rising rent increases renting's total cost over time.

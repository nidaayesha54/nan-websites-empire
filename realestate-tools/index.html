Common questions

What is a good cap rate for a rental property? +
Most US investors look for 6–10% depending on the market. Higher cap rates usually mean cheaper properties or higher-risk areas; expensive coastal cities often trade at 3–5%.
What is the 1% rule in real estate? +
Monthly rent should be at least 1% of the purchase price for a property to cash-flow comfortably. It's a quick screen — many good deals fail it in high-price markets and many bad deals pass it.
How much profit should a house flip make? +
Most flippers target at least $25,000–$30,000 per deal or 10–20% ROI after all costs. The 70% rule says: pay no more than 70% of the after-repair value, minus repair costs.
Is it better to rent or buy in 2026? +
It depends mostly on how long you stay. Buying often breaks even after 5–15 years because closing and selling costs are large; renting is often cheaper for short stays. Run your own numbers in the rent vs buy calculator.

The 2,080-Hour Rule of Thumb

A standard full-time year is 40 hours × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours. So $65,000 ÷ 2,080 ≈ $31.25/hr. But real life varies: unpaid time off, part-time schedules, and actual hours worked change the number — which is why the converter above lets you set your own hours and weeks.

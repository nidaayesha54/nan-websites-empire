Paycheck Estimator

Where does your gross pay go? Enter your salary, filing status, and pay frequency for an estimated net paycheck — federal income tax plus FICA, clearly labeled as estimates.

⚠️ Educational purposes only — not financial advice. Tax figures are estimates, not tax advice.

What This Estimate Includes (and Doesn't)

  • Included: federal income tax (2025 brackets + standard deduction, as an estimate) and FICA — 6.2% Social Security + 1.45% Medicare.
  • Not included: state/local income tax, pre-tax deductions (401(k), HSA, health premiums), or post-tax deductions (Roth 401(k), garnishments).

Your actual paycheck will differ — use this to sanity-check offers and budgets, not for tax filing. Full explainer: How Paychecks Work: Taxes Explained.

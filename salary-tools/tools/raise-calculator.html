Nominal Raise vs Real Raise

A 4% raise with 3% inflation is really about a 1% raise in purchasing power — the formula is (1 + raise) ÷ (1 + inflation) − 1. A 2% raise with 3% inflation is a pay cut in disguise. That's why the "real raise" line above matters more than the headline number.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert salary to hourly?

Divide annual salary by (hours/week × weeks/year). For a standard 40-hour week, 52 weeks: divide by 2,080. Our converter does both directions instantly.

How is take-home pay calculated?

Gross pay minus federal income tax (brackets + filing status), minus FICA (7.65%), minus state tax. Our paycheck estimator shows the federal estimate per paycheck.

What is a good raise percentage?

Average US raises run 3–4%. Above inflation (~3%) is a real raise; below it, purchasing power shrank. See your real raise after inflation.

Is salary or hourly better?

Salary = predictable income, often better benefits. Hourly = paid for every hour, overtime at 1.5×. Compare properly with our salary vs hourly guide.

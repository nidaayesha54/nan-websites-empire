GTA 6 Salaries: What Every Job in Leonida Pays

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Leonida runs on money. The story gates missions behind cash, garages cost money, and the best gear is never cheap — so every player ends up asking the same question: what does each job actually pay? This guide is the salary table for Vice City. Every figure comes from launch-week reporting by PowerPyx, quoted through EGamers, and every figure is reported, not measured by us — treat the numbers as the best public figures we have, not as a promise.

The jobs are listed from the highest reported pay down to the steady early earners, with the chapter each one unlocks and the catch that comes with it. A salary in GTA 6 is never just a number: it is a number, a time, and a risk, all at once.

The top earners: vaults and supercars

At the top of the table sits the large bank vault job, reported to pay up to $205,000 for roughly five minutes of work. It opens only at the end of Chapter 4, through an optional mission called That's Where The Money Is, and it starts with tailing a security van until it parks at a bank. You loot the vault, the cash drawer and the safe, and you leave with a four-star wanted level. It is the highest salary in the game and the last one most players will reach.

Second is selling supercars to a buyer called Randy, at $30,000 or more per car in perfect condition, opening around the middle of Chapter 3 after a mission called Key Cloner. The condition is the whole job: a damaged supercar pays far less, and advanced trackers alert the police once you slow down. A clean delivery is a month's wages in one trip; a scratched one is a lesson in driving carefully.

The mid-range: banks, jewelry and deliveries

Small banks pay a reported $5,000 to $45,000 each, with fourteen of them across the map, opening in Chapter 3 after a mission called Credit Check. The rule is strict: loot both the cash drawer and the safe, or the job does not count. Jewelry stores pay $10,000 to $25,000 once Yasmani's jewelry jobs open in Chapter 3 — disable the security system, crack the back-room safe, and leave before the police arrive.

Pilot Pete deliveries pay a reported $20,000 per run, seven packages on a timer, opening in Chapter 4 as a Strangers and Freaks activity for Jason only. Payday loan stores pay $2,000 to $25,000 depending on the store, with seven of them, opening in Chapter 3 after Credit Check. Smash 'n' grabs — pawn shops and jewelers, thirty-five of them — pay about $1,000 to $25,000 depending on the shop, with the same safe rule as the banks.

The entry level: stolen cars and fence work

At the bottom of the table, and the start of every player's career, are stolen cars. Selling one to Moses typically pays $1,000 or more per car from Chapter 2, after the Moses mission, with a cooldown of about two minutes between sales — and no trackers accepted. Selling a good car at Pay 'n' Spray pays around $4,000 or more from early Chapter 3, but only cars with standard trackers qualify.

Gang operations — thirty-nine of them — pay in narcotics sold to a fence, and the reporting we have does not source a payout figure for them, so they sit off the table entirely. Do not plan your budget around a number nobody has published. The entry-level work is honest, repeatable and always available, and that is worth more than a rumoured jackpot.

How to read the table

Three things decide whether a salary is good: the payout, the time it takes, and the risk. A $205,000 vault job that takes five minutes and a four-star chase is a better hourly rate than a $20,000 delivery that takes half an hour — but only if you survive the chase with the loot. A $1,000 car sale that takes three minutes and no heat is a better return than a $25,000 jewelry job that ends in a shootout and a hospital bill. And in GTA 6, dying or getting caught costs you the cash on your person, so deposit every payout at an ATM before the next job. The bank balance is the only salary that is truly yours.

FAQ

What is the highest paying job in GTA 6? By reported payout, large bank vaults at up to $205,000, opening at the end of Chapter 4. Next is selling supercars to Randy at $30,000 or more per car.

What pays best early in GTA 6? Stolen cars to Moses at typically $1,000 or more per car from Chapter 2. It is low per job, but it repeats without limit and needs no unlocks beyond the Moses mission.

The bottom line: the best salary in Leonida is the vault job at the end of Chapter 4, but the best salary for the chapter you are in is the one that is already open. Work the table from the bottom up, bank every payout, and let the big numbers come when the story does.

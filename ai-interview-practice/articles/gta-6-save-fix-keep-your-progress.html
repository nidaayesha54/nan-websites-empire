GTA 6 Save Fix: How to Save and Keep Your Progress

November 23, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the most painful story from launch week is not a crash or a bug — it is lost progress. Players are finishing long missions, closing the game, and coming back to find hours gone. The autosave cannot be trusted: it only fires after a mission ends, the prologue never autosaves at all, and crashes have already cost players whole evenings — streamer JasonTheWeen lost around ten hours to a single crash. The fix is simple, free, and already in your hands: fifteen manual save slots. This guide explains how to use them, and how to get your progress back when something goes wrong.

How to save manually

Manual saving is one menu away. Pause the game, go to Game, then Save Game, and pick a slot — you have fifteen of them. The habit that protects you is rotation: use two or three slots and take turns, so a bad save never strands you. Always keep one slot from before any tricky mission, so you have a way back to before the mission started. Save after every mission, save before a big purchase, and save before any mission that looks risky. It takes five seconds, and it is the only safety net the game gives you. Make it automatic: mission ends, save. Job done, save. About to try something dangerous, save first.

Why the autosave cannot be trusted

The autosave has three gaps that catch players. First, it only fires after a mission ends — the prologue and mission checkpoints do not trigger it, so a long opening stretch can pass with no save at all. Second, autosaves only appear in the Load Game menu, never in the Save Game menu, which makes players think the game has not saved when it has — or worse, think it has saved when it has not. Third, a crash wipes everything since the last autosave, and that gap can be hours long. JasonTheWeen lost around ten hours this way, and he is far from the only one. Treat the autosave as a bonus, never as your plan. Your plan is the manual save, made by you, after every mission.

The Lucia softlock and other stuck moments

There is also a genuine bug to know about. An early Lucia mission, after leaving a bank, can softlock — the mission will not let you progress, and there is no fix for it yet. The workaround is your manual save: reload your last save from before the mission and restart the console fully. It is the same habit, solving a different problem, and it is the reason the save-before-everything rule exists. A related trap is the money gate: some missions only open once Jason and Lucia have earned enough money, a detail that comes from Rockstar North's Rob Nelson speaking via Famitsu. If no mission marker appears and your cash is thin, the game is not broken — it is waiting for you to earn. Jewelry store robberies, car sales and treasure dives are the verified routes to the cash you need.

Protect your money while you protect your saves

Saving protects your progress, but it does not protect your pocket. Pocket cash is lost when you die or get arrested, and it does not carry into Chapter 2 — so walking around with a fortune is a risk, not a flex. You can only carry $20,000. The verified ways to protect it are simple: stash spare money under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom, and open the BuckMe bank account once the mission "Buck Me" is available, then deposit your cash at any of the 454 ATMs across Leonida. Banked money is safe. And remember that missions cannot be replayed — once a mission ends, its outcome is permanent, so the money you earned is yours to keep and the money you lost is lost. Save first, bank second, and the no-replay rule works for you instead of against you.

The save routine that works

Put it together and the routine is short. Save after every mission, without exception. Rotate two or three slots so one bad save never strands you. Keep one slot from before any tricky mission, and never overwrite it until the mission is done. Bank your cash at an ATM before a risky job, not after it. Never rely on the autosave — check the Load Game menu if you are unsure what the game actually saved. And if a mission softlocks, do not keep pushing at it: reload your pre-mission save, restart the console fully, and try again from a clean state. Five seconds after every mission is the whole cost of never losing an evening again.

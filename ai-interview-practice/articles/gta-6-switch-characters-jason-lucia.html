GTA 6: How to Switch Between Jason and Lucia

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

GTA 6 has two protagonists — Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos — and their story, about escape rather than empire-building, is told from both sides. Naturally, the first thing many players try is switching between them. And naturally, it does not work at first. If you have been pressing buttons trying to swap characters and nothing happens, you are not doing it wrong: free character switching is locked until a specific point in the story. This guide explains when it unlocks and exactly how to do it.

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, thirteen years after GTA 5. The dual-lead structure is one of the game's most talked-about features — and one of the most misunderstood, because the game never really explains the switching rules.

When Switching Unlocks

Free switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks only after the "Breakfast Run" mission in Chapter 2. Before that point, the story chooses the character for you: you play whoever the current mission needs, and there is no way to swap. That is by design, not a bug. The opening chapters introduce both characters and set up their story, and the game keeps you on a fixed path until the two leads are properly established.

So if you are still in Chapter 1, or in the early part of Chapter 2, and the switch does nothing — that is normal. Play through the story until you complete Breakfast Run. The moment it unlocks, the game opens the character switch for the rest of your playthrough.

How to Switch, Step by Step

Once switching is unlocked, the process is simple. Hold Right on the D-pad to open the Partner Menu. That menu shows both characters — Jason and Lucia — and you choose the one you want to control. On PS5, press R1 to confirm the switch; on Xbox, press RB. The game swaps you to the other character, wherever they are in the world.

The key detail is the hold: a quick tap of Right on the D-pad will not open the menu. Hold it down until the Partner Menu appears, then make your choice. If you tap and nothing happens, you are not holding long enough — that is the most common mistake.

Why the Button Does Nothing Before Chapter 2

It is worth repeating, because this is the question players ask most: before Breakfast Run, the switch is not broken, it is not available. The story is introducing Jason and Lucia in a fixed order, and some early missions are written for one character only. The game gives you no switch prompt, no greyed-out option, no explanation — it simply does not respond. That silence is what makes players think something is wrong, when in fact the game is working as intended.

If you are unsure whether you have reached the unlock point, check your mission list: if you have not yet completed Breakfast Run in Chapter 2, keep playing the story. Side activities and exploration will not unlock switching early.

Greyed-Out Portrait? Your Partner Is Busy

Even after switching unlocks, you will sometimes find one character's portrait greyed out in the Partner Menu. That means your partner is busy — for example, they are in a RydeMe ride, or tied up in an activity you cannot interrupt. The switch is temporarily unavailable for that character, not broken.

The fix is to wait and retry. Finish what you are doing, give the partner a moment to become free, and open the Partner Menu again. If the portrait is still greyed out, try moving to a different activity or location first — sometimes the partner needs to finish what they are doing before the game lets you take control.

Switching Tips

Switch between missions, not in the middle of one. Some missions lock you to a single character for their whole duration, and trying to switch mid-mission will do nothing — or worse, distract you at the wrong moment. The Partner Menu is a between-missions tool, and it works best when you are free-roaming in Vice City and the wider Leonida state.

It is also good practice to save manually before a long session with either character. GTA 6's autosave is unreliable — it only fires after a mission ends, and the prologue never autosaves — so get into the habit: Pause, Game, Save Game, after every mission, rotating two or three of your fifteen manual save slots. A crash should cost you minutes, not hours, whichever character you were playing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I switch characters at any time?

After the Breakfast Run mission in Chapter 2, yes — except during missions that lock you to one character, and except when your partner is busy, which greys out their portrait until they are free.

Why does holding Right on the D-pad do nothing?

Either you have not reached the Breakfast Run unlock yet, or you are in a mission that locks the character. Outside missions, after Chapter 2's Breakfast Run, holding Right opens the Partner Menu.

The Bottom Line

Character switching in GTA 6 unlocks after the Breakfast Run mission in Chapter 2 — not before, and not by any trick. After that, hold Right on the D-pad, open the Partner Menu, and press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox. If a portrait is greyed out, your partner is busy: wait and try again. Play the story to the unlock, and the swap becomes second nature.

# USA Search Demand Research — 2026-10-11

**Purpose:** Evidence-based map of what Americans actually search for, to guide problem-solving website clusters (high traffic + low competition + monetizable).

**Method:** Live web searches (Oct 2026) across SEO industry sources — Google Year in Search 2025, Semrush/Ahrefs volume data, WordStream CPC studies, distribb.io 2026 CPC data, local-search industry reports, pay-per-call benchmarks.

---

## 1. TOP 10 USA SEARCH NEED CATEGORIES (with evidence)

### 1.1 Navigational / Utility (highest raw volume)
- YouTube 185M/mo, Amazon 151M, Facebook 124M, Gmail 55.6M, Weather 55.6M, Wordle 55.6M, Google Translate 45.5M, Walmart 37.2M, ChatGPT 30.4M (Semrush/Ahrefs via twinstrata.com, Sept 2025)
- **Takeaway:** Not targetable by new sites (brand navigational). But "Weather," "Calculator" (24.9M/mo, informational intent), and "Translate" show appetite for **free utility tools**.

### 1.2 "Near Me" Local Services (~800M users/month)
- ~800M users/month search with "near me" queries; ~1.5B monthly searches contain "near me" (Medium/Clientomic, HVAC Marketing Xperts)
- "Open now near me" grew 400%; 46% use voice search daily for local info; 72% of local searchers visit a business within 5 miles
- "Food Near Me" alone: 30.4M/mo (transactional intent)
- "Fast food near me": ~15M/mo baseline, 600K+/day
- **Takeaway:** Massive, urgent, transactional. New sites can't rank in the map pack, but CAN own the **informational layer**: cost guides, "what to ask," comparison tools per trade.

### 1.3 How-To / Problem-Solving (structural growth)
- "How do I..." queries hit an **all-time high** in 2025, +25% YoY (Google Year in Search 2025, via Search Engine Land)
- "Tell me about..." +70% YoY
- **Takeaway:** The single strongest format signal for new sites. Question-format, long-tail how-to content is where Google explicitly says demand is growing fastest.

### 1.4 Personal Finance & Money
- "Calculator" 24.9M/mo (informational); debt consolidation loan $25 CPC × 85K/mo; business line of credit $30 × 18K/mo; student loan refinancing $25 × 5.4K/mo (distribb.io 2026)
- FIRE/retirement calculators: proven community demand (dev.to: 28-calculator collection went viral on "no signup, no tracking" positioning)
- **Takeaway:** Evergreen, high CPC, calculator-format wins. "No signup" is a differentiator users explicitly praise.

### 1.5 Insurance (the #1 CPC category)
- Insurance = 24% of Google's AdWords revenue; avg CPC $54.91 (WordStream)
- "car insurance quotes" 1.95M/mo at $45 CPC; "cheap car insurance" 337K/mo at $40; "compare car insurance rates" 14K/mo at $55; "cheap auto insurance in florida" $60 × 3.2K/mo (distribb.io 2026)
- Pet insurance: avg $47/mo; dog lifetime cost ~$34,550; affiliates pay $50–100+/lead (MoneyGeek, thepricer.org, prior research)
- **Takeaway:** Highest monetization per visitor on the internet. Comparison/quote-guide angles are the accessible entry (can't sell insurance, CAN educate + refer).

### 1.6 Legal (local + high value)
- "Car accident lawyer" up to $1,490 CPC (long-tail geo); "personal injury attorney" $130; "divorce attorney" $47+ (seosandwitch, WordStream)
- One "near me" legal case worth thousands; top-3 map pack takes the calls
- **Takeaway:** New sites can't compete on head terms, but "what to do after a car accident in [state]" / settlement calculators / statute-of-limitations guides are weak-competition informational plays with legal-lead affiliate upside.

### 1.7 Home Services (urgency = money)
- Emergency plumber 122K/mo at $20 CPC; emergency garage door repair 88K/mo at $25; "same day plumber" 23K/mo at $25 (distribb.io)
- Pay-per-call payouts: water damage $201, fire damage $193, roofing $130/lead, plumbing $38.50 median (up to $221.95 in top ZIPs), HVAC $36.75 (Lead Smart 2026 benchmarks)
- **Takeaway:** Cost-estimator tools + "what should this cost" guides per trade. Homeowners search price BEFORE calling — own that moment.

### 1.8 Health (huge volume, high competition at head)
- "Symptoms of anxiety" 60.5K/mo, "symptoms of diabetes" 49.5K, "symptoms of a heart attack" 22.2K — all HIGH competition (grindsuccess.com)
- Symptom keywords are dominated by Mayo/WebMD; BUT "how to" treatment long-tails and cost/transparency angles ("how much does an MRI cost without insurance") are thinner
- **Takeaway:** Avoid head symptom terms. Play: health COST calculators, insurance explainer tools, "is it worth it" decision aids.

### 1.9 Education & Career
- "Online colleges" $99.20 CPC; "accredited online college courses" $80.31; "online business degree" $25 × 5.2K/mo; "psychology degree online" $30 × 2.7K/mo (WordStream, distribb.io)
- Salary/career: "salary calculator" ~368K/mo (prior research); profession/state long-tails open
- **Takeaway:** Degree-program keywords print money for affiliates; salary transparency tools have huge volume + thin competition on long-tail.

### 1.10 Pets (spending boom, weak tool competition)
- Dog lifetime cost ~$34,550; annual ~$3,455 (thepricer.org 2025 model); pet insurance $42–47/mo dogs
- **Takeaway:** "How much does a [breed] cost" calculators, pet insurance comparators — emotional + financial, thin tool competition.

## 2. "NEAR ME" & PROBLEM SEARCHES — WHAT DOMINATES

| Pattern | Example | Volume signal | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| "near me" + trade | emergency plumber near me | 122K/mo | Transactional, urgent |
| "cost" + service | how much does a wedding cost | evergreen high | Commercial investigation |
| "how do I" | how do I... (all-time high) | +25% YoY | Problem-solving |
| "calculator" | calculator (generic) | 24.9M/mo | Tool-seeking |
| "is X worth it" | is pet insurance worth it | growing | Decision aid |
| "what happens if" | what happens if interest rates rise | story-driven | Scenario planning |

**Core insight:** Americans search in **problem language**, not solution language. They type the pain ("emergency plumber"), the price question ("how much does..."), or the confusion ("how do I..."). Sites that mirror problem language in H1s and build the tool that resolves it win.

## 3. TWENTY UNDERSERVED SEARCH OPPORTUNITIES

Criteria: real volume evidence + structural reason competition is weak (forums ranking, outdated content, no interactive tool, long-tail fragmentation).

### MONEY CLUSTER (highest CPC)
1. **"debt consolidation loan calculator"** — $25 CPC × 85K/mo on head term; long-tails ("debt consolidation loan for $30k credit card debt") have forum/Quora results in top 10. **Site:** payoff-scenario calculator + lender comparison guide.
2. **"how much does a DUI cost"** — legal cost shock content; thin, outdated articles dominate. **Site:** state-by-state DUI cost estimator ($10K–25K all-in).
3. **"car accident settlement calculator"** — $100+ CPC neighborhood; current tools are lead-gen forms, not real calculators. **Site:** honest settlement-range estimator + "what affects your payout" guide.
4. **"how much does probate cost"** — estate/probate cost questions answered by thin law-firm blogs. **Site:** state-by-state probate cost + timeline estimator.
5. **"is whole life insurance worth it"** — $40+ CPC insurance neighborhood; Reddit threads rank top-5. **Site:** whole-vs-term interactive comparison calculator.

### HOME CLUSTER (urgency + lead value)
6. **"how much does water damage restoration cost"** — $201/lead payout category; cost guides are thin contractor blogs. **Site:** room-by-room restoration cost estimator.
7. **"emergency garage door repair cost"** — 88K/mo at $25 CPC; almost no dedicated cost tools. **Site:** repair-vs-replace cost calculator.
8. **"how much does it cost to redo a bathroom"** — evergreen; top results are 2021–2023 articles with stale prices. **Site:** 2026 bathroom remodel cost estimator by finish level.
9. **"mold removal cost per square foot"** — $65.50/lead payout; thin content. **Site:** mold remediation cost estimator + "when to call a pro" decision tool.
10. **"fence installation cost calculator"** — material × linear-feet math; big-box articles rank but no good interactive tool. **Site:** fence cost calculator (wood/vinyl/chain-link).

### LIFE-EVENT CLUSTER (emotional + expensive)
11. **"how much does IVF cost"** — $15K–30K decision; clinic pages dominate, thin independent cost breakdowns. **Site:** IVF cost calculator by state + insurance-coverage checker guide.
12. **"average cost of a funeral"** — $8K–12K; outdated articles, funeral-home SEO is weak. **Site:** funeral cost planner + price-transparency guide (FTC Funeral Rule angle).
13. **"how much does daycare cost per month"** — state variance is huge; parents search city-specific. **Site:** daycare cost calculator by state/city + nanny-vs-daycare comparator.
14. **"cost of raising a child calculator"** — USDA stopped updating; existing calculators are stale. **Site:** modern child-cost calculator (2026 prices, childcare-inflation adjusted).

### HEALTH-COST CLUSTER (transparency gap)
15. **"how much does an MRI cost without insurance"** — price-transparency searches; hospital pages rank but hide numbers. **Site:** procedure cost lookup by CPT code + cash-price finder guide.
16. **"how much does therapy cost without insurance"** — 60.5K/mo anxiety-symptom adjacent; Open Path/sliding-scale info is scattered. **Site:** therapy cost calculator + low-cost options finder guide.

### CAREER CLUSTER (volume monster)
17. **"salary calculator [profession] [state]"** — 368K/mo head; long-tail state/profession combos have weak competition. **Site:** 5-site cluster (tech salaries, healthcare salaries, teacher salaries, trade salaries, remote salaries).
18. **"how much does a CDL cost"** — trucking career searches; school sites dominate with sales copy. **Site:** independent CDL training cost + ROI calculator.
19. **"electrician apprenticeship pay"** — trade-career content is thin vs. college content. **Site:** trade-career salary + apprenticeship tracker guides.

### PET CLUSTER (spending boom)
20. **"how much does a golden retriever cost per year"** — breed-specific cost questions; forum answers rank. **Site:** breed cost calculator (purchase + annual + lifetime) for top 20 breeds.

## 4. SEASONAL VS EVERGREEN

### Evergreen (build the foundation here)
- Personal finance calculators, insurance explainers, salary tools, health-cost lookups, "how much does X cost" estimators, legal process guides, pet costs
- These compound: a 2026 cost calculator updated yearly on an evergreen URL keeps its rankings (per Search Engine Land seasonality guidance)

### Seasonal spikes (plan content calendar, don't build whole sites)
- **Tax season (Jan–Apr):** "when will I get my refund," IRA contributions, 1099 questions
- **Summer:** moving costs ("how much do movers cost"), AC repair, wedding peak
- **Fall:** open enrollment health insurance (Nov–Dec = $15 CPC "health insurance" spike), Medicare
- **Winter:** heating costs, holiday budgets, New Year fitness (high volume, high competition — avoid as primary)
- **Storm season:** roofers ("storm and hail searches surge overnight" — designmemarketing)

### Strategy
- 80% evergreen tool sites (the clusters already being built)
- 20% seasonal content layered onto existing sites via the daily article system (e.g., open-enrollment guides on insurance-adjacent sites each November)

## 6. TOP 5 MOST ACTIONABLE FINDINGS

1. **"How do I..." is at an all-time high (+25% YoY) — problem-language H1s are the highest-leverage SEO move.** Every new site should lead with the question users actually type, not the category label. (Source: Google Year in Search 2025)

2. **"No signup" is a proven viral differentiator for calculators.** A 28-calculator collection went viral specifically on "no email required before showing results." All our tools already do this — make it a headline feature on every homepage. (Source: dev.to community response)

3. **Home-service COST content is the gap: homeowners search price before calling, and current cost guides are thin contractor blogs.** Water damage ($201/lead), garage door (88K/mo, no dedicated tool), bathroom remodel (stale 2021 prices) — a 5-site "home repair cost" cluster mirrors the ROI cluster strategy exactly.

4. **Insurance + legal long-tails pay $40–130/click but head terms are unwinnable — the play is decision-aid calculators** (settlement estimator, whole-vs-term comparator, DUI cost estimator), not quote forms. Forums ranking top-5 on "is whole life insurance worth it" proves content weakness.

5. **Daycare/child-cost and funeral-cost calculators serve $8K–30K decisions with near-zero modern tools.** USDA stopped updating child-cost data; funeral price transparency is a federal-rule angle (FTC Funeral Rule). These are "high emotion + high money + low competition" — the exact profile that converts to affiliate/lead revenue fastest.

AR Days Benchmarks by Practice Size (2026)

"Good" AR days depend on your practice size and specialty. Here's how the benchmarks generally break down:

Benchmark Ranges

  • Top performers (any size): under 35 AR days
  • Solo / small practices (1–5 providers): 35–45 days is strong; small teams feel every slow payer
  • Mid-size groups (6–25 providers): 35–50 days is typical
  • Large groups (25+ providers): 30–45 days, thanks to dedicated billing staff

Specialty Matters

Surgical specialties with prior-auth-heavy workflows often run 5–10 days higher than primary care. Behavioral health with high patient-responsibility balances can run higher too. Compare yourself against your own trend first, then against peers.

How to Move the Number

  1. Calculate your baseline with our AR days calculator.
  2. Attack the 90+ day bucket — keep it under 15–20% of total AR.
  3. Prevent new denials with a strict eligibility checklist at check-in.
  4. Track and appeal denials fast with a denial tracker.

Most practices that commit to these four steps see AR days drop 10–15 days within two quarters.

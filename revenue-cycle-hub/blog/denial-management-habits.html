Denial Management: 5 Habits of High-Performing Billing Teams

Some practices run at a 3% denial rate while others struggle at 12%. The difference isn't luck — it's habits. Here are the five that matter most.

1. They log every denial the same day

Top teams treat denials like a triage unit: same-day logging, same-week appeals. Use a denial tracker so nothing slips through.

2. They categorize before they appeal

Appealing without categorization is whack-a-mole. Tag each denial by reason — eligibility, auth, coding, timely filing — and the pattern reveals the fix.

3. They fix the front end, not just the claim

Reworking a denied claim costs 3–4× more than submitting it clean the first time. Every repeat denial reason becomes a front-desk checklist item — start with our eligibility checklist.

4. They appeal the big dollars first

Not all denials deserve equal effort. Sort open denials by dollar value and work top-down — the ROI on appeal time is highest there.

5. They review denial trends monthly

A 15-minute monthly review of denial reasons, overturn rates, and AR days catches drift before it becomes a crisis.

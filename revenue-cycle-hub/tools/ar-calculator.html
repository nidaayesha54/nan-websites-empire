AR Days Calculator

AR days = total accounts receivable ÷ average daily charges. It tells you how many days of revenue are stuck waiting to be collected.

How to Lower AR Days

  • Verify eligibility before every visit — front-end errors are the #1 cause of denials.
  • Submit claims within 24–48 hours of the encounter.
  • Work denials within 48 hours — the longer they sit, the less likely they get paid.
  • Follow up on unpaid claims at 15, 30, 45, and 60 days.
  • Track your 90+ day bucket — keep it under 15–20% of total AR.

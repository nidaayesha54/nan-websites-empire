8 Medical Billing KPIs Every Practice Should Track

If you only track collections, you're flying blind. These eight key performance indicators reveal exactly where revenue leaks out of your practice.

1. AR Days — Target: Under 40

Total AR ÷ average daily charges. The single best snapshot of collection health. Calculate yours here.

2. Denial Rate — Target: Under 5%

Denied claims ÷ total claims submitted. Above 10% means front-end processes need urgent repair.

3. Clean Claim Rate — Target: 95%+

Claims accepted on first pass without edits. The cheapest claim is the one you never have to touch twice.

4. Net Collection Rate — Target: 95%+

Payments collected ÷ allowable charges (after contractual adjustments). Below 90% means you're leaving money behind.

5. Days in AR Over 90 — Target: Under 15–20%

The share of AR older than 90 days. Old AR rarely pays — work this bucket aggressively or write it off strategically.

6. Denial Overturn Rate — Target: 70%+

Appealed denials that get paid. A low overturn rate means your appeals need better documentation, not more volume.

7. Cost to Collect — Target: Under 3%

Billing department cost ÷ collections. Rising costs with flat collections signal inefficiency.

8. Patient Collection Rate — Target: 90%+ of patient responsibility

With high-deductible plans, patient balances are a growing share of revenue. Time-of-service collection is the biggest lever.

Putting It Together

Review these KPIs monthly as a dashboard. When one moves the wrong way, drill into the tools: track denials by reason, verify eligibility at the front desk, and watch AR days trend down.

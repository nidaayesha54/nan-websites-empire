Frequently Asked Questions

What is revenue cycle management?
Revenue cycle management (RCM) is the end-to-end process of tracking patient care revenue from appointment scheduling and eligibility verification through claim submission, denial management, and final payment collection.
What is a good AR days benchmark for a medical practice?
Most well-run practices target under 40 AR days. 40–50 days is average, and over 50 days signals collection problems that need attention.
What is the average claim denial rate?
Industry studies put the average initial claim denial rate around 5–10%. Denial rates above 10% usually point to front-end process problems like eligibility or coding errors.

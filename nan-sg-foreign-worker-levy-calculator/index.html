How the Foreign Worker Levy Works

The foreign worker levy (FWL) is a monthly pricing mechanism that regulates the number of foreign workers in Singapore. As an employer you must pay it for every S Pass and Work Permit holder you employ — it begins on the day the pass is issued and ends only when the pass is cancelled or expires.

S Pass — the simple case

Since 1 September 2025, the S Pass levy is a single flat rate of S$650 a month across every sector and every tier (daily rate S$21.37 for part-months).

How quota tiers are determined

For services and manufacturing, the tier depends on your firm's Dependency Ratio Ceiling (DRC) utilisation — the share of your total workforce on Work Permits. The heavier your reliance on foreign labour, the higher the tier and the rate. MOM computes your live quota from CPF payroll data; check Work Permit Online for your real-time headroom before hiring.

Worked example: A services company employing 5 basic-skilled (R2) Work Permit holders at Tier 2 pays 5 × S$600 = S$3,000/month, or S$36,000/year. Upgrading those workers to higher-skilled (R1) status drops the bill to 5 × S$400 = S$2,000/month.

FAQs

Who pays the foreign worker levy — the employer or the worker?

The employer, entirely. The levy cannot be deducted from the worker's salary. Late payment triggers a penalty of S$20 or 2% per month of the outstanding levy, whichever is higher, capped at 30% — and MOM may suspend or cancel your work passes.

Do Employment Pass holders attract the levy?

No. Employment Pass holders sit outside the levy and quota system entirely — no levy is payable for them.

How is the daily rate calculated for part-months?

MOM's formula: (monthly levy rate × 12) ÷ 365, rounded up to the nearest cent. For S Pass: (650 × 12) ÷ 365 = S$21.37.

What is the difference between R1 and R2?

R1 (higher-skilled) workers hold recognised academic qualifications, skills certificates or sufficient relevant work experience in Singapore; everyone else is R2 (basic-skilled). R1 rates are always lower — upgrading skills is the cheapest way to cut the levy.

Is there a quota on how many foreign workers I can hire?

Yes — the Dependency Ratio Ceiling (DRC): services 35%, manufacturing 60%, marine shipyard 75%, construction and process 83.3%. S Pass has sub-quotas of 10% (services) and 15% (other sectors). Use MOM's official quota calculator for your exact headroom.

Does the FDW concessionary rate apply to businesses?

No — the S$60 concessionary FDW levy is for households with a Singapore-citizen child under 16, an elderly person aged 67+, or a certified person with disabilities. It applies to one helper per eligible person, capped at two per household.

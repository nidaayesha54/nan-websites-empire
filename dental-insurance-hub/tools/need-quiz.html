Do I Need Dental Insurance?

Answer 6 quick questions — get an honest recommendation in under a minute.

1. How old are you?



2. When was your last dental checkup?



3. Have you needed major dental work (crowns, root canals, extractions) in the last 3 years?


4. Do any of these apply: smoke, diabetes, gum disease, or grind your teeth?



5. Does your employer offer dental benefits?



6. If a surprise $1,200 dental bill arrived tomorrow, could you pay it comfortably?


Estimates only, not financial advice. This quiz gives a general recommendation — your dentist and plan documents have the final word.

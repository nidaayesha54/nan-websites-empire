GTA 6 Diving: Scuba Gear, Treasure and Sharks

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

The best money in GTA 6 might be at the bottom of the sea. Leonida's waters hide 33 shipwrecks, and each one holds chests, gold doubloons and heirlooms — if you have the gear to reach them and the nerve to dive with the sharks. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and diving is one of its most complete side systems: a story mission that teaches you how, a captain who pays for what you find, and a whole underwater world to explore after the credits. This guide covers how to dive, how to get scuba gear, what to find, and what pays.

It matters because diving is the easiest money in the game that nobody talks about. The wrecks pay around $1,400 each with almost no risk, the gear comes free from a story mission, and the underwater world is one of the most beautiful parts of Leonida — coral, fish, eels, sea turtles, and sharks. The players who never dive miss a third of the map.

How to Dive: The Controls

Swimming is simple, and diving is one button more. On PS5, hold R1 while you are swimming to dive underwater, and press Cross to come back up. Underwater, click L3 to swim faster and hold L2 to strafe or aim. On Xbox, the same actions map to the equivalent buttons — hold the right trigger to dive, and the left trigger to aim.

For real dives you will want scuba gear, and that comes from Kenton — a treasure hunter with a shop called Island Dreams in the Keys. Without the gear, your dives are short and shallow. With it, the whole sea floor opens up, and the metal detector and the harpoon gun come with it.

Island Dreams: The Mission That Teaches You

Scuba gear first comes your way through Kenton's first job, the Island Dreams mission — mission 8 in Chapter 1, mandatory and required for 100% completion. You meet Kenton Rees at his Island Dreams shop by the beach in Key Lento, in Mariana County, and the job starts with taking a bachelor party on a dive to a shipwreck as cover while you search for valuables. You drive Kenton's boat to the spot, change into scuba gear at the rear of the boat, escort the bachelor party through a trench, and search for treasure near the shipwreck with the metal detector.

There are three treasures to find, all close to the wreck: the first sits straight ahead of the shipwreck on the ground below, the second to the right of the first, and the third nearby. Equip the metal detector from the bottom-right slot of the weapon wheel and sweep the sea floor — the detector's light shifts from red to green as you close in, and when it beeps green constantly you are on top of a treasure, marked by a golden chest icon. Dig it up with Triangle when the prompt appears. The mission pays $400 and takes about 10 to 15 minutes — and it is one of the easier places to pick up animal studies, since the creatures you pass in the trench count toward the 100-animal requirement.

The Shark — and the Choice

The mission's ending is the part everyone remembers. As you head back toward the boat, a shark charges the bachelor party, and Dobbs — one of the bachelors — gets trapped underneath a metal grate in the shipwreck. You have a choice: pull the Arras Bullhead speargun from the left side of the weapon wheel, aim with L2 and shoot the shark twice to scare it off and save Dobbs — or let the shark attack and eat him. He dies, but it does not affect your reward or the outcome of the mission, and you still get paid. Your Criminal Profile shifts slightly depending on the choice, but the mission completes either way.

The shark is not a one-off. The sea is full of them — Rockstar names several shark types, and a great white is visible under a marina in Trailer 2 — and the Arras Bullhead is your only defence against them. It does not have a lot of ammo, but it packs a punch, and it is the tool that makes the difference between a dive and a disaster. Fresh water has its own dangers too: Rockstar's screenshots of the Grassrivers wetlands show airboats surrounded by alligators, and the swamp hides deadlier predators than the sea.

Thirty-Three Wrecks: Kenton's Treasures

After Island Dreams, the diving becomes a system of its own. Kenton's Treasures is the activity that tracks it: 33 underwater discoveries in total, mostly wrecks, tracked in the game under Progress, Collections. Kenton gives you one wreck at a time — dive down, sweep the metal detector until it flashes green, dig up the chests, gold doubloons and heirlooms, bring them all to Kenton for cash, and he sends you to the next one. The wrecks named so far are all in southern Leonida, in GameSpot's order: Nuestra Señora del Ocho, Nuestra Señora de la Noche, Nuestra Señora de la Etica Laxa, and Urca de Santa Sierra among them.

The Leonida Keys are the place to start. Rockstar describes the island chain as sitting "right on the doorstep of some of the most beautiful and dangerous waters in all of America", and its own site shows two scuba divers swimming underwater in the Leonida Keys surrounded by aquatic life including coral, fish, an eel, and a sea turtle. Start from the marinas on Key Lento, work south through the chain, and let Kenton guide you from wreck to wreck. Each one pays, each one counts toward 100%, and each one is a part of Leonida that most players never see.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do you dive underwater in GTA 6?

On PS5, hold R1 while swimming to dive and press Cross to come back up. Click L3 to swim faster and hold L2 to strafe or aim. Scuba gear comes from Kenton's Island Dreams mission in Chapter 1.

How do you find treasure underwater?

Equip the metal detector, aim it with L2 until its light turns green, then press Triangle at the prompt. Kenton buys the gold doubloons, chests and heirlooms you find — around $1,400 per wreck.

Are there sharks in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar names several shark types, and a great white appears in Trailer 2. The Arras Bullhead harpoon gun is your defence — and in the Island Dreams mission, you choose whether to save Dobbs from one or let it eat him.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 diving starts with Kenton's Island Dreams mission and ends with 33 shipwrecks at the bottom of the sea: scuba gear, a metal detector, a harpoon gun, and a shark waiting at the end of the first dive. The wrecks pay around $1,400 each, they count toward 100%, and they are the most beautiful part of Leonida most players never see. Get the gear, learn the controls, and dive.

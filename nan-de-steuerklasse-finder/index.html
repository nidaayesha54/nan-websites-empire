Expats, start here: when you register your address (Anmeldung) and get your tax ID, single newcomers are put in class I automatically. Married couples arriving together get IV/IV by default. You do not get class II automatically — apply at the Finanzamt or via ELSTER.

How to Change Your Tax Class

  • Married couples can switch between III/V and IV/IV once per calendar year (usually until 30 November, effective the next year), via ELSTER or your local Finanzamt.
  • Single parents apply for class II with the "Application for income tax reduction" form (main form + children's annex) at elster.de.
  • Changes after major life events (marriage, separation, job loss, parental leave) can also be requested during the year.
  • Your class is visible on your payslip and your annual Lohnsteuer certificate — and stored electronically as ELStAM.

Which tax class am I in as a single expat in Germany?

Almost certainly tax class I — it is assigned automatically when you register your address and receive your tax ID.

We just got married — which class do we get?

The tax office automatically assigns class IV to both partners. If one of you earns much more, consider switching to III/V once a year.

When is the III/V combination worth it?

When one partner earns roughly 60% or more of the household income — the higher earner takes III, the other V, improving monthly cash flow.

I have a second job — which tax class?

Your second job is always class VI (no allowances, highest withholding). Your main job keeps your normal class (I–V).

Does my tax class change my final tax bill?

No. The class only changes how much is withheld from each monthly payslip. The exact annual tax is settled in your income tax return — overpaid tax comes back as a refund.

My spouse lives abroad — which class?

Usually class I (taxed as single). If your spouse is an EU citizen, joint assessment may be possible on application.

Can I be in class II as a single parent?

Yes — if your child is registered at your address and you raise them alone, apply for class II to get the €4,260 relief in your monthly payroll. It is not automatic.

Is this finder legally binding?

No — it is an indicative guide. Your tax office (Finanzamt) decides your ELStAM entry; confirm your class on your payslip.

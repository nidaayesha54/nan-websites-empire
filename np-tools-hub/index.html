Quick Answers

What is the average nurse practitioner salary in 2026?

About $128,500 nationally, ranging from ~$115,000 in lower-paying states to over $160,000 in California. Specialty is the biggest lever — PMHNPs average the highest. Try the NP salary calculator for your exact situation.

Is a DNP worth it compared to an MSN?

Both qualify you for licensure. The MSN is shorter and cheaper; the DNP adds 1–2 years of leadership/research training. Check the numbers on our ROI calculator before you commit.

Which NP specialty pays the most?

PMHNP (~$145,000+), neonatal NP, and adult-gerontology acute care NP. See the full breakdown in our NP salary by specialty guide.

How long does it take to become a nurse practitioner?

2–3 years for an MSN (with a BSN), 3–4 for a DNP. Read the step-by-step 2026 roadmap.

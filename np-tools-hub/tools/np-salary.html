FAQ

How accurate is this NP salary calculator?

It is an estimate based on BLS occupational wage data and published specialty salary surveys. Real offers vary by employer, setting, and negotiation — use it for planning, not as a quote.

Do acute care NPs make more than family NPs?

Generally yes — acute care and emergency NPs earn about 5–8% more than FNPs on average. Read FNP vs ACNP for the full comparison.

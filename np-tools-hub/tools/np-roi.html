NP School ROI Calculator

NP school is a five- or six-figure investment. This calculator adds up your true cost — tuition plus the RN earnings you give up while studying — and shows how fast the NP salary bump pays it back.

What counts as "cost"?

  • Tuition and fees — the obvious part, averaging $60k for an MSN and $140k+ for a DNP.
  • Forgone earnings — the hidden cost. Two years of part-time RN work instead of full-time can mean $85k+ in missed income.
  • Interest — federal grad loans currently accrue interest from day one; this calculator adds a 6% APR estimate.

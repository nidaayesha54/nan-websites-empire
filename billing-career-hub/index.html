❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How much do medical billers make? +
The BLS reports a median of about $50,250 for medical records specialists. Certified billers with experience in high-cost states commonly earn $60,000–$75,000.
Do I need certification to become a medical biller? +
Not legally required, but most employers prefer or require it. Certified billers earn roughly 6–18% more and get hired faster.
Can medical billers work remotely? +
Yes — billing is one of the most remote-friendly healthcare careers. Many RCM firms and payers hire fully remote billers.
How long does training take? +
Most programs run 4–12 months. With focused study, many people are job-ready within a year.

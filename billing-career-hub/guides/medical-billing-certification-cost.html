Don't forget the ongoing costs

  • Membership: AAPC membership ~$199/yr (required for exam discounts and CEU tracking)
  • CEUs: most credentials require continuing education every 2 years — budget $100–$300/yr
  • Retakes: first-attempt pass rates hover around 50–70% for CPC; a retake costs nearly full price

Is it worth it? The math

Certified billers earn roughly 6–18% more. On a $50,000 base, a 10% premium is $5,000/year — a $2,500 total certification investment pays for itself in about 6 months. Run your own numbers with the training ROI comparator.

Ways to cut costs

  • Employer tuition reimbursement (ask — many RCM firms pay for CPC/CPB)
  • Workforce grants and community college programs (often under $1,500 all-in)
  • Self-study with official study guides instead of full courses (works best if you already have experience)
Fees change — verify current exam prices on the official AAPC, AHIMA, NHA, and AMBA websites before budgeting.

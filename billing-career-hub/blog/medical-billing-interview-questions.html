10 Medical Billing Interview Questions (With Answers)

1. "Walk me through the revenue cycle."

Hit every stage: scheduling, eligibility verification, coding, charge entry, claim submission, payment posting, denial management, AR follow-up, patient collections. End with: "…and the goal at every step is clean claims and fast payment."

2. "How do you handle a denied claim?"

"First I read the remit/EOB to identify the denial reason and CARC/RARC codes. Then I check whether it's correctable — eligibility, coding, authorization — fix the root cause, and resubmit or appeal within the payer's timely filing limit. Then I log the pattern so we can prevent repeats."

3. "What's the difference between a rejection and a denial?"

"A rejection never reaches the payer's adjudication system — usually a front-end error like an invalid NPI. A denial was processed and the payer refused payment. Rejections get fixed and resubmitted; denials get worked or appealed."

4. "How do you prioritize AR?"

"Highest dollar and oldest first, but I also segment by payer — some payers have short appeal windows, so those jump the queue. I track everything in the AR aging buckets: 0–30, 31–60, 61–90, 90+."

5. "Tell me about a difficult payer situation."

Use STAR format. Pick a real example: a pattern denial, a downcoding trend, or an authorization dispute. Show you documented, escalated, and fixed the workflow — not just the claim.

6. "How do you stay current with payer rules?"

"Payer bulletins, CMS transmittals, AAPC forums, and my CEUs. I also keep a shared 'payer quirks' doc for the team."

7. "What clearinghouse / PM software have you used?"

Name them honestly: Availity, Waystar, Change Healthcare, Epic Resolute, Athena, eCW. If you're new, say: "I trained on [X] in my program and I'm a fast learner on new systems — the workflows transfer."

8. "How do you handle PHI and HIPAA?"

"Minimum necessary standard, no PHI over personal devices or email, lock screens, verify identity before discussing accounts. In my last role/training, we…"

9. "What's your clean claim rate?"

If experienced: give the number (95%+ is strong). If new: "In my externship I maintained [X]% first-pass acceptance, and I know the industry benchmark is 95%+."

10. "Why medical billing?"

Connect genuinely: problem-solving, detail work, helping providers get paid so they can care for patients. Avoid "I just need a job."

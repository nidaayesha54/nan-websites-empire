How to Get a Remote Medical Billing Job in 2026

Billing is one of the most remote-friendly healthcare careers — but remote postings get 5–10× the applicants. Here's how to win one.

Where remote billing jobs actually hide

  • RCM companies: R1 RCM, Ensemble Health Partners, Conifer Health — they hire remote AR specialists in bulk
  • Payers: UnitedHealth/Optum, Elevance, Humana regularly post remote billing-adjacent roles
  • Staffing firms: Robert Half, Aston Carter, and TEKsystems place remote billers on contract-to-hire
  • Job boards: filter "remote" on Indeed/LinkedIn, but also check AAPC's job board — less competition

What employers require for remote roles

  • Usually 1–2 years of experience (remote entry-level exists but is competitive)
  • A certification — CPB or CPC signals you can work unsupervised
  • Documented productivity: claims/day, clean claim rate, AR days reduced
  • Home office: reliable broadband (25+ Mbps), quiet workspace, HIPAA-compliant setup

How to stand out

  • Quantify everything on your resume: "Worked 400-claim AR queue, reduced 90+ day AR by 18%"
  • Get the certification before applying — it's the #1 remote filter
  • Learn one major payer deeply (e.g., Medicare rules) — specialists beat generalists
  • Apply within 48 hours of posting; remote listings close fast

Watch out for scams

Real employers never ask you to pay for equipment, cash checks, or buy software. If a "remote billing job" wants money upfront, it's a scam.

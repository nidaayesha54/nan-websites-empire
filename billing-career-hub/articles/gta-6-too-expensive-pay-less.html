GTA 6 Too Expensive? 5 Smart Ways to Pay Less

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition — and for a lot of players, that is real money. The game launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, thirteen years after GTA 5, and IGN's review calls it a masterpiece. But a masterpiece at eighty dollars is still eighty dollars, and the search for a cheaper way is exactly what the scammers are counting on: every "free GTA 6 download" and "GTA 6 APK" on the internet is a trap, and the files inside them steal passwords. The good news is that there are five legitimate ways to pay less — none of them a scam, none of them a shortcut that costs you your accounts. This guide lists all five, in order of how much they actually save.

1. Microsoft Rewards: Pay With Points

The most reliable way to cut the price to zero is Microsoft Rewards, Microsoft's own free loyalty program. You earn points for genuine Bing searches, the Daily Set of quizzes and activities on the Rewards dashboard, and Xbox gameplay quests — then redeem them for an Xbox or Microsoft Store gift card, which adds money to your Microsoft account for eligible purchases, including games. Roughly 5,000 points is worth about $5 in rewards, though the exact point prices vary by region and offer, so check the Redeem page on your own account before planning around a number.

The honest caveat is time: earning enough for an $80 game takes weeks of steady activity, not an afternoon. Points also expire if you do not earn or redeem anything for 12 months, and gift-card redemptions may arrive as promotional balance with a window to use them. But the points cost nothing, the program is run by Microsoft, and the gift card buys the real game from the real store. If you already search with Bing and play on Xbox, start the routine now — even a $10 or $25 gift card is $10 or $25 you do not pay.

2. Win a Code: The Kick and Twitch Giveaways

The second way to pay nothing is to win a copy. Kick is giving away GTA VI Ultimate Edition codes through its "Sunset Reward Case": watch any Kick stream for three hours a day to unlock one case a day, open it, and you might win. The campaign runs from November 12 to December 7, 2026, with 10,000 Ultimate Edition codes reported from Kick's own terms, for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. No purchase or subscription is required, Kick states the campaign is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive, and codes are not issued before November 19.

Twitch is running the other giveaway: surprise daily drops of GTA VI Ultimate Edition codes from launch on November 19 through November 30, announced by Twitch CEO Dan Clancy at TwitchCon San Diego on November 13. Twitch has not said how many codes there are or exactly how winners are picked, so the honest description is that the drops are real, the details are thin, and winning is not guaranteed. Both giveaways share one rule: enter only on kick.com, the Kick app, or twitch.tv. Any other site, Discord DM, or YouTube comment offering a code is not part of either promotion — and any giveaway asking for your password, a payment, or a "verification fee" is a scam.

3. Cashback on the Purchase

If you already have a cashback credit card, use it for the purchase and get a percentage of the price back. That is the whole method: buy the game the normal way, from the PlayStation Store or the Xbox Store, and the card pays back a small share of what you spent. It will not make the game free — cashback is a few percent, not a discount — but on an $80 purchase it is real money back in your account, and it stacks with everything else on this list.

The warning matters more than the method: do not open a new credit card just to buy a game. A card opened for one purchase, carried at interest, costs far more than the cashback it pays. This route only makes sense if you already have a cashback card, you already pay it off in full every month, and you were going to buy the game anyway. If any of those is not true, skip this one — the other four ways on this list cost nothing.

4. Trade In Games You No Longer Play

The games sitting on your shelf are store credit waiting to happen. Retailers that take used games will buy them back — or take them in trade — and the credit goes straight toward GTA 6. The value depends on the game: recent, popular titles fetch more, older ones fetch less, and the trade-in value is always lower than the price you paid. Check the trade value before you go, and bring the games with their cases if you have them — complete copies are worth more than loose discs.

5. Wait for a Sale — or a Subscription

The cheapest way to pay less is to wait. GTA 6 is not on Xbox Game Pass or PlayStation Plus at launch, and nothing official says it will join either service — but the history is on the side of patience: GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 both joined Game Pass years after launch, and big games go on sale in seasonal store promotions. The game is not going anywhere, and the price will only fall over time. If $79.99 is too much today, the honest answer is not a fake download — it is a calendar reminder.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the Standard Edition enough, or do I need Ultimate?

Standard is the complete game for $79.99. Ultimate is $99.99 and adds extra vehicles, weapons, outfits and shops. If the price is the problem, Standard is the answer — the story, the world, and the missions are the same.

Can I really get GTA 6 for free?

Yes, two ways: Microsoft Rewards points redeemed for an Xbox gift card, and the Kick and Twitch code giveaways — Kick's Sunset Reward Case runs until December 7, 2026, and Twitch's drops run until November 30. Neither is guaranteed, and neither costs anything to try.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 is $79.99, and that is real money — but the smart ways to pay less are all legitimate: earn Microsoft Rewards points, try the Kick and Twitch giveaways, use cashback you already have, trade in games you no longer play, or simply wait for a sale. What never works is the "free download": every fake APK and installer is malware documented by real security researchers. Pay less with patience, not with your passwords.

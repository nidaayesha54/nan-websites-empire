Binance Adds Five New bStocks on 28 Oct 2026: APPB, BSPB, GEVB, SKDDB and SKUUB

28 October 2026 · New Listing

Binance Spot and Convert added five new bStocks — tokenized securities — on 28 October 2026. Trading in the new pairs opened at 20:00 (UTC+8) on 28 October, which is 12:00 UTC and 17:00 PKT. Binance also enabled spot algorithmic trading bot and smart position bot services for the new listings at launch.

The five new bStocks

TokenTracksTrading pair
APPBAppLovin CorpAPPB/USDT
BSPBBending SpoonsBSPB/USDT
GEVBGE Vernova IncGEVB/USDT
SKDDBGraniteShares 2x Short SK Hynix Daily ETFSKDDB/USDT
SKUUBGraniteShares 2x Long SK Hynix Daily ETFSKUUB/USDT

As with Binance's earlier bStocks, these are tokenized securities: tokens that track the price of a stock or ETF, rather than the shares themselves. The two SK Hynix entries are a matched pair — one tracking a 2x short daily ETF and one tracking a 2x long daily ETF — so they move in opposite directions when the underlying ETF's daily performance changes.

Binance also said that within one hour after the bStocks went live on Spot, users could convert them through Binance Convert into BTC, USDT or other tokens supported by Convert, with no conversion fees on that Convert route.

Also on Binance on 28 October

New pair — USDC/AED. Binance opened spot trading for the USDC/AED pair at 08:00 UTC on 28 October 2026, with Spot Algo Orders enabled for the same pair at the same time. The pair was announced on 26 October 2026.

Delisting — EPIC and QUICK. Binance delisted Epic Chain (EPIC) and QuickSwap (QUICK) on 28 October 2026 at 03:00 UTC, removing all of their spot trading pairs. The delisting was announced on 14 October 2026 following Binance's regular review of listed assets; Binance did not give a token-specific public reason for the removal.

Ethereum wallet maintenance. Binance carried out wallet maintenance on the Ethereum (ETH) network on 28 October 2026 at 06:00 UTC, suspending ETH deposits and withdrawals from 05:55 UTC for an estimated one hour. Trading was not suspended by the maintenance, and deposits and withdrawals were to reopen once the network was judged stable, with no further announcement promised.

Market check on 28 October

Bitcoin traded back above $80,000 on 28 October after slipping below that level on 27 October. KuCoin's daily market report put Bitcoin at $80,908.30 (+0.97%) and Ethereum at $2,350.39 (+1.12%) in data as of 28 October 00:00 UTC, with the Crypto Fear & Greed Index at 51. Other reports on the morning of 28 October put Bitcoin near $80,717–$80,771, Ethereum just above $2,350, Solana near $102, and ZEC near $1,320 after an approximately 8% rise. Total crypto market value was reported at about $2.66 trillion.

Traders were watching the US Federal Reserve's interest-rate decision due later on 28 October, with market reports describing expectations that rates would stay unchanged and attention focused on guidance about December. That context — not any single listing — was the main driver of caution described in the day's market reports.

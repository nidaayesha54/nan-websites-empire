Bitcoin Dips Below $76,000 Then Recovers on 4 Nov 2026 as US Midterm Results Land

4 November 2026 · Market Check

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 4 November 2026 — reporting on 3 November noted that no new spot coin had appeared on Binance since the 29 October security incident, with the exchange's notices since then focused on restoring withdrawals. Today's article therefore covers the day's biggest verified market development: Bitcoin's overnight dip below $76,000 and its recovery during Wednesday trading as US midterm election results came in.

An overnight dip, then a V-shaped recovery

Bitcoin fell from about $77,970 to about $75,740 between 21:45 and 03:10 UTC as midterm results rolled in, according to Bitcoin.com's 4 November coverage. The Business Times reported Bitcoin slipping as much as 1.6% in Asia on Wednesday and trading around $76,600 as of 10:30 a.m. in Singapore. CoinDesk reported that Bitcoin later erased its early losses, trading about 0.5% higher on the day at $77,883, after falling just below $76,000 early on Wednesday. CNBC TV18, via TradingView, put Bitcoin at $76,843, down 0.36%, with Ether edging up 0.06% to $2,259.

The early slide tracked a broader risk-off move: Bitcoin.com reported Nasdaq 100 futures down about 2% overnight after NBC News projected Democrats would win control of the House of Representatives, with CNBC's election desk estimating at least 235 seats, above the 218 needed for a majority. CoinDesk reported that Nasdaq futures later pared their decline to about 0.3% lower as the initial political uncertainty faded. Those price moves are reported market facts, not a forecast of where prices will go next.

US spot Bitcoin ETFs: more than $1 billion out in two sessions

US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded net outflows of $398.6 million on 2 November, following withdrawals of $643.2 million on 30 October, taking combined outflows across the two trading sessions to more than $1 billion, according to CNBC TV18's 4 November market summary. KuCoin's daily market report confirmed the $399 million figure for 2 November and described it as a second consecutive trading day of substantial withdrawals, suggesting institutional buying remained cautious. CoinDesk, citing SoSoValue, reported that US-listed spot ETFs had recorded $515 million in outflows in October and had already seen an equal amount leave in the first two trading days of November.

Sentiment: Fear, but less fearful than the day before

The Crypto Fear and Greed Index stood at 34 on 4 November, in "Fear" territory, according to both KuCoin's daily report and CoinGabbar's market summary. That was up from 26 the previous day, also in Fear, and down from 51 a week earlier, when the reading was Neutral. CoinGabbar, citing CoinGecko data recorded at 4:00 a.m. UTC on 4 November, put the global crypto market capitalisation at $2.66 trillion, down 0.6% over 24 hours, with total trading volume of $64.8 billion, Bitcoin dominance of 57.8% and Ether at 10.3%. Bitcoin.com, using a different time window, reported total market capitalisation down about 2.9% over 24 hours to roughly $2.58 trillion, with Ether near $2,240 and XRP near $1.26.

Binance recovery continues in the background

Binance's withdrawal restoration, covered in NAN Crypto News on 3 November, remained the backdrop to the week's trading. On 3 November the exchange began reopening withdrawals for a further batch of assets from 04:00 UTC in its sixth recovery update after the 29 October incident, covering networks including Ethereum, Solana, BNB Smart Chain, XRP Ledger, Sui, Arbitrum, Optimism, Tron and Base, progressively as security and readiness checks complete. A separate report on 3 November noted Binance had said hourly deposits had passed withdrawals again, and that the exchange was running a trading promotion through 21 November with fee vouchers and stablecoin rewards. No new Binance spot listing date has been announced.

What to watch next

Market coverage on 4 November pointed to two near-term calendar items: US economic data due on Wednesday, including the October ADP employment change and the ISM Services Index, and the US October employment report scheduled for Friday 6 November. KuCoin's report noted near-term attention on Bitcoin support around $76,000–$76,600 and resistance around $79,500–$80,500; those are reported reference levels from that report, not a prediction.

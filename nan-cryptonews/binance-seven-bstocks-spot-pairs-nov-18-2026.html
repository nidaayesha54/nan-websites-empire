Binance Adds Seven bStocks Spot Pairs on 18 November 2026 as Bitcoin Holds Near $64,700

18 November 2026 · New Listing

Binance announced it will open seven new bStocks trading pairs on Binance Spot on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, at 13:00 UTC: EWJB/USDT, EWTB/USDT, EWZB/USDT, EWZSB/USDT, IWMB/USDT, MELIB/USDT and NUB/USDT. The pairs are tokenized securities, not cryptocurrencies — each token tracks a US-listed ETF or company share. Withdrawals for all seven tokens open at 14:00 UTC, and Binance said zero maker fees apply on the seven pairs from the listing time through 30 November 2026 at 23:59 UTC.

The seven new pairs

The five ETF-linked tokens are the iShares MSCI Japan ETF (EWJB), the iShares MSCI Taiwan ETF (EWTB), the iShares MSCI Brazil ETF (EWZB), the iShares MSCI Brazil Small-Cap ETF (EWZSB) and the iShares Russell 2000 Index Fund (IWMB). The two company-linked tokens are MercadoLibre (MELIB) and Nu Holdings (NUB). Binance said Rebalancing Bots for the pairs will be enabled within 24 hours of the spot listing, and Binance Convert will allow users to trade the bStocks against BTC, USDT and other tokens within one hour of the listing, with zero fees. Users can also tokenize direct stock holdings into bStocks at a 1:1 ratio with the underlying stock, with no conversion fees, according to the announcement.

The same seven bStocks will also be added as eligible collateral for cross margin, portfolio margin and portfolio margin Pro at 13:00 UTC on 18 November, with the corresponding pairs opening for margin trading, according to Binance's margin announcement reported the same day. The new pairs follow Binance's earlier bStocks listings on 28 October, when APPB, BSPB and GEVB pairs were added, and continue the exchange's expansion into tokenized US stocks and ETFs, including its move to add more than 7,000 US stocks and ETFs to its app.

Binance Futures launches six TradFi perpetual contracts the same day

Binance Futures will launch six new USDⓈ-margined perpetual contracts on 18 November 2026 at 09:30 UTC, covering SOXX, IGV, ARTY, IBIT, CCJ and ETN, with up to 20x leverage, according to the exchange's announcement. Separately, the Binance Futures ANTHROPICUSDT perpetual contract will be renamed ANTUSDT, with trading resuming at 18:30 Beijing time (10:30 UTC) on 18 November, after a size adjustment completed at 18:25 Beijing time. Anthropic is set to announce its IPO pricing on 18 November (Eastern Time) and start trading on Nasdaq on 19 November under the ticker ANT, with an offering price range of $70 to $78 per share.

Binance Wallet runs a $500 million Anthropic token subscription

Binance Wallet opened a $500 million subscription window on 18 November for tokenized shares tied to Anthropic's initial public offering, running from 06:00 to 14:00 UTC. The subscription uses an indicative price band of 70 to 78 USDC per token, with a 100 USDC minimum commitment and a 500 million USDC cap on total subscriptions. The tokens, issued through xStocks by Backed, give price exposure to Anthropic without direct ownership, voting rights or dividend rights, and xStocks charges a 5% partner service fee on top of the indicative price. Binance Wallet said it adds no fee of its own. The final offering price is set by the issuer and underwriters after the window closes and can land above or below the indicated range. Distribution is scheduled for at least two hours after Nasdaq opens, with the exact timing still to be confirmed. Anthropic filed an amended prospectus with the US Securities and Exchange Commission on 10 November, covering 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each.

Bitcoin flat near $64,700; Fear & Greed Index at 11

Bitcoin traded near $64,700 on Wednesday, 18 November 2026, ending Tuesday almost exactly where it started. Binance data showed Bitcoin at $64,776.51 at 00:57 UTC, up 0.22% in 24 hours, after dipping to $63,336 in mid-morning as US stocks turned lower, according to CryptoNakta. Finnhub data on the Binance BTC/USDT pair showed $64,774.01 at 07:10 UTC, with an intraday range of $63,336 to $65,447. CoinGecko data recorded at 04:00 UTC put Bitcoin at $64,374.68, down 0.9% in 24 hours, with a market capitalisation of about $1.29 trillion and 24-hour trading volume of about $33.94 billion. The global crypto market capitalisation stood at about $2.27 trillion at 04:00 UTC, down 0.1% over 24 hours, with total trading volume of about $79.2 billion, according to CoinGecko.

Ether traded around $1,801 on 18 November, down about 1.5% in 24 hours, according to CoinGecko data at 04:00 UTC. Solana was up about 5% at about $75.19, according to a daily brief locked at 00:00 UTC. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index read 11 on 18 November, in Extreme Fear, down from 12 the day before and its sixth straight day in the extreme fear band since 13 November, according to alternative.me. US spot Bitcoin ETFs saw a net outflow of $185.5 million on 13 November, the most recent session reported.

Nvidia reported $115.8 billion in quarterly revenue after the US market close on 17 November. The US Federal Reserve publishes the minutes of its 27–28 October meeting at 2 p.m. Eastern on Wednesday, 18 November. Binance also said it will remove several spot trading pairs — GAS/TRY, HBAR/BNB, HIVE/TRY, KSM/TRY and ZAMA/TRY — at 03:00 UTC on 20 November 2026. No new Binance spot cryptocurrency listing was announced for 18 November 2026; the most recent spot crypto listing was PREDICT on 29 October, whose trading start was postponed that day.

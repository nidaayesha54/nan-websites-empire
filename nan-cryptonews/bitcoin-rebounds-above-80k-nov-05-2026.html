Bitcoin Rebounds Above $80,000 on 5 Nov 2026 as Post-Midterm Rally Lifts the Market

5 November 2026 · Market Check

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 5 November 2026 — reporting on 4–5 November noted that no new spot coin has appeared on Binance since PREDICT on 29 October, with the exchange's recent notices focused on restoring withdrawals after its 29 October security incident. Today's article therefore covers the day's biggest verified market development: a broad crypto rebound that took Bitcoin back above $80,000 as US midterm election results became clear.

A sharp swing: from $75,700 to above $80,000

Bitcoin fell toward about $75,700 early on 4 November as early midterm results pointed to a Democratic sweep of Congress, then recovered toward $80,000 as US stocks rallied, according to CoinDesk. CoinDesk reported Bitcoin trading around $80,484 during Asian morning hours on 5 November, up 5.7% over 24 hours. CoinStats, using CoinGecko data recorded at 4:00 a.m. UTC on 5 November, put Bitcoin at $80,564.30, up 5.4% over 24 hours, with about $37.4 billion in trading volume and a market capitalisation of around $1.619 trillion. KuCoin's daily market report said Bitcoin rebounded from an election-period low of approximately $75,600 to briefly pass $81,000. Those price moves are reported market facts, not a forecast of where prices will go next.

Ether and Zcash lead the altcoin recovery

Ether rose about 7.5% to $2,428.01, with a market capitalisation near $296.6 billion and 24-hour trading volume of about $16.28 billion, according to CoinStats' CoinGecko snapshot. CoinDesk reported Ether up 8% to about $2,425. Zcash led gains among the larger tokens, rising 11.7% to about $1,318, with about $1.2 billion changing hands over the preceding day, ahead of a network upgrade expected to shorten transaction confirmation times, CoinDesk reported.

Why the market turned

CoinDesk reported that cryptocurrencies joined a broader market rebound after Democrats won both houses of Congress, amid hopes for an end to the Iran conflict and lower oil prices. KuCoin's report noted the S&P 500 up 1.58% and the Nasdaq up 1.44% in the same session, and said short covering had contributed to the rally, with a sustained recovery needing fresh inflows. NDTV's market coverage reported Bitcoin gaining nearly 4.7% to trade around $80,500, with XRP also participating in the recovery near $1.30.

Sentiment: back to Neutral

The Crypto Fear and Greed Index climbed from 34 to 50 on 5 November, a Neutral reading, according to CoinStats. That followed a Fear reading of 34 on 4 November, up from 26 the day before. CoinStats, citing CoinGecko data recorded at 4:00 a.m. UTC on 5 November, put the global crypto market capitalisation at $2.79 trillion, up 5.1% over 24 hours, with total trading volume of $96.8 billion, Bitcoin dominance of 58.1% and Ether at 10.6%.

ETF flows and the Fed watch

US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $1.16 billion in net outflows over three trading sessions, according to SoSoValue data cited by Cointelegraph. The CME FedWatch Tool put the probability of a Federal Reserve rate hike at the 9 December meeting at 81%, CoinStats reported. KuCoin's report noted near-term attention on Bitcoin resistance around $82,000 and support near $78,000 and $76,000; those are reported reference levels from that report, not a prediction.

Binance: still no new spot coin

Binance has added no new spot coin since PREDICT, announced on 29 October, according to reporting on 4–5 November. The exchange's 4 November notice instead kept a 50% taker-fee discount on its TradFi perpetual contracts for VIP 4 to VIP 9 accounts, dropping a planned cut to 40%. Separately, Binance's 29 October announcement for Indonesian users adds 31 new spot trading pairs against the Indonesian rupiah (IDR) in three waves, with the first batch scheduled for 27 November — including HBAR/IDR, MANTA/IDR, SCR/IDR and WLD/IDR — and the second and third waves on 29 November. The exchange also said on 3 November that hourly deposits had passed withdrawals again, and it is running a trading promotion through 21 November with fee vouchers and stablecoin rewards.

What to watch next

KuCoin's daily report listed the calendar items for 5 November: US initial jobless claims for the week ending 31 October, the Bank of England policy rate decision, and the Bank of Japan's September monetary policy meeting minutes, plus an ENA token unlock equivalent to 0.7% of circulating supply, worth approximately $17.7 million. The US October employment report is scheduled for Friday 6 November.

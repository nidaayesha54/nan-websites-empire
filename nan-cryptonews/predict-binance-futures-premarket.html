Binance Futures Opens Pre-Market Trading for PREDICT (Predict.fun)

26 October 2026 · Futures Pre-Market

Binance Futures has launched pre-market trading for the USDT-margined PREDICT/USDT perpetual contract for Predict.fun. According to Binance's announcement dated 25 October 2026, the contract opened for pre-market trading on 25 October 2026, offering up to 5x leverage, with trading available 24/7.

How pre-market pricing works

During the pre-market period, the mark price is calculated every second from the average of trade prices over the last 10 seconds, and mark price changes are limited to ±1% per second during the pre-market period and the transition to the standard contract. The contract will gradually convert into a standard perpetual futures contract once a stable index price is obtained from spot markets. Open orders and positions are not cancelled during this process.

Funding rate

During the pre-market period the funding rate is +0.005% every four hours. After the pre-market period ends, standard perpetual futures rules apply, and the funding rate may reach the limits of +2.00% / −2.00%.

What is Predict.fun?

Predict.fun is a prediction market that lets users predict the outcomes of future events across different categories on BNB Chain. The project's total supply is 1,000,000,000 PREDICT, with a maximum supply of 1,000,000,000 PREDICT.

Binance CFO Kaiser Ng Steps Down as Bitcoin Nears a Fifth Straight Weekly Loss

8 November 2026 · Binance News

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 8 November 2026 — reporting on 7 November noted that no new spot coin has appeared on Binance so far in November, with the exchange's recent notices focused on restoring services after its 29 October security incident. Today's article therefore covers the day's biggest verified development: Binance's finance chief is leaving, just over a week after the record theft from the exchange, while Bitcoin holds near $75,850 and closes in on a rare fifth consecutive weekly loss.

Binance CFO Kaiser Ng departs; Justin Yek takes over

Binance's finance chief Kaiser Ng is leaving the company to pursue another venture, the exchange said on Saturday, 7 November, according to The Business Times. Ng had spent more than five years at Binance, and a transition plan had been implemented over the last half year, Binance co-chief executive officer Yi He said in a statement. Justin Yek, most recently vice-president of finance at Airwallex, is taking over as chief financial officer.

The announcement comes just over a week after Binance said hackers stole about $6.3 billion in cryptocurrency in a sophisticated attack that began months earlier with the compromise of several employees' computers. The exchange has been publishing recovery updates since the 29 October incident, and its ninth update, on 6 November, reopened withdrawals for more assets from 04:00 UTC, including USDE, PENDLE, ZRO, XRP and CAKE. Binance said about 95% of the affected assets had been replenished, and it aims to reopen withdrawals for all tokens on networks that have already been restored by the end of next week. Separately, Binance's spot taker programme, returning 5%, 10% or 15% of fees to its largest spot traders, begins on 9 November and runs to 3 January.

Bitcoin near $75,850 and a fifth straight weekly loss

Bitcoin traded near $75,850 on 8 November, after closing the 7 November session at $75,868, up 0.03% on the day and down 2.21% over seven days, according to a daily market brief using Binance data. Finnhub's Binance feed showed Bitcoin at $75,845.99, with a day range of $75,571 to $76,250. Pluang reported Bitcoin trading around $75,800 and approaching a rare fifth consecutive weekly loss, a streak seen only twice before since 2017. A daily report put Bitcoin at EUR 68,024, down 0.04% over 24 hours and 36.82% below its all-time high of EUR 107,662 set on 6 October 2025, with Bitcoin dominance at 59.34% of a roughly EUR 2.299 trillion total crypto market capitalisation. Those price moves are reported market facts, not a forecast of where prices will go next.

ETF outflows wipe out 2026 institutional gains

US spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded $411.9 million in net outflows on Friday, according to an Investing.com report cited by ad-hoc-news. BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust accounted for $403 million of redemptions, while Fidelity's fund recorded $42.8 million of inflows. The report put total November withdrawals through Friday at roughly $1.28 billion, with Wednesday the only positive session during that period. The outflows follow a week in which US tech stocks sold off sharply, with the S&P 500 and Nasdaq dropping more than 4% and 6% respectively on 5 November.

Ether, Solana and the wider market

Ether traded at about $2,240, up 0.07% on the day, while Solana was down 0.36% at $89.81, according to the daily market brief. Bitcoin open interest stood at $2.61 billion, down 0.8% from the previous day, and the latest Bitcoin funding print was 0.0091%, around the 0.01% baseline, with no sign of one side overleveraging. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index read 28 (Fear), up 3 points from the previous day, and Bitcoin's daily RSI was 38.0, inside the neutral 30–70 band. KuCoin's market note put Bitcoin at $75,953.19, up 0.09% over 24 hours, with funding rates in a neutral range and bearish sentiment on Ether easing.

PREDICT listing still delayed; no date set

Predict.fun's token generation event and Season 1 airdrop claim remain delayed, tied to the postponement of Binance Alpha and Spot trading, with no confirmed listing date. Binance Wallet confirmed five planned $PREDICT airdrop release dates — the token's listing day, plus 6, 13, 20 and 27 November 2026 — and said any missed batch will be combined into the first batch released after launch. The questionnaire deadline for the airdrop is 26 November 2026 at 09:00 UTC. PREDICT was trading at about $10.09 in premarket, according to CoinGabbar, but no listing has taken place.

What to watch next

The US consumer price index release on 10 November is the next big data point, as inflation data feeds into Federal Reserve rate decisions and market sentiment, Pluang reported. Traders are also watching whether Bitcoin can hold the $75,775 support level and the weekly close at $78,232. Binance's spot taker programme begins on 9 November, and the exchange's next withdrawal-recovery targets — HYPE, INJ, APT, NEAR and LTC — are due by the end of next week. Those are reported analyst views and scheduled events, not a prediction by this site.

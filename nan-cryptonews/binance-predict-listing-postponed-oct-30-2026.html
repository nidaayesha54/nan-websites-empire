Binance Postpones PREDICT Spot Listing on 30 Oct 2026 After Security Incident

30 October 2026 · Binance Announcement

Binance has postponed the spot listing of Predict (PREDICT) that was scheduled for 30 October 2026, after the exchange reported a security incident on the same day and temporarily suspended deposits and withdrawals. No new listing time has been announced yet.

What was originally planned

On 29 October 2026, Binance announced it would list PREDICT and open three spot trading pairs — PREDICT/USDT, PREDICT/USDC and PREDICT/TRY — at 10:00 UTC on 30 October 2026 (18:00 UTC+8). The token was to carry a Seed Tag, which Binance uses to flag newer or higher-volatility tokens, and the listing fee was 0 BNB. Withdrawals were scheduled to open on 31 October 2026 at 10:00 UTC. The TRY pair was to be available only to users with verified Binance TR accounts.

Predict.fun is a prediction market on BNB Chain. Binance Futures had already opened pre-market trading for the PREDICT/USDT perpetual contract on 25 October 2026, as covered in our earlier article.

Why the listing was postponed

On 30 October 2026, Binance said in a separate announcement that the PREDICT trading start time, originally scheduled for 10:00 UTC on Friday, had been postponed until further notice, according to PANews, which cited the official Binance announcement. A specific new time is to be announced separately.

The postponement came on the same day Binance reported a security incident. According to Reuters, Binance said the incident had an estimated impact of around $6.3 billion, and that it had worked with blockchain partners to freeze about $3 billion of affected assets so far. Binance's initial investigation suggested the incident may have involved a compromised DevOps engineer's laptop, allowing an attacker to gain access to a service container and submit unauthorised withdrawal transactions. As an immediate precaution, Binance temporarily suspended deposits and withdrawals while security checks were completed, and said it would cover all losses using its Secure Asset Fund for Users (SAFU) and its own funds.

Market reaction reported on 30 October

Reuters reported that cryptocurrency prices fell on Friday after Binance reported the security issue. Bitcoin dropped more than 3% to a six-week low, ether fell more than 3% to a two-month low, and BNB fell over the previous 24 hours. Prices were still moving at the time of reporting, so these figures reflect the day's reported lows, not a fixed price.

Also on 30 October: scheduled pair delistings went ahead

Separately from the security incident, Binance's previously announced delistings took effect on 30 October 2026. Twelve spot trading pairs were removed at 03:00 UTC: AAVE/BTC, ATOM/BTC, CAKE/BTC, DASH/BTC, ETC/BTC, ICP/BTC, PENDLE/BTC, RENDER/BTC, TAO/BTC, THETA/BTC, XAUT/BTC and XTZ/BTC. Ten margin trading pairs were also removed at 06:00 UTC: RE/USD1, KAT/U, BTC/USDS, ETH/USDS, AVNT/U, FET/U, TRUMP/U, APT/U and WLFI/U, plus one Chinese-language pair. Binance has said the delisting of a spot trading pair does not affect the availability of the tokens themselves on Binance Spot — users can still trade the base and quote assets on other available pairs.

What to watch next

Three things remain open as of 30 October 2026: a new date and time for the PREDICT spot listing, the reopening of Binance deposits and withdrawals once security checks are complete, and any further update from Binance on the recovery of the remaining affected assets. Readers should check Binance's official announcements for those updates rather than relying on social media claims about a new listing time.

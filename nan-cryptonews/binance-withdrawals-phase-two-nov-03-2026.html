Binance Reopens Withdrawals for a Second Batch of Assets on 3 Nov 2026

3 November 2026 · Binance News & Market Check

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 3 November 2026 — so today's article covers the day's biggest verified Binance development: the exchange began reopening withdrawals for a further, much larger batch of assets from 04:00 UTC on 3 November (09:00 PKT), following the first phase that went live on 1 November. The move was reported as Binance's sixth recovery update since the security incident disclosed on 30 October, in which roughly $6.3 billion in tokens were affected.

Phase two: what reopened, and how it rolls out

As in phase one, the reopening is progressive, not instant: Binance cautioned that not everything listed will switch on at once, and that each asset opens as its security and readiness checks are completed. Deposits stay open and are unaffected. Binance has repeated that it will bear the financial impact of the stolen funds and that losses will not be passed on to users.

The new batch is concentrated on BNB Smart Chain (BSC), covering a long list including USD1, XUSD, U, ADA, FDUSD, LTC, BTC, ETH, XRP, NEAR, ZEC, WBETH, DOGE, CAKE, SOL, FET, DOT, LINK, SHIB, XLM, ALT, BMT, EDU, HBAR, BANANAS31, BONK, HUMA, PARTI, IOST, LISTA and TUSD — and withdrawals for all bSTOCKS also open. On Ethereum, the batch includes RLUSD, USD1, ENA, USDE, PENDLE, WLD, FDUSD, XUSD, QNT, SHIB, ZRO, FET, NIL, ONDO, ALT, SKY, LINK, ETHFI, REZ, JASMY, PUNDIX, BTC, BAT, STRK, POL, ANIME, LSK, RE, BREV, RLC, AMP, PLUME, BLUR, WBETH, ZBT, ENSO, AVA and TUSD. On Solana, it adds USD1, PUMP, FDUSD, BMT, RENDER, RAY, TRUMP, MET, PENGU, JUP, PYTH, BONK, KMNO, BNSOL, ORCA, WIF, W, HUMA, JTO, IO, 2Z and BOME. Also reopening are XRP (RLUSDFD), SUI (USDC, HAEDAL), Arbitrum (CHIP, ANIME), Optimism (OP, WCT), TRX (TUSD) and Base (ZEN, MIRA, HOME, AWE).

For users, the practical point is unchanged from phase one: if a specific asset is not yet withdrawable, it is still working through checks rather than permanently unavailable, and the withdrawal page itself is the latest status for that asset. Wallets for any assets not in this batch remain under security audits, as Binance said in its phase-one update.

Also on 3 November: proof of reserves and a scheduled pair ending

Today is also the date by which Binance had indicated a full zk-SNARKs proof of reserves would be available, following the 31 October snapshot that reported ten major assets backed above 100%, with Bitcoin at 100.04% and BNB at 101.30%. At the time of writing, the sources checked for this article had not yet confirmed publication of that full proof — so this is flagged as expected, not confirmed. Readers should check Binance's official proof-of-reserves page for the live document rather than assuming it has landed.

Separately, VELODROME/USDT trading was scheduled to end on 3 November at 03:00 UTC, as part of the Velodrome-to-Aerodrome (AERO) migration Binance announced on 16 October, under which each VELODROME token swaps into 0.044 AERO. That is a previously announced schedule item, not a new listing.

Tokenized Anthropic Goes Live on BNB Chain on 19 November 2026 as Bitcoin Falls Below $63,000

19 November 2026 · New Listing

Tokenized exposure to Anthropic's stock goes live on BNB Chain on Thursday, 19 November 2026, the day the artificial intelligence company debuts on Nasdaq under the ticker ANT, Binance founder Changpeng "CZ" Zhao said. The tokens will be available through bStocks, Ondo and xStocks, making Anthropic's Nasdaq listing a live test for blockchain-based equities: eligible crypto users can trade Anthropic-linked tokens directly on BNB Chain rather than going through a traditional brokerage. Anthropic priced its initial public offering at $83 per share on 18 November, above its indicated range of $70 to $78, selling 980 million Class A shares for a gross offering of about $81.3 billion, with the offering expected to close on 20 November.

Binance Wallet's $500 million pre-IPO subscription filled within hours

Binance Wallet opened a $500 million subscription window on 18 November for tokenized shares tied to Anthropic's initial public offering, running from 06:00 UTC and scheduled to close at 14:00 UTC. The window filled its 500 million USDC cap within hours and closed ahead of schedule, according to CoinDesk. Eligible participants could commit a minimum of 100 USDC for a chance to receive ANTx, xStocks' Anthropic-linked token, at Anthropic's offering price, based on an indicative price band of 70 to 78 USDC per token before a 5% xStocks service fee. Committing funds did not guarantee an allocation: Binance said participants could receive a full, partial or zero allocation, with any unallocated USDC automatically refunded, and committed funds were held in a smart contract pending token distribution or refund. At the $83 IPO price, the offering values Anthropic at about $1.5 trillion on a fully diluted basis, including stock options and other securities, according to Nikkei Asia, and demand reportedly reached as much as six times the available shares, according to Bloomberg.

The tokens give price exposure, not ownership

The tokenized Anthropic instruments carry notable structural caveats. Binance stated that the token confers no direct ownership in Anthropic and includes no voting, dividend or shareholder rights, and that xStocks and its allocator or distributor may fulfil allocations using bStocks instead. Binance further cautioned that the underlying reserves may not always consist of Anthropic shares, with cash or other eligible assets potentially held as substitutes, and emphasised that it neither verifies those reserves independently nor guarantees one-to-one backing. The instruments also differ from each other: IPO-allocation tokens such as ANTx give eligible users a chance to subscribe at the IPO price; wrapped or custodied share tokens such as xStocks' ANTx and bStocks are built to mirror the underlying stock's price; Ondo's ANTon is a tokenized structured note delivering economic exposure through a mintable and redeemable note; and pre-IPO perpetual futures, such as contracts listed on Hyperliquid, are cash-settled derivatives that neither deliver shares nor grant IPO access. Binance Futures renamed its ANTHROPICUSDT perpetual contract to ANTUSDT on 18 November, with trading resuming at 10:30 UTC.

Bitcoin falls below $63,000 after Fed minutes point to a December hike

Bitcoin traded near $63,000 on Thursday, 19 November 2026, falling to a four-month low near $62,000. CoinGecko data recorded at 01:30 UTC put Bitcoin at $62,675.46, down 2.7% in 24 hours, with a market capitalisation of about $1.26 trillion and 24-hour trading volume of about $30.98 billion, according to CryptoCompass. Coinglass recorded Bitcoin at $63,500 on 19 November with a 24-hour range of $62,030 to $64,934, and CoinGabbar put Bitcoin at $63,400 on 19 November, down 1.8% on the day and about 15% over seven days. The sell-off followed the minutes of the US Federal Reserve's 27–28 October meeting, published on 18 November, which traders read as pointing to a December interest-rate hike rather than a cut: CME FedWatch odds of a 25-basis-point December hike reached 90%, up from 75.5% a month earlier, according to CoinGabbar. US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded a net outflow of $239.9 million on 17 November, the most recent session reported.

Ether traded around $1,777 on 19 November, down about 2.6% in 24 hours, according to CoinGecko data at 01:30 UTC, while Solana was down about 2% at about $73.64, according to a daily brief locked at 00:00 UTC. The global crypto market capitalisation stood at about $2.24 trillion at 01:30 UTC, down 2.1% over 24 hours, with total trading volume of about $71.3 billion and Bitcoin dominance at 56.6%, according to CoinGecko. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index read 10 on 19 November, in Extreme Fear, down from 11 the day before, according to alternative.me. No new Binance spot cryptocurrency listing was announced for 19 November 2026; the most recent spot crypto listing was PREDICT on 29 October, whose trading start was postponed that day. Binance said it will remove several spot trading pairs — GAS/TRY, HBAR/BNB, HIVE/TRY, KSM/TRY and ZAMA/TRY — at 03:00 UTC on 20 November 2026.

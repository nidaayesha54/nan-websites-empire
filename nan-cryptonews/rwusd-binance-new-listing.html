Binance's New Spot Listing: RWUSD Opens With Zero Fees

26 October 2026 · New Listing

Binance added just one new spot token between 10 and 17 October 2026: RWUSD. Trading on the RWUSD/USDT pair opened on 13 October, with zero trading fees until further notice.

What is RWUSD?

RWUSD is tied to a yield product: it is issued 1:1 against the stablecoin you pay with, pays interest daily, and can be redeemed for USDC. The token also works as margin collateral. In the same week, Binance removed two older tokens from spot trading, QUICK and EPIC — one listing in, two out.

Why listings matter

Every Binance new listing alert sends traders running, because a listing can move a coin's price within minutes. When listings get this rare, the coins that do get one tend to draw the whole crowd's attention — which is exactly why rumours about the "next" listing spread so fast.

What to watch

Treat any "next Binance listing" claim as a rumour until Binance announces it officially. Most recent Binance listings also carry the exchange's Seed Tag, its label for newer, higher-risk projects — a reminder to size any position carefully.

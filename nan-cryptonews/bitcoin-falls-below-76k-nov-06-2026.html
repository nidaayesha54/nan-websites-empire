Bitcoin Falls Below $76,000 on 6 Nov 2026 as Tech Sell-Off Triggers Over $1 Billion in Liquidations

6 November 2026 · Market Check

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 6 November 2026 — reporting on 5–6 November noted that no new spot coin has appeared on Binance since PREDICT on 29 October, with the exchange's recent notices focused on restoring services after its 29 October security incident. Today's article therefore covers the day's biggest verified market development: a sharp crypto sell-off that took Bitcoin back below $76,000, wiping out the previous day's post-midterm gains.

The rebound reverses: Bitcoin back below $76,000

Bitcoin fell as low as about $75,369 in early trading on 6 November, down 6.29% at its worst point, before recovering to around $76,101, according to BusinessToday. NDTV's market coverage reported Bitcoin down 5.51% to about $75,949, while CoinCodex put Bitcoin at $75,710 at press time, down 5.83% over 24 hours. Phemex's BTC/USDT screen showed $75,965 at 03:30 UTC on 6 November, with a 24-hour range of $75,259 to $80,832.83. The fall came a day after Bitcoin had rebounded above $80,000 on 5 November, and it left the coin down about 5.6% over 24 hours and more than 40% below its peak earlier this year, BusinessToday reported. Those price moves are reported market facts, not a forecast of where prices will go next.

Ether and the wider market fall harder

Ether fell about 8% to around $2,230–$2,240. NDTV reported Ether down 7.64% to $2,230, while BusinessToday reported a low of $2,216.58, down 8.51%, before a rebound to about $2,240. XRP dropped about 6.66% to approximately $1.21, and Solana fell 6.14%, NDTV reported. CoinCodex put total crypto market capitalisation at $2.55 trillion, down 5.02% over 24 hours, with total trading volume up 15.53% to $99.05 billion and Bitcoin dominance at 59.75%. NDTV noted that nine of the top ten cryptocurrencies moved lower, showing the reversal extended well beyond Bitcoin.

What drove the sell-off

The crypto downturn followed a sharp decline in US equities on 5 November, with the technology-heavy Nasdaq Composite tumbling 5.52% and the S&P 500 shedding 4.36% amid mounting concerns over artificial intelligence valuations and potential regulatory restrictions, BusinessToday reported. CoinDesk's market-close report put the Nasdaq-100 down 6.4% in the US session, alongside heavy losses in semiconductor shares, according to Phemex. NDTV's coverage said investors were reassessing risk after the sharp sell-off in global equities. BusinessToday also noted that market sentiment was still weighed down by lingering concerns over the $6.3 billion Binance security breach of 29 October, which prompted billions of dollars in withdrawals and raised questions about confidence in digital asset platforms.

Over $1 billion in liquidations

The fall forced the closure of more than $1 billion in leveraged positions over 24 hours. A CoinGlass dashboard capture at about 03:32 UTC on 6 November showed $1.24 billion in liquidations across its tracked instruments, including $325.18 million attributed to Bitcoin, with about 88.6% of the total coming from long positions, Phemex reported. CoinCourier, citing CoinGlass-based feeds, put total crypto-market liquidations at roughly $1.03 billion to $1.08 billion over the same window, with about 89% from longs and Bitcoin-specific liquidations of $255.9 million to $293.3 million. NDTV reported that Bitcoin, Ether and Solana perpetuals alone recorded $283.18 million in liquidations over the 24 hours to the morning of 6 November. Liquidation figures describe the notional value of positions closed under margin rules, not the amount every trader lost or the value of spot Bitcoin sold.

Binance: PREDICT airdrop update, still no new spot coin

Binance has added no new spot coin since PREDICT, announced on 29 October, according to reporting on 4–5 November. The exchange's latest PREDICT update, published on Binance Square on 5 November, said the five planned $PREDICT airdrop release dates — the token's listing day and 6, 13, 20 and 27 November 2026 — and their questionnaire deadlines will remain unchanged. If $PREDICT launches after one or more of those dates, the batch for that date and any earlier unreleased batches will be combined and released in the first batch after the launch, and affected users do not need to submit the questionnaire again. Separately, Binance's 29 October announcement for Indonesian users adds 31 new spot trading pairs against the Indonesian rupiah (IDR) in three waves, with the first batch scheduled for 27 November — including HBAR/IDR, MANTA/IDR, SCR/IDR and WLD/IDR — and the second and third waves on 29 November.

What to watch next

Attention now turns to the US October employment report, scheduled for 6 November, and to movements in global technology stocks for indications of whether the crypto market can stabilise ahead of the weekend, BusinessToday reported. NDTV put Bitcoin's support reference near the observed $75,084 low, with resistance around the $80,838 high; those are reported reference levels, not a prediction. The US October employment report was due at 7:00 p.m. IST (1:30 p.m. UTC) on 6 November, according to NDTV.

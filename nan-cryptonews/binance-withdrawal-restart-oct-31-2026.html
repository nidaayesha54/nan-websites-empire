Binance Targets Weekend Withdrawal Restart After $6.3 Billion Security Incident — Market Check (31 Oct 2026)

31 October 2026 · Binance News & Market Check

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 31 October 2026 — so today's article is a market check built on that day's reported data, centred on Binance's Friday updates about the security incident it disclosed on 30 October and on how the market reacted. The Predict (PREDICT) spot listing postponed on 30 October remains postponed, with no new listing time announced.

What Binance said on Friday about the incident

Binance's own updates, reported through Friday 30 October and into Saturday, put the estimated total impact of the incident at around $6.3 billion in token assets. The exchange said its hot wallets — the online wallets used to process customer transfers — were compromised at around 20:05 UTC on 29 October, and that unauthorised withdrawal transactions followed.

Binance's initial investigation pointed to a compromised laptop belonging to a DevOps (development and operations) engineer, which it said allowed an attacker to reach a service container and submit the unauthorised withdrawals. The exchange said it had found no evidence that wallet private keys leaked, and later on Friday it said investigators had uncovered additional points of breach beyond the laptop. A full technical post-mortem has been promised once the investigation is complete.

On recovery, Binance said it had worked with blockchain partners to freeze about $3 billion in affected on-chain assets, and that the frozen figure could still grow as tracing continues. Stablecoin issuers including Tether and Circle were reported to have frozen portions of the stolen funds as the attackers tried to move them. Binance said it has set aside more than $6.34 billion in company funds and will cover all losses through its Secure Asset Fund for Users (SAFU) and company funds, so that users do not bear the financial impact. It said customer account balances were not touched, that user funds are safe, and that it will publish an updated proof of reserves once remediation is complete.

When withdrawals should resume

Withdrawals were initially suspended as a precaution while deposits were later reported to be working as usual. Binance's timeline for reopening withdrawals shortened through Friday: an update at around 09:00 GMT on Friday put resumption at about 45 hours out, and a second update on Friday evening said withdrawal service should begin resuming in phases within approximately 34 hours, subject to the completion of security checks. Co-CEO Richard Teng said in a video message that the goal is for users to be able to withdraw before Sunday, but that the service will only reopen once security testing is successfully completed. As of Saturday's reporting, withdrawals remained suspended while those checks run.

One claim in circulation needs care: blockchain intelligence firm TRM Labs said it had "high confidence" the attack was carried out by North Korea-affiliated group TraderTraitor, the same group blamed for the $1.5 billion Bybit theft in February 2025. That is an investigator's attribution, not Binance's — Binance has not officially attributed the attack. The FBI said it was aware of the incident and engaged with partners to assess it, and US President Donald Trump said on Friday the United States was looking into the hack.

Liquidations and ETF flows show where the pressure came from. About $427 million in leveraged crypto positions were forcibly closed over 24 hours, with 73% of them longs, affecting roughly 93,000 traders, according to CoinGlass data reported by CryptoTimes. Binance processed about half of all liquidations, including a single $11.43 million BTC/USDT order. US spot Bitcoin ETFs posted $643.33 million in net outflows on Friday, led by BlackRock ($233.7 million) and Fidelity ($201.04 million). Cointelegraph, cited by SpendNode, reported that October as a whole wiped roughly $300 billion from the crypto market, against the seasonal expectation of a green "Uptober".

Other Binance dates landing on 31 October

PREDICT listing still postponed. Binance has not said when, or whether, the Predict spot listing originally scheduled for 30 October will proceed. Its postponement notice says only that further updates will follow.

bStocks zero-maker-fee window ends tonight. The zero maker-fee promotion on the five bStocks pairs opened on 21 October (BOTB, BRKBB, KSTRB, TMFB and VSTB against USDT) ends at 31 October 2026 at 23:59 UTC. The promotion never covered taker fees.

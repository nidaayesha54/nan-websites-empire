Binance Resumes Withdrawals in Phases on 1 Nov 2026 After Covering 95% of $6.3 Billion Incident

1 November 2026 · Binance News & Market Check

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 1 November 2026 — so today's article covers the day's biggest verified Binance development: withdrawals began returning in phases on Sunday morning, three days after the exchange suspended them following the security incident disclosed on 30 October. Binance said it had now covered about 95% of the roughly $6.3 billion in tokens affected, up from about 90% in its previous update.

Phase one: what reopened, and when

Binance said phase one of withdrawals would begin on 1 November 2026 between 06:00 and 09:00 UTC (11:00 PKT to 14:00 PKT). The first batch covers 15 currencies across 15 blockchain networks, chosen by recent user demand, according to Binance co-founder Yi He. The phase-one list reported by BusinessToday and Reuters includes Bitcoin (BTC), Ethereum (ETH), BNB, Tron (TRX), Solana (SOL), XRP, Zcash (ZEC), Dogecoin (DOGE), TON, Polygon (POL), Avalanche (AVAX), Arbitrum (ARB), Base and Sui (SUI), alongside supported stablecoins such as USDT and USDC.

Wallets for all other currencies remain under repeated security audits and perimeter checks, Binance said. Their restoration will be prioritised by market capitalisation, locked funds and user base, and Binance has not announced a definitive timeline for the remaining assets. Binance also warned users to expect possible temporary delays or longer processing times during the initial rollout because of increased transaction volumes, and said deposits stayed open throughout the suspension.

Recovery: 95% covered, and what is still outstanding

Binance said it covered the 95% figure by buying replacement tokens through market and over-the-counter channels using company funds, and reiterated that it will absorb the financial impact rather than pass losses to customers. The remaining roughly 5% consists largely of tokens outside the top 10 that are less liquid and take longer to source in the required quantities. About $3 billion of the stolen assets had previously been frozen with the help of industry partners including Tether and Circle, as reported on 31 October.

Binance also said balances in ten major assets — BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB, SOL, USDC, XRP, USD1, RLUSD and ZEC — are now fully backed by reserves. A new proof-of-reserves snapshot was taken on 31 October at 00:00 UTC, and Binance has refreshed its proof-of-reserves wallet list: going forward, every withdrawal will be processed from the newly listed addresses. A full zk-SNARKs cryptographic proof, which would let users independently verify their own balances are included in the reserves, is expected by 3 November 2026, according to Crypto Briefing and ForexCrunch. A detailed post-incident report is promised once the investigation is complete.

One attribution note: the FBI said on Saturday that the North Korea-affiliated unit TraderTraitor was responsible for the attack, according to Reuters reporting. That is the FBI's attribution; Binance itself has not officially attributed the attack.

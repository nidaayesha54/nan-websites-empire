Crypto Market Check (27 Oct 2026): Bitcoin Slips Below $80,000 as Fed Decision Nears

27 October 2026 · Market Check

Bitcoin slipped below $80,000 on 27 October 2026. Reports based on CoinMarketCap data put Bitcoin at about $79,760–$79,861 on the morning of 27 October, down roughly 0.6% over 24 hours. Ethereum traded near $2,306–$2,327, Solana near $100, XRP near $1.28–$1.29, and BNB near $741 at the same time.

Why traders are cautious this week

Market reports point to two main pressures. First, traders are waiting for this week's US Federal Reserve interest-rate decision, with market-based odds reported at over 90% that rates stay unchanged. Second, US spot Bitcoin ETFs saw heavy outflows just before the weekend: about $635.6 million on 22 October and about $137.9 million on 23 October, roughly $773 million over the two days combined, according to reports citing Decrypt and ETF flow data.

The wider market picture was mixed rather than panicked. Total crypto market value was reported at about $2.6744 trillion, with 24-hour trading volume of about $69.21 billion. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index was reported in the neutral range, at 43–50 depending on the source, with Bitcoin dominance near 60%.

Binance news on 27 October: Brazil changes take effect

Separately, Binance's restrictions for users in Brazil took effect on 27 October 2026, under new Central Bank of Brazil rules. According to Binance's official Brazil notice as quoted in news reports, eight products or services are restricted from 27 October: Loans, Binance Pool, Cloud Mining, Margin, Launchpool, Megadrop, HODLer Airdrops and Alpha 2.0. Reports also say 22 tokens stop trading for Brazilian users as part of the same change. Binance's notice says users' funds and assets are protected, and that the changes are operational. Binance Loans move to a repayment-only mode, while existing margin positions can stay open but no new margin orders can be opened from 27 October.

Binance also announced on 27 October that it will carry out wallet maintenance on the Ethereum (ETH) network on 28 October 2026 at 06:00 UTC, suspending ETH deposits and withdrawals from 05:55 UTC for an estimated one hour. Trading itself is not suspended by that maintenance.

What this is — and is not

This is a market check, not a listing announcement: no new Binance spot listing was announced on 27 October in the sources checked for this article. Readers should also be careful with recycled headlines: a widely reshared story about eight tokens being added to Binance's Monitoring Tag dates from March 2026, not 27 October, and is not new news today.

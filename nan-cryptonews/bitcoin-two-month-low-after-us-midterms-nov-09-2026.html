Bitcoin Falls to Two-Month Low After US Midterms as Binance Starts Spot Taker Fee Programme

9 November 2026 · Market Check

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 9 November 2026 — reporting on 7 November noted that no new spot coin has appeared on Binance so far in November, with the exchange's recent notices focused on restoring services after its 29 October security incident. Today's article therefore covers the day's biggest verified development: Bitcoin and Ether fell to their lowest levels since August in late Sunday trading, after Democrats won control of both houses of the US Congress in the midterm elections, while Binance's limited-time Spot Taker Program Promotion begins today.

Bitcoin drops to $73,708, Ether to $2,223

Cryptocurrencies fell sharply in late Sunday trade, with Bitcoin and Ether touching their lowest levels for more than two months, Reuters reported on 9 November. Bitcoin fell by more than 2% to $73,708, its lowest price since August, and Ether fell about 5% to $2,223, also its lowest since August. KuCoin's daily market report put Bitcoin's intraday low slightly lower, at $73,510, with Ether touching $2,060, before both recovered — Bitcoin back above $75,000. Bitcoin closed Sunday at $74,737.90, down 1.34% on the day, completing a fifth consecutive weekly loss, a streak seen only twice before since 2017.

Why the market fell: a Democratic Congress and tougher rules ahead

Investors are worried that a Democratic Party-controlled US Congress will toughen regulation on crypto, Reuters reported. Democrats won control of both the House and the Senate in the US midterm elections on 3 November, and the new Congress takes over in January 2027. The industry-backed CLARITY Act, a bill that would set federal rules for crypto markets, already stalled in the Senate in September. The market was already on edge after the reported $6.3 billion theft from Binance, the largest crypto theft on record. Those are reported market reactions and expectations — no new crypto law has been passed, and the tougher rules investors fear are expectations, not enacted laws. Bitcoin had jumped above $80,000 just after the election, then gave back the gains; on 5 November it fell from $80,882 overnight to $75,573 in the afternoon, and the slide continued into the weekend. CoinGlass data showed about $856 million in crypto positions were liquidated in 24 hours as the sell-off forced traders out.

ETF outflows continue; CPI data due on 10 November

US spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded approximately $412 million in net outflows on Friday, taking weekly withdrawals to nearly $1.3 billion, according to reports from the CoinSwitch Markets Desk and KuCoin. Stronger-than-expected US jobs data — nonfarm payrolls rose by 165,000 — has also raised expectations of a potential Federal Reserve rate hike in December, which weighs on investor sentiment. Looking ahead, the US consumer price index release on 10 November is the next big data point, as inflation data feeds into Federal Reserve rate decisions and market sentiment. Analysts cited by the CoinSwitch Markets Desk put $74,000–$75,000 as a key support zone, with reclaiming $77,000–$78,000 important for recovery. Those are reported analyst views and scheduled events, not a prediction by this site.

Binance Spot Taker Program Promotion begins today

Binance's limited-time Spot Taker Program Promotion takes effect today, 9 November 2026 at 00:00 UTC, and runs to 3 January 2027 at 23:59 UTC, according to Binance's official announcement, published on 6 November. The promotion offers taker fee discounts of 5%, 10% or 15% to eligible users based on their weekly share of spot taker volume on USDT- and USDC-quoted spot pairs. The entry threshold is a weekly spot taker volume share of 0.05%; users with a share of at least 0.05% but below 0.2% qualify for 5%, at least 0.2% but below 1.5% for 10%, and 1.5% or higher for 15%. No application or enrollment is required — qualifying users are identified automatically. Discounts are not applied directly to trades; they are distributed to the master account's Spot Account before the Friday of the week following the discount period. The first assessment week ran from 2 November to 8 November 2026, with the corresponding discount period running from 10 November to 16 November 2026.

Ether, Solana and the wider market

Ether traded at about $2,127.66 on 9 November at 00:00 UTC, down 5.02% over 24 hours, according to KuCoin's daily market report, while Bitcoin was at $74,842.50, down 1.34%. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index read 29 (Fear), up 1 point from the previous day's 28. Bitcoin exchanges recorded net outflows of approximately 4,655 BTC on 7 November, while Ethereum exchanges saw net inflows of around 29,820 ETH over seven days, according to Delta Exchange research. Those price moves are reported market facts, not a forecast of where prices will go next.

What to watch next

The US consumer price index release on 10 November is the next big data point, as inflation data feeds into Federal Reserve rate decisions. Traders are also watching whether Bitcoin can hold the $75,000 level and the $73,500–$74,000 low from Monday morning, and whether it can reclaim $76,000 before attempting a recovery toward $78,000. Binance's next withdrawal-recovery targets — HYPE, INJ, APT, NEAR and LTC — are due by the end of next week, and the first Spot Taker Program discount period runs from 10 November to 16 November. Those are reported analyst views and scheduled events, not a prediction by this site.

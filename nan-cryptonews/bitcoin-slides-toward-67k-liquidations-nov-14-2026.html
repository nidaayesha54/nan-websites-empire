Bitcoin Slides Toward $67,000 on 14 November 2026 as $1.26 Billion in Positions Are Liquidated and Fear Hits 13

14 November 2026 · Market Check

Bitcoin slid toward $67,000 on Saturday, 14 November 2026, extending a week-long decline and trading at its lowest level since mid-August. Bitcoin traded at about $67,380 at around 03:17 UTC, down about 3.3% over 24 hours, according to CoinGecko data cited by The Crypto Times. About $1.26 billion in crypto derivatives positions was liquidated over 24 hours, with long positions — traders betting prices would rise — making up 86.4% of the total. No new Binance spot listing was announced for 14 November 2026.

The sell-off in numbers

The slide continued through Friday and into the early hours of Saturday. Bitcoin's 24-hour range ran from about $67,032 to $70,045 as of 03:17 UTC on 14 November, and it later dipped to about $66,456 late on Friday evening in the United States, according to CoinGecko data cited by The Crypto Times and CoinLab. Bitcoin had briefly bounced back above $70,000 after the US producer price report on Friday, before giving the gains back. Bitcoin is now down about 11.2% in November so far and 17.7% in the fourth quarter, according to Cointelegraph.

CoinGlass data showed 173,014 traders liquidated over 24 hours for a total of about $1.26 billion. Long positions accounted for $1.08 billion, or 86.4% of the total, while short positions made up $170.34 million. Bitcoin positions made up $635.81 million of the total, with longs at 90.5%, while Ether saw about $289.71 million in liquidations and Solana about $62.54 million. The single largest liquidation was a $17.81 million ETHUSDT position on Binance, and Binance accounted for 39.8% of all liquidations, the largest share of any exchange, according to CoinGlass data cited by The Crypto Times.

The global crypto market capitalisation stood at $2.4 trillion, down 3.1% over 24 hours, with total trading volume of $97.7 billion, according to CoinGecko data recorded on 14 November 2026 at 01:30 UTC. Bitcoin's market capitalisation stood at about $1.37 trillion, with Bitcoin dominance at 57.3%. Ether traded at about $1,927.52, down 3.9% over 24 hours. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index fell to 13, in Extreme Fear, down from 20 on 13 November and 25 a week ago, and down from 53 a month ago.

What drove the drop

Three forces lined up against the market at the same time. First, US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded a seventh straight session of net outflows, with about $185.45 million withdrawn on Friday, 13 November, according to SoSoValue data cited by HawkValue. About $284 million left on Thursday, 12 November, its sixth consecutive session of withdrawals, with BlackRock's iShares Bitcoin Trust accounting for about $204 million of that day's outflows, according to SoSoValue data cited by The Crypto Times. The funds have lost roughly $1.72 billion over the six sessions through 12 November.

Second, expectations of tighter US monetary policy kept building after the US October producer price index, published on 13 November by the Bureau of Labor Statistics. The index rose 0.3% on the month, a tenth below the 0.4% forecast, but 6.5% over 12 months — the highest since November 2022 and a tenth below the 6.6% forecast. Core producer prices, which strip out food and energy, rose 0.3% on the month and 5.0% over the year, in line with forecasts. After the data, the CME FedWatch Tool showed an 89.7% chance of a quarter-point Federal Reserve rate hike in December and a 10.3% chance of a half-point move, meaning traders now fully expect some kind of hike. The 10-year US Treasury yield rose to about 5.15%–5.17% on Friday.

Third, technology stocks came under pressure again on Friday, with the S&P 500 falling about 1.2% and the Nasdaq Composite about 1.5% during the session, before closing down 0.4% and 0.91% respectively. Bitcoin treasury firm Strategy is also facing removal from the MSCI USA Index by 30 November, according to MSCI, which added to pressure on crypto-linked shares. Fed Governor Christopher Waller had said in a 10 November speech in London that he was leaning toward a 25 basis point rate hike in December, and that a 50 basis point hike should be considered if core consumer prices rose about 0.3% or more.

One token went the other way

While almost every major asset fell, Akedo (AKE) surged about 382% in 24 hours to about $0.0129, with trading volume of about $533 million, according to CoinGecko data cited by CryptoCompass. Sanctum rose about 78.8% and MetYa about 48.1%. Moves this large should be read alongside market cap, liquidity and volume, because smaller or thinly traded tokens can jump or drop quickly.

Binance today: no new spot listing

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 14 November 2026. Binance's announcement list shows no new spot coin since PREDICT on 29 October, and no next coin has been named. Binance Wallet's previously announced decision to end all support for the Blast network takes effect on 30 November 2026 at 10:00 UTC.

In other exchange and industry news, the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission approved Backpack Clearing as a derivatives clearing organization on 13 November, and Solana Foundation and Nito AI launched Solana Confidential Compute, an open-source toolkit for privacy-focused AI apps, now open for beta testing on devnet. Promotional posts circulating today about a token called RealToken launching on 14 November with a $654.39 trillion figure are unverified hype and have not been confirmed by any exchange announcement; this site does not report them as fact.

What to watch next

The next focus is whether Bitcoin holds the $67,000 area, whether the US spot Bitcoin ETF outflows ease after seven consecutive sessions of withdrawals, and how December rate expectations move before the Federal Reserve's 8–9 December meeting. The Bureau of Economic Analysis will publish October Personal Consumption Expenditures data, the Fed's preferred inflation gauge, on Wednesday, 25 November, and the Bureau of Labor Statistics will release November consumer price data on 10 December. Those are reported scheduled events and analyst views, not a prediction by this site.

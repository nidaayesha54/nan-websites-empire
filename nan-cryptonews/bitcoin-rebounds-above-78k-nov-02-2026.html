Bitcoin Rebounds Above $78,000 on 2 Nov 2026 as Binance Withdrawals Stabilise

2 November 2026 · Binance News & Market Check

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 2 November 2026 — so today's article covers the day's biggest verified development: Bitcoin steadied above $78,000 on Monday after last week's sell-off, even as Binance reported large customer withdrawals following the return of phase-one withdrawals on 1 November.

Bitcoin steadies after Friday's six-week low

Bitcoin rose about 1% on Monday, trading around $78,300 at 1 p.m. in Singapore, according to Bloomberg via The Hindu BusinessLine. Reuters reported Bitcoin up 0.6% at $78,262.63, after falling to a six-week low on Friday. Ether rose 1.14% to $2,287.18, having also slid to a two-month low last week. Invezz reported Bitcoin above $78,200 on Monday, recovering from a drop to about $76,100, and noted the rebound was still below the 50-day moving average near $80,307, with support reported near $77,831. Those levels are reported market reference points, not a forecast of where the price will go.

The rebound follows a difficult October: Bitcoin fell for four consecutive weeks, and the sell-off intensified after Binance disclosed on 30 October that about $6.3 billion had been stolen in a security incident. Binance has said it will absorb the financial impact rather than pass losses to customers, and that about 95% of the affected amount has now been covered. The FBI has attributed the attack to the North Korea-affiliated unit TraderTraitor, according to Reuters reporting; Binance itself has not officially attributed the attack.

Binance withdrawals: $3.64 billion net outflow

Binance customers pulled a net $3.64 billion, or 2.1% of its assets, as the exchange resumed service after the incident, Reuters reported on 2 November. BusinessToday gave the gross figures behind that net number: total withdrawal volumes of over $4.64 billion, offset by $1.01 billion in deposits. Binance said withdrawals were stabilising after an initial spike and that deposit volumes had remained substantial. The exchange's first withdrawal phase, which began on 1 November between 06:00 and 09:00 UTC, covers 15 networks including Bitcoin, Ethereum, BNB, Solana and XRP. Wallets for other assets remain under security checks, and Binance has not announced a definitive timeline for the remaining assets.

US spot Bitcoin ETFs: $643 million out on Friday

US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded $643 million in net outflows on Friday, their first trading session following the hack, according to Invezz and NDTV's market coverage. That outflow ended a four-day inflow streak and took weekly net outflows to about $202 million. Friday's session also saw heavy futures liquidations reported in earlier coverage, with about $1.57 billion in futures positions liquidated around the incident, according to NDTV's crypto update.

What to watch this week

Traders are watching a packed week ahead, Reuters reported: US midterm elections on Tuesday 3 November, October US jobs data, and central-bank decisions in Britain, Australia, Norway and Sweden. One Binance deadline also falls this week: VELODROME/USDT trading ends on 3 November at 03:00 UTC, as part of the previously announced Velodrome-to-Aerodrome (AERO) migration, under which each VELODROME token swaps into 0.044 AERO. The full proof-of-reserves cryptographic proof Binance indicated for 3 November is also due, according to earlier reporting.

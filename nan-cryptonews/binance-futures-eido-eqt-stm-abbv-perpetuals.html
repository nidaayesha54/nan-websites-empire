Binance Futures Launches EIDO, EQT, STM and ABBV USDT Perpetual Contracts

26 October 2026 · Futures Launch

Binance Futures launched four new USDT-margined perpetual contracts on Monday, 26 October 2026, covering traditional-finance underlyings: the iShares MSCI Indonesia ETF (EIDO), EQT Corporation (EQT), STMicroelectronics (STM) and AbbVie (ABBV). The four contracts opened in a 15-minute window, five minutes apart.

Launch schedule — 26 October 2026

  • EIDOUSDT — 09:00 UTC (14:00 PKT)
  • EQTUSDT — 09:05 UTC (14:05 PKT)
  • STMUSDT — 09:10 UTC (14:10 PKT)
  • ABBVUSDT — 09:15 UTC (14:15 PKT)

The underlyings are the iShares MSCI Indonesia ETF (NYSE Arca) for EIDO, and common stock listed on the NYSE for EQT, STMicroelectronics and AbbVie. These are perpetual derivatives contracts settled in USDT — they track the price of the underlying asset; they are not the shares or ETF units themselves.

Contract terms

According to Binance's announcement, as reported on 26 October 2026, all four contracts share the same terms:

  • USDT settlement and margin
  • Maximum leverage of 20x
  • Minimum notional value of 5 USDT
  • Trading available 24/7
  • Funding rate capped at ±2%, settling every eight hours, with a 0% interest component
  • Multi-Assets Mode supported

Binance notes that the products may not be available in some regions, and that it may adjust trading parameters and margin requirements.

Market backdrop on the day

The launch comes on a cautious day for crypto markets. Early on 26 October 2026, Bitcoin traded near $80,100, close to the $80,000 level it slipped below over the weekend, while Ethereum traded around $2,319–$2,322, according to market reports published that morning. The US Federal Reserve's interest-rate decision is due on 28 October 2026, an event traders are watching ahead of the week's trading.

Binance Pool Shuts Down on 10 November 2026 as Bitcoin Holds Near $75,000

10 November 2026 · Binance News

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 10 November 2026 — reporting on 7 November noted that no new spot coin has appeared on Binance so far in November, with the exchange's recent notices focused on restoring services after its 29 October security incident. Today's article therefore covers the day's biggest verified Binance development: Binance Pool and its related services ended today, 10 November 2026, at 07:00 UTC, closing about six and a half years of Bitcoin mining pool operations, while Binance also launched a third Community Appreciation Campaign offering rewards of up to 15% APR.

Binance Pool ends after six and a half years

Binance announced in October that it would discontinue Binance Pool and related services on 10 November 2026, with miners disconnected from 07:00 UTC. The affected services are Binance Pool, Pool Savings, Cloud Mining and the Transaction Accelerator, and related features including mining pool APIs, hashrate notifications and mining endpoints are also being disabled or discontinued. Binance attributed the closure to an "ongoing review of products and services" and gave no more specific reason.

The pool's activity had already faded sharply before the shutdown. Binance Pool mined only one of roughly 925 Bitcoin blocks found network-wide over the seven days before the shutdown, about 0.11% of the total, and found no blocks at all in the 24 hours before the report, according to CoinDesk reporting published on 9 November 2026. Its latest identified block was block 974,743, mined on 6 November at 01:15 UTC. The pool's hashrate fell from about 26 exahashes per second (EH/s) in December 2025 to about 9.2 EH/s in October 2026, a decline of roughly 65%.

Third Community Appreciation Campaign offers up to 15% APR

Binance also launched its third Community Appreciation Campaign, announced on 10 November 2026, offering eligible users rewards of up to 15% APR across three products. Holders of USD1 can earn up to 10% APR from 10 November 00:00 UTC to 4 December 00:00 UTC by holding USD1 in Spot, Margin, Futures, Funding and Earn accounts, with no individual reward cap on the all-holdings portion. Holders of at least 0.01 USDe for 24 hours can earn up to 10% APR from 11 November 00:00 UTC to 30 November 23:59 UTC, made up of a 5.25% base APR and a 4.75% bonus tiered APR on balances up to 100,000 USDe. Subscribers to KGST Flexible Products can receive up to 15% APR, including a 10% bonus tiered APR and a 5% real-time APR, from 12 November 08:00 UTC to 11 December 23:59:59 UTC. Binance said reward distributions are scheduled for 20 November, 27 November and 4 December 2026, with no distribution on 13 November.

Bitcoin steadies near $75,000 after touching its lowest since August

Bitcoin traded at approximately $74,765 as of 06:00 UTC on 10 November 2026, up about 0.5% over 24 hours, after recovering from a low near $73,700, according to CoinGecko data reported by Crypto Times. Over the previous 24 hours Bitcoin had traded between approximately $73,709 and $75,477. The recovery follows the sharp decline that took Bitcoin to $73,708 on 8 November, its lowest level since August, as reported by Reuters on 9 November. The total crypto market capitalisation stood at about $2.51 trillion, down 1.38% over 24 hours, with about $71.81 billion in 24-hour trading volume. Ether traded near $2,117, down about 0.13% over 24 hours. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index stood at 31–32, indicating fear among market participants.

ETF outflows continue and CPI data is due today

US spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds recorded $201 million in net outflows on 9 November, according to Farside Investors' daily flow data, pushing cumulative November withdrawals to about $1.48 billion. That followed $1.28 billion in net outflows from 2 to 6 November, the largest weekly outflow since late June, according to Farside data reported by The Block. Liquidations across the crypto market totalled roughly $269 million over the prior 24 hours, affecting about 80,801 traders, according to CoinGlass data. Investor attention is now on the US October consumer price index report, due on 10 November at 8:30 a.m. Eastern Time. Consensus expects headline CPI of +0.3% month on month and 3.9% year on year, according to StoneX, and CME FedWatch odds of a quarter-point Federal Reserve rate hike at the 8–9 December meeting stood at 76.8%. The CPI release is a scheduled event, not a prediction by this site.

US Treasury moves against MEXC

Separately, the US Treasury moved to cut off crypto exchange MEXC from the US financial system, alleging at least $16 billion in transactions tied to sanctions evasion and cybercrime, according to reporting summarised by CoinStats on 10 November 2026. No further official detail was available at the time of writing.

What to watch next

The US October CPI release later today is the immediate test for markets, with the December Federal Reserve meeting the next focus. Traders are also watching whether Bitcoin can hold the $74,000–$75,000 zone and reclaim $76,000, and whether US spot Bitcoin ETF outflows ease after $1.48 billion in November withdrawals so far. The first Spot Taker Program discount period runs from 10 November to 16 November, and Binance's withdrawal-recovery targets — HYPE, INJ, APT, NEAR and LTC — are due by the end of next week. Those are reported analyst views and scheduled events, not a prediction by this site.

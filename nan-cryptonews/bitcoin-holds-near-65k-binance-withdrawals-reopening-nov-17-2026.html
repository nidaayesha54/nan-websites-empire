Bitcoin Holds Near $65,000 on 17 November 2026 as Binance Targets Full Withdrawal Reopening

17 November 2026 · Market Check

Bitcoin traded near $64,700 on Tuesday, 17 November 2026, holding close to a three-month low after the weekend crash to about $62,000. CoinGecko data recorded at 1:30 a.m. UTC put Bitcoin at about $64,713.90, down about 0.1% over 24 hours, with 24-hour trading volume of about $32.84 billion and a market capitalisation of about $1.3 trillion. Finnhub data on the Binance BTC/USDT pair showed Bitcoin at $64,695.51 at 07:10 UTC, with an intraday range of $63,905.01 to $65,696.16. The global crypto market capitalisation stood at about $2.29 trillion, up 1.2% over 24 hours, according to CoinGecko data cited by CoinStats. No new Binance spot listing was announced for 17 November 2026.

A week of losses, and sentiment still at Extreme Fear

Bitcoin was down about 13.5% over seven days, according to Moneycontrol, after sliding from around $75,000 at the start of last week to a three-month low near $64,000. Ether traded around $1,820 to $1,838 on 17 November, down about 0.8% over 24 hours according to KuCoin's daily market report, while XRP gained about 1% to about $0.96, according to Brave New Coin. CryptoQuant analysis reported on 17 November showed Bitcoin's Market Value to Realized Value (MVRV) ratio at about 1.1, a level last seen around the 2022 market bottom, and a 30-day apparent demand reading of about negative 247,700 BTC as of 15 November.

The Crypto Fear & Greed Index stood at 12 on 17 November, in Extreme Fear, according to KuCoin's daily market report, which noted the previous reading was 10. Moneycontrol reported the index at 13 on the day, also in Extreme Fear. The sell-off has been driven by continued institutional outflows, tightening crypto liquidity and expectations of another US Federal Reserve rate increase, according to Moneycontrol.

US spot Bitcoin ETFs recorded about $2.42 billion in net outflows between 2 and 16 November, according to Moneycontrol, extending a run of consecutive outflow sessions that began on 5 November. Traders were watching for signs of whether the outflow streak would break, with US retail sales data due at 8:30 a.m. Eastern on 17 November and Nvidia earnings due later the same day.

Binance: full withdrawal reopening targeted by end of next week

Binance said on Saturday, 14 November, that it aims to reopen withdrawals for all remaining networks and assets by the end of next week, as operations normalise after the security breach in which about $6.3 billion was stolen from the platform in late October. "This week, we reopened withdrawals for 87 networks. We will aim to reopen withdrawals for all remaining networks and assets by the end of next week," the company said in an X post reported on 17 November. Binance reopened withdrawals on 87 networks on 14 November, continuing the phased restoration that began on 31 October after withdrawals were paused following the breach.

Binance also launched two promotions starting today, 17 November. A Black Friday campaign for its Lifestyle Marketplace runs from 17 November to 14 December 2026, offering up to 20% cashback to first-time buyers, capped at 5 USDT per order on orders of at least 5 USDT equivalent, with a total reward pool of 10,000 USDT distributed on a first-come, first-served basis. Rewards will be distributed after Binance verifies eligible order activity by 30 December 2026. Separately, a LATAM P2P promotion runs from 17 November to 17 December 2026, offering eligible Latin American users a 25% special annual percentage rate for seven days on up to 500 USDC subscribed to USDC Flexible Products, alongside real-time APR rewards.

Binance also announced it will adjust tick sizes for six USDⓈ-M perpetual futures contracts — CYSUSDT, PLAYUSDT, CFGUSDT, CTKUSDT, 1000000MOGUSDT and DYDXUSDT — at 06:30 UTC on 18 November 2026. The exchange said the change will not affect trading operations or existing orders.

No new Binance spot listing

No new Binance spot coin listing was announced for 17 November 2026; the most recent spot listing was PREDICT on 29 October, whose trading start was postponed that day. Binance's most recent futures additions, the HYPEUSDC and TRXUSDC perpetual contracts, launched on 11 November 2026 at 09:30 and 09:35 UTC with up to 75x leverage and USDC settlement, followed by the NUUSDT and FRHCUSDT equity-linked perpetuals on 12 November. Binance has also announced that support for the Blast network on Binance Wallet will cease at 10:00 UTC on 30 November 2026.

What to watch next

US October retail sales are published at 8:30 a.m. Eastern on 17 November, and Nvidia reports earnings the same day. The Federal Reserve publishes the minutes of its 27–28 October meeting at 2 p.m. Eastern on Wednesday, 18 November. The immediate market question is whether Bitcoin can hold above $64,000 and whether the run of ETF outflows breaks. Those are reported scheduled events, not a prediction by this site.

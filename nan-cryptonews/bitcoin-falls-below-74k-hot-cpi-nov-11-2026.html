Bitcoin Slides Below $74,000 After Hot US Inflation Report as Binance Halts MEXC Transfers

11 November 2026 · Market Check

No new Binance spot listing was announced for 11 November 2026 — reporting on 10 November noted that no new spot coin has appeared on Binance so far in November, with the exchange's recent notices focused on restoring services after its 29 October security incident. Today's article therefore covers the day's biggest verified market development: Bitcoin fell below $74,000 after the US October consumer price index came in hotter than expected, raising the odds of a Federal Reserve rate hike in December, while Binance said it will stop processing transfers involving rival exchange MEXC after a US Treasury designation.

Hot October CPI pushes rate-hike odds higher

The US Bureau of Labor Statistics reported on 10 November that the consumer price index rose 0.4% in October, above the 0.3% consensus forecast, according to reporting by CoinStats and Crypto Feed News. Core CPI, which excludes food and energy, rose 0.3% against a forecast of 0.2%. It was the third straight monthly inflation print above expectations, according to Yahoo Finance data cited by CoinStats. Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller said he would support a 25-basis-point rate increase at the December meeting even if inflation improved, and that a core reading of around 0.3% could justify a 50-basis-point move. Traders now price an 85% chance of a December rate hike, according to Crypto Feed News.

Bitcoin falls toward $73,500 as ETF outflows mount

Bitcoin traded at approximately $73,792 on 11 November 2026, down about 1.94% over 24 hours, according to CoinStats, and was about 41.47% below its all-time high of $126,080. TradingView data showed Bitcoin touching about $72,815 during the sell-off, close to its lowest level since 21 August, according to Crypto Feed News. Over the previous 24 hours Bitcoin traded between approximately $73,400 and $75,477, according to SmartXSignals. Bitcoin had already fallen about $2,200 in 15 minutes on 8 November to roughly $73,900, its lowest level since August. Ether traded near $2,074, down about 2.5% over 24 hours, Solana traded near $84.00, down about 3.7%, and XRP traded near $1.064, down about 3.3%, according to SmartXSignals. The Crypto Fear & Greed Index stood at 32, in Fear, up from 29 on Monday, according to SmartXSignals.

US spot Bitcoin exchange-traded funds lost $1.28 billion in the week of 2 to 6 November, their largest weekly outflow since late June, according to SoSoValue data cited by CoinDesk. Analysts quoted by CoinDesk identified $73,000 as a key support level, with a break below it potentially accelerating selling. US stock and bond markets are closed on 11 November for Veterans Day, and no major US economic releases are scheduled, according to SmartXSignals.

Binance halts transfers with MEXC after FinCEN designation

Binance announced on 10 November 2026 at 08:30 UTC that it would cease processing transactions involving MEXC Global, along with 12 named entities and aliases plus MEXC affiliates, according to CoinCourier. Outgoing transfers from Binance wallets to the listed parties will be declined, while incoming deposits will face temporary holds for compliance review that could lead to account restrictions or offboarding. The move came four days after the US Treasury's Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) designated MEXC Global as a primary money laundering concern on 6 November 2026. Treasury documents allege MEXC facilitated at least $16 billion in illicit transactions from May 2021 through May 2026, including roughly $1 billion in direct dealings with more than 40 Iranian crypto exchangers. FinCEN's order bars US financial institutions from processing transfers involving MEXC, but takes effect only 30 days after publication in the Federal Register; Binance's platform-level restrictions operate immediately and apply globally to its own users.

Binance launches HYPE and TRX perpetual contracts today

Binance Futures will launch the HYPEUSDC perpetual contract at 17:30 Beijing Time on 11 November and the TRXUSDC perpetual contract at 17:35 Beijing Time, according to Lookonchain. Both contracts are settled in USDC, support up to 75x leverage, and will open contract copy trading within 24 hours of their respective launches. These are futures contracts, not new spot listings.

What to watch next

The next focus is the December Federal Reserve meeting on 8–9 December, with traders watching further Fed signals after the hot CPI print. Markets are also watching whether Bitcoin can hold the $73,000 support level, whether US spot Bitcoin ETF outflows ease after $1.28 billion in weekly withdrawals, and whether Binance's withdrawal-recovery targets — HYPE, INJ, APT, NEAR and LTC — are met by the end of next week. The PREDICT airdrop's next scheduled release date is 13 November, with batches consolidated if the token launches after a scheduled date. Those are reported analyst views and scheduled events, not a prediction by this site.

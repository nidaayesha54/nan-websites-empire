Crypto Market Check (25 Oct 2026): Bitcoin Near $80,800, ZEC Leads the Week

26 October 2026 · Market Check

The total crypto market is around $2.7–2.8 trillion. Bitcoin trades near $80,800, up 1.2% on the week. Altcoins have been weak overall: one analyst note says the median top-500 token fell about 5.8% over the 17–18 October weekend, the steepest weekend drop since April.

Who led the last 7 days?

Among large coins, three stood out:

  • $ZEC +5.8% (about $1,218)
  • $BNB +3.6% (about $747)
  • $HYPE +3.2% (about $77)

Bitcoin gained 1.2% over the same week. Strength over a week is a signal to watch, not a guarantee it continues.

Bitcoin sets the mood

BTC sits just under $81K. Analyst levels from 19 October put support at $81,700–$82,000 and $81,000, a demand area at $80,000, and resistance at $82,700–$83,000. Price is now below that support zone. A drop through $80K would pressure altcoins; a reclaim of $82K+ would help them. Spot Bitcoin ETFs also saw about $228M of net outflows during 12–16 October.

Latest Binance listings

Only one spot listing so far in October: RWUSD (13 October). The previous one was $HYPE (24 September). Most recent listings carry Binance's Seed Tag, its label for newer, higher-risk projects. Treat any "next Binance listing" claim as a rumour until Binance announces it.

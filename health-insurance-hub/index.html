The amount you pay each year for covered care before your insurance starts sharing costs. After you meet it, you typically pay coinsurance (a percentage) until you hit your out-of-pocket maximum — then insurance pays 100%. Try our interactive explainer to see it visually.

Premium tax credits cap what you pay for a benchmark Silver plan at a percentage of your income (about 2–9.8% depending on income). The government pays the insurer the difference each month. Estimate yours with our 2026 subsidy estimator.

HMOs cost less but require a primary-care referral for specialists and cover almost nothing out-of-network. PPOs cost more but let you see specialists directly and cover some out-of-network care.

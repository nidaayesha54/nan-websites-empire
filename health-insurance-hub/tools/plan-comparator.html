How true annual cost is calculated

For each plan and each usage scenario: true annual cost = 12 × monthly premium + your share of the medical bills. Your share of bills is min(OOP max, min(bills, deductible) + coinsurance × (bills − deductible)) — you pay the deductible first, then your coinsurance percentage, capped by the out-of-pocket maximum.

  • Low usage ($1,500): routine care year — cheap-premium, high-deductible plans usually win.
  • Medium usage ($8,000): an ER visit or outpatient procedure — balanced plans often win.
  • High usage ($30,000): hospitalization — low-OOP-max plans usually win despite high premiums.

Note: Premiums are sunk cost — you pay them even in a healthy year. That's why comparing premium + expected out-of-pocket beats comparing premiums alone.

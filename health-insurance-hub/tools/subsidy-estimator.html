How the subsidy estimate works

The ACA premium tax credit caps what you pay for a benchmark Silver plan at a percentage of your income, based on where your income falls against the federal poverty level (FPL):

  • FPL (2026 approx): $15,650 for one person + $5,490 per additional household member.
  • Your share: slides from 2.07% of income at 133% FPL to 9.83% at 400% FPL (8.5% cap above 400% under extended rules).
  • Subsidy = benchmark premium − your share. If that's negative, the subsidy is $0.
  • Benchmark: we use a ~$550/month national average second-lowest-cost Silver plan, adjusted up for high-cost states (AK, HI, NY-area).

Note: These are 2026 estimates for education. Subsidy law has changed before and can change again — confirm your actual credit at enrollment on Healthcare.gov or your state exchange.

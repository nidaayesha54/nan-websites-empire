    The three terms, in plain English

    • Deductible: what you pay first each year before insurance helps (except free preventive care and copay visits).
    • Coinsurance: after the deductible, you pay this percentage and the insurer pays the rest — e.g., 20% coinsurance on a $10,000 balance = $2,000 from you.
    • Out-of-pocket max: your yearly safety net. Once your deductible + coinsurance (+ copays) hit this number, the insurer pays 100% of covered care for the rest of the year.

    The math: you pay = min(OOP max, min(bill, deductible) + coinsurance × (bill − deductible)). Try the extremes: a $0 deductible with 50% coinsurance vs a $10,000 deductible with 10% coinsurance — the OOP max decides more than people think.

    Note: Premiums are separate — you pay those every month regardless. The explainer shows cost-sharing only.

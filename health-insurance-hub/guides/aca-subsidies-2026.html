ACA Subsidies in 2026, Explained

2026-10-12 · 7 min read

Roughly 9 in 10 marketplace shoppers qualify for financial help — yet millions never check. Here's exactly how ACA subsidies work in 2026.

The two kinds of help

  • Premium tax credits lower your monthly premium. Available roughly between 100% and 400%+ of the federal poverty level (FPL).
  • Cost-sharing reductions (CSRs) lower your deductible and out-of-pocket costs — but only on Silver plans, and only up to 250% of FPL.

How your subsidy is calculated

The formula caps what you pay for the benchmark Silver plan (second-lowest-cost Silver in your area) at a percentage of your income: about 2.07% of income at 133% of FPL, sliding up to 9.83% at 400% of FPL. The government pays the rest. Get your number with our 2026 subsidy estimator.

Who qualifies?

  • Income roughly 100–400%+ of FPL (about $15,650–$62,600+ for one person in 2026; higher for families).
  • No access to affordable employer coverage and not eligible for Medicaid/Medicare.
  • Lawfully present in the US and filing taxes (married couples generally must file jointly).
  • Below 100% FPL? You likely qualify for Medicaid instead (in expansion states).

How to claim it

  1. Apply on Healthcare.gov (or your state exchange) during open enrollment.
  2. Report your estimated income for the coverage year — most people take it as advance payments that lower each month's bill directly.
  3. Reconcile at tax time: earn more than estimated and you may repay some; earn less and you may get extra credit.

Note: Subsidy rules have been extended and changed before — always confirm current-year rules at enrollment rather than relying on last year's experience. Estimates on this site are educational, not official determinations.

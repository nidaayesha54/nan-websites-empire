Open Enrollment 2026: Dates & Checklist

2026-10-12 · 5 min read

Miss the window and you're generally locked out until next year (unless you qualify for a special enrollment period). Here's everything to handle before the deadline.

Key 2026 dates

  • Healthcare.gov states: open enrollment typically runs November 1 – January 15. Enroll by December 15 for coverage starting January 1.
  • State exchanges (CA, NY, CO, etc.) set their own windows — several extend past January 15. Check your state's exchange.
  • Employer plans: usually a 2–4 week window in the fall — set by your employer, often October/November.
  • Medicare: October 15 – December 7 (separate from marketplace open enrollment).

Your 10-point checklist

  1. Confirm your doctors are still in-network (networks change yearly).
  2. Check your prescriptions' formulary tier and cost.
  3. Estimate next year's care: any planned surgeries, pregnancies, or new diagnoses?
  4. Compare true yearly cost with our plan comparator — not just premiums.
  5. Check your subsidy estimate — income changes alter your credit.
  6. If picking Silver and income-qualifying, remember CSRs only apply to Silver.
  7. Decide on HSA vs FSA contributions for the year.
  8. Review dental/vision add-ons separately — they're rarely in medical plans.
  9. Update income and household info so advance credits are accurate.
  10. Save/print your enrollment confirmation and first premium due date.

Note: Auto-renewal is convenient but dangerous: plans change premiums, networks, and formularies every year. A 20-minute review beats a nasty January surprise at the pharmacy.

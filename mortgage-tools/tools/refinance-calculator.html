How the math works

Both payments use the standard mortgage formula on the same balance. Break-even (months) = closing costs ÷ monthly savings. Lifetime savings = all remaining payments on the old loan − all payments on the new loan − closing costs. A longer new term can lower the payment while raising lifetime cost — watch both numbers.

Tips

  • Forget the old "1% rule" — break-even math beats rules of thumb.
  • A no-closing-cost refinance just rolls costs into a higher rate; compare its break-even too.
  • If you will move or refinance again before break-even, do not refinance now.

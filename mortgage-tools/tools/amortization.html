How the math works

Each month, interest = remaining balance × APR/12; the rest of your fixed payment reduces principal. Early payments are mostly interest — on a 30-year loan at 6.5%, only about 15% of your first payment touches principal. The table recomputes this split for every payment until the balance hits zero.

Tips

  • Extra principal payments early in the loan save the most interest.
  • Use the CSV download to model "what if I pay $200 extra?" scenarios in a spreadsheet.
  • Biweekly payments (26 half-payments/year) equal one extra full payment annually.

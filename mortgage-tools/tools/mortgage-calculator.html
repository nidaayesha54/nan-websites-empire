How the math works

Principal & interest uses the standard amortization formula: M = P·r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1), with r = APR/12 and n = years × 12. Property tax and insurance are divided by 12 and added, plus any PMI, to give PITI — the number lenders actually underwrite.

M = P·r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)  ·  PITI = M + tax/12 + insurance/12 + PMI

Tips

  • Lenders generally want PITI under 28% of gross monthly income.
  • Less than 20% down usually means PMI ($30–$70 per $100k borrowed, monthly) until you reach 20% equity.
  • One extra payment per year on a 30-year loan cuts roughly 4–5 years off the term.

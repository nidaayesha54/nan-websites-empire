GTA 6 Price in the Philippines: What Filipino Players Pay

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and in the Philippines the first question was never the date — it was the price. The dollar price is simple: $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition. The peso price is not simple at all. Converted at the exchange rate it works out to roughly P4,900 and P6,100, but the price you actually pay depends on where you buy, and the cheapest-looking option has a trap hiding inside the box.

The honest answer is that there is no single peso price for GTA 6. Digital, retail and converted prices all point to different numbers, and several of the numbers circulating online come from different dates, before and after the peso price appeared in the stores. This guide lines them all up, explains which one applies to you, and flags the one mistake that has already cost Filipino players money.

The peso prices, side by side

Here is every price that matters, as reported in November 2026. The digital price on the Philippine PlayStation Store is reported at P4,190 for the Standard Edition and P5,290 for the Ultimate Edition. That is the price for players who buy straight from the store on their console, and it is the lowest price available in pesos.

Retail is a different story. Shops like DataBlitz, GameLine and iTech list the boxed version at P4,990 to P5,390 for the Standard Edition, with the Ultimate Edition at P6,100 to P6,500. Those boxes cost more because the shop is selling you a code in a box, not a disc, and the shop adds its own margin on top. Pre-orders at these shops usually asked for a P500 downpayment, with the balance due on pickup.

A third set of numbers comes from straight conversion. GMA News put the base edition at about P4,900 and the Ultimate at about P6,100, converted from $80 and $100. Those figures are useful as a sanity check, but they are not store prices — the exchange rate moves, and your bank adds its own charges on a foreign payment. If a number you see does not match any of the three above, treat it as an estimate, not a price.

The box has no disc — and the code has a catch

Every boxed copy of GTA 6 is a code in a box. There is no disc inside, on PS5 or on Xbox. The box is a keepsake with the cover art on it, and the code inside is the whole game. That matters twice over in the Philippines, because the code in the Asian boxed edition can only be redeemed on PlayStation accounts registered in Hong Kong, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Taiwan or Thailand. Philippine PSN accounts are not on that list.

That is the trap. A player who buys the Asian boxed copy from a local shop and tries to redeem it on a Philippine account will find the code does not work on that account. The fix is to redeem it on an account registered in one of the supported regions — which is how many Filipino players already run their accounts — or to skip the box entirely and buy digital from the store on the console. If your account is Philippine-registered and you have no second account, the digital store is the safe route.

Digital vs retail: which should you buy?

Digital is the simplest path. You buy from the PlayStation Store or Xbox Store, the game pre-loads from November 10, and it unlocks at midnight local time on November 19. Digital pre-orders also included one month of GTA+ and the Vintage Vice City Pack. The only thing you need is enough storage: the download is up to 212 GB, depending on the device, so clear space before pre-load day.

Retail makes sense if you want the box on your shelf, or if you pay in cash and cannot use a card on the digital store. The game is the same, the unlock time is the same, and the code in the box downloads the same 212 GB. What retail does not give you is a disc to resell later, so do not pay a premium expecting resale value. Compare the shop price against the digital price before you hand over the downpayment — the gap between P4,190 digital and P4,990 retail is P800 for a box.

What you need to play

GTA 6 runs only on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. It does not run on PS4 or Xbox One, and there is no PC version — Rockstar has announced no PC date. If you are buying a console for the game, the PS5 Pro in the Philippines was selling for about P56,950 for the 2TB model in mid-2026, and a base PS5 is enough to play the game. Either way, budget for the 212 GB download on top of the game price, and a stable connection for pre-load.

Scams to avoid

Every big launch brings fake offers, and GTA 6 has had more than most. Fake pre-order pages, discount key sites and "free download" offers are all fake — there is no free download, no APK and no PC version, and any site offering one is selling malware or a stolen account. The rule is simple: buy only from the PlayStation Store, the Xbox Store, or a trusted local retailer like DataBlitz or GameOne. If a deal looks far cheaper than P4,190, it is not a deal.

FAQ

How much is GTA 6 in the Philippines? About P4,190 digital for the Standard Edition and P5,290 for the Ultimate, as reported for the Philippine PlayStation Store. Retail boxed copies run P4,990 to P5,390. Converted from dollars, the price is roughly P4,900 and P6,100.

Does the physical copy have a disc? No. The box contains a download code only. The code in the Asian edition works on accounts registered in Hong Kong, Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Taiwan or Thailand — not on Philippine-registered accounts.

Can I play GTA 6 on PS4? No. The game is PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and no PC version has been announced.

The bottom line: in the Philippines, digital at P4,190 is the cheapest safe price, retail costs P800 more for a box with a code inside, and the one thing to check before you pay is which account region your code works on.

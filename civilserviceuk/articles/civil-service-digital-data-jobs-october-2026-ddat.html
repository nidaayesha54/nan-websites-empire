Government Digital and Data (DDaT) roles are among the best-paid in the Civil Service. In the October 2026 cycle, adverts on Civil Service Jobs included senior engineering, content design and digital delivery posts at the Ministry of Justice, HM Courts and Tribunals Service and the Forestry Commission.

Pay across these three adverts ranged from £40,388 to £70,725 — rates designed to compete with the private tech sector. These campaigns carried early-October closing dates; always check the live portal before applying.

Closing dates: the October 2026 DDaT adverts below carried early-October closing dates and may now be closed. Treat this as a guide to the kinds of roles the profession recruits for — the salaries and working patterns shown come from the live adverts; always confirm on civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk.

What Is the DDaT Profession?

The Government Digital and Data profession covers the Civil Service's digital, data and technology roles across departments: service designers, user researchers, delivery managers, data analysts, content designers and engineers — the people who build and run public services.

How DDaT Roles Are Assessed

DDaT roles are assessed against the DDaT capability framework — the skills standard for each role family — alongside the Civil Service Success Profiles framework of behaviours, strengths, experience, technical skills and ability tests. Map your evidence directly to the framework the advert names.

How to Apply, Step by Step

Search the profession. Filter civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk by the DDaT profession to find live digital and data roles.
Read the advert. Note the salary, working pattern, closing date and which DDaT capability levels are assessed.
Match your evidence. Map your experience to the DDaT framework and the Success Profiles behaviours listed.
Write STAR examples. Use Situation, Task, Action, Result with measurable outcomes for each assessed behaviour.
Apply early. Digital roles can attract hundreds of applications — submit well before the closing date.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the DDaT profession?

Government Digital and Data: the Civil Service profession covering digital, data and technology roles across departments.

What salaries do Civil Service digital roles pay?

October 2026 adverts included £58,511–£70,725 (MoJ Senior DevOps Engineer), £42,914–£53,081 (HMCTS Content Designer) and £40,388–£43,482 (Forestry Commission ELM Manager).

Against the DDaT capability framework plus Civil Service Success Profiles.

Can I work remotely in Civil Service digital roles?

On civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk, searchable by the DDaT profession.

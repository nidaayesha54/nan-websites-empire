HM Treasury Graduate Policy Programme 2026–2027: Become a Policy Adviser — Deadline 5 November 2026

The HM Treasury Graduate Policy Programme 2026–2027 recruits Policy Advisers to work at the heart of UK government on the economy, public spending and fiscal policy. Open to graduates of all degree subjects — applications close at 12 noon on 5 November 2026.

Few graduate schemes carry the weight of the HM Treasury Graduate Policy Programme. For the 2026–2027 campaign, HM Treasury is recruiting graduates as Policy Advisers — roles at the heart of UK government, at the centre of decisions about the economy, public spending and the nation’s finances. Applications close at 12 noon on 5 November 2026, and the programme is open to graduates of all degree subjects, not just economics.

What a Policy Adviser at HM Treasury Does

As a Policy Adviser on the programme, you work on the policy issues that shape the UK economy — from public spending decisions and fiscal events such as Budgets and Spending Reviews, to growth, financial services and tax policy. The work is analytical and fast-moving: Treasury policy advisers brief ministers, contribute to submissions that go to the Chancellor, and coordinate with departments across the Civil Service, turning complex information into advice decision-makers can act on.

Who Can Apply

  • Degree subject: all degree subjects are eligible — you do not need an economics degree. Candidates with humanities, science, social science and other backgrounds apply alongside economists.
  • Academic conditions: the advert sets the required academic standard — check the official listing for the exact degree requirement.
  • Nationality: standard Civil Service nationality conditions apply.
  • Right to work: you must hold the right to work in the UK.
  • Security clearance: security-clearance conditions apply; the advert explains the level required and any related nationality or residency conditions.

The broad eligibility is part of the appeal: the programme explicitly opens the door to every discipline, judging candidates on their potential to do the work rather than their degree title. That, plus proximity to the highest levels of government, makes it one of the most sought-after early-career routes in British public life.

How to Apply

Applications are made through the official HM Treasury / Civil Service recruitment advert. Follow these steps:

1
Find the HM Treasury Graduate Policy Programme 2026–2027 advert on the official recruitment portal and read it in full.
2
Check the academic, nationality, UK right-to-work and security-clearance conditions against your own situation.
3
Prepare your application in your own words, with evidence of analytical ability, clear communication and motivation for public service.
4
Submit before 12 noon on 5 November 2026 — late applications will not be accepted.

Who can apply for the HM Treasury Graduate Policy Programme 2026–2027?

What is the entry role on the HM Treasury graduate programme?

About the Role

The Senior Digital Governance and Assurance Manager sits within DVSA's digital function, providing governance and assurance across digital services and projects — making sure delivery meets government digital standards and agreed frameworks. DVSA is continuing to modernise its digital estate, from customer-facing services to the internal tools used by examiners and enforcement staff across Great Britain. The post suits candidates with experience in digital delivery, assurance, risk management or governance in the public or private sector.

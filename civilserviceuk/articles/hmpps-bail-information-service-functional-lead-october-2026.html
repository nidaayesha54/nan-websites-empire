The Role: Functional Lead, Bail Information Service

BIS teams gather and verify information about defendants — accommodation, community ties, risk factors — and present it to courts to inform bail and remand decisions. As Functional Lead, you will be accountable for the performance of that function across your area: setting standards, assuring quality, managing stakeholders including courts and probation colleagues, and leading the people who deliver the service day to day.

This is a leadership role first and a subject-matter role second. You will need to show you can run a high-stakes operational function: managing performance, driving improvement, handling escalations, and maintaining the trust of judicial stakeholders who depend on the accuracy of BIS outputs.

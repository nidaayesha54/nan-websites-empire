Border Force Officer Jobs October 2026: Tests, Eligibility & How to Apply | NAN Jobs Portal

Border Force hires officers through Civil Service Jobs — here is exactly how the application stages, online tests and deadlines work.

Home › Articles › Border Force Officer Jobs October 2026: Tests, Eligibility & How to Apply | NAN Jobs Portal

Published 15 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

Border Force — part of the Home Office — advertises Border Force Officer roles via Civil Service Jobs. Campaigns run for specific ports and airports, such as the Holyhead campaign in the October 2026 cycle, and draw strong fields for this frontline operational career. Officers work at ports, airports and international rail terminals, where shift and unsocial-hours working is common.

Applying takes three stages: confirm eligibility with basic personal information, sit two online tests, then complete the full application after passing. This guide explains each stage and its deadlines.

Deadlines matter: the Holyhead campaign set online-test and application deadlines of 13 October 2026. If you fail to complete the tests or the full application before the deadline, your application is withdrawn. Selection process dates are indicative and may be revised.

The Online Tests: What to Expect

The two tests are the gatekeeper stage: the Civil Service Verbal Test measures your understanding of written information, while the Customer Service Skills test assesses judgement in public-facing scenarios. Complete them as soon as possible — within 24–48 hours of your invite — on a desktop or laptop in a quiet room.

How to Apply, Step by Step

Find the campaign and confirm eligibility. Search civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk for "Border Force Officer", read the advert, then enter basic personal information to confirm you meet its requirements.
Sit the online tests. Complete the Civil Service Verbal Test and the Customer Service Skills test on a desktop or laptop — not a phone or tablet — within 24–48 hours of the invite.
Complete the full application. After passing the tests, fill in the full application form with your experience and supporting evidence.
Meet every deadline. If the tests or full application are incomplete when the campaign closes, your application is withdrawn.

Salaries vary by campaign and are stated on the live advert. Border Force campaigns run for different ports and airports through the year, so watch the portal for the next one if you miss a deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for Border Force Officer roles?

Through Civil Service Jobs: eligibility check, then online tests, then the full application form.

What tests are involved?

The Civil Service Verbal Test and the Customer Service Skills test, both completed before the campaign's deadline.

Can I do the Border Force tests on my phone?

No — the tests cannot be completed on a mobile phone or tablet. Use a desktop or laptop.

What happens if I miss the test deadline?

Your application is withdrawn. Complete the tests within 24–48 hours of your invite.

What nationality requirements apply?

Check each advert. Home Office roles carry Civil Service nationality and residency requirements.

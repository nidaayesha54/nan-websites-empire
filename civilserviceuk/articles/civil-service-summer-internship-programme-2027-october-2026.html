Civil Service Summer Internship Programme 2027: £475/Week, 500 Places — Apply by 5 Nov 2026

The Civil Service Summer Internship Programme 2027 offers undergraduates from lower socio-economic backgrounds an eight-week paid placement at £475 per week — 500 places across UK government departments, closing at midday on 5 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » UK Civil Service Jobs » Articles

Alongside the Fast Stream 2027 graduate schemes, the Civil Service has opened applications for its Summer Internship Programme (SIP) 2027. The programme offers 500 paid internship places to undergraduates from lower socio-economic backgrounds, paying £475 per week for an eight-week placement in a UK government department. Applications close at midday on 5 November 2026.

This is not work experience for the CV alone — successful interns can benefit from an accelerated route through the selection process for a future Civil Service Fast Stream graduate scheme, making it one of the most strategic internships in UK public life.

What Interns Do

Interns take on challenging, project-related work inside a government department, which can include conducting research and gathering information, providing project support, assisting with the preparation of policy documents, and shadowing senior civil servants. The eight-week placement gives genuine insight into how government works — and how the Civil Service recruits, which is invaluable preparation for a future Fast Stream application.

Who Can Apply

  • University: you must be an undergraduate meeting the programme’s university criteria.
  • Socio-economic background: the programme is reserved for candidates from lower socio-economic backgrounds — the advert sets the qualifying measures.
  • Nationality and right to work: standard Civil Service nationality and right-to-work requirements apply.
  • Commitment: you must be available for the full eight-week placement, expected to run from July to August 2027.

How to Apply

Applications are made through the official Civil Service Jobs portal. Follow these steps:

1
Search Summer Internship Programme 2027 on Civil Service Jobs (check the reference number on the advert).
2
Confirm you meet the socio-economic background, university, nationality and right-to-work requirements.
3
Complete the online application honestly and in your own words — the scheme warns against plagiarism and AI-generated content.
4
Submit before midday on 5 November 2026.

Why This Internship Matters

£475 per week for eight weeks is genuinely competitive pay for a summer internship — but the real value is the pipeline advantage: interns gain insider understanding of Civil Service recruitment and a potential accelerated route into the Fast Stream. For undergraduates from lower socio-economic backgrounds, that combination of paid experience and future opportunity makes the SIP 2027 one of the most important deadlines of the autumn. Apply before midday on 5 November.

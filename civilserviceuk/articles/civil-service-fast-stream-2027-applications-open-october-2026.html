Civil Service Fast Stream 2027: Applications Open — Deadline 5 November 2026, Streams & Salaries

Fast Stream and Emerging Talent is recruiting graduates for the Civil Service Fast Stream 2027 — including Property (£31,554, 18 vacancies), Human Resources, Commercial, and Science & Engineering schemes, all closing at midday on 5 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » UK Civil Service Jobs » Articles

The Civil Service Fast Stream — the government’s flagship graduate leadership programme, run by Fast Stream and Emerging Talent (Cabinet Office) — has opened applications for its 2027 intake. Several schemes are now live on the Civil Service Jobs portal, closing at midday on Thursday 5 November 2026. The Fast Stream offers structured rotations, professional qualifications and a direct path towards leadership grades — and applications open only once a year.

What the Fast Stream Offers

Structured placements across departments with real responsibility from the start; professional qualifications (the Property scheme supports accreditation with CIOB, RICS or IWFM); salary progression (Property notes earnings of up to £55,000 on completion); and UK-wide placements with the Civil Service pension and flexible working.

Eligibility — Who Can Apply

  • Education: most schemes require a 2:2 or higher in any degree subject (including degree apprenticeships). The Science and Engineering scheme is stricter — a 2:1 undergraduate degree plus a postgraduate degree in a science or engineering discipline (or chartered status).
  • Existing civil servants can apply without a degree for several schemes.
  • Right to work: you must be eligible to work in the UK; check sponsorship terms on each advert.
  • Security vetting: successful candidates are typically put through Security Clearance (SC), with standard BPSS checks as the minimum.
  • AI and plagiarism warning: the adverts explicitly warn that applications must be truthful and from your own experience — AI-generated or plagiarised content can lead to withdrawal.

How to Apply for Fast Stream 2027

Applications are made through the official Civil Service Jobs portal. Follow these steps:

1
Search Fast Stream 2027 on Civil Service Jobs and pick the scheme(s) that fit your background.
2
Check the person specification and eligibility for your chosen scheme — especially degree requirements for Science & Engineering.
3
Complete the online application and the staged assessments (these typically include work-based scenarios and video/interview stages).
4
Submit before midday on 5 November 2026 — the deadline is strict and schemes can be competitive.
Tip: Fast Stream selection is multi-stage and can take months. Prepare authentic, evidence-based examples from your own experience — the scheme explicitly screens for plagiarism and AI-generated content.

What is the salary for the Property Fast Stream 2027?

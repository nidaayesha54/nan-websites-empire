The Role: Appeal Planning Officer

Appeal Planning Officers are the casework engine of the Planning Inspectorate. When councils refuse planning applications — or fail to decide them — applicants can appeal to the Secretary of State, and PINS officers manage that appeal caseload: gathering evidence, liaising with appellants and local authorities, and preparing cases for inspectors’ decisions.

The home-based working pattern with frequent travel reflects the job’s geography: appeals happen everywhere in England, and officers travel to sites and hearings. It is a role for people who enjoy procedural rigour, clear written communication and working to statutory deadlines — and who are comfortable with regular travel rather than a fixed desk.

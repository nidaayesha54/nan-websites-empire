The Driver and Vehicle Standards Agency (DVSA) — the Department for Transport executive agency responsible for driving and vehicle standards — is recruiting a Head of Organisation Effectiveness and Employee Experience. This is a senior people-change role: the successful candidate will lead transformational people change using systems-thinking approaches, with proven employee-relations expertise in unionised environments and a track record of developing reward and engagement strategy. It is one of the most senior HR-adjacent vacancies currently open in the Civil Service.

The Role: Leading People Transformation at DVSA

DVSA is a large operational agency — driving examiners, enforcement staff and vehicle standards professionals across Great Britain. The Head of Organisation Effectiveness and Employee Experience will lead transformational people change using systems-thinking approaches: diagnosing how the organisation really works, redesigning structures and ways of working, and lifting employee experience across a dispersed, shift-working workforce.

The employee-relations dimension is explicit. DVSA is a unionised environment, so the advert asks for proven ER expertise there — this is not a purely strategic role. You will need to show you can work constructively with trade unions while driving change, alongside developing reward and engagement strategy.

Systems-thinking language in the advert is a strong hint about how DVSA wants the successful candidate to frame their approach — mirror that vocabulary with genuine examples.

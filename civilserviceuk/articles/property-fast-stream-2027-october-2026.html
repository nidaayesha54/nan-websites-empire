Property Fast Stream 2027: 18 Vacancies at £31,554 — Apply by 5 November 2026

The Property Fast Stream 2027 is open: 18 vacancies at £31,554 a year on a four-year programme in government property, sustainability and project delivery — closing midday 5 November 2026.

The Civil Service Fast Stream 2027 family keeps growing: alongside the general and specialist streams, the Property Fast Stream 2027 is recruiting 18 graduates into government property careers at £31,554 a year. Run by Fast Stream and Emerging Talent at the Cabinet Office, the four-year programme closes at midday on Thursday 5 November 2026 (reference 483260).

What You Will Do

Government Property Professionals help manage and improve the UK’s extensive public estate. As a Property Fast Streamer you will rotate across central government departments and agencies, building experience in property management, strategy, procurement, project delivery and leadership — contributing to projects that make government buildings more sustainable, efficient, safe and fit for delivering public services. The programme is designed to build professional knowledge and management skills for a long-term career in government property, including routes to professional accreditation and progression toward senior property leadership roles.

Eligibility

  • Graduates and eligible candidates — the scheme welcomes applicants from a range of degree backgrounds, not just property or surveying.
  • Civil Service nationality rules apply — the vacancy is open to eligible UK nationals, Irish nationals, qualifying Commonwealth citizens and certain other listed groups; right to work in the UK alone does not automatically qualify you.
  • Visa sponsorship is not stated in the advert — confirm with the recruitment team if you would need sponsorship.
  • Security and background checks apply before appointment.

The Wider Fast Stream Picture

Property is one of several 2027 streams sharing the same midday 5 November 2026 deadline and the same £31,554 starting salary — including the general Fast Stream and the Operational Delivery Fast Stream (50 vacancies, reference 483256). If property, sustainability and estates are your thing, this 18-place stream is the most direct route in — but the parallel schemes give you backup options with identical timelines.

The Bottom Line

Eighteen places, a permanent HEO contract, four years of structured development and a starting salary of £31,554 — the Property Fast Stream 2027 is one of the best-paid graduate routes into government property. The midday 5 November deadline is non-negotiable, so start the online tests early.

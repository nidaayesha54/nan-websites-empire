DWP Child Maintenance Service Apprentice Telephony Caseworker: Campaign Timeline & How to Apply

The Child Maintenance Service is recruiting Apprentice Telephony Caseworkers — with a published campaign timeline (sift 27–28 October 2026, interviews late November, starts March 2027) and video interviews via MS Teams.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » UK Civil Service Jobs » Articles

The Child Maintenance Service (CMS) — part of the Department for Work and Pensions — is running a recruitment campaign for Apprentice Telephony Caseworkers. The campaign advert sets out an unusually transparent approximate timeline: sift on 27–28 October 2026, interviews from 25 November to 4 December 2026, results and offers on 9 December 2026, security and ID checks from December 2026 to February 2027, and start dates in March 2027.

It is an apprenticeship route into one of DWP’s most people-facing services: helping separated parents arrange child maintenance, by phone, with full training provided.

Check the advert for the exact closing date and apply early: the sift begins on 27 October.

What the Role Involves

Telephony caseworkers handle child maintenance cases by phone: speaking with parents, gathering information, explaining options and progressing cases through the service’s systems. As an apprentice, you combine the day job with structured apprenticeship training — building customer-service, casework and communication skills while earning. The interview assesses behaviours and strengths, with Communicating and Influencing as the lead behaviour.

Who Can Start an Apprenticeship

  • To start an apprenticeship you generally need to be 16 or over, not in full-time education and have the right to work in the UK — confirm the exact criteria on the advert.
  • No prior DWP or casework experience is implied by the campaign format; the emphasis is on communication behaviours and strengths.
  • Standard Civil Service nationality requirements apply.
  • Be prepared for pre-employment checks: the advert notes these can take up to 8 weeks for external candidates, so gather your documents early.

How to Apply

Applications run through the official Civil Service Jobs portal. Follow these steps:

1
Find the Child Maintenance Service — Apprentice Telephony Caseworker advert on Civil Service Jobs.
2
Read the Candidate Pack and Personal Statement Guidance attachments for full details of the application and selection process.
3
Prepare examples for the Communicating and Influencing behaviour using the STAR method.
4
Submit before the advert’s closing date — the sift starts 27 October 2026, so do not delay.
Tip: video interviews on MS Teams reward clear, structured answers. Practise speaking your STAR examples aloud — communicating and influencing is literally what is being assessed.

Why This Campaign Stands Out

Few Civil Service campaigns publish their full timeline up front — here you know exactly when sift, interviews, offers and start dates fall. An apprenticeship with DWP means paid training, a recognised qualification and a foothold in the UK’s largest government department. With the sift beginning on 27 October, the window to apply is short: get your application in now.

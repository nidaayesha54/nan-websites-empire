Civil Service Student Placement Scheme 2026/27: 110 Paid Placements Launched

A brand-new Civil Service competition launched on 12 October 2026 — around 110 paid student placements across government departments at £25,796 a year. Here are the areas, the eligibility rules and how to get your application in.

Published 14 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

The Civil Service Student Placement Scheme 2026/27 was launched on 12 October 2026, opening a recruitment competition for approximately 110 paid placements across several government departments. Launching the scheme, the Finance Minister described it as an excellent opportunity for talented students to gain valuable, hands-on experience in a professional working environment — and for the Civil Service, one of the largest employers, to bring new talent into work that impacts almost every aspect of societal life.

Placements offer an annual salary of £25,796, an attractive benefits package, and peer support through the Civil Service Student Network. This is a paid, professional placement — not an unpaid internship.

Two competitions: a general competition will fill around 90 placements across areas like policy and law, accountancy, communications and marketing, forestry, environment, science and ICT. A separate competition covers around 20 Construction & Infrastructure placements in architecture, engineering, surveying and planning.

Placement Areas in 2026/27

The range of subject areas is deliberately broad — the scheme is designed so students from many degree disciplines can find a relevant placement. Confirmed areas include:

  • Policy and law — shaping and drafting the rules that govern public life.
  • Accountancy and finance — managing public money across departments.
  • Communications and marketing — how government explains its work to the public.
  • Environment, forestry and science — evidence-based policy and delivery.
  • ICT — digital and technology roles supporting government services.
  • Construction & Infrastructure (separate competition) — architecture, engineering, surveying and planning, with around 20 placements.

Why a Civil Service Placement Pays Off

A year inside government gives you three things graduates struggle to get elsewhere: real responsibility on live projects, a professional network through the Civil Service Student Network, and evidence for future applications — including the Civil Service Fast Stream and departmental apprenticeships, both of which value demonstrable public-sector experience. At £25,796 a year plus benefits, it is also one of the better-paid student placement schemes in the UK.

How to Apply for the Student Placement Scheme

Find the official competition page. The scheme was launched on 12 October 2026 — search the official Civil Service or departmental recruitment pages for the "Student Placement Scheme 2027/28" competition (note the naming may reference the placement year).
Choose your competition. Apply to the general competition for policy, law, accountancy, communications, environment, science or ICT — or the separate Construction & Infrastructure competition for architecture, engineering, surveying or planning.
Prepare evidence of your studies. Placements are for students — show how your degree relates to the placement area and give genuine examples of teamwork, communication and problem-solving.
Meet the Civil Service nationality requirements. As with all Civil Service competitions, UK, Irish, Commonwealth and eligible EEA nationals are broadly eligible — verify the advert's statement.
Submit before the competition closes. Student competitions have fixed closing dates and no extensions — apply early.

Civil Service Apprenticeships 2026/27: Digital, Business & Earn-While-You-Learn Routes

You don't need a degree to start a Civil Service career. From the Digital, Data & Technology apprentice scheme starting March 2027 to Career Launch roles paying £23,512+, here are the apprenticeship routes into UK government.

Published 14 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

Civil Service apprenticeships pay a real salary while you gain a recognised qualification — and some schemes are open to anyone aged 16 or over with no upper age limit. This guide covers the live routes: the Digital, Data and Technology (DDaT) Apprentice Scheme, Civil Service Career Launch, and departmental apprenticeships.

Now open: the DDaT Apprentice Scheme is recruiting for a March 2027 start — you need to be at least 16 by 1 September 2026 and not in full-time education when you start. No previous experience or specific qualifications are required.

Digital, Data & Technology (DDaT) Apprentice Scheme — Starts March 2027

The DDaT Apprentice Scheme is the Civil Service's specialist route into digital careers — no prior experience or qualifications needed, just motivation to learn and an interest in technology.

  • Start date: March 2027.
  • Eligibility: aged 16 or over by 1 September 2026; no upper age limit; must meet Civil Service nationality requirements; not in full-time education when you start.
  • Qualification caution: if you already hold a Level 3 qualification or above in ICT, Software, Web and Telecoms (or a closely related subject), you may not be eligible — check before applying.
  • Assessment: includes an online literacy, numeracy and digital skills assessment, plus technical-skill evaluation during selection.

Career Launch — Business Administrator Apprenticeships

Career Launch is the Civil Service's entry-level apprenticeship programme, aimed at people aged 16 or older who are not in full-time education and hold at least two GCSEs at grades 9–4 (A*–C) or equivalent. Every place is:

  • In a Civil Service government department, on a 2-year fixed-term contract.
  • Enrolled on a Business Administrator Level 3 apprenticeship.
  • At Administrative Officer (AO) grade with a starting salary of at least £23,512 based on 37 hours per week.

Former apprentices call it the best decision for their development — earning while learning, with space to choose a long-term career path.

Departmental Apprenticeships

Departments also advertise their own apprenticeships on Civil Service Jobs year-round — recent examples include UKRI's Executive Support Apprentice (£19,514) and the Met Office's Paralegal Apprentice (£23,400). These windows close fast, often within weeks.

How to Apply for a Civil Service Apprenticeship

Set a Civil Service Jobs alert. Search "apprentice" and save the search — departmental apprenticeships appear year-round and close within weeks.
Check the eligibility fine print. Most schemes need you to be 16+, not in full-time education at start, and meet nationality requirements.
Show motivation, not experience. These schemes explicitly recruit people without experience — your application should evidence genuine interest, reliability and willingness to learn.
Practise the online assessments. DDaT includes literacy, numeracy and digital skills tests — use official practice materials first.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Civil Service DDaT Apprentice Scheme?

The Digital, Data and Technology Apprentice Scheme trains apprentices for digital careers across government. The current campaign starts in March 2027, is open from age 16 with no upper age limit, and requires no previous experience or qualifications.

How much does a Civil Service Career Launch apprenticeship pay?

Career Launch apprentices are at Administrative Officer grade, starting at £23,512 (37 hrs/week) on a 2-year contract, enrolled on a Business Administrator Level 3 apprenticeship.

Do I need qualifications for a Civil Service apprenticeship?

Career Launch requires at least two GCSEs at grades 9–4/A*–C or equivalent. The DDaT scheme requires no specific qualifications — but holders of Level 3+ ICT qualifications may not be eligible for it.

Is there an age limit for Civil Service apprenticeships?

No upper age limit — Career Launch and the DDaT scheme are open to anyone 16 or older who meets the other criteria. Age is genuinely no barrier.

How does the Fast Stream compare to apprenticeships?

The Fast Stream is a graduate programme starting at £31,554; apprenticeships start from 16 with no degree, paying from £23,512. The 2025/26 Fast Stream cycle is closed.

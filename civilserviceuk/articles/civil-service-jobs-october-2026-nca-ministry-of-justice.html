Civil Service Jobs October 2026: NCA, Ministry of Justice & College of Policing Vacancies

The standout Civil Service vacancies of the October 2026 cycle — from NCA leadership to MoJ digital roles and College of Policing training posts, with salaries and application advice.

Published 13 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

The October 2026 Civil Service recruitment cycle has produced some of the most varied vacancies of the year. Law enforcement, justice, digital transformation and policing training are all hiring — and the salaries on offer show just how competitive the Civil Service has become for specialist talent. Below is a department-by-department look at the roles advertised this month.

Important: these roles were advertised in the October 2026 cycle and some campaigns have already closed. Treat this as a guide to the kinds of roles each body recruits for — always check live adverts on civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk before applying.

How to Apply for These Roles

Find the advert. Search civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk by department name. Read the full advert, including the nationality requirements and closing date.
Match the Success Profiles. Note which behaviours, technical skills and experience criteria are assessed, then gather one tailored example for each.
Write STAR examples. Use Situation, Task, Action, Result — around 250 words each — with measurable outcomes and "I" language throughout.
Submit early. Popular campaigns (especially NCA and MoJ digital) can attract hundreds of applications. Apply well before the deadline.

Eligibility Notes

NCA and MoD roles often carry stricter nationality and residency requirements — many require UK nationality and a minimum period of UK residency. College of Policing training roles typically require relevant operational or training experience. DBS and the Security Industry Authority roles involve standard Civil Service pre-employment checks; NCA posts may require developed vetting.

Frequently Asked Questions

What jobs is the NCA recruiting for?

The National Crime Agency recruits across investigations, intelligence, commercial, digital and strategy functions. Recent campaigns included senior commercial and strategy leadership roles advertised on Civil Service Jobs.

Does the College of Policing hire civilians?

Yes. The College employs trainers, subject-matter experts, digital staff and corporate professionals alongside its policing standards work. Many roles are advertised on Civil Service Jobs.

How do I apply for Ministry of Justice jobs?

Ministry of Justice roles — including HMCTS, HMPPS and digital teams — are advertised on Civil Service Jobs. Applications use the Success Profiles framework with behaviour examples and online tests.

Are Civil Service salaries negotiable?

Starting salaries are usually at the bottom of the advertised range, but exceptions can be made for exceptional candidates in hard-to-fill roles. Each advert states the pay range.

Can I apply for multiple Civil Service jobs at once?

Yes — you can apply for as many vacancies as you like. Each application needs tailored behaviour examples, so prioritise the roles that best match your experience.

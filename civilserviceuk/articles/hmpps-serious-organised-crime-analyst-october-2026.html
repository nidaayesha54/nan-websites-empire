The Role: Serious Organised Crime Analyst

As an analyst in the Long-Term and High Security Estate, you will work with intelligence from across the prison system — and from law-enforcement partners — to identify serious organised crime threats, map criminal networks operating in and around high-security prisons, and produce assessments that drive operational decisions. Your products inform searches, moves, security categorisation and joint work with police and other agencies.

Expect to work with intelligence systems and large datasets, apply structured analytical techniques, and present findings clearly to decision-makers who act on them. Accuracy and auditability matter: intelligence products must meet evidential and legal standards.

Who Should Apply

This suits analysts with experience in intelligence, law enforcement, prisons, or wider analytical roles — people who can demonstrate structured analytical thinking, experience with intelligence systems or large datasets, and the ability to write clear, defensible assessments. Knowledge of serious organised crime or the prison system is an advantage but the core requirement is analytical craft.

Prepare examples that show analysis leading to decisions: what the intelligence picture was, what techniques you applied, what you recommended, and what happened as a result.

How to Apply

Five steps to a successful Civil Service application.

Search civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk
The official portal for all Civil Service vacancies. Set up job alerts by keyword, grade and location so you never miss a closing date.
Decode the Success Profiles
Each advert lists which Civil Service Success Profiles elements are assessed: behaviours, strengths, experience, technical and ability. Tailor every section to the listed criteria.
Write STAR behaviour examples
For each behaviour (e.g. "Making Effective Decisions"), write a 250-word example using Situation, Task, Action, Result — with the emphasis on your actions and the outcome.
Pass the sift and interview
Applications are sifted against the essential criteria, then shortlisted candidates face a panel interview on behaviours and strengths, plus any online tests for the role.
Complete pre-employment checks
Conditional offers require right-to-work checks, references and sometimes security clearance. Have your passport, proof of address and employment history ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Civil Service Fast Stream?

The Fast Stream is the Civil Service's accelerated leadership development programme for graduates, offering fast-track progression to senior roles across departments over 3 to 4 years.

What is the difference between EO and HEO grades?

Executive Officer (EO) roles are typically first-line delivery or junior policy roles, while Higher Executive Officer (HEO) roles involve greater responsibility, often managing small teams or leading policy areas.

How are Civil Service applications scored?

Applications are scored against the Success Profiles framework — behaviours (using STAR examples), strengths, experience, technical skills and ability tests, with the exact mix defined in each job advert.

Can non-UK nationals join the Civil Service?

Many roles are open to UK, Irish, Commonwealth and EEA nationals. Some reserved posts require UK nationality only, usually for national security reasons. All offers are subject to right-to-work checks.

What salary does a Grade 7 earn?

Grade 7 salaries typically range from around £55,000 to £68,000 in London and slightly less outside London, varying by department and profession.

Is remote working available in the Civil Service?

Most departments operate hybrid working, typically requiring 60% office attendance. Some operational roles are fully office-based, while digital and policy roles may offer more flexibility.

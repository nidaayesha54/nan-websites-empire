How it works

The US uses marginal tax rates: your income is taxed in layers. For example, a single filer in 2026 pays 10% on the first $12,400 of taxable income, 12% on the next chunk up to $50,400, and so on. Your marginal rate is the rate on your last dollar; your effective rate (always lower) is total tax ÷ taxable income. See the full 2026 bracket tables.

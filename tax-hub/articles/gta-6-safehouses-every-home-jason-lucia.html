GTA 6 Safehouses: Every Home Jason and Lucia Live In

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and its two leads do something no GTA protagonists have done before: they live somewhere. Not in a flat that exists only to save the game, but in a home that changes as the story changes — four of them, one per chapter, each with its own rooms, its own neighbours and its own problems. This guide covers every safehouse in the game, when each one unlocks, what you can do there, and the garages you can buy alongside them.

The safehouses matter because they are the game's answer to a question the series has dodged for years: where do the criminals sleep? In GTA 6 the answer is a place with a bed, a fridge, a television and another person in it — and the home is where several of the game's systems live.

Four safehouses, one per chapter

There are four safehouses in GTA 6, and they unlock through the story — you cannot buy a house, and you cannot choose where to live. The first is the Leonida Keys safehouse in Key Lento, Jason's home from the start of the game, a weathered green wooden house on stilts by the water. It belongs to Brian Heder, the old smuggler who runs Brian's Boat Works and Marina and rents out property on the side. Jason lives there for free, and in return he does Brian's dirty work, collecting debts from tenants included. When Lucia gets out of prison she moves in, and the house becomes theirs until they leave town in the mission Home Sweet Home.

The second is the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn, on the forgotten Gulf coast. It is the motel room from the first trailer — the room where Lucia says "Trust?" — and it unlocks at the start of Chapter 2, through the missions Overnight Charges and Just Moved In. Jason and Lucia leave it in Checking Out, the Chapter 2 finale, which needs at least $15,000 saved before it appears. The third is the Bayside safehouse on Catalan Boulevard in Vice City, unlocked in Chapter 3 when the pair move to the city in Welcome to Vice City — an urban apartment that clearly belonged to someone else before them, full of family photographs and personal details. The fourth is the Ocean Beach safehouse on Vice Beach, unlocked in Chapter 4 when they set up in Ocean Beach.

A home that lives with you

What makes the safehouses different from the apartments in older GTA games is that they are lived in. Jason and Lucia share one home, and each keeps their own schedule inside it — walk in and you will find the other asleep, cooking, or already out of the house. Rockstar's Rob Nelson compared the design to the camp system in Red Dead Redemption 2: other people drift through, the place feels occupied, and the home is a part of the story rather than a pause between missions.

The home is also where several systems live. Sleeping advances the in-game clock and restores health and stamina, and in the morning it gives you a full day to plan. You can eat from the fridge, change outfits, and watch the television, which works much as it did in GTA 5 — only the living room around it looks lived in. One of the warmest scenes in the 27-minute Extended Look is set in one of these houses: Lucia comes in with a bag of clothes for Jason, he takes a beer from the fridge and drops in front of the TV. It is a small scene, and it is the whole point of the safehouse system: the criminals have a home, and the home has a life.

Garages: the only property you can buy

Houses cannot be bought in GTA 6 — property purchases are garages only. You start with room for three vehicles, and you buy garages to get more. Eight garages can be bought with in-game money after you finish Daily Driver in Chapter 2, which opens Vice City Real Estate on the in-game phone. Four more unlock through the story, and three are edition or GTA+ bonuses, for 15 garages in total by one guide's count. The twelve base-game garages provide 16 registered vehicle spaces, and a safehouse's outside parking does not add registered spaces — the garage is where a car is truly yours.

Two garages come with the editions. Shore Court Garage, near Ocean Beach, comes with the Vintage Vice City Pack and holds a classic 1955 Vapid sedan, with a weapon locker and a stash for stolen goods that a local fence buys at a discount. Paradise Garage, in Watson Bay, comes with the Ultimate Edition and is built for the 1967 Vapid Dominator Buggy, with the same locker and stash. Every personal garage has the same idea behind it: a place to keep a car, a place to keep a gun, and a place to drop goods for the fence.

How to use your safehouse well

First, sleep before big jobs. Sleeping restores health and stamina, advances the clock, and in the morning gives you the full day — it is the cheapest preparation in the game. Second, treat the safehouse as your save point: with no mission replay, a manual save before a chapter finale is the only way to play it again, and the bed is where those saves happen. Third, use the garage, not the street, for the cars you care about: a car parked outside a safehouse is not a registered space, and the game will not protect it the way a garage does.

And remember the move. Each chapter moves you to a new home, and the old one stops being yours — the motel stops being your home after Checking Out, and the Keys house is behind you once you leave town. Pack nothing, because there is nothing to pack: the game moves your life with you, and the new home has its own rooms, its own view, and its own version of the same evening routine.

FAQ

Can you buy a house in GTA 6? No. The four safehouses unlock through the story and cannot be bought. Property purchases are garages only — eight can be bought after Daily Driver in Chapter 2.

How many safehouses are in GTA 6? Four: the Leonida Keys house in Key Lento, the Starlet Motel in Port Gellhorn, the Bayside apartment in Vice City and the Ocean Beach safehouse on Vice Beach.

Do Jason and Lucia share a safehouse? Yes. Both live in the same home, and each keeps their own schedule inside it — the other may be asleep, cooking or already out when you walk in.

The bottom line: GTA 6 gives its leads four homes, one per chapter, from Brian Heder's house in the Keys to Ocean Beach — and not one of them can be bought. Sleep there, save there, keep your good cars in a garage, and let the story move you when it is time to move.

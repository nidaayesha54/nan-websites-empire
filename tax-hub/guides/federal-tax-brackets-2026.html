Federal Tax Brackets 2026: Full IRS Tables

The IRS released the 2026 federal income tax brackets in October 2025 (Revenue Procedure 2025-32). All seven rates — 10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, and 37% — are unchanged from 2025; only the income thresholds moved up with inflation. The One Big Beautiful Bill Act also locked this rate structure in permanently.

How marginal rates actually work

Brackets apply to taxable income (after deductions), not your salary. A single filer earning $75,000 takes the $16,100 standard deduction → $58,900 taxable. They pay 10% on the first $12,400 ($1,240), 12% on the next $38,000 ($4,560), and 22% on the last $8,500 ($1,870) — total $7,670. They never pay 22% on all of it.

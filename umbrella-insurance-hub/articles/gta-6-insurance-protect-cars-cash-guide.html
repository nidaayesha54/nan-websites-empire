GTA 6 Insurance: What Actually Protects Your Cars

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Search for GTA 6 insurance and you will find confident guides listing insurance tiers with prices: standard cover for $500 a claim, premium for $1,500, platinum for $5,000, with payout percentages and cooldowns. The problem is that none of that exists in GTA 6. No insurance system has been shown or announced for the game's story mode, and the tier lists circulating online are invented — copied from GTA Online habits and dressed up as GTA 6 facts. This guide gives the honest answer instead: what actually protects your cars and your cash in GTA 6, and what does not.

That distinction matters, because the invented tiers do real damage. A player who believes a platinum policy will refund a destroyed supercar will drive it into a police chase, lose it, and discover that no policy was ever there. The real protection in GTA 6 is quieter, cheaper, and already in the game — if you know where it is.

The tiers that do not exist

Start by clearing away the fiction. Mors Mutual Insurance is the company that handles vehicle claims in GTA Online, where you insure a car at a mod shop and call the company to replace it for a deductible. That company and that system belong to GTA V and GTA Online, and nothing has been shown or announced to suggest they carry over to GTA 6's story mode. The guides that list GTA 6 insurance tiers are describing a GTA Online mechanic with GTA 6 branding on top.

The same applies to the anti-theft gadgets in those guides — remote kill switches, alarm systems with set prices, vehicle recovery services with fixed fees. None of these has been announced or shown for GTA 6. If a guide gives you an exact dollar price for a GTA 6 insurance policy, ask where the number came from: the game's own menus, a preview, or the author's imagination. For the tier lists, the answer is the last one.

What really protects your cars

The real protection for your vehicles is the garage. Garages are the only property you can actually buy in GTA 6, through the Vice City Real Estate app, and they become available after the Daily Driver mission in Chapter 2. You start with three vehicle slots, and there are fifteen garages in total according to one guide — eight buyable, four from the story, and three from edition and subscription bonuses. A car parked in your own garage is safe; a car left on the street is not.

The second layer is the tracker. The WAiNK app on your phone scans a car and reports its locks, alarm, tracker, condition, resale value and registration cost — the game's own way of telling you what protection a vehicle already has. And if a car you own is destroyed, the game gives you ways to get it back through its recovery systems, which is why the destroyed-car guide on this site's sister pages exists. The protection is real; it is just a garage and a tracker, not an insurance policy.

What really protects your cash

Your money has its own protection system, and it is the BuckMe bank. Shops in GTA 6 take pocket cash only up to $999 — the rest of any purchase comes from your BuckMe account — so keeping most of your money in the bank is not just tidy, it is how the game expects you to pay. Deposit your cash at an ATM whenever you pass one, and treat pocket cash as walking-around money, not savings.

The reason is what happens when you die. Pocket cash, stash bags and unowned weapons and vehicles are lost when you are wasted; banked money, owned weapons and owned vehicles are safe. That split is the game's real insurance policy: the bank is the only place your money cannot be taken from you, and the floorboard stash in your first home is only a stopgap before you have an account. Bank early, bank often, and never carry a fortune in your pocket into a firefight.

The cheapest protection is free

Most of the protection in GTA 6 costs nothing at all. Park your best car in the garage and drive a cheap car into risky situations. Bank your cash before a robbery instead of after it. Change your clothes and switch cars after a crime, so the police heat icons have nothing to match. Split from your partner when the police have seen you together. None of these cost a dollar, and together they protect more than any invented policy would.

The habit to build is simple: garage, bank, tracker. Park it, bank it, check it. A player who does those three things will lose far less than a player who trusts a policy that was never in the game — and will spend nothing on the privilege.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there Mors Mutual in GTA 6?

No evidence of it. Mors Mutual belongs to GTA V and GTA Online, and no insurance company of that kind has been shown or announced for GTA 6's story mode.

Can I insure a car at a mod shop in GTA 6?

Not that anyone has shown. Mod shops handle paint, performance and customisation; no insurance product has been announced for them in GTA 6.

What happens if my car is destroyed?

Owned vehicles can be recovered through the game's recovery systems, and cars kept in your own garage are safe. The exact recovery steps depend on where and how the car was lost, and a destroyed-car guide covers the details.

The bottom line: GTA 6 has no insurance company to call, and the tier lists online are invented. What protects you is real and already in the game — a garage for your cars, a bank for your cash, and a tracker that tells you what a car already has.

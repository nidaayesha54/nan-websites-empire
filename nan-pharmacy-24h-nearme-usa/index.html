What counts as a "24-hour pharmacy"?

True 24-hour pharmacies stay open around the clock, including a pharmacist on duty — many are inside hospitals or large chain stores. Note that a 24-hour store (like a supermarket) does not always mean its pharmacy counter is open all night. Hospital outpatient pharmacies and emergency-room pharmacies are often your most reliable overnight option. Our full guide explains the difference and what to do in a medication emergency.

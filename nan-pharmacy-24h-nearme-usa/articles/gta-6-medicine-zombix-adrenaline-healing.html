GTA 6 Medicine: Zombix, Adrenaline and Every Healing Item

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, and it changed one of the oldest habits in the series: healing. In GTA 5 Online you could open a menu mid-fight and eat snacks until you were full again. In GTA 6 that trick is dead. Healing now runs on a small set of items with real rules — pills that get weaker the more you take them, a white line your body cannot heal past on its own, and food that heals only if it carries the right heart icon. This guide covers every healing item in the game, where to find it, and how the system actually works.

The short version: carry Zombix, keep an Adrenaline shot for the hard fights, eat heart-marked food when you are safe, and sleep at your safehouse when you can. Everything else is detail — and the detail is where the system catches players out.

Zombix: the fastest heal, with a catch

Zombix is the main healing item in GTA 6, and it is the fastest way to patch yourself up in a fight. You can buy it at grocery stores or find it lying around Leonida, and it lives on the ITEMS tab of the weapon wheel — hold the wheel button, tap R1 to switch from WEAPONS to ITEMS, and Square uses the health item instantly without leaving the wheel. In the wheel it shows as a red pill bottle with a heart icon.

The catch is tolerance. Take Zombix too often and each dose gets weaker — the game warns you that repeated use diminishes its potency. That is a deliberate design choice: you cannot spam your way through a long firefight the way you could with snacks in GTA 5 Online. Use Zombix when it matters, then get to cover and let the rest of the system do its work.

Adrenaline: health above the maximum

Adrenaline is the second consumable on the wheel, bound to Triangle, and it does something different: it boosts your maximum health. Where Zombix restores what you lost, Adrenaline raises the ceiling, giving you extra health to burn through in a hard fight. You buy it at shops across Leonida, and it is worth keeping in the loadout alongside Zombix — one restores, the other extends.

The two items work as a pair. Adrenaline first, to raise the ceiling, then Zombix to fill the bar you now have. Used in the wrong order you waste the boost; used together before a big fight, they are the closest thing GTA 6 has to armour for your health bar.

Food and drink: read the heart icon

Food and drink still heal in GTA 6, but only if they carry a heart icon — and the colour of the heart matters. A pink heart restores your health up to the normal maximum. An orange heart temporarily boosts your health above the normal maximum, the same idea as Adrenaline in food form. Orange-heart items are the ones to eat before a big fight, since they extend your health bar for a while; the bonus fades once your health drops back below the maximum.

Look at the icon before you buy. A meal with no heart does nothing for your health, and the game does not warn you. Restaurants, vendors and stores across Leonida all stock heart-marked items, and eating restores a solid chunk of health — though overeating adds weight, which the game tracks.

First aid kits and the EpiPen

First Aid Kits are scattered through buildings across Leonida, and using one greatly restores health for Jason or Lucia. They are the best free heal in the game — no purchase, no tolerance, just a kit on a wall or a shelf. Learn where they sit in the places you fight often, and they become part of your route.

The EpiPen is the fast revive: a single-use item for the moments when nothing else is quick enough. Trauma kits and body armour sit in the same equipment menu as the binoculars and the flashlight, and the game treats healing as kit management — what you carry, in what order, and when you use it matters more than how much you own.

Natural regen and the white line

Your health does regenerate on its own, but slowly, and only up to a point. A white line on the health bar marks the limit: natural regeneration stops there, and you need medicine to heal past it. The speed of regeneration scales with your stamina, which is why the gym matters — regular workouts raise your overall HP and make you recover faster. Sleeping at your safehouse restores health too, and a full night restores health and stamina together.

That is the whole system in one sentence: items get you past the white line, food tops you up, and your own body does the slow work underneath. Players who try to fight on regen alone will keep hitting the white line and wondering why they never reach full health.

FAQ

What is the fastest way to heal in GTA 6? Zombix — hold L1 and press Square, or use it from the ITEMS tab of the weapon wheel. Just remember that repeated doses get weaker, so save it for when it counts.

What do the pink and orange hearts mean? Pink restores health up to the normal maximum. Orange temporarily boosts health above the maximum. Both appear on food and drink in shops and restaurants.

Why does my health stop regenerating? Natural regeneration stops at the white line on the health bar. Only medicine — Zombix, a first aid kit or the EpiPen — heals past it. Raising stamina at the gym speeds up the regen below the line.

The bottom line: GTA 6 healing is a kit, not a button. Carry Zombix and Adrenaline, read the heart icons on your food, know where the first aid kits are, and respect the white line — or it will catch you mid-fight.

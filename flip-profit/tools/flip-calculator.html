Flip calculator FAQs

Profit = ARV − (purchase price + rehab + holding costs + selling costs). Holding costs include loan interest, taxes, insurance and utilities for every month you own the property. Selling costs are typically 8–10% of ARV (agent commissions plus closing costs).
MAO = ARV − rehab − holding costs − selling costs − target profit. Our calculator uses a 15% of ARV target profit margin, the minimum most experienced investors accept for the risk involved.

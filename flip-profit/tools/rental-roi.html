Rental ROI FAQs

In most US markets, a cap rate of 6–8% is considered solid for residential rentals. Above 8% is strong (but check why it's priced that way — rough area or deferred maintenance). Below 4% usually means you're betting on appreciation, not cash flow.
Cash-on-cash return = annual cash flow ÷ cash you invested (down payment + closing costs). It tells you the yearly yield on your actual out-of-pocket money. Most investors want at least 8–12%.
The 1% rule says monthly rent should be at least 1% of the purchase price for the property to cash flow. It's a quick screening filter, not a final decision tool — always run the full numbers.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is a loan monthly payment calculated?

Monthly payment = P × r(1+r)n ÷ ((1+r)n − 1), where P is the principal, r the monthly interest rate, and n the number of payments. Our personal loan calculator runs this instantly.

What is a good APR for a personal loan in 2026?

Excellent-credit borrowers see roughly 8–12% APR; average borrowers 13–18%; fair credit can mean 20%+. Improving your credit score before applying is the fastest way to a lower rate. See our rate guide.

Should I pay extra on my student loans?

Extra payments go straight to principal, cutting both total interest and payoff time. Even $100 extra per month can save thousands. Try our student loan payoff planner to see your exact savings.

How do car loans work?

You borrow the vehicle price minus down payment and trade-in, then repay with interest over 36–72 months. Shorter terms mean higher payments but far less total interest. Read how car loans work before visiting a dealer.

Fixed vs variable loan rates — which is better?

Fixed rates never change, so your payment is predictable. Variable rates start lower but can rise. For most consumer loans, fixed is the safer choice unless you plan to repay very quickly. See our full comparison.

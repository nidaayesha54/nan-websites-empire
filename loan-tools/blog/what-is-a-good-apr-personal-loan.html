What Is a Good APR for a Personal Loan?

By NAN Marketing Agency · October 12, 2026 · 4 min read

"Good" is relative — it depends on your credit and the market. But benchmarks exist, and knowing them keeps you from accepting a bad deal.

The Benchmarks

  • Excellent (720+): under ~11% is good; under 9% is great.
  • Good (670–719): under ~16% is reasonable.
  • Fair (580–669): under ~22% is acceptable — but improving your score first usually pays more than accepting this.
  • Anything above 36%: walk away. That is payday-loan territory, not a personal loan.

Context Matters: Compare Against Alternatives

A 14% personal loan is a good deal if it replaces 24% credit-card debt — you would save thousands. It is a bad deal for a vacation you could postpone. Judge the APR against what the money replaces.

5 Ways to Qualify for Less

  1. Pay card balances below 30% utilization before applying.
  2. Prequalify with 3–5 lenders (soft pull only) and make them compete.
  3. Pick the shortest term you can afford — rates drop with term length.
  4. Set up autopay for the typical 0.25% discount.
  5. Consider a credit union — they often beat bank rates by 1–2 points.

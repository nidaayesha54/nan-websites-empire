GTA 6 Radio: The 6 Stations Rockstar Confirmed

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Radio has always been half the soul of a GTA game — the stations you leave on while driving, the DJs you recognise by voice, the songs that become the sound of a city. GTA 6 is no different, except in one respect: Rockstar has only named six stations so far, and the internet has filled the gap with invented playlists. This guide sticks to what Rockstar actually confirmed. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and on October 8, 2026, Rockstar put six radio stations on its GTA VI music page as short previews — roughly one to three minutes each, with DJ banter and three songs apiece. Those six stations, plus a 34-track album and a 30-track original score, are the confirmed music of Leonida. Everything else is a rumour until Rockstar says otherwise.

It is worth knowing the scale of what is confirmed versus what is not. Six stations is a preview, not the full dial — Rockstar itself says more are coming, "from Classic Rock to Talk Radio" — and no station's full song list is public. What follows is the confirmed six, the songs in their previews, and the two bigger releases around them.

The Six Confirmed Stations

Here are the six stations Rockstar named, with their genres and their in-game DJs. Cocoteo FM plays modern Latin — bachata, merengue, dembow and reggaeton — hosted by El Martillo and La Gata. Back Country Radio plays renegade country, hosted by MW and Delta Dawne, with a roving reporter called Mud Dog Mike. AfroBank FM plays amapiano and African classics, hosted by Burna and Palmsy. The Chamber 106.6 plays metal from the 80s onward, hosted by DJ Kerry and DJ Tom. Flash FM plays pop, hosted by Robyn and Alex — and it is the only returning station name Rockstar has confirmed, a Vice City institution from the 2002 game, which Rockstar's page calls "the Vice City institution that delivers the best in pop music". Dirty South Classics plays regional rap and hip-hop classics, hosted by Trick and Trina.

One careful note: Rockstar's page lists the DJs by their in-game names only. Several outlets have reported the real artists behind them — Bad Bunny and RaiNao on Cocoteo, Morgan Wallen and Lana Del Rey on Back Country, Burna Boy and Palms Trax on AfroBank, Kerry King and Tom Araya on The Chamber, Robyn and Alex Consani on Flash FM, Trick Daddy and Trina on Dirty South Classics — and those pairings are widely reported, but Rockstar has not confirmed them. The launch credits will settle it. Until then, treat the real names as reported, not fact.

The 18 Songs in the Previews

Each station preview runs about two minutes and plays three songs, which makes 18 songs Rockstar has officially placed on a GTA 6 station. Cocoteo FM previews Bad Bunny's "EoO", Axel Rulay featuring Farruko and El Alfa's "Si Es Trucho Es Trucho (Remix)", and Romeo Santos and El Chaval de la Bachata's "Canalla". The Chamber 106.6 previews Black Sabbath's "I", Pantera's "I'm Broken" and Slayer's "Raining Blood". Flash FM previews Charli xcx's "Good Ones", MGMT's "Electric Feel" and Zara Larsson and PinkPantheress's "Midnight Sun (Girls Trip)". Dirty South Classics previews David Banner featuring Lil' Flip's "Like A Pimp", JT Money featuring Solé's "Who Dat" and DJ Khaled featuring Trick Daddy, Rick Ross and Pitbull's "Born N Raised".

Those 18 are the only songs Rockstar has officially placed on a station. Every station will have far more tracks in the game, and Rockstar has not published a full list for any of them. Any "complete GTA 6 radio playlist" you see before launch week is invented — the previews contain snippets, not playlists, and the full dial is still unannounced.

The Album and the Score

The radio is only one of three music layers Rockstar is releasing. Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album is a 34-track collection of brand-new, original tracks made with Atlantic Records, announced on September 17 and released on November 19 alongside the game. Nine of its singles were named before launch. Separately, Grand Theft Auto VI: Original Score by Justice is a 30-track adaptive score, released on December 11, with the single "Pusher" named in advance. Rockstar's music page describes the whole offering as "the biggest and widest-ranging selection of in-game radio and podcasts yet" — and podcasts are part of it too, though Rockstar has not said what the podcasts actually are, whether missions cut the audio, or whether they pick up where you left off.

For scale, reporting from GameSpot and The Globe and Mail put the launch lineup at 15 music stations and 2 talk radio channels, with about 240 licensed songs — but that is reporting, not a Rockstar number, and the total has not been confirmed. What is confirmed is the shape: six stations named, an album of 34, a score of 30, and more to come.

How to Listen

Radio in GTA 6 works the way it always has: it plays in vehicles, and you switch stations while driving. The new part is the scale of the choice — six confirmed stations at launch, more coming, plus the album and the score as separate releases you can listen to outside the game. If a station goes quiet while you drive, check the obvious first: the game has a launch-era issue where radio music can go silent, and the verified fix is to switch stations and check the audio settings before assuming the game is broken.

The practical advice is simple: do not trust a playlist that claims to be complete. Listen to the six confirmed stations, enjoy the 18 preview songs, and let the rest of the dial reveal itself as Rockstar names it. The full radio lineup is a launch-week discovery, not a pre-launch list — and the stations worth remembering are the six Rockstar put its name on.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many radio stations are in GTA 6?

Rockstar has confirmed six: Cocoteo FM, Back Country Radio, AfroBank FM, The Chamber 106.6, Flash FM and Dirty South Classics. Reporting puts the launch total at 15 music stations plus 2 talk channels, but Rockstar has not confirmed a total.

Who hosts the GTA 6 radio stations?

Rockstar lists the in-game DJs: El Martillo and La Gata, MW and Delta Dawne, Burna and Palmsy, DJ Kerry and DJ Tom, Robyn and Alex, Trick and Trina. The real artists behind them are widely reported but not confirmed by Rockstar.

Is there a full GTA 6 soundtrack list?

Not yet. Rockstar has confirmed 18 songs on the six stations, 9 album singles from the 34-track album, 2 trailer songs and Justice's "Pusher". No station's full song list is public.

The Bottom Line

Six stations, 18 preview songs, a 34-track album and a 30-track score — that is the confirmed music of GTA 6, and it is plenty to start with. Flash FM is back, the Latin, country, amapiano, metal and Southern rap stations are new, and the rest of the dial is still to be announced. Listen to what Rockstar confirmed, and ignore the playlists it never published.

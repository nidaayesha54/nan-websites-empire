How the math works

Each year, your balance grows by the expected return, then your contributions land: Balancenext = Balance × (1 + r) + your contribution + employer match. Your contribution is salary × your %, and the employer match is salary × min(your %, cap %) × match %. The chart splits every dollar of growth from every dollar contributed so you can see compounding do the heavy lifting.

Tips

  • Always contribute enough to get the full employer match — it is an instant 50–100% return.
  • In 2026, the 401(k) elective deferral limit is $24,500 ($31,000 if 50+). This projector ignores IRS caps — check yours.
  • Fees matter: a 1% higher expense ratio can cost six figures over a career. Prefer low-cost index funds.

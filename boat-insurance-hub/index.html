Frequently Asked Questions

How much does boat insurance cost in 2026?
Most owners pay about 1.5–2% of their boat's value per year. A $30,000 runabout typically costs $450–$600/yr, while yachts and high-performance boats run well over $1,000/yr. Get a personalized number with our cost estimator.
Is boat insurance required by law?
No US state requires liability insurance for recreational boats — but lenders and marinas usually do. Read our guide on whether you need boat insurance.
What does boat insurance cover?
Hull damage, liability for injury and property damage, medical payments, uninsured-boater coverage, and towing/assistance. See our coverage guide for details and exclusions.
Can a winter lay-up lower my premium?
Yes — lay-up endorsements typically cut hull premiums 40–60% during storage months. Use our seasonal calculator to compare.
Are these estimates financial advice?
No. All results are rough estimates for education only — estimates only, not financial advice. Talk to a licensed marine insurance agent before buying.

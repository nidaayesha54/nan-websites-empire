GTA 6 Boats: Airboats, Jet Skis and the Keys

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Most GTA 6 guides talk about the city. This one talks about the water — because Leonida is built on it. Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and its map is a Florida-inspired state surrounded by ocean on three sides, with a major freshwater lake, an extensive swamp system, and a chain of islands connected by bridges and waterways. The water is not scenery. It is a playground: kayaking, jet skiing, airboating, scuba diving, speedboats, fishing and swimming are all in the game, and some of the best places in Leonida can only be reached by boat. Here is every way to get on the water, and where each one works.

Why Water Matters in Leonida

Leonida's geography makes water central to the setting in a way no previous GTA map has managed. Vice City sits on the coast, the Leonida Keys stretch south as a chain of tropical islands, the Grassrivers swamps spread west as a vast wetland, and Lake Leonida sits inland as a major freshwater body. The state has ocean coastline, river systems, lakes and island chains — and the game treats all of them as places to go, not as borders. You can drive across the long overseas highway connecting the Keys, or you can take a boat and go island to island. You can drive around the Grassrivers, or you can take an airboat straight through them. The water is a road system of its own.

That is also why the water matters for the story: Jason works for drug runners in the Keys, and the Keys are a boat world of small towns, docks, trailer parks and shallow waters. The smuggling routes, the hideouts and the getaways all run on the water — learn the boats, and you learn half the map.

Kayaking: New to the Series

Kayaking is the headline water activity, and it is a first for the GTA series: no previous game has let you paddle a kayak. It is confirmed through official Rockstar screenshots and the Extended Look, which shows kayaking through the rivers of Mount Kalaga National Park and the mangrove channels of the Leonida Keys. The kayak is quiet, slow and close to the water — the opposite of a speedboat, and the point of it. It is for exploring the winding waterways, the vegetation, the wildlife and the places a motor would scare away.

The kayak is also the best way to see the parts of Leonida most players will skip. The mangrove channels of the Keys, the calm rivers of Mount Kalaga, the backwaters of the Grassrivers — these are the places where the hidden locations live, where the fishing spots are, and where the world is at its most detailed. Paddle slowly, look around, and the game rewards you for it.

Jet Skis and Speedboats

Jet skiing returns bigger than ever. The named model is the Speedophile Seashark, and the trailers show Jason and Lucia riding together on jet skis, with high-speed rides along Vice City Beach, jumps, waves, and police chases likely to follow. Jet skis are the natural fit for the Keys: fast enough to travel between islands, small enough to slip into shallow water, and fun enough to be worth doing for its own sake. The free-roam activity montage in the Extended Look shows jet skis alongside kayaking, swimming, scuba diving and boating through the swamp — the water is a full activity system, not a single minigame.

Speedboats cover the longer distances. The trailers and screenshots show a range of them — speedboats, yachts, dinghies, dive boats and recreational motorboats — and the Keys are easiest to explore by speedboat, hopping from island to island across the shallow reefs. The Ultimate Edition even includes a boat of its own: the Shitzu Squalo, a pink and blue speedboat docked at Washington Beach, with a weapons crate on board. Boats are also how you outrun the law on the water — and the police have boats of their own, including Coast Guard vessels that patrol the coastline.

Airboats: The Grassrivers Specialists

Airboats are the most distinctive watercraft in the game, and the only way to cross the Grassrivers properly. A flat-bottomed vessel powered by a large rear propeller, an airboat glides across shallow swamp water where a normal boat would ground itself — and the Grassrivers, Rockstar's version of the Everglades, is almost entirely shallow water, tall grass and mud. At least two airboat models have been identified, including the Airgator Airboat 09, and the trailers show airboats cruising through flooded grasslands as flamingos scatter overhead.

Airboats are not just transport: in Trailer 2, Jason pilots a helicopter in pursuit of an airboat through Grassrivers, confirming airboats play a role in story missions as well as free-roam exploration. The swamps also hold the mud-bogging, the hunting and the hidden shacks — and driving deep into the Grassrivers waterways is one of the best ways to lose law enforcement helicopters. Learn the airboat, and the whole western half of the map opens up.

Scuba Diving and the Underwater World

Scuba diving is fully confirmed, and it may be the biggest secret in the game. Footage shows Lucia and Jason diving beneath the surface, exploring shipwrecks, coral reefs and hidden underwater areas off the Leonida Keys. An official screenshot shows a scuba diver in the waters off the Keys, surrounded by eels, sea turtles, coral formations and tropical fish. The coastal waters and reefs are packed with marine life — bottlenose dolphins, gentle manatees, loggerhead sea turtles, and tiger sharks — and the underwater world is a place to explore, not just a backdrop.

Underwater treasure is part of it too: the map guides already list treasure diving among the Keys' side activities, and the scuba system is tied to the game's collectibles. If Leonida's hidden locations are anywhere, they are underwater — take a dive boat out past the reefs, go down, and look around.

Frequently Asked Questions

What boats are in GTA 6?

Jet skis (Speedophile Seashark), kayaks, airboats, speedboats, yachts, dinghies, dive boats and recreational motorboats — plus hover boats, cargo ships and cruise liners visible in the port area. Kayaking is new to the series; airboats are the Grassrivers specialists.

Can you swim in GTA 6?

Yes — swimming is back, with improved animations and underwater exploration, and it pairs with scuba diving for the full underwater system. Leonida's underwater world includes reefs, wrecks, dolphins, manatees, sea turtles and sharks.

Which boat should I use in the Keys?

Jet skis for short hops between islands, speedboats for longer runs across the reefs, and kayaks for the mangrove channels. Airboats are for the Grassrivers swamps, not the Keys — they are built for shallow marsh water.

The Bottom Line

Leonida is a water state, and GTA 6 treats it that way: kayaks in the mangroves, jet skis in the Keys, airboats in the Grassrivers, and scuba diving under the reefs. Learn the boats and you learn half the map — and the half most players will never see is the half that is underwater.

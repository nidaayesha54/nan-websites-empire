GTA 6 Pets: Can You Adopt a Dog in Vice City?

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Of all the questions animal lovers ask about Grand Theft Auto 6, one comes up more than any other: can you get a dog? Can you adopt one, keep it, take it on missions and teach it tricks — like Chop in GTA 5? The trailers show dogs in the streets and yards of Leonida, and that has set off a wave of rumours, wishlist posts and confident-sounding claims online. This guide does something different: it separates what Rockstar has actually shown from what fans hope for, so you know exactly where the line is.

The short answer is that we do not know yet. The longer answer is worth reading, because the history is encouraging — even if the confirmation is not there yet.

What is confirmed: dogs are in Leonida

Dogs appear in both trailers for GTA 6. They are visible in street scenes, yards and residential areas around Vice City and Leonida, and they appear in more than one scene, which suggests they are a regular feature of the world rather than a one-off detail. That is confirmed, because Rockstar itself showed them in released footage.

There is more. In the Extended Look, Jason directly interacts with a dog — and Rob Nelson, who co-runs Rockstar North, told one outlet that players can study, scold or pet a dog, and that the game has even accounted for what happens after a dog does its business: players can pick up a dog's poop using the owner's discarded doggy bag, and can even interfere with the owner, take the bag, and throw the poop at someone else. Pets and pet owners can react differently as you approach them, which is a small detail that says a lot about how alive the world is meant to feel. Domesticated cats have been seen in the footage too, and a python enclosure can be spotted inside Jason and Lucia's safehouse — although it is unclear whether the snake belongs to them or is purely environmental detail.

That is where the confirmed facts end. No trailer shows Lucia or Jason petting a dog as their own, adopting a dog, feeding a dog, or taking a dog on a mission. No announcement, interview or Rockstar statement has described a pet companion system, a dog mechanic, or any way for players to interact with animals as owners. If you read an article that tells you GTA 6 has pet companions, check its source. Unless it quotes Rockstar directly, it is speculation.

What fans hope for: a Chop for Lucia and Jason

The hope is easy to understand, because GTA 5 gave players Chop. Chop is Franklin Clinton's Rottweiler, and he is one of the most loved characters in the series. In GTA 5, players could walk Chop, play fetch with him, teach him tricks, and use his scent-tracking in missions. Chop had a personality, and he felt like a real dog. Fans hope GTA 6 gives Lucia and Jason a similar companion — a dog that lives with them, follows them, and becomes part of the story.

Rockstar has built the systems before, twice. Chop in GTA 5 was a pet in the full sense: a companion with a personality, not a weapon or a tool. And the camp dogs in Red Dead Redemption 2 could be patted and would follow players around. GTA Online added more, too: in the A Safehouse on the Hills update, players can name their pets and pet them. The studio knows how to make a dog matter, and it has done it three times.

But "could happen" is not "will happen". Until Rockstar says so, a companion dog in GTA 6 is a hope, not a fact.

The rumours: what is circulating online

Because Rockstar says so little, the internet fills the gap. The biggest rumour is the claim that GTA 6 lets you adopt a dog from a shelter in Vice City. It comes from wishlist posts and fan-made concept videos, not from Rockstar, and there is no source for it. A second claim says a dog will follow Jason and Lucia everywhere and help in missions. That is Chop's job description, copied onto a game that has never confirmed it. A third says the python in the safehouse is Lucia's pet. It might be — or it might be set dressing in a house that belongs to someone else.

There is also a community report that cuts the other way: one creator who visited Rockstar North reportedly asked about pets and was told that Jason and Lucia's status as fugitives made the feature impractical. That is a community report, not a statement from Rockstar, and it should be treated as unconfirmed — but it is the only account that addresses the question directly, and it is worth knowing about.

What animal lovers should actually expect

Expect dogs everywhere and a pet system nowhere — at least, not one that has been announced. The dogs are in the trailers. The world is full of animals: dogs, alligators, flamingos, deer, pelicans, herons, gulls, wading birds and dolphins have all been spotted in the footage. The interaction system is deep enough to let you pet a stranger's dog and steal their doggy bag. The history is encouraging. And the game launched on November 19, 2026 — if Rockstar has a pet system to announce, it will announce it, and this guide will be updated the day it does.

Until then, enjoy the dogs in the world without planning a build around them. Do not pay anyone for a pet guide, a pet unlock or an adoption trick — there is nothing to unlock yet, and anyone selling it is selling a hope.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you adopt a dog in GTA 6?

That is unconfirmed. No pet adoption, ownership, training or care system has been announced. Dogs appear in the trailers as part of the world, and Jason can pet a dog in the Extended Look — but no trailer shows a dog being adopted or kept.

Is Chop in GTA 6?

There is no confirmation that Chop appears in GTA 6. Chop is Franklin's dog in GTA 5, and GTA 6 follows Jason and Lucia in Leonida — a different city, a different story, and no announced link between them.

What animals are in GTA 6?

Dogs, alligators, flamingos, deer, pelicans, herons, gulls, wading birds and dolphins have all been spotted in the trailers and released footage. Rockstar has said Leonida holds more than 170 animal species, but it has not published a full list.

The bottom line: the dogs are real, the adoption is not — yet. GTA 6 gives you a world full of animals and a history of great game dogs, and the honest answer to "can I adopt one" is still "we do not know". Watch Rockstar's own channels, not the rumour posts, and you will hear it first when the answer changes.

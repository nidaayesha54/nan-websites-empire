Should I Adopt a Pet? 🐶 Readiness Quiz

8 honest questions about your lifestyle, budget, and time. No sign-up, no email — just a straight answer plus your personalized preparation steps.

1. How many hours per day can you spend with a pet (play, walks, care)?

2. Your living situation?

3. Can you comfortably cover $100–$200/month in pet costs?

4. Do you have (or could you build) a $1,000 emergency vet fund?

5. How stable is your life for the next 10–15 years?

6. Does everyone in your household want a pet?

7. Any pet allergies in the household?

8. If you had to travel for 2 weeks, who cares for the pet?

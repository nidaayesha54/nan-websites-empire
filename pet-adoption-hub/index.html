Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions every future adopter asks.

How much does it cost to adopt a pet in the USA?

Adoption fees typically range from $50 to $500 depending on the shelter, location, and animal. Dogs usually cost $75–$350, cats $25–$200. Your first-year total (vet visits, food, supplies) is usually $1,000–$2,000 for a dog. Try our cost calculator for a personalized estimate.

Is adopting a pet better than buying from a breeder?

Adoption is usually cheaper, the pet is already spayed/neutered and vaccinated, and you give a homeless animal a second chance. A responsible breeder makes sense only when you need a specific working or service-dog lineage. Read our full comparison guide before deciding.

What should I bring to a shelter visit?

Bring a photo ID, proof of address, and (if you rent) written landlord permission. Bring your family and any current dogs for a meet-and-greet. Use our shelter visit checklist so you ask every important question.

How long does the pet adoption process take?

Same-day adoptions are common at many shelters. If the shelter requires a home check or reference calls, expect 1–7 days. Rescue groups with foster networks can take 1–3 weeks.

Can I adopt a pet if I rent my home?

Yes, but most shelters require written landlord approval confirming your pet type, breed, and weight limits. Check your lease's pet policy before falling in love with a shelter dog.

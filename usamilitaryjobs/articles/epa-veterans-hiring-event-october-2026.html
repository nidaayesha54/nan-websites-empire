EPA Veterans Hiring Event October 22, 2026: Meet Hiring Managers at Research Triangle Park

The EPA is opening its Research Triangle Park campus to veterans and active-duty service members on October 22, 2026 — with face-to-face meetings with hiring managers. RSVP is required. Here is how to get in.

The U.S. Environmental Protection Agency is opening the gates of its Research Triangle Park campus to veterans and active-duty service members on Thursday, October 22, 2026 — a rare in-person hiring event where job seekers sit down with EPA hiring managers face to face. It runs 10:00 AM to 2:30 PM Eastern at 109 TW Alexander Drive, Durham, North Carolina.

The format is the draw: meetings and informational interviews with hiring managers, not just recruiters. In federal hiring, that face time is gold — the person who may read your application meets you first. The event is posted on the federal government's USAJOBS events page.

One big catch: this event is only for veterans and current service members, space is limited, and walk-ins are not part of the plan. Here's everything you need to get through the gate — literally.

What Happens at the Event

Expect one-on-one and small-group conversations with EPA hiring managers from the RTP campus — the agency's major research hub. You'll learn which teams are hiring, what specialized experience they need, and how their USAJOBS announcements will be written.

Come ready to talk substance: your MOS-to-civilian translation, technical skills, clearances, and education. Hiring managers remember candidates who ask sharp questions about the work — not just the benefits.

Before You Drive to RTP: The Rules

This is a secure federal campus, so the logistics are strict. RSVP through EPA's RSVP form before you show up — no RSVP, no entry. Plan on a security screening at the door and arrive the 30 minutes early the agency asks for.

Bring a REAL ID: a license without the star may not get you past the gate. Also pack your resume, DD-214, transcripts, and any certifications — if a hiring manager wants to move fast, hand them everything on the spot.

What EPA Hires Veterans For

The RTP campus is EPA's research engine: environmental science, engineering, data analysis, laboratory work, and the administration that supports it. Veterans with technical ratings, engineering degrees, or environmental compliance experience fit naturally.

Not a veteran? The same week offers alternatives — but for those who served, this is a purpose-built door into federal environmental careers. Claim veterans' preference on your USAJOBS applications with DD-214 and SF-15 documentation after the event.

How Veterans Attend the EPA Hiring Event

  1. Confirm your eligibility. The event is for veterans and active-duty service members only — bring proof of service.
  2. RSVP through EPA's form now. Space is limited and walk-ins are barred; register before you plan the drive.
  3. Assemble your document kit. Resumes, DD-214, SF-15 and VA rating letter for 10-point claims, transcripts, certifications.
  4. Bring a REAL ID and arrive early. Give yourself the extra 30 minutes for security screening at the gate.
  5. Meet the hiring managers. Ask about live and upcoming announcements; note names and follow up on USAJOBS.
  6. Apply with preference claimed. Submit on USAJOBS with veterans' preference documented — don't let the face time go to waste.

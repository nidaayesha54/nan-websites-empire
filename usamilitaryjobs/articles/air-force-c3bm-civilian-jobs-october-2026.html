Air Force C3BM Civilian Jobs 2026

The Air Force's Command, Control, Communications and Battle Management (C3BM) organization is seeking civilian talent to advance the Department of the Air Force Battle Network — and it is hiring through a digital hiring event conducted entirely through an online intake process. Selected candidates participate in virtual interviews on an ongoing basis throughout the open announcement window. For veterans, transitioning service members, and defense-minded civilians, this is a direct route into the 170,000-member Air Force Civilian Service.

What Is C3BM?

C3BM is the integrating entity for command, control, communications, and battle management within the Department of the Air Force. It aligns technical architecture and acquisition authorities to deliver decision advantage — getting the right information to warfighters faster than adversaries can react. Its professionals oversee major modernization programs including deployable communications infrastructure, cloud-based tactical applications, and the software pipelines accelerating efforts like the Advanced Battle Management System.

To cut through bureaucratic hurdles and drive rapid capability delivery, C3BM leadership anchors the workforce around three operational core values: Ruthless Focus, Violent Execution, and Extreme Teaming. As one leader put it: "We're not ordinary around here — come be extraordinary."

Roles C3BM Is Filling

The hiring push spans the disciplines that keep complex defense programs moving. Contracting and financial specialists secure industry partnerships and execute critical resources. Logisticians and security specialists oversee life-cycle support and protect vital defense technologies. If your background is in acquisition, program finance, supply chain, cybersecurity, or systems security, your skill set maps directly to C3BM's needs.

Why Veterans Fit C3BM

C3BM's mission-first culture and operational tempo will feel familiar to anyone who has served. Veterans bring precisely what battle management demands: comfort with ambiguity, experience operating under pressure, and an instinct for teamwork. The explicit call for DD Form 214 documentation signals that veteran applicants are not just welcome — they are expected. Veterans' preference and special hiring authorities can further strengthen your application, so claim every eligibility you hold.

How to Apply to the C3BM Digital Hiring Event

  1. Watch for the open announcement. Monitor USAJOBS and Air Force civilian careers channels for the C3BM posting and its closing date.
  2. Build a two-page resume. The limit is strict — distill your experience to the most relevant accomplishments and keep contact information prominent.
  3. Gather your documents. College transcripts (unofficial or official), your SF-50 if you are a current or former fed, and DD Form 214 (member copy 2 or 4) if you are a veteran.
  4. Submit through the online intake process. Follow the announcement's instructions exactly — incomplete packages stall.
  5. Prepare for a virtual interview. Interviews happen on a rolling basis, so be ready on short notice; research C3BM's mission and core values beforehand.
Two-page limit is firm: C3BM requires a resume of no more than two pages. Federal resumes are usually longer — for this event, ruthlessly prioritize relevance over completeness, and keep a longer master resume for standard USAJOBS applications.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Air Force C3BM?

C3BM — Command, Control, Communications and Battle Management — is the Department of the Air Force organization integrating technical architecture and acquisition to deliver decision advantage to warfighters, including programs like the Advanced Battle Management System.

How does the C3BM digital hiring event work?

It is conducted entirely through an online intake process. Selected candidates participate in virtual interviews on an ongoing basis throughout the open announcement window — no in-person job fair attendance required.

What documents do veterans need for C3BM applications?

Veterans should submit a DD Form 214 (member copy 2 or 4), if applicable, along with the required two-page resume and college transcripts. Current or former federal employees should include an SF-50.

What is the resume limit for C3BM applications?

Applicants must submit a resume of no more than two pages detailing professional experience and contact information — stricter than the typical multi-page federal resume.

What benefits do Air Force civilian employees receive?

Selectees join the 170,000-member Air Force Civilian Service with comprehensive federal civil service benefits, continuing education opportunities, and specialized career progression across defense programs.

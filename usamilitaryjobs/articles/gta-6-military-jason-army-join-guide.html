GTA 6 Military: Jason's Army Past and Can You Join?

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Jason Duval is the first hero in Grand Theft Auto history with a confirmed military background — and that one fact shapes almost everything about him. He grew up around grifters and crooks, joined the Army to put a troubled adolescence behind him, and came out the other side with skills, scars and a record he cannot shake. The question players keep asking is whether you can follow him into the service. The short answer is no: there is no way to enlist in GTA 6. But the military is all over the game anyway, and this guide explains where.

Jason's service is not a footnote. It is the reason he can handle a rifle, read a swamp at night and stay calm when a plan falls apart. Rockstar built the character around it, and the game keeps showing you the evidence — in his tattoos, his clothes and his own words. You just cannot sign up yourself.

What Rockstar has confirmed about Jason's service

Rockstar's own character profile says it plainly: Jason grew up surrounded by con artists and crooks, and seeking an escape from his troubled teenage years, he spent time serving in the Army. That attempt at changing his circumstances did not entirely work. He ended up in the Leonida Keys, working for local drug runners and doing the same kind of work he had tried to leave behind.

The launch trailer adds the part Rockstar's profile leaves out. In Jason's own words: "I got in some trouble in the Army. Didn't leave on the best of terms. They don't give loans or anything to people like me." He left the service under a cloud, and the game makes sure you know it — it is why he cannot get a loan, why he is living rent-free in Brian Heder's property, and why the easy life he wants keeps getting harder.

The Marauders and North Fort Law

Look closely at Jason and the clues pile up. He has a tattoo of a skull in a beret on a parachute, with a "Marauders" scroll, on his arm. In the launch trailer he wears a T-shirt that reads "U.S. Army Marauder Combat Training School — North Fort Law." Rockstar has not explained the unit, and fans have filled the gap with theories — including the idea that the Marauders were an elite Ranger-style unit, based on the tattoo's design — but the unit itself is unconfirmed. What is confirmed is the name: North Fort Law.

That name has history. North Fort Law was a district in New Guernsey in the very first GTA game, from 1997 — a small piece of series lore that Rockstar has quietly carried forward for nearly thirty years. Whether it is a place, a base or just a training school in GTA 6 is not settled. What is settled is that Jason's Army past is real, it ended badly, and it follows him into every mission.

Jason's Army skills in action

The game shows you what the Army gave him. In the launch trailer, Jason wades through a swamp at sunset with two armed men, wearing night-vision goggles and carrying a silenced rifle. It is the clearest action sequence in the trailer, and it looks nothing like a street criminal at work — it looks like a soldier. He also has a military working dog at his side on at least one job, another detail that only makes sense for a man with a service background.

That is the point of the backstory. Jason is not a random guy who can shoot a gun — he is someone who was trained to shoot a gun, and to hit what he aims at. It explains why he is so capable in a fight, why he stays calm under pressure, and why Lucia trusts him when the plan goes wrong. It also explains the gap between the two leads: Lucia is the fighter, Jason is the professional.

Mile Zero Air Station: the military base

Leonida has a military base, and it is called Mile Zero Air Station, on Goose Key in the Keys. The base holds the game's most desirable military hardware: the P-996 Lazer jet, the Rhino tank, and the Buzzard attack helicopter among others. Getting in is the hard part. Breaking into the airport or the base brings four to five stars of police attention, and the base is guarded accordingly.

Stealing a jet from Mile Zero is one of the classic GTA challenges, and it works the same way it worked at Fort Zancudo in GTA 5: get in, grab the vehicle, get out before the response arrives. What is different in GTA 6 is the wanted system around it — the police remember your face, your clothes and your car, so the escape matters as much as the theft.

Can you join the military in GTA 6?

No. There is no enlistment, no recruitment office and no military career path in the game. Jason's service is backstory, not a job you can take — and the same is true of every other uniform in Leonida. You can wear a police uniform as a disguise, you can steal a military jet, and you can fight the Army if you push the wanted level high enough. You cannot join it.

Whether the military gets involved at six stars, as it did in older games, has not been confirmed. Some guides report military task forces and armoured units at the top wanted level, but that is guide reporting, not a Rockstar statement. Until the studio confirms it, treat the six-star military response as unconfirmed — and treat Jason's Army past as the real military story in GTA 6.

Frequently Asked Questions

Was Jason in the Army in GTA 6?

Yes. Rockstar's character profile confirms it: he joined the Army to escape his troubled teens, and left on bad terms. He is the first GTA hero with a confirmed military background.

Can you join the Army in GTA 6?

No. There is no enlistment or military career in the game. Jason's service is backstory, and the military appears as the Mile Zero Air Station base and possibly at the highest wanted levels.

What is North Fort Law in GTA 6?

The name on Jason's "Marauder Combat Training School" T-shirt. It was also a district in the first GTA game from 1997. Rockstar has not explained what it is in GTA 6.

The bottom line: Jason's Army past is the military story of GTA 6 — a soldier who left on bad terms and cannot go back. You can steal his old employer's jet, but you cannot re-enlist.

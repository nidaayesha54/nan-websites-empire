Navy Civilian Jobs 2026: NUWC Newport Hiring Event (Nov 7) & Direct-Hire Engineering Openings

The Navy is recruiting civilians this fall: NUWC Newport hosts an on-site hiring event on November 7, campus visits continue through October, and direct-hire flyers are open now. Here's where to look and how to apply.

You don't have to wear a uniform to work for the Navy. The Department of the Navy employs well over 200,000 civilians — engineers, scientists, logisticians, IT specialists, and skilled tradespeople — and its commands are actively recruiting this fall. From a November hiring event in Rhode Island to direct-hire flyers you can answer by email today, here are the live civilian pathways worth knowing about.

Navy civilian hiring runs on two tracks: USAJOBS announcements (competitive and merit-promotion) and direct-hire flyers posted by individual commands, which often accept resumes by email. Both are open to the public unless stated otherwise.

NUWC Newport On-Site Hiring Event — November 7

The Naval Undersea Warfare Center (NUWC) Division Newport — the Navy's premier undersea warfare research center — is holding an on-site recruiting fair on November 7, 10 AM–2 PM. Candidates register in advance and upload a resume and transcript into the command's talent acquisition portal before the event. NUWC Newport hires across engineering, science, business, and IT disciplines for undersea systems work.

Direct-Hire Flyer: Naval Oceanographic Office Technicians

The Naval Oceanographic Office (NAVOCEANO) is accepting resumes through December 31, 2026 for Mechanical Engineering Technicians under Direct Hire Authority (Flyer #26-006). Resumes go by email with the flyer number in the subject line and unofficial transcripts encouraged.

The listed salary range runs from $36,409 to $116,362 per year depending on grade, and highly qualified applicants are contacted by email for interviews.

Shipyard and Fleet Civilian Openings

The Navy's shipyards and fleet commands post a steady stream of civilian openings. Recent examples from DoD Civilian Careers postings include Nuclear Production Resources General Foreman (GS-1601-12), Interdisciplinary Engineer (GS-0801/0830/0855-11/12), Ship Progressman (Nuclear), and Nuclear Electrical Systems Inspector roles supporting ship repair, maintenance, and modernization — including positions tied to Guam's naval shipyard operations, with October 2026 closing dates on several announcements.

The Trident Refit Facility in Bangor similarly accepts resumes by email for shipyard trades — electricians, pipefitters, machinists, shipfitters, welders, and more — alongside engineering, IT, and quality-assurance roles.

Military spouses: the Navy recognizes military spouse hiring preferences — including Military Spouse Preference (MSP) for PCS moves and non-competitive appointment authority — which can open doors to Fleet civilian careers. Select the right preference category when you apply and upload your documentation.

How to Apply for Navy Civilian Jobs

  1. Search USAJOBS for Navy announcements. Filter by Department of the Navy and your occupation series.
  2. Watch command career pages for direct-hire flyers. NUWC, NAVSEA warfare centers, shipyards, and NAVOCEANO post flyers that accept email resumes.
  3. Register for the Nov 7 NUWC Newport event if you're in the Northeast — upload your resume and transcript beforehand.
  4. Claim your preferences. Veterans and military spouses: select the right preference category and upload DD-214, SF-15, marriage certificate, or PCS orders as applicable.
  5. Keep resumes tight. Direct-hire flyers often cap resumes at two pages — tailor ruthlessly.
Verify before you apply: flyer deadlines, event registration links, and announcement closing dates change. Always confirm details on the official command page or USAJOBS announcement before submitting.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the NUWC Newport civilian hiring event?

November 7, 10 AM–2 PM — register ahead and upload a resume and transcript.

How do Navy direct-hire flyers work?

Individual Navy commands post flyers for hard-to-fill roles and accept resumes directly — often by email — under Direct Hire Authority.

What is the deadline for the Naval Oceanographic Office technician flyer?

Resumes are being accepted through December 31, 2026, for Mechanical Engineering Technician positions (Flyer #26-006), with salaries from $36,409 to $116,362 per year.

Do Navy civilian jobs give veterans' preference?

Yes. Veterans' preference applies to Navy civilian hiring; disabled veterans claiming 10-point preference submit a DD-214 and SF-15.

Can military spouses get Navy civilian jobs?

Yes — Military Spouse Preference and non-competitive appointment authority are available.

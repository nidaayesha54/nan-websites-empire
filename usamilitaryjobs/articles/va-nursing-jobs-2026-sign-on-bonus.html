VA Nursing Jobs 2026: $12,000 Sign-On Incentive for RNs, $6,000 for LPNs

The VA is paying nurses to join: the Louisville VA Healthcare System has approved a $12,000 sign-on incentive for registered nurses and $6,000 for licensed practical nurses, each with a two-year service obligation. Add 50 days of paid time off and full federal benefits, and this is one of the strongest nursing offers in the country.

The Department of Veterans Affairs remains one of the largest employers of nurses in the United States — and it is competing for talent with real money. The Robley Rex VA Healthcare System in Louisville has approved a $12,000 sign-on incentive for registered nurses (RNs) and a $6,000 sign-on incentive for licensed practical nurses (LPNs), each tied to a two-year service obligation. VA facilities from Hampton to Boston to Coatesville are actively hiring RNs, LPNs, and nursing assistants across all care areas.

The VA Nurse Total Rewards Package

The sign-on bonus is just the headline. VA nursing announcements describe a comprehensive package that rivals or beats most hospital systems:

  • Paid time off: 50 days per year — 26 days of annual leave, 13 days of sick leave, and 11 paid federal holidays.
  • Retirement: Traditional federal pension with 5-year vesting, plus the federal 401(k)-style Thrift Savings Plan with up to 5% in VA matching contributions.
  • Insurance: Federal health, vision, dental, term life, and long-term care insurance — many of which can be carried into retirement.
  • Licensure freedom: One full and unrestricted license from any U.S. state or territory qualifies you, and you can transfer to any of VA's 1,255 facilities nationwide without losing benefits, accumulated leave, or pay.
  • Education support: The Education Debt Reduction Program helps with student loans for difficult-to-recruit direct patient care positions, plus ongoing education and leadership training.

Why Veterans and Military Spouses Should Look Closely

VA nursing is a natural landing zone for military medical personnel transitioning to civilian life — and about one-third of employees at facilities like Hampton VA are veterans themselves. The mission resonates: caring for those who served. Military spouses benefit from the license portability and the ability to transfer between VA facilities when the military moves the family, without resetting benefits or seniority. If you served as a corpsman, medic, or military nurse, your experience translates directly.

How to Apply

  1. Search USAJOBS: Find VA nursing announcements for your target facility on USAJOBS.gov — each facility posts its own vacancies with its own incentives.
  2. Contact the recruiter: Facilities like Louisville publish recruitment office contacts. A short email with your CV can surface opportunities before they post widely.
  3. Verify your license: Confirm you hold a full, unrestricted nursing license from any U.S. state or territory.
  4. Ask about incentives: Sign-on amounts and Education Debt Reduction Program eligibility are facility-specific — confirm in writing what your offer includes.
  5. Prepare for federal onboarding: Expect a background investigation, pre-employment physical, drug screening, and seasonal influenza vaccination requirements for health care personnel.
Incentives are facility-specific: There is no single nationwide VA bonus schedule. The $12,000/$6,000 figures are Louisville's approved amounts — your target facility's announcement governs. Always verify the incentive, the service obligation, and the payout terms before accepting.

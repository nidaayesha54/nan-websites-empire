What Does a Defense Economics Team Do?

Modern competition between states plays out as much in markets, supply chains, and financial systems as on battlefields. A Defense Economics team analyzes how economic levers - trade flows, resource dependencies, financial networks, development finance - shape the strategic environment across Africa, and advises command leadership on where economic tools can advance security objectives. The "startup-mode" description signals a small, fast-moving team building new analytic approaches rather than maintaining legacy processes - attractive to candidates who want ownership and impact early.

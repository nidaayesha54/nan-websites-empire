Veterans Virtual Hiring Fairs Oct 22–28, 2026: G.I. Jobs & Hiring Our Heroes Schedule

Two major virtual hiring events for the military community land in the next week: the G.I. Jobs/Military Friendly Virtual Job Fair on October 22 and Hiring Our Heroes' virtual fair for logistics, skilled trades, and transportation on October 28. Both are free — here is how to attend and stand out.

The military community gets two high-value virtual hiring events in the coming week. On Thursday, October 22, 2026, G.I. Jobs hosts its Military Friendly Virtual Job Fair, connecting veterans, transitioning service members, and military spouses with military-friendly employers nationwide. On Wednesday, October 28, Hiring Our Heroes (HOH) — the U.S. Chamber of Commerce Foundation's military hiring program — runs a Virtual Hiring Fair focused on logistics, skilled trades, and transportation.

Virtual fairs have a real advantage over in-person events: no travel, no geography limits, and the ability to "visit" dozens of employer booths in a single afternoon. For military spouses who move frequently and veterans in rural areas, that access is transformative.

October 22: G.I. Jobs / Military Friendly Virtual Job Fair

G.I. Jobs' virtual fairs bring together employers that have earned Military Friendly designations — companies with proven track records of hiring and retaining veterans. Expect employers across healthcare, technology, manufacturing, logistics, and government contracting. The virtual format typically includes employer booths, live chat with recruiters, and scheduled video interviews.

Register in advance and complete your profile fully before the event opens. Recruiters at virtual fairs search attendee profiles the way they search resume databases — an incomplete profile is invisible. Upload a polished resume and write a concise summary that states your target roles.

October 28: Hiring Our Heroes — Logistics, Skilled Trades & Transportation

HOH's sector-specific virtual fair targets three fields where military experience translates directly: logistics, skilled trades, and transportation. Veterans with supply, maintenance, transportation, and engineering backgrounds will find employers who understand MOS-to-civilian skill mapping without needing it explained.

Sector fairs are efficient: every employer in the virtual room is hiring for roles you can actually fill. If your background is in 88M motor transport, 92Y supply, 91-series maintenance, Seabee construction ratings, or Air Force logistics readiness, this is your highest-yield event of the month.

More Military Hiring Events This Month

The VA's VMSTEP outreach calendar lists additional October events for the military community, including the Camp Lejeune Career Summit for Transportation & Logistics on October 21, a Virtual Hiring Fair for Financial Services on October 21, and a SkillBridge Fellowships information session on October 27. The SkillBridge session deserves special attention from active-duty members within 180 days of separation: SkillBridge lets you intern with civilian employers during your final months of service while still drawing military pay.

Also on the calendar: the Navy Region Mid-Atlantic Hampton Roads Retiree Summit & Career Fair on October 30 in Virginia Beach — an in-person option for retirees and the Hampton Roads military community.

How to Stand Out at a Virtual Hiring Fair

Virtual fairs reward preparation more than charisma. Research the employer list before the event and rank your top ten targets. Prepare a two-sentence pitch: your background plus the role you want. Keep your resume to achievements with numbers — "supervised 12-person maintenance team, 98% readiness rate" beats a list of duties.

During the event, initiate chats rather than waiting — recruiters staff virtual booths in shifts and the early hours have the shortest queues. Ask each recruiter for a direct application link or requisition number, then apply on the employer's site the same day while your conversation is fresh in their system.

Military spouses: mention your military spouse hiring preference and PCS status upfront in chats. Many participating employers have dedicated military spouse programs with remote-friendly roles designed around frequent moves.

How to Attend the Virtual Hiring Fairs

  1. Register for the G.I. Jobs fair (Oct 22). Sign up through the G.I. Jobs virtual job fair registration and complete your attendee profile with an uploaded resume.
  2. Register for the HOH fair (Oct 28). Sign up for Hiring Our Heroes' logistics, skilled trades, and transportation virtual hiring fair.
  3. Research employers in advance. Review the participating employer lists and rank your top targets before the event opens.
  4. Prepare your pitch and resume. Write a two-sentence introduction and tailor your resume with quantified achievements.
  5. Follow up the same day. Apply through the direct links recruiters give you and send thank-you messages referencing your conversations.

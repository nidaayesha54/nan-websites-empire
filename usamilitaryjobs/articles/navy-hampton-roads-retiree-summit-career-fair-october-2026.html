Navy Hampton Roads Retiree Summit & Career Fair Oct 30, 2026: Virginia Beach Hiring Guide

Navy Region Mid-Atlantic hosts its Retiree Summit & Career Fair on October 30, 2026, in Virginia Beach, Virginia — combining retiree benefits briefings with a career fair for military retirees, veterans, and families. Here is who should attend and how to prepare.

Navy Region Mid-Atlantic will hold its Hampton Roads Retiree Summit & Career Fair on Friday, October 30, 2026, in Virginia Beach, Virginia. The event serves the dense military community of the Hampton Roads region — home to Naval Station Norfolk, the world's largest naval base, plus major Air Force, Army, and Coast Guard installations — and it pairs two things retirees need in one day: benefits updates and employment connections.

Retiree summits are a distinct format from standard job fairs. Alongside employer booths, they typically feature briefings from the VA, TRICARE, DFAS (pay), and retiree service organizations on the benefits changes that affect your household. For retirees weighing a second career, that combination — updated benefits knowledge plus face time with employers — is uniquely efficient.

Who Should Attend

The summit is built for military retirees, but the career fair portion typically welcomes veterans, transitioning service members, and military family members as well. If you retired recently and have not yet started a second career, this is your highest-density networking opportunity in the region. If you retired years ago and are considering a career change, the benefits briefings alone justify the trip — TRICARE, VA, and retirement pay rules change regularly, and summits deliver the updates in plain language.

Military spouses in Hampton Roads should also attend: the region's defense contractors, healthcare systems, and port logistics employers actively recruit from the military family community, and many offer the schedule flexibility military life demands.

What Employers Show Up in Hampton Roads

Expect a heavy presence from defense contractors and shipbuilding — Huntington Ingalls Industries (Newport News Shipbuilding), the region's largest industrial employer, regularly recruits veteran talent for skilled trades, engineering, and program management. Federal agencies with major Hampton Roads footprints, including NAVSEA, the shipyards, and DLA, recruit for civilian positions where veterans' preference gives you a genuine edge.

Beyond defense, the region's healthcare systems, port and logistics operators, and state and local government agencies value the reliability and leadership that military retirees bring. Come prepared to discuss how your military leadership translates: "led a 40-person division with a $2M operating budget" speaks every employer's language.

Retiree Benefits Sessions: Don't Skip Them

The summit's briefings are as valuable as the career fair. Typical topics include TRICARE plan updates and open-season choices, VA disability and healthcare enrollment, DFAS retirement pay and SBP (Survivor Benefit Plan) decisions, and state veterans benefits — Virginia offers meaningful state-level benefits for resident veterans and retirees, from tax provisions to education assistance.

Bring your questions about concurrent receipt, VA claims, and TRICARE-for-Life transitions if they apply to you. The representatives at these briefings answer in a day what might take weeks of phone calls to resolve.

Second-Career Strategy for Military Retirees

The strongest second-career strategy for retirees is targeting roles where your clearance, leadership, or technical specialty commands a premium. Active or recent security clearances are among the most valuable credentials in the Hampton Roads job market — defense contractors pay significant premiums for cleared talent, so lead with your clearance status.

Also consider federal civilian service: military retirees can sometimes combine federal civilian employment with retirement pay (with rules depending on your retirement type), and veterans' preference applies to many announcements. Check USAJOBS.gov for the Hampton Roads commuting area before the fair so you can ask agency recruiters about specific announcements.

How to Prepare for the Retiree Summit & Career Fair

  1. Confirm event details. The summit is October 30, 2026, in Virginia Beach under Navy Region Mid-Atlantic — check the official Navy Region Mid-Atlantic channels for venue and time updates.
  2. Prepare a civilian resume. Translate military experience into civilian terms with quantified achievements; bring printed copies for employer booths.
  3. List your benefits questions. Write down your TRICARE, VA, DFAS, and SBP questions in advance so you don't forget them at the briefings.
  4. Research USAJOBS first. Search USAJOBS for Hampton Roads-area federal announcements so you can ask agency recruiters about specific openings.
  5. Lead with your clearance. If you hold or recently held a security clearance, state it upfront — it is your highest-value credential in this market.

When Are Taxes Due in 2026? Key IRS Deadlines

By NAN Marketing Agency · October 12, 2026 · 4 min read

Missing a tax deadline costs real money — penalties and interest start immediately. Here is every date that matters for 2026.

Filing Deadlines

  • April 15, 2026 — tax year 2025 returns due (the return most people file "in 2026").
  • April 15, 2027 — tax year 2026 returns due.
  • October 15, 2026 — extended deadline for 2025 returns (file Form 4868 by April 15 to get it).

Important: Extensions Extend Filing, Not Paying

Form 4868 gives you six extra months to file, but any tax you owe is still due April 15. Pay what you estimate you owe with the extension to avoid penalties and interest. Unsure what you owe? Run our 2026 tax estimator.

2026 Estimated Tax Payment Dates

Self-employed? You likely owe quarterly estimated payments for tax year 2026:

  • Q1: April 15, 2026
  • Q2: June 15, 2026
  • Q3: September 15, 2026
  • Q4: January 15, 2027

What If You Miss a Deadline?

File as soon as possible — the failure-to-file penalty (5% per month) is far worse than the failure-to-pay penalty (0.5% per month). Even if you cannot pay in full, file on time and set up an IRS payment plan.

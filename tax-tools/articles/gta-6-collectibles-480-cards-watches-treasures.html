GTA 6 Collectibles: 480 Cards, Watches and Treasures

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and underneath the story sits a second game: the hunt. There are well over 1,000 collectibles scattered across Leonida — cards in phone boxes, watches at gang hideouts, treasure on the sea floor, flamingos on lawns — and they are not decoration. Some of them count toward 100 percent, some of them unlock cheats, and some of them pay real money when you sell them. This guide lists every type, how many there are, where to look, and which ones are worth your time first.

The hunt matters because it is the longest thing in the game. The story takes 50 to 65 hours; the 100 percent checklist is estimated at roughly 200. Most of that gap is collectibles, and the players who finish the checklist are the ones who started picking things up early instead of leaving them for the end.

Collectible cards: 480 in 18 sets

The biggest set is the cards: 480 of them, in 18 sets. Fifteen of the sets count toward 100 percent, and completing three sets earns the Silver trophy called Don't Touch My Deck. The sets are themed around Leonida life — the Vice City Flamingos, the Vice City Narcos, the Vice City Manatees and the Vice City Club De Futbol each have 24 or 30 cards, and the Liberty City sets reach back to Lucia's old home. Cards hide outside in yellow phone boxes on walls and in tool boxes, and inside buildings on surfaces or stuck to walls — so the hunt is a habit, not a trip: check the wall of every building you enter, and check the phone box on every street you walk.

Cards are also the set with the most practical reward. Completing all 24 Vice City Flamingos cards unlocks the Bubblegum screen filter, one of the post-story visual rewards the game ties to collecting. The cards themselves pay nothing, but the habit of looking for them is what finds the other collectibles too — the watches, the magazines and the tattoos all hide in the same places.

Watches, flamingos and underwater treasure

Luxury watches are the second set: 34 of them, marked with a gold icon, usually found at Gang Operations. They are the most valuable collectibles per item, because they unlock cheats — 10 watches unlocks Move Time Forward, and 20 unlocks Pause Time of Day, both of which are post-story cheats that change how the game plays. Cocaine flamingos are the third: 125 of them across the map, and destroying 50 unlocks the Inflatable Flamingo cheat, while 100 of them count toward 100 percent.

Underwater, the hunt is worth money. There are 33 underwater discoveries in Kenton's shipwreck sets around the Keys, and six shipwrecks around Key Lento hold sellable treasure. The wrecks are the Nuestra Senora de la Noche with 7 items, the Nuestra Senora de la Etica Laxa with 5, the Urca de Cinco Torres with 6, the Nuestra Senora Lujuriosa with 6, the Urca de Santa Sierra with 6, and the Nuestra Senora del Ocio with 10 gold doubloons. You need to finish Island Dreams in Chapter 1 before the hunt starts — after that, Kenton Rees gets back in touch, the map adds offshore search areas, and each dive site's map card tells you how many treasures you have already found. The Noche wreck alone holds a purple gemstone, a serpentine statue, a dragon pipe, an antique candlestick and three other items — early money, found on the sea floor.

The smaller sets: books, tattoos, hoops and stunts

The rest of the hunt is smaller sets with their own rules. There are 34 magazines, books and comics — 27 reads plus 7 Book Swaps — and 18 meditation spots, quiet places across Leonida where meditating at all of them earns a trophy. There are 45 magazine tattoos, 13 for Jason, 15 for Lucia and 17 others, scanned with the WAiNK app, and more than 100 WAiNK scans in total, covering landmarks, curiosities, sculptures and No Flusher tags. Scanning every piece of space debris with WAiNK unlocks the Powdered Sugar filter, and finding all 16 Girlypop vehicles unlocks the Neon Jungle filter.

Then there are the action sets. There are 100 basketball hoops across Leonida, and 90 of them count toward 100 percent — shoot hoops wherever you find a court, because the game is counting. And there are 397 stunts: 130 stunt jumps, 202 Under the Bridge flights, 40 Knife Flights and 25 BASE jumps. The stunts are the loudest part of the hunt and the easiest to leave for last, but they are also the ones that need the map knowledge the rest of the hunt teaches you — by the time you have found the cards, you know the bridges.

How to hunt without burning out

First, pick things up as you go. The cards, the watches and the flamingos are all on the routes the story already takes you, and a player who checks the walls and the phone boxes in every chapter will finish most of the sets without a single dedicated trip. Second, do the shipwrecks early: Island Dreams is a Chapter 1 mission, the treasure pays real money, and the diving gear — the scuba set with its metal detector and the Arras Bullhead speargun — makes the rest of the underwater hunt easier. Third, save the stunts and the hoops for after the story, when you have the whole map, the best vehicles and nothing left to unlock.

And do not chase the cheats first. The watches unlock time cheats and the flamingos unlock a spawn, but all of them are post-story rewards — they only matter once the story is done. The collectibles that matter during the story are the ones that pay: the shipwreck treasure, sold to Kenton, is early money in a game where the early money is the hardest to find.

FAQ

How many collectibles are in GTA 6? Well over 1,000: 480 cards in 18 sets, 34 luxury watches, 33 underwater discoveries, 125 cocaine flamingos, 34 magazines and books, 18 meditation spots, 45 magazine tattoos, 100 basketball hoops and 397 stunts.

Do GTA 6 collectibles unlock cheats? Yes. 10 watches unlock Move Time Forward and 20 unlock Pause Time of Day; 50 cocaine flamingos unlock the Inflatable Flamingo; the full Vice City Flamingos card set unlocks the Bubblegum filter.

Where do you find collectible cards in GTA 6? Outside, in yellow phone boxes on walls and in tool boxes. Inside buildings, on surfaces or stuck to walls.

The bottom line: GTA 6 hides more than 1,000 collectibles, and the hunt is the longest game inside the game. Pick up cards as you go, dive the six shipwrecks early for Kenton's money, and let the stunts and the hoops wait until the story is done.

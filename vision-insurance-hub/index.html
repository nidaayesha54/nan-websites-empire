Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common vision insurance questions.

How much does vision insurance cost in 2026?

Most individual vision insurance plans in the USA cost $13–$20 per month ($156–$240 per year). Employer-sponsored plans are often cheaper — around $5–$13 per month for employees. See our full 2026 vision insurance cost guide for details.

Is vision insurance worth it?

Vision insurance is usually worth it if you wear glasses or contacts — one exam plus a new pair of glasses often covers the full yearly premium. If you only get an annual exam and nothing else, paying out of pocket may be cheaper. Try our eye care cost estimator to run your own numbers.

Does vision insurance cover LASIK?

Most vision plans do not cover LASIK because it's considered elective surgery. Many plans do offer a 15–25% LASIK discount at participating providers. Use our LASIK break-even calculator to compare costs.

How much does an eye exam cost without insurance?

A comprehensive eye exam typically costs $100–$250 without insurance, depending on your location and whether extras like retinal imaging are included.

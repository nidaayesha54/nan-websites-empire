Car Insurance Premium Estimator

Car insurance pricing varies wildly by state, age, and driving history. This estimator multiplies a national base premium by the factors insurers actually use.

How the estimate is calculated

We start from an $800 national base 6-month premium and multiply by five rating factors:

  • State (0.80–1.45): Michigan and Florida are the priciest; Vermont and Maine among the cheapest — reflecting repair costs, litigation, and weather risk.
  • Age: under 25 pays 1.6× (highest crash risk); 40–64 gets 0.9×; 65+ ticks up to 1.1×.
  • Vehicle value: pricier cars cost more to repair/replace (0.85×–1.3×).
  • Driving record: clean 1.0×, ticket 1.15×, at-fault accident 1.30×, DUI 1.80×.
  • Coverage level: liability-only 0.55×, standard 1.0×, full coverage 1.45×.

The range shown is 85%–120% of the central estimate, reflecting how much quotes vary between insurers for the same driver.

Note: Insurers also weigh mileage, credit-based insurance scores (where legal), and garaging ZIP code — so treat this as a planning estimate, then compare real quotes.

How the break-even math works

Full coverage = liability + collision (your car in an at-fault crash) + comprehensive (theft, hail, deer). The question is whether the extra premium is worth the protection on your car's value.

  • Annual extra cost = extra 6-month premium × 2.
  • Break-even years = vehicle value ÷ annual extra cost — how fast you'd pay the car's full value in extra premiums.
  • ≤ 4 years → liability-only usually wins; bank the savings as a self-insurance fund.
  • ≥ 8 years → keep full coverage; one loss wipes out years of savings.
  • 4–8 years → borderline: decide by whether you could replace the car from savings.

Note: Also consider raising your collision deductible (e.g., $500 → $1,000) as a middle path — it cuts the surcharge while keeping catastrophe protection.

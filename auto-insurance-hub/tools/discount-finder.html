How the savings are estimated

Each discount carries an estimated percentage off a $1,800/year base premium (near the national average for full coverage): bundling ~15%, telematics up to ~20%, good student ~10%, multi-car ~12%, and smaller 3–8% discounts for paperless, paid-in-full, low mileage, defensive driving, and loyalty.

Because insurers cap how discounts stack, we apply a realistic 40% total cap — even if your checked discounts sum higher.

Note: Discount names and values vary by insurer and state. Use your results as a checklist when you call your agent — "Am I getting the ___ discount?" is the highest-ROI sentence in insurance.

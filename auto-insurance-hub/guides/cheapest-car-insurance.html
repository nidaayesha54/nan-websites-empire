Why your state matters so much

  • No-fault & litigation: Michigan's unlimited injury benefits and Florida's fraud/litigation rates push premiums sky-high.
  • Weather: hail in Texas, hurricanes in Florida/Louisiana, and deer in the Midwest all show up in comprehensive claims.
  • Repair costs & theft: dense cities mean pricier labor and more theft — urban ZIP codes pay more than rural ones in the same state.
  • State rules: some states ban credit-based pricing or tightly regulate rates, compressing the range.

How to pay less in ANY state

  1. Compare 5+ quotes every renewal. Insurers weight factors differently — the cheapest company for your neighbor may be the priciest for you.
  2. Raise deductibles to $1,000 if you have an emergency fund; it can cut collision/comprehensive 15–30%.
  3. Claim every discount — run our discount finder quiz before you call.
  4. Right-size coverage on older cars with our liability vs full coverage tool.
  5. Try telematics if you're a careful driver — safe drivers routinely save 15–25%.

Note: Moving soon? Get quotes for the new ZIP before you move — a 20-mile move can change your premium by hundreds per year.

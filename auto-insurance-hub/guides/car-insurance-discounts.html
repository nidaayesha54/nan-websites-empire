15 Car Insurance Discounts You Should Claim in 2026

2026-10-12 · 8 min read

Insurers rarely volunteer discounts — you have to ask. Here are 15 worth claiming, with typical savings. First, run our discount finder quiz to see which ones fit you.

The big three (10–25% each)

  • 1. Bundling (up to ~25%): auto + home or renters with one insurer. The single biggest easy win.
  • 2. Telematics / usage-based (up to ~25%): an app or device scores your driving; safe drivers save big.
  • 3. Good student (up to ~15%): B average or better, usually under age 25.

Vehicle & policy discounts

  • 4. Multi-car (~12%): insuring 2+ vehicles together.
  • 5. Paid-in-full (~7%): pay the 6-month term upfront instead of monthly.
  • 6. Paperless + autopay (~5%): e-billing with automatic payments.
  • 7. New car (~10%): some insurers discount vehicles under 3 years old.
  • 8. Safety features (~5%): anti-lock brakes, airbags, anti-theft systems.
  • 9. Green vehicle (~5%): hybrid/EV discounts at several major insurers.

Driver-profile discounts

  • 10. Low mileage (~8%): under ~7,500 miles/year.
  • 11. Defensive driving course (~5%): state-approved courses; required for some senior discounts.
  • 12. Loyalty (~5%): 3–5+ years with the same company.
  • 13. Good driver / accident-free (~10–20%): clean record for 3–5 years.
  • 14. Affinity groups (~5%): alumni, professional, or employer associations.
  • 15. Military (~5–15%): active duty, veterans, and families at military-focused insurers.

Note: Stacking is capped — insurers limit combined discounts (often ~25–40%). Prioritize the big three first, then layer the easy 3–8% wins like paperless and autopay.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much disability insurance do I need?
A common target is 60% of your gross income, since most policies cap benefits there. Our coverage calculator subtracts your other income from your monthly expenses to find your personal number.
What is the difference between short-term and long-term disability insurance?
Short-term disability (STD) pays for weeks to months after a short waiting period (0–14 days). Long-term disability (LTD) starts after a longer elimination period (often 90 days) and can pay for years or until retirement. See our comparator.
How much does disability insurance cost in 2026?
Individual long-term policies typically cost 1–3% of annual income. Age, occupation, health, benefit amount, and elimination period drive the price. Read the full cost guide.
What is an elimination (waiting) period?
How long you must be disabled before benefits begin — commonly 30, 60, 90, or 180 days. Longer periods mean lower premiums. Take our quiz to find your fit.
Is employer disability insurance enough?
Often not: group policies typically replace only 40–60% of salary, cap monthly benefits, and payouts may be taxable. Run your numbers in the coverage calculator.

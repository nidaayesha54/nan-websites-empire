GTA 6 Dogs: The Breeds of Leonida and How to Pet Them

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Dogs are everywhere in GTA 6, and they are not decoration. Leonida is modelled on Florida, and Florida is a place where dogs are on beaches, in parks, in cars and on porches — so Rockstar filled Vice City and the wider state with them, from tiny Chihuahuas on Ocean Beach to German Shepherds at front gates. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with more than 170 animal species confirmed by Game Informer, and this guide covers the dogs: every breed spotted so far, where they live, how to pet them, and the one thing Rockstar has not confirmed yet.

The dogs matter because they are one of the few things in the game you can interact with gently. In a game about heists and chases, a dog you can crouch down and pet is a small, deliberate piece of calm — and the game rewards it, in a small way, if you take the time.

The Breeds Spotted So Far

Four dog breeds are confirmed across multiple sources: Chihuahuas, Dobermans, German Shepherds and Rottweilers. The Rottweiler is the most visible — one appears on a leash in a screenshot with Bae-Luxe, the rap duo Real Dimez's other half, and Rottweilers walk on leashes with their owners on Ocean Beach. German Shepherds appear as guard dogs and police dogs, which is exactly where you would expect them in a Florida city. Dobermans show up in yards and on streets, and Chihuahuas are the small dogs of the city — one was even spotted wearing a purple tutu with fairy wings on Ocean Beach.

Other dogs have been seen in single sources, so treat them as spotted rather than confirmed. A Miniature Pinscher runs on the beach. A Yorkshire Terrier is carried by a woman as Jason drives past. And the trailers show large, muscular, short-coated dogs in yards and on streets — the kind of dog most people would call a pit bull-type, breeds that are among the most common in real Florida. Rockstar has not confirmed the breed of any single dog in the game, so every breed name here is an identification from what can be seen, not an official list.

Pet, Scold or Study

The most famous dog moment in the game comes from Rockstar's Extended Look footage, where Jason walks past a pit bull and the game offers three options: pet, scold or study. Jason crouches down and pets it. Launch-week players confirmed the basics: you can pet both dogs and cats, by holding L2 on PlayStation 5 or LT on Xbox when you are close to one. Reports say petting a dog boosts your Focus — the stat that slows time in combat — and improves your Criminal Profile, the game's record of how you behave. That last part is reported rather than confirmed, so treat it as a bonus, not a promise.

The interaction is not always friendly. The same three options include scold, and players have shown dogs getting hit by car doors and cats thrown out of hijacked cars. How you treat animals affects your character: reports say killing domestic animals for fun pushes your Criminal Profile toward its worst end, and that hurting people and animals without reason slowly moves the profile toward a state you cannot turn back from. That is reported, not confirmed — but the direction is clear. Be kind to the dogs, and the game notices.

Where the Dogs Live

Dogs live where people live. On Ocean Beach they walk on leashes with their owners, and the beach is where you will see the most variety — small dogs carried, big dogs on leads, and the occasional dog running free. In the suburbs they sit in yards and on porches, and in the city they wait outside shops and ride in the backs of pickup trucks. The trailers show medium and large mixed-breed dogs most clearly, the sturdy, short-coated, sun-loving dogs that belong in the heat — the kind locals call Florida brown dogs.

The dogs are part of a much bigger animal system. Game Informer's cover story confirmed more than 170 species across Leonida: alligators in the Grassrivers, flamingos and pelicans on the coast, manatees in the waterways, dolphins offshore, and cats in the city alleys, hunting mice and resting on outdoor ATMs. The dogs are the animals you will meet first, because they live where you live — and they are the easiest way to see how much detail Rockstar put into the living world.

Can You Own a Dog?

Here is the honest answer: no pet ownership has been confirmed. Pets are present in GTA 6, and domestic animals live alongside Leonida's residents, but Rockstar has not announced an adoption, ownership or pet-care system for Jason and Lucia. Some reports mention an adoptable pet mechanic for the apartment, and one guide describes a dog living in the safehouse — but none of that has been confirmed by Rockstar, and no trailer or official announcement has shown Lucia or Jason adopting a dog or taking one on missions.

There is one small, strange detail that is confirmed, and it comes from an interview rather than footage. Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson told the YouTuber TGG that pedestrians walking their dogs will pick up after them with a bag — and that players can then grab that discarded bag and throw it at other people. It is a tiny detail, and it tells you everything about how seriously Rockstar took the dogs: even the poop bags are part of the world.

Frequently Asked Questions

What dog breeds are in GTA 6?

Four breeds are confirmed across multiple sources: Chihuahuas, Dobermans, German Shepherds and Rottweilers. Pit bull-types, Miniature Pinschers, Yorkshire Terriers, French Bulldogs and Labradors have also been spotted or are likely from the Florida setting, but Rockstar has not released an official breed list.

Can you pet dogs in GTA 6?

Yes. Hold L2 on PlayStation 5 or LT on Xbox near a dog or cat to pet it. In the Extended Look, Jason is offered pet, scold or study when he meets a pit bull. Reports say petting boosts Focus and improves your Criminal Profile, but that part is unconfirmed.

Can you own a pet in GTA 6?

Not confirmed. Dogs and cats are present as ambient animals and pets of other people, but Rockstar has not announced any adoption or ownership system for Jason and Lucia.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 dogs are Chihuahuas, Dobermans, German Shepherds and Rottweilers first, with pit bull-types and small companion breeds filling out the streets and beaches of Leonida. You can pet them, and the game quietly rewards you for it. You cannot own one — not yet, and not confirmed. Walk the beach, crouch down when a dog comes over, and enjoy the one interaction in Vice City that asks nothing of you at all.

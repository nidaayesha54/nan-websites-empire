GTA 6 Gangs: Who Actually Runs Leonida?

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Every GTA game has a criminal underworld, and GTA 6 has the most fragmented one yet — not a single family running a city, but a patchwork of bikers, smugglers, a strip-club empire, a music label funded by drug money, and a heist crew that spans the whole state. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and this guide maps the factions Rockstar has actually confirmed — through its own site, the trailers, the screenshots and the Extended Look — and separates them from the rumours, because half the faction lists online are built from fan theories dressed up as facts.

The split matters. Rockstar has directly named only one gang. Two more have been identified from official footage by the community. The rest are crews and criminal businesses from Rockstar's own character profiles. And the old Vice City gangs — the Cubans, the Haitians of 2002, the Diaz organisation — have not appeared in any official GTA 6 material. Leonida is a new continuity, and its criminal ecosystem is built around new names.

The One Gang Rockstar Named: Final Chapter MC

Rockstar has named one gang outright: the Final Chapter MC, an outlaw motorcycle club that controls Ambrosia, the rural sugarcane region in the north of Leonida. Rockstar's own description of the town says the refinery provides the jobs and the bikers provide everything else — and official screenshots show the club riding in force. The Final Chapter MC is not a background detail: it runs the underground economy of a whole region, and Ambrosia is its territory in a way no other faction's home is spelled out.

The club works alongside the Allied Crystal sugar refinery — the legitimate business that anchors the town and the criminal one underneath it. No individual member of the club has been officially revealed by Rockstar, which is worth knowing: the gang is confirmed, its members are not. If a guide names a Final Chapter leader, it is guessing.

The Two Street Gangs Spotted in Footage

Two more street gangs have been identified from Rockstar's own footage — not named by Rockstar, but visible in it. San4San, often shortened to S4S, is a Haitian street gang based in La Perle, Vice City's Little Haiti, marked by S4S chains and murals in Trailer 1. The gang's tags and emblem have been spotted by frame-hunters in official trailer footage, and its territory is the La Perle neighbourhood. PTT Youngin$ is a street gang of Southside Vice City, identified from graffiti and details in official promotional screenshots — and Rockstar's own product copy for the Ultimate Edition describes it as one of the loudest and most socially active gangs in Southside Vice City, with a raid on the group's compound included as one of the edition's extra missions.

Both identifications carry the same caveat: they come from community analysis of official material, not from Rockstar naming them. The PTT Youngin$ raid is the stronger of the two, because Rockstar's own edition copy names the gang. But no member of either gang has been named, no leader has been revealed, and neither gang has a confirmed role in the main story. And one popular theory should be dropped entirely: the idea that San4San connects to the Zoe Pound gang of real Miami history is fan inference from real-world parallels, not something Rockstar has ever confirmed.

The Crews and Criminal Businesses

Beyond the three gangs, Leonida's underworld is run by named crews and criminal businesses from Rockstar's own character profiles. Brian Heder runs a smuggling network out of the Leonida Keys, through Brian's Boat Works and Marina on Key Lento — a functioning boatyard that doubles as a smuggling hub, where Jason collects shakedowns when the story opens. Boobie Ike runs a criminal-legitimate empire from Vice City's Crosstown district: real estate, the Jack of Hearts strip club, and the Only Raw Records label, co-managed with Dre'Quan Priest. Dre'Quan himself is the label's CEO — a former street dealer turned music executive, aged 28, who signed the rap duo Real Dimez to the label. And Raul Bautista is the veteran bank robber of the group — confident, charming and calculating, always scouting for talent willing to take on the riskiest, highest-reward jobs, and the man who recruits Lucia into the heist crew.

Two smaller groups round out the picture, both from trailer analysis: the Thrillbilly Mud Club off-roaders in the Grassrivers and the High Rollerz Lifestyle car crew in Vice City. All of these are observed in official footage, not confirmed as factions by Rockstar, and the honest guide says so.

The Law, and What Is Not Confirmed

The other side of Leonida is just as fragmented. Official trailers and screenshots show liveries and decals for at least eight law enforcement agencies across the state, including the Vice City Police Department and the US Coast Guard — a reminder that the wanted system has many faces, and that the police in GTA 6 act on what they witness rather than on a map marker.

And here is what is not confirmed: Rockstar has not said whether GTA 6 has gang wars or territory control, and no full conquest system like GTA: San Andreas has been announced. Trailer evidence suggests factions hold regional influence, but influence is not a system. The rumoured factions — the Guardia Brothers, a possible Colombian cartel presence, returning names from the original Vice City — come from trailer frame analysis and community speculation, not from Rockstar, and none of them should be treated as fact. The factions that are real are the ones above: one named gang, two spotted in footage, and a handful of crews and businesses that Rockstar put on its own character pages.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many gangs are in GTA 6?

Rockstar has directly named one gang: the Final Chapter MC, which runs Ambrosia. Two more street gangs — San4San and PTT Youngin$ — have been identified from official footage and Rockstar's edition copy. The rest of the underworld is crews and criminal businesses from Rockstar's character profiles.

Are the old Vice City gangs back?

No — nothing from the original game's faction roster has appeared in official GTA 6 material. The Cubans, the Haitians of 2002 and the Diaz organisation are not confirmed. Leonida is a new continuity with new names.

Can you join a gang or take over territory?

Not that Rockstar has confirmed. No gang war or territory control system has been announced, and no faction has a confirmed join mechanic. Factions hold regional influence in the story, but that is not the same as a system you can play.

The Bottom Line

Leonida is run by the Final Chapter MC in Ambrosia, San4San in La Perle, PTT Youngin$ in Southside, Brian's smugglers in the Keys, Boobie's empire in Crosstown, and Raul's heist crew across the whole state. That is the confirmed underworld — one named gang, two spotted in footage, and the crews Rockstar profiled itself. Everything beyond that is a theory, and the theories are not worth your time until Rockstar names the next gang.

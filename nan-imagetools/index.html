FAQs

Are these tools really free?

Yes — all three tools are 100% free with no signup, no watermark and no file limit imposed by us (only your device's memory).

Are my images uploaded anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser using the Canvas API. Images never leave your phone or computer.

What quality setting should I use?

For websites and social media, 70–85% is the sweet spot — most photos look identical to the original.

Can I resize to exact pixel dimensions?

Yes — type any width and height in the resizer, or pick a preset (Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, Open Graph).

Which formats are supported?

Input: JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF, BMP. Output: JPG, PNG, WebP.

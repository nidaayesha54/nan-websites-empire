GTA 6 Voice Actors: Jason and Lucia Finally Named

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — and on the same day, Rockstar finally answered a question fans had asked for three years: who plays Jason and Lucia? The answer was posted in the game's credits on Rockstar's own site: Dylan Rourke plays Jason Duval, and Manni L. Perez plays Lucia Caminos. Until that morning, Rockstar had named no one. This guide covers the confirmed cast, the supporting actors, and how the secret held for so long — and why the fans who guessed right were right all along.

That matters because the cast was the biggest unsolved mystery of the whole marketing campaign. Rockstar published eight character profiles, two trailers, and a 27-minute gameplay showcase without crediting a single actor. The silence was so complete that it became a story of its own — and the reveal, when it came, was quieter than anyone expected: a credits page, posted on launch day, with no announcement at all.

The Two Leads: Dylan Rourke and Manni L. Perez

Jason Duval is played by Dylan Rourke, a television actor whose credits include 9-1-1: Lone Star, Station 19, and Grey's Anatomy. Fans had speculated that Rourke was Jason as far back as 2022, when Rockstar first revealed the game — and despite Rourke never teasing the role, the guess turned out to be exactly right. He heads the main cast section of the credits, and his is the voice players hear first.

Lucia Caminos is played by Manni L. Perez, and her confirmation came two days before launch, not from Rockstar but from The Game Awards: on November 17, the awards named her among its five Best Performance nominees for GTA 6, as Lucia — the first public confirmation that she plays the role. Rockstar's credits, posted on launch day, confirm it. This is not Perez's first time in the series: she previously appeared as a clubgoer in GTA 5, a role she reprised in later versions of the game. Her nomination made her the first actor in the cast to be named anywhere, and it broke the silence two days before the credits did.

The Supporting Cast

The credits name the rest of the characters Rockstar introduced before launch, and the list has a few surprises of its own. Stephen Root plays Brian Heder, Jason's landlord and boss in the Keys — a role Root himself confirmed in an interview with the Associated Press in September, the only actor to speak about the game before launch. Bobby Moynihan, a principal cast member of Saturday Night Live from 2008 to 2017, plays Cal Hampton, Jason and Lucia's comedic sidekick. Mike Forbs plays Boobie Ike, and Brad Carter plays Donnie.

The most talked-about name in the credits is one players were not expecting: Michael Hollick, who voiced GTA 4 hero Niko Bellic, is credited as "The Hitman". Whether that character is Niko himself is unconfirmed — the credit gives a role, not an identity — and it is the one casting detail in the whole list that Rockstar has left open to interpretation. The credits also name the musicians who host the radio stations, and dozens of other characters players meet later in the story, so the full list is best read with a warning: it names people you have not met yet.

Why Rockstar Said Nothing for Three Years

The silence was a choice, and it was total. Rockstar's eight character profiles — Jason, Lucia, Cal, Boobie, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista, and Brian Heder — describe the characters and never name an actor. Neither the GTA 6 site nor the PlayStation Store, Xbox Store, and Rockstar Store listings credited an actor when checked in late October 2026. IGN noted the silence in September, reporting that Rockstar had yet to confirm any GTA 6 actors — and the studio never broke it, not even when the game went gold.

The full credits run for over an hour in-game, and Rockstar posted them online at rockstargames.com/VI/credits, where they can also be opened from the pause menu under Settings, then Account, then Credits. The reveal was not a trailer or a post: it was a page, quietly published on launch day, for players to find. That is how the biggest casting secret in gaming ended — not with a bang, but with a credits roll.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who plays Jason in GTA 6?

Dylan Rourke. Rockstar's credits for the game, posted on its GTA 6 site on November 19, 2026, list him as Jason Duval. Fans had speculated he was Jason since 2022.

Who plays Lucia in GTA 6?

Manni L. Perez. Her name came out first on November 17, when The Game Awards nominated her for Best Performance as Lucia, and Rockstar's launch-day credits confirm the role.

How can I see the full GTA 6 cast list?

On Rockstar's site at rockstargames.com/VI/credits, or in the game from the pause menu under Settings, then Account, then Credits. Be warned: the full list names characters you only meet later in the story.

The Bottom Line

Three years of silence ended on launch day with a credits page: Dylan Rourke is Jason Duval, Manni L. Perez is Lucia Caminos, Stephen Root is Brian Heder, and Bobby Moynihan is Cal Hampton. The fans who guessed the two leads were right all along — and the one name nobody guessed, Michael Hollick as "The Hitman", is the mystery Rockstar left for players to solve themselves.

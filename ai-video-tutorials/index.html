Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the questions every new AI video creator asks.

How do I make my first AI video for free in 2026?

Pick a free AI video generator like Hailuo (MiniMax) or Luma Dream Machine, write a detailed prompt describing the shot, generate a 5–10 second clip, then assemble clips in a free editor like CapCut. Our step-by-step tutorial walks you through the whole process in under 30 minutes.

Which free AI video tools are best for beginners?

Beginners do best with Hailuo (generous free credits), Luma Dream Machine (fast text-to-video) and CapCut (free editing). They cover the full workflow — generation, voiceover, editing and captions — without spending anything.

How many AI videos can I make per month on free plans?

By stacking free tiers across Hailuo, Kling, Luma, Pika and Runway you can realistically produce 20–40 short AI videos per month without paying. Use our free budget planner to see exactly which free plans cover your output.

Can AI videos be used for ads and commercial projects?

Yes — paid plans of Runway, Kling, Luma, Pika, Hailuo and Sora all allow commercial use, which is required for ad campaigns. Free tiers generally forbid commercial use and watermark output. Read the full rules in our AI video ads guide.

How can I check if a video was made with AI?

Look for telltale artifacts like warping hands, flickering textures and unnatural physics, or run the video through the free AI Video Detector, which scans for common AI-generation signals.

GTA 6 Clips: How to Record and Share Your Best Moments

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

GTA 6 is full of moments worth keeping — a perfect getaway, a sunset drive through Vice City, a heist that goes exactly to plan, or one that goes gloriously wrong. The problem is that the best moments happen once, and if you are not recording, they are gone. This guide explains how to record on PlayStation 5 and Xbox, how to cut what you captured into vertical Shorts, and how to share your clips without breaking the reuse rules that get channels struck.

It matters because the launch has made GTA 6 clips the most competitive content on the internet. The creators winning right now are not the ones with the rarest footage — they are the ones who record everything, cut fast, and post within hours. The recording setup takes ten minutes. The habit of using it is what separates a channel from a camera roll.

Recording on PlayStation 5

On PS5, the Create button is your highlight detector. Set it up once: go to Settings, then Captures and Broadcasts, then Captures, and under Create button shortcuts pick Easy Video Clips. After that, one press saves the moment you just played — no menus, no thinking, no missed clip. The console keeps a rolling buffer of your recent play, so the button saves what already happened, not just what happens next.

That buffer is the whole point. The best GTA 6 moments are surprises — a chase that turns, a stunt that lands, a line of dialogue you did not expect — and you cannot predict them. With the Create button set to Easy Video Clips, you press it after the moment, not before it. Make it muscle memory: something great happens, press Create, keep playing. Sort the clips later.

Recording on Xbox

On Xbox, the capture button works the same way. Press the Xbox button to open the guide, then use the capture options to save a clip of what just happened, or start a recording before a mission you expect to be worth keeping. Keep your capture settings short — thirty to sixty seconds is the right length for a clip you will cut into a Short, and short captures are faster to sort, faster to edit and faster to upload.

Whichever console you are on, check two things before launch night: that you have free storage for captures, and that your clips are actually saving where you think they are. A capture setting that points at a full drive saves nothing. Free up space before you play, and test the button once in the first mission so you know it works when the moment that matters arrives.

Cutting a clip into a Short

A raw capture is not a Short. The cut is where a clip becomes content, and the rules are simple. First, cut to vertical 9:16 — the format for Shorts, Reels and TikTok — and keep the action centred, because the sides of a 16:9 capture get cropped. Second, open on the hook: the first one to two seconds decide whether anyone watches, so start on the moment, not the build-up. Third, add captions, because most viewers watch muted — burned-in captions carry the joke or the fact even with the sound off.

Keep it short. Thirty seconds is the sweet spot for a single moment, and sixty is the ceiling for most clips. If the moment needs more time, it is probably two clips, not one long one. And add your own hook text on top in the edit — two or three short words, small and clean, over a striking frame — rather than letting the game fill the screen with interface text.

Sharing without breaking the rules

Record your own play if you own the game — that is your footage, and you can share it. Never download, rip or re-upload someone else's stream or video: that is reused content, and it risks strikes and channel removal. If you do not own the game, AI-generated concept clips are the honest route — clearly labelled as AI, in the title or description, in the video itself, and in the platform's AI disclosure setting.

Post fast. The first twenty-four hours after a moment happens are when people search for it most, and a clip posted the next morning competes with a hundred clips posted the same night. Record, cut, caption, post — in that order, the same session, while the moment is still fresh. And watch every clip yourself before you upload it: a clip with a mistake in it is worse than no clip at all.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I save a clip on PS5?

Set the Create button to Easy Video Clips under Settings, Captures and Broadcasts, Captures. One press then saves the moment you just played, from the console's rolling buffer.

How long should a GTA 6 Short be?

Thirty seconds is the sweet spot for a single moment, sixty is the ceiling. Open on the hook in the first one to two seconds, cut to vertical 9:16, and add captions for muted viewers.

Can I post clips from someone else's stream?

No. That is reused content and it risks strikes and channel removal. Post your own recordings, or clearly labelled AI-generated concept clips if you do not own the game.

The bottom line: set the record button up once, press it after every great moment, cut to vertical, open on the hook, caption everything, and post the same session. Your own footage, cut fast, shared honestly — that is the whole system.

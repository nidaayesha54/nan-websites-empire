AI Video Ads — Step by Step: From Script to Launch

AI has collapsed the cost of video ad production: what once took a crew and a week now takes one person and an afternoon. This tutorial gives you the complete AI video ads workflow — the same steps professional media buyers use, adapted for AI generation.

1

Lock the offer and the hook

Every winning ad has one offer and one call-to-action. Write them as single sentences. Then write five different hooks — the first 3 seconds that stop the scroll. Examples: a shocking visual, a bold claim, a relatable pain point. Test hooks as variants later; the hook is 80% of ad performance.

2

Write the 15–30 second script

Use the proven ad structure: Hook → Problem → Product moment → Proof → CTA. That's roughly 35–75 spoken words. Read it aloud with a timer — if it runs over 30 seconds, cut words, not the CTA.

Beginner? Start with the free first-video tutorial to learn the tools, then come back — this workflow assumes you can generate basic clips.
3

Generate the visual shots

Plan 3–6 shots: a product close-up, a lifestyle context shot, and one "wow" shot (transformation, dramatic angle, satisfying motion). Use the same style anchor in every prompt — e.g. "bright commercial lighting, clean product photography style" — so the shots look like one campaign, not a collage.

Generate each shot 2–3 times and pick the cleanest. Reject anything with warping, extra fingers or melting logos — viewers notice, and you can double-check with the free AI Video Detector.

4

Voiceover, music and sound

Record or generate the voiceover first, then cut the visuals to the voice — never the reverse. Add upbeat royalty-free music at around −20dB under the voice. Sync your hardest cuts to the music's beats; it makes the ad feel 3x more professional.

5

Captions, end-card and compliance

Add large burned-in captions — most ad impressions play muted. Build a 2-second end-card with your logo, offer and CTA button text.

Compliance: use a paid AI plan for ad creative — free tiers forbid commercial use. Enable the platform's AI-generated label where required, follow ad disclosure rules, and never make false claims about the product.
6

Export variants and launch

Export 9:16 (Reels/TikTok/Shorts), 1:1 (feed) and 16:9 (YouTube) versions. Launch with 3 hook variants of the same ad — platforms will find the winner. Add UTM parameters to your link, run for 48 hours, then kill the losers and scale the winner.

Keep it organized

Ad production has the most moving parts of any AI video format. Use the video ad checklist to track every step, and the budget planner to see how many ad variants your free (or paid) tiers cover per month.

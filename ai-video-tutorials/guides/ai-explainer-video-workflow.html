AI Explainer Video Workflow: Script to Finished Video

Explainers are the highest-value AI video format — businesses pay $500–$5,000 for what you can now produce in a day. This workflow takes you from a rough idea to a polished explainer that holds attention to the final second.

1

Outline: problem → 3 points → takeaway

Every explainer answers one question. Structure it as: the problem (15s) → 3 key points (the body) → the takeaway (15s). One idea per scene, and plan 1 scene per 15–20 seconds of narration. More than 8 scenes for a 2-minute video means you're cramming.

2

Write and time the script

Write conversationally — ~130–150 words per minute. Then record a scratch read on your phone and time each section. This timing locks your scene plan. Rewriting after generating visuals wastes your most expensive resource: credits.

3

Storyboard each scene

Make a simple table: scene number, narration line, visual description. Example: "Scene 3 — 'Your data lives in twelve different tools' — visual: cluttered desk with glowing app icons floating, overwhelmed office worker." Lock the script before generating anything.

Track it: the explainer checklist walks you through this entire workflow step by step with auto-saved progress.
4

Generate consistent visuals

The #1 explainer killer is visual inconsistency — scenes that look like different videos. Fix it with a style anchor: one style phrase copied into every prompt, e.g. "flat vector illustration, teal and purple palette, soft gradients, minimal, no text". Generate each scene at 5–10 seconds and pick the cleanest take.

5

Record the final narration

Record in a quiet room or generate with an AI voice (ElevenLabs free tier works for short explainers). Speak slightly slower than feels natural — clarity beats energy for explainers. De-noise, normalize loudness, and cut the visuals to the narration, not the reverse.

6

Assemble, annotate and polish

Assemble scenes to the narration timeline. Add animated text for key terms and numbers — viewers remember what they read and hear. Keep background music subtle (−25dB). End with a clear summary card or CTA.

Export at 1080p. For YouTube, add chapters; for client work, deliver a clean MP4 plus the script. Before delivery, scan the final video with the free AI Video Detector to catch any generation artifacts you'd want to fix first.

Scale the workflow

Once this works, templatize it: save your style anchor, your caption presets and your export settings. Then use the project planner to scope each new explainer and the budget planner to confirm your free tiers cover the month's output.

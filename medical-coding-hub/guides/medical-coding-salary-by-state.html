Remaining states generally fall between $48,000 and $66,000. Remote roles often pay to the employer's market, not yours — a remote coder in Mississippi can earn coastal rates.

What raises your pay fastest

  1. Get certified — CPC holders earn roughly 15–20% more than non-certified coders.
  2. Add a specialty credential — surgical, E/M auditing, or risk-adjustment specialties command premiums.
  3. Learn inpatient (CCS) — facility coding pays more than pro-fee on average.
  4. Move into auditing or education — coding auditors and educators routinely earn $70,000–$90,000+.
  5. Go remote for a high-cost employer — national remote roles often benchmark to higher-cost markets.

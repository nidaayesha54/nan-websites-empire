How the RVU formula works

Medicare pays physicians using this formula:

Payment = (Work RVU × Work GPCI + PE RVU × PE GPCI + MP RVU × MP GPCI) × Conversion Factor

  • Work RVU — the physician's time, skill, and intensity.
  • Practice expense (PE) RVU — overhead: staff, rent, equipment.
  • Malpractice (MP) RVU — liability insurance cost.
  • GPCI — geographic adjustment (1.000 = national average).
  • Conversion factor — dollars per RVU, set annually by CMS (default here is the 2025 value, $32.35 — update it for the current year).
Educational use only. Sample RVUs are approximate and for learning — verify against the current CMS Physician Fee Schedule before any billing use. Actual payment varies by payer contract.

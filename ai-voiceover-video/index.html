Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best AI voiceover generator in 2026?

ElevenLabs leads for realism and voice cloning, Murf AI is best for YouTube narration workflows, and Play.ht offers the widest language coverage including Urdu and Hindi. CapCut's free TTS is the best $0 option for short-form creators.

How do I make AI voiceovers sound natural?

Write conversational scripts with short sentences, expand all numbers and abbreviations into words, add punctuation for pacing pauses, and pick a voice that matches your content's tone. Run your script through a TTS optimizer before generating audio.

How long will my voiceover be?

At a natural 150 words per minute, 150 words ≈ 1 minute of audio. Use our duration calculator to estimate speech time plus pauses from your exact script.

Can I use AI voiceovers for YouTube monetization?

Yes, if you have commercial rights from the voice provider and your video adds original value beyond the narration. Always check the tool's license terms — most paid plans include commercial use.

AI Voiceover Script Optimizer

Paste any script below. The optimizer rewrites it the way AI voices want to read it: numbers become words ("$5.99" → "five dollars and ninety-nine cents"), abbreviations are expanded, URLs and emojis are removed, and pacing line-breaks are added. What the optimizer fixes

  • Numbers → words: "2024" becomes "twenty twenty-four" in year context or "two thousand twenty-four" — TTS engines guess wrong half the time.
  • Money & percentages: "$5.99" → "five dollars and ninety-nine cents", "300%" → "three hundred percent".
  • Times: "3:30 PM" → "three thirty P M" — no more "three colon thirty".
  • Abbreviations: Dr., Mr., e.g., i.e., etc., St., Ave. all expanded.
  • Links & emojis stripped: URLs read aloud ruin narration; emojis are skipped anyway.
  • Pacing: long paragraphs split into sentence lines; ALL-CAPS headings converted to title case with pause marks.

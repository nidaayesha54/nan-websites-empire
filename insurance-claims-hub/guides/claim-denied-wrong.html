Claim Denied by Mistake? 7 Common Errors & Fixes

A shocking number of denials are simple mistakes. Check these before you panic.

1. Wrong billing code (CPT/ICD error)

Fix: Ask your provider's billing office to review and resubmit with the correct code. This alone resolves many denials.

2. Duplicate claim

Fix: If the claim was submitted twice, confirm with your insurer that only one is processed.

3. Wrong patient or policy information

Fix: A single wrong digit in a member ID can trigger a denial. Verify your details with both the provider and insurer.

4. Missing prior authorization

Fix: If authorization was actually obtained, have the provider send the authorization number to the insurer. If it wasn't, ask your doctor to request retroactive authorization.

5. Service coded as "not medically necessary"

Fix: Ask your doctor for a letter of medical necessity and file an appeal with it attached.

6. Out-of-network confusion

Fix: Under the No Surprises Act, many out-of-network emergency and ancillary services are protected. Check whether your situation qualifies.

7. Claim never received

Fix: Providers sometimes fail to file. Call and confirm the claim was actually submitted, then track it with our claim tracker.

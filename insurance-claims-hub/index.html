Frequently Asked Questions

How do I appeal a denied insurance claim?

File an internal appeal with your insurer within the deadline (usually 180 days for health plans). Include a clear letter explaining why the denial was wrong, attach supporting documents, and if denied again, request an external review.

What does an EOB tell me?

An Explanation of Benefits shows what your provider billed, what your insurer paid, what was adjusted, and what you may owe. It is not a bill — don't pay it until you get the actual bill from your provider.

How long does an insurer have to decide a claim?

For most US health plans, insurers must decide pre-service claims within 30 days and post-service claims within 30–60 days, depending on plan type.

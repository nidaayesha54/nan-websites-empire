GTA 6 Online: Not at Launch — What We Know

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — and if you bought it hoping to jump straight into an online mode with your friends, here is the fact that matters most: there is no online mode in GTA 6 at launch. The game is a single-player experience only. The PlayStation Store lists it as offline play only, for one player, and nothing in the finished game contradicts that. This guide explains what that means, what is known about multiplayer coming later, and what to do in the meantime.

It is worth saying plainly because the confusion is everywhere. GTA Online — the multiplayer mode of GTA 5 — is one of the biggest games in the world, and many players reasonably assumed its successor would arrive on day one. It did not. Rockstar has announced no date and no name for GTA 6's multiplayer, and anyone selling you a launch-day online guide is selling you something that does not exist yet.

Single-Player Only at Launch

The launch game is Jason and Lucia's story, and only that story. There is no online lobby, no shared world, no multiplayer menu — the game you bought is the campaign, the open world of Vice City and Leonida, and the activities inside it. That is a deliberate choice, and it is the same shape as the last launch: when GTA 5 arrived in 2013, its online mode followed two weeks later. But there is no equivalent announcement for GTA 6, and no two-week promise has been made. Do not plan your purchase around a date that has not been given.

That also means there is no crossplay question to answer yet — there is nothing to cross between. The multiplayer conversation starts from zero, and everything below is about what comes next, not what is in the game today.

What the Reporting Says About 2027

The strongest signals point to 2027. Bloomberg's Jason Schreier reported in October 2026 that multiplayer was expected the following year, and Twitch chief executive Dan Clancy has pointed the same way. Neither of them is Rockstar, and neither gave a month — but two independent, well-placed voices saying the same thing is the best information available right now. Treat 2027 as the likely window, not a promise.

A court filing has also mentioned a 32-player format, which is the only concrete detail about the shape of the mode that has surfaced. Thirty-two players would be a very different scale from GTA Online's large lobbies, and it suggests a more focused experience — but a court filing is not a product announcement, and the format could change before anything is shown. File it under "interesting, unconfirmed" and move on.

GTA Online Is Not Going Anywhere

While GTA 6's multiplayer is still to come, GTA Online — the GTA 5 mode — keeps being supported. Rockstar has not shut it down, and there is no sign it plans to. That matters for two reasons. First, if you want to play GTA with friends today, GTA Online is still there, still updated, and still the place to do it. Second, it tells you something about Rockstar's thinking: it is not in a hurry to replace a mode that still works, which fits with a 2027 window rather than a rushed launch add-on.

The practical advice is simple. If you bought GTA 6 for the story, you have one of the best-reviewed games ever made, and the online question does not touch it. If you bought it mainly to play with friends, GTA Online is your game until Rockstar says otherwise — and you should not pay for GTA 6 expecting a multiplayer mode that is not in the box.

What to Watch For

When Rockstar does announce multiplayer, it will be through its own channels — a trailer, a newswire post, a store listing update. That is the only signal worth acting on. Until then, ignore fake launch dates and anyone claiming to have played the mode already. The mode has not been shown, named or dated, and the people who tell you otherwise are guessing or lying.

The other thing to watch is the name. Rockstar has not said whether the mode will be called GTA Online, something new, or nothing at all. The name matters because it will tell you whether this is a continuation of the GTA 5 mode or a fresh start — and that, in turn, will tell you whether your GTA Online progress, money and crews carry over. Right now, nobody outside Rockstar knows.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there online multiplayer in GTA 6?

Not at launch. GTA 6 is single-player only, listed as offline play for one player. Multiplayer is expected in 2027 based on reporting from Bloomberg's Jason Schreier and Twitch's Dan Clancy, but Rockstar has announced no date or name.

Can I still play GTA Online?

Yes. GTA Online, the multiplayer mode of GTA 5, is still supported and still running. It is the only GTA multiplayer available right now, and it is the place to play with friends until GTA 6's mode arrives.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 has no online mode at launch — single-player only, offline play, one player. Multiplayer is expected in 2027, with a 32-player format mentioned in a court filing, but Rockstar has announced nothing. Play the story, keep playing GTA Online with your friends, and wait for Rockstar's own announcement before believing any date.

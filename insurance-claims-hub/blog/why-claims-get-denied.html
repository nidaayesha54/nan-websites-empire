5 Reasons Insurance Claims Get Denied (and How to Prevent Each)

Published 2026 · 4 min read

1. Missing prior authorization

Prevent it: Before any non-emergency procedure, call your insurer (or check the portal) and confirm authorization is on file. Get the authorization number in writing.

2. Out-of-network provider

Prevent it: Verify every doctor, lab, and facility is in-network before your visit — including anesthesiologists and labs your in-network doctor might use.

3. Coding errors

Prevent it: After your visit, ask the billing office which codes were submitted. One wrong digit can sink a claim.

4. "Not medically necessary"

Prevent it: Ask your doctor to document medical necessity clearly in your records before the service, and request a letter of medical necessity you can keep on file.

5. Missed filing deadlines

Prevent it: Most plans require claims within 90–180 days of service. Track every claim with our free claim tracker so nothing slips.

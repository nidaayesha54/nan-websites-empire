Your Right to an External Review, Explained

Published 2026 · 4 min read

What is external review?

If your insurer denies your internal appeal, federal law (and most state laws) give you the right to have an independent third party review the decision. The reviewer has no connection to your insurer — and their decision is binding on the insurer.

How do I request one?

  1. Complete your insurer's internal appeal first (usually required).
  2. Your final denial letter must explain how to request external review and the deadline (often 4 months).
  3. File the request with your state's department of insurance or the federal external review process, depending on your plan type.

What are my chances?

External reviewers overturn a meaningful share of denials — particularly "medical necessity" denials where your doctor's documentation is strong. It's free to request, so there's little downside to trying.

Tips for a strong external review

  • Include your doctor's letter of medical necessity
  • Attach the full internal appeal record
  • Reference the specific policy provisions that cover your service

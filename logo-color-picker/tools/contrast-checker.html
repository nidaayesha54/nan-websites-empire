Logo Contrast Checker (WCAG)

A beautiful logo that nobody can read is a failed logo. Pick your text and background colors — we calculate the exact contrast ratio and grade it against WCAG 2.1 standards.

What ratio does a logo need?

Aim for AA (4.5:1) on any logo text — small wordmarks appear at favicon and app-icon sizes. Large display lettering passes at 3:1. AAA (7:1) is ideal for accessibility.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best color for a logo in 2026?

There is no universal winner. Blue dominates tech and finance for trust, red wins food for appetite appeal, and green leads health brands. Use our palette generator to build a scheme for your niche.

How many colors should a logo have?

Most professional logos use 2–3: a primary, an accent, and a neutral. More than four rarely survives at small sizes.

What is brand color psychology?

It studies how hues shape perception — blue signals trust, red signals energy, green signals growth, black signals luxury. See our color psychology guide.

Can You Play GTA 6 With Friends? Co-op Explained

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Can you play GTA 6 with friends? It is one of the most-asked questions about the game, and the answer is the one many players do not want to hear: no — not at launch. GTA 6 is a single-player game, with no online mode, no co-op and no split-screen announced. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Rockstar and Take-Two describe it as a "single-player experience" — the PlayStation Store lists it as 1 player with offline play enabled, and the Xbox Store lists it as single player. This guide explains exactly what that means, what you can play with friends instead, and when multiplayer might actually arrive.

The confusion is understandable, because GTA 5 had one of the biggest online modes in gaming history, and many players bought GTA 6 expecting the same. But GTA 6 is a different game with a different launch: a story about two people escaping Vice City, told over 50 to 60 hours, with no online component attached. The online mode, if it comes, will come later — and nobody at Rockstar has said when.

No Co-op, No Split-Screen, No Online — at Launch

There are three separate questions hiding inside "can I play with friends", and the answer to all three is no at launch. Co-op — playing the story together with a friend — does not exist: the campaign is a single-player story with two leads, Jason and Lucia, and you switch between them alone. Split-screen — two players on one console, one screen each — has not been announced and has never been a feature of a GTA story campaign. And online multiplayer — the GTA Online-style mode — has not been announced either: no name, no date, no player count, no feature list, nothing at all from Rockstar.

The record is consistent. When Take-Two opened pre-orders on June 24, 2026, the announcement described Grand Theft Auto VI as a single-player experience with no mention of an online counterpart. Across ten Newswire posts about GTA VI, from Trailer 1 to the Vice City Collection, none mentions an online mode. Take-Two's November 9 earnings release gives the launch date and platforms and says nothing about an online component. And at a shareholder meeting on September 17, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick confirmed that no recurrent consumer spending is planned at launch — the business way of saying there is no online economy to spend money in yet.

Where the 2027 Talk Comes From

If you have seen "multiplayer in 2027" quoted as fact, here is where it comes from: Twitch CEO Dan Clancy told Bloomberg in September 2026 that he expected a bigger Twitch viewership bump "next year when they launch multiplayer" — meaning 2027. That is a platform executive's expectation, not a Rockstar announcement. Jason Schreier wrote in October that the online component is "expected to be released in 2027". That is reporting, not confirmation. Rockstar has confirmed neither a date nor a window, and there is no title, no beta date, no platform list and no confirmation that a future mode will resemble GTA Online at all.

There is also a datamined hint worth knowing about: a PS5 backend tracker found multiplayer activity entries — joinSession, launchActivity and launchMultiplayerActivity — in the launch build on November 9. That is interesting, and it is not an announcement. Backend entries can exist for testing, for a future mode, or for features that never ship. Treat it as a hint, not a promise, and do not pay anyone who claims it proves a launch date.

What to Play With Friends Instead

The honest answer to "what do I play with friends?" is the game you already have: GTA Online, the multiplayer side of GTA 5, keeps running after November 19, and Take-Two has said it will support it beyond GTA 6's release. Your GTA Online character, cash and cars do not transfer into GTA 6 — Rockstar has announced no migration — but the mode itself is still there, still updated, and still the only Rockstar online world available on launch day.

And the single-player game is not a small consolation prize. The story runs about 50 to 60 hours for a focused run and 70 to 80 hours for most players, with 91 main missions, and the road to 100% completion is roughly 200 hours. That is months of play before any online mode could matter — and the shared experience, for now, is talking about the story with friends who are playing it too, not playing it with them.

The Scams to Avoid

The gap between what players want and what exists has already filled with scams. Any site, video or seller claiming to offer GTA 6 co-op, a multiplayer unlock, an online-mode download or an early-access multiplayer key is lying — there is no such thing to download, and the files on offer are malware. The same applies to "free GTA 6" offers and APK downloads: GTA 6 is a console game, there is no PC version and no mobile version, and anything claiming otherwise is a fake. Buy the game only from the PlayStation Store or the Xbox Store, play it as the single-player game it is, and wait for Rockstar to announce what comes next.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does GTA 6 have co-op?

No. The campaign is single-player only, with two leads you switch between alone. No co-op mode has been announced, and no split-screen mode has been announced either.

Is there an online mode in GTA 6?

Not at launch. Rockstar and Take-Two describe GTA 6 as a single-player experience, and no online mode has been announced. The 2027 date comes from the Twitch CEO's expectation and from reporting, not from Rockstar.

Can I play GTA Online while I wait?

Yes — GTA Online, the multiplayer side of GTA 5, keeps running after GTA 6's launch, and Take-Two has said it will support it beyond the release. Nothing transfers into GTA 6, but the mode itself is still there.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 is a single-player game at launch: no co-op, no split-screen, no online mode, and no date for any of them. The 2027 talk is an expectation, not an announcement. Play the story, play GTA Online on GTA 5 if you want multiplayer, and ignore anyone selling you a co-op unlock that does not exist.

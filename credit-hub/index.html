❓ Frequently Asked

What is a good credit score in 2026? ＋

FICO scores range 300–850. Generally 670–739 is Good, 740–799 Very Good, and 800+ Exceptional. Most lenders offer their best rates at 740+.

How fast can I improve my credit score? ＋

Paying down high credit card balances can lift scores within one billing cycle. Building from poor to good typically takes 6–18 months of consistent on-time payments.

Are these credit tools free? ＋

Yes — every calculator on CreditHub is free, needs no signup, and runs entirely in your browser. Your data never leaves your device.

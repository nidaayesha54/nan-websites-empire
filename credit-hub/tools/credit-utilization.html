Why utilization matters so much

Credit utilization = your revolving balances ÷ your total credit limits. It's the second-biggest FICO factor (~30%), and unlike payment history, it has no memory — your score reflects only the most recently reported ratio. That makes it the fastest lever you can pull.

  • Under 10% — the sweet spot for top scores.
  • Under 30% — the widely cited ceiling; above this, scores start dropping.
  • Per-card matters too — one maxed-out card hurts even when your overall ratio looks fine.

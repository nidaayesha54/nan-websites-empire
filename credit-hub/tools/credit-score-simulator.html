How the simulation works

FICO scores weigh five factors: payment history (~35%), amounts owed (~30%), length of credit history (~15%), new credit (~10%), and credit mix (~10%). This simulator applies published, widely-cited impact ranges to your inputs:

  • Utilization brackets: under 10% / 10–29% / 30–49% / 50%+. Dropping a bracket typically adds 10–25 points, with diminishing returns above 760.
  • Late payments: a single 30-day late mark can cost 60–110 points (higher starting scores fall further) and stays on reports up to 7 years.
  • Hard inquiries: usually −3 to −5 points each and fade within months.
  • Closing old cards: shortens average account age and cuts total available credit — a double hit to utilization and history length.
Educational estimate only. Real scoring models use far more signals. This is not a prediction and not financial advice.

Avalanche vs snowball — which wins?

Avalanche (highest APR first) minimizes total interest — it's mathematically optimal. Snowball (smallest balance first) kills debts fastest for quick psychological wins, which research links to better follow-through.

Our calculator runs both with identical payments so you can see the real dollar difference for your debts. Often the gap is smaller than people expect — which means the "best" method is the one you'll actually stick with.

Why APY gaps matter more than they look

Interest compounds on interest, so a 4-point APY gap doesn't cost you 4% — over a decade it can cost you half your balance in forgone growth. That's why high-yield accounts are the easiest raise most people never ask for. Learn what to look for in our best HYSA guide.

APY already includes compounding

APY (annual percentage yield) is the effective yearly rate after compounding — so future value is simply deposit × (1+APY)years, whatever the bank's compounding schedule. Confused by the jargon? Read APY vs APR explained.

The power of 'boring' compounding

$500/month at 4.50% APY grows to roughly $75,400 in 10 years — about $15,400 of it pure interest. No stock market required, FDIC-insured up to limits. Compare rates with the APY comparison tool.

Why HYSAs beat big-bank savings

Online banks skip branches and pass the savings to you: 4%+ APY vs 0.01% at legacy banks. Same FDIC insurance, wildly different growth. See our 2026 HYSA guide for what to check before switching.

Rates move — plan for it

HYSA rates track the Fed funds rate, so 4.50% today isn't promised forever. Re-run this projector yearly, or lock part of your cash in a CD ladder.

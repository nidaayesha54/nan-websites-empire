CD Ladder Builder

Split savings across 1- to 5-year CDs so money frees up every year while you earn long-term rates.

⚠️ Educational purposes only — not financial advice.

How a CD ladder works

Divide your money into five equal CDs with 1- to 5-year terms. Each year one CD matures — spend it, or roll it into a new 5-year CD at the then-current rate. After year one, you always have a CD maturing within 12 months while earning 5-year yields. Strategy deep-dive: CD ladder strategy explained.

Ladder vs lump sum

Locking everything in a 5-year CD usually pays a touch more — until rates rise or you need cash early and eat a penalty. The ladder trades a sliver of yield for yearly liquidity.

Watch the penalties

Breaking a CD early typically costs 3–12 months of interest. Keep true emergency money in a high-yield savings account instead.

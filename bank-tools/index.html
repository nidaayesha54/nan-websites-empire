Frequently Asked Questions

APY (annual percentage yield) is the effective yearly return on savings including compounding. A 4.50% APY turns $10,000 into $10,450 in one year.
APY is what savings earn (compounding included); APR is what loans cost per year (compounding not included). Compare APY to APY and APR to APR — never cross-compare.

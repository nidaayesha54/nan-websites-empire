GTA 6 Shows Locked? Don't Delete It — Do This

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

You preloaded GTA 6, waited for launch night, and now the game shows a padlock and will not start. Before you panic — and before you do anything drastic — here is the good news: in most cases this is not a bug at all. GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time in your store region, and until that moment arrives the padlock is the game working exactly as designed. This guide explains how the unlock works, what to check, and the one mistake you must not make.

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, with a 212 GB install that most players preloaded days in advance. A padlock on a preloaded game on launch night is one of the most reported "problems" of the launch — and almost always, the fix is patience plus two quick checks.

Why the Padlock Appears

Preloading and unlocking are two different things. Preloading downloads the full 212 GB game to your console early, so you do not have to wait on launch night. Unlocking is separate: the game stays locked until midnight local time in the region of the store you bought it from. The padlock simply means the unlock time has not arrived yet. The store page for the game shows a countdown timer — that timer, not the download bar, is what you should be watching.

This catches people out because the game looks ready: it is installed, it has an icon, and it may even show as "ready to play" in some menus. It is not ready until the clock strikes midnight in your region. If you bought the game from a store in a different region, the unlock follows that region's midnight, not yours.

Step 1: Check the Time, Not the Download

Open the game's page in the PlayStation Store or Xbox Store and look for the countdown or release time. If it says the game unlocks at midnight, and it is not yet midnight where your store region is, there is nothing to fix — waiting is the fix. The countdown is the single most reliable piece of information on the whole page.

Also check the clock on your console itself. If the console's time zone is wrong, the unlock can appear to be hours away when it is not — or the game can appear locked after it should have unlocked. Make sure the time zone matches where you actually are.

Step 2: Do Not Delete the 212 GB Install

This is the mistake that turns a ten-minute wait into a ruined launch night. Deleting the game to "fix" the padlock means downloading 212 GB all over again — hours on most connections, and on launch night, when millions of players are downloading at once, even longer. The lock is time-based, not a corrupt install, so deleting changes nothing except your evening.

If you have already started a re-download, let it finish. Do not cancel it and start again. And once the game is installed, leave it alone until the unlock time passes.

Step 3: Sign In With the Account That Bought It

After the unlock time, the game opens for the account that purchased it. If you are signed in to a different account — a family member's, a second profile, or a shared console account — the game can stay locked even after midnight. Sign in with the account that actually bought GTA 6, and the padlock should clear.

On a shared console this is the most common real cause of a locked game after midnight. Check the purchase in that account's transaction history if you are unsure which account bought it.

Step 4: Update and Restore License (PS5)

If the game is still locked after midnight and you are on the right account, do two things. First, install PS5 system software 26.07-14.23.00 — GTA 6 needs this version to run at launch, and an out-of-date console can leave the game locked. Check under Settings, System, System Software, and install anything pending.

Second, restore your licenses. On PS5, highlight GTA 6 on the home screen, press Options, and choose Restore License. This re-syncs your purchase rights with the console and clears the lock in most cases where the account and the time are both right.

On Xbox

On Xbox, open the Store, go to Full Library, then Owned Games, and find GTA 6 there — if it shows as owned but not installed, choose Install. Also turn on automatic updates and leave the console in Sleep mode rather than shutting it down fully, so downloads and updates complete in the background. If a download queue looks stuck, a full power cycle — hold the power button for ten seconds — clears it.

Still Locked After Midnight?

Work through the list once more: right account, right time zone, console updated, licenses restored, console fully restarted. If the game is still locked, check the PlayStation or Xbox status page — launch-night store issues do happen — and check your purchase history to confirm the order actually completed. In almost every case, one of these steps clears it. The padlock is almost never a broken game.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is GTA 6 locked after I preloaded it?

Preloading only downloads the game. It stays locked until midnight local time in your store region — that is the unlock, and it is separate from the download. The store page countdown shows exactly when it opens.

Will restoring licenses delete anything?

No. Restoring licenses only re-syncs your purchase rights. It does not delete games, saves, or settings.

The Bottom Line

A padlocked GTA 6 on launch night is almost always the regional midnight unlock doing its job. Check the countdown, do not delete the 212 GB install, sign in with the buying account, update the console, and restore your licenses. In nearly every case the game opens on its own the moment the clock allows it.

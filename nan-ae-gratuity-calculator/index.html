How UAE Gratuity Is Calculated

End-of-service gratuity for private-sector expatriate employees is set by Article 51 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021. The rules are the same whether your contract ended by termination or resignation:

  • Less than 1 year of continuous service: no gratuity.
  • 1 to 5 years: 21 days of basic wage for each year of service (pro-rata for part-years).
  • More than 5 years: 21 days for the first five years plus 30 days of basic wage for every year after the fifth.
  • Cap: total gratuity can never exceed two years of basic salary.
  • Base: gratuity is calculated on your last basic wage — housing, transport and other allowances are excluded.
  • Unpaid leave days are excluded from the service count.
Example: basic salary AED 8,000, 7 years of service.
First 5 years: 8,000 ÷ 30 × 21 × 5 = AED 28,000
Next 2 years: 8,000 ÷ 30 × 30 × 2 = AED 16,000
Total gratuity = AED 44,000 (well under the AED 192,000 cap).

Who Is Eligible?

  • Expatriate employees in the private sector (mainland and most non-financial free zones).
  • At least 1 year of continuous service with the same employer.
  • Full-time and part-time workers (part-time is calculated pro-rata).
  • Special regimes apply separately: DIFC uses DEWS, ADGM has its own 2024 employment regulations, UAE nationals are covered by pension funds, and domestic workers fall under a separate law.

Common Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using gross salary — the law uses basic wage only; adding allowances inflates the estimate.
  • Ignoring the cap — the 2-year limit binds once service passes roughly 25 years.
  • Rounding years before the split — the 21/30-day boundary applies exactly at the 5-year mark, including fractions of years.
  • Missing the claim deadline — since a 2024 amendment, employment claims must be filed within 2 years of the employment ending.

FAQs

Is gratuity paid on basic salary or gross salary?

Basic salary only — the wage stated in your MOHRE employment contract, excluding housing, transport and other allowances. Daily wage is the monthly basic salary divided by 30.

Do I get gratuity if I resign?

Yes. Since the 2022 labour law, the gratuity rules are the same whether you resign or are terminated, provided you completed at least one year of continuous service.

What if I worked less than a year?

No statutory gratuity is payable for service under one year of continuous employment.

Is there a maximum gratuity amount?

Yes — the total can never exceed two years of your basic salary (basic salary × 24).

Are part-time workers entitled?

Yes, on a pro-rata basis according to contracted hours.

How do I check my basic salary?

Open your MOHRE app (sign in with UAE Pass → Dashboard → View contract) or the UAE Pass app under Documents → Professional → Labour contract to see the salary breakdown.

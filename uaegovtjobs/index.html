How to Apply

  1. Find federal roles on the UAE federal government jobs portal and ministry career pages.
  2. Apply via Dubai Government careers portal for Dubai entity roles, and TAMM for Abu Dhabi government jobs.
  3. Prepare an ATS-friendly CV in English (an Arabic version helps for federal ministries).
  4. Expat applicants: confirm the role is open to non-Emiratis — many federal posts are prioritized under Emiratisation.
  5. Shortlisted candidates are contacted within 2–6 weeks; employment visas are sponsored by the hiring entity.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are UAE government jobs advertised?

Federal roles appear on the federal government jobs portal and individual ministry career pages. Dubai entity roles use the Dubai Government careers portal; Abu Dhabi uses TAMM.

Can expats apply for UAE government jobs?

Yes — many roles in Dubai and Abu Dhabi government entities hire expats. Some federal posts are reserved for Emiratis under Emiratisation policy; each ad states eligibility.

What is Emiratisation?

The UAE's policy of increasing Emirati employment in government and private sectors. A portion of government roles are prioritized for UAE nationals.

What salaries do UAE government jobs pay?

Mid-level roles typically pay AED 12,000–24,000 per month tax-free, with housing and education allowances common in federal ministries.

Do government employers sponsor visas?

Yes — UAE government entities sponsor employment visas and Emirates ID for expat hires, including family sponsorship in most cases.

How long is the hiring process?

Shortlisting typically takes 2–6 weeks after the closing date, with background checks and medical clearance before the final offer.

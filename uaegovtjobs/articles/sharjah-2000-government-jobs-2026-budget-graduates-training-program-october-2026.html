UAE Government Jobs 2026

Sharjah's 2026 budget funds 2,000 government positions — including 1,000 jobs for Emirati graduates with a paid Practical Training Programme paying AED 6,000 a month.

✅ Updated October 17, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

Sharjah has unveiled one of the largest emirate-level employment programs of the year. Under the 2026 budget, His Highness Sheikh Dr. Sultan bin Mohammed Al Qasimi, Ruler of Sharjah, approved the hiring of 1,000 Emirati men and women into government positions — bringing the total to 2,000 job opportunities across the emirate's cities and regions. The program is explicitly designed for recent graduates: the leadership's message is that no graduate will be left behind.

The standout feature is the Practical Training Programme — a fast-track route where graduates in technical and professional fields are trained and then appointed directly to government entities, receiving a monthly allowance of AED 6,000 while they train. Coordinated by the Sharjah Department of Human Resources with bodies like the Sharjah Roads and Transport Authority, the Department of Public Works and local municipalities, this is a structured bridge from campus to government career.

How the Practical Training Programme Works

The program is the centerpiece of the 2,000-job plan:

  • Who it is for: graduates in specialized technical and professional fields;
  • What happens: participants complete practical training, then move directly into appointments at government entities — no separate job hunt after training;
  • Paid preparation: trainees receive AED 6,000 per month while in the qualifying program;
  • Where you go: placements are coordinated with major bodies including the Sharjah Roads and Transport Authority, the Department of Public Works and local municipalities.

That direct-appointment design removes the biggest risk of training schemes — finishing qualified but still unemployed.

Priority Fields & Where the Jobs Are

The program names four priority specializations: civil and architectural engineering, accounting, information technology and programming — mapping directly onto Sharjah's infrastructure and digital-government agenda. With 2,000 positions spanning all cities and regions, opportunities reach beyond Sharjah city into the Eastern and Central regions.

Eligibility

  • Nationality: UAE nationals (Emiratis) — the program is built around national talent;
  • Education: recent degree holders; technical/professional specializations prioritized for the training track;
  • Residence: positions span all cities and regions of the Emirate of Sharjah;
  • Readiness: willingness to complete the practical training phase before appointment.

How to Apply for Sharjah Government Jobs 2026

1
Register with the Sharjah Department of Human Resources — the body directed to organize interviews and placements.
2
Indicate your specialization clearly — engineering, accounting, IT and programming graduates are prioritized for the training track.
3
Prepare attested degree certificates, Emirates ID, passport and any project or internship evidence in your field.
4
Attend the HR department's interview process when invited — interviews were directed to begin promptly after the announcement.
5
If selected for the Practical Training Programme, complete training (with the AED 6,000 monthly allowance) and transition into your government appointment.

Why This Program Matters

Two thousand budgeted positions with a paid training bridge is the strongest graduate-employment signal any UAE emirate has sent this year. The AED 6,000 training allowance supports graduates while they qualify — and the direct-appointment model means the training leads somewhere concrete. For Emirati graduates in engineering, accounting, IT and programming, this is the most important Sharjah announcement of 2026.

UAE Government Jobs 2026

Dubai Police has opened recruitment for Emirati jobseekers — including Cadet Pilot and University Lieutenant Pilot roles for the Air Wing and a Data Scientist Assistant post — unveiled at Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

Dubai Police has begun accepting applications from Emirati citizens interested in joining its workforce, opening recruitment through its stand at Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026 at the Dubai World Trade Centre, the Emirates News Agency (WAM) reported on 28 September 2026.

The stand is welcoming jobseekers interested in career opportunities across policing, security, administrative and technical fields, giving Emirati talent direct access to information and guidance on joining the force. Among the headline vacancies unveiled at the exhibition: Cadet Pilot and University Lieutenant Pilot for the Dubai Police Air Wing — which plays a crucial role in community service, rescue operations and humanitarian missions — plus a Data Scientist Assistant role reflecting the force's push into AI and predictive policing.

Dubai Police Recruitment 2026 — Key Facts

DetailInformation
OrganisationDubai Police — UAE's oldest and most recognised police force (est. 1956)
Recruitment channelRu'ya Careers UAE 2026 stand, Dubai World Trade Centre

Note: Dubai Police recruitment at Ru'ya 2026 is targeted at Emirati nationals in line with Emiratisation priorities. Expatriate candidates should check the Dubai Police careers portal for any separately advertised civilian or specialist vacancies, which carry their own eligibility conditions.

Roles on Offer

  • Cadet Pilot — train with the Dubai Police Air Wing for community service, rescue operations and humanitarian missions;
  • University Lieutenant Pilot — a graduate-entry pilot pathway within the Air Wing;
  • Data Scientist Assistant — work with databases and open APIs on data analysis, predictive modelling and machine-learning models supporting smart policing;
  • Policing and security roles — patrol, traffic, community policing and public-safety functions across the force's sectors;
  • Administrative and technical roles — HR, IT, forensics support, communications and specialist civilian functions.

Eligibility Requirements

  • UAE national (Emirati) for the Ru'ya 2026 recruitment drive;
  • A minimum of 12 years of schooling, with fluency in Arabic and working knowledge of English;
  • IT or engineering certification/diploma is an advantage, particularly for technical roles;
  • Good physical fitness for uniformed policing streams (aptitude and fitness assessments form part of selection);
  • Clean record and the ability to meet the force's security and medical standards.

How to Apply

1
Visit the Dubai Police careers website and browse the current vacancies across policing, technical and administrative streams.
2
Select the role that matches your qualifications — Cadet Pilot, Data Scientist Assistant or general policing — and read the full job description.
3
Create an account on the portal, complete the application form and upload your CV plus supporting documents.
4
If shortlisted, prepare for the selection stages: aptitude tests, physical fitness assessments and interviews, depending on the role.
5
Ru'ya visitors can also engage the recruitment team at the stand for guidance on requirements and the application steps.

Why Dubai Police

Dubai Police offers what few employers can match: tax-free government salaries, free or subsidised housing and transport, comprehensive medical cover, annual bonuses and end-of-service benefits, in one of the world's most technologically advanced police forces. For young Emiratis, the Air Wing pilot pathways and the new data-science roles show a force deliberately building future-facing careers — from cockpits to machine-learning models — not just traditional policing.

Museum of the Future Jobs 2026 — Apply by Nov 8

Dubai's Museum of the Future has opened its "Future Perfects" recruitment drive — hiring on curiosity and imagination rather than degrees. Applications close 8 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

Dubai's Museum of the Future has opened a new recruitment drive called "Future Perfects", and it breaks the usual hiring mould: the museum says it is looking for people who "may never have imagined working in a museum" yet possess exactly what a museum of the future needs. Applications close on 8 November 2026 through the official auditions portal.

The initiative is a deliberate shift away from traditional recruitment. Azza AlNuaimi, the museum's Chief Operating Officer, said the approach reflects Dubai's belief in recognising potential before it becomes obvious — and that the qualities the museum values most, curiosity, imagination and human connection, "cannot be measured by a degree, a job title or years of experience".

The Museum of the Future is a Dubai government initiative under the Dubai Future Foundation, so these are government-linked roles in one of the world's most visited cultural landmarks. The drive is about people who help visitors understand and experience future possibilities — a role where technology expands what is possible but people remain the crucial link.

Why This Drive Is Different

  • Skills over job titles: the museum explicitly says the qualities it wants cannot be measured by degrees or years of experience — a rare stance in government-linked hiring;
  • People over technology: the initiative rests on the belief that while technology expands what is possible, people play the crucial role in helping visitors understand and experience those possibilities;
  • Hidden talent: the drive targets people who never imagined working in a museum — career-changers, creatives, educators, communicators — rather than only museum professionals;
  • Dubai's talent philosophy: AlNuaimi framed it as Dubai's approach: recognising possibilities early and creating the conditions for people, ideas and ambition to turn them into reality.

Who Should Apply

This drive suits candidates who are strong on human connection: people who can explain complex ideas simply, make visitors feel welcome, and bring imagination to visitor experiences. Useful backgrounds include education, theatre, storytelling, tourism, hospitality, science communication and community work — but the whole point is that no single background is required. If you have ever been told you are "good with people" and "curious about the future", this is the application where those qualities are the qualification.

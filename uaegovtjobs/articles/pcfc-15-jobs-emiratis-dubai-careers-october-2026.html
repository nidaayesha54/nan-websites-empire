UAE Government Jobs 2026

PCFC announced 15 career opportunities for Emirati talent at Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026. Here is the honest October 2026 breakdown: the full field list, who can apply, and how to submit your application on Dubai Careers.

✅ Updated October 21, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

The Ports, Customs and Free Zone Corporation (PCFC) announced 15 career opportunities for Emirati talent during Ru'ya, Careers UAE 2026, the three-day national careers fair held September 28–30, 2026 at Dubai World Trade Centre. The announcement reflects the corporation's leadership direction under Chairman H.E. Abdulla Bin Damithan, who has placed Emirati talent development at the centre of the corporation's hiring strategy.

The eligibility position is straightforward and worth stating plainly: these 15 roles are advertised for UAE nationals through Dubai Careers, the official recruitment platform for UAE nationals in Dubai Government entities. Expat applicants should not expect these particular postings to be open to them — that is the honest reality of most UAE government hiring. For eligible Emirati candidates, here is the full picture: the fields, the career levels, and the application steps.

The 15 Roles: Full Field List

PCFC's 15 openings cut across a wide spread of professional fields — it is not a single-department intake. The fields named in the announcement are:

  • Commercial inspection — trade and market oversight roles;
  • Registration — licensing and registration services;
  • Legal research — legal analysis and regulatory support;
  • Talent management — human resources and workforce development;
  • Customer relations — client-facing service and account roles;
  • Accounting — financial record-keeping and reporting;
  • Financial analysis — budgeting, forecasting and financial insight;
  • Information technology — systems, support and digital roles;
  • Procurement — purchasing and supply-chain functions;
  • Mechanical engineering — engineering roles in ports and infrastructure;
  • Civil engineering — construction and facilities engineering;
  • Media — communications and content roles.

Who Can Apply: Career Levels and the Nationality Rule

The intake spans three career levels:

  1. Recent graduates — entry-level tracks for fresh university talent;
  2. Secondary school certificate holders — roles that do not require a university degree;
  3. Experienced and specialised professionals — mid-career and specialist appointments.
Honest eligibility rule: these 15 opportunities were presented for Emirati talent and are advertised through Dubai Careers, the platform for UAE nationals in Dubai Government entities. Expat professionals should instead watch semi-government companies and private-sector employers for comparable openings.

How to Apply on Dubai Careers: Step by Step

All 15 roles are routed through the official channel:

1
Open dubaicareers.ae and create or sign in to your profile.
2
Search for PCFC or field keywords like "procurement" or "legal research".
3
Open each of the 15 postings and confirm the career level and nationality clause.
4
Prepare your Emirates ID, attested certificates, equivalency (if applicable) and an updated CV.
5
Submit through the portal and track your application in your Dubai Careers dashboard — government recruitment typically runs several stages, including assessments and interviews.

For verified UAE government information and services, start at the UAE official government portal (government.ae) rather than third-party reposts.

PCFC's Emiratisation Record: 77% of Leadership

The hiring push matches PCFC's stated record: 77% of the corporation's leadership positions are held by Emiratis — a signal that nationals are promoted into senior decision-making roles, not just hired at entry level. Under Chairman H.E. Abdulla Bin Damithan, Emirati talent development is treated as a corporate priority.

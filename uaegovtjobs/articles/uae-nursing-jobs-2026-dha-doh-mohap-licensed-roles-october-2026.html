UAE Government Jobs 2026

Healthcare employers across the Emirates are hiring nurses in October 2026 — but your licence must match your emirate. Here is the DHA/DOH/SHA/MOHAP licensing map, the roles open now, and how to apply.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

Healthcare hiring in the UAE is active in October 2026, with current vacancy listings showing Registered Nurse and Assistant Nurse openings across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and the other emirates — in hospitals, clinics and home-healthcare providers, on full-time and part-time contracts.

The single most important thing to understand: most clinical roles require a valid licence from the health authority of the emirate where you will work — DHA in Dubai, DOH in Abu Dhabi, SHA in Sharjah, or MOHAP in the northern emirates. No licence, no clinical job.

The Licensing Map, Emirate by Emirate

  • Dubai — DHA (Dubai Health Authority): licence required for all clinical roles in Dubai, including government hospitals under Dubai Health;
  • Abu Dhabi — DOH (Department of Health): covers Abu Dhabi's public and private facilities;
  • Sharjah — SHA (Sharjah Health Authority): the emirate's own regulator;
  • Northern emirates — MOHAP: the federal Ministry of Health and Prevention licensing system.

Each authority runs its own primary-source verification (DataFlow) and examination or assessment process. Start licensing before you job-hunt — it is the longest lead-time item in the whole process, and employers shortlist licensed candidates first.

Who Employers Want

  • Valid emirate licence (or eligibility letter / in-process status for assistant roles);
  • Relevant nursing qualification and clinical experience — ICU, ER, OT and home-care experience are in steady demand;
  • BLS/ACLS and specialty certifications strengthen applications;
  • Flexibility on shifts and location widens your options considerably.

How to Apply

1
Identify your target emirate and start (or complete) the matching licence: DHA, DOH, SHA or MOHAP.
2
Prepare a clinical CV listing wards, case types, equipment handled and certifications.
3
Apply to hospitals, clinic groups and licensed home-healthcare providers directly — and through reputable healthcare recruiters.
4
Verify every employer independently before sharing personal documents.
5
Never pay for a job offer — legitimate UAE healthcare employers do not charge candidates.
Important: Reputable job listings state clearly that they do not charge candidates for applications, interviews or visas. Any request for payment is a red flag — verify the employer and walk away.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which licence do I need to nurse in Dubai?

A DHA (Dubai Health Authority) licence. Abu Dhabi requires DOH, Sharjah requires SHA, and the northern emirates use the federal MOHAP system.

Can I apply for UAE nursing jobs while my licence is still in process?

For some home-care or assistant nurse roles, yes — listings note these may accept nurses still completing licensing. Most hospital clinical roles require the licence in hand.

What nursing roles are open in the UAE in October 2026?

Current listings show Registered Nurse and Assistant Nurse openings across Dubai, Abu Dhabi, Sharjah and other emirates, in hospitals, clinics and home healthcare.

Are part-time nursing jobs available?

Yes — listings include both full-time and part-time contracts.

How do I avoid nursing job scams in the UAE?

Verify every employer independently before sharing documents, and never pay for applications, interviews or visas — legitimate employers do not charge candidates.

What Happened at Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026

Ru'ya, Careers UAE connects Emirati jobseekers with employers; the 2026 edition drew more than 150 employers across 23+ sectors, with the minister's presence underlining the policy weight behind citizen hiring.

The Dubai Government appeared through one coordinated platform run by the Dubai Government Human Resources Department — 34 entities under a single umbrella spanning transport, municipality, health, policing, education, utilities and economic development.

750+ Vacancies for UAE Nationals — The Honest Picture

The 750+ vacancies presented at the Dubai Government platform were for UAE nationals — citizen hiring is the explicit mission of events like Ru'ya. Expats should instead target semi-government companies, government-owned enterprises and private-sector employers.

For Emirati jobseekers, the fair was genuinely substantial: roles spanned recent graduates, diploma holders and experienced professionals, and recruiters named the seven future-demand fields below.

Honesty note: the fair ended September 30, 2026 — the steps below show how to find the same entities' live vacancies on Dubai Careers today.

The Seven Future-Demand Areas Named at the Fair

Recruitment officials named these seven future-demand areas:

  1. Data analysts — turning government and city data into decisions;
  2. AI experts — building and deploying artificial-intelligence systems;
  3. AI partners — roles linking AI capabilities with business and government functions;
  4. Cloud computing engineers — running the infrastructure modern services sit on;
  5. Digital infrastructure specialists — networks, platforms and digital foundations;
  6. AI ethics specialists — governance, safety and responsible-AI oversight;
  7. Transport and future-technology engineering — mobility systems and emerging engineering fields.

The Emirati Platform: 38,000 Citizens and Location Matching

The Emirati Human Resources Development Council opened registration on its upgraded Emirati platform at the fair. The platform holds data on roughly 38,000 citizens — jobseekers and private-sector employees — and matches a jobseeker's home location to the job's location, removing a major friction point in commuting-heavy Dubai. Nationals can still sign up and keep profiles current for future matching rounds.

How Emiratis Can Still Apply on Dubai Careers

The fair is over, but the entities recruit year-round. Pick up the trail on Dubai Careers:

1
Create or refresh your profile on dubaicareers.ae — the single front door for Dubai government vacancies.
2
Search the 34 exhibiting government entities; many re-post fair roles as live vacancies.
3
Filter for the seven future-demand areas (data, AI, cloud, digital infrastructure, transport technology).
4
Register on the upgraded Emirati platform for location-matched job recommendations.
5
Keep your Emirates ID and attested certificates ready — government applications usually require them at the first stage.

UAE Job Seeker Visa 2026: AI Specialist Visa, 120-Day Entry & Where to Apply

The January 2026 visa reforms added an AI Specialist Visa and kept 60/90/120-day job seeker entry. Who qualifies, the 7 official government job portals and how to turn entry into an offer.

You do not need a job offer to start your UAE hunt. The job seeker visit visa lets skilled graduates enter independently and search in person — no host or sponsor required — and the January 1, 2026 reforms added a dedicated AI Specialist Visa plus expanded visa-on-arrival for Indian nationals. It is the official way to job-hunt on the ground.

Job Seeker Visit Visa: 60, 90 or 120 Days

Issued for 60, 90 or 120 days, it requires a financial guarantee, a bachelor’s degree, and MOHRE skill level 1–3 or a top-500 university degree from the last two years. You apply yourself — no sponsor involved. It does not permit work; it lets you interview, attend exhibitions and convert applications into employer-sponsored offers. The India-UAE CEPA (since 2022) has improved employment mobility for Indian nationals.

The AI Specialist Visa & Where Demand Sits

The new AI Specialist Visa targets artificial intelligence professionals — matching surging government demand for AI, cybersecurity, data and digital-infrastructure specialists, from Digital Dubai to the new federal bodies created in the 2026 budget. If you work in AI, machine learning, data or cybersecurity, this visa aligns with exactly the roles being hired.

From Visa to Offer: 5 Steps

  1. Secure the job seeker visa — degree, skill level, financial guarantee; pick 60, 90 or 120 days.
  2. Build your target list — Dubai Careers, the federal portal, SEHA, RTA and free-zone authorities.
  3. Attend career exhibitions — Ru’ya and the Industrialists Career Exhibition host hundreds of vacancies with on-the-spot interviews.
  4. Interview and negotiate — confirm housing, flights, insurance and end-of-service terms in the bilingual offer.
  5. Convert to an employment visa — your employer sponsors it; you pay nothing.
A job seeker visa is for searching, not working — starting a job before your employment visa issues is illegal. Carry degree attestation and financial-guarantee documents throughout your stay.

FAQs

What is the UAE job seeker visa?

A 60, 90 or 120-day visit visa letting skilled graduates enter independently to search for work — no sponsor. Needs a bachelor's degree, MOHRE skill level 1-3 (or recent top-500 degree) and a financial guarantee.

What is the AI Specialist Visa?

A visa from the January 1, 2026 reforms for AI professionals, matching demand for AI, cybersecurity and data specialists.

Which are the official job portals?

Dubai Careers, the Federal portal, MOHRE Careers, the Abu Dhabi portal, Ajman Kawader, RAK Jobs and Ajman Jobs.

Can I work on a job seeker visa?

No — searching only. You must convert to an employer-sponsored employment visa before starting work.

How does the India-UAE CEPA help?

Effective since 2022, it has improved employment visas and mobility for Indian nationals, alongside expanded visa-on-arrival in the 2026 reforms.

Abu Dhabi Police Jobs 2026: Security Elite Recruitment, Eligibility, Roles & How to Apply

Abu Dhabi Police is recruiting UAE nationals under its Security Elite initiative — from Patrol Officer to Sergeant. Here are the roles, the eligibility bar and exactly how to apply.

Abu Dhabi Police UAE Nationals 2026 Hiring

The Security Elite Recruitment Drive

Abu Dhabi Police has announced new recruitment opportunities for UAE nationals under its “Security Elite” initiative, opening multiple specialised roles across security and field operations. The drive targets young Emirati men and women for an elite security force — one of the most prestigious uniformed career paths in the country.

The recruitment includes positions such as Security Response Patrol Officer, Special Tasks Officer, Security Inspection Officer, First Constable, Corporal, First Corporal, Sergeant and First Sergeant, based at the Abu Dhabi Police General Headquarters.

Eligibility Criteria

Applicants must meet all of the following:

  • Nationality: UAE national
  • Age: between 18 and 35 years
  • Height: at least 160 cm
  • Education: high school diploma or higher
  • National Service: completion of National Service foundational and specialised courses
  • Pass interviews and required assessments

Benefits of Joining

  • Job stability with structured promotion opportunities
  • Employment within an elite security force
  • Specialised allowances on top of base pay
  • Training and development programmes throughout the career

How to Apply

  1. Prepare your documents. Emirates ID, passport, family book, educational certificates and your National Service completion certificate.
  2. Visit the Selection and Recruitment Department located opposite Mushrif Mall, Abu Dhabi — or check the official Abu Dhabi Police website and social media channels for the current application route.
  3. Complete the assessments. Expect interviews, physical/medical checks and background screening.
  4. Stay physically prepared. Security-force selection includes fitness standards — train before you apply.
Only apply through Abu Dhabi Police’s official channels. Genuine UAE police recruitment never asks candidates for application fees — treat any such request as a scam.

National Service: The Key Prerequisite

The National Service requirement is the gatekeeper for this recruitment. Applicants must have completed both the foundational and specialised National Service courses — the UAE’s mandatory military service program for Emirati men (women may volunteer). If you have finished school but not yet completed National Service, you cannot clear this eligibility bar, so plan your application timeline around your service completion.

Keep these documents ready: your National Service completion certificate alongside your Emirates ID, passport, family book and educational certificates. Recruiters check service records early in the process, and incomplete documentation is one of the most common reasons otherwise-qualified candidates are turned away.

What the Assessments Involve

Expect a layered assessment: interviews testing motivation and integrity, medical examinations, physical fitness evaluations and background screening. Security-force selection is deliberately demanding — candidates who train for fitness tests in advance and prepare clear, honest interview answers consistently perform better. Start preparing before the recruitment window opens, not after you apply.

Emirates Post Group — the UAE's government-owned postal operator (a federal entity since 2007, alongside affiliates including the Electronic Document Centre and Wall Street Exchange) — is listing a Senior Software Developer role on its careers portal, with applications invited from across the UAE (Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah). What the Senior Software Developer Role Involves

  • Application development — building and maintaining the Group's digital services across postal, logistics and financial products;
  • Systems integration — connecting new applications with legacy postal and logistics systems;
  • Agile delivery — working in structured development teams with release discipline;
  • Quality and security — meeting the standards expected of a federal entity handling citizen data and payments.

Reported Benefits and Requirements

Treat the following as reported, not confirmed:

  • Benefits cited: medical and sickness insurance, housing allowances, a retirement plan and job security;
  • Qualifications: relevant educational background, experience in a similar role or training certifications;
  • Languages: English required, Arabic an advantage;
  • Operational roles (couriers, customer service): may carry physical requirements;
  • Nationality: not stated — this is the single most important thing to verify, because UAE government entities commonly prioritize Emirati candidates.

How to Apply Safely

1
Go to the official Emirates Post careers portal (emiratespost.ae) and find the Senior Software Developer listing — confirm it is still live.
2
Read the nationality condition first. If it is Emirati-only, do not apply — no exception process exists through unofficial channels.
3
Check the salary band, qualifications and closing date on the official advert — none of these are confirmed in this article.
4
Apply only through the official portal (the Group's Oracle careers link) — never through paid agents or social-media "recruiters".

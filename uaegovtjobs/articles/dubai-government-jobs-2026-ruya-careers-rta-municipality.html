Dubai Government Jobs 2026: 750+ Roles for Emiratis at Ru'ya Careers — RTA & Municipality Hiring

The biggest Dubai government hiring push of the year: more than 750 roles for Emiratis announced at Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026, led by the RTA and Dubai Municipality.

Emirati jobseekers have a new reason to check the careers calendar this October. The Dubai government is offering more than 750 jobs to UAE nationals through Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026, where 34 government entities are hiring, according to Gulf News reporting from early October 2026. The RTA leads with 215 positions and Dubai Municipality with 73. Verification runs through UAE Pass and the Nafis programme (around 38,000 registered nationals), with total vacancies expected to reach roughly 800 within a month.

What Is Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026?

Ru'ya Careers UAE is a flagship careers exhibition for Emirati employment, connecting government employers with UAE national jobseekers. The 2026 edition is the year's standout Dubai government hiring event: 34 entities, more than 750 confirmed roles for Emiratis.

The format is simple: meet recruiters face to face, register on the spot, and note that many entities hire immediately rather than running long recruitment rounds. Missed the event? The announced roles continue to be filled through official government portals.

Who Can Apply?

The 750+ roles announced at Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026 are aimed at Emiratis — UAE nationals — reflecting the Emiratisation push driving this year's Dubai government hiring.

The RTA's 215 roles split between 100 openings for recent graduates and 115 for experienced professionals, while its Future Makers initiative targets 55 graduates for flagship transport projects: the Metro Blue Line, Gold Line, Dubai Loop and air taxis. Dubai Municipality is hiring experienced Emiratis, recent graduates and secondary-school graduates. Whether you are leaving school, fresh out of university, or mid-career, there is a track that fits.

Nafis Programme and UAE Pass

Two platforms sit at the centre of this hiring wave. The Nafis programme is the federal initiative supporting Emirati employment, now with around 38,000 UAE nationals registered. Government employers check candidate registration there when shortlisting, so being registered strengthens your applications.

UAE Pass is the UAE's digital identity — the login used across government services. Employers use it to verify your identity and documents, so activate yours before you start applying. An active UAE Pass account plus Nafis registration is the closest thing to a master key for Emirati government job applications this year.

How to Apply for Dubai Government Jobs 2026

How to apply:

  1. Activate your UAE Pass digital identity — the login for UAE government services.
  2. Register on the Nafis programme so government employers can verify your Emirati jobseeker status.
  3. Shortlist your targets: RTA careers (215 roles), Dubai Municipality recruitment (73 positions), and the Dubai Government careers portal for the wider 34-entity list.
  4. Prepare your documents — Emirates ID, family book (Khulasat Al Qaid), degree or school certificates and an up-to-date CV — then apply online and at careers events like Ru'ya.
  5. Double-check figures on the official RTA, Dubai Municipality and Dubai Government portals — these numbers come from Gulf News reporting in early October 2026 and change as roles are filled.

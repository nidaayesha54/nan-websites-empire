UAE Federal Government Jobs 2026: 7,842 Vacancies, 7 New Entities & Emiratisation Drive

A Federal National Council report puts 7,842 vacancies in the UAE’s 2026 federal budget — alongside seven brand-new entities and 5,200 Emiratis hired into industry a year early. Here’s where the jobs are.

Federal Jobs Emiratisation 2026 Vacancies

7,842 Federal Vacancies in the 2026 Budget

A parliamentary report approved by the Federal National Council (FNC) revealed that the number of job vacancies in the 2026 federal government budget had risen to 7,842. The report — prepared by the FNC’s Committee on Financial, Economic and Industrial Affairs — reflects the outcomes of the recent government restructuring, and it calls for federal initiatives to fill the targeted vacancies.

For Emirati jobseekers, this is the single biggest pool of federal opportunity in years — spanning ministries, councils and newly created entities.

Seven New Federal Entities (Dhs1.315 Billion)

The 2026 budget created seven new federal entities with a combined budget of Dhs1.315 billion:

  • Emirates Council for Research and Development (government affairs)
  • Executive Office for Control and Non-Proliferation (government affairs)
  • National Committee for Combating Money Laundering, Terrorist Financing and Financing of Weapons (government affairs)
  • Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research (social development)
  • Ministry of Family Affairs (social development)
  • National Olympic Committee (social development)
  • Ministry of Foreign Trade (infrastructure and economic resources)

Existing bodies were restructured too: the Ministry of Community Development became the Ministry of Community Empowerment, early childhood education moved under the Ministry of Education, and a Ministry of Sports was established — all in line with the ‘We Are the UAE 2031’ vision.

5,200 Industrial Jobs for Emiratis — A Year Early

Separately, officials announced that the target of 5,000 industrial-sector jobs for Emiratis by 2027 has already been beaten — more than 5,200 job opportunities were provided in 2026, a year ahead of schedule. The milestone was announced ahead of the Industrialists Career Exhibition in Abu Dhabi, where over 70 companies offered more than 1,000 additional vacancies across engineering, operations, health and safety, logistics, finance, administration and technology.

Demand is increasingly concentrated in emerging fields: artificial intelligence, data analysis, cloud computing, digital infrastructure, AI ethics and advanced manufacturing.

How to Apply for Federal Government Jobs

  1. Register on IRecruitment. The UAE federal government job portal (referenced by MOHRE) lets you find and apply for vacancies, track your application status, communicate with recruitment officers and follow up on interviews.
  2. Target the new entities. Fresh organisations like the Ministry of Foreign Trade and the Emirates Council for R&D are building teams from scratch — early applicants face less competition.
  3. Build an Emiratisation-relevant profile. Nafis-linked training, AI and data skills, and engineering qualifications are in the strongest demand.
  4. Attend career exhibitions. Events like the Industrialists Career Exhibition and Ru’ya put hundreds of vacancies and on-the-spot interviews in one hall.
  5. Keep your CV current. Federal portals rank active, complete profiles higher — update qualifications and experience before each application wave.
Federal government roles in the UAE prioritise UAE nationals. Expats should focus on semi-government entities, free-zone authorities and private-sector employers — or the Industrialists/Emiratisation exhibitions if eligible.

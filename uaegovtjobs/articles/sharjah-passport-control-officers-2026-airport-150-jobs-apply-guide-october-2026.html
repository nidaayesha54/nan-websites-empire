UAE Government Jobs 2026

The Sharjah Executive Council has approved hiring 150 passport control officers at Sharjah International Airport in three phases from October 2026 — a major Emirati-talent recruitment drive.

✅ Updated October 17, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

Sharjah is staffing up its airport. On 22 September 2026, the Sharjah Executive Council — chaired by His Highness Sheikh Sultan bin Mohammed bin Sultan Al Qasimi, Crown Prince and Deputy Ruler of Sharjah — approved a plan to recruit 150 passport control officers at Sharjah International Airport in three phases starting October 2026. The decision was reported by WAM and Gulf News, making it one of the best-verified government hiring announcements in the UAE this autumn.

The hiring is driven by growth: rising flight and passenger numbers plus ongoing airport expansion and development projects. Council members framed the plan explicitly as a route for Emirati talent into airport careers, with skills development and career progression built into the recruitment case. For UAE nationals seeking stable government employment — and for experienced expatriates in airport operations — this is a significant, structured intake.

What Passport Control Officers Do

Passport control officers are the front line of border management at the airport:

  • Verifying travel documents and identities of arriving and departing passengers;
  • Operating immigration counters and e-gate supervision during peak flows;
  • Detecting fraudulent documents and handling referrals per federal procedures;
  • Assisting passengers with entry requirements in a high-pressure, shift-based environment.

The three-phase structure suggests the intake will be staggered — early phases likely cover the busiest terminals and peak-season staffing.

Eligibility & Requirements

Official eligibility criteria will be published with each phase, but based on the Council announcement and standard UAE airport recruitment:

  • Nationality: the program is designed around Emirati talent — UAE nationals apply through the Sharjah Human Resources Department;
  • Non-nationals: can email a CV through the airport careers page, where relevant experience in aviation, immigration or customer service helps;
  • Skills development: the Council emphasized training and progression, suggesting structured onboarding rather than experienced-only hiring;
  • Availability: airport operations run 24/7 — expect shift work including nights, weekends and holidays.

Why This Recruitment Matters

Government-to-airport pipelines like this are among the most secure career entries in the UAE: government employment terms, structured training, and a clear progression path inside a growing airport. Sharjah International Airport's expansion means these 150 roles sit inside a longer growth story, not a one-off intake. For Emirati jobseekers especially — the explicit target group — this is the kind of announcement to act on in phase one rather than phase three.

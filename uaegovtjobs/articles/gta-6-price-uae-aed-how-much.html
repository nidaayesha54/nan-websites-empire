GTA 6 Price in the UAE: What Players Pay in Dirhams

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched in the United Arab Emirates on Thursday, November 19, 2026, unlocking at midnight Gulf Standard Time, and the first question for players in Dubai, Abu Dhabi and Sharjah was the price. The answer is refreshingly simple on the PlayStation Store: the Standard Edition costs AED 299.16 and the Ultimate Edition costs AED 373.96. Those are the listed UAE store prices, and they are the numbers this guide is built around — along with the retail prices, the age rule that surprises many buyers, and everything else a UAE player needs before buying.

The UAE is one of the few markets where the price story has a twist: the PlayStation Store charges in dirhams, while the Xbox Store's UAE listing charges in US dollars. That split, plus a strict age classification and different prices at local retailers, means the cheapest way to buy depends on your console and where you shop. Here is the full picture.

The prices: digital first

On the UAE PlayStation Store, the Standard Edition is listed at AED 299.16 and the Ultimate Edition at AED 373.96 — about $81.46 for the Standard Edition at the listed rate. The Ultimate Edition costs roughly AED 75 more and includes the game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade, which is digital only. Both editions are the same game; the extra money buys extra vehicles, weapons, outfits and shops that unlock chapter by chapter through the campaign.

On Xbox, the UAE Microsoft Store lists the game in US dollars instead: $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate. That means an Xbox player in the UAE pays the American price, while a PlayStation player pays the dirham price. Neither is a mistake — it is simply how the two stores price the UAE market, and it is worth knowing before you assume one console is cheaper than the other.

Retail prices: the boxed copy costs more

If you would rather buy a physical copy, expect to pay more than the digital price in most shops. Geekay lists the Standard Edition code-in-box at AED 299 for both PlayStation and Xbox, which matches the digital price almost exactly. Virgin Megastore lists it at AED 329, a premium of about AED 30 for the boxed copy. Jumbo Electronics and Amazon UAE also stock the game, with prices that vary by seller.

One warning applies to every boxed copy: there is no disc inside. The box holds a download code, and you still need to download the full game — up to 212GB — before you can play. Buying the box does not save you the download, and it does not get you playing any earlier than the digital version.

The 21+ age rule

The biggest surprise for UAE buyers is the age classification. The listing on the UAE PlayStation Store and Amazon UAE carries a 21+ age rating issued by the National Media Authority, which is stricter than the 18+ rating the game carries in most of the world. That means players under 21 cannot buy the game from UAE stores, and retailers are expected to enforce the limit.

If you are buying for someone else, or buying with an account registered in another country, check whose rules apply: the rating follows the store you buy from, not the console you play on. A UAE account buying from the UAE store is subject to the 21+ rule, whatever the rating printed on an imported box says.

Launch day in the UAE

The game unlocked at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, 2026, in the UAE, as part of a rolling midnight launch that started in New Zealand and moved across the globe as each country reached midnight. Digital pre-loading opened on November 10, nine days early, so players who pre-ordered could download the 212GB file in advance and start playing the moment the clock struck twelve.

You can buy the game digitally from the PlayStation Store or the Xbox Store, or in person at Virgin Megastore, Jumbo Electronics, Geekay and Amazon UAE. Pre-orders and purchases made before November 20 include the Vintage Vice City Pack plus a free month of GTA+ for digital pre-orders, so if you are buying at launch, that bonus is part of the deal.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play GTA 6 on Xbox in the UAE?

Yes. The game is on Xbox Series X|S as well as PlayStation 5, and the Xbox Store's UAE listing charges $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate in US dollars.

Is there a disc in the boxed copy?

No. Every boxed copy holds a download code instead of a disc, so you still need to download the full game and have enough storage free.

Why is the UAE price different from the US price?

Each store sets its own regional price. The UAE PlayStation Store charges AED 299.16, the US store charges $79.99, and the UAE Xbox Store charges the US dollar price. Taxes and store policies explain the gap, not a different version of the game.

The bottom line: in the UAE, GTA 6 costs AED 299.16 on the PlayStation Store, $79.99 on the Xbox Store, and AED 299 to AED 329 at retailers — with a 21+ age rule on UAE purchases. Buy digital if you want the lowest price, and check the age rule before you buy for anyone else.

How to Find UAE Government Jobs 2026 — Nafis, UAE Pass & Federal Portal Guide

A practical guide to every official channel: the federal portal, Nafis registration, UAE Pass login, PRO/GRO roles and eligibility for Emiratis and expats.

Finding UAE government jobs in 2026 is easier when you know where to look. Federal roles are advertised on the UAE Government federal portal; Dubai and Abu Dhabi entities run their own careers portals. For Emiratis, Nafis registration (around 38,000 nationals) and the UAE Pass digital identity unlock most applications. This guide covers every channel, plus eligibility for Emiratis and expats.

The UAE Government Hiring Landscape in 2026

Hiring runs on two tracks: federal ministries and authorities use the UAE Government portal, while each emirate's bodies — Dubai Municipality, the RTA, Dubai Police, Abu Dhabi entities on TAMM — hire through their own careers pages.

At Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026, the Dubai government offered 750+ jobs to Emiratis across 34 entities (Gulf News, early October 2026): the RTA is filling 215 roles, Dubai Municipality 73, with around 800 vacancies expected within a month.

Start With the UAE Government Federal Portal

For federal roles — ministries, authorities and agencies — the UAE Government portal is the official starting point, with listings stating entity, grade, salary band and eligibility.

Filter by ministry and keyword, and save the searches you check regularly — federal adverts move fast and closing dates are strict. Check whether a role is marked for Emiratis only; many federal posts are prioritised for UAE nationals.

The Nafis Programme Explained

What it is: Nafis is the federal programme supporting Emirati employment (around 38,000 UAE nationals registered), used by government employers to verify Emirati jobseeker status during shortlisting.

Who should register: any Emirati jobseeker — graduates, experienced professionals and secondary-school leavers eyeing roles like the 73 Dubai Municipality positions. Registration is free and puts your profile before employers running national hiring drives such as Ru'ya Careers.

UAE Pass: Your Login Key

UAE Pass is the national digital identity used to log in to UAE government services, including jobs portals. Employers use it to verify your identity and documents, so activate it — with your Emirates ID and a quick face verification in the app — before applying. One login covers you across federal and emirate-level portals.

PRO/GRO Roles: The Common Government-Relations Listing

Browse government vacancies long enough and you will keep seeing PRO (Public Relations Officer) and GRO (Government Relations Officer) titles — government-relations roles handling visas, licences, permits and paperwork between organisations and departments, among the most common UAE job-board listings.

Eligibility: Emiratis vs Expats

Emiratis: the 750+ Ru'ya Careers roles are reserved for UAE nationals; Emiratisation policy prioritises Emiratis across federal posts. Nafis registration plus UAE Pass covers most of the process.

Expats: many Dubai and Abu Dhabi entities hire expatriates — especially in healthcare, education, engineering and technology. Read the eligibility line on the advert: it states whether the role is open to all nationalities or restricted to Emiratis.

How to Apply: Step-by-Step

Follow this order and you will not miss a registration:

  1. Set up UAE Pass — the login for nearly every government jobs portal.
  2. Register on the Nafis programme (Emiratis) to make your profile visible to government employers.
  3. Pick your track: federal portal, Dubai Government careers portal, or TAMM for Abu Dhabi.
  4. Prepare an ATS-friendly CV plus Emirates ID, passport, certificates and (for Emiratis) family book scans.
  5. Apply before the closing date — federal adverts close strictly — and track your applications.
  6. Watch entity career pages directly for PRO/GRO roles; not every vacancy reaches the main portals.

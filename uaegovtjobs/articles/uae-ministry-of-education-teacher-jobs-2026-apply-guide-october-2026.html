UAE Government Jobs 2026

The UAE Ministry of Education is recruiting across the education system in 2026 — classroom teachers, curriculum section heads, academic advisors and educational researchers. Here are the vacancy categories, who can apply, and how to submit your application.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

The UAE Ministry of Education (MoE) remains one of the largest government employers in the Emirates, recruiting professionals across the full spectrum of the education system — classroom teaching, curriculum development, educational research, specialist services and administration. A current vacancy round lists positions at multiple UAE locations, covering both English-medium and Arabic-medium roles.

Because MoE vacancies range from frontline teaching to senior policy and research posts, this recruitment round suits everyone from qualified teachers to curriculum specialists and education researchers.

Eligibility & Nationality Rules

  • Qualifications: Teaching roles typically require a relevant bachelor's degree plus a teaching qualification; leadership and research roles require advanced degrees and experience in education policy or curriculum;
  • Experience: Senior posts (Section Heads, Advisors, Experts) generally require multi-year experience in education or research;
  • Language: Arabic-medium posts require strong Arabic; English-medium posts require strong English — bilingual candidates have the widest options;
  • Nationality: Some government vacancies are limited to UAE nationals. Always check the nationality requirement on each individual posting — it varies by role.

Applicants should check the required qualifications, experience, job location and any nationality restrictions for each position before investing time in an application.

Beyond the Ministry — UAE School Vacancies 2026

The wider UAE education sector is also hiring. Reported 2026 vacancies include:

  • Aldar Education — teaching, leadership, administration and accounting roles across its UAE school network;
  • GEMS Education — primary, early-years and secondary teaching in Dubai, Fujairah and other locations;
  • ADNOC Schools — elementary classroom teaching in Abu Dhabi and the Western Region;
  • Cranleigh Abu Dhabi — teaching, art and school administration.

These private-sector schools complement the MoE's government recruitment and are generally open to expatriate teachers — a useful alternative if a specific MoE post is restricted to nationals.

How to Apply for MoE Jobs 2026

1
Open the UAE Ministry of Education careers page and browse current vacancies by category and location.
2
Filter for roles matching your qualification, subject specialism and language (English vs Arabic medium).
3
Check the nationality requirement on each posting before applying.
4
Prepare an attested CV with degree certificates, teaching licence (if required) and experience letters.
5
Submit through the official portal and track your application — shortlisted candidates are typically contacted for interviews and document verification.
Important: Teaching credentials usually need attestation (home country + UAE embassy + UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs). Start the attestation process early — it is the most common cause of delayed onboarding for expatriate teachers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What jobs are available at the UAE Ministry of Education in 2026?

Reported vacancies span teaching (English and Arabic medium), curriculum section heads, academic advisors, educational researchers, Braille and sign-language specialists, and media/marketing roles.

Can expatriates apply for UAE Ministry of Education jobs?

Some MoE vacancies are limited to UAE nationals while others are open more broadly — each posting states its nationality requirement, so check before applying.

What qualifications do I need to teach in the UAE?

Typically a relevant bachelor's degree plus a teaching qualification, with attested certificates; senior and research roles require advanced degrees and experience.

How do I apply for MoE jobs?

Through the official UAE Ministry of Education careers page — browse vacancies, verify eligibility and nationality rules, and submit an attested CV.

Are there alternatives to MoE government teaching jobs?

Yes — Aldar Education, GEMS Education, ADNOC Schools and Cranleigh Abu Dhabi are all reported to have 2026 vacancies, generally open to expatriate teachers.

Golden Visa Salary Thresholds 2026

The skilled professional Golden Visa needs a salary of at least AED 30,000/month, a degree, and MOHRE skill level 1 or 2. It grants 10 years of residency not tied to one employer — you can change jobs without losing it. The Dubai GDRFA employment route remains active; the federal ICP route has been paused since October 2025. Other routes continue: the AED 2M property route (mortgaged properties qualify since February 2026; bank guarantees accepted since April 2026), outstanding students (GPA 3.75+), and entrepreneurs with startups valued above AED 500,000.

Where to Apply: Dubai Careers & Key Entities

Most in-demand: consultant physicians, nurses, civil/marine engineers, AI and cybersecurity experts, urban planners and financial auditors.

Employment Visa Steps

  1. Get the offer — via Dubai Careers, Bayt, GulfTalent, LinkedIn UAE or Naukrigulf (issued in Arabic and English).
  2. Employer secures the work permit — MOHRE approval; the company pays AED 250–3,450 in fees.
  3. Entry permit issued — GDRFA Dubai, usually within 48 hours, valid 60 days.
  4. Arrive and register the contract with MOHRE within 14 days.
  5. Medical fitness test at an approved centre (1–2 days).
  6. Emirates ID + residence visa — biometrics in the first two weeks; visa within 30 days.
Visa rules were reformed on January 1, 2026 — verify figures on dubaicareers.ae, MOHRE and u.ae before acting. Never pay a recruitment fee: UAE law makes the employer pay.

FAQs

What salary is needed for a UAE Golden Visa in 2026?

At least AED 30,000/month with a degree and MOHRE skill level 1 or 2 — granting 10 years of residency not tied to one employer.

Is the Golden Visa employment route open in 2026?

The Dubai GDRFA route remains active for Dubai-employed professionals; the federal ICP route has been paused since October 2025.

Can expats get UAE government jobs?

Yes — Dubai Careers lists 45 government departments with roles up to Dh40,000 open to expats, all 100% tax-free with allowances.

Are UAE government salaries tax-free?

Yes — 0% income tax. Packages typically run AED 15,000-60,000+ per month plus housing, flights and insurance.

How long does the visa process take?

About 2-4 weeks from offer: work permit, entry permit (within ~48 hours in Dubai), contract, medical, Emirates ID and residence visa.

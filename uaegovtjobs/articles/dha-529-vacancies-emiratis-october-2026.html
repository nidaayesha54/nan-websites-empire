DHA Jobs 2026: 529 Vacancies for Emiratis

Dubai Health Authority is offering 529 vacancies for Emirati talent across medical, technical and administrative roles — plus the 12-month Nokhba training programme, residencies and fellowships.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

The Dubai Health Authority (DHA) — Dubai's government health regulator and provider — is offering 529 job vacancies across medical, technical and administrative specialisations, YaHind reported on September 28, 2026, from the Authority's participation in Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026.

The vacancies are listed on the Authority's platform and were promoted in cooperation with six private healthcare facilities — a public-private collaboration aimed at advancing Emiratisation and widening career pathways for national talent in the health sector.

Honesty first: the 529-vacancy figure comes from a single report (YaHind, Sept 28, 2026) — treat it as reported, not officially confirmed, and verify the live count on DHA's own platform. The Nokhba programme and training-pathway details are separately corroborated by Maagulf's coverage. No salary bands or a closing date were published. This Ru'ya push is aimed at Emirati nationals; DHA's clinical roles for licensed expats are handled separately on the same portals — read each listing's nationality line.

What DHA Is Offering

  • 529 vacancies across medical, technical and administrative specialisations, promoted with six private healthcare facilities;
  • Nokhba programme: a 12-month supervised on-the-job training programme for Emirati talent, under specialist experts — high performers are assessed and then appointed to vacant positions according to the Authority's actual needs;
  • Nokhba career fields: information technology and cybersecurity, health insurance, health auditing and regulation, medical licensing, research and data analysis, plus finance, contracts and procurement;
  • Medical education pathways: internship programmes, residency and fellowship programmes, and Tibb wa Uloom (Medicine and Sciences) for high-achieving Emirati secondary-school graduates — 553 enrolled since 2016, 242 graduated;
  • Track record: Dubai's Unified Internship Programme has benefited 512 doctors, and 182 doctors have taken part in residency and fellowship programmes with private-sector collaboration over the past three years.

Eligibility and Nationality — Plain Facts

  • This drive: the Ru'ya 2026 participation was framed around attracting and developing Emirati talent — on-site interviews at the exhibition were for national talent;
  • DHA generally: the Authority also employs licensed professionals of other nationalities in clinical roles — nationality terms are stated per listing, so read each advert;
  • Licensing: clinical roles require a DHA license or an eligibility letter — have it ready before applying;
  • Nokhba: since its 2023 launch it has attracted Emirati talent in information systems, computer science, public health, nutrition, IT and specialist disciplines.

How to Apply

1
Go to DHA's online platform at mawaheb.dha.gov.ae — the Authority's reported application portal for its programmes.
2
For the Nokhba programme, register on Dubai Careers under the title "Trainee".
3
Open each listing individually — verify the vacancy is still open, read the nationality line, salary band and closing date.
4
Clinical applicants: prepare your DHA license or eligibility letter, updated CV and certificates.
5
Apply only through official portals — DHA recruitment is free; never pay anyone for a job.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many vacancies is DHA offering?

529 vacancies across medical, technical and administrative specialisations — reported by YaHind on September 28, 2026 from the Ru'ya Careers fair. This figure is single-sourced; verify live on DHA's platform.

Who can apply for DHA's 529 vacancies?

This Ru'ya push targets Emirati nationals as part of the Emiratisation strategy. DHA also employs licensed professionals of other nationalities in clinical roles — check each listing's nationality line.

What is the Nokhba programme?

A 12-month on-the-job training programme for Emirati talent under specialist experts. High performers are assessed and then appointed to vacant positions based on the Authority's needs.

What other training pathways does DHA offer?

The Unified Internship Programme for medicine and dentistry, residency and fellowship programmes, and Tibb wa Uloom for high-achieving Emirati school graduates pursuing health studies.

Where do I apply for DHA jobs and programmes?

Applications go through DHA's online platform (mawaheb.dha.gov.ae, as reported). Nokhba trainees can also register via Dubai Careers under the "Trainee" title.

Is DHA recruitment free?

Yes — government recruitment never charges application fees. Walk away from anyone asking for payment.

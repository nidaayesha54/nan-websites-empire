UAE Government Jobs 2026

The Government of Dubai has launched Dubai Careers — a single official portal listing public-sector vacancies across the emirate. Here is how the platform works, who can apply, and how to get your application right.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

The Government of Dubai has launched Dubai Careers, a centralized online platform that lists public-sector job vacancies across the emirate. Instead of navigating dozens of individual government agency websites, jobseekers can now search and apply for government roles in one place — the platform connects applicants directly with Dubai government entities across sectors from finance and transport to culture and public prosecution.

The launch matters because Dubai's government hiring is genuinely active: at Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026 (28–30 September 2026), 34 Dubai government entities offered more than 750 vacancies, with recruitment officials flagging seven future-demand areas — data analysts, AI experts, AI partners, cloud computing engineers, digital infrastructure specialists, AI ethics specialists and transport-related engineering.

What the Portal Offers Jobseekers

  • One search across government: vacancies from participating Dubai government entities in a single listing, instead of scattered agency sites;
  • Direct applications: apply to the relevant entity through the portal's online recruitment system;
  • AI CV analysis: upload your CV and have it analyzed by AI to identify areas or skills that could be improved;
  • Smart job matching: the platform matches available jobs with candidates, including calculating the distance between your home and the workplace;
  • Profile for nationals: Emirati jobseekers can build profiles used across government recruitment drives.

Who Can Apply — Nationality Rules

This is the question every expatriate asks, and the honest answer is: it depends on the vacancy. Government of Dubai entities recruit both Emiratis and expatriates, but eligibility depends on each position's requirements, including nationality restrictions, qualifications and professional experience. The 750 vacancies showcased at Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026 were for UAE nationals — but the portal itself lists vacancies across entities, and each posting states who may apply. Foreign jobseekers should:

  • Read the nationality field on every posting before applying;
  • Focus on specialist roles (AI, data, engineering, finance) where expatriate hiring is most common;
  • Keep expectations realistic — Emiratisation policy means nationals are prioritized for many roles.

How to Apply on Dubai Careers

1
Open the Dubai Careers portal (the official Government of Dubai platform) and browse or search vacancies by entity, sector or keyword.
2
Open each posting and check the nationality requirement, qualifications and experience — do not skip this step.
3
Create or update your profile and CV on the platform; use the AI CV analysis to strengthen it.
4
Submit your application through the portal for each role you genuinely qualify for.
5
Track your applications and respond promptly if an entity contacts you for interviews or assessments.

Participating Entities

The platform brings together a wide range of Dubai government bodies, including the Emirati Human Resources Development Council, Digital Dubai, Department of Finance, Dubai Civil Defence, Dubai Courts, Dubai Customs, Dubai Land Department, Dubai Future Foundation, Dubai Media Incorporated and the Ports, Customs and Free Zone Corporation (which alone offered 15 vacancies at Ru'ya across inspection, legal research, IT, engineering and media). This breadth means opportunities span nearly every professional discipline.

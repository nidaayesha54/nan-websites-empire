DLD Real Estate Jobs Day — Oct 29, 2026

Dubai Land Department hosts the fourth Real Estate Day for Emirati Employment on Thursday 29 October 2026 at Address Sky View — real estate firms hiring UAE nationals, with Nafis and MOHRE support.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

Dubai Land Department (DLD) will host the fourth edition of its Real Estate Day for Emirati Employment on Thursday 29 October 2026 at the Address Sky View Hotel in Dubai, according to the department's announcement dated 21 October 2026. Companies operating across the real estate sector will take part, and the event is designed for UAE nationals — jobseekers, students and graduates.

The event is a direct hiring platform rather than a careers fair in name only: it gives Emirati talent face-to-face access to recruiters, a chance to understand role requirements and professional pathways, and a route into long-term careers in one of Dubai's most active sectors. It is run in partnership with the Ministry of Human Resources and Emiratisation (MOHRE), the Emirati Talent Competitiveness Council (Nafis) and the Emirati Human Resources Development Council.

The track record behind the event is concrete: DLD says its Real Estate Empowerment Programme helped create more than 2,100 employment opportunities for UAE nationals between 2023 and 2026, through collaboration between government entities and private-sector companies. The fourth edition extends that programme into specialised real estate roles, supporting the Dubai Economic Agenda D33 and the Dubai Real Estate Sector Strategy 2033.

What Happens at the Event

  • Direct engagement: meet recruiters from real estate companies operating across Dubai — not just a CV drop;
  • Role clarity: learn the requirements, qualifications and career pathways for specialised real estate roles;
  • Pathway guidance: understand how the Real Estate Empowerment Programme supports long-term career development, not just first jobs;
  • Government support: Nafis and MOHRE presence means questions on Emiratisation support, training and benefits can be answered on the spot.

Why Real Estate, Why Now

Dubai's real estate sector is one of the emirate's largest private employers, and the sector's growth has outpaced the supply of trained Emirati professionals — which is exactly why DLD runs this event. The Dubai Real Estate Sector Strategy 2033 places human capital and sector competitiveness at its centre, and the D33 agenda ties national talent development to economic growth. For Emirati graduates, real estate offers something rare: a sector where local knowledge is a genuine advantage — language, networks and market understanding all matter — and where government-backed programmes actively place nationals.

How to Make the Most of 29 October

1
Prepare a one-page CV in English and Arabic if possible, plus copies of your qualifications and Emirates ID.
2
Research the participating companies beforehand — know which specialisations (brokerage, valuation, property management, development) fit you.
3
Arrive early at Address Sky View — popular employer stands fill up fast at DLD events.
4
Ask about Nafis support — salary top-ups and training programmes that may apply to your role.
5
Follow up the same week: connect with the recruiters you met and check Dubai Careers for DLD-linked vacancies.

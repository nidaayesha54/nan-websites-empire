Dubai Municipality Jobs 2026: 73 Roles for Emiratis

Dubai Municipality is offering 73 government positions for Emirati talent in 2026 — mostly in engineering — with immediate hiring for candidates who meet the requirements.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

Dubai Municipality (Baladiyat Dubai) — one of the oldest and largest government entities in Dubai, responsible for city planning, construction permits, public health, waste management and municipal services — is hiring 73 staff in 2026, Gulf News reported on September 29, 2026, after the municipality's participation in the Ru'ya Careers UAE 2026 hiring drive.

The announcement came through the Dubai Government Human Resources Department's unified platform, which brought together 34 government entities offering more than 750 vacancies for UAE nationals at Ru'ya. The municipality was one of the biggest single contributors on the platform.

Honesty first: this article's vacancy count (73), role categories and hiring timeline come from the Gulf News report, corroborated by UAE Moments' coverage of the same drive. Neither report published salary bands or a closing date. What is confirmed: this hiring round is for UAE nationals (Emiratis) only — expat applicants should not spend time on these 73 posts.

What the 73 Roles Cover

  • Engineering: most of the 73 vacancies are in engineering disciplines — the municipality's core function;
  • Inspection: inspection roles across municipal services and enforcement;
  • Data and AI: the number of data analyst roles has increased recently, and the municipality is recruiting AI experts to prepare for future requirements and rapid technological change;
  • Other specialisations: a spread of administrative and technical posts across the municipality's departments.

Eligibility: Plain Facts (Emiratis Only)

  • Nationality: UAE nationals only for this drive. The reporting states the municipality is accepting applications from experienced Emiratis, recent graduates and secondary-school graduates;
  • Education tiers: experienced professionals (degrees + experience), fresh university graduates, and secondary-school certificate holders — matched to the role;
  • Emiratisation context: Emiratis currently account for about 57.5% of Dubai Municipality employees;
  • What to prepare: updated CV, attested certificates, Emirates ID, and UAE Pass access — the Emirati talent platform is accessed via UAE Pass.

Why "Immediate Hiring" Matters

Gulf News quoted the municipality's HR director saying immediate hiring is available for candidates who meet the job requirements. That is unusual language for a government drive and suggests two things: apply early rather than waiting, and make sure your CV already matches the role before you submit.

The wider platform is also growing: the Emirati Human Resources Development Council said the number of vacancies available through the Dubai Government platform was expected to reach about 800 within a month of the exhibition, as more companies and entities join — so new Municipality roles may keep appearing on Dubai Careers.

How to Apply

1
Open the official Dubai Careers portal and find the Dubai Municipality employer page.
2
Open each advert individually — confirm the role is still open, read the nationality condition, salary band and actual closing date.
3
Create your Dubai Careers profile with an updated CV, attested certificates and your Emirates ID.
4
Register on the Emirati talent platform (accessed via UAE Pass) — it matches your CV against suitable vacancies using AI.
5
Apply only through official portals — the municipality never charges application fees.

UAE Government Jobs 2026

Dubai's Roads and Transport Authority is listing 2026 roles — from Senior Administrator to Chief Officer — on the official Dubai Careers portal, with UAE nationals receiving priority.

✅ Updated October 22, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

The Roads and Transport Authority (RTA) — the Government of Dubai entity behind the Dubai Metro, Tram, Water Canal, vehicle licensing, taxis and the emirate's road network — is listing 2026 vacancies on the official Dubai Careers portal, from administrative posts to chief-officer roles.

RTA is one of Dubai's most sought-after government employers: stable, well-structured and central to the emirate's transport mega-projects. That visibility makes its listings competitive — apply only through official channels.

Honesty first: UAE government jobs are heavily Emirati-priority — many RTA roles accept all nationalities, but nationals receive preference and some roles are Emirati-only. The role list below was reported by a third-party aggregator on October 21, 2026, with no salary bands, vacancy counts or closing dates — verify each advert on the official portal before applying.

What the 2026 RTA Roles Cover

  • Senior Administrator, Support Services and Logistics — running the back-office operations that keep the authority moving;
  • Chief Officer, Dubai Traffic & Roads CEO — senior leadership within the traffic and roads agency.

Eligibility and the Emirati-Priority Rule

Before you apply:

  • Nationality condition: many RTA roles accept all nationalities, but UAE nationals receive preference and some roles are Emirati-only — stated per advert;
  • Education: professional roles usually require a relevant bachelor's; senior roles may ask for a master's; engineering roles need an accredited engineering degree;
  • Documents: keep an updated CV, attested certificates and your Emirates ID ready;
  • Recruitment is free: RTA never charges application fees — walk away from anyone asking for payment.

RTA's 2026 Hiring Context

Gulf News reported (September 30, 2026) that RTA offered 215 jobs at Ru'ya Careers UAE — 100 for recent graduates and 115 experienced candidates — plus 55 graduate roles through the Future Makers initiative tied to the Metro Blue Line, Dubai Loop and air-taxi projects.

How to Apply

1
Open the official Dubai Careers portal and find the RTA employer page.
2
Open each advert individually — read the nationality condition, salary band, qualifications and the actual closing date before investing time.
3
Create your Dubai Careers profile: updated CV, attested certificates, Emirates ID.
4
Tailor your application to the role: RTA screens for role-relevant experience, not generic government-service background.
5
Apply only to roles still open — never through unofficial agents or paid intermediaries.

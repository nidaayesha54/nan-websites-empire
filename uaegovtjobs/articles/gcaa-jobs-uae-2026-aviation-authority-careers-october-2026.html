UAE Government Jobs 2026

The UAE's General Civil Aviation Authority — the federal body regulating the country's skies — is listing 2026 vacancies across Dubai and Abu Dhabi. Here are the roles, who can apply, and how to submit your application.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

The General Civil Aviation Authority (GCAA) is the UAE's federal aviation regulator — the body behind air-traffic safety, airline licensing, airport certification and the oversight of one of the world's busiest aviation sectors. Fresh 2026 GCAA careers listings show the authority hiring across its Dubai and Abu Dhabi operations, spanning safety, technical, administrative and specialist functions.

With UAE aviation still expanding — new airport capacity, growing fleets and the country's position as a global transit hub — the GCAA's regulatory workload keeps growing, and with it the authority's hiring needs.

Always verify the nationality requirement on the individual posting before investing time in an application.

Typical GCAA Role Families

  • Aviation safety inspectors — flight operations, airworthiness and aerodrome safety oversight;
  • Air navigation & technical specialists — supporting the UAE's air-traffic management infrastructure;
  • Licensing officers — pilot, engineer and operator licensing administration;
  • Engineering — systems, infrastructure and technical project roles;
  • Corporate functions — finance, HR, legal, communications and administration.

Eligibility

  • Relevant degree and professional experience — aviation roles typically demand sector-specific qualifications (ICAO-aligned training and licences where relevant);
  • Nationality: varies by posting — some roles are reserved for UAE nationals;
  • English proficiency — the working language of international aviation;
  • A clean professional record for safety-critical positions.

Why the GCAA

Federal-government employment in the UAE brings tax-free salaries, structured allowances and the stability of a regulator whose mandate only grows as the country's aviation sector expands. For aviation professionals, few employers in the region match the GCAA's combination of regulatory authority, technical depth and career stability.

How to Apply

1
Open the official GCAA careers page and browse current vacancies by function and location.
2
Filter by location (Dubai / Abu Dhabi) and check the nationality requirement on each role before applying.
3
Prepare an aviation-focused CV with licences, ratings and type experience listed clearly and verifiably.
4
Submit through the official portal only — never through third-party agents asking for fees.
5
Track your application; shortlisted candidates face interviews and credential verification.
Important: The GCAA never charges application fees. Any “agent” requesting payment for a GCAA job is running a scam — apply only through the authority's official channels and verify every offer independently.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the GCAA?

The General Civil Aviation Authority — the UAE's federal aviation regulator, responsible for air-traffic safety, airline licensing, airport certification and aviation oversight.

Where are GCAA jobs located?

Current 2026 listings cover the authority's Dubai and Abu Dhabi operations.

Can expatriates apply for GCAA jobs?

It depends on the role. Many posts prioritize UAE nationals under Emiratisation, while specialist technical roles are more commonly open to experienced expatriates — check each posting's nationality requirement.

What kinds of roles does the GCAA hire for?

Aviation safety inspectors, air navigation and technical specialists, licensing officers, engineers, and corporate functions such as finance, HR, legal and administration.

How do I apply for GCAA careers?

Through the official GCAA careers portal only. Never pay any agent or third party for a GCAA application — the authority charges no fees.

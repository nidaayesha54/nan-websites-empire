UAE Government Jobs 2026

Abu Dhabi Health Services Company (SEHA) — the capital's largest government healthcare network — is hiring across hospitals in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and Al Dhafra: consultants, nurses, allied health and admin staff.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » UAE Government Jobs » Articles

Abu Dhabi Health Services Company (SEHA), the largest government healthcare network in Abu Dhabi and part of the PureHealth Group, is hiring across its hospitals and clinics in Abu Dhabi, Al Ain and Al Dhafra. Live listings on the company's official Oracle careers portal span consultant and specialist physicians, nursing and midwifery, allied health and pharmacy, and administrative and support roles.

SEHA operates flagship facilities including Sheikh Khalifa Medical City, Tawam Hospital and a network of clinics across the emirate — making it the single biggest government health employer in the UAE capital. For doctors, nurses and allied-health professionals seeking government hospital jobs in the UAE, the SEHA portal is the primary gateway.

Current verified listing categories include Consultant Internal Medicine (Obstetric Medicine), Neonatology Consultant, Consultant Endocrinology (Adult & Pediatric), Chair of Department ENT, Orthopedic Surgery and OB-Gynecology on the physician side; Staff Nurse (Emergency, ICU, Operating Room, PACU), Clinical Resource Nurse and Staff Midwife in nursing; and Inpatient Pharmacist, Sonographer and Genetic Counsellor among allied-health roles — plus facilities, health-information, quality, finance and Emiratisation support positions.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Education: MBBS/MD/DM for physicians; BSc Nursing or MSc Nursing for nurses; Pharm D/BPharm for pharmacists; recognised diplomas for allied-health roles. UAE nationals must hold qualifications from approved schools;
  • Licence: a valid Department of Health (DOH) Abu Dhabi licence or eligibility letter, meeting DOH PQR requirements — consultant roles additionally expect CCT, Arab Board or equivalent;
  • Experience: typically minimum 2 years for Staff Nurse, 3–5 years for Specialist, and 8+ years with leadership experience for Consultant/Chair roles;
  • Language: English mandatory; Arabic is an advantage for patient-facing roles;
  • Documents: CV, passport copy, colour photo, graduation proof and transcripts, licence, experience certificates.

How to Apply for SEHA Jobs

1
Go to the official portal at seha.ae/careers and open the Oracle job-search site — this is the only legitimate application channel.
2
Filter vacancies by category (Medical, Nursing, Allied Health, Admin) and location (Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Al Dhafra).
3
Open the full advertisement and check the DOH licensing, experience and qualification requirements for that exact role.
4
Prepare your documents: CV, passport, photo, degree certificates, transcripts, DOH licence/eligibility letter and experience certificates.
5
Submit your application through the portal and never pay anyone — report any fee requests as fraud.

Why SEHA

SEHA offers government-hospital employment at genuine scale: state-of-the-art facilities, internationally trained colleagues, structured medical training and research pathways, and tax-free UAE packages with the security of the capital's public health system. For licensed clinicians — and for Emirati graduates entering Emiratisation-track support roles — it remains the benchmark government healthcare employer in Abu Dhabi.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for SEHA jobs in Abu Dhabi?

Only through the official portal at seha.ae/careers (Oracle system). SEHA does not accept applications through agents or unofficial channels.

What job categories does SEHA hire for?

Consultant and specialist physicians, nursing and midwifery, allied health and pharmacy, plus administrative and support roles including Emiratisation-track positions.

Is a DOH licence required for SEHA jobs?

Yes for regulated clinical roles — a valid Department of Health Abu Dhabi licence or eligibility letter meeting DOH PQR requirements. Requirements vary by profession.

Can freshers apply for SEHA jobs?

It depends on the role: most clinical posts require 2+ years of experience, but some support and graduate-track roles accept less. Check each advertisement.

Does SEHA charge any recruitment fee?

No. SEHA never asks for money at any stage. Any fee request is a scam — apply only via the official portal.

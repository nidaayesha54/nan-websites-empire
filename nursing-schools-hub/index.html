Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to the most common nursing school questions.

How do I choose a nursing school?

Compare accreditation (ACEN or CCNE), NCLEX first-time pass rates (aim for 85%+), total program cost, clinical placement support, and schedule fit. Our school finder quiz walks you through each factor.

ADN or BSN — which is better?

Both lead to the RN license. An ADN takes 2 years and costs less; a BSN takes 4 years but opens more hospital and leadership jobs. Read our full ADN vs BSN comparison.

How much does nursing school cost?

Community college ADN programs can cost under $15,000 total, while private BSN programs often exceed $80,000. Use our cost comparator to estimate your total investment versus starting RN salary.

What is a good NCLEX pass rate?

A first-time pass rate of 90% or higher is excellent; 80–90% is solid. Rates below 80% are a warning sign. See our NCLEX pass rate guide for the full breakdown.

Can I become a nurse with an online program?

Coursework can be online, but every legitimate program requires in-person clinical rotations. Fully-online programs that skip clinicals are not legitimate — learn more in our 2026 nursing school guide.

7 Prompt Mistakes Killing Your AI Videos (And the 2-Minute Fixes)

You've seen the jaw-dropping AI videos on your feed. Then you try the same tool and get... a blurry slideshow. The tool isn't the problem — your prompt is. Here are the seven mistakes nearly every beginner makes, and how to fix each in under two minutes.

1. Adjective stuffing

"Beautiful amazing stunning gorgeous sunset" — the model has no idea what any of that looks like. Adjectives are opinions; video models need facts: colors, light direction, physical detail.

Fix: swap every adjective for something visible. "Beautiful sunset" → "sun melting into the ocean, sky banded orange-to-violet, long shadows across wet sand."

2. No camera move

The single most common miss. Without camera direction, models default to a static or randomly drifting frame — the #1 cause of "screensaver" videos.

Fix: add three words: slow push-in, aerial descent, 360-degree orbit. Done. Your video now has cinematography.

3. Forgetting that it's video

Prompts like "a mountain lake, pine trees, sunset" describe a photo. Video models need motion verbs: mist drifting, water lapping, branches swaying, clouds racing.

Fix: add one moving element per noun. Lake → "mist drifting across the lake." Trees → "branches swaying." Instant life.

4. Keyword soup

"sunset beach 4k wallpaper ultra hd trending" — that's a search query, not a description. Models parse grammar and relationships, not tag clouds.

Fix: write one flowing sentence. Our prompt enhancer converts keyword soup into proper sentences automatically.

5. Too many heroes

"A knight, a dragon, a castle, villagers, fireworks, a storm" — the model can't compose six focal points in five seconds. Everything becomes mush.

Fix: one hero subject per shot. Knight or dragon. Let the rest be atmosphere.

6. Contradictory energy

"Slow motion, fast-paced, calm but exciting, dark yet bright" — the model averages your contradictions into beige nothing.

Fix: pick ONE energy word and commit. Serene? Triumphant? Tense? Everything else serves it.

7. No negative prompt

Most tools let you list what to avoid — and most beginners leave it blank, then wonder about the six-fingered hands.

Fix: paste this into every negative field: blurry, low quality, distorted faces, extra limbs, watermark, text overlay, flickering, morphing.

The 2-minute prompt audit

Before hitting generate, check your prompt against this list: one subject? Camera move named? Something moving? Lighting described? One mood? Negative prompt set? Six yeses = a prompt ready to impress.

GTA 6 Age Rating: ESRB, PEGI and Worldwide Guide

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it carries the highest age ratings a mainstream game can get in almost every country that rates games. In the United States it is M for Mature, 17 and older. In the United Kingdom and Europe it is PEGI 18. In Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates it is 21 and older. This guide lists every major rating, explains what is behind each one, and answers the question parents ask most: can a younger player play it?

The short answer is no — not legally, and not sensibly. The ratings are not a suggestion and they are not about difficulty. They are about content, and GTA 6's content is the reason every ratings board in the world put it at the top of its scale.

United States: ESRB M, 17+

In the United States, the Entertainment Software Rating Board rates GTA 6 M for Mature, for ages 17 and older. The rating carries seven content descriptors, and the list is worth reading in full, because it is the most detailed account of what is actually in the game: Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol. The rating also notes In-Game Purchases, which covers the game's store and any paid content inside it.

Seven descriptors is a lot — most M-rated games carry three or four — and it reflects the breadth of the game. This is not one scene or one mission that earned the rating; it is the whole world, from the story to the side activities to the radio. An M rating in the US means retailers are not supposed to sell the game to anyone under 17, and the console's own parental controls can block it entirely.

United Kingdom and Europe: PEGI 18

In the United Kingdom and across Europe, PEGI — the Pan European Game Information system — rates GTA 6 as 18, the highest rating it gives. PEGI 18 means the game is suitable only for adults, and in the UK the rating is legally enforceable: it is a criminal offence to sell a PEGI 18 game to anyone under 18. The PEGI rating is based on the same content as the ESRB rating — violence, sexual content, strong language, drugs — and it is the rating that matters most for players in Britain and the EU.

Germany uses its own system, the USK, and it also rates GTA 6 at 18 — "keine Jugendfreigabe", meaning no release to young people. The three big European systems agree, and that agreement is the point: there is no European country where GTA 6 is rated for teenagers.

The Rest of the World

The pattern holds everywhere else. Australia rates the game R18+, its highest rating for games, which restricts sale to adults. Japan's CERO system gives it a Z rating, the highest category, also restricted to adults. South Korea rates it 19 and older. Brazil rates it 18. India's IARC system rates it 18 and older. Every major ratings board in the world has put GTA 6 at its top level.

The strictest ratings are in the Gulf. Saudi Arabia and the United Arab Emirates both rate GTA 6 at 21 and older — higher than anywhere else in the world, and higher than the game's own content would suggest on its own. That reflects local law and local standards, not a different version of the game: the game is the same, and the rating is stricter because the rules are stricter.

What the Ratings Mean for Parents

If you are a parent, the ratings are the answer to the question you are asking: GTA 6 is an adults-only game, in every country that rates games. It is not a game that a 15-year-old can play with a few settings changed, and there is no "family mode" or content filter that makes it suitable for younger players. The content that earned the ratings — the violence, the sexual content, the language, the drugs — is woven through the story and the world, not confined to a corner you can avoid.

What you can do is use the console's parental controls. Both PlayStation and Xbox let you block games by age rating, so an M or PEGI 18 game simply will not launch on a child's account. That is the practical tool, and it is worth setting up before the game is in the house rather than after.

Frequently Asked Questions

What age is GTA 6 rated for?

GTA 6 is rated M for Mature (17+) in the US, PEGI 18 in the UK and Europe, USK 18 in Germany, R18+ in Australia, CERO Z in Japan, 19+ in South Korea, 18 in Brazil and India, and 21+ in Saudi Arabia and the UAE. It is an adults-only game in every country that rates games.

Can a 16-year-old play GTA 6?

Not according to any ratings board in the world. The game is rated 17+ at the lowest and 21+ at the highest, and the content behind those ratings — violence, sexual content, strong language and drugs — runs through the whole game. Use your console's parental controls to block it by age rating.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 is rated for adults everywhere: M 17+ in the US with seven content descriptors, PEGI 18 in the UK and Europe, USK 18 in Germany, R18+ in Australia, CERO Z in Japan, 19+ in South Korea, 18 in Brazil and India, and 21+ in Saudi Arabia and the UAE. There is no country where it is rated for teenagers, and no setting that makes it suitable for younger players.

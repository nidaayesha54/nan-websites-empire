Kenya Annual Leave Payout: How Unused Leave Days Are Paid Out

Kenyan law gives every employee 21 working days of paid annual leave a year — and unused days turn into cash when you leave. This guide explains how the payout works.

Key takeaways:
  • 21 working days of paid leave per year (1.75 days per month of service).
  • Unused days convert to cash at your daily rate when employment ends.
  • Kenyan courts reject ‘use it or lose it’ forfeiture — leave is taken or paid for.

Here's something many Kenyan workers don't realise until their last day: your unused annual leave is money. Whether you resign, your contract ends, or you're terminated, every leave day you accrued but didn't take must be paid out in cash. Kenyan courts have been blunt about this — there is no provision in the Employment Act for leave to simply vanish.

This guide explains how annual leave accrues in Kenya (21 days a year, or 1.75 days a month), how the cash value is calculated, the carryover rules, and what to do if an employer tries to withhold your leave pay.

The Legal Entitlement: 21 Days a Year

Section 28 of the Employment Act, 2007 gives every employee not less than 21 working days of leave with full pay after every 12 consecutive months of service. Because leave accrues monthly, that's 1.75 days per completed month — so a worker with 8 months of service has accrued 14 days.

Accrued leave = completed months × 1.75 days  ·  12 months = 21 days

Full pay during leave means your normal wages — the basic rate without deductions made under the Act. Annual leave is in addition to public holidays, weekly rest days, sick leave, and maternity/paternity leave. Those don't eat into your 21 days. Other countries structure vacation pay differently — Canada's vacation pay system, for example, accrues as a percentage of earnings.

Taking Leave: The Practical Rules

  • Splitting: with your consent, the employer may split your leave across the year — but one unbroken block must be at least two uninterrupted weeks, taken within the leave year, unless your contract says otherwise.
  • Carryover: untaken leave must generally be taken within 18 months of the end of the leave year in which it accrued.
  • No forfeiture: Kenyan courts have repeatedly held that "forfeiture of annual leave is alien to the Employment Act." Leave is either taken or paid for — company policies claiming otherwise don't override the statute.

How the Payout Is Calculated

When employment ends — resignation, termination, or contract expiry — the employer must pay for every accrued but unused day. The cash value uses your daily rate:

Daily rate = monthly salary ÷ 30  ·  Leave payout = unused days × daily rate

Worked Example

A worker earns KES 45,000 a month, has worked 8 months of the leave year, and has taken 5 days of leave:

  • Accrued: 8 × 1.75 = 14 days
  • Unused balance: 14 − 5 = 9 days
  • Daily rate: 45,000 ÷ 30 = KES 1,500
  • Payout: 9 × 1,500 = KES 13,500

That amount is due alongside your final dues — it isn't a favour, and it isn't negotiable downward.

Resignation vs Termination: Same Rule

The payout duty applies whenever employment ends. It doesn't matter whether you resigned, were dismissed, or your fixed-term contract expired — if you accrued the days and didn't take them, they convert to cash. The only thing that changes the math is how many days you actually took. Unpaid overtime is settled the same way — check what you're owed with the Kenya overtime calculator.

Leave Days vs Everything Else

A few distinctions that prevent common errors:

  • Public holidays falling inside your leave don't count as leave days.
  • Weekly rest days (e.g. Sundays) inside your leave period don't count either.
  • Sick leave is a separate entitlement (7 days full pay + 7 days half pay per year after two months of service) and never deducts from annual leave.
  • Maternity leave (91 days) and paternity leave (2 weeks) are also separate.

Track Your Balance Through the Year

Don't wait until your exit interview to discover your balance. Keep your own running tally: months worked × 1.75, minus days taken (count only working days). Ask HR for a written leave statement at least once a year — if their number and yours disagree, reconcile early while records are fresh.

What to Do If Leave Pay Is Withheld

1

Demand it in writing

Send HR your calculation with dates. Cite Section 28 of the Employment Act.

2

Reject "use it or lose it"

Forfeiture clauses have no basis in the Act — courts have struck them down repeatedly.

3

File a complaint

Unresolved claims go to the county labour office, then the Employment and Labour Relations Court.

Can Your Boss Refuse Your Leave Application?

Yes — within reason. The employer controls scheduling and can decline specific dates for operational needs. What the employer cannot do is refuse leave indefinitely until it lapses, or use repeated refusals as a backdoor forfeiture. If your leave requests are systematically denied, put them in writing: a paper trail of denied applications strengthens any later claim that the balance was the employer's doing, not yours.

The healthiest pattern is proactive: book your two uninterrupted weeks early in the leave year, schedule the remainder across quieter periods, and reconcile the balance with HR every quarter.

Planning Around the 18-Month Carryover

The 18-month carryover window is generous but not infinite. Example: leave accrued in the year ending December 2025 should be taken by June 2027. In practice, treat 12 months as your personal deadline and the extra 6 as buffer — balances that roll across multiple years become harder to reconcile and easier for payroll to miscalculate. Remember the court position though: even stale balances don't legally "expire" into nothing; they remain a payable benefit.

Leave and Public Holidays: A Common Payroll Error

Kenya observes around 11–13 public holidays a year, and they do not consume annual leave. If you take 10 working days off spanning a public holiday, only 9 leave days are deducted. Payroll systems that count calendar days instead of working days systematically understate balances — worth checking if your employer's leave portal looks calendar-based. The same applies to your weekly rest day falling inside a leave block.

Frequently Asked Questions

I resigned with 10 unused days. My employer says resigning staff forfeit leave. True?

No. The payout duty applies on resignation exactly as on termination. "Use it or lose it" forfeiture has no basis in the Employment Act.

I've worked 4 months. Do I have any leave accrued?

Yes — 4 × 1.75 = 7 days. If your employment ends now, those 7 days (minus any taken) must be paid out.

Is leave pay calculated on basic salary or gross?

On wages at the basic rate for the leave period — commonly computed as monthly salary ÷ 30 per day. Allowances that form part of your regular wage count; one-off payments don't.

Can I carry leave forward indefinitely?

Untaken leave should be taken within 18 months of the leave year's end. After that it doesn't vanish — but don't let balances pile up for years; reconcile annually.

Does my employer have to pay leave in cash while I'm still employed?

Generally leave is for rest, not cash, during employment — the payout right crystallises when employment ends. Some contracts allow encashment; that's a contractual extra, not the statutory rule.

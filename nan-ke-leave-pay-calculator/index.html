How annual leave works in Kenya

Section 28 of the Employment Act 2007 sets the minimum annual leave rules for all employees:

  • 21 working days of leave with full pay after every 12 consecutive months of service.
  • Accrual: you start earning leave after two consecutive months of service, at 1.75 working days per completed month.
  • Termination payout: if your employment ends after two or more completed months in a leave-earning period, your employer must pay you 1.75 days' full pay per completed month of unused leave.
  • Carry-over: unused leave must be taken within 18 months of the end of the leave-earning period, or it is forfeited.
  • No cash-out while employed: payment in lieu of leave is prohibited except on termination, resignation, or contract end.
  • Public holidays and weekly rest days that fall during your leave do not count against your entitlement.

Example: on a KES 60,000/month salary with 8 completed months and no leave taken, you have accrued 8 × 1.75 = 14 days. One day's pay is 60,000 ÷ 30 = KES 2,000, so your termination payout would be 14 × 2,000 = KES 28,000.

Frequently asked questions

Can my employer refuse to pay my unused leave when I resign?

No. On termination or resignation, Section 28 requires payment of accrued unused leave. Cash in lieu of leave is only prohibited while you remain employed.

How many leave days do I get per month?

You accrue 1.75 working days for each completed month of service — 21 working days over 12 months.

Do public holidays count as leave days?

No. Public holidays and your weekly rest day do not count against your 21-day annual leave entitlement.

What happens to leave I don't use?

Unused leave must be taken within 18 months of the end of the leave-earning period, otherwise it is forfeited. It can only be converted to cash when your employment ends.

How is one day's leave pay calculated?

Leave is paid at full pay. This calculator uses your monthly salary ÷ 30 as the daily rate — a common convention for day-rate benefits in Kenya (used for severance pay of 15 days' wages per year).

GTA 6 Download: Get the Game and the Free Guide App

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the first thing millions of players typed into search was some version of "how to download GTA 6" — about 46,000 searches a month for that one phrase alone. The answer has two parts, and they are easy to mix up. There is the game itself, a 212 GB download from the PlayStation Store or the Xbox Store, on consoles only. And there is the free GTA 6 Guide app, a small Android download that puts the game's tips, fixes, map and news in your pocket. This guide covers both downloads, in the right order, and explains why every other "free GTA 6 download" you will see is a trap.

The only downloads that are safe are the ones that come from the console stores — and the guide app linked below.

How to download the real game

On PlayStation 5, open the PlayStation Store, search for GTA 6, and buy the Standard edition for $79.99 or the Ultimate edition for $99.99. The download is about 212 GB, so check your free space first — the game needs the full 212 GB free before it will start, and a boxed copy is a download code, not a disc. Install the PlayStation 5 system software update 26.07-14.23.00 first, which the console requires before GTA 6 will run. Pre-loading opened on November 10, and the game unlocked at midnight local time in each store region.

On Xbox Series X|S, find GTA 6 in the Store, buy it, and install it from your library under Owned games. Turn on automatic updates and leave the console in Sleep mode rather than full Shutdown — a fully shut-down Xbox downloads nothing overnight. If a download stalls, pause it and resume it from the Downloads queue, then fully power-cycle the console by holding the power button for ten seconds.

If the game shows a padlock after you have paid, do not delete the 212 GB install. A locked game at launch is usually not a bug — it means the game has not unlocked yet in your store region. After the unlock time, sign in to the account that bought the game, install the system update, and on PlayStation use Restore License from the game's Options menu.

Why there is no PC download

There is no PC version of GTA 6 at launch, and Rockstar has not announced a PC date. That single fact is the whole business model of the scam sites: they sell people the download that does not exist yet. A report from October 2026, citing four sources, said the PC version is targeting the first quarter of 2027 — but Rockstar declined to comment, and a separate February 2027 date was an unconfirmed report, not an announcement. The honest comparison is history: GTA 5 arrived on PC about 19 months after consoles, and Red Dead Redemption 2 took just over a year. The demand is real — 243,000 searches a month for "how to play GTA 6 on PC" — the product does not exist, and the gap between the two is where the malware lives.

The free APK offers are a trap

The fake downloads are not harmless dead links. Security researchers have taken them apart, and what is inside is built to steal. One widely shared fake installer, gta6_installer.exe, is only 1.1 MB and carries the Vidar information stealer, which harvests saved passwords, browser sessions and crypto wallets. Netcraft counted 121 separate domains in one CyberLeek campaign alone. Another fake, GTAVI.exe, was flagged by 34 of 68 antivirus engines. A fake ISO image larger than 100 GB — dressed up to look like the real 212 GB game — hides remote-access tools and ransomware. The WARDEN Stealer promises a 72 MB game against a real 212 GB, and drains wallets through fake pre-order pages asking for $100 in crypto.

The red flags are always the same: a download far too small for a 212 GB game, a page that asks you to connect a wallet or pay in crypto, and a timer counting down to make you hurry. A real download never asks for your wallet. If you already downloaded one of these files, do not run it — delete it, run a full antivirus scan, and change the passwords stored in your browser.

The legal free routes

There are two honest ways to play GTA 6 without paying full price. The first is Microsoft Rewards: points from searches and daily tasks can be redeemed for Xbox gift cards, and roughly 5,000 points is worth about $5 towards the game. The second is launch-week giveaways: the Kick Sunset Reward Case ran from November 12 to December 7 with 10,000 Ultimate edition codes for viewers watching three hours a day, and Twitch ran surprise daily drops from November 19 to 30. Codes arrive after the launch date, and they come from the platform running the promotion — never from a download link in a comment.

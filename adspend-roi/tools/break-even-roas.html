Frequently asked questions

Break-even ROAS is the minimum return on ad spend at which your ads neither make nor lose money. It's calculated as Price ÷ (Price − Costs).

Platform averages are meaningless for your business. A 3x ROAS is a disaster if your break-even is 4x, and a triumph if your break-even is 1.8x. Always start from your own numbers.

Include everything tied to fulfilling one order: product cost, packaging, shipping, payment processing fees, and returns. Don't include fixed overhead like rent — that's not per-sale.

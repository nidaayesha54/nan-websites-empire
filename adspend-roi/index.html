Why ROAS matters more than clicks

Clicks and impressions are vanity metrics. ROAS tells you the one thing that matters: does your advertising make money? A campaign with a 2% CTR and 5x ROAS beats a 5% CTR campaign at 1.5x ROAS every single time. Frequently asked questions

ROAS (Return on Ad Spend) measures revenue earned per dollar spent on advertising. ROAS of 4x means you earned $4 for every $1 spent on ads.

A 4:1 ratio is the commonly cited healthy benchmark, but it depends on margins. High-margin digital products can profit at 2x; low-margin ecommerce may need 5x or more.

Divide your product price by your contribution margin (price minus costs). A $50 product with $30 in costs needs $50 ÷ $20 = 2.5x ROAS to break even.

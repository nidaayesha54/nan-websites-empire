GTA 6 Soccer: Fleeca Field, Mambas and Betting

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Vice City is a sports city. It has a football stadium, a basketball arena, team jerseys on the streets, and a real-world marketing campaign built around the Miami Heat. Players keep asking two questions: can you play soccer or football in GTA 6, and can you bet on it? This guide answers both, with what the game actually shows and what it has never promised.

The short version is this: the stadiums are real, the teams are real, and the sports are scenery. There is no playable football match in GTA 6, and no sports betting system has been announced. What the game does have is one of the most detailed sports backdrops in any open world — and that backdrop is worth a visit on its own.

Fleeca Field: the stadium in Rockridge

Fleeca Field is the football stadium of Vice City, located in Rockridge, in Vice-Dale County. It is modelled on LoanDepot Park, the retractable-roof ballpark in Miami's Riverside neighbourhood, home of the Miami Marlins — a $634 million stadium with a roof that, in the game, fans have suggested players may be able to open. The naming rights in the game belong to Fleeca, the banking company that has been a GTA fixture for years, and banners for the home team hang outside the entrance.

The stadium's home team is the Vice City Mambas, the game's version of the Miami Dolphins — a football team with a mamba wearing a football helmet as its logo, a design that echoes the Dolphins' look from 1966 to 2012. The Mambas played at Hyman Memorial Stadium in the older games, and Fleeca Field is their new home. One screenshot, however, shows a man wearing an aqua jersey with "Manatees" written across the front, which suggests the team name may have changed to the Vice City Manatees. Either way, the team is a football team, and the stadium is a football stadium — even though its real-life model is a baseball park.

The Sahara Arena and the Narcos

Basketball gets its own venue: the Sahara Arena, an indoor complex that is the game's version of the Kaseya Center, the $213 million home of the Miami Heat on Biscayne Bay. The arena hosts the Vice City Narcos, the game's basketball team, and it appears in the first trailer — a woman standing in a convertible at night, with the elaborately lit arena glowing in the background. The design borrows heavily from the real arena, with a lighting upgrade that makes it one of the most visible landmarks in the city at night.

The sports scene does not stop at the screen. Rockstar's real-world marketing brought Vice City into Miami itself: the Miami Heat hosted "A Night in Vice City" on November 18, 2026, the day before launch, with a Vice City-themed court, special uniforms and exclusive merchandise, and a "Welcome to Vice City" sign was installed on the Kaseya Center roof. Advertising also appeared at Hard Rock Stadium, home of the Miami Dolphins, during an October game. The game and the real city are marketing each other, and the stadiums are the link.

Can you play football in GTA 6?

Not that anyone has confirmed. The game shows stadiums, jerseys and team branding, but no trailer, preview or review describes a playable football match, a soccer game, or any sports mini-game built around the Mambas or the Narcos. The activities Rockstar has shown are different: basketball, kayaking, scuba diving, fishing, hunting, golf, gyms and shooting ranges. Sports exist in GTA 6 as culture — jerseys, stadiums, radio talk and bar arguments — not as a game you can join.

That is consistent with the series. GTA has always used sports as set dressing: the stadium you drive past, the team whose colours the gangs wear, the match on the bar television. Fleeca Field and the Sahara Arena are the most detailed versions of that tradition yet, and they are worth visiting for the atmosphere alone. But if you are waiting for a kick-off, you will be waiting a long time.

Can you bet on sports in GTA 6?

No sports betting system has been announced for GTA 6, and nothing in the trailers, previews or reviews describes one. The game has casinos and gambling in its history — GTA Online had a casino — but GTA 6 Online is not at launch, and the story mode has shown no betting shops, no bookmakers and no way to put money on a match. The Mambas and the Narcos are teams to wear, not teams to wager on.

That matters because the scams around this launch are real. Fake download sites have hidden malware, fake early-access offers have drained wallets, and any site promising a GTA 6 betting system or a way to win money on the game is selling something that does not exist. If a betting system ever appears, it will come from Rockstar's own channels — and until then, the only money moving on Vice City sports is the money you spend on a jersey.

FAQ

Can you play soccer in GTA 6? No playable football or soccer match has been confirmed. Fleeca Field and the Sahara Arena are stadiums you can visit, and the Mambas and Narcos are teams whose jerseys appear in the game.

Can you bet on sports in GTA 6? No sports betting system has been announced. GTA 6 Online is not at launch, and story mode shows no betting shops or bookmakers.

The bottom line: GTA 6 has the stadiums, the teams and the jerseys — Fleeca Field, the Mambas, the Sahara Arena and the Narcos — but the sports are scenery, not a game you can play or a bet you can place. Visit the stadium, wear the jersey, and keep your money in your pocket.

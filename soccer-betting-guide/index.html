Soccer Betting FAQ

What do American odds like -150 and +200 mean in soccer betting?

-150 means you must risk $150 to win $100 (favorite). +200 means a $100 bet wins $200 (underdog). The minus sign marks the favorite; the plus sign marks the underdog. Try our odds converter to switch between formats.

What is over/under 2.5 goals?

A totals bet on whether a soccer match finishes with 3+ goals (over 2.5) or 0–2 goals (under 2.5). Since soccer can't end with half a goal, there are no pushes — you win or lose. Full explainer.

How does implied probability work?

Divide 100 by decimal odds to get implied probability (e.g. 2.00 = 50%). Bookmaker odds include a margin, so implied probabilities across all outcomes sum above 100% — they are prices, not true predictions.

What is the Kelly criterion?

A bankroll formula that sizes bets proportionally to your perceived edge: (bp − q) / b. It's educational, not a winning system — your win-probability estimate is always uncertain, so most bettors use half or quarter Kelly. Try the calculator.

Are parlay bets a good idea for beginners?

No. Every added leg multiplies both the payout and the risk, and one losing leg loses the whole ticket. Parlays carry a higher house edge than single bets — treat them as entertainment. See the math.

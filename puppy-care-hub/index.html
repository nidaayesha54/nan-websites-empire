❓ New Puppy FAQs

Quick answers to the questions every first-time puppy parent asks.

The essentials are puppy food, food and water bowls, a crate with bedding, a collar with an ID tag, a leash, chew toys, enzyme cleaner, and a first vet appointment booked within 3 days. Our free checklist generator builds a full itemized list by budget tier.

In the USA, the first year typically costs $1,500–$5,000+ depending on size, source (adoption vs breeder), and choices like insurance and training. Use our cost calculator for an itemized estimate.

A common rule: a puppy can hold it about one hour per month of age. Take an 8-week-old out every 2 hours, plus after eating, playing, waking up, and before bed. See our schedule generator for a full day plan.

Puppies can go outside in your own yard right away, but should avoid public areas with unknown dogs until about a week after their final vaccinations (around 16–18 weeks). Read the full guide for the details.

Newborn to 12-week puppies sleep 18–20 hours a day. By 6–12 months that drops to about 12–16 hours. Enforced crate naps prevent overtired, bitey behavior — our guide to the first week home covers the routine.

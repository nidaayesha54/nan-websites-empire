GTA 6 Dogs: Every Breed Spotted in Vice City

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, and the animals of Leonida are not background decoration. The game has more than 170 animal species, a zoo with its own compendium, and — the detail players talk about most — dogs you can walk up to, pet, scold and study. The dogs of Vice City are some of the most detailed animals Rockstar has ever made, and this guide covers every breed spotted so far, where to find them, and exactly what the buttons do.

The short version: approach a dog, focus it with L2, and the game offers you four choices — pet, scold, study, and praise. The dog reacts with its own animation, not a cutscene, and the moment is different every time. Here is the full guide.

Every breed spotted so far

Several dog breeds have already been spotted in GTA 6. Chihuahuas, Dobermans, German Shepherds and Rottweilers all appear in the game, in yards, on streets and on porches across Vice City and beyond. The most famous dog in the game so far is a pitbull: in the extended gameplay footage, Jason and Lucia stop in the hallway of the Victory Arms apartment building during a package pickup, and a brown pitbull is sitting on the floor with a food bowl, a cushion and a framed photo nearby, its owners around the corner.

There is also a Rottweiler on a leash beside Bae-Luxe in the Dazed Digital issue for the game — a reminder that dogs in GTA 6 are part of the world, not just strays. Domesticated cats have been seen too, and a python enclosure sits in the safehouse, though it is unclear whose snake it is. Dogs also turn up out in the world, including on the beach, and the wider map carries other wildlife such as swamp animals and seabirds.

The four buttons: pet, scold, study, praise

The dog interaction works through the same system as the general NPC interaction. Aim at the dog with L2 to focus it — the label DOG appears over it — and the face buttons fill in with what the dog supports. On PlayStation the buttons are: Triangle to pet, Circle to scold, R1 to study. Xbox players get the standard equivalents: Y, B and RB.

Before you do anything, the dog offers a single option: PRAISE. Once you engage, the list expands and scold, pet and study fill in underneath. The animal is tracking the exchange as it happens, so the menu you see on approach is not the menu you see thirty seconds later. When you pet the dog, your character leans down and the dog physically responds — it stands up on its hind legs to interact, with no fade, no camera takeover and no scripted cutaway. If you are standing too far away, the DOG focus label will not appear and the prompts will not populate at all. Close the distance and the lock-on picks it up.

The doggy bag and the treats

Rockstar has accounted for what happens after a dog does its business. The studio's Rob Nelson explained that players can pick up a dog's poop using the owner's discarded doggy bag — and you can even interfere with the NPC, take the bag, and throw the poop at someone else. It is a small, silly detail, and it is exactly the kind of detail that makes Vice City feel alive.

You can also buy treats for dogs at shops and give them out, according to a New York Times report on the game. Pets and pet owners can react differently to you as you approach, which plays into the interaction system: a dog that likes you behaves differently from a dog whose owner you just annoyed. Scolding a dog is exactly what it sounds like — the game shows the option, though what it does to the dog's behaviour over time is not yet clear.

Studying animals: the zoo and the compendium

The Study option is borrowed from Red Dead Redemption 2, and it hints at something bigger. GTA 6 has a zoo you can walk through, and every new species you observe gets logged in an in-game compendium — for the 100% completion checklist you need to study 100 of 255 animals by looking at them through the binoculars. Dogs are the friendliest entry in that list, but the same system covers alligators, flamingos, pythons, panthers, boars, iguanas, raccoons, deer, manatees and pelicans.

Whether the full four-option menu attaches to every animal you meet is not yet confirmed — the complete menu is confirmed on the Victory Arms pitbull, and the study option is the one that carries across to wildlife. What is confirmed is that the animals are not scenery: they react, they remember the exchange, and they are worth stopping for.

Can you own a dog in GTA 6?

Here is the honest answer: no pet ownership system has been confirmed. Pets are present in GTA 6, and the interactions are surprisingly deep, but there is a clear line between interacting with someone else's pet and having one of your own — and Rockstar has not announced an adoption, ownership or pet-care system for Jason and Lucia. Shelter-adoption claims come from wishlist posts and concept videos, not from any source.

The precedent is Chop, Franklin's Rottweiler in GTA 5, who could be walked, played fetch with and trained in scent-tracking missions. GTA 6 has the interaction system to do the same, and the breeds are there, but no Chop-style companion has been confirmed. Until Rockstar says otherwise, the dogs of Vice City are the world's dogs — and the best thing you can do with them is stop, focus, and press Triangle.

FAQ

How do you pet a dog in GTA 6? Walk up to the dog, hold L2 to focus it until the DOG label appears, then press Triangle on PlayStation (Y on Xbox). The dog reacts with its own animation. Circle scolds, R1 studies, and PRAISE appears first.

What dog breeds are in GTA 6? Chihuahuas, Dobermans, German Shepherds and Rottweilers have been spotted, plus the pitbull in the Victory Arms hallway. A leashed Rottweiler appears beside Bae-Luxe in the Dazed issue.

Can you adopt a dog in GTA 6? No adoption or ownership system has been confirmed. You can pet, scold, study and praise dogs, buy them treats at shops, and log species in the animal compendium — but owning one is not announced.

The bottom line: the dogs of Vice City are worth your time. Four breeds spotted, four buttons to learn, one very good pitbull in a hallway — and a doggy bag if you are feeling brave.

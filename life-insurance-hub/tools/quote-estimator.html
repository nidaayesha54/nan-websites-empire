Life Insurance Premium Estimator

Life insurance pricing follows predictable patterns: age is the biggest driver, then smoking, health class, and term length. This estimator applies those multipliers to give you a realistic range.

Disclaimer: Educational purposes only — not financial advice. Estimates, not quotes.

How the estimate is calculated

We start from a base monthly rate per $100,000 of coverage by age band ($12 in your 20s, $15 in your 30s, $25 in your 40s, $45 in your 50s, $85 in your 60s for healthy non-smokers), then apply multipliers:

  • Tobacco: smokers pay roughly 2.2× more.
  • Health class: preferred × 0.9, standard × 1.0, substandard × 1.4.
  • Term length: 10-yr × 0.7, 20-yr × 1.0, 30-yr × 1.35.
  • The low estimate is 80% and the high estimate 125% of the average, reflecting insurer price spread.

Note: These are educational estimates, not quotes. Final premiums depend on underwriting: labs, medical history, family history, and lifestyle.

How the DIME formula works

Coverage needed = Debts + (Income × years) + Mortgage + Education − existing assets. Each letter is one thing your family would need money for if your income stopped tomorrow.

  • D — Debts: everything you owe besides the mortgage: credit cards, auto loans, student loans.
  • I — Income: your annual income times the number of years your family needs support (often 10–20, roughly until kids are independent).
  • M — Mortgage: the remaining balance, so your family can stay in the home.
  • E — Education: future college or schooling costs for your children.

Then subtract liquid assets and any life insurance you already own — that money already covers part of the need.

Note: DIME tends to give a thorough number. A quicker rule of thumb is 10–12× your annual income; DIME is more personalized. Round your result up to the nearest standard face amount ($250k, $500k, $1M).

Renters Insurance FAQs

Quick answers before you dive into the tools.

How much does renters insurance cost in 2026?
About $14–$20/month for a standard $30,000 policy. Prices vary by state — Mississippi and Louisiana are among the priciest, while Montana and South Dakota are cheapest. Estimate yours or read the full cost guide.
What does renters insurance cover?
Your belongings (theft, fire, etc.), liability if you injure someone or damage their property, and living expenses if your rental is uninhabitable after a covered event. See the full coverage guide.
Do I really need renters insurance?
Most renters do: your landlord's policy covers the building, not your stuff. Our honest guide walks through the math and the exceptions.
How much coverage do I need?
Usually $20,000–$40,000 of personal property plus $100,000–$300,000 of liability. Run the belongings calculator or take the coverage quiz for a personal number.
Does renters insurance cover theft outside my apartment?
Yes — personal property coverage follows you. If your laptop is stolen from your car or a coffee shop, your renters policy typically covers it (subject to your deductible).

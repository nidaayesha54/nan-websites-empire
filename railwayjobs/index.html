How to Apply

1
Read the official notification — check eligibility, age limit, fees and vacancy details. Apply through the official RRB portal of your region (rrbcdg.gov.in and other regional RRB sites) or indianrailways.gov.in.
2
Register on the official portal — indianrailways.gov.in, regional RRB portals (rrbcdg.gov.in etc.) — create your account with a valid email and mobile number for the online application.
3
Fill & submit the form — upload photo, signature and documents as specified; pay the fee online and save the confirmation page.
4
Download your admit card — released 2–3 weeks before the exam on the same official portal; check your exam city and shift.
5
Understand the exam pattern — review Prelims/Mains syllabus, marking scheme and previous-year cutoffs, then track results on the official site.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RRB NTPC?
RRB NTPC (Non-Technical Popular Categories) recruits station masters, goods guards, senior clerks and ticket collectors in Indian Railways.
What is the eligibility for Group D?
10th pass or ITI from a recognized board/institute; age 18–33 years as per the notification.
How do I apply for railway jobs?
Applications are online only on the official regional RRB website listed in the notification — never pay any agent.
What is the selection process?
CBT-1 → CBT-2 (where applicable) → PET/skill test → document verification → medical examination.
Can I apply to more than one RRB?
No — apply to only one RRB per centralised notification; multiple applications are rejected.
Where can I download the railway admit card?
The admit card appears on the regional RRB website about 10 days before the CBT exam date.

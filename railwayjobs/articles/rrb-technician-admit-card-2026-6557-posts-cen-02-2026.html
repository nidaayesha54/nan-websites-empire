RRB Technician Admit Card 2026

Hall tickets out for 6,557 Technician posts (CEN 02/2026) — CBT runs 6–9 October 2026. Download steps, exam-day rules and what to carry.

RRB Technician (CEN 02/2026): Key Facts

PostTechnician — Grade I and Grade III
Total Vacancies6,557
Advertisement No.CEN 02/2026
EligibilityITI/Act Apprenticeship or Diploma in relevant trade (post-wise, as per notification)
Application Window30 June – 29 July 2026 (closed)
City Intimation Slip27 September 2026
Admit Card2 October 2026 (released in staggered slots, ~4 days before exam)
CBT Exam Dates6, 7, 8 & 9 October 2026
Official Portalsrrbapply.gov.in · rrb.indianrailways.gov.in

The Railway Recruitment Boards released the RRB Technician Admit Card 2026 on 2 October 2026 for 6,557 Technician posts advertised under Centralised Employment Notice 02/2026. The notification was issued on 29 June 2026 and online applications ran from 30 June to 29 July 2026. The Computer Based Test is being conducted on 6, 7, 8 and 9 October 2026. Candidates download the hall ticket from the official RRB candidate portal using their registration number and password/date of birth.

How to Download the Technician Admit Card

1
Open the RRB candidate portal — go to rrbapply.gov.in.
2
Log in — enter your registration number and password (date of birth in DD/MM/YYYY format), then solve the captcha.
3
Open the CEN 02/2026 admit card link — select the Technician Grade I / Grade III hall ticket.
4
Verify and print — check your name, photo, exam centre, date and shift; take a clear printout for exam day.

The admit card is released online only — it is not sent by post. If your link is not active yet, recheck the portal closer to your exam date, since RRB staggers releases about four days before each shift.

City Intimation Slip vs Admit Card

Many candidates confuse the two documents released for this exam:

  • City intimation slip (27 September 2026): tells you only your exam city and date — useful for travel planning.
  • Admit card (2 October 2026): gives the exact exam centre address, reporting time, shift and exam-day instructions — this is the document you carry to the hall.

Exam-Day Checklist

  • Printed admit card with a clear, uncropped photograph
  • Original photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, voter ID, driving licence or passport) plus a photocopy
  • One recent passport-size photograph
  • Reach the centre well before the reporting time — late entry is not permitted
  • Electronic devices, calculators and study material are barred from the hall

Selection Process After the CBT

Technician selection has three stages: CBT → Document Verification → Medical Examination. There is no second CBT or aptitude stage for this recruitment, so your CBT score carries the full weight of shortlisting for verification.

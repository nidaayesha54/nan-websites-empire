RRB NTPC Graduate Jobs 2026 – 3,548 Posts, Apply by 6 Nov

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have revised the vacancy count for CEN No. 06/2026 (Non-Technical Popular Categories – Graduate) upward to 3,548 posts — higher than the 3,477 tentative vacancies indicated earlier. The application window opened on 8 October 2026 and closes on 6 November 2026 (11:59 PM) at rrbapply.gov.in.

The four posts are Goods Train Manager (2,750), Senior Clerk cum Typist (371), Junior Accounts Assistant cum Typist (300) and Chief Commercial cum Ticket Supervisor (127). In a major change, the typing skill test has been dispensed with for all posts in this CEN. Post-wise breakup, fee-refund rules and the selection structure are below.

Vacancy honesty note: the post-wise breakup (2,750 + 371 + 300 + 127) tallies exactly with the revised 3,548 total reported by multiple outlets, including a PIB-referenced release. The 3,477 figure was the earlier tentative count and is superseded. All figures remain marked “tentative” in the notification itself.

What’s New in This CEN

  • Revised vacancies: 3,548, up from the tentative 3,477.
  • No typing skill test: the computer-based typing test has been dispensed with for all four posts; typing proficiency will be assessed after joining instead.
  • Fee refund on appearing: candidates who appear in CBT-1 get ₹400 back (of ₹500) or the full ₹250 (concession categories).

Eligibility and Age Limit

Qualification: a Bachelor’s degree from a recognised university (or equivalent) for all four posts.

Age limit: 18–33 years as on 01/01/2027 — up to 38 for SC/ST and up to 36 for OBC-NCL candidates.

Selection Process

1
CBT-1 — first-stage computer-based test (1/3rd negative marking).
2
CBT-2 — second-stage computer-based test (1/3rd negative marking).
3
Document Verification — for CBT-2 qualifiers, per RRB cutoffs.
4
Medical Examination — fitness as per the post’s medical standard.

How to Apply Online

1
Open rrbapply.gov.in — the common application portal for all RRBs.
2
Create an account and select your RRB zone — you can apply to only one RRB.
3
Fill the form and upload documents — degree certificate, photo, signature and category certificate if applicable.
4
Pay the fee (₹500 / ₹250) by 8 November 2026 — partly refundable on appearing in CBT-1.
5
Submit by 6 November 2026, 11:59 PM — the modification window runs 9–18 November 2026.

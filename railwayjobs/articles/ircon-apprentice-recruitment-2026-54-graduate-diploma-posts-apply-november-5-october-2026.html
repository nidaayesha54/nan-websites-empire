IRCON International Limited, a Navratna PSU under the Ministry of Railways, has released Advt. No. A01/2026 for 54 apprentices — 36 Graduate and 18 Diploma — under the Apprenticeship Act, 1961. Apply online 16 October – 5 November 2026 on ircon.org; the signed hard copy must reach New Delhi by 17 November 2026.

This is one year of paid, project-site training with a leading railway infrastructure PSU. Selection is purely merit-based on qualifying-exam marks — no written test, no interview.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Graduate Apprentice posts need a full-time degree in the relevant discipline — engineering/technology for Civil, Electrical, S&T and IT; and BBA, B.Com, BA (Hindi), Bachelor of Social Work, LLB, Computer Science or IT for the non-engineering disciplines. Technician (Diploma) Apprentice posts need a diploma in the relevant engineering stream. Distance, part-time and correspondence qualifications are not eligible, and candidates who have already undergone apprenticeship training anywhere are barred.

Age Limit

Candidates must be 18 to 30 years as on 1 October 2026, with relaxation for reserved categories as per rules. Only candidates who passed in 2024, 2025 or 2026 (or on/after 1 July 2023) are eligible to apply.

NATS Registration

You must hold a NATS portal registration number before applying — register on the National Apprenticeship Training Scheme portal first, then fill the IRCON online form.

How to Apply — Online Plus Hard Copy

1
Register on the NATS portal — complete your apprenticeship registration and note your NATS registration number — it is mandatory for the IRCON form.
2
Apply online on ircon.org — fill the application for Advt A01/2026 between 16 October and 5 November 2026, choosing the correct discipline and category.
3
Take a printout and sign it — after online submission, print the application and sign it by hand.
4
Post the hard copy — send the signed application with self-attested documents to IRCON's Saket, New Delhi office — it must reach by 17 November 2026.
5
Track the merit list — IRCON shortlists on qualifying-exam marks; watch ircon.org for the merit list and document-verification call.
Important: this is apprenticeship training, not a regular IRCON job — but one year on live railway projects, with a Navratna PSU on your resume, is exactly the experience private and public recruiters ask for.

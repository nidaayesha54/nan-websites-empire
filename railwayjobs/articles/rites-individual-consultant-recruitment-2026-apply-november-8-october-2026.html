RITES Ltd, the Navratna railway consultancy PSU, has invited online applications for Individual Consultant positions (Advertisement Nos. YP/67/26 to YP/92/26) for its Patna Metro project client. The window opened on 9 October 2026 and closes on 8 November 2026 (11:00 PM) via rites.com / recruit.rites.com.

Roles span signalling, system integration, testing & commissioning, quality assurance, BIM management and site engineering, with pay from ₹55,000 to ₹2,25,000 per month on a 12-month contract (extendable). One caution: portals report 26 vs 27 vacancies — the advertisement-number range suggests 26 — so verify the exact count in the official PDF.

Consultant Roles on Offer

The advertisements cover specialist roles including:

  • Dy. Chief Signalling System Engineer
  • Dy. Chief System Integration / Testing & Commissioning Expert
  • System Integration Expert
  • Quality Assurance Expert
  • BIM Manager
  • Resident Engineer and Site Engineer, plus other specialist roles

Eligibility

Engineering graduates / diploma holders with post-specific experience — the experience bar differs sharply between a BIM Manager and a Site Engineer, so read the individual advertisement (YP/67/26 through YP/92/26) for your role. Age limits are also post-specific, with the crucial cut-off date of 8 November 2026.

Contract Terms

This is an Individual Consultant engagement — 12 months, extendable — not regular RITES employment. Monthly pay ranges from ₹55,000 to ₹2,25,000 depending on the post.

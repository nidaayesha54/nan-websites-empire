What Is CBAT and What Comes After

The Computer Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) is the aptitude stage unique to the ALP recruitment — it tests the perceptual and psychomotor abilities needed to drive trains. After CBAT, the remaining stages are:

  • Document verification — original certificates, ITI/diploma credentials, caste and ID proofs.
  • Medical examination — ALP posts require the stringent A-1 medical standard, including vision standards, so get your eyesight checked early.

The application fee was ₹500 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹250 for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen and all female candidates. Candidates who appeared for the Stage-I (CBT-I) exam received a partial fee refund — ₹400 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹250 for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen and all female candidates — as notified in CEN 01/2025. Keep your application printout and fee payment receipt safe until the recruitment concludes, since RRB may ask for them during document verification.

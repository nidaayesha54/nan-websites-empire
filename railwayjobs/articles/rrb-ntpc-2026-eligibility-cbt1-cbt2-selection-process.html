RRB NTPC 2026 Complete Guide: Eligibility, CBT 1 & 2, Selection Process

Everything graduates need to know about the RRB NTPC 2026 recruitment — 3,477 Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate) posts, eligibility rules, the two-stage CBT exam pattern, skill tests, and the full RRB NTPC selection process.

What Is RRB NTPC?

NTPC stands for Non-Technical Popular Categories — the Railway Recruitment Board's flagship recruitment for office and station-based roles in Indian Railways. It is one of the most competitive graduate-level government exams in India, covering roles like Station Master, Goods Guard, Senior Clerk-cum-Typist and Commercial-cum-Ticket Clerk.

For 2026, the Railway Recruitment Board released an indicative notice for the RRB NTPC Graduate Recruitment 2026 under CEN 06/2026, covering 3,477 Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate) posts. Online applications run 8 October 2026 to 6 November 2026 (11:59 pm). Read the detailed notification on the official RRB portals before applying — it is the final word on eligibility, fees and reservations.

Eligibility Notes

CEN 06/2026 is open to eligible graduates. Exact degree requirements, age limits and relaxation norms differ by post — the detailed notification defines them and is the only document to go by.

  • Educational qualification: a bachelor's degree from a recognised university. Some posts need extra skills (e.g. typing) — check the detailed notification.
  • Age limit: post-wise age limits with relaxation for reserved categories, as per Government of India norms.
  • One-RRB rule: apply to only one RRB in a centralised notification. Multiple applications are rejected.
  • Application fee: payable online; the detailed notification lists category-wise fees and refund provisions.

CBT Stage 1: Exam Pattern

CBT Stage 1 is the first screening round of the RRB NTPC selection process. It follows the long-standing standard NTPC pattern: 100 questions for 100 marks, 90 minutes of exam time, covering Mathematics, General Intelligence & Reasoning, and General Awareness, with negative marking of 1/3 mark per wrong answer.

CBT Stage 2: Exam Pattern

Candidates who clear CBT 1 are called for CBT Stage 2, which follows the standard NTPC Stage 2 pattern: 120 questions for 120 marks, 90 minutes, across the same three subjects, with 1/3 negative marking. Stage 2 carries the real weight in merit shortlisting, so deeper preparation pays off here.

Skill Tests, Document Verification & Medical Exam

  • CBAT (Computer Based Aptitude Test): for posts like Station Master, qualifying candidates face the aptitude test.
  • Typing skill test: applicable posts like Senior Clerk-cum-Typist require a typing proficiency test after the CBTs.
  • Document verification: originals of degree, category, identity and signature proofs, as declared in the application.
  • Medical examination: the final step. Candidates must meet the prescribed medical standards; failing the medical ends the selection here.

Preparation Tips for RRB NTPC 2026

  1. Master the three subjects daily: split study time between maths, reasoning and general awareness; current affairs need a daily 30-minute slot.
  2. Practise with the 1/3 penalty: attempt mocks with negative marking on, and learn to skip doubtful questions.
  3. Stage 2 depth from day one: since CBT 2 decides merit, prepare at Stage 2 difficulty while writing Stage 1 mocks.
  4. Skill prep early: if applying for clerk-typist or station master posts, practise typing or aptitude drills alongside theory.
  5. Documents ready before DV: keep degree, category and ID proofs in one folder so verification is stress-free.

How to Apply for RRB NTPC 2026

1
Read the detailed notification — confirm your post-wise eligibility, age limit and fee for CEN 06/2026 on an official regional RRB website (reached via indianrailways.gov.in or rrbcdg.gov.in).
2
Register online — create your account with a valid email ID and mobile number, and choose only one RRB.
3
Fill the form carefully — enter personal, educational and preference details; upload your photograph and signature in the prescribed size and format.
4
Pay the fee and submit — pay online before 6 November 2026 (11:59 pm); save and print the confirmation page.
5
Track admit card and exam dates — the admit card appears on the regional RRB site roughly 10 days before the CBT; keep checking the official portal.

FAQs

How many posts are there in RRB NTPC 2026?
The indicative notice for CEN 06/2026 announces 3,477 Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate) posts. Check the detailed notification for the post-wise breakup.
What is the last date to apply for RRB NTPC 2026?
Online applications for CEN 06/2026 close on 6 November 2026 at 11:59 pm.
What is the exam pattern of CBT 1 and CBT 2?
CBT 1: 100 questions/100 marks/90 minutes. CBT 2: 120 questions/120 marks/90 minutes. Both test maths, reasoning and general awareness with 1/3 negative marking per wrong answer.
Is there a skill test after the CBTs?
Yes, for applicable posts — CBAT for Station Master and a typing test for posts like Senior Clerk-cum-Typist, followed by document verification and a medical exam.
Can I apply to more than one RRB for NTPC 2026?
No. Apply to only one RRB per centralised notification; multiple applications are rejected.

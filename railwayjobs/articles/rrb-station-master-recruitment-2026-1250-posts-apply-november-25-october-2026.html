The Railway Recruitment Boards have released the indicative short notice for Station Master Recruitment 2026 (CEN No. 08/2026) — about 1,250 vacancies across all 21 RRB zones, in the post's first standalone CEN after its move out of NTPC (Railway Board, 7 Oct 2026). The notice appeared on rrb.indianrailways.gov.in on 21 October 2026; the detailed CEN is expected 26 October 2026, and the online window is tentatively 27 October–25 November 2026 at rrbapply.gov.in.

The Station Master post is among the most sought-after Group-C positions — Level-6 pay (basic about Rs 35,400 + allowances), station-level operational charge, and a strong promotion ladder. Treat all dates and the vacancy count as tentative until the detailed CEN; verify eligibility, fee and medical standards in that document before applying.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Education: Graduation (any discipline) from a recognized university — the established qualification for direct Station Master recruitment; the detailed CEN confirms any specific degree requirements.
  • Age: 20–30 years as on 1 January 2027 (revised minimum age 20, per the 7 October 2026 guidelines). Standard SC/ST/OBC/PwBD relaxations.
  • Medical: A-2 standard; check colour-vision and vision norms in the detailed CEN.
  • Other conditions: As prescribed in the detailed CEN; Aadhaar-based verification instructions were part of the indicative notice.

Selection Process (New Pattern)

1
Computer-Based Test (CBT) — single-stage written exam (reported 100 marks, 120 minutes); 70% weightage in final merit.
2
Computer-Based Aptitude Test (CBAT) — aptitude battery for operational posts; 30% weightage in final merit.
3
Document Verification — original certificates, category certificates and Aadhaar verification.
4
Medical Examination — A-2 standard fitness before empanelment.

The CBT is expected around January 2027 (tentative). Portals report a syllabus leaning towards Science, Mathematics, Data Analysis & Interpretation and Reasoning — treat section-wise marks as unconfirmed until the detailed CEN.

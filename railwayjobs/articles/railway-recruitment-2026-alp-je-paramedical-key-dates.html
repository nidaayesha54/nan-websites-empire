Urgent: RRB Paramedical Closes 14 October 2026

If you hold a paramedical qualification and want a railway job, this is your window — the RRB Paramedical Recruitment 2026 closes on 14 October 2026, just one day from today. Railway hospitals and health units recruit staff nurses, pharmacists, lab technicians and other paramedical roles, and these notifications draw far fewer applicants than NTPC or Group D, which improves genuine candidates' odds.

Do not wait for the last evening: servers slow down, payment gateways fail, and document-upload errors spike on closing day. Read the detailed notification on the official RRB portal, confirm your category-wise eligibility, and submit your form well before the deadline.

RRB NTPC (Graduate): 3,477 Posts, Apply Till 6 November

The biggest graduate-level railway recruitment of the year is the RRB NTPC Graduate Recruitment 2026 under CEN 06/2026 — 3,477 Non-Technical Popular Categories (Graduate) posts for station masters, goods guards, senior clerks and commercial-cum-ticket clerks. The indicative notice is out, and online applications run 8 October 2026 to 6 November 2026 (11:59 pm).

Selection runs through CBT Stage 1, CBT Stage 2, then CBAT or a typing skill test for applicable posts, followed by document verification and a medical examination. Our full walkthrough — eligibility notes, the standard CBT 1 and CBT 2 patterns, and stage-by-stage strategy — is in the RRB NTPC 2026 complete guide.

Assistant Loco Pilot (CEN 01/2026), JE, Technician & Group D

The RRB employment-notice portal also carries the year's earlier centralised notices: CEN 01/2026 for Assistant Loco Pilot, alongside the NTPC, Group D/Level-1, Junior Engineer, Technician and Paramedical category recruitments. Each notice is independent — different eligibility, different timelines — so candidates interested in technical or engineering posts should track their own notice on the regional RRB websites and never mix up deadlines across notifications.

Remember the one-RRB rule: per centralised notification, you apply to only one Railway Recruitment Board. Multiple applications get rejected outright.

How to Track Railway Notices Without Missing a Deadline

1
Bookmark the official portals — regional RRB websites (reached via indianrailways.gov.in and rrbcdg.gov.in) are the only authentic source of notices, dates and corrigenda.
2
Read the detailed notification, not just headlines — eligibility, age relaxation, fee and medical standards live in the full PDF; the indicative notice is only a summary.
3
Register early in the window — create your account on day one, fill the form calmly, and submit before the final 48 hours to dodge server congestion.
4
Keep one email + mobile for everything — all RRB communication, admit cards and result alerts flow to the registered contacts.
5
Never pay any agent — applications are online-only through official portals; anyone demanding money for a railway job is a fraud.

IRCTC East Zone Apprentice Recruitment 2026: 44 Graduate & COPA Seats — Apply by 26 October

The Indian Railway Catering and Tourism Corporation Limited (IRCTC), East Zone, Kolkata has invited online applications for 44 apprentice seats under Notification No. IRCTC/EZ/HRD/Apprentices/2026-27, dated 9 October 2026. Apply online from 9 October to 26 October 2026 on apprenticeshipindia.gov.in or nats.education.gov.in. The seats split into 34 Graduate Apprentice posts (any degree, 50% aggregate) and 10 COPA posts (Matriculation plus ITI in Computer Operator and Programming Assistant, 50% aggregate). Selection is merit-based — no written test, no viva — and there is no application fee.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Graduate Apprentice: a degree in any discipline with at least 50% aggregate marks from a recognized university. COPA: Matriculation (Class 10) plus an ITI certificate in Computer Operator and Programming Assistant with at least 50% aggregate marks. The qualification must be held as on 9 October 2026 — candidates awaiting their result on that date are not eligible.

Age Limit

You must have completed 15 years and not completed 25 years of age as on 9 October 2026. Relaxations as notified: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, 10 years for ex-servicemen and 10 years for PwBD candidates.

Stipend

CategoryMonthly Stipend

Selection Process — Merit Based, No Exam

No written examination and no viva-voce. The panel is prepared on the percentage of marks in the qualifying examination, followed by document verification:

1
Merit List — percentage of marks in the qualifying exam (tie-breaker rules as per the notification).
2
Document Verification — original certificates, marksheets, caste/category certificates and age proof.
3
Engagement — eligible candidates join the 12-month training at the allotted centre.
Training locations: Kolkata, Guwahati, Patna, Howrah, Sealdah, Asansol, New Jalpaiguri, Ranchi or Tatanagar — candidates must be willing to train at any of them.
Important: this is a training engagement under the Apprentices Act — apprentices are released after 12 months with no claim to regular employment in IRCTC.

How to Apply Online

1
Register — create your profile on apprenticeshipindia.gov.in or nats.education.gov.in with an active mobile number and email.
2
Find the opportunity — search the IRCTC East Zone establishment and the openings under Notification No. IRCTC/EZ/HRD/Apprentices/2026-27.
3
Fill and upload — enter your details and marks, then upload photograph, signature and certificates in the prescribed format.
4
Submit before 26 October 2026 — review every field, submit, and save the confirmation. No physical application is needed.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for IRCTC East Zone Apprentice 2026?
26 October 2026. The window runs 9–26 October 2026 via apprenticeshipindia.gov.in or nats.education.gov.in (Notification No. IRCTC/EZ/HRD/Apprentices/2026-27).
How many seats are available in IRCTC East Zone Apprentice 2026?
44 seats — 34 Graduate Apprentice (any degree, 50% aggregate) and 10 COPA (Matriculation + ITI in COPA, 50% aggregate). Seats may increase or decrease.
What is the stipend for IRCTC East Zone apprentices?
Rs 12,300 per month for Graduate Apprentices and Rs 9,600 per month for COPA apprentices, for 12 months. No application fee for any category.
Is there a written exam for IRCTC East Zone Apprentice 2026?
No. Selection is through a merit list on qualifying-exam percentage — no written test, no viva — followed by document verification.
Will IRCTC apprentices get a permanent job after training?
No. It is training under the Apprentices Act; apprentices are released after 12 months with no claim to regular IRCTC employment.

Kochi Metro Apprentice 2026: 70 Posts, No Fee/Exam — Apply by 10 Nov

Key Details

Vacancies70 (Graduate Technical 16 | Graduate Non-Technical 6 | Diploma Technician 28 | ITI Trade 20)
OrganizationKochi Metro Rail Limited (KMRL), GoI–Kerala joint venture
LocationKochi, Kerala
QualificationB.Tech/B.E., 3-yr diploma, BBA/B.Com/BCA or ITI (full-time regular, passed within 5 years)
Age LimitMinimum 18 years; no prior apprenticeship training
Stipend₹12,300 (graduate) / ₹10,900 (diploma) / ₹9,600 (ITI) + ₹1,200 food allowance
Last Date10 November 2026
Apply Linkkochimetro.org
Source / Ad DateKMRL/Apprenticeship/2026-27/HR-Admin/01; window 15 Oct–10 Nov 2026

Kochi Metro Rail Limited (KMRL) is hiring 70 apprentices for the 2026–27 session — and this one is refreshingly simple: no application fee, no written exam, no interview. Selection is on shortlisting and certificate verification alone, with applications closing on 10 November 2026.

The seats span engineers (B.Tech/B.E.), diploma holders, non-technical graduates (BBA/B.Com/BCA) and ITI trade candidates, making it one of the broadest metro apprenticeship calls of the season. Training is for one year at KMRL's Kochi facilities, with monthly stipends from ₹9,600 to ₹12,300 plus a ₹1,200 food allowance.

Category-wise Breakup (70 Posts)

Graduate Technical (B.Tech / B.E.)16
Graduate Non-Technical (BBA / B.Com / BCA)6
Diploma Technician (3-yr diploma)28
ITI Trade Apprentice (electrician, electronics, welder, fitter, plumber)20
Total70

Reservation applies as per central norms across the categories — check the notification PDF on kochimetro.org for the category-wise reservation table.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Graduate Technical: full-time regular B.Tech/B.E. in a relevant discipline.
  • Graduate Non-Technical: full-time regular BBA/B.Com/BCA.
  • Diploma Technician: full-time regular 3-year diploma in a relevant branch.
  • ITI Trade: ITI certificate in electrician, electronics, welder, fitter or plumber trades.
  • The qualifying exam must have been passed within 5 years of the closing date, and candidates must have no prior apprenticeship training under the Apprentices Act. Minimum age 18 years.

Selection Process

1
Shortlisting — on marks in the qualifying examination; no CBT, no interview.
2
Certificate verification — shortlisted candidates produce originals at KMRL.
3
Engagement — one-year apprenticeship training with stipend as per the table above.

Note the fine print: there is no job guarantee after training ends. This is a skill-building engagement under the Apprentices Act, not a path to KMRL employment.

Stipend Details

Graduate Apprentice₹12,300 per month + ₹1,200 food allowance
Diploma Technician Apprentice₹10,900 per month + ₹1,200 food allowance
ITI Trade Apprentice₹9,600 per month + ₹1,200 food allowance

How to Apply

1
Register on the apprentice portal — NAPS (ITI trades) or NATS (graduate/diploma) — and complete your profile with the qualifying details.
2
Download the prescribed application form from the KMRL notification on kochimetro.org and fill it carefully.
3
Submit the form with documents to the KMRL Corporate Office (JLN Stadium Metro Station, Kaloor, Kochi) by 10 November 2026.
4
Watch for the shortlist — merit lists are published on kochimetro.org; keep your phone and email active.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many apprentice posts is Kochi Metro offering in 2026?
70 posts in total — 16 graduate technical, 6 graduate non-technical, 28 diploma technician and 20 ITI trade apprentice slots.
Is there any application fee for KMRL Apprentice 2026?
No. There is no application fee, no written exam and no interview — selection is on shortlisting plus certificate verification.
What is the last date to apply?
10 November 2026. Applications go through the NAPS/NATS portal plus a prescribed form submitted to KMRL's Kochi office.
What stipend do KMRL apprentices get?
Rs. 12,300 for graduates, Rs. 10,900 for diploma holders and Rs. 9,600 for ITI trades per month, plus a Rs. 1,200 food allowance.
Does the apprenticeship guarantee a KMRL job?
No. It is a one-year training engagement under the Apprentices Act with no promise of employment afterwards.
What are the eligibility conditions?
A full-time regular B.Tech/B.E., 3-year diploma, BBA/B.Com/BCA or ITI certificate passed within 5 years, minimum age 18, and no prior apprenticeship training.

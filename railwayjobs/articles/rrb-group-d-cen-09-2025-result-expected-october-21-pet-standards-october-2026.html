RRB Group D Result 2026 Expected by 21 Oct: CEN 09/2025 — How to Check + PET Standards

The Railway Recruitment Boards are expected to declare the RRB Group D Result 2026 (CEN 09/2025) on or before 21 October 2026 on the official portal rrb.indianrailways.gov.in. More than 52,39,760 candidates appeared in the Computer Based Test held from 3 to 25 August 2026, and with the answer key and response sheets already released, the result is the final missing piece of the CBT stage. Along with the result PDF, the boards will publish the zone-wise cutoff marks and activate the scorecard download link — and CBT qualifiers will move straight to the Physical Efficiency Test (PET).

Because the result window is now a matter of days, this is the right time to know exactly how to check your status, what the scorecard will show, and what the PET demands — so there is no scrambling after the PDF goes live. Bookmark your regional RRB website now and keep your registration number and password ready.

How to Check the RRB Group D Result 2026

1
Open your regional RRB website — results are published zone-wise; use the RRB region you applied from.
2
Find the CEN 09/2025 result link — it will be labelled as the Group D CBT result / merit list PDF.
3
Download the merit list PDF — search your roll number with Ctrl+F to confirm qualification.
4
Download your scorecard — log in with your registration number and password once the scorecard link is active.
5
Check the zone-wise cutoff — compare your normalized score against your category cutoff for your zone.

PET Physical Standards: What CBT Qualifiers Must Clear

Qualifying the CBT only gets you to the Physical Efficiency Test — and the PET is qualifying in nature, meaning you must pass it to stay in the race. The standard RRB Group D PET tasks are:

CandidateTask 1Task 2
MaleRun 1000 metres in 4 minutes 15 secondsLift and carry 35 kg for 100 metres in 2 minutes
FemaleRun 1000 metres in 5 minutes 40 secondsLift and carry 20 kg for 100 metres in 2 minutes

Both tasks must be completed — failing either one disqualifies you regardless of your CBT score. With the result expected within days, candidates should already be training: daily timed runs for the 1000m benchmark and progressive weight-carry practice for the lift task. PET is followed by document verification and medical examination before the final panel.

Tip: Do not wait for the result to start PET training. The gap between the CBT result and the PET schedule is usually short — fitness built now is fitness that counts.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the RRB Group D CEN 09/2025 result be declared?

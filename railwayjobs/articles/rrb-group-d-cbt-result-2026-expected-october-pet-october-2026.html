The Railway Recruitment Boards conducted the RRB Group D 2026 Computer-Based Test from 3 to 25 August 2026 for 22,195 Level-1 posts under CEN 09/2025, one of the largest railway recruitments in recent years. The provisional answer key was released on 7 September 2026 with an objection window till 15 September. RRB has not announced an official result date, but reports indicate the CBT result is expected in the 4th week of October 2026, released zone-wise on the respective regional RRB websites. Roughly 52 lakh candidates appeared in the CBT, making the upcoming merit lists among the most watched railway announcements of the year.

How to Check the RRB Group D Result 2026

The result will be published as a zone-wise merit list PDF on each regional RRB website. Follow these steps once it is declared:

1
Visit your regional RRB website — results are released zone-wise, so use the RRB for the zone you applied to.
2
Open the CEN 09/2025 Level-1 result link — look for "RRB Group D Result 2026" in the latest notices section.
3
Download the merit list PDF — it contains the roll numbers of candidates shortlisted for the PET.
4
Search your roll number — press Ctrl+F and enter your roll or registration number.
5
Download your scorecard — log in with your registration details to view your normalized marks, raw scores and qualifying status.

What Comes After the CBT Result: PET and Beyond

Candidates shortlisted in the CBT merit list move to the Physical Efficiency Test (PET) — the second stage of the selection process. The PET is qualifying in nature: performance is pass/fail and does not add to the final merit, which is based on the CBT normalized scores. Shortlisted candidates will be called for the PET as per the zone-wise schedule announced with or after the result.

After the PET, the process continues with document verification — Class 10/ITI/NAC certificates, age proof, caste certificate if applicable and photo ID — followed by the medical examination as prescribed for Level-1 railway posts. The eligibility baseline for Group D is 10th pass, ITI or NAC (NCVT), with the age limit 18–33 years as on 1 January 2026 (relaxation for OBC, SC/ST, PwBD and ex-servicemen as per rules).

RRB NTPC UG 2026: 1,688 Clerk Posts for 12th Pass — Apply by 13 Nov

Key Details

Vacancies1,688 (Commercial cum Ticket Clerk 700 | Accounts Clerk cum Typist 200 | Junior Clerk cum Typist 600 | Trains Clerk 188)
OrganizationRailway Recruitment Boards (RRBs), Ministry of Railways
LocationAll India — all RRB zones
QualificationClass 12 pass (12th) from a recognized board
Age Limit18–30 years as on 1 January 2027 (relaxations as per rules)
SalaryPay Level 2–3 under 7th CPC plus allowances
Last DateApply online by 13 November 2026 (11:59 pm)
Apply Linkrrbapply.gov.in
Source / Ad DateCEN 07/2026 notification; applications 15 Oct–13 Nov 2026

The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the NTPC Undergraduate (CEN 07/2026) notification for 1,688 posts reserved for Class 12 pass candidates. Online applications opened on 15 October 2026 and close on 13 November 2026 (11:59 pm), with fee payment allowed till 15 November and a correction window from 16 to 25 November 2026.

This is the second big NTPC wave of 2026 and it sits alongside the graduate-level NTPC (CEN 06/2026) recruitment — but it is a separate CEN with its own notification, targeting 12th-pass candidates for clerk and ticket-checking posts at Pay Level 2–3. If you cleared Class 12 and want a central government job without a degree, this is the window to watch.

Vacancies are distributed across RRB zones with community-wise reservation. You can apply to only one RRB in this cycle — check the zone-wise table in the CEN 07/2026 PDF before choosing.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Class 12 / Intermediate pass from a recognized board. The 50% aggregate condition does not apply to SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen candidates or to candidates with higher qualifications, per the notification.
  • For Accounts Clerk cum Typist and Junior Clerk cum Typist, typing proficiency (English/Hindi) is tested in the skill stage — practice before CBT-2.

Age Limit (as on 1 January 2027)

  • Minimum: 18 years; Maximum: 30 years
  • Relaxations: SC/ST 5 years, OBC 3 years, PwBD 10 years (plus community relaxation), Ex-Servicemen and others as per government rules. Other allowances follow the standard RRB matrix.

Application Fee

General / EWS / OBC₹500 (₹400 refunded after appearing in CBT-1)
SC / ST / ExSM / PwBD / Women / Transgender / Minorities / EBC₹250 (fully refunded after appearing in CBT-1)

Selection Process

1
CBT-1 (first stage) — 100 questions (Maths 30, Reasoning 30, GA 40), 90 minutes, 1/3 negative marking.
2
CBT-2 (second stage) — 120 questions at a higher standard; shortlisting from CBT-1.
3
Typing skill test (clerk-cum-typist posts only) — qualifying in nature.
4
Document verification and medical examination — final merit on CBT-2 performance.

How to Apply

1
Go to rrbapply.gov.in — the single RRB application portal — and pick one RRB zone for CEN 07/2026.
2
Register with a valid email and mobile number, then fill the form: post preferences, category, and education details.
3
Upload a live photograph, signature and Class 12 certificate in the prescribed sizes.
4
Pay the fee online by 15 November and submit before 13 November 2026, 11:59 pm. Save the printout and application number.
Honesty flag: this is CEN 07/2026 (Undergraduate, 12th pass) — do not confuse it with the graduate-level NTPC CEN 06/2026 wave. Note a minor count conflict on the graduate wave: our earlier coverage listed ~1,250 posts, while current press reporting for CEN 06/2026 mentions 3,548; treat the official CEN PDFs as the final authority. Also verify the zone-wise vacancy table and the 50%-aggregate exemption clauses in the official notification before choosing your RRB.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is RRB NTPC UG CEN 07/2026?
It is the Railway Recruitment Boards' undergraduate-level NTPC recruitment for 1,688 clerk and ticket posts open to Class 12 pass candidates, with applications from 15 October to 13 November 2026.
What is the qualification for RRB NTPC UG 2026?
Class 12 / Intermediate pass from a recognized board. The 50% aggregate rule is waived for SC/ST/PwBD/Ex-Servicemen and candidates with higher qualifications.
What is the age limit for RRB NTPC UG 2026?
18 to 30 years as on 1 January 2027, with standard relaxations for SC/ST, OBC, PwBD and Ex-Servicemen.
What is the application fee for RRB NTPC UG 2026?
Rs. 500 for General/EWS/OBC and Rs. 250 for reserved categories and women. Most of it is refunded after you appear in CBT-1.
What is the selection process?
CBT-1, then CBT-2, then a typing skill test for clerk-cum-typist posts (qualifying), followed by document verification and medical examination.
How do I apply for RRB NTPC UG 2026?
Apply online on rrbapply.gov.in between 15 October and 13 November 2026 (11:59 pm). You may apply to only one RRB zone in this cycle.

RVNL GM/AGM S&T 1 Post — Apply by 12 Nov 2026

Rail Vikas Nigam Limited (RVNL), a Ministry of Railways public sector undertaking, has invited applications for 1 post of General Manager / Additional General Manager (S&T) on a deputation basis, with the place of posting at Kolkata. Eligible serving IRSSE and S&T Department officers must apply through the proper channel within the deadline.

Applications are submitted on the prescribed proforma, with a scanned PDF advance copy sent by email; the controlling officer/zonal railway must forward the application with vigilance/DAR clearance and the last 5 years' APAR grading within 15 days of the closing date.

Eligibility Criteria

For General Manager (GM)

  • Officers holding substantive posts in analogous grade (CDA/IDA) OR
  • Posts in Level-13 (CDA) with 17 years of Group ‘A’ service OR
  • Posts carrying IDA E-7 (₹1,00,000–2,60,000) for 4 years
  • Age: below 58 years as on the closing date

For Additional General Manager (AGM)

  • Officers holding substantive posts in analogous grade (CDA/IDA) OR
  • Level-13 (CDA) with 12–14 years of Group ‘A’ service OR
  • IDA E-6 (₹90,000–2,40,000) for 4 years
  • Age: below 56 years as on the closing date

Preference is given to candidates with experience of working in a construction organisation. There is no application fee mentioned in the notice.

Selection and Terms

Selection is on deputation basis, evaluating service records, vigilance clearance and APAR gradings. The deputation term is five years, subject to the government's approval for exemption from immediate absorption. The selected officer draws parent pay plus deputation allowance.

How to Apply

1
Download the prescribed proforma from the vacancy notice on rvnl.org (dated 14 October 2026).
2
Fill and sign the form, route it through your controlling officer with vigilance/DAR clearance and 5 years of APAR gradings.
3
Email a scanned PDF of the duly filled and signed proforma as an advance copy — before the closing date.
4
Ensure the forwarded application reaches RVNL within 15 days after the closing date.

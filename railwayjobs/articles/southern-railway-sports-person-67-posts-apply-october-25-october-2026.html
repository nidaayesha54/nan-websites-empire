The Railway Recruitment Cell (RRC), Southern Railway, Chennai is recruiting 67 Sports Persons against sports quota under Advertisement No. RRC-02/Sports/2026-27. Online applications are open from 26 September 2026 and close on 25 October 2026, 11:59 PM. These are permanent government jobs at Southern Railway, filled through sports trials and achievement assessment — not through a written CBT.

Posts are offered across Levels 1 to 5 of the pay matrix, with the required education level rising with the level: graduation for Levels 5/4, 12th pass or 10th+ITI for Levels 3/2, and 10th pass or ITI for Level 1.

Vacancy Breakup (67 Posts)

Sports Person — Level 5 / Level 45 (Graduation)
Sports Person — Level 3 / Level 216 (12th pass or 10th pass with ITI/apprenticeship)
Sports Person — Level 146 (10th pass or ITI)
Total67

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification (level-wise)

  • Level 5 / 4: graduation from a recognized university
  • Level 3 / 2: 12th pass OR 10th pass with ITI/apprenticeship
  • Level 1: 10th pass or ITI

Sports Achievement

Candidates must meet the prescribed sports norms for their level — representing the country, state or university in recognized championships, with medals/positions as specified in the advertisement. Sports trials and sports-achievement assessment carry the deciding weight in this recruitment.

Age Limit

18 to 25 years as on 1 January 2027, with relaxations for reserved categories as per railway rules.

Application Fee

UR / General / OBC / EWS₹500
SC / ST / Female / ESM / PwD / Minorities / EBC₹250

Selection Process

1
Sports trials — assessment of playing ability in the applied discipline.
2
Sports-achievement assessment — weightage for medals, participation and level of championship.
3
Educational qualification check, document verification and medical examination — as per railway recruitment norms.

How to Apply Online

1
Open sr.indianrailways.gov.in (RRC Chennai) and find Advt. RRC-02/Sports/2026-27.
2
Read the sports norms carefully — confirm your discipline, level and achievement grade match the level you are applying for.
3
Fill the online form — personal, educational and sports-achievement details with certificates.
4
Pay the fee and submit before 25 October 2026, 11:59 PM — save the confirmation for the trials stage.

RRC NWR Act Apprentice 2026: 2,008 Slots — Apply by 6 November, 10th+ITI Eligibility, Merit Rules

The Railway Recruitment Cell, North Western Railway (RRC-NWR), Jaipur has opened online applications for the engagement of 2,008 Act Apprentices under Notice No. 04/2026 (NWR/AA). The window runs from 7 October to 6 November 2026 (11:59 PM) on rrcjaipur.in — and selection is merit-based with no written examination, decided on your Class 10 and ITI marks.

Note: these are training slots under the Apprentices Act, 1961, not permanent railway posts. Apprenticeship provides practical training in railway workshops across the zone's divisions — Ajmer, Bikaner, Jaipur and Jodhpur.

The merit panel is prepared unit-wise, trade-wise and community-wise — there is no single zone-level merit list, so your competition is limited to candidates who chose the same unit, trade and community.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Passed Class 10 (Matriculation) with at least 50% aggregate marks, plus a National Trade Certificate (NTC) in the notified trade from NCVT or SCVT. You must hold the qualification by the closing date — candidates awaiting their final ITI result are not eligible.

Age Limit

15 to 24 years as on 6 October 2026, with the standard relaxations: 5 years for SC/ST, 3 years for OBC, 10 years for PwBD, and the prescribed relaxation for ex-servicemen. Verify your date of birth against the cut-off before applying.

Selection Process — Merit Based, No Exam

There is no written examination and no interview. Selection runs on a transparent merit formula:

1
Merit List — prepared from the candidate's Matriculation percentage and ITI marks, unit-wise, trade-wise and community-wise. If two candidates tie on marks, the older candidate gets preference; if dates of birth also match, the one who passed Matriculation earlier is placed first.
2
Document Verification — candidates in the merit panel produce original certificates and documents.
3
Medical Examination — fitness in the prescribed format, with the medical certificate signed by a government-authorized doctor of the required rank.

How to Apply Online

1
Visit rrcjaipur.in — open the RRC North Western Railway website and find the "Engagement of Act Apprentices — Notice No. 04/2026 (NWR/AA)" link.
2
Register — with your 12-digit Aadhaar number, an active mobile number and email ID; note your credentials carefully.
3
Fill the form — personal particulars, Class 10 marks, ITI trade and percentage; select only one division/unit with a slot for your ITI trade.
4
Upload and pay — recent colour photograph and signature in the prescribed size and format; pay Rs. 100 online if applicable (no editing is permitted after fee payment).
5
Submit by 6 November 2026 (11:59 PM) — download the application printout and e-receipt; no physical copy needs to be sent to RRC.
Strategy tip: Because merit is unit-wise and trade-wise, compare the trade-wise slot counts across units in the detailed notification before choosing your unit. Submit only one application — multiple applications lead to rejection.

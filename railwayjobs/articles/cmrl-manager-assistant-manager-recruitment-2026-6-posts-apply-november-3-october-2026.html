Chennai Metro Rail Limited (CMRL) has invited online applications for 6 Manager and Assistant Manager posts on contract/deputation, under Employment Notification No. CMRL-HR-CON-DEP-06-2026 dated 14 October 2026: General Manager (Signal & Telecom), Additional General Manager (Safety – Operations & Maintenance), Managers in Telecom, Electrical Traction and Electrical & Mechanical, and an Assistant Manager (Public Relations).

Applications are online only at careers.chennaimetrorail.org and close on 3 November 2026. Posting is at Chennai; selection is an interview plus medical — no written test, no TA/DA. A short window, so prepare documents now.

Engagement and Eligibility

These are contract-basis appointments with a deputation route also available. Government/PSU applicants through the proper channel with at least 2 years in a regular pay scale may be considered for IDA pay on contract upon selection. Qualifications are post-specific: engineering degrees/diplomas in the relevant discipline (Signal & Telecom, Electrical, Safety) with post-qualification experience for technical roles, and a communications background for the AM (Public Relations) post. Age, qualification and experience are reckoned as on 14 October 2026. One outlet mentions a small NEFT/UPI fee for contract-type appointments — unconfirmed; check the official notification before transacting.

Selection Process

1
Shortlisting — minimum eligibility does not guarantee an interview; only shortlisted applicants are notified.
2
Interview — knowledge, skills, comprehension, attitude, aptitude and fitness. No TA/DA is paid.
3
Medical examination — provisionally selected candidates must pass the prescribed medical standard.

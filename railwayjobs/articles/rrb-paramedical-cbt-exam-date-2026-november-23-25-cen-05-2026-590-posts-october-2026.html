The Railway Recruitment Boards (RRBs) have released the official tentative examination schedule for the RRB Paramedical CBT under CEN 05/2026. As per the notice issued on 18 October 2026 on the regional RRB websites, the Computer Based Test for 590 paramedical vacancies will be held on 23 and 25 November 2026. Candidates who applied during the September–October window should now shift into final revision mode.

The 590 posts span Nursing Superintendent, Pharmacist, Health & Malaria Inspector Grade II, Lab Assistant Grade II, Radiographer/X-ray Technician, ECG Technician, Dialysis Technician, Optometrist and Audiologist & Speech Therapist — railway-hospital jobs that combine a central-government career with a clinical role. Here are the dates, the exam pattern and the admit-card timeline.

The dates are officially described as tentative — keep checking your regional RRB website for any revision as November approaches.

RRB Paramedical CBT 2026 — Exam Pattern

ComponentDetails
Duration90 minutes
Total Questions100 multiple-choice questions
SectionsProfessional Ability, General Science, General Arithmetic, General Intelligence & Reasoning, General Awareness
Marking+1 for each correct answer
Negative Marking−0.25 for each wrong answer

Professional Ability carries the heaviest subject weight — it tests the core knowledge of your paramedical discipline (nursing, pharmacy, lab technology and so on). The remaining sections are the standard RRB aptitude mix. With negative marking in play, accuracy matters more than attempts.

Admit Card and Exam City — Timeline

1
City intimation slip — 13 November — about 10 days before the exam, the link to check your exam city, date and shift goes live on regional RRB websites; SC/ST candidates also get travel-authority details.
2
Admit card — about 19 November — the e-call letter downloads roughly 4 days before your exam date; you need your registration number and date of birth to log in.
3
Verify every detail — check your name, photograph, exam date, shift timing, reporting time and venue address the moment you download it — report errors to the RRB immediately.
4
Aadhaar biometric at the gate — Aadhaar-linked biometric authentication will be done before entry; carry your original Aadhaar card or a printout of e-verified Aadhaar.

After the CBT — What Comes Next

Selection under CEN 05/2026 runs through the CBT, Document Verification and Medical Examination. There is no second written stage — your CBT score is the merit score. Shortlisted candidates verify original certificates (qualification, registration with the relevant council where applicable, caste and age proof) and then clear the railway medical fitness standards for the post.

Application window status: online applications for CEN 05/2026 ran 15 September–14 October 2026 and are now closed. A modification window with fee runs 17–26 October 2026 — if you need to correct your form, that window is open right now.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the RRB Paramedical CBT exam 2026?
23 and 25 November 2026, as per the RRB notice dated 18 October 2026 (CEN 05/2026). The dates are tentative.
How many vacancies are in RRB Paramedical CEN 05/2026?
590 vacancies across Nursing Superintendent, Pharmacist, Health & Malaria Inspector, Lab Assistant, Radiographer and other paramedical posts.
When will the RRB Paramedical admit card 2026 be released?
About 4 days before the exam — expected around 19 November 2026. The city intimation slip comes around 13 November.
What is the RRB Paramedical exam pattern?
90-minute CBT with 100 MCQs from Professional Ability, General Science, General Arithmetic, General Intelligence & Reasoning and General Awareness; +1 / −0.25 marking.
Is the RRB Paramedical application window still open?
No — applications closed on 14 October 2026. A paid modification window runs 17–26 October 2026.
Do I need Aadhaar for the RRB Paramedical exam?
Yes — Aadhaar-linked biometric authentication happens at the exam centre; carry your original Aadhaar or e-verified Aadhaar printout.

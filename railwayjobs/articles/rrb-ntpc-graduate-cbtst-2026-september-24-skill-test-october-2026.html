The Railway Recruitment Boards have issued the notice for the Computer Based Typing Skill Test (CBTST) for shortlisted CEN 06/2025 NTPC Graduate candidates. The 2nd stage computer-based test (CBT-II) was held on 10 July 2026, and RRBs published the CBT-II result on 27 August 2026. In the notice dated 21 September 2026, the Boards announced that the CBTST for candidates shortlisted in CBT-II is tentatively scheduled for 24 September 2026. The exam city and date link will be available on RRB websites 10 days before the exam, while e-call letters will be available 4 days before the notified exam date.

What Is the NTPC CBTST and Who Appears for It?

The CBTST is the typing skill test stage of the NTPC selection process, applicable to specific NTPC Graduate posts that require typing proficiency as prescribed in the CEN 06/2025 notification. Only candidates shortlisted through the CBT-II result are called for this stage. Posts that do not carry a skill-test requirement skip this stage and move directly to document verification.

The skill test is qualifying in nature — it does not add marks to the final merit, which is drawn from the CBT-II performance along with the other stages as prescribed. Candidates must, however, clear the minimum proficiency standard; failing the skill test means elimination from that post's race regardless of CBT-II marks.

How to Download the CBTST E-Call Letter

1
Visit your regional RRB website — the CBTST links are hosted on the regional RRB for the zone you applied to.
2
Open the city/date intimation link — available about 10 days before the exam to plan travel.
3
Download the e-call letter — released about 4 days before the notified exam date; log in with your registration number and password or date of birth.
4
Verify all details — check your name, exam date, shift, reporting time and test venue carefully on the call letter.
5
Carry the printout — bring the printed e-call letter along with a valid original photo ID to the test centre.

What Comes After the Skill Test

Once the CBTST is complete, shortlisted candidates move to document verification — graduation certificates, age proof, caste certificate if applicable and photo ID — followed by the medical examination for the relevant medical standard of the post. The final merit list for CEN 06/2025 NTPC Graduate posts is expected to follow in the later part of the year; as per the tentative recruitment calendar, the final result is targeted for December 2026. Keep checking your regional RRB website for each stage's schedule.

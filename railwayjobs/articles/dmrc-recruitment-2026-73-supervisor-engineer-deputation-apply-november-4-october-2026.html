DMRC Recruitment 2026: 73 Supervisor & Engineer Posts (Deputation/PRCE) — Apply by 4 November

The Delhi Metro Rail Corporation Limited (DMRC) has announced 73 vacancies for Supervisor, Engineer and Senior Engineer across the Architecture, Civil/Track, Signaling & Telecommunication (S&T) and Environment departments under Advertisement No. DMRC/PERS/22/HR/2026 (235). The posts are on deputation basis and through Post Retirement Contractual Engagement (PRCE): serving officers of Government departments, Railways, PSUs and Metro organisations can join on deputation, while retired officers can join on PRCE. Fresh candidates are NOT eligible. Apply by 4 November 2026 — by Speed Post to the AGM/HR/P at Metro Bhawan, Fire Brigade Lane, Barakhamba Road, New Delhi, or by email to career@dmrc.org (subject "No. DMRC/PERS/22/HR/2026/235"). No application fee; selection is by personal interview.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Only two categories are eligible: (1) serving officers of Government departments, Railways, PSUs and Metro organisations, for deputation; and (2) retired officers from the same sectors, for PRCE. Fresh candidates are not eligible. Post-wise requirements — department background, grade and experience — are specified in the official advertisement and must be matched exactly.

Age Limit

Deputation: maximum 55 years as on 1 October 2026; tenure initially 5 years, extendable up to 7 years. PRCE: 55 to 62 years as on 1 October 2026; engagement initially 1 year, extendable yearly. All age and tenure calculations are anchored to 1 October 2026.

Remuneration (PRCE)

PostingConsolidated Remuneration
Delhi officeRs 56,300–96,600 per month
OutstationRs 61,900–1,06,300 per month

The figure is fixed based on the retired substantive grade. Deputation follows the standard Government deputation rules; PRCE candidates must clear medical fitness before engagement.

Selection Process — Personal Interview

No written examination. DMRC screens applications against the eligibility, grade and experience criteria, then holds interviews:

1
Shortlisting — tentatively in the 2nd week of November 2026.
2
Personal Interview — tentatively in the 3rd week of November 2026.
3
Medical Fitness — required for PRCE candidates before engagement.
4
Final Result — tentatively in the 4th week of November 2026.

How to Apply

1
Use the prescribed format — per Advertisement No. DMRC/PERS/22/HR/2026 (235), with service certificates, vigilance clearance and last pay certificate (PRCE).
2
Speed Post — to AGM/HR/P, DMRC, Metro Bhawan, Fire Brigade Lane, Barakhamba Road, New Delhi; must reach by 4 November 2026.
3
Email — send scanned copies to career@dmrc.org with subject "No. DMRC/PERS/22/HR/2026/235" before the deadline.
4
Keep proof — the postal receipt or email confirmation. No fee is payable.
For serving officers: route the application through your cadre-controlling authority with vigilance clearance — start that paperwork early.

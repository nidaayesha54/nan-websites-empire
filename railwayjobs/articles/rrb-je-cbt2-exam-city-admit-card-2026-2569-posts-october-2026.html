RRB JE CBT-2 Exam City 2026 Out: 2,569 Junior Engineer Posts — Admit Card Steps

The Railway Recruitment Boards have released the RRB JE CBT-2 exam city intimation 2026 for the Junior Engineer recruitment under CEN 05/2025, covering 2,569 posts. Candidates who qualified the CBT-1 can now check their allotted examination city on the regional RRB portals — and the CBT-2 admit card will follow a few days before the examination. If you cleared the first stage, this is your signal to finalize travel plans and shift into full CBT-2 preparation mode.

The Junior Engineer posts span Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, Signal & Telecommunication and related engineering disciplines across railway zones. CBT-2 is the decisive technical paper: unlike the screening-style CBT-1, it tests your engineering discipline in depth, and its marks — not CBT-1's — drive the merit list. Understanding the exam city slip, the admit card timeline and the CBT-2 pattern now will save you from last-minute panic.

How to Check the RRB JE Exam City Intimation Slip

1
Open your regional RRB website — the RRB zone you applied from.
2
Find the JE CBT-2 city intimation link — listed under CEN 05/2025.
3
Log in — enter your registration number and date of birth or password.
4
Check your exam city and date — note the city, and plan travel and accommodation early.
5
Wait for the admit card — the hall ticket with your exact centre and shift is released a few days before the exam.

The city intimation slip is not the admit card — it only tells you the city so you can plan. Entry to the exam hall requires the actual admit card plus a valid photo ID.

RRB JE CBT-2 Exam Pattern

CBT-2 is a 150-mark objective paper with a 120-minute duration, structured as follows:

  • General Awareness: 15 questions — current affairs, railway knowledge and basic science.
  • Physics & Chemistry: 15 questions — up to 10th/12th standard level.
  • Basics of Computers & Applications: 10 questions.
  • Basics of Environment & Pollution Control: 10 questions.
  • Technical Abilities (your discipline): 100 questions — the core engineering paper.

There is negative marking of 1/3 mark per wrong answer. The technical section carries two-thirds of the paper, so discipline-wise depth matters far more than breadth — revise your branch subjects from standard textbooks and previous JE papers rather than spreading thin across general topics.

Tip: CBT-1 marks do not count toward the final merit — CBT-2 does. Treat CBT-2 as a fresh start and prioritize the 100-mark technical section in your remaining study days.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the RRB JE CBT-2 exam city intimation 2026 released?
Yes. The exam city intimation for the JE CBT-2 (CEN 05/2025, 2,569 posts) has been released on the regional RRB portals.
How many posts are in the RRB JE CEN 05/2025 recruitment?
2,569 Junior Engineer posts across Civil, Mechanical, Electrical, Electronics, S&T and allied disciplines.
Is the city intimation slip the same as the admit card?
No. The city slip only shows your exam city for travel planning. The admit card, with centre and shift details, is released a few days before the exam.
What is the RRB JE CBT-2 exam pattern?
150 questions in 120 minutes: 100 technical (discipline-based), plus General Awareness, Physics & Chemistry, Computers and Environment sections, with 1/3 negative marking.
Do CBT-1 marks count in the final RRB JE merit?
No. CBT-1 is only a screening stage; the final merit is based on CBT-2 marks, followed by document verification and medical examination.

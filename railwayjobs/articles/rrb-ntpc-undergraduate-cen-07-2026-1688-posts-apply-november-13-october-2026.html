RRB NTPC Undergraduate Recruitment 2026 (CEN 07/2026): 1,688 Posts — Apply 15 Oct to 13 Nov

The Railway Recruitment Boards have opened online applications for the NTPC Undergraduate (10+2 level) Recruitment 2026 under Centralised Employment Notice (CEN) No. 07/2026. A total of 1,688 tentative vacancies are on offer for 12th-pass candidates across Commercial cum Ticket Clerk, Accounts Clerk cum Typist, Junior Clerk cum Typist and Trains Clerk posts. The application window runs from 15 October to 13 November 2026 (11:59 PM) on the official portal rrbapply.gov.in — and this is the biggest 12th-level railway opening of the year.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

Class 12 (+2 stage) or its equivalent with at least 50% aggregate marks for General, OBC and EWS candidates. The 50% condition is not insisted upon for SC/ST candidates, Persons with Benchmark Disabilities, Ex-Servicemen, and candidates holding qualifications higher than Class 12. You must possess the qualification by the closing date — candidates awaiting their final result are not eligible. Typing proficiency (English 30 wpm or Hindi 25 wpm) applies to the clerk-cum-typist posts at the skill-test stage.

Age Limit

18 to 30 years as on 1 January 2027, with relaxation for reserved categories as per government rules.

Selection Process

1
CBT Stage-I — computer-based screening test for all applicants.
2
CBT Stage-II — for candidates shortlisted from Stage-I.
3
Typing Skill Test — for the clerk-cum-typist posts (qualifying in nature).
4
Document Verification and Medical Examination — final stages before appointment.

How to Apply Online for RRB NTPC UG 2026

1
Visit rrbapply.gov.in — the single official portal for all RRB applications.
2
Register — create your account with a valid mobile number and email ID; keep them active for all future communication.
3
Select CEN 07/2026 — choose the NTPC Undergraduate notification and your preferred RRB and post preferences carefully.
4
Fill the form and upload documents — photograph, signature and certificates in the prescribed format and size.
5
Pay the fee and submit before 13 November 2026 (11:59 PM) — download the confirmation page. Use the correction window (16–25 Nov) only if you made a genuine error.
Important: Choose your RRB and post preferences carefully — they decide your zone of posting and cannot be changed after the correction window closes. Apply early in the window to avoid last-day server congestion.

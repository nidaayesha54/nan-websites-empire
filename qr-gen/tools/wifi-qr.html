How WiFi QR codes work

Your phone's camera reads a specially formatted string (starting with WIFI:T:…) that contains the network name, password and security type. iPhones (iOS 11+) and most Android phones connect automatically after one tap — no Settings app, no typing.

Where to use it

  • Home: frame it in the hallway for visitors.
  • Airbnb / rentals: add it to the welcome book — fewer "what's the WiFi?" messages.
  • Cafés & shops: table tents with your WiFi QR keep customers happy (and logged in longer).
  • Offices: meeting rooms and reception areas.

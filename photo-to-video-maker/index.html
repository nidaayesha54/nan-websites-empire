How to Turn Photos Into a Video in 4 Steps

New to slideshows? This is the whole workflow. Full tutorial in our step-by-step guide.

1️⃣ Pick & order your photos

Choose 40–80 photos for a 3–5 minute slideshow. Tell a story: opening shot, highlights in the middle, emotional closer. Remove near-duplicates.

2️⃣ Set the pacing

3–5 seconds per photo works for most events. Use our planner to match photo count to your target runtime and music length.

3️⃣ Add transitions & music

One consistent transition style (fades or slides) beats random effects. Add royalty-free music and lower photo duration on the beat.

4️⃣ Export & share

Export 1080p MP4. Upload to YouTube, share privately with family, or loop it on a TV at the party.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best free photo to video maker in 2026?

Canva Video, CapCut and Adobe Express lead for most users — free slideshow templates, transitions and music libraries included. See our full ranking.

How do I turn photos into a video for free?

Upload photos to a free slideshow maker, set 3–5 seconds per photo, add transitions and background music, export as MP4. Our step-by-step guide walks through it.

How many photos do I need for a 5-minute slideshow?

About 75 photos at 4 seconds each, plus intro/outro. Use our slideshow planner to calculate exact numbers for any target length.

Can I add music to a photo slideshow for free?

Yes — Canva, CapCut and Adobe Express all include royalty-free music on free plans. Match the track length to your runtime.

How can I tell if a slideshow video was made by AI?

AI-photo-to-video clips can look deceptively real. Check for morphing artifacts and unnatural motion, or run it through the free AI Video Detector.

How to Apply

Five steps to securing an NHS role.

Search jobs.nhs.uk
The official jobs.nhs.uk portal lists most NHS vacancies in England and Wales, with many trusts also recruiting via trac.jobs. Create a profile and set up alerts for your profession.
Check professional registration
Clinical roles require registration with the NMC, HCPC or GMC. International applicants should start registration and English language requirements early — they take months.
Tailor your application
Address the person specification point by point, giving evidence from clinical placements or experience. Use clear, concise language and quantify achievements where possible.
Attend interview and assessment
Expect values-based interviews aligned with the NHS Constitution, plus clinical scenarios or practical assessments for patient-facing roles.
Complete pre-employment checks
Offers are conditional on right-to-work checks, references, occupational health clearance and an enhanced DBS check for patient-facing roles.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for NHS jobs?

Most NHS vacancies in England and Wales are advertised on jobs.nhs.uk (the NHS Jobs service), with many trusts also using the Trac recruitment system at trac.jobs. Create a profile, complete the application form and track your progress online.

What are NHS pay bands?

NHS roles in England follow the Agenda for Change pay framework, from Band 2 (support roles) to Band 9 (senior leadership), with separate pay scales for doctors, dentists and very senior managers.

Do NHS jobs require professional registration?

Clinical roles require registration with the relevant regulator — the NMC for nurses and midwives, the HCPC for allied health professionals, and the GMC for doctors — usually before your start date.

What checks are done before starting an NHS job?

NHS appointments are subject to right-to-work checks, references, an occupational health clearance and a Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check, which is enhanced for patient-facing roles.

Can international applicants apply for NHS jobs?

Yes — the NHS actively recruits internationally. You will need the relevant UK professional registration, a valid visa with right to work in the UK, and to meet English language requirements set by your regulator.

What is the NHS pension like?

The NHS Pension Scheme is one of the most generous in the UK, with the employer contributing over 20% of pensionable pay. Membership is automatic for most NHS employees, with an opt-out option.

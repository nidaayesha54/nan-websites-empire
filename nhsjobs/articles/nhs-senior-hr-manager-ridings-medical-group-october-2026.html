NHS Senior HR Manager (Visa Sponsorship) — Apply by 15 Nov

The Ridings Medical Group is hiring a Senior HR Manager — £57,507–£64,429 pro rata, visa sponsorship eligible, closing 15 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » NHS Jobs UK » Articles

The Ridings Medical Group, a large training GP practice in the East Riding of Yorkshire, is recruiting a Senior HR Manager to lead workforce strategy and operations across its five sites. The post pays £57,507–£64,429 pro rata depending on experience, is permanent and full time, and — unusually for a non-clinical NHS role — has been assessed as eligible for visa sponsorship. Applications close on 15 November 2026.

The practice operates as a single Primary Care Network (River & Wolds PCN), delivering care to more than 48,000 patients with over 200 colleagues across clinical and non-clinical disciplines — salaried GPs, advanced care practitioners, nurses, healthcare assistants, pharmacists, social prescribers, dispensers and care navigators. As Senior HR Manager you will be the trusted adviser to senior leadership, line-managing the Senior HR Advisor and overseeing everything from recruitment and absence management to disciplinaries, TUPE projects and HR systems.

The Role: Senior HR Manager

This is a genuinely senior, hands-on HR leadership post. You will conduct strategic horizon-scanning on statutory and NHS workforce developments, manage complex employee relations and conflict resolution, assess organisational and legal risk, and lead blended clinical and administrative teams. Governance runs through everything: compliance with evolving healthcare frameworks, DBS and professional-registration checks, right-to-work processes and UKVI sponsorship management for staff who need visas.

You will also drive HR projects, lead consultation to meet TUPE regulations during practice mergers or takeovers, maintain the People HR system, and prepare and deliver training to the management team. It is a role that rewards political acumen — knowing when to use formal authority and when to guide through influence — alongside calm, assertive employee-relations judgement.

An Important Warning on AI-Written Applications

The advert carries an unusually direct message: the practice says the vast majority of applications it now receives are written using AI, and applications that clearly make heavy use of AI will not be shortlisted. Write your application in your own words, sharing real experiences and achievements — generic, AI-flavoured text is explicitly a route to rejection here. (Reasonable adjustments: if you need them due to a disability, you may use AI or other tools to help.)

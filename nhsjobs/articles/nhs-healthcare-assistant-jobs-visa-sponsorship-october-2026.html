NHS Healthcare Assistant Jobs with Visa Sponsorship 2026: Band 3 from £25,760 — Health and Care Worker Visa Guide

Healthcare assistants in NHS settings (SOC 6131) remain eligible for the Health and Care Worker visa — Band 3 roles advertised from £25,760 with explicit sponsorship wording. Here is how to find genuine sponsored vacancies.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » NHS Jobs UK » Articles

The NHS continues to advertise Healthcare Assistant (HCA) roles with explicit visa sponsorship wording on jobs.nhs.uk — one of the most realistic routes for overseas candidates in 2026. Advertised Band 3 roles start from around £25,760; the key is the occupation code: HCAs in NHS settings are nursing auxiliaries and assistants (SOC 6131), which remains eligible for the Health and Care Worker visa with genuine sponsorship.

Any job board still advertising “UK care worker visa sponsorship from abroad” is advertising something legally impossible. What Sponsored HCA Adverts Look Like

Genuine sponsored vacancies carry explicit “Certificate of Sponsorship” wording welcoming applicants who need UK sponsorship. When searching jobs.nhs.uk, exclude adverts that are onshore-only or silent on sponsorship — only adverts with clear sponsorship language are worth your effort.

Who Can Apply — Typical Requirements

  • Qualifications: many HCA roles ask for GCSEs (including English) or equivalent, plus the Care Certificate (often completed on the job).
  • Experience: prior care experience helps but is not always essential — customer-facing transferable skills count.
  • Visa & checks: the Health and Care Worker visa requires proof of English language; enhanced DBS checks, occupational health clearance and references are standard.

How to Find and Apply for Sponsored HCA Vacancies

Only apply through official channels. Follow these steps:

1
Search jobs.nhs.uk — the official NHS recruitment site — for Healthcare Assistant roles.
2
Filter or read each advert for explicit Certificate of Sponsorship wording; skip onshore-only or sponsorship-silent adverts.
3
Check the pay band (typically Band 3) and the trust’s location, shift pattern and contract type.
4
Apply on the trust’s recruitment system (NHS Jobs or Trac) with a tailored supporting statement — and never pay anyone for a job offer or sponsorship: genuine NHS recruitment is free.
Scam warning: no legitimate NHS employer charges for recruitment, sponsorship certificates or “guaranteed” offers. Walk away from anyone asking for payment.

Why This Route Matters

For overseas healthcare workers, the HCA sponsorship route is one of the few genuinely open doors in UK immigration — and it leads somewhere: HCAs can progress to senior HCA, assistant practitioner and nursing associate roles, and onto nursing degree apprenticeships. Only target adverts with explicit sponsorship wording, verify everything on jobs.nhs.uk and GOV.UK, and treat any “pay for sponsorship” offer as a scam.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can overseas applicants get NHS Healthcare Assistant jobs with visa sponsorship?

Yes — healthcare assistants in NHS settings (SOC 6131, nursing auxiliaries and assistants) remain eligible for the Health and Care Worker visa with genuine sponsorship. Target adverts on jobs.nhs.uk carrying explicit Certificate of Sponsorship wording.

Is the UK care worker visa route still open?

No — since 22 July 2025 the UK no longer recruits care workers (SOC 6135) or senior care workers (SOC 6136) from overseas. That route remains closed; HCA roles (SOC 6131) are a different, open occupation.

What is the typical pay for NHS Healthcare Assistant roles?

Advertised Band 3 roles start from around £25,760 (figures vary by trust and shift pattern).

Where should I look for sponsored HCA vacancies?

jobs.nhs.uk — the official NHS recruitment site. Filter for adverts with explicit sponsorship wording and skip onshore-only listings.

Do I have to pay for NHS visa sponsorship?

No. Genuine NHS recruitment is free — never pay anyone for a job offer, sponsorship certificate or ‘guaranteed’ placement.

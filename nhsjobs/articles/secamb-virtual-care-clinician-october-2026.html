What Virtual Care Actually Is

As the advert puts it: “The frontline has changed. Clinical expertise is no longer limited by geography, ambulance availability or hospital walls.” Virtual Care Clinicians undertake autonomous virtual clinical assessments for patients accessing 999, NHS 111 and other referral pathways — using clinical judgement, evidence-based practice and decision-support systems to assess and manage urgent and emergency needs remotely. The work includes developing person-centred care plans, delivering self-care advice and safety-netting, making onward referrals, and supporting access to community, primary care, mental health and urgent care services. You also provide clinical leadership and advice to operational and clinical colleagues. It is explicitly “much more than a telephone-based clinical role” — the trust says you will help shape how Virtual Care grows across the service in the years ahead.

Who Should Apply

  • Registered nurses or HCPC-registered paramedics with post-registration experience in frontline services — the advert calls for demonstrable current clinical knowledge.
  • Clinicians who thrive autonomously: virtual assessment means making decisions without a corridor of colleagues — strong listening, communication and decision-making skills are essential.
  • Tech-comfortable practitioners: you’ll work through decision-support systems and digital platforms all shift.
  • Flexible workers: the advert notes rotating shifts including nights and weekends, with full-time and part-time options.

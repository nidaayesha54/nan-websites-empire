NHS Scotland Mental Health Nurse Jobs 2026: Band 5 £34,544–£43,039 + £8,000 Relocation

NHS Scotland is recruiting registered mental health staff nurses across its health boards this October — Band 5 posts paying £34,544–£43,039, a relocation allowance of up to £8,000, and interviews scheduled for 19, 21 and 27 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 17, 2026
Home » NHS Jobs UK » Articles

NHS Scotland is hiring registered mental health staff nurses in October 2026, with live adverts on the official recruitment portal (apply.jobs.scot.nhs.uk). The current wave offers Band 5 pay of £34,544–£43,039 per annum, a relocation allowance of up to £8,000, and interviews on 19, 21 and 27 October 2026 across the boards.

Example adverts show what is on offer: one post is based on an 18-bed adult acute mental health ward (ref KM261137); another covers the Muirton ward at Seafield Hospital, Buckie — an 8-bed unit interviewing on 21 October 2026. Both welcome newly qualified nurses via Flying Start NHS and list a senior charge nurse for informal enquiries.

Pay, Band and Relocation Package

These are Band 5 posts on the NHS Scotland Agenda for Change scale, paying £34,544–£43,039 per annum, with a relocation allowance of up to £8,000 — a strong draw for nurses considering a move to Scotland. Check the individual advert for the relocation package’s exact eligibility terms.

You also get the standard NHS Scotland package: generous annual leave, the NHS pension and funded continuing professional development. Newly qualified nurses get structured mentorship through the Flying Start NHS programme in their first year of practice.

Who Can Apply — Essential Criteria

  • Registered Mental Health Nurse (RMN) with current NMC registration — or due to qualify and register imminently.
  • Newly qualified nurses are welcome — Flying Start NHS support is provided.
  • Right to work in the UK.
  • A completed application form on Jobtrain — no CV is needed or accepted. The application form is what shortlisting is based on, so give every section the time it deserves.
Note: Adverts can close early if application volume is high. Salaries, interview dates and relocation terms are taken from live October 2026 adverts on apply.jobs.scot.nhs.uk — always verify details on the official advert before applying.

How to Apply for NHS Scotland Mental Health Nurse Jobs

Applications run through Jobtrain on the NHS Scotland portal. Follow these steps:

1
On apply.jobs.scot.nhs.uk, search “mental health nurse”, filtering by board or location.
2
Check the advert’s ward, pay band, interview date and relocation terms — e.g. the Muirton ward post at Seafield Hospital, Buckie.
3
Apply via Jobtrain. Remember: no CV is accepted — shortlisting is based entirely on your application form, so complete every section with concrete examples.
4
Contact the named senior charge nurse for an informal discussion about the ward or team before applying.
5
Apply early. Posts can close when application volume is high, and interviews start on 19 October.

King's Lynn Paediatric Nurse Jobs 2026 – Band 5 (Closing: 23 Oct)

The Role in Detail

The main area of the post is the paediatric emergency department — the front door where acutely unwell or injured children present. As a Band 5 registered children’s nurse, you will deliver high-quality emergency nursing care to children and their families, working as part of a multidisciplinary team. The advert also notes close links with the paediatric ward, paediatric assessment unit and day surgery, giving the successful candidate broader exposure across children’s services at the Trust.

It is a full-time position at 37.5 hours per week on a fixed-term 12-month contract covering maternity leave. For children’s nurses looking to build strong emergency-care experience, a year in paediatric ED at a district general hospital is a genuinely valuable CV-builder — it opens doors to permanent paediatric ED posts, senior roles, and specialist children’s pathways later.

What the Day-to-Day Involves

  • Triage and assessment: assessing children presenting to the emergency department, prioritising care by clinical need.
  • Acute treatment: supporting resuscitation, administering medications, assisting with procedures, and monitoring deteriorating patients.
  • Family-centred care: communicating clearly with worried parents and carers during stressful presentations.
  • Multi-area liaison: working with the paediatric ward, paediatric assessment unit and day surgery for admissions and transfers.
  • Safeguarding: recognising and escalating safeguarding concerns in line with Trust policy.
  • Documentation: maintaining accurate clinical records in line with NMC standards.

Who Should Apply

  • Registered children’s nurses holding current NMC registration — this is the essential qualification.
  • Nurses with a genuine passion for paediatric emergency care, able to stay calm and compassionate under pressure.
  • Those seeking a focused year of emergency experience in a supportive children’s service before taking on a permanent senior post.
  • Applicants comfortable with the fixed-term nature of the post — it is maternity cover, not a permanent contract.

Why King’s Lynn and the QEH

The Queen Elizabeth Hospital serves west Norfolk and surrounding areas as its local district general hospital, with children’s services at the heart of its offer to the community. King’s Lynn itself is an attractive proposition for relocating nurses: lower housing costs than the South East, the Norfolk coast within easy reach, and reasonable transport links. For a children’s nurse, the QEH offers a community feel with genuine case variety — you see real paediatric emergencies without the scale of a major trauma centre.

The Bottom Line

One post, one clear speciality, one urgent deadline: Band 5 paediatric emergency nursing at the Queen Elizabeth Hospital King’s Lynn, on 12-month maternity cover at full Band 5 pay. If you are an NMC-registered children’s nurse who thrives in emergency settings, this is worth an application — but do it quickly, because 23 October 2026 at 23:59 is the hard stop.

NHS Winter Recruitment October 2026: Trust Vacancies & £50k Package | NAN Jobs Portal

NHS trusts are recruiting for winter rotas — including a consultant package worth up to £50,000. Here's how to apply.

Published 15 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

Every autumn, NHS trusts recruit heavily to cover winter-pressures rotas. This October's adverts include an eye-catching incentive from the South West.

Royal Devon University Healthcare NHS Foundation Trust advertised a Consultant in Obstetrics & Gynaecology post (advert dated 11 October 2026), based at North Devon District Hospital, Barnstaple, with cross-site work at the Royal Devon and Exeter Hospital. The package: funded visits to Devon, bespoke family relocation support, and "a very significant financial recruitment and retention allowance of up to £50,000", set according to experience and circumstances.

Close-early warning: NHS adverts state trusts "reserve the right to close positions early" — popular roles can vanish within days; apply the same week.

How NHS Trust Recruitment Works

  • One national portal. Nearly every trust advertises on jobs.nhs.uk — check band, salary, contract and reference number before applying.
  • Values-based recruitment. Short written answers — often around 100 words — on equality, motivation and leadership, scored like the rest of your application.
  • Registration matters. Clinical roles need registration with the GMC, NMC or HCPC — usually before your start date.
  • Relocation is negotiable. Trusts sweeten hard-to-fill posts with funded visits and retention allowances — read the full advert.

How to Apply for NHS Winter Roles

Search jobs.nhs.uk by keyword and location. Enter the role title and your region — and set alerts, since winter adverts close fast.
Read the person specification first. Sifters score against the essential criteria — evidence each one with a specific example.
Answer the values questions carefully. The short answers on equality, motivation and leadership are scored — use the advert's language.
Apply the same week. Trusts close adverts early — speed is a real advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which NHS trusts are recruiting for winter 2026?

Trusts across England advertise winter-pressures roles on jobs.nhs.uk; Royal Devon's October 2026 consultant advert is one example.

What is the £50,000 Royal Devon package?

A recruitment and retention allowance of up to £50,000 for the Consultant in Obstetrics & Gynaecology post, plus funded Devon visits and bespoke relocation support.

How do I find NHS winter vacancies?

Search jobs.nhs.uk and set email alerts by keyword, band and location; check weekly as winter rotas fill.

Why do NHS adverts close early?

Trusts reserve the right to close positions early when they receive enough applications — popular roles can close within days.

Do consultant roles offer relocation support?

Many do; Royal Devon's package includes funded visits to Devon and bespoke family relocation support.

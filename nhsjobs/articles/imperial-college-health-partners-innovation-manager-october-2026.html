NHS Innovation Manager Band 8a (£66k+) — Apply by 1 Nov

Imperial College Health Partners is hiring two Innovation Managers — NHS Band 8a, £66,274–£73,496, closing 1 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » NHS Jobs UK » Articles

Imperial College Health Partners (ICHP) is recruiting two Innovation Managers — one permanent post and one 12-month fixed-term post — to deliver innovation projects across the North West London health and care system. The roles are graded NHS Agenda for Change Band 8a at £66,274–£73,496 including High Cost Area Supplement, full time at 37.5 hours a week on a hybrid basis. Applications close at 11:59pm on Sunday 1 November 2026.

The advert frames the opportunity around ICHP’s mission to improve population health in North West London: supporting complex change by bringing energy, expertise and empathy to the development, delivery and management of innovation projects across the health and care system. Interviews are scheduled for the week commencing 16 November 2026.

The Role: Innovation Manager

Innovation Managers at ICHP work at the join between the NHS, academia and industry — taking promising ideas and turning them into projects that change how care is delivered across North West London. The advert asks for people who can support complex change: that means stakeholder management across organisations with different cultures and incentives, project delivery discipline, and the credibility to influence senior clinical and operational leaders.

Two posts are being recruited together, but their contracts differ: one is permanent and the other is a 12-month fixed term. The work itself — developing, delivering and managing innovation projects — is described identically, so read the vacancy page carefully if contract length matters to you.

Who Should Apply

Band 8a innovation roles typically suit people with a track record of delivering change or improvement projects in health and care — project or programme management experience, evidence of working across organisational boundaries, and the ability to translate strategy into delivery. Read the full vacancy page and person specification before applying, and build your application around concrete examples of complex change you have actually delivered, not just been adjacent to.

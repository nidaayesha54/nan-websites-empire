SWASFT Paramedic Apprenticeship — Closes 25 Oct

South Western Ambulance Service’s Paramedic Apprenticeship (BSc Hons) — £25,760–£27,476 — closes TOMORROW, 25 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » NHS Jobs UK » Articles

South Western Ambulance Service NHS Foundation Trust (SWASFT) runs an internal education pathway — the Paramedic Apprenticeship Programme — in partnership with two universities for staff aspiring to become paramedics. The programme is a Level 6 BSc (Hons) in Paramedic Science over 2 years, paying £25,760 to £27,476 a year (progressing to Band 4 six months in). The advert closes on 25 October 2026 — tomorrow.

On successful completion, apprentices achieve a BSc (Hons) in Paramedic Science and become eligible to apply for registration with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC) as a paramedic. It is an earn-while-you-learn route: study while working, with a workplace mentor and university personal tutor.

Honesty note: This pathway is only open to current SWAST staff — emergency care assistants/emergency ambulance assistants with two years’ frontline service by 31 March 2027 (or 18 months with SWAST plus 6 months in a patient-facing role at another ambulance service). It is not open to the general public. Also note: the 6-month prerequisite bridging module costs £1,750 self-funded, payable on commencement of a Newly Qualified Paramedic role — and completing the apprenticeship does not guarantee appointment as an NQP.

The Programme: How It Works

Delivered through one of two university partners, it combines distance learning, virtual teaching and mandatory face-to-face study days. Before starting, applicants complete a 6-month bridging module. To support completion, learners move onto the Apprenticeship Relief Rota — you keep working operationally as an ECA/EAA while attending university study weeks and placement days, all rostered as contracted hours.

Because of the academic and placement commitments, apprentices cannot stay on their existing rota line. Apprentices must follow the HCPC Code of Conduct and the student code from day one.

Eligibility in Full

  • Current frontline SWAST ECA/EAA with two years’ experience by 31 March 2027 (training time counts; TUPE colleagues eligible with two years in a frontline patient-care role)
  • Or: 18 months with SWAST plus at least 6 months’ experience with another ambulance service in a patient-facing role
  • GCSE Maths and English Language at grade 4 (C) or above — or equivalent Level 2 functional skills — with official certificates emailed to the Paramedic Apprenticeship Team by 2 November 2026 (statements of results not accepted)
  • Minimum 30 hours a week on a current substantive contract when the apprenticeship begins
  • Current Clinical Team Manager as referee — discuss your intention to apply with them first and get their support

Pay, Fees and the Fine Print

  • £25,760–£27,476 a year — rising to Band 4 six months into the apprenticeship
  • Funded by the SWASFT Apprenticeship and Skills Levy
  • Bridging module: £1,750 self-funded, payable to the trust on commencement of an NQP role
  • 37.5 hours, full time, 2-year apprenticeship contract
  • Completion does not guarantee an NQP post — qualifiers are invited to apply through a streamlined process

How to Apply

1
Confirm you meet the service rule. This is internal-only: check your ECA/EAA service length against the 31 March 2027 cut-off (or the 18+6 month alternative).
2
Talk to your Clinical Team Manager. You must nominate your CTM as referee — get their support before you apply.
3
Dig out your GCSE certificates. Official Maths and English certificates must reach the apprenticeship team by 2 November 2026 — statements of results are rejected.
4
Apply before 25 October 2026. The advert closes tomorrow — and your supporting statement will be assessed against Trainee Paramedic requirements, so write it with care and without AI.

The Bottom Line

A funded BSc (Hons) in Paramedic Science, HCPC registration at the end, and Band 4 pay six months in — an outstanding route for current emergency care assistants. But it is internal-only, the bridging module is self-funded, and the advert closes tomorrow, 25 October 2026. If eligible, apply today.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the closing date?

25 October 2026 — tomorrow. GCSE Maths/English certificates must reach the apprenticeship team by 2 November 2026.

Can anyone apply?

No — it is internal to SWAST: current frontline emergency care assistants/ambulance assistants with two years’ service by 31 March 2027 (or 18 months SWAST + 6 months elsewhere).

What qualification do I get?

A Level 6 BSc (Hons) in Paramedic Science over 2 years, leading to eligibility for HCPC registration as a paramedic.

What does it pay?

£25,760–£27,476 a year, progressing to Band 4 six months into the apprenticeship. The bridging module costs £1,750 self-funded.

Does completing it guarantee a paramedic job?

No — completion does not guarantee appointment as a Newly Qualified Paramedic; qualifiers apply through a streamlined recruitment process.

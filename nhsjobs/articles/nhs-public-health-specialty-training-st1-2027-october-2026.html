About the Public Health ST1 Programme

A five-year programme (ST1–ST5) training doctors and other professionals to become consultants in public health — specialists who protect and improve the health of populations. Recruitment is run nationally: apply once through the single process and indicate your regional preferences.

Eligibility — Who Can Apply

Eligibility is defined by the official person specification — read it in full before you start. The route is open to both medical applicants (GMC registration, required clinical experience) and non-medical applicants, but requirements differ and the person specification is the only authoritative source.

  • Medical applicants: check the GMC registration level, foundation-equivalent experience, and mandatory competencies.
  • Non-medical applicants: confirm which degrees and experience are accepted — criteria are enforced at shortlisting.
  • Right to work: confirm your UK eligibility against the official guidance.
  • Evidence: applications that cannot evidence the essential criteria are filtered out early — gather certificates, references and proof of experience now.

Preparing for the Assessment Centre

Shortlisted candidates have roughly seven weeks until the 5–6 January 2027 assessment centre. Selection typically tests analytical and critical appraisal skills, written communication and understanding of public health — practise data interpretation and be ready to discuss your motivation in depth.

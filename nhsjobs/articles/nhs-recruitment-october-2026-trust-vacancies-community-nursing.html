NHS Recruitment October 2026: Community Nursing, Support & Trust Vacancies

NHS trusts are advertising heavily this October — from Band 3 healthcare support roles to Band 6 community nursing leaders earning up to £48,117. Here are the live vacancies, the application rules and how to move fast.

Published 14 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

October is a strong month for NHS recruitment: trusts are filling winter-pressures rotas, community services are expanding, and support-worker pipelines run year-round. Vacancies advertised in early October 2026 show the full spread of NHS careers — clinical, support and leadership — all paid on the confirmed 2026/27 Agenda for Change scales.

Verified October 2026 Vacancies

Barnsley Hospital NHS Foundation Trust — one of the country's best-rated trusts for compassionate leadership and staff wellbeing — advertised Band 3 clinical support roles at £25,760 to £27,476 a year (pro rata), posted 30 September 2026. The roles involve 25-point skin assessments, bladder scans, venepuncture, cannulation and ECGs, working under registered nurses — a strong entry route into clinical careers. Barnsley also offers visa sponsorship checks for eligible international applicants under the Skilled Worker or Health & Care visa routes.

Kent Community Health NHS Foundation Trust advertised a Community Nursing Team Leader post (reference 846-8018218-TS-1) at Band 6, £39,959 to £48,117 a year pro rata, based in Maidstone. Community roles like this are among the fastest-growing in the NHS as care shifts out of hospitals — and this one offers full-time or part-time patterns with a flexible-working-friendly culture. Note that many community roles require a full UK driving licence.

How NHS Trust Recruitment Works

  • NHS Jobs is the hub. Almost every trust advertises on jobs.nhs.uk (now beta.jobs.nhs.uk) — each advert shows the pay scheme, band, salary, contract, working pattern and a unique reference number.
  • Values-based recruitment. Adverts ask for short written answers (often ~100 words) on equality, motivation and leadership — treat these as seriously as your CV.
  • Right to work and DBS. All appointments need right-to-work evidence; clinical roles need DBS checks — some trusts deduct the DBS cost from salary for Band 6+ roles.
  • Person specifications rule. Address every "essential" criterion with specific examples — sifters score against the specification, not your CV alone.

How to Apply for NHS Jobs

Create your NHS Jobs account. Register on jobs.nhs.uk, build your profile once and set alerts by keyword, band, location and working pattern.
Read the person specification first. Score yourself against every essential criterion before writing — if you cannot evidence it, find a closer-matching vacancy.
Write a tailored supporting statement. Use the STAR method for experience examples and answer the values questions in the advert's own language (often ~100 words each).
Check registration requirements. Registered nurse, midwife and AHP roles need current NMC/HCPC registration — international applicants should verify registration and visa eligibility early.
Apply the same week. Trusts close adverts early when applications flood in. Submitting within days of posting is a genuine competitive advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are NHS jobs advertised?

Almost all trust vacancies are advertised on jobs.nhs.uk (now beta.jobs.nhs.uk), showing pay band, salary, contract, working pattern and reference number.

What is values-based recruitment in the NHS?

Adverts ask for short written answers (often ~100 words) on equality, motivation and leadership — scored as part of shortlisting.

Can international nurses apply for NHS jobs?

Many trusts offer Health & Care Worker or Skilled Worker visa sponsorship — check each advert and verify NMC registration and immigration requirements.

Why do NHS adverts close early?

Trusts reserve the right to close positions early when they receive enough applications. Popular roles can close within days, so apply in the same week a vacancy is posted.

Do I pay for my own DBS check?

It depends on the trust and grade — some trusts, including Barnsley Hospital, meet the DBS cost for Band 6 and above roles through salary deduction. Check the advert.

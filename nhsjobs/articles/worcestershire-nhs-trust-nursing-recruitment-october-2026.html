Worcestershire NHS Trust Hiring Now: RGNs, RNAs and HCAs, Bands 3–8a — October 2026

Herefordshire and Worcestershire Health and Care NHS Trust is actively recruiting registered nurses, nursing associates and healthcare assistants across Worcestershire — Bands 3 to 8a, with training and career pathways built in.

Community nursing in the Midlands is hiring at scale: Herefordshire and Worcestershire Health and Care NHS Trust is actively recruiting RGNs, RNAs and HCAs across Worcestershire, covering community hospitals and specialist services at Bands 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 and 8a. The trust’s recruitment pages were updated within the last day — this is a live, current hiring push.

What the Work Looks Like

This is community and specialist care, not acute wards. The trust cares for patients with a wide range of conditions — from wound care and neurological problems to end-of-life care — wrapping nursing, physiotherapy, occupational therapy and mental health support around each patient with the aim of helping them live at home. Teams work closely with GPs and social care partners, making shared decisions about how best to use collective skills and resources. A senior manager on the recruitment pages makes the pitch personal: a trust “where your senior manager knows your name”, where you have “time to deliver the care you feel your patients deserve” and feel supported by your team.

Training and Career Pathways

The trust is explicit that development is part of the deal — and unusually specific about it:

  • Full induction and tailored training for the right candidates — including those who don’t yet have every skill, if they bring the trust’s “get up and go” values.
  • Planned career pathways toward your goals, with support to achieve the trust’s core competencies.
  • ACP training — Advanced Clinical Practitioner development for experienced clinicians.
  • TNA training — Trainee Nursing Associate routes, a strong option for HCAs looking to step up.
  • Specialist degrees to take you where you want to be.

That spread — Bands 3 to 8a in one recruitment push — means the trust is hiring across the whole nursing ladder, from entry-level healthcare assistants to advanced practitioners.

Who Should Apply

  • Registered nurses (RGN) seeking community, specialist or leadership roles up to Band 8a.
  • Registered nursing associates (RNA) — the Band 4 bridge role the NHS is expanding fast.
  • Healthcare assistants (HCA, Band 3) — including career-changers the trust explicitly welcomes with tailored induction.
  • Anyone drawn to community care: smaller teams, named managers, long-term patient relationships and genuine autonomy.

The trust actively invites informal chats and visits to its services — a good sign of a confident employer, and a smart first step before you apply.

How to Apply

1
Browse the trust’s recruitment pages and jobs.nhs.uk for Worcestershire community and specialist vacancies at your band.
2
Arrange an informal chat or service visit — the trust welcomes them, and it strengthens your application.
3
Tailor your application to community values: patient-centred care, teamwork, communication and the “get up and go” attitude.
4
Ask about training pathways (ACP, TNA, specialist degrees) in your application or interview — it signals long-term intent.

The Bottom Line

Bands 3 to 8a, community hospitals and specialist services, real training pathways and a trust that invites you in for a chat first — Worcestershire’s recruitment wave is one of the most applicant-friendly NHS openings this month. If community nursing appeals, start with the informal chat.

The Role in Detail

Healthcare Assistants are the backbone of ward nursing teams, and these ward-based posts put you right at the patient’s bedside: helping with washing, dressing and eating, observing and recording vital signs, and being the calm, friendly presence patients remember long after discharge.

The Trust is recruiting for multiple posts across its wards — a genuine recruitment drive. Full-time working means rotational shifts covering days, nights and weekends, with the usual unsocial-hours enhancements for night and weekend working.

Who Should Apply

  • Experienced Healthcare Assistants or support workers seeking a permanent NHS trust role.
  • Care Certificate holders or equivalent — check the person specification on the live advert for the exact wording.
  • Caring, reliable people who thrive in a team, communicate well with patients and families, and stay calm when a ward gets busy.
  • Applicants comfortable with rotational shift work, including nights and weekends.

Visa Sponsorship — What Is Reported

The advert reportedly states that Skilled Worker visa sponsorship applications will be examined — considered rather than ruled out. That is not a guarantee of sponsorship, so confirm the exact wording on the live jobs.nhs.uk advert.

Why Harrogate and the Trust

Harrogate and District NHS Foundation Trust runs Harrogate District Hospital, serving the Harrogate area and much of North Yorkshire. Harrogate is rated one of the happiest places to live in the UK — a spa town with easy access to the Yorkshire Dales. For an HCA, a trust with many wards is an excellent place to build experience and progress — many HCAs use the role as a launchpad into nurse apprenticeships and registered nurse training.

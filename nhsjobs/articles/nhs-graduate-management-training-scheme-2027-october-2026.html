NHS Graduate Management Training Scheme 2027: Apply Before 28 October 2026

The NHS Graduate Management Training Scheme 2027 closes on 28 October 2026 at 11:30am — with virtual assessment centres in January–February 2027. Here are the key dates and how to apply.

The NHS Graduate Management Training Scheme (GMTS) 2027 — the NHS’s flagship route into health-service management — is open now, but the window is nearly closed: applications close on 28 October 2026 at 11:30am, with virtual assessment centres running January to February 2027. If you are a graduate aiming for NHS leadership, this is the deadline that matters this month.

What the Scheme Is

The GMTS is the NHS’s national management development programme for graduates: trainees rotate through placements across NHS organisations, building hands-on leadership experience in running health services, alongside structured development and study. It is the main pipeline into operational, general and specialist management roles in the NHS — distinct from clinical routes like nursing or medicine, and aimed squarely at future managers and leaders. Trainees typically specialise in areas such as general management, finance, policy or analysis, developing the skills to run services and lead teams of clinical and non-clinical staff.

Who Should Apply

  • Graduates with a genuine commitment to NHS values and public-service leadership.
  • Candidates who want a management career — running services, leading teams and improving care — rather than a clinical role.
  • Applicants comfortable with rotational placements, which may mean relocating within England during the scheme.
  • Check the official scheme pages for the degree, residency and right-to-work requirements for 2027 entry.

How to Apply

1
Open the official NHS Graduate Management Training Scheme pages and read the 2027 entry criteria.
2
Complete the online application — graduate schemes assess motivation, values and potential through written exercises and online tests.
3
Submit before 11:30am on Tuesday 28 October 2026 — the cut-off is mid-morning, not midnight.
4
Shortlisted candidates attend virtual assessment centres in January–February 2027.

If You Miss It — Other NHS Graduate Routes

The GMTS is not the only way in. The NHS also runs healthcare apprenticeships — including healthcare support worker pathways — and individual trusts advertise graduate-level management and administrative roles on jobs.nhs.uk year-round. But for the structured national management scheme, 28 October 2026 is the one date to beat.

The Bottom Line

A mid-morning deadline on 28 October leaves almost no margin for error — start the application this week, confirm the details on the official scheme site, and treat the virtual assessment centres in January and February as your preparation horizon.

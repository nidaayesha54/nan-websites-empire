Shrewsbury & Telford NHS Nursing Jobs October 2026: Staff Nurse £32,073–£39,043 — Closes 30 October 2026

Shrewsbury and Telford Hospital NHS Trust is recruiting nurses this October — a Staff Nurse post at Princess Royal Hospital Telford and a Paediatric Research Nurse/AHP role in the trust's Research & Innovation Department. Both close on 30 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 17, 2026
Home » NHS Jobs UK » Articles

Shrewsbury and Telford Hospital NHS Trust (SaTH) is recruiting nurses this October 2026, with live vacancies at Princess Royal Hospital in Telford and in the trust’s Research & Innovation Department. Both posts are advertised on the trust’s own recruitment site, jobs.sath.nhs.uk, and both close on 30 October 2026.

The headline post is a permanent Staff Nurse role (ref 223-SNM-790) at £32,073–£39,043 per annum — 37.5 hours per week on a 7-day shift pattern covering weekends, nights and bank holidays at Princess Royal Hospital, Telford. Alongside it, a Paediatric Research Nurse/AHP post (ref 223-PRN-794) offers £39,959–£48,117 per annum for 22.5 hours per week in the trust’s Research & Innovation Department.

The Roles in Detail

The Staff Nurse (223-SNM-790) is a permanent, full-time frontline nursing post at Princess Royal Hospital, Telford — a 7-day shift pattern including weekends, nights and bank holidays.

The Paediatric Research Nurse/AHP (223-PRN-794) sits in Research & Innovation (22.5 hrs/week) — for registered nurses or allied health professionals moving into clinical research rather than a clinical rota.

One-Stop Nurse Recruitment Events

SaTH also holds one-stop nurse recruitment events — same-day interview-and-offer days, usually promoted on jobs.sath.nhs.uk and the trust’s social channels. If you miss this advert cycle, watch for the next event.

How to Apply for SaTH Nursing Jobs

1
Go to jobs.sath.nhs.uk and search by reference number: 223-SNM-790 (Staff Nurse) or 223-PRN-794 (Paediatric Research Nurse/AHP).
2
Check the advert’s salary, hours, shift pattern and person specification.
3
Apply through the trust’s application system with a supporting statement tailored to the person specification — NHS shortlisting is scored against it.
4
Watch for the trust’s one-stop nurse recruitment events — a same-day interview-and-offer route if you prefer to meet the team first.
5
Submit before 30 October 2026. Late applications are not accepted, and popular posts can be withdrawn early.

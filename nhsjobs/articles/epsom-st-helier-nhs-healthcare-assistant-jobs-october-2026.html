Epsom & St Helier NHS Trust Healthcare Assistant Jobs: Band 3, £30,630–£32,346 — Apply by 22 October 2026

Epsom and St Helier University Hospitals NHS Trust is recruiting Healthcare Assistants for its surgical ward — Band 3, £30,630–£32,346 including HCAS, closing 22 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » NHS Jobs UK » Articles

Epsom and St Helier University Hospitals NHS Trust — rated Good by the Care Quality Commission — is recruiting Healthcare Assistants for its surgical ward at St Helier Hospital, Carshalton. The posts are permanent, full-time (37.5 hours per week) at Band 3, paying £30,630–£32,346 per annum including HCAS. Applications close at 23:59 on 22 October 2026.

The trust is looking for enthusiastic, dynamic and caring Healthcare Assistants with a passion for working on a surgical ward — joining what it describes as a supportive and friendly team built on kindness, professionalism, positivity and teamwork.

What the Role Involves

Working under the direct supervision and direction of a Registered Nurse / Ward Manager who delegates tasks, the Healthcare Assistant delivers high-quality nursing care to patients as part of a team. Previous experience in the role is preferred, but the trust provides clinical training so you can learn the role — with daily supervision by trained staff to perform clinical skills safely, confidently and competently.

Also Closing: Ward Clerk (21 October 2026)

The trust is also advertising a Ward Clerk post (reference 343-8276542-SC): Band 3, administrative and clerical, part-time 18 hours per week (weekends and bank holidays), paying £30,630–£32,346 pa incl. HCAS Outer, pro rata. The post holder provides dedicated admin support to medical and midwifery staff at ward level — tracking patients from admission to treatment and discharge — and closes at 23:59 on 21 October 2026, a day before the HCA advert.

Who Can Apply

  • Skills: excellent communication and interpersonal skills, and a genuine desire to provide outstanding patient care.
  • Experience: previous HCA experience preferred but not essential — clinical training is provided.
  • Values: the trust expects applicants who share its values of respect, kindness, professionalism, positivity and teamwork.
  • Checks: enhanced DBS, occupational health clearance, right-to-work verification and references are standard.

How to Apply

Applications run through the trust’s recruitment system. Follow these steps:

4
Submit before 23:59 on 22 October 2026 (21 October for the Ward Clerk post).

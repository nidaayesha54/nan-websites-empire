Government Unlocks Thousands of NHS Jobs for Nurses and Midwives: Graduate Guarantee 2026

The government has unveiled measures to unlock thousands of NHS jobs for nurses and midwives — early graduate recruitment, £8 million for Band 5 midwifery conversions and a new online support hub.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » NHS Jobs UK » Articles

The government has announced a package of measures to unlock thousands of NHS jobs for nurses and midwives, aimed squarely at newly qualified graduates facing an uncertain jobs market. The message to the 2026 graduate cohort is direct: “No one who dedicates themselves to a nursing or midwifery career should be left in limbo, when their skills are so urgently needed in the effort to rebuild our NHS.”

The initiative forms part of the government’s broader Plan for Change — improving patient access, reducing waiting times and ensuring a confident, well-supported NHS workforce. For graduates, it promises greater certainty; for the NHS, better workforce planning; and for patients, faster, more consistent care.

The Key Measures

MeasureDetail

Note: Measures are as announced in October 2026. Implementation will run through NHS England and individual trusts — watch trust recruitment pages and jobs.nhs.uk for the resulting vacancies.

£8 Million for Band 5 Midwifery Posts

One of the most concrete commitments is the £8 million investment to convert vacant maternity support worker roles into Band 5 midwifery positions — directly creating registered midwife posts where support-worker vacancies sit unfilled. For midwifery graduates, this means genuinely new Band 5 opportunities rather than reshuffled existing posts.

What It Means for 2026 Graduates

  • A job to apply for at graduation: as England’s Chief Nursing Officer Duncan Barton put it, “Every nurse and midwife deserves the guarantee of a job to apply for when they graduate.”
  • Less limbo: early recruitment before vacancies formally arise is designed to end the frustrating delays between qualifying and starting work.
  • Better support: the new online hub offers refreshed guidance on applications, plus enhanced onboarding and mentorship once in post.
  • Retention signal: the announcement notes there are more nurses and midwives than ever choosing to stay in the NHS — the guarantee extends that stability to new graduates.

How Graduates Should Respond

The guarantee is a commitment to create the opportunities — you still need to secure them. Follow these steps:

1
Use the new online hub for refreshed guidance on NHS applications and the graduate recruitment timeline.
2
Search jobs.nhs.uk for Band 5 nurse and midwife posts at your preferred trusts — and set up job alerts.
3
Apply early: with early recruitment before vacancies formally arise, the first movers get the first interviews.
4
Prepare for values-based recruitment — NHS interviews test the values in the NHS Constitution, not just clinical knowledge.

The Bottom Line

Newly qualified nurses and midwives have long faced an NHS desperate for staff yet a graduate jobs market that kept them waiting. Early recruitment, £8 million in new Band 5 midwifery posts, a dedicated hub and better onboarding are the government’s answer — the guarantee is of a job to apply for, so have your application ready when the vacancies land.

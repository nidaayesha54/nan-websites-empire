Kent Community Health NHS Jobs October 2026: 100+ Vacancies | NAN Jobs Portal

Kent Community Health NHS Foundation Trust is advertising 110+ community vacancies across Kent this October. Here's who can apply and how.

Home › Articles › Kent Community Health NHS Jobs October 2026: 100+ Vacancies | NAN Jobs Portal

Published 15 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

Kent Community Health NHS Foundation Trust (KCHFT) is one of England's largest community health trusts — and this October it is hiring at scale: 110 jobs listed in the Smeeth area, with further adverts around Canterbury and London — 110+ vacancies across Kent in October 2026.

The trust's message to candidates is clear: "The NHS is changing. More emphasis on community-based care means there's never been a better time to join us." Among the live adverts are Therapy Assistant roles: "We are looking for an enthusiastic and adaptable Therapy Assistant to join our professional team."

Close-early warning: KCHFT adverts state trusts "reserve the right to close positions early" — popular roles can vanish within days.

Who Can Apply

At this stage, roles are open to colleagues employed within Kent and Medway NHS Trusts, the Kent and Medway ICB, Medway Community Healthcare, or the Staff Bank. Nursing and AHP vacancies are also open to Student Nurses and Newly Qualified Nurses/AHPs.

What Community Roles Involve

  • Community-based care. Roles are delivered in patients' homes, clinics and community hospitals.
  • Therapy support. Therapy Assistants support physio, occupational and speech-and-language teams with rehabilitation plans.
  • Driving requirement. Many community roles require a full UK driving licence for travel between visits.
  • Right to work. Some adverts do not meet Skilled Worker sponsorship criteria — applicants must evidence UK right to work.

How to Apply for KCHFT Roles

Search jobs.nhs.uk for the role title. Use "Therapy Assistant" or "Kent Community Health" plus the reference number.
Check your eligibility group. Confirm you are within the Kent and Medway trusts, ICB, Medway Community Healthcare, Staff Bank, or the student/newly-qualified nursing and AHP route.
Verify registration and driving requirements. Registered roles need current NMC/HCPC registration; community posts often require a full UK driving licence.
Check sponsorship and apply promptly. Some adverts do not meet Skilled Worker criteria — evidence UK right to work, then submit your tailored application via jobs.nhs.uk early.

NHS Band 5 Midwife (£32k+) — Apply by 28 Oct

Buckinghamshire Healthcare NHS Trust is hiring Band 5 midwives — £32,073–£39,043, full or part time, closing 28 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » NHS Jobs UK » Articles

Buckinghamshire Healthcare NHS Trust is recruiting Band 5 midwives for its inpatient and community maternity services (advert reference 434-SCS8331183, posted 14 October 2026). The posts pay £32,073 to £39,043 a year (pro rata for part time), are offered full time and part time, and close on 28 October 2026.

Band 5 midwives here rotate between inpatient areas — antenatal ward, labour ward and postnatal ward — and community services, including supporting women choosing homebirths on an on-call basis. Experienced Band 6 midwives can choose community working or inpatient care. The trust offers a comprehensive preceptorship package for new starters, so newly qualified midwives are explicitly welcomed.

Honesty note: This is not a bridging programme: only midwives trained in the UK are eligible, and applicants with an NMC pin must have worked in the UK as a midwife. You must also be able to drive and have access to a car for work purposes — an essential criterion. The trust reserves the standard right to close adverts early, so apply promptly.

The Role: Band 5 Midwife

Midwives will be responsible for antenatal, intrapartum and postnatal care across all areas of the trust’s maternity services. The service cares for around 4,800 women and their families, offers a variety of birth choices, has a dedicated bereavement suite, a level 2 neonatal service and close links with the John Radcliffe Hospital in Oxford. You will also have the chance to work alongside specialist services including the trust’s internationally acclaimed spinal unit.

The hybrid rotation model — inpatient plus community — is a genuine development offer: it builds breadth quickly, which is exactly what newly qualified midwives need for a strong foundation.

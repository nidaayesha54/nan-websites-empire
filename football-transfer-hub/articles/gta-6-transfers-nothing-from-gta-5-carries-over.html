GTA 6 Transfers: Nothing From GTA 5 Carries Over

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the question players with years of GTA Online progress keep asking has a simple answer: nothing transfers. Your GTA Online character, your rank, your GTA$, your cars, your properties and your businesses do not carry over into GTA 6. Rockstar has announced no transfer system, no migration programme and no carry-over of any kind — and every piece of evidence says that is not an oversight. It is the plan.

That matters because the rumours say otherwise. Sites and videos have promised transfers, legacy bonuses and account migrations for months, and players have made decisions about their GTA Online money based on those promises. This guide explains what Rockstar has actually said, what history shows, and what the one real link between the two games actually is.

What Rockstar Has Not Announced

The honest answer starts with what does not exist. Rockstar has not announced a system that carries GTA Online characters, ranks, GTA$, properties, vehicles or other progress into GTA 6. The transfer rules Rockstar currently documents apply to moving profiles between editions of GTA 5 and supported console generations — not between GTA 5 and GTA 6. The official GTA 6 store listing identifies the platforms and the release date, but it does not promise compatibility with GTA Online, and it does not say that a Rockstar account, character, rank, bank balance, business, vehicle, clothing, weapon or player-created job will be imported.

That silence is comprehensive. No Newswire post, no Support article and no Take-Two statement addresses a GTA 5 to GTA 6 transfer. Rockstar has said GTA 6 is a single-player experience coming November 19 to PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and that GTA Online has not been announced for it at all — there is no online mode at launch, no online release date and no online character creator confirmed for any date. The existing GTA Online for GTA 5 continues as its own separate game, with its own world, and playing it stays exactly as it works today. Your character stays where it has always been, in GTA Online for GTA 5, which continues running independently.

History: Rockstar Has Never Done This

The history is the strongest evidence. Rockstar has never allowed cross-title progress transfers between different main releases. GTA 4 multiplayer progress did not carry over to GTA 5. Red Dead Redemption progress did not carry into Red Dead Online. Every transfer Rockstar has ever offered moved a GTA Online profile between versions of the same game — from PS3 and Xbox 360 to PS4 and Xbox One, and since March 15, 2022, from PS4 and Xbox One to PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Those were migrations within one game, and they came with limits: one migration per profile, the old profile stayed on the old console, the two were not synced afterwards, and unspent purchased GTA$ were not eligible for transfer across platforms.

GTA 6 is a different game with a different story, different characters and a different map. Jason and Lucia are new protagonists in a new state, Leonida, and there is nothing to import — no GTA 5 story save has a counterpart in GTA 6, because the story is entirely new. The economics point the same way: a fresh multiplayer economy needs everyone to start at zero, and carrying over a decade of GTA Online wealth would break it on day one. That is why every serious reading of the evidence concludes the same thing — a clean slate, with no transfer, and no announcement to wait for.

The One Real Link: GTA+ Points the Other Way

There is exactly one link Rockstar has drawn between the two games, and it points in the opposite direction. Digital GTA 6 pre-orders include a free month of GTA+, and Rockstar describes it as a GTA$500,000 monthly deposit — for your GTA Online character. You pre-order GTA 6 and get something for the game you already play, not the other way around. Rockstar calls GTA+ a membership, and the bonus is the only connection between the two games that Rockstar has announced.

Some sites claim that returning players will receive legacy cosmetic badges and bonuses in GTA 6, or that Social Club identity — gamertags, friend lists and crew affiliations — will carry over. Those are guesses, not announcements, and they should be treated as such. Until Rockstar publishes a specific GTA 6 migration policy, treat every claim about automatic carry-over as unconfirmed, and do not spend or move assets on the assumption that they will appear in the new game. If a transfer is ever announced, it will come from Rockstar directly — and until then, the correct status for every item is unannounced, not guaranteed.

Frequently Asked Questions

Will my GTA Online money transfer to GTA 6?

No. Rockstar has not announced any transfer of GTA$, Shark Card money, bank balances or GTA+ benefits from GTA 5 or GTA Online to GTA 6. Every past transfer moved a profile between versions of the same game only.

Will GTA Online shut down when GTA 6 launches?

No. Rockstar has announced no shutdown date, and GTA Online for GTA 5 continues as its own game with its own world, independent of GTA 6.

Is there any benefit for long-time GTA Online players in GTA 6?

Nothing confirmed. The only announced link is the GTA+ pre-order bonus, which gives GTA$500,000 a month to your GTA Online character — a reward for the old game, not a transfer into the new one.

The Bottom Line

Your GTA Online progress stays in GTA Online. GTA 6 is a fresh start — new characters, new map, new economy — and Rockstar has never moved progress between games in its history. Enjoy the decade you built in Los Santos, and start Leonida with empty pockets like everyone else.

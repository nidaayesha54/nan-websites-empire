Football Transfer Window 2026 Dates

The football transfer window is the period during which clubs can register new players. Outside these windows, deals can be agreed but players cannot be registered to play. Here are the key transfer window 2026 dates for the major leagues.

Winter 2026 Transfer Window Dates

The winter window runs through January 2026 in Europe (typically 1–31 January for the big five leagues) and again in January 2027 after the 2026 summer window. Winter windows are shorter and usually quieter, with clubs signing cover for injuries or title pushes.

Key Deadlines Explained

  • Deadline day: the final day clubs can register new signings; deals announced after the cutoff must wait.
  • Deal sheets: in England, clubs get a two-hour extension if a deal sheet is submitted before the deadline.
  • Free agents: out-of-contract players can sign at any time — windows only govern registered transfers.
  • Loans: domestic loan windows sometimes differ from international ones.

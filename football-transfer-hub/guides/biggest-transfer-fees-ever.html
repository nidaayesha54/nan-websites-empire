Why Is Neymar's Record Unbroken?

Neymar's €222m release-clause activation in 2017 remains untouchable because it was a clause trigger, not a negotiation — PSG simply paid the number Barcelona had set. Since then, financial fair play scrutiny, the pandemic's financial hit, and smarter squad planning have kept even elite deals below €150m.

Will the Record Ever Fall?

Possibly — a generational talent with a giant release clause in an inflated market could do it. But clubs have learned: structured fees, add-ons and wage packages now matter as much as the headline number. Learn how football transfers work to understand the mechanics behind the money.

GTA 6 Clothes: 70 Stores and What Outfits Cost

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

How you dress in GTA 6 is not a side detail — it is one of the game's deepest systems, and it starts almost immediately. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it has around 70 clothing stores across Leonida, from a $9 bargain rack in Port Gellhorn to formal wear in Capri that costs more than some missions pay. Both protagonists, Jason and Lucia, have fully independent wardrobes, and the game lets you mix and match individual items rather than picking pre-built outfits. This guide covers where to shop, what things actually cost, the missions that open the wardrobe, and the five shops that stay locked unless you pay $20 more.

The first thing to know is that you do not create a character in GTA 6 — you style Jason and Lucia. The August 2026 Extended Look showed an in-field clothing wheel that lets you swap outfits, masks and face coverings on the move, and wardrobes sit in safehouses and in clothing stores, marked by a hanger icon on the minimap. From the Outfits tab, a saved outfit can be added to your personal vehicle's trunk — up to five of them, so your wardrobe travels with you.

Your First Outfit: Bargain World

The first clothing store most players meet is Bargain World in Port Gellhorn, and it comes with a name and a price tag: a full outfit can cost as little as $9. The "How Do I Look?" mission in Chapter 2 sends Lucia there to buy a top and pants or shorts, and IGN's launch-day walkthrough logged the prices — $46 for the mannequin's Bargain World Ensemble, or $9 for a whole outfit put together from the bargain rack at the very back, by the rear door. Nearly every clothing store also sells ready-made outfits, so the cheap route is always available if you know where to look.

The second mission that opens the wardrobe is "New Look", at Pearl House Hair Salon. Hair changes there cost around $150 — bring that much, though less will do — and previews are free. The salon carries nine standard hairstyles found across Leonida and six hair colors that every salon stocks. Together, the two missions teach the whole system: buy the clothes, change the hair, and the look is yours.

Seventy Stores, Five Kinds

The roughly 70 stores fall into five kinds, and each kind serves a different look. Casual and street wear is the biggest group: A1 Fashions on El Joven Boulevard, Slinger with branches in Downtown, the Decor District, Peacock Bay, Southside and Tequesta, This / That and Mid in the Decor District and Ocean Beach, and Stock 305 in Stockyard. Formal wear is the smallest group — Nola and Ferruccio Tucci, both in Capri, and expensive enough that you may need to rob a bank for the money. Sportswear is Alpha Sports, with branches in Downtown and four Ocean Beach locations. Souvenir and swimwear stores sell beach wear and accessories. And thrift shops — Nifty Threads Island Wear and Bargain World — sell the cheapest clothing in the game.

Accessories follow their own rule: sunglasses and other accessories can be bought at Ammu-Nation stores as well as clothing shops. The diversity of the stores reflects the cultural variety of Vice City's neighbourhoods, and the game rewards players who shop in more than one of them.

The Five Shops Locked Behind $20

Five customization shops are physical storefronts you can walk past in Vice City and cannot enter unless you own the $99.99 Ultimate Edition — or buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later for the $20 difference from the $79.99 Standard Edition. They are Stock 305, the streetwear clothing store in Stockyard, selling head-to-toe looks for both Jason and Lucia; Sara's Unisex Salon, for signature salon styles including facial hair for Jason and makeup and nails for Lucia; Electric Fang Tattoo, Stockyard's most iconic ink bar, with more than 50 signature tattoos designed by the artist collective FAILE; Rideout Customs Mod Shop in Vice City; and One-Eyed Willie's at Lake Leonida, both vehicle mod shops.

You are not locked out permanently: Rockstar confirmed a separate Grand Theft Auto VI: Ultimate Edition Upgrade that Standard Edition owners can buy at any time. You will know the upgrade applied when the shop doors that previously stayed shut become enterable. The pre-order bonus is separate and applies to both editions: the Vintage Vice City Pack, with retro 1980s outfits for Jason and Lucia, a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan, and a Tommy Vercetti-style tropical weapon skin, included with all purchases made before November 20.

How to Shop Smart

The smartest approach is to start cheap and spend later. The $9 bargain rack at Bargain World gets you a full outfit in Chapter 2, and the mannequin set at $46 is the fastest way through the "How Do I Look?" mission. Save the formal wear for when you have real money — Nola and Ferruccio Tucci are priced for players who have finished bank jobs, not for players who have just arrived in Port Gellhorn. And remember the pocket-cash rule: you can carry a maximum of $20,000 at a time, and money in your pocket is lost when you die or get arrested, so bank your cash at ATMs before a shopping trip.

Use the trunk, too: up to five saved outfits fit in your personal car's trunk, which means you can carry a beach look, a formal look, a heist look and a street look without going home. And use the clothing wheel in the field — swapping a mask or a face covering on the move is faster than finding a store, and it is the difference between being recognised and not being recognised when the police are looking for what they saw.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many clothing stores are in GTA 6?

Around 70, across five kinds: casual and street wear, formal wear, sportswear, souvenir and swimwear, and thrift shops. Accessories are also sold at Ammu-Nation stores.

What is the cheapest outfit in GTA 6?

A full outfit from the bargain rack at Bargain World in Port Gellhorn costs as little as $9. The mannequin's ready-made ensemble there costs $46, and a haircut at Pearl House Hair Salon costs around $150.

Which shops need the Ultimate Edition?

Five: Stock 305 clothing, Sara's Unisex Salon, Electric Fang Tattoo, Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's. Standard Edition owners can unlock them with the $20 Ultimate Edition Upgrade at any time.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 clothes run from a $9 bargain rack to formal wear that costs a bank job, across about 70 stores and five kinds of shop. Start at Bargain World, change your hair at Pearl House, carry five outfits in your trunk, and remember that five shops — including the tattoo parlour and the streetwear store — stay shut until you pay the $20 upgrade. Dress for the job you are about to do, and Vice City will treat you accordingly.

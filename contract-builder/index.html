The Problems We Solve

Freelancers lose thousands every year to handshake deals. These templates fix that.

1

"My client won't pay"

A service agreement with clear payment terms, late fees, and kill fees turns "please pay me" into an enforceable obligation.

2

"The scope keeps growing"

Define deliverables and revision limits in writing. Extra work becomes extra pay — automatically.

3

"They shared my idea"

An NDA signed before you reveal anything keeps your concepts, data, and strategies confidential.

4

"Who owns the work?"

Intellectual property clauses spell out exactly when and how ownership transfers — usually on final payment.

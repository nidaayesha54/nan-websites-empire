Is the wedding guest list manager really free? +
Yes. GuestListPro is 100% free with no signup. Your guest list is saved in your own browser with localStorage.

Click Export CSV on the guest manager page to download a spreadsheet-ready file of all your guests, RSVP statuses, and meal choices.
Can I track plus-ones and meal choices? +
Yes. Each guest entry includes side (bride/groom), RSVP status, plus-one count, and meal choice, with live totals.
Will my guest list be saved if I close the browser? +
Yes, your list is stored in your browser's localStorage and persists between visits on the same device.

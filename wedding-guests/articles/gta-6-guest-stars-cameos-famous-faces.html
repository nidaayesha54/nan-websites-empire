GTA 6 Guest Stars: Famous Faces Hiding in Leonida

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

GTA 6 has the deepest cast list in the series — 202 credited character roles, split into a 57-name main cast and a 145-name full cast — and buried inside it are some of the most famous names in music, sport and comedy. Bad Bunny plays a character and hosts a radio station. An NFL star plays a wide receiver. "Weird Al" Yankovic hosts an '80s station, writes commercials and voices burger ads. This guide is the guest list: every famous face hiding in Leonida, where to find them, and which ones are playing themselves.

It matters because the cameos are easy to miss. Most of them are voices, not faces — a radio host you hear while driving, a character you meet once, a name in the credits you only notice on a second look. The two leads, Dylan Rourke as Jason Duval and Manni L. Perez as Lucia Caminos, were the secret Rockstar kept for years. The guests were the surprise it saved for launch day.

The musicians: Bad Bunny, Lana Del Rey and more

The biggest musical guest is Bad Bunny, who does two jobs in GTA 6: he plays Mitico Mario in the supporting cast, and he hosts Cocoteo FM as El Martillo. Lana Del Rey is Delta Dawne on Back Country Radio, alongside Morgan Wallen as MW. Denzel Curry plays DWNPLY, and the rapper roster continues with Freddie Gibbs, Kodak Black and Sexyy Red, who all appear as themselves — the game credits them by their own names, playing themselves inside Leonida.

The radio booths are where the musicians multiply. Keith Richards and Buddy Guy host Gloriana Blues Radio as DJ Keith and DJ Buddy. Gloria and Emilio Estefan host Radio La Ola as themselves. Kerry King and Tom Araya of Slayer host The Chamber 106.6 as DJ Kerry and DJ Tom. Lindsey Buckingham and Michael McDonald host V-Rock as The Buck and Skipper McD. Producer Scott Storch plays The Pianist. The soundtrack is not just licensed music — it is a cast list of its own.

The comedians: Weird Al, Sudeikis and the talk shows

The most joyful cameo is "Weird Al" Yankovic, who hosts Vice City FM, the game's non-stop '80s station, as DJ Al alongside Brigitte Nielsen as Madame Brigitte. Yankovic confirmed the role himself in a Bluesky post on November 19, and the job is bigger than introducing songs: he wrote some of the game's commercials, voices the Burger Shot advertisements, and Rockstar's credits list him not only as DJ Al but among the game's additional writers and original-music contributors.

The talk radio is just as starry. Jason Sudeikis is Richard Bastion on The Richard Bastion Show on WKTT Talk Radio. Charlamagne tha God and Ivy Rivera host the in-game "Early AF" programme. Jeremy Irons is DJ Dicky on Kaleidoscope FM. The pattern is the same across the dial: real voices, fictional stations, and a world that sounds like it has opinions.

The athletes: Tyreek Hill and the Manatees

NFL star Tyreek Hill revealed that he voices Maleek Rivers, a wide receiver for the fictional Vice City Manatees. It is a small role with a big joke built into it: the fastest player in football, playing a receiver for a fictional Florida team, in a game set in fictional Florida. Hill announced it himself, and the credits confirm it.

The Manatees are one of the game's background gags — a whole fictional sports world running underneath the story, with teams, players and radio call-ins. Hill's cameo is the most famous face in it, but the joke works because the world around him is fully built. Leonida has sports bars, sports radio and sports arguments, and the Manatees are the team everyone argues about.

The actors: SNL, Office Space and the main cast

The main cast mixes stage and screen actors with the musicians and athletes. Saturday Night Live alum Bobby Moynihan plays Cal, Jason's friend, and Stephen Root — the Office Space veteran — plays Brian Heder, the old smuggler of the Keys. Brad Carter plays Donnie, Orlagh Cassidy plays Lori Heder, Manny Perez plays Raul Bautista, Mike Forbs voices Boobie Ike, and Naiqui Macabroad plays Dre'Quan. Martial arts film star Michael Jai White plays Kordell Grant. Taryn Manning plays Vicki, the motel neighbour, Roselyn Sanchez plays Mia Caminos, and Italian actor Sergio Castellitto plays Giorgio Gallo. Bobby Lee plays Andre Ye.

One familiar character returns from GTA 5: Jimmy De Santa is in the full cast, played again by Danny Tamberelli. The credits also name Lexie Shoaibi as Mandy, the tenant who never appeared in pre-release marketing and went viral after her Pipework scene with Jason. And Manni L. Perez, who plays Lucia, was nominated for Best Performance at The Game Awards for the role, ahead of the December 10 ceremony — the first time a GTA lead has been nominated in the category.

How to find them all

Most of the guests are voices, so the way to find them is to listen. Drive with the radio on and cycle the stations: each host announces themselves, and the station list is the guest list. Talk to everyone in the clubs and the streets — the credits list 202 roles, and most of them are out there in the world, not in the story. And check the credits themselves: they are available from the in-game settings menu, and they are the first confirmation for most of the 202 roles, including Jason's actor, whose identity fans had debated since May 2024.

The guests are also a reminder of how the game was made. The credits show performers who auditioned as early as April 2022 without even knowing what the project was. Four years of secret sessions, two leads kept hidden until launch day, and a guest list that spans music, sport, comedy and drama — that is the scale of the production, and it is hiding in plain hearing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Bad Bunny in GTA 6?

Yes — twice. He plays Mitico Mario in the supporting cast and hosts Cocoteo FM as El Martillo.

Who is the NFL player in GTA 6?

Tyreek Hill, who voices Maleek Rivers, a wide receiver for the fictional Vice City Manatees.

Does "Weird Al" Yankovic host a radio station in GTA 6?

Yes. He is DJ Al on Vice City FM, the '80s station, alongside Brigitte Nielsen. He also wrote in-game commercials and voices the Burger Shot ads.

The bottom line: Leonida is full of famous faces, and most of them are voices. Bad Bunny, Tyreek Hill, Weird Al, Lana Del Rey, Jason Sudeikis — turn the radio up, talk to everyone, and read the credits. The guest list is the game.

RSVP Card Wording Examples

By GuestListPro · Updated October 2026 · 5 min read

Getting RSVPs back on time is half the battle of wedding planning. The secret? Make replying effortless. Clear wording, a firm deadline, and — for 2026 — a digital option. Here are copy-paste examples for every style.

Formal RSVP Wording

For traditional, black-tie, or classic weddings:

The favour of a reply is requested
before the first of June, two thousand twenty-six

M________________________

☐ Joyfully accepts    ☐ Regretfully declines

Number of guests attending: ____

Casual RSVP Wording

For relaxed, outdoor, or modern weddings:

Can't wait to celebrate with you!
Please let us know by June 1st:

☐ Wouldn't miss it!    ☐ Sorry, can't make it

Name(s): ________________________
Song that gets you on the dance floor: ________________

RSVP With Meal Choices

If you're offering plated dinner options, include them on the RSVP — it saves you chasing 100 people later:

Please reply by June 1st

☐ Accepts with pleasure    ☐ Declines with regret

Please initial your entrée choice:
____ Chicken    ____ Beef    ____ Vegetarian    ____ Kids meal

Dietary restrictions: ________________

Digital RSVP Tips (2026)

Over 60% of couples now collect RSVPs online. If you go digital:

  • One link, everywhere: put your RSVP link on the invitation, wedding website, and save-the-date.
  • Keep the form short: name, attending yes/no, meal choice, done. Every extra field drops your response rate.
  • Set an auto-reminder: most RSVP tools can nudge non-responders at 2 weeks and 1 week before the deadline.
  • Deadline = 4 weeks before the wedding. Your caterer needs final headcounts 2–3 weeks out, and you need buffer time to chase stragglers.
  • Track everything in one place — use our free guest manager to log every RSVP, meal choice, and plus-one as replies come in.

What If Guests Don't RSVP?

They won't — about 20% of guests need chasing. Here's the playbook:

  1. One week after the deadline: send a friendly group message: "Hey! Final headcount is due Friday — can you let us know either way? 💛"
  2. Still nothing: a personal text or call. Keep it light, not guilt-trippy.
  3. Day of final count: no response = no seat. Mark them declined and move on — you did your part.

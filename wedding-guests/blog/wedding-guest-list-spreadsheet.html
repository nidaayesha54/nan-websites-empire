Wedding Guest List Spreadsheet: Free Template & a Better Alternative

By GuestListPro · October 2026 · 4 min read

Searching for a wedding guest list spreadsheet? You're not alone — it's one of the most-searched wedding planning terms. Here's the honest truth: a spreadsheet works, but it's the hard way. Let us show you both options.

What Columns Your Spreadsheet Needs

A proper wedding guest list spreadsheet needs these columns at minimum:

  • Name — full name as it appears on the invitation
  • Side — Bride / Groom / Mutual
  • RSVP status — Pending / Accepted / Declined / Maybe
  • Plus-ones — number, not just yes/no
  • Meal choice — critical for your caterer's final count
  • Table assignment — fill in after RSVPs close
  • Notes — dietary restrictions, accessibility, VIP flags

The Spreadsheet Problems Nobody Warns You About

  • Version chaos: you, your partner, and your mom all have different copies.
  • Manual math: every headcount requires re-counting rows and plus-ones.
  • No RSVP workflow: updating statuses one cell at a time as 150 replies trickle in.
  • Seating is separate: your chart lives in yet another tab (or on sticky notes).

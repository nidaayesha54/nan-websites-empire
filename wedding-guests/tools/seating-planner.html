Wedding Seating Chart Planner

Enter your table setup, pull in accepted guests from your guest list, and get auto-suggested table assignments. Drag chips between tables to fine-tune.

Seating tips

  • Group by side first — the auto-plan keeps Bride, Groom, and Mutual guests together.
  • Keep a buffer table — leave one table half-empty for last-minute additions.
  • Seat VIPs early — grandparents and wedding party get the best tables; drag chips to adjust.
  • Don't split couples — plus-ones are counted in each guest's headcount.

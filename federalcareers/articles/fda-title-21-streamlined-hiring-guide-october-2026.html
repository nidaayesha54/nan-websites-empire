FDA Title 21 Hiring 2026: How the Streamlined Hiring Authority Works

The FDA hires scientists and technical experts under Title 21 streamlined authority — a faster track created by the 21st Century Cures Act. Here is how it works, how pay is set, and how to apply.

The Food and Drug Administration doesn't hire all its scientists the competitive way. For critical scientific, technical, and regulatory roles, FDA uses Title 21 streamlined hiring authority — power granted by Title 21 of the U.S. Code (21 USC 379d-3a), as amended by section 3072 of the 21st Century Cures Act of 2016 and section 3624 of the Consolidated Appropriations Act of 2023.

A posting published October 9, 2026 shows the mechanics: streamlined authority, a career or career-conditional appointment, and Title 21 Pay Table 7 — Band A (GS-11), Band B (GS-12), based in Silver Spring, Maryland.

For scientists eyeing federal service, Title 21 is a purpose-built door. Here's how the authority works, how pay bands differ from the GS scale, and how to find these postings.

What Title 21 Authority Actually Is

Congress created Title 21 hiring because FDA competes with industry for scientific talent — and the standard competitive process was too slow. The authority lets FDA recruit directly for scientific, technical, and professional positions supporting its public-health mission, with streamlined procedures instead of rating and ranking.

It's an excepted path with permanent-track appointments: career or career-conditional status — the same tenure as competitive hires — with full federal benefits.

Pay Bands Instead of GS Grades

Title 21 positions are paid under dedicated pay tables, not the General Schedule. The October 2026 posting recruited on Title 21 Pay Table 7: Band A at the GS-11 level, Band B at GS-12 — bands calibrated to compete with private-sector scientific salaries.

Practical effect: read the band, not the GS equivalent, when comparing offers — Title 21 bands flex with the scientific labor market.

Who Should Apply — and How to Find Postings

Best fits: life scientists, chemists, engineers, statisticians, medical officers, and regulatory professionals — anyone whose credentials map to FDA's product-review, surveillance, and operations missions. Many Title 21 announcements are open to the public.

Find them on USAJOBS.gov: search “Food and Drug Administration” and read the “Additional Details” section — Title 21 postings name the authority explicitly. Set saved-search alerts; scientific postings can carry short windows. Questions on a live posting go to the listed HR contact (the October announcement lists phone and email).

How to Apply for FDA Title 21 Positions

  1. Search USAJOBS for FDA scientific postings. Filter to the Food and Drug Administration and scan “Additional Details” for the Title 21 authority citation.
  2. Confirm the band and pay table. Title 21 uses bands — verify the band's level against your target compensation before applying.
  3. Document scientific credentials precisely. Degrees, publications, lab and regulatory experience — HR verifies technical qualifications strictly.
  4. Set saved-search alerts. Title 21 scientific postings can have short application windows; email alerts keep you first in line.
  5. Contact the listed HR office with questions. Announcements include phone and email — use them before the closing date, not after.
  6. Submit a complete package early. Transcripts, licenses, and every document the announcement requires — incomplete means ineligible.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Title 21 hiring authority?

A streamlined hiring authority under Title 21 of the U.S. Code (21 USC 379d-3a), created by the 21st Century Cures Act, letting FDA appoint scientific and technical staff outside the normal competitive process.

How is Title 21 pay set?

Under Title 21 pay tables — bands rather than GS grades. A recent posting recruited at Band A (GS-11 equivalent) and Band B (GS-12 equivalent).

Who is eligible for Title 21 positions?

It depends on the announcement — many are open to the public. Scientific, medical, and regulatory backgrounds fit best.

Are Title 21 appointments permanent?

Selectees serve under a career or career-conditional appointment — the standard permanent-track federal appointment.

How is Title 21 different from competitive hiring?

It's streamlined: FDA can recruit scientific talent directly for critical roles without competitive rating and ranking — faster for both sides.

FEHB Open Season 2026: Nov 9–Dec 14 Deadlines & Premium Changes

FEHB Open Season for plan year 2027 runs November 9 through December 14, 2026. Enrollee premiums climb 10.9% on average — the third straight double-digit increase — and four new rules change how feds enroll, add family members, and use GLP-1 medications.

FEHB Open Season 2026 — the once-a-year window to change health benefits for plan year 2027 — runs November 9 through December 14, 2026. The employee share of FEHB premiums rises 10.9% on average: the third straight year of double-digit increases.

The 2027 Premium Numbers at a Glance

The enrollee-share increase works out to roughly $13.37 more per biweekly paycheck for self-only enrollees and $40.25 for family coverage. The government's share — about 75% of the total — rises 8.6%, putting total FEHB premium growth at 9.3%. Postal Service Health Benefits (PSHB) enrollees pay 8.2% more on average (total premiums +6.7%).

Some plans are rising far faster: MHBP is jumping 63% for self-only, 83% for family, and 105% for self-plus-one coverage. OPM attributes the increases to rising prices and utilization across medical services, growth in behavioral health care, and higher prescription drug spending — particularly GLP-1s and specialty drugs. FEDVIP stays cheap: dental +1%, vision +1.6%. In 2027, FEHB offers 118 plans from 45 carriers; PSHB offers 65 options from 17 carriers.

Averages are not your premium: the 10.9% is an enrollee-share average — your plan's increase could be much higher or lower. Compare plans rather than blindly renewing.

Four New Rules That Change How You Enroll

Beyond the price tags, OPM is making structural changes for 2027 that affect how you sign up:

  • Proof required for new family members. Under the FEHB Protection Act of 2025, adding a family member to FEHB/PSHB requires proof-of-eligibility documents (e.g., birth or marriage certificates).
  • FSAFEDS gets a debit card. FSA participants can now choose a Visa FSA card to pay eligible expenses at the point of sale instead of waiting for reimbursement. Note: FSAFEDS must still be re-enrolled every year.
  • New conditions for GLP-1 coverage. All carriers will require enrollees to complete an intensive behavioral therapy program before and during treatment with anti-obesity medications, with 12 to 22 therapy sessions covered per year.
  • Vaccine policy updates. Carriers were told to drop provider payments tied to pediatric and prenatal vaccination rates. CDC-recommended vaccines from in-network providers remain fully covered.

What to Do Before December 14

  1. Compare plans, not just premiums. After three double-digit hikes, favor plans where the government pays up to 75% of the total premium, then check providers and drug coverage.
  2. Gather family documentation now. Adding anyone to coverage? The new FEHB Protection Act proof requirement means eligibility documents must be ready at enrollment.
  3. Re-enroll in FSAFEDS separately. Health plan elections roll over; FSAs do not. Want the new Visa debit card? Act during the window.
  4. Read the GLP-1 terms. Confirm your plan's behavioral-therapy requirement and session coverage before December 14.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is FEHB Open Season 2026?

Open Season runs from November 9 through December 14, 2026. Changes you make take effect for plan year 2027.

How much are FEHB premiums rising for 2027?

FEHB enrollee premiums rise an average of 10.9% for 2027 (≈$13.37 more biweekly for self-only; $40.25 for family). PSHB enrollees see an average 8.2% increase.

Do I have to do anything if I am happy with my current FEHB plan?

Health plan enrollment rolls over automatically, but FSAFEDS accounts require yearly re-enrollment — and with a third straight year of double-digit premium hikes, comparing plans is advised.

What is the new FEHB proof-of-family-member rule?

Under the FEHB Protection Act of 2025, anyone adding a family member to FEHB or PSHB coverage during Open Season must submit documentation proving the person is eligible for coverage.

What are the new GLP-1 coverage rules in FEHB plans for 2027?

All carriers will require enrollees to complete an intensive behavioral therapy program before and during anti-obesity medication treatment, with 12 to 22 sessions covered per year.

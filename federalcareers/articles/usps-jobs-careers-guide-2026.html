USPS Jobs 2026: Pay Rates, October Job Fairs and How to Apply

The Postal Service — the second-largest employer in the United States — is hiring this October, with job fairs in Los Angeles on October 9 and 23 and holiday-season openings nationwide. Pay starts at $19.02 to $21.89 per hour, and you apply at usps.com/careers, not USAJOBS.

Why October Is the Best Time to Apply

USPS hiring peaks August through October: the agency recruits aggressively for the holiday surge, openings post in large volumes, and processing moves faster. November–December brings heavy overtime; summer is the slowest period. Apply now.

USPS Student Program: Apply October 1–19

Students and recent graduates should know about the USPS Emerging Professionals Program (EPP): positions are open to apply October 1 through October 19, 2026, with programs beginning mid-March and mid-July 2027. Roles include Data Analyst, Financial Analyst, Purchasing and Supply Management Specialist, and Treasury Analyst — professional-track careers for bachelor's and graduate degree holders in supply chain, accounting, business, finance, economics, and related fields. Search keyword "EPP" at usps.com/careers.

Basic Requirements

  • Age: Must be 18 years of age or older.
  • Availability: Must be available to work weekends and holidays — especially during the holiday season.
  • Screening: Must pass a drug screening and criminal background investigation.
  • Application: Applications are accepted online only at usps.com/careers. USPS does not use USAJOBS.

How to Apply for USPS Jobs

  1. Go to usps.com/careers: This is the only official application portal. Ignore third-party sites charging fees — applying is free.
  2. Search jobs: Enter a keyword (like a city name) and your state, then hit Start. Postings update frequently, so check back often.
  3. Create your profile: USPS job-fair staff will help you build a profile in person at events like the October 9 and 23 Los Angeles fairs — or do it online anytime.
  4. Complete the assessment: Most positions require an online assessment after you apply. Complete it promptly.
  5. Attend a job fair: Fairs let you ask questions, get application help, and make an impression. Bring ID and be ready to talk about your availability.
USPS promotes from within: The agency emphasizes that accepting a non-career position is the first step toward a long career — USPS promotes largely from within, and entry roles like Mail Handler Assistant can lead to full-time regular positions with full benefits and retirement.

Career Growth at USPS

A postal job offers competitive pay, stability, and advancement across the agency. Converting to career status unlocks health insurance, retirement, paid leave, and life insurance — a clear ladder from $19-an-hour entry work to a secure career.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does USPS pay in 2026?

Current postings show $19.02/hour for Mail Handler Assistants, $21.21/hour for City Carrier Assistants, $21.44/hour for Postal Support Employees, and $21.89/hour for Rural Carrier Associates.

When are the USPS job fairs in October 2026?

Los Angeles is hosting job fairs on October 9 and October 23, 2026, from 9 a.m. to 1 p.m. at the LA District Office. USPS holds fairs nationwide — check about.usps.com newsroom for your area.

Do I apply for USPS jobs on USAJOBS?

No. USPS applications are accepted online only at usps.com/careers. USPS does not hire through USAJOBS.

What is the USPS Emerging Professionals Program?

A student and recent-graduate program with professional roles like Data Analyst and Financial Analyst. For the current cycle, positions are open to apply October 1–19, 2026, with programs starting March and July 2027.

What are the basic requirements for USPS jobs?

You must be 18 or older, available to work weekends and holidays, and able to pass a drug screening and criminal background investigation.

What OSTEM Internships Are — and What They Are Not

NASA runs two student programs that applicants often confuse. OSTEM internships are short-term, project-based placements — 10 weeks in summer, 15 weeks in spring and fall — where interns work on real agency missions (Artemis, Mars science, Earth observation, aeronautics) under NASA mentors. They are paid, but they are not a direct employment pipeline.

The Pathways Internship Program is the long-term route (minimum 12 weeks, typically multi-semester) with a pipeline to full-time civil service employment at NASA after graduation, posted on USAJOBS. OSTEM is the door to experience; Pathways is the door to a permanent career. Many students pursue both.

Eligibility: Who Can Apply

NASA's published requirements for OSTEM interns:

  • Citizenship: U.S. citizens only — no exceptions for international students.
  • Age: 18 or older by the session's published start date.
  • GPA: minimum 3.0 on a 4.0 scale.
  • Enrollment: enrolled full-time or part-time in an accredited certificate or degree program; recent high school graduates may apply for the summer session right after graduation, and recent college graduates for the four sessions after graduation.
  • Majors: a broad range of majors, STEM and non-STEM — you do not have to be a science or engineering student.

Typical hours: 8 a.m. to 5 p.m., Monday–Friday, unless the project description says otherwise.

Deadline warning: some third-party sites list different dates. NASA's own current internship page lists March 1, 2027 for Summer and May 24, 2027 for Fall — apply early and treat the agency page as the authority.

How to Apply for a NASA OSTEM Internship

  1. Create an account in the NASA STEM Gateway. This is the OSTEM application system — applications go through the gateway, not USAJOBS.
  2. Build a complete profile. Show real project experience — NASA assesses teamwork, problem solving, and self-management.
  3. Apply to multiple opportunities. NASA encourages it. Apply across centers (Johnson, Goddard, Ames, Kennedy) and interest areas — engineering, IT, communications, business, science.
  4. Watch the calendar. Summer 2027 closes March 1, 2027 (11:59 p.m. ET); Fall 2027 closes May 24, 2027.
  5. Prep for interviews. Check NASA's virtual information sessions for application and interview tips before you apply.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the NASA OSTEM internship paid?

Yes. Most NASA interns receive a paid stipend award based on academic level and session duration; NASA publishes no fixed stipend amount.

What is the difference between NASA OSTEM and NASA Pathways internships?

OSTEM = short-term projects (summer 10 weeks; spring/fall 15 weeks) for skills and mission exposure. Pathways = long-term program with a direct pipeline to full-time NASA employment.

Do I have to be a STEM major to apply for a NASA OSTEM internship?

No. NASA explicitly opens OSTEM internships to a broad range of majors, STEM and non-STEM.

What is the GPA requirement for NASA OSTEM internships?

Minimum 3.0 on a 4.0 scale (2.9 for Pathways). Applicants must be U.S. citizens and 18+ by the session start date.

Is the Spring 2027 NASA internship window still open?

No. The Spring 2027 OSTEM deadline was September 14, 2026. Still open: Summer 2027 (March 1, 2027) and Fall 2027 (May 24, 2027).

NOAA Hollings Scholarship 2027: $9,500/Year + Paid Internship — Applications Open

Applications are open for the 2027 Ernest F. Hollings Undergraduate Scholarship: $9,500 per year, a 10-week paid NOAA internship, and conference travel funding. Deadline: January 31, 2027.

The National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration (NOAA) has opened applications for the 2027 Ernest F. Hollings Undergraduate Scholarship — one of the most prestigious federal scholarships for science students, combining academic funding with a paid summer internship at a NOAA facility.

Applications opened on September 1, 2026, and the deadline is January 31, 2027, at 11:59 PM Eastern. With roughly three months left, now is the time to line up transcripts, draft the essay, and ask faculty for references.

The program's track record is strong: in April 2026, NOAA selected 128 Hollings scholars from 95 campuses across 40 states, Guam, and Washington, DC.

What the Scholarship Provides

Hollings scholars receive an academic award of up to $9,500 per year for two years of full-time study (junior and senior years). Between those years, scholars complete a 10-week, full-time paid summer internship at a NOAA facility nationwide — roughly $700 per week plus a housing subsidy — working on hands-on projects spanning NOAA's science, research, technology, policy, and education missions.

The award also includes travel funds for the program orientation, the annual Science & Education Symposium, and presenting internship research at national scientific conferences.

Eligibility Requirements

At the time of application, you must: be a U.S. citizen; be a full-time sophomore in a four-year program (or third-year student in a five-year program); hold a 3.0 or higher GPA; and have a declared major in a NOAA mission field — oceanic, environmental, biological, or atmospheric sciences, mathematics, engineering, remote sensing, computer and information science, physical and social sciences, or science teacher education.

For the 2027 cycle, eligible applicants should have an anticipated bachelor's graduation date between December 2028 and August 2029. Community college students planning to transfer to a four-year university in Fall 2027 as juniors are also eligible.

How Applications Are Evaluated

Each application is reviewed by three independent panel members scoring on a 100-point scale. The published criteria: relevant coursework (30%), the education plan and statement of career interest essay (30%), academic references (20%), and additional experience — extracurriculars, honors, volunteer work, and communication skills (20%).

That weighting is your strategy guide: the essay and coursework together are 60% of your score. NOAA's Office of Education also runs informational webinars with application tips — worth attending before you submit.

How to Apply for the 2027 Hollings Scholarship

  1. Confirm your eligibility now. Check citizenship, enrollment status, GPA, major, and anticipated graduation date (December 2028–August 2029 for the 2027 cycle) against NOAA's requirements.
  2. Request your two academic references early. References must come from faculty members — not family, friends, fellow students, or NOAA employees (unless also faculty). Ask before Thanksgiving.
  3. Gather unofficial transcripts from every institution attended; you will upload them into the online application.
  4. Draft the education-plan and career-interest essay. At 30% of your score, this is the highest-leverage piece — connect your coursework to NOAA's mission explicitly.
  5. Submit the complete application by January 31, 2027, 11:59 PM EST. Incomplete applications are not considered, so submit days early.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the deadline for the 2027 Hollings Scholarship?

Applications for the 2027 Ernest F. Hollings Undergraduate Scholarship close on January 31, 2027, at 11:59 PM EST — including all transcripts and reference forms.

How much is the Hollings Scholarship worth?

Scholars receive up to $9,500 per academic year for their junior and senior years, plus a 10-week paid summer internship at a NOAA facility (about $700/week with a housing subsidy) and travel funds for conferences.

Who is eligible for the Hollings Scholarship?

U.S. citizens who are full-time sophomores (or third-year students in five-year programs) at accredited U.S. colleges, with a 3.0+ GPA and a declared major in a NOAA mission field — oceanic, atmospheric, environmental, biological, and related sciences, plus math, engineering, and computer science.

Can community college students apply?

Yes. Community college students planning to transfer to a four-year university in Fall 2027 as juniors are eligible for the 2027 cycle, provided they meet the other requirements.

What does the Hollings application require?

A completed online application, an education-plan and career-interest essay, unofficial transcripts, and two academic references from faculty — references from family, friends, or NOAA employees (unless also faculty) are not accepted.

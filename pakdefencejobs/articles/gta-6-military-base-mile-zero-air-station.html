GTA 6 Military Base: Mile Zero Air Station and the Jet

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Every Grand Theft Auto has a place you are not supposed to go, and in GTA 6 that place is Mile Zero Air Station. It sits on Goose Key in the far southwest of Leonida, a huge military airfield modelled on the real Naval Air Station Key West — and inside its fences are the two most powerful vehicles in the game: a fighter jet and a battle tank. This guide explains where the base is, how to get in, what is waiting inside, and why the man breaking in has a personal reason to know his way around a base.

The base is not a secret. It is on the map from the start, its runways are visible from miles away, and you can drive to it as early as Chapter 1. The hard part is not finding it. The hard part is leaving with something.

Where the base is

Mile Zero Air Station stands on Goose Key in Mariana County, in the southwestern corner of the map, southeast of Key Lento. The name is a joke with a real root: Mile Zero is the start of the highway through the Florida Keys, and the base's real-world model — Naval Air Station Key West — sits at the end of the same chain of islands. In the game, the runways are the landmark: a huge stretch of concrete at the edge of the map that is impossible to miss once you know to look for it.

You can reach it as early as Chapter 1, once the Gone Fishing mission unlocks free roam and you are playing as Jason. Set it as your GPS destination and drive — the in-game GPS will take you straight there. The base is not locked behind story progress beyond that first mission, which is why it has become the first big dare of every new player's game.

Getting in — and what it costs

Getting through the gate is easy, and that is the trap. You can simply ram a car through the main gate, exactly as players did at Fort Zancudo in GTA 5 — but doing it puts you at a four-star wanted level immediately, and the base answers with military gunfire. The flight line, where the prizes sit, is close to the main gate, which is the only mercy the base offers: you do not have to fight your way across the whole airfield.

There is a quieter way in. Guides that have played the released game describe a stunt-jump entrance on the southeastern side of the base, which lets you cross the fence without the frontal assault. It is safer, but it is still a military base: the soldiers inside are armed, the vehicles are armoured, and the wanted level follows you either way. Speed, not stealth, is what gets you out — the whole run is measured in seconds, not minutes.

The prizes inside

Two prizes wait inside, and both are worth the risk. The jet is the P-996 Lazer, a GTA staple that looks like a real F-16 fighter — twin tails instead of the F-16's single one — armed with machine guns and missiles. The moment you climb into it, your wanted level rises to five stars, and the base answers with tanks, soldiers, missiles and gunfire. Getting it into the sky before you are shot down is the whole challenge.

The tank is the Hauners Rhino, a blend of the real M1 Abrams and Germany's Leopard 2A5. Stealing one unlocks the "Challenging Tank" bronze trophy, which is the game's way of admitting how hard the theft is. The base also holds an armoured personnel carrier, a Buzzard attack helicopter, a Mammoth Patriot off-roader and a Botticher 015A plane — a full motor pool for anyone who survives the gate.

Why Jason knows his way around

There is a story reason the base feels personal. Rockstar's own bio for Jason Duval says he joined the army to escape his troubled teens, and now works for drug runners in the Leonida Keys. He is ex-Army, and the base is the world he left behind — which is one reason the theft feels like more than a stunt. The game never says Jason served at Mile Zero, but the connection is there for anyone who reads the character first.

The name North Fort Law is also confirmed — it appears on a shirt in Rockstar's own material, and it is a district name from the very first GTA game in 1997, where a mission sent you into a base to steal top-secret files. But a full visitable base under that name is not confirmed, and the base players can actually enter is Mile Zero. Do not confuse the two: one is a name on a shirt, the other is a runway full of jets.

What to do with a stolen jet

The jet is the fastest way to cross Leonida, and the most dangerous way to be seen. Once you are airborne, the military and the police pursue relentlessly, unloading missiles and gunfire on your aircraft — and you have your own weapons to answer with. The smart move is altitude and distance: get high, get away from the base, and land somewhere the search cannot follow. The wanted system in GTA 6 works on what the police witness, so breaking line of sight and leaving the area is the core of any escape.

And remember the money rule before you go. Pocket cash is lost if you die or get arrested, so bank it at an ATM first. A base run that ends in the Wasted screen costs you more than a jet — it costs you everything you were carrying.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is the military base in GTA 6?

Mile Zero Air Station, on Goose Key in Mariana County, in the southwestern corner of the map, southeast of Key Lento. It is modelled on the real Naval Air Station Key West and is reachable in Chapter 1 after the Gone Fishing mission unlocks free roam.

Can you steal a fighter jet in GTA 6?

Yes. The P-996 Lazer sits on the flight line near the main gate. Ramming the gate gives you four stars, and climbing into the jet raises it to five — so be quick, and get airborne before the base's tanks and missiles find you.

Is there a tank in GTA 6?

Yes. The Hauners Rhino is inside Mile Zero Air Station, and stealing one unlocks the "Challenging Tank" bronze trophy. The base also holds an armoured personnel carrier, a Buzzard helicopter, a Mammoth Patriot and a Botticher 015A plane.

The bottom line: Mile Zero Air Station is GTA 6's Fort Zancudo — a huge base, two legendary prizes, and a wanted level that punishes hesitation. Bank your cash, pick your entrance, and move fast: the jet is thirty seconds from the gate, and the whole base is trying to stop you getting there.

PMA 159 Long Course: Registration Closes 23 October 2026 — Last-Date Checklist

Online registration for the 159 PMA Long Course — the Pakistan Army's route to a regular commission as Second Lieutenant — closes on 23 October 2026. Registration opened 14 September 2026 at joinpakarmy.gov.pk, and only three days remain. This last-date checklist covers eligibility, documents, the exact five registration steps, what happens after you apply — and the fraud warning every candidate must read first.

Do not wait for the last hours: portal server load and a rejected photo are the two most common last-day failures. Register now and keep the final day as a safety margin.

Eligibility Checklist

Confirm every point below before you fill the form:

  • Gender and nationality: male citizens of Pakistan; domicile holders of Azad Jammu & Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan are eligible.
  • Age on 1 May 2027: 17–22 years for intermediate candidates; 17–23 for two-year graduation holders; 17–24 for four-year degree holders (BS, BBA, BA Hons and equivalents).
  • Education: minimum 60% marks in FA/FSc or equivalent. Candidates holding a domicile of, and who actually studied in, specified areas — Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan, Chitral, Dir, Kohistan, Tharparkar, Rajanpur and other listed areas — need 55%.
  • Result awaited: students whose results are pending can apply through the Hope Certificate / "Result Awaited" option on the form.
  • Height: minimum 5 feet 4 inches (162.5 cm); weight must meet the BMI standard.
  • Marital status: unmarried; married serving members of the armed forces aged 20 and above are eligible.
  • Dual nationality: may apply, but foreign nationalities, green cards and residence permits must be surrendered on final selection.
Ineligible: anyone twice rejected by ISSB, anyone who has failed the initial tests three times, candidates dismissed from government service, the convicted, and anyone caught with forged or tampered certificates.

The 5 Registration Steps

1
Open joinpakarmy.gov.pk, press "Apply Now" and select the PMA Long Course.
2
Fill the form: personal details, CNIC or NADRA Form-B (under-18 candidates need a computerised Form-B plus a parent's CNIC), address, domicile district, and education with percentages. Choose "Result Awaited" if your result is pending.
3
Upload the photo: one 1x1 passport-size photograph in formal dress, blue or white background, as a JPEG under 300 KB. This is the step that most often fails — prepare the file before you start.
4
Choose your AS&RC test centre and submit the form.
5
After 5–7 working days, open "Registration Status", select the course, enter your CNIC and download your roll number slip and fee challan. Pay the fee at any National Bank of Pakistan branch — the amount is printed on your challan.

What Happens After You Register

Your roll number slip tells you your test centre, date and time. Initial tests for PMA 159 run from 5 October to 15 December 2026 (weekdays only) at Army Selection and Recruitment Centres across Pakistan. Arrive an hour early — mobile phones, calculators, smart watches and bags are not allowed inside the centre. The full breakdown of every stage — the computerized test pattern, physical standards, medical and interview, and the 4-day ISSB — is covered in our PMA 159 Initial Test Schedule & Pattern Guide; read it before your test date so nothing on the day surprises you.

Documents for test day: original CNIC or Form-B, original Matric and FA/FSc certificates with detailed marks sheets, original domicile, paid fee challan, roll number slip, and three attested passport-size photographs.

Fraud Warning: Read This Before You Pay

Recruitment scams peak in the last days of any deadline. Protect yourself:

  • The Army collects its fee only through the fee challan generated from your own registration, paid at National Bank of Pakistan branches into the account titled "Registration and Examination Fee Personnel Administration Directorate".
  • Any demand to send money to a JazzCash or Easypaisa number, a personal bank account, or a mobile wallet is fraud. The Army never collects fees this way.
  • Nobody can sell you a seat, a test date, or an ISSB pass. An "agent" promising selection for payment is lying.
  • The only official registration site is joinpakarmy.gov.pk (ending in .gov.pk). Do not trust look-alike sites.
  • Report fake calls or messages to the Join Pak Army help desk on 051-5686225, webmaster@joinpakarmy.gov.pk, or the GHQ Facilitation Centre at 051-111-130-786.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date for PMA 159 Long Course registration?

23 October 2026. Online registration at joinpakarmy.gov.pk opened on 14 September 2026 and closes on 23 October 2026 — registration is online only, with no walk-in option.

Can I still register on 23 October 2026?

Yes, the portal stays open until the closing date, but do not leave it to the last hours — server load and missing documents are the two most common last-day failures.

What is the age limit for PMA 159 on 1 May 2027?

17 to 22 years for intermediate candidates, 17 to 23 for two-year graduation holders, and 17 to 24 for four-year degree holders (BS, BBA, BA Hons and equivalents).

What marks are required for PMA 159?

Minimum 60% in FA/FSc or equivalent. Candidates holding a domicile of, and who actually studied in, specified areas such as Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan, Chitral, Dir, Kohistan, Tharparkar and Rajanpur need 55%. Result-awaited candidates may apply through the Hope Certificate / Result Awaited option.

How do I pay the PMA 159 registration fee?

Only through the fee challan generated from your own registration, paid at a National Bank of Pakistan branch. The Army collects its fee only this way — any JazzCash, Easypaisa or personal-account demand is fraud; report it to 051-5686225.

What photo is required for online registration?

One 1x1 passport-size photograph in formal dress, with a blue or white background, uploaded as a JPEG under 300 KB.

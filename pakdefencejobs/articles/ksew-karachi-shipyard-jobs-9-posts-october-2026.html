KSEW Karachi Shipyard Jobs 2026 — 9 Posts, Apply by 2 Nov

Karachi Shipyard & Engineering Works Limited (KSEW) — Pakistan's only shipyard, under the Ministry of Defence Production — is hiring for 9 vacancies across 7 designations on contract: a senior welding manager, supervisory/technical posts for engineers and diploma holders, and trade posts for Middle/Matric holders. Applications must reach KSEW within 15 days of the ad's publication (computed as 2 November 2026).

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nationality: Pakistani nationals; the shipyard is under the Ministry of Defence Production.
  • Education: as per the post-wise table — PEC registration required for the BE-based posts.
  • Experience: mandatory for every post — 5–15 years for Manager/DM Welding, 5 years for HVAC Supervisor, 3+ years for AM Dock Maintenance, 2–3 years for Technical Assistant and the three trade posts.
  • Age: up to 55 years for Manager/DM (Welding); up to 45 years for all others. The ad allows age relaxation for outstanding candidates.

How to Apply — Step by Step

1
Apply online: submit your application on the official KSEW careers page at www.karachishipyard.com.pk/page/careers.
2
Or apply by courier: send your CV with two passport-size photographs, Board/HEC-verified certificates/diplomas/degrees, CNIC and domicile to: General Manager (Human Resources), Karachi Shipyard & Engineering Works Limited, West Wharf Dockyard Road, Karachi – 74000.
3
Watch the window: applications must reach KSEW within 15 days of publication (computed as 2 November 2026). Courier takes days — dispatch early.
4
Only shortlisted candidates are called for test/interview; no TA/DA is admissible.

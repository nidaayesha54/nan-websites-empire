FC Balochistan AD Intelligence Jobs 2026 – 4 Posts, Apply by 31 Oct

Frontier Corps Balochistan (West) is recruiting 4 officers as Assistant Director Intelligence in BPS-17. This is a rare officer-grade civilian-style opening in the FC's intelligence setup — a Grade-17 post, which means it carries the pay scale, allowances and protocol of a gazetted officer. The announcement appeared as a job notice in the Express Tribune Karachi edition of 18 October 2026, and the application deadline is 31 October 2026. If you hold an MA or MSc and have intelligence-related experience — especially connected with western Balochistan — this one is worth your immediate attention.

The Post: What an Assistant Director Intelligence Does

The Assistant Director Intelligence post sits in the FC's intelligence apparatus in western Balochistan — a region where security, border management and coordination with civil administration are daily work. At BPS-17, the role is an officer-grade position: expect responsibilities around intelligence collection and collation, field liaison, reporting, and briefing senior officers. The advertisement's emphasis is clear on one point: candidates with previous intelligence-related experience, particularly experience connected with western Balochistan, will be preferred. That wording signals the FC wants people who already understand the terrain, the tribal dynamics and the security environment of the west — not fresh graduates it must train from zero.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Education: MA or MSc degree. The advertisement does not restrict the discipline in the sourced coverage, but degrees related to international relations, defence and strategic studies, political science or security studies will naturally strengthen a file.
  • Experience: previous intelligence-related experience is preferred, with explicit weight for experience connected with western Balochistan. If you have served in an intelligence, security or law-enforcement role in the region, say so prominently in your application.
  • Nationality: Pakistani citizen, as with all federal government appointments.
  • Age limit: not specified in the available coverage — confirm it from the original newspaper advertisement before assuming eligibility.
Do not use the wrong portal: the FC Balochistan (North) website exists, but it belongs to a different unit (HQ in Quetta) and is not the channel for this West-unit recruitment. Sending your documents to the wrong headquarters is the fastest way to have them ignored.

How to Apply — Step by Step

1
Read the original advertisement. Get the Express Tribune issue of 18 October 2026 (Karachi edition, page 21) — from a library, a newspaper archive or the FC's notice board. Confirm the exact application procedure, the postal address, any prescribed form, and the age limit.
2
Prepare your dossier. Attested copies of your MA/MSc degree and transcripts, CNIC, domicile, experience certificates (especially any intelligence or security-role certificates from western Balochistan), and recent photographs.
3
Write a proper covering application. Address it to the recruitment office at HQ FC Balochistan (West), Dalbandin. State the post name — Assistant Director Intelligence (BPS-17) — and highlight your intelligence-related experience and regional familiarity in the first paragraph.
4
Send it well before 31 October 2026. Dalbandin is a remote posting; postal delays are real. Use registered post or courier with tracking, and keep the receipt. Late applications for FC recruitments are not entertained.

What Happens After You Apply

For officer-grade FC civilian posts, the usual pattern is shortlisting on qualifications and experience, followed by a test or interview at the FC headquarters. Because the advertisement is short on procedural detail, shortlisted candidates should expect direct intimation from the FC. Keep your phone number and address current on the application, and be ready to travel to Dalbandin for any interview stage. No TA/DA is typically paid for FC recruitment interviews unless the advertisement says otherwise.

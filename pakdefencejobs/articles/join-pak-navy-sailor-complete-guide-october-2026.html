Join Pakistan Navy as Sailor 2026 — Complete Guide

Sailors crew the Pakistan Navy's ships and shore establishments — operations, technical, supply and medical branches. This complete guide covers branches, eligibility, the test pattern and every selection stage.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » Pakistan Defence Jobs » Articles

The Pakistan Navy recruits Sailors in batches every year for its ships and shore establishments. Sailors serve in operations, technical, supply, medical and other branches, and the career offers professional training, sea service, and long-term benefits. This guide explains the branches, the eligibility rules, the entrance test pattern, and the full selection process — so you can prepare before the next batch window opens.

Sailor Branches — Where Sailors Serve

Sailors serve across the Navy's branches. Operations branches work on ships' weapons, communications and seamanship. Technical branches maintain engines, electrical systems and weapons equipment. Supply branches handle logistics, stores and administration. Medical branches support naval hospitals and shipboard medical care. Your branch is assigned based on your education, test performance and the Navy's requirements — technical branches generally require Matric Science, while some branches accept Matric Arts.

Eligibility at a Glance

Candidates must be Pakistani male citizens and unmarried. Education is Matric — Science for technical branches, Arts for some other branches. The minimum height reported for sailor recruitment is 5 feet 4 inches (162.5 cm), with weight as per BMI standards. Age limits vary by branch and batch and are stated in each batch advertisement, so always check the current batch notice before applying.

The Entrance Test Pattern

The Pakistan Navy entrance test is conducted on computers at Recruitment Centres and has two parts. The intelligence test (verbal and non-verbal) is the elimination round — you must pass it to unlock the academic paper. The academic test then follows the candidate's stream: Matric Science candidates are tested in English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry and General Knowledge, while Matric Arts candidates are tested in Matric Arts subjects, General Science, General Mathematics, English and General Knowledge. Prepare both parts — many candidates clear the academic paper but fail the intelligence round.

Selection Stages — Step by Step

1
Online registration: register at www.joinpaknavy.gov.pk during a batch window, or visit the nearest Pakistan Navy Recruitment & Selection Centre for manual registration.
2
Registration slip: download and print your registration slip — it carries your test date, time and centre.
3
Initial test: intelligence test followed by the academic test at the assigned centre. Candidates are notified of the date and location by SMS.
4
Physical endurance test (PET): a physical fitness test to the Navy standard.
5
Medical examination and interview: medical screening and an interview at the selection centre.
6
Personality test and temporary selection: a personality assessment, followed by temporary selection of successful candidates.
7
Final medical and final selection: final medical examination at the Naval Medical Checking Centre, Karachi, and final selection of fully fit candidates.

Documents to Carry

Carry your original Matric or Middle marksheets and certificates, CNIC or Form-B, domicile certificate, four passport-size photographs with a blue background, and the original CNIC of your father or guardian. Bring attested copies of your educational documents as well. Original documents are verified at the test centre — photocopies alone are not enough.

Training and Career

Selected sailors undergo professional training at naval training establishments before joining ships and shore units. The career offers a competitive salary, free medical care, accommodation, sea-service allowances, courses and promotion through the ranks, and pension benefits. Technical training in the Navy builds engineering and technical skills that remain valuable throughout a career.

How to Prepare

Revise Matric-level English, Mathematics, Physics and Chemistry (or the Arts-stream subjects), practise intelligence-test questions daily, and build physical fitness early — running and bodyweight exercises take weeks to improve. Keep your documents ready well before the next batch window opens, so you can register the day it opens.

Reminder: Navy recruitment never charges money for registration, roll number slips or selection. Anyone asking for payment is running a scam — report them and apply only through joinpaknavy.gov.pk.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join Pakistan Navy as a Sailor?

Register online at joinpaknavy.gov.pk during an announced batch window, download your registration slip, and appear for the intelligence and academic tests at the nearest Pakistan Navy Recruitment & Selection Centre, followed by physical, medical and interview stages.

What is the test pattern for Pakistan Navy Sailor?

The initial test has two parts: an intelligence test (verbal and non-verbal) and an academic test. Matric Science candidates are tested in English, Maths, Physics, Chemistry and General Knowledge; Matric Arts candidates in Matric Arts, General Science, General Math, English and General Knowledge.

What are the physical standards for Pakistan Navy Sailor?

Candidates must pass a physical endurance test and a medical examination, with height and weight standards as per Navy rules. The minimum height reported for sailor recruitment is 5 feet 4 inches (162.5 cm), with weight as per BMI.

What documents do I need for Pakistan Navy Sailor registration?

Original Matric or Middle marksheets and certificates, CNIC or Form-B, domicile certificate, four passport-size photographs with a blue background, and the original CNIC of your father or guardian.

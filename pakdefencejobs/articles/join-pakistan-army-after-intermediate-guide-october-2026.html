Join Pakistan Army After Intermediate 2026: PMA Long Course vs AFNS vs Soldier — Complete Comparison Guide

Every year, hundreds of thousands of young Pakistanis ask the same question: how do I join the Pakistan Army after Intermediate? The answer is not one route but several — the PMA Long Course for officers, the AFNS for women, direct soldier (sipahi) recruitment for Matric and Intermediate pass candidates, medical corps entries and technical cadet schemes. This guide compares every route open in the 2026 recruitment cycle so you can pick the one that fits your age, qualification and ambition.

The honest starting point: your marks, gender and age decide your routes. Read the comparison, find your row, and apply to the route you genuinely qualify for.

Route 1: PMA Long Course — The Officer Route

The Pakistan Military Academy Long Course is the flagship officer induction: two years of training at PMA Kakul, after which cadets are commissioned as Second Lieutenants. It is the route every Intermediate-pass young man dreams of — and the most competitive. The cycle runs twice a year (the 159 PMA Long Course registration closed on 23 October 2026), with selection through the initial tests at AS&RCs, ISSB, final medical and the GHQ interview. Prepare academically (English, maths, general knowledge), physically (the ISSB's outdoor tasks are demanding) and mentally (the ISSB assesses personality, not just marks).

Route 2: Soldier (Sipahi) — The Direct Route

Soldier recruitment is the Army's largest intake: Matric and Intermediate pass young men register at their nearest Army Selection and Recruitment Centre (AS&RC) or online, pass the initial written, physical and medical tests, and join regimental training. The current soldier/sipahi registration window carries a November 2026 deadline — and soldiers can later rise through the ranks, with routes to Junior Commissioned Officer (JCO) and even officer courses for the outstanding. For candidates whose marks do not reach the PMA bar, soldier is the honest, honourable way into the same uniform.

Route 3: AFNS — The Women's Officer Route

The Armed Forces Nursing Service (AFNS) is the commissioned-officer route for women with FSc (Pre-Medical): selected candidates train in military nursing and serve as officers in the Army Medical Corps setup. It is the principal route for young women who want a commission straight after Intermediate — check the AFNS notice for the current cycle's age, marital-status and academic conditions.

Route 4: JCO and Specialist Entries

Naib Khateeb (JCO) recruitment — currently open with a November 2026 deadline — is the route for religiously qualified candidates into the Junior Commissioned Officer ranks. Alongside it, the Army periodically announces technical, medical and clerical entries (nursing assistants, technicians, clerks, drivers) through the same AS&RC network. These are worth watching if your qualification is vocational rather than academic.

How to Apply — The Standard Drill

Almost every Army route follows the same sequence:

1
Register: online at the Army's recruitment portal or in person at your nearest AS&RC — within the announced registration window.
2
Initial tests: written/academic test, physical test and initial medical at the AS&RC.
3
ISSB (officer routes): four days of psychological, group and outdoor assessment at Kohat, Gujranwala, Malir or Quetta.
4
Final medical and interview: at the Combined Military Hospital and GHQ, followed by the final merit.
Documents: CNIC/B-Form, Matric and Intermediate certificates and DMCs, domicile, photographs and — for soldier entries — the documents listed in the current registration notice. Never pay anyone claiming to arrange selection; Army recruitment is strictly on merit.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the routes to join the Pakistan Army after Intermediate?

PMA Long Course (officer), AFNS (women's nursing officer), soldier/sipahi recruitment, Naib Khateeb (JCO), and technical/medical entries — depending on gender, age and marks.

What is the age limit for the PMA Long Course?

17 to 23 years for male unmarried candidates with Intermediate or Bachelor's qualification.

Is soldier recruitment open now?

Yes — soldier/sipahi and Naib Khateeb (JCO) registrations are open with November 2026 deadlines. Register at your nearest AS&RC or online.

What is the ISSB?

The Inter Services Selection Board — a four-day psychological, group and outdoor assessment at Kohat, Gujranwala, Malir or Quetta for officer-route candidates.

Can women join the Pakistan Army after Intermediate?

Yes — through the Armed Forces Nursing Service (AFNS) with FSc (Pre-Medical), among other announced entries.

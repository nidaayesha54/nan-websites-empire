Join Pakistan Army 159 PMA Long Course 2026: Registration Closing 23 Oct — Eligibility & Selection

The 159 PMA Long Course is the officer-entry route to PMA Kakul — online registration closes 23 October 2026. Eligibility, selection stages and how to register.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Pakistan Defence Jobs » Articles

The Pakistan Army has opened online registration for the 159 PMA Long Course — the flagship route to a commissioned officer's career at the Pakistan Military Academy (PMA), Kakul, Abbottabad. Online registration runs from 14 September 2026 to 23 October 2026 through the official portal www.joinpakarmy.gov.pk.

Candidates from Punjab, Sindh, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, Balochistan, Azad Jammu & Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan can register and select their nearest Army Selection and Recruitment Centre (AS&RC). If you want to join the Army as a commissioned officer after intermediate or graduation, this is the course to target — and the deadline is approaching fast.

Who Can Apply

  • Nationality: Male citizens of Pakistan (and Azad Jammu & Kashmir / Gilgit-Baltistan, as per the advertisement).
  • Marital status: Unmarried, as per the course conditions.
  • Education: Intermediate or higher education with the minimum marks/division prescribed in the official advertisement — check the course notice for the exact requirement.
  • Age: Within the age bracket fixed for the 159 PMA Long Course in the official advertisement.
  • Dual nationality: Dual nationality holders must surrender their other nationality upon final selection.

How to Register Online

1
Visit the official portal www.joinpakarmy.gov.pk.
2
Find 159 PMA Long Course and click the Registration link.
3
Fill the form with your personal details, educational record and contact information accurately.
4
Select your nearest Army Selection and Recruitment Centre (AS&RC) as the test centre.
5
Submit the form and keep checking the website to print your registration / roll number slip, which carries your test date and time.

Selection Process: What to Expect

Stage 1 — Preliminary Selection at AS&RC

  • Written / intelligence tests: Verbal, non-verbal and academic tests covering English, Mathematics, Pakistan Studies, Islamiat and General Knowledge.
  • Physical test: 1.6 km run in 8 minutes, 15 push-ups in 2 minutes, 20 sit-ups in 2 minutes, 3 chin-ups in 2 minutes, and ditch crossing (7'4" × 7'4", 4 feet deep).
  • Initial medical: Basic medical checkup at the AS&RC.
  • Initial interview: Conducted by the recruitment officer.

Stage 2 — ISSB Tests

Candidates who clear the preliminary stage are shortlisted for the Inter-Services Selection Board (ISSB) — a 4 to 5 day evaluation of personality, leadership qualities and psychological profile through psychological tests, group discussions, GTO tasks and a final interview. Recommended candidates then face the final medical board before joining PMA Kakul for training.

Start preparing now: With registration closing on 23 October 2026, register early and begin physical training immediately — the 1.6 km run and strength tests eliminate many otherwise strong candidates.

AS&RCs Across Pakistan

Army Selection and Recruitment Centres operate in Peshawar, Rawalpindi, Lahore, Multan, Faisalabad, Karachi, Hyderabad, Pano Aqil, Quetta, Khuzdar, Dera Ismail Khan, Gilgit and Muzaffarabad, among other stations. Choose the centre nearest to you during online registration — your roll number slip will confirm the test date and reporting time.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date for 159 PMA Long Course registration 2026?

Online registration for the 159 PMA Long Course closes on 23 October 2026 at www.joinpakarmy.gov.pk.

Where is the training for PMA Long Course held?

Training is held at the Pakistan Military Academy (PMA), Kakul, Abbottabad.

What are the physical test standards for PMA Long Course?

The preliminary physical test includes a 1.6 km run in 8 minutes, 15 push-ups in 2 minutes, 20 sit-ups in 2 minutes, 3 chin-ups in 2 minutes and ditch crossing.

What happens after the preliminary selection?

Shortlisted candidates go to the ISSB for 4–5 days of psychological tests, group tasks and interviews, followed by a final medical board.

Can dual nationality holders apply for PMA Long Course?

Dual nationality holders must surrender their other nationality upon final selection.

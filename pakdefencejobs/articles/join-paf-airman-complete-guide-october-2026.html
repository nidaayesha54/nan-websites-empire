Join PAF as Airman 2026 — Trades, Eligibility & Selection Guide

Airmen are the backbone of the Pakistan Air Force — aircraft maintenance, air defence, IT, logistics, medical and transport. This complete guide covers trades, eligibility, physical standards and every selection stage.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » Pakistan Defence Jobs » Articles

The Pakistan Air Force (PAF) recruits Airmen every year across technical, administrative, medical and support trades. Airmen keep the force running: they maintain aircraft, operate radar and air defence systems, run IT networks and logistics, staff medical facilities, and manage transport operations. Along with national service, the career offers job security, professional training and long-term benefits. This guide explains the trades, the eligibility rules, the physical standards, and the full selection process — so you can prepare before the next registration window opens.

Airman Trades — What Airmen Actually Do

Airmen work across the force's operating and support functions. Technical trades maintain aircraft, engines, avionics and ground equipment. Air defence trades operate radar and air defence systems. IT and communication trades run networks and signals. Logistics and administrative trades manage supply, accounts and records. Medical trades staff PAF hospitals and medical facilities, and transport trades (including MTD, mechanical transport drivers) run the force's vehicle fleet. Your trade is chosen at registration, and the eligibility and test subjects follow the trade.

Eligibility at a Glance

Candidates must be Pakistani male citizens and unmarried. The age limit is generally 15 to 21 years, varying by trade. Education is Matric or Intermediate (Science) depending on the trade — technical trades require science subjects, while some administrative and support trades accept other combinations as stated in the trade advertisement. Height ranges from 163 cm to 188 cm depending on the trade, and vision must be 6/6. Candidates from Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan, former FATA, AJK and rural areas receive special relaxation as per PAF rules.

Selection Stages — Step by Step

1
Online registration: register at www.joinpaf.gov.pk, select your trade, choose the nearest PAF Information & Selection Centre, and print your registration slip.
2
Intelligence test: a computerised verbal and non-verbal intelligence test — the elimination round.
3
Academic test: trade-related subjects — for technical trades, English, Mathematics and Physics feature prominently.
4
Initial medical: a basic medical examination at the PAF Information & Selection Centre.
5
Physical fitness test: running, push-ups, sit-ups and chin-ups to the trade standard.
6
Interview: an interview at the selection centre assessing suitability and motivation.
7
Final medical and merit list: final medical examination, then a merit list and training call letter for selected candidates.

Documents to Carry

Carry your original Matric or Intermediate certificate and detailed marks certificate, domicile certificate, CNIC or B-Form (original and photocopy), a character certificate from your educational institution, recent passport-size photographs, and any trade-specific document such as a driving licence for MTD. Candidates who passed Matric or equivalent in 2023 or earlier must bring their original result cards. Original documents are verified at the test centre — photocopies alone are not enough.

Salary, Benefits and Career Growth

PAF airmen receive a competitive monthly salary with free housing and food, free medical care for the family, air and rail travel at 50% discount, courses and deputations abroad, and pension and retirement benefits. Outstanding performers qualify for further professional courses and promotion through the ranks, and technical training in the PAF builds skills that remain valuable after service.

How to Prepare

Focus on Matric Science subjects — English, Mathematics and Physics — and practise intelligence-test questions daily. Build physical fitness early: running and bodyweight exercises take weeks to improve, and the physical test eliminates unprepared candidates. Get medically fit and keep your documents in order well before the registration window opens, so you can apply the day it opens.

Reminder: PAF recruitment never charges money for registration, roll number slips or selection. Anyone asking for payment is running a scam — report them and apply only through joinpaf.gov.pk.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join PAF as an Airman?

Register online at joinpaf.gov.pk, select your trade, download your registration slip, and appear for the intelligence and academic tests at the nearest PAF Information & Selection Centre, followed by medical, physical and interview stages.

What is the age limit for PAF Airman?

Generally 15 to 21 years, varying by trade. Candidates from Balochistan, Gilgit-Baltistan, former FATA, AJK and rural areas receive special relaxation as per PAF rules.

What education is required for PAF Airman trades?

Matric or Intermediate (Science) depending on the trade. Technical trades require science subjects, while some administrative and support trades accept other combinations as stated in the trade advertisement.

What are the physical standards for PAF Airman?

Height 163 cm to 188 cm depending on the trade, vision 6/6, and a physical fitness test covering running, push-ups, sit-ups and chin-ups, plus medical fitness at each stage.

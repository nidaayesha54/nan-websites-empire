PAF K9 Specialist Jobs October 2026: Veterinary & Dog Handler Posts on Contract — Apply with CV

The Pakistan Air Force (PAF) has invited applications from dedicated, experienced and passionate professionals to join as K9 Specialist Personnel on a contract basis. The recruitment notice — published on 1 October 2026 — lists five categories of posts built around the PAF's K9 (working dog) programme: a Veterinary Doctor (DVM), a Veterinary Assistant, Dog Trainers, a Lascar Groomer and a Husbandry Professional. The initial contract period is one year, extendable based on performance and requirement.

Working-dog programmes are critical to airbase security — detection, patrol and guard dogs support the PAF's security apparatus at every base. For veterinary professionals and experienced dog handlers, this is a specialised route into a defence organisation that values real hands-on expertise over paperwork.

PAF K9 Specialist Posts — Positions & Eligibility

S. No.PositionEligibility
1DVM (Veterinary Doctor)DVM degree from a recognised university; registered with PVMC; minimum 2 years relevant experience; strong clinical and surgical skills
2Vet Assistant (Veterinary Assistant)FSc (Pre-Medical) or equivalent; diploma/certification in Vet Assistant; minimum 2 years experience; knowledge of animal first aid & care
3Lascar Groomer (Dog Handler & Groomer)Matric or equivalent; 2–3 years experience in dog handling and grooming; physically fit and animal-friendly
4Dog Trainers (K9 Trainers)Matric or equivalent; professional dog training certification preferred; minimum 2 years experience in training working dogs; knowledge of obedience, aggression control & tracking
5Husbandry Professional (Animal Husbandry)Matric or equivalent (minimum); diploma/certificate in Animal Husbandry preferred; 2–3 years experience in animal care, feeding & management; knowledge of animal nutrition, hygiene & husbandry practices
Note: Applicants must be physically fit, disciplined and committed. The experience bar is genuine — these posts require demonstrated working-dog experience, not just an interest in animals.

Contract Terms

  • Appointment type: Contract basis.
  • Initial period: One year, further extendable based on performance and requirement.
  • Selection: Only shortlisted candidates will be called for the test/interview.
  • Queries: Contact number 0320 5003348 for recruitment queries.

Because these are specialist posts recruited directly by the Air Force (not through a testing agency), the CV and the experience record carry the full weight of the application — make both sharp and specific to working dogs.

How to Apply — Send Your CV

Applications are made directly by CV. Follow these steps:

1
Prepare a professional CV highlighting your working-dog experience — kennels served, dogs trained, veterinary cases handled, certifications held.
2
Attach attested copies of your degree/diploma, PVMC registration (for the DVM post), experience certificates and CNIC.
3
Send your CV to Base Headquarters, PAF Base Lower Topa as instructed in the advertisement.
4
For queries, contact 0320 5003348. Wait for the shortlisting call — only shortlisted candidates are contacted.
Important: The advertisement was published on 1 October 2026 in the national press. Contract posts like these are typically filled quickly — send your CV without delay and confirm the address and deadline against the original newspaper notice.

Why These Posts Are Unique

K9 units are a small, elite world — working-dog experience in a military environment is a credential that opens doors across security, detection and veterinary fields worldwide. A contract year with the PAF's K9 programme gives a veterinary doctor or trainer genuine operational experience with detection and patrol dogs, something civilian kennels rarely offer. For the right candidate, this is less a job advertisement and more a career-defining opportunity.

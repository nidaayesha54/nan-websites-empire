About the Overseas Employment Corporation

The Overseas Employment Corporation operates under the Ministry of Overseas Pakistanis & Human Resource Development as the government's own manpower-export channel. Unlike private recruiters, OEC advertisements carry documented employment terms — salary, contract length and job description are stated upfront in the advertisement itself, as in Ad 210's 1,800 SAR salary and 1-year contract. Applying through OEC means your case moves through an official file: your documents, fee payment (via PSID) and selection are all on record, which gives workers far stronger protection against the fraud that unfortunately surrounds Gulf job offers from unverified agents.

OEC periodically announces requirements received from foreign employers — Gulf states being the most frequent — across technical trades, healthcare, hospitality, driving and other categories. Each announcement carries its own advertisement number and closing date, so workers in the relevant trades should monitor oec.gov.pk continuously rather than waiting for a single annual cycle.

Preparing a Strong OEC Application

  • Match the trade exactly: your diploma and 3 years of experience must be in HVAC or mechanical maintenance — unrelated experience does not count.
  • Get documents attested: keep attested copies of your diploma, experience certificates, CNIC and passport ready; OEC scrutiny rejects incomplete files.
  • Check passport validity: an expired or short-validity passport delays everything — renew early if needed.
  • Pay via PSID only: generate the PSID from the OEC portal and pay through the designated channels; never hand cash to anyone claiming to "confirm" your application.
  • Apply early: with the 20 October 2026 deadline approaching, submit well in advance — only 6 posts are announced, and competition in these trades is always strong.
  • Keep copies of everything: the submitted form, PSID receipt and uploaded documents should be saved and printed for the interview/medical stages.

Join PAF 2026: 138 Accounts & IT Branches Commission — Flying Officer Posts, 65% Marks, Apply at joinpaf.gov.pk

The Pakistan Air Force (PAF) has opened induction for its 138 CSC courses in the Accounts and IT branches. Successful candidates are commissioned as Flying Officers after 24 weeks of training. The age limit is 20 to 26 years (as of 15 April 2027) for male citizens of Pakistan, and the qualification bar is 16 years of education with minimum 65% marks or 2.74 CGPA from an HEC-recognised university. Registration is online at www.joinpaf.gov.pk.

Short-service commission branches are the PAF's professional-officer stream: accountants run the Air Force's financial machinery and IT officers run its networks, data systems and cyber posture. For graduates who want a commission without the four-year cadet courses, these branches are the direct route in.

Selection Procedure — Stage by Stage

The CSC selection pipeline is merit-based and multi-stage:

1
Online registration at www.joinpaf.gov.pk. Fill in your personal and educational details carefully and print your roll number slip with the test date, time and selection centre.
2
Intelligence test + academic e-test (computerised): for the 138 CSC courses the academic test is in the relevant subject.
3
Initial medical examination and interview at the Information & Selection Centre.
4
Physical test: 1 km run in 8 minutes, push-ups 10 in 2 minutes, sit-ups 10 in 2 minutes, chin-ups 2 in 2 minutes.
5
ISSB at Kohat, Gujranwala, Malir or Quetta; final medical at CMH Lahore; AHQ Selection Board interview for CSC candidates; final selection by Air Headquarters on overall merit.
Important: Confirm the registration closing date in the official PAF advertisement — apply early, because selection centres fill up and the physical and academic tests need preparation time.

Documents Required

  • Smart Card / Form-B: NADRA Smart Card for self, along with father and mother CNICs.
  • Educational certificates: original Matric and Intermediate marks sheets (DMC) and degree certificates with the required CGPA/percentage.
  • Photographs: four passport-size photographs; attested photocopies of all documents.

Candidates must bring original documents plus attested copies when visiting the Information and Selection Centre.

Why These Branches Matter

The Accounts branch keeps the Air Force's money honest — budgeting, audit, pay and financial compliance across every base — while the IT branch defends and operates the PAF's digital infrastructure, from networks and data centres to cyber security and AI-enabled systems. A Flying Officer commission in either branch means officer-rank responsibility from day one, a structured service career, and professional experience that stays valuable in civilian life too. Graduates with 65%+ marks in commerce, finance, computer science or engineering should register now and start preparing for the academic e-test and the physical standards.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the PAF 138 Accounts and IT branch inductions?

Short Service Commission courses commissioning Flying Officers in the Accounts and IT branches of the Pakistan Air Force, with 24 weeks of training.

What is the age limit for PAF 138 branches?

20 to 26 years as of 15 April 2027, for male citizens of Pakistan (married or unmarried).

What qualification is required?

16 years of education in the relevant field — commerce/finance for Accounts, CS/IT/engineering for IT — with minimum 65% marks or 2.74 CGPA from an HEC-recognised university.

How do I apply?

Online registration at www.joinpaf.gov.pk — print your roll number slip with the test date, time and selection centre.

What tests are held in the selection?

Intelligence and academic e-tests, initial medical and interview, physical test (1km run, push-ups, sit-ups, chin-ups), ISSB, final medical at CMH Lahore, and the AHQ Selection Board interview.

PN Civilian Apprentice A-2027: Registration Closed — Test & Merit Guide

Online registration for the Pakistan Navy Civilian Apprentice Batch A-2027 — 57 seats of Civil Apprentice (BPS-07) in the PNCA industrial/technical staff (lower formation) — ran from 11 to 20 October 2026 at joinpaknavy.gov.pk, and that window is now closed. No new applications can be submitted. This guide is for the thousands who did register: here is the complete roadmap from here on — the computer-based entry test at PN Recruitment and Selection Centres, the medical examination, the interview of shortlisted candidates, how final merit is worked out, the 3-year apprenticeship at the Pakistan Naval Polytechnic Institute, Karachi, and the BPS-07 appointment with an 8-year service bond that follows.

Eligibility Recap (for registered candidates)

Before the stages begin, make sure you actually clear the gate conditions — discovering a mismatch at the medical stage wastes everyone's time. The advertisement set these conditions:

  • Pakistani male citizen, unmarried.
  • Matric with Science: at least 55% marks (relaxed to 45% for Balochistan domicile).
  • Age 15–19 years as on 1 January 2027 — this is a hard cutoff.
  • Minimum height 5 feet 4 inches (162.5 cm).
  • Your registration slip from joinpaknavy.gov.pk — guard it; it carries your test date and venue.

What Happens Next: The 6 Stages

1
Computer-based entry test. Held at PN Recruitment and Selection Centres (Abbottabad, Peshawar, Rawalpindi, Lahore, Karachi, Quetta and others). Your test date and venue are printed on your registration slip and intimated via SMS — the Navy sends individual intimations, so keep the mobile number you registered with active and charged. Prepare Matric-level science, mathematics, English and general knowledge; a basic familiarity with computers matters because the test itself is computer-based.
2
Medical examination. Candidates who qualify the written test face a medical examination covering vision, hearing, general fitness and any disqualifying condition. Be rested, carry your CNIC/Form-B and slip, and report on the exact date given.
3
Interview of shortlisted candidates. Only those who clear the test and medical are called. Expect questions on your trade interest, general knowledge, current affairs and why you want to join the Navy's technical side. Confidence and honest answers beat memorised speeches.
4
Final merit. Merit is finalised on the combined weight of Matric marks, the entry test score and interview performance. Because Matric marks count, candidates with strong boards results have a real edge even before the test.
5
Call letter and 3-year apprenticeship. Merit-selected candidates receive a call letter and join the 3-year apprenticeship at the Pakistan Naval Polytechnic Institute, Karachi, with a stipend during training. This is hands-on industrial/technical training for the Navy's lower formation — treat it like a full-time job.
6
Appointment in BPS-07. On successful completion of the apprenticeship, you are appointed as Civil Apprentice in BPS-07 with an 8-year service bond. Read the bond terms carefully before signing — it is a legal commitment to serve the Navy for the stated period.

Test-Day Checklist

  • Registration slip (printed) plus original CNIC or Form-B.
  • Original educational documents and domicile for verification.
  • Reach early — Navy selection centres run on strict schedules and late entry is typically refused.
  • No phones, smart watches or calculators inside the test hall; basic computer operation will be needed for the computer-based test.
Honesty flag: the registration window (11–20 October 2026) and quota breakup come from multiple coverage sources of PID (I) 3301-D/26, not from a live browse of joinpaknavy.gov.pk today. Individual test dates are issued on personal registration slips and by SMS — this guide cannot state a single test date, so check your own slip and SMS for your exact schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I still apply for Pakistan Navy Civilian Apprentice A-2027?

No. Online registration at joinpaknavy.gov.pk ran from 11 to 20 October 2026 and is now closed. No new applications are being accepted.

How many seats are there in PN Civilian Apprentice Batch A-2027?

57 seats of Civil Apprentice (BPS-07), Pakistan Naval Polytechnic industrial/technical staff, allocated across provincial, regional, coastal and merit quotas.

Where and how is the A-2027 entry test held?

A computer-based entry test at Pakistan Navy Recruitment and Selection Centres (Abbottabad, Peshawar, Rawalpindi, Lahore, Karachi, Quetta and others). Your test date and venue are printed on your registration slip and intimated via SMS.

How is the final merit for PN Civilian Apprentice A-2027 calculated?

Final merit combines Matric marks, the entry test score and the interview performance. Only shortlisted candidates go to the interview stage.

What happens after selection in PN Civilian Apprentice A-2027?

A 3-year apprenticeship at the Pakistan Naval Polytechnic Institute, Karachi, with a stipend. On successful completion, appointment in BPS-07 with an 8-year service bond.

Is TA/DA paid for the PN apprentice test and interview?

No. No TA/DA is admissible for appearing in the test, interview or medical examination.

Join Pakistan Navy as Sailor Batch C-2026: Matric Base Jobs — Online Registration 8–22 Oct 2026

The Pakistan Navy has announced recruitment for Sailor Batch C-2026. Online registration runs from 8 October to 22 October 2026, and candidates can apply either through the official recruitment website or by visiting their nearest Pakistan Navy Recruitment Centre. These are Matric base jobs — one of the most popular defence recruitments of the year — open to young Pakistani men across the country.

Because the entry requirement starts at Matric, thousands of candidates compete in every Sailor batch. Registering early, choosing the right branch for your marks, and preparing for the initial tests are the three things that separate selected sailors from rejected applicants.

PN Sailor Batch C-2026 — Branches & Eligibility

BranchQualification

Note: The required Matric percentage differs by branch — the Technical branch needs 65%, so match your marks to the branch before registering. Candidates for the Female Medical Technician branch follow the separate eligibility in the advertisement.

General Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 16 to 21 years (as per the advertisement's cut-off).
  • Height: 5 feet 4 inches to 5 feet 7 inches (branch-wise standards in the ad).
  • Gender: Male (female candidates eligible for the Female Medical Technician branch only).
  • Marital status: Unmarried (per the advertised conditions).
  • Documents: Matric certificate, CNIC/B-Form, domicile certificate and photographs.

Only candidates who meet the branch-specific education, age and physical standards should register — the initial tests filter out ineligible applicants immediately.

Selection Process — Stage by Stage

The Sailor selection pipeline is multi-stage and merit-based:

1
Registration (8–22 October 2026): register online at www.joinpaknavy.gov.pk or at your nearest Pakistan Navy Recruitment Centre.
2
Initial test: a written academic test plus an intelligence test. The academic syllabus for Matric Science candidates covers General Knowledge, Matric Science, Maths, Physics, Chemistry and English; Matric Arts candidates are tested on Matric Arts, General Science, General Maths, English and General Knowledge. The intelligence test includes verbal and non-verbal questions. Candidates are informed of the test date and venue by SMS.
3
Physical Endurance Test (PET): candidates who clear the written tests undergo the physical test.
4
Medical examination & interview: followed by a personality test and temporary selection.
5
Final medical: at the Naval Medical Checking Centre, Karachi — fully fit candidates are finally selected for the batch.
Important: Test results are announced on the Pakistan Navy's official website. No TA/DA is admissible for appearing in the tests. Prepare your Matric subjects, English and general knowledge now — the initial academic test is the biggest filter.

How to Register Online

Visit www.joinpaknavy.gov.pk, select the Sailor Batch C-2026 registration option, enter your personal and educational details carefully, choose the branch matching your Matric marks, and submit before 22 October 2026. Keep your registration slip and required documents ready — you will need them at the test centre. Never pay anyone claiming to guarantee selection; Navy recruitment is strictly on merit.

Candidates choosing between branches should be realistic about their Matric marks: the Technical branch's 65% requirement is firm, and registering for a branch you do not qualify for wastes your only chance this batch. Pick the highest branch your marks allow, register in the first week of the window, and use the remaining days before the test to revise Matric science, maths, English and general knowledge.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date for Pakistan Navy Sailor Batch C-2026 registration?

22 October 2026. Registration opened on 8 October 2026 and is done online at joinpaknavy.gov.pk or at Pakistan Navy Recruitment Centres.

What is the qualification for PN Sailor Batch C-2026?

Matric base: Technical branch needs Matric Science with 65% marks, Marine and Naval Police need 60%, and Chef/Steward needs Matric with 50%.

What is the age limit for Pakistan Navy Sailor jobs?

16 to 21 years, with height between 5 feet 4 inches and 5 feet 7 inches (per the advertisement's branch-wise standards).

What tests are held for PN Sailor selection?

An initial academic test plus an intelligence test (verbal and non-verbal), followed by a physical endurance test, medical examination, interview, personality test and final medical at Karachi.

How will I know my test date and venue?

Candidates are informed of the test date and venue through SMS, and results are announced on the Pakistan Navy's official website.

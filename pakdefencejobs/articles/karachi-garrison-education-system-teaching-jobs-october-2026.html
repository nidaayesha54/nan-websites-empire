KGES Karachi Teaching Jobs 2026 — Apply by 24 Oct

Karachi Garrison Education System (KGES) has advertised teaching and academic leadership posts for its Army Public School & College campuses at Saddar and Faisal, Karachi (advertisement in Daily Dawn, 18 October 2026): Academic Coordinator, Section Head, Early Years/Home Teachers, Junior/Middle/Senior Teachers, Matric, Cambridge/IGCSE and SSC/HSSC stream teachers, and PTI (male and female). Applications must reach KGES by 24 October 2026 — eligible candidates should apply today, by post or email.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Education: 2nd division in all qualifications is mandatory across posts.
  • Experience: minimum 3 years' teaching experience in the relevant subject in a reputable school.
  • Age: not more than 50 years.
  • Skills: English proficiency and computer literacy are mandatory for all teaching posts.
  • Preference: CIE-trained teachers preferred for Cambridge/IGCSE stream posts.

Salary Structure (As Reported)

Salary bands below are from the job portal's summary of the ad — confirm against the official notice:

  • Academic Coordinator: Rs. 175,000–225,000 per month.
  • Section Head: Rs. 125,000–150,000 per month.
  • Early Years/Home Teachers: starting Rs. 50,000+; Rs. 55,000–65,000 with 5+ years' experience.
  • Junior/Middle Teachers: starting Rs. 55,000+; Rs. 60,000–65,000 (5+ yrs); Rs. 70,000–75,000 (8+ yrs).
  • SSC/HSSC Teachers: starting Rs. 65,000–75,000; Rs. 75,000–85,000 (3–5 yrs); Rs. 90,000–100,000 (5+ yrs); Rs. 100,000–120,000 (8+ yrs).

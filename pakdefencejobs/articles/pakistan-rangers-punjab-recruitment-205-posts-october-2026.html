Pakistan Rangers Punjab Jobs 2026: 205 Posts — Lady Rangers, Religious Teacher, Trades, Last Date 28 Oct

Headquarters Pakistan Rangers (Punjab), Lahore, has announced a comprehensive recruitment drive for 205 posts: Lady Rangers (Sepoy General Duty), Religious Teacher (BPS-11), Cook, Khakrob (Sanitary Worker) and 83 Miscellaneous Skilled Trades (BPS-01) including electricians, plumbers, masons, painters, carpenters, tailors, dhobis, barbers and malis. The advertisement was published in September 2026, and the last date to apply is 28 October 2026.

Pakistan Rangers is a civil armed force serving under the Ministry of Interior — the Punjab wing guards the eastern border and supports internal security. Recruitment is open to male and female candidates from across Pakistan, with posts distributed on a provincial quota basis. Employment is regular/permanent under Federal Government and Pakistan Rangers rules — not a short contract.

Ineligibility: candidates dismissed from government/military service, convicted by a court, or declared medically unfit are ineligible. Government servants must bring an official NOC. No TA/DA is admissible for tests, interviews or training.

How to Apply

The two categories follow different application routes:

1
For Religious Teacher, Cook, Khakrob and Skilled Trades: send your application with two complete sets of attested document copies, 2 passport-size photographs and a self-addressed stamped envelope to Post Box No. 6080, GPO Lahore by post or courier.
2
For Lady Rangers (General Duty): the advertisement required in-person reporting at the Training Center, Mandi Bahauddin — confirm the current status from the official advertisement before acting.
3
Meet the deadline: postal applications must reach by 28 October 2026.
4
Wait for the test schedule — physical tests, written tests, medical and interviews follow per the official process.
Basic pay perks: BPS-scale pay plus free ration, accommodation and allowances as per Federal Government rules — the standard Rangers service package.

Why Rangers Recruitment Is Different

Unlike the Army, Navy and PAF — which recruit through their own portals — Rangers recruitment still runs on physical reporting and postal applications, and the quota is genuinely national: half the seats go to Punjab and Islamabad, but Sindh, KPK, Balochistan, GB, AJ&K and the merged districts all have reserved shares, plus a 5% minority quota. The Religious Teacher (BPS-11) posts are officer-grade among the trades and need the religious-board degree; the 83 skilled trades are the volume route for literate candidates with a trade. For a permanent, uniformed federal career with full service benefits, this is one of the most accessible 2026 drives — apply by 28 October.

Pakistan Navy Civilian Apprentice Batch A-2027: 57 Posts, Apply by 20 Oct 2026 — Eligibility & Quota

57 Civilian Apprentice (Lower Formation) posts in the PNCA Industrial/Technical Staff category — eligibility, provincial quota, training terms and the 20 October 2026 deadline.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Pakistan Defence Jobs » Articles

The Pakistan Navy has announced induction for Civilian Apprentice (Lower Formation) Batch A-2027 for youth across the country. A total of 57 vacancies will be filled under the PNCA Industrial / Technical Staff category in BPS-07.

Male candidates holding Pakistani nationality, aged 15 to 19 years as on 1 January 2027, are eligible to apply. Online registration runs from 11 October to 20 October 2026 through the official website www.joinpaknavy.gov.pk. Selected candidates undergo 3 years of apprenticeship training followed by an 8-year bond period.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Nationality & gender: Male candidates holding Pakistani nationality.
  • Age: Between 15 and 19 years as on 1 January 2027 — no relaxation is given.
  • Education: Matric with Science securing at least 55% marks; for candidates belonging to Balochistan the minimum is 45%.
  • Commitment: Willingness to complete 3 years of apprenticeship training and the subsequent 8-year bond period.

How to Apply Online

1
Visit the official Pakistan Navy recruitment website www.joinpaknavy.gov.pk.
2
Fill the online registration form with your personal, academic and domicile details accurately.
3
Submit the form and download your Registration Slip — it carries your test date, time and assigned selection centre.
4
Complete online registration between 11 and 20 October 2026.
5
On the entry-test day, report to your assigned recruitment / selection centre at the time on your registration slip.
Beware of fraud: Register only at joinpaknavy.gov.pk. The Navy collects the registration fee on the day of your entrance test, at the centre — anyone asking for money online to register or 'confirm' a seat is not following the Navy's process. The Navy also states that no book is its official test book and no age or marks relaxation is given.

Navy Selection Centres

Pakistan Navy Recruitment and Selection Centres operate in Abbottabad, Peshawar, Hyderabad, Rawalpindi, Rahim Yar Khan, Sukkur, Sialkot, Shaheed Benazirabad, Faisalabad, Karachi, Kharian, Quetta, Gilgit, Gwadar, Lahore, Multan and Muzaffarabad. For further information, candidates can contact their nearest centre — the Karachi centre can be reached on 021-48506703 and Lahore on 042-66961409.

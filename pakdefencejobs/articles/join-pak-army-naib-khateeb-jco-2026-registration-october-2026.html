Join Pak Army as Naib Khateeb (JCO) 2026: Dars-e-Nizami Eligibility, AS&RC Registration by 23 Nov 2026

The Pakistan Army is inducting Naib Khateeb — the Army's religious and Islamic scholar officer — in the 2026 recruitment cycle. It is a Junior Commissioned Officer (JCO) rank, equivalent to Naib Subedar: the dedicated religious leadership role in every unit — leading prayers and providing Islamic guidance and moral education to officers and soldiers.

Naib Khateeb recruitment is built around religious scholarship rather than conventional academics. Registration is in-person only — obtain the prescribed form from the nearest Army Selection & Recruitment Centre (AS&RC) and submit it with attested documents between September 2026 and 23 November 2026.

The Dars-e-Nizami Requirement — Explained

Dars-e-Nizami is the classical Islamic scholarship curriculum of Deeni Madaris — Quran, Hadith, Fiqh, Arabic and Islamic jurisprudence. The Army requires it from a madrassa recognized by the Higher Education Commission (HEC). Strength in Qira'at, Hifz-e-Quran and genuine Arabic fluency earns extra merit — and the AS&RC interview tests real command of the subject, not just paper credentials.

Age, Height & Nationality Rules

  • Civilian candidates: 20–28 years of age.
  • Serving personnel: 22–35 years, with 1–16 years of service.
  • Height: minimum 5'3" (160 cm).
  • Nationality: citizens of Pakistan, AJK and Gilgit-Baltistan may apply.
  • Marital status: unmarried preference does not apply — married candidates are equally eligible.

How to Apply — In-Person Registration at the AS&RC

There is no online application for Naib Khateeb — registration is entirely in person:

1
Visit your nearest AS&RC by 23 November 2026.
2
Obtain the prescribed Naib Khateeb registration form from the centre.
3
Fill the form; attach attested copies of sanad, FA/FSc, CNIC, domicile and photographs.
4
Submit before 23 November 2026 and collect your acknowledgement.
5
Appear for initial tests and interview — religious knowledge, Arabic, Qira'at and suitability assessed.
Important: Plan your visit well before 23 November 2026. Verify your sanad's HEC recognition in advance — the most common reason candidates face trouble at the desk.

Selection Process at the AS&RC

Selection includes initial tests and an interview at the AS&RC probing your Islamic scholarship — Quran, Hadith, Fiqh, Arabic — and your ability to guide troops. Medical fitness and standard suitability checks apply; those clearing the initial stages proceed toward final induction as Junior Commissioned Officers.

Why the Naib Khateeb Role Matters

The Naib Khateeb is the soldier's spiritual anchor — leading prayers, counselling troops and setting the unit's moral tone. For a qualified alim, it combines a lifetime of religious study with a respected JCO career and pension. Holders of a recognized Dars-e-Nizami qualification plus FA/FSc should register at the nearest AS&RC before 23 November 2026.

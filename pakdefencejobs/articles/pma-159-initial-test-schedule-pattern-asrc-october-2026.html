PMA 159 Initial Test 2026: Schedule, Test Pattern & Physical Standards Guide

If you have registered for the 159 PMA Long Course — the Pakistan Army's route to a regular commission as Second Lieutenant — the next milestone is the initial test. These tests run from 5 October to 15 December 2026, on weekdays only, at 14 Army Selection and Recruitment Centres (AS&RCs) across the country. This guide breaks down the complete journey: the test schedule and centres, the computerized test pattern, the physical standards you must clear, the documents to carry, the ineligibility rules, and every stage from the AS&RC to ISSB and the final GHQ merit.

Initial Test Schedule & Centres

Your roll number slip (available 5–7 working days after online registration) carries your exact test date, time and centre. Tests are held at the following AS&RCs:

Test-day rules: arrive one hour early. Mobile phones, calculators, smart watches, laptops, notebooks and bags are not allowed inside the centre. Miss your slot and you may lose your chance for this intake.

Stage 1: The Computerized Initial Test

The written assessment is a computer-based multiple-choice test — so basic computer skills matter. It covers two areas:

  • Intelligence test — verbal and non-verbal reasoning questions.
  • Academic test — English, Mathematics, General Knowledge, Islamiat and Pakistan Studies.

Before the interview, candidates also write a short essay in English and one in Urdu (around 10 lines each) on a "topic of the day" displayed in the hall. This tests written expression in both languages, so practise structured paragraphs in English and Urdu before your test date.

Preparation tip: revise Matric and Intermediate-level English grammar and comprehension, basic arithmetic and algebra, current affairs of the last 6–12 months, Islamiat fundamentals, and key Pakistan Studies topics — ideology, the Pakistan Movement, the constitution and geography.

Stage 2: Physical Standards

Candidates who clear the written test face the physical assessment. The qualifying standards are:

1 km runWithin 8 minutes
Push-ups15 in 2 minutes

HeightMinimum 5 feet 4 inches (162.5 cm)

Train now, not later. The physical test eliminates more candidates than any other stage. Start running daily — build comfortably past the 1 km standard — and practise push-ups and sit-ups with correct form so you do not get reps disqualified on the day.

Stage 3: Initial Medical & Interview at the AS&RC

After the physical test comes an initial medical examination and an interview with the selection officer. The interview is scored out of 20 and assesses your confidence and communication, English and Urdu, current affairs, geography and Pakistan Studies, plus sports and hobbies. Candidates who clear the interview receive the ISSB form — a failed interview ends the journey for this course.

Stages 4–6: ISSB, Detailed Medical & Final Merit

4
ISSB (Inter Services Selection Board): shortlisted candidates are called to a 4-day assessment by psychologists, group testing officers and deputy presidents. ISSB centres are at Kohat, Gujranwala, Malir and Quetta. ISSB selects officers for all three services.
5
Detailed medical: ISSB-recommended candidates undergo a full medical examination at a Combined Military Hospital (CMH).
6
GHQ final merit: the final selection is made by GHQ on overall performance across all stages. Selected candidates undergo 2 years of training at the Pakistan Military Academy, Kakul, before commissioning.
Note: the ISSB schedule published in September 2026 covered the 158 PMA Long Course only; 159 course dates are issued with individual call-up letters. Watch the official ISSB and Join Pak Army websites after your initial test.

Documents to Take to the AS&RC

Carry originals plus one attested set of photocopies:

  • Original CNIC (or computerised Form-B with a parent's CNIC, if under 18).
  • Original educational certificates and detailed marks sheets (Matric and FA/FSc or equivalent).
  • Original domicile certificate.
  • Passport-size photographs (three, attested).
  • Character certificate.
  • The paid fee challan and your roll number slip.
  • Repeaters: the original ISSB "Not Recommended" letter and one copy.

Who Cannot Apply: Ineligibility Rules

  • Anyone rejected twice by ISSB.
  • Anyone who has failed the initial tests three times.
  • Candidates dismissed from government or armed service.
  • The convicted in a criminal case.
  • Anyone caught with forged or tampered certificates — a permanent ban from all commissions.
  • Candidates permanently declared medically unfit or who test positive in drug screening.
Honest advice: the Army treats concealed facts and tampered documents more severely than an honest rejection. If any of the above applies to you, wait for the route that fits you rather than risking a permanent ban.

Frequently Asked Questions

When are the PMA 159 initial tests held?

From 5 October to 15 December 2026, on weekdays only, at 14 Army Selection and Recruitment Centres across Pakistan. Your exact date, time and centre are printed on your roll number slip.

What subjects are in the PMA 159 initial written test?

A computerized test of intelligence (verbal and non-verbal) plus academics: English, Mathematics, General Knowledge, Islamiat and Pakistan Studies. Before the interview, candidates also write a short essay in English and one in Urdu.

What are the PMA 159 physical test standards?

A 1 km run in 8 minutes, 15 push-ups in 2 minutes, and 15 sit-ups in 2 minutes. Height must be at least 5 feet 4 inches (162.5 cm) and weight must meet the BMI standard.

What documents do I take to the AS&RC?

Original CNIC or computerised Form-B, original educational certificates with detailed marks sheets, original domicile, passport-size photographs, a character certificate, the paid fee challan and the roll number slip.

Who cannot apply for PMA 159?

Anyone twice rejected by ISSB, anyone who has failed the initial tests three times, candidates dismissed from government service, the convicted, and anyone caught with forged or tampered certificates.

How long is training at PMA Kakul?

Two years. Final selection is made by GHQ on overall performance across the initial tests, ISSB and the detailed medical examination.

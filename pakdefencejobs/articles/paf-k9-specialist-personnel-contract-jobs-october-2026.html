PAF K9 Specialist Personnel Jobs 2026 — Contract, Apply Now

The Pakistan Air Force is looking for dedicated, experienced professionals to join its working-dog programme as K9 Specialist Personnel. These are contract appointments for an initial period of one year, extendable on the basis of performance and requirement. The advertisement, published in the Express newspaper on 1 October 2026, covers five categories — from DVM veterinary doctors and vet assistants to dog handlers/groomers, K9 dog trainers and animal husbandry professionals. If you work with dogs or farm animals professionally and have two to three years of relevant experience, this is a direct path into a defence establishment's K9 section. Applications go by CV to the Base Headquarters, PAF Base Lower Topa, and only shortlisted candidates are called for test and interview.

The Five K9 Specialist Categories

The advertisement lists five distinct roles. Each has its own qualification and experience bar — match yourself against the right one before sending your CV.

1. DVM / Veterinary Doctor

Requires a DVM degree from a recognized university, registered with the Pakistan Veterinary Medical Council (PVMC), plus a minimum of 2 years of relevant experience with strong clinical and surgical skills. This is the senior medical role in the K9 setup — you will be the person responsible for the health of the working dogs.

2. Vet Assistant

Requires FSc (Pre-Medical) or equivalent plus a diploma or certification in Vet Assistant, with a minimum of 2 years of experience and knowledge of animal first aid. The hands-on support role beside the veterinary doctor.

3. Lascar Groomer (Dog Handler and Groomer)

Requires Matric or equivalent with 2 to 3 years of dog-handling and grooming experience. Candidates must be physically fit and animal-friendly. This role is about daily handling, grooming, and kennel-side care of the dogs.

4. Dog Trainers (K9 Trainers)

Requires Matric or equivalent, with a professional dog-training certification preferred and a minimum of 2 years of experience training working dogs. You must know obedience training, aggression control and tracking — the core working-dog skill set for security duties.

5. Husbandry Professional (Animal Husbandry)

Requires a minimum of Matric, with a diploma or certificate in animal husbandry preferred, and 2 to 3 years of experience in animal care, feeding and management. Knowledge of animal nutrition, hygiene and husbandry is essential.

General Eligibility and Contract Terms

  • Contract: initial period of one year, extendable on the basis of performance and requirement. These are not permanent PAF appointments.
  • Fitness and conduct: all applicants must be physically fit, disciplined and committed — working-dog duties are physically demanding.
  • Experience: the 2 to 3 years of relevant experience is the common thread across categories; gather your experience certificates and references before applying.
  • Selection: only shortlisted candidates will be called for test and interview.

How to Apply — Step by Step

1
Prepare your CV and documents. Include your qualification certificates, PVMC registration (for DVM), diplomas/certifications, experience certificates, CNIC copy, and a contact number that stays reachable.
2
Send your CV to the Base Headquarters, PAF Base Lower Topa. The advertisement directs applications to the Base HQ at Lower Topa — use registered post or courier with tracking so you have proof of dispatch.
3
Confirm the intake status first. Call 0320 5003348 for queries before sending or travelling — because no closing date was announced, the intake may fill at any time.
4
Wait for shortlisting. Only shortlisted candidates are called for test and interview. Keep your phone active and your documents ready for the interview stage.

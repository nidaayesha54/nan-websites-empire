Join Pakistan Army as Soldier to Officer 2026: PMA 159 Route & JCO Promotion Boards — Full Schedule

Already serving in the Pakistan Army? Two upward routes are live in 2026 — Soldier to Officer through PMA Long Course 159 (registration closes 23 October 2026) and Soldier to JCO through the December 2026 and June 2027 promotion boards.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Pakistan Defence Jobs » Articles

Serving soldiers of the Pakistan Army have two recognised paths upward in 2026: Soldier to Officer through PMA Long Course 159 and Soldier to JCO through the GHQ promotion boards. The officer route is open now — online registration for PMA Long Course 159 runs from 14 September to 23 October 2026 — while the JCO route runs through the December 2026 and June 2027 promotion boards. Both routes are for in-service personnel, and the procedures, documents and timelines are different, so read this guide carefully before choosing your path.

The Army actively encourages capable soldiers to rise through the ranks — the PMA Long Course route produces commissioned officers who train for two years at the Pakistan Military Academy Kakul alongside civilian cadets.

Route 1 — Soldier to Officer via PMA Long Course 159

StageSchedule
Online registration14 September 2026 – 23 October 2026
Preliminary tests at AS&RCs5 October 2026 – 15 December 2026
ISSB testsJanuary – March 2027
Training starts at PMA KakulMay 2027
Training duration2 years at Pakistan Military Academy Kakul
Note: Soldiers selected through this route undergo the same 2-year rigorous training at PMA Kakul as civilian cadets. Registration is online only on the official portal, and the roll number slip is issued a few working days after registration.

Route 2 — Soldier to JCO (Promotion Boards)

  • No separate advertisement: there is no individual application process — your unit recommends you automatically when you complete the service criteria.
  • Next promotion boards: December 2026 and June 2027 at GHQ.
  • Training: no long academy course — only a 3–4 month promotion cadre at the respective Regimental Centre (e.g., Punjab Regimental Centre, Mardan).
  • Keep your service record, ACRs and medical category clean — the board decides on your file.

Documents Required

For the PMA Long Course Application (Soldier to Officer)

  • CNIC and B-Form / domicile
  • Matric, FA/FSc, BA/BS result cards
  • NOC from your Unit Commanding Officer — the most important document; without it you cannot proceed
  • Service certificate

For JCO Promotion (Internal)

  • Service Record Book
  • Promotion cadre certificates
  • ACRs of the last 5 years
  • Medical category certificate
Important: Married soldiers can also apply for the officer route (only for serving personnel). Forged or altered certificates make a candidate permanently ineligible for every commission.

How to Register — Soldier to Officer via PMA 159

Registration is online only and runs every day, weekends included, until 23 October 2026. Follow these steps:

1
Open joinpakarmy.gov.pk, press “Apply Now” and choose PMA Long Course (serving candidate category).
2
Fill in your personal details, CNIC or Form B, address, domicile district and education with percentages. If your result is pending, choose “Result Awaited”.
3
Upload a 1x1 passport-size photo in formal dress with a blue or white background, as a JPEG under 300 KB.
4
Choose your test centre and submit. Obtain your NOC from your Unit Commanding Officer — you will need it for the process.
5
After 5 to 7 working days, open “Registration Status”, enter your CNIC and download your roll number slip and fee challan; pay the fee at a National Bank of Pakistan branch.

Perks & Benefits After Selection

The jump from soldier to officer is the single biggest career leap available to serving personnel — it changes pay, status, housing, education for children and post-retirement benefits entirely.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date for PMA Long Course 159 registration for soldiers?

Online registration runs from 14 September 2026 to 23 October 2026 on joinpakarmy.gov.pk.

How does a soldier become a JCO in Pakistan Army?

There is no separate advertisement — the unit recommends eligible soldiers automatically when they complete the service criteria. Promotion boards are held in December 2026 and June 2027 at GHQ.

What is the most important document for the soldier-to-officer application?

The NOC from your Unit Commanding Officer — it is the most important document in the application.

How long is the training for soldier-to-officer selectees?

2 years of rigorous training at the Pakistan Military Academy, Kakul — the same as civilian cadets.

Can married soldiers apply for PMA Long Course?

Married soldiers can apply, but only serving personnel are eligible for this route.

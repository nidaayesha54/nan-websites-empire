PN Cadet 2027-A: Registration Closed 11 Oct — What's Next (Tests, ISSB & Selection Stages)

Online registration for Pakistan Navy PN Cadet Term 2027-A closed on 11 October 2026 — here is the complete roadmap of what registered candidates face next, from preliminary tests at the selection centres to the ISSB and final merit.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Pakistan Defence Jobs » Articles

Online registration for the Pakistan Navy's PN Cadet Term 2027-A closed on 11 October 2026 (the window ran from 13 September to 11 October 2026). If you registered in time and downloaded your registration slip, your journey has only just begun. This guide walks you through every stage that comes next — preliminary tests, medical examination, initial interview, the ISSB, and the final merit list — so you know exactly what to prepare for and when.

If you missed this round, PN Cadet courses run on a regular cycle, and understanding the full selection pipeline now puts you ahead for the next term.

Two special provisions applied to this term: pre-medical students could apply provided they complete Additional Mathematics before 15 March 2027, and F.Sc Part-I appearing candidates with at least 65% marks could apply with a hope certificate from their college head — but they must secure the required 60% in the final result or face disqualification at any stage. O/A Level candidates must submit an IBCC equivalence certificate.

What Happens Next — Selection Stages

Registered candidates now move through the Navy's standard officer-selection pipeline:

1
Preliminary tests at PN Recruitment & Selection Centres: report on the date and time on your registration slip. Expect an intelligence test (verbal and non-verbal) and an academic test covering English, Physics and Mathematics.
2
Initial medical examination: candidates who clear the tests undergo the initial medical at the selection centre.
3
Initial interview: shortlisted candidates face the preliminary interview panel.
4
ISSB tests: recommended candidates are called for the Inter Services Selection Board — the multi-day assessment of leadership, personality and aptitude. (The ISSB's tentative schedule lists PAF/related boards from 8 October to 11 November 2026 for the current cycle.)
5
Final medical at CMH: ISSB-recommended candidates undergo the final medical examination.
6
Final merit & joining: the Naval Headquarters prepares the final merit list, and selected cadets join the Pakistan Naval Academy for training.
Important: At every stage, candidates proceed only if they clear the previous one — there is no skipping. Keep your original documents (academic certificates, CNIC/B-Form, domicile, photographs) ready for verification at each step.

PN Recruitment & Selection Centres

Candidates report to the centre chosen at registration. The Navy's selection centres include:

Abbottabad, Faisalabad, Gilgit, Gwadar, Hyderabad, Karachi, Kharian, Lahore, Multan, Muzaffarabad, Peshawar, Quetta, Swat, Sialkot, Rawalpindi, Rahim Yar Khan, Sukkur and Shaheed Benazirabad. Contact your nearest centre if you have queries about your test call-up — and never trust forwarded or unofficial advertisements; verify everything on www.joinpaknavy.gov.pk.

Frequently Asked Questions

When did PN Cadet 2027-A registration close?

Online registration ran from 13 September 2026 to 11 October 2026 and is now closed.

What happens after registration for PN Cadet 2027-A?

Registered candidates go through preliminary tests (intelligence + academic) at PN Recruitment & Selection Centres, initial medical, initial interview, ISSB tests, final medical at CMH, and then the final merit list.

What is the age limit for PN Cadet 2027-A?

Civilian (unmarried) candidates: 16½ to 21 years on 15 May 2027. Service candidates: 17 to 23 years on 15 May 2027.

What education is required for PN Cadet?

Matric and F.Sc or O/A Level with minimum 60% marks, with Physics, Maths and Chemistry (engineering branches) or Physics, Maths and Computer Science/Statistics (operations & supply).

I missed the 2027-A registration. What should I do?

Prepare for the next term: secure 60%+ in F.Sc with the required subject combination, keep your documents ready, and register on joinpaknavy.gov.pk as soon as the next advertisement opens.

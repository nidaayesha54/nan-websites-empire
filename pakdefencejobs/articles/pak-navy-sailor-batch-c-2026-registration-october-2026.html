Eligibility Criteria

  • Gender: male candidates only for this sailor intake.
  • Age: 16 to 21 years on the cut-off date given in the advertisement.

  • Nationality: Pakistani citizen; unmarried as per standard sailor terms.

How to Register — Step by Step

1
Open the official portal: go to www.joinpaknavy.gov.pk and select the Sailor Batch C-2026 registration section.
2
Fill the online form carefully. Enter your CNIC/B-Form number, educational details, branch preference and a mobile number that stays active — your roll number and test date arrive by SMS.
3
Submit and save your confirmation. Note your registration/roll-number slip and keep a screenshot. Do not wait until the evening — portals slow down on the last day.
4
Alternative route: if the portal does not respond, report in person at the nearest Pakistan Navy Recruitment & Selection Centre with your original documents before closing today.

What Happens After Registration

Once you register, your roll number and test date are sent via SMS to the mobile number on your form. After that come the academic (initial) test, the physical test, the medical examination, and the subsequent selection stages including interviews and the final merit list. Keep your documents — Matric certificate, B-Form/CNIC, domicile and photographs — ready for the centre visit.

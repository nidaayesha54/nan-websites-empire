Join Pakistan Army as Soldier (Sipahi) 2026: Online Registration 1 Sep–16 Nov, AS&RC Till 23 Nov 2026

The Pakistan Army has opened its 2026 soldier (Sipahi) recruitment drive for young men nationwide. Online registration at joinpakarmy.gov.pk runs 1 September to 16 November 2026; in-person registration at AS&RCs and Regimental Centres runs 1 September to 23 November 2026. Age is calculated as of 24 November 2026.

Seven soldier categories are open — General Duty, Technical, Clerk, Driver, Military Police, Cook and Sanitary Worker. Every Sipahi post is pensionable, and a quota is reserved for non-Muslim minorities.

Age & Height Relaxation Rules

  • Graduates: +1 year age relaxation.
  • Relatives of shuhada/war-injured: +2 years age relaxation.
  • Balochistan, GB, AJK and merged KPK districts: +3 years age relaxation.
  • Height relaxation: for Balochistan, GB and merged districts candidates and children of shuhada — except Sanitary Worker, Cook and Clerk.

Carry proof (domicile, shaheed's service documents, or degree) to the centre to claim any relaxation.

Documents Required at Registration

  • Original educational certificates along with 2 attested photocopies of each.
  • 4 passport-size photographs (recent).
  • CNIC or B-Form issued by NADRA.
  • Domicile of your district; for AJK candidates, the State Subject Certificate.

How to Apply — Online & Walk-in Registration

Online registration is NOT compulsory for soldier posts — walk-ins are accepted at the nearest Army Selection & Recruitment Centre:

1
Register online (optional) at joinpakarmy.gov.pk by 16 November 2026, or prepare a walk-in.
2
Walk into the nearest AS&RC (15 centres nationwide, including Rawalpindi, Lahore, Multan, Quetta, Peshawar and Karachi) or Regimental Centre by 23 November 2026.
3
Carry your documents: original certificates plus 2 attested copies, 4 photographs, CNIC/B-Form and domicile.
4
Complete registration at the centre and collect your roll number slip.
5
Appear for the written test, expected in the last week of November 2026.
6
Clear the physical, medical and interview stages for a place in the final merit list.
Important: Online closes 16 November; AS&RC walk-ins run till 23 November 2026. Register early — centres rush near closing.

After Registration — What Comes Next

Registered candidates face an initial written test (expected last week of November 2026), then physical tests, medical and interview. Only stage-clearers make the merit list. Revise Matric basics and train physically now. A soldier's career brings pension, family medical care and the soldier-to-officer route for high achievers.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to register for Pakistan Army Soldier 2026?

Online closes 16 November 2026; AS&RC and Regimental Centre walk-ins stay open until 23 November 2026.

Is online registration compulsory for soldier (Sipahi) posts?

No. Walk-ins are accepted at the nearest AS&RC during the window.

What is the age limit for joining Pakistan Army as a soldier?

About 17.5–23 as of 24 November 2026: +1 year graduates, +2 relatives of shuhada/war-injured, +3 Balochistan, GB, AJK, merged KPK districts.

What is the minimum height and qualification for General Duty Sipahi?

5'6" (167.5 cm) with Matric or above.

Are Cook and Sanitary Worker soldier posts pensionable?

Yes — all Sipahi posts are pensionable, with a minority quota reserved.

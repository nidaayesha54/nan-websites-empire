How to Apply

1
Read the official advertisement (joinpakarmy.gov.pk, joinpaf.gov.pk, joinpaknavy.gov.pk) and check eligibility.
2
Register online on the respective force's website or visit the nearest AS&RC / recruitment centre.
3
Appear for the initial test, medical examination and physical fitness test.
4
Clear the ISSB / final interview stages as applicable to the course.
5
Check the final merit list and reporting instructions on the official website.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I join the Pakistan Army as a soldier?

Register online or at the nearest Army Selection and Recruitment Centre, then pass the initial test, medical and physical.

What is the age limit for PMA Long Course?

17 to 23 years for intermediate candidates, as announced in each course advertisement.

How do I apply for PAF as an Airman?

Apply online at the PAF website or visit the nearest PAF Information & Selection Centre before the closing date.

Can females join the armed forces?

Yes, through Lady Cadet Course, AFNS, and various PAF/Navy branches for female candidates.

Are these listings official advertisements?

Listings here are illustrative samples. Always verify on the official forces' websites.

What documents are needed?

Original CNIC/B-Form, educational certificates, domicile, and recent photographs.

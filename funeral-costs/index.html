Common questions

How much does a funeral cost in 2026? +
A traditional funeral with viewing and burial typically costs $15,250–$17,200. A cremation with a memorial service costs $8,500–$10,300. Direct cremation (no service) costs $2,550–$3,300. Prices vary by state.
Do I have to accept the funeral home's package? +
No. Under the FTC Funeral Rule, funeral homes cannot force you to buy a package. You may select only the items you want, and they must give you an itemized price list.
Can I buy a casket somewhere else? +
Yes. The FTC Funeral Rule explicitly allows you to buy a casket from any seller — online retailers are often far cheaper — and the funeral home may not charge a handling fee.
Is this site really free? Will anyone contact me? +
Completely free, no signup, and no — no one will ever call or email you. This site exists so families have honest information during one of life's hardest moments.

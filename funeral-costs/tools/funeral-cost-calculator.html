Frequently asked questions

What does the average funeral cost include? +
A traditional funeral typically includes a basic services fee, embalming, viewing facilities, casket, cemetery plot, grave opening/closing, vault, headstone, transportation, and miscellaneous fees like death certificates.
Why do funeral costs vary by state? +
Local cost of living, cemetery real estate prices, and regional competition all play a role. Two funeral homes in the same state have been found $7,355 apart for the same service.
Is embalming required by law? +
Usually not. No federal law requires embalming, and most states don't require it either — especially for cremation or prompt burial. Ask; don't assume.

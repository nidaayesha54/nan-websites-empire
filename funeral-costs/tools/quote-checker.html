Frequently asked questions

Is my data private? +
Yes. Everything runs in your browser — your quote is never uploaded, stored, or shared with anyone.
What if every item looks overpriced? +
Get a second quote from another funeral home. Prices for the identical service have been found more than $7,000 apart in the same state. Comparison is your strongest tool.
Can I negotiate with a funeral home? +
You can always ask. More importantly, you can decline any item you don't want — embalming, viewing, limousines — and the funeral home must honor that under federal law.

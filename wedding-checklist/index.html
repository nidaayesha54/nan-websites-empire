Frequently asked questions

What is the first thing to do when planning a wedding? +
Set your budget first — everything else (venue, guest count, vendors) flows from that number. Then pick your wedding date and book the venue, since popular venues sell out 9–12 months ahead.
How far in advance should I start planning a wedding? +
12 months is the sweet spot for a full wedding. With 6 months you can still pull it off by prioritizing venue, dress, and photographer. Our timeline planner adjusts to whatever date you enter.
What should I do 12 months before my wedding? +
Set your budget, draft your guest list, choose your wedding party, book your venue, and hire a planner if you want one. These five moves lock in the foundation everything else builds on.
Is this wedding checklist really free? +
Yes. The checklist, timeline planner, and countdown are 100% free with no signup. Your checklist progress saves in your browser automatically.
What do most couples forget when planning a wedding? +
Marriage license timing, vendor meals, a rain plan, tipping envelopes, and eating on the wedding day. Our month-by-month checklist includes all of these so nothing slips through.

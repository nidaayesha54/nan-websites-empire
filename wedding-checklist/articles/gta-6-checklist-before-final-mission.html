GTA 6 Checklist: 20 Things Before the Final Mission

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

The final mission in GTA 6 is a point of no return, and the game does not warn you loudly enough. Once it is done, the story is over: missions cannot be replayed, there is no New Game Plus, and the choices you made are permanent. Cheats unlock after the final mission, free roam continues, and the map stays open — but the story you just finished is finished. This checklist covers the 20 things to do before you start the finale, so nothing you wanted is left behind.

Think of it the way you would think of a wedding checklist: the big day is the deadline, and everything on the list has to be done before it, not after. Tick each item off, save, and then walk into the finale knowing you left nothing on the table.

Money first: bank it, then spend it

1. Bank your cash. Pocket cash is lost when you die or get arrested, and it does not carry into Chapter 2 — so deposit everything at an ATM before the finale. Banked money is safe, and safe money is what buys the next upgrade. 2. Stash spare money under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom. 3. Finish the money gates: some missions will not start until you have reached a cash target, so check your balance before you commit to the finale. 4. Sell cars you do not want at a Pay 'n' Spray — a single car can bring in several thousand dollars, but you can only sell a limited number each day, so do it early.

5. Tow an ATM if you need quick cash — 168 of Leonida's 454 ATMs can be hooked up to a tow truck and ripped out of the wall, but it brings the police, so do it before the finale, not during it. 6. Rob a jewelry store while you still can: Victoria Lee Jeweler in Capri and Blanc and Giroux in the Decor District pay around $20,000 each.

Vehicles and property: fill the garage

7. Buy the garages you want. Eight garages can be bought with in-game money after the Daily Driver mission, and the 12 base-game garages provide 16 registered vehicle spaces. 8. Register every car you want to keep — a stolen car only becomes yours when you register it, and safehouse parking does not add garage spaces. 9. Customise your favourite car: neons, engine swaps, the works — and save manually after a big purchase, because a crash can cost you the build. 10. Do the driving you have been putting off: the Thrillbilly Mud Club, the car meets, the long coastal drives. The map stays open after the story, but the story missions will not wait for you.

People and places: finish the relationships

11. Max the relationship. Jason and Lucia's bond has four levels, Hot Together at the top, and it rises with time together — greeting, holding hands, pool, mini golf, kayaking, robbing stores together. Whether you nurtured it will have a bearing on how their story plays out, so finish it before the finale. 12. Visit the four safehouses one last time: the Keys stilt house, the Starlet Motel, Bayside and Ocean Beach. The first one burns down in Chapter 1 — if you have not seen it yet, you never will. 13. Meet the people you have not met: the radio hosts, the club owners, the side characters. The credits list 202 roles, and most of them are out there in the world, not in the story.

14. Fish, hunt and dive before the end. Hunting unlocks in Chapter 2 and pays hundreds to thousands per trip. A diving tour near the Keys pays $400, and treasure sites can be worth tens of thousands each. Fishing is catch and release — do it for the calm, not the cash.

Systems and saves: protect the playthrough

15. Rotate your saves. GTA 6 gives you 15 manual save slots — keep a spread across the campaign, one roughly every 7% of the story, and keep one slot from before the finale itself. The autosave only fires after a mission ends, and it cannot be trusted with your progress. 16. Change your clothes and hair: 70 stores, salons, $150 haircuts at Pearl House, 50-plus tattoos at Electric Fang. The finale is the most screenshotted moment in the game — look the part.

17. Take the photos. Photo Studio opens from the pause menu with 17 filters, five lights, depth of field and exposure controls, and the phone camera has 17 filters of its own. Photograph the places you will miss. 18. Check the wanted system one last time: the police act only on what they witness, so a quiet approach keeps you free and a loud one brings the whole city down. 19. Stock up on food and drink: food heals, and Burger Shot is always open. 20. Say goodbye to the map: drive the full length of Leonida once, from the Keys to Vice City, with the radio up. It takes about eleven minutes by car, and it is the best last thing you can do before the end.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I keep playing after the final mission?

Yes — free roam continues and cheats unlock once the final mission is done. But there is no New Game Plus and no mission replay, so anything tied to the story is gone.

What is the most important thing to do before the finale?

Bank your cash and rotate your saves. Pocket money is lost on death or arrest, and a manual save from before the finale is your only way back.

How long is the story?

50 to 100 hours per IGN, with about 70 hours for one reviewer's first playthrough. Do not rush the checklist — the finale will wait.

The bottom line: the finale is the deadline, and the checklist is the plan. Bank the cash, fill the garages, max the relationship, rotate the saves — and then walk in knowing you left nothing behind.

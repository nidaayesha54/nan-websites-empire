GTA 6 in Urdu: Can You Play It in Urdu?

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

If you speak Urdu and you are waiting for GTA 6, here is the short answer first: the game has no Urdu version. There are no Urdu menus, no Urdu subtitles and no Urdu voices, and nothing Rockstar has published suggests one is coming. That is disappointing for the millions of Urdu speakers who play games in Pakistan and around the world — but it does not mean you cannot play. This guide explains exactly what languages GTA 6 does support, what English-only voices mean in practice, and how Urdu-speaking players are getting through the game.

The question matters because GTA 6 is one of the biggest releases in gaming history, and its audience is genuinely global. Players in Pakistan pay real money for it — the standard edition costs Rs 5,999 and the Ultimate Edition Rs 7,499 — and they deserve a straight answer about what they are buying, not a vague promise that a translation might appear later.

The 13 languages on the list

GTA 6 supports 13 languages for menus and subtitles, according to the game's PlayStation support page and its Microsoft Store listing. The list is: English, French, German, Italian, Spanish from Spain, Spanish from Mexico, Portuguese from Brazil, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Take-Two chief executive Strauss Zelnick gave the same count of 13 at the company's shareholder meeting on September 17, 2026.

That is the full list. Urdu is not on it. Neither are Arabic, Hindi, Turkish, Dutch or European Portuguese — only Brazilian Portuguese made the cut, so players in Portugal get Brazilian spelling and phrasing. The pattern is the same as GTA 5, which had the same 13 interface and subtitle languages. If your language was not in GTA 5, do not expect it in GTA 6.

Voices are English only

The second thing to know is that only one of those 13 languages has voice acting: English. Every other language gets translated menus and subtitles over English dialogue. Jason, Lucia, the radio hosts, the pedestrians — every voice you hear is in English, and the other 12 languages read along underneath.

That is how Rockstar has always worked. The studio has never dubbed a GTA game: GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 were English voices with subtitles too. So the experience for a player using French or Japanese subtitles is English voices plus translated text — and for an Urdu speaker, it is English voices plus English text, unless you are comfortable reading one of the 13 supported languages.

How to play as an Urdu speaker

The practical route for most Urdu-speaking players is English subtitles, and the game gives you tools to make that easier. Subtitles can be resized, given a background and labelled with speaker names, so dialogue stays readable even in fast scenes. The game also has a screen narration setting that reads on-screen text aloud, though it works in English only, and an audio descriptions toggle — both sit in the Audio section of Settings.

One thing Rockstar has not published is how the game picks its language in the first place — whether it follows your console's system language, has a setting of its own, or both. Check your console language before launch night, and expect the game's own Settings menu to be the final word once the game unlocks.

Watch out for fake Urdu patches

Because so many players want an Urdu version, the gap is exactly the kind of thing scammers exploit. Fake "Urdu language pack" and "Urdu patch" downloads have been one of the oldest tricks in GTA scam history, and the GTA 6 launch has made it worse: fake installers and fake PDF guides have already been caught carrying password-stealing malware, including one strain that arrived as a 796 KB file claiming to be a 72 MB download.

The rule is simple. If a download promises Urdu subtitles, an Urdu dub or any language Rockstar has not listed, it is not real. No community patch has been announced, and no fan translation can add voices that were never recorded. Download only from the PlayStation Store, the Xbox Store or Rockstar's own channels, and treat every other "language pack" as a trap.

Will Urdu ever be added?

Language lists can change after launch, and some coverage suggests more translations could be added later — but Rockstar has not promised anything, and the studio's history points the other way. GTA 5 kept the same 13 languages for its whole life, and GTA 6 launched with the same 13. Turkish players have campaigned hard for subtitles and have not got them either, which tells you how little pressure moves this list.

That is the honest picture. Urdu is not in GTA 6, and hoping for a patch is not a plan. What Urdu-speaking players do have is the same game everyone else has, in English, with subtitle tools that make it playable — and a community of millions of players in the same position, sharing guides and translations of their own.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does GTA 6 have Urdu subtitles?

No. Urdu is not among the 13 subtitle languages. The list is English, French, German, Italian, two kinds of Spanish, Brazilian Portuguese, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean and two kinds of Chinese.

Is there an Urdu voice option in GTA 6?

No. All voices in the game are in English. No other language has a dub, and Rockstar has never dubbed a GTA game.

Can I change the language in GTA 6?

Rockstar has not published how the game's language is chosen — console setting, in-game option, or both. Check your console language before launch and the game's Settings menu after it unlocks.

The bottom line: GTA 6 does not speak Urdu, and no patch will make it do so. Play it in English with subtitles, download nothing that promises otherwise, and you will get the same game as everyone else.

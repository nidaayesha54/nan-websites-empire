What a PIP Level 2 Staff Investigator Does

Police Staff Investigators do the investigative heavy lifting without holding a warrant: taking statements, gathering and reviewing evidence, preparing case files, interviewing witnesses and suspects under caution, and working with CPS disclosure requirements. PIP — the Professionalising Investigation Programme — is the national framework: PIP Level 1 covers volume crime, while PIP Level 2 means serious and complex investigations — the level this Nottinghamshire post sits at. Staff investigators typically work inside CID or dedicated investigation teams, carrying their own caseloads and seeing cases through from allocation to court file. It is detailed, deadline-driven work where the quality of your file can decide whether a case proceeds.

Why Staff Investigator Roles Are Growing

Forces across the UK are expanding civilian investigation capacity — and the current market shows it. Alongside Nottinghamshire’s PIP 2 post, Surrey Police is currently seeking temporary PIP 1 Investigators at around £15.16 per hour across Reigate, Guildford and Staines to support investigation teams. The pattern is clear: forces need accredited investigators faster than warranted-officer pipelines can supply them, and staff investigator posts — permanent, fixed-term and temporary — are filling the gap. For candidates, that means more doors into investigative policing than ever before.

Who Should Apply

  • Accredited investigators: PIP Level 2 accreditation (or a credible route to it) is the core requirement for a post at this level.
  • Ex-officers and staff: former warranted officers, PCSOs and police staff with investigation experience are the classic applicant pool.
  • Detail-obsessed case builders: file quality, disclosure compliance and court deadlines are the job — precision matters more than pace.
  • Candidates happy with fixed-term: 12 months is a solid run, and staff investigator contracts are frequently extended or made permanent.

British Transport Police Recruitment 2026: Police Officer Jobs — Apply by 1 November 2026

British Transport Police — the Guardians of the Railway — is recruiting police officers for its Inner London new-recruit campaign, closing midnight Sunday 1 November 2026, alongside its first-ever PCEP cohort.

British Transport Police (BTP) — the national railway police, known as the Guardians of the Railway — is recruiting police officers for its Inner London new-recruit campaign, closing midnight Sunday 1 November 2026. BTP has also launched its first-ever Police Constable Entry Programme (PCEP) cohort in England and Wales.

With a high starting salary and generous benefits, BTP offers policing with a national remit — the rail network, stations and passengers across the country. Below: the closing date, the locations, the new PCEP cohort, and how to apply.

The Inner London Campaign: Apply by 1 November 2026

The current new-recruit campaign focuses on Inner London, but BTP recruits for stations across England, Wales and Scotland — including Maidstone, Ashford, Colchester, Southend, Stevenage, Luton, Brighton, Guildford, Croydon, Southampton, Newcastle, Birmingham, Leeds, Bristol, Exeter and Reading.

The deadline is midnight Sunday 1 November 2026, but BTP can close the campaign early — treat the opening weeks as your real window.

BTP’s First PCEP Cohort

In October 2026 BTP launched its first Police Constable Entry Programme (PCEP) cohort in England and Wales — a paid, structured entry into the office of constable requiring no university qualification during probation, opening policing to applicants whose strengths lie in life experience rather than academics.

The first cohort is now training; if it succeeds, PCEP intakes are likely to become a regular fixture of BTP recruitment — watch future campaign pages.

Transferee and re-joiner campaigns run separately — experienced officers should not apply to new-recruit campaigns.

Apply through the official BTP campaign pages:

2

3

4
Submit well before midnight on Sunday 1 November 2026 — the campaign can close early once enough applications arrive.
5
Prepare for the assessment stages — typically online assessment, interview and pre-employment vetting checks.

Why BTP Is Worth a Look

Policing the railways is policing with a clear national mission — keeping millions of passengers, staff and the rail network safe. Officers work everywhere from busy Inner London stations to regional hubs, with a high starting salary, generous benefits and the UK policing pension. With the new PCEP cohort widening entry routes, October 2026 is a strong moment to consider BTP — apply before the 1 November deadline, or before the campaign closes early.

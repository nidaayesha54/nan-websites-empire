Met Police Designing Out Crime Officer (DOCO) Jobs: £40,519 Salary — Closes 3 November 2026

The Metropolitan Police is recruiting a Designing Out Crime Officer (DOCO) — £40,519 starting salary, crime-prevention design work across London, closing 3 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026

The Metropolitan Police Service is recruiting a Designing Out Crime Officer (DOCO) — a specialist police-staff role focused on preventing crime through better design of buildings, spaces and developments. The starting salary is £40,519 (including allowances), with posts based at Bow, Bromley and Twickenham. Applications close at 11:55pm on 3 November 2026.

DOCOs are the Met’s crime-prevention design specialists: they work with developers, planners and local authorities to make new and existing environments harder targets for crime — prevention work that sits upstream of enforcement.

What a Designing Out Crime Officer Does

DOCOs apply crime-prevention-through-environmental-design principles: reviewing planning applications and developments, advising on layouts, lighting, access control and natural surveillance, and working with partners so that crime risks are designed out before buildings open. It is a role that blends policing knowledge with planning and design literacy — ideal for candidates from crime-prevention, planning, architecture or community-safety backgrounds.

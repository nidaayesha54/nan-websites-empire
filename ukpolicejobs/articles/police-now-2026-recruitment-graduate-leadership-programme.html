Police Now 2026 Recruitment: Graduate Leadership Programme & Staff Roles

Everything about joining Police Now in 2026 — the National Graduate Leadership Programme, direct-entry routes, staff careers, benefits and the application process.

Published 13 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

Police Now has become one of the most talked-about routes into British policing. The social enterprise recruits, trains and develops outstanding graduates and career changers to become police officers and leaders — with a mission to transform communities by reducing crime and increasing public confidence. Alongside its officer programmes, Police Now also employs a growing team of staff professionals across its London headquarters and regional hubs.

Whether you want to wear the uniform or support policing from behind the scenes, here is how Police Now recruitment works in 2026.

The National Graduate Leadership Programme

The flagship National Graduate Leadership Programme recruits graduates to train as neighbourhood police officers, with a two-year development journey that combines frontline policing with leadership training and a postgraduate qualification. Participants focus on tackling crime and vulnerability in their communities — from antisocial behaviour and burglary to domestic abuse and exploitation — while being developed as future police leaders.

  • Who it's for: graduates with a 2:1 or above (or predicted), strong leadership potential and a commitment to public service. No policing background required.
  • What you get: a salaried police officer role from day one, intensive initial training, ongoing coaching, and a recognised postgraduate qualification.
  • Where: partner forces across England and Wales — you apply to Police Now and are matched with participating forces.
Always verify: programme dates, force allocations and eligibility criteria change each intake. Confirm the current details on policenow.org.uk before applying.

Direct Entry and Other Officer Routes

Beyond the graduate programme, Police Now runs direct-entry routes that bring experienced professionals into policing at higher ranks or into specialist functions. These routes suit career changers with leadership, investigative or technical backgrounds who want to skip the traditional constable-to-sergeant climb. Entry requirements, training length and force availability vary by route — check the current campaigns on the Police Now website.

Police Now Staff Roles

Not everyone at Police Now wears a uniform. The organisation employs staff across programme delivery, partnerships, marketing and communications, finance, technology and operations. Recent staff vacancies have included roles such as Grants & Contracts Officer, supporting the organisation's funding and partnership work.

Staff roles are ideal for professionals in the charity, public or private sectors who want mission-driven work without joining as an officer. They are advertised on the Police Now careers pages and sometimes on charity-sector job boards.

How to Apply: Step by Step

Choose your route. Decide between the National Graduate Leadership Programme, direct entry, or a staff role — each has its own application window on policenow.org.uk.
Submit the online application. Expect competency and motivation questions plus your education and employment history. Tailor every answer to policing values.
Complete video interview and assessments. Graduate applicants typically face a video interview followed by an assessment centre with role-plays, written exercises and group tasks.
Pass fitness, medical and vetting. Officer routes require the police fitness test, a medical, and background vetting. Start fitness preparation early.
Accept and prepare. Successful candidates receive offers conditional on final checks, then begin the intensive training programme.

Eligibility Notes

Officer applicants must have the right to work in the UK, meet residency requirements for vetting, and pass medical and fitness standards. Certain convictions and financial difficulties (such as undischarged bankruptcy) can bar entry — the Police Now website publishes full eligibility guidance. Staff roles follow standard UK employment checks.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Police Now National Graduate Leadership Programme?

It is Police Now's flagship programme recruiting graduates to become neighbourhood police officers and police leaders, with intensive training, a postgraduate qualification and a focus on community crime and vulnerability.

Do you need a degree for Police Now?

The National Graduate Leadership Programme requires a 2:1 degree or above (or predicted). Police Now also runs other entry routes and recruits staff roles that do not require degrees.

What staff roles does Police Now recruit?

Police Now employs staff across operations, partnerships, marketing, finance and grants — for example Grants & Contracts Officers — with hybrid working and 27 days annual leave plus bank holidays.

What benefits does Police Now offer?

Benefits include hybrid working, 27 days annual leave plus bank holidays, a health cash plan, and coaching and professional development support.

How do I apply to Police Now?

Applications are made through policenow.org.uk. The graduate programme involves an online application, video interview, assessment centre and fitness, medical and vetting checks.

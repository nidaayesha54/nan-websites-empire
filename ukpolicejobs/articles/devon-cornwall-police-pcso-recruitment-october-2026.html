What Is a PCSO?

A Police Community Support Officer is a uniformed, paid member of police staff — not a police constable, but a visible frontline presence in the neighbourhoods they serve. PCSOs patrol on foot, reassure the public, tackle anti-social behaviour and support constables at incidents, holding defined legal powers such as detaining suspects for up to 30 minutes. For many, it is the ideal first step into policing: paid frontline experience, with the option to apply for constable entry programmes later.

Who Can Apply — Eligibility Basics

No degree is needed to become a PCSO. Standard UK police staff eligibility applies — confirm exact criteria on the advert:

  • Age: at least 18 at joining.
  • Residency: normally three years’ continuous UK residence for vetting.
  • Fitness and health: pass the police fitness test and a medical.
  • Vetting: enhanced background, financial and association checks.
  • Driving: check if a licence is required for your preferred posting.

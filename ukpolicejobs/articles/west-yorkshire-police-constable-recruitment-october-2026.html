West Yorkshire Police Constable Recruitment 2026: Uniform & Detective PCEP — Apply by 25 October

West Yorkshire Police is recruiting for two Police Constable entry routes — Uniformed Constables and Detective Constables via the PCEP — starting at £32,256, closing 25 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » UK Police Jobs » Articles

West Yorkshire Police is now recruiting for its two Police Constable entry routes: Uniformed Police Constables and Detective Constables, both through the Police Constable Entry Programme (PCEP). The closing date for applications for both routes is Sunday 25 October 2026 at 11:55pm — although the force reserves the right to change this depending on application numbers, so early applications are advised.

West Yorkshire is one of England’s largest forces, and the PCEP is its no-degree entry route: a two-year programme combining classroom training with operational policing across the force’s districts.

The Two Entry Routes

PCEP — Uniform

For people who want to be a uniformed Police Constable: after the 17-week Wakefield training course, officers move into Response or Neighbourhood Policing roles in one of the five operational districts.

PCEP — Detective

For people who want to be a Detective Constable: the two-year PCEP route for the inquisitive — aimed at those drawn to investigation work. Applicants should check the full eligibility criteria, including qualification requirements (confirmed results required; predicted grades not accepted at the point of application for some elements).

Other West Yorkshire Entry Routes

  • PCDA (Police Constable Degree Apprenticeship): a three-year integrated degree apprenticeship leading to a BA Honours in Professional Policing Practice awarded by Leeds Trinity University — requires GCSE English and Maths plus a Level 3 qualification.
  • DHEP (Degree Holder Entry Programme): no further DHEP intakes are intended.
  • IPLDP routes: individuals cannot join the IPLDP Detective or Uniform routes after 31 March 2024.

Eligibility and Assessment

Standard police entry requirements apply: age 18 at application, fitness, medical, drug and eyesight tests, and vetting. A full manual UK or EU driving licence by the time of formal offer is an advantage. Expect a rigorous assessment process followed by an in-force interview, then medical and vetting checks before any job offer — the force warns that failing to respond promptly to requests for information or appointments can affect progression.

How to Apply

Applications are made online through the force’s official site. Follow these steps:

1
Go to the West Yorkshire Police recruitment pages and choose your route — PCEP Uniform or PCEP Detective.
2
Check the full eligibility criteria (qualifications, fitness, vetting) before applying.
3
Complete the online application and the staged assessments honestly.
4
Submit before 11:55pm on Sunday 25 October 2026 — the force may close early if oversubscribed.

What entry routes is West Yorkshire Police recruiting for?

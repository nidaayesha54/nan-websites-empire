PSNI Recruitment October 2026: Student Officers, £35k Salary | NAN Jobs Portal

The Police Service of Northern Ireland opened recruitment for student officers and police staff on 12 October 2026 — a £35,000 starting salary, full training, and a policing degree from Ulster University. Here is what the campaign offers and how to apply.

Home › Articles › PSNI Recruitment October 2026: Student Officers, £35k Salary | NAN Jobs Portal

Published 15 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

On Monday 12 October 2026 the Police Service of Northern Ireland launched a major recruitment campaign for student officers — with police staff roles included in the drive too. The timing is symbolic: the launch coincides with the organisation's 25th anniversary, marked by a fresh, seven-week multi-faceted advertising campaign.

The campaign runs across broadcast and print media, plus social and digital platforms. Chief Constable Jon Boutcher says he wants the PSNI to be truly representative of the community it serves — and "an employer of choice" for people across Northern Ireland.

Apply early: major campaigns draw high volumes. Submit your application through the official PSNI recruitment portal as soon as possible.

New recruits receive comprehensive training and ongoing professional development — plus the opportunity to achieve a degree in criminology and policing from Ulster University: a genuine "earn while you learn" offer on a £35,000 starting salary.

Chief Constable's Message: Policing Is the Finest Public Service

Chief Constable Jon Boutcher's pitch is simple: the PSNI should reflect the community it polices. In his words, "Policing is more than a job... being a police officer is the finest public service a person can do." That message — service, representation and professional development — sits at the heart of this recruitment drive.

How to Apply to the PSNI

Start at the official PSNI recruitment portal. All applications go through the PSNI's official site — never through third parties.
Choose your route. Decide between the student officer programme, a police staff role, or — if you already serve — the Transferee Scheme.
Prepare your application. Gather your documents and think through your motivation: the PSNI wants people who understand policing as public service.
Complete the stages. Expect online assessment, interview, fitness test, medical and vetting before any final offer.
Start training — and your degree. Successful student officers begin comprehensive training alongside their Ulster University degree programme.
Transferee Scheme: serving officers in other police services with policing skills and experience can apply to transfer directly to the PSNI. The scheme opened on 12 October 2026.

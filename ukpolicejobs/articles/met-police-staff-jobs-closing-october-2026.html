Spotlight Roles

Police Staff Investigator — Fraud Pursue (Economic Crime)

Investigating fraud within the Economic Crime command — a growth area as financial crime moves online. Closes 19 October 2026.

Who Can Apply — Typical Requirements

  • Investigator roles usually require investigative experience, knowledge of criminal-justice processes and often accreditation (e.g. PIP or equivalent) — check each advert.
  • Technical roles (engineers, analysts, digital intelligence) look for relevant professional skills and tooling experience rather than policing background.
  • Vetting: all Met roles require security vetting to the appropriate level.
  • Right to work in the UK is required; check sponsorship terms on each advert.

How to Apply

Applications go through the Met’s official police careers portal. Follow these steps:

1
Open the Met Police careers portal and filter staff vacancies by closing date.
2
Read the role profile and person specification — staff adverts detail the exact skills, accreditation and vetting level.
3
Prepare a comprehensive CV and personal statement (PDF or Word), evidencing how you meet the criteria.
4
Submit before 23:55 on the stated closing date — the earliest deadlines are 19 October.

Why Police Staff Roles Are Worth a Look

Police staff vacancies rarely get the attention of officer intakes — which means less competition for roles with real responsibility, from taking down county-lines finances to building the digital evidence in modern-slavery cases. The October closing cluster spans investigations, technology and corporate services, so there is a route for more backgrounds than the uniform alone suggests. Check the board, match your skills to the person specification, and apply before the 19 October deadlines pass.

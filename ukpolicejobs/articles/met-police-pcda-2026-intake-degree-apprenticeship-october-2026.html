Met Police PCDA 2026 Intake Open: Police Constable Degree Apprenticeship — 3-Year Funded Route

The Met is accepting applications for its 2026 police officer intake — choose between the three-year fully funded Police Constable Degree Apprenticeship (PCDA) and the two-year Police Constable Entry Programme (PCEP).

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » UK Police Jobs » Articles

The Metropolitan Police Service is accepting applications for its 2026 police officer intake, with two main entry routes: the Police Constable Degree Apprenticeship (PCDA) — a three-year, fully funded route combining university study with operational training, leading to a degree — and the Police Constable Entry Programme (PCEP) — a two-year route at a Met training centre plus on-the-job learning, with no degree.

The Met’s current message to applicants is direct: “We’ve accepted the challenge – will you?” The force is recruiting officers to help restore trust, reduce crime and maintain high standards across London’s 32 boroughs, serving more than nine million residents.

Note: Apply for your chosen entry route — but be aware that if your chosen route becomes fully subscribed, the Met may offer you an alternative route based on your skills, qualifications and experience. Route availability and start dates are as advertised; confirm on the official Met careers site.

What the Met Is Looking For

The Met says it wants people who share its values of integrity, courage, accountability, respect and empathy — officers and staff from all backgrounds who are committed to bringing offenders to justice, protecting vulnerable people and delivering change for London’s communities. As the Met’s recruitment messaging puts it: “You do not need to have all the answers. What matters is your integrity, your compassion, your resilience and your willingness to step forward and serve.”

Standard police eligibility applies: right to work in the UK, residency requirements, fitness and medical standards, and vetting — check the full criteria on the official advert before applying.

Pay, Benefits & Career Paths

Met adverts quote a starting salary of around £40,776, rising to around £56,000 as a constable (inclusive of allowances), with specialist paths including dog and mounted units, cybercrime, the Thames Marine Unit, financial investigation and domestic-abuse victim support — plus varied progression and promotion opportunities.

How to Apply for the 2026 Intake

Applications run through the Met’s official careers channels. Follow these steps:

1
Go to the official Met Police careers site (search “Met Careers”) and choose your entry route — PCDA or PCEP.
2
Check the eligibility criteria (age, residency, fitness, vetting) before you start — do not assume.
3
Complete the online application and the staged assessments honestly; the process tests values and judgement, not just knowledge.
4
If successful, expect a post-July 2026 start date for candidates applying from this point onwards.
Tip: the Mayor of London has publicly encouraged Londoners of all backgrounds, skills and experience to consider policing — the force is actively seeking a service that represents all of London’s communities.

Why Apply Now

The PCDA offers something rare: a fully funded degree earned while doing the job, with a starting salary and pension most graduate schemes can’t match. Applications are open now, with start dates running into the second half of 2026 — the sooner you apply, the sooner your journey begins.

Met Police Entry Programmes 2026: PCEP, PCDA & DCEP Applications Close 30 November

The Metropolitan Police’s September 2026 intake entry programmes — PCEP, PCDA and DCEP — all close on 30 November 2026. Compare the three routes and pick yours.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » UK Police Jobs » Articles

The Metropolitan Police has its three main constable entry routes open simultaneously for the September 2026 (09-2026) intake — and all three close on the same day: 30 November 2026 at 23:55 GMT. Whether you want the standard constable route, a degree apprenticeship or the detective pathway, the application window is the Police Constable Entry Programme (PCEP), the Police Constable Degree Apprenticeship (PCDA) and the Detective Constable Entry Programme (DCEP), all advertised on the Met’s police careers portal.

Which Route Fits You

  • PCEP suits applicants who want the direct route into uniformed response policing without a degree programme attached.
  • PCDA is the earn-while-you-learn option — you train as a police officer while studying for a policing degree, ideal if higher education appeals but you want to be operational from the start.
  • DCEP is for those drawn to investigations — building the evidence, interview and case-building skills of a detective from day one.

Eligibility Essentials

All Met officer routes require you to meet the standard thresholds — age, right to work, UK residency history, health and fitness, and background vetting. Each advert on the careers portal sets out the exact criteria, including any degree requirements for PCDA and the investigative aptitude expectations for DCEP. Read the full person specification for your chosen route: applying to the wrong route is one of the most common avoidable mistakes.

The Bottom Line

Three routes, one deadline: 30 November 2026. The Met’s 09-2026 intake is the biggest single officer window of the season — decide whether you are PCEP, PCDA or DCEP material, and get the application in with time to spare for the assessment stages.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do Met Police 09-2026 entry programme applications close?

All three routes — PCEP, PCDA and DCEP — close at 23:55 GMT on 30 November 2026.

What is the difference between PCEP, PCDA and DCEP?

PCEP is the standard constable entry route; PCDA is the degree apprenticeship combining officer training with a policing degree; DCEP is the direct detective constable entry route.

Are Special Constable applications open?

Yes — the 08-2026 Special Constable (volunteer police officer) window closes 31 December 2026.

Can serving officers transfer to the Met?

Yes — the 202512 Transferring Officers window closes 31 December 2026, as does the 202512 Rejoiner Officers window.

Where do I apply?

Through the Met&rsquo;s police careers portal, where each 09-2026 route has its own advert with full eligibility criteria.

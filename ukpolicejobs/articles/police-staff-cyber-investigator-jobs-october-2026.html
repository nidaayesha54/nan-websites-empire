Cyber, Fraud and Digital Roles

The Cyber Intelligence Investigator (Cyber Crime Unit, closes 19 Oct) leads the tech lineup, joined by the Senior LFR Engineer (closes 20 Oct) and the Digital Intelligence and Investigation Officer tackling modern slavery (closes 23 Oct). Fraud Pursue and Disclosure roles close 19 October.

What Does a Police Staff Investigator Do?

Police staff investigators own criminal investigations end to end: interviewing victims, witnesses and suspects under PACE, gathering intelligence, and preparing case files for the Magistrates' and Crown Courts.

Spotlight: Nottinghamshire PIP2 Investigator

Nottinghamshire Police (via Adecco) seeks a Police Staff Investigator (PIP Level 2), £33,603–£38,169 a year, on a 12-month fixed-term contract: 37 hours a week, Monday to Friday, hybrid, joining the Public Protection – Children at Risk of Exploitation team. Vetting needs 5 years' UK residency.

Greater Manchester Police Jobs 2026 – Constable Routes Open, Apply

Greater Manchester Police is recruiting constables through the Police Constable Entry Programme and Detective entry — starting salary £32,255, covering all 10 boroughs of Greater Manchester.

Greater Manchester Police (GMP) — one of the largest forces in England and Wales — is recruiting police constables through the Police Constable Entry Programme (PCEP) and the Detective entry route, according to the national Joining the Police site as of 1 October 2026. With a starting salary of £32,255, a constable scale rising to £52,015, and 621 full-time-equivalent officers recruited in the year to 31 March 2026 (Home Office figures), GMP remains one of the biggest and most active police recruiters in the country.

The Two Routes: PCEP and Detective Entry

GMP offers constable candidates two main entry pathways:

Police Constable Entry Programme (PCEP)

The PCEP is a two-year entry programme leading to a degree-level qualification through the national Policing Education Qualifications Framework — no prior degree is required. It combines operational policing with structured study, and is designed for candidates who want the full breadth of frontline policing: response, patrol, neighbourhood work and investigations as their career develops.

Detective Entry

The Detective entry route fast-tracks candidates with an investigative mindset into detective training, combining the detective qualification pathway with operational policing. It is the route for people whose ambition is investigations — interviewing, evidence analysis, case-building and court — rather than the general uniformed pathway. Both routes lead to warranted constable status, but they build very different careers, so choose according to your genuine preference.

Constable Pay 2026/27

Constable pay is set nationally, and GMP advertises pay point 1 at £32,255 — effective from 1 September 2026, with no regional allowance attached. Progression through the constable scale takes you to £52,015 at the top with service and competency. This is the standard England and Wales pay structure: every recruit starts on the same national figure, which means GMP’s offer is directly comparable with any force outside the London and South-East regional allowances.

Recruitment on a Large Scale

GMP is a force that hires in volume. Home Office figures show the force recruited 621 full-time-equivalent officers in the year to 31 March 2026 — a reminder that Greater Manchester maintains a substantial ongoing recruitment pipeline rather than occasional small intakes. Large intakes bring practical advantages for candidates: more frequent windows, bigger cohorts (so you train alongside a large peer group), and a wide range of posting opportunities across the force’s 10 boroughs — from Manchester city centre to the outlying districts.

Eligibility: The Basics

The standard national eligibility applies: you must be at least 17 to apply and 18 to start, hold the right to work in the UK with indefinite leave to remain, and be able to meet three years’ UK residency for vetting purposes. You must also pass the fitness test and medical, and clear recruitment vetting — so your background, finances and associations will be checked. GMP publishes its full eligibility and vetting detail on its own recruitment pages: read it before you start your application.

Special Constables — Another Way In

If you are not ready for a full-time application, GMP also runs regular Special Constable intakes — four intakes in 2026/27 alone: May, August and November 2026, and March 2027. Special Constables are volunteer warranted officers with the same powers as regular constables within their force area, committing a minimum of 16 hours a month. Many regular officers began as Specials: it is a credible way to experience policing, build your application record and test whether the job suits you before going full-time.

The Bottom Line

Greater Manchester Police offers two real routes into warranted policing — PCEP and Detective entry — with a £32,255 national starting salary, large ongoing intakes across 10 boroughs, and a Special Constable pathway as a credible alternative way in. Because windows can close without notice, the decisive step is simple: verify the live status on GMP’s own site and apply while the route you want is open.

What the Digital Division Does

Police Scotland’s Digital Division is the technology engine of Scotland’s single national police service — responsible for the systems officers and staff rely on every shift: command and control, mobile working, digital evidence, cyber capability and the core ICT estate. An ICT Project Manager in this environment typically owns delivery of major technology programmes end to end: business cases and planning, supplier and stakeholder management, risk and dependency control, and landing new systems with thousands of operational users. Policing ICT is unforgiving — systems must work on night shift in a storm as well as they do in the office — which is exactly why the force pays senior rates for proven delivery leadership.

Who Should Apply

  • Experienced ICT project managers: a track record of delivering major technology projects — ideally in complex, regulated or public-sector environments.
  • Structured delivery professionals: PRINCE2, Agile or equivalent delivery credentials, with evidence of managing budgets, suppliers and senior stakeholders.
  • Public-service motivation: the mission is keeping Scotland safe — candidates who can connect delivery discipline to operational policing outcomes stand out.
  • Scotland-based (or willing): check the advert for the base location and any hybrid or travel expectations across the force area.

Police Staff Technology — A Growing Market

This post is part of a wider surge in police technology hiring. The College of Policing is simultaneously advertising a PoliceAI Senior AI Engineer at £68,680–£74,930 (remote, closing 21 October), and the Home Office is recruiting four Digital Deputy Directors at £86,000–£100,000. Across policing and the Home Office, digital and data capability is now a standing recruitment priority — and police-staff technology roles offer something rare: senior private-sector-grade salaries with public-sector pensions and purpose. For ICT project managers, forces and policing bodies are increasingly competitive employers.

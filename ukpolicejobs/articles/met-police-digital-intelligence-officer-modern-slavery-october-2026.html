The Role: Digital Intelligence Against Modern Slavery

Modern slavery investigations increasingly live in the digital world — communications data, device analysis, open-source intelligence and digital forensics are central to identifying victims, mapping exploitation networks and building prosecutions. The Digital Intelligence and Investigation Officer supports investigators with the technical collection, analysis and presentation of digital evidence in modern slavery cases. It is a staff (non-warranted) role, suiting candidates with digital investigation, intelligence analysis or cyber backgrounds who want their skills applied to safeguarding vulnerable people.

Why This Team Matters Now

The role sits in a fast-moving policy area: on 9 October 2026 the Home Office announced that modern slavery victims on Skilled Worker visas are free to leave an abusive sponsor and work for anyone else once formally recognised through the National Referral Mechanism — a change aimed at victims trapped between exploitation and their immigration status. Stronger victim protections mean more referrals and more investigations, which is exactly the workload this digital intelligence post supports.

Why this approach works

The NCLEX is not a memorization test — it is a clinical judgment test. Research on nursing exam prep consistently shows that students who complete 1,500+ timed practice questions with rationale review pass at far higher rates than students who only re-read textbooks. Our tools are built around that evidence: the study planner structures your weeks, the practice quiz builds your question stamina, and the readiness assessment tells you honestly when to schedule your exam date.

Frequently asked questions

The NCLEX (National Council Licensure Examination) is the computerized exam every nursing graduate in the US must pass to earn a nursing license. The NCLEX-RN is for registered nurses; the NCLEX-PN is for practical/vocational nurses.

The NCLEX-RN has a minimum of 75 and a maximum of 145 questions, including 15 unscored pretest items. The exam shuts off when the computer determines with 95% confidence whether you are above or below the passing standard.

First-time pass rates for US-educated candidates are typically around 85-90% for the NCLEX-RN and 80-85% for the NCLEX-PN. Structured study plans and 1,500+ practice questions significantly improve your odds.

Most candidates study 4-8 weeks, 2-4 hours per day. The key is consistent timed practice questions with rationale review — not just reading content. Use our free study planner to build a day-by-day schedule.

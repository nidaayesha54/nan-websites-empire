What "ready" actually means

Ready doesn't mean you know everything — it means you consistently score 65%+ on timed practice questions, your scores are stable or improving, and you can manage a full-length exam without burning out. If this assessment says you're not ready yet, that's useful data, not a failure: build your schedule with the study planner, drill questions with the practice quiz, and retake this assessment in 2-3 weeks.

Indonesia Severance Pay 2026: The Complete Guide to UP, UPMK and Compensation of Rights

Losing a job in Indonesia triggers a three-part termination package under the Job Creation Law. This guide breaks down UP, UPMK and compensation of rights — with the real tables.

Losing a job is stressful enough without having to decode what your employer owes you. In Indonesia, the law is unusually specific: termination triggers a three-part package set by Article 156 of the Manpower Law (as amended by the Job Creation Law, UU Cipta Kerja) and detailed in Government Regulation No. 35 of 2021. Get the calculation right and the difference can be many months of salary.

This guide explains each component — severance pay (UP), long-service pay (UPMK), and compensation of rights (UPH) — the tables behind them, how the reason for termination changes everything, the special final tax on severance, and what to do if your employer won't pay.

The Three Parts of the Package

Every termination settlement in Indonesia has the same skeleton:

1. Severance pay (Uang Pesangon — UP): based on length of service, scaled by the reason for termination. 2. Long-service pay (Uang Penghargaan Masa Kerja — UPMK): a loyalty reward from 3 years of service upward, always at full rate. 3. Compensation of rights (Uang Penggantian Hak — UPH): things you already earned — unused leave, housing/medical allowance, relocation costs.

Part 1: Severance Pay (UP)

UP is measured in months of your wage (basic salary + fixed allowances), rising with years of service and capped at 9 months:

Less than 1 year1 month's wage
1 to under 2 years2 months
2 to under 3 years3 months
3 to under 4 years4 months
4 to under 5 years5 months
5 to under 6 years6 months
6 to under 7 years7 months
7 to under 8 years8 months
8 years or more9 months

Part 2: Long-Service Pay (UPMK)

UPMK rewards loyalty and starts at 3 years of service, always paid at the full 1× rate regardless of why employment ended:

3 to under 6 years2 months' wages
6 to under 9 years3 months
9 to under 12 years4 months
12 to under 15 years5 months
15 to under 18 years6 months
18 to under 21 years7 months
21 to under 24 years8 months
24 years or more10 months

Part 3: Compensation of Rights (UPH)

UPH settles what you already earned but haven't received:

  • Unused annual leave, cashed at the daily wage (monthly wage ÷ 30) per day.
  • Housing and medical allowance — historically valued at 15% of the total UP + UPMK (the Manpower Law's Article 156(4) formula; note that some commentators argue the omnibus-era framework narrowed this, so confirm how your employer applies it).
  • Relocation costs where you moved cities for the job, plus return travel where applicable.

Why the Reason for Termination Matters

This is the part most people miss. GR 35/2021 applies a multiplier to the severance pay (UP) — not to long-service pay — depending on why employment ended:

Employer termination without cause2× UP
Employee death (paid to heirs)2× UP
Prolonged illness / disability2× UP
Retirement1.75× UP
Merger / acquisition1× UP
Efficiency / downsizing1× UP (some cases 0.5×)
Closure with no losses1× UP
Closure: force majeure or 2+ years of losses0.5× UP
Employee misconduct (after due process)0.5× UP
Voluntary resignationNo UP — UPMK (1×) if 3+ years, plus UPH

Notice the resignation row: resigning voluntarily forfeits severance pay entirely, but you keep long-service pay (if you served 3+ years) and your compensation of rights. This is a common and costly misunderstanding.

Worked Example

Monthly wage IDR 15,000,000, 10 years of service, terminated by the employer without cause:

  • UP: 9 months (10 years → 9-month tier) × 2 = 18 months → IDR 270,000,000
  • UPMK: 4 months (9–12 year band) × 1 = IDR 60,000,000
  • Housing & medical: 15% × 330,000,000 = IDR 49,500,000
  • Package before tax ≈ IDR 379,500,000 — about 25 months of salary, before unused leave.

Tax on Severance: The Final PPh 21

Severance packages face a special final income tax (PPh 21 Final), calculated on the lump sum without deducting the standard non-taxable income (PTKP). The published tiers are:

First IDR 50 million0%
Next IDR 50 million5%
Above IDR 100 million15%

On the IDR 379.5M example above: 0% on the first 50M, 5% on the next 50M (IDR 2.5M), 15% on the remaining 279.5M (IDR 41.925M) — total tax about IDR 44.4M. Very large payouts can reach higher brackets, so confirm with a tax advisor for exceptional amounts.

Fixed-Term (PKWT) Workers: Different Rules

Fixed-term contract workers don't use the UP/UPMK tiers. When the contract ends, they receive compensation of one month's wage per 12 months of contract, pro-rated for shorter or longer contracts. A 6-month contract earns half a month's wage; an 18-month contract earns 1.5 months.

Don't Forget JKP Unemployment Insurance

Separate from the employer's package, the Job Creation Law created JKP (Jaminan Kehilangan Pekerjaan) — unemployment insurance providing cash benefits, training access, and job placement for eligible laid-off workers. It doesn't replace severance; it sits on top of it. Ask BPJS Ketenagakerjaan about your eligibility.

If Your Employer Won't Pay

1

Negotiate in writing

Present your calculation and request settlement. Keep every message.

2

Bipartite then mediation

Formal bipartite negotiation, then mediation at the local Manpower office (Disnaker).

3

Industrial Relations Court

Unresolved disputes go to the PHI, which can order payment.

Negotiating Your Separation: Practical Tips

The statutory package is the floor — many separations, especially of senior staff, settle above it through negotiation. A few principles help:

  • Get the reason in writing. Because the UP multiplier swings from 0.5× to 2×, the stated reason for termination is worth real money. A "mutual separation" with no clear reason defaults to negotiation — don't sign a letter that misstates the reason.
  • Verify your wage base. Recompute "one month's wage" from your own payslips (basic + fixed allowances). Employers sometimes quietly use basic salary only.
  • Count every year. Service is measured in completed bands; crossing from 7 years 11 months to 8 years moves UP from 8 to 9 months. If your termination date is near a band boundary, the exact last day matters.
  • Don't trade statutory rights for speed. Pressure to "sign today for a quick payout" usually means the offer is below entitlement. You can negotiate; bipartite talks are your legal right.

Severance, THR, and the Same Wage Concept

Indonesia's labour protections reuse one definition everywhere: one month's wage = basic salary + fixed allowances. It drives THR, UP, UPMK, and the 15% housing/medical component. Learn it once from your payslip and you can audit every entitlement yourself — which is precisely why keeping old payslips matters. Variable bonuses, attendance-based transport, and commissions sit outside it.

Frequently Asked Questions

I resigned after 5 years on IDR 7M salary. What do I get?

No severance pay (UP) on resignation — but 5 years earns long-service pay: 2 months × IDR 7M = IDR 14M, plus unused leave cashed at the daily wage and the housing/medical component.

My employer says the 15% housing allowance no longer applies. Is that true?

There is genuine legal debate here: the omnibus framework's treatment of Article 156(4) is disputed among practitioners. Some employers still pay it; others don't. Get the position in writing and consider legal advice for large amounts.

Is there a payment deadline?

The full settlement should be paid when the employment relationship ends. Persistent delays can be raised with Disnaker or taken to the Industrial Relations Court.

Do I get severance if my fixed-term contract simply expires?

You get the PKWT contract-end compensation (1 month's wage per 12 months, pro-rata) — not the UP/UPMK tiers, which apply to permanent (PKWTT) workers.

Can my employer pay severance in instalments?

The regulations contemplate settlement at termination. Instalment arrangements happen in practice but should be in a written agreement — never accept a verbal promise for a large package.

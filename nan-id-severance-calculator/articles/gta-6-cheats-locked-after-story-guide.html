GTA 6 Cheats: Locked Until You Finish the Story

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and one of the first questions players asked was the same one they asked about every GTA before it: are there cheat codes? The answer is yes — but Rockstar changed the rules. In GTA 6, cheats are not available from the start. They are a reward for finishing the game, locked behind the final mission, and each one has to be earned separately. If you type a code during the story, nothing happens. This guide explains how the system actually works, which codes have been reported since launch, and why most of the cheat lists ranking on Google right now should not be trusted.

That matters because the cheat-code search is a minefield. Before launch, no code could possibly be verified — the game was not out — yet dozens of sites published full lists anyway. After launch, the real system turned out to be different from what anyone guessed. Knowing how cheats really work in GTA 6 saves you from wasting an evening on codes that were invented, and from downloading a "trainer" that is actually malware.

How Cheats Work in GTA 6

Launch-day guides agree on the shape of the system, even where they disagree on the details. GTA 6 has 61 cheats, and none of them is available during the story. A Cheats list appears in the Progress menu only after the final mission, and each cheat unlocks through its own challenge — finishing the story, catching 100 fish, escaping the police 25 times, completing 100 workouts, and dozens more. Four cheats unlock the moment the story ends: the $250,000 money cheat, Unlimited Focus, Attack Force, and Cycle Weather. The rest you earn by playing.

Entering a code works through the in-game phone. Press Up on the D-pad to open the phone, hold L1 and press R1 on PlayStation (LB and RB on Xbox), then enter the D-pad sequence. Every reported code is a D-pad sequence, so the inputs are identical on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Unlike GTA 4 and GTA 5, no phone-number cheat has been found — there is nothing to dial. At launch, the inputs for 17 of the 61 cheats were public, reported by several outlets at once. The most famous is the money cheat: Up, Right, Down, Left, Up, Left, Down, Right adds $250,000 to your cash — but only after the story is finished and the cheat is unlocked. Entering a known input before the credits does nothing, because no cheat is unlocked until the credits roll.

What Using a Cheat Costs You

Cheats are not free. The moment you use one, trophies and achievements switch off for that session, and so does saving — a rule confirmed by hands-on testing from Eurogamer and reported by Screen Rant. That means a cheat session is a playground session: fun, but nothing you do in it is kept. If you want the money, the weapons or the chaos, use the cheat, enjoy it, and then reload a clean save to keep playing properly. Cheats only work in the single-player story, and there is no cheat that skips the story itself — the story is the price of admission to the whole system.

The story is long, too. Engadget's reporting puts it at 91 main missions, so the Cheats list is not something you will stumble into in your first weekend. That is the point of the design: Rockstar wants the cheats to be a reward for players who finished the game, not a shortcut around it. If you are still in Chapter 1, the cheat system does not exist for you yet, and no code on the internet will change that.

Why Most Cheat Lists Online Are Fake

Here is the honest part, and it matters more than any code. Rockstar has never published a cheat list — not for GTA 3, not for GTA 5, and not for GTA 6. The codes live inside the shipped game, and the community writes them down after release. Before launch, no outside source could possibly hold a correct GTA 6 code, because the codes only existed inside Rockstar's build. Any list published before November 19, 2026, was invented, and any page stamped "verified" with a date from before launch is admitting it made the data up.

The lists that appeared after the preload opened on November 12 are a different case: dataminers pulled input strings out of the build, which gives a list that is probably right but has never been played. Only a code entered on a retail console, in the released game, and seen to work counts as confirmed. So treat every code as reported until you have entered it yourself and watched it work — and never download a "GTA 6 trainer", "cheat tool" or "demo" to get codes. Rockstar has not released a demo, and security researchers have already traced fake GTA 6 downloads to password-stealing malware. The codes are free, they are in the game, and anyone charging you for them or asking you to download something is running a scam.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do GTA 6 cheats exist?

Yes. Launch guides list 61 cheats, and they appear in the Progress menu under Cheats after the final mission. Each one unlocks through its own challenge, and the inputs for 17 of them were public at launch.

How do you enter a cheat in GTA 6?

Open the in-game phone with Up on the D-pad, hold L1 and press R1 (LB and RB on Xbox), then enter the D-pad sequence. The code only works after the story is finished and that specific cheat is unlocked.

Is there an infinite money cheat in GTA 6?

No permanent one is confirmed. The reported money cheat adds a fixed $250,000 and needs the main story finished. Money generator sites are scams — there is no code that prints money forever.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 cheats are real, but they are the end of the game, not the beginning: 61 codes, locked behind the story, entered as D-pad sequences on the phone, and paid for with your trophies and your saves for the session. Finish the 91 missions first, earn the codes, and ignore every list dated before launch day — the only codes that count are the ones that work in your game, on your console, after the credits roll.

# Niche-Cluster Research — USA Website Empire
**Date:** 2026-10-11 | **Market:** USA (EN) | **Method:** live search + SEMrush traffic snapshots + SERP competition analysis

**Strategy:** CLUSTERS, not random sites. 5 related websites per niche = topical authority. Each site owns one angle/keyword of the niche.

---

## How niches were scored
`Score = Traffic (1-10) × Low-Competition (1-10) × Monetization (1-10)`

| Evidence source | What it showed |
|---|---|
| SEMrush traffic snapshots (Aug 2026) | "salary calculator" 368K/mo, "take home pay" 301K/mo, "bmi calculator" 5M/mo, "calorie calculator" 3.35M/mo, "tdee" 450K/mo, "tip calculator" 1.83M/mo |
| SEO tool report (Jun 2026) | "ROI / Margin / Markup" 10–20K/mo, medium competition, low-medium effort |
| Pet economics 2026 | Medium dog in US urban market: $2,500–$7,500/year; pet insurance $480–960/yr, up 15–25% YoY |
| Wedding data (The Knot 2026 study) | Avg US wedding $34,200; "name change after marriage" 20–40K/mo; "marriage green card cost" 10–20K/mo at $10–35 CPC |
| Tool-factory backlog (Sep 2026) | Name-change calculator CPC $2–6; VA/immigration calculators $10–35 CPC; attorney bids relentless |

**Key pattern:** Head terms in every money/finance niche are owned by giants (calculator.net, salary.com, payscale.com, The Knot, Zola). The WIDE-OPEN gap is **long-tail + tool-specific angles** — new domains can rank for "X calculator" long-tails in 3–6 months while giants fight over head terms.

## #1 — ROI / INVESTMENT CALCULATOR CLUSTER (Score: 7 × 8 × 7 = 392)
**Why traffic is high:** "ROI / Margin / Markup" = 10–20K/mo head; long-tail (roas calculator, flip calculator, stock return calculator) adds 50K+/mo. Investors, marketers, and flippers all search ROI math constantly.
**Why competition is low:** No dominant brand owns "ROI calculator" — SERPs are fragmented small tools (volumecalculator.co, bravecalculator.com, ultimatefinancecalculator.com), many thin/outdated. No CalcNest-style authority exists for ROI.
**Monetization:** Display ads (Ezoic/Media.net) + B2B SaaS affiliates (accounting software, ad platforms) + investment platform referrals.

| # | Site concept | Core tool | Target keyword |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **ROIHub** | General ROI calculator (gain, cost, time → ROI %, annualized) | "roi calculator" |

| 3 | **FlipProfit** | House fix-and-flip profit calculator (purchase, rehab, ARV, holding) | "house flipping calculator" |
| 4 | **StockROI** | Stock/crypto return calculator (CAGR, dividends, DCA) | "stock return calculator" / "crypto profit calculator" |
| 5 | **BizROI** | Small business investment + break-even ROI | "business roi calculator" |

## Honorable mention (not in top 6)
**Health/fitness calculators** — biggest raw traffic (BMI 5M/mo, calorie 3.35M/mo; tdeecalculator.net does 1.19M USA visits/mo) but head terms are locked by calculator.net; long-tail only. Good cluster candidate for wave 2.

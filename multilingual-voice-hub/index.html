Yes — tools like HeyGen, Rask AI, and Captions translate, voice, and even lip-sync. Plan word-count differences first with our free dubbing planner: translations run 10–30% longer than English.

How do I test which browser voice speaks my language?

Use our free language voice tester: type any text, pick a language from your device's voices, and hear it instantly. Nothing is uploaded or stored.

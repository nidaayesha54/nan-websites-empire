Telehealth vs In-Person Visits: When Each Makes Sense

By DoctorClinicHub · October 2026 · 5 min read

Virtual visits are now a normal part of US healthcare — and they're usually cheaper. Here's how to choose.

Cost comparison

TelehealthIn-person
Self-pay visit$50–$150$100–$300+
Typical copay$0–$40$25–$75
Time costMinutesTravel + waiting room

Telehealth works well for

  • Follow-ups and medication management
  • Mental health therapy and psychiatry check-ins
  • Minor issues: colds, rashes (with photos), UTIs, pink eye
  • Dermatology triage (photos first, in-person if needed)
  • Chronic condition check-ins (diabetes, hypertension)

See someone in person for

  • Anything needing a physical exam, X-ray, or lab draw
  • New lumps, severe pain, or worsening symptoms
  • Ear infections in children (need an otoscope exam)
  • Procedures: stitches, vaccinations, biopsies
  • Emergencies — always the ER, never a video call

Quality check

For appropriate conditions, studies show telehealth outcomes comparable to in-person care — with higher patient satisfaction on convenience. The key is matching the visit type to the problem.

Insurance note

Most US insurers now cover telehealth similarly to in-person visits, but copays and eligible visit types vary by plan. Check your plan's telehealth policy before booking.

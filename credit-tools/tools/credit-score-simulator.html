How the estimate works

This simulator mirrors the well-known FICO factor weights: payment history 35%, amounts owed (utilization) 30%, length of history 15%, new credit 10%, credit mix 10%. Each slider is normalized to 0–1, weighted, and mapped to the 300–850 range. It is an educational model — your real score comes from bureau data and proprietary formulas.

Score bands

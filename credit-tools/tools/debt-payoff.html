How the math works

Each month the simulator accrues interest on every balance (APR/12), applies your minimums to all debts, then throws the extra payment at the priority debt — highest APR for avalanche, smallest balance for snowball — cascading to the next debt as each is cleared. Avalanche almost always minimizes interest; snowball delivers faster first wins.

Tips

  • Never miss minimums — one 30-day late can cost 60–110 score points.
  • If minimums do not cover interest, no strategy works: raise payments or negotiate APRs first.
  • Consider a 0% balance-transfer card for high-APR debt — but only with a payoff plan before the promo ends.

How the math works

DTI = total monthly debt payments ÷ gross monthly income × 100. Lenders use two flavors: front-end (housing only) and back-end (all debts). This calculator shows back-end DTI, the number that decides most mortgage approvals.

DTI = (housing + auto + cards + student + other) / gross monthly income × 100

Lender thresholds

  • Under 36% — good; comfortably qualifies for conventional mortgages.
  • 36–43% — caution; the upper limit for many qualified mortgages.
  • Over 43% — high; most conventional lenders decline, FHA may stretch to 50%+ with compensating factors.

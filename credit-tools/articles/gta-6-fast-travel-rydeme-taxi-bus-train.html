GTA 6 Fast Travel: RydeMe, Taxis, Buses and Trains

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Leonida is enormous — about twice the size of GTA 5's map — and driving everywhere takes real time. So how do you fast travel in GTA 6? The short answer is that there is no map-warp: you cannot open the map, pick a spot and teleport there. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and instead of a teleport button it gives you something more in character: a rideshare app, taxis, buses and trains, and a character switch that quietly works as a teleport. This guide covers every method, what each one costs, how to unlock them, and the one rule that can lock you out of your ride.

It matters because the map is too big to cross on foot every time. Kotaku timed a south-to-north crossing at 3 hours 21 minutes on foot, about 11 minutes by car, and under 6 minutes by plane — and most players will cross it dozens of times. Knowing the travel system is not a luxury in this game; it is how you get anything done.

RydeMe: The Rideshare App

RydeMe is GTA 6's smartphone rideshare app — the in-game version of Uber — and it is the closest thing the game has to fast travel. You unlock it after the "Buck Me" story mission in Chapter 1, when you open your BuckMe bank account: the app arrives with the account. To use it, press Up on the D-pad to open your phone, select the RydeMe app, and choose where to go — your current safehouse, an existing map waypoint, or a new destination you pick on the spot. A driver is dispatched to your location, and you can see them coming on the minimap.

Once you are in the car, hold L2 on PS5 or LT on Xbox to interact with the driver, then press Triangle on PS5 or Y on Xbox to skip the journey — and you arrive at your destination immediately. Skipping costs an additional $100 on top of the regular fare, and longer journeys cost more than shorter ones, but the total is still cheap compared to the time it saves. One warning: if you pull a gun out while in the car, or commit a criminal act near the vehicle, your RydeMe account gets suspended for about 15 to 20 minutes of in-game time, and the app becomes inaccessible. Behave in the back seat, or walk.

Paradise Taxi: The Classic Cab

The other way to get a ride is the old-fashioned one: a taxi. Paradise Taxi becomes available early in the story, after the "Gone Fishing" mission, and you call one from your phone's contacts — it should be listed as Paradise Taxi. Get in when it arrives and pick where to go: home, back to your car, or a spot you choose. The same skip rule applies — hold L2 to interact with the driver, then Triangle to skip the ride for the extra $100.

Taxis have one limit the app does not: a cab may not be able to reach you on a small island, so get back to the mainland or a bridge first. Fares are not confirmed in a published table yet, so keep some cash on hand. And remember the pocket-cash rule: you can carry a maximum of $20,000 at a time, and if you get wasted or busted you lose what you are carrying — so bank your money at ATMs before a long night of taxi rides.

Buses and Trains: The Slow, Honest Way

GTA 6 also has working public transport. Buses run routes across the state and pick up at bus stops — they appear on the minimap, and other passengers wait with you. Trains call at stations along their lines, and you wait on the platform until one arrives on its own timetable. On supported routes, you can select a destination and skip the journey after boarding, so the bus and the train are fast travel too — just on a schedule, not on demand.

They are not as flexible as a taxi: a bus goes where its route goes, and a train goes where its line goes. But they are the calm way to see Leonida — sit back, watch the state go by, and arrive without having driven a single mile. For a player who wants to learn the map, the bus is a tour. For a player who needs to be somewhere now, the taxi is the answer.

Character Switching: The Secret Teleport

The fastest way across Leonida is not a vehicle at all: it is the other protagonist. Free switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks after the "Breakfast Run" mission in Chapter 2, and when you switch, you appear where the other character is — which is usually near their current safehouse. If one of them is far outside Vice City and the other is near your destination, switching is an instant move across the map, for free.

To switch, hold Right on the D-pad to open the Partner Menu, then press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox to confirm. Hold the button — a quick tap will not open the menu. If one portrait is greyed out, your partner is busy — in a RydeMe ride, for example — and you will need to wait and retry. And some missions lock you to a single character for their whole duration, so the switch is a between-missions tool, not a way out of a job.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there fast travel in GTA 6?

There is no map-warp teleport, but there is fast travel through vehicles: RydeMe rides and Paradise Taxis let you skip the journey for an extra $100, buses and trains let you skip on supported routes, and switching characters moves you to the other lead instantly.

How do I unlock RydeMe?

RydeMe unlocks after the "Buck Me" story mission in Chapter 1, when you open your BuckMe bank account. Paradise Taxi unlocks earlier, after the "Gone Fishing" mission.

Why is my RydeMe app suspended?

Pulling a gun out in the car or committing a crime near the vehicle suspends your RydeMe account for about 15 to 20 minutes of in-game time. Wait it out, and the app comes back.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 has no teleport button, but it does not need one: RydeMe and Paradise Taxi skip the drive for $100, buses and trains run on their own schedules, and switching to the other protagonist is a free teleport across the state. Unlock RydeMe in Chapter 1, keep $100 spare for the skip, behave in the back seat, and Leonida shrinks to a manageable size.

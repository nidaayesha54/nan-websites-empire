🧩 SSML Helper

SSML (Speech Synthesis Markup Language) controls pauses, emphasis, pronunciation, and pacing. Pick tags, type your text, and copy production-ready markup — compatible with ElevenLabs, Amazon Polly, Google Cloud TTS, and Azure.

SSML cheat sheet




TagUse it for


<break time="500ms"/>Natural pauses at commas and before big reveals


<emphasis level="strong">Stress on keywords like "free", "never", "secret"


<prosody rate="90%">Slow down for tutorials, speed up for excitement


<say-as interpret-as="digits">Phone numbers and codes read digit-by-digit


<sub alias="…">Fix abbreviations ("CEO" → "C. E. O.")




Note: exact tag support varies by provider — ElevenLabs, Amazon Polly, Google, and Azure each document their own flavor. Test every snippet before publishing. Learn the full playbook in our guide: how to make TTS sound natural.

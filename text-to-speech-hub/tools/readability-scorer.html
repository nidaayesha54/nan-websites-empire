What the scores mean for voiceovers

  • Reading Ease 70–100: conversational and clear — ideal for YouTube, ads, and podcasts.
  • Reading Ease 50–69: fine for documentaries and explainers, but simplify long sentences.
  • Reading Ease below 50: too dense to sound natural — rewrite before recording.
  • Grade level 6–8: the sweet spot for spoken content; most viral narrations sit here.

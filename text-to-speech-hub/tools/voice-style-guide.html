🎭 Voice Style Guide

Pick a tone and get a ready-to-use sample script plus the exact voice, rate, pitch, and pause settings to match it. Preview the script with your browser's voices.

Why voice style matters

A documentary voice reading a comedy script — or a cheerful voice reading financial disclosures — instantly breaks trust. Matching tone to content is the single fastest way to make synthetic speech believable.

Quick matching rules

  • Tutorials & explainers → professional, rate ~1.0, pitch ~1.0
  • YouTube & social → friendly, rate 1.05–1.15, pitch slightly up
  • Stories & trailers → dramatic, rate 0.8–0.9, pitch down
  • Meditation & sleep → calm, rate 0.75–0.85, long pauses

For deeper control, take your chosen style into the SSML helper and add precise pauses and emphasis, or test your script's fit with the readability scorer.

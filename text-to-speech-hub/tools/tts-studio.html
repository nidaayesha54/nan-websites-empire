How to use the TTS studio

  1. Paste your text — scripts, blog drafts, or e-book chapters. Longer text is fine; the studio speaks it in order.
  2. Pick a voice — your browser lists every voice installed on your device. US English voices are sorted first.
  3. Dial in the delivery — slow the rate for tutorials, raise the pitch for upbeat intros, or lower it for dramatic narration.
  4. Preview before you produce — test pacing here, then paste your script into a premium tool like ElevenLabs for the final render.

Want a pro-level result? Pair this studio with our voice style guide for tone-matched settings and the SSML helper for precise pauses and emphasis.

❓ Frequently asked questions

What is text-to-speech (TTS)?

Text-to-speech is technology that converts written text into spoken audio. Modern AI voices sound remarkably human and are used for YouTube narration, audiobooks, podcasts, and accessibility.

Can I use free TTS tools for YouTube videos?

Yes, many free TTS tools allow commercial use — but always check the license. Our voice studio runs entirely in your browser with your system's voices: free for any use.

How do I make TTS sound natural?

Use SSML tags for pauses and emphasis, keep sentences under 20 words, match the voice to your content's tone, and fine-tune rate and pitch. Read the full guide.

What is SSML?

SSML (Speech Synthesis Markup Language) is XML markup that controls how TTS engines speak — pauses, emphasis, pronunciation, pitch, and rate. Try our free SSML builder.

How long does it take to speak 1,000 words?

At an average 150 words per minute, 1,000 words take about 6–7 minutes. Paste your script into our readability scorer for an exact estimate.

GTA 6 Motorcycles: Every Bike, Ranked by Speed

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and for two wheels it is the richest game in the series. Fifteen motorcycles — superbikes, cruisers, dirt bikes, scooters and even a rental bicycle — are scattered across Leonida, from the nightclubs of Vice City to the mud tracks of Mount Kalaga. This guide ranks every bike by speed, explains what each one is good for, and tells you where to find it.

The fastest bike in the game is the Nagasaki Carbon RS, a superbike modelled on the Ducati 1199 with a top speed of 310 km/h and handling rated 90 out of 100. It is the bike to steal if you want to outrun anything on the highway. Second is the Principe Alvino V1, an agile Italian superbike based on the Ducati Panigale V2, at 280 km/h with handling of 88 — explosive acceleration and razor-sharp handling, and a lightweight frame that makes it a track monster on Vice City's open highways. Third is the Dinka Double-T, a Honda CBR-based superbike at 270 km/h, and behind it the Nagasaki SFB 1000, a sleek high-performance sports bike with an aerodynamic silhouette. Sport bikes dominate the speed rankings, and they spawn where the money is: outside nightclubs, in wealthy neighborhoods, and at dealerships.

Cruisers, dirt bikes and the odd ones out

Not every bike is built for speed. The cruisers are built for the ride. The Western Zombie Bobber is a distinct chopper — loud, showy, and popular with biker gangs — and the Western Sovereign is an elegant modern chopper with retro 1950s styling, comfortable and curvaceous, modelled on the Harley-Davidson Road King. Cruisers spawn at biker bars, tattoo parlors and in rural areas. The dirt bikes live off-road: the Maibatsu Sanchez, a popular dirt bike that excels on mud and sand, and the Maibatsu Manchez, a lightweight cross-country racer, both spawn at motocross tracks, farms and beach areas around Leonida, along with the antique Dinka Enduro, a 1970s dual-sport that handles well on asphalt and dirt alike. The Enduro has a special place in the game: it is Jason's safehouse bonus vehicle in the Ultimate Edition, with an exclusive Army fatigue livery and long-travel suspension. Then there are the odd ones: the Pegassi Faggio scooter, a Piaggio Vespa-style runabout at 70 mph, the three-wheeled electric Maibatsu Boomazoom mobility scooter, and the Walter Lombike, a bicycle available for rental at locations around Leonida as part of a public transit programme.

The full list: all fifteen bikes

Here is the whole garage, by class. Superbikes: the Nagasaki Carbon RS, the Principe Alvino V1, the Dinka Double-T and the Nagasaki SFB 1000. Sports bikes: the Pegassi Bati 801 and the Principe Lectro, an electric streetfighter. Cruisers and choppers: the Western Zombie Bobber, the Western Zombie Chopper, the Western Sovereign 107, the Western Nightblade and the LCC Avarus, a three-wheeled custom. Dirt bikes: the Maibatsu Sanchez, the Maibatsu Manchez, the Dinka Enduro and the Bravado Aphid, an ultra-lightweight dirt bike built for off-roading. Scooters: the Pegassi Faggio and the Shift-E Fan-E, a lightweight electric scooter popular with commuters for its convenience and affordability. And the odd ones: the Nagasaki Street Blazer quad bike, the Maibatsu Boomazoom three-wheeled electric mobility scooter, and the Walter Lombike rental bicycle. That is fifteen motorcycles plus the rental bike, and the list is still being updated as players find more — the guides say they will keep adding bikes as they are uncovered.

Two bikes deserve a special mention. The Dinka Enduro is the Ultimate Edition's safehouse bonus vehicle, a rugged dual-sport with an exclusive Army fatigue livery and long-travel suspension, modelled on the Honda XL350 and XR500 — a bike that bridges pavement and dirt, and the one Jason starts with if you bought the top edition. The other is the Faggio, the humble scooter that has been in the series since Vice City: 70 mph, a Piaggio Vespa look, and the bike you ride when you do not care about speed at all. Rental bike racks have been spotted on Vice City streets, and the Lombike is part of a public transit programme, so even the slowest ride in the game has a place. A bike is not just transport in Leonida. It is a statement about how you ride.

What to ride, and what to watch out for

Bikes are the fastest way through Vice City traffic, and the riskiest way to fight. A sport bike will lane-split through dense traffic during a high-speed getaway in a way no car can match, but you are exposed — a crash that dents a car will throw you off a bike, and the Shitzu PCJ-600, the franchise staple that has appeared in every GTA since Vice City, is the balanced pick if you want speed without the superbike's twitchiness. Rental bike racks have been spotted on Vice City streets, so you do not even need to steal one to try. One honest note: these are community-tracked vehicle details from launch-era guides, and they may change as more information arrives — Rockstar has not published a bike list of its own. But the pattern is clear. If you prefer two wheels to four, Leonida has a bike for every road, every mud track and every getaway.

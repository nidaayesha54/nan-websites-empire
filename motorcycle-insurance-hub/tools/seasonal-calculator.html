    How seasonal motorcycle insurance works

    With a lay-up policy, your insurer suspends the expensive parts of your coverage (liability, collision) during declared storage months, while comprehensive stays on — so theft, fire, vandalism and storm damage are still covered. When riding season returns, full coverage is reactivated, usually with a phone call.

    Watch out for these catches

    • Lender rules: if you have a loan, your lender probably requires year-round full coverage — a lay-up policy may violate your loan terms.
    • State requirements: some states penalize registered vehicles without continuous liability. You may need to surrender plates or file a storage affidavit.
    • Coverage gaps: never fully cancel — a coverage lapse can raise your future rates more than the lay-up saves.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does motorcycle insurance cost on average in the USA?
Most riders pay $500 to $1,500 per year. Sport bikes and riders under 25 can pay $2,000+, while a liability-only policy on a used cruiser can be under $300/year. Try our quote estimator for your numbers.
Is motorcycle insurance required by law?
Nearly every state requires at least liability coverage for motorcycles. Florida is the main exception, but riding uninsured there is still risky — one at-fault accident can cost tens of thousands.
What is the cheapest way to insure a motorcycle?
Liability-only coverage on a smaller, used standard or cruiser bike, plus a clean record, a completed safety course, and bundling discounts. Our cheap insurance guide walks through all 12 tactics.
Does motorcycle insurance cover my helmet and gear?
Usually barely — many policies include only $0 to $1,000 of gear coverage. An accessory endorsement ($1,000–$3,000+) covers helmets, jackets and custom parts for a few dollars a month. Details in our gear coverage guide.
Can I get seasonal motorcycle insurance for winter?
Yes — a lay-up/seasonal policy drops liability and collision during non-riding months and keeps comprehensive. Compare the savings with our seasonal calculator.

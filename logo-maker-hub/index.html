Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about making logos for free.

Is LogoMakerHub really free?

Yes. Every tool on LogoMakerHub runs entirely in your browser and is 100% free, with SVG and PNG downloads and no watermark.

What format should I download my logo in?

SVG for anything scalable (websites, print, merchandise) and PNG for quick digital use. SVG is a vector format, so it stays sharp at any size.

Can I use my logo commercially?

Yes — logos you create here are yours to use commercially. For trademark protection of a brand name, consider registering it with your local trademark office.

Do I need design skills to make a logo here?

No. Pick a tool, type your business name, choose a style or icon, and download — the whole process takes under two minutes.

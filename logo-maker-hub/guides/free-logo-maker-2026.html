Best Free Logo Makers in 2026 (Truly Free, No Watermark)

Searching for a free logo maker in 2026 is frustrating: half the tools that advertise "free" slap on a watermark or demand $20–$60 at the download screen. This guide separates the genuinely free tools from the freemium traps, so you can make a professional logo without spending a cent.

What "truly free" means

A genuinely free logo maker lets you: design in the browser, preview everything, and download usable files (SVG or high-res PNG) with no watermark and no forced signup. If a tool hides the download button behind a paywall, it is not free — it is a trial.

Our picks for 2026

1. LogoMakerHub tools (this site) — free, no signup

Our text logo maker and icon logo maker run entirely in your browser. Six typography styles, eleven vector icons, custom colors, and instant SVG + PNG downloads. No account, no watermark, no upsell — the whole thing is the free tier.

2. Canva Logo Maker — free with account

Canva's free plan includes thousands of logo templates and free PNG downloads. Downsides: you need an account, many premium elements cost money, and exported logos are raster-only on the free plan. Great for brainstorming; weaker for print.

3. Looka (trial) — paywalled at download

Looka's AI generates beautiful concepts for free, but downloading any usable file starts around $20. Use it for inspiration, not for a free final logo.

4. Hatchful by Shopify — free, retired features

Hatchful was a solid free option, but Shopify has wound it down. If you find a working mirror, exports are free — just verify the files are high resolution.

5. Inkscape — free professional vector editor

If you want total control and are willing to learn, Inkscape (open-source Illustrator alternative) makes genuinely professional logos for free. The learning curve is the price you pay.

Free vs. freemium: the honest comparison

  • Truly free tools give you final files with no strings — best for startups, side projects, and MVPs.
  • Freemium tools are better design playgrounds with bigger template libraries, but budget $20–$65 if you want the download.
  • AI logo generators produce striking concepts fast, but check the license: some retain rights to generated marks.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best truly free logo maker?

Browser-based tools like the ones on LogoMakerHub are truly free — no account, no watermark, no paywall at download. Many big-name 'free' logo makers charge $20–$60 to unlock downloads.

Can I trademark a free logo?

You can trademark a brand name, but trademark offices generally require the logo to be original. Simple text and generic icons are harder to protect — consult a trademark attorney for your market.

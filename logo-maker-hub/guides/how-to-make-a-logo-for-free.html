How to Make a Logo for Free: Step-by-Step Guide

You can make a professional logo for free in under ten minutes. This guide walks through the exact steps — from choosing a name treatment to exporting files you can actually use — using free tools only.

Step 1: Lock in your name treatment

Decide exactly how your brand name appears: capitalization, spacing, and whether you include a tagline. "Acme Co" and "ACME CO" read very differently. Write it down and stay consistent everywhere.

Step 2: Choose a style that matches your industry

  • Bold sans-serif — tech, fitness, modern services.
  • Script — beauty, weddings, boutique brands.
  • Serif — law, finance, heritage brands.
  • Minimal / outline — startups, portfolios, premium minimalism.

Open our free text logo maker, type your name, and click through the six styles to compare them side by side.

Step 3: Add an icon mark (optional but powerful)

An icon makes your logo recognizable at favicon size and on social profiles. In the icon logo maker, pick a simple shape — star, bolt, leaf, shield — in one brand color. Simple beats clever: the best icons are readable at 16 pixels.

Step 4: Pick your colors deliberately

One primary color plus one neutral is enough. Blue signals trust, green signals growth, black signals luxury, orange signals energy. Keep contrast high — your logo must work on both light and dark backgrounds. (Deeper dive: logo color psychology.)

Step 5: Test every size that matters

Upload your design to the logo size checker and inspect it as a 16px favicon, a circular social avatar, and a large print file. If the text vanishes at small sizes, create a simplified icon-only version — most major brands maintain one.

Step 6: Download the right files

  • SVG — your master file. Vector, infinitely scalable, tiny file size. Use for web and print.
  • PNG (transparent) — for social media, documents, and anywhere SVG is not accepted.
  • Favicon — export a 32px PNG or ICO for the browser tab.

Both downloads are one click in our tools — get your SVG now.

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to make a logo for free?

With a free online tool, 5–10 minutes: pick a style, type your name, choose colors, and download. The strategy (name, colors, testing sizes) is what takes the thought.

What file format do I need for a logo?

Get an SVG (vector) master for scalability plus a PNG for everyday digital use. Ask a printer for a CMYK PDF if you run physical signage.

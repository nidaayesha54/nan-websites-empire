Logo Size Checker & Guide

Upload your logo (or use the sample) and see how it holds up at the sizes that matter: browser favicon, social profiles, app icons, and print.

Your logo

Favicon — 16×16px

Browser tab icon. Simple shapes survive; fine text disappears.

Retina favicon — 32×32px

High-DPI tab and bookmark icon.

Social profile — ~98×98px

Instagram / Facebook / X display picture (shown circular).

App icon — 180×180px

iOS app icon base size; rounded corners applied by the OS.

Print / large — 500px+

For print, export at 300 DPI (a 2-inch logo = 600px). Always keep a vector SVG master.

💡 Rule of thumb: if your logo is readable at 16px, it works everywhere. If text vanishes, use your icon mark alone at small sizes — make one with our icon logo maker.

10 Free Logo Maker Tools Compared: What’s Actually Free in 2026?

Published October 2026 · 6 min read

"Free logo maker" is one of the most misleading phrases on the internet. We tested ten popular tools and scored each on one question: can you walk away with a usable logo file without paying?

The scorecard

  • LogoMakerHub — Truly free. SVG + PNG, no signup, no watermark. Best for: fast, clean text and icon logos.
  • Canva — Free with account; PNG downloads free, no SVG on free plan. Best for: template browsing.
  • Adobe Express — Free tier with watermark-free PNG exports. Best for: Adobe ecosystem users.
  • Looka — Free to design, ~$20+ to download. Best for: AI concept inspiration only.
  • Tailor Brands — Free preview, paid downloads from ~$4/month. Best for: full brand kits if you pay.
  • Wix Logo Maker — Free to design, paid downloads. Best for: Wix site owners.
  • DesignEvo — Free low-res downloads; high-res costs. Best for: quick drafts.
  • Ucraft — Free SVG downloads historically; verify current terms. Best for: simple vector marks.
  • LogoMakr — Free low-res, paid high-res. Best for: experimenting.
  • Inkscape — 100% free professional vector editor. Best for: DIY designers willing to learn.

Our verdict

If you need a finished, usable logo today at zero cost, use a truly free browser tool (like ours) and export an SVG master. If you want AI-generated concepts and have $20–$65 to spend, the freemium AI tools earn their price. Either way, run the result through our logo size checker before publishing — and skim the mistakes checklist.

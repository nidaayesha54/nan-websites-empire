Logo Color Psychology: What Your Brand Colors Say

Published October 2026 · 5 min read

Color is the fastest signal your logo sends — customers form impressions in milliseconds, before reading a single letter. Here is what the most common brand colors communicate, and how to choose.

What each color says

  • Blue — trust, stability, professionalism. The default for tech, finance, and healthcare.
  • Red — energy, urgency, appetite. Food brands, sales, entertainment.
  • Green — growth, health, nature. Eco brands, wellness, finance.
  • Black — luxury, power, sophistication. Fashion, premium services.
  • Orange — friendliness, creativity, value. Startups, kids' brands.
  • Purple — imagination, premium, wisdom. Beauty, creative agencies.
  • Yellow — optimism, attention. Use sparingly — it strains at small sizes.

How to pick yours

  1. Choose one primary color that matches your industry's expectations — or deliberately breaks them to stand out.
  2. Add one neutral (charcoal, white, warm gray) for text and backgrounds.
  3. Test the logo in black and white. If it fails there, the color is doing too much work.
  4. Check contrast: your logo must stay readable on light and dark backgrounds.

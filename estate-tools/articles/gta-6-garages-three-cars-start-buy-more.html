GTA 6 Garages: 3 Cars to Start, 8 More to Buy

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Keeping a car in GTA 6 is not automatic. Steal a car, park it, come back later — and it may be gone. The game only keeps the cars you register, and you only have room for three of them at the start. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and this guide explains the whole vehicle system: how to register a stolen car, how many you can own, how to buy more space, and what happens when the police take your car away.

It matters because cars are not just transport in this game — they are storage. Your personal vehicle carries your weapons and your outfits in its trunk, so losing a car means losing your loadout too. Learning the garage system early is the difference between a collection and a series of disappearances.

Three Cars to Start — and What That Means

Early in the game you have storage space for only three vehicles. That figure comes from Rockstar North's Rob Nelson, speaking to Famitsu before launch: a stolen car can be registered as your own, but with three stored you have to sell one to make room for a new personal vehicle. That is a total for the early game, not a number of cars per garage — and Rockstar has not published a per-garage capacity, so treat any number you see for a single garage as a guess.

The rule that matters most is the registration rule. A car that is not registered disappears when you restart the game — it is not yours, and the game does not keep it. A registered car is a personal vehicle, kept at your safehouse, and it stays. Until Chapter 2, your personal vehicle is the only car the game keeps for you: anything else you park at home is gone when you come back. In Chapter 1 that vehicle is Jason's '70 Ganado, and it doubles as storage — weapons and outfits in the trunk, ready whenever you need them.

Buying Garages: 8 to Buy, One Car Each

Garages open after Daily Driver, the Chapter 2 mission that follows Moses. From that point you can buy garages in the Vice City Real Estate app on your phone — eight of them, starting at around $4,000, with better locations costing more, and roughly $160,000 for the full set. Each garage should hold a single car, according to launch-week reporting, so plan on one garage per car you register beyond your safehouse spots. Buying every one earns the Little Boxes trophy, and registering ten cars to the Scooter Bros app earns On the Spot. Garages the story gives you do not count toward the trophy.

Two garages come with the editions rather than the market. Shore Court Garage near Ocean Beach comes with the pre-order bonus and holds the '55 Vapid Stanier, and Paradise Garage in Watson Bay comes with the Ultimate Edition and holds the '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy. Rockstar's own text gives each one a weapon locker and a secure place to deposit stolen goods to be fenced — but it has not said how many cars either garage holds, or whether they count toward the three starting slots.

Safehouses, Parking Spots and Scooter Bros

Safehouses work differently from garages: they cannot be bought. They unlock as the story moves on, each chapter giving you its own safehouse, and you cannot go back once a chapter ends. Safehouses come with parking spots, and a stolen car left in one of those spots should stay put even unregistered — the motel in Chapter 2 has a spot outside your room, and the Chapter 3 apartment should have two, according to launch-week reporting. That is the early-game workaround for the three-car limit: the safehouse spots are free storage, even before you can buy a garage.

Once a car is registered, you do not have to go and fetch it. The Scooter Bros app on your phone brings a vehicle you own to where you are — a delivery of your own car, not a spawn, and a free one: Jason's Ganado is brought back for free by Joey. And the personal vehicle's trunk is the real reason to keep a car close: it holds your weapons, so you can change your equipped loadout on the go — two sidearms and two longarms at a time — and it holds up to five saved outfits, so your wardrobe travels with you.

The Impound: What Happens When the Police Take Your Car

If the police find your personal car after you abandon it during a crime, officers guard it and then have it towed. You can sneak back and take it before that happens — but after the impound, getting it back costs more. An impounded car waits at the impound lot marked on your map; which lot it goes to depends on where you are in the story. At the lot, pay the fine, or take the car out through the gate and lose the wanted level that follows. If the office will not let you pay, breaking it out is the fallback.

The Chapter 1 mission Unwanted Attention teaches the same break-out at the Hamlet impound, and it is worth learning there, when the stakes are low. The broader lesson is to treat your registered cars as assets with a recovery cost: a car left behind after a crime can be impounded, a car that is not registered simply vanishes, and a car in a garage you own is the only one that is truly safe.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many cars can you own in GTA 6?

Three personal vehicles at the start, according to Rockstar North's Rob Nelson. Buying garages around the map raises the limit — eight garages are buyable after the Daily Driver mission in Chapter 2, starting at around $4,000 each.

Do stolen cars disappear in GTA 6?

Yes, if they are not registered. An unregistered car vanishes when you restart the game. Register it as a personal vehicle and it is kept at your safehouse — but you need a free space first.

How do I get my impounded car back?

Go to the impound lot icon on your map and pay the fine, or break the car out through the gate. If the police are still guarding it, sneak back and take it before they tow it.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 garages are simple once you know the rules: three cars to start, register what you want to keep, buy garages after Daily Driver for more room, and never leave a registered car behind after a crime. The Ganado is your first car, the trunk is your armoury, and the impound lot is where carelessness sends you. Keep the cars you love registered, garaged and close — Leonida is too big to walk.

Common questions

Do I need a will if I don't have much money? +
Yes. A will names guardians for minor children and an executor, and keeps the state from deciding those matters. Wealth is only one part of estate planning.
How much can you inherit without paying federal estate tax? +
An estimated $13.6 million per individual in 2026 (about $27.2 million for married couples using portability) is exempt. Amounts above that face a 40% federal rate. State taxes may apply at much lower thresholds.
What happens if I die without a will? +
State intestacy laws decide who inherits and who raises minor children. The process is slower, costlier, and may not reflect your wishes at all.
Do beneficiary designations override a will? +
Yes — retirement accounts, life insurance, and POD/TOD accounts pass directly to the named beneficiary no matter what your will says. Keep them updated after every major life event.

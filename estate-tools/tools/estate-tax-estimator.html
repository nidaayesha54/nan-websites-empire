Frequently asked questions

What is the federal estate tax exemption for 2026? +
An estimated $13.6 million per individual — about $27.2 million for married couples using portability. The IRS adjusts it annually for inflation, so confirm the current figure before making decisions.
What is the federal estate tax rate? +
A flat 40% — but only on the amount above the exemption, not the whole estate. An $18M taxable estate for a single filer faces 40% on roughly $4.4M.
Do states have their own estate taxes? +
Yes — about a dozen states plus DC levy estate taxes, often with exemptions of just $1–2 million. Several states also impose inheritance taxes on beneficiaries. Federal exemption means nothing at the state level.

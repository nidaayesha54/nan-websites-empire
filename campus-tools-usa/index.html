Frequently Asked Questions

Are these college tools really free?

Yes. Every tool is 100% free with no signup, no account, and no limits.

Which universities are these tools designed for?

Any accredited USA college or university — Harvard, Stanford, MIT, Yale, Princeton, state schools, and community colleges. The 4.0 GPA scale is standard across US higher education.

Do you store my grades or personal data?

No. All calculations run in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, stored, or shared.

How accurate is the GPA calculator?

It uses the standard US 4.0 scale: total grade points ÷ total credit hours. Confirm with your registrar, as some schools use +/- variations.

GTA 6 Hidden Locations: Where to Look First

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Every GTA game hides things, and GTA 6 is already hiding them. The game launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and within a day, players had found a literal Easter egg on top of the tallest tower in Vice City, Bigfoot in a swamp, and an apartment modelled on Dexter Morgan's. But the honest warning first: Rockstar has not confirmed any hidden location or secret reward in GTA 6. Everything below is either confirmed by Rockstar, reported by a named outlet, or a sensible place to look — and this guide labels each one. Do not trust any guide that does not.

The Map: Six Regions, and Where to Look in Each

Leonida has six regions, and each one hides things in its own way. Vice City is the Miami-inspired heart of the map, with Ocean Beach, Downtown, the port and the airport — and Rockstar calls it a return to modern-day Vice City. The Leonida Keys are the island chain to the south, with small coastal towns, boat docks, trailer parks and shallow ocean waters. The Grassrivers are the vast Everglades-inspired swamps to the west, all waterways, mud and hidden shacks. Port Gellhorn is the faded coastal town. Ambrosia is the inland sugar country. And Mount Kalaga National Park is the mountain wilderness in the north, with rivers, forests and the kayaking routes.

The hunting rule is simple: the further a place is from the main roads, the more likely it is hiding something. Rooftops, the port, the classic districts from the 2002 game, the underwater reefs, the swamp channels, the mountain trails — start with the places that look like they were built to be found, and work outward from there.

Reported: The Easter Egg, Bigfoot, and Dexter's Apartment

The best-known finds so far all come from named outlets, and all are labelled Reported — Rockstar does not comment on Easter eggs. The first is a literal painted Easter egg in a nest on top of Vice City's tallest tower: a climb players say takes six to seven minutes, and throwing the egg triggers a short cutscene. The second is Bigfoot in Gullet Marsh — GameSpot places him there and says a story mission gives you a close encounter. The third is a Dexter Morgan apartment on Washington Island, near the Vice Beach Top Fun Drones shop, modelled on the apartment from the TV show: you cannot go in.

Those three are the finds with sources behind them — GameSpot, Kotaku and other outlets, checked on November 19. Treat anything beyond them with caution. The internet is already full of "hidden location" lists with no source, no screenshot and no outlet behind them, and most of those are guesses dressed up as discoveries. If a guide does not name its source, it does not have one.

Confirmed: The Callbacks Rockstar Put in Writing

A few things are confirmed, because Rockstar put them in its own descriptions. The Vercetti Estate — Tommy Vercetti's mansion from GTA Vice City — is named in the official description of the Ultimate Edition revolvers, which Rockstar says are sourced from the estate. The PTT Youngin$ Illegal Goods Store is a confirmed Ultimate Edition raid that yields special items. And Washington Beach and Ocean Beach are confirmed classic Vice City districts, named in Rockstar's own bonus descriptions. These are not rumours: they are Rockstar's own words, and they tell you where to look.

The Vintage Vice City Pack is another confirmed callback: the pre-order bonus includes a weapon pattern inspired by Tommy Vercetti's palm-tree shirt from the 2002 game. Rockstar is telling you that the old Vice City is still in this one — and the places that mattered in 2002 are the places to check first in 2026.

How to Hunt: The Tools That Reach Hidden Places

Rockstar confirmed four ways to reach the places most players skip: scuba diving, snorkelling, base jumping and airboats. Scuba diving takes you to the shipwrecks, coral reefs and hidden underwater areas off the Leonida Keys. Snorkelling is the shallow-water version, for the reefs and the shoreline. Base jumping takes you off the tallest buildings and the cliffs of Mount Kalaga. And airboats take you through the Grassrivers swamps, where a normal boat would ground itself.

Read the murals and graffiti in Vice City, too — GTA 5's biggest clue was painted on a wall, and Rockstar hides its secrets in plain sight. The same goes for the collectibles: card sets, watches, cocaine flamingos, underwater treasure and WAiNK scans are the trail markers for the hidden locations. Follow them, and they will take you to the places the game wants you to find.

What Not to Believe

The hunt attracts liars, and the red flags are always the same. Any "hidden location" that requires a download, a survey, or a payment is a scam. Any list that claims a secret without a source, a screenshot, or a named outlet is a guess. Any video that promises a hidden location "before the story ends" is probably showing a place you can walk to at any time — the game does not gate its geography behind the story. And any claim that Rockstar "confirmed" a hidden location should be checked against Rockstar's own site: Rockstar has confirmed regions, activities and callbacks, but it has not confirmed a single hidden location or secret reward.

The honest position is the one this guide takes: the hunt has started, a few finds have sources, and the rest is looking. The best hidden locations in GTA history were found by players looking in the right places, not by guides listing them on day one. Go looking.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are there confirmed hidden locations in GTA 6?

No. Rockstar has not confirmed any hidden location or secret reward. The finds so far — the Easter egg tower, Bigfoot, Dexter's apartment — are reported by named outlets like GameSpot, not confirmed by Rockstar. The confirmed items are callbacks in Rockstar's own descriptions, like the Vercetti Estate.

Where should I look first?

Start with the six regions: Vice City rooftops and the port, the Leonida Keys reefs and islands, the Grassrivers swamps by airboat, and Mount Kalaga by kayak. Use scuba diving for the underwater spots, and follow the collectibles — they are the trail markers.

How do I reach the tallest tower Easter egg?

Climb the tallest tower in Vice City — players report the climb takes six to seven minutes. The nest with the painted Easter eggs is at the top; take one and throw it for a short cutscene. This is reported, not officially confirmed.

The Bottom Line

No hidden location in GTA 6 is officially confirmed — but the hunt is real, and the first finds are already in: a literal Easter egg on the tallest tower, Bigfoot in Gullet Marsh, a Dexter apartment on Washington Island. Go to the six regions, use the boats and the diving gear, read the walls, and follow the collectibles. The secrets are out there. Go find them.

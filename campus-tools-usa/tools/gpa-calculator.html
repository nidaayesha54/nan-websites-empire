How College GPA Works (4.0 Scale)

Your GPA is the weighted average of your grades. Each letter grade converts to grade points, multiplied by credit hours:

Formula: GPA = Σ (grade points × credit hours) ÷ Σ credit hours

Tip: High-credit courses move your GPA more. A 4-credit A helps four times more than a 1-credit A — prioritize accordingly.

FAQs

How is college GPA calculated?

Total grade points divided by total credit hours, on the 4.0 scale.

Semester vs cumulative GPA?

Semester covers one term. Cumulative combines all credits weighted by hours — never average semester GPAs directly.

What GPA do scholarships need?

Usually 3.0+; competitive awards 3.5+; summa cum laude 3.9–4.0.

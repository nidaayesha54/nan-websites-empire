How the Planner Works

Subjects are weighted by difficulty (1–5) and days remaining until each exam. Harder, sooner exams get priority. The final 2 days before each exam are reserved for review and practice problems — research consistently shows retrieval practice beats re-reading.

Study smarter: 25-minute focused blocks (Pomodoro) with 5-minute breaks outperform 3-hour marathons. Sleep consolidates memory — never sacrifice it the night before.

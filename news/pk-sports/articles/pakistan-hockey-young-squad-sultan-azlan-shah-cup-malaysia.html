Pakistan Hockey Take Young Squad to Malaysia for Sultan Azlan Shah Cup

November 21, 2026

Pakistan's national hockey team has departed for Malaysia to take part in the 32nd Sultan Azlan Shah Cup 2026, with the selectors placing their faith in a young and largely inexperienced squad and leaving out several senior campaigners, The News reported on November 20.

A young side, and notable omissions

The prestigious six-nation tournament will be played in Ipoh, Malaysia, from November 20 to 28, 2026. Hosts Malaysia will be joined by Pakistan, Australia, South Korea, Canada and the United States. The 18-member final squad announced by the Pakistan Hockey Federation (PHF) has drawn considerable attention, not so much for who has been included but for who has been left out. Former captain Ammad Shakeel Butt and first-choice goalkeeper Ali Raza are the most notable omissions from the touring party. Abu Bakar captains the side, with goalkeepers Abdullah Ishtiaq and Waqar, defenders Sufyan Khan, Muhammad Abdullah and Nadeem Khan, midfielders Moin Shakeel, Hammaduddin, Samiullah, Muhammad Imad, Zakaria Hayat and Umar Mustafa, and forwards Rana Waheed, Ghazanfar Ali, Ahmed Nadeem, Abdul Rehman, Hanan Shahid and Afraz.

The road to the final

Pakistan begin their campaign against Canada in the opening match on November 20. They will then face the United States on November 21 in their second league fixture. After a day's rest, the Green Shirts will take on the toughest challenge of the group stage when they meet Australia on November 23. Pakistan will then face South Korea on November 24, before rounding off their league matches against hosts Malaysia on November 26. The final and classification matches for all positions will be played on November 28, with the top two teams after the single-league round competing for the title. The tournament is an important test for Pakistan's rebuilding side, and a chance for the young squad to prove itself against established hockey nations.

Sources: The News, November 20, 2026

Sajjad Ibrahim Hits Record 17 Sixes in a First-Class Innings in Quaid-e-Azam Trophy

November 23, 2026

Peshawar Whites batter Sajjad Ibrahim created a national record by hitting 17 sixes in a first-class innings during his 77-ball 139 against Larkana, Dawn reported on November 23.

A 77-ball blitz

Ibrahim's innings powered Peshawar Whites to 555 for six in reply to Larkana's 235 in the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Silver competition. Seventeen sixes in a single first-class innings is a new national record, and the 77-ball tempo of the knock made it one of the most destructive innings seen in Pakistan's domestic first-class competition in recent years. The innings underlined the changing shape of the domestic game, where even the longer format now regularly produces the kind of hitting once reserved for white-ball cricket.

Around the grounds

Elsewhere in the fourth round of the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Gold, centuries by Atiq-ur-Rehman and Asim Ali Nasir put Faisalabad in a commanding position against Karachi Blues, with Faisalabad reaching 426-4 in 87 overs to take a 241-run lead — Atiq unbeaten on 135 and Asim on 132. Peshawar Blues were dismissed for 122, conceding a 67-run first-innings lead to Sialkot Blues, before Niaz Khan's 7-61 left them chasing 268. Shaheen Shah Afridi took four wickets for 81 as Islamabad dismissed Fata for 216, while Lahore Blues defeated Bahawalpur by an innings and 62 runs and AJK secured a nine-wicket victory over DMJ.

Sources: Dawn, November 23, 2026

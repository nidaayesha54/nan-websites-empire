Babar and Shaheen Headline Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Opening Round as 69th Edition Begins

November 3, 2026

Pakistan Test captain Babar Azam and One-day International skipper Shaheen Shah Afridi will headline the opening round of the 69th edition of the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy, which gets under way across Islamabad, Abbottabad and Rawalpindi on Tuesday, Dawn reported on November 3.

The headline clash

Babar will turn out for Lahore Whites, who face Shaheen-led Fata at the Diamond Cricket Ground in Islamabad, in what promises to be one of the early highlights of the premier first-class competition. The Pakistan Cricket Board announced the schedule and team rosters on Monday for the Gold and Silver competitions, which will run simultaneously and feature 20 regional sides.

The format

Eight teams will compete in the Gold tier — Karachi Blues, Faisalabad, Fata, Lahore Whites, Islamabad, Peshawar Blues, Multan and Sialkot Blues — with the competition running from November 3 to December 12 and the five-day final scheduled for December 15 to 19. The 12-team Silver competition provides a pathway to the top tier for next season, with group matches from November 3 to 30 and the final between the two group winners scheduled for December 3 to 7.

Sources: Dawn, November 3, 2026

Pakistan Turn to Sri Lanka Tests on November 9 With Babar's Form the Big Question

November 6, 2026

Pakistan's two-Test home series against Sri Lanka begins on November 9 in Rawalpindi, with the hosts coming off a domestic round that offered sharply contrasting form lines for their two senior batters, Wisden and Geo Super reported on November 6.

The form guide

The first round of the 2026 Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Gold concluded on Friday, November 6. Mohammad Rizwan built Peshawar Blues' imposing first-innings total of 503 for four declared with an unbeaten 200 against Faisalabad, while Babar Azam's return to domestic cricket for Lahore Whites produced scores of 28 and 42 in a 241-run defeat to FATA. FATA won that encounter on the final day, with debutant Fawad Arshad scoring 115 and Mohammad Farooq 123, while Sialkot Blues' clash with Karachi Blues ended in a draw after Mohammad Huraira slammed twin centuries of 117 and 141 and Omair Bin Yousuf scored an unbeaten century on the final day to hold Sialkot for a draw.

What the series will test

The Rawalpindi opener is Pakistan's first Test assignment since the tri-series triumph, and the home side will be expected to set the tone on familiar pitches. The selectors will have watched the domestic round closely: Rizwan's double century underlines his red-ball form, while Babar's struggles — including a controversial first-innings dismissal and a second-innings edge behind the wicket — leave the captain short of runs and short of time before the series begins. Sri Lanka, meanwhile, arrive as opponents Pakistan will be expected to beat at home, which only raises the pressure on the batting order to deliver.

The countdown

With three days between the end of the domestic round and the first Test, there is little time for further match practice. The Rawalpindi Test will therefore be as much a test of temperament as of technique for Pakistan's senior batters — and for Babar in particular, a chance to turn a frustrating domestic week into the start of a strong home series.

Sources: Wisden, Geo Super, Mathrubhumi, November 6, 2026

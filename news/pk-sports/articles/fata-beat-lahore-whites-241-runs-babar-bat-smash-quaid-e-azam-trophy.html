FATA Thrash Lahore Whites by 241 Runs as Babar Smashes Bat After Another Failure

November 6, 2026

FATA beat Lahore Whites by 241 runs on the final day of the opening round of the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy Gold, with Pakistan Test captain Babar Azam managing only 28 and 42 before smashing his bat on the pitch in frustration, Wisden and Geo Super reported on November 6.

How the match was won

At the Diamond Cricket Ground in Islamabad, FATA established control early after Lahore Whites opted to field. Fawad Arshad's resilient 115 anchored FATA's first innings of 362, before Lahore were bowled out for 234 despite Ali Zaryab's 51 and Aamir Jamal's 74. Mohammad Farooq then smashed 123 to set Lahore a daunting target of 412, with the Whites folding for just 170 in their final turn at the crease. Shahid Aziz took four wickets for 32 and Afaq Afridi four for 46 in the second innings, while wicketkeeper-batter Zain Bin Farooq top-scored for Lahore with 44.

Babar's frustrating return

Babar's return to domestic cricket did not go as planned. In the first innings he scored 28 off 40 balls before a controversial caught-behind dismissal off Afaq Afridi. In the daunting fourth-innings chase of 412, he managed 42 off 77 deliveries, including six boundaries, before Shahid Aziz bowled a length delivery outside the off stump that moved away slightly; Babar attempted to drive away from his body and edged it to wicketkeeper Rehan Afridi. Following his second-innings dismissal in the 34th over, a visibly fuming Babar was seen smashing his bat against the pitch before returning to the dressing room, and his dismissal triggered a collapse as Lahore crumbled to the heavy defeat.

What it means with Sri Lanka looming

With the opening Test against Sri Lanka commencing on November 9 in Rawalpindi, Babar has run out of time to iron out technical flaws in domestic cricket, Mathrubhumi noted. The contrast with Mohammad Rizwan, who struck an unbeaten double century for Peshawar in the same round, will only sharpen the questions around the captain's form as the Test series approaches.

Sources: Wisden, Geo Super, Mathrubhumi, November 6, 2026

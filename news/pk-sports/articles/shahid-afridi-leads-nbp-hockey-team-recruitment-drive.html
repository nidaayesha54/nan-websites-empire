Shahid Afridi Leads Recruitment Drive for New NBP Hockey Team

November 10, 2026

Former Pakistan cricket captain Shahid Afridi has begun the process of selecting players for a new National Bank of Pakistan (NBP) hockey team, in a move aimed at supporting the revival of the national sport, The Express Tribune reported on November 10.

A new institutional side

Afridi, who serves as an adviser to NBP, visited Islamabad with his sports team, which has started shortlisting talented players for the proposed side, the Pakistan Hockey Federation (PHF) said in a press release. The formation of a permanent hockey team by one of the country's major national institutions is being seen as an encouraging development for the sport, which has struggled to regain its former standing.

Details still to come

The player selection process is now under way, although details of the squad size and the team's competitive schedule were not provided. The initiative comes as Pakistan hockey looks to rebuild, with the national team preparing for the Sultan Azlan Shah Cup and eyeing qualification for the Los Angeles Olympics 2028, having failed to qualify for the Games since London 2012.

Sources: The Express Tribune, November 10, 2026

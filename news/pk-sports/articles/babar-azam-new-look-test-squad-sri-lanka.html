Babar Azam to Lead New-Look Pakistan Test Squad Against Sri Lanka

October 26, 2026

Pakistan on 24 October 2026 named a 14-member squad for their two-Test home series against Sri Lanka, with Babar Azam appointed captain in Rawalpindi and Lahore. The selection is a sharp break with the recent past: Shan Masood, Mohammad Rizwan and Imam-ul-Haq were left out of the squad, Salman Ali Agha is back, and three uncapped players have earned maiden Test call-ups.

The omissions that dominate the debate

Former captain Masood, Rizwan and Imam have all been left out of the squad. Rizwan and Imam are still under investigation over their conduct on the England tour, which makes their absence from this series more notable. Masood's exclusion also draws attention: he was said to be 'fearful' of a possible drop, despite his 90 in the series finale against England. Ten players from Pakistan's England tour have been omitted from the initial squad in total, including Aamir Jamal, Khurram Shahzad, Mohammad Ali, Sajid Khan, Abdullah Shafique and Ubaid Shah. The PCB announcement did not specify individual reasons for the exclusions, beyond the reported investigations concerning Rizwan and Imam.

The new faces, and a returning one

Left-handed batsman Mohammad Taha and Mohammad Suleman, along with promising batsman Sameer Minhas, have received their maiden call-ups following impressive performances in domestic cricket. Taha, 26, is the leading run-scorer in the ongoing President's Trophy Grade-I first-class tournament, having amassed 730 runs in seven matches, including three centuries with 225 being his highest score. Suleman has scored 652 runs in the same competition, also hitting three centuries, while Sameer has made 405 runs in seven matches during his maiden first-class season after impressing with Pakistan's Under-19 side. Salman Ali Agha is back in the squad after Pakistan left him out during the August-September tour of England; he answered with a triple century and a century for Kingsmen Academy in the ongoing President's Trophy Grade-I 2026/27.

What the series looks like

The ICC World Test Championship series will be played in Rawalpindi and Lahore from November 9 to 21, with the first Test at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium from November 9 to 13 and the second at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore from November 17 to 21. Babar will be joined by Abdullah Fazal, Arafat Minhas, Azan Awais, Mohammad Abbas, wicketkeepers Mohammad Ghazi Ghori and Saad Baig, Razaullah and Saud Shakeel, who have retained their places, with Ali Usman also included. Two more fast bowlers will be added to the squad from the PCB Patron's XI after a three-day warm-up match against Sri Lanka from November 4 to 6 at the Islamabad Club. Earlier on Saturday, PCB chairman Mohsin Naqvi described the Test squad as full of young blood — and against Sri Lanka, that youth gets its examination.

Sources: Outlook India, October 24, 2026; The Express Tribune; Dawn.

England Meet Sri Lanka in Lahore as Tri-Series Builds to October 31 Final

October 29, 2026

England meet Sri Lanka in Lahore on Thursday in the final league match of the ODI tri-series, the last fixture before the tournament final at Gaddafi Stadium on Saturday, October 31. The seven-match tournament, featuring Pakistan, England and Sri Lanka, has been played in Rawalpindi and Lahore from October 18 to 31.

How the series has unfolded

Sri Lanka booked their place in the final with a commanding eight-wicket victory over Pakistan in Lahore on October 27. After winning the toss and opting to bowl, Sri Lanka's pacers dismantled Pakistan's batting line-up, with left-arm quick Dilshan Madushanka delivering a spell of 5 for 34. Pakistan were bowled out for 110 in 25.3 overs, with Babar Azam top-scoring with 29, before Sri Lanka chased the target down in 18.1 overs, finishing at 114 for 2, led by Kamindu Mendis with an unbeaten 62.

What Thursday's match means

Thursday's Lahore fixture is the last chance for the sides to settle the final line-up before Saturday's decider. The series is captained by Shaheen Shah Afridi for Pakistan, Harry Brook for England and Kusal Mendis for Sri Lanka, and it serves as preparation for the 2027 ODI World Cup, which South Africa, Zimbabwe and Namibia will host.

The road to the final

The tournament comprised six league matches followed by the final, with the opening fixtures in Rawalpindi before the action moved to Lahore. The final at Gaddafi Stadium on October 31 will bring the curtain down on a home season that continues with two Tests against Sri Lanka in November.

Supporters should check official listings for the exact start time of Thursday's day-night fixture.

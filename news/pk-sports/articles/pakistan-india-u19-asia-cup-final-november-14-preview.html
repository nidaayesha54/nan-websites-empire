Pakistan Face India in U19 Asia Cup Final on Saturday as Defending Champions Chase Back-to-Back Titles

November 13, 2026

Pakistan will face arch-rivals India in the final of the Under-19 Asia Cup at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground in Oman on Saturday, November 14, with the first ball at 10:00 am Pakistan time, Dawn reported on November 13.

Unbeaten Pakistan, revenge-minded India

Pakistan enter the final as favourites, having gone the entire tournament unbeaten and as defending champions. They beat India by three wickets in the group stage, powered by Hamza Zahoor's hundred, and thrashed Bangladesh by nine wickets in Thursday's semi-final, with Rizwanullah taking 5-30. India, who beat Afghanistan by eight wickets in the second semi-final with Lakshya Raichandani scoring 93, are hunting a record-extending ninth U19 Asia Cup title and revenge for last year's final, which Pakistan won by 191 runs after Sameer Minhas smashed 172 in a total of 347.

Trophy intrigue off the field

The final carries off-field intrigue: India's senior men's and women's teams previously refused to accept Asia Cup trophies from ACC president Mohsin Naqvi, and Naqvi remains ACC chief, so who presents the trophy in Oman on Saturday is being watched closely. The match will be shown live on the Sony Sports Network and SonyLIV in India and on the Starzplay app in the UAE.

Sources: Dawn, November 13, 2026; The National, November 13, 2026; Menafn, November 13, 2026

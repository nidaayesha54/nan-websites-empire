Abdul Samad to Lead Pakistan Shaheens at Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026 in Qatar

November 3, 2026

The Pakistan Cricket Board has announced the 15-member Pakistan Shaheens squad for the upcoming 50-over edition of the ACC Men's Asia Cup Rising Stars 2026, to be played in Qatar from November 19 to 29, The Express Tribune reported on November 3.

The squad and the group

Abdul Samad will lead defending champions Pakistan Shaheens, placed in Group B alongside India A, Nepal and the United Arab Emirates. The full squad is: Abdul Samad (captain), Ahmed Daniyal, Akif Javed, Danish Aziz, Farhan Yousuf, Haider Ali, Hasan Nawaz, Maaz Sadaqat, Mohammad Hasnain, Muhammad Imran Randhawa, Rohail Nazir (wicketkeeper), Saad Masood, Shahzaib Khan, Shamyl Hussain and Sufyan Moqim. Shaheens won the last 50-over edition in 2023 in Sri Lanka, beating India A by 128 runs.

The fixtures

Shaheens begin their campaign against UAE on November 20, face India A on November 22 and Nepal on November 24. The semi-finals are scheduled for November 26 and 27, with the final on Sunday, November 29. Reports differed slightly on whether the Nepal game is November 24 or 25, so readers should check the official fixture list for the confirmed date.

Sources: The Express Tribune, Geo Super, A-Sports, November 3, 2026

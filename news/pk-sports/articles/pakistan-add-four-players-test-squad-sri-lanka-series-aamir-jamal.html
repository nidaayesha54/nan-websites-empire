Pakistan Add Four Players to Test Squad for Sri Lanka Series as Three Earn Maiden Call-Ups

November 7, 2026

Pakistan have added pace-bowling all-rounder Aamir Jamal, fast bowlers Ali Shabbir and Saqib Khan, and batsman Muhammad Tayyab Arif to their squad for the upcoming two-match Test series against Sri Lanka, the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) said on Saturday, The Express Tribune reported on November 7.

Three maiden call-ups

Batter Muhammad Tayyab Arif, teenaged fast bowler Ali Shabbir and seamer Saqib Khan have earned their maiden national call-ups, while Jamal, who has represented Pakistan in nine Tests, three ODIs and six T20Is, returns on the back of a strong domestic run. To make room for the quartet, Ali Usman and Abdullah Fazal have been released from the squad. All three newcomers represented the Patron's XI in the three-day warm-up match against Sri Lanka at Islamabad Club from November 4 to 6.

The squad and the schedule

The revised 16-member squad is: Babar Azam (captain), Aamir Jamal, Ali Shabbir, Arafat Minhas, Azan Awais, Mohammad Abbas, Mohammad Ghazi Ghori (wicketkeeper), Mohammad Suleman, Mohammad Taha, Muhammad Tayyab Arif, Razaullah, Saad Baig (wicketkeeper), Salman Ali Agha, Sameer Minhas, Saqib Khan and Saud Shakeel. The opening Test is scheduled to begin at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on November 9, with the second Test to be played from November 17 to 21 at Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore. Both matches are part of the ICC World Test Championship 2025-27 cycle.

What it means

The late changes, confirmed just over 40 hours before the first Test, show the selectors rewarding domestic form at the last moment — and put three debutants in line for a home debut. With Babar Azam short of runs after his domestic scores of 28 and 42 in the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy, the fresh faces add energy to a squad that needs a strong start to its home series.

Sources: The Express Tribune, Reuters, ICC, November 7, 2026

India Beat Pakistan 2-1 to Win 10th SAFF Title as Pakistan Players Give Guard of Honour

November 18, 2026

India came from behind to beat Pakistan 2-1 and lift their 10th SAFF Championship title in Dhaka on Tuesday, completing a hat-trick of consecutive titles, The Times of India reported on November 18.

Pakistan's maiden final ends in heartbreak

Pakistan, playing their maiden SAFF Championship final, made a strong start and took the lead through Alamgir Ghazi in first-half stoppage time. India fought back after the break to win 2-1 and claim the trophy, ending Pakistan's hopes of lifting their first SAFF title. Dawn reported that Pakistan's hopes ended in heartbreak on Tuesday as the defending champions held on in Dhaka.

A guard of honour and handshakes

After the final, Pakistani players lined up to applaud the Indian champions, giving them a guard of honour, and players from both teams shook hands and exchanged high-fives during the medal ceremony. The gesture stood out amid the strained sporting relations between the two neighbours, and in sharp contrast to the refusal to shake hands that has become common in India-Pakistan cricket matches since the 2025 Asia Cup, a practice that continued through the Under-19 Asia Cup, the Women's World Cup and the Women's Asia Cup in 2026. Football, however, provided a different example: the two teams had exchanged greetings earlier in the tournament as well, with India captain Bijoy Varghese shaking hands with Pakistan skipper Yousuf Ijaz Butt before their group-stage match on November 5.

Sources: The Times of India, November 18, 2026; The Daily Jagran, November 18, 2026; Dawn, November 18, 2026

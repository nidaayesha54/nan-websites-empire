Pakistan and Sri Lanka Head to Decider in Rawalpindi as Second Test Starts November 17

November 14, 2026

Pakistan and Sri Lanka will play the second and final Test of their two-match series at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium from Tuesday, November 17, with the series level at 0-0 after the first Test ended in a draw on Friday.

A venue moved by smog

The second Test was originally scheduled for Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore, but the Pakistan Cricket Board moved it to Rawalpindi because of Lahore's hazardous winter smog. The city was ranked the world's most polluted on November 10, with its air quality index crossing 301 into the hazardous category, making five days of outdoor Test cricket medically unviable there. Both Tests of the series will now be played at the same ground.

What is at stake

The first Test was marred by bad light and lost overs, with Pakistan declaring on 434 for 9, Sri Lanka replying with 446, and Saud Shakeel's fighting 97 helping the hosts hold on for a draw at 284 for 8. With the series on the line and World Test Championship points at stake, both teams will look for a result in the decider, which runs from November 17 to 21 with play starting at 9:30am Pakistan time each day.

Sources: Najam Sports, November 2026; The Express Tribune, November 10, 2026

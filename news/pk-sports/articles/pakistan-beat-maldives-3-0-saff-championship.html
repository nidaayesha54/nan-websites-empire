Pakistan Beat Maldives 3-0 for First SAFF Championship Win in Eight Years

November 8, 2026

Pakistan bounced back from their opening-match defeat, cruising to a comfortable 3-0 victory over the Maldives in their second Group B fixture of the South Asian Football Federation (SAFF) Championship 2026 at Dhaka's National Stadium on Sunday. A dominant first-half display, capped by a double from forward Najeebullah Bettani, earned the Green Shirts their first win of the tournament.

An eight-year wait ends

Najeeb Ullah scored a brace as Pakistan thumped the Maldives 3-0 in their SAFF Championship group-stage match, Sportstar reported. England-born forward McKeal Abdullah scored the other goal. It was Pakistan's first win in the SAFF Championship in over eight years — their last victory was against Bhutan on September 8, 2018. The win marked a strong response from Pakistan following their narrow 3-2 defeat against arch-rivals India in their opening Group B fixture, a game in which Pakistan found the net twice in second-half stoppage time in a late surge.

Semi-final hopes hang on India

Pakistan have now completed their group-stage matches, and anything but a draw in the match between India and the Maldives will see Pakistan qualify for the knockouts, something they last did in 2018, Sportstar reported. Geo Super put it the same way: Pakistan hope India beat the Maldives in the final group match to qualify for the semi-finals. After eight years without a win in the tournament, the Green Shirts will now watch the final group game as supporters rather than players — hoping their rivals do them a favour.

Sources: Sportstar (The Hindu), November 8, 2026; Geo Super, November 8, 2026

India Edge Pakistan by One Wicket Off the Last Ball to Win U19 Asia Cup Final

November 14, 2026

India pulled off a stunning one-wicket win off the final ball over Pakistan to clinch the Under-19 Asia Cup at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground in Oman on Saturday, ending Pakistan's reign as defending champions.

Pakistan's big total, India's great escape

Pakistan, who won the toss and chose to bat, posted 337 for 9 in their 50 overs, built on Usman Khan's 107, Abdul Qadir's 80 and Farhan Yousaf's 60. Chasing a formidable 338, India were in all sorts of trouble at 252 for eight in the 42nd over. But late-order batters Manal Chauhan, with 47 off 32 balls, and Mohit Ulva, unbeaten on 49 off just 26 deliveries, held their nerves to drag India across the line at 339 for 9. With three runs needed off the final ball, Ulva came down the track and hammered a boundary through extra cover off leg-spinner Ahmed Hussain to seal the match.

Ninth title for India

The win gives India a record-extending ninth U19 Asia Cup title, their first since 2021, and revenge for the group-stage defeat to Pakistan earlier in the tournament, when Hamza Zahoor's hundred powered Pakistan to a three-wicket win. It also avenges last year's final, which Pakistan won by 191 runs. Lakshya Raichandani top-scored for India with 80, while Aaryavir Sehwag made 58 and was named Player of the Tournament.

Sources: The Tribune, November 14, 2026; The Week, November 14, 2026; NDTV Sports, November 14, 2026

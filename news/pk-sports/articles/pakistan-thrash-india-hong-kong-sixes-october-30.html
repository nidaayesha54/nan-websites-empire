Pakistan Thrash India by Four Wickets at Hong Kong Sixes, Chasing 89 in 3.5 Overs

October 30, 2026

Pakistan, the defending champions, registered their first victory of the Hong Kong Sixes 2026 by defeating arch-rivals India by four wickets at the Mission Road Ground in Mong Kok on October 30. Pakistan chased a target of 89 in just 3.5 overs to register the win.

India's innings stalls at 88

Pakistan captain Abbas Afridi won the toss and elected to bowl first. India were restricted to 88 for five in their six overs, the lowest score posted by any side on the opening day of the tournament. Shashank Singh was the lone bright spot with the bat, hammering 40 runs off 11 balls, including two fours and five sixes, after a top-order collapse.

Afridi's five sixes in the opening over

Chasing 89, Pakistan made an explosive start as captain Abbas Afridi smashed five consecutive sixes in the opening over bowled by his counterpart Krunal Pandya, helping his team collect 31 runs. Afridi combined with Khawaja Nafay to plunder 28 runs off T. Natarajan's bowling, reaching 73 for one at the halfway stage. Nafay struck 41 off 12 balls, while Afridi made 35 off just eight deliveries, before Irfan Khan took Pakistan home with 13 balls remaining. Pakistan finished on 92 for two, and Afridi was named Player of the Match.

What it means for Group C

The win was especially significant because Pakistan had lost their opening Group C match to the United Arab Emirates by one wicket earlier in the day, leaving them on the brink of elimination. The Hong Kong Sixes 2026 features 12 teams divided into four groups of three, with the group winners advancing to the Cup semi-finals and the final set for November 1. India, now at the bottom of Group C, must beat the UAE on October 31 by a big margin to stay alive.

For the defending champions, the victory keeps their title defence on track — and delivers one of the most one-sided chases in the tournament's recent history.

Babar Azam Says Revamped Pakistan Ready to Fight Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi Test

November 8, 2026

Captain Babar Azam hopes an overhauled Pakistan can make a fresh start in the first Test against Sri Lanka that begins in Rawalpindi on Monday after the disastrous series against England, AFP reported on November 8. The two-match series is Pakistan's first since its 3-0 whitewash in England, where seven players were sent home after the second Test.

A new-look side

Head coach Sarfaraz Ahmed and bowling coach Umar Gul were sacked in a September overhaul, with white-ball coach Mike Hesson brought in to the Test set-up, AFP reported. The Pakistan Cricket Board added pace bowling all-rounder Aamer Jamal and three uncapped players — batter Mohammad Tayyab Arif and fast bowlers Ali Shabbir and Saqib Khan — to the squad on Saturday, taking its strength to 16. The first Test is scheduled to begin at the Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on Monday, with the first ball scheduled to be bowled at 9.30 a.m. PKT. The second Test will be played at Lahore's Gaddafi Stadium from November 17 to 21.

Two teams rebuilding

Both matches are part of the ICC World Test Championship 2025-27 cycle, in which Pakistan currently occupy the bottom position (ninth) while Sri Lanka are seventh. Sri Lanka are also undergoing a Test reboot, with white-ball skipper Kusal Mendis taking over as captain from Dhananjaya de Silva last month. The Rawalpindi Test will be Mendis's first in charge, having led the island nation in six T20Is and 27 ODIs. Pakistan last won the teams' series 2-0 in 2023, and will aim to build on their white-ball form after the hosts' team won the three-match T20I series 2-1 and triumphed in the ODI tri-nation series as well.

Sources: AFP via Geo News / Gulf News, November 8, 2026; Dawn, November 8, 2026

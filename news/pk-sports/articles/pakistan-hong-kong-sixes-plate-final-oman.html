Pakistan Beat Hong Kong to Reach Hong Kong Sixes Plate Final Against Oman

October 31, 2026

Pakistan defeated the host country by five wickets in the second Plate semi-final of the Hong Kong Sixes 2026 at Mission Road Ground, Mong Kok, on Saturday, to set up a final against Oman, Geo Super reported.

Abbas Afridi's blitz

Batting first, Hong Kong posted 106 for 3 in their allotted six overs. In reply, Pakistan chased down the 107-run target in just 4.3 overs, losing five wickets in the process. Captain Abbas Afridi once again led from the front with a sensational 55 off just 13 deliveries, smashing three fours and seven sixes. Following Afridi's retirement, Abdul Samad contributed a quickfire 17 off four deliveries, striking one four and two sixes to complete the chase.

From group disappointment to a Plate final

Six-time champions and defending title-holders Pakistan had earlier been eliminated from contention for the main Cup trophy after finishing second in Group C, as only group winners qualify for the main Cup semi-finals. Pakistan suffered a last-ball defeat against the UAE in their opening fixture on Friday, then bounced back emphatically against India, restricting their arch-rivals to 88 for 5 before completing the chase in just 3.5 overs. They will now face Oman in Sunday's Plate final.

Sources: Geo Super, October 31, 2026

Pakistan Beat Hong Kong in Padel Qualifier but Miss Out on World Cup

October 29, 2026

Pakistan defeated Hong Kong 2-1 in their final Group C tie in Kuwait City, but failed to qualify for the FIP World Cup 2026 after losing their opening two ties against Saudi Arabia and Kuwait in the Asia-Africa qualifiers, according to reports published on October 29, 2026.

A winning finish

Pakistan secured victories in the first two matches against Hong Kong at Oro Padel to clinch the tie, finishing their campaign with a win after a difficult start. Huzaifa Abdul Rehman and Huzaima Abdul Rehman gave Pakistan a strong start, defeating Hong Kong's J. McKee and T. Y. A. Yao 6-1, 6-1 in the opening match. Abdullah Adnan and Muhammad Salar extended Pakistan's lead by winning the second match. Hong Kong avoided a clean sweep as A. Khan and S. Z. Lo Escoto defeated Danish Ahmed and Muhammad Azhar 6-1, 6-1 in the third match.

Where qualification slipped away

Pakistan had earlier lost 1-2 to Saudi Arabia in their opening tie on October 26, despite a victory by Huzaifa Abdul Rehman and Huzaima, and then suffered a 0-3 defeat against Kuwait on October 27, with Kuwait winning all three matches. Those two results left Pakistan unable to advance, even with the closing win over Hong Kong.

How the qualifiers work

The Asia-Africa qualifiers, held from October 26 to 30, feature 14 men's national teams divided into four groups. The group winners advance to the knockout stage to determine the qualifying teams for the FIP World Cup 2026, scheduled to take place in Doha, Qatar, from November 2 to 7.

For Pakistan's padel squad, the campaign ends with a consolation victory and a clear lesson: in a short group format, the opening ties decide everything.

After the Tri-Series Triumph, Pakistan Turn to Tests Against Sri Lanka on November 9

November 2, 2026

Pakistan will now play a two-match Test series against Sri Lanka starting on November 9, following their ODI tri-series triumph in Lahore, ETV Bharat reported on November 2.

What the tri-series showed

The Express Tribune's editorial on November 2 called the tri-series win — also featuring England — a major turning point, and Pakistan's first tri-series title since 2008. New batters Hassan Nawaz, Saad Baig and Maaz Sadaqat played without fear, all-rounder Arafat Minhas brought energy, and the fast-bowling trio of Shaheen Shah Afridi, Haris Rauf and Naseem Shah worked together to dismantle batting lineups. The final belonged to veteran Fakhar Zaman, whose 127 off 99 balls, with 11 fours and six sixes, made a chase of 326 look comfortable.

What the Tests will test

The red-ball series will ask different questions of the same core: patience, discipline and depth over five days rather than one-day explosiveness. Sri Lanka, beaten in the Lahore final after posting 325 for 8, will look to Kamindu Mendis — who led their final batting effort — to anchor their reply. Full squads, venues and session timings for the Tests had not been detailed in the reports checked at writing time; readers should check the official series announcement for confirmed fixtures.

Sources: ETV Bharat, The Express Tribune, November 2, 2026

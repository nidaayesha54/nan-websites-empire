Babar Azam Equals Record for Most 50-Plus Scores by a Pakistan Captain

November 9, 2026

Babar Azam equalled the record for the most 50-plus scores by a Pakistan captain on Monday, reaching the milestone with his half-century in the first innings of the first Test against Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi, News18 reported.

The record

The half-century was Babar's 144th score of fifty or more in international cricket across all three formats, and his 70th as Pakistan captain across formats, matching Misbah-ul-Haq's milestone. Babar, who is leading Pakistan in his 153rd international match this week, batted at No. 4 and scored 50 runs off 58 balls, smashing seven fours, before Dilshan Madushanka picked up his wicket in the post-lunch session. It was his 35th Test half-century.

The hundred still eludes him

Despite looking in good touch, Babar again failed to convert a fifty into a hundred. He has now gone 41 innings without a Test century, his last coming against New Zealand in Karachi in December 2022, Khaleej Times reported. Pakistan, ninth and last in the 2025-27 World Test Championship standings, hold a 23-17 advantage over Sri Lanka in 59 previous Test meetings, with 19 matches ending in draws.

Sources: News18, November 9, 2026; Khaleej Times, November 9, 2026

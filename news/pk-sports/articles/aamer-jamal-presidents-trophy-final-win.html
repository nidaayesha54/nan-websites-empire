Aamer Jamal's 7-32 and Fifty Lead Markhor to President's Trophy Final Win

October 30, 2026

Aamer Jamal delivered an all-round performance in the President's Trophy final to help Markhor IT Solutions lift the trophy on Friday, October 30, according to Wisden. Having been bowled out for just 112 batting first, Markhor needed an inspiring performance to get back into the game — and Jamal provided it.

Seven wickets to turn the final

Jamal snared 7 for 32 in the first innings to bowl out State Bank of Pakistan for 172, keeping the deficit to just 60. The seam-bowling all-rounder delivered 14 overs in the innings, including four maidens, in a spell that dragged his side back into the contest.

A fifty from number eight

He then starred with the bat as well, scoring 55 from number eight after Atizaz Habib Khan was retired hurt. He was the second top run-getter in the innings behind Abuzar Tariq's 85, powering the side to 296 and setting a defendable target of 237. The bowling unit did the rest to bundle out SBP for 185, with Khalid Usman taking four for 38, while Jamal took a solitary wicket in the second innings after bowling 14 overs for 39 runs.

A response to his Test axing

The 30-year-old all-rounder played only three games in the tournament, scoring 118 runs and picking up 10 wickets at an average of 18.20. Jamal was notably one of the seven players sent home from England in the middle of the tour after Pakistan lost the second Test at Lord's by 194 runs, and he could not find a spot in Pakistan's squad for the upcoming two-Test series against Sri Lanka. He has played nine Tests to date, managing 355 runs at an average of 23.66 with two fifties and 23 wickets at 35.21 apiece with two five-wicket hauls.

A match-winning final like this one is exactly the kind of statement a player on the outer makes — and selectors will have noticed.

Sources: Wisden, October 30, 2026.

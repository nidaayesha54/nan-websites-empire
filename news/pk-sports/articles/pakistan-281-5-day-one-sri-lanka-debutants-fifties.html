Debutants Tayyab and Taha Hit Fifties as Pakistan Dominate Day One Against Sri Lanka

November 9, 2026

Pakistan reached 281-5 at stumps on the opening day of the first Test against Sri Lanka at Rawalpindi Cricket Stadium on Monday, after bad light brought an early end to play, Geo News and Khaleej Times reported.

A breezy opening stand

Sri Lanka won the toss and chose to field on what appeared to be a green pitch, a decision that seemed to backfire. Debutant Tayyab Arif controlled the morning with 57 off 51 balls in a brisk 86-run opening stand with Azan Awais, who made 30. Sri Lanka hit back with the wickets of both openers in the space of five balls, but Pakistan still went to lunch at 137 for 2, with captain Babar Azam unbeaten on 31.

Four fifties, none a hundred

Babar reached his fifty after lunch — 50 off 58 balls with seven fours — before Dilshan Madushanka dismissed him, leaving Pakistan at 159 for 3. Fellow debutant Mohammad Taha dug in for a patient 51, and Saud Shakeel finished unbeaten on 57 off 112 balls, with Ghazi Ghori not out on 7. Pakistan went to tea at 214 for 3. Sri Lanka's decision to go with a pace quartet and without a frontline spinner could prove costly, with the pitch offering little to the fast bowlers. The second and final Test is in Lahore from November 17.

Sources: Geo News, November 9, 2026; Khaleej Times, November 9, 2026

Pakistan Miss Azlan Shah Cup Final After Dramatic 5-5 Draw With Malaysia

November 27, 2026

Pakistan failed to qualify for the final of the Sultan Azlan Shah Cup after surrendering a late lead to draw 5-5 with hosts Malaysia in a thrilling final group-stage match in Ipoh on Thursday, The Express Tribune reported on November 27.

A late lead lost twice

The result left both teams level on 10 points, but Malaysia booked their place in the final on superior goal difference, plus nine against Pakistan's plus six. Pakistan appeared to have secured a crucial victory when Sufyan Khan converted a penalty corner with three minutes remaining to put the Green Shirts 5-4 ahead. But Malaysia had the final say as Faizal Saari completed his hat-trick by converting a late penalty stroke, earning the hosts a dramatic draw. Saari had earlier struck twice in the 49th minute to wipe out Pakistan's 4-2 lead, which had come through a brace from Nadeem Ahmad and goals from Muhammad Ammad Butt and Hannan Shahid.

Third-place playoff next

Pakistan will now face South Korea in the third-and-fourth-place playoff on Saturday, while Malaysia take on Australia in the title clash on the same day. The draw was another frustrating outcome for Pakistan, whose campaign featured attacking promise but recurring defensive lapses at crucial moments, and the failure to protect a lead three times in one match ultimately proved costly in their pursuit of a place in the final.

Sources: The Express Tribune, November 27, 2026; Dunya News, November 27, 2026; Geo Super, November 27, 2026

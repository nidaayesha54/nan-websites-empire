Sri Lanka Crush Pakistan by Eight Wickets to Reach Tri-Series Final

October 28, 2026

Sri Lanka stormed into the final of the ODI tri-series with a commanding eight-wicket victory over Pakistan at the Gaddafi Stadium in Lahore on Tuesday, knocking England out of contention. Left-arm pacer Dilshan Madushanka led the bowling attack with career-best figures of five for 34 as Pakistan were bundled out for just 110 in 25.3 overs after electing to bat.

A one-sided afternoon

Babar Azam top-scored for Pakistan with 29, but the hosts struggled to build partnerships and lost wickets at regular intervals; Sahan Arachchige provided valuable support with two wickets for 24 runs. Sri Lanka chased down the modest target in just 18.1 overs, finishing at 114 for two, with Kamindu Mendis anchoring the innings with an unbeaten 62 off 60 balls and Pavan Rathnayake remaining not out on 23.

The victory secured Sri Lanka's place in the October 31 final against Pakistan, who had already qualified after winning their first three matches, and ended England's hopes of reaching the title clash. Sri Lanka will face England in their final league match on Thursday before meeting Pakistan again in the final, where the hosts will need a very different batting performance.

Sources: News Today, Outlook India, October 28, 2026

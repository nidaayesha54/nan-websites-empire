Pakistan Shaheens Beat Bangladesh A by Five Wickets to Reach Rising Stars Final

November 28, 2026

Defending champions Pakistan Shaheens booked their place in the Asia Cup Rising Stars final with a five-wicket victory over Bangladesh A in Doha on Friday, Cricket World reported on November 28.

A century in vain for Bangladesh

After winning the toss and opting to bat, Bangladesh A managed 232 for eight in their allotted 50 overs on the back of an unbeaten century from Shahadat Hossain, who scored 103 off 118 balls, including seven fours and three sixes, and shared a 95-run stand with Mahidul Islam Ankon, who made 42. Sufyan Moqim spearheaded Pakistan's bowling with three for 29, while Hasan Nawaz and Ali Raza took two wickets each to keep Bangladesh A to a chaseable total.

The Shaheens' chase and the final ahead

Chasing 233, the Shaheens lost both openers cheaply at 21 for two, but Hasan Nawaz and Shahzaib Khan steadied the innings with a 110-run stand for the third wicket. Nawaz made 62 off 91 balls and Shahzaib 50 off 85, before captain Abdul Samad's quick 44 off 33 balls and Farhan Yousaf's unbeaten 46 off 39 balls carried Pakistan home at 233 for five in 45.2 overs, with 28 balls to spare. Nawaz, who also took two wickets for 30 runs, was named player of the match. Pakistan will now face Sri Lanka A in the final on Sunday, November 29, at 3pm Pakistan time at the West End Park International Cricket Stadium in Doha.

Sources: Cricket World, November 28, 2026; MSports, November 27, 2026; Good News Pakistan, November 28, 2026

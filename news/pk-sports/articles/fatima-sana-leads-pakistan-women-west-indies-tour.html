Fatima Sana Returns to Lead Pakistan Women on West Indies Tour

November 25, 2026

All-rounder Fatima Sana has returned to lead the Pakistan women's team after the Pakistan Cricket Board (PCB) announced a 15-member squad on Wednesday for next month's tour of the West Indies, Dawn reported on November 25.

Six matches in Dominica

The tour will feature three T20 Internationals and three One-day Internationals (ODIs), with the latter forming part of the ICC Women's Championship 2025-29. All six matches will be played at Windsor Park in Roseau, Dominica, from December 8 to 20. The T20Is are scheduled for December 8, 10 and 12, while the ODIs will take place on December 15, 17 and 20. The ODI series carries particular significance for Pakistan's hopes of direct qualification for the next Women's World Cup in 2029, with the side having already lost two of the three series it has played in this championship cycle, according to ESPNcricinfo.

Back from the Big Bash

The 25-year-old Fatima was not part of the 14-member squad for the ICC Women's Emerging Nations Trophy due to her commitments in the Women's Big Bash League (WBBL) in Australia, where she featured in her maiden season with the Perth Scorchers. Leg-spinner Tuba Hassan has been recalled to the squad, while Muneeba Ali will serve as vice-captain and wicketkeeper. The squad is: Fatima Sana (captain), Ayesha Zafar, Gull Feroza, Momina Riasat, Muneeba Ali (wicketkeeper), Najiha Alvi (wicketkeeper), Nashra Sundhu, Sadaf Shamas, Shawaal Zulfiqar, Sidra Amin, Syeda Aroob Shah, Tasmia Rubab, Tuba Hassan, Umm-e-Hani and Waheeda Akhtar. Head coach Shane Deitz takes charge for the first time on the tour.

Sources: Dawn, November 25, 2026; The Express Tribune, November 25, 2026

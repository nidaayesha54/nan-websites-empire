Pakistan Declare on 434 as Saud Hits 93 and Debutant Saqib Strikes Twice Against Sri Lanka

November 10, 2026

Pakistan piled on 434 runs and then removed Sri Lanka's top three to leave the visitors firmly on the back foot at stumps on day two of the first Test in Rawalpindi, still 320 runs behind, ESPNcricinfo reported on November 10.

Saud anchors the declaration

Pakistan resumed on 281 for 5 and added 153 runs before captain Babar Azam declared at 434 for 9 in 106.3 overs. Saud Shakeel top-scored with 93 off 199 balls, with nine fours, falling seven runs short of his fifth Test century before Asitha Fernando had him caught behind. Ghazi Ghori made 48 in a 96-run sixth-wicket stand with Shakeel, and debutant Arafat Minhas registered his maiden Test half-century with 52. Asitha Fernando took 3 for 107 and Dilshan Madushanka 3 for 108. Five Pakistan batters, including three debutants, scored half-centuries in the innings.

Debutant Saqib strikes twice

Sri Lanka's reply began badly, with opener Pasindu Sooriyabandara dismissed for a seven-ball duck by Mohammad Abbas. Debutant Saqib Khan then claimed his maiden international wicket, dismissing Dinesh Chandimal for one, and later removed a well-set Lahiru Udara for 42 to leave Sri Lanka on 65 for 3. Kamindu Mendis (40 not out) and Dhananjaya de Silva (22 not out) steadied the innings with an unbeaten 50-run stand, taking Sri Lanka to 114 for 3 in 27.1 overs. Bad light brought play to an early close for the second day running, with only 66.2 overs possible on the day. Sri Lanka need 121 more runs to reach 235 and avoid the follow-on when play resumes on day three.

Sources: ESPNcricinfo, November 10, 2026; Geo News, November 10, 2026

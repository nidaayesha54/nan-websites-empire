Pakistan Crush Bangladesh by Nine Wickets to Reach U19 Asia Cup Final

November 12, 2026

Defending champions Pakistan beat Bangladesh by nine wickets in the semi-final of the U19 Asia Cup at the Al Amerat Cricket Ground in Oman on November 12.

A clinical performance

Bangladesh were bowled out for 138 in 31.4 overs, with Abdullah Al Muhi top-scoring on 50 and Rizwanullah taking 5-30. Pakistan chased down the target at 141-1 in 21.5 overs, powered by Hayam Khan's 69 and Usman Khan's unbeaten 64.

India await in the final

It was Pakistan's fourth consecutive win in the tournament, having beaten the UAE, India and Nepal in the group stage. Pakistan will face India in the final, after India beat Afghanistan by eight wickets in the second semi-final, with Lakshya Raichandani scoring 93.

Sources: Geo Super, November 12, 2026; ARY News, November 12, 2026; Grassroots Cricket, November 12, 2026

Fakhar Zaman's 127 Powers Pakistan to Tri-Series Title in Record-Equalling 326 Chase

November 1, 2026

Pakistan beat Sri Lanka by three wickets in the final of the ODI tri-series at Gaddafi Stadium, Lahore, on Saturday, October 31, chasing down a challenging 326-run target with nine balls to spare to lift the title, The Express Tribune and Oman Observer reported on November 1.

Fakhar leads the recovery

Sri Lanka, sent in to bat after Pakistan won the toss, posted 325 for 8, built on Kamindu Mendis's maiden ODI century of 131 off 128 balls and Pavan Rathnayake's 77, the pair sharing a 146-run third-wicket partnership. Pakistan's chase began disastrously, with Maaz Sadaqat out for 5 and Babar Azam for 0 with the total at 14. Fakhar Zaman then struck a commanding 127 off 99 balls, with 11 fours and six sixes, his 12th ODI century, completing 4,000 ODI runs when he reached 108, and sharing a 149-run third-wicket partnership with Saad Baig, who scored 74 off 75 balls for his maiden ODI half-century.

A finish sealed with a six

With Pakistan five wickets down for 230, Arafat Minhas smashed a quickfire 61 off 31 balls, with four sixes and three fours, and put on 94 for the sixth wicket with Hasan Nawaz, who remained unbeaten on 37 off 45 balls. Minhas fell with Pakistan on 324 and Faheem Ashraf was dismissed for a first-ball duck, before captain Shaheen Shah Afridi, unbeaten on 6, hit the winning six to take Pakistan to 331 for 7 in 48.3 overs. The chase equalled the record for the highest successful run chase in an ODI final, matching India's 326 against England at Lord's in 2002, and gave Pakistan its first tri-series title at home after failing in the finals of 1994, 2004 and 2025, according to the Oman Observer.

Sources: Express Tribune, Oman Observer, NDTV, November 1, 2026

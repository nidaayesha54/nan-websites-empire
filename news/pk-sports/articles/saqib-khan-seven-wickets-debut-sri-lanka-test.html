Debutant Saqib Khan Takes Seven Wickets as Pakistan Fight Back Against Sri Lanka in Rawalpindi Test

November 12, 2026

Debutant fast bowler Saqib Khan took 7-130 from 34 overs on day four in Rawalpindi on November 12, sparking Sri Lanka's collapse from an overnight 404-4 to 446 all out, Dawn reported.

A debut for the record books

Saqib is only the second Pakistani pacer after Mohammad Zahid to take seven wickets on Test debut. Pakistan had declared on 434-9, conceding a 12-run first-innings lead, before fighting back with the ball.

Rain looms over the finish

Pakistan reached 57-1 in their second innings — a 45-run lead, with Azan Awais 22 not out and Mohammad Taha 24 not out — before bad light and then rain ended play. With more rain forecast for November 13, the match is heading for a draw, and the second Test is also in Rawalpindi from November 17.

Sources: Dawn, November 12, 2026; The Papare, November 12, 2026

Pakistan Crowned Champions of 23rd Asian Team Squash Championship After Beating South Korea 2-0

November 7, 2026

Pakistan's men's squash team won the gold medal at the 23rd Asian Team Squash Championship after defeating South Korea in the final at the Mushaf Squash Complex, Islamabad, on Saturday, 24NewsHD reported on November 7.

How the final was won

Pakistan secured a decisive 2-0 lead in the final, with Noor Zaman and Muhammad Asim Khan winning their respective matches to seal the title. The victory marks Pakistan's 16th gold medal in the tournament's history and their first Asian Team Squash title since 2016. In the semi-final on Friday, Pakistan had blanked Hong Kong China 2-0: Noor Zaman beat Lau Tsz Kwan 5-11, 11-5, 11-5, 11-8, and Mohammad Asim Khan beat Wong Chi Him 11-4, 6-11, 11-7, 11-3. South Korea reached the final by beating defending champions Malaysia 2-1 in the other semi-final.

A home triumph

The five-day event, running from November 3 to 7, featured 44 players from six countries — Pakistan, Hong Kong China, Malaysia, South Korea, Iran and Uzbekistan — at the Mushaf Squash Complex under the Pakistan Sports Board. Pakistan's men had made a strong start, beating Uzbekistan and South Korea 3-0 in their pool ties before the semi-final win over Hong Kong China. Pakistan's women, meanwhile, faced a 2-1 defeat at the hands of Iran in their group match.

What it means

For a sport in which Pakistan once dominated the world, a first Asian team title in a decade is a meaningful marker. The final was played on home courts in front of a home crowd, and the unbeaten run through the tournament — pool ties, semi-final and final — suggests a team finding its footing again at the continental level.

Sources: 24NewsHD, Dawn, November 7, 2026

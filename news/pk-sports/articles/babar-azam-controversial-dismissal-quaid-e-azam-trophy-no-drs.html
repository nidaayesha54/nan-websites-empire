Babar Azam's Controversial Dismissal Sparks DRS Row in Quaid-e-Azam Trophy

November 4, 2026

Pakistan star Babar Azam was visibly disappointed after a contentious umpiring decision ended his innings for Lahore Whites against FATA in the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy on Wednesday, with the absence of the Decision Review System in domestic cricket leaving him no route to challenge it, Geo Super and A-Sports reported.

What happened

Babar, batting on 28 from 40 deliveries including six boundaries, appeared to let a delivery from FATA pacer Afaq Afridi pass his bat before wicketkeeper Rehan Afridi completed the catch behind the stumps on the final delivery of the 28th over at the Diamond Cricket Ground, Islamabad. Afaq appealed, and the on-field umpire raised his finger. Replays appeared to show the delivery making contact with Babar's thigh pad rather than his bat, reports said, and Babar pointed towards his pad to indicate the same before walking off.

The wider debate

The dismissal reignited calls for the PCB to introduce DRS in domestic competitions so players can challenge contentious calls, with fans and commentators criticising both the decision and the broadcast camera quality. Fawad Arshad led FATA's recovery with 115 off 190 deliveries as FATA were bowled out for 362, then extended their lead to 150 by stumps on day two at 22-1 in their second innings. Babar, returning to domestic first-class cricket after a disappointing ODI tri-series in which he scored 118 runs in five matches, will lead Pakistan in the Test series against Sri Lanka from November 9.

Sources: Geo Super, A-Sports, ABP Live, November 4, 2026

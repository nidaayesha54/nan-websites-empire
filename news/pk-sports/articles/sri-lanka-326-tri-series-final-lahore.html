Sri Lanka Set Pakistan 326 to Win the ODI Tri-Series Final in Lahore

October 31, 2026

Sri Lanka set Pakistan a 326-run target in the final of the ODI tri-series at Lahore's Gaddafi Stadium on Saturday, October 31, after Pakistan won the toss and elected to field first, Geo News and Aaj TV reported.

Kamindu's maiden ODI century

Openers Avishka Fernando and Kamindu Mendis put on a 50-run partnership before Fernando departed for 38 off 49 deliveries, ending an 87-run opening stand. Kamindu continued to lead the charge and reached his maiden ODI century, eventually departing for 131 off 128 deliveries, featuring 12 fours and two sixes, after captain Shaheen Shah Afridi trapped him lbw, ending a 146-run third-wicket partnership with Pavan Rathnayake.

A chase under way in Lahore

Asitha Fernando and Maheesh Theekshana added valuable runs in the final two overs to take Sri Lanka to 325 for 8. For Pakistan, Naseem Shah and Haris Rauf picked up two wickets each. At the time of writing, Pakistan's chase was under way, with Fakhar Zaman bringing up his 12th ODI century — joining Saeed Anwar as only the second Pakistani batter to score two hundreds in an ODI final — and Pakistan at 193 for 3 after 30 overs, according to live coverage. The final result was not yet decided when this article was published; readers should check the live scoreboard for the outcome.

Sources: Geo News, Aaj TV, Outlook India live coverage, October 31, 2026

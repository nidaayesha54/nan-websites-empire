Rizwan's Unbeaten Double Century Puts Peshawar in Command Against Faisalabad

November 4, 2026

Peshawar captain Mohammad Rizwan struck an unbeaten double century to put his side in a commanding position against Faisalabad Region on the second day of their opening-round fixture in the Quaid-e-Azam Trophy 2026-27 at the Abbottabad Cricket Stadium on Wednesday, Geo Super and ARY News reported.

The innings

Resuming on 111 not out from 142 balls, Rizwan reached 200 off 252 deliveries, hitting 35 fours and a six, in his second first-class double hundred and his first in 12 years, Wisden reported. He shared a 246-run fourth-wicket stand with Najab Khan, who scored 133, after the pair had put on 179 on day one, and then an unbroken 130-run partnership with Muhammad Haris as Peshawar declared at 503-4 after 115.2 overs. Rizwan had come in at 127-3 on day one after being left out of Pakistan's squad for the two-match Test series against Sri Lanka starting November 9.

The state of play

In reply, Faisalabad reached 118-2 in 35.2 overs at stumps, trailing Peshawar by 385 runs. Elsewhere on day two, defending champions Karachi Blues held a slender 39-run first-innings lead over Sialkot after being bowled out for 280 in reply to 241, FATA bowled out Lahore Whites' opponents for 362 with Fawad Arshad scoring 115, and Multan took command against Islamabad on half-centuries from Imran Rafiq and Sharoon Siraj, Geo Super reported.

Sources: Geo Super, ARY News, Wisden, Dawn, November 4, 2026

De Silva and Kusal Mendis Hit Centuries as Sri Lanka Reach 404-4 on Day Three

November 11, 2026

Dhananjaya de Silva and Kusal Mendis showed great staying power as both posted centuries to lift Sri Lanka to 404 for 4 on day three of the first Test against Pakistan in Rawalpindi on Wednesday, Geo News reported on November 11.

A 249-run stand turns the Test

De Silva was unbeaten on a polished 167 off 241 balls, with 20 fours, bringing up his 14th Test hundred, while Mendis was 117 not out off 188 balls, with 10 fours and four sixes, in an unbroken fifth-wicket stand of 249 that brought Sri Lanka within 30 runs of Pakistan’s total of 434 for 9 declared. Mendis became the first Sri Lankan to score a century in his maiden Test as captain, after taking the reins from De Silva before this series. The stand is Sri Lanka’s highest for the fifth wicket against Pakistan, passing the 207 by Thilan Samaraweera and Tillakaratne Dilshan in Lahore in 2009.

Pakistan toil as light ends play

Sri Lanka resumed on 114 for 3 and lost just one wicket all day, adding 288 runs, with Kamindu Mendis making 58 before edging Mohammad Abbas to wicketkeeper Ghazi Ghori. Abbas was the pick of Pakistan’s bowlers with 2 for 64, while debutant Saqib Khan took 2 for 105. Bad light brought an early end to the day’s play, with 23 overs still to be bowled. The second Test is also in Rawalpindi, starting November 17, having been shifted from Lahore due to poor weather.

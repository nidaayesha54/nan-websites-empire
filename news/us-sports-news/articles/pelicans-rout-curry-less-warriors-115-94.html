Pelicans Rout Curry-Less Warriors 115-94 as Golden State Struggles Without Its Star

November 17, 2026

The New Orleans Pelicans beat the Golden State Warriors 115-94 on Monday night, with the Stephen Curry-less Warriors struggling badly in one of their most disappointing performances of the season, Stadium News reported on November 17.

A lifeless Warriors showing

Warriors struggle without Stephen Curry in a 115-94 loss to the Pelicans, as NBA analysts and fans reacted to the disappointing performance, with the team described as lifeless in the defeat. The loss added to a difficult stretch for Golden State, which has also been weighing how to replace the injury-riddled Kristaps Porzingis in its frontcourt.

Elsewhere on Monday night

Giannis Antetokounmpo, now with the Miami Heat, posterised Jabari Smith Jr. during the Heat's clash with the Houston Rockets on Monday night, and he now faces his former team, the Milwaukee Bucks, for the first time on Wednesday at Kaseya Center in Miami. The Heat star said he does not want the matchup to become a bigger spectacle than it needs to be. Also on Monday, Cleveland beat Chicago behind Donovan Mitchell's 39 points, and Orlando beat Boston.

Sources: Stadium News, November 17, 2026

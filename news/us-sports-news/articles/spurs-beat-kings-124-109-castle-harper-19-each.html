Spurs Beat Kings 124-109 as Castle and Harper Score 19 Each

November 7, 2026

Stephon Castle had 19 points and seven assists as the San Antonio Spurs beat the Sacramento Kings 124-109 on Friday night in San Antonio, FanDuel Research and ESPN reported on November 7.

A balanced Spurs attack

Castle's 19 points led a balanced San Antonio effort: rookie Dylan Harper also scored 19, Devin Vassell added 19 of his own, and the Spurs improved to 7-2 on the season. The win keeps San Antonio among the early pacesetters in the Western Conference, with a young core that is producing across the board rather than leaning on a single star. The Spurs shot their way past a Kings side that has now lost eight of its first nine games.

Kings' struggles deepen

Domantas Sabonis recorded 17 points, 12 rebounds and five assists for Sacramento, and Dylan Cardwell added nine points and two blocks, but the Kings fell to 1-8 and remain near the bottom of the Western Conference. Sacramento's next test comes against the Minnesota Timberwolves, and with the season still young, the Kings need a turnaround soon before the gap to the play-in places grows too wide.

Sources: FanDuel Research, ESPN, November 7, 2026

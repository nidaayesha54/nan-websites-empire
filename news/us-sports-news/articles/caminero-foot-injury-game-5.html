Caminero Says He'll Play Game 5 Despite Foot Injury: 'If My Foot Is Broken, I'll Play Tomorrow'

October 28, 2026

All-Star slugger Junior Caminero injured his left foot during the Tampa Bay Rays' late rally in Game 4 of the World Series on Tuesday night, but he says he will play Wednesday in Game 5. Caminero began limping after reaching on Joey Ortiz's throwing error leading off the 12th inning, ran first-to-third on Liam Hicks' single, and was then replaced by pinch runner Cedric Mullins.

"If my foot is broken," Caminero said, "I'll play tomorrow." He did not reveal the extent of the injury, saying it did not matter with Tampa Bay on the brink of elimination.

Why the Rays need him

The 23-year-old hit 42 home runs with 101 RBIs while playing all 162 games in the regular season, but he has just one homer and five RBIs in 13 postseason games, and the Rays are hitting .195 with a single home run in the World Series. Manager Kevin Cash left no doubt about his plans: "He'll play tomorrow on one leg. I'll make sure of it." Game 5 is Wednesday at 8 p.m. ET at Tropicana Field, with Milwaukee's Jacob Misiorowski starting for the Brewers, who lead the series 3-1.

Sources: NBC Sports/AP, October 28, 2026

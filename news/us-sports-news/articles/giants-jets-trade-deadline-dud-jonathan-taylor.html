Giants Didn't Buy, Jets Didn't Sell: What Now After a Trade Deadline Dud in New York?

November 11, 2026

After days of anticipation, the NFL trade deadline came and went with a whimper for the New York teams, the New York Post reported on November 11, with the Giants declining to buy and the Jets declining to sell.

Giants pass on Taylor

The Giants were heavily linked with running back Jonathan Taylor, given the Colts' sell-off and Big Blue's need for a difference-maker at running back. But the Giants did not meet the Colts' substantial asking price for Taylor, and they also passed on a defensive tackle, with the price for a difference-maker there expensive as well — the Jaguars gave up a first-round and a second-round pick to acquire Jordan Davis from the Eagles. The Giants enter the back nine of the season two games ahead of the Cowboys in the NFC East, with three likely playoff teams — the Jaguars, 49ers and Seahawks — among their next five opponents.

Jets stand pat — and a trade request surfaces

The Jets, meanwhile, held on to their veterans despite being one of the league's most obvious sellers given their place in the standings. The Post's Brian Costello confirmed that edge rusher Will McDonald IV asked for a trade amid frustration over his minimal role on defence — a request the Jets did not grant before the deadline. Elsewhere, the Packers were revealed to have offered a fourth-round pick for Colts defensive tackle Grover Stewart before pulling the deal, and the Broncos cut two cornerbacks, including Super Bowl champion Ugo Amadi, after trading for Daiyan Henley.

Sources: New York Post, November 11, 2026

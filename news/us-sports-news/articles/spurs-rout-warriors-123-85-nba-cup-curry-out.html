Spurs Rout Warriors 123-85 to Stay Unbeaten in NBA Cup as Curry Sits Out With Oblique Tear

November 14, 2026

The San Antonio Spurs continued their commanding run in the NBA Cup, remaining unbeaten after a dominant 123-85 victory over the Golden State Warriors on Friday, USA Today reported on November 14.

Curry sidelined for at least two weeks

The Warriors were without their star, Stephen Curry, who is sidelined for at least two weeks due to a right oblique tear. Without him, Golden State had no answer for San Antonio, which controlled the game from early on in the Friday night Cup group-play matchup.

A statement in the West

The win kept the Spurs unbeaten at 2-0 in Cup group play and near the top of the Western Conference standings, where San Antonio sits at 11-2 alongside the Lakers. The 38-point margin was one of the most lopsided results of the Cup's group stage so far, and it underlined the Spurs' status as one of the early-season surprises of the 2026-27 campaign.

Sources: USA Today, November 14, 2026; Field Level Media, November 14, 2026

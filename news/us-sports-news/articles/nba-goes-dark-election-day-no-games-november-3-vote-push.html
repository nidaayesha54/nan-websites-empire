Why There Are No NBA Games Today: The League Goes Dark for Election Day

November 3, 2026

There are no NBA games on Tuesday, November 3 — not because of a scheduling gap, but by design. For the fourth time in five years, the league has gone dark on Election Day to encourage voting, USA Today reported.

A deliberate blank date

The league did not schedule any games on Tuesday because the first polls close on the East Coast at 6 p.m. ET, with many more at 7 p.m. ET, and holding games would interfere with the ability of people who vote in person to do so, USA Today reported. It is one of only three days the NBA typically avoids scheduling games, alongside Thanksgiving and Christmas Eve. The league first began the Election Day initiative in November 2022 and also went dark on Election Day in 2023 and 2024.

Monday's message, Wednesday's return

On Monday, November 2, each team played, and at each of the 15 games players wore T-shirts that said 'VOTE' across the chest. Golden State coach Steve Kerr spoke before his team's game against Phoenix about the importance of voting. The NBA has also published voting resources for fans, including polling-site information and a voter-registration check. The league returns in full force on Wednesday, November 4, with a slate of 14 games.

Sources: USA Today, November 3, 2026

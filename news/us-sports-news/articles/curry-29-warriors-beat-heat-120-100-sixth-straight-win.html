Curry Scores 29 as Warriors Beat Heat 120-100 for Sixth Straight Win

November 6, 2026

Stephen Curry scored 29 points, 22 of them in the second half, as the Golden State Warriors won their sixth straight game by beating the Miami Heat 120-100 on Thursday night in San Francisco, Reuters and Sportsnet reported.

A third-quarter takeover

Miami, playing the second night of a back-to-back after an overtime win over the Los Angeles Clippers on Wednesday, led 61-58 early in the third quarter before Golden State took over. The Warriors opened with an 18-3 run featuring 11 points from Curry, including two three-pointers, and outscored the Heat 38-23 in the third quarter to take a 15-point lead into the fourth. Curry finished 9 of 19 from the field with six three-pointers, falling one point short of his fourth straight 30-point game. Yaxel Lendeborg added 17 points, eight rebounds and six assists, Draymond Green had a game-high 11 assists, and Golden State's bench outscored Miami's 53-27.

Klay Thompson's return to Chase Center

The night also marked Klay Thompson's first appearance at Chase Center as a member of the Heat, and the former Splash Brother received a warm ovation before tipoff before scoring 11 points. Giannis Antetokounmpo had 20 points and 14 rebounds for Miami, Davion Mitchell contributed 22 points and eight assists, and Bam Adebayo added 18 points and 10 rebounds, but the Heat fell to 3-6 and dropped to 26th in the league in defensive rating. The Warriors, who have not lost since an 0-3 start, are now tied with the San Antonio Spurs for the longest winning streak in the NBA. Golden State hosts the Orlando Magic on Saturday.

Sources: Reuters, Sportsnet, November 5-6, 2026

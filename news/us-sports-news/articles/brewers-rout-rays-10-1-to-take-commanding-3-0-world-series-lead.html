Brewers Rout Rays 10-1 to Take Commanding 3-0 World Series Lead

October 27, 2026

Milwaukee moved to the brink of a World Series title on Monday night, dismantling Tampa Bay 10-1 in Game 3 behind an eight-run fifth inning and four hits from Jackson Chourio. The Brewers now lead the series 3-0, with Game 4 set for Tuesday night at Tropicana Field in St. Petersburg, Florida.

Brice Turang and William Contreras each went 3 for 5 in the rout, as Milwaukee's lineup overwhelmed Tampa Bay pitching in the decisive fifth inning. The Rays managed just one run on three hits on the night, a continuation of an offensive drought that has defined the series so far.

One win from history

The Brewers are now one victory from a championship that would crown a first-time World Series winner, with both Milwaukee and Tampa Bay chasing the first title in franchise history. Milwaukee swept the Dodgers in the National League Championship Series to reach the Fall Classic, while Tampa Bay beat Cleveland in six games in the American League Championship Series. The Brewers finished the regular season 103-59, the best record in baseball, and have carried that dominance into October.

History favors Milwaukee heavily. Teams leading a World Series 3-0 have almost always gone on to win it, and the Rays now face the prospect of the first World Series sweep since 2012, when the San Francisco Giants finished off the Detroit Tigers. Tampa Bay had hoped the Tropicana Field crowd would spark a comeback, but the Game 3 blowout has left the home team searching for answers.

Game 4: elimination game for Tampa Bay

Game 4 starts at 8 p.m. ET on Tuesday and will be broadcast nationally on Fox, with Joe Davis and John Smoltz calling the game. Griffin Jax is scheduled to open the game on the mound for the Rays, who are in desperate need of offense after scratching together just one run in Game 3. Milwaukee has yet to announce its Game 4 starter, though Dustin May is reported as a likely candidate.

If the Rays win, Game 5 would follow on Wednesday at Tropicana Field. If Milwaukee completes the sweep, the celebration will begin on the road, capping a postseason run that has seen the Brewers lose just once in the National League Championship Series and dominate the World Series to date.

Sources: Associated Press, Sarasota Herald-Tribune

Giants Edge Texans 27-21 in Overtime Behind McCarthy Debut and Skattebo's Return

October 26, 2026

The New York Giants secured a 27-21 overtime victory against the Houston Texans on Sunday, fueled by a strong debut from J.J. McCarthy and a bounce-back rushing performance by Cam Skattebo.

The win counts among the biggest of Week 7 for the Giants, who found a way to close out a tight game in the extra period after a back-and-forth afternoon. McCarthy's debut gave the offense a spark, while Skattebo's physical running set the tone on the ground.

Skattebo's defiant message after rare penalty

The game's most talked-about moment may have come away from the scoreboard. Skattebo was flagged for unnecessary roughness in the first quarter after lowering his helmet into Texans safety M.J. Stewart during a run, a rare call against an offensive player.

Entering Week 7, only four lowering-the-helmet penalties had been called across the NFL this season, with just one assessed against an offensive player, Texans tight end Foster Moreau. Skattebo made it clear after the game that he has no plans to change his physical running style, even if the league keeps penalizing or fining him for it.

As reported by Athlon Sports, Skattebo said the NFL loves to post him when he does things like that but then also wants to take things from him, adding that he will run the ball the way he wants to run it, and if he keeps getting flags or fines, he guesses he will play for free.

A fine already on the books

The penalty was not Skattebo's first brush with the league's helmet rules. The NFL fined him $7,073 on October 2 for impermissible helmet use in a Week 3 win over the Tennessee Titans, so Sunday's flag puts him squarely back in the league's spotlight.

Whether a further fine follows is now a talking point around the league. Players around the NFL will be watching closely, since enforcement of the lowering-the-helmet rule against ball carriers has been inconsistent, and Skattebo's case has become the most visible test of it this season.

What the win means

For the Giants, the overtime win is a building block. A debut at quarterback that produces points, a running game that imposes its will, and a defense that holds up in overtime are the ingredients of a team finding its identity midseason.

For Houston, the loss stings, especially one decided in overtime after the Texans had chances to close it out. The Texans now regroup with the same questions every close-loss team faces: how to finish games, and how to handle a Giants team that refused to go away.

Week 7's other big storyline, the Seattle Seahawks' 20-3 win over the previously unbeaten Kansas City Chiefs on Sunday Night Football, only adds to a weekend that reshaped the early playoff picture. The Giants, for their part, will take the win and the headlines, and Skattebo will take his running style with him into next week, fines or not.

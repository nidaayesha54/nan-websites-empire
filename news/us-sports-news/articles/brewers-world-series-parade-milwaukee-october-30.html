Brewers Celebrate First World Series Title With Victory Parade in Milwaukee

October 31, 2026

The Milwaukee Brewers' championship week continued on Friday, October 30, with a victory parade in Milwaukee celebrating the club's first World Series title.

From Tampa Bay to Milwaukee

The Brewers clinched the title on October 28 at the Tampa Bay Rays, then made it back to Wisconsin in time to surprise fans at Lambeau Field on Thursday night, hoisting the Commissioner's Trophy while walking out of the tunnel between the first and second quarters of the Green Bay Packers' 27-19 win over the Carolina Panthers. Several Brewers players took a Lambeau Leap, and the team watched the rest of the game from a private box wearing cheeseheads reading '2026 WS Champions'.

A Wisconsin celebration

The parade on Friday capped a week in which Wisconsin sport took center stage: a World Series title on Wednesday, a trophy cameo at Lambeau on Thursday, and a championship parade on Friday. The Packers, who beat Carolina 27-19 to move to 5-3, made sure the good vibes statewide kept rolling, closing out the Panthers in the fourth quarter after the Brewers' appearance had set the tone for the night.

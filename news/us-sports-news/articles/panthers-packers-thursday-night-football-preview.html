Panthers vs. Packers on Thursday Night Football: Two Wounded Contenders Meet at Lambeau

October 29, 2026

Thursday Night Football heads to Lambeau Field tonight, where the Green Bay Packers host the Carolina Panthers at 8:15 p.m. ET on Prime Video. Both teams arrive wounded, both need a win, and the standings make the stakes stranger than the records suggest.

Two teams coming off bad losses

Green Bay is 4-3 and sits last in the NFC North after a 20-17 loss at Detroit on Sunday, despite quarterback Jordan Love throwing for 274 yards and two touchdowns. Carolina is 3-3 yet leads the NFC South, even after a 43-13 home loss to Tampa Bay in which Bryce Young threw for just 163 yards with a touchdown and two interceptions. Green Bay enters as a 3.5-point home favorite, and most national experts are picking the Packers — though nearly all see a close game.

Injuries shape the matchup

Carolina's offensive line is battered: Taylor Moton is out, Ikem Ekwonu is on the physically unable to perform list, and Jaelan Phillips is questionable, while Jonathon Brooks, Jaycee Horn and Xavier Legette are also sidelined. Green Bay has its own absences, with Jayden Reed and Javon Hargrave on injured reserve and Micah Parsons on the PUP list. The running game offers Green Bay a path: its ground attack ranks last in the league, but Carolina's run defense has been one of its weaknesses all season.

What a win changes

A Packers victory would lift Green Bay to 5-3 heading into a mini-bye week and keep pace in a crowded NFC North. A Panthers win would strengthen Carolina's grip on the NFC South and serve as a statement road victory for a young team. Kickoff is 8:15 p.m. ET, with pregame coverage from 7 p.m. ET.

Sources: NDTV Sports, USA TODAY Sportsbook Wire, Athlon Sports, October 29, 2026

NBA Monday: Pelicans Hand Grizzlies First Loss in OT, Knicks Roll Past Nets

November 3, 2026

Monday, November 2 delivered a full NBA slate — the last before the league goes dark for Election Day — with an overtime thriller in Memphis, a one-sided New York derby, and big scoring nights in Los Angeles and Oklahoma City, Reuters reported.

Pelicans end Memphis' perfect start

Zion Williamson scored 25 points and grabbed 11 rebounds, and Jeremiah Fears sank two clutch free throws with 7.3 seconds left in overtime as New Orleans beat host Memphis 135-134, handing the Grizzlies their first loss of the season and ending a franchise-record-matching six-game winning streak to open the campaign. Ty Jerome scored the last nine points of regulation to force overtime and finished with 40 points, but missed a 15-foot attempt for the win. New Orleans won its third straight after opening 0-4. Elsewhere, Sacramento beat Dallas 115-100 for its first win of the season, behind Darius Acuff Jr.'s 25 points.

Knicks, Lakers and Thunder win

The New York Knicks beat the Brooklyn Nets 103-90, extending their winning streak against their city rival to 15 games, with Landry Shamet scoring a season-high 22 points off the bench. Luka Doncic scored 42 points with 13 assists as the Lakers beat Miami 138-117, topping 40 points for the fourth time this season. Shai Gilgeous-Alexander scored a season-high 43 points, including the go-ahead three-pointer with 33 seconds left, as Oklahoma City edged Cleveland 117-112.

Sources: Reuters, November 3, 2026

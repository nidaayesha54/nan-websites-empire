Lightning End McDavid's 23-Game Point Streak as Tampa Bay Beats Oilers 3-1

November 21, 2026

Tampa Bay beat Edmonton 3-1 on Thursday night, holding Connor McDavid pointless for the first time this season and ending his 23-game point streak and Edmonton's six-game winning streak on the same night, the FSD Morning Brief reported on November 20.

A streak ends in Tampa

McDavid, the league's leading scorer, had recorded a point in every game this season until the Lightning shut him down. The win was a statement for Tampa Bay, which managed to contain the sport's most dangerous offensive player for a full 60 minutes on home ice.

A busy night around the league

Elsewhere on Thursday, Lane Hutson scored both Montreal goals in a 2-1 overtime win at Carolina, Berkly Catton recorded his first career hat trick in Seattle's 5-1 win over Vancouver, and Mark Stone passed Jonathan Marchessault for the Vegas franchise scoring record in Dallas' 4-3 shootout win at Vegas. Colorado shut out Anaheim 4-0, and Los Angeles rallied from 3-0 down to beat Toronto 4-3 in a shootout, stretching Toronto's winless run to eight straight games.

Sources: FSD Morning Brief, November 20, 2026

Tatum Scores 34 as Celtics Rout Hornets 119-80 for Sixth Straight Charlotte Loss

November 7, 2026

Jayson Tatum scored a game-high 34 points as the Boston Celtics routed the Charlotte Hornets 119-80 on Friday night in Boston, handing Charlotte its sixth consecutive loss, USA Today reported on November 7.

Tatum sets the tone early

Tatum came out of the gates hot, scoring 18 points in the first quarter and 24 in the first half, finishing the night with 34 points. The Celtics made 24 three-pointers on 55 percent shooting from long range, while Charlotte made only 11 on 22 percent shooting, finishing 11-for-50 from beyond the arc. The 39-point margin was Charlotte's fourth loss by 15 points or more during its losing run, and the Hornets have now dropped to 3-7 and the No. 11 spot in the Eastern Conference after starting 3-1 through their first four games.

Hornets lose another key player

Charlotte lost one of its best players to injury for the second game in a row, with Brandon Miller leaving in the second quarter with a groin injury. Naz Reid led the Hornets in scoring with 14 points and six rebounds on 6-for-17 shooting. The Hornets now face another tough test on Monday against the Cleveland Cavaliers, and will have to bring considerably more fight — and hope for better injury news — if they are to stop the slide.

Sources: USA Today, November 7, 2026

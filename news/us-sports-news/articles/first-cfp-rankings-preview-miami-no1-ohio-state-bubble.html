First College Football Playoff Rankings Tuesday: Miami Projected No. 1, Ohio State on the Bubble

November 2, 2026

The first College Football Playoff rankings of the 2026 season will be unveiled on Tuesday, November 3, starting at 7 p.m. ET on ESPN, after a chaotic Week 9 that saw seven ranked teams fall.

The projected top

USA TODAY's projections put undefeated Miami (8-0) first, followed by Georgia (7-1), Utah (8-0), Indiana and UCLA, while Bleacher Report's top 25 also has Miami first ahead of Georgia, Utah, Indiana and UCLA, with Notre Dame and BYU in the mix. Miami survived a scare at North Carolina 38-35, Georgia dominated Florida 27-8, and Utah beat Cincinnati 49-25. The committee's top 25 will be revealed in parts through the one-hour show.

Ohio State's dilemma

Ohio State (6-2) fell 30-18 at USC in its first game without injured quarterback Julian Sayin, with true freshman Luke Fahey stepping in. Projections differ sharply on the Buckeyes: USA TODAY keeps them sixth, NCAA.com's prediction drops them to 11th, and Bleacher Report drops them to 13th — but all agree they have no margin for error left. Miami's next test comes quickly: the Hurricanes travel to Notre Dame in Week 10, a game that could shake up the bracket again within days.

Sources: USA TODAY, Bleacher Report, NCAA.com, Athlon Sports, November 1-2, 2026

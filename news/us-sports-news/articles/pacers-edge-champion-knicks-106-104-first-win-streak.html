Pacers Edge Reigning Champion Knicks 106-104 for First Win Streak of the Season

November 14, 2026

The Indiana Pacers held off the reigning champion New York Knicks 106-104 on Friday night in Indianapolis, securing their first win streak of the season, the Indianapolis Star reported on November 14.

A lead that nearly slipped away

Indiana led by 13 points with six minutes left, but the advantage shrank to six with three minutes remaining and four with 26 seconds left. Karl-Anthony Towns got the ball off the Knicks' inbound, turned and shot a 16-foot jumper that bounced off the rim and fell away, and the Pacers held on. It was an NBA Cup group-play game, and the win gave Indiana its first Cup victory.

Back-to-back wins at last

With the win over the Washington Wizards on Wednesday and the Knicks on Friday, the Pacers (5-8) have strung together two straight wins for the first time in the 2026-27 season. Indiana outrebounded New York 50-43 and limited the champions to just eight offensive rebounds. Star point guard Tyrese Haliburton remains sidelined with a hamstring injury, and Aaron Nesmith left Friday's game in the final seconds with an apparent hand injury.

Sources: Indianapolis Star, November 14, 2026; USA Today, November 14, 2026

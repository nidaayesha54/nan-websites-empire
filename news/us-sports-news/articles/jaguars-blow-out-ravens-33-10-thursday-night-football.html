Jaguars Blow Out Injury-Hit Ravens 33-10 to Kick Off NFL Week 9

November 6, 2026

The Jacksonville Jaguars cruised to a 33-10 victory over the host Baltimore Ravens on a rainy Thursday night to kick off Week 9 of the NFL season, improving to 6-2 while Baltimore lost its fourth consecutive game to fall to 4-5, Reuters and NBC Sports reported.

A one-sided night in Baltimore

Trevor Lawrence passed for 242 yards and one touchdown, completing 16 of 26 passes, and Cam Little kicked four field goals as Jacksonville took the lead for good on the opening drive. Bhayshul Tuten and Chris Rodriguez Jr. rushed for touchdowns, and Parker Washington turned a Lawrence pass into a 50-yard scoring catch-and-run just before halftime to make it 17-3. The Jaguars outgained the Ravens 319 yards to 227 and forced three turnovers. It was Jacksonville's eighth win by 20 or more points since the start of last season, and the Jaguars have now won five of their past six games.

The Ravens' quarterback crisis

Baltimore was unable to mount a passing attack with Lamar Jackson sidelined by a right thumb injury and backup Tyler Huntley out with a right toe injury. Rookie third-string quarterback Joe Fagnano made his first professional start and finished 10 of 18 for 60 yards with two interceptions. Derrick Henry was held to 57 yards on 17 carries, and the Ravens have now been held under 14 points in four straight games for the first time in team history, according to ESPN. Justice Hill provided Baltimore's only touchdown on a 65-yard run in the fourth quarter, the longest run of the season against Jacksonville's defence. The Jaguars now sit third in the AFC standings at the season's halfway point.

Sources: Reuters, NBC Sports, USA Today, November 5-6, 2026

NFL Week 8: Bills Rout Ravens 36-10, Broncos Edge Chiefs 28-24 in Comeback

November 2, 2026

Sunday, November 1 delivered a full day of Week 8 drama, with Buffalo handing Baltimore a lopsided defeat, Denver taking over first place in the AFC West, and two overtime thrillers, recaps reported on November 2.

Bills dominate, Ravens lose both quarterbacks

Josh Allen threw for 289 yards and two touchdowns and set the franchise record for career touchdown passes as the Bills (6-1) beat the Ravens 36-10 in Orchard Park, holding Baltimore to 144 yards of total offense. Baltimore (4-4) lost Lamar Jackson to a right thumb injury on the opening play and backup Tyler Huntley to a right toe injury in the first half; Jackson was later placed on injured reserve, according to Field Level Media. Allen also rushed for a score in Buffalo's third straight win.

Broncos rally, Bengals and Cardinals win in OT

Denver (6-2) overcame a 24-13 deficit to beat Kansas City 28-24, with Jonah Coleman scoring the go-ahead touchdown with 1:56 remaining and a goal-line stand in the final seconds — Malcolm Roach sacking Patrick Mahomes, who threw for 321 yards and three touchdowns, to end it. Cincinnati beat Tennessee 43-36 in overtime behind Joe Burrow's five touchdown passes and game-winning rushing score, while Arizona rallied from 17-3 down to beat Dallas 33-30 in overtime. Washington also beat Philadelphia after erasing an early 10-0 deficit, handing the Eagles a sixth straight loss.

Sources: Field Level Media via Northland News Radio, WDRC, November 2, 2026

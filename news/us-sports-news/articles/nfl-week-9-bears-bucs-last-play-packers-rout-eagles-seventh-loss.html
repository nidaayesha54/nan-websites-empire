NFL Week 9: Bears Win on Last Play as Eagles Slump to Seventh Straight Loss

November 9, 2026

NFL Week 9 delivered a full slate of drama on Sunday, with a last-play field goal in Chicago, a blowout in Foxborough and a seventh straight defeat for the Philadelphia Eagles, according to results compiled from ESPN box scores and Associated Press reports.

Bears 27, Buccaneers 24

Cairo Santos kicked a 38-yard field goal as time expired on Sunday night after Tampa Bay had levelled the game with 2:51 left through Bucky Irving's 6-yard touchdown run and a Baker Mayfield two-point conversion. The Bears improved to 7-2 while the Buccaneers fell to 3-6.

Packers 45, Patriots 27 — and Eagles 11, Giants 19

Jordan Love threw for 372 yards and three touchdowns and ran for another as Green Bay scored 21 points off four New England turnovers. In the NFC East, the New York Giants beat the Philadelphia Eagles 19-11, leaving the Eagles at 2-7 with their longest losing run since 2012, while the Giants (5-2-1) lead the division. Elsewhere, the Cincinnati Bengals beat the Atlanta Falcons 34-17 in the Madrid game at the Santiago Bernabeu, the Kansas City Chiefs beat the New York Jets 35-12 as Patrick Mahomes shook off an elbow scare, the Dallas Cowboys edged the Indianapolis Colts 27-23 in overtime, the Denver Broncos beat the Carolina Panthers 26-21, the San Francisco 49ers powered past the Las Vegas Raiders 38-17, the Detroit Lions beat the Miami Dolphins 19-9, the New Orleans Saints edged the Cleveland Browns 23-22 in overtime, the Houston Texans routed the Los Angeles Chargers 44-22, and the Seattle Seahawks beat the Arizona Cardinals 17-3. Week 9 wraps up on Monday Night Football when the Buffalo Bills visit the Minnesota Vikings.

Sources: The Sporting Base, November 9, 2026; Sportradar, November 9, 2026

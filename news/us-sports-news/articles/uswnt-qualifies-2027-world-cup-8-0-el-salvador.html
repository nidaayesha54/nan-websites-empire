USWNT Qualifies for 2027 World Cup With 8-0 Rout of El Salvador

November 28, 2026

The U.S. women's national team pummeled El Salvador 8-0 at Texas Health Mansfield Stadium and secured a spot in the 2027 Women's World Cup, ESPN reported on November 28.

Sanchez stars with a hat trick

Ashley Sanchez scored three goals in the first half, earning a perfect 10 rating from ESPN, as the United States made short work of its opponent. Head coach Emma Hayes had warned before the game that it would be the opposite of a formality, but the defending champions' dominance left no doubt about the outcome, and the result means the squad is in for the 2027 tournament.

Messi exits the MLS playoffs

Across the American soccer calendar, the picture was less celebratory for Inter Miami: Columbus knocked out the defending champions and Lionel Messi with an extra-time winner in the MLS playoffs, a loss that leaves neutral fans looking for a second team to follow. The U.S. women's qualification, by contrast, keeps the national team's focus firmly on next summer's World Cup and the defence of its place among the world's best.

Sources: ESPN via HeadlinesBriefing, November 28, 2026; New York Post, November 27, 2026

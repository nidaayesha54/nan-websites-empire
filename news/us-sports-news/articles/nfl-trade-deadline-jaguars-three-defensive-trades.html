NFL Trade Deadline: Jaguars Go All-In With Three Defensive Trades as Bills Roll On

November 10, 2026

The Jacksonville Jaguars have officially entered win-now mode, completing three defensive trades on Monday in an aggressive effort to strengthen their Super Bowl chances, hours before the NFL trade deadline arrives at 4 p.m. ET on Tuesday, November 10.

Three deals in one day

Jacksonville acquired defensive tackle Jordan Davis from the Philadelphia Eagles, safety Jessie Bates from the Atlanta Falcons in exchange for a 2027 second-round pick and a 2028 fifth-round pick, and defensive tackle Davon Godchaux from the New Orleans Saints, reshaping a defence that was already performing at a high level. The New York Jets also traded safety Andre Cisco to the Detroit Lions for a 2028 sixth-round draft pick, while the Baltimore Ravens acquired quarterback Jacoby Brissett from Arizona on November 6 after injuries devastated their quarterback depth.

Bills dominate on Monday Night Football

Buffalo delivered another statement performance on Monday Night Football, defeating Minnesota 23-6 behind a dominant defensive effort. The Bills improved to 7-1 while handing the Vikings their second consecutive defeat, raising fresh questions about Minnesota's offence without Kyler Murray. The Bills have held consecutive opponents under 200 total yards and forced six turnovers. Elsewhere, the Indianapolis Colts — who have already traded guard Quenton Nelson and defensive lineman DeForest Buckner amid a five-game losing streak — are fielding calls about star running back Jonathan Taylor, while the New York Giants have engaged the Colts about Taylor and expressed interest in a running back and a defensive lineman.

Sources: Democrat and Chronicle, November 10, 2026; The Chairshot, November 10, 2026

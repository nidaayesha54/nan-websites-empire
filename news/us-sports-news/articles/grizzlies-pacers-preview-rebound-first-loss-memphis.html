Grizzlies Look to Rebound From First Loss as Pacers Visit Memphis

November 4, 2026

The Memphis Grizzlies will attempt to bounce back from their first loss of the season when they host the Indiana Pacers on Wednesday night, Reuters reported.

How Memphis lost

Memphis was the NBA's last unbeaten team before falling 135-134 in overtime to the visiting New Orleans Pelicans on Monday. Trailing by nine points with two minutes left, the Grizzlies relied on starting point guard Ty Jerome, who scored nine straight points to force overtime and finished with 40 points, his second game this season with 40 or more — but his potential game-winning shot in the closing seconds of overtime fell short. Memphis was attempting to start a season 7-0 for the first time.

A wounded Pacers side

Indiana has lost five straight games, but nearly overcame an exceptionally short-handed roster to earn a home win over Atlanta on Monday, falling 108-104 after Quenton Jackson's go-ahead three-point attempt with 43 seconds left missed. Indiana outscored Atlanta 48-40 in the second half of that game. Memphis enters at 6-1 and is favoured at home; the result had not been played at the time of writing.

Sources: Reuters, November 4, 2026

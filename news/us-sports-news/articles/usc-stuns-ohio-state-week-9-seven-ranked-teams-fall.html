USC Stuns No. 2 Ohio State 30-18 as Seven Ranked Teams Fall in College Football's Wild Week 9

November 1, 2026

It was a spooky Halloween in Week 9 of college football, with seven ranked teams falling throughout the day, NCAA.com reported on November 1 — headlined by Southern California's stunning 30-18 win over No. 2 Ohio State.

The upset of the day

USC earned a 30-18 victory over Ohio State in Los Angeles, handing the Buckeyes their first Big Ten loss of the season. The Trojans scored twice in the first quarter and kept Ohio State out of the end zone until late in the fourth, despite allowing 464 yards. Ohio State played with true freshman Luke Fahey in place of the injured Julian Sayin. Elsewhere, Iowa shut out No. 25 Wisconsin 30-0, Iowa State walked off No. 24 Oklahoma State 51-49, Oregon topped No. 20 Northwestern 34-26, Syracuse took down No. 19 SMU 30-27, No. 13 Texas Tech beat No. 22 Arizona 34-26, and No. 3 Georgia dominated No. 17 Florida 27-8.

What it means for the rankings

The chaos comes days before the first College Football Playoff rankings reveal on Tuesday, November 3, and with seven ranked teams falling there could be significant movement in the committee's top 25. Miami survived a scare at North Carolina 38-35 and Indiana needed overtime to beat Minnesota 23-16, while Texas beat Mississippi State 31-3 and Ole Miss routed Auburn 45-15. Bleacher Report's updated expert rankings, published November 1, dropped Ohio State from second to 13th and kept Miami first ahead of Georgia, Utah, Indiana and UCLA — an editorial ranking, not the committee's official list.

Sources: NCAA.com, PFF, Bleacher Report via Eastern Herald, November 1, 2026

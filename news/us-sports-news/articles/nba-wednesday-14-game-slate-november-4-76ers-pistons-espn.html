NBA Wednesday: 14 Games Return After Election Day, Led by 76ers at Pistons on ESPN

November 4, 2026

The NBA returns in full force on Wednesday, November 4, after going dark for Election Day, with a 14-game slate tipping off at 7 p.m. ET, schedules showed.

The headline games

The night opens with the Philadelphia 76ers (7-1) at the Detroit Pistons (6-1) at 7 p.m. ET on ESPN, a meeting of two of the East's early pacesetters, alongside Atlanta at Washington. The late ESPN game sends Dallas (4-3) to San Antonio (5-2) at 9:30 p.m. ET, while the Los Angeles Lakers (6-2) visit the Sacramento Kings (1-6) at 10 p.m. ET and Miami (2-5) visits the LA Clippers (1-6) at 10:30 p.m. ET.

The rest of the slate

Elsewhere, Milwaukee (1-6) visits Boston (5-2), Chicago (1-6) visits New York (3-4), Indiana (2-6) visits Memphis (6-1), Utah (1-6) visits New Orleans (3-4), Cleveland (4-3) visits Oklahoma City (5-2), Minnesota (5-3) visits Houston (7-1), Toronto (4-3) visits Denver (3-3), Portland (5-1) visits Phoenix (1-4), and Charlotte (3-5) visits Brooklyn (1-6). No results from Wednesday's games were available at the time of writing; this preview is based on the published schedule and records entering the night.

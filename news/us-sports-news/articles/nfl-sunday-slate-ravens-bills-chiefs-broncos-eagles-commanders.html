NFL Sunday: Ravens-Bills and Chiefs-Broncos Headline a Packed Slate Capped by Eagles-Commanders

November 1, 2026

Sunday, November 1 brings a full NFL slate, headlined by a pair of heavyweight afternoon matchups and a primetime divisional clash to close the night, according to published schedules.

The afternoon headliners

The 1:00 p.m. ET window includes the Baltimore Ravens at the Buffalo Bills at Highmark Stadium, the Dallas Cowboys hosting the Arizona Cardinals, the Detroit Lions hosting the Minnesota Vikings, and the Pittsburgh Steelers hosting the Cleveland Browns. The late afternoon brings the Los Angeles Rams against the Los Angeles Chargers at SoFi Stadium and the Kansas City Chiefs at the Denver Broncos at Empower Field at Mile High, plus the New England Patriots at the Miami Dolphins.

Sunday Night Football closes the day

The nightcap is the Philadelphia Eagles at the Washington Commanders at Northwest Stadium, kicking off at 8:20 p.m. ET on NBC. At the time of writing, none of Sunday's games had been played, so no results are reported here; readers should check the live scoreboard for final scores. The slate follows a Thursday night win for Green Bay over Carolina and a Saturday of college football chaos, keeping a crowded football weekend rolling into November.

Sources: NFL schedule listings (Sync2Cal, SportsCal, Bookies), November 1, 2026

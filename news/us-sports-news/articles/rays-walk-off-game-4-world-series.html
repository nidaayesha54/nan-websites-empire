Rays Stun Brewers in 12th to Avoid Sweep and Force World Series Game 5

October 28, 2026

The Tampa Bay Rays refused to be swept. Pinch hitter Ben Williamson delivered a walk-off single in the 12th inning on Tuesday night, completing a wild 4-3 comeback over the Milwaukee Brewers in Game 4 of the World Series at Tropicana Field and forcing a Game 5 on Wednesday.

How the 12th unfolded

Milwaukee looked set to celebrate its first World Series championship in 57 years when rookie shortstop Cooper Pratt, capping a four-hit night, homered to lead off the top of the 12th for a 3-2 lead. The champagne was reportedly ready and the plastic lining was being unrolled. Instead, the bottom of the inning unravelled for the Brewers: third baseman Joey Ortiz threw Junior Caminero's routine grounder past first base, Liam Hicks singled, Jonny DeLuca singled home the tying run, Chandler Simpson loaded the bases, and Williamson bounced a chopper into right field to score pinch runner Nick Fortes.

"Just shows the resiliency of this group," Williamson said afterwards. "We continue to fight and that's been the motto this series and this whole season." Ortiz was blunt about his error: "There's no excuse. Not one at all. I should make that play every time."

The Brewers still lead the best-of-seven series 3-1, and they will turn to ace Jacob Misiorowski in Game 5 on Wednesday at 8 p.m. ET in St. Petersburg. It was the fourth game in World Series history in which a team staved off elimination with an extra-inning victory. Tampa Bay needs three more.

Sources: USA TODAY, NBC Sports, Philstar/AFP, October 28, 2026

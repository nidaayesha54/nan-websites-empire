Spurs Top Trail Blazers 125-103 in Portland

November 8, 2026

The San Antonio Spurs beat the Portland Trail Blazers 125-103 on Saturday night at the Moda Center in Portland, according to Sportradar's results listing for November 7, 2026.

A second win in two nights

The victory came a day after the Spurs beat the Sacramento Kings 124-109, giving San Antonio back-to-back road wins on the weekend. The 22-point margin in Portland was the more comfortable of the two results, and it keeps the Spurs rolling in the early weeks of the 2026-27 NBA season.

Sunday's slate

The NBA schedule continues on Sunday, November 8, with the unbeaten Philadelphia 76ers visiting the Chicago Bulls at the United Center, the unbeaten Detroit Pistons hosting the Los Angeles Lakers at Little Caesars Arena, and the Milwaukee Bucks facing the Boston Celtics at Fiserv Forum, among seven games on the day.

Sources: Sportradar, November 8, 2026

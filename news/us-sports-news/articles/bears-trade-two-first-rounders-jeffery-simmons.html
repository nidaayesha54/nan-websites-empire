Bears Go All-In: Chicago Lands Jeffery Simmons From Titans for Two First-Round Picks

October 30, 2026

The Chicago Bears have gone all-in. On Thursday, October 29, the Bears agreed to acquire All-Pro defensive tackle Jeffery Simmons from the Tennessee Titans, sending two first-round picks as part of a blockbuster package ahead of the November 10 trade deadline.

The terms of the deal

Chicago will send its first-round picks in 2027 and 2028, plus a fourth-round pick in 2027, to Tennessee in exchange for Simmons and a fifth-round pick in 2028, the teams confirmed. Simmons is the first defensive tackle in the common draft era, since 1967, to be swapped for multiple first-round picks.

Simmons, 29, was a first-round pick in 2019 and has spent his entire NFL career with Tennessee. He has 414 career tackles, 72 for losses, nine forced fumbles, 46.5 sacks and 96 quarterback hits in 106 regular-season games, and has four sacks and six tackles for loss in seven games this season. He signed a three-year extension in July worth a reported $105.8 million, keeping him under contract through 2030.

Why each side did it

The Bears, 5-2 and second in the NFC North, are clearly in pursuit of their first Lombardi Trophy since the famed 1985 team won it all. Titans coach Robert Saleh did not sugarcoat the short-term upshot, saying the trade “sucks” but was “necessary,” while general manager Mike Borgonzi said the team was not actively shopping Simmons until Bears general manager Ryan Poles reached out on Wednesday night.

For Tennessee, 1-6 and sitting near the top of the 2027 draft order, the deal banks two future first-rounders for a rebuild — though it costs the team its best player and sack leader. Simmons is reported to debut for Chicago on Monday night at Seattle, where the Bears will learn fast whether the price was worth it.

2026 Sports Equinox: World Series, WNBA Finals and Monday Night Football Share One Night

October 26, 2026

There might not be a better Monday on the sports calendar than October 26. It is officially the 2026 sports equinox, a rare convergence when the calendars of the biggest leagues in North America overlap to create a sports fan's dream night.

This year, the sports equinox features games in all five major professional leagues, plus championship action in two of them. That means the World Series, the WNBA Finals, Monday Night Football and regular-season NBA and NHL games are all on the schedule for the same evening.

World Series Game 3: Brewers at Rays

The headline baseball matchup is World Series Game 3, with the Milwaukee Brewers visiting the Tampa Bay Rays at 8 p.m. Eastern on FOX. Milwaukee has established a 2-0 lead in the series, with William Contreras and Sal Frelick playing key roles alongside reliever Aaron Ashby, so Tampa Bay faces a near must-win game on its home field.

A 3-0 series deficit has historically been almost impossible to overcome in the World Series, which gives Game 3 enormous stakes for the Rays and a chance for the Brewers to take a stranglehold on the championship.

WNBA Finals Game 5: Dream at Valkyries

At the same 8 p.m. Eastern hour, the WNBA Finals continue with Game 5, with the Atlanta Dream visiting the Golden State Valkyries. A Finals game this deep into a series is one of the most-watched spectacles in basketball, and the late-series stakes guarantee a charged atmosphere.

The game airs on USA Network, CNBC and Peacock, giving fans multiple ways to watch the championship showdown.

Monday Night Football: Cowboys at Eagles

The NFL contributes one of its fiercest rivalries: the Dallas Cowboys visit the Philadelphia Eagles at 8:15 p.m. Eastern on ESPN and ABC. Cowboys-Eagles games routinely draw some of the largest regular-season audiences of the year, and this one lands in the middle of a crowded championship night.

The matchup also carries divisional weight, with both teams fighting for position in the NFC East as the season enters its second half.

A full NBA and NHL slate

The NBA adds nine games to the night, including the Washington Wizards at Charlotte, the Chicago Bulls at Boston, Dallas at Miami, and a late showcase between the Golden State Warriors and the Denver Nuggets at 10 p.m. Eastern. Golden State arrives still searching for its first win of the season after an 0-3 start, while Denver sits at 1-1.

The NHL rounds out the equinox with its own slate of games, including the Kings against the Rangers. With five leagues and two championships sharing one night, October 26 is the kind of evening sports fans circle on the calendar years in advance, and the kind that only comes around when every league's schedule happens to align.

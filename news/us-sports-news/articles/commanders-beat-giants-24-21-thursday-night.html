Commanders Edge Giants 24-21 as Daniels' Late TD to Diggs Seals Thursday Night Win

November 13, 2026

The Washington Commanders secured a dramatic 24-21 win over the New York Giants on Thursday night in what many deemed a must-win game, Heavy.com reported on November 13.

A late rally

Washington trailed 21-17 in the fourth quarter after quarterback Jayden Daniels threw interceptions on back-to-back drives. Daniels finished 23 of 33 for 249 yards, three touchdowns and two interceptions.

The standings

Washington inched closer to the Giants in the NFC East with the victory and now holds the tiebreaker over New York, sitting just two games behind them for first place. The Commanders hold a 3-5-1 record, while the Giants sit at 5-3-1. Washington does not play again until Monday night in Week 11, when it hosts the Cincinnati Bengals.

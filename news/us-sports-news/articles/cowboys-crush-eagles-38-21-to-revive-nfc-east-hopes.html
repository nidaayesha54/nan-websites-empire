Cowboys Crush Eagles 38-21 to Revive NFC East Hopes

October 27, 2026

Dallas bounced back in the NFC East race on Monday night, defeating Philadelphia 38-21 behind a stellar performance from CeeDee Lamb and Javonte Williams, who combined for 229 yards and three touchdowns. Quarterback Dak Prescott threw three touchdown passes as the Cowboys ended a two-game losing run and secured their first win at Philadelphia since 2021.

The victory lifted Dallas to 3-4 and kept its division hopes alive, while the Eagles fell to 2-5 after a fifth consecutive defeat. Philadelphia, which won the Super Bowl in 2025, has seen its season unravel since a 2-0 start, losing star quarterback Jalen Hurts to a potentially season-ending blood clot and struggling to stop the slide since.

A rivalry game that turned early

The Cowboys took control behind an efficient offense and a defense that kept Philadelphia from finding any sustained rhythm. Lamb and Williams did the damage on the ground and through the air, combining for 229 yards and three touchdowns, while Prescott's three scoring passes kept the Eagles chasing the game. Dallas closed out the win in front of a hostile Philadelphia crowd, a result that reshapes the early NFC East picture.

For the Eagles, the loss extends a miserable stretch that has seen the defending champions lose five straight. The absence of Hurts has loomed large, and the team has struggled to replace his production and leadership. BBC Sport's week-seven roundup noted that few would have predicted Philadelphia's collapse at the start of the season, given the roster that won the title last year.

Elsewhere in week seven

Week seven also saw the end of the season's last unbeaten runs in other games. Kansas City lost at defending champions Seattle, and San Francisco suffered its first defeat, leaving Minnesota as the league's only unbeaten team. The Chiefs' loss to Seattle came in a primetime matchup between the teams with the season's best offense and best defense by total yards, and it marked the return of Kenneth Walker, last season's Super Bowl Most Valuable Player, to Seattle after joining Kansas City in the off-season.

For Dallas, the win offers a path back into contention. The Cowboys still trail in the division, but with the Eagles reeling and the season not yet at its midpoint, Monday night's result keeps their hopes alive and sets up a crucial stretch of games in the weeks ahead.

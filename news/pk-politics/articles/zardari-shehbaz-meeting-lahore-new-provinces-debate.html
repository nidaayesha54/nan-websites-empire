Zardari Meets PM Shehbaz in Lahore as New-Provinces Debate Rolls On

November 14, 2026

President Asif Ali Zardari met Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif at the prime minister's Model Town residence in Lahore on Saturday, as the debate over creating new provinces continued to dominate national politics, Dawn reported on November 14.

What the two leaders discussed

According to a statement from the Prime Minister's Office, the two leaders held detailed discussions on the country's overall political, economic and security situation. The prime minister briefed the president on his recent visit to Turkiye and his participation in the 31st Conference of the Parties, where he highlighted Pakistan's climate action efforts. They also exchanged views on cooperation among allied parties, the continuation of consultations and progress on public welfare projects. National Assembly Speaker Sardar Ayaz Sadiq, former prime minister Raja Pervaiz Ashraf, Senator Saleem Mandviwalla, Law Minister Azam Nazeer Tarar, Prime Minister's Adviser Rana Sanaullah and MNA Hamza Shehbaz were also present.

Presidency clarifies Nawabshah meeting

The meeting came two days after Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi's unscheduled late-night visit to meet the president in Nawabshah. After media reports claimed Naqvi and the president had discussed the possible formation of new provinces, the Presidency clarified that "no such discussion" took place, explaining that matters concerning national security and the regional situation were discussed instead. The last publicly reported meeting between the two leaders before Saturday was on October 10, a day before the federal government imposed emergency and governor's rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Sources: Dawn, November 14, 2026; The Express Tribune, November 14, 2026

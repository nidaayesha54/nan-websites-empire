Shehbaz Briefs Nawaz Sharif on K-P Security and Economic Challenges at Jati Umra

November 8, 2026

Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif met Pakistan Muslim League-Nawaz (PML-N) leader Nawaz Sharif in Lahore on Sunday and briefed him on the security situation in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa (K-P), the country's economic challenges and the government's ongoing reform agenda, the Prime Minister's Office (PMO) said. The meeting took place at Jati Umra, where the two leaders held detailed consultations on national political matters and steps to improve the economy, according to the PMO.

Who attended, and what was discussed

Deputy Prime Minister and Foreign Minister Ishaq Dar and Punjab Chief Minister Maryam Nawaz also attended the meeting, the PMO said. State-run Radio Pakistan reported that the prime minister briefed the PML-N president on the law and order situation in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa and on recent initiatives taken for the development and prosperity of the province, and that the meeting also deliberated on ways to improve the national economy further. The meeting took place as consumers faced another increase in electricity prices: the National Electric Power Regulatory Authority (NEPRA) notified a Rs2.36 per-unit increase on Saturday under the monthly fuel cost adjustment for September.

Governor's rule and the courts

The meeting followed the imposition of emergency and governor's rule in K-P on October 11, after President Asif Ali Zardari approved the summary on PM Shehbaz's advice under Articles 232 and 234 of the Constitution. The federal government cited a deterioration in the law and order situation and a rise in terrorist attacks as grounds for the decision. Terrorist incidents in the province rose by 195 per cent between March 2024 and September 2026, according to security reports presented to the federal cabinet. In a related development, the Peshawar High Court has deleted from its cause list all writ petitions filed against the imposition of emergency and Governor's Rule in Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa, in compliance with directives issued by the Federal Constitutional Court. Among the petitions removed are those seeking the recovery of Khyber-Pakhtunkhwa Chief Minister Sohail Afridi, Provincial Assembly Speaker Babar Saleem Swati and Junaid Akbar, The Express Tribune reported.

Sources: The Express Tribune, November 8, 2026; Dawn, November 8, 2026

Bilawal Calls for Political 'Reset', Says System Was 'Deliberately Destroyed'

November 17, 2026

Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP) Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari on Tuesday called for a political reset of the country's system, saying its deterioration was not an accident but the result of deliberate decisions, Geo News reported on November 17.

'The system did not collapse on its own'

Speaking at the Karachi Press Club, Bilawal said the political system had not collapsed on its own but had been deliberately destroyed. "The system needs a reset, but it must be a political reset," he said, adding that he was also responsible and that mistakes must be corrected. He said the solution did not lie in creating new provinces, but in restoring confidence in state institutions through political dialogue, and warned that any forced division of Sindh would spill blood in Karachi and Hyderabad.

Petroleum levy, prisoners and reserved seats

Bilawal said the provinces had been denied about Rs1.7 trillion of their share of the petroleum levy, and that all provinces were contributing from their own shares for national interest and security. He called for the release of political prisoners, the return of reserved seats to the opposition, and for the government to restore the confidence of all political stakeholders, including Maulana Fazlur Rehman, PTI founder Imran Khan and his party. He said he considered Imran Khan a political prisoner and wanted the day when no prime minister is jailed after leaving office. Information Minister Attaullah Tarar responded that the debate over good governance and new provinces could not be stopped, saying good governance was the democratic right of the people.

Sources: Geo News, November 17, 2026; The Express Tribune, November 17, 2026

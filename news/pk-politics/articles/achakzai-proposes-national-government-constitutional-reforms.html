Achakzai Proposes National Government and Sweeping Constitutional Reforms

November 25, 2026

Tehreek Tahafuz Ayeen-i-Pakistan (TTAP) head and Leader of the Opposition in the National Assembly Mahmood Khan Achakzai on Tuesday called for the formation of a national government and comprehensive constitutional and political reforms to steer the country out of the mounting crises it was facing, Dawn reported on November 25.

Decisions through consultation

Addressing a multi-party conference at the Javed Iqbal Auditorium of the Lahore High Court Bar Association, Mr Achakzai said decisions of national importance should be made through consultations among elected representatives and all democratic political forces, instead of being left to a handful of individuals. He stressed the need for constitutional supremacy, parliamentary sovereignty and the 'restoration of democracy', saying Lahore and its bar associations had historically played a significant role in the struggle for constitutional rule.

A 'dignified exit' from politics

Proposing a national government comprising major state institutions, political parties and other stakeholders, he said the new setup should address past injustices, introduce constitutional reforms and provide the military a 'constitutional and dignified exit from politics'. The Express Tribune reported that Mr Achakzai warned Pakistan could face disintegration if constitutional violations, political victimisation and injustices continued, and called on political parties to pledge not to join hands with undemocratic forces. PTI Chairman Gohar Ali Khan, addressing the same conference, called for the withdrawal of the 26th and 27th constitutional amendments, saying the changes were against the system and must be reversed to restore justice and public confidence in the judiciary, and urged parties to agree on a new Charter of Democracy.

Sources: Dawn, November 25, 2026; The Express Tribune, November 25, 2026

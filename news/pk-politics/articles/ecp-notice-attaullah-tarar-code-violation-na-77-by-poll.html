ECP Takes Notice of Alleged Code Violation by Attaullah Tarar Ahead of NA-77 By-Poll

November 7, 2026

The Election Commission of Pakistan (ECP) on Saturday took notice of an alleged violation of the Code of Conduct by Information Minister Attaullah Tarar during by-election campaigning in NA-77 Gujranwala, Dawn reported on November 7.

What the ECP said

According to a press release issued by the commission, the minister visited the constituency by helicopter and addressed a campaign event for Rashid Basheer Virk, who is contesting the National Assembly seat NA-77 (Gujranwala-I). The move breaches a provision of the Code of Conduct that bars public office holders, including federal ministers, from participating in campaign activities. The ECP added that the result of the constituency could be withheld until a final decision is made on the matter.

Two by-polls on November 8

By-elections in NA-77 and Punjab Assembly constituency PP-203 (Sahiwal-VI) are scheduled for November 8, both of which fell vacant after the deaths of their respective PML-N lawmakers. NA-77 fell vacant following the death of five-time MNA Chaudhry Mehmood Bashir Virk on September 11 after a prolonged illness. PP-203 fell vacant after Chaudhry Muhammad Hanif Jutt, who had won the seat in a by-election in November 2025, died of cardiac arrest on September 17, 2026.

What to watch next

With polling a day away, the commission's notice puts the focus on how strictly the code of conduct is enforced against sitting ministers — and whether the warning of a withheld result in NA-77 materialises. The by-polls are the immediate test of the electoral machinery ahead of the Punjab local government elections, whose first phase the ECP has set for February 7, 2027.

Sources: Dawn, November 7, 2026

Azma Bokhari Challenges Salik Hussain to Quit Ministry Amid War of Words With Maryam Nawaz

November 12, 2026

Punjab Information Minister Azma Bokhari on November 11 challenged federal minister Chaudhry Salik Hussain to relinquish his portfolio rather than criticise Maryam Nawaz's Punjab government while remaining part of the PML-N-led federal government, Dawn reported.

A row over Gujrat funds

The row began after Salik alleged corruption by the Punjab chief minister and discrimination in development funds for Gujrat. Salik accused Bokhari of \"derailing\" his rapprochement attempt and vowed to contest Maryam Nawaz's Rs5 billion defamation suit against him.

Two ministries, one coalition

The PML-Q holds two ministerial posts in the coalition — Salik at the federal level and his brother Shafay in Punjab — a split that has made the public spat between the allies all the more awkward for the government.

Sources: Dawn, November 12, 2026

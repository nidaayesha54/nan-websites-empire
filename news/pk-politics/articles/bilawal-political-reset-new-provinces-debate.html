Bilawal Calls for 'Political Reset' Instead of New Provinces

November 11, 2026

PPP Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari on Tuesday called for a “political reset” to steer the country out of what he described as its worst-ever economic crisis, urging the ruling and opposition parties to set aside their differences and work together, Dawn reported on November 11.

Trust with allies and opposition

Bilawal, who was addressing a ceremony at the CM House in Karachi to mark the completion of one million houses under the Sindh Peoples Housing for Flood Affectees project, urged the prime minister to build trust with allies and the opposition to tackle the economic crisis. He said parties must not indulge in “unnecessary debates” amid pressing challenges, and that the PPP sees strong local government in Punjab as key to resolving issues.

The new-provinces debate

The remarks come as Defence Minister Khawaja Asif’s recent statement regarding the creation of 12 more provinces revived the debate on administrative units, only weeks after Interior Minister Mohsin Naqvi had called for a “system reset” through the creation of new administrative units. Reports have suggested a plan to divide Pakistan administratively into 12 provinces through a possible 28th Constitutional Amendment, with a nationwide referendum also being contemplated. The PPP’s Punjab chapter has strongly rejected any move towards the creation of new provinces, while the Muttahida Qaumi Movement-Pakistan has reassured its support for the plan.

Sources: Dawn, November 11, 2026

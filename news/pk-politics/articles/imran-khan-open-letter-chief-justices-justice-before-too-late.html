Imran Khan Appeals to Three Chief Justices for 'Justice Before It's Too Late'

November 17, 2026

Incarcerated former prime minister Imran Khan has made an impassioned plea for justice in an open letter to the chief justices of the country's three superior courts, urging the top judges to implement judicial orders regarding his and his wife Bushra Bibi's medical treatment, The Express Tribune reported on November 17.

Letter unveiled by counsel

The letter, addressed to Chief Justice Yahya Afridi of the Supreme Court, Chief Justice Amin-ud-Din Khan of the Federal Constitutional Court and Chief Justice Sardar Muhammad Sarfraz Dogar of the Islamabad High Court, was unveiled on Monday by Salman Safdar, counsel for Imran and Bushra in a number of cases. Safdar, along with Sardar Latif Khosa, had met the couple at Adiala Jail on November 11 on the order of the Islamabad High Court. In the letter, Imran said he was in solitary confinement and that his blood pressure had become uncontrolled, citing readings of 130, then 150, then 170, and last week 152 and 195 on November 10. "I will only be able to prove my innocence if I stay alive," he wrote, appealing on the basic grounds of human compassion.

Appeals hearing set for November 18

The Islamabad High Court is hearing the couple's appeals against their convictions in the £190 million case, also called the Al-Qadir Trust case, in which an accountability court sentenced Imran to 14 years in prison and Bushra to seven years. A two-member division bench comprising Chief Justice Dogar and Justice Muhammad Asif will hear the appeals on Wednesday, November 18, and the court has already directed both parties to present their final arguments, Dunya News reported.

Sources: The Express Tribune, November 17, 2026; Dunya News, November 17, 2026

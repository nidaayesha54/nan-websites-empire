Peshawar ATC Reissues Arrest Warrant for Sohail Afridi in Radio Pakistan Attack Case

November 11, 2026

An anti-terrorism court (ATC) in Peshawar on Wednesday reissued arrest warrants for former chief minister Sohail Afridi, whose government was rendered nonfunctional following the imposition of governor’s rule in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, and a PTI activist in the Radio Pakistan attack case, Dawn reported on November 11.

Case dates back to May 2023 unrest

The case relates to the attack, vandalism and arson at the Radio Pakistan Peshawar building during the May 9-10, 2023 unrest. Judge Wali Muhammad Khan reissued the warrants for Afridi and PTI activist Amir Khan Chamkani after their earlier warrants, issued on October 28, were returned unserved. Afridi has not been seen since the imposition of governor’s rule and emergency in KP on October 11. The court is currently conducting the trial of 75 accused in the case, including serving and former lawmakers.

Bail extended for other PTI leaders

The court also extended interim pre-arrest bail for PTI leaders and former provincial ministers Taimur Saleem Jhagra and Kamran Bangash, as well as the party’s Peshawar president Irfan Saleem, until November 17. Accepting the request, the court adjourned the hearing until November 17.

Sources: Dawn, November 11, 2026

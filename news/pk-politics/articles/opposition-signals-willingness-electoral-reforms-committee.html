Opposition Signals Willingness to Join Electoral Reforms Committee After PPP Outreach

October 30, 2026

Opposition leaders have indicated their willingness to join a proposed parliamentary committee on electoral reforms following an outreach by the Pakistan Peoples Party (PPP), with a formal response expected after consultations within the next few days, according to Geo News.

The PPP outreach

A PPP delegation met opposition leaders at the Parliament House in Islamabad on October 30, on the instructions of party Chairman Bilawal Bhutto-Zardari, and invited them to become part of the electoral reforms committee. The PPP delegation included former Senate chairman Nayyer Bokhari, Senator Sherry Rehman, Senator Waqar Mehdi and Nadeem Afzal Chan. Tehreek-e-Tahafuz-e-Ayin-e-Pakistan chief and National Assembly Opposition Leader Mahmood Khan Achakzai and Senate Opposition Leader Allama Raja Nasir Abbas attended, along with PTI leaders Senator Ali Zafar, Senator Noorul Haq Qadri and Hussain Ahmad Yousafzai.

A 30-member panel

Minister for Parliamentary Affairs Tariq Fazal Chaudhry said the National Assembly had already approved the formation of the electoral reforms committee and that the Speaker would soon issue its formal notification, with the finalisation of members' names still under way. The proposed committee would comprise 30 parliamentarians — 20 from the National Assembly and 10 from the Senate — with seats distributed among political parties according to their numerical strength in the two houses. PTI Chairman Barrister Gohar Ali Khan has demanded equal representation for the government and opposition on the panel.

What the committee would examine

According to reports, the committee is expected to examine alleged rigging in the 2024 general elections as well as subsequent by-elections and elections in Gilgit-Baltistan and Azad Jammu and Kashmir, with its recommendations to be presented before parliament for approval. The PPP proposed that the government and opposition jointly formulate recommendations aimed at ensuring transparent, free and fair elections.

Achakzai and Abbas sought time to consult the PTI leadership before giving a formal response, so the opposition's participation is signalled rather than confirmed for now.

Sources: Geo News and 24NewsHD, October 30, 2026.

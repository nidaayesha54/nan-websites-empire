Opposition Leaders Move Constitutional Court Seeking Release of Sohail Afridi and Detained Lawmakers

November 14, 2026

The leaders of the opposition in both houses of parliament on Saturday approached the Federal Constitutional Court (FCC), seeking the immediate release of Sohail Afridi, the non-functional Khyber Pakhtunkhwa chief minister, along with eight other elected members of the provincial assembly and six members of the National Assembly, Dawn reported on November 14.

Detained since governor's rule

Afridi has not been seen since the Centre imposed governor's rule and emergency in KP on October 11. The very next day, a federal minister confirmed that he and some of his cabinet members were in preventive custody. While the government has transferred powers to KP Governor Faisal Karim Kundi, the PTI has asserted that Afridi "remains and shall continue" to serve as the KP chief minister with the confidence and support of party founder Imran Khan.

What the petition asks

National Assembly Opposition Leader Mehmood Khan Achakzai and Senate Opposition Leader Raja Nasir Abbas jointly filed the petition, seeking enforcement of fundamental rights and constitutional protections against the alleged unlawful arrest, detention and confinement of Afridi, KP Assembly Speaker Babar Saleem Swati and other lawmakers. The petition raised questions about the supremacy of the Constitution, the sanctity of the democratic mandate and constitutional limits on executive authority. On Thursday, PTI lawmaker Shandana Gulzar had also approached the FCC seeking the release of Afridi, the provincial assembly speaker and other party workers.

Sources: Dawn, November 14, 2026

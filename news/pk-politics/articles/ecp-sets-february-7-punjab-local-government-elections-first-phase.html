ECP Sets February 7 for First Phase of Punjab Local Government Elections as KP Polls Stay Halted

November 6, 2026

The Election Commission of Pakistan has set February 7, 2027 for the first phase of Punjab's local government elections, The News reported on November 6, a day after the commission suspended all preparations for the local government polls in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa.

Punjab moves ahead, KP stays on hold

The announcement gives Punjab a firm date for the long-delayed local polls, with the first phase scheduled for early February next year. In Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, by contrast, the commission's suspension of preparations remains in force, meaning no polling schedule has been set for the province. The contrast between the two provinces underlines how uneven the road back to local government has become, with Punjab's electoral machinery now moving towards a date while Khyber Pakhtunkhwa's remains frozen.

By-polls first, on November 8

Before the local polls, Punjab faces by-elections on November 8 in two constituencies that fell vacant after the deaths of their sitting PML-N lawmakers: National Assembly seat NA-77 (Gujranwala-I) and Punjab Assembly seat PP-203 (Sahiwal-VI), The Express Tribune reported. The Pakistan Army and Pakistan Rangers (Punjab) will be deployed from November 7 to 9 to assist in maintaining security during the by-polls, under an order issued by the Ministry of Interior and Narcotics Control on November 4. NA-77 fell vacant after the death of five-time MNA Chaudhry Mehmood Bashir Virk on September 11, while PP-203 fell vacant after the death of MPA Chaudhry Muhammad Hanif Jutt on September 17.

What to watch next

Attention now turns to the Election Commission's detailed schedule for Punjab, including the timetable for delimitation, nomination papers and polling staff deployment, and to whether the suspension in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa is lifted in time for the province to hold its own local polls. With the Punjab by-polls on November 8 serving as the immediate test of the electoral machinery, the coming weeks will show whether the commission can run the February local polls on schedule.

Sources: The News, November 6, 2026; The Express Tribune, November 6, 2026

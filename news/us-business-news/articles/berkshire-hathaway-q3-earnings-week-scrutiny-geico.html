Berkshire Hathaway Enters Earnings Week Under Scrutiny as GEICO Weakens

November 2, 2026

Berkshire Hathaway enters its third-quarter 2026 earnings week with analysts expecting revenue growth but a lower per-share result than a year earlier, according to previews published on November 2.

What analysts expect

A Yahoo Finance preview cited by reports put the Zacks consensus at $97.3 billion in revenue, up 2.5 percent year over year, and $5.91 per Class B share, down 5.6 percent. The consensus had moved 4.4 percent higher over the previous 30 days, though Zacks' most accurate estimate of $5.87 sat below consensus, and its model did not conclusively signal a beat. Zacks noted Berkshire had beaten consensus in three of its previous four quarters, with an average surprise of 12.04 percent.

Insurance versus industrials

Insurance operations are expected to benefit from pricing, retention and higher average auto premiums, potentially offsetting softer GEICO results: GEICO's pre-tax underwriting earnings fell about 40 percent in the first half of 2026 as claims severity and expenses rose, and its combined ratio worsened to 91.2 percent from 83.5 percent. Offsetting that, manufacturing, service, retailing, BNSF and Berkshire Hathaway Energy generated roughly $6.9 billion after tax in the first half, nearly 20 percent higher year over year, with $21.7 billion of operating cash flow.

OpenAI Revenue Hit $10 Billion in Q3, but Losses Grew to $10.9 Billion, Report Says

November 11, 2026

OpenAI's revenue hit $10 billion in the third quarter, with adjusted operating losses increasing to $10.9 billion, according to a Wall Street Journal report cited by Investopedia on November 11.

Growth — at a steep price

The figures, reported as a second likely AI mega-IPO takes shape this week, show revenue growing 50 percent over the past quarter even as the cost of building and running its models keeps climbing. OpenAI's spending on computing infrastructure, talent and data centres has made it one of the biggest spenders in the AI race, and investors are watching closely whether the company can turn its scale into sustainable profits. The report lands as rival Anthropic prepares its own listing, with a roadshow under way ahead of a planned November 18 IPO at a valuation of about $1.4 trillion.

The AI money question

The numbers sharpen the central question hanging over the AI boom: how long can the biggest labs keep burning cash to stay ahead? OpenAI has been raising money at a rapid pace and signing massive infrastructure deals, while its products — led by ChatGPT — now reach hundreds of millions of users. For now, investors appear willing to fund the losses in exchange for growth, but the widening gap between revenue and spending is becoming harder to ignore as markets grow more selective about AI valuations.

Sources: Investopedia / Wall Street Journal, November 11, 2026

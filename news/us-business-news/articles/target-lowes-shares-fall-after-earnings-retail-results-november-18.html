Target and Lowe's Shares Fall After Earnings as Retail Results Test the Consumer

November 18, 2026

Shares of Target and Lowe's slipped on Wednesday after the big-box retailers published quarterly results, Investopedia reported on November 18, in a busy week for retail earnings.

A mixed retail read

Lowe's shares were down about 4 percent in premarket trading and Target about 2 percent, according to market data compiled ahead of the open. Both companies reported before the bell on Wednesday, with investors weighing what the results say about consumer spending heading into the holiday season. TJX, the parent of TJ Maxx, also reported on Wednesday morning, with its shares trading higher before the open.

Walmart still to come

The big retail report of the week arrives on Thursday, when Walmart reports before the open. Options data suggests Walmart's stock could move as much as 6 percent by the end of the week, Investopedia reported. Palo Alto Networks reports after the close on Wednesday. The results land as the market looks for fresh signals on the consumer after Home Depot and Disney both beat expectations on Tuesday.

Anthropic Sets $70-$78 IPO Price Range Ahead of Nasdaq Debut on November 19

November 14, 2026

Anthropic has set a price range of $70 to $78 per share for 825 million Class A shares ahead of an expected Nasdaq debut on November 19, 2026, under the ticker ANT, according to the company's amended S-1 filing of November 10.

One of the largest listings ever

The range implies a fully diluted valuation of about $1.33 trillion at the midpoint and about $1.4 trillion at the top, according to reports, compared with $965 billion in May 2026. The public offering would raise up to $64.4 billion, and a separate $26 billion private placement lifts the total to as much as $90.4 billion. Pricing is planned for November 18, with first trades on November 19, according to CNBC and Bloomberg, citing people familiar with the matter.

Revenue surge behind the valuation

Anthropic reported revenue of $33.24 billion during the first nine months of 2026, compared with $2.55 billion in the same period of 2025, and its annualised revenue run rate increased to around $81 billion by October 31. Google, Nvidia, AMD, Microsoft and other technology companies have agreed to purchase $26 billion of Anthropic shares through the private placement linked to the IPO.

Sources: Phillip Nova, November 12, 2026; Equiti, November 12, 2026

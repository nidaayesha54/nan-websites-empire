Berkshire Hathaway Operating Earnings Fall 5.8% as Buybacks Hit $9.6 Billion

November 8, 2026

Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway reported a 5.8 percent year-over-year decline in operating earnings to $12.7 billion for the third quarter, while simultaneously spending $9.6 billion on share repurchases, the Associated Press reported on November 7.

Buying its own stock while earnings slip

The combination tells its own story: even as operating earnings fell, Berkshire chose to return capital aggressively through buybacks, spending $9.6 billion in the quarter. The results arrived in a week when the midterm election outcome and a sharp rotation out of technology stocks dominated markets, and when Wall Street strategists were split over whether the post-election rout was a buying opportunity or the start of something deeper.

What to watch next

Berkshire's quarterly report is one of the most closely watched in American business, both for what it says about Buffett's conglomerate — spanning insurance, railroads, energy and consumer brands — and for what its cash deployment signals about the market. With the company buying back stock at this pace while operating earnings decline, investors will be watching the next quarter for whether the earnings slide was a one-off or the start of a softer stretch for Berkshire's operating businesses.

Sources: Associated Press, November 7, 2026

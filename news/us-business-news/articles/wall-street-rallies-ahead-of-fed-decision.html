Wall Street Rallies for Second Day as AI Stocks Lead Ahead of Fed Decision

October 28, 2026

US stock markets closed higher on Tuesday, buoyed by a rejuvenated artificial intelligence trade, strong economic data and declining crude oil prices. All three major indexes ended in positive territory for a second straight session.

The numbers

The Dow Jones Industrial Average rose 16 points to close at 52,377.57 after a choppy session that saw it down nearly 130 points at its intraday low. The S&P 500 gained 0.6% to finish at 7,722.60, while the tech-heavy Nasdaq Composite jumped 1.18%, or 308.43 points, to 26,477.81. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose 3.6% after Broadcom's $63 billion financing deal tied to Anthropic, and the CBOE Volatility Index fell 4.7% to 17.52. A total of 16.1 billion shares changed hands, below the recent 20-session average.

"The buy-the-dip trade is taking place," said Briefing.com analyst Patrick O'Hare. "You have the market being led by the AI stocks and particularly the semiconductors and those larger hyperscaler names." Nvidia rose 0.91%, while AMD gained 5.45% and Broadcom 4.00%.

Not everything rallied: six of the 11 S&P sectors closed lower, a reminder that the advance was carried by a powerful technology pocket rather than broad participation. The next test arrives Wednesday, when the Federal Reserve announces its rate decision and Alphabet and Microsoft report earnings after the bell.

Sources: Zacks, Malay Mail/AFP, Newspim, October 28, 2026

Fed Decision Day: Markets Price a Hold as Warsh Faces First Big Test

October 28, 2026

The Federal Reserve announces its interest rate decision at 2:00 p.m. ET on Wednesday, with Chair Kevin Warsh's press conference set for 2:30 p.m. Markets are widely expecting a hold, pricing roughly a 90% chance that the target range stays at 3.75% to 4.00% after September's 25-basis-point hike, the first in three years.

What to watch

The statement and press conference will be read for signals about a possible December move. Associated Press reporting named Beth Hammack and Lorie Logan as possible dissenters favouring a hike, which would be a notable split if it materialises. The 10-year Treasury yield closed at 4.99%, down a basis point on the day, with the 2-year at 4.70%.

The decision lands in the middle of a crowded earnings day: Alphabet and Microsoft headline 46 S&P 500 companies reporting, and futures were mixed ahead of the open, with the Dow down 0.2% and the Nasdaq up 0.4% in early trading. For investors, the question is less whether the Fed holds, and more what Warsh says about what comes next.

Sources: Investopedia, Financial Adviser markets briefing, October 28, 2026

Stocks Look to Rebound as Nasdaq Futures Rise 1.8% Despite Fed Rate-Hike Fears

November 11, 2026

US stocks looked poised to recoup some of their recent losses on Wednesday morning, with futures tied to the Nasdaq 100 up 1.8 percent, the S&P 500 up 0.9 percent and the Dow 0.2 percent higher, Investopedia reported on November 11.

Tuesday's sell-off

All three indexes finished Tuesday down after an inflation reading strengthened expectations for an interest rate hike. The Dow fell 0.3 percent, or 157.86 points, to close at 52,231.89, the Nasdaq Composite declined 0.7 percent to 25,065.87 and the S&P 500 lost 0.4 percent to 7,520.93, according to Zacks. The consumer price index rose 0.4 percent in October, and Fed Governor Christopher Waller said a 50-basis-point hike should be considered in December if inflation stays hot. Markets are now pricing in a 25-basis-point December hike at about 87 to 90 percent, according to CME FedWatch.

Too soon to call the bottom

Analysts cautioned that it is too soon to say the worst is over. Tech and AI-related stocks sit about 10 percent below their recent peak after a plunge over the past few weeks, and the rebound in the Nasdaq-100 ETF has stalled just below its downtrend from late October, Reuters reported. \"You can't have a 5% down day and close on the lows and then think the lows are in,\" said Mark Newton, head of technical strategy at Fundstrat Global Advisors. Stock markets are open on Veterans Day, though bond markets are closed, and the 10-year Treasury yield touched 5.03 percent on Tuesday.

Sources: Investopedia, November 11, 2026; Zacks, November 11, 2026

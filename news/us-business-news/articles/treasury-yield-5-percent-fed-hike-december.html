Treasury Yield Tops 5% as Markets Price in a December Fed Rate Hike

November 12, 2026

The benchmark 10-year US Treasury yield was near 5.02 percent on November 12 after hitting 5.03 percent on Tuesday, Investopedia reported, as bond trading resumed after the Veterans Day closure.

A December hike now expected

Investors now price in a roughly 91 percent chance of a 25-basis-point Federal Reserve rate hike at the December 8-9 meeting, and a 9 percent chance of a 50-basis-point hike, according to CME Group's FedWatch tool. The shift follows a hot inflation reading that strengthened expectations the Fed will move next month.

Stocks under pressure

Nasdaq 100 futures were about 1.2 percent lower and S&P 500 futures down 0.6 percent in early Thursday trading, weighed down by Nvidia's slide. Cisco and Applied Materials report earnings after the close, with Cisco expected to post about $4.2 billion in profit on $18.1 billion in revenue and Applied Materials about $10.3 billion in revenue.

Sources: Investopedia, November 12, 2026

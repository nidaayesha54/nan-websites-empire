OpenAI Nears a $30 Billion Round at a $1.25 Trillion Valuation, Reports Say

October 26, 2026

OpenAI is reportedly close to securing another mega round of private funding rather than plowing ahead with an initial public offering. The AI lab is working on raising up to $30 billion at a $1.25 trillion pre-money valuation in a round led by Coatue and investors in the United Arab Emirates, sources told CNBC, as reported by TechCrunch on October 22, 2026. Terms are still being negotiated, both outlets report, so they could ultimately change — and OpenAI declined to comment.

The figure is striking even by OpenAI's standards, and it is already lower than an earlier report. Bloomberg reported a month earlier that the lab was working on raising $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation. The Business Times, citing people familiar with the matter, reported on October 23 that the value discussed had shifted down by $150 billion to $1.25 trillion, with investor commitments expected to be finalized by the end of the following week. Coatue Management is in talks to co-lead, and OpenAI has also been in talks with multiple UAE investment funds, among other firms including BlackRock, Bloomberg previously reported.

Why the UAE piece matters

The UAE funding could also be coupled with a deal to secure more computing capacity for OpenAI from the UAE, CNBC reported — a detail that matters because so much of the company's spending goes toward chips and data centers. OpenAI's last completed round, in March 2026, brought in $122 billion in committed capital at an $852 billion post-money valuation, and the company filed confidential paperwork for an IPO in June. A completed round at the newly reported valuation would put OpenAI above the $1 trillion threshold and potentially establish it as the most valuable private company ever.

The timing is notable. Technology stocks fell in the same week after mixed quarterly earnings from Meta and Tesla fueled investor concerns that massive AI spending has gone too far, and rival Anthropic is reportedly preparing its own public debut — with CNBC reporting it is seeking a $1.5 trillion to $2 trillion valuation. OpenAI has pushed back its IPO plans until 2027, citing its focus on AI safety efforts, according to Bloomberg.

What to watch

Until commitments are signed, the $1.25 trillion figure remains a reported target, not a done deal. If it closes, it resets expectations for OpenAI's eventual public listing and signals that, even in a jittery market for AI stocks, the largest private investors are still willing to write the largest checks. If terms slip again, it will be read just as closely — as a measure of how much appetite remains for AI at any price.

Sources: TechCrunch, CNBC via TechCrunch, Business Times, Bloomberg via Business Times

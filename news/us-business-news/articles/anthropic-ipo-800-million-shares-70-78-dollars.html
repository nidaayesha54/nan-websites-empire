Anthropic IPO Moves Forward With Plan to Sell Over 800 Million Shares at $70 to $78

November 10, 2026

Claude maker Anthropic's planned IPO is chugging ahead. The company said in a regulatory filing that it planned to sell more than 800 million shares — with shareholders selling another 25 million — and establish an expected range of $70 to $78 apiece, Investopedia reported on November 10.

One of the biggest AI listings yet

The filing puts firm numbers on an offering that has been one of the most anticipated in the AI industry. Anthropic, which builds the Claude family of AI models, is reportedly preparing to start marketing its shares ahead of a possible listing, with prospective buyers discussing valuations of $1.1 to $1.3 trillion, well below the $2 trillion floated in early October, according to Bloomberg reports relayed by AI World Wire. The IPO comes in the same week Anthropic reportedly struck a $3.4 billion deal to secure gas-powered generators to power its AI data centres, underlining how electricity, not just chips, now limits AI expansion.

A crowded week for AI money

Investors were also tracking Nvidia and CoreWeave shares on Tuesday morning as the AI trade continued to digest the fallout from the investigation into Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang. The Anthropic filing lands as markets weigh a pivotal inflation report and the Federal Reserve's December decision, with the company's listing expected to be a key test of investor appetite for AI stocks after last week's steep sell-off.

Sources: Investopedia, November 10, 2026

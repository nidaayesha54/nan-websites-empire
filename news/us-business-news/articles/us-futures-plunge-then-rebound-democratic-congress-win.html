US Futures Plunge, Then Rebound, as Democrats Sweep Congress

November 4, 2026

US stock futures lurched lower, then recovered, on Wednesday after Democrats won control of both chambers of Congress, with the early sell-off led by AI and technology stocks and the rebound helped by President Donald Trump saying he would accept the results, reports said.

The overnight drop

Nasdaq-100 futures fell as much as 2.4 percent and S&P 500 futures as much as 1.4 percent while counting was under way, as investors weighed the prospect of tighter rules on data centres and AI, possibly higher taxes on companies, and fights over the budget and the debt ceiling. The dollar weakened to a more than one-month low, while the 10-year Treasury yield eased to about 4.9 percent after topping 5.3 percent a day earlier, a 24-year high.

The rebound

By Wednesday morning the losses had largely been erased: Dow futures were up about 789 points, or 1.5 percent, S&P 500 futures up about 1.1 percent and Nasdaq futures up about 1 percent in later readings, helped by AMD and Arista earnings and by Trump's conciliatory comments. Analysts urged caution, noting that futures can reverse quickly and that gridlock — a Democratic Congress facing a Republican president with veto power — remains the most likely policy outcome. Investors now await Friday's October jobs report and next week's inflation data.

Sources: Reuters via Gulf News, Economic Times, Invezz, Eastern Herald, November 4, 2026

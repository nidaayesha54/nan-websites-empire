Pfizer Raises 2026 Forecast After Third-Quarter Beat Led by Eliquis and Cancer Drugs

November 3, 2026

Pfizer raised its 2026 forecast on Tuesday, November 3, after third-quarter results beat Wall Street expectations, as growing demand for newer medicines helped offset declining sales of its Covid-19 products, Bloomberg and Dow Jones reported.

The quarter in numbers

Third-quarter revenue was $16.58 billion, little changed from $16.65 billion a year earlier but above the roughly $15.87 billion analysts expected. Adjusted earnings were $0.87 per share, ahead of the $0.73 to $0.74 analysts expected, while reported net income fell 8 percent to $3.24 billion. Sales of blood thinner Eliquis rose 25 percent to $2.52 billion, and the company pointed to strong demand for recently acquired cancer drug Padcev. Revenue excluding Comirnaty and Paxlovid grew 7 percent on an operational basis.

The raised outlook — and a new CFO

Pfizer now expects full-year revenue of $62 billion to $64 billion, up from $60.5 billion to $62.5 billion, and adjusted earnings of $2.95 to $3.05 per share, up from $2.80 to $3.00. The outlook includes a $650 million licensing-related acquired research charge, worth about $0.10 per share. Separately, Pfizer named Cecile Guegan as permanent chief financial officer, effective November 1, after she served as interim CFO since August.

Sources: Bloomberg via The Edge Singapore, Dow Jones via Morningstar, Reuters via Devdiscourse, November 3, 2026

Wall Street Futures Rebound After a Brutal October for Tech Stocks

November 2, 2026

US stock futures started the week higher on Monday after Friday's brutal selloff, with Dow futures up more than 165 points and S&P 500 futures up about 30 points, CNBC TV18 reported via TradingView on November 2.

October's damage

The tech-heavy Nasdaq lost almost 7 percent in October, its worst month since the April 2025 tariff rout, as worries about negative cash flow and the sheer scale of AI capital spending pushed up the cost of insuring corporate defaults. Global markets steadied in Asia on Monday as investors hoped Apple earnings later in the day would offer relief from AI cash-burn worries, while oil prices eased after talks continued between Iranian and Qatari mediators.

A packed week ahead

Beyond Apple and Palantir on Monday night, the week brings AMD on Tuesday night, Qualcomm, Arm and Uber on Wednesday, and the October jobs report at 8:30 a.m. ET on Friday, November 6 — alongside the midterm elections on Tuesday, November 3, which polls suggest could deliver a Democratic sweep of Congress. S&P 500 futures edged up 0.4 percent and Nasdaq futures bounced 0.8 percent in early trading, according to Business Recorder's market roundup.

Sources: CNBC TV18 via TradingView, Business Recorder, JorgAI, November 2, 2026

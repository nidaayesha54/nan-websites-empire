Wall Street Mixed on Election Day as Nasdaq Sits in Correction and Oil Swings

November 3, 2026

US stock futures traded mixed ahead of Tuesday's market open as investors weighed corporate earnings and awaited the midterm elections, with Apple and Palantir shares sliding despite better-than-expected results, Reuters and TipRanks reported on November 3.

A correction in tech

The Nasdaq-100 closed more than 10 percent below its all-time closing high on Monday, confirming that the tech-heavy index has entered a correction, as investors grow wary of Big Tech's soaring AI spending and little evidence of adequate returns. On Monday the Nasdaq fell 1.07 percent and the S&P 500 declined 0.51 percent, while the Dow gained 0.41 percent. On Tuesday morning, Nasdaq 100 futures were up about 0.3 to 0.7 percent, S&P 500 futures were roughly flat to slightly lower, and Dow futures were down about 0.3 to 0.9 percent in early readings that moved through the morning.

Oil, earnings and the week ahead

Apple fell about 3.8 to 4.4 percent in premarket trading after warning memory-chip costs would pressure its holiday-quarter margin, and Palantir slid about 4.6 percent in early trading despite raising guidance. Oil swung through the session: Brent crude fell below $102 a barrel at one point on an unconfirmed report about a draft memorandum from Qatari mediators on Iran, then rose again after reports of a US strike on Iran's Qeshm Island, with Brent around $105.78 and WTI around $97.50. Investors also awaited September's JOLTS job-openings report and Friday's jobs report, plus AMD earnings after Tuesday's bell.

Sources: Reuters via Kitco and Business Times, Bloomberg via Swissinfo, TipRanks, November 3, 2026

Wall Street on Election Eve: Dow Slips, S&P and Nasdaq Gain Ahead of Results

November 4, 2026

Wall Street closed mixed on Tuesday, November 3, as investors positioned for the midterm election results and digested a heavy earnings slate, with the Dow slipping while the S&P 500 and Nasdaq gained, reports said.

The closing numbers

The Dow Jones Industrial Average fell 197.26 points, or 0.38 percent, to 51,786.72. The S&P 500 gained 27.52 points, or 0.36 percent, to 7,568.92, and the Nasdaq Composite rose 207.65 points, or 0.83 percent, to 25,372.01, according to the Wall Street Journal figures cited in coverage. The session came a day after the Nasdaq-100 closed more than 10 percent below its all-time closing high, confirming a correction in the tech-heavy index.

What drove the split

Apple and Palantir shares slid despite better-than-expected results, on margin and valuation worries, while AMD and Arista rallied after the bell on strong AI-linked results. After the close, futures weakened as projections showed Democrats winning both chambers, before recovering on Wednesday. The week ahead still holds the October jobs report on Friday and September's JOLTS job-openings data, which investors will watch for the economy's direction under a newly divided government.

Sources: Wall Street Journal figures via Eastern Herald, Reuters, November 4, 2026

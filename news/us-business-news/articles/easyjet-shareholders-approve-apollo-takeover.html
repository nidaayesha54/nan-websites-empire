easyJet Shareholders Approve £5.7 Billion Apollo Takeover

November 12, 2026

easyJet shareholders have approved Apollo's £5.7 billion ($7.6 billion) takeover of the airline, Reuters reported on November 11.

A decisive vote

The deal, at 715p ($9.50) per share through Apollo-backed Eagle Bidco, won the backing of 97.07 percent of scheme shares at the Court Meeting, while 83.29 percent of voting shareholders supported it. The general-meeting resolution passed with 96.67 percent in favour.

What remains

The takeover still needs court sanction and regulatory approvals. easyJet expects completion before the end of the first quarter of 2027, when it would leave the London Stock Exchange after more than 25 years as a listed company.

Sources: Reuters, November 11, 2026; Simple Flying, November 11, 2026

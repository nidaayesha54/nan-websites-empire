Exxon and Chevron Beat Profit Forecasts as a $6.3 Billion Binance Hack Hits Crypto Stocks

October 31, 2026

Oil supermajors Exxon Mobil and Chevron both beat quarterly profit expectations, benefiting from soaring profit margins as energy supply disruptions arising from the US-Israeli war with Iran and Russia's war in Ukraine lifted refining economics, Reuters reported on October 31.

Energy leads a down day

Energy was one of the few bright spots on Friday's down session, with six of the 11 S&P 500 sectors rising, led by Energy at 1.91%. The strength contrasted with the broader market, where the S&P 500 fell 1.82% and the Nasdaq 2.86%. Oil prices rose about 2% on the day, adding to concern about the health of a US economy that many investors see as dependent on the AI buildout for its momentum.

Crypto stocks slide after the Binance hack

Cryptocurrency-related firms fell after reports that hackers stole $6.3 billion from Binance, the world's biggest cryptocurrency exchange. Coinbase, Strategy and Robinhood dropped between 7.2% and 10.6%, and Bitcoin fell about 3%. The episode is a reminder of how quickly exchange security news can spill into listed crypto stocks, even on a day when the dominant market story is AI spending rather than digital assets.

Sources: Reuters via Business Times, Bilyonaryo, October 31, 2026

Trade Deficit Widens 16% to $14.6 Billion as Imports Hit $26 Billion in July-October

November 7, 2026

Pakistan's trade deficit increased 16 percent to nearly $15 billion during the first four months of this fiscal year after imports hit $26 billion amid continued sluggish export growth, a gap that may keep Islamabad dependent on foreign creditors longer than planned, The Express Tribune reported on November 7.

The numbers

The Pakistan Bureau of Statistics (PBS) reported on Friday that the gap between imports and exports widened to $14.63 billion during July to October, $2 billion or 16 percent more than the same period last year, Dawn reported. In absolute terms, export proceeds stood at $11.34 billion in July-October 2026, up from $10.45 billion in the corresponding period last year, an increase of 8.52 percent. Imports, meanwhile, surged 12.59 percent to $25.96 billion, compared with $23.06 billion in the corresponding period last year. In October alone, export proceeds recorded a meagre growth of 2.84 percent to $2.93 billion.

Why exports are struggling

Export growth has slowed mainly in the Middle Eastern and European markets since the war in the Middle East, Dawn reported. Exports to Afghanistan have also remained suspended since October 2025, affecting one of Pakistan's major export markets. In FY26, Pakistan's merchandise exports not only missed the annual target by $4.87 billion but also contracted, falling 5.97 percent to $30.13 billion. The government has now projected goods exports of $34 billion this fiscal year, against $30.8 billion last year.

The financing question

The figures were released two days after Finance Minister Muhammad Aurangzeb disclosed that Pakistan had secured yet another rollover of $3 billion in debt from Saudi Arabia last month. Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif has promised that the 25th International Monetary Fund programme, ending next September, would be the last in the nation's history — a vow that will face its real test in the trade numbers in the months ahead. The rupee appreciated by one paisa to Rs276.66 to the dollar on Friday, according to the central bank.

Sources: Dawn, The Express Tribune, November 7, 2026

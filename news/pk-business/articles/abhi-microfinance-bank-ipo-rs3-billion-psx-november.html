ABHI Microfinance Bank Plans Up to Rs3 Billion PSX IPO, Book-Building on November 5-6

November 1, 2026

ABHI Microfinance Bank plans to raise between Rs2 billion and Rs3 billion through an initial public offering of 132 million ordinary shares on the Pakistan Stock Exchange, with a floor price of Rs15 per share, according to details disclosed to the exchange and reported by Business Recorder, ProPakistani and Profit on October 30 and 31.

How the offer is structured

The book-building portion comprises 99 million shares, or 75% of the total issue, for institutional and high-net-worth investors, while the remaining 33 million shares are reserved for the general public. The shares have a Rs10 par value, with the Rs15 floor price including a Rs5 premium; the price band allows bids to rise up to 50% above the floor, to a maximum of Rs22.50 per share. At the floor price the offering would raise about Rs1.98 billion, and at the ceiling about Rs2.97 billion. The public portion is fully underwritten by Arif Habib Limited, Topline Securities Limited, Growth Securities (Private) Limited and Dawood Equities Limited.

Dates, use of funds and ownership

Registration of eligible participants runs from November 2 to 6, closing at 3 pm on the final day, with expressions of interest to the National Clearing Company of Pakistan between November 2 and 6 and bidding from 9 am to 5 pm on November 5 and 6; retail subscription is scheduled for November 12 and 13. The bank said gross proceeds at the floor price would primarily be used to expand its loan portfolio and strengthen its capital base. Ownership disclosures show ABHI (Private) Limited holding a 73.73% stake and TPL Corp Limited the remaining 26.27%. Management estimates a profit of about Rs1.5 billion in the second half of 2026, taking the projected full-year profit to around Rs3 billion — a management forecast, not a reported result, and the final IPO price will be set by institutional demand during book-building, not by the floor price alone.

Sources: Business Recorder, ProPakistani, Profit by Pakistan Today, PSX disclosures, October 30 - November 1, 2026

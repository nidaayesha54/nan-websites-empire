Pakistan's Refineries Report 1,860% Profit Jump as War-Driven Margins Surge

November 1, 2026

Pakistan's major oil refineries reported a nearly twentyfold surge in combined profit for the latest quarter through September, benefiting from increasing refining margins amid global energy disruptions, Nikkei Asia reported on November 1, citing disclosures to the Pakistan Stock Exchange.

The numbers

All four listed refineries reported extraordinary increases in net profit, with their combined net profit exceeding Rs57 billion, compared with Rs2.9 billion in the corresponding quarter last year — a gain of more than 1,860%. The top earner was Attock Refinery, based in Rawalpindi, at Rs18.65 billion after tax, followed by Pakistan Refinery in Karachi at Rs13.97 billion, Cnergyico at Rs12.88 billion and National Refinery at Rs11.57 billion. Pakistan's fifth refinery, Pak-Arab Refinery, is not listed and has not released a financial statement.

A windfall expected to be short-lived

The gains come as fuel prices rise due to the ongoing Middle East conflict between the US and Iran, on top of the Ukraine war, creating a global fuel shortage that has hit Pakistani consumers hard: according to government fuel-price notifications as of October 20, petrol prices had risen 47.6% since the start of the war, while diesel prices had jumped 41.2%. Experts cited in the report said the unusually strong earnings look to be unsustainable, and unlikely to provide the companies sufficient funding for the costly modernisation of the country's ageing refineries, according to a research report by Sherman Securities, a Pakistani brokerage house.

Sources: Nikkei Asia, PSX disclosures, Sherman Securities, November 1, 2026

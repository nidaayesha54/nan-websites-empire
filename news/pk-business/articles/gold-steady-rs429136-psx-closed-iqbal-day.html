Gold Steady at Rs429,136 a Tola as PSX Stays Shut for Iqbal Day

November 9, 2026

Gold prices in Pakistan remained unchanged on Monday, November 9, 2026, following a sharp decline in the local market at the end of last week, The Neutral and Pakistan Observer reported.

The rates

According to the latest reported rates from the All-Pakistan Gems and Jewellers Sarafa Association, the price of 24-karat gold stands at Rs429,136 per tola, with 10 grams of 24-karat gold at Rs367,914. The market remained stable after gold prices dropped by Rs6,500 per tola on Friday, November 6. The price of silver per tola stood at Rs5,668. In the international market, gold was reported at around $4,066 per ounce. The figures reflect the latest reported association rates, and buyers should confirm the latest rate with their local jeweller before making a purchase.

A quiet Monday

Trading was quiet in Pakistan on Monday because the Pakistan Stock Exchange remained closed on November 9 on account of Allama Iqbal Day, with the State Bank of Pakistan also declaring a holiday, Mettis Global reported. The market resumes on Tuesday after a week in which the KSE-100 staged its strongest weekly rally in six months, gaining 7,477 points to close at 173,887.

Sources: The Neutral, November 9, 2026; Pakistan Observer, November 9, 2026; Mettis Global, November 5, 2026

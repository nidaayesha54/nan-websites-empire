KSE-100 Surges 5,449 Points to 179,335 as US-Iran Tensions Ease

November 10, 2026

Massive buying was observed at the Pakistan Stock Exchange on Tuesday after US President Donald Trump said US military action against Iran is not necessary \"at this point,\" with the benchmark KSE-100 gaining over 3%, its biggest single-day gain in recent weeks, Business Recorder reported on November 10.

A record-breaking session

The index opened higher and held its gains through the session to close near its intraday high of 179,424.04. At close, the benchmark index settled at 179,335.24, up 5,448.56 points or 3.13% from the previous close of 173,886.69. The day's low stood at 176,273.72. Total market capitalisation increased to Rs19.397 trillion from Rs18.909 trillion, while the ready market recorded a turnover of 972.432 million shares with a traded value of Rs50.67 billion. The market had been closed on Monday for Iqbal Day, and the rally extended last week's strongest weekly performance in nearly six months, when the KSE-100 surged 7,477.44 points, or 4.5%.

Oil falls, remittances rise

The rally came as international oil prices extended their decline on Tuesday, with Brent crude futures falling to $91.88 a barrel and US West Texas Intermediate to $86.07, easing worries about supply disruptions. Investor sentiment also drew support from strong remittance inflows: Pakistan received $3.89 billion in October 2026, up 8.5% from $3.59 billion in September and 13.8% from $3.42 billion in October 2025, according to the State Bank of Pakistan.

Sources: Business Recorder, November 10, 2026; UrduPoint, November 10, 2026

PSX Snaps Four-Day Rally as KSE-100 Falls 1,493 Points to 173,887 on Profit-Taking

November 6, 2026

The Pakistan Stock Exchange witnessed profit-taking on Friday as investors booked gains following a strong rally earlier in the week, pulling the benchmark KSE-100 Index down 1,493 points, or 0.85 percent, to close at 173,887, The Express Tribune reported on November 6.

A volatile session

The market opened with sharp fluctuations, initially declining towards the intraday low of 172,809.89 before recovering some of its losses, Business Recorder reported. At close, the KSE-100 settled at 173,886.68, shedding 1,493.18 points against the previous close of 175,379.86, after touching an intraday high of 174,369.19. In the regular market, 998.6 million shares changed hands, worth Rs46 billion, ARY News reported, with 567 companies traded. Of those, 277 declined, 190 advanced and 100 closed unchanged. UBL, LUCK, ENGROH, FFC and PPL were the biggest negative contributors to the index, according to Bloom Pakistan.

Why sellers stepped in

Reports attributed the pullback to rising global oil prices and renewed concerns over a possible military confrontation between the United States and Iran, which weighed on investor confidence, Bloom Pakistan reported. The Karachi Post added that concerns over potential regulatory restrictions on artificial intelligence stocks in the United States triggered a sell-off in global equity markets, dragging local shares lower. The local unit closed the day at 276.66 against the US dollar, a gain of Re0.01, Business Recorder reported.

A strong week despite the dip

Friday's decline ended a four-day winning streak that had carried the benchmark to a record close of 175,379.86 on Thursday, when it gained 3,366.38 points. The KSE-100 still ended the week on a positive note, gaining 7,477 points, or 4.49 percent, week-on-week to close at 173,887, The Express Tribune reported. Two sessions do not make a trend, and the sharp midday slide on Friday showed that profit-taking is never far away at these levels — but the breadth of the week's advance suggests the rally was built on more than a handful of heavyweights.

Sources: The Express Tribune, Business Recorder, ARY News, Bloom Pakistan, November 6, 2026

Pakistan Ranks 106th on 2026 Global Hunger Index as Report Questions Budget Priorities

November 4, 2026

Pakistan scored 25.5 on the 2026 Global Hunger Index, placing it 106th among 122 countries and worse than every functioning state in South Asia, according to a report cited by IANS on November 4 — figures presented here as reported, not independently verified by this site.

The reported figures

According to the report, published in the European Times news portal and cited by IANS, undernourishment affects 16.1 percent of the population, child stunting among under-fives stands at 33.6 percent, child wasting at 9.8 percent and under-five mortality at 5.6 percent. The federal budget for 2026-27 allocated PKR 3 trillion to defence services, up 17.6 percent, with the physical assets line for weapons procurement rising 39.6 percent to PKR 925.8 billion, while health received PKR 53.3 billion in total, of which PKR 24.3 billion covered the health and nutrition development portfolio.

The wider context

The report notes that the government's own Economic Survey records public health expenditure at 0.8 percent of gross domestic product, and that military pensions, carried separately at PKR 822 billion, exceed the full health allocation more than 15 times over. It also notes that Pakistan abolished the wheat support price mechanism under reforms tied to its IMF programme, then presided over procurement that collapsed to roughly 480,000 tonnes in Punjab against a three million tonnes target. These are the report's claims as relayed by IANS; official responses to the report had not been published at the time of writing.

Sources: IANS via Ten News, European Times, November 4, 2026

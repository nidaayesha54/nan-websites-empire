Fauji Fertilizer's Nine-Month Profit Hits Rs71 Billion on Strong Urea Demand

October 28, 2026

Fauji Fertilizer Company (FFC) has reported a strong nine-month performance, with consolidated profit climbing to Rs71.02 billion, nearly Rs8 billion higher than a year earlier, on the back of stronger fertilizer demand and investment income. Standalone nine-month profit reached approximately Rs64.16 billion.

The numbers

Consolidated revenue climbed to approximately Rs373.24 billion during the nine-month period, growth of nearly 15.7%. Gross profit increased around 14.9% to Rs124.64 billion, while operating profit advanced 13.9% to approximately Rs92.08 billion. Profit before taxation stood at Rs108.62 billion, with a tax expense of nearly Rs37.60 billion. Rising borrowing costs remained a concern, with finance expenses increasing about 37.1% to Rs7.60 billion.

Operational figures showed stronger demand for key products: urea offtake increased to approximately 2.16 million tonnes from 1.96 million tonnes a year earlier, and prilled urea production at Goth Machhi and Mirpur Mathelo rose around 2% to 1.91 million tonnes. Gas supply constraints, however, cut granular urea production by about 15% to 281,000 tonnes, and Sona DAP production fell to 586,000 tonnes from 622,000 amid raw material availability challenges and elevated phosphoric acid prices. On the stock exchange, FFC shares were trading near Rs549-550 on October 28, compared with about Rs537 in the previous session. The growth comes as the company continues to benefit from its expanded operations following the merger with Fauji Fertilizer Bin Qasim Limited.

Sources: Daily Pakistan, October 28, 2026

Gold Falls Rs6,500 a Tola to Rs429,136 as Rupee Holds at Rs276.66

November 8, 2026

Gold prices in Pakistan fell by Rs6,500 per tola to Rs429,136 on Sunday, UrduPoint reported on November 8. The drop tracked weaker international bullion prices as global markets digested the week's sharp moves in oil and equities.

Rupee steady, reserves up

The rupee closed at Rs276.66 against the US dollar in the interbank market on Friday, edging up one paisa, according to exchange-rate data cited by UrduPoint. In the open market, the US dollar was quoted at Rs277.50 for buying and Rs279.75 for selling, MM News reported on November 8. Pakistan's foreign exchange reserves rose by $110 million to $26.864 billion, UrduPoint reported, citing the Times of India, with State Bank of Pakistan-held reserves at $21.568 billion as of October 30. The stronger reserve position helped support the rupee and exporters' confidence amid regional tensions, the report said.

What it means for buyers

For households, the gold correction brings some relief after a year of record highs, though prices remain far above their levels of a year ago. The State Bank's policy rate remains at 12 per cent following its action on October 28. With the MSCI review due on November 11 and oil prices still volatile, currency and bullion traders will be watching global cues closely in the coming week.

Sources: UrduPoint, November 8, 2026; MM News, November 8, 2026

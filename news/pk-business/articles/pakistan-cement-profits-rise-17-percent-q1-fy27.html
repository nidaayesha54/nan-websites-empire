Pakistan Cement Makers' Profits Rise 17% to Rs34.2 Billion in First Quarter of FY27

October 31, 2026

Combined profitability of cement companies listed on the Pakistan Stock Exchange increased by 17% year on year to Rs34.2 billion during the first quarter of FY27, compared with Rs29.1 billion in the corresponding period last year, Profit by Pakistan Today reported on October 31, citing AKD Research's sector review.

Domestic demand leads

Total cement dispatches increased by 4% to 8.57 million tonnes from 8.27 million tonnes a year earlier. Domestic dispatches rose 10% to 7.1 million tonnes, increasing their share of total sales to 83% from 78%. Exports, however, declined 20% to 1.5 million tonnes, partly due to the closure of the Afghan border. Combined revenue increased 17% to Rs143.35 billion from Rs122.56 billion, while gross profit grew 25% to Rs50.32 billion, with gross margins improving to 35.1% from 32.9%.

A strong quarter, a weaker export story

Profit before tax increased 17% to Rs49.28 billion, while taxation rose by the same percentage to Rs15.11 billion. On a quarterly basis, revenue rose 15%, gross profit 10% and net profit 18%, while total dispatches rose 12% from 7.68 million tonnes in the preceding quarter. The picture is one of a domestic construction recovery carrying the sector, even as the export channel — hit by the Afghan border closure — continues to drag.

Sources: Profit by Pakistan Today, AKD Research, October 31, 2026

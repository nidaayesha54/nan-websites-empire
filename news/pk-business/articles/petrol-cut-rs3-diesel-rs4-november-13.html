Petrol Cut by Rs3 and Diesel by Rs4 a Litre From November 13, OGRA Notifies

November 13, 2026

The Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority has revised the ex-depot prices of petroleum products, reducing high-speed diesel by Rs4.00 a litre and petrol by Rs3.00 a litre, effective November 13, 2026, Mettis Global reported.

Relief continues at the pump

The cut extends the run of fuel-price reductions in recent weeks, following a Rs8 cut in petrol and Rs7 in diesel announced earlier in the month. Lower fuel prices ease transport costs for households and businesses and feed into the broader inflation picture.

Markets in the same week

The fuel relief came in a week when the rupee edged up 0.77 paisa against the US dollar to close at Rs276.61 on Thursday, while the benchmark KSE-100 index fell 1,710.04 points, or 0.95 percent, to 177,373.76 on Thursday amid reports in some media about the possible release of former prime minister Imran Khan on bail, which weighed on investor sentiment.

Sources: Mettis Global, November 13, 2026

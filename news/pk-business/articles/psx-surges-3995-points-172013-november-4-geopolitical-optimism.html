PSX Surges 3,995 Points to Close Above 172,000 on Geopolitical Optimism

November 4, 2026

The Pakistan Stock Exchange witnessed a strong bullish session on Wednesday, with the benchmark KSE-100 Index gaining 3,994.62 points, or 2.38 percent, to close at 172,013.48 points, Business Recorder, UrduPoint and ARY News reported on November 4.

The session

The index had closed at 168,018.87 points in the previous session, after Tuesday's 1,454.80-point gain, and traded between an intraday high of 172,287.00 and a low of 168,355.26 on Wednesday. Trading volume jumped to 841.6 million shares from Tuesday's 517.3 million, with the value of traded shares at Rs35.3 billion, and total market capitalisation rising to Rs18.777 trillion from Rs18.396 trillion. The market opened strongly, gaining 1,707.48 points to 169,726.34 by 10:04 a.m. and crossing 170,000 in the early minutes of trading.

What drove the rally

Reports attributed the rally to institutional buying and improving sentiment over geopolitical developments following the US midterm election results, alongside easing international crude oil prices — Brent crude fell to about $101.85 a barrel during the session. On Tuesday, easing oil prices and optimism over potential negotiations between the United States and Iran had already lifted sentiment. Whether the momentum holds will depend on those geopolitical threads and on Friday's US jobs report, which global markets are watching.

Sources: Business Recorder, UrduPoint, ARY News, Express Tribune, November 4, 2026

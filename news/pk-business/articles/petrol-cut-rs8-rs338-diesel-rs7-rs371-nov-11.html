Petrol Cut by Rs8 to Rs338 a Litre, Diesel by Rs7 in Sixth Straight Reduction

November 11, 2026

The federal government on Tuesday slashed the price of petrol by Rs8 per litre and that of high-speed diesel (HSD) by Rs7 per litre, with the revised rates taking effect on November 11, Dawn reported.

Petrol down nearly 11% since October 31

According to a notification issued by the Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA), petrol will now cost Rs338 per litre, down from Rs346, while HSD will cost Rs371 per litre, down from Rs378. Petrol has fallen by Rs39.91, or 10.6 per cent, since October 31, when it stood at Rs377.91, while diesel has fallen by Rs31.96, or 7.9 per cent, from Rs402.96 over the same period. The latest reduction follows an earlier cut on November 7, when petrol was reduced by Rs6.59 per litre and diesel by Rs4.25 per litre.

Daily pricing and taxes

The government has been revising petroleum prices daily since July 17, when it replaced the weekly pricing system in response to volatility in international oil markets. The reductions follow a sharp correction in international petroleum markets as tensions between the United States and Iran eased. The government continues to levy Rs114 per litre in taxes and duties on petrol and Rs100 per litre on diesel. Under an arrangement announced on November 2, oil refineries will contribute to the petroleum levy by bearing a cumulative impact of Rs15 billion over 30 days.

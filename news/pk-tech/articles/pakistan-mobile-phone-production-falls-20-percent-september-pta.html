Pakistan Mobile Phone Production Falls 20% in September, but Local Assembly Still Meets 96% of Demand

November 3, 2026

Local mobile phone manufacturing and assembly in Pakistan declined by 20 percent year on year in September 2026, despite a strong monthly recovery, according to the latest data released by the Pakistan Telecommunication Authority (PTA), PKRevenue reported on November 3.

The September numbers

Local companies manufactured and assembled 2.42 million units in September 2026, compared with the corresponding period last year, but production increased by 46 percent month on month, indicating an improvement over August. Cumulatively, domestic production declined by 7 percent year on year during the first nine months of 2026, with 21.08 million units produced in January-September 2026 compared with 22.78 million in the same period of 2025. Mobile phone imports declined by 48 percent year on year in September but increased by 38 percent month on month to 110,000 units.

Local assembly still dominates supply

Local manufacturing and assembly met 96 percent of Pakistan's mobile phone demand in September 2026, compared with 95 percent in August. VGO Tel led the locally assembled market during the first nine months of 2026 with production of 2.50 million units, followed by Infinix with 1.96 million and Itel with 1.59 million, PKRevenue reported.

Sources: PKRevenue, PTA data, November 3, 2026

Pakistani Startup WonderTree Wins GSMA AI Innovation Fund From 625 Global Applicants

November 13, 2026

Pakistani healthtech startup WonderTree has won a grant from the GSMA Innovation Fund for Impactful AI, making it one of eight global grantees selected from 625 applications across 40 countries, TechJuice reported on November 13.

AI therapy through play

WonderTree tackles a critical gap in Pakistan's healthcare infrastructure: over 1.5 million children with special needs lack access to specialised education and therapy. The startup uses artificial intelligence and augmented reality to turn physical therapy and special education exercises into motion-based games.

What the grant brings

The grants offer up to £250,000 alongside 18 months of tailored venture-building support, funded by the UK Foreign, Commonwealth and Development Office through the GSMA. With the funding, WonderTree plans to launch a mobile application to make its platform more accessible and affordable.

Sources: TechJuice, November 13, 2026; NewzTodays, November 13, 2026

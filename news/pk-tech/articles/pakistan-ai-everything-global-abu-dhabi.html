Pakistan Showcases AI Innovations at Abu Dhabi's AI Everything Global 2026

October 26, 2026

Pakistan is showcasing its growing capabilities in artificial intelligence and emerging technologies at AI Everything Global 2026, one of the region's leading public-private gatherings on AI, bringing together more than 500 exhibitors and 150 investors from 70 countries in Abu Dhabi. Pakistan has established a strong presence at the event, featuring five leading Pakistani technology companies presenting innovative AI-driven solutions and services.

What Pakistan put on show

The five companies are presenting solutions in areas including AI data intelligence, AI surveillance, AI sales solutions and sovereign AI. The pavilion has been established under the Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB), with Pakistan's participation facilitated by the Commercial Section of the Pakistan Diplomatic Mission in the UAE, led by Trade and Investment Counsellor Ali Zeb Khan, in coordination with PSEB and the Ministry of Information Technology and Telecommunication. Sovereign AI — systems designed to keep data and control within a defined ownership boundary — is a growing priority for Gulf governments, and its presence on Pakistan's stand signals an ambition to sell into government and enterprise buyers, not just consumer apps.

An ambassador's pitch for talent

The Pakistan pavilion was inaugurated by Shafqat Ali Khan, Pakistan's envoy to the UAE. The ambassador interacted with participating Pakistani companies and highlighted the importance of enabling Pakistan's technology sector to play a proactive role in the rapidly evolving global AI landscape. Ambassador Khan underscored the need to invest in AI talent development, particularly by equipping the younger generation with advanced AI, coding and digital skills. He emphasised that building a future-ready workforce would be critical to enhancing Pakistan's competitiveness and enabling its technology companies to capitalise on emerging opportunities in the global digital economy.

The trade numbers behind the pavilion

Pakistan's IT exports to the UAE rose 42% to $536 million in the 2025-26 financial year from $376 million in 2024-25, according to Pakistani officials. Pakistan is seeking to further expand its technology exports and promote "Made in Pakistan" and "Developed in Pakistan" products in international markets. The two-day exhibition, held in Abu Dhabi on October 6 and 7, brought together companies, startups, investors and technology experts to showcase AI and its applications across different industries. For the five firms on the pavilion, the real measure of the trip will be the partnerships and deals that follow — but the export trend they are riding is already moving in the right direction. For the five firms on the pavilion, the value of the trip lay not only in the exhibition floor but in the investor meetings and partnership conversations that a gathering of more than 500 exhibitors and 150 investors makes possible.

Sources: The Express Tribune, October 7, 2026; Geo News; ARY News.

Pakistan's First Biomass Energy Plant Becomes Operational in Sajawal, Sindh

November 3, 2026

Pakistan's first biomass energy demonstration plant has become operational in Sajawal, Sindh, to support rural livelihoods, reduce greenhouse gas emissions and ease pressure on forest resources, Dawn reported on November 3.

What the plant is

Located in Pinnah Reserved Forest, the plant was set up under the Global Environment Facility-funded Promotion and Application of Sustainable Biomass Energy Technologies in Pakistan (PASBET) project, implemented by the United Nations Development Programme (UNDP) in partnership with the Ministry of Climate Change and Environmental Coordination. UNDP and the Sindh government jointly inaugurated the plant, the first of four demonstration sites planned under the initiative to showcase scalable woody biomass-based energy technologies.

A five-year, $3.5 million programme

PASBET is a five-year, $3.5 million project that will promote sustainable biomass energy technologies across Sindh, Punjab, Balochistan and Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. The reports checked did not give the plant's generation capacity or the number of households it will serve, so those figures have not been stated here.

Sources: Dawn, November 3, 2026

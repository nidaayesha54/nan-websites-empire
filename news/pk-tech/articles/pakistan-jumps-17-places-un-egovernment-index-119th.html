Pakistan Jumps 17 Places in UN E-Government Index to 119th, Its Best Rank Ever

November 23, 2026

Pakistan has achieved a major improvement in the United Nations E-Government Development Index (EGDI) 2026, jumping 17 places from 136th to 119th — its best rank ever, surpassing the previous best of 122nd in 2004, according to the National Information Technology Board (NITB).

Online services lead the climb

The EGDI is the UN's report card on how well governments serve people through technology, scored every two years on three equal parts: online services, telecom connectivity, and human capital. Pakistan's score is up 23 percent since 2024, and online services is where the country shines, with a score of 0.84 against an overall 0.63 — the third-biggest lead of any country in the survey. The improvement is credited to the NITB delivering more than 200 services in two years, including e-Office, Pak App, the Hajj Portal, Pakistan Asaan Khidmat Centres and Apostille services.

Millions served digitally

In the last 18 months, the board says it has collected Rs42.3 billion in digital tax, served 4 million citizens digitally, and brought 47,340 e-Office users across more than 700 sites, with 150 digital citizen services now available. The NITB has also introduced 'PARVEEN', an AI citizen assistant that speaks Urdu, English, Punjabi, Sindhi and Pashto, guides users to services and prints queue tokens. The stated goal is an even higher ranking in the 2028 survey, under Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif and Federal IT Minister Shaza Fatima Khawaja.

Sources: National Information Technology Board, November 22, 2026

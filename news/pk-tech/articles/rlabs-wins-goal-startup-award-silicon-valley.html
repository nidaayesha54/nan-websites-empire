Pakistani Startup RLabs Wins Top Award at Silicon Valley November Summit

November 8, 2026

RLabs, a Pakistani startup building extended-reality simulators and AI training tools, has won the GOAL Startup Award at the Silicon Valley November Summit, putting Pakistan's growing startup scene on one of tech's biggest stages. The award was announced at the summit in California, which ran from November 2 to 5 and was organised by Plug and Play, the global innovation platform and venture capital firm, Dawn reported on November 7.

One of 18 startups chosen to pitch

RLabs was one of just 18 startups from around the world selected to pitch under Plug and Play's Global Overseas Acceleration and Learning (GOAL) programme. The summit brought together more than 3,000 attendees, including founders, venture capitalists, corporate executives, innovation leaders and government partners, and centred on artificial intelligence and deep tech, with live demonstrations and pitches from more than 300 startups across over 20 industry programmes. RLabs is led by Harris Ahmed, a 2018 graduate of the National University of Sciences and Technology (NUST) in Islamabad, and is incubated at the National Incubation Centre for Aerospace Technologies (NICAT). Ahmed noted that RLabs had been training a proprietary large language model to power a modular, cross-platform simulation engine that cuts development time by 75 per cent.

A proud moment, and a busy season

Federal Minister for Information Technology and Telecommunication Shaza Fatima Khawaja congratulated the startup, calling the win a moment of immense pride for the ministry and its tech innovation arm, Ignite. The win comes at a busy moment for Pakistan's tech sector. On October 10, Prime Minister Shehbaz Sharif inaugurated the newly renamed Daanish IT Park, the country's first national technology facility, bringing incubation centres, innovation hubs and startups together under one roof. The World Bank is also expected to help establish a $50 million Fund of Funds to support the country's early-stage venture capital ecosystem, with approval expected in early 2027.

Sources: Dawn, November 7, 2026; PakTechStan, November 8, 2026

PAK-US Tech Connect 2026 Takes 16 Pakistani Tech Firms Across America

October 26, 2026

Through Pak-US Tech Connect 2026, Pakistan is bringing a cohort of top-tier companies known for strong security, clear governance, proven AI capabilities and measurable business impact to the United States. Led by the Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB), the multi-city roadshow included engagements in Silicon Valley on October 16, Dallas on October 19, Washington, D.C., on October 21, and Chicago on October 23, 2026.

Who went, and what they sell

The 16 participating companies are Systems Limited, 10Pearls, NETSOL Technologies, IBEX Global Solutions, Techlogix Pakistan, Arbisoft, Folio3 Software, CureMD Healthcare, Tkxel, Strategic Systems International (SSI), Emumba, Abacus, TMC, Nextbridge, Excellence Delivered (ExD), and Addo AI. The delegation comprises Pakistani technology leaders with expertise in artificial intelligence, enterprise software, cloud computing, fintech, cybersecurity, healthcare technology, data engineering and customer experience management. Alongside them, two emerging startups, Swich and Heyeve.ai, represent Pakistan's startup ecosystem. Swich is a fintech with an innovative Web3 merchant financial operating system, while Heyeve.ai focuses on immersive, AI-powered legal training.

Why the United States matters so much

The roadshow reflects Prime Minister Muhammad Shehbaz Sharif's vision of a deeper economic partnership with the United States by facilitating direct business-to-business engagement between Pakistani technology companies and CIOs, CTOs and other senior decision-makers at Fortune 1000 enterprises. The United States is an important destination for Pakistan's technology industry: PSEB officials have said the US accounts for more than half of Pakistan's annual technology exports, making it a significant market for software development and IT-enabled services. In Dallas, Pakistan's Ambassador to the United States, Rizwan Saeed Sheikh, told the gathering that close ties between Pakistan and the US in the technology sector are a necessity rather than a choice, noting that the two countries are among the world's five most populous nations and complement each other in IT and other fields.

What success looks like — and what it does not guarantee

The roadshow is intended to help Pakistani businesses establish direct relationships with prospective clients, demonstrate their capabilities and explore long-term commercial partnerships. In Chicago, the closing leg drew a large number of companies from across the American Midwest, with Illinois State Representative Justin Slaughter and State Senator Laura Murphy among the speakers. But participation in business meetings does not automatically translate into new contracts: as of the published reports, organisers had not disclosed the total value of any signed deals, confirmed investment commitments or additional export revenue generated by the initiative. The honest test of Tech Connect 2026 will come in the months ahead, in signed contracts and export figures — not in the applause at the roadshow itself.

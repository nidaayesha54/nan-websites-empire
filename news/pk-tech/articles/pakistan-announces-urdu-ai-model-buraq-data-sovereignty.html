Pakistan Announces Urdu AI Model 'Buraq' to Keep National Data at Home

October 27, 2026

Pakistan's IT minister has announced Buraq, a government-backed Urdu AI model, at a summit in Seoul on October 22. According to Federal Minister for Information Technology and Telecommunication Shaza Fatima Khawaja, the model will keep all Pakistani data inside the country, addressing concerns about data sovereignty as artificial intelligence tools spread.

Buraq will understand and generate text in Urdu and other regional languages. The decision to build the model from scratch follows a striking gap in global AI training data: Urdu makes up just 0.03 percent of global web content gathered by Common Crawl, which means most existing AI models struggle with the language, TechJuice reported.

Sovereign computing and $1 billion commitment

To support Buraq, Pakistan plans to set up sovereign computing power so that data processing stays within the country. The government has committed $1 billion in funding through 2030 for AI, regulatory changes and public-sector AI projects, including AI deployments in welfare, healthcare and tax systems. The country recently launched its first local AI cloud through a partnership between Telenor Pakistan and Data Vault Pakistan.

The minister also highlighted four digital foundations that Buraq will build on: NADRA's digital identity system, which serves over 100 million Pakistanis; the State Bank's Raast instant payment platform; and the national data exchange WASL, which handles secure data sharing between government agencies.

Human oversight at the centre

Khawaja stressed that AI should help people make decisions, not make decisions for them, and said the government will keep human oversight at the centre of all AI systems. Pakistan's National Data Governance Policy sets rules for storing, sharing and protecting government data. The minister also noted that Pakistan became the first country to sign the Digital and AI Compact at the Seoul summit, describing AI as the last layer, not the first, of digital development.

Sources: TechJuice, October 2026; The Express Tribune, October 21, 2026.

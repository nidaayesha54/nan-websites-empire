PTA Expands Network Quality Surveys to Lahore and Karachi After Twin Cities Checks

November 6, 2026

The Pakistan Telecommunication Authority is expanding its mobile network quality inspections to Lahore, Karachi, Peshawar and other major cities after completing a recent assessment of telecommunications services in Islamabad and Rawalpindi, ProPakistani reported on November 6.

What the twin cities survey found

The regulator launched a special field inspection campaign in the twin cities on Thursday, with PTA Chairman Hafeezur Rehman personally leading the exercise alongside authority members, directors general and senior officials, Dawn reported. Teams examined 4G coverage, voice quality, call connection success rates, call continuity and mobile data speeds along the capital's busiest roads. The PTA identified shortcomings in call quality and internet services in certain areas of Rawalpindi and Islamabad, and the chairman issued instructions to representatives of Jazz, Zong and Ufone on the spot to address the identified issues and improve service delivery, Bloom Pakistan reported. Telecom operators told the regulator that temporary disruptions were linked to ongoing network upgrades, as companies shift from 3G to 4G and upgrade several locations from 4G to 5G, installing new equipment and adjusting newly acquired spectrum.

What comes next

The authority plans to conduct similar field surveys in Lahore and Karachi in the coming days to assess network coverage, call quality, internet connectivity and overall service performance, with representatives from the Ministry of Information Technology and Telecommunications and mobile network operators taking part. Meanwhile, the PTA is preparing a detailed report on its recent inspections in Islamabad and Rawalpindi, covering network performance, service quality, connectivity issues and areas requiring improvement, before submitting it to the authority for consideration and further action. Following the auction of 5G spectrum, the regulator will also focus on the rollout of fifth-generation mobile services in major cities, alongside improvements to existing 4G networks.

The wider backdrop

The expansion follows a season of scrutiny for the regulator: Jazz emerged as the top performer in the PTA's live network quality survey in Islamabad and Rawalpindi on November 5, while the PTA chairman told a National Assembly committee earlier in the week that five show-cause notices had been issued to mobile operators over poor service in the last three months, though fines had not been collected because operators obtained court stay orders. Whether the Lahore and Karachi surveys produce the same mix of praise and shortcomings will be the next measure of how far the operators' upgrades have actually reached consumers.

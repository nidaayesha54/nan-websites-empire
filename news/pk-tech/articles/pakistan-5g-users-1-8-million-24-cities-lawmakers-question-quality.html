Pakistan's 5G User Base Reaches 1.8 Million Across 24 Cities — but Lawmakers Question Service Quality

November 4, 2026

Pakistan has around 1.8 million 5G users, with services now introduced in 24 cities, Federal Minister for Information Technology and Telecommunication Shaza Fatima Khawaja told the National Assembly Standing Committee on Information Technology and Telecom on Tuesday, CW Pakistan reported on November 4.

The growth figures

Commercial 5G services began in March 2026 after the spectrum auction on March 10. In August, officials had reported about 1.3 million users across 23 cities and more than 1,200 active sites, meaning roughly 500,000 users have been added in under three months, an increase of nearly 38 percent. The number of operational 5G sites has now reached 1,782, according to figures reported after the meeting. PTA Chairman Hafeez ur Rehman also told the committee that 96 million Pakistanis use YouTube, watching around 560 million videos a day, and that Pakistanis make one billion WhatsApp calls daily.

The quality questions

Committee members did not dispute the growth but questioned the quality of networks users already rely on, asking how Pakistan could move towards 5G when users in several areas still struggle with basic 3G and 4G connectivity. One member said mobile internet services had deteriorated since the introduction of 5G, and the committee chairman noted the uneven spread of infrastructure, pointing out that Sukkur, the third largest city in Sindh, has only two 5G sites. The minister acknowledged challenges including infrastructure theft and electricity-related problems affecting tower uptime.

Sources: CW Pakistan, Express Tribune, November 4, 2026

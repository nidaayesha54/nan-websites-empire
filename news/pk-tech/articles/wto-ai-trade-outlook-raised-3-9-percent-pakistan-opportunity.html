AI Demand Lifts Global Trade Outlook to 3.9% — and Opens a Door for Pakistan

November 2, 2026

Strong investment in artificial intelligence infrastructure is improving the global trade outlook, with rising demand for semiconductors, servers and other advanced technologies supporting international commerce and creating new opportunities for developing economies, Wealth Pakistan reported from Islamabad on November 2.

The WTO's upgraded forecast

The World Trade Organization has raised its 2026 global merchandise trade volume growth forecast to 3.9 percent from 1.9 percent, citing resilient supply chains and stronger-than-expected investment in AI-related infrastructure. According to the WTO's October Global Trade Outlook and Statistics, AI-enabling goods accounted for 47 percent of the increase in the value of global merchandise trade during the first half of 2026, while trade in these products expanded by 67 percent year over year. The WTO expects AI-related investment to remain an important driver of merchandise trade through 2027.

What it could mean for Pakistan

Muhammad Hafeez, founder and CEO of Nexa Robots Pvt Ltd, told Wealth Pakistan the demand could create opportunities beyond advanced chip manufacturing — in software development, industrial automation, systems integration and technical services. Kazim Ullah, software engineer and co-founder of Devspire Solutions, said the impact extends across sectors, with openings in AI-enabled software, cloud services, data management and technology-enabled business services — practical avenues, he said, for building domestic expertise and expanding technology exports.

Sources: Wealth Pakistan / Greenpost, WTO October Global Trade Outlook, November 2, 2026

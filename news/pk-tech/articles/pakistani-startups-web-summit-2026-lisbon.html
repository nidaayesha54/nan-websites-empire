Pakistani Startups Head to Web Summit 2026 in Lisbon as Delegation Takes the Global Stage

November 9, 2026

Pakistani founders are heading to Lisbon this November for Web Summit 2026, one of the world's largest annual technology gatherings, running from November 9 to 12 in Lisbon, Portugal, Techiefied reported on November 8.

Who is going

Two startup organisations, 92 Startups from Lahore and SouthLabs from Layyah, are co-leading a cohort of emerging tech ventures. The delegation features companies working across artificial intelligence, cloud software, e-commerce and sustainable agriculture, including SouthLabs, an AI incubator and agri-tech platform; Zemoworks, which builds intelligence tools into daily workflows; Encore Digital, a digital transformation consultancy; Texnity, an official WhatsApp Business API provider and Meta Business Partner; Vertical Guild, which builds custom AI products and autonomous software agents; Dilkibaat, an AI-powered behavioural health platform for South Asian communities; and Shopignitor, an AI e-commerce platform that builds online stores from a single text prompt.

A showcase at the embassy

The delegation will also feature an exclusive showcase at the Embassy of Pakistan in Lisbon. SouthLabs, based in Layyah in South Punjab, is an incubator dedicated to artificial intelligence and agri-tech startups outside the major cities, while 92 Startups is a Lahore-based ecosystem builder focused on incubation, mentorship, investor access and global market entry. The trip comes days after Pakistani AI startup RLabs won the GOAL Startup Award at the Silicon Valley November Summit, adding to a run of international recognition for the country's tech sector.

Sources: Techiefied, November 8, 2026

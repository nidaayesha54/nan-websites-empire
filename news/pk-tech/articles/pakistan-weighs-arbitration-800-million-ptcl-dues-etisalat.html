Pakistan Weighs Arbitration Over $800 Million in Unpaid PTCL Privatisation Dues

November 4, 2026

Pakistan is considering international arbitration against UAE-based Etisalat, now known as e&, to recover approximately $800 million in outstanding payments from the privatisation of Pakistan Telecommunication Company Limited (PTCL), along with the opportunity cost since 2008 and applicable damages, Dawn reported via Profit on November 4.

The 20-year dispute

The dispute dates back to 2005, when the government sold a 26 percent stake in PTCL, along with management control, to Etisalat International Pakistan for $2.59 billion. Of that total, $1.76 billion was received, while approximately $800 million remains unpaid. Etisalat tied the balance to the transfer of PTCL properties; of the 3,246 properties covered in the latest briefing, 32 remain untransferred, despite 99 percent having been transferred.

What happens next

The matter was discussed during a joint meeting of the Senate standing committees on information technology and privatisation on Tuesday, where officials said the Office of the Attorney General for Pakistan had been directed to implement the federal cabinet's decision under Rule 24 of the Rules of Business, 1973. In July 2026, a committee headed by the adviser to the prime minister on privatisation recommended considering international arbitration under the sale and purchase agreement. The IT secretary described the matter as sensitive, and the rest of the session was held behind closed doors, reports said.

Sources: Dawn via Profit by Pakistan Today, Thursday Times, November 4, 2026

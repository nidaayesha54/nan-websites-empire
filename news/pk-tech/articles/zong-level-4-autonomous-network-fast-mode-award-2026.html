Zong Wins International Award for Pakistan's First Level 4 Autonomous Network Platform

November 14, 2026

Zong, Pakistan's leading technology services enterprise and a wholly owned subsidiary of China Mobile, has won the Operational Excellence Leader award for Innovation at The Fast Mode Awards 2026 for its AI-driven Self-Intelligent RAN Optimization Platform, TechnologyPlus reported on November 14.

What Level 4 autonomy means

Developed in-house, the platform marks Pakistan's first deployment of Level 4 autonomous network operations on a live commercial network, representing a significant advance in intelligent network management. The system uses artificial intelligence to optimise the radio access network on its own, reducing the need for manual intervention in day-to-day network tuning.

Why it matters for users

Autonomous network optimisation aims to keep coverage and data speeds steadier as traffic shifts across the network, with faults spotted and corrected faster than manual teams could manage. For a market where mobile data is the main way most people get online, the technology points to how Pakistani operators plan to run their networks as data demand keeps growing.

Sources: TechnologyPlus, November 14, 2026

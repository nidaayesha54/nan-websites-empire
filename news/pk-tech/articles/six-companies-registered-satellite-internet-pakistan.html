Six Companies, Including Starlink, Registered for Satellite Internet in Pakistan

November 8, 2026

Pakistan's race to bring satellite internet services is getting momentum, with six companies including Elon Musk's Starlink officially registered in the country, Daily Pakistan reported on November 8. However, a major regulatory hurdle remains, as several operators await security clearances and mandatory approvals before launching commercial services.

Who is registered

New documents from the Securities and Exchange Commission of Pakistan (SECP) reveal that six companies have completed their corporate registration. According to the SECP's written response to a Senate standing committee, four companies were incorporated after the establishment of the Pakistan Space Activities Regulatory Board (PSARB). The registered companies are Aether One (SMC-Private) Limited, Flynautix (Private) Limited, Galaxyspace Pakistan (SMC-Private) Limited, Space P (Private) Limited, Starlink Internet Services (Private) Limited and Spacesail Pakistan (Private) Limited. The SECP confirmed that all six companies have fulfilled the corporate law requirements necessary for registration in Pakistan.

Clearances still pending

Despite completing corporate registration, three major satellite internet companies are still facing a critical obstacle. According to the SECP documents, security clearance for the foreign directors and shareholders of Starlink Internet Services Pakistan, Galaxyspace Pakistan and Spacesail Pakistan remains pending with the Ministry of Interior. These approvals are essential before the companies can advance through the remaining regulatory process. Space P (Private) Limited, a subsidiary of Pakistan's National Command Authority (NCA), is the only company among the six to have submitted the required PSARB No Objection Certificate (NOC) to the SECP. The SECP has clarified that registering a company is only the first step towards entering Pakistan's satellite internet market, and companies must also obtain the necessary regulatory authorisations before offering services to consumers.

Sources: Daily Pakistan, November 8, 2026; SECP

vivo Launches V80 Flagship in Pakistan With ZEISS Portrait System and 7,200mAh Battery

November 10, 2026

vivo has officially launched the V80 flagship smartphone in Pakistan, the company announced on Tuesday, pitching the device around its ZEISS portrait camera system and a large 7,200mAh battery, Daily Pakistan reported on November 10.

Camera and battery headline the launch

The V80 carries a ZEISS Portrait System aimed at professional-grade photography, alongside a 7,200mAh BlueVolt battery that vivo says supports long workdays and international travel, with 90W FlashCharge restoring power quickly. The launch campaign, titled \"Capture Your Rise,\" features vivo Pakistan brand ambassador Atif Aslam.

Availability and pre-orders

The vivo V80 will be officially available for purchase in Pakistan from November 17, 2026, through vivo's official online store, e-commerce stores including Daraz Mall, Alfa Mall and Digital Mall, and physical retailers such as the Experience Stores in Lahore, Hyderabad and Karachi. Customers who pre-order between November 11 and November 16 will receive exclusive launch offers, including a VIP card and earbuds.

Sources: Daily Pakistan, November 10, 2026

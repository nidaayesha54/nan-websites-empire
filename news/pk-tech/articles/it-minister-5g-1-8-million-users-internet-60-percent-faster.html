IT Minister Says Internet 60% Faster as 5G Reaches 1.8 Million Users

November 11, 2026

Pakistan’s internet is 60 per cent faster than it was two and a half years ago and 5G now has around 1.8 million subscribers, Federal Minister for Information Technology and Telecommunication Shaza Fatima Khawaja told the Margalla Dialogue on Tuesday, November 10, TechProlonged reported on November 11.

Spectrum and fibre expansion

The minister said an auction that offered 480 MHz of spectrum, which she called the biggest the country has held, took total spectrum on offer from 274 MHz to more than 750 MHz. Fibre-to-the-home coverage, measured through house passes, has increased from fewer than three million to more than six million. She described 5G as launched, and operators have begun selling hardware, with Jazz’s 5G device going on sale at its stores on November 2 for Rs18,000. The speed figures are the minister’s own claims, and she named no survey or measurement behind them.

AI and the Digital Nation vision

Much of the minister’s talk was about artificial intelligence. She cited a forecast of a $4.8 trillion AI economy by 2033 and said the prime minister had announced a $1 billion investment in AI, focused on infrastructure, skills, innovation and connectivity. She said the Digital Nation Pakistan Act established the Pakistan Digital Authority, which is developing a National Digital Master Plan, and that Pakistan is developing a National Data Governance Policy and piloting a National Data Exchange Layer, with the National AI Policy guided by keeping humans in the loop for decisions involving technology.

Sources: TechProlonged, November 11, 2026; TechJuice, November 11, 2026

'Avengers: Doomsday' and 'Dune: Part Three' Both Open in Pakistan on December 18

November 13, 2026

\'Avengers: Doomsday\' and \'Dune: Part Three\' both open in Pakistan on Friday, December 18, 2026, with Cinepax, Universal Cinemas and CUE Cinemas all listing the two films for that date, Pakera reported on November 13.

Two blockbusters, one Friday

Disney's own page gives \'Avengers: Doomsday\' a release date of December 18, 2026, and the \'Dune: Part Three\' site gives December 18 for North America, with some countries getting it two days early — though no Pakistani chain listed a December 16 show. Cinepax has set up four records for Doomsday: 2D English, 2D Urdu, 3D English and 3D Urdu, and it is expected to draw the bigger opening-weekend crowd as the follow-up to \'Avengers: Endgame\'.

What is still unknown

Running times have not been published by either studio and the chains disagree on Doomsday by 15 minutes; Pakistan's final ratings are not on any chain page yet, and no chain has published a ticket price for either film. No IMAX hall was found for either film in Pakistan, so fans should pick the largest screen available in their city.

Sources: Pakera, November 13, 2026

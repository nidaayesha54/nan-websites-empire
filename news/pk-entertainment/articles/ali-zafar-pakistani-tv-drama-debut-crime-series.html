Ali Zafar to Make His Pakistani TV Drama Debut in a New Crime Series

October 26, 2026

Pakistani singer and actor Ali Zafar is set to make his acting comeback with an upcoming crime series, marking a major new chapter in his career on screen. Known for his musical talent, distinctive style and versatile performances, Ali Zafar has enjoyed success in Bollywood as well as earned considerable appreciation in Pakistani cinema — and now, after years of fans urging him toward television, he is finally coming to the small screen on home turf.

A debut years in the making

According to reports, the big-budget crime series will be directed by Anas Ali and feature a star-studded cast, including Humaima Malik, Feroze Khan and Sonya Hussyn in key roles. The project is described as a high-budget, mafia-themed crime story. Fans have long awaited Ali Zafar's return to small-screen drama: he had previously agreed to be part of Lahu, a drama featuring Sajal Aly and Bilal Abbas Khan, before eventually stepping away from the project. His last television appearance was in the serial College Jeans, making this new series his first major small-screen lead in years — and, as several reports describe it, his debut in a Pakistani web or television series in the modern sense.

The ensemble around him

Humaima Malik joins him as a lead, returning to the screen after a period of selective television appearances. She broke through in Shoaib Mansoor's Bol and later played the fierce Daro in the record-breaking The Legend of Maula Jatt, before returning strongly in Jindo. Feroze Khan and Sonya Hussyn round out the key cast, both well known to drama audiences through memorable television performances across a wide variety of roles. Putting all four names together in a crime-themed story is a combination that few viewers saw coming, and it is precisely why the announcement has caught fire online.

What is still unknown

The makers have yet to announce the storyline, streaming platform or release date, and the series remains untitled in published reports. What is known is the genre, the director and the scale of ambition: a large-scale production aiming at high-quality, big-budget storytelling in local television. For Pakistani viewers, the project matters beyond one star's comeback — it reunites talented actors who have captivated audiences across music, film and television, and it tests whether a crime saga with this calibre of cast can raise the bar for the whole industry. Until the title and airdate land, fans will have to settle for the announcement itself — which, judging by the reaction, was more than enough.

World Culture Festival 2026 Opens in Karachi With 150 Countries Taking Part

October 30, 2026

The third edition of the World Culture Festival (WCF 2026), organised by the Arts Council of Pakistan, Karachi, aims to be all inclusive and pluralistic, council president Ahmed Shah said at a press conference on Thursday evening marking the soft launch of the event. The festival formally starts on Friday, October 30, with the first batch of international artists already arriving in Karachi.

Scale of this year's festival

This year, more than 80 movies, selected out of 6,000 entries from all over the globe, will be screened during the event. Among those who will put their talent on display are Grammy-winning musicians and Oscar-nominated artists. In 2025 the festival spanned 39 days with participation from 140 countries; this time the event will run for 25 days with 150 countries taking part.

Culture as a bridge

Shah said the world is faced with all kinds of challenges as conflicts between countries take place, and that the festival tries to bridge the world through the power of culture and art, because during the event artists from countries such as America, Russia, Palestine and Iran will be seen in action. Sindh Culture Minister Syed Zulfiqar Ali Shah also addressed the press conference, and the evening was to be followed by a qawwali performance by Hamza Akram.

What audiences can expect

With film screenings, music, theatre and performances from 150 countries packed into 25 days, the festival is positioning Karachi as a meeting point for global culture. The Arts Council's festival has grown into one of the city's flagship cultural events, drawing international performers alongside local talent.

Programme details and schedules are expected to be announced by the Arts Council as the festival gets under way.

Sources: Dawn, October 30, 2026.

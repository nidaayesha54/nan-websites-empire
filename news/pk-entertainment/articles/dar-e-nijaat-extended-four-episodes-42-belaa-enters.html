'Dar-e-Nijaat' Extended by Four Episodes to 42 as Popularity Keeps Growing

November 15, 2026

The growing popularity of Pakistani drama Dar-e-Nijaat has led its makers to extend the series, giving viewers more time to follow its unfolding story and characters, tob.news reported on November 15.

From 38 episodes to 42

Initially planned to conclude after 38 episodes, the drama will now run for 42. The makers may consider adding more episodes if its popularity continues, reports Pakistani entertainment outlet Reviewit.pk. Digital media presenter and drama reviewer Sonia Rao revealed the revised broadcast plan in her vlog. Written by Umera Ahmed and directed by Saqib Khan, Dar-e-Nijaat is produced by Three's Entertainment. The series airs on ARY Digital and is also available on the ARY Plus app.

A new character enters

The serial's biggest mystery is also about to unfold as a new character, Belaa, enters the story. Pakistani actress Durefishan Saleem has officially welcomed Sahar Hashmi as Belaa in the popular drama serial, leaving fans excited for the much-awaited new chapter. The serial has reportedly crossed 3.1 billion views across social media platforms, with its highest TRP reaching 17.8 and average digital views standing at 51 million, according to 24 News HD.

Sources: tob.news, November 15, 2026; 24 News HD, November 15, 2026

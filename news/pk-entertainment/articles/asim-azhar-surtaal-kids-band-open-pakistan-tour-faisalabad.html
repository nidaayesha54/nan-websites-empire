Asim Azhar Gives Lahore's Surtaal Kids Band a Major Stage as His Pakistan Tour Opens in Faisalabad

November 6, 2026

Singer Asim Azhar has invited Lahore-based Surtaal Kids Band to perform as the opening act for his upcoming Asim Ali Tour, allowing the young musicians to showcase their talent at concerts across Pakistan, The Express Tribune reported on November 4.

Three cities, three nights

The band will join Azhar at three stops on the Pakistan leg of the tour: Faisalabad on November 6, Lahore on November 7 and Islamabad on November 8. The performances will mark the children's first appearance on a major concert platform. "Surtaal Kids Band is coming to the Asim Ali Tour! 6th Nov | FAISALABAD | 7th | LAHORE | 8th Nov ko | ISLAMABAD with @asimazhar Bhai. See You There!" the academy wrote in its announcement.

A classroom band goes big

The young musicians come from Lahore's Surtaal Music Academy, where music educator Zia-ul-Haq provides structured music education to children, Minute Mirror reported. The invitation gives the academy a national stage for its students, and the upcoming concerts will give the young musicians another opportunity to reach larger audiences and gain experience performing alongside an established Pakistani singer. Aaj English TV reported that the children had already shown off their singing in a visit with Azhar, performing a version of his hit song 'Jo Tu Na Mila'.

What to watch

The Faisalabad opener on November 6 is the first test of the experiment: a children's band opening for one of Pakistan's biggest pop stars, in front of a full concert crowd. If the three nights land well, the Surtaal Kids Band will have gone from a Lahore music classroom to a national tour in a single week — and Azhar will have given the tour a story that goes beyond the headliner.

Sources: The Express Tribune, Aaj English TV, Minute Mirror, November 4-5, 2026

Faysal Quraishi and Hareem Farooq Decry Alleged 'Smear Campaign' Against 'Mango Jutt'

October 31, 2026

Actors Faysal Quraishi and Hareem Farooq have raised concerns over alleged paid negative publicity targeting their Punjabi-language film Mango Jutt, arguing that coordinated online narratives could hurt not only an individual production but also Pakistan's already struggling cinema industry, The Express Tribune reported on October 29.

Reviews before watching, actors allege

Quraishi addressed the issue in an Instagram video, questioning the practice of publishing negative reviews without watching a film and warning that paid propaganda could undermine the industry's efforts to bring audiences back to cinemas. He noted that many serious film critics wait two to three days, for Friday, Saturday and Sunday to pass, before writing a review. Farooq, who produced and stars in the film, shared screenshots on Instagram that appeared to raise questions about whether some negative posts about the film had been paid for.

Showtimes under pressure

In a separate Instagram post, Farooq highlighted reports that several cinemas had dropped the film, while others had reduced its showtimes or moved screenings away from prime-time slots. For a small industry fighting to rebuild cinema-going, the actors argue, the stakes of an online pile-on go beyond one film's box office — a point that has reignited debate about how films are reviewed and promoted in Pakistan.

Sources: Express Tribune, October 29, 2026

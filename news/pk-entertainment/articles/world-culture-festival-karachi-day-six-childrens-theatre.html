Children's Theatre Takes Centre Stage on Day Six of World Culture Festival Karachi

November 4, 2026

The sixth day of the 25-day 3rd World Culture Festival Karachi 2026, organised by the Arts Council of Pakistan Karachi, featured two children's theatre productions, music, film screenings, dance and the Urdu-English theatre play English Only, The Express Tribune reported on November 4.

The children's programme

The programme for children opened with Little Red Riding Hood, presented by Poland's Miniatura City Theatre. Norwegian theatre group Erler Teaterkompani presented the adventure-filled production Gangster Hunting with Daisy Detective. Under the film screening segment, Polish Featurette presented four short films from Poland: Blue Bird, Momo & Lulu, Dream Up the Title and The Town That Drove Away.

The evening show

At 7 p.m., the Urdu-English theatre production English Only, directed by Awab Bin Farhat, was staged. The festival, which runs through November 22 at the Arts Council of Pakistan, Karachi, brings together over 1,000 international artists from 150-plus countries alongside 500 Pakistani performers, with daily programming across theatre, music, dance and visual arts and public access via complimentary registration, earlier festival coverage reported.

Sources: Express Tribune, November 4, 2026

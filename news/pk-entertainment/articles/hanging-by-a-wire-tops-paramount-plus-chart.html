Pakistan's Oscar Entry 'Hanging by a Wire' Tops Paramount+ Chart in North America

November 8, 2026

Pakistan's Oscar entry Hanging by a Wire has debuted at No. 1 on Paramount+'s North American movie chart, Business Recorder reported on November 5. It is the first Pakistani film to top the streaming platform's chart in the region. The Urdu- and Pashto-language documentary reached the top spot on October 28 and 29.

The Battagram rescue

Mohammed Ali Naqvi wrote, directed and produced the film, and Bilal Sami co-wrote and produced it. The film tells the story of the dramatic 2023 rescue of eight passengers trapped in a cable car above a ravine in Battagram, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa. It is also the first documentary Pakistan has chosen as its entry in the Academy Awards' Best International Feature Film category. The film premiered in the World Cinema Documentary Competition at the 2026 Sundance Film Festival.

What's next

The film is set for release in Pakistan in early 2027, with dates yet to be announced, Business Recorder reported. Its chart-topping run in North America gives the documentary rare visibility ahead of the Oscars race, and marks a milestone for Pakistani non-fiction cinema on a major global platform.

Sources: Business Recorder, November 5, 2026

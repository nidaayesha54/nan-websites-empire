'Ghost School' Opens in Pakistani Cinemas After a Long Censor Battle in Sindh

October 31, 2026

Ghost School, the first feature from British-Pakistani filmmaker Seemab Gul, opened in Pakistani cinemas on Friday, October 30, 2026, after a journey home that took in the Toronto International Film Festival, more than a dozen festivals and a best director award in London — and a ban in the province where it was shot.

A film about a school, not a horror story

Despite the title and talk of a jinn, Ghost School is a drama about a 10-year-old girl trying to get her village school reopened, with only a touch of the supernatural. Gul financed the film herself and is paying for its distribution. It is a cinema release, not a drama serial, and no streaming deal has been announced; outside Pakistan it has been showing in German cinemas since September and continues to play at festivals.

The Sindh question

In August, the censor board in Sindh — the province where the film was shot and where its story is set — declared it unfit, but the Sindh High Court ordered the board on October 19 to take a fresh decision within 10 days. At the time of writing, no new Sindh certificate had been reported, while the federal censor board and Punjab's board both cleared the film without cuts. Screenings in Sindh therefore remain dependent on that fresh decision, even as the film opens in Punjab.

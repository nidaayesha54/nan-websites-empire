'Uswah' Finale Airs Tonight on Geo at 7 PM After a Week of Twists

November 23, 2026

Uswah spent the week walking toward its end, and the finale airs on Monday, November 23, 2026, at 7 PM on Geo Entertainment, according to a UrduPoint weekly roundup published on November 23.

A week of goodbyes and missing money

Episode 60 was the second-last episode. During the week, Uswah left Essa's house, Essa tracked her down through a jacket, five million rupees went missing from the company account, and Essa and Fariya held their henna. The build-up has left the finale with plenty to resolve: the missing money, the state of Uswah and Essa's relationship, and where the characters land once the last episode ends.

A crowded week on the small screen

The week also brought cliffhangers elsewhere: Nashtar ended Sunday with gunfire and no word on Dawood's safety, Bano's Episode 40 is due on Monday, November 23, at 9 PM, and Nashtar's Episode 36 follows on Friday, November 27, at 8 PM. Off screen, 7th Sky Entertainment's Marie Khan confirmed that Tere Bin 2 is going ahead and that the script is ready, though no shooting schedule has been announced. With Uswah closing tonight and Bayhiss taking over the 7 PM slot from Tuesday, Geo's evening schedule is turning over fast — and the finale's ratings will show whether the week's twists paid off.

Sources: UrduPoint, November 23, 2026

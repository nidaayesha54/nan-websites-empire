Sardaar Ji 3 Arrives on Chaupal OTT After Skipping Indian Theatres

November 10, 2026

Sardaar Ji 3, starring Diljit Dosanjh, Hania Aamir and Neeru Bajwa, has finally arrived on digital screens in India, with the film premiering on Chaupal OTT on November 9, 2026, BrandSynario reported on November 10.

Why Indian cinemas missed it

The Punjabi horror-comedy skipped an Indian theatrical release in June 2025 amid India-Pakistan tensions and objections surrounding its Pakistani cast. The Federation of Western India Cine Employees urged the Central Board of Film Certification to withhold certification, and the producers chose an overseas release rather than an Indian theatrical opening. The situation changed following the April 22, 2025, Pahalgam attack, which led to a sharp deterioration in relations between the two countries, although Diljit Dosanjh has said the film was shot in February 2025, before the political situation changed.

A franchise favourite returns

Directed by Amar Hundal, Sardaar Ji 3 is the third instalment in the popular Punjabi horror-comedy franchise, bringing back its supernatural humour with a new adventure involving a haunted location in the United Kingdom. The film also features Gulshan Grover alongside Pakistani performers including Nasir Chinyoti, Daniel Khawar and Saleem Albela. Its digital premiere has brought renewed attention to a project that became caught in the middle of India-Pakistan tensions in 2025.

Sources: BrandSynario, November 10, 2026

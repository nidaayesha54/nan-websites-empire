PlayFest 2026 Brings Bilal Saeed, Zoha Waseem and Afusic to PNCA Islamabad Today

November 7, 2026

PlayFest, a full-day family and lifestyle festival powered by Red Maple Education System, is scheduled for November 7, 2026 at the Pakistan National Council of the Arts (PNCA), Islamabad, NewsGuru reported on November 4.

What's on the programme

The announced entertainment lineup includes Bilal Saeed, Zoha Waseem and Afusic, with XIN as host. The programme also includes a night concert, the Biggest Bubble Show, a Kids DJ Rave Party, 50-plus stalls, 10-plus kids' zones, giveaways and brand activations. The event is designed to bring Islamabad and Rawalpindi together around music, food, family, culture, creativity and community, with a programme built for shared discovery and participation across generations. The mix is deliberately broad: a concert for the evening crowd, dedicated zones and activities for children, and stalls and food for families spending the whole day at the venue.

A festival weekend in the capital

PlayFest lands on the same weekend as the second Pakistan Tourism, Sports and Family Festival, which runs from November 6 to 8 at the Pakistan Sports Complex in Islamabad, with tourism exhibitions, sporting competitions, cultural performances and family entertainment. Together, the two events give the capital a packed weekend of live music, food and family activities, and organisers of the tourism festival expect around 150,000 visitors across its three days.

What to watch next

With the night concert as the day's headline, the turnout at PNCA will be the measure of whether the festival's mix of music and family programming has found its audience — and whether PlayFest becomes a fixture on Islamabad's events calendar. For the twin cities, a successful first edition would add another annual date to a calendar that has been growing steadily with large-scale public festivals.

Sources: NewsGuru, November 4, 2026

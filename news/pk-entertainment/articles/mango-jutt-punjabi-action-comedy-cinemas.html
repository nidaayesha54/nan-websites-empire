'Mango Jutt' Punjabi Action-Comedy Hits Cinemas

October 26, 2026

Pakistani actress and producer Hareem Farooq's latest Punjabi action-comedy film, Mango Jutt, has been released nationwide on October 16, 2026, after a star-studded premiere in Lahore. The film's grand premiere took place at CineStar in Township, Lahore, on October 15, where several celebrities, media representatives, social media influencers and other guests gathered to celebrate the release.

A Punjabi story for all of Pakistan

Produced by Hareem Farooq under the banner of Hameem Productions, Mango Jutt is a Punjabi action comedy that brings together action, humour, romance and Punjabi cultural elements. Hareem, who also plays the lead role, attended the premiere alongside the film's cast and crew. The movie stars Faysal Quraishi, Shamoon Abbasi, Qaiser Piya and comedy actor Iftikhar Thakur, among others, with Ayesha Jahanzeb and Honey Albela also in supporting roles. Abu Aleeha has written and directed the film. Speaking to The Express Tribune at the film's music launch in Karachi, Farooq said the project had been a challenging but rewarding experience, adding that bringing it to the screen was a success in itself. "My message is that people should watch this film with an open heart. It is for all of Pakistan. Just because it is a Punjabi film does not mean it is only for Punjab. It is a film for the entire country," she said.

Music, trailer buzz and a starry premiere

Music is another feature of the film, with Ali Mustafa and Ali Allah Ditta directing its original soundtrack. Singers including Falak Shabbir, Zeb Bangash and Jabbar Abbas have contributed to the music. The trailer had already drawn attention, with its mix of cops, goons, romance and comedy, and its closing scene — Shamoon Abbasi's antagonist dragging the heroine away as his henchmen hold Jutt down — setting up the film's action payoff. The Lahore premiere drew showbiz personalities including Fawad Khan and Wahaj Ali, whose friendly interaction inside the cinema drew attention from fans. Farooq said audience feedback had been encouraging and expectations had risen as the film's release approached.

What Mango Jutt means for Lollywood

Mango Jutt arrives as part of a growing lineup of Pakistani movies planned for theatrical release this year, and its bet is a distinctive one: that Punjabi-language storytelling, played with colour and confidence, can draw audiences from across the country rather than only from Punjab. Farooq said visiting Karachi for the launch was particularly meaningful because of the affection she had received from the city, and that completing the film had already made the journey worthwhile. Whether the film becomes a box-office hit will be decided by audiences in the weeks ahead — but as a statement of intent for regional-language cinema, Mango Jutt is already hard to ignore.

'Avengers: Doomsday' Final Trailer Drops: Steve Rogers' Family, the X-Men and Doctor Doom

November 24, 2026

Marvel Studios has released the final trailer for Avengers: Doomsday, Anthony and Joe Russo's superhero epic, less than a month before it hits theatres on December 18, Animation World Network reported.

A family secret at the heart of it

The trailer, which Marvel uploaded to YouTube on Monday, November 23, runs about three minutes and opens not with a battle but with a family: Steve Rogers, played by Chris Evans, and Peggy Carter, played by Hayley Atwell, raising a son in the quiet life Steve chose at the end of Avengers: Endgame, and arguing about whether to tell the boy his father was Captain America. Then the Fantastic Four arrive, and Doctor Doom, played by Robert Downey Jr., delivers the trailer's thesis: 'All of you have lived stolen lives. But now you must give them back.' Polygon reported that the trailer ends with the moment X-Men fans have waited decades for — James Marsden's Cyclops shouting 'To me, my X-Men!' in live action for the first time.

The biggest Marvel event since Endgame

The film assembles the Avengers, the X-Men and the Fantastic Four on screen together for the first time in Marvel's cinematic history, with heroes from three different Earths forced to band together against an existential threat. ScreenRant's breakdown counted 13 new scenes in the final trailer, including Hailee Steinfeld's Kate Bishop getting her first shot and Shang-Chi using the Ten Rings to climb one of the Chrysler Building's eagles. The trailer passed 4 million views on Marvel's channel within about 90 minutes of release, and it was the second-most trending video on YouTube in Pakistan on November 24. Avengers: Doomsday is the first Avengers film since Endgame in 2019, and a special sneak peek is due in ABC's holiday special on November 30.

Sources: Polygon, November 23, 2026; ScreenRant, November 23, 2026; Animation World Network, November 23, 2026

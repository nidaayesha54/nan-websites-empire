Durefishan Addresses Zaryab's Modesty Debate in Dar-e-Nijaat

November 9, 2026

Pakistani actress Durefishan Saleem has summed up the heated debate going on around her character Zaryab Habib in the drama Dar-e-Nijaat, 24NewsHD reported on November 9.

The scene that started it

The actress addressed a particular scene in which Zaryab appears reluctant to wear a sleeveless dress because she does not feel comfortable in it. The scene got people talking on social media, with viewers interpreting Zaryab's clothing preference in connection with modesty and faith.

A drama dominating the conversation

Dar-e-Nijaat, starring Durefishan alongside Sheheryar Munawar as Abu Bakar, has become Pakistani television's most talked-about drama of the season, with the lead couple's slow-burn romance — including their first dinner date in the episode aired around November 6 — sending fans into a frenzy online. The show's latest episodes have drawn praise for the tender chemistry between Abu Bakar and Zaryab after a painful journey marked by heartbreak, betrayal and emotional struggles, Daily Pakistan reported on November 7.

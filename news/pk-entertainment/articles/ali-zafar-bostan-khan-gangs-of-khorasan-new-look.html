Ali Zafar's Bostan Khan Look for 'Gangs of Khorasan' Leaves Fans Curious

November 2, 2026

Pakistani singer and actor Ali Zafar has surprised fans by revealing his new look for his upcoming project 'Gangs of Khorasan,' where he will play a character named Bostan Khan, GTV News reported on November 2.

The reveal

Zafar shared a glimpse of the character on social media, sporting a thick moustache, glasses and a rugged look — a complete departure from his usual appearance. Fans were left curious about the role, with the central question being whether Bostan Khan will be portrayed as a hero or a villain. Details about the character remain under wraps, GTV noted.

Why fans are watching

The transformation has driven the conversation precisely because so little else is known: no release date, plot synopsis or full cast details were given in the reports checked. Viewers will now wait to see how Zafar brings the mysterious role to life in 'Gangs of Khorasan.' The buzz also fits a wider moment for Pakistani screen talent crossing borders in attention — Indian actor Shoaib Ibrahim praised Pakistani drama 'Dar-e-Nijaat' on November 2, saying he and wife Dipika Kakar regularly watch the show.

Sources: GTV News HD, 24 News HD, November 2, 2026

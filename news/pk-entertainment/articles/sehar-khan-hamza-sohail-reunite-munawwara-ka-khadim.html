Sehar Khan and Hamza Sohail Reunite for New Drama 'Munawwara Ka Khadim'

November 20, 2026

Sehar Khan and Hamza Sohail are reuniting for an upcoming drama titled Munawwara Ka Khadim, and their on-screen comeback has already created excitement among viewers, Social Diary reported on November 20.

The Fairy Tale duo returns

Sehar Khan and Hamza Sohail became a much-loved television pairing through their characters, Umeed and Farjad, in Fairy Tale. Now, the actors are joining hands for Munawwara Ka Khadim. Although details about their characters and storyline remain under wraps, audiences are curious to see whether they will recreate the same magic in a completely different story.

A star-studded team behind the camera

The upcoming drama is written by Sarwat Nazir, whose previous projects include Jama Taqseem and Dastak. Shahzad Kashmiri, known for directing popular dramas such as Parizaad, Sabaat and Yaqeen Ka Safar, is directing the project. MD Productions is producing the drama, and the teaser of Munawwara Ka Khadim is expected to be released soon.

Sources: Social Diary, November 20, 2026

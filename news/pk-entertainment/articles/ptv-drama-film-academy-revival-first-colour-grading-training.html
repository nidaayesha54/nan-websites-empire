PTV to Revive Drama and Film Academy With Pakistan's First Local Colour Grading Training

November 2, 2026

Federal Minister for Information and Broadcasting Attaullah Tarar on Monday directed the formulation of a comprehensive strategy to revive the Pakistan Television (PTV) Drama and Film Academy as a modern training institution, Aaj English TV reported on November 2.

What the revival promises

Chairing a high-level meeting to review plans for the academy's revival and the revamping of PTV Home, Tarar said the institution would equip young people with professional skills in filmmaking, drama production and digital content creation through specialised training programmes, modern technical facilities and practical, industry-oriented models, working with the private sector and universities. He said Pakistan was launching, for the first time, an advanced training programme in colour grading — the post-production adjustment of colour and tone — adding that professionals previously had to travel abroad to learn it.

What is not yet announced

Tarar called the initiative 'game-changing' for Pakistan's creative industry and said young people's creative projects would be encouraged for broadcast on PTV News, PTV Home and other PTV platforms. The reports checked did not give a start date, fees, budget, private-sector partner names or student intake numbers, so those details have yet to be announced. The revival is part of the government's stated commitment to promoting the media and creative industries, the minister said.

Sources: Aaj English TV, ARY News, The Thursday Times, CW Pakistan, November 2, 2026

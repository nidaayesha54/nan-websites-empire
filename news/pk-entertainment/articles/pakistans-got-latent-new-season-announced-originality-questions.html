'Pakistan's Got Latent' New Season Announced, Faces Originality Questions

November 16, 2026

Comedian Samay Raina's controversial show 'India's Got Latent' has once again become a talking point across the border. Pakistan has announced a new season of its version, titled 'Pakistan's Got Latent', The Times of India reported on November 16. The announcement has several social media users questioning the show's originality.

Who is on the panel

According to reports, the new season will feature comedians and entertainers such as Sharhabil Siddiqui and Usman Mazhar. Muzammil Masiya and Mathira are also part of the panel. Artists Shakeel and Rauf Lala are expected to appear as well.

The format it follows

Samay Raina's 'India's Got Latent' gained popularity for its unconventional talent-show format, unfiltered comedy and unusual contestant performances. The new season premiered in June 2026 and is available on both YouTube and Netflix, with its finale episode airing on Sunday, November 15, 2026. It is a Pakistani comedy and talent show modelled after the Indian original, and the announcement has drawn mockery online over the lack of originality, reports say.

Sources: The Times of India, November 16, 2026; The Daily Jagran, November 16, 2026

Faran Tahir Returns to Marvel as Raza in Vision Quest After 18 Years

October 29, 2026

Pakistani-American actor Faran Tahir is making headlines once again as he returns to the Marvel universe after 18 years, reprising his role as the villain Raza in the sixth episode of Marvel Studios' series Vision Quest, according to reports published on October 29, 2026.

From Iron Man to Vision Quest

Tahir originally portrayed Raza, the ruthless antagonist who kidnapped Tony Stark, in the blockbuster film Iron Man in 2008. Nearly two decades later, he is back in the same role, sporting a noticeably different look and appearance while bringing the character back to the screen.

What happens in the episode

In the latest episode, Tahir is seen alongside a group of armed associates as they attempt to capture Vision, adding another layer of suspense to the unfolding storyline. The return has been welcomed by fans as a nostalgic callback to the early days of the Marvel Cinematic Universe.

Why it matters for Pakistani entertainment

His return to the Marvel universe is more than just a nostalgic moment for fans; it is also a proud milestone for Pakistan's entertainment industry. As a Pakistani-origin actor continuing to make his mark on the international stage, Tahir represents the growing presence and talent of Pakistani artists in global cinema.

For audiences who first met Raza in 2008, the comeback closes an 18-year circle, and it underlines how long careers in franchise storytelling can run.

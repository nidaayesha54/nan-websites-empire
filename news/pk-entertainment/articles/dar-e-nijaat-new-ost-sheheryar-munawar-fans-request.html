Sheheryar Munawar Announces New 'Dar-e-Nijaat' OST After Fans Kept Asking for It

November 1, 2026

Pakistani actor Sheheryar Munawar has announced the release of a new OST for the drama Dar-e-Nijaat after fans repeatedly requested the song, 24NewsHD reported on November 1.

Who made the new track

The new track is sung by Naveed Nashad and Sehar Gull Khan, with lyrics written by Qamar Nashad and music composed by Naveed Nashad. The latest OST has also sparked comparisons with the drama's original soundtrack, 'Hijr-e-Muhabbat', according to the report.

The drama behind the song

Dar-e-Nijaat is written by Umera Ahmed and directed by Saqib Khan, and airs on ARY Digital. The drama follows the lives of Abu Bakar, Zaryab and Zamil, and has attracted considerable interest from viewers — interest that, in this case, turned into repeated requests for a new song, and now a new OST.

Sources: 24NewsHD, November 1, 2026

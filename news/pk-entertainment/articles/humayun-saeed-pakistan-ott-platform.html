Humayun Saeed Calls for Pakistan's Own OTT Platform to Take Dramas Global

October 28, 2026

Actor and producer Humayun Saeed has called for Pakistan to build its own over-the-top (OTT) streaming platform, arguing that a local service could help Pakistani dramas reach international audiences. He added that international productions communicated schedules clearly, while Pakistan's entertainment industry still had room to improve its organisation.

'I do not see myself as a star'

Despite his success, Saeed said he did not consider himself a star and viewed acting as his profession. "I do not see myself as a star. This is just my work. You are not a star for doing it," he said, naming Fahad Mustafa and Fawad Khan as examples of stars. He said he preferred to work in his own way, enjoyed producing projects and maintained a small social circle.

His recent projects include the 2025 romantic comedy Love Guru, directed by Nadeem Baig and co-starring Mahira Khan, and the television drama Main Manto Nahi Hoon, which also aired in 2025. He has also been associated with the Jawani Phir Nahi Ani franchise, with Jawani Phir Nahi Ani 3 reportedly in development. A homegrown platform, his argument runs, would give that kind of work a direct route to viewers abroad, without waiting for foreign services to pick it up.

Sources: Aaj TV, October 28, 2026

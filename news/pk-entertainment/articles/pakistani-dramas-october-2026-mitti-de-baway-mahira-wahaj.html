Pakistani Dramas to Watch in October 2026: 'Mitti De Baway' Leads the Month

October 27, 2026

October 2026 has brought a fresh roundup of Pakistani dramas for viewers settling into the autumn season, with new arrivals and longer-running titles sharing the weekly schedule. The month's freshest new entry is Mitti De Baway, positioned as the new arrival going into October, with Mahira Khan and Wahaj Ali leading the cast on Green Entertainment in a Wednesday and Thursday 8:00 PM slot.

For viewers who want something lighter, the roundup points to Dekh Zara Pyaar Se, described as a gentler romance-driven watch compared with the heavier social dramas and mystery titles on the list. The serial stars Yumna Zaidi and Hamza Sohail, with Saima Akram Chaudhry credited as writer.

Thrills, cybercrime and family secrets

For a suspense-first pick, the roundup tags Muamma as its mystery-thriller option, anchored by Saba Qamar and Usman Mukhtar on Hum TV. The cybercrime and digital privacy angle belongs to Aik Aur Pakeezah on Geo Entertainment, with Sehar Khan named as the lead, reflecting how online safety and privacy have become mainstream drama territory.

Family drama is represented by Maa, a Green Entertainment serial in which Raaha, played by Aina Asif, slowly uncovers the reality of her birth and family story. The roundup is presented as unranked, ordered for practical viewing rather than scored by quality, moving from the newest-highlighted entry to longer-running titles.

What to pick

The month's slate covers the main bases of Pakistani television: a star-led new serial in Mitti De Baway, a soft romance in Dekh Zara Pyaar Se, a mystery in Muamma, a contemporary issue drama in Aik Aur Pakeezah, and a family story in Maa. Viewers looking for one new commitment this month will find the strongest case in the Mahira Khan-Wahaj Ali pairing, while those who prefer suspense or social themes have ready alternatives in the same weekly rotation.

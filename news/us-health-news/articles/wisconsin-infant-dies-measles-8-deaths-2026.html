Wisconsin Infant Dies of Measles as US Death Toll Reaches 8 in 2026

November 8, 2026

The Wisconsin Department of Health Services confirmed a measles death in an infant this week, marking the state's first measles fatality since 1990 and the third US measles death reported in a single week, INAUF Daily reported on November 8. The death is part of a nationwide surge that has pushed US measles cases past 5,100 across 45 states in 2026 — the highest level since 1991.

Eight deaths, two counts

State health agencies have now verified at least eight measles-associated deaths this year — six in Pennsylvania, one in New York and one in Wisconsin — while the CDC's public portal currently reflects four confirmed deaths, as the agency updates its standardised reporting criteria, INAUF reported. The New York death was an unvaccinated seven-month-old boy from Denmark in northern Lewis County, the state's first documented measles fatality in more than three decades. In Pennsylvania, Jefferson County remains the national epicentre with 1,467 cases, including 88 new cases in the most recent reporting week, according to a November 7 pathogen update. Wisconsin's outbreak has reached 431 cases in 13 counties, the highest total in more than 35 years, with nine people hospitalised, the Eastern Herald reported. The CDC's latest count put the national total at 5,182 confirmed cases, with 96 percent of cases among people who were unvaccinated or whose vaccination status was unknown.

Elimination status under review

The Pan American Health Organization is scheduled to review the US measles elimination status, held since 2000, this month, according to the November 7 pathogen update. Measles was declared eliminated in the United States in 2000, meaning the virus was no longer spreading continuously within the country, but the sustained outbreaks of the past two years have put that status at risk. Wisconsin health officials are warning that anyone who was in the emergency department at Marshfield Medical Center in Weston on Thursday afternoon, November 5, may have been exposed to measles, and Marathon County is offering free MMR vaccinations on Sunday morning for people who were there and are not immune. Two doses of the MMR vaccine are about 97 percent effective, according to the CDC.

Sources: INAUF Daily, November 8, 2026; Eastern Herald, November 8, 2026; Pathogen Update, November 7, 2026

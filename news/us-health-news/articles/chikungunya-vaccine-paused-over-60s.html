US Health Officials Pause Chikungunya Vaccine for Over-60s After 17 Serious Events

November 13, 2026

US health officials have advised pausing the use of the chikungunya vaccine for people aged 60 and over, after 17 serious adverse events — including two deaths — were reported in older recipients worldwide, News One USA reported on November 13.

What prompted the pause

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention and the US Food and Drug Administration said the live-attenuated vaccine IXCHIQ should not be used among people 60 years and older until additional reports are further investigated. The agencies investigated five hospitalisations for cardiac or neurologic events following vaccination, and are aware of additional reports among people aged 62 to 89 in other countries.

Younger adults unaffected

The vaccine, manufactured by Valneva, contains a live, weakened version of the chikungunya virus and was the first chikungunya vaccine licensed in the United States. The pause applies only to people aged 60 and over; the vaccine remains available to younger adults.

Sources: News One USA / NDTV, November 13, 2026

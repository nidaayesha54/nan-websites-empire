FDA to Review uniQure's Huntington's Gene Therapy, Decision Due by May 2027

November 2, 2026

Dutch gene therapy developer uniQure said on Monday that the US Food and Drug Administration had accepted its application for its Huntington's disease treatment and would decide on the drug by May 1, 2027, Reuters reported on November 2. US-listed shares of the company were up 2.5 percent in premarket trading.

An accelerated review after a reversal

The FDA granted the therapy a faster review under its accelerated approval pathway, which can allow speedier approval for serious diseases based on early evidence while requiring the company to confirm the benefit in future studies. The agency is also considering holding a meeting of outside experts to discuss the application. The decision ends a lengthy standoff during which a senior agency official described the treatment as a 'failed product'; the regulator later reversed course and agreed to review it.

What the application rests on

UniQure's application is based on early- to mid-stage study results comparing treated patients with a group from an external Huntington's disease database. The company said it has treated 51 patients across its studies so far, after AMT-130 failed to slow disease progression in a four-year trial — the result that sent its shares sharply lower months ago. UniQure plans to begin US patient screening by year-end for a 200-patient follow-up trial, with two-thirds receiving the gene therapy and the rest standard care. Acceptance for review is not an approval.

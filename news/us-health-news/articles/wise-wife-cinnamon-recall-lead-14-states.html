Wise Wife Ground Cinnamon Recalled in 14 States Over Elevated Lead Levels

November 1, 2026

A fall baking staple has been recalled in more than a dozen states for potential lead contamination, with the Food and Drug Administration announcing on October 29 that SLR Food Distribution Inc. is recalling its Wise Wife Brand Ground Cinnamon because it could contain elevated levels of lead.

Which product is affected

The recalled cinnamon comes in a 110-gram (3.88-ounce) clear plastic container with a black screw-top cap, carrying UPC 0688474302754 on the back label and a Best Before date of 4/25/2027. It was distributed to retail and grocery stores in Connecticut, Indiana, Kansas, Massachusetts, Michigan, Minnesota, North Carolina, Nebraska, New York, Ohio, Pennsylvania, Texas, Virginia and Wisconsin between September 8, 2025, and June 28, 2026. The problem came to light after the Connecticut Agricultural Experiment Station tested a sample and found elevated lead levels; the Hicksville, New York-based company then voluntarily initiated the recall.

What consumers should do

No illnesses have been reported in connection with the recall, according to the FDA. Customers should not consume the recalled spice and should either discard it or return it to the place of purchase for a full refund, the agency said. Ground cinnamon has been under closer scrutiny since 2024, when the FDA issued a public health alert listing more than a dozen brands flagged for lead, and another cinnamon brand sold in six states was recalled for the same reason last month.

Sources: FDA via USA Today, People, Reader's Digest, October 29-31, 2026

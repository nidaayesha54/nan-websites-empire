Trump's Closing Midterm Pitch From the Lincoln Memorial: 'I'll Become Mr Veto'

November 2, 2026

President Donald Trump will make his final pitch to voters on Monday from the Lincoln Memorial Reflecting Pool in Washington, a day before midterm elections that will decide control of Congress, according to White House announcements reported on November 2.

A Washington closing argument

The address is set for 2 p.m. ET and is described by the White House as Trump's closing message before the midterms. Rather than travelling to a battleground state on the final day, Trump chose the Reflecting Pool — a site tied to his controversial, problem-plagued renovation effort, which he said in April would take days and cost about $1.5 million but finished nearly three months behind schedule at more than ten times that estimate, the Associated Press reported. Trump told reporters his Sunday evening rally in Kansas, after a stop in Iowa, was 'my last stop' on the rally trail.

'I'll become Mr Veto'

In an interview with the New York Post's Miranda Devine, Trump laid out his plan if Democrats win Congress: 'I guess I'll become Mr Veto. I'm not going to let them ruin it,' adding, 'If we didn't win, I'd veto everything. I'll be vetoing, vetoing.' A veto rejects a bill passed by Congress, which can only be overridden by two-thirds of both chambers. Trump has used the power only twice in his second term, against ten vetoes in his first. All 435 House seats and 35 Senate seats are on the ballot on Tuesday, November 3.

Sources: Associated Press via The Week, ET Now, Times Now, November 2, 2026

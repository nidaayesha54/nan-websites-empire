Senate Republicans Block Iran War Powers Vote as Democrats Move to Cut Off War Funding

November 20, 2026

Senate Republicans blocked the latest Democratic effort to end the war in Iran on Thursday, in the first war powers vote since the midterm elections, NPR Illinois and SecureResearch reported on November 20.

A first test of the new majorities

The Washington Post described the vote as the first test of Republican support for the conflict since the party lost control of Congress in the midterms, with most Republicans voting together against any limits, according to the New York Times. Separately, Politico reported that a group of Democrats is closing in on a proposal to cut off Iran war funding as part of a spending deal to avert a government shutdown in three weeks, and Roll Call reported lawmakers are demanding unreleased estimates on base damage, with Iran war costs reported to have surpassed $48 billion.

Democrats use their leverage

Democrats who won this month's election are looking to use their influence to block funding for the war in Iran before officially taking charge of Congress. Congress must pass a funding measure to avoid a shutdown by December 11, and Democrats hold leverage since their votes are essential for the Republican-controlled Senate to meet the 60-vote threshold, NPR's Claudia Grisales reported.

Sources: NPR Illinois, November 20, 2026; SecureResearch US Politics Brief, November 20, 2026

Congress Returns for Brutal Lame-Duck Stretch With Shutdown Deadline Looming

November 9, 2026

Lawmakers are back on Capitol Hill this week for the first time since the midterm elections, and the outgoing Republican majority has a heavy list of responsibilities before Democrats take over in January, Fox News reported on November 9.

The shutdown clock

Current federal funding levels run out on December 11, and without another deal in place, Congress risks plunging the government into another shutdown. The lame-duck session must therefore pass funding legislation while also advancing pending bills and presidential nominations before the new Congress is sworn in on January 3, 2027.

Leadership races first

The first order of business this week is choosing new leaders for the next Congress. Senate Majority Leader John Thune is running for the minority leader role unopposed for now, while on the Democratic side House Minority Leader Hakeem Jeffries is expected to glide into the speaker's role after leading his party to a commanding midterm victory. Democrats' leadership elections will take place the week after the Republicans' do. The transition comes as Democrats prepare an aggressive oversight agenda for January, including investigations into the Trump administration and a possible impeachment vote on Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth.

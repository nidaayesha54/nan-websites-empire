House Blocks Hegseth Impeachment Bid in 211-204 Vote, Shelving Massie Push

November 11, 2026

House Republicans voted 211 to 204 on Tuesday to refer articles of impeachment against Secretary of War Pete Hegseth to the House Judiciary Committee, effectively blocking a direct floor vote and shelving the measure for the remainder of the current congressional session, News18 reported on November 11.

Massie led the push alone

The push to oust Hegseth was led by outgoing Republican Representative Thomas Massie of Kentucky, who reintroduced eight articles of impeachment against the Secretary of War, citing unauthorised actions. Massie was the sole Republican to join Democrats in opposing the committee referral. House Speaker Mike Johnson dismissed the initiative as an unnecessary distraction, maintaining that House leadership had secured the necessary votes to prevent the resolution from advancing to a full impeachment debate.

No second attempt planned

Massie said after the vote that he would not introduce another impeachment resolution. The procedural move, spearheaded by Johnson and Republican leadership, means the effort is effectively dead for this session, even as Democrats — who take control of the House in January — have vowed aggressive oversight of the Trump administration, including investigations into Hegseth. Incoming Speaker Hakeem Jeffries and other senior Democrats had rallied behind the impeachment measure and called Hegseth reckless.

Sources: News18, November 11, 2026; Seven Lights Daily Brief, November 11, 2026

Democrats Lead Generic Ballot by 8.2 Points as Early Voting Passes 51 Million

November 1, 2026

With three days to go before the midterm elections, Democrats are leading national polls as millions of Americans cast their ballots early, Al Jazeera reported on November 1.

A record early vote

More than 51 million Americans have already cast ballots in person or by post, according to the University of Florida's Election Lab, up from nearly 46 million who voted before Election Day at the last midterms in 2022. The early-vote surge has drawn particular attention in Iowa and Kansas, where competitive Senate races are under way: Iowa had recorded more than 430,000 early ballots by Saturday morning, with registered Democrats accounting for more than 46% against 34% for Republicans, while Kansas had recorded more than 420,000 ballots, according to the Associated Press.

What the final polls say

According to RealClearPolling's latest national congressional ballot average, Democrats lead Republicans by 8.2 percentage points, 51.4% to 43.2%. A separate Economist/YouGov survey released on Thursday found that 44% of registered voters expect Democrats to control the House after the election, compared with 31% who expect Republicans to retain control. Polls are snapshots, not results: voters will decide all 435 House seats and 35 Senate seats on Tuesday, November 3, and close races can take days to call. President Trump is scheduled to close his campaign with rallies in Iowa and Kansas on Sunday, while Democrats make their final appeals in states including Texas, Michigan and Alaska.

Sources: Al Jazeera, RealClearPolling, Economist/YouGov, November 1, 2026

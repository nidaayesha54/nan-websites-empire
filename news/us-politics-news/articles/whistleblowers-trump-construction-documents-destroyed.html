Whistleblowers Say Documents on Trump's Building Projects Are Being Destroyed

November 10, 2026

House Democrats are demanding that the Interior Department retain documents related to President Donald Trump's construction projects around Washington after whistleblowers warned that key paperwork has already been destroyed before Democrats take power in January, CNN reported on November 10.

Preservation letters go out Tuesday

On Tuesday, Representative Jared Huffman, the top Democrat on the House Natural Resources Committee, plans to issue letters to the Interior Department as well as other federal agencies and private companies, requesting that they \"preserve all records and data responsive to oversight\" the committee oversees. Multiple credible whistleblowers have approached Huffman's committee in recent weeks to voice concern that the Interior Department is getting rid of records in anticipation of forthcoming scrutiny, congressional sources told CNN. The Interior Department called the whistleblower claims \"100% false.\"

Arch, golf course and garden in the crosshairs

The committee, which holds jurisdiction over national parks and federal lands, is set to become a chief antagonist and auditor of projects Trump has deeply and personally invested in, including a proposed triumphal arch, the East Potomac Golf Links, fencing at Lafayette Square and the Tidal Basin development concept plan that includes the president's Garden of American Heroes. The administration is signalling it will accelerate the projects over the next 60 days to lay as much groundwork as possible before the Democrats take over Congress. Committee aides expect a high-profile hearing on one or several of the projects early in the new Congress.

Sources: CNN, November 10, 2026

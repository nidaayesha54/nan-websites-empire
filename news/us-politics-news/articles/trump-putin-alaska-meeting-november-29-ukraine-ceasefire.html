Trump to Meet Putin in Alaska on Sunday in Push for Ukraine Ceasefire

November 28, 2026

President Donald Trump is preparing to meet Russian President Vladimir Putin in Alaska on Sunday, November 29, in an attempt to secure a ceasefire in Ukraine, according to a briefing on November 28 citing the Council on Foreign Relations.

High stakes, cautious expectations

The meeting could become one of the most consequential diplomatic encounters of the year, but expectations should remain cautious: no agreement has yet been reached. Trump has indicated that Russia could face very serious consequences if Putin refuses to end the war. The encounter comes as Russian attacks on Ukraine continue and as the confrontation between the United States and Iran dominates the international agenda.

What is on the table

The Alaska summit is aimed at finding a path to a ceasefire after years of war, and it follows months of U.S., European and Russian negotiations over the terms of any settlement. Details of the agenda have not been made public, and neither side has said what concessions, if any, are under discussion. The outcome of the meeting is likely to shape the next phase of the war and of Western diplomacy toward Moscow.

Sources: Council on Foreign Relations Daily News Brief, November 28, 2026; AI Chat World News, November 28, 2026

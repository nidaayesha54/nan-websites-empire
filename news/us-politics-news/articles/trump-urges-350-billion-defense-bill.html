Trump Urges Senate to 'Immediately' Advance $350 Billion Defence Funding Bill

November 13, 2026

US President Donald Trump on Thursday urged Senate leadership to immediately advance a $350 billion defence funding bill, calling for a major investment in the country's military capabilities, The Tribune reported on November 13.

The president's appeal

In a post on Truth Social, Trump said the funding was necessary to strengthen the US military and give American troops a decisive advantage, while accusing Democrats of seeking to obstruct the investment as they prepare to take over Congress in January. War Secretary Pete Hegseth said on Tuesday that at least $350 billion through reconciliation was needed to make a historic investment in the department.

A lame-duck push

Senate GOP Conference Chair Tom Cotton said earlier this week that Senate Republicans had committed to making every possible effort to pass a reconciliation bill before the next Congress begins. The push follows the House's passage in July of a $1.15 trillion package for the 2027 National Defense Authorization Act, which has faced Democratic resistance in the Senate.

Sources: The Tribune (India) / ANI, November 13, 2026

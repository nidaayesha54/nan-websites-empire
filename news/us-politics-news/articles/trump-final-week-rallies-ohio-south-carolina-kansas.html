Trump's Final Midterm Sprint: Ohio Rally Done, South Carolina and Kansas Next

October 29, 2026

President Donald Trump is closing the midterm campaign with a rapid-fire rally schedule, moving from Ohio on Wednesday to South Carolina on Thursday and Kansas on Saturday, as both parties make their final appeals before the November 3 election.

Ohio: a third stop in a battleground state

Trump rallied supporters in Chillicothe, Ohio, on Wednesday evening, his third Ohio campaign stop of the midterm cycle, appearing on behalf of Republican gubernatorial candidate Vivek Ramaswamy and Senator Jon Husted. During the event he hinted Ohio could land a proposed Space Academy if Husted wins, pointing to Wright-Patterson Air Force Base and NASA's Glenn Research Center near Cleveland. Husted faces former Democratic Senator Sherrod Brown, who is seeking a comeback, while polling reported by local outlets suggests Ramaswamy's opponent Amy Acton may hold a slight lead.

South Carolina on Thursday, Kansas on Saturday

Trump is scheduled to appear in Rock Hill, South Carolina, on Thursday, with remarks set for about 12:30 p.m. ET at the Rock Hill Sports and Event Center, in support of Republican Senator Darline Graham's bid for a full term. On Saturday, October 31, he returns to Kansas for a second rally in the state this month, at Salina Regional Airport's Hangar 509, backing Senator Roger Marshall against Democratic challenger Adam Hamilton.

Two competing closing messages

The same day Trump campaigned in Ohio, former President Barack Obama addressed Democrats in the Atlanta suburbs of Georgia, giving voters competing final-week visions. Trump has urged supporters to treat the midterms as if he himself were on the ballot, while Obama has framed the election as a chance to say “enough is enough” after what he called a decade of chaos. With early voting winding down in many states, both parties are now focused on one thing above all: turnout.

Sources: Columbus Dispatch, Ad Astra Radio, campaign event listings, October 28-29, 2026

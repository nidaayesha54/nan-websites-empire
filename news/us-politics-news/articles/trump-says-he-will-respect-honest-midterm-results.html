Trump Says He Will Respect Midterm Results 'If They're Honest'

October 28, 2026

President Donald Trump said on Tuesday that he would respect the results of the upcoming midterm elections if they were honest, regardless of which party wins control of Congress. Speaking to reporters as he departed for a rally in Texas, he was asked directly whether he would accept the outcome even if Republicans lost their congressional majority.

"I will, if they're honest elections," Trump said, in remarks reported on October 28.

The stakes on November 3

Voters will decide all 435 seats in the House of Representatives, 35 Senate seats and dozens of governors' races and state offices. Polling averages and prediction markets have recently pointed toward Democratic gains, though several Senate contests remain rated as toss-ups.

Asked how he would work with Democrats if Republicans lost their majority despite his campaigning, Trump pointed to his veto power. "We'll figure a way. Look, I have strong veto. I hope that that won't be the case," he said. His answer leaves the central question of the campaign's final week intact: whether a divided Congress would mean negotiation, gridlock, or both.

Sources: Anadolu Agency, October 28, 2026

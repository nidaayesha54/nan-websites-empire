Texas Breaks: Talarico Wins Senate, Hinojosa Ousts Abbott in First Democratic Statewide Sweep Since 1994

November 4, 2026

Texas, long the emblem of Republican dominance, broke for Democrats on Tuesday: James Talarico won the open Senate race and Gina Hinojosa defeated three-term Republican Governor Greg Abbott, the first Democratic statewide victories in Texas since 1994, CNN and the Indian Express reported on November 4.

The Senate race

Talarico, a Democratic state legislator, defeated Republican Attorney General Ken Paxton, who had been endorsed by President Trump, in what became the most expensive Senate race in US history, CNN reported. It was the first Democratic Senate win in Texas since 1988. In Maine, Democrat Troy Jackson ousted five-term Republican Senator Susan Collins by almost seven points, and in Kansas, Democrat Adam Hamilton defeated Senator Roger Marshall — the party's first Kansas Senate win since 1932.

The governor's race — and the wider map

Hinojosa's defeat of Abbott was the night's other Texas shock, and she had campaigned in part on a moratorium on new data centres, a pledge markets noticed on Wednesday. Elsewhere, Democrat Amy Acton won the Ohio governorship, and in Georgia, Keisha Lance Bottoms became the first Black woman elected governor in US history and Georgia's first Democratic governor since 1998, reports said. Democrats also toppled Republican state legislative supermajorities in states including Indiana and Missouri, and won House races in districts Trump had carried by 20 points two years earlier.

Sources: CNN, Indian Express, Al Jazeera, November 4, 2026

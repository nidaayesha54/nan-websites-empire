FEC Filing System Restored After 41-Hour Outage That Hid Last-Minute Midterm Spending

October 29, 2026

The Federal Election Commission restored its electronic filing system on Wednesday night, October 28, after a nearly 41-hour outage that left last-minute campaign spending hidden from public view in the final days before the November 3 midterm elections. The agency said most affected services were back online, with some search and notification tools still being restored.

What went dark, and why it mattered

The system went offline late on Monday, October 26, blocking the mandatory 24- and 48-hour reports that disclose spending by super PACs and other outside groups in the closing stretch of a campaign. According to Reuters, the agency took its systems offline to contain a cyberattack; the FEC itself has not publicly confirmed a motive or identified any attacker, and reports differ on whether a breach was attempted or completed.

Campaign finance watchdogs warned that the blackout fell at the worst possible moment. Outside groups had been spending an average of more than $50 million a day in the week before the outage, according to OpenSecrets research director Brendan Glavin, with more than $250 million reported on October 20 alone. With filings blocked, voters had no window into the final week's spending surge.

No fines for filings missed during the outage

The FEC told filers it would not fine campaigns for reports missed during the outage, provided they are submitted within 24 hours of the system's return, according to reporting on the agency's email to filers. The filings blocked since Monday are now expected to land in a rush as committees catch up.

Officials and watchdogs alike stressed one important limit to the damage: the FEC oversees campaign finance disclosure, not elections themselves. It does not run polling places, count ballots, or operate voting machines, so the outage should have no effect on voters' ability to cast ballots on November 3.

Sources: Reuters, The Hill, State and Federal Communications, October 28-29, 2026

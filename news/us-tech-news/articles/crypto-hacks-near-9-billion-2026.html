Crypto Hacks Near $9 Billion in 2026 as Exchanges Keep Getting Breached

November 10, 2026

Crypto hacks have reached roughly $8.78 billion so far in 2026, with exchanges breached and attackers finding ways around security controls, raising doubts about how safely people can hold digital assets, the IT Security News overnight roundup reported on November 10.

Breaches hit universities and gaming too

Overnight coverage was dominated by money and data taken through breaches, AI tooling moving into security products, and state-linked or ransomware attacks on critical services. Vanderbilt University confirmed it is responding to a cyberattack claimed by the BYOD ransomware group, which says it encrypted university databases and stole student, employee and research data. Japanese gaming company SEGA disclosed that a breach at its third-party newsletter provider exposed the email addresses of 2.2 million subscribers, and a French grocery sector faced disruption from hacking that France has attributed to Iran.

Security vendors scramble

N-able took its hosted N-central environments offline on November 9 after a researcher published 33 alleged zero-day flaws with exploit code, and told customers running on-premises servers to shut them down until further notice. On the vendor side, Anthropic's Mythos model, which is strong at finding software bugs, will be offered through Cisco and IBM enterprise security products, and former Palantir executive Michael LoSapio has been nominated to lead CISA, an agency that has had no Senate-confirmed director throughout President Trump's second term.

Sources: IT Security News Roundup, November 10, 2026

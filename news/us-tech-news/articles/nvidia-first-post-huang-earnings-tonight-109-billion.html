Nvidia Reports First Post-Huang Earnings Tonight in a $109 Billion Test of the AI Trade

November 17, 2026

Nvidia will release its first earnings report tonight following Jensen Huang's departure as chief executive, with the chipmaker's fiscal third-quarter results due after the market close on Tuesday, TradingKey reported on November 17.

What Wall Street expects

Wall Street consensus expects Nvidia to report fiscal third-quarter revenue of about $108.7 billion and earnings of $2.47 per share, with data centre revenue of about $101.8 billion, according to Visible Alpha figures cited by TradingKey. Nvidia's own guidance calls for revenue of $108 billion, plus or minus 2 percent. Key focuses include Blackwell chip shipments, data centre revenue, gross margin and fourth-quarter guidance. Options pricing implies an 8.5 percent move in either direction after the report, according to Reuters figures cited in market briefings.

Kress takes the call

CFO Colette Kress will field questions about succession, product roadmap continuity and customer confidence for the first time as interim CEO, with the earnings call scheduled for 5 p.m. ET. The stock fell 4.1 percent on Monday to $156.14 and is down nearly 24 percent this month, weighed down in part by Huang's departure last week. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent has publicly called for an outside hire as CEO and questioned the company's China sales, adding to the scrutiny on tonight's call.

Sources: TradingKey, November 17, 2026; PreMarket Daily, November 17, 2026

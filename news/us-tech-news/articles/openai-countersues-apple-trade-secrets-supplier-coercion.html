OpenAI Countersues Apple Over Trade Secrets and Supplier Coercion

November 28, 2026

OpenAI has filed a counterclaim against Apple, alleging trade secret theft and threats to suppliers, The Decoder reported on November 28, in the latest escalation of the legal dispute between the two former partners.

What OpenAI alleges

According to the report, OpenAI's countersuit, filed in California, alleges that Apple stole trade secrets and coerced shared suppliers. Apple has responded to the filing, the report said. The countersuit turns the tables in a legal fight that began with Apple, and it lands at a moment when the relationship between the iPhone maker and the ChatGPT developer, once close partners, has visibly soured.

A wider week of AI legal battles

The filing caps a week in which legal pressure across the AI industry intensified, with multiple disputes converging on questions of trade secrets, chip exports and market power. OpenAI is separately developing an internal platform, codenamed Athena, that sits between raw business data and AI agents to standardise metric definitions, The Decoder reported. Neither company has publicly detailed the full claims in the countersuit, and the case is expected to play out in California courts in the months ahead.

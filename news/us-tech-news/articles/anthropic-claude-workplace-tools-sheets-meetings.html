Anthropic Answers OpenAI With Claude Sheets, Meetings and Data as AI Rivals Chase Google and Microsoft

November 11, 2026

Anthropic has answered OpenAI's business push with a cluster of workplace tools, launching Claude Sheets, Claude Meetings and Claude Data at a San Francisco event, Kaupr Daily reported on November 11.

Claude goes to work

Claude Sheets brings spreadsheets with built-in AI editing, Claude Meetings joins Google Meet calls and starts drafting plans while people are still talking, and Claude Data — formerly Claude Dashboards — is now generally available. Anthropic also announced new interactive connectors into apps like Adobe, Atlassian and HubSpot. The launch comes as OpenAI opened a beta Business Hub that lets companies create and verify profiles inside ChatGPT, pushing both AI labs further into territory long held by Google and Microsoft.

The new front line

The rival launches underline how the AI race has shifted from chatbots to workplace software, with each company trying to become the default AI layer inside offices. Anthropic's push comes just days before its planned November 18 IPO, which is expected to be one of the biggest AI listings yet, while OpenAI's revenue reportedly grew 50 percent over the past quarter even as its losses widened. Both companies are betting that businesses will pay for AI that does real work inside their existing tools, not just answers questions.

Sources: Kaupr Daily, November 11, 2026

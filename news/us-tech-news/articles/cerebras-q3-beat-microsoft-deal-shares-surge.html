Cerebras Shares Surge 17% on Profit Surprise, Raised Forecast and Microsoft Deal

November 13, 2026

Cerebras Systems' shares surged about 17 percent on Friday after the AI chipmaker raised its annual sales forecast and posted a surprise profit, Reuters reported on November 13, in a rally that would add nearly $4 billion to its market value if the gains hold.

A beat on every line

The company reported adjusted earnings of 12 cents per share, against an expected loss of 14 cents, according to analysts polled by LSEG. Core revenue reached $230.9 million, up 70 percent year on year and ahead of the $214.7 million consensus. Cerebras raised its 2026 adjusted revenue forecast to $940 million to $960 million, up from $880 million to $890 million, and reiterated plans to triple revenue in 2027.

The Microsoft validation

Chief executive Andrew Feldman disclosed a cloud deal with Microsoft, signed in October, with revenue expected to begin in the first quarter of 2027. The deal adds another major cloud provider to Cerebras' customer roster alongside Amazon and Oracle. The rally also calmed investor anxiety over AI spending that was compounded this week by Nvidia chief executive Jensen Huang's resignation.

Sources: Reuters, November 13, 2026; CNBC via BlockWest, November 13, 2026

OpenAI Launches GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol, Cutting Top Model's Price by 20 Percent

November 19, 2026

OpenAI released GPT-6.2 Astra and GPT-6.2 Sol on November 17, the company announced, pitching Astra as its most capable system for complex professional projects, including building financial models and handling scientific work, according to Bloomberg reporting published by LiveMint.

Cheaper, and a benchmark claim

Astra's standard API prices fall 20 percent from its predecessor, to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens, with cached input dropping 60 percent to $0.40. Sol targets everyday tasks at $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens. Both models support 1.05-million-token contexts and outputs of up to 128,000 tokens. OpenAI said Astra outperformed Anthropic's most powerful Fable model on a benchmark for multistep professional work at a fraction of the cost, and Artificial Analysis places Astra second on its Intelligence Index, behind only Claude Fable 5.2.

Safety questions remain

The release follows OpenAI scrapping a GPT-6.1 Astra update in late September, saying it fell short on safeguards. Astra is OpenAI's first released model rated High for AI self-improvement, and both models rate Critical for cybersecurity in the company's own safety findings, with the UK AI Security Institute yet to retest the updated safeguards. Early testers have also pushed back on reliability, with one developer calling Astra the company's worst model ever. The models are available through paid ChatGPT Work and Codex subscriptions and the API.

Sources: OpenAI announcement via Pivot News, November 19, 2026; Softonic, November 19, 2026; Artificial Analysis via LinkedIn, November 19, 2026

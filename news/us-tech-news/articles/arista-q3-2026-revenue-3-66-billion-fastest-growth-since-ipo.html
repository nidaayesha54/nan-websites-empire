Arista Revenue Jumps 58.5% to $3.66 Billion, Its Fastest Growth Since the IPO

November 4, 2026

Arista Networks reported its fastest revenue growth since its 2014 initial public offering on Tuesday, November 3, as spending on the networks connecting AI accelerators kept accelerating, with shares rising about 9 percent in after-hours trading, reports said.

The quarter

Revenue for the quarter ended September 30 reached $3.6576 billion, up 58.5 percent from $2.3083 billion a year earlier and up 20.5 percent from the second quarter, beating Arista's own guidance of about $3.3 billion and the roughly $3.33 billion analysts expected. GAAP net income rose to $1.56 billion from $853 million, and adjusted earnings were $1.20 per share against a $1.08 estimate. Chief executive Jayshree Ullal called it “another momentous quarter with the highest year-over-year revenue growth since our IPO and record profitability.”

The 2027 outlook

For the fourth quarter, Arista forecast revenue of $3.9 billion to $4.0 billion, above the roughly $3.6 billion analysts expected, and raised its 2026 revenue outlook for the fourth time to about $13.3 billion. On the earnings call, Ullal gave a first look at 2027: revenue growth of 38 to 40 percent, to about $18.5 billion, with AI networking expected to roughly double to about $7 billion. Those are forecasts, and the stock had already fallen more than 20 percent from its October high before the report.

Sources: Arista earnings release via TickerRoast, MarketBeat, Dow Jones via Morningstar, November 3-4, 2026

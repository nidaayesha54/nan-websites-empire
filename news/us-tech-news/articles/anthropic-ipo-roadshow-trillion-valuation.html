Anthropic to Kick Off IPO Roadshow at $1.25–$1.75 Trillion Valuation

November 8, 2026

Anthropic is pushing forward with a pre-Thanksgiving initial public offering, targeting a valuation of $1.25–$1.75 trillion and aiming to raise $80–$100 billion, The Decoder reported on November 8. The figure is well below the $2 trillion valuation the company had previously hoped for, after a sharp selloff in technology stocks this week.

Roadshow next week, listing before Thanksgiving

Anthropic will begin its investor roadshow next week, the week of November 9, aiming for a Nasdaq listing ahead of Thanksgiving on November 26, according to a daily AI briefing published on November 8. The listing would be one of the largest technology debuts ever, and it comes in a week when investors have been questioning the scale, financing and eventual returns from AI infrastructure spending. OpenAI, Anthropic's chief rival, closed an initial $11 billion tranche of funding at a $1.25 trillion valuation this week, so the two companies are now being priced in the same range by the market.

A test of the AI trade

Whether Anthropic can hold its valuation through the roadshow will be read as a verdict on the wider AI trade after a brutal week for the sector. The Nasdaq fell more than 10 percent below its October 6 record close during Thursday trading, entering correction territory. An independent investigation by safety organisation METR, reported this week, found that Anthropic's AI-assisted AI development pipeline has roughly doubled the rate of model improvement compared with a year ago, with no sign of slowing — a claim the company will no doubt lean on as it makes its case to investors.

Sources: The Decoder, November 8, 2026; AI Briefing, November 8, 2026

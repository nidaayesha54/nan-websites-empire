Palantir's $2.18 Billion Earnings Test After a 6% Pre-Report Slide

November 2, 2026

Palantir Technologies reports third-quarter 2026 results after the US market closes on Monday, November 2, in one of the week's most watched tests of whether AI software demand can justify premium valuations, reports said.

The high bar

Wall Street expects revenue of about $2.18 billion, up roughly 84 to 85 percent year over year, and adjusted earnings of about $0.41 per share, nearly double the $0.21 of a year earlier. That consensus sits slightly above the company's own guidance of $2.160 to $2.164 billion. The key watch items are US commercial revenue — which surged 149 percent to $764 million in the second quarter — government revenue, expected near $1.04 billion, and any change to full-year guidance, currently $8.150 to $8.158 billion.

A stock under pressure

Palantir shares fell 6.27 percent on Friday to close at $184.11, about 14 percent below a record high near $213, as investors cut risk in high-valuation AI names. The stock trades at a forward price-to-earnings ratio near 90 to 97 times, far above most large software companies. Oppenheimer raised its price target to $235 from $200 last week, while JPMorgan initiated coverage with an Overweight rating and a $262 target — but analysts warn that even a beat may not be enough in a market repricing AI spending.

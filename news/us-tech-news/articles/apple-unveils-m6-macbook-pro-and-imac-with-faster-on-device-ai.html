Apple Unveils M6 MacBook Pro and iMac With Faster On-Device AI

October 27, 2026

Apple on Tuesday unveiled a new 14-inch MacBook Pro and iMac powered by its latest M6 chip, pitching faster performance for running artificial intelligence tasks directly on the devices rather than in the cloud. The launches arrive a day before Apple hosts an event in Los Angeles on October 28, which Bloomberg News has reported is expected to feature the company's first touch-screen MacBook Pro.

The 14-inch MacBook Pro starts at $1,999, while the new iMac starts at $1,499, according to Reuters. Customers can pre-order both products from Wednesday in 35 countries and regions, including the United States, with availability starting November 6.

What the M6 promises

Apple said the M6 MacBook Pro delivers up to 30 percent faster graphics and up to 25 percent faster AI performance than its M5 predecessor, along with up to 24 hours of battery life, which the company described as the longest ever in a Mac. The new iMac delivers up to five times faster on-device AI performance than the previous M4 model, Apple said.

The emphasis on on-device AI reflects a broader industry shift toward running AI features locally, where tasks such as image processing, transcription, and writing assistance can run without sending data to remote servers. Apple has positioned its chips as central to that strategy, pairing faster neural processing with the power efficiency that has become a hallmark of its in-house silicon.

Ahead of the Los Angeles event

The M6 launches set the stage for Apple's October 28 event in Los Angeles, which the company has billed as a showcase event. Bloomberg News has reported that the event is expected to feature Apple's first touch-screen MacBook Pro, a product long requested by some Mac users but previously resisted by the company. Apple has not confirmed the details of what will be shown at the event.

The timing also places Apple's hardware announcements in the middle of a busy week for big technology companies, with several major firms due to report quarterly earnings in the coming days. Investors will be watching for signs of whether heavy AI spending is translating into growth, and hardware launches that showcase AI capabilities are part of how Apple makes its case to consumers and developers alike.

Sources: Reuters

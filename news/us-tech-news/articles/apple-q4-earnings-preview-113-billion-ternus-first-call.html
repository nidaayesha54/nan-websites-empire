Apple's $113 Billion Earnings Test: What to Watch in Ternus's First Call

November 2, 2026

Apple reports its fiscal fourth-quarter 2026 results on Monday, November 2, after the market closes, in what investors see as a crucial test for the AI-era market after October's selloff erased more than $2 trillion in Nasdaq-100 value, reports said.

What Wall Street expects

Analysts expect revenue of about $113.4 billion, up nearly 11 percent from $102.47 billion a year earlier, and earnings of about $1.99 per share, up from $1.85. IPhone revenue is projected at about $56.5 billion, up roughly 15 percent, though the quarter captures only nine days of iPhone 18 Pro sales; the foldable iPhone Duo, launched October 23, falls into the December quarter. Services revenue is expected at about $31.7 billion, and Apple guided gross margin of 47 to 48 percent, down from 50.1 percent in the prior quarter as memory costs rise.

Why this call matters

The report will be chief executive John Ternus's first earnings call since succeeding Tim Cook on September 1. Investors will focus on holiday-quarter guidance — analysts project about $153 billion in December-quarter revenue — and on whether Apple's lighter AI spending, which helped it pass Nvidia as the world's most valuable company at $5.03 trillion on Friday, can keep delivering growth while rivals face scrutiny over data-center costs.

Sources: Reuters, TipRanks, EBC, Coinpaper, November 2, 2026

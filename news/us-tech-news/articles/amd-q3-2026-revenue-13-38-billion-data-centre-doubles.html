AMD Revenue Jumps 45% to $13.38 Billion as Data Centre Sales Nearly Double

November 4, 2026

Advanced Micro Devices reported third-quarter 2026 results after the market closed on Tuesday, November 3, with revenue rising 45 percent year over year to $13.38 billion, above the roughly $13.09 billion analysts expected, and adjusted earnings of $2.09 per share against a $1.95 forecast, reports said.

The data centre engine

Data centre revenue surged 97 percent to about $8.5 billion, up from $4.34 billion a year earlier, and now accounts for roughly 64 percent of AMD's total revenue, driven by Instinct AI accelerators and EPYC server processors. Gaming revenue fell 40 percent year over year to $773 million, though that still beat the $655 million analysts expected. Shares rose about 6 to 7 percent in after-hours trading, recovering part of a slide that had seen the stock fall for three straight sessions on worries about AI spending.

A bold 2027 signal

For the fourth quarter, AMD forecast revenue of about $17 billion, plus or minus 3 percent, above the roughly $16.2 billion analysts expected. Chief executive Lisa Su also told analysts the company could grow revenue by more than 80 percent in 2027, well above its previous long-term ambition of 35 percent or more — a forecast, not a result, but one analysts said could imply more than $94 billion in 2027 revenue. The next test for the AI trade comes with Nvidia's earnings on November 17, reports noted.

Sources: AMD results via Sharesify, Global Insight, InvestingLive, TickerRoast, November 3-4, 2026

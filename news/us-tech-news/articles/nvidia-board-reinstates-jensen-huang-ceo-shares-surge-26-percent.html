Nvidia Board Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO as Shares Surge 26 Percent

November 23, 2026

Nvidia's board has voted unanimously to reinstate Jensen Huang as president and chief executive officer and as a member of the board, effective immediately, the company announced early on Monday, November 23, ending a leadership crisis that had pushed the stock to a 52-week low.

A dramatic reversal

Early on Monday, shares rose about 9 percent in pre-market trading as President Donald Trump pressed Nvidia's board to bring Huang back, Crypto Briefing reported. The rally accelerated after the reinstatement announcement, with shares jumping more than 25 percent before the opening bell, Reuters reported, and Yahoo Finance put the pre-market gain at 27 percent. Investor's Business Daily reported Nvidia stock soared 30 percent after the market open. On November 20, Nvidia had closed at $125.53, a 52-week low, after a 14 percent drop in a single session. The selloff was driven by Huang's resignation as CEO on November 12 amid a scandal, and the board's decision settles the question that drove last week's selloff: Huang is back in charge, effective immediately.

The numbers behind the comeback

On November 17, Nvidia reported third-quarter fiscal revenue of $115.8 billion, up 103 percent year over year, with data centre revenue of $107.6 billion, up 110 percent from a year earlier. Nvidia projected fourth-quarter revenue of $130 billion, plus or minus 3 percent. The selloff that followed Huang's exit was driven by the leadership vacuum, regulatory inquiries into the potential diversion of advanced chips to China, and doubts about the sustainability of AI data-centre spending. The board had said it had no plans to reinstate Huang, with chief financial officer Colette Kress serving as interim CEO, before Trump's public backing and the market rout forced a rethink. Nvidia has spent the AI boom as the most important supplier in the sector, with its graphics processing units becoming the default hardware for training large AI models.

Sources: Crypto Briefing, November 23, 2026; Investor's Business Daily, November 23, 2026

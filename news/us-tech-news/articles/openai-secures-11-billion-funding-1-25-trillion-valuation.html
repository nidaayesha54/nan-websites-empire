OpenAI Secures $11 Billion in Funding at a $1.25 Trillion Valuation

November 7, 2026

OpenAI has closed on new funding of $11 billion, the initial part of a round that values the company at $1.25 trillion, Bloomberg News reported on Friday, citing people familiar with the matter, Reuters reported on November 6.

A smaller round than first planned

The company had earlier aimed to raise $30 billion at a $1.4 trillion valuation, the Financial Times reported, before cutting the target by $10 billion to $20 billion at the $1.25 trillion valuation. The $11 billion now closed is the initial portion of that round. OpenAI's chief financial officer, Sarah Friar, told CNBC on Friday that the company would press ahead with its US data-centre expansion plans despite the Democratic midterm sweep, and pushed back against suggestions it had scaled back its fundraising ambitions amid the sharp AI stock selloff. She said the business grew 70 percent quarter over quarter in the third quarter, with enterprise demand doubling over the same period.

The funding comes amid an AI stock rout

The fundraising lands in a turbulent week for AI shares: fears that the Democratic sweep may slow data-centre construction wiped about $2.5 trillion off the US stock market on Thursday, according to Reuters. The turmoil is also raising questions about rival Anthropic's widely awaited listing, with prospective investors reportedly questioning whether the startup can begin its roadshow next week and stay on track for a November listing, and pushing for a more attractive valuation than the recently discussed $1.5 trillion. OpenAI's ability to close $11 billion at the $1.25 trillion valuation, even in a falling market, signals that investors still see the ChatGPT maker as the AI sector's benchmark bet.

Sources: Reuters, Bloomberg News, November 6, 2026

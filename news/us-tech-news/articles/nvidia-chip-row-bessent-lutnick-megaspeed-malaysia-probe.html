Nvidia Chip Row Escalates as Bessent Blames Lutnick and Malaysia Opens Diversion Probe

November 14, 2026

The widening Nvidia scandal has shaken Silicon Valley, ended Jensen Huang's more than three-decade tenure at the chipmaker and placed US Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick under pressure within President Donald Trump's administration, BusinessToday reported on November 14.

Bessent points at Commerce

US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent blamed Lutnick's department for failing to blacklist Megaspeed International, a Singapore-based company suspected of helping Chinese firms bypass US export restrictions on advanced Nvidia artificial intelligence chips. Bessent said on Friday that the Treasury Department, the Pentagon, the US Trade Representative's office and the State Department had all supported placing Megaspeed on Washington's trade blacklist. The White House said Trump continues to have complete confidence in Lutnick.

Malaysia's probe dates to April 2025

Malaysian police opened an investigation in April 2025 into US allegations of illicit procurement and diversion of high-performance AI chips, including those produced by Nvidia, through Malaysia, Home Minister Saifuddin Nasution Ismail said on Saturday. Megaspeed reportedly traded more than $4 billion worth of Nvidia chips over two years, and the Commerce Department's Bureau of Industry and Security opened its own investigation in 2024 into whether the processors were being redirected to China.

Sources: BusinessToday, November 14, 2026; KLSE Screener, November 14, 2026

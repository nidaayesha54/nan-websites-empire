Nvidia and Jensen Huang Sued by Shareholders Over Alleged Chinese Spy Affair

November 10, 2026

Nvidia and chief executive Jensen Huang have been sued by shareholders over the alleged Chinese spy scandal that has shaken the world's most valuable chipmaker, The Indian Express reported on November 10.

What the lawsuit alleges

The proposed class action, filed in federal court in San Jose, California, by Rosen Law Firm on behalf of investor Mark Kistenmacher, accuses Nvidia of misleading shareholders by failing to disclose Huang's extramarital affair with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based data centre company Megaspeed whom US intelligence agencies reportedly believe was spying for China. Anyone who bought Nvidia securities between October 9, 2025, and November 8, 2026, could be allowed to join the suit. Huang admitted to the affair on Sunday, November 8, and Nvidia's board has opened an independent investigation into whether he was the target of a suspected foreign-intelligence operation.

FBI looking in, stock hit hard

President Donald Trump confirmed on Monday that the FBI is \"looking into\" the matter, the New York Post reported, adding that Huang told him he had no idea his mistress was a suspected Beijing honeypot. China has denied the allegation, calling it unsubstantiated. Nvidia's stock slid more than 4 percent on Monday, the first trading day after the controversy escalated, reducing the company's market capitalisation by approximately $190 billion, according to The Indian Express. Huang is also expected to face questions from Congress, with House committee chairs requesting a joint closed-door briefing on November 18.

Sources: The Indian Express, November 10, 2026; New York Post, November 10, 2026

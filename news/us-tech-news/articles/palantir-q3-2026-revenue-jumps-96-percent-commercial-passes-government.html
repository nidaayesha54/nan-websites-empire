Palantir Revenue Jumps 96% to $2.31 Billion as US Commercial Sales Pass Government

November 3, 2026

Palantir Technologies reported third-quarter 2026 results on Monday, November 2, with revenue of $2.313 billion, up 96 percent year over year and above the roughly $2.18 billion consensus estimate, as its artificial intelligence software demand accelerated, reports said.

A milestone in the business mix

US commercial revenue surged 148 percent year over year to $984 million, passing US government revenue of $953 million, up 96 percent, for the first time in the company's history. Total US revenue reached $1.937 billion, up 119 percent, and GAAP net income more than doubled to $1.128 billion. Adjusted earnings per share were $0.44, ahead of the $0.41 consensus, and adjusted operating income was $1.427 billion, a 62 percent margin.

Guidance raised — but shares still fell

Palantir raised full-year 2026 revenue guidance to $8.530 to $8.534 billion, up from $8.150 to $8.158 billion, and guided fourth-quarter revenue to $2.649 to $2.653 billion. Despite the beat and the raise, shares fell in premarket trading on Tuesday, with analysts pointing to slowing total contract value growth — total contract value rose just 6 percent year over year to $2.918 billion — and to a broader repricing of high-valuation AI names.

Sources: Motley Fool, CNBC via Seoul Economic Daily, Ad-hoc News, November 2-3, 2026

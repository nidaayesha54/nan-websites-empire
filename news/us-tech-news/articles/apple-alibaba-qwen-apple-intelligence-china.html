Apple and Alibaba Bring Qwen AI to Apple Intelligence in China

November 1, 2026

Apple and Alibaba announced on October 31 a partnership to integrate Qwen, Alibaba's artificial intelligence model, into Apple Intelligence for users in China, according to reports on the announcement.

What Chinese users will get

Chinese users will be able to use Qwen's text and image understanding, content generation and other capabilities within Siri, writing tools and visual intelligence. The features will open for testing with the latest iOS developer beta starting November 4 and are set to launch officially for Chinese users with the release of iOS 27.3 in December.

Why the partnership matters

Apple has previously relied on partnerships to bring AI features to markets where its own models face regulatory or competitive constraints, and China is one of its largest markets. The Qwen integration arrives days before Apple reports quarterly earnings on Monday, November 2, when investors will be watching for signs of demand for its devices and AI features. The announcement is a partnership disclosure, not a product launch: the features remain in testing until the December software release.

Sources: Longbridge, October 31, 2026

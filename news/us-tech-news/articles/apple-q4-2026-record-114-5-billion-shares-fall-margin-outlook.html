Apple Posts Record $114.5 Billion Quarter — Then Shares Fall on Margin Warning

November 3, 2026

Apple reported its fiscal 2026 fourth-quarter results on Monday, November 2, after the market closed, posting quarterly revenue of $114.5 billion, up 12 percent year over year, and diluted earnings per share of $2.10, up 14 percent — both ahead of Wall Street expectations of about $113.4 billion and $1.99, Apple and Reuters reported.

A record quarter, led by iPhone and Services

iPhone revenue rose about 17 percent to a September-quarter record of $57.58 billion, Mac revenue rose 24 percent to $10.8 billion, and Services revenue reached a new all-time high of $31.57 billion. Gross margin was 47.9 percent, including a favourable impact of about one percentage point from tariff refunds. Full-year revenue reached a record $479 billion, and the board declared a dividend of $0.27 per share, payable November 16 to shareholders of record on November 12.

Why the stock fell anyway

On the earnings call, chief executive John Ternus — in his first call since succeeding Tim Cook on September 1 — called current memory-price increases unprecedented and expected them to worsen. Apple guided December-quarter gross margin to 45 to 46 percent, down from 47.9 percent, and Services growth of 8 to 9 percent, below the 10.7 percent analysts expected, while still forecasting 7 to 10 percent total revenue growth, which would be a record holiday quarter. Shares fell as much as 4.43 percent during the call before recovering to close after hours down about 2.8 percent at $328.81.

Sources: Apple press release, Reuters, 9to5Mac, Invezz, November 2-3, 2026

GTA 6 Map Size: How Big Is Leonida, Really?

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

How big is the GTA 6 map? It is the question every player asks before they even start the game, and the answer is messier than the headlines suggest. Rockstar has said the state of Leonida is about twice the size of GTA 5's map and about three times the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2 — but it has never published an area in square kilometres. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and this guide separates what Rockstar actually said from the fan estimates, gives you the one serious post-launch measurement, and tells you how long it really takes to cross Leonida — on foot, by car, and by plane.

The confusion is understandable, because the numbers online disagree wildly. Some guides say 160 square kilometres, some say 175, some say 83, and some quote figures that were built from leaked footage years before launch. They cannot all be right. The honest answer is that the map is huge, the official numbers are comparisons rather than measurements, and the only number measured inside the released game comes from a single guide — so this article labels every figure for what it is.

What Rockstar Actually Said

The official numbers all come from Rockstar North's co-studio heads, speaking in interviews around the August 2026 gameplay reveal and the September 2026 Game Informer cover story. Rob Nelson, the game's head of development, told YouTuber TGG that the map is about twice the size of GTA 5's map and about three times the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2. He went further on the details: Vice City alone is about twice the size of Los Santos, and the built-up areas outside the city — the suburbs, small towns and settlements across Leonida — add up to about eleven times what GTA 5 had outside Los Santos. Aaron Garbut, the other co-studio head, told Game Informer the same "about twice GTA 5" figure and called Leonida Rockstar's biggest map yet, with more interiors and more density than any game the studio has made.

The newest comparison came in Edge magazine's October cover story, where Rockstar said Leonida's total landmass is nearly as large as the maps of GTA 4, GTA 5 and Red Dead Redemption 2 combined. Note the wording: nearly as large, not bigger — and "about twice" and "about three times" are rounded comparisons given in interviews, not survey measurements. Rockstar has never published a square-kilometre figure for Leonida, and it never published one for GTA 5 either. Every absolute number you see is someone else's maths, built on top of Rockstar's ratios.

The 175 km² Measurement — and Why It Is the Only One

After launch, the first measurement of the in-game map appeared, and it is the only post-launch measurement so far: PowerPyx measured Leonida at 175 square kilometres, about 67.5 square miles, roughly 16 by 10.9 kilometres, with about 35% of that area water. Take away the water and the simple maths gives about 114 square kilometres of land. It is a single source, measured from the in-game map, and no second independent measurement has appeared yet — so credit it to PowerPyx and treat it as the best available figure, not as settled fact.

The pre-launch estimates are history now, and they show why the single measurement matters. Before launch, fan estimates of 83 to 170 square kilometres circulated, built from trailers and leaked footage — the long-running community mapping project sat at about 125 square kilometres of land, and the simple "twice GTA 5" maths gave roughly 100 square kilometres of land or 150 to 160 counting water the way most GTA 5 figures do. Those were reasonable guesses at the time, but they were guesses. The 175 figure is measured from the game itself, and until a second measurement appears, it is the number to use — with its source attached.

How Long Does It Take to Cross?

The numbers become real when you translate them into travel time. Kotaku timed a full south-to-north crossing of Leonida at 3 hours and 21 minutes on foot, about 11 minutes by car, and under 6 minutes by plane. That is the scale of the state in the terms that matter to a player: walking it takes an evening, driving it takes a coffee break, and flying it takes a loading screen and a song.

It also explains why fast travel matters so much in this game. With a map this size, driving everywhere is a choice, not a requirement — the RydeMe app, Paradise Taxis, buses and trains all exist because Rockstar knows the state is too big to cross on foot every time. And it explains the six regions: Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park. Each one is a different kind of place — city, islands, swamp, faded resort town, sugar country and national park — and the size is not empty space. It is six different worlds stitched into one state.

What "Twice GTA 5" Feels Like in Play

The ratio that matters most in practice is not the total area but the built-up area. Vice City alone being twice Los Santos means the city itself is a bigger, denser place to drive through — and the eleven-times figure for the built-up areas outside the city means the suburbs and towns are where the real growth is. GTA 5 had one big city and a mostly empty countryside; Leonida has a bigger city and eleven times as much built-up world around it. That is why the map feels bigger than the raw numbers suggest: it is not just more land, it is more places.

Frequently Asked Questions

How big is the GTA 6 map in square kilometres?

Rockstar has not published an official figure. The only post-launch measurement is PowerPyx's 175 square kilometres, about 16 by 10.9 kilometres, with roughly 35% water. Rockstar's own numbers are comparisons: about twice GTA 5 and about three times the playable area of Red Dead Redemption 2.

Is Vice City bigger than Los Santos?

Yes — about twice the size, according to Rob Nelson at Rockstar North. And the built-up areas outside the city are about eleven times what GTA 5 had outside Los Santos.

How long does it take to cross the whole map?

Kotaku timed it at 3 hours 21 minutes on foot, about 11 minutes by car, and under 6 minutes by plane, south to north. That is why the fast-travel options — RydeMe, taxis, buses and trains — matter so much in this game.

The Bottom Line

Leonida is about twice GTA 5 and three times Red Dead Redemption 2's playable area, Vice City alone doubles Los Santos, and the best measured figure is 175 square kilometres with about a third of it water. Those are the numbers that matter — the official comparisons from Rockstar, the one measurement from inside the game, and the crossing times that turn them into something you can feel. Ignore the pre-launch estimates; they were guesses, and the game is out now.

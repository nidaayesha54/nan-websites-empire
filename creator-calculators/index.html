Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you calculate.

Are these calculators really free?

Yes. Every calculator is 100% free with no signup, no account, and no usage limits. Your numbers never leave your browser.

How accurate is the freelance rate calculator?

It uses the standard industry formula: (desired income + expenses + taxes) ÷ billable hours. It gives you a solid baseline — adjust up for your experience level and local market rates. Our in-depth guide walks through the whole method.

Why does YouTube revenue vary so much by niche?

Advertisers pay more to reach high-value audiences. Finance and business channels earn CPMs of $15–$30, while lifestyle channels earn $3–$8. Geography matters too — US/UK/CA/AU viewers pay the most.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser with JavaScript. Nothing is uploaded, tracked, or stored on our servers.

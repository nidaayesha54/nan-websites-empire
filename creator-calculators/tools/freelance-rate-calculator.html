How the freelance rate formula works

Gross needed = (Take-home goal + Expenses) ÷ (1 − Tax rate)
Hourly rate = Gross needed ÷ Billable hours

Example: you want $3,000 take-home, spend $400 on expenses, pay 20% tax, and bill 100 hours/month:

($3,000 + $400) ÷ (1 − 0.20) = $4,375 gross
$4,375 ÷ 100 hours = $43.75/hour minimum

Why most freelancers undercharge

  • They forget taxes. A 20–30% tax rate means you must earn far more than your target take-home.
  • They count all hours as billable. Realistically only 50–70% of work hours are billable.
  • They skip the buffer. Unpaid revisions and admin eat profit — price a 15% cushion in.

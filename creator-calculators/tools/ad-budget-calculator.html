💡 Rule of thumb: keep ad cost ≤ 30% of revenue for a healthy margin. Start with the 14-day test budget to gather data, then scale winners to the daily budget. Meta needs ~50 conversions/week to optimize — the test budget covers that.

How the ad budget formula works

Max CPA = Product price × 0.30 (keep 30% margin)
Daily budget = (Target sales × Max CPA) ÷ 30
14-day test = Max( $20/day × 14, Max CPA × 50 conversions )

Example: $49 product, 100 sales/month target → max CPA $14.70 → monthly spend $1,470 → $49/day. A 14-day test at ~$50/day buys ~50 conversions — enough for Meta’s algorithm to optimize.

For lead generation

Value per lead = Deal value × Close rate
Max CPL = Value per lead × 0.30

Example: $500 deals, 10% close rate → each lead worth $50 → max CPL $15.

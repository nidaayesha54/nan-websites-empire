How YouTube revenue is calculated

Monthly revenue = (Monthly views ÷ 1,000) × RPM × Geography factor
Yearly revenue = Monthly revenue × 12

RPM (revenue per mille) is what you earn per 1,000 views after YouTube takes its 45% cut. It’s driven by your niche and where your viewers live — a finance channel with a US audience can earn 8–10× more per view than an entertainment channel with a global audience.

Why niche matters more than views

  • Finance ($12–$25 RPM): banks and brokers pay premium CPMs to reach investors.
  • Tech ($8–$15 RPM): SaaS companies bid aggressively for buyer audiences.
  • Entertainment ($2–$4 RPM): huge views, but advertisers pay little for casual viewers.

100K monthly views in finance ≈ $1,200–$2,500/mo. The same views in entertainment ≈ $200–$400/mo.

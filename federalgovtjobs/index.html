How to Apply

1
Open the FPSC advertisement and confirm you meet the qualification and age criteria.
2
Apply online on fpsc.gov.pk and download the test fee challan.
3
Deposit the fee at the designated bank and keep the original receipt.
4
Download your admission certificate and appear for the MCQ screening test.
5
Pass the descriptive test and interview; final recommendations are posted on FPSC.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for federal government jobs?

Most federal posts are advertised by FPSC; apply online, pay the fee challan, and appear for the test.

What is the FPSC test pattern?

Usually an MCQ screening test, followed by a descriptive exam and interview for BS-17 posts.

Is there provincial quota in federal jobs?

Yes, federal posts follow the provincial/regional quota system announced in each advertisement.

What documents are needed at interview?

Original CNIC, domicile, degrees, fee receipt, and experience certificates if required.

Are these listings official advertisements?

Listings here are illustrative samples. Always verify on the official FPSC portal.

Can I apply for multiple posts?

Yes, but submit a separate application and fee for each post.

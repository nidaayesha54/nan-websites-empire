Ministry of Planning APS & Accountant Jobs 2026 — Apply by 2 Nov

The Ministry of Planning, Development & Special Initiatives is hiring an Assistant Private Secretary (APS) and an Accountant, one vacancy each at PPS-6 equivalent scale, under the project "Strengthening of M/o PD&SI in M&E of PSDP" — the ministry's monitoring and evaluation arm for the Public Sector Development Programme.

According to the live National Jobs Portal listing checked on 22 October 2026, both posts close on 2 November 2026. The engagement is purely contract-based and merit-driven — these are not permanent civil posts.

Honesty flag: the closing date differs across sources. The NJP portal listing showed "Available Till Nov 02, 2026" when checked on 22 October 2026, while some aggregators read the ad's "within 15 days of publication" instruction as 4–5 November 2026. Treat the live NJP listing as authoritative and re-confirm the closing date before you submit.

The Project

The two posts are advertised against "Strengthening of M/o PD&SI in M&E of PSDP", the ministry's monitoring-and-evaluation capacity effort for the Public Sector Development Programme — the vehicle for federal development spending.

The posts are filled on a purely contract and merit basis, with detailed terms of reference on the ministry's website, pc.gov.pk.

Posts Announced

Both are single-vacancy contract posts at PPS-6 equivalent scale:

  • APS, EQV. PPS-6 — BA/BSc, shorthand 80/40 wpm minimum, 2 years' relevant experience; max age 50.
  • Accountant, EQV. PPS-6 — MBA (Finance)/M.Com/CA Inter/ACCA Foundation, 1 year of cash-book and AGPR reconciliation experience; max age 45.

Government servants apply through the proper channel. Incomplete applications are rejected, only short-listed applicants are interviewed, and no TA/DA is admissible.

A Related Post: Chief Statistician (SPPS-II)

The same ministry is also hiring a Chief Statistician — also the CEO of the Pakistan Bureau of Statistics — on SPPS-II for a five-year term in Islamabad.

Requirements: an advanced degree (preferably PhD) in statistics/economics; 20 years of experience with 5 in senior management; max age 60. Unlike the NJP posts, this application goes by post to the Joint Secretary (PBS), Room 218, 2nd Floor, P-Block, Pak Secretariat, Islamabad, within 15 days of publication.

Honesty flag: the Chief Statistician deadline differs across sources — one aggregator estimated about 25 October 2026 while newspaper-style listings say 26 October 2026. Verify the exact closing date on the official listing before applying.

Eligibility Snapshot

  • APS: BA/BSc; shorthand 80/40 wpm; 2 years' experience; max age 50.
  • Accountant: MBA (Finance)/M.Com/CA Inter/ACCA Foundation; 1 year of cash-book and AGPR reconciliation experience; max age 45.
  • Chief Statistician (separate notice): advanced statistics/economics degree; 20 years' experience; max age 60.

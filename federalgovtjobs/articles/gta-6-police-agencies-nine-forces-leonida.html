GTA 6's 9 Police Agencies: Every Force in Leonida

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Leonida does not have one police force — it has nine. Fans who went through the first trailer frame by frame found nine different agency names on cars, uniforms and boats, and the game's world is built around that layered system: city police in the city, sheriffs in the countryside, state troopers on the highways, and a federal force on the water. No GTA map has ever been policed this way before.

That matters because the police you meet depends on where you are. In GTA 5, the countryside had sheriffs and the city had the LSPD, and that was mostly it. In GTA 6, every part of the map has its own force with its own vehicles and its own look, and learning who patrols where is the difference between a clean getaway and a chase you cannot shake.

City Police: Four Departments

The city layer has four departments that fans have identified. The Vice City Police Department is the primary force in Vice City itself, with white sedans and SUVs patrolling the neon streets. The Vice-Dale Police Department covers Vice-Dale County, the broader area around the city — though its name appears only on a Trailer 1 exit sign, so it should be treated as less certain than the others. Ocean Beach Police Department and Port Gellhorn Police Department cover their own neighbourhoods, and the Vice Beach Police Department has its own supercar, the Tempesta, which Rockstar and West Coast Customs built as a real-life car on November 6, 2026, alongside five other GTA 6 vehicles.

These are the police you will meet most often, because most of the game's missions and side activities happen in and around Vice City. They are also the police with the most vehicles in the game: the trailers show a Bravado Buffalo STX Pursuit, a standard Police Cruiser, the armoured Police Riot truck and a range of police SUVs, all wearing different liveries for different departments.

County and State: Sheriffs, Troopers and Prisons

Outside the city, the county layer takes over. The Leonard County Sheriff's Office patrols the countryside with green-and-white SUVs, and the Kelly County Police Department covers its own area. An Ambrosia County Sheriff has also been spotted on a road sign, which would make a third county force. This is the GTA 5 countryside pattern taken further: leave the city and the police change, and so do the vehicles, the uniforms and the response.

The state layer has two agencies. The Leonida Highway Patrol is the state highway patrol, modelled on the Florida Highway Patrol, with yellow sedans on the highways. The Leonida Department of Corrections runs the prison system, including Leonida Penitentiary — the prison Lucia is released from at the start of the story, and the place Jason drives to collect her in the second trailer. Then there is POACH, the Protection of Animals and Controlled Hunting agency, Leonida's wildlife enforcement, which serves as the equivalent of Florida's Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission. POACH is the one that will matter if you go hunting in the swamps.

The Federal Layer and What It Means for You

The federal layer is the United States Coast Guard, which operates in the coastal waters around Vice City and the Leonida Keys, with a response boat inspired by the real RB-S II confirmed in vehicle listings. That completes the nine: four city departments, two county forces, two state agencies and one federal force, all visible across the trailers and the screenshots Rockstar released in May 2025.

One warning matters more than the list itself: these names come from fans, not from an official Rockstar list. Rockstar has never published a roster of GTA 6 law enforcement agencies, and the agencies are identified from what is visible on cars, uniforms and boats in the trailers. The list is the best reading of the evidence, not an official document — and the FIB, the IAA and NOOSE are notably absent from it, which means none of them has been shown in the game at all.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many police agencies are in GTA 6?

Nine have been spotted by fans in the trailers: four city departments, two county forces, two state agencies and the US Coast Guard, plus the POACH wildlife agency. Rockstar has not published an official list.

Which police force patrols the countryside?

The Leonard County Sheriff's Office and the Kelly County Police Department, with an Ambrosia County Sheriff also spotted on a road sign. The Leonida Highway Patrol covers the highways.

Is the FIB one of the nine agencies?

No. The FIB has not been shown in any GTA 6 trailer or screenshot. The nine agencies fans identified do not include it, the IAA or NOOSE.

The Bottom Line

Leonida is policed in layers, and every layer has a name, a vehicle and a territory. Learn which force patrols where you are, and you will know what kind of chase you are in before the sirens start — and remember that the list comes from fans reading the trailers, not from Rockstar, so treat it as the best map we have, not the official one.

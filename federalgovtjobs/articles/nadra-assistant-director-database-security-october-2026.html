NADRA Assistant Director (Database Security) Jobs 2026: Eligibility, Requirements — Apply by 25 Oct 2026

NADRA's Headquarters in Islamabad is hiring an Assistant Director (Database Security) under the NADRA Employees (Service) Regulations, 2002 — online applications close on 25 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Federal Government Jobs Pakistan » Articles

The National Database & Registration Authority (NADRA), Ministry of Interior & Narcotics Control, has announced a specialist vacancy at its Headquarters, G-5/2 Islamabad. Under Regulations 9, 10 and 11 of the NADRA Employees (Service) Regulations, 2002, NADRA is seeking a highly skilled and experienced professional for the position of Assistant Director (Database Security). The deadline for submission of applications is 25 October 2026, and only online applications will be accepted.

Database security sits at the heart of NADRA's mission — the Authority guards the national citizen database, so this role carries genuine national-level responsibility and is a strong career move for information-security professionals.

Note: This is a specialist Headquarters post under the NADRA Employees (Service) Regulations, 2002 — it is not part of the district-based contract recruitment announced separately for KP districts.

Educational Requirements

  • Bachelor's (4 years) in Computer Science, Information Technology, Cyber Security or Information Security, OR Electrical/Electronics Engineering with relevant IS certifications, or an equivalent qualification in a related field — from an HEC-recognised university.
  • Degrees must be attested by HEC; attested educational documents must be provided at the time of joining.

Experience Requirements

  • Minimum 2 years of post-qualification experience in a reputable organisation, in any of the following areas:
    • Information Security
    • Information Technology
    • Database Administration
    • Network Device Administration

The experience bar is deliberately modest at two years — but it must be post-qualification and in a reputable organisation, so internships and unrelated IT support work may not count. Document your experience precisely against one of the four listed areas.

General Terms & Conditions

  • 5 years general age relaxation is already included in the 37-year age limit.
  • Attested educational documents from HEC or the respective regulatory body must be provided at joining.
  • If the selected candidate declines the offer, it may be extended to reserve candidates.
  • Selected candidates must provide medical fitness and character certificates.
  • No TA/DA is admissible for the test/interview.
  • Electronic gadgets such as mobile phones and smart watches are not allowed during the test/interview.
  • Females, minorities, transgender persons and differently-abled candidates are encouraged to apply.

Why This Post Matters

NADRA's databases underpin CNIC issuance, voter lists, immigration systems and a growing web of government services — making database security one of the most sensitive technology roles in Pakistan's public sector. For a CS/IT or cyber-security graduate with two-plus years of solid experience, this Assistant Director post offers a rare direct entry into a federal institution at officer level, with the professional credibility that comes from securing national-scale systems. With the 25 October 2026 deadline approaching, eligible professionals should prepare their HEC-attested documents and apply online without delay.

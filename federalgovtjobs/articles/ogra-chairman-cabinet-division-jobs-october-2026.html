OGRA Chairman Jobs 2026: Cabinet Division Invites Applications — SPPS-I, 20 Years Experience, Last Date 30 Oct

The Cabinet Division, Government of Pakistan, has invited applications for the position of Chairman, Oil and Gas Regulatory Authority (OGRA) — the top leadership post of the country's oil and gas regulator. The appointment requires a Pakistani citizen with at least a master's degree (minimum 16 years of education) in a relevant discipline and at least 20 years of relevant experience, including three years as the head of a public or private sector organization. The advertisement was published in The News International on 16 October 2026, and eligible candidates must apply online through the National Job Portal within 15 days of publication.

OGRA regulates the oil and gas sector — licensing, tariffs, standards and consumer protection across the petroleum value chain. Its chairman leads the authority's operations and the implementation of regulatory policy, making this one of the most senior regulatory appointments of the year.

Who Should Apply

This is a senior leadership appointment, not an entry or mid-career post. The ideal profile is a Pakistani professional — from regulation, energy, law, finance, engineering or public administration — who has already led an organization and understands the petroleum sector's regulatory landscape. The three-year “head of organization” condition is the real filter: it rules out candidates whose experience, however long, is entirely in subordinate roles.

The age ceiling of 61 and the SPPS-I remuneration place this in the same league as other federal regulatory appointments — the package reflects the seniority of the role rather than a BPS scale.

How to Apply

1
Open the National Job Portal at njp.gov.pk and find the Chairman OGRA advertisement under the Cabinet Division.
2
Verify you meet every condition — 16 years of HEC-recognized education, 20 years of relevant experience, 3 years as head of an organization, age within the 61-year ceiling on the closing date.
3
Prepare your documents — degrees, experience certificates (clearly showing the head-of-organization tenure), CNIC and photographs.
4
Submit online within the 15-day window. Late applications are not entertained.
Apply only through the National Job Portal. The Cabinet Division does not accept applications by post, email or through intermediaries.

Why the OGRA Chairmanship Matters

The chairman of OGRA sits at the intersection of energy policy and market regulation: the authority licenses operators, determines tariffs, enforces standards and handles consumer complaints across oil and gas. With the energy sector's ongoing tariff and licensing questions, the next chairman's term will shape how Pakistan's petroleum market is regulated for four years. For the rare professional who meets the 20-year-plus-leadership bar, this is a capstone appointment — the kind of role that defines a career. If that is you, prepare the file now: the 15-day window from 16 October closes fast.

FPSC Digitalization Project IT Jobs 2026 — 20 Contract Posts

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) has announced contract-based jobs under its PSDP-funded project titled "Digitalization of In-House Processes & Automation of Examination System of FPSC", approved by the Central Development Working Party of the Ministry of Planning, Development & Special Initiatives.

About 20 specialized posts are reported on Project Pay Scales (PPS), from a Project Director/Project Manager at PPS-10 to software developers and system/network engineers at PPS-7 — all contract-based, one year extendable, on competitive lump-sum packages reported well above equivalent BPS salaries.

The Project

The hiring is tied to a PSDP development project, not to permanent FPSC posts. "Digitalization of In-House Processes & Automation of Examination System of FPSC" is approved by the Central Development Working Party of the Ministry of Planning, Development & Special Initiatives. It reportedly aims to digitize the Commission's internal processes and automate its examination system end to end.

Posts Announced

The reported posts, by Project Pay Scale, are:

  • Project Director / Project Manager (up to PPS-10) — project lead; reported to need public-sector IT management plus PPRA procurement experience.
  • QA Manager (PPS-8) — quality assurance leadership.
  • Database Specialist (PPS-8) — RDBMS administration and disaster-recovery planning.
  • Information / Cyber Security Officer (PPS-8) — risk assessment, encryption and vulnerability testing.
  • Software Developers (PPS-7) — reportedly React, Angular, JavaScript, Node.js, Python, Java and PHP.
  • System / Network Engineers (PPS-7) — networks, firewalls and cloud infrastructure.
  • Subject Specialists — English, Mathematics & General Ability, Computer Science & IT, Management Sciences & Public Administration and Medical & Allied Health, for exam test-bank compilation.

Contract Terms

All reported posts are contract appointments for one year, extendable on competitive lump-sum PPS packages, reported well above the equivalent Basic Pay Scales. These are not permanent FPSC posts.

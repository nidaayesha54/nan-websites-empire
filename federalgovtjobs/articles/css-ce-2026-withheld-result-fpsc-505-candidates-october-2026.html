FPSC Declares CSS CE-2026 Withheld Result: 2 of 505 Candidates Qualify

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) has settled one of the longest-running CSS result disputes of the year. Under public notice No. F.2/23/2026-CE dated 18 September 2026, the Full Commission (183rd meeting) decided the representations filed by 505 candidates whose CSS CE-2026 written examination results had been withheld over eligibility. The outcome: 2 roll numbers were declared qualified, while 503 were declared non-qualified — a tally reported by the CSS portal covering the announcement, counted from the FPSC's own annexes.

Honesty note: the 2-versus-503 breakdown is the reporting portal's own count of the roll numbers in the FPSC annexes, not a figure quoted in the notice text itself. If you are one of the 505 candidates, verify your own roll number against the official FPSC lists rather than relying on anyone else's tally.

What a "Withheld" Result Actually Means

"Withheld" does not mean the FPSC lost your paper — it means the FPSC deliberately did not declare the written result because the candidate was found ineligible under the CSS CE Rules 2019. Eligibility issues — age, education, documents, quota or other rule-based disqualifications — freeze the result before it is announced, and the candidate is only dealt with once their representation is decided.

This also changes how the 2 qualified roll numbers should be read. Even a qualified roll number only means the written part was passed — subject to eligibility scrutiny. Under Rule 11(20) of the CSS CE Rules 2019, an ineligible candidate's candidature is cancelled whether or not they appeared in or qualified the written examination. Passing the written paper does not cure an eligibility defect; it only clears the written hurdle while the eligibility question is resolved separately.

What Happens Next for the Qualifying Candidates

The notice's logic is simple: only candidates who establish their eligibility proceed to the remaining stages of the examination — the medical examination, the psychological assessment and the viva voce. The 2 qualified roll numbers are therefore the only ones from the 505 whose journey can continue, and even they must satisfy FPSC's eligibility scrutiny before moving forward.

For the 503 non-qualified candidates, the Commission's decision closes the withheld-result chapter of CE-2026. Aspirants in that group should weigh their options carefully: depending on the reason behind the decision, the honest path may be preparing afresh for the CSS 2027 cycle — the MPT for which has already been held — rather than spending another year on representations.

Key takeaway: 2 of 505 is a brutal ratio, and it underscores how strictly FPSC applies eligibility rules. For future cycles, settle every eligibility doubt before applying — a withheld result is the costliest way to discover a disqualification.

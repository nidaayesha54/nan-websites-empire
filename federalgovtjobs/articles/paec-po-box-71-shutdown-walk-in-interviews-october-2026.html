PAEC PO Box 71 Jobs 2026: Nuclear Shutdown Walk-In Interviews 21 Oct–6 Nov — Technicians, Scientific Assistants & Drivers

A public sector organization associated with Pakistan Atomic Energy (advertised as P.O. Box No. 71, Karachi) has announced walk-in interviews for shutdown-activity posts at Chashma, Multan and Karachi. The interviews run from 21 October to 6 November 2026, with each post assigned its own interview date and city. The advertisement was published on 11 October 2026. Posts cover Tech-I, Senior Technician, Scientific Assistant-I, Junior Assistant-I, Caretaker-III, Driver-III, Special Vehicle Operator and Cook-II/Tandoorchi/Waiter-II — a mix of technical, administrative and support roles for a Karachi-based shutdown posting.

Nuclear power-plant shutdowns are intensive maintenance windows: the plants pause, specialist teams move in, and hundreds of technicians, assistants and support staff are engaged for the duration. These contingent engagements — 80 to 110 days at fixed wages — are a genuine entry into the nuclear power sector's workforce.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 18 to 35 years, relaxable for candidates with relevant technical expertise and shutdown/turnaround maintenance experience.
  • Qualification: relevant technical education and experience as per the post — technicians need the relevant trade/technical background; Scientific Assistant-I needs the relevant science qualification; Jr. Assistant-I needs admin/accounts competence; Driver-III and Special Vehicle Operator need the relevant licence and driving experience.
  • Employment terms: 80 to 110 days, on a contingent basis with fixed wages; selected candidates are posted at Karachi for the shutdown activity.
  • TA/DA: no TA/DA is admissible for appearing in the interview.

The official notice describes the employer only as “A Public Sector Organization” — however, the interview venues, Chashma (site of the Chashma Nuclear Power Plant) and KANUPP Colony, Karachi (site of the Karachi Nuclear Power Plant), are both PAEC-associated locations.

How to Appear in the Walk-In Interview

These are walk-in interviews — no online application. Follow these steps:

1
Match your post and date. Check the eligibility criteria and the interview schedule for your relevant field and position in the advertisement.
2
Prepare your documents: original educational degrees, DAE/diplomas, experience certificates, CNIC and domicile — plus one set of attested photocopies and passport-size photographs.
3
Report on time: reach the designated venue in Chashma, Multan or Karachi between 08:00 AM and 02:00 PM on the specified date for your post.
4
Confirm queries in advance: contact the Sr. Admin Officer (RFO), P.O. Box No. 71, Karachi — inquiry tel 021-99241469 (Ext: 3160 & 3195).
Important: Appear only on the date and at the venue assigned to your post — the schedule differs by post and city. Verify against the official advertisement before travelling.

Why Shutdown Work Matters

Shutdown and turnaround maintenance is a specialised world: plants schedule it in tight windows, and the technicians who keep those windows do some of the most demanding industrial work there is — instrumentation calibration, mechanical overhauls, HVAC servicing and round-the-clock support. For DAE holders and technicians, an 80–110 day shutdown engagement inside the nuclear power sector is career capital: the safety culture, the documentation standards and the discipline of nuclear-plant work read well on any industrial CV. Administrative and driver staff get a shorter but equally real exposure to a high-security federal organization. Appear prepared, documents in order, on the right date — walk-ins reward the punctual.

Pakistan Digital Authority PDA Jobs October 2026: 6 Positions in Islamabad — Apply by Email

The Pakistan Digital Authority is recruiting accounting and cybersecurity professionals for its Islamabad team — six positions from accounting associates to a senior penetration tester, with applications by email to jobs@pda.gov.pk.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Federal Government Jobs Pakistan » Articles

The Pakistan Digital Authority (PDA) — the independent, autonomous federal body driving Pakistan's digital transformation — is building its team in Islamabad. Its latest recruitment notice lists six positions, ranging from an accounting associate to a senior penetration tester. Applications go directly to jobs@pda.gov.pk, and PDA will review applications received within the first 15 days of the advertisement's publication — so interested professionals should apply immediately.

PDA's mandate is to enable the people of Pakistan to become a digital nation — leveraging digital technologies, responsible use of data, innovative service delivery models and robust digital public infrastructure to accelerate sustainable economic development, improve citizen well-being and modernise governance. To do this, it is hiring exceptional professionals with vision, integrity and a proven track record in technology, policy, operations and digital transformation.

Eligibility — What PDA Is Looking For

PDA is hiring professionals rather than filling a standard gazetted cadre, so the bar is set by role rather than by a uniform age or degree rule. Based on the positions announced:

  • Accounting positions: Suit candidates with accounting and financial-reporting backgrounds — degrees in accounting, finance or commerce with relevant professional experience in reporting and analysis.
  • SOC Engineer / Security Operations Engineer: Suit candidates with hands-on experience in security operations centres, threat monitoring, incident response and security tooling.
  • Senior Penetration Tester: A specialist role for experienced offensive-security professionals with proven penetration-testing and vulnerability-assessment experience.
  • Data Protection Officer: Suits professionals with knowledge of data governance, privacy frameworks and compliance.

Candidates should map their CV to the specific Job ID they are targeting — PDA explicitly asks for the Job ID and position name in the email subject, which means generic applications risk being missed.

How to Apply — Email Application Process

Unlike commission-based federal recruitment, PDA accepts applications by email. Follow these steps:

1
Prepare a cover letter and a CV of up to four pages, tailored to the position and Job ID you are applying for.
2
Write the Job ID and position name in the email subject line (for example: “PDA-J0163 — Associate SOC Engineer”).
3
Send the application to jobs@pda.gov.pk.
4
Apply within the first 15 days of the advertisement's publication — PDA reviews applications in this window first.
Important: PDA states that recruitment may continue beyond the initial 15-day review period if a position remains unfilled, but the first review happens within 15 days of publication — early applicants get the first look.

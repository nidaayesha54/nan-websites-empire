Ministry of Railways Jobs 2026: Director (PPS-10) & Deputy Director (PPS-09) — Apply via NJP by 30 Oct

The Ministry of Railways, Government of Pakistan, has advertised two contract posts in Islamabad: Director / Business Development (PPS-10) and Deputy Director (Financial Analyst) (PPS-09). Both posts sit in the Strengthening of Planning, Development, Monitoring and Evaluation Directorate (SPD&ME) project. Candidates apply online through the National Job Portal (njp.gov.pk), and the last date is 30 October 2026 as listed on Pakistan Railways' official careers page.

These are project-pay-scale posts for experienced professionals: the Director slot asks for 12+ years of experience and the Deputy Director slot for 7+ years post-qualification experience. The SPD&ME project is the ministry's internal reform engine — planning, development, monitoring and evaluation — so the two hires will work on how Pakistan Railways plans and tracks its own development portfolio.

Who Should Apply

  • Director / Business Development (PPS-10): an MBA/MS professional with 12 or more years of relevant experience — someone who has built or grown businesses, partnerships or commercial operations and can bring that discipline into a federal ministry's planning function.
  • Deputy Director (Financial Analyst) (PPS-09): an MS Finance, MSc Economics, ACCA or CA professional with 7+ years of post-qualification experience — the financial modelling and analysis counterpart inside the same directorate.

Both posts are contract appointments under PPS, so the compensation follows the project's pay structure rather than BPS — check the TORs for the package.

How to Apply

1
Open the National Job Portal at njp.gov.pk and search for the Ministry of Railways SPD&ME posts.
2
Read the detailed TORs for your post — qualification, experience, pay package and posting conditions are spelled out there.
3
Complete the online application with your educational record, experience history, CNIC and documents.
4
Submit before 30 October 2026. Only online applications through NJP are accepted.
Posting flexibility: although the project is based in Islamabad, selected candidates may be posted anywhere in Pakistan — apply only if you can relocate.

Because these are PPS contract posts rather than BPS appointments, they are designed for mid-career professionals rather than fresh graduates: the ministry is buying 12 and 7 years of proven experience for a reform project, not training beginners. Candidates already in the private sector — consultants, bankers, finance managers, business development heads — are the natural applicant pool. Review the TORs carefully for the package, the contract duration and the reporting structure, then submit a complete online application before 30 October 2026.

Why These Posts Matter

The SPD&ME directorate is where Pakistan Railways decides what to build, how to fund it and whether it is working — planning, development, monitoring and evaluation in one place. A Director for Business Development inside that function suggests the ministry wants commercial thinking in its planning: partnerships, revenue ideas, project structuring. The Financial Analyst post provides the numbers discipline behind it. For senior professionals — particularly chartered accountants and finance specialists — PPS-09/PPS-10 project posts in a federal ministry are a way to work at policy scale without a permanent civil-service entry. Two seats, one deadline: 30 October 2026.

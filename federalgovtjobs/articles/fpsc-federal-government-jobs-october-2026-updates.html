How FPSC Consolidated Advertisements Work

FPSC announces federal government vacancies through consolidated advertisements published every month, each carrying a number (e.g. Consolidated Advertisement No. 10/2026). A single advertisement bundles posts from ministries, divisions and federal departments — from assistants and stenographers to section officers, doctors, engineers and lecturers. Each advertisement fixes:

  • The post-wise breakup with department, basic pay scale and number of vacancies
  • Qualification, experience and age limits for every post
  • Quota distribution (provincial/regional, women, minority quotas)
  • The closing date for online applications — usually about a month after publication
  • The test fee and payment method

What "Phase-III/2026" Means: FPSC Test Phases

After applications close, FPSC groups the screening and written tests into phases announced through public notices. A phase covers the tests for a batch of advertisements at centres across the country. The phase notice tells candidates their test dates, centres and reporting instructions, and admission certificates are uploaded to the candidate dashboard before each phase begins.

Typical FPSC Screening Test Pattern

For most posts, FPSC holds an MCQ-based screening test — commonly 100 marks covering English, general knowledge, Pakistan studies, Islamiat, basic mathematics and the subject relevant to the post. Candidates who qualify are shortlisted for the descriptive examination (for higher posts), followed by psychological assessment and interview. Final merit is determined under the Commission's regulations.

How to Track New FPSC Announcements

1
Check fpsc.gov.pk regularly — new consolidated advertisements, phase notices and press releases appear in the announcements section.
2
Read the full advertisement PDF before applying; portal summaries cannot replace the official eligibility terms.
3
Note the closing date of each advertisement and apply at least a few days early.
4
Download your admission certificate from the FPSC dashboard as soon as it is uploaded for your phase.
5
Follow NAN Jobs Portal for plain-language summaries of each new FPSC announcement with key dates.
KP candidates: your 12–19 October 2026 test is postponed, not cancelled. Keep preparing and watch fpsc.gov.pk for the revised schedule.

What FPSC Candidates Should Do This Month

October is a high-activity window for federal recruitment, so a little organisation now pays off all year:

  • Complete your one-time registration on fpsc.gov.pk if you have not already — you cannot apply for any advertisement without it.
  • Keep a deadline diary: every consolidated advertisement has its own closing date; missing one means waiting a full month for the next cycle.
  • Prepare the screening syllabus in parallel: English, general knowledge and your subject area are tested in almost every FPSC paper.
  • Verify fee receipts: keep every bank challan receipt filed with the advertisement number written on it.
  • Ignore rumours: test dates, syllabus changes and results come only from fpsc.gov.pk — forwarded WhatsApp messages about "leaked papers" or "confirmed dates" are invariably fake.

Candidates at KP centres should additionally re-check the website weekly for the revised Phase-III/2026 schedule, while candidates elsewhere should focus fully on their announced test dates.

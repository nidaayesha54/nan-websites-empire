Finance Division Tax Jobs 2026 – 4 Posts, Apply by 15 Nov 2026

The Finance Division, Government of Pakistan, has opened applications for four specialist positions in its Tax Policy Office. According to the advertisement published in Daily Jang on 18 October 2026 (reported by careerjoin.com), the Division is hiring one Tax Lawyer (SPPS-III) and three Analysts for Personal Income Tax (SPPS-III) to strengthen tax policy formulation and administration at the federal level. All four posts will be filled through the National Jobs Portal, and the closing date for online applications is 15 November 2026.

Tax policy roles at the Finance Division are highly competitive openings for law and finance professionals. Because the detailed job descriptions, qualifications and experience for each post are published separately on finance.gov.pk, applicants should study those documents before submitting.

Honesty flag: this article is based on a single job-portal report of the Jang advertisement of 18 October 2026 (careerjoin.com). Detailed job descriptions, qualifications and experience for each post are downloadable from finance.gov.pk — confirm every requirement from the official source before applying.

The Posts on Offer

The advertisement covers two distinct roles, both placed in the Tax Policy Office at the SPPS-III level of the Special Professional Pay Scales:

  • Tax Lawyer (SPPS-III) — 1 post. Full duties, qualifications and experience are in the downloadable job description on finance.gov.pk.
  • Analyst – Personal Income Tax (SPPS-III) — 3 posts. Full duties, qualifications and experience are in the downloadable job description on finance.gov.pk.

Eligibility and Appointment Rules

The advertisement does not spell out degree requirements or years of experience — those are in the detailed job descriptions on finance.gov.pk. Two appointment conditions are stated outright:

  • Establishment Division O.M. No.1/11/2018-E.6 dated 09.04.2024 (as amended) applies to these appointments.
  • Only shortlisted applicants fulfilling the prescribed criteria will be called for interview, and no TA/DA will be admissible for appearing in the interview.

The Finance Division also reserves the right to cancel the recruitment process at any stage without assigning any reason.

What Gets an Application Rejected

The advertisement is explicit about three automatic grounds for rejection:

  • Incomplete applications will not be entertained.
  • Applications with non-relevant qualifications will not be entertained.
  • Applications received after the closing date will not be entertained.

In practice: apply only if your qualification matches the detailed job description, and submit well before the portal closes — late or partial submissions are discarded.

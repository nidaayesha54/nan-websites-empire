SIFC Division Jobs October 2026: Manager (PPS-10) &amp; Research Associate (PPS-07) — Invest Pakistan Project, Last Date 18 Oct 2026

The Special Investment Facilitation Council (SIFC) Division, Cabinet Secretariat (Prime Minister’s Office), Islamabad, has announced contract-based hiring for its PSDP-funded ‘Invest Pakistan’ project: a Manager (HR & Coord/Protocol) on PPS-10 and a Research Associate on PPS-07. Applications close at 11:59 PM PST on 18 October 2026 via sifc.gov.pk.

The Manager post (maximum age 62) needs at least 8 years of post-qualification experience, including 2 years in a lead role covering HR, coordination, protocol and project handling. The Research Associate post (maximum age 45) targets finance graduates with strong analytical and writing skills for financial documentation, summaries and presentations. Both are contract appointments on the Project Pay Scale (PPS).

Eligibility Criteria

  • Education: 16 years of education — MA/MSc/MBA, or a BS degree for the Research Associate post.
  • Experience: Manager: 8+ years post-qualification with 2 years in a lead HR/coordination/protocol role. Research Associate: preferably 3 years.
  • Age limit: Maximum 62 years for the Manager post and 45 years for the Research Associate post.
  • Skills: The Research Associate needs strong analytical and writing skills for financial documentation and presentations.

The Manager role suits a veteran who has run HR and coordination functions and handled protocol at an institutional level. The Research Associate role is for finance professionals who can turn data into crisp documentation — a writing sample will speak louder than the degree.

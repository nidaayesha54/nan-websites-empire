PSEB Jobs 2026 – IT Project Posts, Apply Online by 25 Oct

The Pakistan Software Export Board (PSEB), an organization working under the Ministry of IT and Telecommunication, is hiring staff for its PSDP-funded project titled "Revamping of IT Industry Landscape". Three posts are advertised — Assistant Program Manager–HR Skills (PPS-08), Research Consultant (PPS-08) and Assistant Program Officer–HR Skills (PPS-07) — all on a contract basis for the project. Applications are accepted online only through the National Jobs Portal (njp.gov.pk), and the last date to apply is 25 October 2026.

With the closing date just days away, eligible applicants should not wait for the last day — the portal gets congested near deadlines and late submissions are rejected outright.

Honesty flag: vacancy counts differ across the portals reporting this advertisement. One source confirms two vacancies for the Assistant Program Manager–HR Skills post, while no stated counts are available for the Research Consultant and Assistant Program Officer–HR Skills posts. This article states only what the sources confirm — it does not assume or invent a total.

The Project and the Posts

The hiring is project-based rather than for permanent PSEB posts. "Revamping of IT Industry Landscape" is a Public Sector Development Programme (PSDP) project, and every advertised position is a contract appointment tied to it. The posts are:

  • Assistant Program Manager–HR Skills (PPS-08) — 2 vacancies confirmed by one reporting source.
  • Research Consultant (PPS-08) — vacancy count not stated in the reporting sources.
  • Assistant Program Officer–HR Skills (PPS-07) — vacancy count not stated in the reporting sources.

The project's office address, as listed in the advertisement, is the 6th Floor, New State Life Tower, Opposite Saudi Pak Tower, Blue Area, Islamabad.

Eligibility Criteria

The advertised requirements for these posts are:

  • Education: minimum 16 years of education from an HEC-recognized university, in the discipline specified for each post.
  • Experience: 2 to 6 years of relevant experience, depending on the post.
  • Preference: will be given to applicants with experience in government-funded development projects.

Match your own discipline and experience band against the post-specific requirements on the portal before applying.

How to Apply (Online Only)

1
Go to the National Jobs Portal (njp.gov.pk). Applications are accepted online only — incomplete or late applications will be rejected.
2
Find the PSEB "Revamping of IT Industry Landscape" advertisement and select the post that matches your qualification and experience.
3
Complete the online application carefully and submit it before 25 October 2026.
4
Keep your original documents ready. Applicants called for interview must bring their original documents with them.

FPSC Project Jobs 2026: Digitalization of Examination System — 20 PPS Posts, Apply Within 15 Days

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) has announced multiple project vacancies for its “Digitalization of In-house Processes & Automation of Examination System” initiative. The recruitment covers around 20 positions on PPS scales — from Project Director (PPS-10) down to software developers and network engineers (PPS-07). The posts are based in Islamabad, appointments are contract-based (initially one year, extendable), and applications must be submitted online at fpsc.gov.pk within 15 days of the advertisement.

This is a rare chance to work on the digital transformation of Pakistan's federal recruitment machinery itself — selected professionals will help modernise the examination and recruitment systems used by FPSC for every federal post in the country.

Eligibility Criteria

Qualification and experience requirements vary by post. In general, applicants should hold:

  • Technical posts (cyber security, database, software development, network engineering): BS in Computer Science, Software Engineering, Information Technology, Cyber Security or Database Management — with relevant professional experience preferred.
  • Subject specialists: qualifications in the relevant discipline (Computer Science & IT, Education, Mathematics, English, Management Science & Public Administration, or Medical & Allied Health).
  • Management posts (Project Director, QA Manager, Procurement Officer): relevant degrees in management, public administration or the applicable field, with leadership and project experience.

Check the detailed advertisement on the FPSC website for the exact qualification, experience, age and domicile conditions against each post — do not apply unless you meet them.

How to Apply Online at fpsc.gov.pk

Applications are accepted online only. Follow these steps:

1
Open www.fpsc.gov.pk and find the advertisement for the Digitalization of In-house Processes & Automation of Examination System project.
2
Read the full notice and match your qualification and experience against the specific post you want.
3
Complete the online application form with your personal, educational and experience details.
4
Upload the required documents in the prescribed format.
5
Submit within 15 days of the advertisement's publication and keep the application reference for your record.
Important: The application window is short — within 15 days of the advertisement — so do not wait for the last day. Verify the exact closing date on the FPSC website.

Why This Project Matters for Your Career

Working inside FPSC's own digital transformation means building systems that the entire federal recruitment pipeline will depend on — examination automation, candidate data management and secure online processes. For software developers, network engineers, database specialists and cyber-security professionals, the project offers production-scale systems work with national visibility, while subject specialists get to shape the testing content of federal examinations. Contract terms are a trade-off against the prestige and experience value — weigh both before applying.

Candidates should also note the project's Islamabad location and the PPS pay structure, which differs from regular BS scales — confirm the exact salary package and contract terms in the official advertisement before applying. With the 15-day application window, the smartest move is to apply early: FPSC systems can slow down under last-day load, and an incomplete submission cannot be fixed after the closing date.

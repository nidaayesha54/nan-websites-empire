FPSC Test Schedule October 2026: Phase-3/2026 Tests 12–19 Oct — 524 Vacancies, Centres & Pattern

Consolidated Advertisement 03/2026 enters the test phase 12–19 October — 68 cases, 524 vacancies. Admission certificates, test centres, pattern and key posts explained.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Federal Government Jobs Pakistan » Articles

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) has moved its Consolidated Advertisement 03/2026 (Phase-3/2026) into the examination stage, with General Recruitment tests scheduled from 12 to 19 October 2026. The advertisement — published on 11 August 2026 with applications closing on 31 August 2026 — carries 68 recruitment cases and 524 vacancies from BS-16 to BS-20 across federal ministries and departments.

Major postings include FGEI teaching staff (98 SST Male, 41 Computer Instructors), PIMS medical staff (50 Medical Officers, 70 Charge Nurses), ICT Police Inspectors (23) and FIA posts (Superintendent and Assistant Director Legal). Here is the schedule, how to get your admission certificate, and what to expect in the test.

Major Postings in the Advertisement

  • Ministry of Defence — FGEI: Secondary School Teacher (Male) BS-17 (98 posts), Computer Instructor (Male) BS-17 (27), Computer Instructor (Female) BS-17 (14), Drawing Mistress BS-16 (6).
  • PIMS, Ministry of National Health Services: Medical Officer BS-17 (50 posts), Charge Nurse BS-16 (70 posts).
  • Ministry of Interior — ICT Police: Inspector BS-16 (23 posts, male and female; age 20–28 with 5 years relaxation; physical and running test required).
  • Ministry of Interior — FIA: Superintendent BS-16 (22 posts), Assistant Director (Legal) BS-17 (12 posts).
  • Gilgit-Baltistan Education: Lecturer (Male) BS-17 across 17 subject categories (29 posts).
  • Pakistan Meteorological Department: Director/Principal Meteorologist BS-19, Deputy Director/Senior Meteorologist BS-18 and technical posts.
  • Board of Investment (PM's Office): DG BS-20, Directors BS-19, Deputy Directors BS-18, Assistant Directors BS-17 and PROs BS-16 — tests postponed until further notice.

How to Get Your Admission Certificate

1
Visit the official FPSC website www.fpsc.gov.pk.
2
Open the Admission Certificate section and enter your CNIC or tracking details.
3
Download and print your Online Admission Certificate — it shows your test centre, reporting time, test date and instructions.
4
Verify the centre address a day early if the city is new to you.
5
Carry your original CNIC and the printed admission certificate to the test centre.
Note: FPSC has postponed the Phase-III/2026 General Recruitment Tests in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa (scheduled 12–19 October) due to the prevailing situation in the province, and will issue a revised schedule later. Tests in all other provinces and areas proceed as per the announced timetable.

Test Pattern and Marking

FPSC General Recruitment tests are MCQ-based. For the updated general recruitment tests, FPSC applies negative marking of 0.25 mark for each wrong answer — so attempt questions you are unsure about with care. The test typically covers the job-related subject area plus general knowledge, current affairs, Pakistan studies, Islamiat and basic English and mathematics. Shortlisted candidates proceed to the descriptive or interview stage under FPSC regulations.

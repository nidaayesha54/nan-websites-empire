PMDC Jobs October 2026: Marketing/Export Promotion Officer (Salt Business) — Deadline 20 Oct 2026

The Pakistan Mineral Development Corporation (PMDC), a state-owned enterprise under the Ministry of Energy (Petroleum Division), invites applications for a Marketing/Export Promotion Officer — Salt Business (1 post) on an open-term contract at a market-based negotiable salary. Applications close on 20 October 2026 via pmdc.gov.pk/careers.

The officer will drive marketing and export promotion for PMDC’s salt business. Required: at least 3 years of post-qualification experience in sales, marketing, export promotion or international business development — ideally in salt, minerals, food products, FMCG or commodities. Qualification: a Bachelor’s or Master’s in Business Administration, Marketing, International Business, Commerce, Economics or a related field. Age limit: 35 years on the last date.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Education: Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Business Administration, Marketing, International Business, Commerce, Economics or a related field.
  • Experience: Minimum 3 years of post-qualification experience in sales, marketing, export promotion or international business development, preferably in salt, minerals, food products, FMCG or commodities.
  • Age limit: 35 years on the last date of application.
  • Terms: Open-term contract; market-based negotiable salary.

Export-promotion experience is the heart of this role: PMDC wants someone who understands international buyers, trade documentation and commodity pricing. If your background is in FMCG or food-product exports, frame your experience around markets developed and volumes moved.

About the Role

PMDC is the federal state enterprise for mineral development, and salt is one of its commercial lines with real export potential. The officer will be the commercial face of that business — identifying buyers and expanding export volumes.

For marketing professionals with commodity or FMCG experience, this is a chance to own a product line’s growth inside a state enterprise, with market-negotiated compensation. One seat means a tight shortlist — apply with a results-focused CV.

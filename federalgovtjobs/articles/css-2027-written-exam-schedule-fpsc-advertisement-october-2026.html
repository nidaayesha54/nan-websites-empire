CSS 2027 Written Exam Schedule: FPSC Advertisement 8 Nov, Apply 10–25 Nov

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) has set the CSS 2027 timeline, giving aspirants a planning anchor for the coming months. The MCQs-based Preliminary Test (MPT) for CSS 2027 was held on 10 October 2026 — rescheduled from the earlier date of 27 September 2026 — and passing it is the gate to the written examination. Per FPSC's advance notice F.2/1/2026-CE of 5 July 2026 and CSS prep-portal reporting, the written examination is scheduled to begin on 27 January 2027, the formal advertisement will be published on 8 November 2026, online applications will open from 10 to 25 November 2026, and the deadline for hard-copy submission is 4 December 2026.

Honesty note: every date connected to the written examination is tentative until the FPSC's written advertisement of 8 November 2026 confirms it. The timeline comes from FPSC's advance notice and prep-portal reporting — but the 8 November advertisement is the binding document. Read it on the FPSC website before acting on any date here.

What the MPT Result Means for You

The MPT is a screening test, nothing more. It consists of 200 MCQs in 3 hours and 20 minutes, and candidates must score at least 33% to qualify. Three points matter for anyone who sat it:

  • Passing the MPT only makes you eligible to apply for the written examination — it does not submit the written-exam application on your behalf. A separate application in November is mandatory.
  • The MPT does not consume a written attempt. Appearing in the screening test has no effect on the number of attempts you are allowed for the CSS written examination itself.
  • The MPT fee was Rs 250 — keep the fee receipt and your MPT record safe; they may be needed when filing the written-exam application.

If you did not qualify the MPT, the CSS 2027 written examination is closed to you this year.

Eligibility Snapshot

The age rule for CE-2027 is 21 to 30 years as of 31 December 2026. The proposal to raise the upper age limit to 35 was rejected for CE-2027 — the 30-year ceiling stands. FPSC is conducting the examination in 19 test cities, so check the advertisement for your city and centre allocation rules.

One decision you should start making now: pick your optional subjects early. Subject choice becomes final once applications close, and late changes are not entertained. Use the window between now and the application period to lock in optionals you can actually score in — the 8 November advertisement will not give you extra time to second-guess.

Fee for the Written Examination

The MPT fee was Rs 250. For context, the CE-2026 written examination fee was Rs 2,200 — but the CSS 2027 written fee will be announced in the 8 November advertisement, and we will not guess it here. Wait for the official notice rather than budgeting on last year's figure; fee changes between cycles are common.

How to Apply for the CSS 2027 Written Exam

1
Watch the FPSC website for the 8 November advertisement. This is the binding notice — it carries the confirmed dates, fee, eligibility rules and centre list. FPSC publishes results and schedules on its own website only.
2
Apply online between 10 and 25 November 2026 through the FPSC's application portal. Have your MPT record, educational documents, CNIC and photographs ready before the window opens.
3
Dispatch the hard copy in time to reach FPSC by 4 December 2026. Treat the last date as a received-by date, not a posted-by date — courier delays are the classic reason applications fail.
4
Finalize your optional subjects before applications close. The choice is final once the window shuts.
5
Check FPSC notices weekly. Date shifts — like the MPT's own rescheduling from 27 September to 10 October — do happen, and the FPSC website is the only official channel.
Remember: all written-exam dates remain tentative until the 8 November 2026 advertisement. Plan around them, but confirm everything against that notice.

The CSS 2027 cycle rewards early planners: the MPT is done, the written examination looms in late January 2027, and the November application window is short. Sort your documents, optionals and fee position now so the advertisement is a formality, not a scramble.

FPSC Postpones 17 Board of Investment Posts 2026: DG to PRO — Revised Schedule & What's Next

Five Board of Investment cases — from Director General (BS-20) to PRO (BS-16) — removed from the Phase-III test schedule. What the hold means and what to do next.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Federal Government Jobs Pakistan » Articles

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) has postponed tests for 17 posts at the Board of Investment (BOI), Prime Minister's Office, until further notice. According to the FPSC notification, the recruitment process for five cases has been put on hold, and the tests have been removed from the Phase-III examination schedule that started on 12 October 2026.

This is separate from the postponement of Phase-III tests in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa — the BOI hold applies to specific senior posts regardless of the test centre. Here are the affected posts, what candidates should do, and how to track the revised schedule.

What the Postponement Means

  • Tests removed from schedule: The five BOI cases were taken out of the Phase-III/2026 General Recruitment test programme starting 12 October 2026.
  • Recruitment on hold: The entire recruitment process for these cases — not just the test dates — is paused until further notice.
  • No new dates yet: FPSC has said further updates regarding the recruitment process will be announced in due course; no revised schedule has been issued.
  • Applications remain valid: Candidates who applied against these cases before the closing date do not need to re-apply; they should wait for the revised schedule.

What Candidates Should Do Now

1
Check the official FPSC website www.fpsc.gov.pk regularly for the revised test schedule.
2
Keep your application tracking details, fee deposit proof and CNIC safe — you will need them when the new schedule is announced.
3
Do not travel to any test centre for these cases until FPSC issues fresh dates.
4
Continue preparing for the written test — the syllabus and pattern remain the same when tests are rescheduled.
5
Watch the FPSC notices section rather than relying on unofficial social media posts for the new dates.
Important: This hold affects only the five Board of Investment cases. All other Phase-III/2026 tests — including FGEI, PIMS, ICT Police, FIA and PMD posts — proceed as per the announced schedule, except in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa where tests are separately postponed.

Why FPSC Postpones Tests

FPSC occasionally holds or reschedules recruitment when departments request it or when administrative circumstances require — the Commission did the same for the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Phase-III tests (12–19 October) on 11 October 2026, citing the prevailing situation in the province and requests from candidates and invigilation staff. When tests are postponed, FPSC issues a public notice and later publishes a revised schedule with fresh admission certificates.

How to Track the Revised Schedule

The fastest way to stay updated is the notices and press releases section of www.fpsc.gov.pk. When the revised schedule is announced, FPSC issues fresh Online Admission Certificates showing the new test date, centre and reporting time. Check your certificate again before travelling — centres can change in a revised schedule.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which FPSC posts have been postponed at the Board of Investment?

Seventeen posts across five cases: Director General (BS-20, 1), Director (BS-19, 4), Deputy Director (BS-18, 6), Assistant Director (BS-17, 3) and Public Relations Officer (BS-16, 3).

When will the postponed BOI tests be held?

FPSC has not announced new dates yet. The Commission says further updates will be announced in due course on www.fpsc.gov.pk.

Do candidates need to re-apply for the postponed posts?

No. Candidates who applied before the closing date keep their applications; they should wait for the revised test schedule.

Are other FPSC Phase-III/2026 tests also postponed?

No. Other Phase-III tests proceed as scheduled from 12 October 2026, except in Khyber Pakhtunkhwa where they were separately postponed.

Where can I check the revised FPSC test schedule?

In the notices section of the official FPSC website www.fpsc.gov.pk, which also issues fresh Online Admission Certificates.

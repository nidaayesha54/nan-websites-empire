CEASDC Islamabad Jobs 2026 — Closing 24–25 Oct (Apply Now)

The Centre of Excellence for Autism Spectrum Disorder Company (CEASDC) in Islamabad is hiring a CEO, directors, therapists, teachers and support staff under the Ministry of Federal Education. The deadline is today or tomorrow depending on the source — apply now.

CEASDC — the Centre of Excellence for Autism Spectrum Disorder Company — is a specialized public sector organization operating under the Ministry of Federal Education & Professional Training, Government of Pakistan, in Islamabad. It has advertised a range of posts: CEO, directors, therapists, teachers and other posts, covering clinical, educational and support roles for children with autism spectrum disorder. The Posts on Offer

Leadership: CEO and Directors

CEASDC is hiring a Chief Executive Officer and directors to lead the centre's clinical and educational work. These are senior leadership posts for professionals with experience in special education, autism services or public-sector programme management. The detailed eligibility for these posts is published on the HEC ETC portal entry for this recruitment.

Clinical and Teaching Posts (PPS-07)

The Behaviour Support Therapist (PPS-07) needs a BS or MS in Psychology, ABA or Special Education plus 2 years of experience, with an age limit of 30 years. The Lead Special Education Teacher (PPS-07) needs a master's in Special Education plus B.Ed or M.Ed and 2 years of experience, also with an age limit of 30 years. These are the core service-delivery roles: designing behaviour support plans, leading special education classrooms, and working with families of children with autism.

Support Posts (PPS-05 and PPS-06)

The Paramedic / Para Educator (PPS-06) needs Intermediate, a bachelor's degree or a diploma in Early Childhood Education, with an age limit of 22 years. The Allied Health Assistant (PPS-06) needs a bachelor's degree in Allied Health or Psychology, also with an age limit of 22 years. The Attendant (PPS-05) needs a Matric pass plus childcare experience, with an age limit of 22 years.

How to Apply

1
Open the HEC ETC portal at https://etc.hec.gov.pk (linked above).
2
Create your profile with your CNIC number, active email address and mobile number.
3
Open the CEASDC application form and enter your personal, educational and experience details accurately.
4
Download the 1Bill fee invoice from the portal and pay the Rs 1,200 challan fee through an ATM, mobile banking, EasyPaisa, JazzCash or a bank app.
5
Return to the portal and click Verify Online Payment to confirm the fee.
6
Check every field, then submit the final application before the closing time — treat 24 October 4:00 pm as the deadline.
Closing now: with sources reporting 24 October 4:00 pm and 25 October, the only safe course is to apply today. Confirm the exact closing time on the HEC ETC portal, and do not wait until the last hours — portal traffic on closing day can cause failures.

FPSC Consolidated Advertisement Phase-2/2026: Lecturers at Navy Cadet College, PIMS Professors & Mint Officers — Apply Online

The Federal Public Service Commission (FPSC) has issued its Consolidated Advertisement Phase-2/2026, opening federal vacancies across several ministries and departments. The advertisement brings together posts in defence education (Lecturers at the Junior Naval Academy / PN Cadet College Ormara and PNS Rehnuma Karachi), health (Professors at PIMS Islamabad), finance (officers at the Pakistan Mint, Lahore), law (Federal Service Tribunal) and education (a Vice Principal for Islamabad Model Schools) — plus a Data Processing Assistant at FPSC itself. Applications are accepted online at fpsc.gov.pk.

Consolidated advertisements bundle many small federal recruitments into one notice — which means this is a good round to scan for a post that matches your exact qualification and domicile quota.

Eligibility — Post by Post

  • Lecturer (BS-17, Navy education institutions): Master's degree or equivalent in the relevant subject, per the FPSC notice — these are teaching posts at the Navy's cadet college and training institutions.
  • Professor (BS-20, PIMS): senior medical/academic posts requiring the prescribed postgraduate medical qualification and teaching experience — one seat is on the women's quota.
  • Security Officer (BS-17) & Labour Officer (BS-16), Pakistan Mint: per the advertised qualification and experience for each post.
  • Deputy Registrar (BS-18, Federal Service Tribunal): the relevant legal/administrative qualification and experience.
  • Vice Principal (Female, BS-18): for qualified women educators under the Federal Directorate of Education.
  • Data Processing Assistant (BS-16, FPSC): the prescribed computer/IT qualification.

Candidates must fulfil the qualification, experience, age, domicile and gender conditions exactly as stated in the official FPSC advertisement.

How to Apply Online at fpsc.gov.pk

Follow these steps:

1
Open www.fpsc.gov.pk and open the Consolidated Advertisement Phase-2/2026 notice.
2
Identify the post whose qualification, scale and domicile quota match your profile.
3
Complete the online application form with your personal, educational and experience details.
4
Upload the required documents in the prescribed format and note the FPSC fee instructions for the current fee.
5
Review and submit before the closing date stated in the advertisement. Keep the application confirmation.
Important: FPSC accepts online applications only. Always verify the closing date, fee and eligibility in the official advertisement — consolidated notices close on the date printed in the ad.

What to Watch After Applying

After the closing date, FPSC publishes the screening-test schedule for each case number. Quota-sensitive posts like these — several seats here are allocated to Sindh (rural/urban) domicile — can have smaller applicant pools, which improves the odds for eligible candidates. Keep your documents ready and watch the FPSC website for test dates and roll number slips.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is FPSC Consolidated Advertisement Phase-2/2026?

A consolidated FPSC notice covering federal vacancies in BS-16 to BS-20 across the Pakistan Navy's education institutions, PIMS Islamabad, Pakistan Mint Lahore, the Federal Service Tribunal, the Federal Directorate of Education and FPSC itself.

Which Lecturer posts are in this advertisement?

3 Lecturer (BS-17) posts at the Junior Naval Academy / PN Cadet College Ormara and PNS Rehnuma Karachi under the Pakistan Navy, Ministry of Defence.

What is the closing date for FPSC Phase-2/2026?

The closing date is stated in the official FPSC advertisement — verify it on fpsc.gov.pk before applying.

How do I apply for FPSC consolidated advertisement posts?

Online only at www.fpsc.gov.pk. Select the post matching your qualification and domicile quota, complete the form, upload documents and keep the confirmation.

Do the posts have domicile quotas?

Yes. Each post carries its own domicile/quota allocation (e.g., Sindh rural/urban merit, Punjab merit, women's quota) — check your eligibility against the quota before applying.

PAEC Jobs 2026: Technicians & Assistants — Apply by 15 Nov

The Pakistan Atomic Energy Commission (PAEC) has announced technical and support-staff vacancies through three public notices issued in October 2026: S-14/2026 (Peshawar), S-11/2026 (Dera Ghazi Khan) and S-15/2026 (Tandojam, Sindh) — seven posts in total on SPS-3 and SPS-04 scales.

Each notice has its own closing date: 1 November 2026 (Peshawar), 6 November 2026 (DG Khan) and 15 November 2026 (Tandojam). Applications are online only via the PAEC portal at hrd1902.com.pk; applications arriving by any other method are rejected.

Honesty flag: the notices state no vacancy numbers, so total intake is unpublished. Salary figures (e.g. PKR 35,000–55,000) on aggregator pages are market estimates, not from the notices (SPS-3/SPS-04 scales only). These are separate from the earlier PAEC shutdown walk-in announcement (PO Box 71, Karachi).

Posts Announced

All three notices share the same general terms but different post lists. Read the notice for your location carefully:

Public Notice S-15/2026 — Tandojam, Sindh (last date 15 Nov 2026)

  • Technician-I (Civil), SPS-04 — 3 years DAE (Civil) in 1st Division from a recognized Polytechnic Institute.
  • Scientific Assistant-II, SPS-3 — F.Sc in 1st Division.

Public Notice S-14/2026 — Peshawar (last date 1 Nov 2026)

  • Scientific Assistant-II, SPS-3 — F.Sc (Pre-Medical) 1st Division (different requirement from the Tandojam notice's same-titled post).
  • Technician-II (Medical), SPS-3 — Matric Science (1st Div) + 1-year course and 3 years' experience, or 2-year course and 2 years' experience, or F.Sc (Pre-Medical) + 1-year diploma.
  • Junior Assistant-II (Admin), SPS-3 — FA/FSc (1st Div) + 6-month Office Automation course or equivalent.

Public Notice S-11/2026 — Dera Ghazi Khan (last date 6 Nov 2026)

  • Tech-II, SPS-03 — F.Sc (Pre-Engineering) 1st Division — not the same post as the Peshawar Technician-II (Medical).
  • Moazzin / Qari, SPS-03 — Matric or equivalent, Hafiz Quran and Qari.

Terms and Conditions

The notices share one set of general instructions:

  • The posts are purely temporary but likely to continue indefinitely, under the CPF or Provident Fund (PF) scheme.
  • Where 1st Division is required, the organization accepts only one 2nd Division in your entire academic career from Matric onwards.
  • Complete qualification and experience before the closing date; only experience gained after the minimum qualification counts.
  • Serving government or semi-government employees need an NOC from their employer at the test/interview stage.
  • All credentials must be from recognized institutes; only Pakistani nationals can apply.
  • Meeting the criteria does not guarantee shortlisting — higher marks, division, CGPA or experience win. No TA/DA is paid.

Contact Details (Per Notice)

  • S-11/2026 (DG Khan): Sr. Admin Officer (Estt.), Post Box 27, Dera Ghazi Khan — 064-9261087.
  • S-15/2026 (Tandojam): Pr. Administrator, P.O. Box 70060, Tandojam — 022-9250501.

How to Apply Online

1
Pick the right notice. Scientific Assistant-II differs between Tandojam and Peshawar; Tech-II is not Technician-II (Medical).
2
Visit hrd1902.com.pk — the only accepted application route. Applications arriving by any other method are rejected.
3
Select your notice (S-14/2026, S-11/2026 or S-15/2026) and fill the online form with correct details.
4
Submit before your notice's last date: 1 Nov (Peshawar), 6 Nov (DG Khan), 15 Nov 2026 (Tandojam). Keep marksheets, CNIC and experience letters ready.
Important: vacancy counts and salary amounts are not stated in the notices — ignore aggregator pages quoting precise totals. These online notices are separate from the earlier PAEC shutdown walk-in announcement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the last dates for PAEC Public Notices S-11, S-14 and S-15/2026?

Peshawar (S-14/2026): 1 Nov 2026; DG Khan (S-11/2026): 6 Nov 2026; Tandojam (S-15/2026): 15 Nov 2026.

How can I apply for PAEC jobs 2026?

Apply online only at the PAEC portal, hrd1902.com.pk — applications arriving by any other method are rejected.

Which posts are included in PAEC Public Notices S-11, S-14 and S-15/2026?

Seven: Technician-I (Civil), Technician-II (Medical), Tech-II, Scientific Assistant-II (in two notices), Junior Assistant-II (Admin) and Moazzin/Qari — SPS-3 and SPS-04.

Are PAEC public notice jobs permanent?

No — purely temporary but likely to continue indefinitely, under the CPF or Provident Fund (PF) scheme.

What is the age limit for PAEC jobs 2026?

The age limit is 35 years on the closing date of the advertisement, with relaxation as per the rules.

What is the difference between Tech-II and Technician-II (Medical)?

Tech-II (DG Khan, S-11/2026) needs F.Sc (Pre-Engineering) 1st Division; Technician-II (Medical) (Peshawar, S-14/2026) is a medical post with Matric Science or F.Sc (Pre-Medical) routes.

How to Apply for FPSC Jobs Online 2026 — Registration, Fee & Screening Test

The complete fpsc.gov.pk application walkthrough: one-time registration, online form, fee challan, screening test stages and the mistakes that get forms rejected.

✅ Updated October 13, 2026
Home » Federal Government Jobs Pakistan » Articles

Every federal government job announced by the Federal Public Service Commission is applied for online at fpsc.gov.pk — FPSC does not accept paper applications. The process has four parts: one-time registration, filling the online application for your chosen advertisement, depositing the test fee, and then appearing in the screening test and subsequent stages. This guide explains each part in detail.

Step 1: One-Time Registration on fpsc.gov.pk

Before you can apply for any post, create your one-time profile on the FPSC website. You register with your CNIC, an active email address and a mobile number — these become your permanent login credentials for all future applications. Fill your profile with accurate personal and educational details, because the same profile feeds every application you submit. Keep your login information safe; you will need it to download admission certificates and check results.

Step 2: Fill the Online Application Form

1
Open the consolidated advertisement on fpsc.gov.pk and read the full advertisement PDF for your post — qualification, experience, age limit and quota.
2
Log in to your FPSC profile and select the advertisement number and post from the online application section.
3
Complete the form. Most fields pull from your profile; add post-specific details such as experience and the exam centre preference carefully.
4
Upload your photograph in the required format and size, and attach any documents the advertisement asks for.
5
Preview everything. Check name spellings, CNIC digits, qualification entries and centre selection against your documents.
6
Submit before the closing date and note your application tracking number.

Step 3: Test Fee & Challan

Each FPSC advertisement specifies a test fee, deposited through a bank challan at designated banks (or the payment channels stated in the advertisement). The fee is non-refundable, so verify your eligibility before paying. Deposit the fee before the closing date and keep the original receipt — you will present it at later stages. Applications without the deposited fee are not processed.

Step 4: Screening Test and Beyond

StageWhat Happens
Screening (MCQ) testTypically 100 marks: English, general knowledge, subject knowledge and related areas. Admission certificate is uploaded to your FPSC dashboard before the test.
Descriptive examinationFor higher posts, qualifying candidates attempt written papers in the relevant subjects.
Psychological assessmentWritten psychological tests and group tasks for posts where prescribed.
InterviewPanel interview with original documents; eligibility is scrutinised in detail.
Final merit & recommendationMerit list published on fpsc.gov.pk; FPSC recommends selected candidates to the concerned department.

Mistakes That Get FPSC Applications Rejected

  • Applying without meeting the exact qualification or experience stated in the advertisement
  • Name or CNIC mismatches between the form and your documents
  • Depositing the fee after the closing date or at a non-designated bank
  • Selecting the wrong exam centre or quota category
  • Waiting until the last day — portal congestion has cost many candidates their submission
Remember: FPSC communicates through fpsc.gov.pk and your registered email/mobile. Treat messages from any other source about test dates or "guaranteed selection" as fraud.

Documents Checklist for FPSC Applications

Assemble these before you start filling any FPSC form — hunting for papers mid-application is how mistakes happen:

  • CNIC (valid, with clear front and back scans)
  • Recent photograph meeting the portal's size and background requirements
  • Domicile certificate of your province/region for quota purposes
  • Degrees, transcripts and result cards for the required qualification
  • Experience certificates on official letterheads for posts requiring experience
  • Registration certificates (PMDC/PMC, PEC, PNC or relevant body) for professional posts
  • Fee challan receipt from the designated bank

At the interview stage FPSC scrutinises original documents line by line against your online form. Any mismatch — a different spelling of your father's name, an undeclared degree, an expired registration — can end your candidature even after you have cleared the tests. Accuracy at the application stage is part of the selection.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I register for FPSC jobs?

Create a one-time profile on fpsc.gov.pk with your CNIC, email and mobile number. This profile is used for all future FPSC applications.

What is the FPSC test fee and how do I pay it?

Each advertisement specifies the fee, deposited via bank challan at designated banks before the closing date. The fee is non-refundable.

Does FPSC accept paper applications?

No. FPSC accepts applications online only through fpsc.gov.pk.

What comes after the FPSC screening test?

Qualifying candidates proceed to descriptive examinations (for higher posts), psychological assessment, interviews and final merit leading to departmental recommendation.

How do I get my FPSC admission certificate?

FPSC uploads admission certificates to your candidate dashboard on fpsc.gov.pk before each test phase. Download and print it in time.

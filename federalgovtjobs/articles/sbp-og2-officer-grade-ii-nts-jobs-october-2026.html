SBP OG-2 Jobs 2026: State Bank Officer Grade-II via NTS — Online Registration Open, Last Date 30 Oct 2026

The State Bank of Pakistan (SBP) — the country's central bank — has opened applications for Officer Grade-II (OG-2) posts through the National Testing Service (NTS). The online application link became active on 15 October 2026, and the deadline for submitting applications is 30 October 2026. Pakistani citizens — including those from Azad Jammu & Kashmir and Gilgit-Baltistan — who meet the eligibility criteria can apply through the NTS website.

SBP officer grades are among the most prestigious banking careers in Pakistan: OG-2 is the entry officer grade, and the central bank offers a market-based compensation package, structured career progression and training exposure with institutions such as the IMF, World Bank and ADB. How to Apply Through NTS

Applications are submitted online through NTS. Follow these steps:

1
Open the NTS website (nts.org.pk) and find the SBP OG-2 online application link — it has been active since 15 October 2026.
2
Complete the application in one go: personal information, academic information and experience information (if any). The system does not save un-submitted data.
3
Upload a photograph and fill every mandatory field — date of birth, education and contact details. Applications lacking required information are not considered.
4
Submit and collect the auto-generated email with your application form and fee challan. Deposit the test fee through the stated channel.
5
Submit before 30 October 2026. Applications submitted in person or by email are not accepted — online only.
Important: Once submitted, the data cannot be amended — review every entry before final submission. For inquiries, contact NTS at SBPITOTS@nts.org.pk, 021-34546930 or 051-8444441.

What Happens After Applying

NTS will notify shortlisted candidates via SMS and send admit cards to their postal addresses, as well as uploading them to its website, for the written test at designated test centres. NTS publishes the details of qualified candidates on its website; only shortlisted candidates are contacted for further selection processes. Note that the bank reserves the discretion to assign you any test centre near your preferred one.

Why SBP OG-2 Is Worth It

An SBP officer career sits at the top of Pakistan's financial-sector hierarchy: the central bank sets monetary policy, supervises the banking industry and manages the country's reserves, and its officers work at the centre of economic decision-making. The bank offers a market-based compensation package with a well-rounded benefits structure, and it encourages females, minorities, transgender persons and persons with disabilities to apply — the recruitment is run as an equal-opportunity process. Candidates who provide false information or tamper with documents are disqualified from the current and any future SBP recruitment, so the integrity bar is high from the first form.

With the 30 October deadline approaching, eligible graduates should complete the NTS application now rather than in the final days: NTS portals slow down under last-day load, and an un-submitted form is the same as no form at all.

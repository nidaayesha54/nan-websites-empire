WAPDA 400+ Jobs 2026: Line Man, Clerk, Assistant — Apply by 30 Oct

The Water and Power Development Authority (WAPDA), a federal government organization of Pakistan, has announced a large recruitment round with more than 400 vacancies. The reported posts include Line Man, Junior Clerk, Assistant, Technician and Driver, with required education ranging from Matric to Graduation or DAE depending on the post. Candidates from all over Pakistan — male and female — are reported to be eligible to apply.

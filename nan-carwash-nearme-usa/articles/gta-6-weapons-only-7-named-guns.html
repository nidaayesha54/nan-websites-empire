GTA 6 Weapons: Only 7 Guns Have Real Names

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Search for a GTA 6 weapons list and you will find pages claiming 66 weapons, complete with damage tables, prices and unlock methods — published before most players had even finished the first chapter. Here is the honest version: Rockstar has named only three guns on its own site, four more names can be read on screen in its trailers and artwork, and everything else is a category, a guess, or a list built from footage that was never meant to be a catalogue. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and this guide gives you the weapons list as it actually stands: what Rockstar named, what can be read on screen, what the trailers show, and what nobody knows yet.

The distinction matters because the long lists are not harmless. A player who plans a build around a weapon that does not exist, or spends money chasing a gun from a fake list, wastes time and cash on a fiction. The honest list is shorter — and it is the only one worth using.

The Three Guns Rockstar Named

Rockstar has named exactly three GTA 6 guns on its own site, and all three come from the editions and bonuses pages. Jason's sidearm is the Girardi ES9 pistol. Lucia's sidearm is the Klose K17 pistol. And the Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers are a his-and-hers set — scoped revolvers with palm-tree grips, described as coming from the Vercetti Estate — that arrive with the Ultimate Edition, along with engraved versions of both pistols. That is the complete list of weapons Rockstar has put in writing.

Two details are worth noting. First, the Girardi ES9 and Klose K17 are the characters' personal sidearms in the base game — the Ultimate Edition versions are engraved variants, not the only way to get them. Second, the pre-order bonus adds a Tommy Vercetti weapon pattern — a tropical skin for "most guns" — which is a cosmetic finish, not a weapon of its own. Rockstar has not published a full weapon list, a damage table, a launch-day arsenal total, carry limits, unlock methods or shop prices.

Four More Names You Can Read on Screen

Beyond the three named guns, four more names appear in Rockstar's own footage and artwork, readable on screen or engraved on the guns themselves. The Moreland 850 shotgun and the Duke 556 assault rifle appear as pickups in the Extended Look showcase. The Mustang .357 is engraved on a revolver in an Ambrosia screenshot. And the Nipper .38 is Lucia's pistol on the cover art. These are names from Rockstar's own material — but they are not Rockstar confirmations in the same way as the three above, because Rockstar has never listed them in writing. On-screen names can also change before launch, so treat them as strong evidence rather than settled fact.

That brings the honest count to seven named guns: Girardi ES9, Klose K17, Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers, Moreland 850, Duke 556, Mustang .357 and Nipper .38. Any list longer than that is mixing evidence levels, and the best guides say so plainly.

What the Trailers Show (Without Names)

The trailers and screenshots show far more than seven guns — just without names attached. The categories visible across the official videos include pistols, submachine guns, rifles, shotguns, a sniper rifle, a light machine gun and a grenade launcher, plus a Molotov cocktail, a switchblade, a baseball bat and hammers for close work. The SMGs get the most screen time of any category after pistols, and the launch-day guides identify models like an MP5-style SMG, a Micro Uzi and a MAC-11 from the footage — but those identifications come from fans and outlets reading the frames, not from Rockstar, and the in-game names for those weapons are still unknown.

One structural change is worth knowing, though it comes from launch-era reporting rather than Rockstar: GTA 6 moves away from the unlimited weapon wheel of GTA 5 toward a system closer to Red Dead Redemption 2. The reports describe carrying two handguns on your person, concealed so nearby NPCs do not react, and two long guns visible on your back or in a holster. Treat that as reported, not confirmed — but if it holds, the days of carrying every gun at once are over, and choosing a loadout becomes part of the game.

The 66-Weapon Lists, Explained

The longest lists circulating — 66 weapons, split into 53 guns, 9 melee weapons and 4 explosives, with a "find 50 for 100% completion" rule — come from launch-day guides built by playing the released game, not from Rockstar. They may turn out to be right, and the early numbers are consistent across several guides. But they are still guide data, assembled in the first days after launch, and the responsible way to use them is as a checklist to verify as you play, not as a shopping list to plan around before you have even opened the game.

The same caution applies to the shop system. Ammu-Nation is reported to have 24 stores across Leonida, and most guns are bought there — but Rockstar has not published the shop list, the prices, or the unlock rules. The full arsenal will only be known once players have mapped it, and the guides that admit that are the ones to trust.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many weapons are in GTA 6?

Rockstar has named three guns in writing and four more names appear on screen in its own footage — seven named guns in total. Launch-day guides report 66 weapons in the game, but Rockstar has not published a full list or a total, so treat longer lists as guide data to verify as you play.

What are Jason and Lucia's guns?

Jason's sidearm is the Girardi ES9 pistol and Lucia's is the Klose K17 pistol. The Ultimate Edition adds engraved versions of both, plus the Hawk and Little Morgan revolvers as a his-and-hers set.

Can I carry every weapon at once like in GTA 5?

Probably not. Launch-era reporting describes a Red Dead Redemption 2-style system — two handguns concealed and two long guns visible — instead of GTA 5's unlimited wheel. That is reported, not confirmed by Rockstar, so check it in your own game.

The Bottom Line

Seven guns have real names in GTA 6, and three of them come from Rockstar's own words. The rest of the arsenal is visible in the trailers, counted in launch-day guides, and waiting to be mapped by players. Use the honest list, verify the long ones as you play, and do not pay anyone for a "complete" weapons guide that Rockstar itself has not published.

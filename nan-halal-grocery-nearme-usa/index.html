How to use the map

Type your city and state into the search box, or tap "📍 Use my location" to sort every halal market by distance from you. Tap any marker to see the store's name and address, then hit Get Directions for turn-by-turn navigation.

What counts as a halal market?

A true halal grocery sells 100% halal-certified meat, and most also carry imported groceries — spices, basmati rice, dates, olives, frozen samosas, and fresh produce. Many stores also have an in-house halal butcher counter. New to halal shopping?

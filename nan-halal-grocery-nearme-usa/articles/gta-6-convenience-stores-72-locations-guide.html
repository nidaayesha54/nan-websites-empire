GTA 6 Convenience Stores: All 72 Shops and What to Buy

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and one of the most ordinary things in Leonida turns out to be one of the most useful: the convenience store. There are 72 of them across the state, from the Keys to the swamps, and they sell the three things every player needs most — medicine, food and groceries for the fridge at home. They are also the only shops in the game where you can buy the fastest healing item in GTA 6, and the only place where a bag of groceries is a gameplay decision rather than set dressing.

That matters because convenience stores are not just background. They are marked on your map, they have a filter of their own, and what you buy in them decides how long you survive your next fight. Knowing which chain is which, what is worth buying, and what the clerk keeps behind the counter is the difference between walking into a gunfight prepared and walking in with an empty inventory.

72 Stores and How to Find Them

There are 72 convenience store locations in GTA 6, according to PowerPyx's launch-era count, spread across all six regions of Leonida. When you unfog the map, they get marked automatically with a blue shopping bag icon, and you can use the map filter to see how many you have found: open the map, go to Food and Drink, then Convenience Stores, and the list shows every store name with a counter in the bottom left that tracks the total discovered.

The chains are the familiar Florida mix. 24/7 has a branch in Key Lento, modelled on a real Circle K on the Overseas Highway, and it is the chain Jason is shown robbing in the game's promotional material. Xero Food Mart has branches from Southwind to Belville to Buckskin Swamp. LTD Food Mart is in Capri. Ron Stop'N'Snack is everywhere in Vice-Dale County, from Southside to Little Cuba to Bayside. Poppers, Ramon's Groceries, Cane Grocery, Trotman's Supermarket and Huck's Pump N Go fill out the rural counties, and Mami's Grocery Market is a 24-hour corner grocery just off El Joven Boulevard in Southside, where Jason walks out eating a bag of chips.

What to Buy: Zombix, Pet Food and Groceries

The most important thing a convenience store sells is Zombix, the medicine that is the fastest way to heal in GTA 6. It costs $15, and you buy it from the clerk — it is often not stocked on the shelves like food and drink, so talk to the person behind the counter. You can also find Zombix lying around Leonida for free, but the store is the reliable source, and the Kotaku launch tips say it plainly: buy Zombix at most convenience stores, because it is the healing item you will want before combat.

The second thing is pet food, which you feed to the domestic animals you meet in the world — a small detail that shows how much of Leonida is alive beyond the missions. The third is groceries: food you can put in the fridge at home, from candy and protein bars to apples and produce. Gameplay footage from launch week shows a player walking into the Leonida Key Lento Mini Mart, picking up items from shelves labelled buy some groceries, putting them in a brown paper bag, paying at the register and leaving — the ordinary errand, played straight, in a game about grand theft auto.

The Grocery System That Got Cut

Here is the honest footnote: the grocery system was supposed to be bigger. Rob Nelson, who co-runs Rockstar North, confirmed that the studio built a system for buying groceries and restocking the safehouse fridge, then cut it to balance realism against ease of play. The convenience-store snacking you see in the game — the hot dog, the apple, the bag of chips — is the surviving half of a bigger plan, and the fridge at home is the other half that did not make it.

That does not make the stores useless. Food still heals, Zombix still saves lives, and the 72 stores are still the most reliable shops in the game — they are open, they are everywhere, and they are marked on your map. The grocery run is a small thing, but in a game where eating before a fight is the difference between winning and losing, the shop on the corner is worth knowing by name.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many convenience stores are in GTA 6?

72, according to PowerPyx's launch-era count. They are marked on the map with a blue shopping bag icon, and the map filter under Food and Drink shows how many you have discovered.

Where do you buy Zombix in GTA 6?

At most convenience stores, for $15, from the clerk — it is not on the shelves. You can also find it lying around Leonida for free.

Can you buy groceries in GTA 6?

Yes. Convenience stores sell groceries you can put in the fridge at home, and launch-week footage shows players shopping for candy, protein bars and apples. The fuller grocery-restock system was built and then cut before launch.

The Bottom Line

Leonida's 72 convenience stores are the most useful shops in the game: Zombix for $15 from the clerk, food that heals, pet food for the animals, and groceries for the fridge. Learn the chains, use the map filter, and stock up before a fight — because in GTA 6, the corner store is not scenery. It is your pharmacy.

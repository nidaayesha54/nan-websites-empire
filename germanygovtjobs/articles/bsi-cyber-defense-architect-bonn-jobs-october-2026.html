Germany Government Jobs 2026

The BSI — Germany's federal cyber security authority — is hiring a Cyber Defense Architect in Bonn for its national Cyberdome platform: permanent, E11 TVöD, German C1 required.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » Germany Government Jobs » Articles

The Bundesamt für Sicherheit in der Informationstechnik (BSI) — Germany's federal cyber security authority — is hiring a Cyber Defense Architect (Sachbearbeiter/in w/m/d) for its Referat C 31 "Strategie und Architektur" in Bonn. The position, reference BSI-2026-111, is permanent (unbefristet), offered full-time or part-time, and graded E 11 TVöD (or the equivalent BBesO pay grade), plus a €200-per-month BSI allowance on top of the tariff salary. The application deadline is 13 November 2026.

This is one of the most technical roles the German federal administration is advertising right now: the architect will shape the Cyberdome, the federal government's planned national cyber-defence platform that is meant to detect, analyse and respond to cyberattacks semi-automatically. The BSI, working with the Federal Ministry of the Interior, is building the Cyberdome as a nationwide shield that fuses sensor data from authorities, critical-infrastructure operators and industry into a single threat picture — and Referat C 31 owns the strategy and overall architecture behind it.

Honesty first: this posting requires German at minimum C1 level — it is not an English-language role, and the work is done entirely in German. The vacancy states no explicit citizenship requirement, but the BSI is a federal security agency where security vetting is the norm, so it is realistically aimed at German-speaking candidates with work authorization in Germany. The vacancy is single-sourced: it is published on service.bund.de, the federal government's official vacancy publication platform, and applicants should verify it through the linked official application system.

What You Will Do

The architect joins the team that defines how the Cyberdome works technically. Day-to-day work includes:

  • Designing and implementing the system architecture and security services of the Cyberdome — secure, cloud-native, highly available platform structures;
  • Planning and documenting APIs, data flows and technical integration concepts, applying security and resilience mechanisms across platform components;
  • Acting as the first point of contact for agile DevSecOps teams on technical design and implementation questions, supporting architecture and technology decisions and resolving integration conflicts;
  • Supporting the CI/CD process and infrastructure automation (Infrastructure as Code);
  • Continuously verifying compliance with the target architecture, security policies and quality requirements, and maintaining technical reference architectures, system documentation and operations manuals;
  • Evaluating and testing new tools and security technologies in lab or proof-of-concept environments, and preparing technical status reports for the programme leadership.

Who Should Apply

The BSI is looking for a profile that is strong on both architecture and operations:

  • A completed university degree (Diplom-FH or Bachelor) in computer science, technical informatics or a comparable programme;
  • Solid knowledge and hands-on experience implementing modern IT and cybersecurity architectures and designing and using cloud-native systems and distributed environments;
  • Relevant experience in DevSecOps, CI/CD pipelines and infrastructure automation;
  • Confident handling of API and integration architectures and data flows, plus a basic understanding of network, identity and security architectures and common cybersecurity standards;
  • German at minimum C1 (oral and written) and good to very good English;
  • Willingness to travel regularly (predictable business trips) and to keep learning — the posting explicitly asks for the motivation to develop continuously.

Salary and Benefits

The role is paid at E 11 TVöD (gehobener Dienst), or the comparable BBesO grade for civil servants, with the exact step depending on experience. On top of the tariff salary the BSI pays a monthly BSI allowance of €200. The posting also advertises a crisis-proof public-sector job with the perspective of Verbeamtung (civil-servant status), flexible working arrangements and mobile working within Germany, structured onboarding and strong training offers, relocation-cost support or separation allowance, and a discounted public-transport ticket (Job-Ticket or Deutschlandticket option).

How to Apply

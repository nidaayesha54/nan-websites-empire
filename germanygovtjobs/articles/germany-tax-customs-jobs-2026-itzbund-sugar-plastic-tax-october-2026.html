Germany Government Jobs 2026

New sugar and plastic taxes could create more than 1,000 public-sector positions in Germany — most of them in Customs and the federal IT provider ITZBund. Here is what the plans say, which employers to watch, and where federal jobs are advertised.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » Germany Government Jobs » Articles

Germany's planned sugar and plastic taxes are set to become one of the biggest public-sector hiring stories of 2026. According to legislative drafts reported from the Federal Ministry of Finance, the government anticipates needing more than 1,000 new positions to administer the two taxes — a workload calculated at 1,046 full-time positions in total. Separately, the budget plan indicates that the Customs administration alone is slated to increase its number of positions by 934 next year.

Whether you are already in Germany or planning a move under the Skilled Immigration Act, this is a hiring wave worth watching — Customs (Zoll) is one of the federal government's largest employers.

What ITZBund and Customs Do

  • Customs (Zoll): part of the Federal Ministry of Finance — enforces customs law, collects excise duties, fights smuggling and undeclared work. New tax administration means new assessment, audit and enforcement posts;
  • ITZBund (IT Service Centre of the Federal Government): the federal administration's central IT provider — builds and runs the digital systems behind tax collection. The plastic and sugar taxes each require new IT systems, hence the ITZBund headcount;
  • Federal Ministry of Finance: policy and oversight roles, including the small number of positions estimated within the ministry itself.

IT specialists, administrators, auditors and tax professionals are the profiles most likely to benefit — and ITZBund roles are among the most English-friendly corners of the federal administration.

The Debate Around the Hiring Wave

The plans have drawn sharp criticism. The president of the German Taxpayers' Association called the creation of over 1,000 jobs for two new taxes "predictable bureaucratic madness", arguing the government should review existing expenditure instead of covering budget deficits with new taxes. For jobseekers, the political debate matters less than the outcome: if the legislation passes as drafted, the positions will need to be staffed — and federal recruitment runs on fixed, transparent procedures regardless of the politics.

What to Watch Next

The key milestone is the legislative process: once the sugar and plastic tax laws are finalized, staffing follows through the federal budget. Jobseekers should set up alerts on Interamt and karriere.bund.de for Zoll and ITZBund now, so they are first to see postings as the hiring wave materializes. Given the reported scale — over a thousand positions — this could be one of the largest single federal hiring pushes of 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many new government jobs are planned for Germany's sugar and plastic taxes?

Legislative drafts reported from the Federal Ministry of Finance anticipate 1,046 full-time positions in total — 788 + 200 in Customs, 28 + 23 at ITZBund, plus a handful of ministry roles.

Will these be new hires or reassigned staff?

The Ministry of Finance has not commented on whether the roles will be filled by new recruits or existing staff — watch official postings for confirmation.

Where are German federal government jobs advertised?

On karriere.bund.de (the Federal Government career portal) and Interamt (interamt.de), the main public-sector job board.

What is ITZBund?

The IT Service Centre of the Federal Government — the central IT provider for the federal administration, expected to need 51 new positions for the two taxes.

What pay scale applies to Customs and ITZBund jobs?

Typically the TVöD tariff scale for employees (pay groups like E9–E13 depending on the role); civil-servant posts follow the BBesG scale.

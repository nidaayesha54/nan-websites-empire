Germany Government Jobs 2026

Germany's federal jobs run through Interamt — and right now the Federal Agency for Nature Conservation is hiring office staff with a 29 October 2026 deadline. Here's how the portal works.

✅ Updated October 17, 2026

If you want a job with the German federal government (Bund), there is one portal to learn: Interamt (interamt.de) — the central application platform where federal agencies post vacancies and receive online applications. Postings are published on service.bund.de, but the application itself usually runs through Interamt after a one-time registration.

A live example shows how it works: the Bundesamt für Naturschutz (BfN) — the Federal Agency for Nature Conservation — is currently recruiting a Bürosachbearbeiter/in (m/w/d) (office administrator) with applications closing 29 October 2026 (Stellen-ID 1497728 on Interamt). This guide explains the Interamt process step by step, using the BfN posting as a real-world walkthrough.

How Interamt Works — The 5-Minute Explainer

Interamt is the German public sector's shared e-recruiting system, used by federal ministries, federal agencies and many Länder and municipal employers:

  • One registration: create a single Interamt account and reuse it for every federal application;
  • Stellen-ID search: every posting has an ID (like the BfN's 1497728) — enter it to jump straight to the vacancy;
  • Structured application: fill in personal data in the portal's forms and upload documents as PDFs;
  • Status tracking: the portal shows your application's progress through the selection stages;
  • Deadlines are hard: the portal typically closes applications automatically at the stated deadline.

The BfN Role: What an Office Administrator Does

The Federal Agency for Nature Conservation (bfn.de) is the federal scientific authority for nature conservation. A Bürosachbearbeiter there handles the administrative engine room: correspondence and filing, meeting and travel organization, budget-adjacent clerical tasks, and coordination across specialist units. The posting advertises the family-friendly, modern public-employer package Germany uses to attract administrative talent:

  • Family-friendly authority culture — family service, parent-child office;
  • Deutschland-Jobticket subsidy for commuting;
  • Flexible working-time models and mobile working;
  • Individual development through diverse professional training;
  • Good transport connections and a sustainable, modern work environment.

Documents You Need (German Standard)

The BfN posting lists the classic German public-sector application set. Prepare these as PDFs before the deadline:

  • Complete personal data entered in Interamt + a cover letter;
  • Lückenloser, tabellarischer Lebenslauf — a complete, gap-free tabular CV;
  • Qualification and degree certificates;
  • Work references / official appraisals if available;
  • Relevant training certificates; disability status proof if applicable.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Interamt?

Interamt (interamt.de) is the central online application portal for German federal public-sector jobs, used by federal ministries, agencies and many Länder and municipal employers. One registration covers all applications.

What is the deadline for the BfN office administrator job?

29 October 2026, via Interamt under Stellen-ID 1497728. The portal typically closes applications automatically at the deadline.

What documents do I need for a German federal job application?

A cover letter, a complete gap-free tabular CV, qualification/degree certificates, work references or appraisals if available, and relevant training records — all as PDFs uploaded in Interamt.

What is service.bund.de?

The official federal publication portal for public-administration vacancies across the Bund, Länder, cities and municipalities. It publishes the ads; applications usually run through Interamt or the hiring body's own channel.

What benefits does the BfN advertise?

A family-friendly authority culture, Deutschland-Jobticket subsidy, flexible working-time models, mobile working, individual training and development, and good transport connections.

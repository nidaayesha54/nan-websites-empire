One current example: the Landkreis Main-Spessart is recruiting an Arbeitsvermittler/in (m/w/d) for its Jobcenter in Karlstadt, with applications closing 25 October 2026.

For candidates with administrative training, social-sector experience or a relevant degree who want meaningful public-service work with tariff-protected conditions, Jobcenter advisory roles are among the most accessible E 9-range positions in German local government.

What an Arbeitsvermittler Does

Employment advisors (Arbeitsvermittler) are the caseworkers of the German welfare-to-work system:

  • Advising and supporting jobseekers receiving citizen's income (Bürgergeld) — profiling, integration agreements, placement;
  • Matching clients with vacancies, training measures and employer contacts;
  • Documenting decisions in case-management systems and applying social legislation (SGB II);
  • Cooperating with employers, training providers and internal specialist teams.

It is demanding people-work with real social impact — and a secure tariff framework around it.

Pay & Benefits: TVöD-VKA E 9c

Pay group E 9c sits in the upper-middle of the municipal tariff (TVöD-VKA), typical for gehobener Dienst roles. The Main-Spessart posting adds the extras German public employers use to compete:

  • Employer-funded occupational pension worth an additional 7.75% of monthly pay;
  • Performance-oriented pay (LOB) — bonuses on top of the tariff salary;
  • Flexible working: flexitime, mobile working and part-time arrangements;
  • Health management programs and bike leasing via salary conversion;
  • Structured personnel development and training concepts.

Typical Requirements for Jobcenter Advisory Roles

  • Completed training as an administrative assistant, or a degree in public administration, social work or a related field — requirements vary by posting, so check the official text;
  • Knowledge of social legislation (SGB II / SGB III) is a strong advantage;
  • Communication skills, resilience and structured casework habits;
  • Very good written and spoken German; EU driving licence is sometimes requested for field duties.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the deadline for the Main-Spessart Jobcenter Arbeitsvermittler job?

Applications close on 25 October 2026. Public-sector deadlines are strict — submit through the stated channel before that date.

What does TVöD-VKA E 9c pay?

E 9c is an upper-middle municipal tariff group for gehobener Dienst roles. Exact monthly pay depends on experience level (Stufe) — the posting also lists an employer-funded pension top-up of 7.75% and performance bonuses.

Germany Government Jobs 2026

Germany's domestic intelligence service, the Bundesamt für Verfassungsschutz, is hiring this October — from a senior Fachreferent role paying up to €89,580 to office-assistant and driver positions, with application deadlines of November 8–12, 2026.

✅ Updated October 20, 2026
Home » Germany Government Jobs » Articles

Das Bundesamt für Verfassungsschutz (BfV) — Germany's domestic intelligence service — is actively recruiting in October 2026, with three current postings on its official careers pages: a Fachreferent (senior specialist) for operational-technical support in counter-terrorism, a Büroassistenz (office assistant) for data protection, and a Kraftfahrer (driver) minijob. Deadlines run 8–12 November 2026.

The headline role is the Fachreferent (m/w/d) for operativ-technische Fachunterstützung im Bereich Islamismus/islamistischer Terrorismus, based in Berlin in the higher service (Höherer Dienst). The role covers steering operational-technical support, identifying relevant software products, overseeing technical testing, and developing operational security measures — essentially the technical backbone of counter-terrorism analysis. Pay is Entgeltgruppe 13 TV EntgO Bund: €62,489 to €89,580 gross per year, with the possibility of later civil-servant status (Verbeamtung).

At the other end of the scale, the BfV is also hiring a Büroassistenz für den Datenschutz (deadline 8 November) and a Kraftfahrer minijob (deadline 10 November) — entry points that require no university degree.

The Fachreferent Role in Detail

  • Required: German citizenship and a Master's degree in economics/management with a focus on management and digitalisation;
  • Interest in or knowledge of Islamism / Islamist terrorism topics;
  • Conceptual working ability, strong organisation, gender and diversity competence;
  • Above-average communication and teamwork skills, confident demeanour, good English;
  • Willingness to travel on duty; security clearance (Sicherheitsüberprüfung) for intelligence-service work;
  • Benefits: permanent contract, flexible working hours, part-time options, overtime compensation as time off, onboarding and mentoring, and later Verbeamtung (civil-servant status) possible.

Büroassistenz and Kraftfahrer Roles

  • Büroassistenz (Datenschutz): office-management background (e.g. Kaufmann/-frau für Büromanagement), written and spoken German, knowledge of legal basics, preferably experience in administration and data protection;
  • Kraftfahrer minijob: preferably one year of documented driving experience;
  • Both require German citizenship; women, applicants with migration background and severely disabled applicants are expressly encouraged.

How to Apply

1
Open the BVA online application portal (bewerbung.dienstleistungszentrum.de) and enter the procedure number: AWV-2026-112 (Fachreferent), AWV-2026-122 (Büroassistenz) or AWV-2026-084 (Kraftfahrer).
2
Complete the online form and upload degree certificates, CV and proof of citizenship before the deadline (8, 10 or 12 November 2026).
3
Prepare for written and oral aptitude tests — passing them is a precondition for further processing.
4
Expect a security vetting process (Sicherheitsüberprüfung) appropriate to intelligence-service employment.
5
Questions: the BVA Servicezentrum Personalgewinnung can be reached at +49 (0) 22899/358-87500.

Why Intelligence-Service Careers Are Competitive

BfV positions combine public-service security (permanent contracts, federal pay scales, pension) with genuinely unusual work — the Fachreferent posting advertises "gesellschaftlicher Mehrwert" (societal value) and current political relevance as core selling points. The citizenship requirement and security vetting narrow the field considerably, which means qualified German citizens face less competition here than in general federal administration postings.

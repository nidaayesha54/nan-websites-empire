GTA 6 in Germany: €69.99 at Lidl, USK 18 and Prices

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and in Germany the launch looked a little different from anywhere else. The game costs the same as in the rest of the eurozone — €79.99 for the Standard Edition and €99.99 for the Ultimate Edition — but a German supermarket undercut every electronics chain in the country, the age rating is the strictest one the German board can give, and the box on the shelf does not contain what most buyers expect it to contain.

That matters because Germany is one of the biggest games markets in Europe, and the German launch had its own rules. The price, the rating, the language and the box itself all work differently here than they do in the United States or the United Kingdom — and knowing the German specifics before you buy is the difference between paying €79.99 and paying €69.99 for exactly the same game.

The German Price: €79.99, or €69.99 at Lidl

The headline price in Germany is the eurozone price: €79.99 for the Standard Edition and €99.99 for the Ultimate Edition, the same on the PlayStation Store and the Microsoft Store. That matches the rest of the eurozone and sits below the UK price of £69.99 and £89.99 only because of the exchange rate — in euros, the game costs the same from Lisbon to Berlin.

But the cheapest way to buy GTA 6 in Germany was not a games shop at all. Lidl Germany's online store listed the game for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S at €69.99, ten euros under the list price, with sales opening on Thursday, November 12. The discount applied only to customers with the Lidl Plus app — anyone without the app paid the usual €79.99 — and Lidl did not sell the game anywhere else. It was the kind of deal a discount supermarket chain can offer that electronics chains cannot match, and it made the German launch the cheapest in Western Europe for anyone with the app installed.

USK 18: The Strictest German Rating

GTA 6 carries the top age rating everywhere it is sold, and in Germany that means USK 18 — the German adults-only rating, shown on the box as a red badge. The rating was finalised across all boards on October 1, 2026, exactly 47 days before launch, when Rockstar replaced the long-standing Rating Pending badge with the finished certificates on its website and on the PlayStation and Xbox store pages. In the United States the game is ESRB Mature 17+, in the United Kingdom and most of Europe it is PEGI 18, in Australia it is R 18+, in Japan it is CERO Z, and in Germany it is USK 18.

The rating reflects what is in the game. The ESRB listing carries seven content descriptors — Blood and Gore, Intense Violence, Mature Humor, Nudity, Strong Language, Strong Sexual Content, and Use of Drugs and Alcohol — the same seven that GTA 5 carries on modern platforms. The ESRB listing also includes In-Game Purchases, but not the stricter Includes Random Items label used for loot boxes, which fits with what Rockstar has said about the game having no monetisation beyond the edition upgrade and GTA+ membership.

The Box, the Language and the Pre-Load

The box is where German buyers get their biggest surprise. Like almost every retailer's version, the boxed copy of GTA 6 holds a download code, not a disc — you download the full game either way, which on PS5 is about 209.75 GB to download and about 212 GB installed. The Ultimate Edition is digital only, so there is no boxed version of it at all, and the pre-order bonus — the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ — has to be redeemed within 180 days of placing the pre-order, and cannot be redeemed on child accounts with age-restricted content settings.

The language is the other German-specific detail. The voice acting is English only, so German players get the game exactly as it was recorded, with subtitles and interface text in German. Pre-loading opened on November 10 — at midnight local time on PS5 and at 00:00 GMT on Xbox Series X|S — and the game unlocked worldwide on November 19. If you bought the Lidl copy, the code in the box still needed the same download, so the €10 saving was real, but the 212 GB download was not optional.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does GTA 6 cost in Germany?

€79.99 for the Standard Edition and €99.99 for the Ultimate Edition on the PlayStation Store and the Microsoft Store. Lidl Germany sold it for €69.99 with the Lidl Plus app from November 12.

What is the German age rating for GTA 6?

USK 18, the adults-only rating. The game is rated 18 in most of the world: PEGI 18 in Europe, ESRB Mature 17+ in the United States, R 18+ in Australia and CERO Z in Japan.

Does the boxed GTA 6 in Germany contain a disc?

No. The box holds a download code, not a disc, so you download the full game either way. The Ultimate Edition is digital only and has no boxed version at all.

The Bottom Line

In Germany, GTA 6 is a €79.99 game with a USK 18 badge, an English-only voice track and a box that holds a code instead of a disc — and for a few days in November, it was a €69.99 game if you had the Lidl Plus app. The price, the rating and the download are the same as the rest of Europe; the supermarket deal was the one part that was purely German.

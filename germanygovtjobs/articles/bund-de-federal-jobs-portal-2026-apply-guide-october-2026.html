Germany Government Jobs 2026

Stellen.bund.de is the German federal government's official jobs portal — the single front door to careers across federal ministries, agencies and institutions. Here is how it works and how to apply.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » Germany Government Jobs » Articles

If you want a job with the German federal government, there is exactly one portal that matters: stellen.bund.de, the official federal jobs board. Every federal ministry, higher federal authority and federal institution advertises its vacancies there — from the Foreign Office to the Federal Environment Agency, from customs administration to the armed-forces administration.

Demand for federal talent is structural, not cyclical. Germany's public sector employs more than five million people, and employer associations report a shortfall of well over 300,000 staff nationwide — from daycare expansion and rescue services to approval procedures for the economic transformation. Digitalisation alone is generating sustained demand for IT, cybersecurity, data and administrative specialists across federal authorities.

Note: Do not confuse the tracks. Beamte are appointed for life with special status (no right to strike, state pension); Tarifbeschäftigte are regular employees under the TVöD-Bund collective agreement with negotiated pay scales (Entgeltgruppen E1–E15Ü). Most external applicants enter as Tarifbeschäftigte — Beamte careers usually start via Vorbereitungsdienst (preparatory service).

What the Portal Lists

  • Ministerial roles — policy officers (Referenten), administrators and specialists across all federal ministries in Berlin and Bonn;
  • Federal agency positions — from the Federal Environment Agency to the Federal Office for Migration and Refugees;
  • IT and digitalisation roles — software development, cybersecurity, data science and eGovernment, including some English-friendly postings;
  • Customs, police and armed-forces administration — via their respective federal authorities;

Eligibility Requirements

  • Citizenship: Beamte appointments generally require German or EU citizenship; Tarifbeschäftigte roles are more open, though security-relevant posts may restrict;
  • Qualifications: each posting states its own — from completed Ausbildung to university degrees for the höherer Dienst;
  • German language: the working language of federal administration is German; most postings require fluent written and spoken German (exceptions exist in IT and research);
  • Security clearance: roles in security authorities require Überprüfung nach dem Sicherheitsüberprüfungsgesetz;
  • Non-EU applicants generally need a residence title permitting employment (e.g. EU Blue Card, Opportunity Card) for employee-track roles.

How to Apply via Stellen.bund.de

1
Open stellen.bund.de and use the search and filters — by authority, career group (Laufbahngruppe), location (Berlin, Bonn, nationwide) and full/part-time.
2
Read the Stellenausschreibung in full: note the reference number (Kennziffer), the exact deadline and whether it is a Beamten- or Tarifbeschäftigten-Stelle.
3
Prepare German-style documents: tabular Lebenslauf, Zeugnisse (school and work references) and a tailored cover letter — incomplete applications are routinely rejected.
4
Submit through the portal's online application system before the deadline; late applications are not considered in German public competitions.
5
Track your application and prepare for structured selection — assessment centres and panel interviews are standard for federal roles.

Why the Federal Portal Beats Job Boards

Commercial job boards scrape federal vacancies late, incompletely and sometimes inaccurately. Stellen.bund.de shows every federal competition with its official deadline, reference number and full requirements — and applications go straight into the authority's own system. With hundreds of thousands of unfilled public-sector positions nationwide and digitalisation creating new federal roles every month, checking the portal weekly is the single highest-value habit for anyone targeting a Bund career in 2026.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is stellen.bund.de?

The official jobs portal of the German federal government, listing vacancies across federal ministries, higher federal authorities and federal institutions.

What is the difference between Beamte and Tarifbeschäftigte?

Beamte are appointed civil servants with lifetime status; Tarifbeschäftigte are public-sector employees under the TVöD-Bund collective agreement. Most external hires enter as Tarifbeschäftigte.

Do I need German citizenship for federal jobs?

Beamte appointments generally require German or EU citizenship. Employee-track (TVöD) roles are more open, though some posts have citizenship or security-clearance restrictions.

Do I need to speak German?

Yes, for the large majority of federal roles — German is the working language of the administration. Some IT and research postings are English-friendly.

How do I apply for federal government jobs in Germany?

Through stellen.bund.de: find the Stellenausschreibung, prepare German-style application documents (Lebenslauf, Zeugnisse), and submit via the portal before the stated deadline.

Germany Government Jobs 2026

Germany's publicly funded research infrastructure — NFDI consortia, universities and national networks — is hiring in 2026, with TVöD pay scales, fully remote roles and English as a working language. Here is how this corner of the public sector works and how to apply.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » Germany Government Jobs » Articles

Beyond ministries and municipalities, Germany has a large publicly funded research sector that hires on public-sector terms: universities, Leibniz and Helmholtz institutes, and the NFDI (Nationale Forschungsdateninfrastruktur) consortia building Germany's national research-data infrastructure. These employers pay on the TVöD tariff scale, offer the job security of the public sector, and are among the most English-friendly public employers in the country.

A representative current posting illustrates the pattern: the German BioImaging Society (GerBI) is hiring an RDM Facilitator for Bioimaging Community Services within the NFDI4BIOIMAGE consortium — a fully remote, Germany-based role paid at TVöD E13, with applications closing 30 October 2026.

What the Role Involves

The posting seeks a candidate with a strong scientific background and interest in research infrastructure development — ideally a doctorate in biology, biotechnology, bioinformatics or a related life-science discipline (or equivalent experience). The work includes:

  • Integrating the OMERO services portfolio into the NFDI4BIOIMAGE service portfolio;
  • Supporting research data management, metadata standards and image-data formats;
  • Training, coaching and scientific consulting for the bioimaging community;
  • Developing services with an entrepreneurial approach and digital collaboration tools.

The employer highlights flexible working arrangements, family-friendly conditions and professional development — standard for the German public research sector.

Why Research Infrastructure Is a Smart Target

  • English as working language: many consortia operate in English — excellent English is often the requirement, with German merely desirable;
  • TVöD pay transparency: the pay group (e.g., E13) is stated on the posting, so you know the salary band upfront;
  • Remote and flexible work: fully remote roles exist, unusual in the traditional German public sector;
  • Fixed-term but renewable: project funding (e.g., 36 months) with goals to develop sustainable models beyond initial funding;
  • International profiles welcome: science and research-data roles routinely hire internationally.

How to Find and Apply for These Roles

1
Monitor NFDI consortium career pages, university job portals and research-network sites — postings are scattered, not centralized.
2
Check Interamt (interamt.de) too — some research institutions advertise there alongside classic public-sector roles.
3
Prepare a German-style application: tabular CV, certificates, and a motivation letter — even when the working language is English.
4
Verify degree recognition: foreign doctorates and degrees should be checked in the anabin database or via the Statement of Comparability.
5
Apply before the stated deadline (e.g., 30 October 2026 for the GerBI role) — late applications are not considered.
Tip for non-EU applicants: these roles typically qualify for the EU Blue Card (TVöD E13 pay comfortably exceeds the 2026 threshold) or the Skilled Immigration Act route — check current thresholds on Make it in Germany before applying.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NFDI and why does it hire internationally?

The Nationale Forschungsdateninfrastruktur is Germany's publicly funded national research-data infrastructure; its consortia hire research-data professionals and often work in English, welcoming international profiles.

What does TVöD E13 pay mean?

It is pay group E13 on the TVöD public-sector tariff scale — the standard band for roles requiring a doctorate-level qualification. The exact salary step depends on experience.

Are German public research jobs remote?

Some are — the GerBI RDM Facilitator role is fully remote within Germany, though most university and institute roles are on-site or hybrid.

Do I need German for research infrastructure roles?

Not always. Many postings require excellent English with German only desirable (e.g., B2) — but check each posting individually.

What is the deadline for the GerBI RDM Facilitator role?

30 October 2026, per the posting — verify on the employer's page before applying.

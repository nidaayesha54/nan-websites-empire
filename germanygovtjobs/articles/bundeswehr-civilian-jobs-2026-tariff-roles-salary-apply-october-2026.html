Bundeswehr Civilian Jobs 2026: 1,000 New Positions, TVöD Pay & How to Apply

Germany's 2026 defence budget adds 1,000 extra civilian Bundeswehr positions — many open to non-German nationals. The numbers, TVöD pay and application steps.

You do not need a uniform to work for the Bundeswehr. Behind 180,000+ soldiers stands a large civilian workforce — engineers, IT specialists, administrators, medics and logisticians — and the 2026 defence budget expands it: on top of 2,000 planned civilian posts, the cabinet approved 1,000 extra civilian positions. Many technical, administrative and medical roles are open to non-German nationals under tariff (Tarifbeschäftigte) contracts — one of 2026’s most accessible doors into German public-sector work.

The 2026 Budget: Posts & Targets

On July 30, 2025, the cabinet approved 10,000 additional military and 2,000 civilian posts, then added 1,000 more civilian positions beyond the June 2025 estimate. Long-term targets: 202,483 military and ~77,899 civilian posts by 2031. Defence Minister Boris Pistorius cites rising applications, and the force aims to recruit 20,000 volunteers in 2026 after 25,000+ joined in 2025 — its best result in 12 years.

Who Can Apply & How Pay Works

Technical, administrative and medical civilian roles are open to non-German nationals under Tarifbeschäftigte status, paid on the TVöD (Bund) tariff with transparent, seniority-based groups. In-demand: IT and cybersecurity (an acknowledged shortage), engineering, medical and skilled trades. Direct Beamter appointment remains exceptional for third-country nationals — start as a tariff employee; Verbeamtung may follow after years of service.

Conscription Reform 2026

New rules took effect January 1, 2026: 18-year-olds get an information letter; men must complete a questionnaire, women participate voluntarily. From July 1, 2027, 18-year-old men face mandatory medical examinations (Musterung). Service itself stays voluntary — 6–11 months at ~€2,600/month gross plus a driving-licence subsidy — with parliament able to reinstate demand-based conscription if volunteers fall short.

How to Apply: 5 Steps

  1. Browse civilian vacancies on bundeswehrkarriere.de, filtering for Tarifbeschäftigte roles.
  2. Check language and qualifications — typically B1–B2 German plus recognised credentials; some IT roles are English-friendly.
  3. Prepare a German-format application — tabular Lebenslauf, certificates, qualification recognition where required.
  4. Pass assessment and vetting — aptitude tests, interviews, proportionate security screening.
  5. Confirm the TVöD pay group (Entgeltgruppe) stated in the ad before signing.
Civilian Bundeswehr jobs carry TVöD pay, strong job security and pension rights — but civilian applications jumped 41%. Apply to multiple postings and keep recognition documents ready.

FAQs

How many civilian jobs is the Bundeswehr adding in 2026?

2,000 initially planned plus 1,000 extra approved — beyond the June 2025 estimate. The 2031 target is around 77,899 civilian posts.

Can non-Germans work as Bundeswehr civilians?

Yes — many technical, administrative and medical roles are open to non-German nationals under Tarifbeschäftigte status with TVöD pay.

What is TVöD pay?

Germany's public-sector collective agreement setting transparent pay groups by role and seniority — stated in every job ad.

Is military service mandatory in 2026?

No — service is voluntary (6-11 months at ~€2,600/month gross). From July 1, 2027, 18-year-old men face mandatory medical examinations.

Where do I apply?

On bundeswehrkarriere.de, filtering for civilian / Tarifbeschäftigte positions, with a German-format CV and recognised qualifications.

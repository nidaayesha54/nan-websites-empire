Germany Government Jobs 2026

The BAMF is hiring a policy Referent for its recruitment unit 11F in Nürnberg: E13 TVöD-Bund, two-year fixed term, law or admin master's required, apply by November 3.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » Germany Government Jobs » Articles

The Bundesamt für Migration und Flüchtlinge (BAMF) — Germany's federal office for migration and refugees — is hiring a Referent/in (w/m/d) im Bereich Grundsatz for its Referat 11F "Personalgewinnung" (recruitment) in Nürnberg. The position, reference BAMF-2026-363, is graded E 13 TVöD-Bund (höherer Dienst — the top tariff bracket for federal employees) and is offered full-time or part-time on a two-year fixed-term contract without objective reason (sachgrundlos). Applications are accepted exclusively through the Interamt online system and close on 3 November 2026.

This is a policy-level role at the heart of federal HR: Referat 11F designs the BAMF's entire recruitment process — it drafts the framework, coordinates internal and external selection procedures, and continuously develops the rules. As the policy Referent, you take on conceptual, coordinating and leadership-support tasks for the head of unit, including technical supervision of the policy staff, clarifying legal questions around the recruitment process, initiating the basic security check (SÜ1) required under the asylum law for BAMF staff, evaluating and improving working processes, and drafting internal reports, leadership templates and official statements. Your legal expertise is also used for grading reviews (Stufenprüfungen) under § 16 TVöD-Bund.

Honesty first: the posting's diversity statement welcomes applications regardless of nationality, but it sets a high bar — a scientific university degree at master's level, legal-grade precision in German, a mandatory security check (SÜ1), and ZAB recognition for foreign degrees. Crucially, because the contract is sachgrundlos (fixed term without objective reason), anyone who has previously been employed by the Bund cannot be considered. This is a role for German-speaking legal and administrative specialists — not a generalist or English-language opening.

What You Will Do

As the policy Referent in Referat 11F you combine legal precision with process design:

  • Take the lead on legal questions around the recruitment process and draft house-internal reports, leadership templates, talking points and official statements;
  • Initiate the basic security check (SÜ1) under § 5 Abs. 6 AsylG and design necessary process changes around it;
  • Evaluate, implement and continuously develop working processes, and further develop the process handbook for the administrative area;
  • Carry out grading reviews and step placements (Stufenprüfungen) under § 16 TVöD-Bund and keep developing the review process;
  • Exercise technical supervision of the policy staff supporting the head of unit.

Who Should Apply

The BAMF sets a deliberately high bar for this höherer-Dienst role:

  • A completed scientific university degree at master's level with at least the grade "gut" — preferably law with at least 13 points in total across both state examinations, or alternatively administrative science, political science or business administration;
  • Administrative experience and ideally first leadership experience are an advantage;
  • Knowledge of the relevant legal foundations — tariff and civil-service law, the Security Screening Act (SÜG), § 5 Abs. 6 AsylG — is ideal;
  • Readiness to undergo the basic security check (SÜ1) promptly;
  • Demonstrated leadership, social, communicative and methodological competence plus gender and diversity competence — with a high degree of conscientiousness and motivation;
  • Foreign degrees must be recognised by the ZAB (Central Office for Foreign Education) — without this proof the application cannot be considered.

How to Apply

1
Register on Interamt if you are not already registered — this is the only application channel; the BAMF does not accept email applications.
2
Upload your complete application documents in the online procedure before 3 November 2026, quoting reference BAMF-2026-363. No cover letter or motivation letter is required.
3
If your degree is from outside Germany, attach the ZAB statement of comparability — without it your application is excluded.
4
Expect an in-person Assessment Center in Nürnberg as part of the selection process, plus the SÜ1 security check. For procedural questions call the BAMF Service Center at 0911/943-0 or write to service@bamf.bund.de.

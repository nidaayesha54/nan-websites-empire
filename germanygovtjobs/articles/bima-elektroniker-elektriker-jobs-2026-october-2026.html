Germany Government Jobs 2026

The Federal Institute for Real Estate (BImA) is hiring electricians, electrical fitters and installers across Germany — trade roles with federal benefits, applications close 18 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » Germany Government Jobs » Articles

The Bundesanstalt für Immobilienaufgaben (BImA) — Germany's Federal Institute for Real Estate — is hiring Elektroniker/-innen, Elektromonteure/-innen, Elektroinstallateure/-innen and Elektriker/-innen (w/m/d) — electronics technicians, electrical fitters, electrical installers and electricians. The vacancy was published on service.bund.de on 22 October 2026 and applications close on 18 November 2026.

BImA is one of Germany's largest property owners: it manages the federal government's real estate portfolio — ministries, military properties, forests and housing — across the whole country. Its technical staff keep that estate running: electrical installations, building services, safety systems and energy infrastructure. For qualified tradespeople, a federal employer means crisis-proof employment, tariff pay and the public-sector pension scheme (VBL) — benefits that private contractors rarely match.

Honesty first: this is a German-language trade role — safety-critical electrical work requires clear communication in German, and the listing is published in German. The vacancy is single-sourced from the service.bund.de listing, so verify the exact locations, pay grade and required trade qualifications on the live listing before applying.

What BImA Electricians Do

  • Electrical installations: installing, maintaining and repairing electrical systems in federal buildings;
  • Building services: keeping lighting, power distribution, safety and communication systems operational;
  • Inspections and testing: statutory safety inspections (DGUV) and documentation of electrical installations;
  • Energy and modernisation: supporting energy-efficiency upgrades across the federal estate;
  • Emergency response: fault-finding and rapid repair when systems fail in occupied government buildings.

Who Should Apply

  • A completed Ausbildung (vocational qualification) as Elektroniker, Elektromonteur, Elektroinstallateur or Elektriker — or a comparable recognised qualification;
  • Working German — safety instructions, technical documentation and team communication are all in German;
  • Experience in building services or industrial electrical work is an advantage;
  • Reliability and safety consciousness — federal properties include security-sensitive sites where background checks apply;
  • A driving licence is commonly expected for roles covering multiple properties.

Why Federal Trade Jobs Are Worth It

Trade roles at federal agencies are among the most secure jobs in Germany: tariff pay under TVöD, the VBL supplementary pension, 30 days of annual leave, and structured working hours with overtime compensation. BImA adds the scale of a nationwide employer — internal transfer opportunities across regions, training for additional qualifications, and the stability that comes with a federal budget rather than a contractor's order book.

Germany Government Jobs 2026

The North Rhine-Westphalia Police is recruiting IT talent for its three-year dual-study Bachelor of Arts in Administrative Informatics (Verwaltungsinformatik) — combining police administration with hands-on digital skills.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » Germany Government Jobs » Articles

The North Rhine-Westphalia (NRW) Police is actively recruiting IT talent for its dual-study programme in Administrative Informatics (Verwaltungsinformatik) — a three-year course that ends with a Bachelor of Arts degree recognised across Europe and a career inside one of Germany's largest police forces.

The programme — advertised as RIA-IT (Regierungsinspektor-Anwärter Verwaltungsinformatik) — is aimed at specialists who know public administration with all its diverse areas of responsibility, processes, legal foundations and specialist applications, and have the skills to map that knowledge digitally and shape "Administration 4.0". During the three-year course, students learn programming fundamentals while discovering how the administration of tomorrow will work, combining administrative knowledge with IT skills in practice.

Graduates start their careers in one of three higher state authorities: the Landeskriminalamt (LKA NRW) in Düsseldorf, the Landesamt für Polizeitechnische Dienste (LZPD NRW) in Duisburg, or the Landesamt für Ausbildung, Fortbildung und Personalangelegenheiten (LAFP NRW) in Selm. Study takes place at the University of Applied Sciences for Police and Public Administration NRW (HSPV NRW) in Cologne (for LKA and LZPD) or Münster (for LAFP).

Note: This is a Beamtenlaufbahn (civil-service career track) in the gehobener Dienst — graduates enter as Regierungsinspektor-Anwärter with civil-servant status prospects, not as ordinary private-sector trainees. That means job security, structured promotion and the public-sector pension scheme from the start of your career.

What You Will Do After Graduation

The programme is built for the digitalisation of policing and administration. After graduation, typical fields include:

  • Open government and eGovernment — digital citizen services and transparent administration;
  • HR management systems — digital personnel processes for tens of thousands of police employees;
  • Software development — building and maintaining specialist police applications;
  • Training and personnel development systems at the LAFP.

Eligibility Requirements

  • University entrance qualification — Abitur, Fachhochschulreife or an equivalent recognised qualification (standard for gehobener Dienst dual study);
  • German citizenship or EU citizenship as a rule for appointment as a Beamter;
  • Very good German language skills (working in administration means working in German law and German documentation);
  • Interest in both IT and administration — the programme explicitly seeks people who want to combine the two, not pure coders;
  • Meeting the health, fitness and character requirements for civil-service appointment.

How to Apply

1
Go to polizei.nrw/ria-it — the official application page for the NRW Police IT dual-study programme.
2
Check the current application window and requirements for the next intake, and confirm which authority (LKA, LZPD, LAFP) you are applying to.
3
Prepare your school-leaving certificates and a CV showing any IT experience, projects or relevant coursework.
4
Submit the online application and prepare for the police selection procedure (aptitude testing and interviews).
5
On acceptance, you begin the three-year dual study alternating between HSPV NRW and practical phases at your authority.

Why This Programme Stands Out

Germany's public sector is desperate for digital talent — and police IT is where some of the most sensitive, most interesting systems live. The RIA-IT route offers what private-sector junior roles cannot: a paid dual study, a Europe-recognised Bachelor's degree, civil-servant career prospects and a guaranteed specialist role in one of three major state authorities on graduation. For Abitur graduates torn between computer science and public service, it removes the need to choose.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the NRW Police RIA-IT programme?

A three-year dual-study Bachelor of Arts in Administrative Informatics (Verwaltungsinformatik) run by the North Rhine-Westphalia Police, combining police administration with IT and software skills.

Where do graduates work?

At the LKA NRW in Düsseldorf, the LZPD NRW in Duisburg or the LAFP NRW in Selm — covering criminal investigation IT, police technical services and training/personnel systems.

What degree do you get?

A Bachelor of Arts recognised throughout Europe, earned at the HSPV NRW in Cologne or Münster.

Who can apply for RIA-IT?

Typically holders of a university entrance qualification (Abitur/Fachhochschulreife or equivalent) with German or EU citizenship and an interest in both IT and public administration.

How do I apply?

Online at polizei.nrw/ria-it — check the current intake window, submit certificates and CV, and complete the police selection procedure.

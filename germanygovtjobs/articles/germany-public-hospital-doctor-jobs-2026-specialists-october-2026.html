The Honest Truth: German Language Is Required

This is the single most important fact in this article. Public-sector doctor roles in Germany normally require German language proficiency, typically at B2 or C1 level. This is not a formality: physicians take histories, explain diagnoses and coordinate with German-speaking teams every day, and the language level is assessed as part of the licensing pathway. A specialist qualification does not substitute for it — if your German is weak, language study is the first investment, not applications.

Getting Your Degree Recognized: The Approbation Process

A foreign medical degree does not automatically authorise you to practise in Germany. You must complete the recognition process (Anerkennung) leading to the Approbation — the German licence to practise:

  • Follow the official procedure on anerkennung-in-deutschland.de — your primary source, not third-party agents;
  • Prepare certified copies of your degree, specialist certificates and proof of experience;
  • Procedures differ by federal state (Land), and language certification at the required level is a standard component;
  • Start early — recognition takes months, so run language study and document preparation in parallel.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which specialist doctor jobs are listed for Germany in October 2026?

Internal medicine, anesthesiology, and gynecology/obstetrics posts in public hospitals and clinics across 25+ German cities — listed on the Make it in Germany portal, updated 18 October 2026.

Do I need German language to work as a doctor in Germany?

Yes — typically B2 or C1. Doctors communicate with patients and teams in German, and the level is assessed in the licensing pathway.

What is the Approbation process?

Official recognition of foreign medical qualifications in Germany (see anerkennung-in-deutschland.de). Your degree must be recognised before you can practise.

What is the 2026 EU Blue Card salary threshold for doctors?

€45,934.20 gross per year, as a shortage occupation. The standard threshold is €50,700.

How do I apply for these doctor listings?

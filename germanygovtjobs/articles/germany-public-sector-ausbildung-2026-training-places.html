Germany Public Sector Ausbildung 2026: Training Places, Pay & Application Guide

The public sector is one of Germany’s biggest trainers — with Bavaria alone offering 1,300+ training places. Here is how Ausbildung in administration, justice and enforcement works in 2026.

Ausbildung Training Public Sector

The Public Sector Trains at Scale

Germany’s dual vocational training system (Ausbildung) is world-famous — and the public sector is one of its biggest users. Authorities across the country train school-leavers as administrative assistants, judicial clerks, enforcement officers and IT specialists, combining paid practical work with classroom study over two to three years. It is the most direct route into a secure government career without a university degree.

The headline number for 2026/27: Bavaria alone is offering more than 1,300 training places in its public sector, across administration, justice and the justice enforcement service.

Bavaria’s 1,300+ Training Places

The Free State of Bavaria’s training program — run through the LPA (Landespersonalausschuss) — is aimed at recent graduates and career starters. Key facts:

  • 1,300+ training places from 2027 in administration, justice and justice enforcement
  • No prior public-administration experience required — the program is built for beginners
  • Full details and the selection process on the LPA website (lpa.bayern.de); the “Bayern Direkt” hotline (089 12 22 20) answers questions

Training Tracks in the Public Service

Public-sector training generally follows two models:

  • Dual Ausbildung (Angestellte track): a standard vocational contract — e.g. Verwaltungsfachangestellte/r (administrative assistant) — with a training salary and strong takeover chances.
  • Vorbereitungsdienst (Beamte track): preparatory service as a Beamtenanwärter/in (civil-servant-in-training), leading to appointment in one of the career groups — mittlerer Dienst (intermediate service) for most school-leavers, gehobener Dienst for Abitur/Fachhochschule graduates.

How to Apply for a Public-Sector Ausbildung

  1. Pick your track. Decide between the dual Ausbildung (employee track) and the Beamtenanwärter preparatory service — the latter requires German/EU citizenship for most posts.
  2. Check the school-leaving requirement. Many intermediate-service tracks ask for Mittlere Reife; higher tracks require Abitur or equivalent.
  3. Apply through the official portal. Bavaria via the LPA; federal via karriere.bund.de; everywhere else via Interamt.de or the authority’s own career page.
  4. Prepare for the selection test. Public-sector training selection typically includes a written aptitude test (reasoning, German, maths) and an interview.
  5. Apply early. Training places for the following year are often advertised a full year in advance — deadlines pass quickly.
Takeover rates after public-sector Ausbildung are high — authorities train to fill their own future posts. If long-term security matters to you, this is one of Germany’s most reliable career starts.

FAQs

What is an Ausbildung in the German public sector?

A 2–3 year paid vocational training combining practical work in an authority with classroom learning — the standard route into administrative, justice and enforcement careers.

How many public-sector training places does Bavaria offer?

Bavaria advertised more than 1,300 training places from 2027 across administration, justice and the justice enforcement service (via the LPA).

Do I need experience to start a public-sector Ausbildung?

No — the programs are designed for recent school-leavers and career starters. A school-leaving certificate (typically Mittlere Reife or Abitur depending on the track) is the usual requirement.

What is a Beamtenanwärter?

A civil-servant-in-training: trainees preparing for Beamte status, who complete a preparatory service (Vorbereitungsdienst) before appointment to a career group.

Where do I apply for public-sector Ausbildung places?

Through the LPA (for Bavaria), karriere.bund.de for federal training, Interamt.de, and each state’s or city’s own career portal.

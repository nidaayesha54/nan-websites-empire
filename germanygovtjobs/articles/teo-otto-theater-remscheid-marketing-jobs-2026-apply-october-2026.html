The Teo Otto Theater in Remscheid is hiring a Marketing, Disposition and Vermietung coordinator at TVöD E 9c — permanent, full-time, with applications closing November 5, 2026.

✅ Updated October 22, 2026
Home » Germany Government Jobs » Articles

The Stadt Remscheid is hiring a Teamkoordination Marketing, Disposition und Vermietung (w/m/d) for the Teo Otto Theater — the city's municipal theater with around 120 events per year. The position is classified at Entgeltgruppe 9c TVöD-VKA (gehobener Dienst), is unbefristet (permanent), starts 1 January 2027, and closes for applications on 5 November 2026.

Municipal cultural institutions like this one rarely advertise marketing leadership roles at TVöD 9c — most sit lower in the tariff structure. The role combines press work, social media, event disposition (scheduling) and venue rental (Vermietung), making it a genuine all-rounder position for someone who wants culture-sector marketing with public-sector security.

Honesty on language: the posting lists no explicit German level, but a public-facing press and social-media role in a German municipality realistically requires fluent German, around C1. The vacancy was cross-verified on two independent sources with a clean deadline and complete details.

What the Role Covers

  • Marketing coordination — leading the theater's marketing strategy across print, digital and outdoor channels;
  • Press work — writing press releases, maintaining media contacts and handling public communication in German;
  • Disposition — scheduling and coordinating the venue's roughly 120 annual events;
  • Vermietung — managing venue rentals to external organizers, a revenue stream for the theater;
  • Evening and weekend presence — availability during performances is an explicit requirement.

Qualifications and What TVöD E 9c Means

The formal bar is a bachelor's or FH (Fachhochschule) diploma in marketing, communication, media, cultural management or business administration with a marketing focus — or demonstrably equivalent professional experience — plus hands-on marketing and communication practice. E 9c TVöD-VKA sits in the gehobener Dienst (upper service), the bracket for roles requiring a degree or equivalent, at 39 hours per week full time in NRW municipalities. The city states part-time arrangements are generally possible, and the contract is unbefristet — permanent from day one, with the standard public-sector protections that implies.

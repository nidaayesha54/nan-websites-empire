How to Apply

  1. Search federal openings on bund.de — the official portal for federal administration jobs.
  2. Prepare a tabular CV (Lebenslauf) with photo and certified copies of degrees — German hiring is formal.
  3. Note the pay group: TVöD E9–E13 for employees, or A9–A13 for Beamte (civil servant) career tracks.
  4. Submit applications by the listed deadline; public-sector processes run 6–12 weeks.
  5. Non-EU applicants need a valid residence/work permit — many roles require German at B2 level or higher.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where do I find German government jobs?

Federal openings are listed on bund.de. State and municipal roles appear on the portals of the 16 Länder and city administrations.

What is TVöD and what are E9–E13 groups?

TVöD is the collective agreement for federal public employees. E9–E13 are pay groups — higher numbers mean higher qualifications and pay. Beamte (civil servants) use A-groups instead.

What is the difference between Angestellte and Beamte?

Angestellte are public employees under TVöD; Beamte are appointed civil servants with lifetime tenure, separate pension rights, and typically higher job security.

Do I need to speak German?

Most roles require German at B2 level or above, and applications are expected in German. Some research and EU-facing roles accept English.

Can non-EU citizens apply?

Yes, with a valid residence and work permit. EU citizens have equal access; non-EU applicants should check the Blue Card EU or work-visa requirements for their role.

What do German public-sector jobs pay?

TVöD E9–E11 roles pay roughly €3,900–€6,100 per month; E13 specialists €5,600–€7,900; Beamte A9–A13 €3,400–€6,300, with generous pension benefits.

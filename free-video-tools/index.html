❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Which free AI video tool has no watermark in 2026?

CapCut, Canva Video, Clipchamp, Luma Dream Machine, Kling, Haiper and Vidnoz AI all offer free exports without a visible watermark, though most limit clip length or monthly credits. Filter the directory by "No watermark" to see them all.

Can I make AI videos completely free?

Yes — tools like CapCut, Luma, Kling and Canva Video have genuinely usable free tiers. The catch is usually clip length (5–10 seconds for AI generation) or monthly credit caps. Our comparator lays out the limits side by side.

What is the best free AI video generator for beginners?

CapCut and Canva Video are the easiest starting points — free, watermark-free and template-driven. If you want cinematic AI clips, try Luma Dream Machine or Kling. Take the fit quiz for a personal pick.

How can I tell if a video was made with AI?

Look for overly smooth motion, morphing backgrounds and inconsistent hands or text. For a deeper check, run it through AI Video Detector, a free tool that analyzes videos for AI-generation signals.

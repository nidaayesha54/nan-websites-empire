GTA 6 Night Shift: Working After Dark in Vice City

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Vice City changes after dark. The neon comes on, the clubs open, and the city fills with a different crowd — and a different kind of work. Players keep asking the same question: can you work the night shift in GTA 6? The honest answer is more interesting than a simple yes or no, because the game gives you night work in some forms and withholds it in others.

This guide covers what the night actually offers: the clubs, the rideshare app, the buses, the random encounters that only happen after dark — and the jobs that players expect to find but that the game has never confirmed. Knowing the difference saves you hours of hunting for a shift that does not exist.

What the night gives you: clubs, rides and routes

At night you can visit clubs in Vice City and Port Gellhorn, and the nightclub guide in the game lists three venues and the cost of a private dance. The clubs are places to spend money, not earn it — but they are also where the night crowds gather, and the random encounters that spin out of them are some of the most detailed in the game. Night is when Leonida's strangers come out, and the side missions that start with a stranger's call often start after dark.

Movement at night is its own system. A taxi app on the in-game phone, called Ryde Me, works as fast travel — call a car, ride to your stop, and if your partner is in the back it becomes a conversation. Bus stops let you ride the whole route or skip straight to your stop, and the buses keep running after dark. Neither one pays you, but both are how a working player gets around the city without burning fuel or drawing attention.

The rideshare question: passenger or driver?

The Ryde Me app is the centre of the night-shift question, because it looks like a job and acts like a service. You hail the car, you ride in it, and the fare is yours to pay — the app is a way to travel, not a way to earn. A video guide published on launch day shows a taxi ride unlocking after a mission for a character called Cal, and the ride itself is a guided trip, not a shift you can clock into.

The older GTA games had a taxi driver side mission — pick up passengers, deliver them on time, earn fares, with tips for smooth driving — and players who remember San Andreas keep looking for it in Vice City. As of launch week, no review or guide we have read describes a taxi driver job, a paramedic job or a vigilante job in GTA 6. Missing from footage does not mean missing from the game, but it does mean nobody has found one yet, and you should not plan your first week around it.

Night work that does pay

What does pay after dark is the same work that pays during the day: the robbery loop. Stores, jewelry shops, payday loan stores and banks do not close at night, and the night is often the better time to hit them — fewer witnesses on quiet streets, and the wanted system in GTA 6 runs on what the police actually see. A quiet street at night is a different place from a crowded boulevard at noon, and the game treats it that way.

Deliveries pay at night too. Pilot Pete's delivery runs, reported at $20,000 per run, and the fence network for stolen goods both run around the clock. The night shift in GTA 6 is not a job you clock into — it is a schedule you set yourself, and the players who earn the most after dark are the ones who treat the night as cover, not as a shift.

How to work the night well

First, sleep before you work. Sleeping at a safehouse restores health and stamina and, in the morning, gives you a full day to plan. Second, use the night for movement and the day for the jobs that need crowds — or the other way round, if your style is the opposite. Third, remember that the police remember your outfit: buying new clothes removes the heat, and splitting Jason and Lucia up or switching cars throws the pursuit off. The night rewards the player who plans it, and punishes the one who wanders into it unprepared.

FAQ

Can you be a taxi driver in GTA 6? Not that anyone has confirmed. The Ryde Me app lets you ride in taxis as a passenger, but no review or guide describes a playable taxi driver job in GTA 6.

What jobs can you do at night in GTA 6? Robberies, deliveries and fencing all run after dark. Clubs are open at night in Vice City and Port Gellhorn, and buses and the Ryde Me app keep running.

The bottom line: GTA 6 has no night shift to clock into, but it has a whole night to work. Use the dark as cover, ride the bus or the app when you need to move, and save the job hunting for the work the game actually offers.

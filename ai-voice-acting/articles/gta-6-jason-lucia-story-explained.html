GTA 6's Two Heroes: Jason and Lucia's Story Explained

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 has two heroes — and that is the first thing to understand about it. Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos share the story of GTA 6, a tale about escape rather than empire-building, set in Vice City and the wider Leonida state. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, thirteen years after GTA 5, and IGN's review — watched 7.44 million times on launch day — calls the game a masterpiece. The dual-lead structure is one of the most talked-about parts of that praise, and this guide explains who Jason and Lucia are, what their story is about, and how playing two characters changes the game.

The two-lead structure matters because it changes the shape of the story. GTA 5 had three protagonists and a story about building an empire. GTA 6 has two, and its story is about getting out — about escape, not conquest. That is a different kind of tale, and it is told from two sides at once, which is why the game keeps both characters in focus from the very beginning.

Who Are Jason and Lucia?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos are the two protagonists of GTA 6, and the game is built around both of them. They are partners in the story's central escape — two people trying to get out of the life they are in, in a city that does not let go easily. Vice City is their home and their trap at the same time: neon beachfront boulevards, beaches, swamps, and countryside, all of it beautiful, and all of it part of the world they are trying to leave behind.

The game introduces them in a fixed order, and the opening chapters are about setting them both up — who they are, what they want, and what is keeping them in Vice City. That introduction is why the game does not let you switch freely at first: the story needs to establish both characters before it hands you the choice of who to play.

A Story About Escape, Not Empire

The distinction matters. In GTA 5, the story was about building something — money, power, a crew, an empire. In GTA 6, the story is about escape: two people trying to get out, and everything that stands in their way. That changes the tone of the whole game. The missions, the choices, and the risks all serve a different question — not "how much can we build?" but "how do we get free?"

It also changes how the missions feel. A story about escape makes every job a means to an end, and every setback a threat to the plan. The stakes are personal in a way that empire-building never quite is: the characters are not trying to win Vice City, they are trying to leave it. That is the emotional core of GTA 6, and it is the reason the two leads matter — because an escape story needs two people, and it needs to be told from both sides.

Two Perspectives, One Story

The story is told from both characters' points of view, and that is what makes the dual-lead structure more than a gimmick. You see the same events from Jason's side and from Lucia's side, and the two perspectives fill in what the other leaves out. A scene that looks one way through Jason's eyes can look very different through Lucia's — and the game uses that to tell a richer story than either character could carry alone.

That structure also shapes how you play. You are not choosing a favourite and sticking with them: you are living the story through both of them, and the game moves between them as the story demands. It is a different rhythm from a single-protagonist game, and it takes a little while to settle into — but once it does, the two perspectives become the thing that makes the story feel complete.

How Switching Between Them Works

Free switching between Jason and Lucia unlocks only after the "Breakfast Run" mission in Chapter 2. Before that point, the story chooses the character for you: you play whoever the current mission needs, and there is no way to swap. That is by design, not a bug — the opening chapters introduce both characters in a fixed order, and the game keeps you on that path until the two leads are properly established.

Once switching is unlocked, hold Right on the D-pad to open the Partner Menu, then press R1 on PS5 or RB on Xbox to confirm the switch. Hold the button — a quick tap will not open the menu. If one character's portrait is greyed out, your partner is busy — for example, in a RydeMe ride — and you will need to wait and retry. And remember that some missions lock you to a single character for their whole duration: the Partner Menu is a between-missions tool, and it works best when you are free-roaming in Vice City and the wider Leonida state.

Their World: Vice City and Leonida

Jason and Lucia's story plays out across Vice City and the wider Leonida state — neon beachfront boulevards, beaches, swamps, and countryside, with night lighting that IGN's review singled out as jaw-dropping: headlights piercing the dark, city lights from above, beach storms rolling in. The world is dense and living, with real traffic and roadworks, deep car culture, and a wanted system in which the police act only on what they witness.

The world is also full of things to do beyond the story: fishing, pool, side jobs, radio stations, and an in-game phone and social media layer. Those activities are not just filler — they are the texture of the life Jason and Lucia are trying to escape, and the game is at its best when you take the time to live in it between missions. The story is about escape, but the world is worth staying in for a while first.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I play as Lucia from the start?

No — the story chooses the character for you until free switching unlocks after the "Breakfast Run" mission in Chapter 2. Before that, you play whoever the current mission needs. Once it unlocks, hold Right on the D-pad and choose in the Partner Menu.

Is GTA 6 a direct sequel to GTA 5's story?

GTA 6 tells a new story with new leads — Jason and Lucia — set in Vice City and the wider Leonida state. It is a tale about escape rather than empire-building, and it stands on its own: you do not need to have played GTA 5 to follow it.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6's story belongs to two people: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, partners in an escape from Vice City. Two perspectives, one story, and a world that rewards taking your time. Play through to the Breakfast Run unlock, switch freely after that, and let both sides of the story tell themselves — that is how GTA 6 is meant to be played.

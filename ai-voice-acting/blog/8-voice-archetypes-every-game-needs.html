8 Voice Archetypes Every Game Needs

October 12, 2026 · 5 min read

Great casting is pattern recognition. Across thousands of games, the same eight voice archetypes cover roughly 90% of all characters. Learn them, and you can cast an entire game in an afternoon. Each archetype below includes direction notes and a sample line — paste them into our TTS preview tool to hear the difference.

1. The Hero

Confident, warm, mid-range. Never shouts without reason — hero voices project. "I will not let this city fall — not while I still stand."

2. The Villain

Low, slow, controlled. The best villains sound like they're enjoying themselves. "You really thought you could defy me? How... amusing."

3. The Comic Sidekick

Bright, fast, bouncy. Exists to release tension — direct them like a sugar rush. "Wait wait wait — did that dragon just wink at me?"

4. The Wise Mentor

Deep, calm, measured. Every line sounds like it cost them something to learn. "The path you fear is the path you must walk, child."

5. The Child

High, innocent, wondering. Keep energy up and sentences short. "Wow! Are we really going to the moon?"

6. The Narrator

Neutral, cinematic, invisible. The narrator should never steal the scene. "Long ago, in a kingdom forgotten by time..."

7. The Robot / AI

Flat cadence, clipped phrasing. Slightly too precise to be human. "Threat. Level. Assessed."

8. The Monster

Guttural, heavy, low. Fewer words = scarier. "You should not have come to my cave."

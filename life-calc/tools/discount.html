How discount math works

Sale price = original price × (1 − discount ÷ 100). So a $80 item at 30% off costs $80 × 0.70 = $56, and you save $24.

Quick mental-math tricks

  • 10% off: move the decimal one place left ($80 → $8 off).
  • 20% off: double the 10% amount.
  • 25% off: divide the price by 4.
  • 50% off: halve the price.
Watch out: "extra 20% off sale price" is not the same as 50% off — discounts stack multiplicatively, not additively.

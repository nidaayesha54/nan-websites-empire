How the age calculator works

Your exact age is the time elapsed between your date of birth and today, broken into years, months, and days. It accounts for leap years automatically — a "year" here means a full trip around the sun from your birthday, not a fixed 365 days.

Fun facts about your age

  • Total days lived is simply the number of calendar days between the two dates.
  • Next birthday countdown tells you how many days until you turn one year older.
  • Born on February 29? In non-leap years we count your birthday as March 1 for the countdown.

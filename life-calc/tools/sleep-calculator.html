How sleep cycles work

Your brain cycles through light sleep, deep sleep, and REM roughly every 90 minutes. Waking at the end of a cycle feels natural; waking mid-cycle — especially during deep sleep — causes grogginess.

How to use this calculator

  • Pick your must-wake time (work, school, alarm).
  • Choose a bedtime from the list — each is a whole number of 90-minute cycles.
  • We add ~15 minutes for the average time it takes to fall asleep.
  • 5–6 full cycles (7.5–9 hours) is the sweet spot for most adults.
Tip: Consistency beats perfection. Going to bed at the same time every night matters more than the exact minute.

Standard tipping guide (USA)

Splitting tips fairly

  • Agree on the tip % before the bill arrives to avoid awkward math.
  • Tip on the pre-tax amount in most US states — it's the common convention.
  • Someone's card covering the bill? Everyone sends their share via cash or payment app.

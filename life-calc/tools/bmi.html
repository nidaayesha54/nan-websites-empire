What is BMI?

Body Mass Index is weight in kilograms divided by height in meters squared. It's a quick screening tool — not a diagnosis. Athletes with lots of muscle, for example, can show a high BMI while being very fit.

Note: This calculator is for general information only and is not medical advice. Talk to a healthcare professional about your health.

GTA 6 Preload Not Working? Fixes That Actually Work

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and for many players the first boss fight was not in Vice City — it was the download bar. GTA 6 is a 212 GB install, and a progress bar sitting at 0% is the most frustrating sight in gaming. The good news: a stuck preload or download is usually fixable in minutes, and the fixes differ on PS5 and Xbox. This guide covers both, plus the locked-game confusion that looks like a download problem but is not one at all.

First, Check the Basics Before You Fix Anything

Before you change settings, rule out the simple causes — most stuck downloads are one of these:

  • Free space: you need 212 GB free. The console needs that much space before the download can finish. If your drive is nearly full, the download can stall at 0% with no clear error — delete games or captures until you have 212 GB free.
  • Check the countdown timer. If you preloaded before launch, your console may show a countdown to the unlock time rather than download progress. A timer counting down to midnight is not a stuck download — it is a game waiting to unlock.
  • Check your system software (PS5). GTA 6 requires PS5 system software 26.07-14.23.00. If your console is not on that version, install the update first — an outdated system can block the download.

If the space is there, the software is current, and the bar is genuinely stuck rather than counting down, move on to your console's fixes below.

PS5 Fixes: Stuck Download or Preload

Work through these in order — most PS5 downloads unstick at step two or three:

1. Install system software 26.07-14.23.00

Go to Settings, then System Software Update, and install any available update. GTA 6 needs version 26.07-14.23.00, and a console behind that version can refuse to start or finish the download. Do this first — every other fix assumes the software is current.

2. Pause and Resume the download

Open Downloads/Uploads, find the GTA 6 download, pause it, wait a few seconds, and resume it. This re-establishes the connection and is the single most effective fix for a bar stuck at 0% — always try it before anything more drastic.

3. Restore Licenses if the game looks locked

If the game shows a padlock or looks unavailable rather than downloading, the license may need re-syncing. Highlight the game, press Options, and choose Restore License. This re-checks your purchase and clears the false "locked" state.

4. Free up 212 GB — and then check again

Even if you thought you had space, verify it: the storage screen shows what is actually free, and 212 GB is a large amount. Free the space, then pause and resume the download again.

Xbox Fixes: Stuck Download or Preload

Xbox downloads get stuck for different reasons:

1. Install from your owned games

If the game is not downloading at all, go to the Store, then Full library, then Owned games, and choose Install — the reliable path when the store page or a queue entry misbehaves.

2. Turn on automatic updates and use Sleep mode

Two settings decide whether your Xbox downloads while you are away. Turn on automatic updates so the console fetches the game without waiting for you, and use Sleep mode rather than shutting it down — a console on Shutdown downloads nothing. If you started a 212 GB download and then fully shut the console off, that is why it is still at 0%.

3. Full power-cycle for a stuck queue

If the download queue itself is stuck — the bar frozen, the queue not advancing — do a full power-cycle: hold the power button for 10 seconds until the console fully shuts down, then power it back on and check the queue. This clears the stuck state a normal restart does not, and the download should resume from where it stopped.

Locked Game or Stuck Download? Know the Difference

The padlock deserves its own section, because it causes more unnecessary re-downloads than any real bug. A locked game is usually not a bug: GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time in your store region. If midnight has not arrived there, the padlock is correct — the game is waiting, not broken.

That distinction matters because the wrong response is expensive. Do not delete the 212 GB install to fix a padlock — you would throw away hours of downloading to solve a problem time alone solves. Instead, check the countdown timer, sign in to the account that bought the game, and on PS5 use Restore License (highlight the game, press Options, choose Restore License). If midnight in your region has passed and the game is still locked, then something is genuinely wrong — but until then, the clock is the answer.

What to Do After the Download Finishes

Once the bar hits 100%, confirm your PS5 is on system software 26.07-14.23.00 — and remember GTA 6's autosave fires only after a mission ends, and the Prologue never autosaves. Learn the manual save (Pause, Game, Save Game) and use it after every mission.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is my GTA 6 download stuck at 0%?

Usually not enough free space (you need 212 GB), outdated PS5 software (26.07-14.23.00), or a stalled connection. On PS5, Pause and Resume in Downloads/Uploads usually restarts it; on Xbox, install from Store → Full library → Owned games.

How big is the GTA 6 download?

The install size is 212 GB on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S — free that much space before downloading.

My game shows a padlock. Is the download broken?

Usually not. The game unlocks at midnight local time in your store region — the padlock means it has not unlocked yet. Do not delete the 212 GB install; sign in to the buying account and, on PS5, use Restore License.

The Bottom Line

A GTA 6 download stuck at 0% is almost always one of a few fixable things: not enough free space for the 212 GB install, PS5 software behind 26.07-14.23.00, or a stalled connection that Pause and Resume clears. On Xbox, install from your owned games, keep automatic updates on, use Sleep mode instead of Shutdown, and power-cycle (hold power 10 seconds) for a frozen queue. And if the game shows a padlock, check the clock first — it unlocks at midnight in your store region, and deleting 212 GB to fix a padlock only makes the wait longer.

GTA 6 Price in India: ₹5,999, Editions and the Real Cost

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched in India on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the price is the first thing Indian players need to know: ₹5,999 for the Standard Edition and ₹7,499 for the Ultimate Edition, the same on both console stores. That is almost exactly double what GTA 5 cost in India at launch in 2013 — about ₹3,000 — and in dollars it works out to roughly $63, against the $79.99 charged in the United States. This guide covers the Indian prices, the cheapest way to actually play, and the traps to avoid when buying.

That matters because the game is only half the bill. The console is the other half, and in India the console costs ten times the game. Knowing the cheapest route in — and the region-lock rule that can waste ₹5,999 in one click — is worth more than knowing the price alone.

The Prices: ₹5,999 and ₹7,499

The prices are the same on both platforms. The PlayStation Store India lists the Standard Edition at ₹5,999 and the Ultimate Edition at ₹7,499, and the Xbox Store India lists exactly the same two prices for Xbox Series X|S. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade — the sixteen extras, sold separately — is ₹1,500, so a Standard buyer can add the Ultimate content later for the same total. The game unlocked at midnight local time in India on November 19, and pre-loading opened on November 10.

The physical version is sold as a code in a box, not a disc — even in stores. This week, the game landed on Blinkit, in a collectible box available in select areas across 11 cities, and there is no disc inside, only a download code, because Rockstar is selling the game purely as a download. The 212 GB download is the same whether you buy digital or boxed, so the box saves you nothing on storage — only the wait for the download.

The Real Cost: The Console Is the Bill

The cheapest way into GTA 6 in India is the Xbox Series S. Microsoft's list price for the Series S starts at about ₹31,990, and retail listings put it at ₹38,000 to ₹40,000 on Amazon India, Flipkart, and Croma — so with the ₹5,999 game, the cheapest route in comes to roughly ₹38,000 to ₹54,500, depending on the retailer and whether you find one near list price. The PlayStation 5 is the pricier alternative: Sony's own storefront lists the PS5 Slim Digital Edition at ₹64,990, and the Disc Edition at ₹69,990, so with the game the PlayStation route comes to roughly ₹71,000 to ₹77,500. The PS5 Pro is the most expensive option of all, and it was out of stock on major listings in October.

One detail saves PlayStation buyers money: the extra ₹4,995 for the PS5 with a disc drive buys you nothing for GTA 6 at launch, because the boxed game holds a download code, not a disc. If GTA 6 is the reason you are buying the console, the Digital Edition is the right one — and the Series S is the cheapest way in of all.

How to Buy Safely in India

Buy from the store for your console account — the PlayStation Store India or the Xbox Store India — and check region compatibility before buying a boxed code, because an imported PlayStation code may not redeem on an Indian account. That is the one mistake that wastes the whole ₹5,999: a code from another region, redeemed on the wrong account, will not work, and the refund is not guaranteed. The two stores also charge differently: PlayStation charges immediately at pre-order, while Microsoft may wait until about ten days before launch to charge a card.

There is no PC version to wait for, and no PC date announced — a shift for a series so many Indian players first met on a computer, though GTA 5 took the same route and reached PC in 2015. The game is on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only for now. Anyone selling a "GTA 6 PC download" or an APK in India is running the same scam as everywhere else: there is no PC build, and fake downloads have been traced to password-stealing malware.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does GTA 6 cost in India?

₹5,999 for the Standard Edition and ₹7,499 for the Ultimate Edition, on both the PlayStation Store India and the Xbox Store India. The Ultimate Edition Upgrade is ₹1,500 on top of Standard.

What is the cheapest way to play GTA 6 in India?

An Xbox Series S plus the game — roughly ₹38,000 to ₹54,500 in total, depending on the retailer. The PlayStation 5 route costs roughly ₹71,000 to ₹77,500 with the game.

Does the boxed GTA 6 in India contain a disc?

No. The box holds a download code, not a disc — including the collectible box sold on Blinkit. You download the full 212 GB either way.

The Bottom Line

In India, GTA 6 is a ₹5,999 game with a ₹7,499 Ultimate Edition, the same on both consoles, and the console is the real bill: a Series S and the game for roughly ₹38,000, or a PS5 and the game for roughly ₹71,000. Buy from the Indian store for your console, check the region before buying a boxed code, and skip the disc drive — in 2026, GTA 6 is a download, and the box is just a box.

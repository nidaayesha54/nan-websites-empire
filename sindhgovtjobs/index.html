How to Apply

1
Read the SPSC consolidated advertisement and note the post, quota and closing date.
2
Register on the SPSC online portal and complete the application form.
3
Pay the application fee challan at the designated bank before the last date.
4
Download the admission slip and appear for the screening/written test.
5
Shortlisted candidates face the interview; final results appear on the SPSC website.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for SPSC jobs?

Register on the SPSC portal, fill the online form for your post, deposit the fee challan, and submit before closing.

What is the age limit for Sindh government jobs?

Usually 21 to 28 years plus applicable relaxation as announced in each advertisement.

Is Sindh domicile required?

Yes, a valid Sindh domicile (urban/rural as per quota) is required for most provincial posts.

How do I download the admission slip?

Admission slips are available on the SPSC website a week before the test date.

Are these listings official advertisements?

Listings here are illustrative samples. Always verify on the official SPSC portal.

What posts does SPSC advertise?

Lecturers, assistants, sub engineers, medical officers, and many other provincial posts.

Sindh Government 571 Jobs 2026: Criminal Prosecution Department Walk-In Interviews 19–31 Oct — BPS-01 to BPS-04

The Criminal Prosecution Services Department, Government of Sindh, has announced 571 jobs in the Office of the Prosecutor General Sindh (Karachi) and the District Public Prosecutor offices across the province. There is no online form and no written test for these posts: selection is through walk-in interviews scheduled from 19 to 31 October 2026. The recruitment covers BPS-01 to BPS-04 support posts — Naib Qasid, Daftari, Driver, Dispatch Rider, Chowkidar and Sanitary Worker — and requires Sindh domicile. The advertisement reference is INF-KRY 4363-26, published in early October 2026.

For candidates with primary-level education or basic literacy, this is one of the largest direct walk-in recruitments in Sindh this year — and because interviews run district by district, candidates interview in their own district.

Sindh Prosecution Services 571 Jobs — Posts & Seats (Head Office)

The Karachi head office carries 232 posts; the remaining 339 posts are spread across the District Public Prosecutor offices in Sindh.

District posts: the 339 district-office posts are distributed across South, East, West, Central, Malir, Hyderabad, Sukkur, Larkana, Mirpurkhas, Shaheed Benazirabad and other districts. For a district post, your domicile must be from that district.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Age: 18 to 30 years, with age relaxation as allowed by Sindh government rules.
  • Domicile: Sindh domicile and PRC for head office posts; the relevant district's domicile for district posts. Quota: rural 60%, urban 40%.
  • Qualification: literate to primary pass, depending on the post; Driver needs a car/LTV licence with 2 years' experience; Dispatch Rider needs a motorcycle licence.
  • Gender: male and female candidates with Sindh domicile can appear as per the advertisement.

No TA/DA is admissible for appearing in the walk-in interviews.

How the Walk-In Interview Works

There is no application form to submit in advance. Follow these steps:

1
Prepare your documents: a written job application, attested copies of your educational certificates, CNIC, domicile and PRC, driving licence (for Driver/Dispatch Rider posts) and passport-size photographs.
2
Carry originals: bring the original documents along with the attested copies — the interview panel will verify them on the spot.
3
Check your district schedule: appear at the Karachi head office (4th Floor, Administration Block, High Court of Sindh) or your district's Public Prosecutor office on the date assigned to your district/post, between 19 and 31 October 2026.
4
Arrive early: walk-in interviews work on a first-come basis — reach well before the start of the session with all documents in order.
Important: For head-office posts you need a Sindh domicile and PRC; for district posts, the domicile of that district. Candidates must appear on their assigned date — confirm it against the official schedule in the advertisement.

Tips for the Walk-In Interview

Walk-in interviews for support posts are decided in minutes, so presentation matters: dress neatly, keep your documents in a clean folder in the right order (application, CNIC, domicile/PRC, education, licence, photos), and answer the panel's questions about your post directly — a Driver should be able to talk about log-book maintenance; a Dispatch Rider about road safety and licence validity. The panel is checking reliability and basic competence, not academic depth. Because 571 posts are spread across every district, the competition in your district is far smaller than it looks on paper — showing up prepared is already half the battle.

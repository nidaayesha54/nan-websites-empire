Sindh Government Jobs October 2026 — Police, Teaching & Health Application Guide

Police, teaching and health recruitment in Sindh explained: who hires, how each stream works, the documents you need and how to spot fake advertisements.

✅ Updated October 13, 2026
Home » Sindh Government Jobs » Articles

Sindh's government departments recruit thousands of candidates every year across police, teaching, health and administrative services. While the SPSC handles officer-level recruitment, departments like Sindh Police, the School Education Department and the Health Department run their own recruitment for many posts. This October 2026 guide explains how each stream works, what eligibility looks like, and how to apply through official channels — without falling for fake advertisements.

Sindh Police Jobs: How Recruitment Works

Police recruitment in Sindh generally follows a fixed sequence: advertisement with district-wise vacancies, physical measurement and running tests (height, chest and endurance standards are fixed in the advertisement), a written test, interviews and a final medical examination. Physical standards are non-negotiable, so measure yourself against the advertisement's requirements before applying. Domicile of the relevant district or division is typically required for district-quota posts.

Teaching Jobs in Sindh

Teacher recruitment in Sindh has run through large advertised drives as well as SPSC for lecturer-level posts. Candidates generally need the prescribed teaching qualification (such as B.Ed/M.Ed or the subject degree specified in the advertisement), Sindh domicile, and must clear the written test and interview stages. Because policies and testing bodies for teacher recruitment change between drives, always read the current advertisement rather than relying on a previous year's process.

Health Department Jobs

Health department recruitment covers a wide range: medical officers and specialists (usually through SPSC), Lady Health Visitors, technicians, dispensers and support staff (often through departmental processes). Professional posts require the relevant degree plus valid registration with the medical or nursing council. Keep your registration current — expired registration is a common reason for rejection at scrutiny.

Documents Checklist for Sindh Government Applications

  • CNIC and Sindh domicile with PRC (where required)
  • Recent passport-size photographs
  • Educational certificates, degrees and transcripts
  • Professional registration certificates (medical, nursing, engineering) where applicable
  • Experience letters for posts requiring experience
  • Character certificates and other documents specified in the advertisement

How to Apply — and How to Avoid Scams

1
Verify the advertisement. Check that the ad appears on the official portal (SPSC or the department's website) — not just on social media.
2
Read eligibility fully. Confirm qualification, age, domicile and physical standards against your own profile.
3
Apply through the stated channel only — online portal or the submission address given in the ad — before the closing date.
4
Never pay anyone unofficially. Genuine government recruitment never asks for money outside the official fee challan, and no agent can secure you a post.
5
Track your application on the official portal for test dates, roll numbers and results.
Verify before you apply: Recruitment rules, testing bodies and schedules change between announcements. Always confirm the current advertisement on the official SPSC or departmental portal before acting on any summary.

Staying Updated Through October 2026

New Sindh announcements can appear any week, so build a simple tracking habit: check the official SPSC portal every few days for fresh consolidated advertisements, scan the appointments/jobs pages of major newspapers, and follow this portal's latest-articles section for plain-language summaries. When a new advertisement drops, act fast — note the closing date immediately, gather documents the same day, and submit your application at least 2–3 days before the deadline. Most candidates who miss out do so not for lack of merit but for lack of timely information.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who recruits for Sindh government jobs?

SPSC handles officer-level posts (BS-16 and above), while Sindh Police, the School Education Department and the Health Department run recruitment for many of their own posts.

What is required for Sindh Police constable recruitment?

Advertisements fix physical standards (height, chest, running), a written test, interview and medical. District domicile is typically required for district-quota posts.

How are teachers recruited in Sindh?

Through advertised recruitment drives and SPSC for lecturer-level posts, generally requiring the prescribed teaching qualification plus written test and interview.

What documents do I need for Sindh government job applications?

CNIC, Sindh domicile with PRC where required, educational certificates, professional registration where applicable, photographs and experience letters.

How can I avoid fake Sindh government job ads?

Verify every advertisement on the official SPSC or departmental portal, apply only through the stated channel, and never pay anyone outside the official fee challan.

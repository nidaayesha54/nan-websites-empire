SPSC Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026, dated 25 September 2026, carries 54 posts in BPS-16 to BPS-18 across Sindh government departments — and the window is closing fast. Online applications close on 23 October 2026 at 5:00 pm, which means there are only 3 days left from the date of this update. The SPSC portal stops accepting submissions the moment the clock hits five.

With 54 posts covering medical officers, information officers, assistants, IT roles and planning posts, this is one of the bigger consolidated advertisements of the season. But a big advertisement also means heavy last-day traffic — and SPSC applications are online only at spsc.gov.pk, with manual applications rejected outright. This checklist covers exactly what to verify before you hit submit, and the common errors that cost applicants their chance every single advertisement cycle.

Last-Minute Checklist — Before You Submit

1
Register / log in on spsc.gov.pk early. Do not wait for the final evening — portal traffic on closing day is heavy.
2
Select the correct post and department — match the post title to the advertisement's serial number. Applying to the wrong post is not fixable after closing.
3
Verify CNIC and date of birth character by character. These fields are frozen after the closing date — a typo here can invalidate your application.
4
Confirm domicile/PRC, age and qualification against the post's requirements. SPSC re-verifies all of these against your documents later.
5
Choose your exam centre carefully — it is frozen after closing and cannot be changed.
6
Generate the PSID and pay Rs 500 via 1Bill. Confirm the payment reflects on your application before 5:00 pm on 23 October.
7
Submit the online form before 5:00 pm and save/print the confirmation. A half-filled form in drafts does not count.
8
Government employees: apply through the proper channel as required — have your department's permission route sorted before the deadline.

Common Rejection Reasons — Don't Make These Mistakes

  • Wrong CNIC or date of birth — the most common self-inflicted rejection; double-check before submitting.
  • Incomplete application — missing fields, unuploaded photo/signature, or unpaid fee.
  • Applying by post or by hand — manual applications are rejected; SPSC accepts online submissions only.
  • Domicile/PRC, age, qualification or exam centre frozen after closing — none of these can be corrected later, so enter them right the first time.
  • Government employees skipping the proper channel — applying without your department's route risks disqualification at verification.
  • Post count changes — the number of posts may change without notice.
3-day warning: every consolidated SPSC advertisement sees its heaviest traffic in the final 24 hours. Fee payments via 1Bill can also take time to reconcile. Apply and pay today or tomorrow — do not plan to submit in the final hour.

Why This Advertisement Matters

The 36 medical posts (32 Medical Officer + 4 Lady Medical Officer) in the Home Department make this advertisement especially attractive to MBBS graduates, while the 10 Assistant (BPS-16) posts in the Law department are the classic graduate-level entry route. The IT posts in the Anti-Corruption Establishment — Deputy Director IT / Senior Manager IT at BPS-18 and Assistant Director IT / System Analyst at BPS-17 — are rare senior technical seats that draw experienced professionals. If you qualify, the Rs 500 fee and a careful online submission are a small price for a shot at a permanent provincial post. Confirm every detail on spsc.gov.pk and apply before 23 October 2026, 5:00 pm.

What is the closing date and time for SPSC Ad 05/2026?

Online applications close on 23 October 2026 at 5:00 pm — the SPSC portal stops accepting applications after that time.

What is the application fee for SPSC Ad 05/2026?

Rs 500 per post, paid via PSID on the 1Bill network (1-Link, bank or mobile banking).

Yes — government employees must apply through the proper channel as required by the advertisement.

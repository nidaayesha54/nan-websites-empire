SPSC Lady Medical Officer Jobs 2026: 4 Posts (Home Department) — Ad 05/2026, Last Date 23 Oct 2026

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC), Hyderabad, has announced 4 posts of Lady Medical Officer (BPS-17) in the Home Department under Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026, dated 25 September 2026. These medical-officer seats serve the district and prison medical services under the Home Department. The last date to apply is 23 October 2026 — online only at spsc.gov.pk, with the form closing at 5:00 pm on the closing day.

These seats are among the specialist medical openings in the 54-post consolidated advertisement. Candidates must hold the prescribed medical qualification — MBBS with valid PMC/PMDC registration is the standard requirement for Medical Officer posts — and a Sindh domicile with PRC. The number of posts may change without notice, so applying early is the safer strategy.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualification: the prescribed medical qualification stated in the official advertisement — MBBS with valid PMC/PMDC registration is the standard for Medical Officer posts; confirm the exact requirement on spsc.gov.pk before applying.
  • Domicile: Sindh domicile with PRC; rural/urban quota applies per the advertisement.
  • Age: as stated in the official advertisement for this case.
  • Fee: Rs. 500 per post, paid through a PSID / 1Bill invoice on any online channel. Manual applications are not accepted.

All eligibility conditions must be complete by the closing date — SPSC allows no changes after submission.

How to Apply Online on spsc.gov.pk

Follow these steps to submit your application:

1
Open www.spsc.gov.pk (or the official SPSC mobile app) and register or log in to the candidate portal.
2
Go to the “Available Jobs” feature and select Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026.
3
Choose Lady Medical Officer (BPS-17) in the Home Department and fill in your details.
4
Generate the PSID and pay the Rs. 500 application fee through the 1Bill invoice option.
5
Submit before 23 October 2026 (5:00 pm) and keep a copy — no hard copy needs to be sent.
Important: The form closes at 5:00 pm on the closing day, and the number of posts may change without notice. SPSC helplines: (022) 9200694, 9200246, 9200162.

What a Lady Medical Officer in the Home Department Does

Lady Medical Officers in the Home Department provide healthcare in the department's institutions — district facilities and the prison medical service: outpatient care, referrals, medical examinations and record-keeping. BPS-17 gazetted-officer status and structured provincial service benefits make these a strong first posting for women doctors entering public service in Sindh.

SPSC Result 2026: Check Merit List & Interview List Online — Step-by-Step Guide

Written-test results, merit lists and interview lists — how to check your SPSC result 2026 online by roll number, and what to do after you qualify.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Sindh Government Jobs » Articles

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC) has been releasing new written-test and interview results through October 2026, and thousands of candidates are checking their qualification status online. SPSC declares results through official press releases from its headquarters on Thandi Sarak, Hyderabad, and publishes merit lists, interview lists and final results on its website.

This guide explains how to check your SPSC result 2026 by roll number, what the recent Procurement Specialist (BPS-19) result means for qualifiers, and what documents you must submit after passing the written stage.

Latest SPSC Result: Procurement Specialist (BPS-19)

SPSC officially declared the pre-interview written-test result for the high-profile post of Procurement Specialist (BPS-19) in the Sindh Public Procurement Regulatory Authority (SPPRA) on 15 September 2026, under Press Release No. PSC/EXAMS.S/2026/348. The written evaluation was held out of 100 marks, and the datum marks worked out from the marks statement set the Open Merit (rural) cutoff at 58 marks. Candidates who cleared the cutoff have advanced to the interview phase.

After qualifying: Successful candidates must submit complete dossiers — attested copies of academic credentials, master's degrees from HEC-recognized universities and permanent residence certificates — to the office of the Assistant Director Recruitment-III within seven days of the result. Missing this deadline can forfeit your candidacy.

How to Check Your SPSC Result 2026 Online

1
Visit the official SPSC website www.spsc.gov.pk.
2
Open the Results section (written-test results, interview lists and merit lists are posted separately).
3
Find your post — results are listed by advertisement number and post title.
4
Open the result PDF or list and search for your roll number (and name, where published).
5
Note your marks, merit position and the interview schedule or document-submission deadline mentioned in the notification.

Understanding SPSC Merit Lists

  • Written-test result: Shows marks obtained and who qualifies for the interview round.
  • Interview list: Names or roll numbers of candidates called for the interview, with the schedule.
  • Final merit list: Prepared on the basis of written-test and interview marks combined — this determines the final selection.
  • Opening / closing merit: The highest and lowest merit positions in each quota category (open merit, women, minority, differently-abled).
  • Eligibility list: Published before the written test to confirm whose applications were accepted.

What to Do After You Qualify the Written Test

Qualifying the written test is only the first step. SPSC typically requires qualified candidates to submit a complete dossier of attested documents within a short deadline — in the recent Procurement Specialist result, the window was just seven days. Keep attested copies of your degrees, transcripts, domicile/PRC, CNIC and experience letters ready in advance so you never miss a document-submission window. Then prepare for the interview round, which carries significant weight in the final merit.

SPSC Results Announced in October 2026

SPSC has posted new written and interview results during October 2026. If you appeared in a test earlier this year — including lecturer, medical officer, engineering and administrative posts — check the results section of www.spsc.gov.pk now. Results are announced post by post, so a result you are waiting for may already be live.

Tip: Always verify results on the official SPSC website. Unofficial result pages may carry outdated or incomplete lists — the press release number and date on the official notification are your proof of authenticity.

Frequently Asked Questions

How can I check my SPSC result 2026 online?

Visit www.spsc.gov.pk, open the Results section, find your post by advertisement number, and search the result list for your roll number.

What was the cutoff for the SPSC Procurement Specialist result 2026?

The written-test result declared on 15 September 2026 set the Open Merit (rural) cutoff at 58 marks out of 100.

What documents are needed after qualifying the SPSC written test?

Qualified candidates must submit attested copies of academic credentials, master's degrees from HEC-recognized universities and permanent residence certificates within the deadline given in the notification.

How long do I have to submit documents after the result?

The deadline is set in each result notification — for the recent Procurement Specialist result it was seven days from the result announcement.

Where are new SPSC results published?

SPSC publishes results through press releases from its Hyderabad headquarters and on the official website www.spsc.gov.pk.

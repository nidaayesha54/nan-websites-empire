SourceDaily Dawn, 22 October 2026

The Sindh Government Lyari General Hospital, Karachi (Office of the Chairperson, Management Board) has published a notice in Daily Dawn of 22 October 2026 for 23 Class-IV and support staff titles (BPS-01 to BPS-04) from South Karachi domicile holders. These are regular, sanctioned and budgeted posts — with statutory allowances, medical coverage and pension/gratuity.

Only domicile and PRC holders of District South, Karachi can apply. The age limit is generally 18–35 years (Driver: 21–35; Sanitary Worker: 18–30), with a 5-year general age relaxation (notification dated 06.03.2025). Quotas for women, minorities, persons with disabilities and transgender persons are observed. Applications must reach by courier by 4 November 2026 — hand, email or late applications will not be entertained.

Honesty flag: The ad lists 23 titles but no total seat count — only Naib Qasid (1), Sanitary Worker (1) and Ward Boy (1) show seat counts. Confirm exact seats in the Daily Dawn notice.

How to Apply — Courier Only

There is no online application form for these posts. Follow these steps exactly:

1
Prepare your application on plain paper with attested copies of educational certificates, CNIC, domicile & PRC (Form-D) of District South Karachi, 2 photos, experience certificates where applicable, and an NOC if you are a government servant.
2
Label the envelope with the post name on the top-right corner, plus your address, mobile number, PTCL number and email.
3
Send by courier ONLY to: Office of the Chairperson, Management Board, SMBBMCL/SGLGHK, Tannery Road, Lyari, Karachi South.
4
Ensure it reaches by 4 November 2026. Hand, email or late applications will not be entertained.
5
Keep originals ready — shortlisted candidates bring them to the interview (Office of the Deputy Medical Superintendent, Admin). No TA/DA is paid.
Apply by courier only — hand/email applications are rejected; any recommendation (sifarish) gets an application rejected.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for Lyari General Hospital jobs 2026?

4 November 2026 — applications must reach the Chairperson's Office by courier within 15 days of the Daily Dawn notice (22 Oct 2026).

Which posts are open at Lyari General Hospital?

23 Class-IV and support staff titles (BPS-01 to BPS-04): Driver, Electrician, Lift Operator, Nursing Orderly, Pump Driver, Dark Room Assistant/Attendant, Lab Attendant, OT/X-Ray Attendant, Aya/Dai, Dental Attendant, Dhobi, Ward Servant, Chowkidar, Cook, Daie, Mali, Naib Qasid, Sanitary Worker and Ward Boy.

Who can apply for these hospital posts?

Only domicile and PRC holders of District South, Karachi.

How do I apply — is there an online form?

No online form. Send your application with 2 passport photos and attested copies ONLY by courier to the Chairperson's Office, SMBBMCL/SGLGHK, Tannery Road, Lyari, Karachi South. Hand/email applications are not entertained.

Is there any age relaxation for these posts?

Yes — 5 years' general relaxation in the upper limit, per the Sindh Government notification dated 06.03.2025.

Are these regular government posts?

Yes — regular, sanctioned and budgeted posts (BPS-01 to BPS-04) with civil service allowances, medical coverage and pension/gratuity.

The School Education & Literacy Department (SELD), Government of Sindh has announced 750 Arts Teacher (BPS-14) posts across all districts of the province, per the advertisement released in October 2026. This is one of the largest teacher recruitments of the year for Fine Arts graduates, and posts are distributed district-wise.

Unlike many provincial teaching announcements, this one routes applicants through their District Education Officer (ES&HS) office with a prescribed application form, and it makes the Sukkur IBA Graduation Category screening test result a hard eligibility condition. The deadline is 15 November 2026 — district offices typically reject late or incomplete applications outright.

Eligibility gate: two conditions are non-negotiable — a Fine Arts graduation (2nd division or better) and 40% in the Sukkur IBA Graduation Category screening test. If you have not taken or cleared the IBA screening, this advertisement is not for you.

Eligibility Criteria — Check Before You Apply

  • Education: Graduation in Fine Arts with at least 2nd Division from an HEC-recognized university. Degrees from unrecognized institutions will not be accepted.
  • IBA screening: at least 40% marks in the Sukkur IBA Graduation Category screening test — attach your result card with the application.
  • Age: 21 to 28 years on the closing date; the government's standard age relaxation applies as per rules.
  • Domicile: valid domicile and PRC (Form-D) of the concerned district — you apply against the district where your domicile is registered, not where you currently live.
  • CNIC & documents: valid CNIC and attested copies of all academic documents.

Quota Policy

  • 15% women quota — reserved for female candidates.
  • 5% minorities quota — reserved for minority candidates.
  • 5% differently-abled persons quota — reserved for persons with disabilities, as per the official policy.
  • Quota claims require the relevant certificates — attach them.

How to Apply — Step by Step

1
Get the prescribed application format — download it from the official advertisement notice or collect it from the District Education Officer (ES&HS) office of your district.
2
Prepare your documents: attested copy of CNIC, 2 recent photographs, domicile, PRC (Form-D), Fine Arts degree(s), and the Sukkur IBA screening result card.
3
Fill the form carefully — your district selection must match your domicile/PRC.
4
Submit to the District Education Officer (ES&HS) office of your district — in person or as the advertisement instructs.
5
Apply by 15 November 2026. Keep the submission receipt and a full set of copies — you will need them at the interview stage.
Important: this is a district-office submission process, not an online portal application. Verify the exact office address and timings from the official advertisement, and never hand your documents or any fee to unauthorized intermediaries.

Why These Posts Matter

For Fine Arts graduates, permanent government teaching posts at BPS-14 are rare — most provincial teaching drives focus on primary or science streams, so a 750-post arts-teacher recruitment is an unusually large window. The catch is the IBA screening gate: only candidates who already cleared the Sukkur IBA Graduation Category test with 40%+ can compete. If you hold the result card, file a complete application early — late and incomplete submissions are the two most avoidable ways to lose out.

The District Education Officer (Primary) Kemari, Karachi, has announced 5 Junior Clerk (BPS-11) posts through an advertisement in the Daily Jang on 17 October 2026 (INF/KRY NO: 4567/26). Junior Clerk is the classic clerical entry point in Sindh's education department — a permanent BPS-11 seat with a clear promotion path.

Note: the ad does not print a fixed closing date. It asks candidates to apply within 15 days of publication — roughly 1–2 November 2026 from 17 October. Treat this as an estimate only: confirm the exact date on the Sindh Job Portal at sjp.gos.pk. Applications are online only.

Eligibility Criteria

Candidates must meet all of the following requirements:

  • Education: graduation with at least 2nd division from an HEC-recognized university.
  • Computer certificate: MS Office certificate from an SBTE or Trade Testing Board-recognized institute.
  • STS certificate: required by the ad — confirm exactly which test result this refers to in the original advertisement.
  • Domicile: Sindh domicile/PRC is mandatory.
  • Government employees: must obtain an NOC from their department and bring the original NOC to the interview.
Honesty flag: the age limit was not stated in the report reviewed. Check the original advertisement or the Sindh Job Portal for the exact age criteria.

How to Apply Online

Applications are accepted online only through the Sindh Job Portal. Follow these steps:

1
Open sjp.gos.pk (Sindh Job Portal) and log in or create an account.
2
Find the DEO (Primary) Kemari ad — reference INF/KRY NO: 4567/26 (Junior Clerk, BPS-11).
3
Fill the online form — match your CNIC, name, domicile and qualification details exactly with your documents.
4
Upload the required documents — degree, MS Office certificate, STS certificate, Sindh domicile/PRC and CNIC, as instructed on the portal.

6
Watch the portal for the interview schedule — shortlisted candidates are informed through the schedule uploaded there.

Selection Process

Candidates are called before the Departmental Selection Committee per the schedule uploaded on the Sindh Job Portal. Bring all original documents on the interview day — degrees, certificates, domicile/PRC, CNIC and, for government employees, the original NOC. No TA/DA is admissible.

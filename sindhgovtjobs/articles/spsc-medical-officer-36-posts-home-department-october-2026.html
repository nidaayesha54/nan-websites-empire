SPSC Medical Officer Jobs 2026: 36 Posts in Sindh Home Department — MBBS, Last Date 23 Oct 2026

The Sindh Public Service Commission has opened 36 Medical Officer and Lady Medical Officer (BPS-17) posts in the Home Department under Consolidated Advertisement 05/2026 — MBBS doctors can apply online until 23 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Sindh Government Jobs » Articles

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC), Hyderabad, has invited online applications for Medical Officer (Male) and Lady Medical Officer posts in the Home Department, Government of Sindh. These posts appear in Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026, dated 25 September 2026, and together they cover 36 vacancies in BPS-17. The last date to apply is 23 October 2026, and SPSC accepts online applications only — manual or paper applications will not be entertained.

These are among the largest single-department allocations in the consolidated advertisement, making them the headline opportunity for MBBS graduates in this round of SPSC recruitment.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualification: MBBS from a recognised institution, with registration with the medical authority and a completed one-year house job.
  • Age: 21 to 28 years.
  • Domicile: Sindh domicile and PRC required; rural/urban quota applies per the advertisement.
  • Fee: Rs. 500 per post, paid by generating a PSID and paying through the 1Bill invoice/service option.

Candidates serving in government or semi-government organisations must apply through the proper channel. Incorrect CNIC numbers, wrong dates of birth and incomplete applications lead to rejection, so fill the form with extreme care.

How to Apply Online on spsc.gov.pk

SPSC does not accept paper applications for this advertisement. Follow these steps:

1
Open the SPSC website at www.spsc.gov.pk (you can also use the official SPSC mobile app) and register or log in to the candidate portal.
2
Open the “Available Jobs” feature and select Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026.
3
Choose Medical Officer (Male) or Lady Medical Officer and fill in your personal, education and experience details.
4
Generate the PSID and pay the Rs. 500 application fee through the 1Bill invoice option.
5
Submit the form before 23 October 2026 at 5:00 pm and keep a copy of the submitted application. No hard copy needs to be sent.
Important: On the closing date the online form closes at 5:00 pm. Domicile, PRC, quota, age, qualification, experience and exam centre cannot be changed after the closing date. The number of posts may also change without notice, per the advertisement.

Selection Process

After the closing date, SPSC will announce the test schedule for these posts. Candidates should expect a written test followed by interviews for shortlisted candidates, with final selection on merit under the Commission's regulations. Test dates have not been announced yet — keep checking the SPSC website and this portal for the roll number slips and schedule.

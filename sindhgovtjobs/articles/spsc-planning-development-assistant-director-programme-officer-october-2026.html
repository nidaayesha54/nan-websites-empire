SPSC Planning & Development Jobs 2026: Assistant Director & Programme Officer (BPS-17) — Ad 05/2026, Last Date 23 Oct

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC), Hyderabad, has announced two BPS-17 posts in the Planning & Development Department, Government of Sindh, under Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026, dated 25 September 2026: 1 post of Assistant Director and 1 post of Programme Officer. The last date to apply is 23 October 2026, with the online form closing at 5:00 pm — applications are accepted online only at spsc.gov.pk.

The Planning & Development Department is the province's central planning body — the office that shapes development schemes, coordinates sector plans and monitors project implementation across Sindh. BPS-17 officer posts here are desk roles with real influence: drafting scheme documents, reviewing project proposals and tracking development spending. Both posts are on open merit, and male and female candidates are eligible. The qualification, experience, age and quota for each post are stated in the official advertisement — read the exact criteria on spsc.gov.pk and do not apply on assumed criteria.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualification, experience and age: exactly as stated for each post in the official advertisement on spsc.gov.pk — check the Assistant Director and Programme Officer cases separately.
  • Merit: open merit; both male and female candidates are eligible.
  • Government servants: apply through the proper channel.
  • Fee: Rs. 500 per post via a PSID / 1Bill invoice on any online channel; manual applications are not accepted.

Each post has its own criteria — read the two case details separately before selecting your post.

How to Apply Online on spsc.gov.pk

Follow these steps to submit your application:

1
Open www.spsc.gov.pk (or the official SPSC mobile app) and register or log in to the candidate portal.
2
Go to the “Available Jobs” feature and select Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026.
3
Choose Assistant Director or Programme Officer (BPS-17) and fill in your details.
4
Generate the PSID and pay the Rs. 500 application fee through the 1Bill invoice option.
5
Submit before 23 October 2026 (5:00 pm) and keep a copy — no hard copy needs to be sent.
Important: The form closes at 5:00 pm on the closing day; the number of posts may change without notice. Manual applications will not be entertained.

What These Posts Involve

The Planning & Development Department is where Sindh's development schemes are conceived, appraised and tracked. An Assistant Director works on scheme preparation, PC-I/PC-II documentation and coordination with executing departments; a Programme Officer handles programme-level monitoring and progress reporting. Both build planning and project-management skills valued across government and the development sector — a strong foundation in a BPS-17 gazetted posting.

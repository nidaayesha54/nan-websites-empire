SSGC Jobs October 2026: 5 Corporate Communication Posts — Manager & Deputy Managers, Last Date 26 Oct

Sui Southern Gas Company Limited (SSGC) has announced career opportunities for five specialized roles in its Corporate Communication Department in Karachi. The posts are Manager–Branding & CSR and four Deputy Manager positions covering conventional media & media complaints, social media engagement, analytics & performance management, and communication collaterals. Applicants need the specified MBA or MS qualifications from an HEC-recognized university, and the last date to apply is 26 October 2026 — within 15 days of the notice's publication on 11 October.

SSGC is one of Pakistan's largest public-sector utilities, serving Sindh and Balochistan. Its Corporate Communication Department handles brand management, media relations, CSR programmes and internal communications — so these are professional corporate roles inside a government-owned company, carrying market-competitive compensation packages rather than BPS scales.

Location: all five posts are based in Karachi. SSGC is an equal opportunity employer and encourages women to apply.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manager–Branding & CSR: brand management, CSR programme management, brand audits, stakeholder and NGO relations, reporting.
  • Deputy Manager–Conventional Media & Media Complaints: press releases, print and electronic media relations, release orders, complaint handling, media monitoring.
  • Deputy Manager–Social Media Engagement: social media strategy, engagement, content and digital campaigns.
  • Deputy Manager–Analytics & Performance Management: data analysis, KPI tracking, data modelling, dashboards, data visualization, reporting.
  • Deputy Manager–Communication Collaterals: collateral design and production, vendor coordination, printing, publications, multilingual communication — including translations into Urdu, English, Sindhi, Balochi, Pashto and Brahvi where needed.

Eligibility & Important Instructions

  • All qualifications must come from an HEC-recognized university.
  • The careers page warns that any inconsistency between the portal information and your resume leads to disqualification — keep both consistent.
  • SSGC shortlists and selects on merit alone.
  • Internal candidates get continuity of service under company policy.
  • The posts carry a market-competitive compensation package — no fixed salary figures are published.

How to Apply Online

1
Open the SSGC careers page at ssgc.com.pk/careers and go to Current Openings.
2
Find your post by its job code (8589-01 to 8593-01) and read the full job description, qualification and experience requirements.
3
Complete the online application form — personal, educational and professional details — and upload your CV, certificates (including your MBA/MS), experience certificates, CNIC and a recent photograph.
4
Submit before 26 October 2026. Keep a copy or screenshot of your submitted application.
Selection: shortlisting on merit, written tests where applicable, and interviews. Late or incomplete applications are not entertained.

Why These SSGC Posts Matter

The Manager post leads the branding and CSR agenda while each Deputy Manager owns a distinct lane - media, social, analytics or collaterals - and shortlisted candidates face interviews after merit screening. Corporate roles inside a utility like SSGC are a distinct career lane: the work is corporate-grade — brand campaigns, media buying, analytics dashboards, stakeholder programmes — while the employer is a public-sector company with structured employment terms, annual increments, medical facilities, provident fund and pension plans. For media and marketing professionals in Karachi, these five seats are a rare chance to do corporate communication at utility scale. Apply through the SSGC careers portal before 26 October, and make sure your portal entries match your resume exactly — the company disqualifies on inconsistency.

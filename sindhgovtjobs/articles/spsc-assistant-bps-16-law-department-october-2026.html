SPSC Assistant Jobs 2026: 10 BPS-16 Posts in Law Department — Apply by 23 Oct 2026

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC), Hyderabad, has announced 10 posts of Assistant (BPS-16) in the Law, Parliamentary Affairs & Criminal Prosecution Department, Government of Sindh. These posts appear in Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026, dated 25 September 2026. The last date to apply is 23 October 2026, and applications are accepted online only.

Assistant (BPS-16) posts are the classic graduate-level clerical-officer role in the provincial secretariat system — office management, noting and drafting, and file work in a law department. For fresh graduates with a Sindh domicile, this is one of the most accessible BPS-16 openings in the current SPSC round.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualification: Graduation with at least second division from an HEC-recognised university.
  • Age: 21 to 31 years.
  • Domicile: Sindh domicile and PRC required; rural/urban quota applies per the advertisement.
  • Experience: No separately specified experience bar — the graduate qualification is the key criterion.

The qualification, domicile and age must be complete by the closing date. Domicile, PRC, quota, age, qualification, experience and exam centre cannot be changed after the closing date, so verify every entry before submitting.

How to Apply Online on spsc.gov.pk

Follow these steps to submit your application:

1
Open www.spsc.gov.pk (or the official SPSC mobile app) and register or log in to the candidate portal.
2
Go to the “Available Jobs” feature and select Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026.
3
Choose Assistant (BPS-16) and fill in your personal, education and experience details.
4
Generate the PSID and pay the Rs. 500 application fee through the 1Bill invoice option.
5
Submit the form before 23 October 2026 (5:00 pm on the closing day) and keep a copy of the submitted application. No hard copy needs to be sent.
Important: On the closing date the online form closes at 5:00 pm. The number of posts may change without notice, per the advertisement. Manual applications will not be entertained.

What an Assistant in the Law Department Does

Assistants in a provincial law department handle the office backbone of legal administration: maintaining files and case references, preparing notes and drafts, processing correspondence with the courts and other departments, and supporting senior officers in parliamentary and prosecution matters. The work builds strong administrative, drafting and record-management skills that transfer across the provincial bureaucracy — a solid foundation for a long-term government career.

Assistants are also the officers who keep a department moving: files flow through their desks, deadlines are tracked by their diaries, and senior officers depend on their drafting and coordination. Candidates who are organised, write clear English and Urdu, and can manage records systematically will find this post both manageable and rewarding from the first month. With ten seats on offer and only a graduation requirement, the competition will be stiff but the entry bar is the lowest among current SPSC openings — a combination worth acting on before 23 October.

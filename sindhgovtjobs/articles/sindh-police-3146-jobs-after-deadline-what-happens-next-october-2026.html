Sindh Police 3,146 Jobs 2026: Applications Closed 15 Oct — Physical Tests, STS Screening & Merit Process Next

The Sindh Police Department closed applications on 15 October 2026 for its 3,146-post shaheed/deceased/invalidated quota recruitment — reserved exclusively for the eligible legal heirs of Sindh Police employees who lost their lives, were permanently invalidated or were incapacitated in the line of duty, under the Sindh Police (Shaheed, Deceased, and Permanently Invalidated or Incapacitated Quota) Recruitment Rules-2021. A separate 139-post Assistant Sub-Inspector (ASI) recruitment through STS ran alongside it, taking the combined drive to 3,285 posts. With the deadline gone, the process now moves to physical tests and the written screening stage.

If you applied before 15 October, this is the guide to what comes next: the test sequence, the standards you will be judged on, and how the final merit is drawn.

What Happens Next — The Selection Sequence

After the 15 October closing date, the Sindh Police and the testing agency work through the following stages:

1
Scrutiny of applications. Application forms are checked against the quota conditions — the shaheed/deceased/invalidated heirship documents, Sindh domicile, age (18–28 years for constable posts, 21–28 for ASI, calculated as of 15 October 2026) and the driving licence for Driver posts. No relaxation in the upper age limit, qualification or eligibility is granted in this drive.
2
Physical measurement and endurance tests. Male candidates: minimum height 5 feet 5 inches, chest 33 inches minimum with 1.5 inches expansion; female candidates: minimum height 5 feet with sound health. Endurance: 1,600 metres run in 8 minutes for male candidates (10 minutes for Driver Police Constable posts), and an 800 metres walk/run in 14 minutes for female candidates.
3
Written screening test. ASI candidates applied through the SIBA Testing Services (STS) portal (apply.sts.net.pk) with a Rs. 1,500 challan; their screening is run through STS. Constable-post candidates follow the test schedule issued by the department.
4
Interview and final merit. Shortlisted candidates are interviewed, and the final merit is drawn under the Recruitment Rules 2021. Test venues and dates are communicated by SMS and published on the official websites.
Important: No TA/DA is admissible for tests or interviews. Candidates should watch sindhpolice.gov.pk and the STS portal for the test schedule — and keep their application submission proof safe.

Physical Standards — Prepare Now

The physical stage is where many candidates fall: height and chest are measured against fixed minimums with no relaxation, and the endurance test is strictly timed. Candidates should be training now — daily running for the 1,600-metre standard, and the chest-expansion measurement for male candidates. For Driver Police Constable posts, the licence condition matters as much as the running: a valid motor car driving licence on the closing date was required, with an LTV licence to be obtained within the three-year probation.

After the Tests — What to Watch

Candidates who clear the physical and written stages should expect the interview call next, with the final merit list published afterward. Because this recruitment was run under a Supreme Court judgment and a Government of Sindh notification, the process is under close scrutiny — the department has committed to the quota rules, and every seat is filled strictly from the eligible heirship pool. Keep your documents — CNIC, domicile, PRC, heirship certificates, education and licence — ready for verification at every stage, and follow only the official websites and SMS for your test date and venue.

Frequently Asked Questions

Has the Sindh Police 3,146 jobs deadline passed?

Head Constable (11), Police Constable (2,717), Driver Police Constable (430) and Assistant Sub-Inspector (139) — a combined 3,285 posts under the shaheed/deceased/invalidated quota.

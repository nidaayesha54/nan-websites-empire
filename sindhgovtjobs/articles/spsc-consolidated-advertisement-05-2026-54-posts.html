SPSC Advertisement 05/2026: 54 Posts — Medical Officers, Assistants & IT Posts, Last Date 23 Oct 2026

Consolidated Advertisement 05/2026 offers 54 posts across the Home, Law and Anti-Corruption departments — fee Rs 500 via PSID, online applications close 23 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Sindh Government Jobs » Articles

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC) has issued Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026, dated 25 September 2026, offering 54 Sindh government posts across several departments. Online applications close on 23 October 2026 (up to 05:00 PM), and the application fee is Rs 500, paid through a PSID on 1Bill.

The biggest block is 32 Medical Officer (BPS-17) posts in the Home Department, followed by 10 Assistant (BPS-16) posts in the Law Department, plus Lady Medical Officer, IT and Programme Officer posts.

Key Posts in Detail

32 Medical Officer (BPS-17) — Home Department

The largest block: 32 Medical Officer posts across Sindh districts. Candidates need MBBS from an HEC-recognized university, PMDC registration and a one-year house job. Age 21 to 28 years, plus general age relaxation.

10 Assistant (BPS-16) — Law Department

Ten Assistant posts in the Law Department — a strong opening for graduates seeking an administrative role in the provincial government. Check exact qualification and age requirements in the official advertisement.

4 Lady Medical Officer (BPS-17) — Home Department

Four Lady Medical Officer posts for female candidates: MBBS, PMDC registration and a one-year house job, age 21 to 28 years plus relaxation.

IT Posts — Enquiries & Anti-Corruption Establishment

Two IT vacancies under the Services, General Administration and Coordination Department: Deputy Director IT / Senior Manager IT (BPS-18) — five years of IT experience, age 28 to 35; and Assistant Director IT / System Analyst (BPS-17) — degree holders, age 21 to 28. Both are open-merit posts.

Programme Officer (BPS-17) — Planning & Development

One Programme Officer post for candidates with 16 years of education in Computer Science, IT or Software Engineering (second division) from an HEC-recognized university, aged 21 to 28.

Application Fee and How to Pay

The fee is Rs 500, payable through a PSID on 1Bill generated from the SPSC portal, before submitting your application.

How to Apply Online for SPSC Ad 05/2026

1
Visit the official SPSC website www.spsc.gov.pk and open the online application section.
2
Select Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026 and choose your post.
3
Fill the online form with personal, educational and experience details exactly as on your documents.
4
Generate your PSID and pay the Rs 500 fee through 1Bill.
5
Upload all required documents on the portal before the closing date — SPSC has directed candidates to complete document upload before the deadline.
6
Submit the application before 23 October 2026 (05:00 PM) and keep a print for your record.
Update, 11 October 2026: SPSC now requires candidates to upload their documents on the portal before the closing date. Do not wait until the last day — incomplete document uploads can cost you the application.

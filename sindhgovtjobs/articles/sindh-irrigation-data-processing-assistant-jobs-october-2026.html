The Irrigation Department, Government of Sindh — through the Office of the Chief Engineer, Sukkur Barrage Left Bank Region, Sukkur — has invited applications for 11 posts of Data Processing Assistant (BPS-12) on a regular (non-contract) basis. Recruitment is being conducted by SIBA Testing Services (STS) on behalf of the department, and applications go through the STS online portal. The advertisement is dated 10 October 2026, and the last date to apply is 24 October 2026 — so this one is closing soon.

The post carries the government pay scale of BPS-12 on a regular footing, which is what makes this opening stand out from the many contract-based advertisements around it. Posting stations are spread across the region: 2 posts at Rohri Canal Circle Hyderabad, 3 posts at Nasir Division Hyderabad (District Hyderabad), posts at Irrigation Division Hala (District Matiari), plus further locations in the Hyderabad, Shaheed Benazirabad, Moro/Naushahro Feroze and Sukkur regions. Candidates must hold the domicile of Hyderabad or Matiari districts, and a 5-year upper age relaxation applies as per the Sindh Government notification.

Eligibility Criteria and Duties

Only candidates holding the domicile of Hyderabad or Matiari districts are eligible to apply. A 5-year upper age relaxation is admissible as per the Sindh Government notification. Confirm the exact educational requirement, experience (if any) and other conditions in the official STS listing, since those details come from the advertisement itself.

  • Domicile: Hyderabad and Matiari districts (Sindh).
  • Age relaxation: 5 years upper age relaxation per Sindh Government notification.
  • Recruitment body: SIBA Testing Services (STS) conducts the test and screening on behalf of the Irrigation Department.
  • Shortlisting: only eligible and shortlisted candidates will be called for interview.
  • Duties of the post: assisting computer and data operators and disposing of official government work through the computer.
  • No TA/DA will be paid for the test or interview.

How to Apply

Applications go online through the SIBA Testing Services portal — do not send documents by post unless the STS listing asks for them:

1
Open the STS portal at apply.sts.net.pk and find the Irrigation Department Data Processing Assistant listing.
2
Create your account and fill the online application form with correct personal, educational and domicile details (Hyderabad or Matiari).
3
Upload the required documents and double-check every entry before submitting.
4
Submit before 24 October 2026 — the deadline is only days away, so do not wait for the last day.
5
Keep a printout of your application and any fee receipt for the test day.
6
Watch for the STS test schedule — only shortlisted candidates will be called, and no TA/DA is paid.
Closing soon: The last date is 24 October 2026 — barely two days from today (22 October). Apply on the STS portal right away if you meet the domicile requirement.

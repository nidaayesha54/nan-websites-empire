The Government of Sindh's Agriculture, Price and Supply Department is hiring for the Project Management Unit (PMU) of the Fruit and Vegetable Mandi Project, Larkana. 17 posts are on offer across 11 categories on contract for up to three years, with monthly salaries from Rs 40,000 (Naib Qasid) to Rs 350,000 (Director Finance).

Senior roles pay well — Procurement Specialist at Rs 325,000, M&E Expert at Rs 300,000 and three engineers at Rs 250,000 each. There is no online portal: applications go as a physical package to the Project Director's office, and the closing date is 2 November 2026.

Eligibility Criteria and Contract Terms

These are project posts, not permanent seats — contracts run up to three years, tied to project funding and performance. Confirm the exact degree, discipline and experience requirements in the original newspaper ad before applying.

  • Senior posts (Director Finance, Procurement Specialist, M&E Expert): relevant qualification with experience — confirm the exact criteria in the ad.
  • Project Engineers (Civil, Electrical, Mechanical): engineering degrees in the respective disciplines — confirm in the ad.
  • Admin/HR Officer and Accounts Officer: relevant qualifications with experience — confirm in the ad.
  • Support staff (Office Assistant, Driver, Naib Qasid): qualifications as per the original ad.
  • Contract terms: up to 3 years, subject to availability of funds and satisfactory performance.

How to Apply

There is no online portal and no application fee for this project — submit a complete physical package to the Project Director's office:

1
Prepare your application package — updated CV, attested copies of degrees, experience certificates and your CNIC.
2
Address it to the Project Director, Office of Deputy Commissioner Larkana.
3
Ensure it reaches the office before 2 November 2026 — late or incomplete packages are not entertained. Send early to beat postal or courier delays.
4
Keep copies of everything you submit.
5
Confirm your eligibility against the original newspaper ad before sending your package.

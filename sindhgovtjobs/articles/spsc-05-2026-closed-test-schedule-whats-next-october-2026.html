SPSC 05/2026 Closed 23 Oct — Test Schedule & What's Next

SPSC Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026 closed on 23 October 2026 with 54 posts. Test dates are not announced yet — here is what happens next, how to check your status, and how to prepare while you wait.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » Sindh Government Jobs » Articles

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC) closed online applications for Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026 on 23 October 2026 at 5:00 pm. The advertisement, dated 25 September 2026, offered 54 posts in BPS-16 to BPS-18 across five Government of Sindh departments. If you applied, the next question is obvious: when is the test? The honest answer is that SPSC has not announced test dates yet — and any date circulating on social media before SPSC publishes it should be treated as a rumour. Here is what the advertisement covered, what happens next, and how to prepare.

Honesty flag: no test dates have been announced for this advertisement as of this writing. We have not invented a schedule, and you should not trust one from unofficial sources. SPSC publishes the test schedule and admit slips on its own website, and that is the only source to rely on.

What the Advertisement Covered

The 54 posts span five departments. The Home Department took the largest share: 32 Medical Officers (Male) and 4 Lady Medical Officers, all BPS-17, with an age limit of 21 to 28 years and a rural/urban quota split. The Information Department offered 4 Information Officers (BPS-17) for candidates with a master's degree in a relevant field. The Law, Parliamentary Affairs & Criminal Prosecution Department offered 10 Assistants (BPS-16) for graduates aged 21 to 31. The Planning & Development Department offered one Assistant Director and one Programme Officer (BPS-17). The Services, General Administration & Coordination Department offered the two IT posts: Deputy Director IT / Senior Manager IT (BPS-18, age 28–35, five years' experience) and Assistant Director IT / System Analyst (BPS-17, age 21–28).

What Happens Next — Stage by Stage

1
Application scrutiny: SPSC checks every application against the post's eligibility, age, domicile and fee payment. Incomplete applications and applications from candidates who do not meet the conditions are rejected.
2
Test schedule announcement: SPSC publishes the screening test schedule on spsc.gov.pk. Check the website regularly — do not rely on forwarded messages.
3
Admit slips: eligible candidates download their roll number slips from the SPSC candidate portal before the test date.
4
Screening test: candidates appear for the written screening test at their allocated centre, carrying their admit slip and original CNIC.
5
Interviews: candidates who qualify the screening test are called for interview before the Commission, with original documents.
6
Final merit: SPSC recommends successful candidates to the department, and appointment follows departmental verification.

What You Cannot Change Now

The advertisement is explicit: domicile, PRC, quota, age, qualification, experience and exam centre cannot be changed after the closing date. If you entered a wrong CNIC number or date of birth, the advertisement warns that incorrect details lead to rejection. Candidates serving in government or semi-government organisations must apply through the proper channel — and if you have not already done so, sort out your NOC now, because you will need it at the interview stage.

How to Prepare While You Wait

Use the waiting period for post-specific preparation. Medical Officer candidates should revise MBBS-level clinical subjects. Information Officer candidates should prepare journalism, mass communication and current affairs. Assistant candidates should practise general knowledge, English and basic arithmetic. IT candidates should revise computer science fundamentals and their specialisation. Keep your fee payment receipt, application printout, CNIC, domicile, PRC and original degrees in one file — you will need them at the interview, and hunting for them at the last minute is a needless risk.

Reminder: the advertisement also states that the number of posts may change without notice, and SPSC can correct errors at any stage. The final position stands as published on spsc.gov.pk.

Frequently Asked Questions

No. As of the last check, SPSC has not announced test dates for Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026. Watch the SPSC website at spsc.gov.pk and do not trust dates circulated on social media until SPSC publishes them.

How many posts were in SPSC Advertisement 05/2026?

54 posts in BPS-16 to BPS-18, including 32 Medical Officers, 4 Lady Medical Officers, 4 Information Officers, 10 Assistants and 2 IT posts, plus Assistant Director and Programme Officer posts.

SPSC Information Officer Jobs 2026: 4 BPS-17 Posts in Sindh Information Department — Apply by 23 Oct 2026

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC), Hyderabad, has announced 4 posts of Information Officer (BPS-17) in the Information Department, Government of Sindh. These posts appear in Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026, dated 25 September 2026, and are distributed across open merit and quota categories. The last date to apply is 23 October 2026, and SPSC accepts online applications only.

Information Officer posts in the provincial Information Department are communication-focused roles — media relations, press coordination and public information — making them a natural target for journalism, mass communication and media studies graduates.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualification: A Master's degree in the relevant field (such as mass communication, journalism or a related discipline) from an HEC-recognised university — check the advertisement for the exact degree requirement.
  • Experience: 3 to 5 years of relevant experience in the media/communication field, as stated in the advertisement.
  • Domicile: Sindh domicile and PRC required, as per the quota of the post.
  • Age limit: Per the age bracket stated in the official advertisement — verify before applying.

The qualification and experience must be complete by the closing date. Domicile, PRC, quota, age, qualification, experience and exam centre cannot be changed after the closing date, so verify every entry before submitting.

How to Apply Online on spsc.gov.pk

Follow these steps to submit your application:

1
Open www.spsc.gov.pk (or the official SPSC mobile app) and register or log in to the candidate portal.
2
Go to the “Available Jobs” feature and select Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026.
3
Choose Information Officer and fill in your personal, education and experience details.
4
Generate the PSID and pay the Rs. 500 application fee through the 1Bill invoice option.
5
Submit the form before 23 October 2026 (5:00 pm on the closing day) and keep a copy of the submitted application. No hard copy needs to be sent.
Important: Manual applications will not be entertained. Candidates serving in government or semi-government organisations must apply through the proper channel. Incorrect CNIC numbers, wrong dates of birth and incomplete applications lead to rejection.

Selection Process

After the closing date, SPSC will announce the test schedule for these posts. Candidates should expect a written screening test followed by interviews for shortlisted candidates, with final selection on merit under the Commission's regulations. Test dates have not been announced yet — keep checking the SPSC website and this portal for roll number slips and the schedule.

Because the Information Department values communication skills, shortlisted candidates should prepare to demonstrate media awareness and writing ability at the interview stage. Build a small portfolio of your published or professional communication work — press releases, reports, campaign material — and be ready to discuss how you would handle public information for a government department.

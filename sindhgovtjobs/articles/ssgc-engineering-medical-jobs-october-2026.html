SSGC Engineering & Medical Jobs 2026: Chief Manager, Distribution Engineers, Medical Officers — Last Date 2 Nov

Sui Southern Gas Company Limited (SSGC) has announced engineering and medical vacancies in addition to its corporate communication hiring: Chief Manager / Engineer posts on the engineering and BCS routes, Engineer (Distribution) posts for Sindh and Balochistan, and Medical Officer / Lady Medical Officer posts. The last date to apply is 2 November 2026, and applications go through the official SSGC careers portal.

These are professional-grade posts inside Pakistan's gas distribution utility for the southern zone: engineers work on the transmission and distribution network, while the medical posts serve the company's workforce. All carry market-competitive compensation packages under company employment terms.

Eligibility & Employment Terms

  • Experience: 2 to 10 years depending on the post — the Chief Manager/Engineer posts additionally require four years of experience as a Functional or Team Lead.
  • Age limit: the advertisement does not specify age limits — check the individual job details on the official SSGC careers portal for any additional eligibility conditions.
  • Salary: market-competitive compensation packages; no specific monthly figures are published.
  • Equal opportunity: SSGC encourages women to apply across these posts.
  • Internal candidates: continuity of service is governed by company policy.

Why These Posts Matter

Keep your CV, experience letters and registration certificates ready before you start the online form - incomplete applications are not entertained, and the careers portal closes on the stated date. For engineers, SSGC is infrastructure-scale work: distribution networks, metering, pressure management and the metering-to-billing chain across two provinces. The Chief Manager/Engineer posts — with their Team Lead prerequisite — are leadership seats, not entry roles: they suit engineers already managing functions or teams. The Distribution Engineer posts in Sindh and Balochistan are the field-engineering core of the company. For doctors, the Medical Officer posts are corporate healthcare roles with a utility's employment benefits. Check the portal entry for your exact post, confirm your PEC/PMDC standing, and apply before 2 November 2026.

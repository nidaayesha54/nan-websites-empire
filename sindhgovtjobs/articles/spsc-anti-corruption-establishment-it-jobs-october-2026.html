SPSC Anti-Corruption Establishment IT Jobs 2026: Deputy Director & Assistant Director IT — Apply by 23 Oct 2026

The Sindh Public Service Commission has announced two IT officer posts in the Enquiries & Anti-Corruption Establishment under Consolidated Advertisement 05/2026 — Deputy Director IT (BPS-18) and Assistant Director IT (BPS-17), with applications closing on 23 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Sindh Government Jobs » Articles

The Sindh Public Service Commission (SPSC) has announced two IT vacancies in the Enquiries & Anti-Corruption Establishment under the Services, General Administration & Coordination Department, Government of Sindh. The posts — Deputy Director IT / Senior Manager IT (BPS-18) and Assistant Director IT / System Analyst (BPS-17) — appear in Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026, dated 25 September 2026, with one vacancy for each role on open merit. The deadline to apply is 23 October 2026, and applications are accepted online only.

IT officer posts in the Anti-Corruption Establishment are rare — these roles combine systems work with the Establishment's investigative and enquiry functions, making them attractive for IT professionals who want a public-sector career with real responsibility.

Posts & Eligibility

PostScalePostsAge LimitKey Requirement
Deputy Director IT / Senior Manager ITBPS-18128 to 35 yearsBachelor's degree in IT or Computer Science + 5 years of relevant IT experience
Assistant Director IT / System AnalystBPS-17121 to 28 yearsBachelor's degree in IT or Computer Science (at least second division)
Note: Both positions are listed under open merit for male and female candidates. The main difference between the two posts is experience — the senior post requires five years of relevant IT experience, while the Assistant Director post requires the degree without a separately specified experience bar.

About the Enquiries & Anti-Corruption Establishment

The Enquiries & Anti-Corruption Establishment is Sindh's dedicated anti-corruption body, handling enquiries and investigations into corruption in provincial government departments. Its IT wing supports case management systems, digital records, data analysis and the technology infrastructure behind investigations. Officers posted here work at the intersection of technology and accountability — a profile that looks strong on any CV.

Eligibility Criteria in Detail

Deputy Director IT / Senior Manager IT (BPS-18)

  • Qualification: Bachelor's degree in IT or Computer Science (at least second division) from a recognised university.
  • Experience: Minimum 5 years of relevant IT experience.
  • Age: 28 to 35 years.
  • Quota: Open merit.

Assistant Director IT / System Analyst (BPS-17)

  • Qualification: Bachelor's degree in IT or Computer Science (at least second division) from a recognised university.
  • Age: 21 to 28 years.
  • Quota: Open merit.

Candidates must hold the required qualification and experience by the closing date. Domicile, PRC, quota, age, qualification, experience and exam centre cannot be changed after the closing date, so verify every entry before submitting.

How to Apply Online on spsc.gov.pk

Follow these steps to submit your application:

1
Open www.spsc.gov.pk (or the official SPSC mobile app) and register or log in to the candidate portal.
2
Go to the “Available Jobs” feature and select Consolidated Advertisement No. 05/2026.
3
Choose Deputy Director IT or Assistant Director IT and fill in your personal, education and experience details.
4
Generate the PSID and pay the Rs. 500 application fee through the 1Bill invoice option.
5
Submit the form before 23 October 2026 at 5:00 pm and keep a copy of your application. No hard copy is required.
Important: Manual applications will not be entertained. Candidates serving in government or semi-government organisations must apply through the proper channel. Incorrect CNIC numbers, wrong dates of birth and incomplete applications will lead to rejection.

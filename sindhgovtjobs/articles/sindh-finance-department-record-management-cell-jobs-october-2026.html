SourceDaily Dawn advertisement, 21 October 2026

The Finance Department, Government of Sindh, has announced 58 vacancies for its new Record Management Cell, established under ADP Scheme No. 788/2026-27 titled "Establishment of Record Management Cell at Finance Department". The cell's work covers record preservation in hard and digital formats, the maintenance of data repositories, and reliable document retrieval for the department's day-to-day business. Hiring is on a purely temporary contract basis, with fixed monthly salaries of Rs 42,000 to Rs 60,000 depending on the post. The advertisement was published in the Daily Dawn on 21 October 2026, and applications must reach the given P.O. Box before 5 November 2026.

Nine categories of posts are on offer — Assistant, Scanning Technician, Computer Operator, Clerk, Binder, Record Sorter, Helper, Chowkidar, plus one further support category as listed in the advertisement. Posts are located in Karachi and various districts across Sindh, and the qualification bar is wide: Primary Pass is enough for Helper and Chowkidar posts, while technical posts require a CIT, DIT or IT diploma and the top posts go up to a Bachelor's degree. The age limit is 18 to 40 years. Because these are temporary project-style engagements, there are no government allowances — pay is a fixed package only.

Eligibility Criteria and Contract Terms

The qualification requirement runs from Primary Pass for Helper and Chowkidar posts up to a Bachelor's degree for the higher posts, with CIT, DIT or IT diplomas required for the Scanning Technician and Computer Operator posts. Candidates must be between 18 and 40 years of age. Confirm the exact post-wise education and experience against the original Daily Dawn advertisement before applying.

  • Senior/support posts (Assistant, Clerk): education up to Bachelor's degree as per the ad.
  • Technical posts (Scanning Technician, Computer Operator): CIT/DIT/IT diploma or equivalent computer qualification.
  • Support staff (Binder, Record Sorter, Helper, Chowkidar): Primary Pass and above as per the ad.
  • Contract terms: purely temporary engagement, terminable on one month's notice; fixed salary with no allowances.
  • Posting: Karachi and various districts across Sindh.

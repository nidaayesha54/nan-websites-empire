The Sindh Institute of Child Health & Neonatology (SICHN) — a Government of Sindh project under the SICHN Act 2018 — has announced 74 job titles across 14 departments in Karachi. The ad (reference INF-KRY: 4548/26) closes on 28 October 2026 — online applications only via the SICHN careers portal.

All positions are on a one-year contract, extendable on satisfactory performance, with training opportunities and a route into postgraduate training programmes. The institute encourages women to apply, and the domicile should preferably be of Sindh province. Only shortlisted candidates are called for interview with original documents; a written test may be held if applications outnumber posts. No TA/DA is paid, and the Selection Board's decision is final.

Honesty flag: The advertisement lists job titles by department only — per-post seat counts and exact requirements are on the SICHN careers portal. Confirm your post's details there before applying.

General Conditions for Applicants

  • Contract: one year, extendable on satisfactory performance; only post-qualification experience counts.
  • Domicile: preferably Sindh province — check the portal entry for your post.
  • NOC: candidates already working in government, autonomous or semi-autonomous bodies must apply through proper channel with a departmental NOC.
  • Equal opportunity: SICHN is an equal opportunity employer and encourages females to apply.
  • Selection: only shortlisted candidates are interviewed with original documents; a written test may be held if applicants outnumber posts. No TA/DA.

How to Apply

Applications go online only through the SICHN careers portal — no postal or email applications:

1
Open the SICHN careers portal — www.sichn.com.pk/careers.
2
Browse vacancies by department and open your title — check its seat count, qualification and experience.
3
Fill the online form with correct personal, educational and professional details.
4
Upload your documents — updated CV, education and experience certificates, PMDC/PNC registration where applicable, CNIC, a recent photo and NOC if you are a government employee.
5
Review everything before final submission.
6
Submit before 28 October 2026 — do not wait for the last day.
Closing soon: The last date is 28 October 2026, and only online applications are accepted — incomplete applications will not be considered.

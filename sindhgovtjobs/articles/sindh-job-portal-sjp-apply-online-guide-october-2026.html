Sindh Job Portal (SJP) 2026 — How to Apply Online (Guide)

The Sindh Job Portal (sjp.gos.pk) is where Sindh government departments advertise their posts — Irrigation, Finance and more. This guide covers registration, the STS certificate requirement, and submitting a correct application.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » Sindh Government Jobs » Articles

Many Sindh government jobs are advertised with the same instruction: apply online through the Sindh Job Portal. The portal, at sjp.gos.pk, is the Sindh government's online recruitment system for departmental posts — the vacancies that departments advertise directly, rather than through the Sindh Public Service Commission. Recent examples include the Irrigation Department's Data Processing Assistant posts and the Finance Department's Record Management Cell posts. This guide explains how the portal works, why the STS certificate matters, and how to submit an application that is not rejected on a technicality.

Honesty flag: this is a guide to using the portal, not a job advertisement — it has no closing date of its own. Each departmental post carries its own deadline, eligibility and STS requirement, stated in its advertisement. Do not confuse the Sindh Job Portal with SPSC — they are separate systems, and applying on the wrong one does not count.

Sindh Job Portal vs SPSC — Which One Does Your Post Use?

This is the most common source of confusion. SPSC (spsc.gov.pk) handles commission posts advertised through SPSC consolidated advertisements, such as Advertisement No. 05/2026 — medical officers, information officers, assistants and similar BPS posts. The Sindh Job Portal (sjp.gos.pk) handles departmental posts advertised directly by departments — for example, the Irrigation Department's Data Processing Assistant (BPS-12) posts and the Finance Department's Record Management Cell posts. The advertisement itself tells you which system to use: if it says "apply through the Sindh Job Portal", use sjp.gos.pk; if it says SPSC, use spsc.gov.pk. Applying on the wrong portal means your application simply does not exist for that post.

The STS Certificate — Why It Matters

Several Sindh departmental posts require a Sukkur IBA Testing Service (STS) screening test certificate. For example, the Irrigation Department's Data Processing Assistant posts require a bachelor's degree or DIT diploma, one year of relevant experience, and a minimum of 40% marks in the STS Graduation Category test. The SELD Arts Teacher posts similarly required 40% in the STS Graduation Category test. If your post requires an STS certificate, you must upload it with your application — without it, your application is incomplete. Check the advertisement for the exact test category and minimum marks; they differ by post.

Step-by-Step: Register and Apply

1
Open sjp.gos.pk (linked above) and create your account or log in with your existing credentials.
2
Complete your profile accurately: personal details, education, experience, domicile and PRC.
3
Find the departmental post you want and read it in full — eligibility, STS requirement, age limit and district domicile rule.
4
Upload scanned copies of your STS certificate, degree or DIT diploma, experience certificate, CNIC, domicile, PRC and recent photograph (plus NOC if you are a government employee).
5
Select the post for your district and review every field — district domicile matters, and the wrong district can disqualify you.
6
Submit before the post's closing date. Only online applications submitted through the portal are accepted.

Domicile and District Rules

Sindh departmental posts are usually tied to district domicile: you apply against the vacancies of your home district, and you need a valid domicile certificate and PRC (Form-D) for that district. The quota share for initial appointment follows Sindh government rules, and the rural/urban split applies where stated. Applying against the wrong district, or without a valid PRC, is a common reason for rejection. Government employees must obtain an NOC from their parent department and present the original at the interview.

After You Apply

After submission, eligible and shortlisted candidates appear before the Departmental Selection Committee with original documents, as per the schedule uploaded on the Job Portal. No TA/DA is admissible for the interview. Incomplete applications or applications received after the due date are not entertained. Keep your profile updated as you gain qualifications and experience, because the same profile serves every future departmental application.

Reminder: the Sindh Job Portal is for Sindh departmental posts. Federal posts use the National Job Portal (njp.gov.pk), and SPSC commission posts use spsc.gov.pk. Always check the advertisement to see which system your post uses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Sindh Job Portal (SJP)?

The Sindh Job Portal at sjp.gos.pk is the Sindh government's online recruitment portal for departmental posts. Sindh government departments advertise their vacancies there, and candidates apply online through a single profile.

Is the Sindh Job Portal the same as SPSC?

No. SPSC (spsc.gov.pk) handles commission posts advertised through SPSC consolidated advertisements. The Sindh Job Portal handles departmental posts advertised directly by departments, such as the Irrigation Department. Check the advertisement to see which system your post uses.

Do I need an STS certificate to apply on the Sindh Job Portal?

It depends on the post. Several Sindh departmental posts require a Sukkur IBA STS screening test certificate — for example, a minimum of 40% marks in the Graduation Category test for posts like Data Processing Assistant. The advertisement states whether an STS certificate is required.

What documents do I need to apply on the Sindh Job Portal?

Your CNIC, a valid email address and mobile number, educational certificates, STS screening certificate where required, experience certificate, domicile certificate, PRC and a recent photograph. Government employees must also arrange an NOC.

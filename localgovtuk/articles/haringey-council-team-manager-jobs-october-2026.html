What the Role Involves

The Team Manager leads services for older people within Haringey’s health and social care operation — the frontline of council support for residents who need care, safeguarding and help to live independently. The role is described as strategic yet hands-on: translating legislation and policy into practical action on the ground, while managing staff, budgets, performance and partnership working across health and care.

  • Service leadership: managing the team that assesses needs and arranges care for older residents across the borough.
  • Strategy into action: turning statutory duties, national guidance and local policy into day-to-day practice.
  • Multi-agency working: collaborating with NHS partners, care providers, housing and voluntary services.
  • Quality and performance: driving standards, managing caseloads and budgets, and responding to inspection and safeguarding demands.

Who Should Apply

Haringey is looking for someone with a strong background in health and social care who can combine strategic thinking with operational delivery — a leader comfortable both planning services and steadying a busy team through difficult decisions. Experience managing staff and services in adult social care is the clear core requirement.

Note: Details are as listed on Haringey Council’s official jobs portal at the time of research on 21 October 2026. The advert may close early once enough applications are received — do not wait until the deadline. Always verify the live listing before applying.

Contract, Hours and Salary

The post is permanent at 36 hours per week — Haringey’s standard full-time week. The PO5 grade carries a starting salary of £53,919–£57,150 per annum. Work is based at Alexandra House, 10 Station Road, Wood Green, London N22 7TR, in the heart of Haringey’s civic centre area.

Benefits of Working for Haringey Council

Haringey advertises a competitive council package alongside the salary:

  • Up to 31 days’ annual leave plus bank holidays.
  • Hybrid working potential — a blend of office and remote working where the role allows.
  • Health and wellbeing support for staff.
  • Generous Local Government Pension Scheme membership.
  • Discounts on leisure and culture services.

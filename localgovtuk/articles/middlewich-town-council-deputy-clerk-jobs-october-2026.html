Middlewich Council Deputy Town Clerk Jobs 2026 — Apply by 22 Nov

Middlewich Town Council in Cheshire is hiring a Deputy Town Clerk / Operations Manager and an Administration Officer — both full-time, based at Victoria Buildings on Lewin Street. The Deputy role closes on Sunday 22 November 2026; the Admin Officer role closes earlier, on 1 November.

Middlewich Town Council is recruiting for two full-time posts at its offices in the Cheshire market town: a Deputy Town Clerk / Operations Manager and an Administration Officer. Both are 37-hour-a-week roles based at Victoria Buildings, Lewin Street, Middlewich CW10 9AS, with applications through the council’s official staff-vacancies page. The Deputy Town Clerk advert closes at 12:00pm on Sunday 22 November 2026, while the Administration Officer post closes earlier — at midnight on Sunday 1 November 2026.

Deputy Town Clerk / Operations Manager

This is the senior of the two posts — a full-time, 37-hour-a-week leadership role at NJC LC2, SCP 24–28, paying £36,581–£40,444 per annum. Candidates holding CILCA (Certificate in Local Council Administration) can be appointed on a higher range of £41,177–£43,149.

  • Deputy to the Town Clerk: supporting the chief officer and stepping in to lead when needed.
  • Operations: running the council’s services, projects and staff day to day.
  • Democratic support: backing council meetings, committees and civic processes.

Administration Officer

The Administration Officer post is a full-time, 37-hour week (Monday to Friday) role graded at NJC SCP 15, £31,015 per annum — the council’s front-line administrative engine room.

  • Office administration: correspondence, records, bookings and general business support.
  • Resident contact: first point of contact for public enquiries at Victoria Buildings.
  • Meeting support: preparing agendas and minutes, supporting democratic processes.

This advert closes at midnight on Sunday 1 November 2026 — just days away — so download the pack and apply immediately.

Why Town and Parish Councils Are a Smart Career Path

Town and parish councils are the most local tier of government in England — and often overlooked by jobseekers who only watch the big county and city councils. They offer stable, pensionable local government employment on National Joint Council pay scales, with varied, community-facing work. For anyone with admin or local government experience — or working towards CILCA — the parish tier is an underrated route in.

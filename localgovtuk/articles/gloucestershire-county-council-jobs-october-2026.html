Gloucestershire Council Jobs 2026 – 3 Roles Open (Closing: 25 Oct)

Gloucestershire County Council is hiring for three posts at Shire Hall and Gloucester Main Library — Administrator, Casual Library Assistant and Senior Commissioning Manager. Two vacancies close on 25 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 21, 2026
Home » UK Local Government Jobs » Articles

Gloucestershire County Council currently has three live vacancies on its official careers pages — an Administrator for the Connect to Work employment programme, a Casual Library Assistant at Gloucester Main Library, and a Senior Commissioning Manager leading contract and commissioning work. Salaries run from £16.22 an hour to £59,753 per annum, and two vacancies close on 25 October 2026.

The Three Vacancies in Detail

1. Administrator (Connect to Work) — req 14338

The Administrator post sits in the council’s Connect to Work programme, which helps people overcome barriers into employment. The role is full-time at 37 hours a week on a fixed-term contract of two years, based at Shire Hall, Block 4, Gloucester.

  • Salary: £25,185–£25,989 per annum
  • Contract: Fixed term, 2 years
  • Closes: 25 October 2026
  • Requisition: 14338

Employment-support administration suits organised, people-focused candidates motivated by helping others into work.

2. Casual Library Assistant — req 14962

The Casual Library Assistant role is based at Gloucester Main Library, covering during periods of leave and staff vacancies. It is a zero-hours / casual post paid at £16.22 per hour — flexible hours rather than a fixed rota.

  • Salary: £16.22 per hour
  • Contract: Casual / zero hours
  • Closes: 25 October 2026
  • Requisition: 14962

Casual council posts like this suit students, returners to work, or anyone wanting public-sector experience around other commitments.

3. Senior Commissioning Manager — req 15006

The Senior Commissioning Manager is the senior post of the three: a permanent, full-time (37 hours) leadership role at Shire Hall, Gloucester, centred on contract management, commercial practice and commissioning across council services.

  • Salary: £55,717–£59,753 per annum
  • Contract: Permanent, full time
  • Closes: 2 November 2026
  • Requisition: 15006
Note: Vacancies, salaries and closing dates are as listed on Gloucestershire County Council’s official careers pages at the time of research on 21 October 2026. Councils can withdraw or close adverts early — always verify the live listing before applying.

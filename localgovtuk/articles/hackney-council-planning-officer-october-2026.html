London Borough of Hackney is hiring a Planning Officer — £41,739–£47,367 (SO1-PO1), development management, closing 3 November 2026.

The London Borough of Hackney is recruiting a Planning Officer — an excellent entry point into a Development Management career, or a next step for a current Development Management Officer ready to handle more complex applications. The post pays £41,739–£47,367 (SO1–PO1) on the 2026/27 salary scale, and applications close on 3 November 2026 at 22:59.

The role centres on handling planning applications to tight deadlines and service-level agreements: assessing proposals, negotiating with applicants and agents, and making sound recommendations that balance development needs with Hackney’s planning policies. The Role: Planning Officer (Development Management)

Development Management is the front line of the planning system: the officers who decide — or recommend decisions on — the planning applications that shape a borough. At Hackney you will handle a caseload of applications, working to tight deadlines and service-level agreements so the council offers an efficient, effective service to applicants and residents alike.

For an aspiring planning officer this is a genuine career-maker: Development Management experience is the core currency of planning careers, and Hackney’s caseload — dense, urban, high-profile — builds judgement fast. For an existing DM officer, the draw is stepping up to more complex applications with real responsibility.

An Application Without a Supporting Statement

Notably, this advert states that the application process replaces a supporting statement — instead, the council asks you to showcase your knowledge and skills using your own voice through the application itself. Take that instruction seriously: generic, templated answers will stand out for the wrong reasons. Show how you think about development management — how you weigh policy, negotiate improvements to schemes, and handle the pressure of deadline-driven caseloads.

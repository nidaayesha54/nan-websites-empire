Manchester City Council Jobs October 2026: Benefits Officer Apprentice — Closes 20 October 2026

Manchester City Council is recruiting in October 2026 — a permanent Benefits Officer Apprentice (hybrid) and a part-time Business Support Officer for the Direct Payments Audit Team. Salaries, hours, eligibility rules and closing dates.

✅ Updated October 17, 2026
Home » UK Local Government Jobs » Articles

Manchester City Council is advertising two vacancies in October 2026. The standout is a permanent Benefits Officer (Apprentice) (35 hours/week, hybrid) combining benefits casework with a Level 4 Revenues and Benefits Welfare Practitioner apprenticeship (~18 months), closing 11:59pm, 20 October 2026. The Direct Payments Audit Team is also recruiting a permanent part-time Business Support Officer (17.5 hours per week, hybrid), Grade 3 at £26,427–£26,827 per annum pro rata, closing at 11:59pm on 23 October 2026.

Important: both roles are open to Manchester residents only — applications from outside the Manchester local authority area will be rejected. There is no visa sponsorship; you must already have the right to work in the UK.

Benefits Officer (Apprentice) — the Details

The Benefits Officer Apprentice is permanent, full-time (35 hours per week) and hybrid, combining benefits casework with a Level 4 Revenues and Benefits Welfare Practitioner apprenticeship of about 18 months.

Earn while you train, finish with a nationally recognised Level 4 qualification and step into a permanent role. Posts are competitive — apply early and show your welfare-rights and customer-service motivation. Deadline: 11:59pm, 20 October 2026.

Business Support Officer — Direct Payments Audit Team

The vacancy sits in the Direct Payments Audit Team, which checks how direct payments for social care are spent. The Business Support Officer is permanent, part-time (17.5 hrs/week), hybrid, Grade 3: £26,427–£26,827 pa pro rata (bar at £26,427).

Expect audit administration, record-checking and cross-team coordination — accuracy and attention to detail matter. Closes 11:59pm, 23 October 2026.

Eligibility: Manchester Residents Only

Both adverts carry the same residency rule, and it is strict:

  • Manchester residents only: applications from outside the Manchester local authority area will be rejected.
  • No visa sponsorship: the council does not offer sponsorship for these posts.
  • Right to work in the UK: you must already hold it — do not apply if you cannot evidence it.

Check that your address falls within the Manchester local authority boundary (not the wider Greater Manchester conurbation) before applying.

How to Apply for Manchester City Council Jobs

Apply through jobs.manchester.gov.uk:

1
Find the Benefits Officer (Apprentice) or Business Support Officer (Direct Payments Audit Team) advert.
2
Confirm the residency rule (Manchester local authority area) and right to work in the UK — both non-negotiable.
3
Register, complete the form and map your experience to the person specification — for the apprentice role, show customer-service and welfare-interest motivation; for the audit role, show admin accuracy and compliance aptitude.
4
Submit before the deadline: 11:59pm, 20 October 2026 (apprentice) or 23 October 2026 (business support).
5
Monitor your email for shortlisting and interview invitations.

The Bottom Line

Two roles, two deadlines, one strict eligibility rule. The Benefits Officer Apprentice closes at 11:59pm, 20 October 2026; the part-time Business Support Officer on 23 October. If you live in Manchester and hold the right to work, apply early.

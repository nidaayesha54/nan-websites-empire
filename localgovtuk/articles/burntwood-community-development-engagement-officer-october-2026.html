The Role: Community Development and Engagement Officer

Community development officers are the connective tissue of a town: they design and deliver projects, build partnerships with community groups, businesses and public bodies, and turn local enthusiasm into lasting improvements. At Burntwood, the brief is explicitly to develop exciting projects and partnerships that deliver lasting benefits — a role with real creative scope and visible local impact.

The role is based at the Old Mining College Centre in Chasetown, the council’s hub for community activity. Expect a mix of office-based planning and out-and-about engagement — including some evening and weekend work when community events and meetings demand it.

What the Wave Tells You

Several things stand out: the £35,570–£36,581 band appears repeatedly across different areas and closing dates, suggesting ongoing recruitment in a core service area; the £48,738–£49,871 Westmorland-wide role is the senior post of the batch; and the earliest deadline — 25 October 2026 — is only days away, so that application cannot wait.

How to Read Council Adverts

  • Salary bands reflect the council’s pay scale for the grade — starting salary is usually at the bottom of the band.
  • “Westmorland-wide” means the role covers the whole council area; “county-wide” postings may involve travel across Cumbria.
  • First-advertised dates show how fresh the vacancy is — roles advertised 12–15 October are the newest.
  • Always open the full advert for the job title, reference number, hours, contract type and person specification.

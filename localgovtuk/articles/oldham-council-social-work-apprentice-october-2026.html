Oldham Council Social Work Apprentice 2026: £32,578 Salary + Paid Degree — Apply by 1 November

Oldham Council is recruiting Social Work Apprentices in Children’s Social Care — £32,578 a year while you train, university fees paid, qualifying with a BSc in 30 months. Deadline: 1 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » UK Local Government Jobs » Articles

Oldham Council is currently recruiting Social Work Apprentices in Children’s Social Care — one of the most generous earn-while-you-learn offers in local government right now. Apprentices are paid £32,578 a year whilst they train, with university fees paid for, qualifying as social workers through a 30-month BSc (Hons) Social Work programme. The deadline for applications is 23:59 on Sunday 1 November 2026.

The course is delivered part-time over two and a half years at the Manchester Metropolitan University campus in central Manchester — typically one or two days a week on campus, then three or four days a week on placement — and is designed with employers to produce resilient, knowledgeable and compassionate social workers.

How the Apprenticeship Works

Over 30 months you train and qualify as a social worker through the Social Work Apprenticeship Programme: campus study at Manchester Met combined with real placements in Children’s Social Care. You will work with children, families, social workers, team managers, foster carers and other professionals — building a supported workload as you progress, always with guidance from your Apprenticeship Lead and Team Manager, and contributing written work including clear, accurate notes and reports.

Who Can Apply

  • Age 18 and over — there is no upper age limit, so career changers are welcome.
  • Applicants who already have a degree can still apply (the apprenticeship is at undergraduate level).
  • Ideal candidates have some previous work experience (paid or voluntary); experience in children’s social care is an advantage.
  • Access to your own vehicle for work is ideal.
  • The post is subject to safer recruitment measures and will require a DBS check.

How to Apply

Applications go through the Greater Jobs portal for Oldham Council. Follow these steps:

1
Find the Social Work Apprentice advert (reference 001774) on the Greater Jobs portal.
2
Read the job description, person specification and programme information booklet attached to the advert.
3
Join the Q&A session on Monday 26 October at 6pm on Microsoft Teams (contact Yvonne Seabright for the link) to ask questions first.
4
Submit your application before 23:59 on Sunday 1 November 2026.
Tip: the 26 October Q&A is a free intelligence-gathering opportunity — attend it, then tailor your application to what the team says they are looking for.

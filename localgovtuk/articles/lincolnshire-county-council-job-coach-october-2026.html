Lincolnshire Job Coach (Part-Time) — Apply by 1 Nov

Lincolnshire County Council is hiring a part-time Job Coach at InspireU, Gainsborough — £28,153–£31,015 pro rata, closing 1 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » UK Local Government Jobs » Articles

Lincolnshire County Council is recruiting a part-time Job Coach to join its expanding Careers team at InspireU, Gainsborough. The post pays £28,153–£31,015 pro rata, involves flexible working with travel across Lincolnshire, and applications close on 1 November 2026.

The role supports learners across the council’s InspireU sites — coaching people towards employment and skills outcomes as part of the council’s careers and employability offer. The council describes itself as a Smarter Working employer: hybrid working is a feature of the role, with a nominated contractual work base and travel as needed. Interviews are scheduled across 9–13 November in Louth, Grantham, Horncastle, Lincoln and Market Rasen.

The Role: Job Coach

Job Coaches work directly with learners — many of them young people or adults facing barriers to employment — helping them build skills, confidence and routes into work. At InspireU in Gainsborough you will be part of a careers team that is expanding to meet demand, working across sites in Lincolnshire and adapting your coaching to each learner’s needs.

The advert stresses the council’s values — collaborative, adaptable, eco-conscious, supportive and responsible — and asks for candidates who bring them to life. Under the Immigration Act’s Fluency Duty, the ability to converse at ease with customers and give advice in accurate spoken English is essential for the post.

Contract, Pay and Working Pattern

  • £28,153–£31,015 pro rata — part time, flexible working required
  • Hybrid (Smarter Working): nominated contractual work base, with travel across the area as part of the role
  • Local Government Pension Scheme access, Green Book terms, minimum 25 days leave plus bank holidays, salary-sacrifice and discount schemes
  • Interviews 9 November (Louth), 10 November (Grantham and Horncastle), 11 November (Lincoln), 13 November (Market Rasen) — express your location preference on the application form

Application Advice

The council is explicit: your personal statement must clearly state why you applied, how you meet the essential and desirable criteria in the job description, and what you bring to the role, the team and the wider council — and you should read the Job Information Pack first. Once submitted, an application is final and cannot be amended or reinstated, so proofread before you click submit. Unsuccessful candidates may be considered for a talent pool for up to three months for equal positions.

How to Apply

1
Read the job description and Information Pack. Open the official careers page via the Apply Link and study the essential and desirable criteria — the sift is built around them.
2
Confirm the sponsorship position. The council cannot offer Skilled Worker sponsorship for this post — check this against your own circumstances first.
3
Write a criteria-mapped personal statement. Address each essential criterion with a concrete example, state your location preference, and say what you bring to the team.
4
Submit before 1 November 2026. The closing date can be brought forward if applications surge — and a submitted application cannot be amended, so apply carefully and early.

The Bottom Line

Part-time flexibility, hybrid Smarter Working, Local Government pension terms and a genuinely people-focused coaching role — the Lincolnshire Job Coach post is a strong option for employability and careers professionals in the county. The hard limits: no visa sponsorship, and a closing date that can move forward. Apply before 1 November 2026.

Deadline reminder: The Job Coach advert closes on 1 November 2026, with interviews 9–13 November. The council can bring the closing date forward — apply early.

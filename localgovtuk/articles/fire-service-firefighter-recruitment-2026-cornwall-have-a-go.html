Fire Service Firefighter Recruitment 2026: Cornwall Have-a-Go Sessions & Wholetime Intakes

Fancy a career in the fire service? Cornwall is running Have-a-Go sessions this October, wholetime intakes open from January 2027, and several services are mid-recruitment right now. Here are the dates, the fitness standards and how to apply.

Published 14 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

UK fire and rescue services recruit in campaigns, not continuously — miss a window and you can wait a year for the next. This guide maps the live autumn 2026 timelines, eligibility rules and physical tests.

This October: Cornwall's Have-a-Go sessions run at Tolvaddon (Wed 14 Oct, 6–8pm), Bodmin (Sat 17 Oct, 10am–12pm & 2–4pm), Falmouth (Thu 29 Oct, 6–8pm) and St Austell (Sat 31 Oct, 10am–12pm & 2–4pm). Try the equipment and meet firefighters — on-call applications open 23 November, wholetime in January 2027.

Service-by-Service Timelines — Autumn 2026

  • Cornwall Fire and Rescue Service: Have-a-Go sessions throughout October 2026; on-call firefighter applications open 23 November; wholetime firefighter applications open January 2027. Open to anyone 18+.
  • Hereford & Worcester Fire and Rescue Service: wholetime application form opens 14 December 2026 and closes 6 January 2027; online assessments 8–10 February 2027; job-related tests w/c 1 March 2027; interviews w/c 22 March 2027; offers May 2027; induction at the Fire Service College in August 2027.
  • County Durham & Darlington FRS: registration and eligibility screening runs 21 September – 18 October 2026 (closing this week); ability tests 19 October – 1 November; role-related physical tests 16–20 November.
  • Avon Fire & Rescue Service: registration closed 16 August; fitness and strength tests are happening 9, 13 & 14 October 2026; practical physical tests 19–23 October; interviews 9–13 November; job offers 25 January 2027; training starts March/April 2027.
  • Tyne & Wear FRS: firefighter recruitment is closed for 2026 — future opportunities will be announced during 2027.

Check your local service's recruitment page now and register for alerts — windows are short.

Eligibility: Can You Become a Firefighter?

Requirements are consistent across services and refreshingly straightforward:

  • Age 18 or over and the right to work in the UK.
  • A standard DBS (criminal records) check.
  • Fitness: 1.5-mile run in 12 minutes or less, bleep test to level 8.8, 25kg shoulder press; confident 25m swim desirable.
  • Role-related tests: ladder climb, casualty evacuation, ladder lift, enclosed-space agility, equipment assembly and equipment carry.

No specific qualifications are required — services recruit on potential, values and fitness, and actively encourage applications from under-represented groups.

How to Apply to the Fire Service

Pick your service and route. Wholetime (full-time) is fiercely competitive; on-call/retained firefighters respond from home or work via alerter and can be a faster route in — Cornwall opens on-call applications 23 November.
Attend a taster session. Cornwall's October Have-a-Go events let you try equipment and speak to serving firefighters before committing — book via the Cornwall FRS website.
Start fitness training now. The run and bleep-test standards take weeks of preparation — train before the window opens.
Complete the online application. Windows are short (Hereford & Worcester's is just three weeks) — have your employment history and examples ready in advance.
Pass each stage in order. Online assessments → job-related physical tests → interview → fitness test and medical → pre-employment checks → offer. You must pass each stage to progress.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do firefighter applications open in 2026/27?

Cornwall: on-call apps from 23 Nov 2026, wholetime from Jan 2027; Hereford & Worcester: 14 Dec 2026 – 6 Jan 2027. Check your local service.

What fitness tests do firefighters take?

1.5-mile run in ≤12 minutes, bleep test to 8.8, 25kg shoulder press, plus ladder climb, casualty evacuation and enclosed-space tests.

Do I need qualifications to become a firefighter?

No specific qualifications are required. Services recruit on potential, values, fitness and the right to work in the UK, with a DBS check. You must be 18 or over.

What are Cornwall's Have-a-Go sessions?

October 2026 taster events (Tolvaddon 14th, Bodmin 17th, Falmouth 29th, St Austell 31st) — try equipment and meet firefighters before applying.

What is the difference between wholetime and on-call firefighters?

Wholetime firefighters are full-time and station-based; on-call firefighters have other jobs and respond via alerter. On-call recruitment is often more frequent.

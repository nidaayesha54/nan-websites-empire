Council Social Worker Jobs October 2026: Salaries, Locum Rates & Where Councils Are Hiring

Councils are hiring social workers urgently this October — permanent salaries to £46,385 at Wiltshire Council and London locum rates near £40 an hour. Here is where the jobs are, what you need, and how to apply.

Published 14 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

Social work is one of the hardest roles for councils to fill — and October 2026 vacancies show it. Councils across England are advertising permanent children's and adults' social worker posts with market supplements, while London boroughs are paying locum rates of £35 to £40 an hour to plug gaps. For qualified, Social Work England–registered practitioners, this is a seller's market.

This guide covers the verified October 2026 vacancies, the permanent-vs-locum decision, and exactly what you need to apply.

Verified vacancy: Wiltshire Council is recruiting a Level 3 Social Worker for its Continuing Healthcare team in Trowbridge at £44,910–£46,385 per year (including a 7.5% market supplement), 37 hours a week — with interviews scheduled for 29 October and 4 November 2026.

Where Councils Are Hiring — October 2026

  • Wiltshire Council — Level 3 Social Worker, Continuing Healthcare team, Trowbridge; £44,910–£46,385 (incl. 7.5% market supplement); 37 hrs; interviews 29 Oct & 4 Nov 2026.
  • Lambeth Council (London) — locum Children's Social Worker (CAT team) at £39.04/hr umbrella; adult social care/hospital roles at £39.87/hr umbrella.
  • Southwark (London) — locum Children's Social Worker, Assessment & Intervention team, £37.52/hr umbrella.
  • Barnet (London) — locum Looked After Children social worker, £37.54/hr umbrella.
  • Sheffield / Barnsley (South Yorkshire) — locum MASH (Multi-Agency Safeguarding Hub) social workers at £35–£37/hr.
  • Wakefield Council — Senior Social Worker, Children's Locality team, £35–£37/hr contract.
  • BCP Council (Bournemouth) — locum Child Protection social workers at up to £43/hr.

The pattern is clear: children's services dominate demand — MASH teams, assessment and intervention, looked-after children and child protection — with adult social care and hospital social work also hiring strongly in London.

Permanent vs Locum: The Maths

A permanent Level 3 post (£44,910–£46,385) brings the council package: pension, sick pay, leave and progression. A London locum at £39/hr earns roughly £75,000 a year equivalent before tax — but with no sick pay, pension or security, usually via an umbrella company.

What You Need to Apply

Social Work England registration. Non-negotiable — every advert requires current SWE registration. Only SWE-registered social workers with UK right to work are considered.
Relevant post-qualifying experience. Children's roles typically want recent experience in the specific team type — MASH, assessment, child protection or court work. Match your CV to the advert's specialism.
Apply direct for permanent posts. Council vacancies (like Wiltshire's) are applied for on the council's own jobs site — tailor your supporting statement to the person specification.
Register with agencies for locum work. Specialist social-work agencies hold the locum market — one registration can put you in front of multiple boroughs.
Prepare for panel interview. Wiltshire is interviewing 29 October and 4 November — expect scenario questions on safeguarding, the Care Act / Children Act, and multi-agency working.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which councils are hiring social workers in October 2026?

Wiltshire Council is recruiting a permanent Level 3 Social Worker (£44,910–£46,385); London boroughs including Lambeth, Southwark and Barnet are hiring locums at £37–£40/hr; Sheffield, Barnsley, Wakefield and BCP Council also have live vacancies.

What is the salary for a council social worker in 2026?

Permanent Level 3 posts advertise £44,910–£46,385 (Wiltshire, incl. market supplement); London locum rates run £35–£40/hr umbrella.

Do I need Social Work England registration?

Yes — every council and agency advert requires current Social Work England registration plus UK right to work. Unregistered applicants are not considered.

What is a MASH social worker?

MASH stands for Multi-Agency Safeguarding Hub — the team where police, health and children's services share information on safeguarding referrals. MASH experience is in high demand in October 2026 vacancies.

Is locum or permanent social work better?

Locum pays more per hour but offers no sick pay, pension or security; permanent posts bring the full council package and progression.

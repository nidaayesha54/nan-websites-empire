Surrey Council Reorganisation 2027: Two New Unitary Councils from April 2027 — Jobs & What It Means

Surrey County Council is undergoing local government reorganisation — moving from a two-tier system to two new unitary councils in April 2027. What the shake-up means for council jobs, recruitment and applicants.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » UK Local Government Jobs » Articles

Surrey is heading for the biggest shake-up of its local government in a generation. The county is moving from a two-tier system to two new unitary councils in April 2027 under the government’s Local Government Reorganisation (LGR) programme. For jobseekers, that means one thing above all: reorganisation creates recruitment — new structures need new teams, and the transition period itself generates roles across programme management, service transformation and frontline delivery.

Surrey is not alone — LGR is reshaping councils across England — but its April 2027 go-live date makes it one of the most consequential reorganisations on the current horizon for council careers.

What Reorganisation Means for Council Jobs

  • Transition roles now: programme managers, transformation leads and service-integration specialists are needed to deliver the change itself — council job boards already advertise roles referencing LGR and service transformation.
  • New structures from 2027: two unitary councils mean duplicated senior and specialist functions — from directors to service heads to frontline teams — creating fresh vacancies as the new organisations staff up.
  • Continuity roles: most frontline services (social care, highways, waste, libraries) continue through reorganisation — councils still recruit for these throughout the transition.
  • TUPE protections: existing council staff transferring to the new unitaries are generally protected under TUPE regulations — reorganisation is not a redundancy programme for most staff.

Roles in Demand During Reorganisation

Based on how LGR programmes typically recruit, the roles most associated with the transition include:

  • Programme and project managers (service transformation, integration)
  • HR, OD and change specialists (workforce transition)
  • Finance and legal professionals (new council setup, contracts)
  • Digital, data and customer-service roles (merged systems and service design)
  • Frontline social care, housing and highways staff (services that must keep running)

How to Position Yourself for Surrey Council Jobs

Whether you already work in local government or want in, follow these steps:

1
Monitor Surrey County Council’s jobs portal and the district/borough council sites for LGR, transformation and programme roles.
2
Search for “local government reorganisation”, “transformation” and “unitary” keywords on council job boards.
3
Evidence change-management, service-integration or multi-agency experience — these are the skills reorganisation programmes buy.
4
Register for job alerts on the relevant council portals so new unitary-council vacancies reach you first in 2027.

The Bottom Line

Reorganisations are disruptive — but for jobseekers they are also one of the most reliable generators of council vacancies in local government. Surrey’s move to two unitary councils by April 2027 will create demand for transformation professionals now and for full council workforces next year. If you want a local-authority career in the South East, start watching Surrey’s job boards now: the transition hiring has already begun, and the main wave is still to come.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is happening to Surrey councils in 2027?

Surrey is moving from a two-tier council system to two new unitary councils in April 2027 under the Local Government Reorganisation programme.

Will council staff lose their jobs in the reorganisation?

Existing staff transferring to the new unitary councils are generally protected under TUPE regulations — reorganisation is primarily a structural change, not a redundancy programme.

What jobs does local government reorganisation create?

Transition roles (programme managers, transformation leads, HR/change specialists, finance/legal) during the change, plus full staffing of the new unitary councils' structures from 2027.

When do the new Surrey unitary councils start?

April 2027, with transition planning under way through 2026-27.

How can I find Surrey council jobs related to the reorganisation?

Monitor Surrey County Council's jobs portal and district/borough sites, searching for ‘local government reorganisation’, ‘transformation’ and ‘unitary’ keywords, and set up job alerts.

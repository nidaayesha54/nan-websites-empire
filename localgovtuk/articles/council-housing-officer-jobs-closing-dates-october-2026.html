Council housing teams are recruiting this October — housing officers managing tenancies and estates, housing support officers helping homeless households resettle, and housing assistants keeping locality offices running. Live adverts carry closing dates of 27 October and 1 November 2026 — a time-sensitive window for local-authority careers.

Housing is one of the most resilient areas of council recruitment: demand for tenancy management, homelessness prevention and estate services does not disappear in a downturn, and the roles offer the local government pension, structured progression and the satisfaction of visible community impact.

What Each Role Involves

Housing Officer

The frontline tenancy role: estate inspections, safe and attractive neighbourhoods, and customer service and continuous improvement. Midlothian wants a visible, approachable team member with excellent communication — and welcomes transferable skills from customer service, social care, community work or education, not just housing experience.

Housing Support Officer

Supporting people experiencing homelessness or at risk of it: tenancy sustainment, rent-arrears advice, resettlement help and signposting to health and crisis services. The Perth & Kinross post is fixed term to 30 October 2027 at £33,261–£36,508.

Housing Assistant

The entry point: clerical duties (word processing, filing, IT systems) plus direct tenant advice in a busy locality housing office, supporting housing officers. South Lanarkshire’s part-time post (28 hours) pays £20,146–£22,687 and closes 27 October 2026 — note that CVs are disregarded in their process, so the application form itself must carry your case.

Who Can Apply — Eligibility

  • Experience: previous housing experience is described as advantageous but not essential in several adverts — transferable skills are explicitly welcomed.
  • Skills: customer care, communication, organisation, workload prioritisation and IT (notably Word and Excel for assistant roles).
  • Checks: enhanced DBS/PVG checks, right-to-work verification, references and medical fitness are standard pre-employment requirements.
  • Application format: some councils (e.g. South Lanarkshire) disregard CVs — read the advert’s application instructions carefully.

How to Apply for Council Housing Jobs

Council jobs are advertised on regional portals. Follow these steps:

1
Search the council’s official jobs portal (e.g. myjobscotland.gov.uk for Scottish councils) for “housing” roles.
2
Read the full advert and job profile, noting whether CVs are accepted.
3
Evidence the person specification with concrete examples of transferable experience.
4
Submit before the stated closing time — 27 October and 1 November 2026 for the highlighted adverts.

Gloucester City Council Jobs October 2026: Housing Team Leader £43,149–£47,665 — Apply by 8 November 2026

Gloucester City Council is recruiting this month — from a Housing Team Leader delivering statutory homelessness services to a Cemeteries & Crematorium Supervisor and Guildhall hospitality roles. Full salary, hours and closing-date details.

Home » UK Local Government Jobs » Articles

Gloucester City Council has five live vacancies on its jobs board in October 2026, covering housing, bereavement services and hospitality — led by a permanent Housing Team Leader in Housing Services at £43,149–£47,665 per annum (closes 8 November 2026). Also advertised: a Cemeteries & Crematorium Supervisor, a Guildhall Café/Bar Shift Leader and casual bar staff.

Recruitment is administered by Gloucestershire County Council on behalf of the city council, so you apply through the shared jobs portal rather than to Gloucester City Council directly.

The Roles: Salaries, Hours and Closing Dates

Housing Team Leader — £43,149–£47,665 per annum

The flagship vacancy is a permanent, full-time Housing Team Leader in Housing Services (37 hours per week). The role leads the team delivering the council’s statutory homelessness duties under Part 7 of the Housing Act — the legal framework covering what councils must do for people who are homeless or threatened with homelessness. Expect responsibility for casework oversight, decision quality, team performance and partnership working with landlords and support services. Candidates will typically need strong homelessness or housing-options experience and a solid grasp of Part 7 legislation. Applications close on 8 November 2026.

Cemeteries & Crematorium Supervisor — £28,608–£30,515 per annum

A permanent, full-time Cemeteries & Crematorium Supervisor pays £28,608–£30,515 per annum (37 hours per week). Bereavement-services roles combine operational supervision of cemeteries and the crematorium with sensitive, dignified service for bereaved families — funeral-services or grounds-management experience is usually valued. Closes 25 October 2026, so this needs a quick turnaround.

Guildhall Café/Bar Shift Leader — £26,016–£26,427 per annum

The Guildhall — Gloucester’s city-centre entertainment venue — is hiring a Café/Bar Shift Leader at £26,016–£26,427 per annum (37 hours per week). Shift leaders run bar and café operations during live events: front-of-house staff, cash handling, licensing compliance and customer service. Hospitality or licensed-trade experience is the natural route in. Applications close on 25 October 2026.

Guildhall Casual Bar Staff — £13.28 per hour

The Guildhall is also taking on casual bar staff at £13.28 per hour — flexible shifts around events and gigs, and a proven route into permanent council hospitality roles. Closes 1 November 2026.

How to Apply for Gloucester City Council Jobs

Because recruitment is handled through the shared portal, the application process follows these steps:

1
Go to careers.gloucester.gov.uk and search current Gloucester City Council vacancies.
2
Open the advert and read the job description and person specification in full.
3
Tailor your application to the essential and desirable criteria — evidence Part 7 Housing Act knowledge and team-leadership experience with concrete examples.
4
Submit well before the closing date: 25 October 2026 (cemeteries supervisor, café/bar shift leader), 1 November 2026 (casual bar staff) or 8 November 2026 (Housing Team Leader).
5
Watch your email for shortlisting and interview invites.

The Bottom Line

Five vacancies across three service areas, paying from £13.28 an hour to £47,700 a year. The Housing Team Leader is the standout for housing professionals, but the two 25 October closing dates need your attention first — check the official adverts and apply early.

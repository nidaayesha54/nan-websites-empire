Bradford Council HGV Driver — Apply by 15 Nov

Bradford Council is hiring HGV Class 2 waste driver collectors — £29,071–£32,046, four-day week — closing 15 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » UK Local Government Jobs » Articles

Bradford Council (City of Bradford Metropolitan District Council) is recruiting HGV (Class 2) Waste Driver Collectors for its waste collection services, including the new food waste collection service. The posts pay £29,071 to £32,046 with potential overtime, offer a four-day working week (Tuesday to Friday), and close on 15 November 2026.

And there is an immediate way in: the council is holding Driver Recruitment Open Days this weekend — Saturday 24 and Sunday 25 October, 9am to 12 noon at Stocksbridge Depot, Royd Ings Avenue, Keighley BD21 4BZ. No appointment is necessary — just turn up, meet the drivers and crews, see the collection vehicles, and talk to managers about vacancies, pay, training and career opportunities.

The Role: Waste Driver Collector

Waste driver collectors drive HGV collection vehicles on household, recycling and garden waste rounds across the district, working as part of a collection crew. The work is physical and outdoors in all weathers, with real responsibility: you are responsible for a vehicle and equipment asset worth up to £250,000, for the safety of your crew and the public, and for the reliability of a frontline service residents depend on every week.

The new food waste collection service is a major expansion — Bradford is building its waste workforce now, which means genuine opportunities for new joiners to establish themselves in a growing service.

Pay, Hours and Benefits

  • £29,071–£32,046 with potential overtime opportunities
  • Four-day working week (Tuesday to Friday) — regular, predictable working hours
  • Local Government Pension Scheme and generous annual leave entitlement
  • Supportive teams and great colleagues — the council’s own pitch for the role

Requirements

  • Category C (Class 2) HGV licence — the minimum licence requirement
  • Awareness of vehicle maintenance and operator requirements, daily vehicle checks, and basic maintenance (refuelling, cleaning, oils, water, grease points)
  • Competence with ancillary systems: camera systems, in-cab devices, weighing systems, bin lift and compaction equipment
  • Fire safety awareness — including establishing a 3-metre safety zone and training new crew members in vehicle fire evacuations

£29k–£32k, a four-day week, a local government pension, and a service that is growing with the new food waste collections — Bradford Council’s HGV waste driver posts are a strong option for Class 2 drivers in West Yorkshire.

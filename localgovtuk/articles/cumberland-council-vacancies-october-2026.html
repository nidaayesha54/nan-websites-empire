What the Roles Involve

Council catering and domestic roles sit inside the services that keep schools, care settings and council facilities running day to day:

  • Cook (CMB2714e — £21,784 pro rata): the higher-hours cook post — typically preparing meals to nutritional standards in a school or care kitchen, managing food hygiene, allergens and stock. The salary suggests a substantial part-time or near-full-time contract.
  • Cook (CMB2715e — £11,428 pro rata): the lower-hours cook post — likely fewer days or shorter shifts, suiting someone looking for part-time council work around other commitments.
  • Relief Domestic (CMB2712e — £13.70/hr): flexible relief cleaning and domestic duties across council sites — ideal for workers who want variable hours rather than a fixed rota.

All three are Allerdale-based in North West Cumbria — the former Allerdale district now part of Cumberland Council following local government reorganisation.

Why Council Catering and Domestic Roles Are Worth a Look

  • Simple applications: “easily apply by CV” — no 1,500-word supporting statements, no Civil Service behaviours framework. Your CV does the talking.
  • Local government terms: council employment typically brings the Local Government Pension Scheme, structured pay and family-friendly policies even on part-time contracts.
  • Part-time friendly: two of the three posts are explicitly part-time or relief — genuinely flexible public-sector work.
  • Stable demand: school and care catering is perennial — these roles recur, and relief workers who prove reliable are first in line for permanent posts.

How to Apply

1
Go to Cumberland Council’s council job vacancies page (cumberland.gov.uk) and find the vacancy references CMB2714e, CMB2715e and CMB2712e.
2
Check the contracted hours behind each pro-rata salary — the headline figure only makes sense with the hours attached.
3

4

The Bottom Line

Three live Cumberland vacancies, simple CV applications, part-time and relief options, and local-government terms — if you’re in North West Cumbria and want steady council work, the 2 November deadline on the main Cook post is the one to beat. Check the hours, polish the CV, apply.

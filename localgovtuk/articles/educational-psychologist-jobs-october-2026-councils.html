Educational Psychologist Jobs October 2026: Council Vacancies

Liverpool and Barnsley councils are recruiting educational psychologists — closing dates, HCPC requirements and how to apply.

Home › Articles › Educational Psychologist Jobs October 2026: Council Vacancies

Published 15 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

Educational psychologists are in high demand across local government. A chronic national shortage of educational psychologists means councils are competing to recruit them — and this October 2026, Liverpool City Council and Barnsley Council both have educational psychologist vacancies live. Barnsley's closing date is 16/10/2026; Liverpool's advert is listed for 16 October 2026.

This guide covers both vacancies and how to build a strong application in time.

Liverpool City Council: Flexible, Family-Friendly EP Roles

Liverpool City Council (jobs.liverpool.gov.uk) is recruiting Educational Psychologists, welcoming qualified EPs, Assistant EPs and Trainee EPs due to qualify in 2026 or 2027. It offers flexible, family-friendly working — hours manageable between 6:00 and 22:00, additional leave accrual, agile working kits for remote work, supportive peer and senior supervision, and involvement in strategic city-wide initiatives including social inclusion, early years, the virtual school, electively home educated children, parent forums and underrepresented groups.

Barnsley Council: Vacancy Closing 16/10/2026

Barnsley Council (barnsley.gov.uk/jobs) has an Educational Psychologist vacancy closing 16/10/2026. Candidates must be professionally qualified Educational Psychologists registered with the Health and Care Professions Council (HCPC); an enhanced DBS check is required.

Note that Barnsley does not accept CVs — applications must use the official form, and you must demonstrate against the job profile. For an informal discussion, contact Dr Naomi Robinson or Jo Patterson (Principal Educational Psychologists).

How to Apply for Council EP Jobs

Check your HCPC registration. A professional EP qualification and HCPC registration are non-negotiable.
Read the job profile. Applications are scored against the advert — evidence early years, inclusion and multi-agency experience.
Use the official application form. Barnsley does not accept CVs — evidence every requirement.
Prepare references and DBS documents. Enhanced DBS checks are standard — gather documents now to avoid delays.
Request an informal chat. Barnsley names Dr Naomi Robinson and Jo Patterson — a quick conversation sharpens your application.
Submit before the closing date. With a 16/10/2026 deadline, apply as soon as you're ready — not on the final day.

Why Councils Are Competing for EPs

With a chronic national shortage, councils are competing with flexible working and supervision offers. HCPC-registered EPs — and trainees qualifying in 2026 or 2027 — should apply now, and verify salary and closing dates on the live advert.

Frequently Asked Questions

What qualifications do I need to be an educational psychologist?

A professional educational psychology qualification and registration with the HCPC.

When do the October 2026 council applications close?

Barnsley's closing date is 16/10/2026; Liverpool's advert is listed for 16 October 2026 — check the live advert.

Can trainee educational psychologists apply?

Liverpool welcomes applications from trainees due to qualify in 2026 or 2027.

What working pattern do council EP roles offer?

Flexible and agile working — Liverpool offers hours between 6:00 and 22:00 with remote-working kits.

Do councils accept CVs for EP roles?

Not always — Barnsley requires the application form only and does not accept CVs.

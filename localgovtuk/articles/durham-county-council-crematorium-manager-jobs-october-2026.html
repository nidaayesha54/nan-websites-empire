What the Role Involves

The Crematorium and Site Manager leads the day-to-day running of Durham Crematorium and its grounds — one of the most sensitive public services a council provides. The role blends operational leadership with compassion, supporting bereaved families at a difficult time.

  • Running services: overseeing cremation services, chapels and grounds so every service runs smoothly and with dignity.
  • Leading staff: managing, rostering and supporting the crematorium team day to day.
  • Regulations: working within cremation law, environmental requirements and health & safety standards.
  • Families and funeral directors: providing a professional, respectful service to bereaved families and funeral professionals.
  • Site standards: keeping buildings, grounds and memorial areas in excellent condition.

Who Should Apply

Expect the council to look for proven management and facilities experience — ideally from bereavement, funeral services, or another demanding public-facing environment. Leadership of a small team, budget awareness and calm professionalism matter most. Check the full person specification on the advert, as the panel will shortlist strictly against it.

Contract, Hours and Salary

The post is permanent and full-time at 37 hours per week, paid £33,673–£37,563 per annum — a solid North East management salary with council stability and the Local Government Pension Scheme. It is based at Durham Crematorium itself.

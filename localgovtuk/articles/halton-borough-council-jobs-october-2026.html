Halton Borough Council Jobs October 2026: SEND, Social Work Roles

Halton Borough Council in Cheshire is hiring — SEND inclusion admin, social work academy roles and more, with 34 days' leave and an LGPS pension.

Home › Articles › Halton Borough Council Jobs October 2026: SEND, Social Work Roles

Published 15 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

Halton Borough Council in Cheshire is recruiting this October 2026, with all vacancies advertised on careers.halton.gov.uk. Standout roles include an Administration Officer for a new SEND inclusion service and an Advanced Practitioner post in the Social Work Academy.

Halton's benefits package is strong: annual holiday starting at 34 days per year including bank holidays (rising with long service), a defined-benefit Local Government Pension Scheme, 3x salary life cover, free car parking at council sites, and an employee benefits platform (discounted shopping, car leasing, gym memberships, wellbeing hub and EAP).

Administration Officer — Experts at Hand Team

Listed 18 October 2026, this Administration Officer role supports the Experts at Hand Team — a new, ambitious service run with NHS partners, strengthening inclusion for children and young people with SEND in mainstream settings. Duties include arranging meetings and visits, maintaining records and databases, managing communications and preparing documentation.

Advanced Practitioner — Social Work Academy

Listed 11 October 2026 at 37 hours, the Advanced Practitioner post sits in Children's Social Care's Social Work Academy, which provides coaching, support and professional development for social workers and wider Children's Social Care and Early Help colleagues — from ASYE newly qualified staff through to experienced practitioners and managers.

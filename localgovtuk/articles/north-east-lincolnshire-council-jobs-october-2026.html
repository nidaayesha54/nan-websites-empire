Council jobseekers in North East Lincolnshire have a fresh bulletin to work through: North East Lincolnshire Council (NELC) published “This week’s essential new jobs” on 15 October 2026, listing new vacancies across public health, highways, road safety, children’s social care and residential care — plus last-chance closing-soon roles and a voluntary board position. Applications go through nelincs.gov.uk/jobs, with new vacancies also shared on the council’s LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram.

What Stands Out in This Week’s Bulletin

  • Highways hiring in depth: a Strategic Lead for Highway Maintenance and Operations plus a Senior Traffic and Road Safety Technician and a Road Safety Engineer — three rungs of the highways ladder in one bulletin, suggesting the service is building capacity.
  • Public health leadership: a Programme Manager for Domestic Abuse is a senior, specialist public-health commissioning role — exactly the kind of post that rarely appears and attracts national applicants.
  • Children’s social care: a Social Worker in Children’s Assessment and Safeguarding plus a Senior Care Officer in residential care — children’s services remain the hardest area for councils to staff, so these are genuine priority vacancies.
  • An entry route: the Civilian Work Attachment offers a way in for people building experience — worth a look if you’re early-career.

Closing Soon — Last Chance

The bulletin also flags last-chance vacancies nearing their deadlines: an Engineer Operations Supervisor and a Finance Officer. “Closing soon” on a 15 October bulletin means these could shut any day — if either fits your profile, treat them as apply-immediately roles. There is also a voluntary role: Independent Chair of the Immingham, Habrough and Stallingborough Neighbourhood Board — a community-governance position that builds exactly the local-leadership experience councils value.

How to Apply

1
Go to nelincs.gov.uk/jobs and search the role titles from the 15 October bulletin.
2
Read each advert’s closing date, salary and person specification — council adverts close on their own schedules.
3
Follow the council on LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram, where NELC shares its latest vacancies each week.
4
For the closing-soon roles (Engineer Operations Supervisor, Finance Officer), apply now — don’t wait for the weekend.

The Bottom Line

Seven new roles spanning public health, highways, road safety, social work and care, plus two closing-soon posts — North East Lincolnshire’s 15 October bulletin is a strong weekly haul for a single council. Check nelincs.gov.uk/jobs today, and follow the council’s social channels so next week’s bulletin reaches you first.

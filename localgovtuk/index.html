How to Apply

Five steps to landing a council job.

Find vacancies on council websites
Every council publishes jobs on its own careers page. Aggregators like LG Jobs and GOV.UK's Find a job service list roles from many councils in one place.
Read the person specification
Council recruitment is competency-based. The person specification lists essential and desirable criteria — your application is scored directly against these, so address each one.
Complete the application form
Most councils use standardised online forms rather than CVs. Give specific examples for each competency, keep answers concise, and check professional registration requirements (e.g. Social Work England).
Attend interview
Expect a panel interview built around the competencies in the person specification, sometimes with a presentation, written exercise or — for social work — a case scenario.
Pass pre-employment checks
Offers are conditional on right-to-work checks, references and, for regulated roles, an enhanced DBS check. Keep your documents and employment history ready.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find council jobs near me?

Each council advertises vacancies on its own website's jobs or careers page. Aggregators such as LG Jobs list roles from many councils, and GOV.UK's Find a job service also carries local government vacancies.

What qualifications do I need for a council social worker role?

Qualified social worker posts require a degree in social work (or equivalent) and registration with Social Work England. Newly qualified roles often include an Assessed and Supported Year in Employment (ASYE) programme.

Do council jobs require DBS checks?

Roles working with children, vulnerable adults or in regulated activity require an enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service (DBS) check. All appointments also include right-to-work checks and references.

What is the Local Government Pension Scheme?

The LGPS is a defined benefit pension scheme for council employees, with employer contributions typically around 15-20% of pay. Membership is automatic on starting, with an opt-out option.

Are council jobs term-time only?

Many school-based roles such as teaching assistants are term-time only, with pay pro-rated for the weeks worked. Most corporate council roles are full-year positions.

Can I work for a council remotely?

Many councils offer hybrid working for office-based roles. Frontline roles in social care, environmental health, highways and schools generally require on-site presence.

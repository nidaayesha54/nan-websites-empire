GTA 6 Characters: Every Name Rockstar Has Revealed

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Who is in GTA 6? It sounds like a simple question, and the answer is smaller than the internet makes it look. Rockstar has officially profiled eight characters on its GTA VI site — nine people, because one of them is a duo — and everything else you have read is either a mention, a rumour or a leak. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and this guide lists every name Rockstar has actually revealed, who each person is, and which names you should not trust yet.

That matters because the character lists online are full of names Rockstar never confirmed. Leaked names, trailer nicknames and voice-actor theories have been circulating for years, and most of them are not in the game. This guide sticks to what Rockstar has published — the character profiles, the edition descriptions and the store text — and labels everything else for what it is.

The Two Leads: Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos

The story belongs to two people. Jason Duval is an Army veteran working for drug runners in the Keys, and Rockstar's tagline for him is that "Jason wants an easy life, but things just keep getting harder." He grew up around grifters and crooks, tried the Army to shake off his troubled teens, and landed back where he started — doing what he knows best. Lucia Caminos is fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary, and her tagline is that "Lucia's father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk." She has a plan, and meeting Jason is either the best or the worst thing that ever happened to him.

The store description calls it their story: an easy score goes wrong, and the two of them are pulled into a criminal conspiracy across the state of Leonida. You play both of them, switching freely after the "Breakfast Run" mission in Chapter 2, and the story is told from both sides — the same events look different through Jason's eyes and Lucia's. It is a tale about escape rather than empire-building, and it stands on its own: you do not need to have played GTA 5 to follow it.

The Supporting Cast: Six Profiles, Eight People

Beyond the leads, Rockstar has profiled six more characters — eight people in total, because one profile is a duo. Cal Hampton is Jason's friend, a paranoid stay-at-home type whose tagline asks "what if everything on the internet was true?" — he listens in on Coast Guard radio at home and shares a contact named Brian with Jason. Boobie Ike is a Vice City street legend turned businessman, and his tagline is "it's all about heart — the Jack of Hearts": real estate, a strip club and a recording studio, built out of his time in the streets. Dre'Quan Priest is the producer at the heart of that studio — more hustler than gangster, Rockstar says, who dealt on the street only as a means to an end, with music always the real goal — and he runs Only Raw Records with Boobie.

Real Dimez is the rap duo signed to Only Raw Records: Bae-Luxe and Roxy, two people under one profile. Raul Bautista is a bank robber, the crew's professional. And Brian Heder is the smuggler who employs Jason in the Keys — a classic smuggler of the old school, whose wife Lori is also named in Rockstar's official material. The profiles were published with Trailer 2 on May 6, 2025, and they have not changed since: checked ten days before launch, Rockstar's site still listed the same eight profiles, and the launch trailer and launch gallery added no new names.

Also Named — but Not Profiled

A few more names appear in Rockstar's official material without getting a full profile. Lori is Brian Heder's wife. Wyman is a car collector and fixer. DWNPLY is a rapper. And Macca the Gator is a TV mascot — a character inside the game's television, not a person you meet on the street. The Extended Look, Rockstar's roughly 27-minute gameplay video from August 27, introduced more characters as well, but Rockstar has not added them to its character pages — so those names come from viewers' recaps of the video rather than an official list, and this guide does not present them as confirmed.

One more thing Rockstar has not announced: a single voice actor, for any character. Any casting names you have seen attached to Jason, Lucia or anyone else come from fan research, not from Rockstar, and should be treated as speculation until the studio says otherwise. One performer, Stephen Root, is widely identified as Brian Heder — but that identification is reported, not announced, and Rockstar's own pages credit no actor at all.

The Names to Ignore

The rumoured names are the longest list of all, and none of them is confirmed. Billy, Kai, Chester, Dale, Danny, Donnie, Iris, Sam, Shanese, Tit, Vicky, YJ and Zach all come from reported leaks, fan databases or trailer analysis — Rockstar has not confirmed any of them as final story characters. Some of them come from the 2022 leak, which predates the finished game by years; others are guesses built from a face in a trailer or a name on a poster. They may appear, they may not, and a guide that lists them as the cast is guessing.

The honest count, then, is nine people across eight profiles, plus four more names mentioned in passing. That is the full cast Rockstar has revealed — and the full cast is almost certainly much bigger, because a 50-to-60-hour story with 91 missions needs far more people than nine. The rest are waiting in the game itself, and the only way to meet them is to play it.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, the two playable leads. Jason is an ex-Army veteran working for drug runners in the Keys; Lucia is fresh out of Leonida Penitentiary. You switch between them freely after the "Breakfast Run" mission in Chapter 2.

How many characters has Rockstar revealed?

Eight official profiles covering nine people: Jason, Lucia, Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez (Bae-Luxe and Roxy), Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. Lori, Wyman, DWNPLY and Macca the Gator are also named in official material without full profiles.

Who voices Jason and Lucia?

Rockstar has not announced any voice actors for GTA 6. Any casting names you see online are fan research, not confirmation — including Stephen Root, widely identified as Brian Heder but not announced by the studio.

The Bottom Line

The GTA 6 cast Rockstar has revealed is nine people: two leads, six supporting profiles, and a rap duo that counts as two. Everything else — the leaked names, the trailer guesses, the casting theories — is unconfirmed. Play the story, meet the rest in the game, and do not trust a character list that cannot show you its source.

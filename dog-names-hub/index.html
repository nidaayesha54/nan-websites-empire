What is the most popular dog name in 2026?

Bella and Luna top the charts for female dogs in the US, while Max and Charlie lead for males. Browse all 235+ trending names in our popular names browser.

What are some good unique dog names?

Unique favorites include Juniper, Atlas, Nova, Zephyr, Wren, and Cosmo. See 200+ unique dog names with meanings.

How do I choose the perfect dog name?

Pick a 1–2 syllable name that's easy to shout, avoid names that sound like commands, test it out loud, and match your dog's personality. Read our 9 pro tips.

Should dog names be one or two syllables?

Yes — trainers recommend 1–2 syllables because dogs respond fastest to short, punchy sounds. Max, Luna, Milo, and Bear are perfect examples.

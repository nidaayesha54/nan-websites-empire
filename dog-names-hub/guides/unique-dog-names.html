How to invent your own unique name

Can't find "the one"? Create it with these tricks. The best invented names follow the same rules trainers love: short, punchy, and easy to shout. A made-up name that passes the back-door test will serve you better than a "real" name that doesn't.

  1. Combine two favorites — Luna + Maple = "Luple"? Okay, maybe workshop that one. But mashups work!
  2. Translate a trait — your dog's personality in another language (Yuki for a white pup, for example).
  3. Steal from maps — towns, rivers, and mountains make gorgeous names: Aspen, Juno, Skye.
  4. Use the generator — set our dog name generator to "Unique" style and reshuffle until something clicks.
  5. Test the shout test — yell it across the house. If it feels good and your dog's ears perk up, it's a winner. More on this in how to choose the perfect dog name.

How It Works

  • Question detection: finds sentences ending in "?" plus statements starting with What, How, Why, Can, Is, and similar question words.
  • Answer pairing: the 1–2 sentences after each question become its answer.
  • Fully editable: every question and answer can be fixed up before export.
  • Bot-ready: use the exported pairs as rules in the chatbot builder.

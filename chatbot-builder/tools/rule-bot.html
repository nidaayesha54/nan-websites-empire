Tips for Great Rules

  • Add several keywords per rule (e.g. "price, cost, how much") so the bot catches different phrasings.
  • Keep replies short and helpful — one clear answer beats a paragraph.
  • Always set a friendly fallback reply that guides the visitor to rephrase.
  • Test with real questions your customers ask, not just perfect examples.

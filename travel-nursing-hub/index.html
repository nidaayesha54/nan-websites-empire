Frequently Asked Questions

How much do travel nurses make per week in 2026?

Most assignments pay between $1,800 and $3,500 per week, combining taxable hourly wages with tax-free housing and meal stipends. High-demand specialties like ICU, ER, OR and Labor & Delivery typically earn at the top of the range. Use our pay analyzer to decode any offer.

What is a travel nurse pay package?

It combines a taxable hourly rate with tax-free stipends for housing and meals/incidentals, plus bonuses for travel, completion, referral and extension. Our pay guide breaks down every component.

How long are travel nurse contracts?

Standard contracts run 13 weeks, though 4, 6, 8 and 26-week assignments exist. Extensions are common — see the become-a-travel-nurse guide for what to know before you sign.

Do travel nurses need a compact nursing license?

A Nurse Licensure Compact (NLC) multistate license lets you practice in all compact states. Nurses in non-compact states need endorsement in each assignment state, which agencies usually reimburse. More in our agency guide.

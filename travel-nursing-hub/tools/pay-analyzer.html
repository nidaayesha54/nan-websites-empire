How this analyzer works

Recruiters quote the biggest weekly number possible. This tool separates the package into taxable wages, tax-free stipends and bonuses, then computes your effective hourly rate — the single number that lets you compare travel offers with staff jobs or with each other.

Why the tax-free share matters

A dollar of tax-free stipend is worth more than a dollar of taxable wages. If roughly half your package is tax-free, your take-home can rival a staff job paying a much higher headline rate. Learn more in our 2026 travel nurse pay guide and the tax tips article.

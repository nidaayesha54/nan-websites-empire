Why compare total contract value?

Recruiters sell the weekly rate, but bonuses, contract length and stipend splits change the math. A $2,600/week offer with no bonuses can lose to a $2,500/week offer with a $2,000 sign-on bonus. Always compare the entire contract — weekly pay × weeks + all bonuses — before you choose.

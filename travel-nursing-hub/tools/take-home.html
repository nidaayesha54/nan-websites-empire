Why take-home beats the weekly rate

Two nurses can earn the same $2,600/week gross and take home very different paychecks. The nurse with $1,600 in tax-free stipends keeps far more than the nurse whose package is mostly taxable wages. This calculator shows the gap so you can negotiate the split, not just the total.

How the estimate works

We annualize your taxable wages, apply simplified 2026 federal brackets and the standard deduction, add your selected state rate, and include FICA at 7.65%. Tax-free stipends pass through untouched. Real paychecks vary with pre-tax deductions, local taxes and multi-state filing — treat this as a planning estimate.

GTA 6 Fast Travel: RydeMe, Taxis and Every Shortcut

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and its map is enormous. Leonida stretches from the beaches of Vice City to the swamps of the Grassrivers and the islands of the Keys, and driving from one end to the other takes real time — time you might rather spend on missions. The first thing most players ask is whether the game has fast travel, and the honest answer is that it has no teleport button on the map. What it does have is a transport system built into the world: rides you can book, skip, and pay for, which gets you across the state almost as fast as a teleport would.

This guide covers every way to move quickly in GTA 6: the RydeMe rideshare app, the Paradise Taxi service, and the buses and trains that run on fixed routes. You will learn when each one unlocks, what it costs, and the situations where each one beats the others — plus the places where no ride will take you at all.

RydeMe: the rideshare shortcut

RydeMe is GTA 6's version of a rideshare app, and it is the quickest way to cross Leonida. Open your phone with Up on the D-pad, pick the app, and choose your destination: your current safehouse, your map marker, or a new destination you pick on the map. The app shows the fare before you book, so you know the price before the car arrives. The car shows up as a white car icon on the minimap — get in, and the driver takes you there.

The trick is the skip. Once you are in the car, hold L2 on PlayStation or LT on Xbox to open the driver menu, then press Triangle or Y to skip the journey entirely. The skip costs $100 on top of the regular fare, and the fare itself rises with distance while the skip price stays flat — so the skip is the best value on long trips across the map. RydeMe unlocks during the BuckMe mission in Chapter 1, when you set up your BuckMe bank account, because the app takes payment from that account. If the app is not on your phone yet, keep playing the story and use a taxi in the meantime.

Paradise Taxi: available from the start

Paradise Taxi is the cab company you start with, and it is available right after the first mission, Gone Fishing — before RydeMe exists. Call it from your phone's Contacts, under All Contacts, and set a waypoint for where you want to go. The driver asks where you are headed, and you pick the destination on the map. Speeding up and skipping work the same way as with RydeMe: hold L2 or LT, then Triangle or Y to skip for $100.

There are two differences worth knowing. First, the fare only shows up once you are in the car, and taxis take it from the cash you are carrying — so you may need to stop at an ATM first. Second, if you do not want to pay the skip, you can pay the driver $5 to hurry up, and another $5 to go faster still, though the driver will start crashing through traffic. You can also just ride along and enjoy the view, or chat with the driver from the same menu.

Buses and trains: the slow option

Buses and trains run on fixed routes and schedules, so you have to go to a stop or a station and wait for one. There is no skip and no booking — you ride in real time, like a passenger. That makes them the slowest way to cross Leonida, but they cost little and they are always there, even before you unlock anything else.

Use them for short hops inside Vice City, or when you are broke and cannot afford the $100 skip. For a cross-map trip, a RydeMe skip will always beat them. The one thing buses and trains do well is atmosphere: they are a good way to see the city at street level while you are still learning it, and they never ask you to pay a fare from your bank account.

Where fast travel does not work

Your destination has to be reachable by road. Bookings to remote swamps or some islands get cancelled, and the same happens if you call a ride from somewhere a car cannot reach — walk or swim to a road first, then call again. The game will not teleport you out of a place a car cannot drive to, so plan your last stretch on foot.

Two more rules to remember. Do not attack your driver: doing so can get you temporarily suspended from RydeMe, which leaves you walking. And remember that skipping is a paid convenience, not a free one — if you are saving every dollar for a garage or a weapon, the $100 skip adds up fast. Spend it on the trips that save you the most time, and drive the short ones yourself.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a teleport in GTA 6?

No. There is no way to pick a spot on the map and appear there. The closest thing is booking a RydeMe or taxi ride and paying $100 to skip the journey once you are in the car.

How much does skipping a ride cost?

The skip costs a flat $100 on top of the regular fare, no matter how far you are going. The fare itself rises with distance, so the skip is best value on long trips.

When does RydeMe unlock?

RydeMe appears on your phone during the BuckMe mission in Chapter 1, when you set up your BuckMe bank account. Before that, use Paradise Taxi.

The bottom line: Leonida is too big to walk, and GTA 6 gives you rides instead of a teleport. Learn RydeMe first, keep a taxi in your contacts for the early game, and save the $100 skip for the trips that are actually long.

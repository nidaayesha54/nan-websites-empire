Crypto Profit Calculator: How to Find Your True Net Gain

"I bought at $60k and sold at $75k — that's 25% profit, right?" Not quite. Between exchange fees on both sides, spreads, and network costs, your real gain is always smaller than the headline number. Here's how to calculate crypto profit correctly — and the mistakes that quietly eat returns.

The correct profit formula

Net profit = (Sell price × coins × (1 − sell fee)) − (Buy price × coins × (1 + buy fee))

Our free crypto profit calculator runs this instantly and also shows your ROI % and breakeven sell price.

Worked example

You buy 0.5 BTC at $60,000 with a 0.5% buy fee, and sell at $75,000 with a 0.5% sell fee:

  • Cost: 0.5 × $60,000 = $30,000 (+ $150 buy fee = $30,150)
  • Revenue: 0.5 × $75,000 = $37,500 (− $187.50 sell fee = $37,312.50)
  • Net profit: $7,162.50 (not the $7,500 the headline prices suggest)
  • ROI: 23.9% · Breakeven sell price: ≈ $60,604

Fees took $337.50 — small here, but on tighter trades or higher-fee exchanges they dominate.

5 mistakes that shrink crypto gains

  1. Forgetting the buy fee. Everyone remembers the sell fee; the buy fee is equally real.
  2. Ignoring the spread. Market orders fill slightly worse than the quoted price, especially on low-liquidity pairs.
  3. Counting unrealized gains as profit. Until you sell, it's paper. Taxes, fees, and volatility all apply at exit.
  4. Overtrading. Ten round trips at 1% total fees each = ~10% drag. Fewer, larger trades usually win.
  5. Forgetting taxes. In the US, selling crypto at a gain is generally taxable. Set aside accordingly — see a tax professional.

Breakeven: the number most traders skip

Your breakeven sell price is where after-fee revenue exactly covers your all-in cost. Knowing it turns vague hope ("I need it to go up") into a concrete target. The calculator displays it for every trade you enter.

FAQ

How do I calculate profit on crypto? +
Net profit = (sell price × amount × (1 − sell fee)) − (buy price × amount × (1 + buy fee)). A crypto profit calculator automates this and shows ROI % and breakeven price.
Do I pay taxes on crypto profits? +
In the US, crypto is treated as property: selling at a gain is generally a taxable event (short- or long-term capital gains depending on holding period). Rules vary by country — consult a tax professional.

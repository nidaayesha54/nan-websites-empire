Why fees matter in crypto

A "25% gain" on paper can shrink fast: pay 0.5% buying and 0.5% selling and you've given up roughly 1% of the round trip — more on leveraged or frequent trades. This calculator subtracts both fees so the profit you see is the profit you'd actually keep.

The formula

Net profit = (Sell price × coins × (1 − sell fee)) − (Buy price × coins × (1 + buy fee))

Breakeven = (Cost + buy fee) ÷ (coins × (1 − sell fee)) — the sell price where you neither gain nor lose.

FAQ

How do crypto exchange fees affect profit? +
You pay fees on both sides: a buy fee on your cost and a sell fee on your revenue. On small trades, fees can erase several percent of gains — this calculator subtracts both so you see true net profit.
What is the breakeven price for a crypto trade? +
The sell price at which your after-fee revenue exactly equals your cost including buy fees. Our calculator shows it automatically — any sale above breakeven is profit.

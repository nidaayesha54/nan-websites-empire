Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate my stock return? +
Total return = (sell value − buy cost + dividends received) ÷ buy cost × 100. Our free stock return calculator does this automatically, including annualized return over your holding period.
What is a good annualized return on stocks? +
The S&P 500 has historically averaged around 10% per year before inflation (roughly 7% inflation-adjusted) over long periods. Individual results vary widely.
What is dollar-cost averaging? +
Investing a fixed amount on a regular schedule regardless of price. Try our DCA calculator to project long-term growth, or read our DCA vs lump sum guide.

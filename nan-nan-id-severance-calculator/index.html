How Severance Is Calculated in Indonesia

Article 156 of the Manpower Law (as amended by the Job Creation Law, UU Cipta Kerja) plus Government Regulation No. 35 of 2021 sets a three-part termination package:

1. Severance Pay (Uang Pesangon — UP)

Based on length of service, capped at 9 months of wage:

Less than 1 year1 month's wage
1 to under 2 years2 months
2 to under 3 years3 months
3 to under 4 years4 months
4 to under 5 years5 months
5 to under 6 years6 months
6 to under 7 years7 months
7 to under 8 years8 months
8 years or more9 months

2. Long-Service Pay (Uang Penghargaan Masa Kerja — UPMK)

Paid from 3 years of service upward, always at 1×, up to 10 months:

3 to under 6 years2 months
6 to under 9 years3 months
9 to under 12 years4 months
12 to under 15 years5 months
15 to under 18 years6 months
18 to under 21 years7 months
21 to under 24 years8 months
24 years or more10 months

3. Compensation of Rights (Uang Penggantian Hak — UPH)

Covers what the worker already earned but has not used:

Unused annual leave cashed at the daily wage (monthly wage ÷ 30) + housing and medical allowance of 15% of the total severance and long-service pay + relocation costs where the worker moved cities for the job.

Worked Example

Monthly wage IDR 15,000,000, 10 years of service, terminated by the employer without cause: UP = 9 × 2 = 18 months → IDR 270,000,000; UPMK = 4 months → IDR 60,000,000; housing & medical = 15% × 330,000,000 = IDR 49,500,000. Package before tax ≈ IDR 379.5M — about 25.3 months of salary.

Why the Reason for Termination Matters

GR 35/2021 applies a multiplier to the severance pay (not to long-service pay) depending on why employment ended:

Employer termination without cause2× UP
Merger / acquisition1× UP
Efficiency / downsizing1× (some cases 0.5×)
Closure: force majeure or losses for 2+ years0.5× UP
Closure: no losses1× UP
Employee misconduct0.5× UP
Voluntary resignationNo UP — UPMK (1×) if 3+ years, plus UPH
Employee death2× UP to heirs
Retirement1.75× UP
Prolonged illness / disability2× UP

Tax on Severance (PPh 21 Final)

Severance packages are subject to a special final income tax, calculated on the lump sum without deducting the standard non-taxable income (PTKP): 0% on the first IDR 50 million, 5% on the next IDR 50 million, 15% on anything above IDR 100 million.

FAQs

I resigned after 5 years with IDR 7M salary. What do I get?

No severance pay (UP) on resignation, but you are entitled to long-service pay: 5 years → 2 months × IDR 7M = IDR 14M, plus unused leave and the 15% housing/medical allowance.

Does the employer also pay unemployment support?

Under the Job Creation Law, workers also access the JKP unemployment insurance program (Jaminan Kehilangan Pekerjaan), which provides cash benefits and retraining — separate from the employer's severance package.

Do contract (PKWT) workers get severance?

Fixed-term PKWT workers get a different compensation: 1 month's wage per 12 months of contract (pro-rata for shorter or longer contracts) when the contract ends — not the UP/UPMK tiers.

What counts as wage for severance?

Basic salary plus fixed allowances — the same "one month's wage" concept used for THR. Variable bonuses and commissions are excluded.

Is there a deadline for payment?

The full termination settlement should be paid at the end of the employment relationship. Delays can be raised with the local Manpower office (Disnaker) or the Industrial Relations Court (PHI).

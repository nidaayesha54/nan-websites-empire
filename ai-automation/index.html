❓ Frequently asked questions

What is AI automation?

AI automation uses artificial intelligence plus workflow tools (like Zapier, Make, or ChatGPT) to complete repetitive business tasks — drafting content, answering FAQs, triaging email, enriching leads — without manual effort.

How much does AI automation cost?

Most small businesses start free: ChatGPT's free tier plus free plans from Zapier, Make, or HubSpot cover the first automations. Paid stacks typically run $20–$100/month.

What should I automate first?

Start with high-frequency, low-judgment tasks: meeting notes to action items, lead follow-ups, inbox triage, and content repurposing. Take our free quiz for a ranked plan.

Do I need coding skills to automate?

No. No-code tools like Zapier, Make, and ChatGPT Custom GPTs handle most business automations. Coding helps for advanced cases but isn't required to start.

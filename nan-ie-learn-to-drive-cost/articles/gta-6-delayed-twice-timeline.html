GTA 6 Delayed Twice: The Full Timeline to Launch Day

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026 — a date that had already been on the calendar for more than a year. Getting there took two delays, about twelve extra months, and a trailer that promised 2025. About 221,000 people a month search for the GTA 6 release date, and the question behind most of those searches is the same one: was it delayed again? The answer is no — but the road to launch day is worth knowing, because it explains why Rockstar's dates are trusted now, and why the game arrived when it did.

That matters because the delay story is also a trust story. Every time Rockstar moved the date, it said sorry, gave a reason, and then held the new date. The November 19 date survived for 375 days without moving — the longest any GTA 6 date had ever stood. This is the full timeline, with every date sourced to Rockstar's own announcements.

The Timeline: From Trailer to Launch

December 2023: the first trailer gives a 2025 release window. The trailer was published in early December 2023, a day ahead of its planned schedule after an early copy appeared online, and it pointed at 2025 with no month attached. In May 2024, Take-Two narrowed that window to fall 2025. For a year and a half, 2025 was the promise.

May 2, 2025: the first delay. Rockstar moved the game to May 26, 2026, and said it was very sorry the date was later than players expected. Take-Two backed the move the same day. Four days later, on May 6, 2025, Trailer 2 arrived — a 2 minute 47 second look at Jason and Lucia that drew more than 475 million views across platforms in its first day, which Rockstar called the biggest video launch of all time.

November 6, 2025: the second delay. Rockstar moved the game again, to Thursday, November 19, 2026, saying sorry for adding time to "a long wait" and that the extra months would let it finish the game with the polish players expect. From that day, the date never moved again. Pre-orders opened on June 25, 2026, at midnight local time. Digital pre-loading opened on November 10, 2026. And on November 9, 2026 — ten days before launch — Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick told IGN the game is "not going to be delayed again", and said on the earnings call that Rockstar has completed development of GTA 6.

Why Rockstar Delayed It

Rockstar's stated reason was the same both times: more time to finish the game to the standard players expect. The studio has not published a detailed breakdown of what needed the extra months, and no single verified cause explains the whole wait. The context is worth knowing, though. GTA 5 arrived in September 2013, and much of the time since was spent on Red Dead Redemption 2 and on a GTA 6 production that grew far more demanding than a prettier map — a dense, living Vice City and a whole state of Leonida, with night lighting, real traffic, and hundreds of enterable interiors. In September 2022, Rockstar also acknowledged that an unauthorised party had obtained confidential material, including early development footage. That intrusion is a real event in the game's history, but no source ties it to either delay as a cause.

What the delays bought, by every account of the finished game, was polish: a 30 fps target held across consoles, a wanted system rebuilt around what police actually witness, and a story that reviewers called a masterpiece on launch day. Whether the extra year was worth it is a question only players can answer — but the date held, and the game arrived finished.

What the Delays Cost Players

The cost was time: roughly a year added to a wait that had already lasted since GTA 5 in 2013. The first delay pushed the game from fall 2025 to May 2026; the second pushed it from May to November 2026. For players who had waited thirteen years, another twelve months felt like a lot — and the internet spent both delays asking whether a third one was coming. It never did. The November 19 date stood for 375 days, through two earnings calls and a full pre-order season, and Take-Two's November 9 results listed it unchanged.

The lesson for the next Rockstar launch is simple: the studio delays early, apologises plainly, and then holds the new date. Both GTA 6 delays came months before their dates, not weeks — and the only late slips in Rockstar's history are one to three weeks long, all from the PlayStation 2 era, before digital pre-loads existed. A date Rockstar sets a year out is a date it intends to keep.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many times was GTA 6 delayed?

Twice. From the original 2025 window to May 26, 2026 (announced May 2, 2025), and from May 26, 2026 to November 19, 2026 (announced November 6, 2025).

Is GTA 6 delayed again?

No. The last delay was announced on November 6, 2025, and November 19, 2026 held for more than a year. The game launched on that date, and Take-Two's CEO ruled out another delay ten days before launch.

Why did Rockstar delay GTA 6?

Rockstar said it needed more time to finish the game with the polish players expect. It has not published a detailed breakdown of what the extra months were spent on.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 was delayed twice — 2025 to May 2026, then May to November 2026 — and then it launched on the date Rockstar set in November 2025, with no third delay. Two apologies, one year added, and a date that held for 375 days: that is the whole story, and it is why the next Rockstar date is worth believing.

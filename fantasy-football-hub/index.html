Fantasy Football FAQ

What is fantasy football?

Fantasy football (fantasy soccer) is a game where you pick a squad of real footballers within a budget, then score points based on their real-life performances — goals, assists, clean sheets and bonus points.

How much budget do I get?

The classic format gives you £100m to spend on 15 players (or 11 starters in simplified formats). Our team planner enforces the £100m cap while you build.

How do I pick a good captain?

Pick an attacking player with a home fixture against a weak defence who takes penalties and plays 90 minutes. Read our full captain guide for the 5-rule framework.

Are the player stats on this site real?

No — all player names and stats on our tools are sample data for demonstration. Update them with current-season stats from the official game before gameweek decisions.

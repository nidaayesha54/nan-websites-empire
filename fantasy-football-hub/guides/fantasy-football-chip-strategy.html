Fantasy Football Chip Strategy: When to Play Every Chip

Chips are one-use power-ups that can be worth 30–100+ extra points when timed well — or nearly nothing when wasted. This guide covers the Wildcard, Free Hit, Bench Boost and Triple Captain: what each does, and exactly when to play it.

Wildcard: the full rebuild

The Wildcard lets you make unlimited transfers for one gameweek with no point hits. Use it when your team is structurally broken — multiple injuries, bad fixture swings, or a strategy pivot (e.g. switching from template to differential mode).

  • Best timing: 1–2 weeks before a big double gameweek, so you can load up on players with two fixtures.
  • Avoid: burning it in the first 6 weeks for one bad gameweek — patience pays.

Free Hit: the one-week squad

Free Hit gives you a completely new squad for a single gameweek, then your old team returns automatically. It's the perfect tool for blank gameweeks — weeks when several teams don't play and your normal squad would field 8 players.

  • Best timing: the biggest blank gameweek of the season.
  • Avoid: using it in a normal week — its value comes from dodging blanks, not chasing form.

Bench Boost: all 15 players score

Bench Boost makes your 4 substitutes' points count for one gameweek. For this to be worth it, your bench must be playing and scoring — four £4.0m non-starters turn the chip into a 4-point waste.

  • Best timing: a double gameweek, after a Wildcard the week before built you a bench of 15 doublers.
  • The classic combo: Wildcard in GW-1 → Bench Boost in the double GW. This is the highest-ceiling chip play in the game.

Triple Captain: 3× on your armband

Triple Captain triples (instead of doubles) your captain's score for one week. Save it for a double gameweek where your captain plays twice — that's two chances at a haul, tripled.

  • Best timing: a double gameweek, on an elite penalty-taking attacker with two kind home fixtures.
  • Avoid: playing it in a single gameweek "because he might haul" — the math heavily favours doubles.

The season chip plan (template)

  • Early season: hold everything. Learn your squad's shape first.
  • Before the biggest double gameweek: Wildcard, then Bench Boost.
  • Biggest blank gameweek: Free Hit.
  • Second double gameweek: Triple Captain.

Adjust to your season's actual calendar — the principle never changes: chips belong on double and blank gameweeks, because that's where they gain the most points over a normal week.

Chip mistakes that cost 50+ points

  • Playing Bench Boost with a dead bench (non-playing £4.0m players).
  • Triple Captain in a single gameweek on a rotation risk.
  • Wildcarding after one bad week instead of planning around doubles.
  • Forgetting chips expire at season end — an unused chip is worth zero.

10 Fantasy Football Mistakes Beginners Make

Last updated: October 2026. Most new fantasy managers lose thousands of ranks to the same handful of avoidable errors. Fix these ten and you'll instantly play like a veteran.

1. Captaining a defender

Defenders have low ceilings — doubling a 6-pointer gains you 6 points; doubling an attacker's 13-pointer gains you 13. Always captain attacking players. See our full captain framework.

2. Chasing last week's points

Transferring in the player who just scored a hat-trick is the classic trap — you're buying at peak price after the points already happened. Buy next week's points: kind fixtures, not last week's highlights.

3. Taking hits every week

A −4 point hit needs a genuine swing to pay off. One hit for an injured star is fine; hits for sideways "upgrades" bleed points all season. Plan with free transfers instead.

4. Ignoring the fixture calendar

Fantasy is won in the fixtures. Owning great players through a brutal run of away games at top sides is how good teams score badly. Map the next 6–8 weeks with our fixture difficulty tracker.

5. Wasting chips early

Triple Captain in gameweek 4 "because he might haul" is burning 20+ expected points. Chips belong on double and blank gameweeks — read the chip strategy guide before touching one.

6. An expensive bench

£6m+ sitting on your bench every week is £6m not scoring. Keep bench players cheap, playing, and from teams with kind fixtures.

7. Forgetting the vice-captain

If your captain doesn't play a single minute, your vice-captain takes the armband. Always set one — a blank vice slot has cost managers entire seasons.

8. Too many players from one team

Stacking four players from your favourite club feels fun until they lose 3–0 and your whole gameweek collapses. Spread risk across teams.

9. No plan for blank and double gameweeks

When teams don't play (blanks) or play twice (doubles), unprepared managers field 8 players while rivals field 15. Mark these weeks on your calendar months ahead and save chips for them.

10. Setting the team and forgetting it

Fantasy rewards active managers. Check team news before each deadline, set your captain weekly, and review your structure monthly. Ten minutes a week beats ten hours in August.

The fix-it checklist

  • ✅ Captain an attacker with a kind home fixture — use the framework
  • ✅ Structure your £100m sensibly — build it here
  • ✅ Hold chips for doubles and blanks
  • ✅ Bank a free transfer whenever your team is healthy

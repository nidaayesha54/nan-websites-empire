Fantasy Football Squad Structure Explained

Last updated: October 2026. You have £100m and 11 starting slots. Where the money goes decides your season more than any single player pick. Here are the three proven ways to structure a fantasy football squad — and how to choose between them.

The premium-heavy 3-5-2

Spend big on five midfielders and two elite forwards; go cheap at the back (one solid £5.5–6.5m defender plus value picks) and a set-and-forget £4.5m goalkeeper. Midfielders earn bonus points for goals and assists more reliably than any other position, and five of them maximises your exposure to attacking returns.

Best when: clean sheets look unpredictable, or premium midfielders are clearly the best players in the game. Risk: thin defence punishes you in low-scoring weeks.

The balanced 3-4-3

Three elite attackers up front, four strong midfielders, and a defence built from one premium plus rotation-friendly £4.5–5.5m picks from teams with kind fixtures. This is the most flexible structure — it pivots easily between template and differential play.

Best when: several forwards are genuine captain candidates each week. Risk: premium forwards are expensive, so your midfield depth can suffer.

The defensive-rock 4-4-2 / 5-3-2

Invest in two or three premium defenders from the best defences plus an elite goalkeeper, and hunt value in attack. Clean sheets are worth 4 points per defender plus bonus — a double clean sheet from a £6m defender rivals a forward's goal.

Best when: 2–3 teams are clearly the best defences in the league with long kind fixture runs. Risk: if those teams concede, your whole structure collapses at once.

Golden rules for any structure

  • Never spend more than £9m combined on goalkeepers. Your backup keeper is dead money.
  • Keep £0.5–1.0m in the bank early. Flexibility beats a "perfect" XI when injuries hit.
  • Maximum 3 players per real team in classic formats — don't build around one club.
  • Your bench should cost under £13m total. Every million on the bench is a million not scoring.

GTA 6 Sports Teams: Manatees, Narcos and Stadiums

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and Vice City is not just a crime city — it is a sports city too. Rockstar's official screenshots and artwork show two professional teams with real branding, real jerseys and real stadiums: the Vice City Manatees, a football team, and the Vice City Narcos, a basketball team. They appear on characters, on billboards and in the world itself, and they give Leonida something no GTA map has had before — a sports culture you can see everywhere you look.

That matters because sports in GTA have always been a side note. This time the teams are part of the city's identity, and knowing who they are makes the map make sense: the stadiums are landmarks, the jerseys are collectibles, and the question every player asks — can you actually play these sports? — has an answer that is more careful than the hype suggests.

The Vice City Manatees: Football in Aqua

The Manatees are Vice City's football team, and their branding is a direct nod to the real Miami Dolphins: aqua jerseys, a marine mascot, and a stadium that mirrors a real Miami venue. Dre'Quan Priest wears a Manatees jersey in official artwork, and the woman on the game's cover art wears one too — Rockstar is not hiding this team, it is putting it on the box. The old GTA football team, the Mambas, played at Hyman Memorial Stadium in the earlier Vice City games; the Manatees appear to have replaced them as the city's team.

Their home is Fleeca Field, a stadium in Rockridge, Vice City, modelled on LoanDepot Park — the $634 million retractable-roof ballpark in Miami's Riverside neighbourhood. The naming rights belong to Fleeca, the in-game bank, which is exactly the kind of joke Rockstar loves. Banners for the Manatees hang outside the entrance, and the stadium's retractable roof is visible in official images. One detail worth noting: the real Hard Rock Stadium, Miami's actual football ground, does not appear to be in the game — the fictional Fleeca Field is the football home instead.

The Vice City Narcos: Basketball at the Sahara Arena

The Narcos are the basketball team, and they play at the Sahara Arena, an indoor complex modelled on the Kaseya Center — the $213 million home of the Miami Heat on Biscayne Bay. A member of the PTT Youngin$ gang wears a Narcos jersey in official images, and the arena's design borrows heavily from the real building, including its waterfront position and lighting. Vice City never had a basketball team in the old games, so the Narcos are a new piece of the city's identity.

The crossover is real, too. The actual Miami Heat staged a "Vice City Night" at the Kaseya Center on November 18, the night before launch, with a GTA-themed court and Vice City jerseys — and the Heat's Vice City jerseys are part of a Nike and Cactus Jack collection. That is marketing, not gameplay, but it shows how seriously Rockstar is treating the sports angle: the teams exist in the real world as well as in Leonida.

Can You Actually Play These Sports?

Here is the honest answer: Rockstar has not confirmed that basketball or football are playable in GTA 6. The official images show Jason Duval with a basketball hoop outside his home, and another screenshot appears to show Jason and Cal Hampton playing basketball together. Those scenes have led to speculation that basketball could become an interactive activity — but Rockstar has not confirmed whether players can join full games, join teams or attend professional sporting events.

History says to be hopeful but patient. Basketball was playable in San Andreas, where a minigame unlocked after the "Tagging up Turf" mission and challenged you to score as many baskets as possible in a set time, with tricks and dunks that changed with your character's weight. Other GTA games offered golf, tennis, darts, pool, bowling and motor racing as side activities. GTA 6's full list of playable side activities remains unannounced, so for now the teams are confirmed culture and the games are confirmed scenery — and anything more is a hope, not a fact.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are GTA 6's sports teams?

The Vice City Manatees (football, playing at Fleeca Field) and the Vice City Narcos (basketball, playing at the Sahara Arena). Both appear in official Rockstar artwork and screenshots, with branded jerseys worn by named characters.

Can you watch a game at Fleeca Field or the Sahara Arena?

Rockstar has not confirmed whether players can attend professional sporting events in the game. The stadiums are confirmed locations in the world; what you can do inside them has not been announced.

Is basketball playable in GTA 6?

Not confirmed. Screenshots show Jason playing street basketball, but Rockstar has not said whether basketball is a playable activity, and the game's full side-activity list is still unannounced.

The Bottom Line

Vice City's sports teams are real in the world of GTA 6: the Manatees and the Narcos have jerseys, stadiums and a place in the city's culture, and their real-world inspirations are easy to spot. What they are not — yet — is confirmed gameplay. Enjoy the stadiums as landmarks, keep an eye out for the jerseys, and wait for Rockstar to say whether you will ever get to play the games yourself.

GTA 6 in Brazil: Price, Portuguese Subtitles and Release

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 arrived in Brazil on Thursday, November 19, 2026, unlocking at midnight Brasilia time, and Brazilian players had two questions before anything else: how much does it cost, and is it in Portuguese? The price is R$449.90 for the Standard Edition and R$549.90 for the Ultimate Edition, the same on the PlayStation Store and the Microsoft Store. The language answer is a split: the menus and subtitles are in Brazilian Portuguese, but the voices are in English, like in every country. This guide covers the price, the language, the age rating, and what to watch out for before you buy.

Brazil is one of the biggest GTA markets in the world, and the launch here came with its own details — a ClassInd 18 rating, tax added at checkout, and a boxed copy with no disc inside. If you are buying in reais, here is everything that is different about the Brazilian release.

The price in reais

The Standard Edition costs R$449.90 and the Ultimate Edition costs R$549.90, on both console stores. The R$100 difference buys the Ultimate Edition Upgrade: extra vehicles, weapons, outfits and shops that unlock chapter by chapter through the campaign. The upgrade is also sold separately for R$100, so you can start with Standard and upgrade later for the same money.

One detail catches many buyers out: in Brazil, tax is added at checkout, so the amount you actually pay is higher than the number on the store page. The same applies in the United States, Canada and Mexico, while in the UK, the eurozone, Australia and the UAE the listed price already includes tax. Check the final total before you confirm the purchase, not just the headline price.

Portuguese subtitles, English voices

Brazilian Portuguese is one of the thirteen languages for subtitles and the interface, and it is the only Portuguese in the menu — there is no Portuguese from Portugal option. The confirmation comes from the game's own product page, which lists Portuguese (Brazil) alongside English, French, Italian, German, Russian, Japanese, Polish, Korean, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, and Spanish in its Mexican and Spanish variants.

The voices, though, are English in every country. Brazil dubs almost everything, and Brazilian dubbing is famous for good reason, so many players hoped for a dubbed version — but no Portuguese voice acting has been announced, and the studio has not provided localized dubbing for its major titles in the past. If you play with Brazilian Portuguese text, you will read the story in Portuguese and hear it in English.

Rating, unlock and the boxed copy

The game carries a ClassInd 18 rating in Brazil, which means it is restricted to adults. It unlocked at midnight Brasilia time on November 19, as part of the rolling midnight launch that began in New Zealand, and pre-loading opened on November 10 so players could download the file — up to 212GB — before launch day.

If you buy the physical version, know what is inside the box: a download code, not a disc. The boxed copy ships with a code you redeem, and then you download the full game exactly as if you had bought it digitally. The Ultimate Edition is digital only, so there is no boxed version of it at all.

Is R$449.90 too much?

The price is the most argued-over part of the Brazilian launch. A survey conducted between November 13 and 18 with 2,146 respondents found that 37% of Brazilians who intended to play, or were still undecided, considered R$449.90 high enough to give up or postpone the purchase. That is more than a third of the interested audience hesitating over the price alone.

For comparison, the game costs $79.99 in the United States and £69.99 in the UK, and at R$449.90 it sits near the top of what a single game has ever cost in Brazil. There is no cheaper legal route: the price is the same on both console stores, and the only way to pay less is to wait for a sale or a promotion. Be wary of third-party key sellers promising the game for far less — the game's code rules and regional store terms make cheap keys a gamble, and a code that does not work cannot be refunded by the store.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does GTA 6 have Brazilian Portuguese voices?

No. Brazilian Portuguese covers the subtitles and the interface only. The voices are in English in every country, and no dub has been announced.

What time did GTA 6 unlock in Brazil?

Midnight Brasilia time on Thursday, November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

Is there a disc in the Brazilian boxed copy?

No. The physical version comes with a download code inside the box and no disc, so you still need to download the full game.

The bottom line: in Brazil, GTA 6 costs R$449.90, speaks English with Brazilian Portuguese text, carries a ClassInd 18 rating, and unlocks at midnight. The price is high enough that a third of interested players are waiting — but if you buy, buy from the console stores, and check the final total with tax before you confirm.

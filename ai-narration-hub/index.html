❓ Frequently asked questions

How many words is one hour of audiobook narration?

About 9,000 words at the standard pace of 150 words per minute. Our Narration Pacer gives you an exact estimate for your manuscript plus chapter budgets.

What is the best AI narration tool for audiobooks in 2026?

ElevenLabs leads for natural long-form narration; PlayHT and Murf are strong for multi-voice projects. See the full comparison in our 2026 guide.

How should I pace chapters in an audiobook?

Aim for 10–25 minutes per chapter (1,500–3,750 words at 150 wpm) and end on tension or curiosity. The chapter pacing planner builds your word budget automatically.

Can I sell AI-narrated audiobooks on Audible?

Audible's ACX only accepts AI narration through its own Virtual Voice program. Google Play Books, Kobo, and Findaway Voices accept AI narration with disclosure — check each platform's current rules. More in our blog.

How do I keep character voices consistent across chapters?

Write a voice sheet for every character — pitch, pace, accent, and a sample line — and check it before each session. Our Voice Consistency Tracker does exactly this and saves it in your browser.

GTA 6 Next Mission Not Showing? Check Your Cash

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

You have finished the last mission, you are standing in Vice City with nothing to do, and no mission marker appears anywhere. The story feels stuck. Before you assume the game is broken, check the one thing most players never think to check: your wallet. In GTA 6, some missions only open once Jason and Lucia have earned enough money — and if your cash is short, the next mission simply does not appear.

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The money gate is one of the game's least explained systems, and it is responsible for a large share of the "my game is stuck" reports from launch week. This guide explains how to tell a money gate from a real bug, how to earn the cash, and what to do if it genuinely is a bug.

Cause 1: The Money Gate

Not every missing mission marker is a bug. Some missions in GTA 6 are money-gated: the next mission only opens once Jason and Lucia have earned enough money. This comes from Rockstar North's Rob Nelson, speaking via Famitsu — the story is paced so that the characters need a certain amount of cash before the next job becomes available. If no marker appears and your wallet is thin, the game is not stuck. It is waiting for you to earn.

The fix, then, is to go and make money. The moment your cash crosses the threshold, the marker appears and the story continues. Players who do not know about the gate can spend hours wandering, restarting, and reinstalling — all for a problem that a few good jobs would solve.

How to Earn the Cash to Unlock the Next Mission

The fastest verified money methods at launch all come from the same guide, and they pay very differently. Jewelry store robberies pay around $20,000 each — Victoria Lee Jeweler in Capri, and Blanc and Giroux in the Decor District, are the named targets, and a snap gun from Chapter 3 breaks in silently. Stealing cars and selling them is the low-effort staple: take a car right next to a Pay 'N' Spray and sell it there. Kenton's treasure dives pay around $1,400 per wreck with almost no risk, and they unlock after the mission "Island Dreams". The biggest paydays are the bank vaults — up to $205,000 — but those only come at the end of Chapter 4.

For a money gate, the mid-tier methods are usually enough: a jewelry robbery or two, plus a few car sales, will clear most thresholds. You do not need a bank vault to open the next mission — you need enough cash, and the jewelry stores are the fastest route to it.

Protect the Cash You Earn

Earning the money is only half the job; keeping it is the other half. Pocket cash is lost when you die or get arrested, and it does not carry into Chapter 2 — so do not walk around with a fortune in your pocket. Stash spare money under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom, and open the BuckMe bank account once the mission "Buck Me" is available, then deposit your cash at ATMs. Banked money is safe. Pocket money is a donation to the next mugger, cop, or bad decision.

This matters doubly at a money gate: if you earn the threshold cash and then lose half of it dying on the way to the mission, the marker can vanish again. Deposit first, then go to the mission.

Cause 2: The Lucia Softlock

There is also a genuine bug to know about. An early Lucia mission, after leaving a bank, can softlock: the mission simply will not let you progress. There is no official fix for this one yet. The workaround is to reload your last manual save from before the mission and restart the console fully — which is why keeping a separate save slot from before tricky missions matters so much.

How do you tell the softlock from the money gate? The money gate shows no marker and no active mission — the game is waiting. The softlock happens inside a mission that has already started: you are in the mission, and it will not advance. If a mission is running and frozen, reload a save. If no mission is running and no marker appears, check your cash and go earn.

How to Avoid Losing Hours

Both problems are cheaper to prevent than to fix. Save manually after every mission — Pause, Game, Save Game — and rotate two or three of your fifteen manual save slots, keeping one slot from before any tricky mission. Remember that GTA 6's autosave only fires after a mission ends, and the prologue never autosaves, so the manual habit is your only real safety net. And remember that missions cannot be replayed: every dollar you earn and every choice you make on a mission sticks, so save before the risky ones.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I know if it is the money gate or a bug?

No marker and no active mission, with low cash, is the money gate — go earn. A mission that has started but will not advance is the softlock — reload your last manual save from before the mission.

What do I lose when I die in GTA 6?

Your pocket cash. Money in your pocket is lost on death or arrest and does not carry into Chapter 2, so deposit it at ATMs or stash it under the floorboards in Jason's bedroom.

The Bottom Line

A missing mission marker in GTA 6 is usually your wallet, not a bug: some missions only open once Jason and Lucia have earned enough money. Earn it — jewelry robberies, car sales, and treasure dives are the verified routes — bank it before you lose it, and the marker appears. If a mission has started and will not advance, that is the Lucia softlock, and a manual save from before the mission is the way out.

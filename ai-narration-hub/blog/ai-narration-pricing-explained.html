AI Audiobook Narration Pricing Explained: 2026 Costs Compared

Oct 12, 2026 · 6 min read

The first question every author asks about AI narration: what does it actually cost? The honest answer is "it depends on the book" — but the math is simple once you know the units. Let's break it down.

The unit that matters: cost per finished hour (PFH)

Audiobook pricing is quoted per finished hour — one hour of final, edited audio. At the standard 150 words per minute, one finished hour ≈ 9,000 words. A typical 90,000-word novel = 10 finished hours.

Human narration: the baseline

OptionTypical cost10-hour book total
Professional narrator (PFH rate)$250–$500+/finished hour$2,500–$5,000+
Royalty-share deal (ACX)$0 upfront, split royalties$0 upfront
Narrating it yourselfEquipment + your timeWeeks of recording

AI narration: the real numbers

AI tools price by characters or credits, not finished hours. Rough conversion: 9,000 words ≈ 50,000–55,000 characters including spaces. So a 10-hour book needs roughly 500,000+ characters of generation — plus re-renders for fixes (budget 15–20% extra).

Character allowances vary by tool and plan — always check the current plan limits against your manuscript's character count before you start.

The hidden costs nobody mentions

  • Re-renders: mispronunciations and bad takes cost credits. Budget 15–20% extra characters.
  • Your QC time: listening to 10 hours at 1x speed is 10 hours of your life. Non-negotiable.
  • Editing tools: a basic audio editor handles chapter assembly and loudness normalization.
  • Proof-listening: a second pair of ears catches what you miss. Worth every penny.

The cheapest path to a finished book

  1. Draft free: use free tiers and our TTS preview to test pacing and catch script problems at $0.
  2. Size the plan: count your manuscript's characters, add 20%, and pick the cheapest plan that covers it — our Narration Pacer gives you the word count to start from.
  3. One paid month: generate the whole book in a focused sprint rather than paying for months of a plan you barely use.
  4. Compare tools first: see our 2026 AI narration tools comparison so the plan you buy matches your book.
Bottom line: a 10-hour AI-narrated audiobook typically costs $20–$100 in tool credits vs $2,500–$5,000+ for a human narrator — roughly a 50x saving. The tradeoff is your time in directing and QC. For most indie authors, that's a trade worth making.

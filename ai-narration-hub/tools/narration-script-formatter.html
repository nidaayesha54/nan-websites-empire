📝 Narration Script Formatter

Paste raw manuscript text and get a narration-ready script: chapter breaks, [PAUSE] markers, dialogue tags, emphasis marks, and pronunciation notes for tricky words.

Why format before you narrate

Pause markers prevent the #1 amateur mistake: rushing. A 1-second beat after each paragraph lets the listener's brain catch up.
Pronunciation notes are non-negotiable for fantasy, sci-fi, and nonfiction with foreign terms. Decide every pronunciation once, before chapter 1.
Dialogue tags remind you to shift energy for spoken lines — narration is steady, dialogue is alive.

Consistency rules the pros follow

Record each character's sample line and play it back before every session — your "voice anchor" for the day.
Narrate chapters in story order when possible. Jumping around makes voice drift much harder to catch.
Keep the same mic distance and room for the whole book. Listeners forgive a lot — except volume that jumps between chapters.

Also useful: format your script and preview the pacing before recording.

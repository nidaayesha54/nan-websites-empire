Pacing tips

Most audiobooks run 8–12 hours — that's 72,000–108,000 words at 150 wpm. Check where your manuscript lands.
Keep chapters between 10–25 minutes. Chapters over 30 minutes make listeners lose their place (and their patience).
End chapters on tension or an unanswered question — the #1 retention trick in audio fiction.

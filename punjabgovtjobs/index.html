How to Apply

1
Check the PPSC advertisement number and read eligibility, quota and age requirements carefully.
2
Apply online at the PPSC website and generate the application fee challan.
3
Deposit the fee at any designated bank branch and keep the receipt safe.
4
Download your admission certificate and appear for the written test.
5
Qualify the test, attend the interview, and check the final recommendation list on PPSC.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for PPSC jobs?

Apply online on the PPSC website against the advertisement number, deposit the fee challan at the bank, and submit before the deadline.

What is the age limit for Punjab government jobs?

Generally 21 to 28 years plus 5 years general relaxation, varying by post and advertisement.

Can candidates from other provinces apply?

Only candidates with Punjab domicile can apply against Punjab quota posts; check each advertisement.

How are test dates announced?

Test schedules are published on the PPSC website and admission certificates are downloadable online.

Are these listings official advertisements?

Listings here are illustrative samples. Always verify on the official PPSC or department portal.

What is the PMS exam?

The Provincial Management Service exam recruits Assistant Commissioners and Section Officers through PPSC.

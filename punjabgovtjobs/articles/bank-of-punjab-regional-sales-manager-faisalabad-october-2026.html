What EB & CM Means

In banking terminology, EB & CM stands for Employee Banking and Cash Management — the business of offering banking services to companies and institutions. Employee Banking covers salary accounts and related services for a company's workforce, while Cash Management covers corporate deposits, collections, payments and liquidity solutions for institutional clients. The Regional Sales Manager leads this business across the Faisalabad region: building relationships with corporate clients, growing the employee-banking and cash-management book, and hitting regional sales targets.

About the Bank of Punjab

The Bank of Punjab is an established Pakistani commercial bank offering deposit accounts, financing, payment solutions and other financial services. It recruits professionals across consumer banking, information technology, operations, risk management and relationship management, and follows an equal opportunity employment approach with career development opportunities. As a public-sector bank, its roles combine commercial banking targets with public-sector employment stability.

What the Role Involves

The Regional Sales Manager is a regional-level leadership role, not a branch job. Expect responsibility for the EB & CM business across the region: managing relationships with corporate and institutional clients, driving sales performance for employee-banking and cash-management products, coordinating with branch teams on service delivery, and reporting regional business results to the Consumer Banking Group. Candidates should have a track record in banking sales and client relationship management at a senior level, since the grade range (Band 7 to Band 6) indicates a senior managerial appointment.

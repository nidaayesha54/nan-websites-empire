PHC Manager (Inspections) Sargodha Jobs 2026 – Apply by 28 Oct

The Punjab Healthcare Commission (PHC) — the autonomous health regulatory body of the Government of Punjab under the PHC Act 2010 — has announced Manager (Inspections) posts for its regional offices, including Sargodha. The post is open to MBBS/BDS doctors with at least 6 years of relevant experience, with a non-refundable application fee of Rs. 1,000 payable through the prescribed challan. The last date is 28 October 2026, and applications are accepted online only through the PHC careers portal at os.phc.org.pk.

What the PHC Advertisement Covers

The Sargodha post is part of a larger PHC recruitment announcement. Manager (Inspections) posts are listed for Lahore, Rawalpindi, Bahawalpur, Sargodha and Dera Ghazi Khan — all requiring MBBS/BDS with six years of experience. The same ad also mentions Deputy Director (Operations) for Multan and Deputy Director (Inspections) for Lahore, Faisalabad and Multan (10 years of experience, Rs. 2,000 fee), an Officer (Administration) post in Lahore (Rs. 1,000 fee), and Cleaner posts on daily wages in other cities — Cleaner applications go by hard copy with a Rs. 500 pay order to the Lahore head office, not the online portal. Verify the full post list on the official portal.

Eligibility Criteria for Manager (Inspections)

  • Qualification: MBBS or BDS, recognized by PM&DC.
  • Experience: minimum 6 years of relevant experience, preferably in healthcare delivery or inspections.
  • Application fee: Rs. 1,000 (non-refundable) via the prescribed challan — attach the scanned paid copy with the online form.
  • Apply mode: online only, via the PHC careers portal (os.phc.org.pk).
  • Employment type: contract basis.

How to Apply Online — Step by Step

1
Open the PHC careers portal at os.phc.org.pk and find the Manager (Inspections) advertisement.
2
Download the job description and fee challan form from the portal or phc.org.pk, as directed in the ad.
3
Pay the Rs. 1,000 fee through the prescribed challan and keep a scanned copy of the paid receipt.
4
Fill in the online application with your MBBS/BDS credentials, PM&DC recognition and 6 years of experience, and attach the scanned paid challan.
5
Submit before 28 October 2026 — late or incomplete applications are not accepted.
Honesty flag: this article is based on a single jobs blog citing the PHC advertisement (Daily Jang, around 15–16 October 2026) — the original ad was not verified directly. The exact per-city vacancy count, salary and age limit are not stated in our source. Apply only through os.phc.org.pk, and re-check the fee, deadline and post list there before paying anything. Never share bank details or OTP codes with anyone claiming to process your application.

Why This Opening Is Worth Considering

Regulatory posts appear far less often than clinical vacancies, and the six-year experience bar limits competition to senior doctors. With an online-only process closing 28 October 2026, submit by 26 October to avoid last-day portal congestion.

Punjab Job Portal 2026 — How to Apply Online (Guide)

The Punjab Job Portal (jobs.punjab.gov.pk) is where Punjab government departments advertise their posts — PERA, transport authorities, health departments and more. This guide covers registration, fees, and submitting a correct application.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » Punjab Government Jobs » Articles

Many Punjab government jobs are advertised with the same instruction: apply online through the Punjab Job Portal. The portal, at jobs.punjab.gov.pk, is the Punjab government's online recruitment system for departmental posts — the vacancies that departments and authorities advertise directly, rather than through the Punjab Public Service Commission. Recent examples include the Punjab Enforcement and Regulatory Authority (PERA) and the Punjab Masstransit Authority. This guide explains how the portal works, how it differs from PPSC, and how to submit an application that is not rejected on a technicality.

Honesty flag: this is a guide to using the portal, not a job advertisement — it has no closing date of its own. Each departmental post carries its own deadline and fee rule, stated in its advertisement. Do not confuse the Punjab Job Portal with PPSC — they are separate systems, and applying on the wrong one does not count.

Punjab Job Portal vs PPSC — Which One Does Your Post Use?

This is the most common source of confusion. PPSC (ppsc.gop.pk) handles commission posts advertised through PPSC advertisements, such as Advertisement No. 11/2026 — doctors, veterinary officers, intelligence officers and similar gazetted or BPS posts. The Punjab Job Portal (jobs.punjab.gov.pk) handles departmental posts advertised directly by departments and authorities — for example, PERA's 110 contract posts and the Masstransit Authority's assistant director posts. The advertisement itself tells you which system to use: if it says "apply online through the Punjab Job Portal", use jobs.punjab.gov.pk; if it says PPSC, use ppsc.gop.pk. Applying on the wrong portal means your application simply does not exist for that post.

Step-by-Step: Register and Apply

1
Open jobs.punjab.gov.pk (linked above) and create your account with your CNIC number, a valid email address and an active mobile number.
2
Complete your profile: personal details, education, experience, domicile and skills — exactly as they appear on your certificates.
3
Find the departmental advertisement you want and read it in full — eligibility, experience, age limit and the fee rule.
4
If the post charges a processing fee, pay it exactly as the advertisement instructs (for example, through a Bank of Punjab deposit slip) and upload the receipt.
5
Complete the online application form, upload the required documents, and review every field before submitting.
6
Submit before the post's closing date and save the confirmation. Only shortlisted candidates are contacted for tests or interviews.

Fees: Free Posts and Paid Posts

Fees on the Punjab Job Portal vary by post. Many departmental posts are free to apply for. Others charge a processing fee stated in the advertisement — for example, some posts require a non-refundable fee paid through a Bank of Punjab deposit slip, with the receipt uploaded to the portal. The fee rule, the amount, the bank account and the upload requirement are all in the advertisement itself. If an advertisement does not mention a fee, do not pay anyone who asks for one — that is a scam, not a government charge.

Common Mistakes That Get Applications Rejected

The most common rejection reasons are avoidable: applying on the wrong portal (PPSC instead of the Job Portal, or vice versa), submitting after the closing date, missing fee receipts where a fee is required, and wrong CNIC numbers or dates of birth. Another frequent mistake is sending applications by email, courier or hand delivery — departmental portal posts accept online applications only, and offline applications are rejected outright. Government employees must also apply through the proper channel with an NOC, or their application can be rejected at the document stage.

After You Apply

After submission, the hiring department screens applications against the advertised criteria. Shortlisted candidates are called for a test, interview, or both, and no TA/DA is admissible. If you are not shortlisted, you will usually hear nothing — that is normal. Keep your profile updated as you gain qualifications and experience, because the same profile serves every future departmental application.

Reminder: the Punjab Job Portal is for Punjab departmental posts. Federal posts use the National Job Portal (njp.gov.pk), and PPSC commission posts use ppsc.gop.pk. Always check the advertisement to see which system your post uses.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Punjab Job Portal?

The Punjab Job Portal at jobs.punjab.gov.pk is the Punjab government's online recruitment portal for departmental posts. Punjab government departments and authorities advertise their vacancies there, and candidates apply online.

Is the Punjab Job Portal the same as PPSC?

No. PPSC (ppsc.gop.pk) handles commission posts advertised through PPSC advertisements. The Punjab Job Portal handles departmental posts advertised directly by departments and authorities such as PERA and the Punjab Masstransit Authority. Check the advertisement to see which system your post uses.

Do Punjab Job Portal posts charge an application fee?

It depends on the post. Some portal posts are free to apply for, while others charge a processing fee stated in the advertisement — for example, some posts require a fee paid through a Bank of Punjab deposit slip. Always check the advertisement for the fee rule of your post.

What documents do I need to apply on the Punjab Job Portal?

Your CNIC, a valid email address and mobile number, educational certificates, experience certificates, domicile certificate and a recent photograph. Government employees must also arrange an NOC from their parent department.

Rescue 1122 Punjab Jobs 2026: 239 Temporary Posts via CTS — Drivers, EMTs, Medical Officers, Last Date 6 Nov

The Emergency Services Department, Government of the Punjab, has announced 239 temporary vacancies for Rescue 1122 across seven posts — from 108 Rescue Driver (LTV) and 92 Emergency Medical Technician (EMT) posts to 28 Fire DERT Rescuers, 5 Emergency Officers (Medical) and 6 female-only hostel and attendant posts. The recruitment is handled by the Central Testing Service (CTS), and the last date to apply is 6 November 2026. The advertisement appeared in The Nation on 14 October 2026.

Every post in this advertisement is temporary, non-transferable, with no right to regularization — that is stated explicitly. Even so, Rescue 1122 remains one of the most applied-to services in Punjab: field, medical and driving roles with a defined pay scale (PPS-02 to PPS-07) and district-level postings in Lahore.

Locations: Headquarters (5 Medical Officer posts), Emergency Services Academy (6 female hostel/attendant posts) and District Lahore (228 field posts). The advertisement does not name a city for Headquarters or the Academy beyond Punjab.

Eligibility & Conditions

  • Age: 20 to 35 years for most posts; 24 to 32 years for Emergency Officer (Medical).
  • Education: Matriculation to MBBS depending on the post — DAE holders for Fire DERT Rescuer, nursing/paramedical qualifications for EMT, MBBS for the Medical Officer posts.
  • Nature: temporary, non-transferable, no right of regularization.
  • Selection: written test, psychological test, physical test and skill test (for relevant technical posts), followed by an interview — the final interview is held at Emergency Services Headquarters, Thokar Niaz Baig, Lahore.

How to Apply Through CTS

CTS runs the application process — candidates apply both online and by post:

1
Apply online on the CTS website (ctspak.com) — fill the prescribed application form for your post.
2
Send the application form by post to CTS with the required documents and fee as stated in the advertisement.
3
Meet the 6 November 2026 deadline — applications received after the closing date are not entertained.
4
Prepare for the test battery — written, psychological, physical and (for technical posts) skill tests, then the interview at the Emergency Services Headquarters.
Tip: keep your CTS application printout, fee receipt and postal receipt — you will need them at every stage from the written test to the interview.

Which Post Fits You

Matric holders with an LTV licence have the biggest window: 108 Rescue Driver posts — the largest single group in the advertisement. Nursing and paramedical graduates get the second largest: 92 EMT posts, the backbone of Rescue 1122's pre-hospital care. DAE holders in engineering trades can compete for the 28 Fire DERT Rescuer seats (with a skill test). MBBS doctors have the 5 Emergency Officer posts at headquarters — the only officer-grade slot in this round. And the female-only hostel and attendant posts are the entry route into the Emergency Services Academy staff. Match your qualification honestly, apply through CTS by 6 November, and verify every condition in the official advertisement on rescue.gov.pk or ctspak.com.

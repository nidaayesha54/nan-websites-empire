How to Apply Online for PPSC Jobs 2026 — ppsc.gop.pk Step-by-Step

The complete ppsc.gop.pk walkthrough: document checklist, 8-step online form, fee payment, and what happens after you submit — including the 26 Oct 2026 deadline.

✅ Updated October 13, 2026
Home » Punjab Government Jobs » Articles

The Punjab Public Service Commission accepts job applications through its online portal only — there is no paper or in-person submission. Whether you are applying for Advertisement 11/2026 (closing 26 October 2026) or any future PPSC advertisement, the process on www.ppsc.gop.pk follows the same steps. This guide walks you through each one so your application is accepted without errors.

Before You Start: Document Checklist

Keep the following ready before opening the form — it makes the process far quicker:

  • Valid CNIC (front and back scans in the required size)
  • A recent passport-size photograph with a light background
  • Punjab domicile certificate
  • Educational degrees, transcripts and result cards for the required qualification
  • Experience letters for posts requiring post-qualification experience
  • Valid registration with the relevant council for professional posts (where applicable)
  • An active mobile number and email address — all PPSC communication comes through these

Step-by-Step: Applying on ppsc.gop.pk

1
Open the official portal. Go to www.ppsc.gop.pk — type the address yourself rather than clicking links from unofficial pages.
2
Find the advertisement. Open the "Advertisements" section and select the advertisement number you want to apply for (e.g. 11/2026), then click "Apply Online" against your chosen post.
3
Read the post requirements. The portal shows the qualification, experience, age limit and quota for the post. Confirm you meet every condition before proceeding.
4
Fill the application form. Enter your personal details, CNIC, domicile district, educational qualifications and experience exactly as they appear on your documents. Even small mismatches can cause rejection at scrutiny.
5
Upload documents. Upload your photograph, CNIC and other required documents in the prescribed format and file size. Blurry or oversized uploads are a common reason for form errors.
6
Deposit the fee. Generate the fee challan/deposit slip and pay the prescribed application fee through the designated bank channels mentioned in the advertisement. Keep the receipt.
7
Review and submit. Preview the complete form, correct any mistake, and submit. Note your application/transaction number.
8
Print and save. Download and print the submitted application form and fee receipt. You will need them at the test and interview stages.

Application Fee & Payment

Every PPSC advertisement specifies an application fee, payable through the designated banks before the closing date. The fee is non-refundable, so confirm your eligibility before paying. Applications without the deposited fee are not processed. For Ad 11/2026, the fee details are given in the advertisement on the portal.

After Submission: What Happens Next

  • Admission certificate: PPSC uploads roll number slips / admission certificates on the portal before the test. Download and print yours.
  • Written/screening test: Appear on the announced date with your original CNIC and documents.
  • Interview: Shortlisted candidates are called for interview with original documents.
  • Final result: Merit lists are published on www.ppsc.gop.pk.

Common Mistakes That Get Applications Rejected

  • Applying for a post whose qualification or age criteria you do not meet
  • Typos in name, CNIC number or qualification details that differ from documents
  • Uploading an old or non-compliant photograph
  • Paying the fee after the closing date — or not at all
  • Submitting at the last minute and missing the 26 October 2026 deadline due to portal load
Tip: Submit your Ad 11/2026 application at least 2–3 days before the closing date. Portal traffic spikes on the final day every year. For help, call PPSC on UAN 042-111-988-722.

Tracking Your Application Status

After submitting, log in to www.ppsc.gop.pk periodically to check your application status. PPSC publishes lists of accepted and rejected applications before the test, along with the reason for rejection — if your name appears in the rejected list, the notice tells you exactly what went wrong. Admission certificates (roll number slips) are also uploaded to the portal ahead of the test date; download and print yours as soon as it appears, and report any discrepancy to PPSC immediately on UAN 042-111-988-722.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the PPSC application process fully online?

Yes. PPSC accepts applications only through its online portal www.ppsc.gop.pk. No paper or in-person applications are entertained.

What documents do I need to apply for PPSC jobs?

CNIC, recent photograph, Punjab domicile, educational degrees and transcripts, experience letters where required, and professional registration where applicable.

How do I pay the PPSC application fee?

Generate the fee slip from the portal and deposit the prescribed fee through the designated bank channels mentioned in the advertisement.

Can I edit my PPSC application after submitting it?

Review the preview carefully before submitting, as submitted forms generally cannot be edited. Contact PPSC on 042-111-988-722 if you face a genuine issue.

What is the closing date for PPSC Advertisement 11/2026?

26 October 2026. Submit your online application well before the deadline to avoid last-day portal congestion.

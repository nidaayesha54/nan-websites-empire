Who Can Apply: Eligibility Overview

  • Domicile: Punjab domicile is required, with quotas (open merit, women, minority and others) applied as stated in the advertisement.
  • Qualification: Each post lists its own required degree or diploma — medical qualifications for Senior Registrar posts, engineering degrees for engineering posts, commerce qualifications for accounts posts, and so forth.
  • Experience: BS-17 and BS-18 posts generally require a stated number of years of relevant post-qualification experience.
  • Age: The age bracket is fixed per post, with general age relaxation applicable under the rules.
Also in October 2026: PPSC Advertisement 10/2026 separately announced 1,958 Charge Nurse (Female) BS-16 posts in the Specialized Healthcare & Medical Education Department on a 3-year contract — requiring BSc Nursing with PNC registration, female candidates aged 21–28 (plus 8 years general relaxation). It is a separate advertisement with its own terms.

Selection Process

After the closing date, PPSC follows its standard multi-stage process. A written or screening test is held where applicant numbers are large, testing subject knowledge and general ability. Candidates who qualify are shortlisted for interviews, where academic record, experience and suitability are assessed. Final selection is made strictly on merit under the Commission's regulations, and results are published on the PPSC website.

Preparation Strategy for PPSC Ad 11/2026

With the closing date on 26 October 2026, tests are likely to follow within a few months, so serious candidates should begin preparation alongside the application. PPSC written tests typically combine subject-specific questions (often 80% of the paper) with general ability portions covering English, Pakistan studies, Islamiat, basic mathematics and current affairs.

  • Master your subject: For medical posts, revise clinical knowledge from your degree syllabus; for accounts, engineering and planning posts, focus on core professional concepts.
  • Practice past papers: PPSC repeats question styles frequently — solving the last five years of papers for similar posts is the highest-yield preparation.
  • Build speed: Most PPSC screening tests are 100 MCQs in 100 minutes; practice under strict timing.
  • Stay updated: Refresh your knowledge of Punjab-specific facts — departments, districts and recent government initiatives — which often appear in the general portion.

Biggest Opportunity: PPSC Advertisement 11/2026 — 299 Posts

The Punjab Public Service Commission's Advertisement No. 11/2026 is the single largest Punjab recruitment open right now: 299 vacancies across 13 posts in the Livestock and Dairy Development, Specialized Healthcare and Medical Education, Punjab Police (Special Branch), Youth Affairs and Sports, Agriculture and PHATA departments. The headline numbers: 107 Senior Registrar posts in teaching hospitals, 101 livestock posts (including Senior Veterinary Officers), 30 Intelligence Officer posts in the Special Branch, 37 Tehsil Sports Officer posts, plus deputy directors and accounts officers. Applications close 26 October 2026 on ppsc.gop.pk — all posts are on contract (2 to 5 years) and require Punjab domicile.

Uniformed Posts: Punjab Police Constable — 1,170 Seats

The Punjab Police constable recruitment offers 1,170 posts in the Rahim Yar Khan and Rajanpur districts, with applications closing 31 October 2026. Matric-qualified candidates with Punjab domicile who meet the physical standards can apply — and because the drive is district-based, candidates from those districts have a direct route in. Constable is the classic entry into the Punjab Police, and district drives like this one are the main intake channel for new recruits.

Technical & Professional: GEPCO, WAPDA, Teaching

  • GEPCO Assistant Manager (IT) — three streams (database administration, network support, meter data management) for CS/IT graduates with experience; through NTS; last date 23 October 2026.
  • WAPDA Deputy Manager, Lahore — a single management post advertised in Daily Jang on 6 October; last date 20 October 2026.
  • WAPDA Foundation Deputy Manager (Tech) BPS-18 + NJHPC Assistant Audit Officer — for retired power-sector engineers and finance graduates; last date 20 October 2026.
  • Teaching internships (STI / CTI) — the School Teacher Intern and College Teaching Intern programmes remain the biggest teaching-route recruitments of the year, with the CTI 2026-27 session (8,084 posts) running through cti-hep.punjab.gov.pk.

How to Apply — Step by Step

Every recruitment above follows the same basic drill, with different portals:

1
Match yourself to the ad. Check qualification, age, domicile and experience against the exact post — PPSC and police recruitments reject mismatched applications without appeal.
2
Use the right portal. PPSC: ppsc.gop.pk. GEPCO: NTS (nts.org.pk). WAPDA Foundation: email secretary.pwf@gmail.com or post. Teaching: the CTI/STI portals in the ad.
3
Pay the fee correctly. PPSC and NTS recruitments need the prescribed fee through the stated channel — keep the receipt.
4
Apply in deadline order — 20 Oct (WAPDA) first, then 23 Oct (GEPCO), 26 Oct (PPSC), 31 Oct (Punjab Police).
Warning: Punjab government recruitment is free of agents. No officer, testing agency or website can guarantee selection. Pay fees only through the official channels in the advertisement.

What to Expect After Applying

After the closing dates, each recruiting body moves on its own timetable: PPSC will announce screening-test schedules and syllabi on its website; the Punjab Police will run physical tests for constable candidates; NTS will issue roll number slips for GEPCO; and WAPDA bodies will shortlist directly. Keep your application receipts, CNIC and domicile copies together — and keep checking this portal and the official websites for test schedules through November.

Also this month from the Punjab government: the Livestock Card Phase II — Rs 16 billion in interest-free loans for livestock farmers (Rs 27,000 per animal), plus free tagging and FMD vaccination.

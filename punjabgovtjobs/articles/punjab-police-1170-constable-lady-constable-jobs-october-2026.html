Punjab Police 1,170 Constable Jobs 2026 — Apply by 31 Oct

The Punjab Police, advertised by the DIG Establishment II, Central Police Office, Lahore (Ad ref IPL-10492), is recruiting 1,170 Constable, Lady Constable and Driver Constable posts in BPS-07. This is a district-specific recruitment: the posts are divided between Rahim Yar Khan and Rajanpur, and only domicile holders of these two districts can apply. Application forms will be accepted from 12 to 31 October 2026 at the District Police Lines of the candidate's own district, in person, with a Rs 300 form fee.

These are permanent BPS-07 government posts — a major opportunity for young candidates from southern Punjab seeking a uniformed career. The recruitment follows the standard Punjab Police process: physical measurement and physical test, written test, and interview (Driver Constable candidates also face a driving test). Because the ad sets different standards for settled areas and de-excluded (tribal) areas, candidates must read the advertisement section for their own area carefully before applying.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Domicile — the strictest condition: only holders of Rahim Yar Khan or Rajanpur domicile may apply. Candidates from Lahore, Multan or any other district are not eligible for this advertisement.
  • Education: Matric with at least 50% marks in settled areas. Driver Constable candidates additionally need a valid driving licence.
  • Age (as of 31 October 2026): Constable/Lady Constable 18–22 years in settled areas; 18–25 years in Rajanpur's de-excluded (tribal) areas; 18–30 years in Tehsil Rojhan. Driver Constable 21–30 years. No relaxation is admissible in age, height or chest.
  • Physical standards — settled areas: male height 5 feet 7 inches, female 5 feet 2 inches; chest 33 x 34.5 inches. Running: 1.6 km in 7 minutes (male), 1.6 km in 10 minutes (female).
  • Physical standards — de-excluded (tribal) areas: male height 5 feet 5 inches; chest 31 x 32.5 inches. Check the original notice for the area-specific running standard.
  • Quotas: 15% women, 5% minorities, 10% ex-servicemen.
  • Government employees: must apply through proper channel and attach an NOC with the form.
Honesty flag: physical standards and age limits differ between settled areas and de-excluded (tribal) areas — read the advertisement section for your own area carefully, especially the Urdu text which carries the area-wise breakdown. The ad's Urdu text was partially hard to read in secondary reports, so verify every standard in the original notice at your district Police Lines or on punjabpolice.gov.pk before submitting your form.

How to Apply — Step by Step

1
Download the form from punjabpolice.gov.pk (the official Punjab Police website), or collect it from your district Police Lines.
2
Fill the form completely and attach attested copies of your CNIC, domicile, educational certificates, photographs and NOC (if you are a government employee).
3
Pay the Rs 300 form fee as instructed on the form.
4
Submit in person at the District Police Lines of your own district (Rahim Yar Khan or Rajanpur) between 12 and 31 October 2026. There is no online submission for this recruitment.
5
Prepare for the physical tests — measurement and running come first; only candidates who clear them move to the written test and interview.

Selection Process

Selection is conducted in stages: (1) physical measurement (height/chest against the prescribed standard), (2) physical test (1.6 km run within the time limit), (3) written test, and (4) interview. Driver Constable applicants additionally undergo a driving test. Merit is district-wise, and the quoted quotas for women, minorities and ex-servicemen are applied as per the advertisement.

Closing soon: forms are accepted only from 12 to 31 October 2026 — and submission is in person at the district Police Lines. Do not wait for the last day; visit the Police Lines early so any documentation issue can be fixed in time. Never pay any agent who promises selection — recruitment is on merit through the tests above.

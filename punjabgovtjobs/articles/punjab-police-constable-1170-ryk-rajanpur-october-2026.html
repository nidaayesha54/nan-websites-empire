Punjab Police Constable Jobs 2026: 1,170 Posts in Rahim Yar Khan & Rajanpur — Eligibility, Last Date 31 Oct 2026

Punjab Police has announced recruitment for 1,170 Constable, Lady Constable and Driver Constable (BPS-7) posts in Rahim Yar Khan and Rajanpur districts — applications are open from 12 October to 31 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Punjab Government Jobs » Articles

The Punjab Police has announced the latest recruitment notification for Constable, Lady Constable and Driver Constable (BPS-7) posts in the districts of Rahim Yar Khan and Rajanpur. A total of 1,170 vacancies are on offer, and only candidates who are domiciled in these two districts can apply. Applications on the prescribed form are invited from 12 October 2026 to 31 October 2026, and the application form can be downloaded from the official Punjab Police portal.

This is one of the largest district-level police recruitments of the year, with 718 constable posts for Rahim Yar Khan alone. Male and female candidates, driver candidates and minority and retired-armed-forces quota applicants should all read the eligibility standards carefully before submitting their forms at the district Police Lines.

Driver Constable Eligibility

  • Age: 21 to 30 years

Quotas

The recruitment includes reserved quotas: 15% for women, 5% for minorities and 10% for retired armed forces personnel. Candidates claiming a quota must provide the supporting documents with their application.

How to Apply for Punjab Police Constable Jobs 2026

Applications are submitted on the prescribed form with the Rs. 300 form fee. Follow these steps:

1
Download the prescribed application form from the official Punjab Police portal at punjabpolice.gov.pk.
2
Fill the form carefully and attach attested copies of your CNIC, domicile, educational certificates, photographs and the Rs. 300 form fee.
3
Submit the duly filled form along with documents at the respective District Police Lines (Rahim Yar Khan or Rajanpur) within the submission window.
4
Prepare for the physical measurement and running test — these are qualifying stages before the written test.
5
Keep your documents ready for verification at each stage of the selection process.
Important: The application window is 12 October to 31 October 2026. Age is calculated as of 31 October 2026. Forms submitted after the closing date will not be accepted.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many constable posts are announced by Punjab Police in 2026?

1,170 posts in total: 718 in Rahim Yar Khan, 300 in Rajanpur (including de-excluded area), 150 in Tehsil Rajanpur tribal area, and 2 Driver Constable posts — all in BPS-7.

What is the last date to apply for Punjab Police constable jobs 2026?

Applications are accepted from 12 October 2026 to 31 October 2026. Age is calculated as of 31 October 2026.

Who can apply for these Punjab Police jobs?

Only residents of Rahim Yar Khan and Rajanpur districts holding the domicile and CNIC of the respective district.

What is the running test for Punjab Police constable?

Male candidates must complete 1.6 km in 7 minutes; female candidates must complete 1.6 km in 10 minutes.

What is the application fee for Punjab Police constable jobs?

Rs. 300 with the prescribed application form, downloadable from punjabpolice.gov.pk and submitted at the district Police Lines.

GEPCO Jobs 2026: Assistant Manager (IT) Posts — Database, Network & Meter Data Management, Last Date 23 Oct 2026

The Gujranwala Electric Power Company Ltd (GEPCO) has announced vacancies for Assistant Manager positions in its IT streams. The advertised posts cover database administration, network support and meter data management — core technology functions of a modern electricity distribution company. Applications are processed through the National Testing Service (NTS), and the last date for submission of applications is 23 October 2026.

Power distribution companies are steadily digitising their grids, metering and billing systems — which makes GEPCO's IT posts a strong opportunity for computer science graduates who want a public-sector career in Punjab's energy sector.

Note: Candidates need a Bachelors or Master's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology or Computer Software Engineering from an HEC-recognised university, along with relevant experience in the stream they apply for. Candidates must be between 21 and 33 years of age.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Education: Bachelors or Master's in Computer Science, Information Technology or Computer Software Engineering from an HEC-recognised university.
  • Experience: Relevant professional experience in the relevant IT stream is required — document your work precisely against the post.
  • Age: 21 to 33 years.
  • Test agency: Recruitment is conducted through NTS (National Testing Service).

Because these are specialised IT roles, the NTS screening is expected to emphasise subject knowledge in the relevant domain — database systems for the DBA post, networking for the network support post, and data systems for meter data management.

How to Apply through NTS

GEPCO processes these applications through NTS. Follow these steps:

1
Open the NTS website at nts.org.pk and find the GEPCO Assistant Manager (IT) advertisement.
2
Download the application form and the fee challan for the advertised post.
3
Fill in the form carefully and attach scanned copies of all academic and experience credentials with the online application.
4
Deposit the prescribed test fee through the designated bank/channel stated on the challan.
5
Submit the application before 23 October 2026. The date, time and venue of the test/interview will be communicated later through letters, SMS and the NTS website.
Important: The deadline is 23 October 2026. GEPCO's head office is at 565-A, Model Town, Gujranwala — but applications go through NTS, not directly to the company. Keep the fee receipt and application printout safe.

Why These Posts Matter

GEPCO serves one of Pakistan's largest distribution territories, and its IT backbone — from billing databases to network operations and smart metering data — is expanding every year. An Assistant Manager (IT) role in a DISCO offers hands-on responsibility for production systems, a structured public-sector career path, and experience that stays valuable across the energy and IT industries. With the 23 October deadline approaching, eligible CS/IT graduates should apply through NTS without delay.

The three streams also cover the full technology stack of a distribution company: databases hold the billing and customer records, networks carry the operational traffic, and meter data systems feed the revenue cycle. Experience in any one of these areas is directly relevant to the post, so tailor your application to show domain-specific work rather than generic IT experience.

PPSC Livestock Veterinary Jobs 2026 — Apply by 26 Oct

The Punjab Public Service Commission, through Advertisement No. 11/2026 (published 9 October 2026), has announced 101 veterinary posts in the Livestock & Dairy Development Department — one of the largest blocks in the 299-post advertisement. The posts are split into 61 Senior Veterinary Officer / Assistant Director / Deputy Director posts under Case No. 04-RK/2026 and 40 Veterinary Officer posts, serving the department's Extension Wing (Animal Health Cadre).

Per reports, the senior posts are aimed at experienced veterinary professionals, with qualifications reported at Ph.D., M.Phil, M.Sc. or DVM level plus experience and valid PVMC registration, in BS-17/BS-18. The 40 Veterinary Officer posts are reported as requiring a DVM degree with valid PVMC registration. These appointments are on a contract basis. This article covers only the 101 Livestock & Dairy Development posts; the other Advertisement 11/2026 posts are covered in separate earlier articles on this site.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualification: reported as DVM with valid PVMC registration for Veterinary Officer posts, and Ph.D./M.Phil/M.Sc./DVM with experience and valid PVMC registration for the senior posts — verify the exact qualification and experience against the official PPSC advertisement.
  • Domicile: Punjab domicile is required.
  • PVMC registration: valid registration with the Pakistan Veterinary Medical Council is required for all posts in this block.
  • Appointment type: contract basis — this is not a permanent appointment.
  • Mode of application: online only through ppsc.gop.pk; paper applications are not accepted. Pay the fee through the generated PSID via 1Link (banks, mobile banking or ATMs) before the closing date.
Honesty flag: secondary reports differ on the grade split of the 61 senior posts (BS-18 vs BS-17) and the exact qualification and experience for Senior Veterinary Officer, Assistant Director and Deputy Director. Confirm the grade, qualification, experience and age limit in the official PPSC advertisement (Ad 11/2026, Case 04-RK/2026) before applying.

How to Apply Online — Step by Step

1
Open the official PPSC portal at ppsc.gop.pk and go to the online-apply section.
2
Select Advertisement No. 11/2026 and the right post — Case 04-RK/2026 for the senior posts, or the Veterinary Officer entry for the 40 junior posts.
3
Fill the online form with your personal, educational, PVMC registration and domicile details exactly as in your documents.
4
Generate the PSID and pay the fee through 1Link (bank, mobile banking or ATM) before the deadline.
5
Submit before 26 October 2026 and save the confirmation. PPSC accepts no late submissions.

Selection Process

Selection is expected to run through shortlisting, a written test (where applicable), and an interview. Keep your DVM degree, valid PVMC registration, experience certificates and domicile ready, and read the general instructions of Advertisement 11/2026 on the PPSC website.

Closing soon: the deadline is 26 October 2026. Complete the PSID/1Link fee payment at least a day early — bank-channel delays near the deadline are a common reason applications lapse.

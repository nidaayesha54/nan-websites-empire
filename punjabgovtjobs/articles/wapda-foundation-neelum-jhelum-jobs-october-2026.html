WAPDA Foundation & Neelum Jhelum Jobs October 2026: Deputy Manager (Tech) BPS-18 + Assistant Audit Officer — Last Date 20 Oct 2026

The Pakistan WAPDA Foundation (PWF) and the Neelum Jhelum Hydropower Company (Pvt.) Ltd. (NJHPC) have announced recruitment through separate advertisements in October 2026. The Foundation is hiring a Deputy Manager (Technical) for its Power Transformers Engineering Services Unit (PTESU) at BPS-18, aimed at retired engineers from WAPDA, the distribution companies (DISCOs) or the National Grid Company (NGC). NJHPC is hiring an Assistant Audit Officer (equivalent to BPS-16) through the Open Testing Service (OTS). Both positions are contractual on open merit, and the last date to apply is 20 October 2026.

These are specialised postings: one rewards decades of engineering service inside the power sector, while the other opens a finance-career door in Pakistan's flagship hydropower company. Because both close on 20 October, eligible candidates should act immediately.

Note: The NJHPC advertisement confirms one vacancy for Assistant Audit Officer. The PWF advertisement does not explicitly state a vacancy count for the Deputy Manager post. The PWF address is Lahore; the NJHPC recruitment address is Islamabad.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Deputy Manager (Tech): a BSc in Electrical Engineering plus the specified experience; the post is designed for retired WAPDA, DISCO or NGC employees holding a regular promotion in BPS-18 with a clean service record.
  • Assistant Audit Officer: M.Com/MBA with a Finance major, or B.Com with the required experience, or the honours-degree route stated in the advertisement — with relevant experience.
  • Recruitment basis: open merit for both posts.

Candidates should confirm the exact qualification, experience and age conditions in the official advertisement before applying — specialised contractual posts often have narrow, non-negotiable criteria.

How to Apply

The two posts follow different application routes:

1
Deputy Manager (Tech): prepare a typed application letter, CV, attested copies of educational certificates and degrees, CNIC, copy of the retirement order and copy of the regular promotion order in BPS-18.
2
Submit by email: scan all supporting documents with your application and send to secretary.pwf@gmail.com — or dispatch physical copies to the Managing Director, Pakistan WAPDA Foundation, 1st Floor Haleem Plaza, 10-Temple Road, Lahore.
3
Assistant Audit Officer: apply through the OTS portal at ots.org.pk — complete the online registration, submit the prescribed form and deposit slip as instructed.
4
Make sure your application reaches the specified office before 20 October 2026. For NJHPC, applications must reach within 15 days of the advertisement's publication.
Important: Only the two application channels above are valid — PWF by email or post, NJHPC through OTS. Deal with no agent or middleman claiming to arrange these posts.

Why These Posts Matter

Power-transformer engineering is the backbone of the electricity grid: the PTESU works on the very equipment that steps voltage up and down across the transmission network. A BPS-18 Deputy Manager slot there is senior technical responsibility, and the retired-engineer channel reflects how hard this experience is to replace. On the finance side, NJHPC operates one of Pakistan's largest hydropower projects, and an audit officer inside it works at the intersection of project finance and public-sector accountability — a strong line on any accountant's CV. Both windows close on 20 October, so eligible candidates should apply now.

For retired WAPDA, DISCO and NGC engineers, the PWF route is a rare “second innings” mechanism inside the power sector family — contractual, but substantive work on the grid. For commerce graduates, NJHPC's single audit post is competitive by definition; apply through OTS carefully, keep your deposit slip and application printout, and check the OTS portal for the test schedule after the closing date.

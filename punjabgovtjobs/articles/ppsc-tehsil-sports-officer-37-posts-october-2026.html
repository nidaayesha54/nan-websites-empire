PPSC Tehsil Sports Officer Jobs 2026: 37 Posts (Youth Affairs & Sports) — Ad 11/2026, Last Date 26 Oct 2026

The Punjab Public Service Commission (PPSC) has announced 37 posts of Tehsil Sports Officer / Caretaker (BS-16) in the Youth Affairs & Sports Department through Advertisement No. 11/2026, published in Daily Jang on 9 October 2026. The sports-officer case is one of 13 cases in the advertisement, which carries 299 posts in total. The last date to apply is 26 October 2026, and applications are accepted online only through the PPSC portal at ppsc.gop.pk.

The standout feature of this opening is the eligibility route: alongside a Master's in Physical Education or Sports Sciences, graduates who have represented Pakistan as international players can also apply. For athletes whose playing careers kept them away from higher studies, this is a rare BS-16 entry point into the Punjab government.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Education: Master's degree in Physical Education / Sports Sciences with at least second division, OR a Graduate who is an international player.
  • Age: 18 to 30 years for male candidates; 18 to 33 years for female candidates. Age relaxation is already included in these limits.
  • Quota: seats are reserved for women, minorities and special persons under the PPSC quota policy stated in the advertisement.
  • Fee: paid online only through PPSC-approved channels — 1Link member banks, ATMs, mobile/internet banking, JazzCash, Easypaisa, UPaisa and microfinance banks. The amount is listed on ppsc.gop.pk.

In-service government servants apply online like everyone else but must produce an NOC/DPC at the interview. Read the Important Instructions on ppsc.gop.pk before filling the form.

About the Tehsil Sports Officer Role

A Tehsil Sports Officer is the sports department's representative at the tehsil level — organising local tournaments, maintaining sports grounds, coordinating with schools and clubs, and spotting young talent for provincial and national competition. The caretaker element relates to the upkeep of public sports infrastructure. It is field work as much as office work, and BS-16 status brings structured promotion prospects within the Youth Affairs & Sports Department.

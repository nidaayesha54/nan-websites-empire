GTA 6 Price in Pakistan: What Players Actually Pay

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and in Pakistan the first question was never the date — it was the price, and whether the game could even be bought here at all. The short answer: there is no rupee price, because Pakistan has no PlayStation Store. The longer answer is that Pakistani players have been buying the game anyway, through foreign accounts and local shops, at prices that swing from about Rs 22,000 to Rs 38,000 depending on the route.

This guide explains every route, what each one actually costs, and the one offer — around Rs 13,000 — that is not a bargain at all.

Why there is no rupee price

Sony runs PlayStation Stores only in countries on its supported list, and Pakistan is not one of them. Rockstar's pre-order country lists do not include Pakistan either. That means no local-currency price, no Pakistani PlayStation Store, no local payment methods on the store, and no rupee figure anywhere in the buying process.

Players therefore use accounts from other regions and pay in that region's currency, by international card or with a gift card from that region. Most Pakistani PlayStation owners use a UAE account, and some use a US account. The game itself is identical — the only thing that changes is the store you pay in.

What players actually pay

Here is every route, as reported in November 2026. On a UAE PlayStation Store account, the Standard Edition is listed at AED 299.16 and the Ultimate Edition at AED 373.96, as checked on November 10 — about $81 and $102. On a US account, the price is the headline one: $79.99 for Standard and $99.99 for Ultimate, plus card fees.

Converted to rupees, the US price works out to about Rs 22,200 for Standard and Rs 27,700 for Ultimate, at the State Bank of Pakistan's weighted average rate of Rs 277.31 to the dollar from September 24, 2026. Those are estimates, not store prices — your bank sets its own dollar card rate and may add charges and taxes on a foreign payment, so the amount taken from your account will be higher.

Local shops are the other route. Boxed copies in Pakistani shops are listed from about Rs 23,000 upward, with most listings between Rs 23,000 and Rs 38,000; one retailer, GameSource, lists the game at Rs 22,999 to Rs 28,499. The box holds a download code, not a disc, and the code is region-locked — check the region before anyone buys one abroad for you. The advantage of the box is simple: you can buy it from a local shop, in rupees, without a foreign store account.

When it unlocked in Pakistan

The game unlocked at midnight local time on November 19, 2026, rolling across the world one time zone at a time. For Pakistani players the exact minute depended on the store account: an Indian account unlocked at 11:30pm PKT on November 18, a UAE account at 1:00am PKT on November 19. Pre-loading opened on November 10 — midnight on PS5, 5:00am PKT on Xbox — and the download is up to 212 GB, so players who pre-loaded were playing at midnight instead of downloading.

Gaming zones in Lahore, Karachi, Islamabad, Rawalpindi, Faisalabad, Multan and Peshawar stayed open through the night for the launch. If you are still buying, the pre-load window has passed — budget a full download before you plan a launch-night session.

The Rs 13,000 trap

Offers around Rs 13,000 are reported to be shared accounts, not your own copy of the game. Sharing accounts breaks PlayStation's terms, and accounts bought this way can be locked — losing the game and the money. The same warning applies to every "free download", APK and PC version offer: there is no free download, no APK and no PC version, and all of them are fake. A price far below Rs 22,000 is not a discount; it is a different product, and a risky one.

The safe routes are three: a UAE or US store account with your own card or a gift card from that region, a boxed code from a trusted local shop with the region checked first, or waiting for a sale. In September 2026, retailers in Karachi, Lahore and Islamabad were selling the PS5 Pro itself for around Rs 350,000 — the console costs far more than the game, and it is worth remembering that before paying a premium for either.

FAQ

How much does GTA 6 cost in Pakistan? About Rs 22,200 for the digital Standard Edition ($79.99) and Rs 27,700 for Ultimate ($99.99), before bank charges. Boxed copies in local shops run Rs 23,000 to Rs 38,000. There is no rupee price from Sony or Rockstar.

Is there a PlayStation Store in Pakistan? No. Pakistan is not on Sony's supported country list, so players use accounts from other regions — most often the UAE — and pay in that store's currency.

Can I play GTA 6 on PS4? No. The game is PS5 and Xbox Series X|S only, and no PC version has been announced.

The bottom line: in Pakistan, the game costs about Rs 22,200 through a foreign store account or Rs 23,000 and up from a local shop, and anything near Rs 13,000 is a shared account that can get locked. Pay the real price, through a real account, and the game is yours.

Suthra Punjab Faisalabad 524 Sanitary Worker Jobs – Walk-in 28 Oct

The Suthra Punjab Agency Faisalabad has announced 524 Sanitary Worker vacancies under the provincial government's cleanliness initiative, advertised in Daily Dunya around 15 October 2026. The agency will recruit on a daily-wage basis, with walk-in interviews on 28 October 2026, 10:00 AM to 4:00 PM at the Suthra Punjab Agency Complex, University Road, near the DC Office, Faisalabad. There is no online application — candidates appear in person with their documents.

This is a major opening for workers with limited formal education: no degree or formal certificate is required. Candidates must be able to read and write, have relevant work experience in sanitation or cleaning work, and be physically fit for sanitation duties. The maximum age limit is 45 years — and with 524 posts, this is one of the largest single-city sanitation recruitment drives in Punjab this year.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Education: ability to read and write — no mandatory degree or formal educational certificate.
  • Experience: relevant work experience in sanitation or cleaning work; experienced candidates are preferred.
  • Physical fitness: candidates must be physically fit to perform sanitation duties.
  • Age limit: maximum 45 years.
  • Employment type: daily wages — temporary and non-transferable, with no regularization.
  • Application mode: walk-in interview only; no separate online application has been announced.
Honesty flag: this is a daily-wage, temporary and non-transferable engagement — it does not lead to permanent employment or regularization, and the wage rate is not announced. Most reports say the requirement is simply the ability to read and write (we follow the majority wording), and there is no official online portal — the outbound link above is news coverage, not an application page. Never pay anyone who promises to "secure" your selection — selection happens at the walk-in interview only.

How to Attend the Walk-in Interview — Step by Step

1
Prepare your documents the day before: original CNIC plus one photocopy, a handwritten job application, and two recent passport-size photographs.
2
Reach the venue early — the Suthra Punjab Agency Complex, University Road, near the DC Office, Faisalabad — on 28 October 2026, before the 10:00 AM start.
3
Register at the interview desk with your CNIC and handwritten application; interviews run until 4:00 PM, but arriving early improves your chances of being seen.
4
Attend the interview — be ready to discuss your sanitation or cleaning experience and confirm you meet the age and fitness requirements.
5
Note that no TA/DA is provided — arrange your own travel to the venue.

Tips for a Successful Walk-in

Walk-in interviews for 524 posts will draw large crowds. Arrive well before 10:00 AM — candidates are typically processed in order of arrival. Carry your original CNIC (not just the photocopy) and a legible handwritten application stating the post, your name, CNIC number and contact number. If you have any proof of past sanitation or cleaning work, bring it along — experienced candidates are preferred. The reported agency contact is 041-9200106 if you need to confirm the venue before travelling. Remember: nobody can charge you a fee for this selection — anyone asking for money is running a scam.

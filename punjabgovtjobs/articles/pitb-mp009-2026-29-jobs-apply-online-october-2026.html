PITB Jobs October 2026 (MP009-2026): 29 Contract Posts — Apply Online by 4 Nov

The Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) has opened applications for 29 contract posts under advertisement code MP009-2026 (IPL-11054), published on 17 October 2026 in the Daily Express. The vacancies span software development, mobile app development, programme management, operations, customer support and creative roles — a broad recruitment drive covering both technical specialists and non-technical staff. The last date to apply is 4 November 2026, and applications are accepted online only through the official portal jobs.punjab.gov.pk.

PITB is the Punjab government's flagship IT arm, running everything from citizen service portals to province-wide digital programmes. Contract posts here are high-profile, project-funded roles — so competition will be stiff, particularly for the software engineering and programme management seats. Details below come from job portals reporting the advertisement — the official advertisement and jobs.punjab.gov.pk remain the final authority on counts, qualifications and closing dates.

PITB MP009-2026 — Posts Announced

The advertisement lists posts across several tracks. Technical roles include Senior Software Engineer, Software Engineer and Associate Software Engineer, a Principal Mobile App Developer, and specialist infrastructure positions: Network Administrator (Central Surveillance Room), Incident Response Officer, Infrastructure Automation Officer (Datacenter) and Joint Director (GPU Infrastructure). On the management side, PITB is hiring Programme Managers / Senior Programme Managers, Programme Officers / Senior Programme Officers / Assistant Programme Officers (with district-level postings across Punjab), Assistant Manager, Manager, Assistant Officer (Operations) and Regional Coordinator posts.

Support and creative roles round out the list: DC Support Officer, Computer Operator, Domain Specialist, Machine Operator, Marketing & Strategy Lead, Startup Growth Lead, Video Animator / Photographer, Receptionist and Customer Support Representative. This round has something for intermediate holders, fresh graduates and experienced specialists alike.

A note on post counts: the job portal ixjob (updated 19 October 2026) reports 29 posts, while one other portal's headline claims "100+". Counts differ across portals — check the official advertisement and jobs.punjab.gov.pk, the only authoritative sources, before applying.

Eligibility & Terms

  • Education: 12 to 16 years of education depending on the post — intermediate for machine operator, receptionist and support roles; bachelor's or master's for engineering, programme management and lead positions.
  • Age limit: maximum 45 years for most posts, and up to 55 years for the Joint Director (GPU Infrastructure) post.
  • Job type: contract basis — these are not regular government posts.
  • Locations: most posts are based in Lahore; programme management posts are spread across multiple districts of Punjab.
  • Apply mode: online only at jobs.punjab.gov.pk — there is no paper application route for this advertisement.

How to Apply for PITB Jobs (MP009-2026)

PITB's portal application is straightforward, but the 4 November deadline leaves little room for delay:

1
Open jobs.punjab.gov.pk and create or sign in to your applicant account.
2
Find advertisement MP009-2026 and select the post matching your qualification — read the post-specific education and experience requirements carefully.
3
Complete the online application form with accurate education, experience and district/domicile details, and upload the required documents as specified.
4
Submit before 4 November 2026 — applications are online only, so submit early rather than waiting for the final hours when the portal is slowest.
Tip: PITB contract shortlists usually run on merit plus a written test or interview. Tailor your CV to the exact post title — "Associate Software Engineer" and "Software Engineer" are different roles with different experience bars.

Which Post Fits You

Software developers have the clearest runway: Associate, Software and Senior Software Engineer posts, plus a Principal Mobile App Developer seat for experienced mobile developers. IT infrastructure specialists should look at the Datacenter, surveillance room and incident response roles — the Joint Director (GPU Infrastructure) is the senior-most post in this round, with the age bar raised to 55. Management professionals get the programme manager and programme officer ladder, including district-level postings across Punjab. Fresh graduates and non-technical candidates can target Customer Support Representative, Computer Operator, Machine Operator, Receptionist, Video Animator / Photographer, and the Marketing & Strategy and Startup Growth Lead posts. Match the post to your exact qualification, apply online only at jobs.punjab.gov.pk before 4 November 2026, and always verify the official advertisement.

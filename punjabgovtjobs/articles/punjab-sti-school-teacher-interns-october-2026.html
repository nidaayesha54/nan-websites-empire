Punjab STI Jobs 2026: School Teacher Interns — Rs 38,000–45,000 Stipend, Apply by 18 Oct 2026

The Punjab School Education Department has opened applications for School Teacher Interns (STI) across government schools — monthly stipends of Rs 38,000 to Rs 45,000, with online applications closing on 18 October 2026 at 11:59 pm.

✅ Updated October 15, 2026
Home » Punjab Government Jobs » Articles

The Punjab School Education Department has announced new STI Jobs for government schools across the province. Under this recruitment, eligible male and female candidates will be engaged as School Teacher Interns (STI) on a temporary basis, with monthly stipends ranging from Rs. 38,000 to Rs. 45,000 depending on the school level. According to advertisement IPL-10494, vacancies are available at primary, elementary, high and higher secondary schools, and applications can be submitted online until 18 October 2026 at 11:59 pm.

Selected interns will serve from 3 November 2026 to 31 May 2027. This is a fixed-term internship opportunity — a strong option for fresh graduates and experienced teachers alike who want classroom experience in the government school system while earning a competitive monthly stipend.

Punjab STI Jobs 2026 — Monthly Stipend

School LevelMonthly Stipend
Primary schoolsRs. 38,000
Elementary schoolsRs. 40,000
High & higher secondary schoolsRs. 45,000
Note: These positions are temporary and valid only for the announced internship period (3 November 2026 to 31 May 2027). The department has stated that the internships will not be converted into permanent government appointments, but successful interns will receive a certificate after completing their placement.

Eligibility Criteria for STI Jobs 2026

Age Limit

Applicants must be between 21 and 45 years of age. Both male and female candidates are eligible.

Educational Qualification (by School Level)

School LevelRequired Qualification
Primary schoolsBA or BSc degree
Elementary schoolsMA, MSc, BS Honours, BA or BSc
High & higher secondary schoolsMA, MSc or BS Honours in the relevant subject

For high and higher secondary positions, the qualification must be in the relevant subject the candidate will teach. Candidates holding B.Ed or M.Ed qualifications will receive preference during recruitment — however, these professional qualifications do not replace the basic academic requirements listed above.

How to Apply Online for Punjab STI Jobs 2026

Applications are submitted online. Follow these steps:

1
Open the online application portal announced by the Punjab School Education Department under advertisement IPL-10494.
2
Select the school level you are applying for — primary, elementary, high or higher secondary — and confirm you meet the qualification for that level.
3
Fill in your personal, educational and contact details, and choose the district/school where you want to serve.
4
Upload the required documents (degree, CNIC, domicile and photograph) in the prescribed format.
5
Review the application carefully and submit before 18 October 2026 at 11:59 pm. Keep a printout of the submitted form.
Deadline: Applications close on 18 October 2026 at 11:59 pm. Late or incomplete applications will not be accepted.

Who Should Apply

This internship is ideal for fresh BA/BSc and MA/MSc graduates looking for their first teaching experience, as well as experienced teachers who want to work in the government school system. The higher secondary stipend of Rs. 45,000 per month is competitive, and the completion certificate adds value to a teaching CV. B.Ed and M.Ed holders should definitely apply, since they get preference in the selection process.

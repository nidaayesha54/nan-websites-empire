Punjab Health Department MO Jobs 2026 — Apply by 25 Oct

The Health & Population Department, Government of the Punjab, has invited applications from medical graduates for the post of Medical Officer on a Special Pay Package — including a posting at DHQ Hospital Sheikhupura, the primary secondary-referral facility in that district. Reports also indicate openings for Women Medical Officers and a Lab Technologist position in the same recruitment. Applications are accepted online only through the Health Department's official portal, and only shortlisted candidates will be contacted for interviews.

The last date to apply is 25 October 2026 at 11:59 PM. Per reports, the Medical Officer post at DHQ Hospital Sheikhupura requires an MBBS degree recognized by PMDC, a 1-year house job completion certificate, and valid PMDC permanent registration, along with a Punjab domicile. Because this is a special pay package post rather than a regular BPS appointment, the usual pay-scale structure does not apply. This article covers only this Health & Population Department recruitment; PPSC's 107 Senior Registrar posts are covered in a separate earlier article on this site.

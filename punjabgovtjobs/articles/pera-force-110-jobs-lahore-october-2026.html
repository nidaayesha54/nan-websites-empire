PERA Force Jobs October 2026: 110 Contract Posts — IT, Audit & Internal Affairs, Last Date 29 Oct 2026

The Punjab Enforcement and Regulatory Authority (PERA) has announced 110 contract jobs in Lahore and across Punjab, spanning IT leadership roles (AI/ML and Full Stack Team Leads), audit and HR posts, and 100 Internal Affairs posts in district offices. Applications are online only through the Punjab Job Portal (jobs.punjab.gov.pk), and the last date to apply is 29 October 2026.

PERA is a relatively new enforcement authority, and this recruitment mixes two worlds: senior specialist posts at its Directorate General in Lahore under a Special PERA Pay Package (SPPP), and a large district-level field force. The 100 district Internal Affairs posts — 38 Deputy Managers and 62 Assistant Managers — are the bulk of the intake, spread across the District Enforcement and Regulatory Board secretariats.

Pay structure: the notice does not print fixed salaries — pay falls under the negotiable Special PERA Pay Package (SPPP-2 to SPPP-5). Also note: some listings of this advertisement quote 112 posts while the post list adds up to 110 — verify the final number on the official advertisement.

Eligibility — Read Before You Apply

The advertisement itself does not print the degree, experience or age conditions for the posts — it directs candidates to the PERA website for the detailed terms of each position. This makes the “before you apply” homework more important than usual:

  • IT posts (AI/ML Lead, Full Stack Lead, System Analyst, Data Analyst, System Support Officer): expect requirements around relevant degrees (CS/IT/Data Science) and professional experience — confirm the exact conditions on the PERA website.
  • Internal Affairs posts (district offices): 100 posts across Punjab's districts — the district-based allocation means your posting will be in a district secretariat.
  • Audit/HR posts: professional qualifications and experience conditions apply — check the official notice.
  • Nature of appointment: all posts are contract-based under the Special PERA Pay Package.

How to Apply Online

Applications are online only — follow these steps:

1
Visit the PERA website first and read the detailed eligibility, experience and age conditions for your chosen post.
2
Register on the Punjab Job Portal at jobs.punjab.gov.pk, fill the application form, upload your CNIC, photograph and documents.
3
Select your post carefully — the Internal Affairs posts are district-office posts, while the IT and leadership posts are at the Directorate General in Lahore.
4
Submit before 29 October 2026. Keep a screenshot of your submitted application.
No fee is mentioned in the reports of this advertisement — but confirm on the Punjab Job Portal whether any processing fee applies to your post.

Why PERA Posts Are Worth a Look

Two things make this recruitment stand out. First, the IT leadership posts — Team Lead AI & Machine Learning and Team Lead Full Stack under SPPP-3 — signal that PERA is building a modern digital enforcement stack, and senior technologists in the public sector are rare enough that competition for these two seats will come from the private sector too. Second, the 100 district Internal Affairs posts give the recruitment its volume: for candidates across Punjab's districts, these are accessible contract posts in a new authority rather than a legacy department. Check the PERA website for the fine print, register on the Punjab Job Portal, and apply before 29 October.

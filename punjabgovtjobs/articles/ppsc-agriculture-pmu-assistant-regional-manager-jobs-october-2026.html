PPSC Agriculture Assistant Regional Manager Jobs 2026—Apply by 26 Oct

The Punjab Public Service Commission, through Advertisement No. 11/2026 (announced 9 October 2026), has opened 8 Assistant Regional Manager (BS-17) posts in the Programme Management Unit (PMU), Agriculture Department — listed as Case No. 59-RG/2026 (Sr. 189). Per reports, the posts are open to agricultural, civil or mechanical engineers holding a Punjab domicile, and the appointment is on contract (2 to 5 years per the advertisement).

Applications are accepted online only at ppsc.gop.pk, with the application fee paid through PSID/1Link channels. The closing date is 26 October 2026 — so eligible engineers should apply well before the deadline, as PPSC does not accept late or paper applications. This article covers only these 8 PMU Agriculture posts; the other Advertisement 11/2026 posts (livestock/veterinary, Senior Registrar, Intelligence Officer, Tehsil Sports Officer and PHATA) are covered in separate earlier articles on this site.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Qualification: reported as agricultural, civil or mechanical engineering degrees — verify the exact discipline and experience requirement against Case 59-RG/2026 in the official PPSC advertisement.
  • Domicile: Punjab domicile is required.
  • Appointment type: contract basis, 2 to 5 years per the advertisement — this is not a permanent appointment.
  • Mode of application: online only through ppsc.gop.pk; paper applications are not accepted.
  • Fee: pay through the generated PSID via 1Link (banks, mobile banking or ATMs) before the closing date.
Honesty flag: the post-wise qualification and experience details should be confirmed in the official PPSC advertisement (Case 59-RG/2026) — secondary reports summarize them and may omit details such as exact engineering discipline or experience. Do not apply on a mismatched case number: select Advertisement 11/2026 → Case 59-RG/2026 (Sr. 189) in the online form, and double-check the fee amount and age limit from the official notice.

How to Apply Online — Step by Step

1
Open the official PPSC portal at ppsc.gop.pk and go to the jobs/online-apply section.
2
Select Advertisement No. 11/2026 and choose Case 59-RG/2026 — Assistant Regional Manager (BS-17), PMU Agriculture Department (Sr. 189). Applying under the wrong case number invalidates the application.
3
Fill the online form with your personal, educational and domicile details exactly as in your documents.
4
Generate the PSID and pay the fee through 1Link channels (bank branch, mobile banking or ATM) before the deadline.
5
Submit before 26 October 2026 and save/print the application confirmation for your records. PPSC accepts no late submissions.

Selection Process

Like other PPSC contract posts, selection for Assistant Regional Manager will typically run through shortlisting on academic/eligibility criteria, a written test (where applicable), and an interview. Candidates should keep their engineering degree, experience certificates (if any) and domicile ready in advance, and read the general instructions of Advertisement 11/2026 — including the fee refund and rechecking policies — on the PPSC website.

Closing soon: the deadline is 26 October 2026. Because payment goes through PSID/1Link, complete the fee payment at least a day before the closing date — bank-channel delays near the deadline are a common reason applications lapse. Apply only on the official ppsc.gop.pk portal.

PPSC PHATA Jobs 2026: Deputy Director Town Planning/Engineering & Assistant Accounts Officer — Ad 11/2026, Last Date 26 Oct

The Punjab Public Service Commission has announced posts in the Punjab Housing & Town Planning Agency (PHATA) — part of the Housing Urban Development & Public Health Engineering Department — through Advertisement No. 11/2026, covering 2 posts of Deputy Director Town Planning, 2 posts of Deputy Director Engineering and 12 posts of Assistant Accounts Officer. Applications close on 26 October 2026 and are accepted online only at ppsc.gop.pk.

These are senior technical and finance posts at BS-17/18 scale, offered on contract for 2 to 5 years. Town planners need a BSc in City & Regional Planning with valid PCATP registration, engineers need a BSc in Civil Engineering with valid PEC registration, and the accounts seats ask for ACCA, M.Com or BBA (or an equivalent accounting qualification). For professionals already working in housing, planning or public finance, this is a direct route into a provincial agency.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Deputy Director Town Planning (2 posts): BSc in City & Regional Planning with valid PCATP registration; age male 21–35, female 21–43.
  • Deputy Director Engineering (2 posts): BSc in Civil Engineering with valid PEC registration; age male 21–33, female 21–38.
  • Assistant Accounts Officer (12 posts): ACCA / M.Com / BBA or an equivalent accounting qualification.
  • General conditions: BS-17/18 posts on contract for 2 to 5 years; only post-qualification experience counts; registration valid on or before the closing date; in-service candidates need NOC/DPC at the interview; fee online only; quotas for women, minorities and special persons.

Age windows differ between the two Deputy Director posts, so confirm the exact limit for your post in the advertisement.

About the PHATA Posts

PHATA is Punjab's housing and town-planning body — planning housing schemes, developing urban land and executing public housing projects. Deputy Directors in town planning shape scheme layouts and approvals, Deputy Directors in engineering oversee the civil works, and Assistant Accounts Officers keep project finances, audits and payrolls in order. Contract posts of 2 to 5 years at BS-17/18 scale place professionals inside one of Punjab's largest development agencies.

PPSC Senior Registrar Jobs 2026: 107 Posts in Punjab Teaching Hospitals — Eligibility, Last Date 26 Oct 2026

107 Senior Registrar (BS-18) posts in five medical specialties across Punjab teaching hospitals — qualification routes, contract terms and the 26 October 2026 deadline.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Punjab Government Jobs » Articles

The Punjab Public Service Commission has announced 107 Senior Registrar jobs for doctors at teaching hospitals across Punjab under Advertisement No. 11/2026. These BS-18 vacancies fall under the Specialized Healthcare and Medical Education Department, and applications remain open until 26 October 2026.

Selected candidates will be appointed on a three-year contract, which may be extended based on good performance. The vacancies span five medical specialties, with Medicine and Surgery accounting for the bulk of the seats. Complete eligibility details are below.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Basic qualification: MBBS or an equivalent medical qualification from an institution recognized by the relevant medical regulator.
  • Postgraduate qualification: A recognized postgraduate qualification in the relevant specialty — such as FCPS, MS, FRCS, MRCP, MD, Diplomat of the American Board or another recognized equivalent.
  • Alternative route: If candidates with the preferred specialist qualification are not available, PPSC may consider applicants with a relevant postgraduate qualification in General Medicine or Surgery along with three years of post-graduation practical experience in the required specialty.
  • Registration: Valid professional registration with the relevant medical council is required.
  • Contract: Initial appointment is on a three-year contract, extendable on satisfactory performance.

53 Senior Registrar Medicine Posts

Medicine carries the highest number of vacancies with 53 posts. Candidates need an MBBS or equivalent from a recognized institution plus a postgraduate qualification such as FCPS, MS, FRCS, MRCP or MD in the relevant specialty. Where specialist-qualified candidates are not available, the fallback route — a postgraduate qualification in General Medicine with three years of post-graduation practical experience in the specialty — keeps the door open for experienced physicians.

How Doctors Can Apply

1
Visit www.ppsc.gop.pk and open the Apply Online section.
2
Select Advertisement No. 11/2026 and choose the Senior Registrar post in your specialty.
3
Enter your personal details, MBBS and postgraduate qualifications, experience and PMDC registration exactly as on your documents.
4
Upload a recent photograph and the required documents in the prescribed format and size.
5
Deposit the prescribed application fee through the designated bank channels.
6
Submit before 26 October 2026 and keep a print of the submitted application.
Important: Age limits, applicable relaxations and reserved-seat provisions vary by post and candidate category. Read the detailed advertisement for your specialty before applying, and keep your PMDC registration valid through the selection process.

After the closing date, PPSC holds a written or screening test for specialties with large applicant pools, followed by interviews for shortlisted doctors. Final merit is determined under the Commission's regulations and published on the PPSC website. Keep checking the portal for your roll number slip and test schedule.

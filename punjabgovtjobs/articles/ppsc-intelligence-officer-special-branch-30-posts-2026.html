PPSC Intelligence Officer Jobs 2026: 30 Special Branch Posts, Eligibility, Test & Last Date 26 Oct 2026

30 Intelligence Officer (BS-14) posts in the Special Branch of Punjab Police — two eligibility routes, age limits, 100-mark written test and the 26 October 2026 deadline.

✅ Updated October 14, 2026
Home » Punjab Government Jobs » Articles

The Punjab Public Service Commission has announced 30 Intelligence Officer vacancies in the Special Branch of Punjab Police under Advertisement No. 11/2026, published on 9 October 2026. The posts are in BS-14 and will be filled on a two-year contract, which may be extended. Successful candidates can be posted anywhere in Punjab, and applications remain open until 26 October 2026.

What makes this advertisement notable is its two eligibility routes: fresh graduates with basic computer knowledge can apply, and so can experienced personnel from the intelligence wing of a law-enforcement agency. Here are the complete details.

Eligibility — Two Routes to Apply

Applicants can qualify through either of two routes set out in the advertisement:

  • Route 1 — Graduates: A graduation degree (second division) from an HEC-recognized university, along with basic computer knowledge.
  • Route 2 — Experienced: A graduation degree plus at least five years of experience in the Intelligence Wing of a law-enforcement agency. Only practical experience gained after completing the required qualification counts.
  • Domicile: Punjab domicile is required.

Written Test Pattern

Shortlisted candidates will sit a written test of 100 marks with a duration of 90 minutes. The test is expected to cover intelligence-related aptitude, general knowledge, current affairs and basic computer knowledge. Candidates who clear the written test proceed to the interview stage under PPSC's regulations.

How to Apply Online

Applications are accepted online only through the PPSC portal:

1
Visit www.ppsc.gop.pk and open the Apply Online section.
2
Select Advertisement No. 11/2026 and choose the Intelligence Officer post in the Special Branch.
3
Fill the form with personal, educational and experience details exactly as on your documents.
4
Upload a recent photograph and the required documents in the prescribed format.
5
Deposit the prescribed application fee through the designated bank channels.
6
Submit before 26 October 2026 and save a print of the submitted application.
Important: If you are applying through the experience route, make sure your experience letters clearly show that the experience was gained after your graduation — pre-qualification experience does not count.

Why These Posts Matter

Special Branch posts are among the most sought-after uniformed civilian positions in Punjab because they combine the prestige of the police department with an intelligence and analysis role. With 30 seats on a single advertisement and two entry routes, this is a rare opening for both fresh graduates and serving personnel. For queries, PPSC can be reached on UAN 042-111-988-722.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the last date to apply for PPSC Intelligence Officer jobs 2026?

Applications for the 30 Intelligence Officer (BS-14) posts close on 26 October 2026 through www.ppsc.gop.pk.

What is the qualification for Intelligence Officer in Punjab Police Special Branch?

A second-division graduation from an HEC-recognized university with basic computer knowledge, or graduation with five years of experience in the intelligence wing of a law-enforcement agency.

What is the age limit for Intelligence Officer?

Male candidates: 21 to 35 years with five years of relaxation (maximum 40). Female candidates: 21 to 35 years with eight years of relaxation (maximum 43).

What is the test pattern for Intelligence Officer?

A written test of 100 marks with a 90-minute duration, followed by an interview for shortlisted candidates.

Is the Intelligence Officer post permanent?

The posts are offered on a two-year contract, which may be extended.

How Long Is GTA 6? Story Length, Hours and 100%

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and it is a very big game — but how big, exactly? The question every player asks before starting is how long the story takes, and the answer depends on how you play. The main story alone runs about 65 to 80 hours. Add the side content, and you are looking at 90 to 100 hours. Go for everything, and the total passes 200 hours. This guide breaks down the mission count, the chapters, and the hours, so you can plan your playthrough properly.

The numbers come from the first wave of launch reporting, and they do not all agree exactly — which is normal for a game this size, played by different people at different speeds. Where the sources disagree, this guide says so, and gives you the range rather than a single number that pretends to be exact.

How Many Missions: 91 Across 5 Chapters

The story is built from 91 main missions, according to PC Gamer — though some outlets count 93, and the difference comes down to how a few branching or optional missions are counted. Either way, the shape is the same: five chapters, with the third chapter the longest at 26 missions. That structure matters, because it tells you where the story's weight sits. The early chapters set up Jason and Lucia; the middle chapter is the long, dense heart of the game; and the final chapters drive toward the escape the whole story is about.

Five chapters and ninety-odd missions is a big campaign by any standard — GTA 5's story had 69 missions, so GTA 6 is roughly a third longer before you count a single side activity. If you play two hours a night, the main story alone will take you more than a month. That is worth knowing before you start, because it changes how you should think about the game: this is not a weekend, it is a season.

Main Story Hours: 65 to 80

GameSpot puts the main story at more than 65 hours, and PowerPyx gives a range of 60 to 80 hours — which lines up well. Beebom is a little lower, at 50 to 65 hours for the main story, but that is the fast end of the range: a focused player, skipping most side content, moving steadily through the missions. Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson has talked about an 80-hour playthrough, which sits at the top of the range and probably reflects a player who takes their time with the world along the way.

The honest answer, then, is 65 to 80 hours for the story if you play at a normal pace, with some side content along the way. If you rush — main missions only, no detours — you might finish closer to 50 hours. If you linger, talk to everyone, and explore Vice City between missions, you will be well past 80 before the credits roll. None of these numbers is wrong; they describe different ways of playing the same game.

With Side Content: 90 to 100 Hours

Add the side content and the total grows fast. GameSpot's estimate is 90 to 100 hours with side content included — and GTA 6 has a lot of side content. Fishing, pool, side jobs, the in-game phone and social media layer, the activities shown in the Extended Look — jet skiing, weight lifting, boating, canoeing, basketball — and the open world itself, which rewards wandering. A hundred hours is not a stretch target; it is what happens naturally if you let the game breathe.

This is also where the chapter structure helps you plan. The third chapter is the longest, with 26 missions, so it is the place to expect the biggest time commitment — and the best place to take a break from the story and do side activities, since the world is fully open to you by then. The game is designed to be lived in between missions, and the hours reflect that.

Going for 100%: 200+ Hours

For completionists, the number jumps again. GameSpot puts a 100% run at more than 200 hours, and Beebom gives a similar figure of around 200 hours for a platinum trophy run. That is the total for everything: every mission, every side activity, every collectible, every challenge, every trophy. It is a serious commitment — five hours a week would take you the better part of a year — but it is also the honest scale of what Rockstar built.

Most players will not do that, and they do not need to. The story is the game, and the side content is there to enjoy, not to complete. But if you are the kind of player who wants to see everything, go in with your eyes open: 200 hours is the real number, and it is worth planning for rather than discovering halfway through.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many hours is the GTA 6 story?

About 65 to 80 hours at a normal pace, with 91 missions across 5 chapters. A fast, story-only run can come in closer to 50 hours, while a slower player who explores will pass 80. With side content, expect 90 to 100 hours, and a full 100% run takes 200+ hours.

Which chapter is the longest in GTA 6?

Chapter 3, with 26 missions — more than a quarter of the whole story. It is the dense heart of the campaign, and the best place to mix in side activities between missions.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 is a 65-to-80-hour story across 91 missions and 5 chapters, with Chapter 3 the longest at 26 missions. Play it straight and you might finish in 50 hours; live in the world as well and you will pass 100; chase everything and the total is 200+. Plan for a season, not a weekend — and take your time, because the world is worth it.

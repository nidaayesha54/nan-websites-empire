The Rule of 72, Explained Simply

By NAN Marketing Agency · October 12, 2026 · 4 min read

How long until your money doubles? The Rule of 72 answers in seconds: divide 72 by your annual return percentage.

The Formula

Years to double ≈ 72 ÷ annual return %

  • 7% return → 72 ÷ 7 ≈ 10.3 years to double
  • 10% return → 72 ÷ 10 ≈ 7.2 years to double
  • 4% return → 72 ÷ 4 ≈ 18 years to double
  • 12% credit-card debt → 72 ÷ 12 ≈ 6 years for your balance to double if unpaid

That last one shows the rule cuts both ways — it works for debt too.

How Accurate Is It?

Surprisingly good for returns between 4% and 15%. At 7%, the rule says 10.3 years; the exact math says 10.24 years. It gets less accurate at very high rates, but for everyday investing it is close enough for mental math.

Use It in Reverse

Want to double your money in 8 years? 72 ÷ 8 = 9% — that is the return you need. Instantly tells you whether a goal is realistic.

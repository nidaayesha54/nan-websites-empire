What to do next

  1. Request an itemized bill if you only got a summary — you have the right to see every line item.
  2. Call the billing department and dispute each flagged charge calmly, in writing if possible.
  3. Ask for financial assistance — nonprofit hospitals must have charity-care policies.
  4. File a complaint if balance billing violates the No Surprises Act: call the federal helpdesk at 1-800-985-3059 or visit cms.gov/nosurprises.
  5. Contact the CFPB at consumerfinance.gov for medical debt and billing complaint resources.

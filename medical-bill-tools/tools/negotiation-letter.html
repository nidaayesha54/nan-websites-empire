Tips that actually work

  
    
    
  • Always get an itemized bill first. Never negotiate a summary — errors hide there.
    • 
    
  • Ask for the "self-pay" or "prompt-pay" discount. 20–40% off is common for lump-sum payment.
    • 
    
  • Mention financial assistance. Nonprofit hospitals must screen you for charity care if you ask.
    • 
    
  • Get every agreement in writing before you pay a cent.
    • 
    
  • Keep copies of the letter, the bill, and all correspondence.

Medical Debt Payment Plan Calculator

See exactly how long your medical debt takes to pay off — and how much interest you'll pay. Try asking your provider for a 0% interest plan; many offer them if you ask.

Before you agree to a plan

  • Ask for 0% interest first. Many hospital payment plans are interest-free — but only if you ask.
  • Negotiate the balance down first with our negotiation letter, then set the plan on the smaller number.
  • Check financial assistance. Nonprofit hospitals must offer charity-care screening — you may owe far less.
  • Never put medical debt on a high-interest credit card unless you've compared the math above.
  • Know your rights: the CFPB explains how medical debt appears (or doesn't) on credit reports.

No Surprises Act Eligibility Checker

Since January 1, 2022, federal law protects you from many surprise medical bills. Answer these questions to see if your bill may be covered.

What the No Surprises Act does

  • Bans surprise billing for emergency services — even out-of-network — limiting your cost to in-network cost-sharing amounts.
  • Bans out-of-network charges for ancillary care (anesthesiology, radiology, labs) at in-network facilities.
  • Covers air ambulance but not ground ambulance (a major gap).
  • Requires good faith estimates for uninsured/self-pay patients, with a patient-provider dispute process if the final bill is $400+ over the estimate.
  • Sets up a federal complaint process: call 1-800-985-3059 or visit cms.gov/nosurprises.

General information only — not legal advice. Protections depend on your plan type and state. Confirm at cms.gov/nosurprises.

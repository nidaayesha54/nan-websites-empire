Common Questions

How common are errors on hospital bills?

Medical billing advocates report that a large share of hospital bills contain errors — some estimates run as high as 80%. Common issues include duplicate charges, services you never received, and incorrect billing codes. Always request an itemized bill and review it line by line.

Can I negotiate a medical bill?

Yes. Hospitals and providers routinely offer discounts for prompt or cash payment, and most have financial assistance (charity care) policies. Nonprofit hospitals are required to have written financial assistance policies. Ask for an itemized bill first, then call the billing department to discuss options.

What is the No Surprises Act?

A federal law effective January 1, 2022 that protects patients from surprise medical bills in emergencies and from out-of-network providers at in-network facilities. It caps what you can be charged and sets up a dispute process. Learn more at cms.gov/nosurprises.

Does medical debt affect my credit score?

Rules have tightened: medical collections under $500 no longer appear on credit reports, and the CFPB has moved to remove medical debt from credit reports entirely. Check the latest guidance at consumerfinance.gov. This page is general information, not legal advice.

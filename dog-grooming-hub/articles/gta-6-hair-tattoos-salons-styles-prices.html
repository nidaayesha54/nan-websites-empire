GTA 6 Hair and Tattoos: Salons, Styles and Prices

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

How you look in GTA 6 is a system, not an afterthought — and it starts almost as soon as the story does. Haircuts, tattoos, makeup and nails are all in the game, spread across salons and shops in Vice City and beyond, with prices that range from a $150 haircut to a tattoo parlour you cannot enter at all unless you pay $20 more. GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and this guide covers every grooming option: where to go, what it costs, what the missions teach you, and the five shops that stay locked.

It matters because the game gives you two characters with fully independent wardrobes — Jason and Lucia each have their own hair, their own tattoos and their own style — and the grooming system is how you tell them apart. A haircut is not a cosmetic extra in this game. It is how you disappear after a job.

Your First Haircut: Pearl House

The first salon most players meet is Pearl House Hair Salon, and it comes with a mission and a price tag. The "New Look" mission sends you there, and a haircut costs around $150 — bring that much, though less will do — with previews free. The salon carries nine standard hairstyles found across Leonida and six hair colours that every salon stocks, so the choice is wide even before you start shopping around. Together with the clothing mission at Bargain World — a full outfit for as little as $9 from the bargain rack, or $46 for the mannequin's ready-made set — the two missions teach the whole system: buy the clothes, change the hair, and the look is yours.

Hairstyles are not just decoration. The August 2026 Extended Look showed an in-field clothing wheel that lets you swap outfits, masks and face coverings on the move, and changing your look after a job is part of how you stay out of trouble — the police are looking for what they saw, and a different hairstyle is part of a different person. Use the wheel in the field: swapping a mask or a face covering on the move is faster than finding a store, and it is the difference between being recognised and not being recognised when the police are looking.

Tattoos: Electric Fang and the 50+ Designs

The tattoo parlour is Electric Fang Tattoo in Stockyard, described as the city's most iconic ink bar, with more than 50 signature tattoos designed by the artist collective FAILE. It is also one of the five shops you cannot enter unless you own the $99.99 Ultimate Edition — or buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade later for the $20 difference from the $79.99 Standard Edition. That is the catch at the heart of the grooming system: the best ink in the game is behind a paywall, and the game does not hide it. You can walk past the door. You cannot walk through it.

The other four locked shops are Stock 305, the streetwear clothing store in Stockyard; Sara's Unisex Salon, for signature salon styles including facial hair for Jason and makeup and nails for Lucia; Rideout Customs Mod Shop in Vice City; and One-Eyed Willie's at Lake Leonida, a vehicle mod shop. All five are physical storefronts in Vice City that stay shut until the upgrade is applied — and you will know it worked when the doors that stayed shut become enterable. The pre-order bonus is separate and applies to both editions: the Vintage Vice City Pack, with retro 1980s outfits for Jason and Lucia, a '55 Vapid Stanier sedan, and a Tommy Vercetti-style tropical weapon skin.

Salons, Barbers and the Rest of Leonida

Beyond the headline shops, salons and barbers are spread across Leonida's six regions — Vice City, the Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park — and each neighbourhood's shops reflect its character. The nine standard hairstyles and six hair colours are the baseline everywhere, but the salons themselves are part of the world: Pearl House is the salon the story sends you to, and the others are the ones you find by exploring. Hair changes are per-character, so Jason's cut does not touch Lucia's, and both characters have their own wardrobes, their own outfits and their own style.

The wardrobe system ties it all together. Wardrobes sit in safehouses and in clothing stores, marked by a hanger icon on the minimap, and from the Outfits tab a saved outfit can be added to your personal vehicle's trunk — up to five of them, so your wardrobe travels with you. That means a haircut, a tattoo and five outfits in the trunk is a full disguise kit, carried in the car, ready whenever a job goes wrong.

How to Groom Smart

The smartest approach is to start cheap and spend later. The $150 haircut at Pearl House is the mission price, and it is worth paying once to learn the system — but the real money goes on the formal wear and the tattoos, and those are priced for players who have finished bank jobs, not for players who have just arrived in Port Gellhorn. Save the expensive looks for when you have real money, and use the cheap ones to get through the early chapters.

And remember the pocket-cash rule: you can carry a maximum of $20,000 at a time, and money in your pocket is lost when you die or get arrested, so bank your cash at ATMs before a shopping trip. A haircut is a small purchase, but a tattoo session and a new wardrobe add up — and losing the money for them on the way home is the most avoidable mistake in the game.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a haircut cost in GTA 6?

Around $150 at Pearl House Hair Salon, with previews free. The salon carries nine standard hairstyles and six hair colours. The "New Look" mission sends you there early in the story.

Which shops need the Ultimate Edition?

Five: Electric Fang Tattoo, Sara's Unisex Salon, Stock 305 clothing, Rideout Customs and One-Eyed Willie's. Standard Edition owners can unlock them with the $20 Ultimate Edition Upgrade at any time.

Can I change my look in the field?

Yes. The in-field clothing wheel lets you swap outfits, masks and face coverings on the move, and up to five saved outfits fit in your personal car's trunk. Hair changes need a salon visit.

The Bottom Line

GTA 6 grooming runs from a $150 haircut at Pearl House to a tattoo parlour with 50 designs that stays shut until you pay $20 more. Start with the mission haircut, learn the wheel and the trunk, and save the expensive ink for when you can afford it. In Vice City, a new hairstyle is not vanity — it is a getaway plan.

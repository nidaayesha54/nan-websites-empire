1. Image to PDF: Turn Photos Into a Single PDF Document

The Image to PDF tool (our flagship) works like a pocket scanner. Upload JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF or BMP images, arrange them in the order you want, pick a page size, and download one clean PDF.

How to convert images to PDF (step by step)

  1. Upload your images. Tap the upload zone and pick photos from your phone gallery, or drag & drop files from your computer. You can add as many as you like.
  2. Reorder pages. Use the ↑ ↓ arrows to arrange images in the right sequence — page 1 first, and so on. Remove any image with the ✕ button.
  3. Choose a page size. "Fit to image" keeps each page the exact shape of its photo (best for mixed sizes). A4 Portrait, A4 Landscape or US Letter fit every image neatly onto standard pages — ideal for printing or official submissions.
  4. Convert & download. Hit "Convert to PDF". Your PDF downloads instantly.

Tips for phone scanning

  • Good light, flat surface. Photograph documents in daylight on a plain background; keep the phone parallel to the paper to avoid skewed pages.
  • One photo per page. Cleaner than cramming two pages into one shot — the PDF reads better and prints correctly.
  • Check the order twice. A 20-page assignment with pages shuffled is worse than no PDF at all. Use the reorder arrows before converting.
  • CNIC, degrees, certificates: "Fit to image" keeps ID cards at their natural proportions; A4 works best for full-page documents.
Quality note: Images are embedded at up to 1600px on the long edge at 92% JPEG quality — sharp enough for reading, printing and official uploads, while keeping file sizes reasonable for email and WhatsApp.

2. Merge PDF: Combine Multiple PDFs Into One File

Got a CV in one PDF, certificates in another, and experience letters in a third? Employers and universities usually want one single file. The Merge PDF tool combines two or more PDFs into one document in seconds.

How to merge PDFs

  1. Upload your PDF files (at least two).
  2. Arrange them with the ↑ ↓ arrows — the top file becomes the first pages.
  3. Click "Merge PDFs" and download the combined file.

Common uses: job applications (CV + cover letter + certificates), university admissions (transcripts + documents), visa files, tax paperwork, and combining monthly invoices into one yearly file.

Limitation: Password-protected (encrypted) PDFs cannot be merged without their password. Remove the password in your PDF reader first, then merge.

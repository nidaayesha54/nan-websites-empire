GTA 6 Map as a PDF: Download and Print the Leonida Map

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Everyone wants a map of Leonida on their wall — or at least on their second screen. GTA 6's world is about twice the size of GTA 5's map, with six regions, hundreds of places and a coastline that takes real time to learn. And the good news is that you can get one as a PDF, two different ways: Rockstar gives away a printable map poster, and the fan mapping community has built interactive maps you can save as PDFs yourself. This guide explains both routes, step by step, and warns you about the maps you should never trust.

The first thing to know is that Rockstar has not released an interactive map, and has not published a map PDF. What it has released is better than a PDF for most players — and what the fans have built fills the gap the rest of the way.

The printable map Rockstar gives away

Rockstar's own GTA VI media page offers downloadable artwork, and among it is a Leonida map poster you can print at home. Go to the VI / MEDIA page on rockstargames.com, look for the Leonida map artwork under Art Poster, and select the LEONIDA MAP download — it is separate from the cover art. The download is a ZIP file, and inside it is a single JPG image named LEONIDAMAP POSTER PRINTABLE, which you can open and print at home.

That file is the real thing: an illustrated map titled THE GREAT STATE OF LEONIDA, showing the whole peninsula with its counties, roads, towns and landmarks — Vice City with its neighbourhoods, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, the Grassrivers, the Leonida Keys running down to Mile Zero Air Station, and Mount Kalaga National Park. It has a legend, a scale, a compass rose and grid coordinates, and it is designed to be printed, not just viewed on a screen.

Do not confuse it with the physical poster. A double-sided paper copy is sold separately through the Rockstar Store, and a map poster is also included in the $399.99 Goodtime State box. The free digital download is the same artwork, and it costs nothing.

The fan maps you can save as PDF

For an interactive map — one you can search, zoom and filter — the fan community is the only source. The longest-running project is the State of Leonida map, an interactive reconstruction built from trailers, screenshots and community analysis, with roads, landmarks, points of interest and real-life comparisons. Its latest version discussed by fans is version 13, published on June 24 and maintained by a mapper known as Yanis.

Another project, Leonida Atlas, is an open-source app that hosts the same kind of community data: about 2,198 places, with roughly 540 marked as supported and the rest marked unknown confidence. Places without a known position are left unplaced rather than guessed, which is the honest way to build a map from footage. A third project, hosted at gta6map.github.io, describes itself as an interactive map of every landmark identified so far, and its README says the landmark data and map tiles are available for use and labels the project public domain.

To save any of these as a PDF, open the map on a desktop browser, wait for every tile and label to load, set the region and marker layers you need, then choose Print and Save to PDF. In Chrome or Edge that is Ctrl+P on Windows and Linux, or Cmd+P on macOS, then Save to PDF; Firefox and Safari offer an equivalent PDF printer or save command. If the print preview comes out blank or misses canvas tiles, capture the visible map at the highest resolution available, place that image in a document or graphics app, and export the document as a PDF instead. Name the files in reading order — Leonida north-west, Leonida south-east — and record the map's date or revision in your own notes, because these maps change as the community learns more.

Which maps to avoid

Not every map with GTA 6 on it is honest. The most common fake is the blockout from the 2022 Rockstar breach — an early map from 2014 and 2015, with no foliage, basic textures and floating labels, including areas that were scrapped. It is ten years old, it was never the real map, and it is easy to pass off as one. The fan projects above work from released footage only, and they say so.

The second thing to remember is that every fan map is approximate. Fans rebuild the map by triangulation: line up several landmarks you can identify in one shot and work out roughly where the camera was standing. Some positions come from older material, not from Rockstar, and the projects label their confidence for that reason. A fan map is a best guess, however good it is — and any map sold as final is a reconstruction.

And a warning for sellers: Rockstar has not released an interactive map, so any site charging for a "complete GTA 6 map PDF" is selling community work or a guess. The printable poster is free from Rockstar, and the interactive maps are free from their builders. Pay nothing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a GTA 6 map PDF from Rockstar?

No. Rockstar has not published an interactive-map PDF. What it does offer is a free printable Leonida map poster as a JPG inside a ZIP on its VI / MEDIA page — and the fan community's interactive maps can be saved as PDFs from a desktop browser.

How do I save the GTA 6 fan map as a PDF?

Open the community map on a desktop browser, wait for every tile and label to load, set the region and marker layers you need, then press Ctrl+P (Windows/Linux) or Cmd+P (macOS) and choose Save to PDF. If the preview is blank, screenshot the map at full resolution and export that image as a PDF instead.

How big is the GTA 6 map?

Rockstar art director Aaron Garbut described Leonida as about twice the size of GTA 5's map. It has six confirmed regions: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga National Park.

The bottom line: the best map of Leonida is free, twice over. Download Rockstar's printable poster for the wall, save a fan map as a PDF for the second screen, and ignore anything that charges you for either — or claims to be the final map before Rockstar has drawn one.

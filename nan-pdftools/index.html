FAQs

Is this really free? Any limits?

Yes — 100% free with no file-size limits imposed by us. Very large files are limited only by your device's memory, because everything runs locally in your browser.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. All PDF processing uses the pdf-lib library inside your browser. Files never leave your phone or computer — that is why the tools also work offline after the page loads.

Which image formats can I convert to PDF?

JPG, PNG, WebP, GIF and BMP. Images are converted to high-quality JPEG pages inside the PDF.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

No — encrypted PDFs cannot be opened without the password, so merging and rotation skip them. Remove the password first in your PDF reader.

Will the PDF quality drop?

Images are embedded at up to 1600px on the long edge at 92% JPEG quality — sharp for documents and printing. Merged and rotated PDFs keep their original content untouched.

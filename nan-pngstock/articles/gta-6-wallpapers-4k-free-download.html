GTA 6 Wallpapers: 27 Free 4K Downloads From Rockstar

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, and Rockstar's own site has been giving away the game's art for months: 27 wallpapers, free to download, in sizes from a phone screen to an ultrawide monitor, with the smallest desktop version in 4K. They are the key art of Jason and Lucia, the cover art, six of the game's other characters and postcards of all six regions of Leonida — and they sit in the Artwork & Wallpapers section of the media page on Rockstar's GTA 6 site.

This guide lists every wallpaper in the set, explains which size fits which screen, and tells you how to download them — plus the warning that matters most: the wallpaper apps and sites using the GTA 6 name have nothing to do with Rockstar, and fake GTA 6 apps on phones have been used to push ads and worse. Get the art from the source.

Every wallpaper in the set

The set has grown since it first appeared. As checked on November 16, 2026, there are 27 wallpapers. Seven are Jason and Lucia key art: Lock and Load, Robbery, Beach, 01, 02, Motel and Rooftop — the Rooftop piece, with Jason in a US Army T-shirt beside Lucia on a glass-railed rooftop over the water at dusk, was added last, as the 26th and 27th images. Six more are the same scenes with the game's logo stamped on them.

Two are cover art: the game's cover itself and the cover of Grand Theft Auto VI: The Album. Six show the supporting cast Rockstar introduced in May 2025: Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista and Brian Heder. The last six are the "Visit Leonida" postcards, one per region: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia, Grassrivers and Mount Kalaga National Park. Jason and Lucia do not get solo portraits in the set — they appear together in all seven pieces of key art instead.

Which size fits your screen

Most wallpapers come in six sizes, and Rockstar labels each one with its dimensions. Landscape is 3840 by 2160 — full 4K — for desktops, laptops and TVs. Ultrawide is 5120 by 2160 for 21:9 monitors. Phone is 2160 by 4800 for iPhones and Android phones. Portrait is 1080 by 1920 or 2160 by 3840 for vertical screens. Tablet is 3840 by 3840, square for either orientation. Square is 2160 by 2160.

Twenty of the 27 come in all six sizes. The six with the game's logo and the album cover come in landscape, square and portrait only, so they suit a desktop or a phone but not an ultrawide screen. Jason and Lucia 02 has a second phone version. Pick by the shape of your screen, not by the file size — a 4K landscape image cropped to a phone will cut the subject in half.

How to download them

Go to the media page on Rockstar's GTA 6 site and open Artwork & Wallpapers. Pick a wallpaper, then a size from the menu — the menu shows each version's dimensions in pixels — and press the download button. For the whole set, use Download All: it is one zip file of about 246 MB with everything inside.

On a phone, download only the single phone or portrait version you want. The full zip is large, and you would have to unzip it before you could use any of the images. On a desktop, the zip is the easy route — download once, and you have every size for every screen you own.

Wallpapers on PS5 and Xbox

The art does not stop at your phone and computer. On PS5, open the Welcome hub, choose Change Background, then Games — the hub shows five GTA 6 images in that tab, ready to use as the console background. On Xbox, the cover art is available as a 1920 by 1080 image sized for the Xbox Home screen, which you set as a custom background from the console's settings.

That is the safe way to dress up a console. The unsafe way is the wallpaper app in the phone store with GTA 6 in its name and a five-star rating bought with ads. Those apps have nothing to do with Rockstar, and some have pushed ads and malware onto phones. If the art does not come from Rockstar's own site or your console's own hub, do not install it.

About Us

Ireland Learn to Drive Cost Calculator is a free online tool run by NAN Studio — part of NAN Marketing Agency — a team that builds free, practical calculators and tools for everyday questions.

What this tool does

Ireland Learn to Drive Cost Calculator estimates the total cost of learning to drive in Ireland — the theory test, learner permit, 12 Essential Driver Training (EDT) lessons, the driving test fee and the full licence fee.

How it works

The calculator runs 100% in your browser. You enter your figures, the page computes the result instantly, and nothing you type is sent to our servers or stored anywhere.

Why we built it

Official rates and rules are often buried in long government pages. We do the reading, turn the rules into a simple step-by-step tool, and keep it free for everyone — no sign-up, no paywall, no spam.

A note on accuracy

We base our calculations on the latest published official rates and rules, but figures are estimates and rules change. For decisions that matter, always verify with the official source or a qualified professional — see our Disclaimer.

The Road to a Full Licence in Ireland

For first-time Category B (car) learners the RSA requires 12 Essential Driver Training (EDT) lessons with an Approved Driving Instructor, and you must hold your learner permit for at least 6 months before sitting the practical test. The typical journey:

Realistic all-in totals land between €1,200 and €2,700 — the biggest variable is how many lessons you take beyond the 12 mandatory EDTs, and whether you pass first time (the national pass rate is roughly one in two, so budgeting a retake is sensible, not pessimistic).

Tips to Keep Costs Down

  • Block-book EDT: many instructors discount the full 12-lesson EDT programme versus single lessons.
  • Practise between lessons with a sponsor (a driver holding a full licence 2+ years) — regular private practice cuts the number of paid lessons you need.
  • Take pretest lessons near your test centre so you practise the actual test routes.
  • Check insurer offers — some partner with driving schools for discounted EDT bundles.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many driving lessons do I legally need in Ireland?

12 one-hour EDT lessons with an Approved Driving Instructor, logged on the RSA's MyEDT system. You cannot sit the practical test until all 12 are complete.

How much is the driving test in Ireland?

The practical car driving test fee is €85 (2026 RSA fee). The theory test is €45, the learner permit is €35 and the full licence application is €55.

How long does it take to get a full licence?

Typically 6–12 months: theory prep, 6 months minimum on the learner permit, 12 EDT lessons, then a test wait that can run 8–21 weeks depending on the centre.

What happens if I fail the driving test?

You rebook (currently an 11-week average wait nationally) and pay the €85 test fee again. Most candidates take a few extra lessons before the retest.

Can I practise in my own or a family car?

Yes, with a qualified sponsor driver and valid insurance for the vehicle — you must display L-plates and the sponsor must hold a full licence for at least 2 years.

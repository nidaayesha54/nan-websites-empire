How to Apply

1
Read the official notification — check eligibility, age limit, fees and vacancy details. Apply on ibps.in for IBPS exams, sbi.bank.in/careers for SBI, and rbi.org.in for RBI recruitments.
2
Register on the official portal — ibps.in, sbi.bank.in/careers, rbi.org.in — create your account with a valid email and mobile number for the online application.
3
Fill & submit the form — upload photo, signature and documents as specified; pay the fee online and save the confirmation page.
4
Download your admit card — released 2–3 weeks before the exam on the same official portal; check your exam city and shift.
5
Understand the exam pattern — review Prelims/Mains syllabus, marking scheme and previous-year cutoffs, then track results on the official site.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the IBPS PO exam?
The IBPS PO/MT exam recruits probationary officers for 11 public-sector banks through Prelims, Mains and Interview.
What is the eligibility for bank PO?
A bachelor's degree in any discipline; age 20–30 years for IBPS PO (relaxation as per rules).
How do I apply for IBPS exams?
Register on ibps.in during the CRP notification window — one application covers all participating banks.
What is the selection process for SBI PO?
Prelims → Mains → Psychometric test → Group Exercise & Interview.
Is there an interview for IBPS Clerk?
No — IBPS Clerk selection is through Prelims + Mains only, followed by provisional allotment.
Where can I download the bank exam admit card?
Call letters are released on the respective official site — ibps.in, sbi.bank.in/careers or rbi.org.in.

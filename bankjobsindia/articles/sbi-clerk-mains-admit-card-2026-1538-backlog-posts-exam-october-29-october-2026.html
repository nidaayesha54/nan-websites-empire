How to Download the SBI Clerk Mains Admit Card

1
Visit sbi.co.in — open the SBI website and go to the Careers section, then Current Openings.
2
Find the Junior Associates Mains call-letter link — look for the Clerk (Junior Associates) backlog-drive Mains admit card notice.
3
Log in — enter your registration number or roll number with your password/date of birth and the captcha.
4
Download and print — save the PDF and take at least two printouts — a digital copy on the phone is not accepted at the gate.
5
Verify every field — name, photograph, signature, exam date, shift, reporting time and centre address — contact the SBI helpdesk immediately if anything mismatches.

SBI Clerk Mains — Exam Pattern

The SBI Clerk Mains is an objective online test of 190 questions for 200 marks: General/Financial Awareness (50), General English (40), Quantitative Aptitude (50) and Reasoning Ability & Computer Aptitude (50), with ¼-mark negative marking. There is no interview — the Mains score decides the merit, followed by a local-language test where applicable.

With ten days to go, the highest-return work is full-length mocks under the real sectional timing plus a daily current-affairs revision of the last 4–6 months — the GA section is the cheapest 50 marks in the paper.

Exam-Day Checklist — 29 October

1
Carry the printed admit card — plus one original photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, voter ID or driving licence) — photocopies alone are not enough.
2
Reach early — report at the time printed on the call letter; late entry is not permitted after the gate closes.
3
Bring two passport photos — recent colour photographs as a backup for the attendance sheet.
4
Follow the rough-sheet rule — use only the rough sheets provided at the centre; personal calculators and notes are banned.

The Export-Import Bank of India (Exim Bank) Business Development Officer (BDO) Recruitment 2026 has a crucial update: the bank has extended the application deadline from 15 October to 25 October 2026 through an addendum dated 15 October 2026. With only days left, experienced banking professionals can still apply for 12 contractual BDO posts across 11 Indian cities.

Candidates need a 2-year full-time postgraduate qualification in Marketing or Finance (preferably an MBA), at least 55% marks in both graduation and post-graduation, and a minimum of 2 years of post-qualification experience in banks, financial institutions or upper-layer NBFCs — specifically in marketing of corporate loans syndication and corporate credit. The compensation is a headline ₹14.68 lakh per annum.

Posting cities: Ahmedabad (1), Bengaluru (1), Chandigarh (1), Chennai (1), Hyderabad (1), Indore (1), Kolkata (1), Lucknow (1), Mumbai (2), New Delhi (1) and Pune (1).

Eligibility Criteria

  • Education: minimum 2-year full-time postgraduate degree in Marketing/Finance, preferably MBA with Finance or Marketing specialization.
  • Marks: minimum 55% aggregate (or equivalent CGPA) in both graduation and post-graduation.
  • Experience: minimum 2 years of post-qualification experience in Banks, Financial Institutions or Upper Layer NBFCs
  • Age: not more than 35 years as on 31 August 2026; relaxation per GoI guidelines.

Contract and Compensation

The appointment is contractual for 3 years, further extendable by 2 years at the bank’s discretion. The advertised compensation is ₹14.68 lakh per annum, with fixed and variable components in an 80:20 ratio, plus annual increments for two years of work experience.

Selection Process

1
Shortlisting — in the ratio of 1:10 (based on post-graduation percentage if more than 10 candidates are eligible).
2
Personal interview — at the bank’s office in Mumbai or New Delhi. No written examination is specified.

How to Apply (Email Only)

Important: despite some job headlines saying “Apply Online”, the official advertisement instructs candidates to email their applications in the prescribed format — there is no conventional online application form.
  1. Download the advertisement and the prescribed application format from the Careers section of www.eximbankindia.in.
  2. Fill the format completely and attach it as your resume.
  3. Email it to the bank’s HR email address given in the advertisement with the subject line “BDO Application: Mr./ Ms. (Full Name)” before 25 October 2026.
Deadline reminder: applications must reach the bank by 25 October 2026. This is the extended date — the original deadline of 15 October 2026 has passed, so do not rely on older versions of the advertisement.

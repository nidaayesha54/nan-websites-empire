IBPS RRB 2026: 13,706 Posts Advertised

The headline bank recruitment of October 2026 is the IBPS RRB Common Recruitment Process, advertising 13,706 posts for officers and office assistants across regional rural banks. Check the last date on ibps.in and apply well before the closing day — bank portals slow down in the final hours.

Bank of India Specialist Officer: 205 Posts

Bank of India has advertised 205 Specialist Officer (SO) posts. Specialist officer recruitments target candidates with specific professional qualifications — IT, marketing, law, agriculture, HR and other streams as per the notification's stream-wise breakup. If you hold a relevant degree, this is the month's best fit for specialised profiles. Verify the exact last date in the official Bank of India notification.

SBI Wealth Management 2026: 207 Contractual Posts

The State Bank of India advertised 207 posts under its Wealth Management recruitment 2026 — including Zonal Head, Regional Head, Relationship Manager-Team Lead, Investment Specialist and similar roles. These are contractual positions for experienced candidates, not freshers. The application deadline was 5 October 2026 and has passed — but SBI's wealth vertical hires regularly, so watch sbi.co.in/careers for the next drive.

SBI PO Prelims Marks 2026: Scorecard Active

Candidates who appeared for the SBI PO preliminary examination can now download the SBI PO Prelims Marks 2026 scorecard — the link is active on the SBI careers portal. Check your marks against the expected cutoffs; if you qualified, shift full attention to mains preparation without delay.

How to Track Bank Recruitments (Never Miss a Notification)

1
Bookmark the official portals — ibps.in for all IBPS exams, sbi.co.in/careers for SBI roles, and each bank's own careers page for specialist and contractual hiring.
2
Read the full notification before applying — confirm eligibility, age limit, reservation, fee and the exact last date.
3
Apply early in the window — servers choke on the last two days. Upload photo, signature and documents in the specified format.
4
Save everything — keep the submitted application, fee receipt and registration number. You will need them to download the call letter.

Application Tips for Bank Jobs

Match your profile to the post before you apply: officer roles suit fresh graduates preparing through prelims-mains-interview, while specialist officer and contractual roles reward domain experience. Keep scanned documents ready — photo, signature, certificates and ID proofs — so every application takes minutes. Verify every date on the official portal.

Eligibility Notes

Eligibility differs by recruitment: IBPS RRB officer and office assistant posts generally require graduation with age limits per scale; specialist officer posts need stream-specific qualifications; SBI Wealth Management roles were contractual and experience-based. Exact criteria always come from the official notification.

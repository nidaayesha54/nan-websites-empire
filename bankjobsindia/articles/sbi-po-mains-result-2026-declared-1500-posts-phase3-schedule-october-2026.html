SBI PO Mains Result 2026 Declared: 1,500 Posts — Psychometric Test 31 Oct, Interview from 18 Nov

The State Bank of India has declared the SBI PO Mains Result 2026 on 15 October 2026 on its official website, sbi.co.in. The result, published as a PDF of qualified roll numbers, covers the recruitment of 1,500 Probationary Officers (advertisement CRPD/PO/2026-27/09). Candidates who appeared in the Phase-II mains examination held on 12 September 2026 can now check whether they have moved to Phase 3 — the psychometric test tentatively on 31 October 2026, followed by the group exercise and interview from 18 November 2026.

How to Check the SBI PO Mains Result 2026

1
Visit sbi.co.in — open the Careers section on the official SBI website.
2
Open the PO recruitment page — find the "SBI PO Mains Result 2026" link under current openings.
3
Download the result PDF — the merit list contains the roll numbers of candidates qualified for Phase 3.
4
Search your roll number — use Ctrl+F (or Cmd+F on Mac).
5
Save the PDF — qualified candidates should keep a printout for the Phase-3 call letter process.

Phase 3 — What Qualified Candidates Face Next

Psychometric Test (31 October 2026, tentative)

The psychometric test is qualifying in nature — it assesses personality traits and behavioural fit for the Probationary Officer role rather than ranking you on marks. Answer honestly and consistently; there are no "right" answers to memorise, but contradictory responses are flagged. SBI will announce the admit card download date and time on the official website — check sbi.co.in regularly from the third week of October.

Group Exercise and Interview (from 18 November 2026)

This is the scored stage of Phase 3 and carries real weight in the final merit. The group exercise tests communication, leadership and teamwork on a banking or current-affairs topic, while the personal interview probes your background, banking awareness and motivation for joining SBI. Final selection combines the mains score with the Phase-3 performance as per the official notification — so treat these five weeks as seriously as the mains preparation.

How to Prepare for the Group Exercise and Interview

  • Banking awareness: SBI's recent results and initiatives, RBI policy stance, digital banking trends, and the roles of a Probationary Officer.
  • Current affairs: the last 4–5 months of economic and banking news — budget, monetary policy, major mergers and schemes.
  • Your own profile: prepare a crisp 2-minute introduction, your academic background, work experience if any, and a convincing answer to "Why SBI?".
  • Mock group discussions: practice speaking concisely, listening, building on others' points and summarising — dominance without substance backfires.
  • Documents: keep originals ready — degree certificates, 10th/12th marksheets, caste/EWS certificate if applicable, photo ID and the mains admit card and result printout.
Note: The psychometric test date (31 October) is tentative and the interview schedule starts 18 November as per the bank's announcement. SBI will publish the exact call-letter dates on sbi.co.in — verify every date on the official site.

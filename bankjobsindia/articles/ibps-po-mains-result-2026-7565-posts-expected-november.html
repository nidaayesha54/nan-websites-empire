The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection conducted the IBPS PO Mains 2026 examination on 4 October 2026 for candidates who cleared the prelims held on 22–23 August 2026 (prelims result declared 23 September 2026). The recruitment — CRP PO/MT-XVI — carries 7,565 revised vacancies for Probationary Officer/Management Trainee posts across participating public sector banks. The mains result is expected in November 2026; based on the previous cycle's timeline of roughly 50 days after the mains exam, candidates can watch for it from the fourth week of November.

How to Check the Mains Result

1
Visit ibps.in — the official IBPS website.
2
Open the CRP PO/MT section and select CRP-PO/MT-XVI.
3
Click the Mains Result link once it is activated after the official declaration.
4
Log in with your registration/roll number and password or date of birth (plus captcha).
5
Download and save the result and the later scorecard — keep them for the interview stage.

Remember: the prelims score is qualifying only and does not count toward the final merit — final selection rests on mains plus interview scores. Candidates who do not qualify should use the scorecard to identify weak sections for the next cycle.

Preparing for the Interview

  • Banking awareness: recent RBI policy moves, budget announcements and the financial performance of your preferred banks.
  • Personal background: your degree subject, work experience and a clear answer for "why banking".
  • Personality test: the new personality-test stage is mandatory — practise structured, honest responses rather than memorised scripts.
  • Documents: keep your result printout, scorecard, degree certificates and category proofs ready for verification at the interview.

Frequently Asked Questions

When will the IBPS PO Mains result 2026 be declared?
The IBPS PO Mains Result 2026 is expected in November 2026, for the mains exam held on 4 October 2026 under CRP PO/MT-XVI. Check the result on ibps.in using your registration number and date of birth.
How many vacancies are there in IBPS PO 2026?
7,565 revised vacancies for Probationary Officer/Management Trainee posts — 3,017 UR, 2,069 OBC, 1,161 SC, 741 EWS and 577 ST.
How do I check the IBPS PO Mains result 2026?
Visit ibps.in, open the CRP PO/MT section, select CRP-PO/MT-XVI, click the Mains Result link, and log in with your registration/roll number and password or date of birth.
What comes after the IBPS PO mains result?
Qualifiers face the Personality Test and Interview in November–December 2026, followed by provisional allotment in January 2027. Final selection is based on mains and interview scores.
Is the IBPS PO prelims score counted in the final merit?
No. The preliminary exam is qualifying only. The final merit is based on the mains examination and interview scores.

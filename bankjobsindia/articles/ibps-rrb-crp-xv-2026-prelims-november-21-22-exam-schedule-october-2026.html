The IBPS RRB CRP-XV 2026 exam cycle is now about a month out: Officer Scale-I prelims are scheduled for 21 & 22 November 2026, with Office Assistant prelims on 6, 12 & 13 December 2026. Applications closed on 27 September 2026, and the cycle covers 28 Regional Rural Banks across 26 states and 2 UTs.

This is an exam-preparation article, not an open-apply window. The full schedule, post-wise eligibility, the disputed vacancy total and the selection structure per post are below — plus an important honesty note: the November/December exam dates come from aggregator reporting, so re-confirm them on ibps.in before making travel or leave plans.

Vacancy-Count Honesty Note

Genuinely disputed across sources: 13,706 (original indicative figure — Tripura Gramin Bank had not reported), ~13,745 (revised via the 9 September 2026 corrigendum) and 13,757 (CareerPower). The corrigendum revision makes ~13,745 the best-supported figure, but all counts are indicative and subject to change at allotment.

  • Officer Scale-II & III: Single exam → Interview.

Frequently Asked Questions

When is the IBPS RRB Scale-I prelims 2026?
21 & 22 November 2026; Office Assistant prelims follow on 6, 12 & 13 December 2026. Exam dates are as reported by aggregators — confirm on ibps.in.
How many vacancies are there in IBPS RRB CRP-XV 2026?
Originally 13,706 (indicative), revised to ~13,745 via a 9 September 2026 corrigendum; some sources cite 13,757. The figure is indicative and subject to change at allotment.

No — Office Assistant selection is prelims + mains only; interviews apply to Officer Scale-I/II/III.

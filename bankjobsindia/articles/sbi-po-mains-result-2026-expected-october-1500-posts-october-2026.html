The State Bank of India conducted the SBI PO Mains Examination 2026 on 12 September 2026 for 1,500 Probationary Officer vacancies (1,446 regular and 54 backlog). The Mains result has not been declared yet; SBI's official schedule lists the Main Examination result for September/October, and media reports consistently place it in October. Candidates should rely on the official SBI website for the result rather than unofficial links or social-media forwards.

How to Check the SBI PO Mains Result

The result will be published as a PDF listing the roll numbers of candidates selected for Phase III.

1
Visit sbi.bank.in — the official State Bank of India website (avoid third-party result links).
2
Open the Careers section and select the Probationary Officer recruitment page.
3
Find the Mains result link — look for "SBI PO Mains Result 2026" once activated.
4
Open the PDF and search — press Ctrl+F and enter your roll number to check whether you are shortlisted for Phase III.
5
Save the PDF — download it for your records and start preparing for Phase III immediately.

The Mains scorecard is not released along with the result — it will be published with the final results. The Mains cutoff will also be declared along with the final results.

What Happens After the Result: Phase III

Candidates who qualify in the Mains move to Phase III of the SBI PO selection process, tentatively scheduled for October/November 2026. This stage comprises three components:

ComponentWhat It Tests
Psychometric TestPersonality traits and suitability for the Probationary Officer role
Group Exercise (20 marks)Teamwork, leadership and communication in a group task
Personal Interview (30 marks)Banking awareness, current affairs and overall suitability

The preliminary examination is qualifying in nature — its marks are not added to the final merit. Final selection is based on performance in the Main Examination and the Phase III assessment, as prescribed by SBI. Shortlisted candidates should begin preparing for the next stage now rather than waiting for the interview or Group Exercise call letter.

How to Prepare for Phase III

  • Banking awareness: stay current on banking trends, RBI policy, digital banking and SBI's recent developments — interviewers assess sector awareness, not just qualifications.
  • Group discussion practice: mock group exercises with peers build comfort with the format and improve communication under pressure.
  • Psychometric honesty: the psychometric test evaluates personality attributes — answer consistently rather than gaming it.
  • Documents ready: keep degree certificates, age proof, category certificates and photo ID organised; shortlisted candidates will need them quickly.

SBI PO Salary and Career

Selected Probationary Officers receive a salary in the range of approximately ₹36,000 to ₹63,840, plus applicable allowances. The PO role is SBI's flagship officer entry — a two-year probation with training, branch exposure and fast-track promotion opportunities, which is why this recruitment attracts lakhs of applicants every cycle.

SBI SCO Recruitment 2026: 6 AI Specialist Officer Posts (Chief AI Officer, DGM) — Apply by 9 November

The State Bank of India has opened one of 2026's most talked-about lateral hirings — six artificial-intelligence specialist posts. Through Advertisement No. CRPD/SCO/2026-27/30, issued by the Central Recruitment & Promotion Department (CRPD), SBI is inviting online applications for 6 Specialist Cadre Officer (SCO) posts in the AI domain — led by a General Manager & Chief AI Officer. Applications run from 19 October to 9 November 2026 on sbi.bank.in, with an upper pay range of Rs. 90 lakh to Rs. 1.6 crore per annum.

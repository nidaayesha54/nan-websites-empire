IBPS Clerk (CSA XVI) Prelims Admit Card 2026 Out: 11,403 Posts — Download &amp; Exam Pattern

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection has released the IBPS Clerk (Customer Service Associate) XVI prelims admit card 2026 for 11,403 posts across participating public sector banks. Candidates can now download the hall ticket from the official IBPS website at ibps.in by logging in with their registration number and password or date of birth. With over eleven thousand vacancies, the CSA-XVI cycle is the largest bank clerk recruitment of 2026 — and the prelims is the first gate every aspirant must clear.

The prelims admit card carries your exact exam date, shift timing, reporting time and venue — download it now rather than on exam eve, verify every detail, and keep a printed copy ready. This guide covers the download steps, the prelims exam pattern, what to carry on exam day, and what follows after the prelims result.

IBPS Clerk Prelims Exam Pattern

The prelims is a 100-mark online test of 60 minutes, with sectional timing:

SectionQuestionsMarksTime
English Language303020 minutes
Numerical Ability353520 minutes
Reasoning Ability353520 minutes
Total10010060 minutes

There is negative marking of 0.25 marks per wrong answer. Prelims marks are only qualifying — they do not count toward final allotment — but you must clear both the sectional and overall cutoffs to reach the mains. With 11,403 posts, cutoffs will be competitive in high-vacancy states, so accuracy under time pressure is the deciding skill.

Exam-Day Checklist & What Comes Next

  • Carry: printed admit card, an original photo ID (Aadhaar, PAN, passport, voter ID or driving licence) and a recent passport photograph.
  • Reach early: entry closes at the reporting time on your call letter — late candidates are not admitted.
  • No electronics: mobile phones, smart watches and calculators are barred from the hall.
  • After prelims: the prelims result shortlists candidates for the mains examination (200 marks), and final provisional allotment is based on mains marks alone.
Tip: Because each section is separately timed, do not get stuck — attempt easy questions first in every section, then return to the tougher ones with remaining time.

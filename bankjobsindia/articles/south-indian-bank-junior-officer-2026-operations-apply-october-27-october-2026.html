The South Indian Bank Junior Officer (Operations) Recruitment 2026 is open for a short 12-day window: online applications run from 16 October to 27 October 2026. The post is a contractual 3-year engagement (renewable at the bank’s discretion) with an annual CTC of ₹5.14 lakh, and postings are concentrated in Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Maharashtra and Goa.

This is an experienced-hires recruitment, not a fresher opening: a minimum of two years’ work experience in banks or NBFCs is mandatory, alongside a regular-stream graduation with at least 50% marks. There is no application fee, and selection is through group discussion and/or personal interview — no written exam.

Honesty Note

Vacancy count not disclosed: the notification does not publish a fixed number of posts; every reporting source describes the intake as “various” or “as per the bank’s requirements”. The domicile requirement is strict — only candidates domiciled in Tamil Nadu, Puducherry, Maharashtra or Goa are eligible. All details are aggregator-reported; verify the notification PDF on southindianbank.bank.in before applying.

Salary, Contract and Benefits

The engagement is contractual for three years initially, renewable based on performance. The compensation is a consolidated annual CTC of ₹5.14 lakh, which includes the NPS contribution, insurance premium and performance-linked variable pay — aggregators estimate the monthly gross at roughly ₹42,800. Non-monetary benefits include accident and health insurance, paid by the bank.

Selection Process

1
Shortlisting — applications are screened on eligibility and the volume of applications received.
2
Group Discussion and/or Personal Interview — the qualifying stage, as decided by the bank.
3
Document verification + medical examination — appointment is subject to fitness and background verification.

How to Apply

  1. Visit the official recruitment portal recruit.southindianbank.com/RDC/ (reached via southindianbank.bank.in).
  2. Open the Junior Officer (Operations) 2026 advertisement and read the notification PDF carefully — especially the domicile and regular-stream requirements.
  3. Fill the online form, upload documents, and submit before 27 October 2026. Save a printout of the submitted application.
Deadline reminder: the window closes on 27 October 2026 and the notification states no applications will be accepted after the closing date or through any other mode. Apply early to avoid last-minute technical glitches.

IOB SO Admit Card 2026 — 291 Posts, Exam 31 Oct

Indian Overseas Bank (IOB) has released the Specialist Officer (SO) and Generalist Officer admit card 2026 on 16 October 2026. The recruitment drive fills 291 vacancies — including Manager (IT), Manager (Branch Management), Senior Manager (Credit) and other specialist/generalist posts — under advertisement number HRDD/RECT/04/2026-27.

The online examination is scheduled for 31 October 2026. Candidates can download their call letters from the official recruitment portal via iob.bank.in using their registration number/roll number, password or date of birth, and the on-screen CAPTCHA.

How to Download the IOB SO Admit Card 2026

1
Visit iob.bank.in and open the “What's New” section on the homepage.
2
Find the notice — “Call Letter for Online Examination for Recruitment of Generalist / Specialist Officers in Various Scales – 2026-27”.
3
Click the link — it opens the recruitment portal (ibpsreg.ibps.in) login window.
4
Enter your registration number/roll number, password or date of birth, and CAPTCHA, then submit.
5
Download and print the call letter — verify every detail (name, roll number, exam date, shift, centre, reporting time) before exam day.

Details to Check on the Hall Ticket

  • Candidate name, date of birth, roll/registration number, photograph and signature
  • Exam name, exam code and advertisement number
  • Exam date (31 October 2026), shift and reporting time
  • Exam centre name and complete address; exam-day instructions

Any incorrect information or discrepancy should be reported to the bank through its official recruitment communication channels before the examination.

Documents to Carry

  • Printed copy of the IOB SO admit card 2026 — clear printout mandatory for entry
  • Original photo identity proof — Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Driving Licence or Voter ID
  • Self-attested photocopy of the same identity document, as required by the instructions
  • Recent passport-size photograph if instructed in the call letter

Selection Process and Exam Pattern

Selection is through an online examination followed by a personal interview, with document verification and medical examination for final selection. The written test covers the professional and aptitude areas for the applied scale — confirm the exact paper structure for your post in the detailed notification.

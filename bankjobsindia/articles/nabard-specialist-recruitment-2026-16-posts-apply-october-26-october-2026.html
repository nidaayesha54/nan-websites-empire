NABARD Specialist Recruitment 2026: 16 Cyber & IT Posts — Apply by 26 October, up to Rs. 40 LPA

The National Bank for Agriculture and Rural Development (NABARD) has released Advertisement No. 04, inviting online applications for 16 specialist posts in cyber security and information technology on a contract basis. Applications are open from 9 October to 26 October 2026 on nabard.org. The headline figure: consolidated remuneration ranging from Rs. 12 lakh to Rs. 40 lakh per annum depending on the post — making this one of the best-paid specialist openings in the public banking sector right now, and a rare no-written-exam route for experienced cyber and IT professionals.

Posts on Offer

The 16 vacancies cover the full cyber-security and banking-IT spectrum:

  • SOC (Security Operations Centre) Analyst
  • Information Security Analyst
  • Network Security Analyst
  • Ethical Hacker & Penetration Tester
  • Lead Auditor
  • Cyber & Network Security Specialist
  • IT Infrastructure & Banking Specialist
  • Cyber Security Analyst

Eligibility and qualification requirements vary by post — typically a B.E./B.Tech in CS/IT or equivalent with relevant professional experience and certifications, as detailed post-wise in the official advertisement. Because this is a lateral specialist hiring, experience requirements are substantive: read the post-wise experience table carefully before applying.

Selection Process — No Written Exam

Unlike NABARD's officer-grade recruitments, this specialist hiring has no written examination. Selection runs through:

1
Shortlisting — applications are screened against the post-wise eligibility, qualification and experience criteria; only shortlisted candidates are called forward.
2
Personal Interview — the decisive stage, assessing domain expertise, experience depth and suitability for the role.
3
Pre-recruitment Medical Examination — the final formality before engagement.

Candidates currently working in government offices, public sector undertakings, nationalized banks or financial institutions should keep a No Objection Certificate from their employer ready for the interview stage.

How to Apply Online

1
Visit nabard.org — open the official NABARD website and find the Specialist Recruitment 2026 (Advt 04) notification.
2
Match yourself to one post — compare your degree, certifications and years of experience against the post-wise eligibility table; apply for the single post that fits best.
3
Register with a valid email and mobile number — all communication about shortlisting and the interview will go to these.
4
Fill the form, upload documents and pay the fee — passport-size photograph, signature, degree and experience certificates in the prescribed formats.
5
Submit by 26 October 2026 — download the application printout; the interview date will be notified to shortlisted candidates.
Who should apply: This is a lateral, experience-led hiring — not a fresher opportunity. Cyber-security analysts, SOC operators, penetration testers and banking-IT professionals with the notified experience should treat the 26 October deadline as hard, since NABARD specialist advertisements rarely get extensions.

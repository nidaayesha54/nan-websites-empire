The Reserve Bank of India has officially released the RBI Assistant Mains Result 2026 on 24 September 2026 for the Main Examination held on 7 June 2026. As per the result, 1,489 candidates have qualified for the next and final stage — the Language Proficiency Test (LPT) — in the recruitment of 650 Assistant posts. The result is available in PDF format on the RBI recruitment portal, where candidates can search their roll number to confirm their qualifying status.

How to Check the RBI Assistant Mains Result

1
Visit the official RBI website — open rbi.org.in, the only official source for the result.
2
Navigate to Opportunities@RBI — select "Current Vacancies" and open the Results section on the recruitment page.
3
Click the result link — titled "Recruitment of Assistants 2026 – Main Examination Result/Marksheet".
4
Search your roll number — use the search option (Ctrl+F) to find your Roll Number or Registration Number in the PDF.
5
Download and save the PDF — and carefully verify the details and instructions mentioned in the result document.

Eligibility for the 2026 cycle required a bachelor's degree in any stream with at least 50% marks from a recognised university, with the age limit 20 to 28 years as on 1 February 2026 and relaxation as per the RBI Assistant Recruitment Rules. The application fee was ₹450 for General/OBC/EWS and ₹50 for SC/ST candidates, payable via debit card, credit card, net banking or UPI.

The Language Proficiency Test: What to Expect

With the declaration of the Mains result, shortlisted candidates move to the Language Proficiency Test (LPT) — the final step of the RBI Assistant selection process. Key points:

  • The LPT is conducted in the official/local language of the RBI office you applied for — languages include Hindi, Gujarati, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Marathi and Malayalam, depending on the office.
  • The test assesses your ability to read, write and speak the prescribed language at a working level.
  • There is no interview stage in RBI Assistant recruitment — the sequence is Prelims → Mains → LPT → final selection.
  • Mains examination marks are used for the merit process, subject to fulfilling the prescribed recruitment conditions and qualifying the LPT.

Keep checking the official RBI recruitment page for the LPT schedule notice. Use the waiting period to practise reading and writing the relevant language formally — official correspondence formats, precis-style writing and reading comprehension — rather than only conversational fluency.

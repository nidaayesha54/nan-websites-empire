NICL AO Recruitment 2026: 321 Administrative Officer Posts — Apply by 28 October, Prelims 17 Nov

The National Insurance Company Limited (NICL) has released its Administrative Officer (Scale-I) Recruitment 2026 notification for 321 posts — 200 Generalist and 121 Specialist officers. Online applications run from 8 October to 28 October 2026 on nationalinsurance.nic.co.in, with Phase-I scheduled for 17 November 2026 and Phase-II for 22 December 2026. With a starting basic pay of Rs. 82,485 and approximate monthly emoluments of Rs. 1,14,000 in metros, this is the highest-paying insurance officer recruitment open right now — and the deadline is ten days away.

Eligibility Criteria

Age Limit

21 to 30 years as on 1 October 2026 — that is, candidates must have been born between 2 October 1996 and 1 October 2005, both dates inclusive. Category-wise relaxation applies as per government rules.

Educational Qualification

Generalists need a graduation or postgraduation degree in any stream with 60% marks (55% for SC, ST and PwBD). Specialists need stream-specific qualifications: Finance needs CA or CMA; Legal needs a law degree; IT, Automobile and Risk Engineering need relevant engineering credentials; Agriculture needs a four-year agri degree; Company Secretaries need ACS from ICSI. Match your degree against the discipline-wise thresholds in the detailed advertisement before registering.

Selection Process and Exam Pattern

NICL AO selection runs through three stages — prelims, mains and interview:

1
Phase-I (Prelims) — 17 November 2026 — 100 marks in 60 minutes with sectional timing: English, Reasoning and Quantitative Aptitude. Qualifying in nature for the mains shortlist.
2
Phase-II (Mains) — 22 December 2026 — 250-mark objective test in 180 minutes plus a 30-mark qualifying English descriptive test (30 minutes). Specialist candidates face an additional professional/technical knowledge test. Wrong objective answers attract one-fourth negative marking.
3
Interview — the final merit weights the mains objective score and interview at 80:20, so mains performance dominates the final list.

Selected officers may be posted or transferred anywhere in India. Save the detailed advertisement — it remains the primary reference through every later stage.

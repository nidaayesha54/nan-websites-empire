IBPS SO Prelims Result 2026 Out (CRP SPL-XVI): 1,405 Posts — Mains on 1 Nov, Check Now

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection has declared the IBPS Specialist Officer (CRP SPL-XVI) prelims result 2026 on 15 October 2026. The result decides which candidates advance to the Main Examination on 1 November 2026 for 1,405 Specialist Officer (Scale-I) posts across participating public-sector banks. The result link stays active only till 22 October 2026 — check your qualifying status now and shift immediately into mains preparation.

Each specialty has its own educational bar — IT Officers need a BE/B.Tech or postgraduate degree in the relevant discipline, Agricultural Field Officers need an agriculture-allied degree, and Law Officers need an LLB with enrolment — so the mains paper is heavily specialty-specific.

Mains on 1 November — How to Prepare in Two Weeks

The mains is the real filter for Specialist Officers: unlike the prelims, it leans heavily on professional knowledge of your discipline, alongside reasoning, English and general/financial awareness.

  • Professional Knowledge first: this section decides the merit. IT Officers should revise DBMS, networking, cybersecurity basics and programming concepts; AFOs should cover agronomy, horticulture and allied schemes; Law Officers should revise banking and commercial laws; Rajbhasha candidates should drill Hindi grammar and translation; HR candidates should cover HRM fundamentals and labour laws.
  • Reasoning and English: maintain prelims-level speed with one sectional practice set daily — these sections protect your overall score.
  • General awareness with a banking tilt: RBI notifications, banking schemes, budget provisions for your sector, and major appointments of the last six months.
  • Mock discipline: at least four full mains-pattern mocks before 1 November, with error analysis after each. The mains has sectional timing — practice switching gears between sections.

After the mains comes the interview and then provisional allotment to participating banks on a merit-cum-preference basis. Keep your degree certificates, professional registrations (for Law Officers) and category certificates ready now — document verification follows quickly after the interview list.

Note: The mains admit card is issued separately on ibps.in in the days before 1 November. Download it as soon as it goes live and verify your centre and shift.

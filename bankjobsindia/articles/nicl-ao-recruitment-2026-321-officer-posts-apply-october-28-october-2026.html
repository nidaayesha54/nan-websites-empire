National Insurance Company Limited (NICL) is recruiting 321 Administrative Officers (Scale I) — and the window is almost shut. Applications, open since 8 October 2026, close on 28 October 2026, barely four days away. Phase-I prelims is scheduled for 17 November 2026 and Phase-II mains for 22 December 2026.

This is a PSU general insurer officer cycle — not to be confused with UIIC's separate AO recruitment. The 321 seats split into 200 Generalist and 121 Specialist posts spanning finance, legal, IT, automobile engineering, risk engineering, company secretarial, agriculture and a backlog IT seat.

Eligibility Criteria

Educational Qualification

  • Generalist: graduate or postgraduate in any stream with 60% marks (55% for SC/ST/PwBD) as on 1 October 2026.
  • Specialist: discipline-specific — CA/CMA for Finance, law degree for Legal, B.E./MCA in CS-IT for IT, Automobile Engineering degree, Risk Engineering degree, ACS/FCS for Company Secretaries, agriculture degree for the Agriculture stream.

Age Limit (as on 1 October 2026)

21 to 30 years, with the usual relaxations for SC/ST/OBC/PwBD as per government rules.

Selection Process and Exam Pattern

1
Phase-I Prelims (17 Nov 2026) — objective test, 100 marks, 60 minutes (English, Reasoning, Quantitative Aptitude).
2
Phase-II Mains (22 Dec 2026) — 250 marks, 180 minutes: objective sections plus a 30-mark descriptive English test.
3
Interview — final selection on Mains 80% + Interview 20% weightage.

The structure rewards descriptive-English practice — the 30-mark descriptive paper in Phase-II is where many objective-test toppers stumble.

Pay, Bond and Fine Print

Reports put the starting basic pay at ₹82,485, with total metro emoluments around ₹1,14,000 per month. Note the 4-year service bond reported across sources — read the bond clause in the official notification before applying. Interview call letters will be notified on the NICL website after Phase-II.

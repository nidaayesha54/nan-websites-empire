IBPS CSA-XVI 2026: Prelims 10-11 Oct, Mains 27 Dec

Call letter out — download your admit card, check the prelims pattern, the exam-day checklist and how to prepare for the mains.

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has released the preliminary call letter and information handout for the Customer Service Associate (CSA-XVI) 2026-27 recruitment cycle. If you applied for the IBPS CSA 2026 exam, your prelims are scheduled for 10 and 11 October 2026, and the mains follows on 27 December 2026. With the IBPS CSA-XVI admit card available from 1 October 2026, download it now, verify your details and plan your exam day.

How to Download the IBPS CSA-XVI Admit Card from ibps.in

1
Visit the official portal — open ibps.in in your browser. Never download the call letter from third-party links; the official site is the only genuine source.
2
Find the CSA-XVI link — look for the "CRP CSA-XVI" or "Common Recruitment Process for Customer Service Associates" section on the homepage and click the call letter download link.
3
Log in securely — enter your registration number or roll number along with your date of birth or password.
4
Verify and print — check your name, photo, exam centre, date and shift on the call letter. Print it on A4 paper and keep a digital backup; carry the printed copy to the exam hall.

IBPS CSA 2026 Prelims Exam Pattern (Standard CSA Pattern)

The IBPS CSA preliminary examination follows the standard CSA pattern with three sections: English Language, Reasoning Ability and Quantitative Aptitude, each with separate time. The prelims is qualifying in nature — the score decides who moves to the mains. Read the information handout released with the call letter carefully, because it carries the official instructions on marking, section timing and exam-day rules.

Why the Information Handout Matters

The information handout is easy to skip but hard to afford. It explains the exam-day procedure, the documents you must carry, and the items banned in the hall. Going through it once before 10 October saves you from last-minute surprises at the centre gate.

Exam-Day Checklist for 10-11 October 2026

Reach the centre well before the reporting time printed on your call letter — late entry is not permitted. Carry your printed call letter, one original photo ID (such as Aadhaar, PAN, driving licence or voter ID), and a recent passport-size photograph matching the one in your application. Electronic gadgets, watches, bags and study material are generally not allowed inside the hall, so travel light and check the handout for centre-specific rules.

What to Do Between Prelims and Mains (27 December 2026)

Prelims is qualifying, but the gap until the 27 December mains is where the real competition happens. Once your prelims shift ends, restart mains-level preparation immediately instead of waiting for the result: revise the full mains syllabus, practise full-length mock tests under timed conditions, and build banking-awareness from the months leading up to the exam. Keep visiting ibps.in for the mains call letter, and verify every update on the official portal rather than on social-media forwards.

Eligibility Notes

Eligibility for IBPS CSA-XVI was fixed at the application stage — typically a graduation degree in any discipline and an age limit specified in the official notification, with relaxations for reserved categories as per government rules. If you already hold a valid call letter, your eligibility has been accepted for the exam. Always rely on the official ibps.in notification for exact criteria.

Frequently Asked Questions

When was the IBPS CSA-XVI 2026 call letter released?
The preliminary call letter and the information handout for IBPS CSA-XVI 2026 were released on 1 October 2026 on the official portal ibps.in.
What are the IBPS CSA-XVI 2026 exam dates?
The preliminary examination is scheduled for 10-11 October 2026 and the mains examination is scheduled for 27 December 2026.
Where do I download the IBPS CSA-XVI admit card?
Download it only from ibps.in by logging in with your registration number or roll number and your date of birth or password. Avoid third-party download links.
What should I carry to the IBPS CSA-XVI exam centre?
Carry the printed call letter, a valid original photo ID, and a recent passport-size photograph. Reach the centre before the reporting time shown on the call letter.
Is there an interview in IBPS CSA-XVI?
No — IBPS CSA selection is through the preliminary and mains examinations only, followed by provisional allotment. There is no interview stage.

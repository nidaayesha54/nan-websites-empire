How to Apply (Offline)

An offline application has more failure points than an online form — follow this sequence:

1
Find the notification — open rbi.org.in, go to Opportunities / Current Vacancies and download the Pharmacist recruitment PDF.
2
Read the whole notice — note the eligibility terms, any fee, the RBI office address and the envelope superscription.
3
Get the application format — RBI offline notices attach a prescribed format; fill it in block letters, sign and affix a recent photograph.
4
Attach self-attested copies — pharmacy qualification, State Pharmacy Council registration, age proof and caste certificate if applicable.
5
Post it early — send by speed post or registered post to the address in the notice. Post at least 5–7 days early.
Why offline posts still matter: Offline recruitments draw far fewer applications than online ones — a clean, complete application reaching on time faces much lighter competition.

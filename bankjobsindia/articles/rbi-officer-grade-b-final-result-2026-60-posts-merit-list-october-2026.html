The Reserve Bank of India (RBI) has declared the Officer Grade B (DR) Final Result 2026 on 15 October 2026, closing one of the most prestigious recruitment cycles in Indian banking. The result covers 60 vacancies — 40 in the General cadre, 10 in DEPR and 10 in DSIM — under Advertisement No. RBISB/BA/01/2026-27.

The General cadre final merit list is published as a roll-number-wise PDF on the RBI opportunities portal. Candidates who appeared for the Phase-2 examination and the September–October interviews can now check whether they made the final selection.

How to Check the RBI Grade B Final Merit List

1
Visit opportunities.rbi.org.in — open the RBI's official recruitment portal.
2
Go to Current Vacancies → Results — find the result section for Officers in Grade B (DR), Panel Year 2026.
3
Open the final result notice — select the General cadre final result link.
4
Download the merit-list PDF — the PDF lists roll numbers of candidates recommended for appointment — search yours with Ctrl+F.
5
Follow the notice's instructions — recommended candidates get joining formalities, medical and document instructions in the accompanying notice.

Salary and Career — Why Grade B Is the Dream Post

Selected officers start on a basic pay of Rs 55,200 per month on the scale Rs 55,200–2,850(9)–80,850–EB–2,850(2)–86,550–3,300(4)–99,750, with initial gross monthly emoluments of approximately Rs 1,16,914 (without housing). On top of the pay come dearness, local, house-rent, family and grade allowances under RBI rules — plus the unmatched prestige of a central-banking career with structured promotion to Grade C and beyond.

If your roll number is not in the list: RBI's Grade B cycle repeats annually — the Phase-1/Phase-2 pattern stays broadly stable, so this year's papers and cutoffs are your best study material for the next attempt.

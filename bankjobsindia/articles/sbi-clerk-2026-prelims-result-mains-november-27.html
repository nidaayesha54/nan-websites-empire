SBI Clerk 2026: Prelims Done, Mains Next

Preliminary exam held 26–27 September for 7,680 Junior Associate posts — result awaited, mains on 27 November 2026. Dates, cutoffs and preparation plan.

SBI Clerk (Junior Associate) 2026: Key Facts

PostJunior Associate (Customer Support & Sales)
Total Vacancies7,680 regular vacancies (CRPD/CR/2026-27/17)
EligibilityGraduation in any discipline
Age Limit20–28 years as on 1 April 2026
Application Window11 – 31 August 2026 (closed)
Prelims Admit Card21 September 2026
Prelims Exam26 & 27 September 2026 (online)
Prelims ResultAwaited — expected October 2026
Mains Exam27 November 2026
Official Websitesbi.bank.in

The State Bank of India conducted the SBI Clerk Preliminary Examination 2026 on 26 and 27 September 2026 for 7,680 regular Junior Associate (Customer Support & Sales) vacancies under Advertisement No. CRPD/CR/2026-27/17. Applications were accepted from 11 to 31 August 2026 and drew around 13 lakh applicants. The prelims call letter was released on 21 September 2026. (A separate special backlog drive ran under a different advertisement — make sure you follow the regular-drive links.) The prelims result is awaited and expected in October 2026, with the Main Examination scheduled for 27 November 2026.

Selection Stages

1
Preliminary exam — qualifying only; marks do not count toward the final merit.
2
Main exam (27 November 2026) — the decisive written stage; sectional and overall cutoffs apply.
3
Local language test — candidates must be proficient in the opted local language; those who studied it in Class 10/12 are typically exempted.

Mains Preparation Plan (6 Weeks)

With the mains on 27 November, prelims-qualified candidates have roughly six weeks. Prioritise:

  • Quantitative aptitude and reasoning: the two heaviest sections — practise data interpretation sets and puzzle-heavy reasoning daily.
  • General/financial awareness: banking news from the last 4–6 months, RBI policy, budget and SBI-specific developments.
  • English: reading comprehension speed and error-detection accuracy under timed conditions.
  • Mock tests: two full-length mains mocks per week with section-wise analysis; SBI mains rewards balanced sectional scores, not one strong section.

How to Check the SBI Clerk Result

  • Visit the official SBI careers portal at sbi.bank.in and open the Current Openings section.
  • Find the recruitment titled "Recruitment of Junior Associates (Customer Support & Sales)" under CRPD/CR/2026-27/17.
  • Open the prelims result link when activated and log in with your registration number and password/date of birth.
  • Download the result PDF and keep it for the mains admit card stage — SBI will not send call letters by post.

SBI publishes the prelims result and the mains call letter only on sbi.bank.in under Current Openings. Since nothing is sent by post, download and print each document as soon as it appears, and keep checking the portal through November for the mains admit card release ahead of the 27 November exam.

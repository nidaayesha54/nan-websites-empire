Nov 1 IBPS SO Mains: Call Letter Out — 1,035 Posts, Download Now

Key Details

Vacancies1,035 Specialist Officer (Scale I) — CRP SPL-XVI (one source claims 1,043 — see flag)
OrganizationInstitute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS)
LocationAll India — participating public-sector banks
QualificationVaries by post (B.E./MCA for IT, law degree, agri degree etc. — see notice)
Last DateMains call letter out 22 Oct 2026; exam 1 Nov 2026
Apply Linkibps.in
Source / Ad DatePrelims result 15 Oct 2026; mains admit released 22 Oct 2026

The Institute of Banking Personnel Selection (IBPS) has released the Mains call letter for CRP SPL-XVI — the Specialist Officer recruitment covering 1,035 posts in participating public-sector banks. The call letter went live on 22 October 2026 and the Mains exam is on Sunday, 1 November 2026.

The Prelims result was declared on 15 October 2026; only shortlisted candidates can download the Mains admit card. The six SO streams in this cycle are IT Officer, Agricultural Field Officer, Law Officer, Rajbhasha Adhikari, HR/Personnel Officer and Marketing Officer — all Scale I.

Post Breakup (1,035)

  • IT Officer (Scale I) — B.E./B.Tech/MCA or equivalent in CS/IT-related disciplines
  • Agricultural Field Officer — 4-year degree in Agriculture/Horticulture/Allied specializations
  • Law Officer — Bachelor's degree in Law with enrollment as an advocate
  • Rajbhasha Adhikari — PG in Hindi with English (or vice versa) at degree level
  • HR/Personnel Officer — graduate plus PG Diploma/degree in Personnel Management/HR
  • Marketing Officer — graduate plus MMS/MBA/PGDBA/PGDM in Marketing

Age eligibility is the standard 20–30 years for SO Scale I; the detailed criteria sit in the original CRP SPL-XVI notification, not the admit-card notice.

How to Download the IBPS SO Mains Admit Card

1
Go to ibps.in and open “Recent Updates” on the homepage — the CRP-SPL-XVI Mains call-letter link is listed there (posted 15–22 Oct 2026).
2
Log in with your Registration Number/Roll Number and Password/Date of Birth.
3
Verify every field — name, photo, signature, post applied for, exam centre, date and shift — before downloading.
4
Print on A4 and affix the same photograph used in the application; carry the printout with an original photo ID (Aadhaar/PAN/passport) on 1 November.

Exam-Day Rules

  • No phones, smart watches, calculators or study material inside the hall — possession is treated as malpractice.
  • Reach the centre by the reporting time on the call letter; late entry is barred.
  • The mains is an online CBT — Rajbhasha, Law, AFO and HR streams have a professional-knowledge-heavy pattern; IT and Marketing candidates should recheck their stream's paper structure in the notification.

What Comes After Mains

Mains qualifiers are shortlisted for the interview; the final allotment is on the Mains + Interview combined score. Keep your category, education and experience documents ready — provisional allotment interviews run within weeks of the mains result.

Honesty flag: vacancy count conflicts across sources — the Times of India, Telegraph India and CareerIndia all report 1,035 posts, while one source claims a revised 1,043. We use 1,035 (majority, reputable outlets). The exam date is consistently reported as 1 November 2026 (Sunday), which checks out on the calendar. Post-wise qualification and age details above are the standard CRP SO norms — confirm against the CRP SPL-XVI notification PDF on ibps.in.

How to Apply

A five-step guide to landing a UK government job.

Find the vacancy
Search official listings on GOV.UK's Find a job service and civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk for Civil Service roles. Filter by department, grade and location.
Understand Success Profiles
Civil Service applications are scored against Civil Service Success Profiles — behaviours, strengths, experience and technical skills. Read the advert's person specification carefully.
Write a tailored application
Use the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result) for behaviour examples, typically 250 words each. Address every essential criterion.
Complete tests and interviews
Expect online situational judgement tests, followed by a panel interview covering behaviours and strengths, plus any role-specific assessments.
Pass pre-employment checks
Offers are conditional on right-to-work checks, references and, for some roles, security clearance (CTC, SC or DV). Keep documents ready to speed things up.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for UK government jobs?

Most Civil Service roles are advertised on civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk, while other public sector jobs appear on GOV.UK's Find a job service. Create an account, complete the online application form and submit before the advertised closing date.

What are Civil Service Success Profiles?

Success Profiles is the Civil Service recruitment framework. Applications are assessed against behaviours, strengths, experience, technical skills and ability, depending on the role and grade.

Do I need UK citizenship to work in the Civil Service?

Most Civil Service roles require UK, Irish, Commonwealth or EEA nationality, and all appointments are subject to right-to-work checks. Specific nationality requirements are listed in each job advert.

What grades exist in the UK Civil Service?

Grades run from Administrative Assistant (AA) and Administrative Officer (AO) through Executive Officer (EO), Higher Executive Officer (HEO), Senior Executive Officer (SEO), to Grade 7, Grade 6 and the Senior Civil Service.

How long does Civil Service recruitment take?

The process typically takes 6 to 12 weeks from the closing date, including sifting, interviews and pre-employment checks such as references and security clearance where required.

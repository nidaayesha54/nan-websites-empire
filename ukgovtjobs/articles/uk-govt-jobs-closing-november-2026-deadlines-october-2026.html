Four UK government and police recruitment campaigns close between 25 October and 15 November 2026. These are the deadlines — and how to apply before they shut.

From a detective constable route with West Yorkshire Police to HMRC’s graduate valuation surveyor scheme, the Civil Service Fast Stream 2027 and counter-terrorism student placements, these are the November 2026 deadlines to put in your diary now.

The 4 Closing Deadlines

1. West Yorkshire Police — PCEP Detective Constable (closes Sun 25 Oct 2026, 23:55)

The first deadline to hit is this Sunday. West Yorkshire Police is recruiting for its PCEP Detective Constable route — the entry path for people who want to investigate crime as detectives rather than join the uniformed patrol route first. Pay follows the national police constable scale, and the force recruits from across the West Yorkshire region. Applications close at 23:55 on Sunday 25 October 2026 — a true last-minute-of-the-day deadline, so treat Saturday as your real cut-off.

2. HMRC — Graduate Valuation Surveyor (closes 1 Nov 2026)

HM Revenue & Customs is hiring 20 Graduate Valuation Surveyors at £32,500 a year nationally (rising to £37,235 in London). The role involves valuing land and property for tax purposes — the technical work behind taxes like stamp duty and inheritance tax. It’s a professional graduate scheme, so expect a structured route toward chartered surveyor status. Applications close on 1 November 2026.

3. Civil Service Fast Stream 2027 (closes 5 Nov 2026)

The Civil Service Fast Stream — Whitehall’s flagship graduate leadership programme — closes for 2027 entry on 5 November 2026. Schemes span generalist, digital, finance, project delivery and more, with placements across government departments. It’s one of the most competitive graduate routes in the UK, and the application involves online tests and assessment stages, so starting early in the window matters. If you’re graduating in 2027, this is the one not to sleep on.

4. Counter-Terrorism Policing — Student Placements (closes 15 Nov 2026)

The last of this batch: Counter-Terrorism Policing is offering student placements for undergraduates, with applications closing on 15 November 2026. Placement years in this world give students a rare look inside national security policing — analytical and support work that genuinely contributes to live operations. As with all police roles, expect strict vetting and eligibility checks, so read the advert’s requirements line by line before you apply.

How to Apply

1
Shortlist by deadline, not by preference. The West Yorkshire Police route closes this Sunday (25 Oct) — start there, then work forward through HMRC (1 Nov), Fast Stream (5 Nov) and CT placements (15 Nov).
2
Check eligibility first. Police routes need right-to-work and vetting clearance; HMRC and Fast Stream campaigns set their own nationality and qualification rules. Don’t invest hours in an application you can’t qualify for.
3
Use the right portal. HMRC and Fast Stream campaigns run through the Civil Service Jobs portal; police roles are advertised on each force’s own recruitment pages.
4
Submit at least 48 hours early. Recruitment portals slow down or crash near popular deadlines — the Fast Stream is notorious for it. “23:55 on Sunday” means Saturday for you.
5
Keep documents ready. ID, right-to-work evidence, degree certificates or transcripts, and employment history for vetting — gather them before you start the form, not during it.

How Not to Miss These Deadlines

  • Watch the clock format: 25 October’s deadline is 23:55, but some government adverts close at midday — always read the exact time on the advert.
  • Set two reminders: one a week before, one 48 hours before. Campaigns rarely reopen once closed.
  • Don’t assume an extension: departments sometimes extend deadlines, but most don’t. Plan as if the published date is final.

The Bottom Line

Four genuine opportunities, four hard deadlines, three weeks. The detective route shuts this Sunday, HMRC’s £32,500 surveyor scheme follows on 1 November, the Fast Stream closes on 5 November, and counter-terrorism placements wrap up on 15 November. Pick the ones that fit, verify each advert live, and apply early.

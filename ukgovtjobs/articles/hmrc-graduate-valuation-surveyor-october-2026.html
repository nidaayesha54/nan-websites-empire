HMRC Graduate Valuation Surveyor Jobs 2026: £32,500 Salary, 20 Vacancies — Deadline 1 November 2026

HMRC is recruiting 20 Graduate Valuation Surveyors on its graduate programme — £32,500 national / £37,235 London salary, with applications closing 1 November 2026.

HM Revenue & Customs (HMRC) is recruiting for its Graduate Valuation Surveyor programme — a specialist graduate route into one of the UK’s largest property functions. The campaign lists 20 vacancies, paying £32,500 nationally and £37,235 in London, with applications closing on 1 November 2026.

Valuation surveyors in government work on the property valuations that underpin tax, estates and public-asset decisions — a niche but high-demand specialism where a government graduate programme is an unusually strong entry point.

About the Role

Graduate valuation surveyors join HMRC’s property profession and work towards chartered status (RICS), gaining structured training in property valuation, inspection and negotiation while contributing to the department’s work valuing land and property for tax and public-sector purposes. Like most Civil Service graduate schemes, it combines real operational responsibility with professional development, mentoring and the Civil Service pension.

The scheme is distinct from HMRC’s better-known customer service and compliance graduate routes — this one is aimed at candidates with an interest in property, surveying and valuation, and typically requires or favours a relevant accredited degree or equivalent qualification.

Who Can Apply

  • Qualification: graduate-level entry — check the advert for whether an RICS-accredited degree (or a non-cognate degree with a conversion pathway) is required for this year’s cohort.
  • Nationality: standard Civil Service nationality requirements apply; confirm the full list on the advert.
  • Location: national and London postings are reflected in the two salary bands — indicate your location preference when applying.
  • Right to work: you must be eligible to work in the UK; check whether visa sponsorship is offered, as Civil Service graduate schemes vary.

How to Apply

Applications run through HMRC’s official recruitment channels. Follow these steps:

1
Find the Graduate Valuation Surveyor advert on HMRC’s careers site or the Civil Service Jobs portal.
2
Check the qualification route — accredited surveying degree, conversion route or equivalent — and the location/salary band.
3
Complete the online application and any initial assessments (graduate schemes typically use situational judgement and work-sample stages).
4
Submit before the 1 November 2026 deadline — do not leave it to the last day in case the campaign closes early.

Why This Scheme Stands Out

Most graduates chasing property careers target private consultancies — but HMRC’s scheme offers structured RICS training, a permanent Civil Service contract from day one and a salary that competes with many private-sector graduate roles. With only 20 places, competition will be intense, which is why preparation matters: research the valuation work HMRC’s property function does, prepare genuine motivation for public-sector property, and make sure your qualification route matches the advert before you invest time in the assessments.

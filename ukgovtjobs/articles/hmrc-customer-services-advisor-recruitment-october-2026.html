HMRC Customer Services Advisor Jobs October 2026: £28,016 Salary & How to Apply

HMRC is hiring Customer Services Advisors at £28,016 a year — but the online test deadline is 2:00pm on 19 October 2026.

Home › Articles › HMRC Customer Services Advisor Jobs October 2026: £28,016 Salary & How to Apply

Published 15 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

HM Revenue & Customs is hiring Customer Services Advisors in the October 2026 cycle: £28,016 a year, a Civil Service pension with a 28.97% employer contribution, and hybrid working. Advisors are HMRC's front line, handling customer enquiries about tax, benefits and credits across phone and digital channels.

This guide covers the salary, benefits, the four-stage recruitment process and the online test deadline you cannot afford to miss.

Deadline alert: sit the Civil Service Customer Service Test within 24–48 hours of your invite. Final deadline: 2:00pm on 19 October 2026. Note the test system is down for maintenance on Sunday 11 October 2026, 4:00pm–7:00pm.

Salary and Benefits

The headline is the pension: the Civil Service Defined Benefit Pension Scheme comes with a 28.97% employer contribution. On top of that:

  • 25 days annual leave, increasing by one day per year of qualifying service, up to 30 days.
  • Flexible and hybrid working, plus family-friendly policies for parents and carers.
  • Coaching and development to build your skills and progress within the Civil Service.

How the Recruitment Process Works

HMRC assesses candidates under the Civil Service Success Profiles framework (behaviours + ability), across four stages:

Eligibility form. Confirm you meet the basic eligibility and nationality requirements.
Application form. Complete your application on the Civil Service Jobs portal.
Civil Service Customer Service Test. Sit the online test within 24–48 hours of your invite — the hard deadline is 2:00pm on 19 October 2026.
Video interview. Assessed against the Civil Service Behaviours listed below.

Behaviours Assessed

Your application and interview are assessed against three behaviours:

  • Managing a Quality Service — consistently excellent customer service.
  • Delivering at Pace — working efficiently under pressure.
  • Communicating and Influencing — clear, persuasive communication.

Nationality, Checks and Recruitment Event

Successful candidates face a standard criminal record check and the Baseline Personnel Security Standard (BPSS). Eligible: UK and Irish nationals, Commonwealth nationals with right to work in the UK, and eligible EU, Swiss, Norwegian, Icelandic and Liechtenstein nationals and qualifying family members.

HMRC is also holding a Teams recruitment event on Tuesday 13 October 2026, 12:00pm–1:00pm UK time — register via the link in the advert.

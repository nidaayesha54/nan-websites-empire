Home Office & DWP Jobs October 2026: Presenting Officer & DWP Roles Closing Soon

Two of Whitehall's biggest recruiters are hiring this October. The Home Office is advertising Presenting Officer roles closing 26 October, while DWP vacancies close 21 October — here are the details you need before the deadlines pass.

Published 14 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

Two of Whitehall's biggest recruiters have live campaigns closing in the second half of October 2026 — the Home Office and the Department for Work and Pensions (DWP). These rolling operational campaigns hire hundreds at a time and are among the most accessible routes into the Civil Service.

Two deadlines to mark: DWP vacancies close at 11:55pm on 21 October 2026, and Home Office Presenting Officer roles close at 11:55pm on 26 October 2026. Both departments warn that they may raise assessment benchmarks if application numbers are high — apply early, not on closing day.

Home Office: Presenting Officer (Closes 26 October)

The Home Office is advertising Presenting Officer roles (vacancy reference 479224) — a legal and tribunal-facing operational role that involves presenting the Home Office's case at immigration and asylum hearings. It is a respected route for candidates with an interest in immigration law and public administration.

  • Closing date: 11:55pm, Monday 26 October 2026.
  • Where to apply: applications must be submitted through the official Civil Service Jobs vacancy — the advert cannot be applied to anywhere else.
  • Reasonable adjustments: the Home Office states it can adapt its recruitment process for applicants who need adjustments because of a disability — contact the Government Recruitment Service well before the closing date.
  • Reserve list: a reserve list may be maintained for up to 12 months after the campaign, with appointments made in merit order by location.

DWP: Disability Living Roles (Closes 21 October)

The Department for Work and Pensions is advertising roles in its disability and health-related services area (vacancy reference 484702), with the application window closing at 11:55pm on 21 October 2026.

  • Visa sponsorship: new visa sponsorship is not available for these positions — applicants must already be able to work in the UK.
  • Working pattern: hybrid working is not guaranteed and is subject to suitability and business requirements — check the advert for the office you would be based in.
  • Checks: successful candidates undergo criminal record and security checks.
  • Interview: the process uses behaviour questions (including five behaviour-based interview questions in recent DWP campaigns) plus strength-based questions asked on the day.
  • High volumes: DWP may raise the assessment benchmark if application numbers are high — check email, including spam folders, for updates.

How to Apply Before the Deadline

Create your Civil Service Jobs account. Register free, complete your profile once, and set up job alerts for Home Office and DWP — new adverts arrive weekly.
Read the nationality statement. Both departments broadly recruit UK, Irish, Commonwealth and eligible EEA nationals; DWP is explicit that new visa sponsorship is unavailable for these roles.
Write behaviour evidence with STAR. Both campaigns assess Civil Service Behaviours — use your own genuine examples. AI can help you draft, but the experience must be yours.
Submit at least 48 hours early. Portals slow on closing day, and both departments warn that high volumes can raise the sift benchmark — late applications risk missing the higher bar.
Prepare for strength-based questions. These are not shared in advance, unlike behaviour questions which may be provided five days before interview. Practise concise, authentic answers about what motivates you.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do the Home Office Presenting Officer jobs close?

The Presenting Officer campaign (reference 479224) closed at 11:55pm on 26 October 2026, via the official Civil Service Jobs vacancy only.

When did the DWP October 2026 vacancies close?

DWP vacancies (reference 484702) closed at 11:55pm on 21 October 2026; new visa sponsorship was unavailable.

Does DWP offer hybrid working?

Hybrid working is not guaranteed for these DWP roles — it is subject to suitability and business requirements. Check the specific office and advert before applying.

Can I use AI in a Civil Service application?

AI can support your application, but never present AI-generated material or someone else's experience as your own — plagiarised applications may be withdrawn.

How long are Civil Service reserve lists held?

Reserve lists for these campaigns may be held for up to 12 months, with appointments in merit order by location.

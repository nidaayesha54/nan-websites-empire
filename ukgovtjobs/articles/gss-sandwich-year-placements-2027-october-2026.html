What the Government Statistical Service Is

The GSS is the professional community for everyone working in UK official statistics — the statisticians and data analysts behind the numbers that shape policy: inflation figures, employment statistics, crime data, health stats and census results. A sandwich placement puts you inside that machinery for your degree’s placement year, typically between your second and final year of study. You won’t be shadowing — placement students in government statistics typically do real analysis on real datasets: cleaning and linking data, producing tables and charts, quality-assuring outputs and presenting findings to policy teams.

Why a GSS Placement Year Pays Off

  • Paid professional experience: £25,501 a year is a strong sandwich-year salary, and it’s a genuine analyst role, not a photocopying internship.
  • Fifteen cities to choose from: with postings from Glasgow and Newcastle to Newport, Pontypridd and Fareham, you can often stay near home or your university city.
  • Clear graduate routes: a strong placement year is the natural springboard to the GSS graduate schemes and the wider Civil Service Fast Stream family — including the Operational Delivery and Property streams already open for 2027.
  • Portfolio material: you leave with real analytical outputs, government referees and a year of Civil Service behaviours evidence.

Who Can Apply

Sandwich placements are designed for undergraduates whose degree includes a placement year. The GSS recruits from a wide range of quantitative and analytical degree subjects — statistics and maths are obvious fits, but economics, social sciences, data science and other analytical degrees are typically in scope. As with all Civil Service campaigns, check the advert for the nationality and right-to-work requirements: the standard Civil Service nationality rules apply, and right to work in the UK alone does not automatically qualify you.

The Bottom Line

A paid £25,501 placement year inside the UK’s official statistics system, in your choice of 15 cities, with a midday deadline on 10 November — if your degree has a sandwich year, this is one of the strongest options on the board. Apply early and pick your cities carefully.

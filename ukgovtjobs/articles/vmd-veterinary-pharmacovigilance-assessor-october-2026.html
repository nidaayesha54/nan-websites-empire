VMD Veterinary Pharmacovigilance Assessor — Apply by 2 Nov

The Veterinary Medicines Directorate is hiring a Veterinary Pharmacovigilance Assessor (Inspections) — Senior Scientific Officer grade, £47,557 a year, closing 2 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » UK Government Jobs 2026 » Articles

The Veterinary Medicines Directorate (VMD) — the Defra executive agency responsible for the safety, quality and efficacy of veterinary medicines in the UK — is recruiting a Veterinary Pharmacovigilance Assessor with a focus on inspections of Marketing Authorisation Holders (MAHs). The post is at Senior Scientific Officer (SSO) grade on £47,557 a year, and applications close on 2 November 2026.

The role sits in the VMD’s Pharmacovigilance team, which safeguards health through the detection and assessment of safety signals, regular benefit-risk reviews of veterinary medicinal products, inspections of MAHs, evidence-based regulatory decision making and clear communication with stakeholders. Unlike a desk-only signal-review post, this vacancy has a specific inspections focus: you will assess how authorisation holders actually run their pharmacovigilance systems in practice.

The Role: Inspections-Focused Pharmacovigilance

Pharmacovigilance is the science of detecting, assessing and preventing adverse effects of medicines once they are on the market. At the VMD, the Pharmacovigilance team monitors veterinary medicines across the UK — and this assessor post carries an inspections brief, meaning you will evaluate the pharmacovigilance systems of the companies that hold marketing authorisations, checking that their safety monitoring, reporting and risk management genuinely work.

Day-to-day work will draw on signal detection and benefit-risk assessment, alongside evidence-based regulatory decision making. The team’s findings feed directly into published standards and the VMD’s wider regulatory work, so accuracy and defensible scientific judgement matter as much as throughput.

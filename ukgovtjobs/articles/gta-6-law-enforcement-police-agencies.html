GTA 6 Law Enforcement: Every Police Agency in Leonida

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 has a layered law enforcement system, and it is one of the most detailed parts of the game. At least nine distinct agencies operate across the state of Leonida, spanning city, county, state and federal jurisdictions, each with its own vehicle liveries, uniforms and areas of operation. They are visible throughout both trailers and in the screenshots the studio released in May 2025, and community analysis of that footage has mapped them into a system that mirrors real Florida's layered policing. This guide lists every agency, where it works, and what it drives.

The variety matters for players, because different agencies patrol different regions: the city police handle Vice City proper, a county agency covers the surrounding county, and the highway patrol works the interstate roads. Knowing who is chasing you tells you how the chase will behave — and which roads to avoid.

City police departments

The Vice City Police Department, the VCPD, is the primary city police force, serving Vice City itself. The department uses a white-and-blue livery on its patrol cars and is modelled after the Miami Police Department, with a round seal on the doors reading the city name and police department. VCPD vehicles are visible in the first trailer at around 1:07 and throughout the second trailer, and they are the force you will meet most often in the city.

Two smaller city forces cover the neighbouring districts. The Vice Beach Police Department operates in Vice Beach, the game's version of Miami Beach, with its own distinct livery visible on a Buffalo STX Pursuit cruiser in the studio's screenshots. The Port Gellhorn Police Department serves the town of Port Gellhorn, using Vapid Police Cruiser Interceptors with its own livery — a small-town force for a small town, with a fraction of the VCPD's presence.

County agencies

Outside the city limits, the counties take over. The Vice-Dale Police Department covers Vice-Dale County, the broader county surrounding Vice City, and is modelled after the Miami-Dade County sheriff's office — the county-level department for the area most players will drive through on their way out of town. The Leonard County Sheriff's Office operates in Leonard County, the game's version of Broward County, with a green and white SUV livery visible in the first trailer.

One county agency should be treated as unconfirmed. Kelly County is reported to include Port Gellhorn in the southwest of the state, but the county name itself appears only on a trailer exit sign, and the agency has not appeared in first-party material. Treat any guide that lists a Kelly County force as fact with caution until the game or the studio confirms it.

State and federal agencies

On the highways, the Leonida Highway Patrol takes over. Modelled after the Florida Highway Patrol, it runs a black and tan Gauntlet Interceptor seen in the first trailer, and it is the force you will meet on the long interstate drives between regions. The Leonida Department of Corrections operates the state's prison system, including Leonida Penitentiary — the prison where the story begins.

Two more agencies work the wilder parts of the map. Protection of Animals and Controlled Hunting, known as POACH, is Leonida's wildlife enforcement agency, the version of Florida's Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission. In one of the studio's screenshots, POACH officers are shown pursuing poachers fleeing through the Grassrivers in a Bison hauling a trailer, using Vapid police SUVs with POACH-specific livery. On the water, the United States Coast Guard operates in coastal waters around Vice City and the Leonida Keys, with a response boat inspired by the real RB-S II confirmed in vehicle listings.

Vehicles and gear

The fleets are as varied as the agencies. The main cruiser models come from the Vapid and Bravado brands: the Vapid Police Cruiser Interceptor is the most commonly confirmed patrol car, the Bravado Police Buffalo STX serves both the VCPD and Vice-Dale Police, and an older-generation Vapid Stanier LE, in the style of a Crown Victoria, appears in the second trailer's highway sequence. The Leonard County Sheriff runs a cruiser with a pushbar and LED lightbar, rammed by a pursuing car in the second trailer.

For heavier work, there is the Brute Police Riot, an armoured tactical response vehicle in the style of a Lenco Bear, used for riot and tactical operations, and the Buckingham Police Maverick helicopter, which hovers above the window escape in the second trailer. A Western Sovereign police motorcycle appears in the studio's screenshots, and the water fleet includes rigid-hulled inflatable police boats, fast patrol boats and airboats for the swamps. A tactical unit with an armoured truck and plainclothes officers in unmarked cars has also been identified in the footage.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many police agencies are in GTA 6?

At least nine have been identified from trailer and screenshot analysis, across city, county, state and federal levels. The studio has not published a list of agencies, so the count comes from what is visible in the footage.

Which agency chases you where?

The VCPD handles Vice City proper, county agencies cover the surrounding counties, and the Leonida Highway Patrol works the interstate roads. The Coast Guard and POACH cover the water and the swamps.

Can you play as a cop in GTA 6?

No. The game has two protagonists, Jason and Lucia, and no playable police role has been announced.

The bottom line: Leonida is policed by at least nine agencies, from the Vice City Police in the city to the Highway Patrol on the interstates and the Coast Guard on the water. Learn which force owns which road, and you will know what kind of chase you are in before the first siren sounds.

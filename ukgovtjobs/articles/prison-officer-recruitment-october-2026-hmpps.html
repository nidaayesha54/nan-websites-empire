Prison Officer Jobs October 2026: HMPPS Recruitment, £34,928 Salary

HMPPS is recruiting prison officers across England and Wales at £34,928 to £35,875 — campaigns close 15 and 29 October 2026.

Published 15 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

HMP Prison & Probation Service (HMPPS) is running several prison officer recruitment campaigns in the October 2026 cycle across England and Wales — no degree required.

The flagship Prison Officer Scheme (job ref 22401) closes at 11:55pm on 15 October 2026. Applications go through the official HMP Prison & Probation Service recruitment website.

Visa warning: prison officer roles are not eligible for new Skilled Worker visa sponsorship (immigration rules changed 22 July 2025). You must already have the right to work in the UK to apply.

Salary and Pay

Standard campaigns start at £34,928. At HMP Styal, HMP Garth and HMP Berwyn the starting salary is £35,875 for a 39-hour week including 20% unsocial — the premium for evening, night and weekend working.

HMP Styal is a women's prison: under Schedule 9 of the Equality Act 2010, that campaign is open to female applicants only.

How the Selection Process Works

Recruitment follows four stages:

Initial application. Apply through the official HMP Prison & Probation Service recruitment website.
Online test. Assesses your judgement and numerical skills.
Online assessment centre (OAC). Behaviour-based assessment.
Medical and fitness test. Includes eyesight, hearing and blood pressure screening.

The SSCL Recruitment Enquiries Team can be reached on 0345 241 5358 (Mon–Fri, 8am–6pm) or at Moj-recruitment-vetting-enquiries@gov.sscl.com.

Behaviours Assessed

The four assessed behaviours are: Communicating and Influencing, Managing a Quality Service, Making Effective Decisions, and Caring.

Eligibility and Fitness Standards

You must be at least 18 at the start of employment and meet the Civil Service nationality requirements. Everyone must pass a medical and fitness assessment, meet the eyesight standard in both eyes and the hearing standard (hearing aids are not permitted during training). Category A (high security) prisons require 3 years' UK residency.

Tattoos: permitted unless offensive, discriminatory, violent or intimidating. Facial tattoos are generally not acceptable, except for cultural, religious or medical reasons.

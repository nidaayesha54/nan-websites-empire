UK Government Jobs October 2026: Latest Civil Service Vacancies

Your monthly roundup of the UK's government recruitment scene — the latest Civil Service vacancies, the salaries on offer, and exactly where to find them before the deadlines pass.

Published 13 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

October is traditionally one of the busiest months in the UK public sector recruitment calendar. Departments return from the summer slowdown with refreshed budgets, new policy priorities and — crucially — fresh hiring campaigns. In October 2026, thousands of roles are being advertised across Whitehall departments, executive agencies, non-departmental public bodies and the wider Civil Service, from entry-level operational posts to senior leadership roles paying six figures.

This guide rounds up the latest UK government jobs for October 2026: the departments hiring right now, the salaries being offered, and the official portals where every vacancy is advertised.

Heads up: the roles below were advertised in the October 2026 recruitment cycle, but some campaigns closed early in the month. Government recruitment moves fast — always verify live listings, salaries and closing dates on the official portal before applying.

Where UK Government Jobs Are Advertised

Almost every Civil Service vacancy in the UK is advertised in one official place: civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk, the government's central recruitment portal. It is free to use, lets you filter by department, grade, location and working pattern, and lets you set email alerts for new adverts matching your criteria.

  • Civil Service Jobs (civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk) — the main portal for Civil Service roles across all departments and agencies.
  • GOV.UK Find a job — covers wider public sector vacancies, including some local government, NHS and charity roles.
  • Department and agency careers pages — some specialist bodies, such as the police, intelligence agencies and regulators, run their own recruitment microsites.
  • Professional networks — senior and specialist posts are sometimes promoted through Civil Service professional communities on LinkedIn.

How to Catch New Vacancies Before They Close

Government adverts typically stay open for two to four weeks, and popular campaigns can close early once enough applications arrive. To avoid missing out:

  1. Set up job alerts on Civil Service Jobs for your preferred departments, grades and locations — alerts land in your inbox the moment a matching advert goes live.
  2. Follow departmental campaigns. Big recruiters like DWP, HMRC, the Home Office and the Ministry of Justice run rolling campaigns for operational roles (Work Coaches, Tax Specialists, Border Force Officers) several times a year.
  3. Check mid-week. New adverts are most commonly published Tuesday to Thursday; a quick check twice a week keeps you ahead of the closing dates.
  4. Keep your CV and behaviour examples ready. Civil Service applications use the Success Profiles framework — having pre-written STAR examples for common behaviours means you can apply within days, not weeks.

How to Apply for UK Government Jobs

Create your account. Register free on Civil Service Jobs, complete your profile and upload your personal details once — they auto-fill every application.
Read the advert fully. Check the grade, salary, location, working pattern, eligibility and the closing date. Note which Success Profiles elements the role is assessed against.
Write your application. Tailor every behaviour example to the advert using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result), usually around 250 words each.
Complete online tests. Many roles require situational judgement or numerical tests before sifting — practise with the official example tests.
Submit before the deadline. Portals often slow down on closing day. Submit at least 48 hours early and keep the confirmation email.

Eligibility at a Glance

Most Civil Service roles require UK, Irish, Commonwealth or EEA nationality, and every appointment is subject to right-to-work checks. Some specialist posts (particularly in defence, security and intelligence) require UK nationality and may involve developed vetting. Residency requirements vary — check each advert's nationality and residency statement before investing time in an application.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where are UK government jobs advertised?

Civil Service roles are advertised on civilservicejobs.service.gov.uk. Other public sector jobs appear on GOV.UK's Find a job service and on individual department or agency recruitment pages.

How many government jobs are advertised each month?

Thousands of vacancies are advertised every month across Whitehall departments, executive agencies and arm's-length bodies, from entry-level operational roles to senior leadership posts.

What is the typical closing-date window?

Most adverts stay open for two to four weeks. Some campaigns recruit on a rolling basis, while senior or specialist roles may close earlier once enough applications are received.

Can I work remotely in the Civil Service?

Yes — many Civil Service roles are hybrid or fully remote, including recent vacancies in training, policy and digital roles. The working pattern is always stated in the job advert.

Do government jobs require security clearance?

Only some roles require clearance. The level — CTC, SC or DV — is stated in the advert, and clearance is usually arranged after a conditional offer is made.

What the Migration and Borders Group Does

The Migration and Borders Group sits at the heart of the UK’s immigration and borders system — the policy and operational machinery behind visa decisions, asylum processing, border control and the digital services millions of travellers and applicants use each year. Digital Deputy Directors in this group typically lead large delivery portfolios: product and platform teams building and running the digital services that caseworkers, border officers and the public rely on. It is mission-critical technology at national scale — the kind of role where a failed service is front-page news and a successful one is invisible.

Who the Home Office Is Looking For

Deputy Director is senior leadership in the Civil Service — the grade at which you own outcomes, not just outputs. Typical expectations for a post of this kind include:

  • Senior digital delivery leadership: running multi-team digital product or platform portfolios, ideally in large, complex or regulated environments.
  • Strategic influence: shaping technology strategy with senior stakeholders across policy, operations and ministers.
  • Delivery at pace and scale: modern product practices — user-centred design, agile delivery, live service operations.
  • People leadership: building and leading senior teams of product managers, engineers, designers and data specialists.
  • Security and resilience mindset: services that must be secure, available and trustworthy by design.

Check the advert’s person specification — Home Office senior digital adverts are scored against published essential criteria, and your evidence needs to match them exactly.

Pay, Location and Working Pattern

The £86,000–£100,000 range places these posts among the best-paid Civil Service digital leadership roles advertised this month. The multi-location offer — London, Glasgow, Croydon, Manchester or Sheffield — reflects the Home Office’s distributed digital estate, but the catch is explicit: frequent travel to 2 Marsham Street, London is required if you are based elsewhere. Candidates should weigh that travel commitment against the location flexibility before applying.

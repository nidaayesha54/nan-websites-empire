Northern Ireland Civil Service Student Placements 2027/28: 110 Places at £25,796 — Apply by 23 October

The Northern Ireland Civil Service has opened its 2027/28 Student Placement Scheme — around 110 paid placements at £25,796 a year, closing at noon on Friday 23 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » UK Government Jobs 2026 » Articles

Students looking for a paid year in government should look at Belfast: the Northern Ireland Civil Service Student Placement Scheme 2027/28 was launched on 12 October 2026 by Finance Minister John O’Dowd, offering around 110 placements across government departments at £25,796 a year. Applications close at noon on Friday 23 October 2026 — a tight window, with placements starting in July–September 2027.

110 Places — How They Are Split

Two competitions will fill the placements:

CompetitionPlacesAreas
General competitionAround 90Policy and law, accountancy, communications and marketing, forestry, environment, science, ICT
Construction & InfrastructureAround 20Architecture, engineering, surveying, planning
Note: Place numbers, salary and closing date are as announced on 12 October 2026. This is the Northern Ireland Civil Service scheme — separate from the UK-wide Civil Service Student Placement Scheme. Verify live details on the official NI recruitment portal.

Pay, Benefits and Support

Placements carry an annual salary of £25,796 plus an attractive benefits package, and students get peer support through the Civil Service Student Network. Launching the scheme, Minister O’Dowd said it offers “an excellent opportunity for talented students to gain valuable, hands-on experience in a professional working environment”, adding that the Civil Service is “one of the largest employers here and the work happening across our departments impacts almost every aspect of societal life.”

What Placement Students Actually Do

Current placement students describe the variety as the standout feature. Erin Bradley, on placement in the Architecture Team within Construction and Procurement Delivery at the Department of Finance, assists with design work, drawings and meetings on public-sector construction projects — and encourages applicants, saying there is “such a wide range of opportunities available across lots of subject areas”. Business Studies student Sean McClure, on placement in HR at the Department for Infrastructure, supports the Resourcing Team managing information for around 1,300 staff, building confidence through real responsibilities.

Who Can Apply

  • Students across a wide range of subject areas — from policy and law to engineering and surveying.
  • Apply to the general competition or the Construction & Infrastructure competition depending on your field.
  • Check the official eligibility criteria on the advert, including academic stage and right-to-work requirements.

How to Apply

Follow these steps:

1
Open the Northern Ireland Civil Service recruitment portal and find the 2027/28 Student Placement Scheme.
2
Choose the general or the Construction & Infrastructure competition.
3
Complete the application with examples relevant to your subject area — the deadline is noon, Friday 23 October 2026.
4
Successful candidates start their placements in July–September 2027.

The Bottom Line

110 paid placements at £25,796 a year, real projects from architecture to HR resourcing, and a launch quote from the Finance Minister himself — this is a flagship NI opportunity. But noon on 23 October is less than a week away. If you are eligible, apply now; if you miss it, set an alert for next year’s competition.

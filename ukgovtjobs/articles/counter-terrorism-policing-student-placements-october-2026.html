Counter Terrorism Policing Student Placements 2027/28: 7 Streams — Apply by 15 November 2026

Counter Terrorism Policing HQ is recruiting students for 2027/28 placements across 7 streams — from data and technology to communications and corporate services, closing 15 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » UK Government Jobs 2026 » Articles

Counter Terrorism Policing (CTP) HQ is recruiting students for 2027/28 placements across seven streams — a rare chance to spend a placement year inside the national policing network that protects the UK from terrorism. The placements span change, strategy, data and analysis, corporate services, communications, technology and national security, and all seven close at 23:55 on 15 November 2026.

These are not generic internships: CTP placements put students on real national-security work, from analysing data that supports investigations to delivering the change programmes that modernise counter-terrorism policing.

What the Placements Involve

Student placements in CTP HQ typically combine real project work with structured development: you could be supporting analytical products, helping deliver a change programme, contributing to corporate planning or working on technology delivery — all within a vetted, security-conscious environment. Placement students gain national-security context that few graduate schemes can match, plus a professional network inside UK policing and the wider public sector.

Who Can Apply

  • Students: placements are aimed at university students, typically on sandwich or placement years — check each stream’s advert for the exact academic requirements.
  • Vetting: expect security vetting appropriate to a national-security environment; start the process early.
  • Right to work in the UK is required; check nationality requirements on each advert.
  • Commitment: placements run through the 2027/28 academic cycle.

How to Apply

Applications go through the official police careers portal. Follow these steps:

1
Open the police careers portal and search for the Counter Terrorism Policing student placement streams.
2
Pick the stream that matches your degree and interests — data, technology, communications, change or corporate.
3
Check the eligibility, vetting and academic requirements on the stream’s advert.
4
Submit before 23:55 on 15 November 2026 — vetting takes time, so early applications help.

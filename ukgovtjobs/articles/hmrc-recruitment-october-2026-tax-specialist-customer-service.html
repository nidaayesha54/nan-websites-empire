HMRC Recruitment October 2026: Tax Specialist Programme & Customer Service Jobs

HMRC is one of the biggest hirers in the Civil Service this October. From the prestigious Tax Specialist Programme to frontline Customer Service Advisor roles, here are the deadlines, salaries and application steps.

Published 14 October 2026 · NAN Jobs Portal editorial team

HM Revenue and Customs is running one of the busiest Civil Service recruitment cycles of autumn 2026: the flagship Tax Specialist Programme (TSP) and accessible Customer Services Advisor roles at Administrative Officer grade. The deadlines are tight — here are the salaries, tests and application steps.

Heads up: the Tax Specialist Programme closes at 11:55pm on 26 October 2026, and HMRC's online tests have a hard completion deadline of 2:00pm on 19 October 2026. Do not leave your application until the final day — Civil Service portals slow down on closing dates.

HMRC Tax Specialist Programme 2026 — Closing 26 October

The Tax Specialist Programme is HMRC's flagship entry route into tax professionalism — a structured development programme that trains you to become a tax specialist across areas such as compliance, policy and tax law. It is the programme behind many of HMRC's senior tax professionals, and competition is always fierce.

The 2026 campaign (job reference 484623) is advertised through the official Civil Service Jobs portal. Key details confirmed in the advert:

  • Closing date: 26 October 2026 at 11:55pm — applications after this will not be accepted.
  • Working pattern: you must be able to attend an HMRC office for at least 60% of your working time.
  • Reserve opportunities: candidates who perform strongly but are not offered a TSP place may be considered for other suitable tax professional roles at Higher Officer or Officer grade.
  • Checks: successful candidates must complete a criminal record check and baseline personnel security checks.

Customer Services Advisor Roles — £28,016 a Year

For applicants who want to join HMRC without a specialist tax background, Customer Services Advisor roles remain one of the most accessible Civil Service entry points. Recent adverts in the October cycle offered a salary of £28,016 per year with a generous benefits package:

  • Employer pension contributions of 28.97% into the Civil Service Defined Benefit Pension Scheme.
  • 25 days' annual leave, rising by one day per year of qualifying service up to 30 days.
  • Flexible and hybrid working policies, family-friendly policies and coaching and development opportunities.

Candidates face an online Customer Service Test before a video interview assessing Civil Service Behaviours.

How to Apply for HMRC Jobs

Find the advert. Search "HMRC" on Civil Service Jobs and filter by location, grade and working pattern. Read the whole advert — especially the eligibility and behaviours sections.
Complete the application form. For TSP, write strong evidence for the assessed behaviours using the STAR method (Situation, Task, Action, Result), around 250 words each.
Take the online test promptly. HMRC recommends completing the Customer Service Test within 24–48 hours of invitation. The hard deadline for the October cycle was 2:00pm on 19 October 2026.
Attend the video interview. Customer Services Advisor interviews are conducted by video and assess Civil Service Behaviours — have genuine examples ready.
Pass pre-employment checks. Successful candidates complete criminal record and baseline personnel security checks before a formal offer.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the HMRC Tax Specialist Programme?

HMRC's flagship development programme training tax specialists across compliance, policy and tax law — a structured, competitive route into a professional tax career.

When does the HMRC Tax Specialist Programme 2026 close?

The 2026 campaign (reference 484623) closed at 11:55pm on 26 October 2026. Check Civil Service Jobs for the next campaign.

What is the salary for an HMRC Customer Services Advisor?

Recent October 2026 adverts offered £28,016 per year at Administrative Officer grade, with 28.97% employer pension contributions and 25 days' leave rising to 30.

What behaviours does HMRC assess?

HMRC uses Civil Service Success Profiles: Customer Services Advisor roles assess Managing a Quality Service, Delivering at Pace, and Communicating and Influencing, plus an online test.

Can non-UK nationals apply to HMRC?

UK, Irish, Commonwealth and eligible EEA nationals are broadly eligible — check each advert's nationality statement.

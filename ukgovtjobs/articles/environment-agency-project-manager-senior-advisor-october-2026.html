Environment Agency Jobs 2026: £50k–£60k Roles — Apply by 25 Oct

Two Environment Agency vacancies close at 23:55 GMT on 25 October 2026 — a Project Team Manager paying £50k–£60k and a flood-risk senior adviser at £40k–£50k.

The Environment Agency — Defra’s arm’s-length body for protecting England’s environment — has two vacancies closing at 23:55 GMT on Sunday 25 October 2026: a Project Team Manager (vacancy 36356) at £50,001–£60,000, and an FCRM Mapping and Modelling Strategic Programming Senior Advisor (vacancy 35470) at £40,001–£50,000. Both are live on the Agency’s own jobs board.

These are senior specialist roles. If you work in project delivery, flood-risk modelling, or strategic programming — and want that expertise inside a mission-driven public body — this weekend’s double deadline matters.

Role 1: Project Team Manager (Vacancy 36356)

The headline role: Project Team Manager in Programme and Project Management, paying £50,001–£60,000, based in Bridgwater or Bristol. The Agency delivers one of the country’s largest environmental and flood-defence programmes, and project team managers sit at the centre of it — leading delivery teams and owning budgets and timelines.

The band is firmly senior level. Expect the advert to ask for a track record of delivering complex projects end to end and managing people — plus the Civil Service pension and the flexible working the Agency is known for.

Role 2: FCRM Mapping and Modelling Senior Advisor (Vacancy 35470)

The second vacancy is the FCRM Mapping and Modelling Strategic Programming Senior Advisor, at £40,001–£50,000 across various locations. FCRM is Flood and Coastal Erosion Risk Management — the programme deciding where England’s flood and coastal defences are built.

“Mapping and modelling” is the technical engine behind those decisions: flood models and risk data driving billions of pounds of investment. The advisor works at national level — prioritising schemes and shaping modelling capability.

Also on the Board: Internal Auditor (Closes 9 Nov)

We also spotted an Internal Auditor vacancy in Birmingham or Bristol at £40,001–£50,000, closing 9 November 2026 — an extra two weeks for qualified auditors after the 25 October pair closes.

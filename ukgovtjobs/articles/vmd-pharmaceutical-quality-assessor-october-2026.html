VMD Pharmaceutical Quality Assessor — Apply by 16 Nov

The Veterinary Medicines Directorate is hiring a Pharmaceutical Quality Assessor — Senior Scientific Officer grade, £47,557 a year, closing 16 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 23, 2026
Home » UK Government Jobs 2026 » Articles

The Veterinary Medicines Directorate (VMD) — Defra’s executive agency for veterinary medicines regulation — has a second scientific vacancy open: a Pharmaceutical Quality Assessor working on marketing authorisations for veterinary medicines. Like its sister pharmacovigilance post, this one is at Senior Scientific Officer (SSO) grade on £47,557 a year, with applications closing on 16 November 2026.

The Quality team assesses the quality data that companies submit in support of applications for marketing authorisations for veterinary medicines — and for applications to vary those authorisations. The team also plays a significant role in the VMD’s regulatory science strategy, including developing regulatory and scientific guidelines, assessing product defects and supporting the VMD’s international work, such as training other veterinary medicines regulators.

The Role: Quality Assessment of Veterinary Medicines

Before a veterinary medicine can be sold in the UK, its manufacturer must prove it is of consistent quality. That is where this assessor comes in: you will evaluate quality data dossiers — manufacturing processes, controls, stability and specifications — submitted by pharmaceutical companies, and make evidence-based regulatory recommendations on whether authorisations should be granted or varied.

Beyond casework, the post contributes to regulatory science: developing guidelines, assessing product defects and supporting international capacity-building. It is a role with genuine scientific ownership — your assessments directly shape which products reach the market and under what conditions.

Who Should Apply

This is a specialist post for scientists with pharmaceutical quality experience — think formulation, analytical chemistry, quality assurance or regulatory assessment backgrounds.

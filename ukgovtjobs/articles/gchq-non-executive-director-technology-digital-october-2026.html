The Role: Technology and Digital Lead NED

The Technology and Digital Lead is the board’s senior independent voice on all things technology: advising on digital strategy, challenging major technology investments, and helping the agency keep pace with a threat and technology landscape that changes constantly. GCHQ’s mission spans signals intelligence, cyber security and support to military operations, so the NED’s perspective needs to cover both cutting-edge engineering and disciplined governance.

Expect the role to involve regular board and committee attendance, engagement with senior executives, and periods of intense focus around major decisions or incidents. NED appointments at agencies like GCHQ are typically fixed-term public appointments rather than permanent employment.

Who Should Apply

This is not an entry-level or mid-career post. GCHQ will be looking for people with a demonstrable track record at the top of technology or digital leadership — for example, chief technology or chief digital officer experience, board-level technology governance, or leadership of large-scale digital transformation in complex, high-stakes organisations. Experience of the national security, defence or critical national infrastructure sectors would be an advantage, but exceptional technology leaders from other sectors should not rule themselves out.

As with all public appointments, you will need to demonstrate the Nolan principles of public life and be prepared for developed vetting. Read the person specification carefully: the sift will test evidence against each criterion, not just your job title.

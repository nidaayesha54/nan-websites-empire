National Highways Graduate Jobs 2026: Graduate Generalist £30,000 Salary — Apply by 19 October 2026

National Highways is recruiting graduates onto its Graduate Generalist programme — £30,000 salary with rotations across operations, major projects and customer operations — applications close 19 October 2026.

National Highways — the government-owned company that manages England’s motorways and major roads — has opened recruitment for its Graduate Generalist programme. The scheme pays £30,000 and takes graduates through rotations across operations, major projects and customer operations. Applications close on 19 October 2026.

For graduates who want a public-sector career with real operational scope, this is a standout opportunity. National Highways runs one of the country’s most visible infrastructure networks — the strategic road network used by millions of drivers every day — and the generalist programme is designed to build the next generation of its leaders through hands-on rotations rather than desk-based observation.

About the Programme

The Graduate Generalist programme is built around rotations across the organisation. Graduates spend time in operations — the day-to-day running of the network, from traffic management to incident response; major projects — the large-scale road investment programme that upgrades and expands the strategic network; and customer operations — the services that keep road users informed and supported.

This breadth is the programme’s main selling point. Instead of being channelled into a single specialism, generalists build a rounded picture of how a major public infrastructure company works — ideal for graduates aiming at future leadership, project management or operational roles across the public sector. Expect mentoring, structured development and real responsibility in each placement, with performance assessed against the behaviours National Highways expects of its future leaders.

National Highways employs thousands of people across England, and its graduate schemes are a recognised entry point into transport and infrastructure careers — a sector where public-sector experience is highly valued.

Who Can Apply

  • Graduate status: the programme is open to graduates — check the advert for the degree level and subjects required for this year’s intake.
  • Leadership potential: generalist schemes typically assess communication, problem-solving, resilience and the ability to work with diverse teams.
  • Mobility: rotations may span different locations and teams — check the advert for base locations and any travel expectations.
  • Right to work: you must be eligible to work in the UK; confirm sponsorship position on the advert.

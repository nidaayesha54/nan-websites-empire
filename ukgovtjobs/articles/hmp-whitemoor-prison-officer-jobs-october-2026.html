The Role: Prison Officer (Band 3) at HMP Whitemoor

HMP Whitemoor is one of a small number of high-security prisons in England and Wales. As a Prison Officer, you keep the establishment safe while working directly with prisoners — challenging behaviour, supporting rehabilitation, and acting as the key worker for the men in your care. The job is far more people-focused than its popular image, and HMPPS looks for people who are firm, fair and consistent — calm under pressure and decent in every interaction.

Eligibility and Vetting

High-security checks are stricter than standard prison roles — read these first:

  • Residency: at least 3 years’ UK residency, required for the vetting that applies here.
  • Vetting: Counter Terrorist Check (CTC) clearance is mandatory. If you cannot clear CTC, you cannot be appointed to a high-security establishment.
  • Fitness: expect a fitness test and medical assessment as part of the process.
  • Qualifications: none needed. Literacy, numeracy and IT skills are assessed during the process, not required up front.

Salary and Benefits

  • £36,816 — 39-hour week including 20% unsocial hours (the standard contract)
  • £34,928 — 37-hour week  |  £39,334 — 41-hour week

Officers also get the Civil Service pension, 25 days of leave rising to 30 with service, and a promotion ladder from Band 3 to supervising officer and beyond. New joiners complete paid Prison Officer Entry Level Training (POELT) first.

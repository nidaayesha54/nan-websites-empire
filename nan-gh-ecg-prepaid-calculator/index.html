How ECG Prepaid Works

  • Buy credit through the ECG PowerApp or by dialling *226# — no mobile-money charges or E-Levy fees on these channels.
  • Units = amount paid ÷ tariff rate for your category. The lifeline tariff (0–30 kWh at 0.8690 GH¢/kWh) is a subsidy for low-income households.
  • Check your balance in the ECG PowerApp, via *226#, or by pressing "07" on most keypad meters.
  • Units depleting fast? Check for hidden faults (leakage, earthing problems, faulty appliances) or an outstanding arrears balance being deducted from each purchase. Smart meters also record more accurately than the old meters they replaced.
  • Postpaid customers pay an extra GH¢12 monthly service charge; prepaid customers do not.

FAQs

How many units will I get for GH¢50 or GH¢100?

Use the calculator above: select your category and enter the amount. As a guide, a standard residential customer gets about 0.51 kWh per cedi — so GH¢100 buys roughly 50.8 kWh.

What is the lifeline tariff?

A subsidised rate of 0.8690 GH¢/kWh for households consuming 0–30 kWh per month, designed to keep basic electricity affordable for low-income customers.

Why did I get fewer units than expected?

Common reasons: you were moved out of the lifeline band (above 30 kWh), arrears were deducted from your purchase, your tariff changed after a quarterly PURC review, or your new smart meter records consumption more accurately than your old one.

Where can I buy ECG prepaid credit?

The ECG PowerApp, USSD *226#, or authorised vendors. Avoid paying cash to anyone claiming to be from ECG — all payments go through official channels.

Is prepaid cheaper than postpaid?

The tariff rates are the same, but postpaid customers pay a GH¢12 monthly service charge that prepaid customers don't. Prepaid also helps you control spending and avoid estimated bills.

How often do ECG tariffs change?

PURC reviews tariffs quarterly, and Ghana runs on a Multi-Year Tariff Order (currently 2026–2030). The calculator uses the latest published rates; estimates are indicative.

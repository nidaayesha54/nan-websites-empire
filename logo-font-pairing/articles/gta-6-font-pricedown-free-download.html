GTA 6 Font: Pricedown and Where to Get It

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and one of the first things every designer asked was the same: what font is that? The answer is not one font but three, and the honest version of it starts with a warning: Rockstar has not named the font. Everything below is identification by the design community — careful, well-evidenced, and worth trusting — but it is identification, not a statement from the studio. This guide gives you the answer, the history, and where to get each font free.

The short version: the wordmark is Pricedown, the big "VI" is custom artwork, and the text on Rockstar's own GTA VI site is a different font entirely. If you only remember one name, remember Pricedown — it is the one that makes a design read as GTA.

The Answer: Pricedown

The wordmark — the "Grand Theft Auto" lettering — matches Pricedown letter for letter, according to the typography community's identification. Put the game's wordmark next to Pricedown and the match is exact: the same condensed capitals, the same sharp serifs, the same swagger. That is not a coincidence. Pricedown has been the font of the series since GTA III, and every GTA wordmark since then has used it. When a font has been the face of a franchise for more than two decades, the new game is not going to change it.

That continuity is the point. The wordmark is not just a font; it is the brand. A player who has seen a GTA cover in any year since 2001 recognises those letters instantly, and the recognition is the value. If you are making fan art, a poster, or a thumbnail and you want it to read as GTA in half a second, Pricedown is the typeface that does it.

Who Made Pricedown

Pricedown was designed by Ray Larabie and released through his foundry Typodermic on November 30, 1997. It is a reinterpretation of Pinto Flare, the typeface used by the American television show The Price Is Right — which is where the name comes from. Larabie took the game-show lettering, tightened it, condensed it, and turned it into something that could carry a logo. The result is a font that feels like a prize announcement and a warning at the same time, which is exactly what a GTA wordmark needs to feel like.

The font comes in several weights, and the one designers reach for most is Pricedown Black — the heaviest cut, which is the version that matches the game's wordmark most closely. It is available free from Typodermic and from DaFont, under a desktop licence: free for personal use, with a commercial licence available from the foundry if you want to use it in work you sell. That is the normal shape of a free font, and it is worth respecting: free to download is not the same as free to sell.

The VI Is Not a Font

The big "VI" in the game's logo is not a font at all. It is custom artwork — drawn lettering, made for this game, and not available as a typeface anywhere. The shapes follow Futura Bold, the geometric sans-serif that has been the backbone of poster design for a century, and the free match designers use is Archivo Black, which captures the same heavy, geometric feel. If you need a "VI" that looks right, Archivo Black is the honest substitute: it is not the same lettering, and nobody should claim it is, but it is the closest free letterform to it.

This is the detail most guides get wrong, because it is tempting to say "the GTA 6 font is Pricedown" and stop there. The truth is two-part: the wordmark is Pricedown, and the numerals are artwork. A design that uses Pricedown for the words and Archivo Black for the numerals will look right, and a design that tries to set "VI" in Pricedown will look wrong — because Pricedown's numerals are not the shape Rockstar drew.

The Other Font on Rockstar's Site

There is a third font in the story, and it is the one Rockstar actually uses on its own website. The text on Rockstar's GTA VI site is set in GTAArtDeco, a typeface from the Colophon Foundry, released in November 2023. It is a deco-style face that matches the Art Deco hotels and neon signage of Vice City, and it is the font of the game's marketing text — the paragraphs, the captions, the interface of the site itself.

GTAArtDeco is not downloadable as a free font, and it is not the font of the logo. It is the font of the words around the logo, and confusing the two is the easiest mistake in this whole subject. If you are setting body text for a GTA 6 fan site and you want it to feel like Rockstar's own pages, GTAArtDeco is the reference — but you will need a lookalike, because the real thing is a commercial typeface.

Where to Get Pricedown Free

Getting Pricedown is straightforward. Download Pricedown Black from Typodermic, the foundry that publishes it, or from DaFont, where it is listed under the same name. Install it like any other font, and it will appear in your design tools. The desktop licence covers personal use — posters for yourself, fan art, a thumbnail for a video you are not selling. If the work is commercial, buy the licence from Typodermic; it is the foundry's work, and the price is the price of using it properly.

Archivo Black, the free match for the "VI" lettering, is available free from Google Fonts, which makes it the easiest part of the whole setup: one search, one click, and it is in your library. Pair the two — Pricedown Black for the words, Archivo Black for the numerals — and you have the closest free version of the game's logo type that exists.

Frequently Asked Questions

What font does GTA 6 use?

The wordmark matches Pricedown, by Ray Larabie of Typodermic, used by the series since GTA III. The big "VI" is custom artwork following Futura Bold — Archivo Black is the free match. Rockstar's site text uses GTAArtDeco from Colophon Foundry. Rockstar has not named any of these itself.

Is Pricedown free to download?

Yes. Pricedown Black is free from Typodermic and DaFont under a desktop licence for personal use. Commercial use needs a licence from the foundry. Archivo Black is free from Google Fonts.

The Bottom Line

The GTA 6 font is Pricedown for the wordmark, custom artwork for the "VI", and GTAArtDeco for Rockstar's own site text — three fonts, three jobs, and only the first one is free. Download Pricedown Black from Typodermic or DaFont, pair it with Archivo Black from Google Fonts for the numerals, and remember the warning: Rockstar has not named the font, so every claim about it is identification, not a statement from the studio.

05 How to choose the right logo font

  1. Match personality first. List 3 adjectives for your brand, then pick fonts that embody them. Still unsure? Take the brand font quiz.
  2. Test at tiny sizes. A font that looks great at 200px may be unreadable at 32px. Always preview small.
  3. Check the license. "Free download" doesn't always mean free for commercial logos. Verify before you launch.
  4. Pair it properly. One display font + one quiet body font is the professional formula — try our font pairing tool.
  5. Customize something. The best logos tweak at least one letter — a ligature, a custom dot, a modified terminal — so the wordmark is ownable.

GTA 6 Trailers: Every Video and What It Showed

November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S — and before a single player pressed a button, five videos had already told the story of the game. About 9.5 million people a month search for the GTA 6 trailer, and the numbers behind those videos are records in themselves: the most-viewed game trailer in a day, the biggest video launch of all time, and a first look that passed 300 million views. This guide lists every video Rockstar released, with its date, its length, its song, and what it actually showed — and what none of them showed at all.

That matters because the trailer list is also a record of what Rockstar chose to hide. No cast names until launch day. No PC date. No online mode. The five videos are the whole public record of GTA 6 before release, and knowing which is which is the difference between watching the real thing and watching a fan remake.

Trailer 1: December 2023 — The Record Breaker

The first trailer arrived in December 2023, a day ahead of its planned schedule after an early copy appeared online. It runs 1 minute 30 seconds, set to Tom Petty's "Love Is a Long Road", and it introduced the world in a single sweep: Lucia in prison, Jason in the Keys, Vice City at night, and the exchange that became the game's most quoted line — "Trust?" — "Trust." In its first 24 hours it drew 90,421,491 YouTube views, a Guinness World Record for a videogame trailer, with 8.9 million likes — also a record. YouTube's own count was higher, more than 93 million. By late October 2026, the same upload had passed 300 million views, making it the most-watched Rockstar video for GTA 6. It also gave the game its only date at the time: a 2025 release window, with no month attached.

Trailer 1 is the one that set the template. Everything after it — the delays, the second trailer, the launch — was measured against a 91-second video that broke the internet on a Monday night in December.

Trailer 2: May 2025 — The Story Trailer

The second trailer arrived on May 6, 2025, four days after the first delay, and it is a different kind of video: 2 minutes 47 seconds, captured in-engine on a PlayStation 5, set to The Pointer Sisters' "Hot Together", and focused on the story rather than the city. It opens with Jason on a roof in the Keys, fixing shingles on Brian Heder's house, and it follows Jason and Lucia through the life they are trying to escape — jobs, heists, and the road that keeps pulling them back. Rockstar said it drew more than 475 million views across all platforms in its first day, calling it the biggest video launch of all time; on YouTube alone the count was about 84 million, which is less than Trailer 1's YouTube total, because the two numbers measure different things.

Trailer 2 also did something the first trailer did not: it put eight character profiles on Rockstar's site the same day — Jason, Lucia, Cal Hampton, Boobie Ike, Dre'Quan Priest, Real Dimez, Raul Bautista, and Brian Heder — with bios that described the characters and named no actors. The cast stayed secret for another eighteen months.

The Extended Look: August 2026 — The First Gameplay

On August 27, 2026, Rockstar changed the format entirely. "Grand Theft Auto VI: An Extended Look" is not a trailer but a presentation: 26 minutes 48 seconds of gameplay captured on a PlayStation 5, the first gameplay Rockstar had shown for the game. It is where the systems became visible — the safehouse as a lived-in place, with Lucia bringing home a bag of clothes and Jason taking a beer from the fridge; the wanted system, in which police act only on what they witness; the jobs, the driving, and the night lighting that reviewers later singled out as jaw-dropping.

The Extended Look is the video that turned the marketing into information. Before it, everything about how GTA 6 plays was inference from 91 seconds of trailer. After it, players could see the game — and the launch trailer that followed was a reminder, not a revelation.

The Launch Trailer and the Pre-Load Video

The launch trailer arrived on October 15, 2026, about five weeks before the game, running 1 minute 30 seconds to Justice's "Flower in a Hurricane", featuring Tame Impala, SZA, and King Gizzard & The Lizard Wizard. It is a celebration rather than an explanation: the city, the characters, and the scale, cut to a song. Then, on November 10, 2026, came the last video before launch — a 30-second "Pre-Load Now" spot, telling players the 212 GB download was open. There is no Trailer 3, and Rockstar has not announced a date for one.

Five videos in three years: two trailers, one gameplay presentation, one launch trailer, and one pre-load spot. That is the whole public record — and the game that launched on November 19 was, by every account, bigger than all five of them put together.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many GTA 6 trailers are there?

Five videos in total: Trailer 1 (December 2023), Trailer 2 (May 2025), the Extended Look gameplay presentation (August 2026), the launch trailer (October 2026), and the 30-second pre-load video (November 2026).

Which GTA 6 trailer got the most views?

Trailer 1 holds the YouTube record: 90,421,491 views in 24 hours per Guinness World Records, and more than 300 million total. Trailer 2 holds the all-platform record: more than 475 million views across platforms in its first day, per Rockstar.

Where can I watch the GTA 6 trailers?

On Rockstar Games' YouTube channel and in the media gallery at rockstargames.com/VI/media, where Rockstar hosts its press-friendly artwork as well. Watch those — not re-uploads, which may be edited or mislabelled.

The Bottom Line

Five videos told the whole story of GTA 6 before launch: a record-breaking first look in December 2023, a story trailer in May 2025, twenty-seven minutes of gameplay in August 2026, a launch trailer in October, and a pre-load spot in November. Watch them in that order and you will see the game the way Rockstar wanted you to see it — and you will notice what they never showed: the cast, the PC date, and the online mode that is still to come.

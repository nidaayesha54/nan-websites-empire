What does a baby cost in Australia? (2026 data)

Published estimates put baby-only costs for the first year at roughly $5,000–$15,000 (MyBudget, 2026) or $4,310–$9,620 (Canstar, 2024) — before childcare, private maternity care and any income lost during parental leave. Including pregnancy through the first year, the range is about $4,500–$12,000 on the Medicare public route and $18,000–$35,000 private (Dovelyne, 2026 estimates).

The three layers of first-year cost

  1. One-off setup — pram ($249–$2,500), cot ($249–$1,000), car seat ($349–$799, required by law), mattress, monitor ($49–$649), carrier, initial clothes. The most visible cost — and the most compressible via secondhand.
  2. Ongoing essentials — nappies (8–12/day for a newborn), wipes, formula if used (~$150/month), clothes every few months, extra laundry and power. Relentless and easy to underestimate.
  3. Lost income — usually the biggest number. The gap between your normal pay and leave payments dwarfs every pram and cot combined.
Government support (2026): Paid Parental Leave is $1,004.70/week before tax with an individual income limit of $186,487 and a family limit of $386,525. The Child Care Subsidy covers up to 90% of the hourly rate cap for families earning under $88,520, tapering 1% per $5,000 above. Check Services Australia for current figures before you plan.

Ways to keep the first year affordable

  • Buy big gear secondhand — prams, cots and carriers lose value fast but stay safe if you check recalls.
  • Model the income gap before shopping: work out your actual household income during leave, including Paid Parental Leave and any employer top-up.
  • Start a "baby fund" as early as possible — even small weekly savings compound into a buffer.
  • Compare childcare gap fees after the subsidy, not headline daily rates.

FAQs

How much does a baby cost per month in Australia?

For essentials alone, most families spend roughly $300–$600/month in the first year. Add childcare gap fees or lost income and the monthly figure can be several times higher.

Is private maternity care worth the cost?

Private care adds thousands in out-of-pocket costs (obstetrician fees plus hospital excess) versus mostly Medicare-covered public care. The choice is about continuity of carer and comfort, not safety.

What government payments can I get?

Eligible families can receive Paid Parental Leave, Family Tax Benefit, Child Care Subsidy and possibly Parenting Payment. Use the Services Australia Payment Finder for a personal estimate.

How much is Paid Parental Leave in 2026?

$1,004.70 per week before tax. Individual income limit $186,487; family limit $386,525. Confirm current rates with Services Australia.

What is the biggest hidden cost?

Lost income. A household dropping from two incomes to one (or one-and-a-half) for 6–12 months usually finds that gap is larger than every item of baby gear combined.

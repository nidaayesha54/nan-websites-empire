GTA 6 Has No Sound on PS5? Check This Button First

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

You launch GTA 6 on PS5, the picture is perfect, Vice City looks incredible — and there is no sound at all. No music, no dialogue, no engine noise. Before you dig through settings menus or assume the game is broken, check one button. It takes ten seconds, and it is the cause of this problem more often than any setting in the game.

GTA 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S. The no-sound problem is one of the most reported small issues of launch week, and the fix is usually embarrassingly simple — which is exactly why people miss it.

The One-Button Fix

The DualSense controller has a mute button on its underside, just below the PlayStation button. A quick tap of that button toggles the microphone mute. But holding the button down does something much bigger: it mutes ALL sound output from the console. If you held that button — even accidentally, during an intense moment, or while picking the controller up — your PS5 went silent, and nothing in the game can fix it, because the mute happened at the controller, not in the game.

Look at the button now. If the orange light next to it is blinking, the controller is muted. Press the button once to restore sound. That is the whole fix for most players: one press, sound returns immediately.

Step by Step: Unmute in Ten Seconds

First, look at the bottom edge of your DualSense controller and find the small horizontal mute button with the microphone icon. Second, check the light: a blinking orange light means all sound output is muted. Third, press the button once — a single tap, not a hold — and listen. The sound should come back straight away. If it does not, press it once more and check again: it is easy to tap twice and re-mute by accident.

This is worth doing even if you are sure you never touched the button. The button sits exactly where your fingers rest when you grip the controller, and a long press during gameplay is easy to do without noticing — especially in a game as intense as GTA 6's opening missions.

If the Button Is Not the Problem

If the mute light is off and there is still no sound, work through the console's audio settings. On PS5, go to Settings, then Sound, then Audio Output, and confirm the output device is the one you are actually listening to — TV speakers, soundbar, or headset. If a headset is connected, the sound may be routing to it while you are listening to the TV. Unplug the headset and test again.

Then check the obvious: the TV's own volume and mute, the input the console is on, and the HDMI cable. Reseat the HDMI cable at both ends — a loose connection can carry picture without sound. Try a different HDMI port if one is free. These checks take two minutes and rule out the whole chain between the console and your ears.

Sound in Menus but Not in the Game?

If you can hear menu sounds but nothing inside GTA 6 itself, the problem is in the game's audio settings rather than the console. Open the in-game settings, go to the audio menu, and check the master volume and the individual sliders — music, effects, and dialogue can all be set separately. Also check that a radio station is actually selected: if the radio is off, Vice City goes quiet in a way that feels like a bug but is not. If only the music is missing while dialogue and effects play normally, that is a separate radio issue with its own fixes.

No Sound Only in GTA 6?

If every other game and app has sound and only GTA 6 is silent, fully quit the game and relaunch it: highlight GTA 6, press Options, and choose Close Game, then start it again. If that does not work, check for a game update — launch-week patches have addressed audio issues — and restart the console fully rather than using rest mode. A clean launch after an update resolves most game-specific audio problems.

If the problem started after a crash or freeze, the same advice applies: restart the console, relaunch clean, and check the mute button once more before assuming anything else.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why did my sound disappear in the middle of the game?

Most often, the DualSense mute button was held down accidentally — it sits where your fingers grip the controller. A blinking orange light next to the button confirms it. One press restores the sound.

Does the mute button affect the microphone only?

No. A quick tap mutes the microphone, but holding the button mutes all sound output from the console. That is why the whole game can go silent from one button press.

The Bottom Line

No sound in GTA 6 on PS5 is usually one button: the DualSense mute button, held down by accident, silences everything. Check the blinking orange light, press once, and the sound comes back. If that is not it, work through the console's audio output, the TV and HDMI chain, and the game's own audio settings — in that order — and the sound will almost always return without reinstalling anything.

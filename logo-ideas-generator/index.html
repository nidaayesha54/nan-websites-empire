Frequently Asked Questions

How does the logo idea generator work?

Pick your industry and a design style, then the generator creates six unique logo concepts — each with a brandable name, a description of the visual idea, a color palette, and an SVG mockup preview you can screenshot and share with a designer.

Is the logo ideas generator free?

Yes. Every tool on LogoIdeasGenerator is 100% free with no signup — the idea generator, the color psychology guide, and the logo style quiz.

Which logo color is best for my business?

It depends on your industry and the emotion you want to trigger. Blue builds trust (finance, tech, healthcare), red creates urgency and appetite (food, retail), green signals nature and growth (eco, finance), and black/gold communicate luxury. Use our interactive color psychology guide to explore every color.

Can I use the generated logo ideas commercially?

The concepts are starting points for brainstorming, not finished trademark-ready logos. Use them to brief a designer or recreate the idea as original artwork, and always check trademarks before launching.

What is the best logo style for a small business in 2026?

Simple, scalable marks win: minimal geometric logos, wordmarks with a custom twist, and adaptive icons that work from favicon to billboard. Take our free logo style quiz to find the direction that fits your brand personality.

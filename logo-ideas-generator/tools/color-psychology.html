Quick rules for logo color

  • One hero color + one neutral. Two-color logos are more memorable than rainbow ones.
  • Design in black first. If the mark works in pure black, it will survive fax, embroidery, and single-color print.
  • Check contrast. Your logo must stay legible on light and dark backgrounds — always make a reversed version.
  • Mind culture. White means purity in the West and mourning in parts of Asia; red means luck in China and danger in the West.

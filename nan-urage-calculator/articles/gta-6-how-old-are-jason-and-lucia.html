How Old Are Jason and Lucia in GTA 6? The Honest Answer

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Ask how old Jason and Lucia are in GTA 6 and you will get a confident number from almost anyone — 30, 27, 34, 25. Every one of those numbers is a guess. Rockstar has never published an age for either of its two heroes, and the character profiles the studio put out in May 2025 give no age, no date of birth and no height for anyone in the cast. That has not stopped the question from becoming one of the most searched GTA 6 questions of the year, so here is the honest answer: there is no number, and here is everything that is actually known.

The reason the question will not die is that the game keeps giving you clues without ever giving you the answer. Jason talks about his past like a man who has lived through a few chapters already. Lucia comes out of prison with the manner of someone who has been hardened, not someone fresh out of school. The trailers show two adults, not teenagers, and that is about as far as the evidence goes.

What Rockstar has actually said: nothing

Rockstar published profiles for eight GTA 6 characters on May 6, 2025, the day the second trailer went out. Those profiles are the studio's own words on its cast, and they say a lot about Jason and Lucia — and nothing about how old they are. Jason grew up around grifters and crooks, joined the Army to put a troubled adolescence behind him, and ended up in the Leonida Keys working for drug runners. Lucia's father taught her to fight as soon as she could walk, and she starts the story freshly released from the Leonida Penitentiary, determined to make only smart moves from now on.

Read those profiles closely and you can work out a floor, not a number. Jason joined the Army after his teenage years, so he is an adult with a few years of life behind him — but the profiles do not say how many. Lucia has served prison time and carries herself like someone who has already made her big mistakes. Any article that prints a birth year for either of them is inventing it, no matter how confident it sounds.

The estimates everyone quotes

The most common fan estimates put both leads in their late twenties, with Jason a little older than Lucia — Jason around 30, Lucia somewhere between 26 and 28. Those numbers come from how the characters look and sound in the trailers, and from the shape of their backstories: a man who has already been through the Army and washed up in the Keys is unlikely to be 22, and a woman fresh out of prison with Lucia's manner is unlikely to be 19.

Estimates are not evidence, and they should not be treated as such. Two people can watch the same trailer and land five years apart, and both will be equally sure. The estimates are useful only as a rough sense of the characters' stage of life: two young adults, old enough to have a past, young enough for the story to be the start of something rather than the end of it.

The actors' ages are not the characters' ages

One number that is real belongs to the performers. The end credits list Dylan Rourke as Jason Duval and Manni L. Perez as Lucia Caminos, and Perez has said herself that she turned 34 in September 2026, posting a birthday photo with the caption "34 years baby!" That is a confirmed fact about the actor — and it is not a fact about the character.

It is worth saying plainly because articles keep blurring the two. An actor's age tells you nothing about the age of the person they are playing, and Rockstar has never said the two match. Perez being 34 does not make Lucia 34, any more than a 50-year-old playing a 30-year-old makes the character 50. Keep the two numbers in separate boxes and the confusion disappears.

Do the characters age as you play?

There are strong reports that Jason and Lucia change physically as the story goes on — hair and beard growth, evolving gaits, subtle changes in how they carry themselves over time, in the style of Red Dead Redemption 2. Some reports go further, suggesting wrinkles, tanning and skin-tone changes as in-game time passes. None of that has been confirmed by Rockstar, so treat it as reported rather than settled — but it fits the studio's habit of building deep realism into its worlds, and it is one of the reasons the age question keeps coming up.

If the aging system is real, it also explains why Rockstar might never print a number: a character who ages on screen does not need a birth certificate. What matters is where they start and how far they travel, and the game shows you both.

The old-timers put it in perspective

For fans doing the maths on the series timeline, the numbers get fun. CJ was 24 at the start of GTA San Andreas in 1992, which makes him 58 by 2026. Tommy Vercetti, the hero of Vice City, would be 75. Michael De Santa was in his late forties in GTA 5 in 2013, which puts him around 60 now. None of them has been announced as returning, and Rockstar has confirmed no returning characters at all — but the maths is a reminder of how long this series has been running, and how young its new leads are by comparison.

There is also an age gate to watch for. Reports suggest Rockstar may tie age checks to console platform verification or to its Social Club account system, to comply with age-assurance rules. That is unconfirmed, and the game's ratings already do the job on paper: the game is rated for adults, 17 and up in the ESRB system and 18 under PEGI. Whatever gate appears, it will be about the player, not the characters.

Frequently Asked Questions

How old is Jason in GTA 6?

Rockstar has not published his age. Fan estimates put him in his late twenties to early thirties, possibly born around 1995 to 1998 — but those are estimates, not facts.

How old is Lucia in GTA 6?

Rockstar has not published her age either. Fan estimates put her a little younger than Jason, roughly 26 to 28. Her voice actor, Manni L. Perez, is 34 — that is the actor's age, not Lucia's.

Will Jason and Lucia age during the game?

Reports suggest gradual physical aging — hair and beard growth, changing gaits — in the style of Red Dead Redemption 2. Rockstar has not confirmed it.

The bottom line: there is no age to report for Jason or Lucia, and anyone who gives you one is guessing. What is real is that both are young adults with a past, played by adults, in a game rated for adults — and that is all the number you need.

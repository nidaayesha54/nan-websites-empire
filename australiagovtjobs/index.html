How to Apply

  1. Search and apply via apsjobs.gov.au — the official Australian Public Service jobs portal.
  2. Prepare a pitch (usually 600–800 words) addressing the selection criteria for your APS level.
  3. Upload your résumé and pitch before the closing date — APS roles close at 11:59pm Canberra time.
  4. Complete any online testing or interview stage; shortlisting takes 2–4 weeks after closing.
  5. Foreign applicants need a valid Australian work visa — most APS roles also require citizenship or eligibility for it.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I apply for Australian Public Service jobs?

All APS roles are advertised on apsjobs.gov.au. You submit a résumé plus a short written pitch addressing the selection criteria for the level you're applying at.

What do APS4, APS6 and EL1 mean?

They are APS classification levels. APS3–APS5 are operational roles, APS6 is senior advisory/policy work, and EL1 is executive level — team leadership with salaries typically above $110,000 AUD.

Do I need Australian citizenship to apply?

Most ongoing APS roles require Australian citizenship, though some agencies consider permanent residents or visa holders for non-ongoing contracts. Visa status is stated on each ad.

What salary do APS jobs pay?

APS4 roles pay roughly $77,000–$84,000 AUD, APS6 $96,000–$108,000 AUD, and EL1 $113,000–$128,000 AUD, plus 12% superannuation and strong leave entitlements.

How long does APS recruitment take?

Shortlisting usually happens 2–4 weeks after the closing date, with interviews and offers 6–10 weeks after closing.

What is a 'pitch' in APS applications?

A 600–800 word statement showing how your skills match the selection criteria. It matters more than your résumé — tailor it to the specific role and level.

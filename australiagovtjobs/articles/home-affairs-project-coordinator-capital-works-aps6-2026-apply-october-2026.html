The Department of Home Affairs is hiring an APS6 Project Co-ordinator for Capital Works and Infrastructure across eight states — paying $99,734 to $111,701 plus 15.4% super, closing November 3, 2026.

✅ Updated October 22, 2026

The Department of Home Affairs (Property Branch — Capital Works and Infrastructure, Business Enabling Services) is hiring a Project Co-ordinator at APS Level 6, paying $99,734 to $111,701 plus 15.4% superannuation. It is an ongoing (permanent) full-time role advertised across eight states and territories, with applications closing 3 November 2026 at 11:59pm AEST.

This is the team that plans, delivers and monitors the department's capital works — infrastructure upgrades, detention facility projects, accommodation fit-outs — alongside procurement, contract management, governance and risk. It is project delivery inside one of the Commonwealth's largest and most operationally complex portfolios.

The standout detail: no mandatory degree is stated — experience in project management, construction, property, infrastructure or contract management is highly regarded.

What the Project Co-ordinator Does

  • Capital works delivery — planning, delivering and monitoring infrastructure upgrades and accommodation fit-outs;
  • Detention facility projects — overseeing works within the department's operational estate;
  • Procurement and contracts — running tender processes and managing contractor performance;
  • Governance and risk — maintaining the reporting and risk frameworks that keep major projects compliant;
  • Budget stewardship — tracking project spend against Commonwealth financial rules;
  • Stakeholder management — coordinating across the Property Branch and operational business areas.

Why No Degree Requirement Matters

Most APS6 project roles quietly expect a degree; this one instead treats hands-on delivery experience as highly regarded — a genuine opening for site managers and private-sector delivery leads. You still need proof: a CV, a one-page statement of claims and two referees.

Eligibility and Security Requirements

  • Australian citizenship — required;
  • Baseline (AGSVA) clearance — obtain and maintain; less onerous than NV1 but still a formal vetting process;
  • Home Affairs Onboarding Check (or ESC) — the department's own employment suitability screening;
  • Contact: Louise Brett, 0417932959, louise.brett@homeaffairs.gov.au — use her only for genuine vacancy questions, not to submit an application.

How to Apply

1
Open the official vacancy on the Home Affairs recruitment portal and re-confirm the 3 November 2026, 11:59pm AEST closing time.
2
Draft a one-page statement of claims built on delivered projects — budgets managed, milestones hit, contracts administered, risks controlled.
3
Line up two referees who can directly vouch for your project delivery, and warn them the department may call quickly.
4
State your preferred location among the eight advertised jurisdictions — the multi-state footprint means genuine geographic choice.
5
Submit before the AEST deadline; even if you miss the immediate vacancy, strong candidates enter the 18-month merit pool.

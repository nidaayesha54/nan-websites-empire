The successful candidate will own the full lifecycle of the Project Development Support (PDS) Grants program — from application management and SmartyGrants tracking, through guidance material and templates, Commonwealth Grants Framework compliance and stakeholder capability building, to high-quality grants and budget advice for SES officers and Ministers.

What the Grants Program Manager Will Do

  • Manage the end-to-end lifecycle of the Project Development Support (PDS) Grants program, including application management;
  • Track grants and workflows in SmartyGrants, the grants-management system used across government;
  • Develop guidance material and templates for applicants and assessors;
  • Ensure grants processes comply with the Commonwealth Grants Framework;
  • Build grants-management capability among program stakeholders;
  • Provide high-quality grants and budget advice to SES officers and Ministers.

About the Economic Implementation Section

The Economic Implementation Section delivers innovative, high-profile non-ODA programs in Southeast Asia, advancing Australia's diplomatic engagement and economic strategy. It sits where program delivery meets policy: grants must be managed with full rigor while supporting fast-moving diplomatic and economic priorities.

For a grants professional, that makes this more than an administrative role. You will be advising senior decision-makers, building capability across stakeholders and shaping how Australian programs are delivered across the region.

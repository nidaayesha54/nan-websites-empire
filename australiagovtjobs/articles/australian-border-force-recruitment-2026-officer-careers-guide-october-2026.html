Australian Border Force Recruitment 2026: Officer Careers, Pay & How to Apply

The ABF is recruiting Assistant Border Force Officers for 2026/2027 — APS2 pay with 15.4% super, closing 30 June 2027, frontline work at airports and cargo terminals. Full guide here.

Border Force Home Affairs 2026 Intake

Home › Articles › Australian Border Force Recruitment 2026: Officer Careers, Pay & How to Apply

The Australian Border Force — the enforcement arm of the Department of Home Affairs — is running a 2026/2027 register for Assistant Border Force Officers at APS2 level. These irregular and intermittent officers assist frontline teams during peaks in the movement of people and goods: at international airports, in maritime and air cargo, and in administration. It is the current front door into federal border enforcement.

The 2026/2027 APS2 Intake (Requisition 141608)

The register covers casual and non-ongoing roles, predominantly in Western Australia, at APS2: $62,775–$68,425 plus 15.4% superannuation (part-time pro-rata). Initial terms run up to 12 months, extendable by 6 months (18 months maximum). Work is mainly outside standard hours in a 24/7 operational environment. Applications close 30 June 2027 at 11:59pm AEST — late applications are not accepted. You must attach a one-page applicant response (minimum 11pt, Word/PDF) on your relevant skills, motivation and examples, with your name and requisition 141608 in the footer.

Remote postings add allowances: roughly +15–25% in the Northern Territory, +20% in the Torres Strait and +10% hazard pay in the Pilbara/Kimberley. Promotion is merit-based — APS3 to APS4 typically follows within 6–12 months of training.

Eligibility, Training & How to Apply

You must be an Australian citizen and pass background, medical and psychometric assessments. New officers complete a 12–24 week ABF College program before field assessment, usually entering at APS3. Operational roles demand operational readiness — sustained medical, fitness and psychometric standards, Operational Safety training, and willingness to carry personal defence equipment including a firearm. Some postings need a driver’s licence (4WD/medium rigid), an ASIC and a Working with Children clearance.

  1. Find the register on APS Jobs or Home Affairs careers (requisition 141608).
  2. Apply via “ourPeople” — Home Affairs’ recruitment system, before the closing date.
  3. Write the one-page response — skills, motivation, examples; 11pt minimum, name + 141608 in the footer.
  4. Review the Work Level Standards for your classification before writing.
  5. Prepare clearance documents — identity, referees and licence details for the checks that follow.
Register roles are a proven entry path — strong performers are considered for ongoing APS positions. But the register is not a job offer, and the 30 June 2027 deadline is firm.

FAQs

Is the ABF hiring in 2026?

Yes — a 2026/2027 register for casual and non-ongoing Assistant Border Force Officers (APS2) is open, closing 30 June 2027.

What does an Assistant Border Force Officer earn?

$62,775-$68,425 plus 15.4% superannuation (part-time pro-rata), with remote and operational allowances on some postings.

What are the eligibility requirements?

Australian citizenship, background checks, medical and psychometric assessments, and operational readiness including Operational Safety training.

How long is ABF training?

A 12-24 week intensive program at the ABF College before field assessment and probation, usually entering at APS3.

How do I apply?

Through Home Affairs' 'ourPeople' system before the deadline, with a one-page response document carrying your name and requisition 141608 in the footer.

Australia Government Jobs 2026

The Australian Antarctic Program launched its annual recruitment drive on 1 October — more than 200 contracts to live and work at Antarctic research stations, with applications closing 31 October 2026.

✅ Updated October 18, 2026
Home » Australia Government Jobs » Articles

The Australian Antarctic Program has launched its annual recruitment drive, offering more than 200 contracts for people to live and work at research stations in Antarctica during the 2027–28 season. Applications opened on 1 October and close on 31 October 2026 — a tight four-week window for one of the most distinctive employment opportunities connected to the Australian Government.

Successful candidates will spend between six months and over a year at stations including Casey, Davis and Mawson — among the most remote work environments on the planet — supporting scientific research, station operations and logistics across the continent.

Roles Available

  • Trades: electricians, carpenters, mechanics, plumbers;
  • Operations: logistics coordinators, aviation support staff, field training officers;
  • Hospitality & services: chefs and station support staff;
  • Technical: telecommunications officers;
  • Professional: medical practitioners;
  • Leadership: station leaders and senior management roles.

Most positions do not require prior Antarctic experience. Applicants must demonstrate relevant qualifications and the ability to live in an isolated community for extended periods — teamwork, resilience and psychological fitness matter as much as technical skill.

Pay: What the Numbers Mean

Base salaries for entry-level and trade roles start between AUD 79,000 and AUD 90,000. When Antarctic allowances for remote work and overtime are factored in, total packages frequently exceed AUD 120,000, and senior and leadership positions can reach AUD 150,000 or more. With accommodation, meals and living costs covered on station, Antarctic contracts offer one of the highest effective savings rates of any Australian job.

Eligibility

  • Relevant qualifications and experience for the role you target;
  • Medical and psychological fitness for isolated, extreme environments;
  • Demonstrated ability to live and work in a small, isolated community for months at a time;
  • Appropriate Australian work rights — check the specific posting, as most roles require citizenship or permanent residency.

How to Apply

1
Open the Australian Antarctic Program jobs portal and filter the 2027–28 season roles by trade or profession.
2
Shortlist every role that matches your qualifications — tradespeople can often apply across multiple station postings.
3
Prepare evidence of qualifications, licences/tickets and references; remote-posting or expedition experience strengthens your case.
4
Address the isolated-community requirement explicitly in your application — assessors weight it heavily.
5
Submit before 31 October 2026 — late applications are not accepted for the season intake.

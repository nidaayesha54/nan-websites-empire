The Defence and Veterans' Service Commission (DVSC) — the new non-corporate Commonwealth entity established on 29 September 2025 after the Royal Commission into Defence and Veteran Suicide — is building its workforce through a Temporary Employment Register open from APS Level 4 to Executive Level 2. Applications close 31 January 2027 at 11:30pm AEDT.

This is a rare chance to join a brand-new agency at the ground floor. The DVSC's mandate: independent oversight of system reforms across the Defence and veteran ecosystem, to prevent suicide and promote wellbeing for serving and ex-serving ADF members. The register is open to all members of the community, not just existing public servants. Because the DVSC is transitioning its employment framework, successful candidates may initially be on-boarded via the Department of the Prime Minister and Cabinet under a shared-service arrangement.

What the DVSC Does

The DVSC holds Royal Commission-grade powers — conducting hearings, summonsing people to give evidence under oath, and issuing search warrants and notices to produce. Its key functions:

  • Monitoring and inquiring into issues relevant to suicide and suicidality for serving and ex-serving ADF members;
  • Improving supports for serving and ex-serving ADF members;
  • Promoting understanding of suicide risks and wellbeing, including through engagement with people with lived experience.

How the Register Works

A temporary employment register is not a single job ad — it is how a new agency staffs itself before its full structure exists. Key mechanics to understand:

  • Non-ongoing engagements: initial engagement of up to 12 months, eligible for one contract extension of up to 6 months (maximum 18 months);
  • Not a guarantee: you are only contacted if a matching opportunity arises;
  • No ongoing conversion: ongoing engagement or promotion under the Public Service Act 1999 is not possible through this register;
  • Capability areas sought: inquiries; legal; oversight and evaluation; communications and engagement; research and analysis; corporate and governance; people, wellbeing and integrity;

Eligibility and a Serious Note

The gates: Australian citizenship at the closing date, a minimum Baseline security clearance before commencement, a National Criminal History Check, and possibly a six-month probation period.

The DVSC's own advertising also carries an unusual and honest caution: the work is sensitive — it centres on suicide prevention and suicidality — and potential candidates should consider how it will affect them personally before applying.

5
Submit before the AEDT deadline and note: this is an unclassified web-based recruitment system — your application must not contain any classified or sensitive information.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the DVSC Temporary Employment Register?

It is the Defence and Veterans' Service Commission's open register for non-ongoing APS Level 4 to Executive Level 2 roles in Canberra, used to fill vacancies as they arise. Applying does not guarantee an offer.

What salaries are offered?

APS4 $80,505–$86,097 through to EL2 $147,052–$175,016 plus 15.4% superannuation, under the PM&C-linked pay framework.

When does the register close?

Applications close 31 January 2027 at 11:30pm AEDT, submitted through PM&C Current Opportunities.

Do applicants need to be Australian citizens?

Yes — applicants must be Australian citizens at the closing date to be employed in the APS, unless the Agency Head agrees otherwise in writing. Baseline security clearance and a criminal history check are also required.

Can this register lead to a permanent job?

No — ongoing engagement or promotion under the Public Service Act 1999 is not possible through this register; it is for non-ongoing engagements of up to 12 months, with one extension of up to 6 months.

What skills is the DVSC looking for?

Inquiries, legal, oversight and evaluation, communications and engagement, research and analysis, corporate and governance, and people, wellbeing and integrity capabilities.

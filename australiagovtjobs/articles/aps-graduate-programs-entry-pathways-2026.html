APS Graduate Programs & Entry Pathways 2026 — How to Start a Federal Career

Everything a new graduate needs to know: the main APS entry pathways, who each is for, typical timing, eligibility and how to apply through APSJobs.

APS Jobs Graduate Programs 2026 Guide

Why Graduate Programs Are the Best Door In

A graduate program is the fastest, most structured way to start an Australian Public Service career. Instead of competing for one-off roles, you join a formal intake: paid rotations across different teams, a permanent APS position at the end, mentoring from senior officers, and a cohort of other graduates learning the ropes with you.

For the APS itself, graduate intakes are the main talent pipeline — agencies invest heavily in them because graduates become the future policy officers, data analysts, service designers, and leaders. In 2026, with agencies modernising services and growing digital and analytical workforces, graduate pathways remain the single most reliable entry route for people at the start of their careers.

The Main APS Entry Pathways

Agency graduate programs

The largest agencies run their own annual graduate programs — Services Australia, the ATO, Defence, the Department of Home Affairs, and the Department of Finance are among those that typically recruit graduates each year. Programs usually run for around twelve months with rotations, structured learning, and a permanent ongoing placement on completion.

Generalist recruitment rounds

Alongside agency-specific programs, generalist recruitment rounds place graduates across multiple agencies in policy, program management, and corporate roles. These suit applicants with broad degrees — arts, business, law, communications — who want policy or program work but haven't locked in a single agency.

Indigenous programs

Dedicated pathways exist for Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander peoples, including graduate and entry-level programs designed to grow Indigenous representation across the APS. These typically offer the same structured development as mainstream programs with additional cultural support.

Eligibility Basics

Before you apply, confirm the fundamentals — every ad states its own requirements, and they are non-negotiable:

  • Australian citizenship: generally required for ongoing APS employment, including graduate programs. Check the ad for the exact requirement.
  • Degree status: most graduate programs target students in their final year or recent graduates (typically within the last few years — the exact window is stated on each ad).
  • Security and checks: some agencies and streams require security clearances or background checks; start these early if offered a place.
  • Indigenous programs: may require confirmation of Aboriginal and/or Torres Strait Islander heritage, as stated on the ad.

How to Apply via APSJobs

Applications for graduate programs are lodged through APSJobs (apsjobs.gov.au), the official Australian Public Service portal. Follow these steps:

  1. Create your APSJobs account early and set up alerts for "graduate" at your preferred agencies — application windows can be short.
  2. Read the program's candidate pack carefully: rotations, locations, degree requirements, and the selection criteria you'll be assessed against.
  3. Complete the online application — usually a short form, your CV, and responses to screening questions or a written pitch.
  4. Sit the assessments — many programs use online cognitive or behavioural testing before interviews.
  5. Attend interviews or assessment centres — behavioural questions mapped to APS capability frameworks, sometimes with group exercises.
  6. Referees and offer — successful candidates are placed into the next intake and assigned rotations.
Missed the graduate intake window? General entry-level APS3/APS4 roles advertised year-round on APSJobs are a strong alternative — many current graduates joined the APS this way. See our full APSJobs application guide for how to pitch for those roles.

FAQs

When do APS graduate programs open for applications?

Timings vary by agency, but most major programs advertise mid-year for intakes starting early the following year. Set APSJobs alerts so you don't miss the window — verify exact dates on the official portal.

Do I need a specific degree for an APS graduate program?

Many programs accept any degree, while specialist streams (law, accounting, ICT, data) require relevant qualifications. The ad always states the degree requirement.

Do graduate programs require Australian citizenship?

Generally yes for ongoing graduate positions — citizenship requirements are stated on each ad, so confirm before applying.

What happens after I finish a graduate program?

Graduates are typically placed into an ongoing APS role — often at APS4 level — in one of their rotation areas, with a clear path to APS5/APS6 within a few years.

What if I miss the graduate intake?

Apply for entry-level APS3/APS4 roles advertised year-round on APSJobs, or look at non-ongoing contracts — both are proven alternate doors into the APS.

Australia Government Jobs 2026

In a landmark policy shift announced in October 2026, Australia now lets permanent residents from New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the United States and Canada apply to join the Australian Defence Force. Here is who qualifies and how to apply.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026
Home » Australia Government Jobs » Articles

Australia has opened a new front in its military recruitment drive: permanent residents from New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the United States and Canada can now apply to join the Australian Defence Force (ADF). The policy — reported on 9 October 2026 — marks a significant shift in Australia's military recruitment strategy, expanding eligibility beyond Australian citizens as part of broader reforms to modernize the Defence Force and address staffing challenges.

The change taps into a pool of skilled, already-settled migrants who know Australian life and can meet the ADF's entry and security standards — and it arrives as defence readiness climbs the national agenda.

Note: Permanent residency is mandatory — temporary visa holders are not covered by this policy. Citizenship pathways for serving members are expected to be addressed in ADF policy, so confirm the latest position with Defence Force Recruiting before applying.

Who Qualifies Under the New Policy

The policy applies to a defined group. Based on the announcement:

  • You must hold permanent resident status in Australia;
  • You must have resided in Australia for a minimum of two years;
  • You must be a citizen of one of the four listed countries — New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the United States or Canada;
  • You must meet the ADF's normal entry requirements — age, education, medical and fitness standards for your chosen role;
  • You must satisfy security clearance requirements before final approval.

The security-clearance step is unchanged and non-negotiable: it will still be required before any appointment is finalized, and processing times can be lengthy, so apply early.

Why Australia Made This Change

The government has stated that the change aims to strengthen military capability by tapping into a broader pool of skilled individuals. Defence recruitment has struggled with staffing numbers, and the reform follows a series of efforts to enhance national security and defence readiness. By including long-settled permanent residents from close partner nations, the ADF gains access to people who are already integrated into Australian communities — while keeping the security bar exactly where it is.

Roles and Pathways to Explore

The ADF recruits across the Navy, Army and Air Force into hundreds of roles — combat, engineering, logistics, cyber, medical, aviation and technical trades. Permanent residents should:

  • Browse the official Defence Jobs portal and filter by service and role category;
  • Check role-specific entry requirements — some specialist roles may still carry citizenship requirements;
  • Attend an ADF information session to understand training pipelines;
  • Consider whether general entry or officer entry fits your education and experience.

How to Apply

1
Confirm you meet the core criteria: permanent residency, 2+ years in Australia, and citizenship of NZ, UK, US or Canada.
2
Visit the official Defence Jobs website and choose a role that matches your skills and interests.
3
Complete the online application and attend the assessment session (aptitude testing, medical, fitness and interview stages).
4
Undergo security vetting — start early, as clearance processing takes time.
5
On selection, begin initial military training at Kapooka (Army), HMAS Cerberus (Navy) or RAAF Base Wagga (Air Force), depending on your service.
Important: This is a fast-moving policy area. Verify the current eligibility rules on the official Defence Jobs portal before you apply — announcements can be refined in the weeks after launch.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can permanent residents join the ADF in 2026?

Yes — under a policy announced in October 2026, permanent residents from New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the United States and Canada can apply to join the Australian Defence Force.

How long must I have lived in Australia to join the ADF as a permanent resident?

At least two years, with permanent resident status required.

Which nationalities are eligible under the new ADF policy?

Citizens of New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the United States and Canada who hold Australian permanent residency.

Do ADF security checks still apply to permanent residents?

Yes — security clearance requirements remain mandatory and must be satisfied before final approval.

Can permanent residents apply for all ADF roles?

The general policy opens recruitment broadly, but some highly sensitive roles may retain citizenship requirements — confirm with Defence Force Recruiting for your target role.

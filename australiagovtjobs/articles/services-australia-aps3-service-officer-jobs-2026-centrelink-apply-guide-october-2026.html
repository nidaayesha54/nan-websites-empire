Australia Government Jobs 2026

Services Australia is recruiting entry-level APS3 Service Officers for Centrelink and Medicare service delivery — hybrid roles supporting Australians when they need government services most.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » Australia Government Jobs » Articles

Services Australia — the agency behind Centrelink and Medicare — is recruiting APS3 Service Officers for service-delivery roles advertised nationally. These are entry-level positions: no prior public-service experience is required, making them one of the most accessible doors into the Australian Public Service in 2026.

Service delivery staff respond to a broad range of complex customer circumstances in a fast-paced environment. Officers connect with customers in person, on the phone and online to deliver Centrelink and Medicare services — work the agency describes as rewarding, challenging and different every day, because every customer is different.

What Service Officers Do

Service delivery officers are the human face of Australia's welfare and health-payments systems. Day-to-day work includes:

  • Responding to customers across phone, online and face-to-face channels about Centrelink payments and Medicare services;
  • Handling complex circumstances — no two customer situations are the same, from new claims to payment problems;
  • Moving between channels and tasks as workload demands change throughout the rostered day;
  • Applying legislation, policy and guidelines to make accurate, fair decisions on customer matters;
  • De-escalating difficult interactions with empathy while protecting the integrity of public funds.

The agency is explicit about the personal qualities it wants: officers who are empathetic, resilient and flexible, ready to help people and willing to move between customer-service channels as the day demands.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Australian citizenship is required for ongoing APS employment;
  • No specific degree or prior APS experience — these are entry-level roles and the agency recruits on capability, not credentials;
  • Strong communication skills and the resilience to handle difficult customer interactions;
  • Willingness to work rostered hours (7am–7pm, Monday–Friday) and possible shiftwork;
  • Ability to obtain and maintain the required security and suitability clearances.

Why Start at Services Australia

Services Australia is one of the largest employers in the APS, and its service-delivery workforce is the engine room of Australian social policy. Starting as an APS3 Service Officer puts you inside the biggest operational agency in the country, with clear progression to APS 4–6 team-leader and specialist roles, formal learning through the APS Academy and mobility across the entire public service. For anyone seeking a stable, meaningful entry into government work in 2026, few doors open wider than this one.

Frequently Asked Questions

What does an APS3 Service Officer do?

Service Officers deliver Centrelink and Medicare services to customers in person, on the phone and online — responding to complex customer circumstances in a fast-paced environment.

Do I need experience to apply for APS3 roles?

No. These are entry-level positions; Services Australia recruits on capability — communication skills, empathy and resilience — rather than prior public-service experience.

What are the working hours for Service Officers?

Rostered days between 7am and 7pm, Monday to Friday, with possible shiftwork depending on operational requirements. Roles are advertised as hybrid.

What is the salary for APS3 Service Officers?

Entry-level APS 1–3 roles typically sit around $57,000–$85,000 base salary, plus the APS 15.4% superannuation contribution. Exact pay is set by the agency's enterprise agreement.

Do I need Australian citizenship?

Yes — Australian citizenship is required for ongoing APS employment, including Services Australia service-delivery roles.

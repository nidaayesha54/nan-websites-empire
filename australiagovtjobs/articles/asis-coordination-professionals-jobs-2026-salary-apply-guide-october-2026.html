What Coordination Professionals Do at ASIS

ASIS is Australia's foreign human-intelligence agency. While operational detail is classified, coordination professionals in such agencies typically:

  • Coordinate activity across operational, analytical and corporate teams;
  • Manage sensitive schedules, tasking and information flows with strict need-to-know discipline;
  • Prepare briefings and reporting for senior leadership;
  • Liaise with partner agencies domestically and internationally;
  • Maintain the administrative backbone that lets intelligence operations function securely.

The salary band ($120,199–$175,338) signals experienced hires — people who can operate with minimal supervision in high-trust environments.

Eligibility & Requirements

Intelligence recruitment has the strictest gates in the APS:

  • Australian citizenship — mandatory, with no exceptions for intelligence roles;
  • Security clearance — candidates must be able to obtain and hold a high-level clearance, which involves extensive background vetting;
  • Discretion and judgement — selection heavily weights integrity, sound judgement and the ability to handle classified material;
  • Relevant experience — coordination, program management, stakeholder engagement or operational support at a senior level;
  • Assessment process — expect multi-stage assessment including interviews, psychological evaluation and vetting interviews.

The Vetting Reality — Read This First

ASIS vetting examines your background in depth: employment history, finances, associations, travel and personal conduct. The process takes months, and not every strong candidate clears it. Before applying, consider honestly whether your history is vetting-friendly — undisclosed issues discovered during vetting end candidacies. This is the single biggest filter in intelligence recruitment, more decisive than qualifications.

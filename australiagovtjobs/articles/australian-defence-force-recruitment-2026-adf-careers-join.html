Australian Defence Force Recruitment 2026: ADF Careers, Pay, Training & How to Join

The Australian Defence Force is hiring across trades, technical and health roles in 2026 — with paid training from day one and 16.4% super. Here is what’s on offer and how to join.

ADF Defence Jobs 2026 Recruitment

Why the ADF Is Hiring at Scale in 2026

The Australian Defence Force — Navy, Army and Air Force — is one of the country’s largest and most distinctive employers. In October 2026, defence recruitment ads across SEEK and the official channels show demand spanning hands-on trades, technical specialties and health professions. For Australians weighing a secure, well-paid career change, the pitch is unusually strong: get paid to train from day one, with no prior experience required for many roles.

Beyond uniformed service, the Department of Defence also recruits APS civilians — ordinary public-service employees doing defence-related work. Both paths are covered here.

What’s on Offer: Pay, Super and Housing

The October 2026 ADF recruitment packages include several headline benefits:

  • Paid from day one of training — even with zero experience
  • 16.4% superannuation on uniformed roles, well above the standard rate
  • Subsidised housing or a rent allowance
  • Job security and structured progression through the ranks

Roles Hiring Now

Current recruitment advertising (October 2026) features roles such as:

  • Fitter and turner / welder — responsible for welding and fabrication of a ship’s hull and structural fittings
  • Electrician — maintaining a ship’s electrical power, generation and distribution systems
  • Marine engineer — operating engineering systems so ship engines, gas turbines and steering remain serviceable
  • Electronics technician — maintaining weapons systems and surveillance equipment
  • Health professionals — protecting the health of ADF members from environmental threats

Civilian Defence roles advertised alongside include an APS5 Logistics Officer contributing to Defence capability, and an APS6 Program Support Officer (Governance) supporting the AUKUS program at $99,733–$112,431 plus super.

How to Join the ADF in 2026

  1. Explore roles on the official ADF careers site. Browse by service (Navy, Army, Air Force) and by your interests — trades, technical, health, aviation or officer entry.
  2. Check eligibility early. Citizenship, age and medical/fitness standards are assessed upfront — confirm you meet them before investing time.
  3. Complete the aptitude and assessment process. This includes aptitude testing, interviews and medical/fitness assessments.
  4. Choose uniformed or civilian. If you want defence work without military service, apply for APS civilian roles through APSJobs instead.
  5. Accept and start paid training. Successful recruits begin paid training immediately, with housing support from the start.
ADF recruitment cycles run year-round but specific trade intakes can fill — if a role you want is advertised, start the process early rather than waiting for the “perfect” time.

FAQs

Do I need experience to join the ADF?

Many advertised ADF trades roles require no experience at all — you get paid to train from day one and learn the trade in service.

What superannuation does the ADF pay?

ADF roles advertised in October 2026 offer 16.4% superannuation, well above the standard rate, plus subsidised housing or a rent allowance.

What roles is the ADF hiring for in 2026?

Fitter and turner, electrician, marine engineering, electronics technician and health roles, alongside civilian APS Defence positions such as APS5 logistics officers.

Can civilians work for Defence without joining the ADF?

Yes — Defence also hires APS civilians (for example APS5–APS6 program and logistics officers) through APSJobs for non-uniformed work.

Is Australian citizenship required for the ADF?

Defence service roles generally require Australian citizenship — confirm the exact eligibility on the official Defence recruitment page before applying.

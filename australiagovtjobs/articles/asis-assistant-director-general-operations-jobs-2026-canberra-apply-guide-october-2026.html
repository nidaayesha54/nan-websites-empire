Australia Government Jobs 2026

The Australian Secret Intelligence Service (ASIS) is hiring an Assistant Director-General Operations in Canberra, with applications closing October 26, 2026 — a rare senior leadership opening in Australia's foreign intelligence agency.

✅ Updated October 20, 2026
Home » Australia Government Jobs » Articles

The Australian Secret Intelligence Service (ASIS) is recruiting for Assistant Director-General Operations in Canberra, with the vacancy listed as closing on 26 October 2026. It is a rare senior leadership opening in Australia's foreign intelligence agency — the kind of role that is advertised publicly only occasionally and attracts strong competition from across the public service, the ADF and the private sector.

The Assistant Director-General Operations sits near the top of ASIS's operational structure. The role oversees intelligence operations and operational support, requiring proven senior leadership in complex, high-risk environments. The advertisement sets out specific senior-leadership and intelligence-experience selection criteria, so applicants are advised to read the criteria document carefully before starting an application.

Employment conditions are similar to Australian Public Service conditions and include superannuation. ASIS states it supports a diverse and inclusive workplace and invites candidates needing reasonable adjustments to contact the agency — although, consistent with intelligence-agency practice, no individual feedback is provided to applicants.

Important: Intelligence-agency recruitment is not like ordinary APS recruitment. Applications require demonstrated senior leadership and intelligence experience — this is not an entry-level pathway. ASIS also notes it does not provide feedback to unsuccessful applicants.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Australian citizenship — mandatory, with no exceptions for intelligence roles;
  • Assessment as suitable to hold and maintain a TOP SECRET–Privileged Access (TS-PA) security clearance, under the Protective Security Policy Framework;
  • Senior leadership experience in intelligence, defence, law enforcement or comparably complex operational environments;
  • Capacity for strategic thinking, operational judgment and people leadership at the executive level;
  • Willingness to relocate to Canberra and meet ASIS's suitability standards.

The Security Clearance Reality

The TS-PA clearance is the highest standard vetting level in the Australian system. Assessment covers identity, background, financial position, associations and personal history in depth, and the process takes many months. Candidates with extensive foreign connections, unresolved financial issues or a reluctance to undergo deep background investigation will not be suitable. If you cannot realistically hold a TS-PA clearance, do not spend effort on this application — apply instead to the APS jobs on APSJobs or the Defence civilian roles this portal also covers.

5

Alternatives If This Role Is Out of Reach

Senior intelligence leadership roles are few and highly contested. If you want a public-service security career with more accessible entry points, consider ASIO and ASD graduate and analyst pathways, the Australian Federal Police, or APS intelligence-policy roles in the Attorney-General's Department and the Department of Home Affairs — all advertised on APSJobs and covered regularly on this portal.

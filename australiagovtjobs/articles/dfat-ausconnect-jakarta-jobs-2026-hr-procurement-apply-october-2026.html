Australia Government Jobs 2026

DFAT's AusConnect platform lists two Jakarta roles — HR & Safeguards Officer (DT Global) and Procurement & Administration Officer (Abt Global) — both closing October 28, 2026.

The DFAT AusConnect opportunities platform — the official jobs board for Australia's aid and development programs — has listed two Jakarta-based roles, both posted on 15 October 2026 and both closing on 28 October 2026: an HR & Safeguards Officer with DT Global and a Procurement & Administration Officer with Abt Global. Both positions support Australian aid programs operating across Asia.

AusConnect is the Department of Foreign Affairs and Trade's central portal for aid-program opportunities: jobs, consultancies and requests for proposals connected to Australian development assistance. These two Jakarta roles are delivered through DFAT's managing contractors — DT Global and Abt Global — which implement Australian-funded programs across the Indo-Pacific.

Important distinction: These are contractor roles managed by DT Global and Abt Global under Australian aid programs — not direct DFAT or Australian Public Service appointments. Australian citizenship is typically required for APS roles, so read the eligibility section of each listing carefully before applying.

The Two Jakarta Roles

Both roles are based in Jakarta, Indonesia and sit within the Administration and Health sectors of Australia's aid program. Here is what each listing covers:

Role 1: HR & Safeguards Officer — DT Global

  • Employer: DT Global, a managing contractor delivering Australian aid programs;
  • Regions: South and Central Asia;
  • Sectors: Administration and Health;
  • Focus: human resources and safeguards — the HR and protection frameworks that keep aid programs compliant and safe for staff and communities.

Role 2: Procurement & Administration Officer — Abt Global

  • Employer: Abt Global, a managing contractor delivering Australian aid programs;
  • Regions: South and Central Asia plus South-East and East Asia;
  • Sectors: Administration and Health;
  • Focus: procurement and administration — the purchasing, contracting and office-management functions that keep program delivery moving.

What These Roles Support

Both positions support Australian aid and development programs across Asia. Administration, HR, safeguards and procurement staff are the operational backbone of every aid program: they handle recruitment, compliance, purchasing and office management so technical teams can focus on delivering health and development outcomes.

Jakarta is a regional hub for Australian aid delivery in Asia, which is why program-support roles like these are advertised there. Candidates with administration, procurement or HR experience in international development should find both listings worth a close read.

Consultancies and RFPs on the Same Portal

AusConnect is not only a jobs board — it also publishes requests for proposals (RFPs) and consultancies for the aid program. One current example is a consultancy for the design of the Bougainville fisheries office, also closing on 28 October 2026. If you are an independent consultant rather than a jobseeker, browse the RFP section of the same portal for project-based opportunities.

APS Bulk Recruitment 2026: How Federal Agencies Hire in Volume

Federal agencies hire hundreds of officers at a time through bulk rounds, registers and merit pools — one application can cover roles for 12 months. Here is how it works, what it pays and how to apply.

APS Jobs Bulk Recruitment 2026 Hiring

Home › Articles › APS Bulk Recruitment 2026: How Federal Agencies Hire in Volume

Many Australian Public Service jobs are never advertised one at a time. Across 2026, agencies are hiring through bulk recruitment rounds, candidate registers and merit pools — one advertisement filling dozens of vacancies over 12 months. Suitable applicants are assessed once and placed in a merit pool (commonly active up to 18 months) that agencies draw from as roles open. One strong application can keep you in front of multiple teams for a year.

How Bulk Rounds Work

A bulk round is one ad covering multiple vacancies at the same classification, sometimes across teams or locations — recent Registry Support Officer campaigns explicitly advertised bulk recruitment for opportunities over the next 12 months. Agencies also run ongoing registers (e.g., Home Affairs’ recruitment system) where one lodged application is considered as needs arise. The model suits high-volume areas: service delivery, registry support, policy delivery, call centres and seasonal surges.

Eligibility, Clearances & How to Apply

You must be an Australian citizen for employment under the Public Service Act 1999, and obtain the stated security clearance (Baseline minimum is common) plus police and identity checks. The RecruitAbility scheme invites applicants with disability who meet minimum requirements to further assessment. Note: some 2026 ads require you to declare any AI use in preparing your application.

  1. Search APS Jobs (apsjobs.gov.au) for “bulk recruitment”, “register” or “merit pool”.
  2. Watch closing dates — popular rounds can close early once the pool fills.
  3. Submit a one-page pitch + CV addressing the criteria directly (many ads cap cover letters at 750 words).
  4. Declare AI use if asked, per the agency’s instructions.
  5. Stay contactable — merit-pool offers can arrive months after assessment.
A merit-pool place is not a job offer — it means you were found suitable and will be considered as vacancies arise. Keep applying to other rounds and never resign until you hold a signed offer.

FAQs

What is APS bulk recruitment?

One advertisement covering multiple vacancies at a classification — often for opportunities over 12 months. Suitable applicants may enter a merit pool used as vacancies arise.

How long does a merit pool last?

Commonly up to 18 months, during which agencies draw from the ranked list as positions open.

Do I need Australian citizenship?

Yes — employment under the Public Service Act 1999 requires it, plus the security clearance and checks in the ad.

What pay can I expect?

Recent bands: $78,755-$84,284 for registry-style roles, from $82,499 + 15.4% super at the AFP, $95,000-$105,000 + super at APS6.

Where are bulk rounds advertised?

On apsjobs.gov.au — search 'bulk recruitment', 'register' or 'merit pool' with your classification.

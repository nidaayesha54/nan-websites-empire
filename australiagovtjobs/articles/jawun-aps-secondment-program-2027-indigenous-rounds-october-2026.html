Australia Government Jobs 2026

The Jawun APS Secondment Program has opened its 2027 rounds: Round 1 secondments run February–April 2027 across 12 regions, with agency nominations closing Monday, 9 November 2026.

✅ Updated October 19, 2026
Home » Australia Government Jobs » Articles

The Jawun APS Secondment Program — the Australian Public Service's flagship Indigenous secondment scheme — has published its 2027 secondment rounds, and the clock is ticking on the first one. Round 1 secondments run between February and April 2027, with agency nominations closing on Monday, 9 November 2026.

Under the programme, APS employees are seconded to Indigenous Regional Organisations across 12 regions, lending their professional skills — from data analysis and finance to legal, policy and communications — to community-led projects. Placements depend on matching a secondee's skills with what the regional organisation actually needs, which makes it one of the most hands-on leadership-development experiences available inside the APS.

If you are an APS employee interested in a February–April 2027 secondment, you need to start the conversation with your manager and agency coordinator now — internal approvals take time.

Skills in Demand for 2027

Jawun publishes the skill sets its Indigenous partner organisations are seeking, and the 2027 list is strikingly broad. In-demand capabilities include:

  • AI competencies and knowledge, data management and analysis, data visualisation;
  • Finance and accounting — capital investment, audit skills, economics;
  • Legal and policy, research and evaluation, grant writing and fundraising;
  • HR — recruitment and retention, HR system development, leadership mentoring and coaching;
  • Communications — brand strategy, marketing and media, graphic design, website design and development;
  • Project and business planning, strategic planning, governance (corporate, data and board development);

Eligibility Requirements

  • You must be a current APS employee — the programme runs on agency nominations, not open public applications;
  • Your agency must nominate you by the round cut-off date (9 November 2026 for Round 1);
  • You need manager and agency support for release during the secondment window;
  • Your skills should match the needs of an Indigenous Regional Organisation — matching is done placement by placement;
  • Willingness to work in regional and remote Australia (or virtually, for the Cape York stream).

How to Apply for a 2027 Secondment

1
Check the APS Academy's Jawun programme page for the 2027 round dates and confirm the 9 November 2026 Round 1 nomination cut-off.
2
Speak to your manager about release for a February–April 2027 secondment and how it fits your development plan.
3
Contact your agency's Jawun coordinator to start the nomination process — agencies submit nominations, not individuals.
4
Prepare a skills summary mapped to the in-demand list (data, finance, legal, HR, communications, project planning).
5
Once nominated, work with Jawun on matching to an Indigenous Regional Organisation whose needs fit your skills and preferred region.

Why Jawun Secondments Are Career Gold

A Jawun secondment is one of the few APS experiences that simultaneously delivers leadership development, cultural capability and real project delivery outside the Canberra bubble. Secondees routinely cite it as the most formative assignment of their public-service careers — and agencies increasingly recognise it in promotion and talent discussions. With Round 1 nominations closing 9 November 2026, APS employees eyeing a February start need to move this month.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Jawun APS Secondment Program?

A programme that seconds APS employees to Indigenous Regional Organisations across 12 regions, matching public servants' professional skills with community-led project needs.

When do 2027 Round 1 nominations close?

Monday, 9 November 2026, for secondments running February–April 2027.

Who can apply for a Jawun secondment?

Current APS employees, through agency nomination — it is not an open public recruitment.

Which regions host Jawun secondments?

12 regions: East Kimberley, West Kimberley, Pilbara and South West Australia (WA); Central Australia and North East Arnhem Land (NT); Central Coast and Inner Sydney (NSW); Far West Coast and Lower River Murray (SA); Cape York virtual (QLD); Goulburn Murray (VIC).

What skills are in demand for Jawun 2027?

AI and data skills, finance and accounting, legal and policy, research, HR, communications and marketing, project planning, governance and education/training capabilities.

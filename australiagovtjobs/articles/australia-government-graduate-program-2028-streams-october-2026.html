Australia Graduate Program 2028 — Apply Mar 2027

The Australian Government Graduate Program opens 1 March 2027 across four streams — Generalist, Economist, Data and Digital — with starting salaries of $67,000 to $94,270 plus 15.4% super. Here's the full eligibility and preparation guide.

✅ Updated October 24, 2026
Home » Australia Government Jobs » Articles

The Australian Government Graduate Program (AGGP) — the whole-of-government entry pathway into the APS — has published its 2028 intake stream pages, with applications opening on 1 March 2027 for the Generalist, Economist, Data and Digital streams. Starting salaries run from $67,000 to $94,270 depending on stream and placement agency, with the APS's standard 15.4% superannuation on top.

This is the single biggest graduate employer in the country: one application can place you in policy, economics, data or digital roles across dozens of agencies, in Canberra or in state offices. Unlike agency-specific intakes (such as ASIO's), the AGGP is a generalist gateway — and the degree window is closing soon, so final-year students should read the eligibility rules carefully now.

Honesty flag: applications do not open until 1 March 2027 — this is a preparation guide, not a live vacancy. The degree completion window (1 Jan 2022 – 31 Dec 2026) means 2026 graduates are in their final eligible year: if you finish your degree in 2026, this is your intake.

The Four Streams Compared

  • Generalist — $70,000–$89,000: policy and program design, development and delivery across agencies. Suits any discipline; rewards analytical thinking, communication, judgement and adaptability. 12 months, multiple locations;
  • Economist — $67,000–$85,000: economic and research-based projects with Australia's leading policy analysts and forecasters. Needs economics, econometrics, or a major/minor in economics. 12–24 months, on-site or hybrid;
  • Data — $69,761–$94,270: data-driven projects supporting policy and services. Needs strong data-technical skills plus critical thinking. 12 months, hybrid and on-site;
  • Digital — $68,000–$89,000: digital technology and innovation projects. Needs a bachelor or higher in a digital-technical field. 12 months, on-site, remote or hybrid.

Eligibility — Read This Before March

  • Degree window: your most recent eligible qualification must have been completed between 1 January 2022 and 31 December 2026 — 2022 graduates are in their last eligible year alongside 2026 finishers;
  • Citizenship: you must be an Australian citizen at the time of application — permanent residents are not eligible;
  • Clearances: you must be able to obtain and maintain an Australian Government security clearance (AGSVA) and pass police, character and health checks;
  • Stream-specific: Economist needs the economics background; Digital needs a digital-technical degree; Data needs demonstrable data-technical skills. Generalist is the open door for all other disciplines.

How to Prepare Before 1 March 2027

1
Register for updates on the official AGGP stream pages so you are notified the day applications open.
2
Confirm your degree completion date falls inside the 2022–2026 window and that you hold (or will hold) Australian citizenship by March 2027.
3
Pick your stream now and build evidence: for Data/Digital, document technical projects; for Economist, keep your transcripts ready; for Generalist, collect policy, writing and teamwork examples.
4
Study the APS Values and the Work Level Standards — AGGP selection is heavily behavioural, and pitches are scored against them.
5
Line up referees early and draft your pitch using the STAR method — March applications reward candidates who prepared in October.

Frequently Asked Questions

When do applications open for the Australian Government Graduate Program 2028?

Applications open on 1 March 2027 for the Generalist, Economist, Data and Digital streams. Register for updates on the APSJobs career pathways pages now.

What is the salary for AGGP 2028 graduates?

Starting salaries range from $67,000 to $94,270 depending on the stream and placement agency — Generalist $70,000–$89,000, Economist $67,000–$85,000, Data $69,761–$94,270, Digital $68,000–$89,000 — plus 15.4% superannuation.

Who is eligible for the AGGP?

You must have completed a bachelor degree (AQF Level 7) or higher between 1 January 2022 and 31 December 2026, be an Australian citizen at the time of application, and be able to obtain a security clearance.

Do I need an economics degree for the Economist stream?

Relevant degrees include economics, econometrics or any degree with a major or minor in economics.

How long is the graduate program?

The Generalist, Data and Digital streams run 12 months full-time; the Economist stream runs 12–24 months.

Can non-citizens apply for the AGGP?

No — Australian citizenship at the time of application is mandatory across all streams.

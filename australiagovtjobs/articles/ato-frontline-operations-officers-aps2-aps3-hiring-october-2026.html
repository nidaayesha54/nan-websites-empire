Australia Government Jobs 2026

The Australian Taxation Office is recruiting APS2 and APS3 Frontline Operations Officers — an entry-level federal pathway with full training provided, a reported salary band of $64,095–$78,705 plus 15.4% super, and no prior experience required.

✅ Updated October 16, 2026

The Australian Taxation Office (ATO) is one of the biggest graduate and entry-level recruiters in the Australian Public Service — and its Frontline Operations Officer campaign is the clearest example. Recruited at the APS2 and APS3 levels, these roles are pitched at people looking for a fresh start in 2026: the ATO provides full training, ongoing support and career progression, and explicitly states that no prior experience is a problem.

Reported remuneration for the campaign is $64,095 to $78,705 plus 15.4% superannuation — a solid entry-level federal package that grows with progression to higher APS classifications.

What the Role Involves

Frontline Operations Officers are the ATO's customer-facing workforce. The work typically includes:

  • Handling inbound enquiries from individuals and businesses about tax, super and related obligations;
  • Using ATO systems and technology to look up records, process requests and resolve issues;
  • Juggling multiple tasks in a fast-paced, target-driven environment;
  • Working to quality and timeliness standards while maintaining courteous service;
  • Escalating complex matters to senior officers where needed.

The ATO says it is looking for strong communication skills, confidence with technology and the ability to juggle tasks in a fast-paced environment — not a tax background. Training covers everything else.

Why This Is a Strong Entry Point

  • Genuine no-experience entry into the APS — rare at this pay band;
  • Full paid training plus ongoing support and a structured career path;
  • 15.4% superannuation — well above the national guarantee;
  • Career progression to APS4 and beyond, including specialist streams;
  • Federal job security and the mobility of an APS career across agencies.

For school leavers, career changers, retail and hospitality workers, or anyone wanting out of insecure casual work, this campaign is one of the most accessible federal doors in 2026.

Eligibility Requirements

As with all APS roles, you must:

  • Be an Australian citizen (citizenship is a standard APS eligibility requirement);
  • Be willing to undergo police and identity checks;
  • Obtain and maintain the required security clearance (typically Baseline for these roles);
  • Meet any probationary requirements that apply.
Tip: The ATO strongly encourages applications from First Nations peoples, people with disability, culturally and linguistically diverse backgrounds and LGBTQIA+ community members — its recruitment pages consistently carry this commitment.

How to Apply via APSJobs

1
Open apsjobs.gov.au and search for Frontline Operations Officer or filter by the ATO as the agency.
2
Read the candidate information pack carefully — it explains the work sample tests many entry-level campaigns use.
3
Prepare a current resume and a pitch/cover letter (check the word/page limits — exceeding them means only the limit is assessed).
4
Address the selection criteria with real examples of communication, technology use and multitasking — retail, hospitality and customer-service experience all count.
5
Submit before the closing date and time listed on the posting (APSJobs uses AEST/AEDT — don't get caught by the timezone).
Important: Entry-level ATO campaigns are popular and can close once sufficient applications are received. If a Frontline Operations posting is live, apply promptly — and keep an eye on APSJobs, because the ATO runs these intakes regularly.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the salary for ATO Frontline Operations Officers?

Reported figures for the APS2/APS3 campaign are $64,095 to $78,705 plus 15.4% superannuation — confirm the exact band on the live posting.

Do I need experience to apply for ATO Frontline Operations roles?

No. The ATO provides full training and states that no prior experience is required — it looks for communication skills, confidence with technology and multitasking ability.

What APS level are Frontline Operations Officers?

APS2 and APS3 — entry-level classifications with progression pathways to higher levels.

Do I need to be an Australian citizen?

Yes — Australian citizenship is a standard eligibility requirement for APS employment, along with police checks and security clearance.

How do I apply for ATO jobs?

Through the APSJobs portal (apsjobs.gov.au) — submit a resume and cover letter addressing the selection criteria before the closing date.

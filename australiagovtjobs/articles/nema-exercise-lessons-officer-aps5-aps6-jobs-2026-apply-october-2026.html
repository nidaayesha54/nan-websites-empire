Australia Government Jobs 2026

The National Emergency Management Agency is hiring Exercise and Lessons Officers at APS5 and APS6 levels in Canberra — paying $88,834 to $111,701 plus 15.4% super, closing November 3, 2026.

✅ Updated October 22, 2026
Home » Australia Government Jobs » Articles

The National Emergency Management Agency (NEMA), in the Home Affairs portfolio, is hiring two Exercise and Lessons Officers in Canberra — one at APS5 ($88,834–$96,829) and one at APS6 ($99,734–$111,701), each plus 15.4% superannuation. Both are ongoing roles with applications closing 3 November 2026 at 11:59pm AEST.

These are the roles behind Australia's crisis preparedness. Exercise and lessons officers design and run the emergency-management exercises that test how governments respond to disasters, then harvest the findings — from real events and simulations alike — and turn them into concrete improvements in the national response system.

The encouraging news for career-switchers: no emergency-management degree is required — the criteria are project management, stakeholder engagement and communication skills. The hard gates are Australian citizenship, a National Police Check and an NV1 (AGSVA) clearance.

What an Exercise and Lessons Officer Does

  • Design exercises — planning multi-agency emergency simulations, from tabletop scenarios to full field exercises;
  • Capture lessons — gathering structured findings from exercises and real disaster responses;
  • Drive improvement — turning lessons into recommendations that change how the system responds;
  • Stakeholder coordination — working with states, territories and Commonwealth agencies.

APS5 vs APS6 — Which Level Fits You

The two roles sit in the same team with the same deadline, but the bar differs:

  • APS5 ($88,834–$96,829 + super): the delivery role — running exercises, drafting reports, managing stakeholders;
  • APS6 ($99,734–$111,701 + super): the leadership role — exercise/lessons-management or emergency-management experience desired;
  • Both: ongoing, full- or part-time, one immediate vacancy each, plus an 18-month merit pool across every state and territory.

Eligibility and Security Requirements

These are the non-negotiable gates — fail one and the application cannot proceed:

  • Australian citizenship — required for both roles;
  • NV1 (AGSVA) clearance — you must be able to obtain and maintain Negative Vetting Level 1; this involves a detailed background investigation;
  • National Police Check — standard pre-employment screening;
  • Application format: a CV plus a one-page Applicant Response addressing the selection criteria — keep it tight and evidence-based.

How to Apply

1

Frequently Asked Questions

What does a NEMA Exercise and Lessons Officer do?

They plan and deliver emergency-management exercises, capture lessons from real events and exercises, and turn findings into improvements across the national crisis-response system.

What is the salary for the NEMA roles?

APS5 pays $88,834–$96,829 and APS6 pays $99,734–$111,701, each plus 15.4% superannuation.

Do I need an emergency-management degree?

No formal emergency-management qualification is required; project management, stakeholder engagement and communication skills are the stated criteria.

What clearance is required?

When do applications close?

Both roles close on 3 November 2026 at 11:59pm AEST.

Australia Government Jobs 2026

The Australian High Commission in Papua New Guinea is bulk hiring for Driver and Admin Assistant roles, with applications closing Friday, October 23, 2026.

✅ Updated October 20, 2026
Home » Australia Government Jobs » Articles

The Australian High Commission in Papua New Guinea is hiring for multiple Driver and Admin Assistant vacancies, advertised on 10 October 2026, with applications closing on Friday, 23 October 2026. Because the commission is filling several positions at once, this is one of the best-value overseas postings of the month for support-staff jobseekers in Port Moresby.

High Commission support roles are the backbone of a diplomatic mission: drivers keep the mission's transport operations safe and on schedule, while admin assistants handle the paperwork, scheduling and office coordination that lets diplomatic staff focus on their work. Bulk recruitment like this usually means the commission is building a talent pool as well as filling immediate vacancies — so even strong candidates not selected immediately may be considered for later openings.

The commission notes that due to the large volume of applications expected, only shortlisted candidates will be contacted. A clean, complete application submitted before the deadline matters more here than for most postings.

Honest note on eligibility: High Commission support roles are locally engaged positions — they are filled from the local labour market in Papua New Guinea, not flown in from Australia. If you are not already living in PNG with the right to work there, this round is not for you.

What the Roles Involve

  • Driver: safe transport of High Commission staff, official visitors and cargo; vehicle maintenance checks; route planning in Port Moresby traffic; strict adherence to security protocols;
  • Admin Assistant: office administration, document preparation, filing, scheduling, data entry and front-desk support across mission sections;
  • Both roles require reliability, discretion and professional conduct — you represent the Australian Government in daily dealings;
  • Comfort working in a fast-paced environment with good communication skills is listed as essential.

Eligibility Requirements

  • Right to live and work in Papua New Guinea (locally engaged recruitment);
  • Driver: valid PNG driver's licence, clean driving record and several years of professional driving experience;
  • Admin Assistant: administrative or office experience, computer literacy and strong written and spoken English;
  • Ability to pass security and background checks required for diplomatic-mission employment;
  • Professional references and the ability to start at short notice are advantages.

How to Apply

1
Open the High Commission's hiring notice (published 10 October 2026) and follow its application link.
2
Prepare a concise CV highlighting relevant experience — years of driving experience and licence class for the driver role; admin skills, software knowledge and office experience for the assistant role.
3
Attach licences, certificates and references as instructed — incomplete applications are routinely screened out in bulk intakes.
4
Submit before Friday, 23 October 2026.
5
Expect contact only if shortlisted; keep your phone and email details current.

Why Bulk Intakes Are Worth Targeting

When a mission advertises multiple positions at once, the shortlist is larger and the competition per place is lower than for single-vacancy postings. Driver and admin roles also have clear, objective criteria — a clean licence and a solid reference beat a fancy CV. If you are in Port Moresby with the right experience, this is a straightforward, well-paid route into employment with the Australian Government's local mission network.

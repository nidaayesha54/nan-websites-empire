What VA Police Officers Do

VA Police Officers perform police duties at VA-controlled property. According to the announcement, the role covers law and rules enforcement, physical and personal security operations, patrol duties, control desk duty, coordination with local courts, and crime prevention activities for the local jurisdiction. Day-to-day work includes:

  • Investigations — conducting long- and short-term investigations to detect and apprehend individuals violating laws, analyzing facts to identify suspects, and developing case information.
  • Coordination with prosecutors — working with U.S. and other prosecuting attorneys on case development to perform arrests and prosecutions.
  • Protecting civil rights — protecting the civil rights of all individuals on VA-controlled property while preventing, detecting, and investigating crimes.
  • Patrol and response — patrolling assigned areas on foot or by motor vehicle, subduing persons causing disturbances, and assisting citizens in emergencies.
  • Evidence and warrants — following procedures for search and seizure of evidence and contraband, and obtaining warrants.
  • Reports and first aid — preparing shift reports, performing traffic control, and providing basic first aid and CPR.

How Direct Hire Changes the Process

Direct Hire Authority is the key detail in this announcement. Under DHA, the agency may appoint any qualified candidate without the usual rating-and-ranking process, which typically shortens hiring timelines for shortage occupations. Standards do not drop: applicants must still meet all qualification, medical, suitability, and background requirements for a federal law-enforcement position. What changes is speed — qualified applicants are referred as vacancies arise rather than waiting for a single competitive certificate.

The rolling structure also changes your strategy. The announcement lists an initial cut-off of September 18, 2026, with further cut-offs every two weeks. If you apply after a cut-off passes, your application rolls to the next one — but the announcement closes on November 9, 2026, so late applicants have fewer referral chances. Apply early.

Verify before you apply: this announcement is open continuous, which means VA can close it early if hiring needs are met. Check the live USAJOBS page for the current status, duty locations, grade, and pay before submitting.

Who Should Apply

The announcement is open to all U.S. citizens. VA Police work suits candidates with law-enforcement, military police, security, or corrections backgrounds, but the announcement does not restrict applicants to those fields — what matters is meeting the stated qualifications, passing the background investigation, and meeting medical and physical standards.

How to Apply for VA Police Officer Jobs

  1. Open the official announcement. Use the USAJOBS link in the Key Details table and read the full vacancy notice, including qualifications and required documents.
  2. Prepare a federal resume. Include month and year dates, hours per week, and detailed duty descriptions for every relevant position.
  3. Submit before the next cut-off. Applications are referred at two-week intervals, so earlier submission means earlier consideration.
  4. Watch for the November 9, 2026 close. USAJOBS closes at 11:59 PM Eastern on the closing date; do not wait for the final day.
  5. Stay reachable. VA may contact you for assessments, interviews, or suitability steps between cut-offs.

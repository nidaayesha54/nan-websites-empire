GSA is the federal government's landlord and buyer: it manages the federal building portfolio, oversees major construction and renovation projects, and runs government-wide procurement programs. Its engineers work on some of the most complex public buildings in the country — courthouses, federal office buildings, and historic landmarks — which makes GSA engineering roles among the most technically demanding in civilian federal service.

Structural engineers at GSA review designs, inspect construction, and ensure that federal facilities meet seismic, safety, and sustainability standards. In California, seismic resilience is a central part of the job, and San Francisco's federal buildings sit in one of the most seismically active regions in the country.

What a GSA Structural Engineer Does

GSA structural engineers provide engineering oversight for federal construction and renovation projects. Typical duties include reviewing structural designs and calculations submitted by architecture and engineering firms, conducting site inspections during construction, evaluating seismic retrofit proposals, and ensuring compliance with federal building codes and GSA's own design standards.

In the San Francisco region, expect a heavy emphasis on seismic evaluation and retrofit work. GSA maintains a large inventory of older federal buildings in California that require ongoing seismic assessment, and engineers in this role help prioritize and scope those projects. The work blends office-based design review with field inspections at active construction sites.

Typical Qualifications and Requirements

Structural engineer announcements at this level generally require a bachelor's degree in engineering (with coursework in structural analysis and design) from an accredited program, plus specialized experience in structural design, analysis, or construction oversight. A Professional Engineer (PE) license is commonly required or strongly preferred for independent design-review authority.

Because this is a summary of a listing rather than the full announcement, check the official USAJOBS posting for the exact education, experience, and licensure requirements, the occupational series and grade, and whether the position is open to the public or limited to specific hiring paths.

Pay, Benefits, and Career Path

The advertised $127,829 starting salary reflects the San Francisco locality pay area, one of the highest in the federal system. Federal engineers also receive the standard federal benefits package: health insurance, retirement under FERS, Thrift Savings Plan with agency matching contributions, and generous annual and sick leave.

GSA engineering careers offer a clear technical career ladder — from project-level design review to senior technical authority roles overseeing regional programs. Engineers who build expertise in seismic retrofit or sustainable design can become sought-after specialists across the federal building portfolio.

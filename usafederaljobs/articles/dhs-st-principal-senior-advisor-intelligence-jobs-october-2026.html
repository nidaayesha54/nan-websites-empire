About the Role: Principal Senior Advisor for Intelligence and Exquisite Technologies

This is not an entry-level posting. The Principal Senior Advisor serves as a top technical and strategic counselor inside S&T, the research and development arm of the Department of Homeland Security. The role centers on intelligence — how DHS collects, analyzes, and acts on threat information — and exquisite technologies, the advanced, high-impact capabilities that give operators a decisive edge.

Senior Level: What This Pay Plan Means

The announcement is classified at the Senior Level (SL), a federal pay category distinct from both the General Schedule and the Senior Executive Service. Senior Level positions cover high-level scientific, technical, and professional work — think chief scientists, senior technical advisors, and principal investigators — rather than managerial executive roles.

What DHS S&T Does

The Science and Technology Directorate is DHS's primary research and development organization. It develops the tools, technologies, and knowledge products that frontline operators — from Border Patrol agents to cybersecurity analysts — rely on every day. Its portfolio spans everything from explosives detection and biometric identity management to cybersecurity resilience and disaster response innovation.

Working at S&T means operating at the intersection of science, policy, and operations. The directorate partners with national laboratories, universities, and private industry, and its leaders regularly brief Congress and interagency partners. A principal advisor for intelligence and exquisite technologies would sit near the center of that ecosystem.

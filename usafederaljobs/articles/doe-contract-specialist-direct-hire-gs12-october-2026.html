DOE Contract Specialist Direct Hire 2026: GS-12, Up to $129,742 — Closes Oct 23

The Department of Energy's Western Area Power Administration is hiring Contract Specialists under Direct Hire Authority at GS-7 through GS-12 — with applications closing October 23, 2026. Here are the duties, pay, and steps.

The Department of Energy's Western Area Power Administration (WAPA) is hiring Contract Specialists under Direct Hire Authority — and the window is short. Two announcements close October 23, 2026: a full-performance GS-12 track paying $89,508–$129,742 and a developmental GS-7–11 ladder running $50,460 to $108,251.

Both postings are open to the public and serve WAPA's procurement offices across the Mountain West: Lakewood and Montrose, Colorado; Billings, Montana; and Huron and Watertown, South Dakota. Note the catch: this is not a remote position — selectees must be physically present at a duty station.

Contract specialists run the pre- and post-award life of federal contracts — supplies, services, IT, construction, and architect-engineer work under the Federal Acquisition Regulation (FAR). Direct Hire Authority means no competitive rating and ranking: qualified applicants move straight toward the selecting official.

The Two Announcements: GS-12 and the GS-7–11 Ladder

The senior posting (announcement 26-WA-01496-13086409-DHA) seeks a full-performance GS-12 Contract Specialist handling complex, multidisciplinary supply, service, IT, construction, and A&E contracts plus interagency agreements — at $89,508–$129,742.

The developmental posting (26-WA-01506-13087378-DE) covers GS-7 through GS-11 at $50,460–$108,251, for candidates building procurement skills under the FAR in support of planning, awards, and administration of basic procurements. Early-career professionals with contracting coursework or internships fit the lower rungs.

Duties and the Five Duty Stations

Expect the full contracting lifecycle: market research, solicitation, source selection, award, administration, and closeout — with WAPA's mission adding power-industry complexity. The developmental role explicitly supports planning, award, and administration of basic procurements across contract types.

Five duty stations are listed: Lakewood and Montrose (CO), Billings (MT), Huron and Watertown (SD). Cost-of-living and locality pay vary by location — Montana and South Dakota stations stretch the salary further than Denver-adjacent Lakewood.

Why Direct Hire Changes the Odds

Under Direct Hire Authority, WAPA skips the competitive rating and ranking that normally slows federal hiring — and veterans' preference points do not apply. That shortens the path from application to interview for every qualified candidate.

Your resume is the entire argument: document FAR familiarity, contract types handled, dollar thresholds, and procurement systems (FPDS, SAM.gov). The announcement closes October 23, 2026 — with only days left, apply now rather than polishing indefinitely.

How to Apply for the DOE Contract Specialist Jobs

  1. Find both announcements on USAJOBS.gov. Search “Contract Specialist” filtered to the Department of Energy; confirm the announcement numbers above.
  2. Pick the right ladder. Experienced 1102s target the GS-12; students and early-career applicants fit the GS-7–11 developmental track.
  3. Document FAR and contract experience. Name contract types, dollar values, and systems like FPDS and SAM.gov — HR verifies specialized experience strictly.
  4. Attach transcripts if using education. The developmental ladder may credit qualifying education; include official transcripts.
  5. Submit by October 23, 11:59 PM Eastern. Direct-hire postings can close early at application caps — do not wait for the deadline.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the Western Area Power Administration?

WAPA is a Department of Energy power marketing administration that sells wholesale hydropower across the West. Its procurement offices award and manage the contracts that keep the grid running.

What does a federal contract specialist do?

Pre- and post-award work on supply, service, IT, construction, and architect-engineer contracts under the Federal Acquisition Regulation — from solicitation to contract closeout.

What is Direct Hire Authority?

It lets agencies appoint qualified candidates without competitive rating and ranking for shortage occupations — your application goes to the hiring manager faster, with no veterans' preference points applied.

Are these remote positions?

No. The announcements state this is not a remote position — selectees must be physically present at one of the listed duty stations.

What are the two announcements?

A GS-12 full-performance posting ($89,508–$129,742) and a GS-7–11 developmental ladder ($50,460–$108,251). Both close October 23, 2026.

State Department Diplomatic Technology Officer Jobs 2026: IT Careers in U.S. Diplomacy

Diplomatic Technology Officers build and protect the IT systems behind American diplomacy worldwide. The State Department is hosting a DTO application webinar on October 22 — here is what the role involves and how to apply.

The U.S. Department of State is recruiting Diplomatic Technology Officers (DTOs) — IT professionals who use their technology skills to build and protect the systems that power American diplomacy worldwide. From secure communications to the networks that keep U.S. embassies and consulates running, DTOs keep the machinery of diplomacy online.

To help candidates understand the position and the hiring process, the Department is hosting a DTO Application Process Webinar on Thursday, October 22, from 10:30 AM to 12:00 PM EDT. Webinar link details will be emailed to registrants by Wednesday, October 21, so interested applicants should register ahead of time.

One critical eligibility note: the opportunities discussed are only available to U.S. citizens. If you are a citizen with IT skills and an interest in public service, this is one of the most mission-driven technology careers in the federal government.

What Diplomatic Technology Officers Do

DTOs sit at the intersection of technology and foreign policy. They design, deploy, secure, and support the information systems that U.S. diplomats depend on — both at headquarters in Washington and at posts around the world. That can mean hardening networks against cyber threats, keeping classified and unclassified systems interoperable, and providing the technical backbone for everything from visa processing to crisis communications.

Unlike a typical corporate IT role, the “customer” is American foreign policy itself: when a DTO keeps systems running during a natural disaster or a security incident abroad, the impact is immediate and global.

The October 22 Application Webinar

The State Department's DTO Application Process Webinar (October 22, 10:30 AM–12:00 PM EDT) is designed to walk candidates through the position and exactly how the application process works. These sessions typically cover eligibility, the specialized experience the Department is looking for, how the USAJOBS announcement is structured, and common mistakes that get applications screened out.

Register in advance — the Department notes that webinar link details go out to registrants by email on October 21, so last-minute sign-ups risk missing the joining instructions.

Eligibility and How to Apply

DTO opportunities are open only to U.S. citizens. Beyond citizenship, candidates need relevant IT experience or education matching the announcement's specialized experience requirements — typically spanning systems administration, networking, cybersecurity, or related disciplines.

State Department technology positions are advertised on USAJOBS.gov. Build a federal-format resume that maps your IT experience directly to the announcement's requirements, gather transcripts and any certifications, and submit the complete package before the closing date shown on the posting.

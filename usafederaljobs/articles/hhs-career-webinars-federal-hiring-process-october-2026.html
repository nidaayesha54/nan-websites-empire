HHS Career Webinars Oct–Dec 2026: Free Federal Hiring, USAJOBS & Resume Sessions

The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services is running a free virtual career webinar series from October 21 through December 9, 2026 — with sessions on navigating USAJOBS, writing a two-page federal resume, assessments, interviews, and veterans and military spouse hiring paths.

The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) has opened registration for a series of free virtual career webinars running from October 21 through December 9, 2026. Led by HHS recruiters, the sessions help job seekers understand the federal hiring process, navigate USAJOBS, build competitive federal resumes, and handle assessments and interviews.

HHS is one of the largest civilian employers in the federal government, with operating divisions including the CDC, NIH, FDA, and CMS. Roles span health science, policy, administration, IT, finance, and communications.

The series is taught by the people who screen applications. Federal hiring has its own language — occupational series, grades, hiring paths, assessments — and these sessions translate it into a practical plan. Below is the full schedule and how to get the most out of each session.

What You Will Learn in Each Session

October 21: Navigating the Federal Hiring Process on USAJOBS

Attend this session first. Recruiters walk through finding announcements on USAJOBS, decoding duties, qualifications, "who may apply," and required documents, and completing the assessment questionnaires that screen many applicants out — plus what makes a package competitive rather than merely complete.

October 22: HHS Careers 101

The Careers 101 session covers the department's mission, operating divisions, and available career paths — public health, scientific, grants management, policy, and administrative roles. If you want federal health work but are unsure which agency or job series fits, start here.

November 10: Creating Your 2-Page Resume

Many announcements now request two-page resumes. This session shows how to compress experience, qualifications, and measurable accomplishments into that format without losing the detail hiring managers screen for. Bring a draft — you will leave with concrete edits.

November 17: Beyond the Uniform — Veterans and Military Spouses

Dedicated to veterans and military spouse hiring authorities: how to use USAJOBS filters for these paths and translate military experience into a federal resume civilian hiring managers understand. Military spouses with PCS-related eligibility questions should prioritize this one.

December 9: Assessment Process and Interview Tips

The final session covers the USA Hire assessment — the timed online test many announcements require — and preparation for structured federal panel interviews with behavior-based questions. Attend shortly before you start applying in earnest.

Who Should Attend

The series is open to anyone exploring federal service — recent graduates, career changers, health professionals, veterans, and military spouses. Beginners should start with the October sessions; the November and December sessions suit applicants who already have a USAJOBS profile. Veterans and military spouses should prioritize November 17 for hiring authorities like Veterans Recruitment Appointment (VRA) and military spouse noncompetitive eligibility.

How to Get the Most Out of the Webinars

Treat each session like a working meeting. Create your USAJOBS profile before the October sessions and have a resume draft ready for November 10. Prepare two or three specific questions — which divisions are hiring in your field, what grade your experience maps to, which announcements to watch. Specific questions get useful answers; generic ones get generic answers.

How to Register for the HHS Career Webinars

  1. Find the official registration links. The webinar series was announced by HHS recruiters on professional networking channels — look for the HHS careers webinar flyer with the registration link for each session date.
  2. Register for each session separately. Registration links are session-specific, so sign up for every date you plan to attend rather than assuming one registration covers the series.
  3. Add each session to your calendar. All sessions run 2:00–3:00 PM Eastern Time. Convert to your local time zone and set a reminder 30 minutes before start.
  4. Prepare your materials in advance. Have your USAJOBS profile created and a resume draft ready before the October and November sessions.
  5. Join a few minutes early. Virtual sessions can fill up; joining early secures your spot and lets you test audio before the recruiter begins.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are the HHS career webinars free to attend?

Yes. The HHS career webinar series is free and virtual. Registration is required for each session, and the sessions are led by HHS recruiters.

Who can attend the HHS webinars?

Anyone exploring federal employment can attend, including recent graduates, career changers, veterans, and military spouses. You do not need to be a current federal employee.

What is the schedule for the HHS career webinar series?

Sessions run October 21 (USAJOBS navigation), October 22 (HHS Careers 101), November 10 (2-page resume), November 17 (veterans and military spouses), and December 9 (assessments and interviews), all 2:00–3:00 PM Eastern.

Do I need to register for each session separately?

Yes. Each webinar has its own registration link, so sign up for every session you plan to attend.

Will the webinar recordings be available afterward?

Recording availability has not been confirmed in the announcement. If you cannot attend live, check the registration page or HHS careers channels for any posted recordings.

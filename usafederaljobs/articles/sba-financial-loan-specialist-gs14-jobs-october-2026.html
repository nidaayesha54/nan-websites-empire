SBA Financial and Loan Specialist Jobs 2026: GS-14, $143,913–$187,093, Closes Oct 26

The U.S. Small Business Administration is hiring a Financial and Loan Specialist (GS-14) for its Office of Capital Access in Washington, DC — paying up to $187,093 per year, with applications closing October 26, 2026.

The U.S. Small Business Administration (SBA) is hiring a Financial and Loan Specialist at the GS-14 level in the Office of Capital Access in Washington, DC. The announcement is open to the public, and the complete application package must be submitted by October 26, 2026, at 11:59 PM Eastern Time.

This is a full-time, permanent federal position paying $143,913 to $187,093 per year. The role is aimed at professionals with SBA 7(a) lending experience who want to shape the policies, procedures, and training behind one of the federal government's most important small-business programs.

The SBA is pitching this as mission-driven work: lending policy is not just technical requirements and procedures — it is a direct lever for helping entrepreneurs, strengthening communities, and expanding access to capital for small businesses nationwide.

What the Financial and Loan Specialist Does

The selectee will help shape policies, procedures, and training for SBA's 7(a) loan programs — the flagship SBA loan guarantee program that helps lenders extend financing to small businesses that might not otherwise qualify for conventional loans. Day-to-day work involves tackling complex lending issues, developing clear guidance, and sharing expertise with SBA colleagues, participating lenders, and national trade groups.

It is a policy-influencing role rather than a front-line loan-processing job: your work sets the rules of the road for how 7(a) lending operates across the country.

Who Should Apply

The SBA is looking for professionals with SBA 7(a) lending experience — people who have worked in or alongside the program and understand its mechanics, pain points, and regulatory landscape. Ideal candidates enjoy wrestling with complex issues, writing clear guidance, and explaining technical lending concepts to diverse audiences.

Because the position is open to the public, both current federal employees and private-sector lending professionals can compete, provided they meet the specialized experience requirements stated in the USAJOBS announcement.

Pay, Grade, and Appointment Type

At GS-14, this is a senior professional grade. The advertised salary range of $143,913–$187,093 reflects the Washington, DC locality pay area. The appointment is full-time and permanent, carrying the full federal benefits package: health insurance, retirement under FERS, Thrift Savings Plan with agency matching, and generous leave.

How to Apply for the SBA Financial and Loan Specialist Job

  1. Find the announcement on USAJOBS.gov. Search for “Financial and Loan Specialist” with the Small Business Administration as the agency, and confirm you are viewing the GS-14 Washington, DC posting.
  2. Read the specialized experience requirements closely. GS-14 announcements demand at least one year of specialized experience equivalent to GS-13 — tailor your resume to mirror the announcement's language about 7(a) lending, policy development, and guidance writing.
  3. Build a federal-format resume. Include employer names, month/day/year dates, hours worked per week, and detailed accomplishment bullets tied to lending policy or program work.
  4. Gather supporting documents. Transcripts (if qualifying by education), SF-50 (current/former feds), and veterans' documentation if claiming preference.
  5. Submit before the deadline. The complete package must be in by October 26, 2026, 11:59 PM ET — USAJOBS timestamps are strict, so apply at least a day early.

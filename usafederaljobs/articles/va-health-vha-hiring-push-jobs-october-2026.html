What the VHA Is Recruiting For

VHA is the largest integrated healthcare system in the United States, serving millions of military veterans. Its October 2026 hiring push looks beyond medicine: the announcement confirms the administration is seeking candidates in healthcare, information technology, administration, engineering, and finance, plus other disciplines.

The message is clear: you do not need to work specifically in medicine to contribute to VHA's mission — technical, financial, and administrative departments have openings too, part of an ongoing effort to keep the whole system adequately staffed.

Critical-Need Roles This Week

VA's "Hiring Veterans: Jobs of the Week" listing for October 19, 2026 names these nationwide critical-need positions:

  • Food Service Worker and Cook — nationwide
  • Peer Specialist — nationwide

The same listing also features roles nationwide: a Dental Lab Technician in Phoenix, a Lead Medical Support Assistant in Los Angeles, a Dental Hygienist in D.C., Nursing Assistants across Florida, and a Senior Social Worker in Shreveport. Veterans Canteen Service roles — food service workers, baristas, cooks, and sales clerks — are open in more than a dozen cities.

Why VHA Jobs Appeal to Veterans

Military service translates directly into the skills federal healthcare employers value: logistics, discipline, crisis response, and leadership. VA actively encourages veterans to apply, and many clinical announcements note that non-citizens may be appointed when qualified U.S. citizens cannot be recruited.

Some VHA physician postings also advertise recruitment/relocation incentives and the Education Debt Reduction Program (EDRP) — student-loan repayment worth up to $200,000 over one to five years. Check individual announcements for eligibility.

Rolling deadlines: many VHA postings are open-continuous announcements with cutoff dates every few weeks rather than a single closing date. Apply early to land in the next referral batch.

How to Apply for VHA Jobs

  1. Search the official listings. Visit usajobs.gov and filter open VHA vacancies by location, pay grade, and job category — or browse VA's weekly jobs listing linked in the Key Details table.
  2. Build a federal-format resume. Include hours worked per week, supervisor names, and exact dates for every role; clinical roles need license details.
  3. Attach supporting documents. Transcripts, DD-214 for veterans' preference, and SF-50 if you are a current federal employee.
  4. Submit before the cutoff. USAJOBS closes announcements at 11:59 PM Eastern on the closing date; open-continuous postings have recurring referral cutoffs.

The U.S. Department of Health and Human Services (HHS) has opened a Staff Assistant vacancy in Washington, DC, with a short application window running October 23–26, 2026. This is a senior-level, onsite-only position, and it carries an unusual twist: the announcement closes when 50 applications are received, which may happen before the stated closing date. Eligibility is narrow — this announcement is open only to individuals with disabilities under Schedule A and to internal HHS federal employees — so read the eligibility section carefully before applying.

What the Staff Assistant Does

This is a senior-level staff role supporting HHS leadership. According to the announcement, the Staff Assistant uses in-depth knowledge of laws, regulations, policies, and procedures to keep senior leadership current on program and legislative developments affecting HHS programs. Core responsibilities include:

  • Reviewing legislation and reports — analyzing regulations, instructions, and management reports tied to HHS programs.
  • Analyzing new techniques and legislation — assessing innovative approaches to administering HHS programs and working on special assignments involving sensitive, confidential program issues.
  • Coordinating task forces — developing and coordinating the work of program task forces and work groups on programs, issues, and problems.
  • Advising senior managers — presenting recommendations to senior managers on program-related matters.

The 50-Application Cap: Why Speed Matters

Most federal announcements stay open until their closing date. This one does not. The announcement states it will close once 50 applications are received, even if that happens before October 26, 2026. For a senior-level, Washington-based HHS role, 50 applications can arrive quickly. Treat October 23 as the real opening day and apply immediately rather than planning around the October 26 date.

Date conflict flagged: the posting mirror shows both a window of October 23–26, 2026 and an expiry field of November 8, 2026. The October 26 date with the 50-application cap is the operative window in the announcement text — but verify the live closing date on the official USAJOBS announcement before applying, since mirrors can lag.

Who Can Apply — Read This First

Eligibility for this announcement is unusually narrow. It is open only to two groups: individuals with disabilities appointed under Schedule A, and internal HHS federal employees. It is not open to the general public, and it is not remote — the announcement states the position is onsite only in Washington, DC. If you do not fall into one of the two eligible groups, this announcement is not for you, and applying anyway will result in an ineligible rating.

For Schedule A applicants, standard documentation rules apply: be ready to provide proof of disability from a licensed medical professional, a vocational rehabilitation specialist, or a veterans' benefits agency, along with a federal resume that demonstrates the senior-level analytical and advisory experience the role requires.

How to Apply for the HHS Staff Assistant Job

  1. Confirm you are eligible. You must be a Schedule A candidate or a current internal HHS federal employee; U.S. citizenship is required.
  2. Open the official announcement. Use the USAJOBS link in the Key Details table and read the entire vacancy notice first.
  3. Prepare your documents. A federal resume with month and year dates, plus Schedule A proof of disability if claiming that authority.
  4. Apply immediately. The announcement closes at 50 applications — do not wait for October 26.
  5. Save your confirmation. Keep the USAJOBS submission confirmation and any tracking number for follow-up.

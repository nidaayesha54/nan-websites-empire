Which Parks Are Hiring Seasonal Rangers

Yosemite National Park announced multiple seasonal positions for summer 2027 — ranger, natural/cultural resource, and maintenance roles from animal packers to trail workers. Its application window opened October 18, 2026, and could close early once capacity is reached.

The NPS Alaska Region's Seasonal Staffing Office posted its Park Ranger (Interpretation) Seasonal announcement on October 19, 2026, with entry-on-duty expected March–July 2027 — open to the first 300 applicants or until October 26, 2026.

Other October 2026 announcements included Aquatic Nuisance Species and Wilderness Volunteer Coordinator roles with April 2027 entry, a YCC Crew Leader at Yellowstone for May 2027 entry, and interpretation roles at Glacier and Canyonlands.

What a Seasonal Park Ranger Does

Interpretive Rangers develop and present educational programs — guided walks, talks, and presentations — for park visitors. Their work builds a stewardship ethic and public support for protecting park resources.

Aquatic Nuisance Species Rangers protect park waters from invasive species, while YCC Crew Leaders supervise Youth Conservation Corps crews on trail and resource projects. Most appointments run up to 1039 hours; exact seasons vary by weather, project needs, and funding.

Typical Qualifications

Interpretation announcements typically require a bachelor's degree with related coursework or a combination of education and experience, plus specialized experience for higher grades. Some postings also ask for first aid/CPR certification. Because requirements differ by series and grade, read the qualifications section of the exact announcement you apply to.

Don't wait: many NPS announcements use applicant caps instead of fixed closing dates — the Alaska posting closes at 300 applications, and Yosemite's window ran as little as 24 hours. Submit early.

How to Apply for NPS Seasonal Park Ranger Jobs

  1. Open the official announcement. Go to usajobs.gov and search "Park Ranger Seasonal" under the National Park Service, or use the link in the Key Details table.
  2. Check the cap and closing date. Capped announcements can shut down without warning — confirm the cutoff before investing time.
  3. Meet the NPS resume requirements. The NPS has specific resume requirements for seasonal hiring — review them before submitting.
  4. Prepare your documents. Transcripts, DD-214 if claiming veterans' preference, and any certifications required.
  5. Submit before the cutoff. USAJOBS closes at 11:59 PM Eastern on the closing date — and applicant caps are often hit in days.

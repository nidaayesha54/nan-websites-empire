Bureau of Prisons Direct Hire Events 2026: RN and Correctional Officer Jobs — Up to $34K Sign-On

The Federal Bureau of Prisons is holding back-to-back direct hire events in Minnesota this month for registered nurses and correctional officers — with sign-on bonuses up to $34,000 and on-the-spot hiring. Here is how to attend and apply.

The Federal Bureau of Prisons (BOP) is holding back-to-back direct hire events in Minnesota this month — and these are not ordinary job fairs. At both events, BOP recruiters will guide qualified candidates through the USAJOBS application on site, review resumes in person, and, for those who meet qualifications, begin the hiring process the same day.

The headline numbers explain the urgency: a sign-on bonus of up to $34,000 for registered nurses and up to $21,000 for correctional officers. Few federal agencies advertise five-figure recruitment incentives this openly — a signal of how aggressively BOP is filling frontline positions right now.

The events run October 24–25 at the Federal Medical Center in Rochester and October 27–28 at the FCI Sandstone Training Center, 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Central — open to the public.

What's On the Table: Sign-On Bonuses and Direct Hiring

BOP's pitch is unusually concrete. The $34,000 registered-nurse sign-on bonus targets one of the hardest federal specialties to staff, while the $21,000 correctional-officer bonus sweetens an entry path that already carries law enforcement retirement. Bonuses of this scale are recruitment incentives — designed to pull experienced professionals into public service.

The direct-hire format matters as much as the money: in-person resume reviews, guided USAJOBS applications, and the chance to start the hiring process on the spot if qualified.

The Long-Term Pitch: Law Enforcement Retirement and Benefits

Beyond the sign-on check, BOP sells the career: law enforcement retirement eligibility after 20–25 years of service — earlier than standard federal retirement — plus comprehensive health, dental, life, and vision insurance, a pension and Thrift Savings Plan, and generous paid time off including federal holidays and sick leave.

BOP promotes from within — officer and nurse roles ladder into supervisory and headquarters positions — and the pension-plus-early-retirement math often closes the private-sector pay gap.

Who Should Show Up

The events target registered nurses — especially correctional healthcare — and correctional officer candidates seeking federal law enforcement careers. Career changers exploring federal service are welcome too.

Bring resume copies, licenses or transcripts, ID, and certifications. Arrive early, dress professionally, and be ready to talk qualifications.

How to Attend the BOP Direct Hire Events

  1. Pick your event and date. Rochester (Oct 24–25) or Sandstone (Oct 27–28) — both run 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Central; arrive earlier in the day for more recruiter time.
  2. Prepare your documents. Bring multiple resume copies, nursing licenses or transcripts, a photo ID, and any certifications relevant to the role.
  3. Get your resume reviewed on site. BOP staff will walk you through the application and help you navigate USAJOBS — use their feedback before you submit.
  4. Complete the application at the event. If you meet qualifications, you can begin the hiring process the same day — don't leave without submitting.
  5. Follow up on USAJOBS. Track your application status online after the event and respond promptly to any HR requests.

Frequently Asked Questions

When and where are the Bureau of Prisons direct hire events?

Two events in Minnesota: October 24–25, 2026 at the Federal Medical Center in Rochester (2110 East Center Street) and October 27–28, 2026 at the Federal Correctional Institution Sandstone Training Center (2300 County Road 29, Sandstone). Both run 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM Central.

What are the sign-on bonuses?

Up to $34,000 for Registered Nurses and up to $21,000 for Correctional Officers — among the most generous federal recruitment incentives being advertised right now.

What benefits come with BOP careers?

Comprehensive health, dental, life, and vision insurance, a pension plus Thrift Savings Plan, generous paid time off including federal holidays and sick leave, and law enforcement retirement eligibility after 20–25 years of service.

Can the hiring process really start on the spot?

Yes. BOP's announcement says if you meet the qualifications, you could begin the hiring process at the event — recruiters will review your resume and guide your USAJOBS application in person.

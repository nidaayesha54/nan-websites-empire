DIA Intelligence Jobs at U.S. Cyber Command 2026

The Defense Intelligence Agency is hiring for intelligence analysis, counterintelligence, HUMINT, and cyberspace mission roles at U.S. Cyber Command — with two hiring events on October 15 and 16.

The Defense Intelligence Agency (DIA) is hiring intelligence professionals for positions at U.S. Cyber Command — and it is taking its recruiters on the road this week. If you are interested in intelligence analysis, counterintelligence, HUMINT, or mission management and cyberspace, two hiring events on October 15 and 16, 2026 offer a direct line to DIA recruiters. Here is what is open, who should attend, and how to prepare.

The Mission: Threat Intelligence for Cyber Warfighters

DIA's role at U.S. Cyber Command centers on providing American warfighters with actionable threat intelligence in cyberspace. That means tracking foreign cyber actors, analyzing adversary capabilities and intentions, and turning raw collection into assessments that drive real-world cyber operations. It is among the most operationally relevant intelligence work in the federal government today.

Cyber Command's mission has only grown in importance as nation-state hacking, ransomware, and information operations dominate the threat landscape. Intelligence professionals supporting the command work at the nexus of national security policy, military operations, and cutting-edge technology.

Roles DIA Is Filling

DIA is recruiting across several core intelligence disciplines for its Cyber Command support mission. While individual USAJOBS announcements carry the exact position descriptions and grade levels, the hiring push covers:

  • Intelligence analysis — all-source and cyber-focused analysts who assess adversary capabilities.
  • Counterintelligence — professionals who detect and neutralize foreign intelligence threats.
  • HUMINT — human intelligence collection and operations support.
  • Mission management — coordinators who align collection and analysis with operational needs.
  • Cyberspace operations support — technical specialists bridging intelligence and cyber missions.

These roles typically require or involve eligibility for high-level security clearances, and many are located in the National Capital Region near Cyber Command's Fort Meade headquarters.

Clearances and Qualifications

Intelligence community positions at this level generally require U.S. citizenship and eligibility for a Top Secret / Sensitive Compartmented Information (TS/SCI) clearance. The October 16 in-person event is explicitly aimed at current TS/SCI holders, which tells you the hiring timeline is aggressive — DIA wants people who can start contributing quickly.

Relevant backgrounds include military intelligence occupational specialties, prior IC or law enforcement analytic experience, cybersecurity and computer science degrees, and critical foreign language skills. DIA also values the operational perspective that transitioning service members and veterans bring, which is why the October 15 virtual fair targets the military community specifically.

How to Pursue DIA Cyber Command Roles

  1. Register for the October 15 virtual Corporate Gray fair if you are a service member, veteran, or military spouse — or plan to attend the October 16 in-person event if you hold a current TS/SCI clearance.
  2. Prepare a federal-format resume highlighting intelligence, cybersecurity, or analytic experience with specific accomplishments.
  3. Search USAJOBS for DIA announcements supporting U.S. Cyber Command and note each posting's closing date and required documents.
  4. Be ready to discuss your clearance status — active clearances dramatically speed up IC hiring.
  5. Follow up after the event with the recruiters you met and submit complete USAJOBS applications before deadlines.
Event details can change: Confirm times, registration links, and attendance requirements through DIA's official channels or the event organizers before traveling or blocking your calendar. Always verify position details on the official USAJOBS announcement.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is DIA hiring for at U.S. Cyber Command?

DIA is hiring for intelligence analysis, counterintelligence, HUMINT, mission management, and cyberspace roles supporting U.S. Cyber Command's mission of providing warfighters with actionable threat intelligence in cyberspace.

When are the DIA hiring events in October 2026?

A virtual Corporate Gray fair for military members, veterans, and spouses is set for October 15, 2026, and an in-person Transition Careers event for current TS/SCI clearance holders is set for October 16, 2026.

Do I need a security clearance to apply?

Most DIA intelligence positions require eligibility for a TS/SCI clearance. Holding an active clearance, especially TS/SCI, makes you significantly more competitive and can speed up hiring.

Can veterans apply for DIA intelligence jobs?

Yes. The October 15 virtual hiring event specifically targets military members, veterans, and military spouses, and veterans' preference applies to eligible DIA civilian announcements.

Where are these positions located?

Many DIA positions supporting U.S. Cyber Command are in the National Capital Region near Fort Meade, Maryland. Check each USAJOBS announcement for the official duty location.

Life Insurance FAQ

What is the DIME method?
DIME = Debt + Income + Mortgage + Education, minus liquid assets. It's the most widely recommended formula for estimating life insurance needs.
Is 10x income enough life insurance?
The 10x rule is a quick starting point, but it ignores debts and mortgages. The DIME method above is more accurate for most families.
How much does life insurance cost?
A healthy 30-year-old can often get a $500K 20-year term policy for roughly $20–30/month. Rates rise sharply with age and health issues.

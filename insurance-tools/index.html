Frequently Asked Questions

How much life insurance do I need?
A common rule is 10–15× your annual income, but the DIME method (Debt + Income replacement + Mortgage + Education, minus assets) gives a more accurate personal number. Try our free calculator.
What affects car insurance premiums the most?
Your state, age, driving record, vehicle type, and coverage limits are the biggest factors. Young drivers and drivers with accidents pay the most. See our estimator.
Is a high deductible health plan worth it?
It depends on your expected medical costs. Compare the true yearly cost: annual premiums + expected out-of-pocket spending. Our comparator does this math.
Are these estimates financial advice?
No. All results are rough estimates for education only — not financial, legal, or insurance advice. Talk to a licensed agent before buying.

GTA 6 Crossplay: Will PS5 and Xbox Play Together?

November 20, 2026 · 4 min read

Grand Theft Auto 6 launched on November 19, 2026, on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S, and the first question many players asked was the obvious one: can PS5 and Xbox players play together? The answer is no — but not for the reason most people think. There is no crossplay in GTA 6 at launch because there is no multiplayer in GTA 6 at launch. The game is single-player only, and there is nothing to cross between. This guide explains what that means, what history tells us, and what to watch for when multiplayer eventually arrives.

That distinction matters, because a lot of the crossplay discussion online treats it as a feature Rockstar chose to leave out of a multiplayer mode. It is not. The mode itself does not exist yet. Asking whether GTA 6 has crossplay today is like asking whether a house has a shared garden before the house is built — the question will matter later, but right now there is nothing for it to apply to.

No Multiplayer, No Crossplay

The facts are simple. GTA 6 launched as a single-player experience, listed as offline play for one player. Rockstar has announced no date and no name for its multiplayer mode, and reporting points to 2027. Until that mode exists, crossplay is a question with no answer — and anyone claiming to know whether it will be included is guessing. Rockstar has never confirmed crossplay either way, for GTA 6 or for any future mode.

That silence is worth noting. Rockstar does not usually announce features years in advance, and the absence of a crossplay promise is not evidence that crossplay will never happen. It is evidence of nothing at all. The honest position is that we do not know, and we will not know until Rockstar shows the multiplayer mode itself.

Thirteen Years Without Crossplay

The history does not help the optimistic case. GTA Online — the multiplayer mode of GTA 5 — never had crossplay in thirteen years. PlayStation players played with PlayStation players, Xbox players played with Xbox players, and PC players played with PC players, for the entire life of the mode. That is a long track record, and it is the main reason many players expect GTA 6's multiplayer to launch without crossplay too.

But history is not destiny. The industry has moved a long way since 2013: crossplay is now normal in most big multiplayer games, and players expect it. Rockstar's games are built on a scale that makes crossplay technically harder than in a smaller shooter, but the expectation from players is real, and Rockstar knows it. The thirteen-year record is a reason to be cautious, not a reason to be certain.

Cross-Save and Cross-Buy: Also Unannounced

Two related questions come up just as often, and they have the same answer: nothing announced. There is no cross-save in GTA 6, which means progress made on PS5 does not carry to Xbox, and there is no cross-buy, which means buying the game on one console does not give you the other version. If you own both consoles, you need to pick one — or buy the game twice.

That matters more than it might seem, because it affects the most practical decision players face right now: which console to buy GTA 6 on. The answer is to choose based on where your friends will play when multiplayer arrives, and where you prefer to play the story today. The two decisions are linked, and both are yours to make without any help from Rockstar's announcements.

What Is Not Crossplay

Two features get confused with crossplay, and both are worth clearing up. Remote Play is not crossplay: it lets you stream the game from your own PS5 to another device, but you are still playing alone, on your own console, in a single-player game. And Xbox Cloud Gaming is not crossplay either: it lets Xbox One owners stream GTA 6 from launch, because the game was never released on Xbox One hardware, but streaming is still single-player. Neither feature puts you in a shared world with players on another console.

The confusion is understandable — both features involve playing across devices — but they are about access, not about playing together. Crossplay means two players on different platforms in the same multiplayer session. Nothing in GTA 6 does that today, because there is no multiplayer session to join.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can PS5 and Xbox players play GTA 6 together?

No. GTA 6 has no multiplayer mode at launch, so there is no crossplay. Rockstar has never confirmed whether crossplay will be included when multiplayer arrives, and GTA Online never had it in thirteen years.

Does GTA 6 have cross-save?

No cross-save or cross-buy has been announced. Progress and purchases stay on the console you bought the game on. Choose your platform based on where you want to play the story and where your friends will be when multiplayer arrives.

The Bottom Line

There is no crossplay in GTA 6 at launch — there is no multiplayer to cross at all. Rockstar has confirmed nothing either way, and GTA Online's thirteen years without crossplay is a reason for caution, not certainty. Pick your console for the story today, and wait for Rockstar to show the multiplayer mode before believing any crossplay claim.

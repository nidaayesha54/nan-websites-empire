What is EOBI?

The Employees' Old-Age Benefits Institution (EOBI) is Pakistan's federal pension scheme for private-sector workers. Employers contribute 5% of the minimum wage and employees contribute 1% every month. After retirement, the worker receives a monthly pension for life.

EOBI Pension Formula (2026)

Monthly Pension = (Average Monthly Wage × Years of Service) ÷ 50
In simple words: every year of insured service adds 2% of your average monthly wage (last 12 months) to your pension. If the formula gives less than Rs 11,500, you get the minimum of Rs 11,500.

Example

Average wage Rs 40,000 × 25 years ÷ 50 = Rs 20,000/month. A worker with Rs 30,000 wage and 15 years gets (30,000 × 15) ÷ 50 = Rs 9,000 → raised to the Rs 11,500 minimum.

2026 Updates

  • Minimum pension Rs 11,500/month (raised from Rs 10,000).
  • Formula-based pensions increased by 15%.
  • 14.5-year rule: workers with 14 years and 6 months of service are now treated as completing 15 years — previously rejected applicants can re-apply.

Eligibility

  • Age 60 (men) or 55 (women); mine workers at 55 with 10 years mining service.
  • At least 15 years of insured contributions (14.5 accepted under the 2026 ruling).
  • Employer must have actually paid the monthly contributions.
  • Invalidity pension: available after 5 years with medical board approval.
  • Survivors' pension: spouse (even after remarriage), then minor children, then parents.

How to Check Your EOBI Record

  1. Visit the official EOBI website (eobi.gov.pk).
  2. Open "Insured Person Tracking" / "Individual Information".
  3. Enter your 13-digit CNIC number (without dashes).
  4. Review your contribution history and fix missing years before retirement.

FAQs

What if I have less than 15 years of service?

You get a one-time old-age grant: one month's average wage for each year of contributions (needs 2+ years).

Is the pension paid for life?

Yes, the old-age pension is paid monthly for the pensioner's lifetime, and the spouse continues to receive the survivors' pension afterwards.

When is the pension paid each month?

Between the 1st and 5th of every month, usually via partner banks after biometric verification.

What is the 14.5-year rule?

A 2026 court ruling: 14 years and 6 months of insured service now counts as 15 years, making thousands of previously rejected workers eligible.

Does the employer have to be registered?

Yes — only insured employment (where the employer paid EOBI contributions) counts toward your pension.

What Is a Unit Converter?

A unit converter takes a measurement in one unit and expresses it in another. The magic is simple: every pair of units is linked by a conversion factor — a fixed number you multiply (or divide) by.

Example: 1 inch is defined as exactly 2.54 centimeters. So 10 inches = 10 × 2.54 = 25.4 cm. That is all a converter does — instantly and without rounding mistakes.

The Core Formulas

Length

  • Inches → cm: multiply by 2.54
  • Feet → meters: multiply by 0.3048
  • Miles → km: multiply by 1.60934

Weight

  • Pounds → kg: multiply by 0.45359237
  • Ounces → grams: multiply by 28.349523125

Temperature

  • °C → °F: (°C × 9/5) + 32
  • °F → °C: (°F − 32) × 5/9
  • °C → K: °C + 273.15

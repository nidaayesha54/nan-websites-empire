Quick Answers

How much does small business insurance cost? +
Most US small businesses pay $500–$2,500/year for a Business Owner's Policy. General liability alone averages $400–$1,500/year. Try our cost estimator for your situation.
Do I need business insurance if I have an LLC? +
Yes. An LLC shields personal assets from business debts; insurance pays for lawsuits, injuries, and damage. See the LLC vs insurance guide.
General liability vs professional liability? +
General liability covers injury and property damage claims. Professional liability (E&O) covers claims your advice or service caused financial loss. Service businesses usually need both.
Is business insurance legally required? +
Workers' compensation is required in nearly every state once you hire employees. Commercial auto is required if you own business vehicles. Other coverages are often required by clients or landlords.

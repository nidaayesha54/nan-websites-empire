How industry changes the price

Insurers price risk, and risk varies enormously:

  • Construction & trades: highest premiums — workers' comp alone can run $5+ per $100 of payroll.
  • Restaurants: above average — slip-and-fall claims, liquor exposure, equipment breakdown.
  • Retail: moderate — foot traffic and inventory drive property + liability costs.
  • Professional services: low physical risk, but E&O is the big-ticket item.
  • Home-based/freelance: lowest — often just a $400–$600 GL policy or a rider.

7 factors that raise or lower your premium

  1. Industry risk class — the single biggest factor.
  2. Revenue — higher revenue = more exposure = higher premiums.
  3. Employee count & payroll — drives workers' comp directly.
  4. Location — lawsuit-friendly states and disaster zones cost more.
  5. Claims history — clean record earns discounts; claims raise rates for years.
  6. Coverage limits & deductibles — higher deductibles cut premiums 10–25%.
  7. Years in business — established businesses with track records pay less.

How to pay less without losing protection

  • Bundle into a BOP instead of buying GL + property separately.
  • Raise deductibles to a level you could actually pay tomorrow.
  • Pay annually — monthly installments often add 3–8% in fees.
  • Shop 3+ agents — identical coverage routinely quotes 20–40% apart.
  • Document safety practices — training logs and checklists earn credits.

Workers' Comp Pricing, Explained

Why is field payroll so much more expensive to insure? +
Injury frequency and severity are far higher for manual work. A clerical class can cost $0.30–$0.50 per $100 of payroll while a roofing or framing class can exceed $20 — same formula, wildly different risk.
What is the experience mod? +
A multiplier (usually 0.50–2.00) reflecting your claims history versus similar businesses. Few claims → discount below 1.00; frequent claims → surcharge. It's the biggest lever you control after class codes.
Can I skip workers' comp if I only use contractors? +
Careful — misclassified employees are a classic audit penalty. If someone works set hours, uses your equipment, and can't work for others, states often treat them as employees regardless of the 1099.
Is this a real quote? +
No. This tool uses illustrative sample rates for education. Real pricing needs exact class codes and underwriting — talk to a licensed agent.

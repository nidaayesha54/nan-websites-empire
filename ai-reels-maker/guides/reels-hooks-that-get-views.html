50+ Reels Hooks That Get Views (Copy-Paste Formulas)

The hook is the first 3 seconds of your reel — the line (and visual) that stops the scroll. Below are 50+ proven hook formulas across 5 categories. Replace the brackets with your niche, or skip the manual work and use our free Viral Hook Generator for unlimited niche-specific hooks with copy buttons.

❓ Question hooks

Questions create an open loop the brain wants to close.

  • Why is nobody talking about [topic]?
  • Are you sabotaging [topic] without knowing it?
  • What if everything you knew about [topic] was wrong?
  • Still struggling with [topic]? Watch this.
  • Why does this [topic] only take 10 minutes?
  • Have you been doing [topic] wrong your whole life?
  • What would you do with [result] from [topic]?
  • Why doesn't anyone teach this about [topic]?
  • Are you busy or just avoiding [topic]?
  • What is [thing] trying to tell you?

⚡ Shock hooks

Bold claims and pattern interrupts that demand attention.

  • Stop doing [topic] like this.
  • I wasted 2 years on [topic] before learning this.
  • Nobody tells beginners this about [topic].
  • This [topic] truth will make you angry.
  • Banks don't want you to know this about [topic].
  • Rich people treat [topic] completely differently.
  • This [topic] broke my group chat.
  • Nobody believes this cost under $25.
  • Your comfort zone is killing [topic].
  • This AI does [topic] in 10 seconds.

📋 List hooks

Numbers promise structured, skimmable value.

  • 3 [topic] mistakes killing your progress
  • 5 [topic] tips that actually work in 2026
  • 7 signs [topic] is working (you'll miss #4)
  • The only 3 [topic] rules you need
  • 3 ingredients. One viral [topic].
  • 5 [topic] upgrades under $10
  • 7-day [topic] under $500 — full breakdown
  • 5 free AI tools for [topic]
  • 3 [topic] habits of unstoppable people
  • 5 signs [thing] loves you

📖 Story hooks

Stories trigger curiosity about the outcome.

  • How I fixed [topic] in 30 days
  • I tried this [topic] hack for 90 days — results shocked me
  • My [topic] transformation: what actually changed
  • Day 1 vs day 60 of fixing [topic]
  • From paycheck-to-paycheck to saving 30%: my [topic] story
  • I cooked this [topic] for a chef — his reaction…
  • Thrift flip: $8 jacket to designer look
  • My $400 [trip] that felt like $4,000
  • 365 days of [topic]: the honest recap
  • I let AI run [topic] for 30 days

🚫 Myth-bust hooks

Challenging beliefs sparks comments — comments boost reach.

  • The biggest myth about [topic] — debunked
  • [Topic] advice from 2019 is holding you back
  • Stop believing this [topic] lie
  • Experts don't want you to know this about [topic]
  • The [topic] lie keeping you stuck
  • Motivation is a myth — do this instead
  • Stop following this outdated [topic] advice
  • Fashion rules that are officially dead
  • Travel influencers lie about [topic]
  • Why most AI [topic] advice is wrong

How to use these formulas

  1. Match the hook to the payoff. Never use a shock hook for a mild tip — clickbait mismatch kills trust and retention.
  2. Put it on screen too. Say the hook AND burn it in as text — most viewers start muted.
  3. Test 3 formats per week. Track which category gets the best 3-second retention, then double down.
  4. Generate, don't guess. Our Hook Generator produces 10 niche-tuned hooks per click — build a swipe file of 100+.

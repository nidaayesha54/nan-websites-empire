7 AI Reels Mistakes Killing Your Views (2026)

Your reels aren't flopping because the algorithm hates you. They're flopping because of fixable mistakes. Here are the seven silent reach-killers we see most — and the 10-minute fix for each.

1. The 5-second throat-clear

Opening with "Hey guys, welcome back…" burns the only seconds that matter. Fix: start mid-sentence on the payoff. Generate openers with our Hook Generator.

2. No burned-in captions

85% of viewers start muted. No captions = instant swipe. Fix: CapCut auto-captions, one tap, karaoke style.

3. Robotic AI voiceover pacing

Default TTS reads like a GPS. Fix: bump speed to 108–112%, add 0.3s pauses before punchlines, and cut filler words from the script.

4. Three ideas in one reel

Cramming tips dilutes retention — viewers leave confused. Fix: one reel, one idea. Use the Script Writer to enforce hook → body → CTA structure.

5. Mixed niches on one account

Fitness Monday, finance Tuesday, cooking Wednesday confuses the algorithm about who to show you to. Fix: one niche per account for 90 days.

6. Posting at random times

Sporadic posting never lets the algorithm learn your slot. Fix: 7–9 PM ET daily at the same time — build the calendar with our Posting Planner.

7. Engagement bait

"Comment YES if you agree" gets flagged as bait and throttled. Fix: ask genuine questions your niche actually debates — see myth-bust hooks.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a vet visit cost in 2026?
A routine checkup typically costs $50–$100. Add vaccines ($75–$150), dental cleanings ($300–$800), or diagnostics and the total climbs fast. Use our free estimator for your region and pet type.
Why are vet bills so expensive?
Vet clinics use the same advanced equipment and training as human medicine, without the insurance subsidies most people have. Read why vet bills are so expensive for the full breakdown.
Is pet insurance worth it?
It depends on breed risk, age, and your savings. Run your numbers through our insurance vs. self-insuring analyzer to see your personal break-even point.
How much should I save for a pet emergency?
Aim for $2,000–$5,000 to start, more for high-risk breeds or seniors. Our emergency fund calculator gives you a personalized target.
Are these estimates veterinary advice?
No. Cost estimates only — not veterinary advice. Always consult your veterinarian for medical guidance about your pet.

GTA 6 Animal Care: Pets, Wildlife and What Happens to Them

November 20, 2026 · 6 min read

Leonida has more animals than any Grand Theft Auto world before it — over 170 species, from alligators in the Grassrivers to cats napping on top of ATMs in Vice City. And for the first time in the series, the game lets you do something with them: walk up to a dog and you get three choices — pet it, scold it or study it. That small menu is the heart of GTA 6's animal system, and this guide explains how animal care works in a world with no vet clinics and no pet ownership.

The first thing to understand is what the game does not have. There is no veterinary clinic in GTA 6, no way to take an injured animal to a doctor, and no way to adopt a pet and take it home. What the game does have is a wildlife system built on observation, interaction and enforcement — and for players who love animals, it is one of the richest parts of the world.

Over 170 species, from alligators to iguanas

Game Informer confirmed on September 29, 2026 that GTA 6 includes more than 170 animal species across land, air and sea, with more than 60 kinds of birds and over 30 land mammals. Rockstar later told the Guardian the bird count stands at 62 species. It is the biggest animal list in GTA history, and it covers Florida's real ecosystem: flamingos, manatees, pelicans and alligators as natives, and green iguanas, cane toads, feral cats and rhesus macaque monkeys as invasive species.

The animals are spread across every habitat in the state. Alligators turn up in swamps, urban canals, backyard swimming pools and even inside a convenience store. Flamingos wade and take off over the marshes. Dolphins travel in pods through open water, and a hammerhead shark patrols the reef channels. In the city, dogs are on every beach, path and porch, cats sleep on ATMs and rooftops, and iguanas cross roads and climb palm trunks — just like in real Florida.

Pet, scold and study

Walk up to an animal and the game gives you options. Jason meets a dog in the opening of the Extended Look showcase and the prompts read Pet, Scold and Study — and those three verbs cover the whole interaction system. Petting a dog is the friendliest thing you can do in the game, and one report says it boosts Jason's reputation, though that is single-source and should be treated as reported rather than confirmed. Scolding is for animals that get in your way. Studying is the important one: examining an animal ticks it off a checklist in your wildlife journal, the same kind of system Red Dead Redemption 2 used for its compendium.

Birdwatching works the same way, through binoculars: spot a bird, log it, and it goes into the journal. Animals come in Rare, Discoverable and Legendary tiers, and the rare and legendary ones can be tracked down — though Rockstar has not said which animals belong to which tier. The journal is the game's way of turning animal watching into a goal, and for completionists it is one more list to finish alongside the collectibles.

Dogs: the pets you cannot keep

Four dog breeds are confirmed in GTA 6: chihuahuas, Dobermans, Rottweilers and German Shepherds, with a Miniature Pinscher running on a beach, a Doberman sitting on a sidewalk and a Yorkshire Terrier being carried by a woman also spotted in the footage. A long-furred German Shepherd appears in newer screenshots, and a chihuahua in a purple tutu closes the final montage of the Extended Look.

You can pet them, scold them and study them — but you cannot own one. No pet ownership has been announced, and no adoption system exists in the game. That is a deliberate choice, and it is worth saying plainly because the question comes up constantly: the dogs of Leonida are part of the world, not part of your inventory. The closest thing to a companion animal is Jason's military working dog, which appears at his side on a job — and even that dog belongs to the mission, not to you.

Working dogs and guard dogs

Not every dog in Leonida is friendly. Police K-9 units chase you down and pin you when the wanted level climbs, following your scent through mud, sawgrass and shallow creeks — and the only reliable ways to lose them are deep water, scent-masking sprays or crossing through animal habitats where alligator scent disrupts the trail. Chained pit bulls guard properties, and their owners will set them on you if you stare at the wrong one for too long.

That is the other side of animal care in GTA 6: the animals are not decoration, they are part of the systems. A guard dog is a security system. A K-9 is a pursuit mechanic. An alligator in a swimming pool is a hazard. The game treats its animals as living parts of the world, with jobs of their own — and the player's job is to learn which ones are safe to pet and which ones are not.

Hunting, fishing and POACH

The care system has a harder edge too. Hunting earns money from Chapter 2, and it is one of the best-paying honest jobs in the game — track a target animal across Lummox County and each trip can earn hundreds to thousands of dollars. Fishing is the opposite: catch and release only, so fish cannot be kept or sold. The two activities sit side by side as the game's statement about its wildlife: some animals are a resource, some are a sight to enjoy, and the game lets you decide which is which.

Enforcing the line is POACH — Protection of Animals and Controlled Hunting, Leonida's wildlife agency, the version of Florida's Fish and Wildlife Conservation Commission. POACH officers appear in the first trailer and chase poachers through the Grassrivers by airboat. They are the game's animal police, and they are the reason the wildlife system has teeth: hunt the wrong animal in the wrong place, and someone will come looking for you.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you adopt a dog in GTA 6?

No. Pet ownership has not been announced. You can pet, scold and study dogs, but you cannot take one home.

Is there a vet in GTA 6?

No vet clinic has been shown. Animal care in the game means the pet, scold and study system, the wildlife journal, hunting and POACH enforcement — not medical treatment.

How many animals are in GTA 6?

Over 170 species, including 62 bird species and more than 30 land mammals, per Game Informer and Rockstar. Alligators, flamingos, manatees, pelicans, dogs, cats, iguanas and sharks are all confirmed.

The bottom line: Leonida's animals are not pets and not scenery — they are a system. Pet the friendly ones, study the rare ones, respect the guard dogs, and remember that POACH is watching.

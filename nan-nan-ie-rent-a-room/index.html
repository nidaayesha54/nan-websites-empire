How Rent-a-Room Relief Works

If you rent a room (or rooms) in your principal private residence, the first €14,000 of gross rental income per year is exempt from income tax, USC and PRSI. Key rules for 2026:

  • The €14,000 cliff: stay at or under it and you pay zero tax. Exceed it by any amount and the entire rental income becomes taxable — there is no tapering.
  • Gross means gross: include any payments for meals, laundry, heating or other utilities in the €14,000.
  • Residential use: for 2026 the letting must be for residential purposes — generally stays of more than 28 consecutive days. Students in term-time digs are excepted.
  • Short-term lets (under 28 days) such as Airbnb-style hosting do not qualify and are taxed as normal rental income.
  • No expenses: you cannot deduct expenses from the rent while claiming the relief.
  • It does not apply to lettings between connected parties, and the room must be in the home you actually live in.

Worked Example

Charge €900/month for 12 months = €10,800/year. That is inside the €14,000 limit, so the full €10,800 is tax-free — saving roughly €2,160 in income tax at the 20% rate (or €4,320 at 40%). Raise the rent to €1,200/month = €14,400/year and the relief is lost entirely: all €14,400 becomes taxable.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much rent is tax-free in Ireland under Rent-a-Room?

€14,000 per year of gross rental income for 2026 — income tax, USC and PRSI free — provided the room is in your principal private residence and the letting is residential.

What happens if I go over €14,000?

The whole amount becomes taxable, not just the excess. This "cliff" is the most important feature of the relief — a €14,001 rental year is taxed in full.

Do meals and bills I charge count toward the limit?

Yes. The €14,000 covers rent plus any amounts paid for food, laundry, heating or other services connected with the letting.

Can I claim expenses as well?

No — if you claim Rent-a-Room Relief you cannot deduct expenses. (If you go over the limit and lose the relief, normal rental-expense deductions apply.)

Does Airbnb income qualify?

Generally no: short-term lets under 28 days are excluded for 2026. Students and long-stay residential tenants are the qualifying cases.

Do I need to register or file anything?

You still declare the income on your tax return and claim the relief. Keep records of rent received and tenancy dates.
